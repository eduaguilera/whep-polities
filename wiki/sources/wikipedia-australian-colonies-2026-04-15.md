# Australian colonies — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

To establish sourced start dates and federation-end dates for the six pre-federation Australian colonies so the WHEP precolonial-Australia chain (`AUS-1901-2025` predecessors) has external citations beyond `[database]`.

## What it adds

Key dates per colony (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-04-15):

- **Colony of New South Wales**: founded 7 February 1788 (First Fleet, Arthur Phillip); Tasmania separated 3 December 1825; Victoria separated 1 July 1851; Queensland separated 6 June 1859; federated 1 January 1901.
- **Colony of Victoria**: proclaimed separate colony 1 July 1851 (carved from southeastern NSW); federated 1 January 1901.
- **Colony of Queensland**: proclaimed 6 June 1859 by Letters Patent from Queen Victoria, formally separating from NSW; federated 1 January 1901.
- **Van Diemen's Land / Tasmania**: proclaimed independent colony 3 December 1825 (Darling); renamed Tasmania 1 January 1856; federated 1 January 1901.
- **Colony of South Australia**: proclaimed at Glenelg 28 December 1836 (Governor Hindmarsh); federated 1 January 1901.
- **Colony of Western Australia (Swan River Colony)**: founded 12 August 1829 (James Stirling); renamed Colony of Western Australia 6 February 1832; federated 1 January 1901.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia snapshots, not peer-reviewed. Dates are the conventional legal/ceremonial proclamation dates; *de facto* European settlement and tribal displacement extend earlier and continue after these dates. Territorial adjustments between colonies (e.g., NSW ceding land to SA, Queensland's Gulf of Carpentaria changes) are not captured in these single-year datapoints.
