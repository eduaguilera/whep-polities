# France in the long nineteenth century / French colonial empire

## Why it was ingested

Provide territorial-change dates and regime-transition dates for
the FRA-1800-1919 polity page. This is the initial Wikipedia
baseline for a draft page; it will be superseded by Biger 1995
and other academic sources in future ingests.

## What it adds

<a id="1815"></a>
### §1815 — Congress of Vienna

France was "granted a quite generous peace settlement, being
restored to its 1792 boundaries" after the Napoleonic Wars. All
Napoleonic conquests in Europe were lost.

<a id="1830-algeria"></a>
### §1830-algeria — French invasion of Algeria

"In 1830, France invaded Algeria, and in 1848 this north African
country was fully integrated into France as a department."
Algeria was "fully conquered by 1903" per the colonial empire
article.

<a id="1860-nice-savoy"></a>
### §1860-nice-savoy — Nice and Savoy annexation

"Piedmont ceded its provinces of Nice and Savoy to France in
March 1860." Context: reward for French support against Austria
in the 1859 war. docs/03_ENTRIES_RATIONALE.md:99 records this as
8,261 km².

<a id="1871-alsace-lorraine"></a>
### §1871-alsace-lorraine — Loss of Alsace-Lorraine

"With the French defeat in the Franco-Prussian War of 1870,
France lost Alsace and portions of Lorraine to Germany." The
Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871) formalized the cession. The
CShapes polygon for France pre-1919 reflects this loss
(62.43 sq degrees vs 64.35 post-1919).

<a id="1881-tunisia"></a>
### §1881-tunisia — Tunisia protectorate

"The French protectorate of Tunisia lasted from 1881 to 1956."
"Groundwork for occupation was laid on April 24, 1881, when the
French deployed 35,000 troops." Cross-reference: Biger 1995
gives the Treaty of Bardo date as 12 May 1881
[biger-1995 §tunisia].

<a id="1919-versailles"></a>
### §1919-versailles — Treaty of Versailles

Alsace-Lorraine returned to France. CShapes records the polygon
change at 1919-06-28 (Treaty of Versailles signing date). The
area increase of ~1.92 sq degrees represents Alsace-Lorraine.

<a id="population"></a>
### §population — Population data

~28 million (1789), 36 million (1850), ~39 million (1880).
Industrial workforce: 23% in 1870, 39% in 1914. Farm population:
40% still farmers in 1914.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia is not an academic source. The article gives no
verbatim Treaty of Frankfurt text and provides few specific
day-precision dates for the 1815 settlement. Colonial dates
are sparse — the Indochina section was not fully extracted.
Biger 1995's FRANCE entry (not yet ingested) will provide
exact boundary dates.
