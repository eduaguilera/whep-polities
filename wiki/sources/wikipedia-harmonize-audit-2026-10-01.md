# Wikipedia articles for the harmonize-audit territories (accessed 2026-10-01)

## Why it was ingested

A polity audit of a colleague's harmonized IIA/FAO dataset (source slug
[whep-normalize](whep-normalize.md), § The harmonized dataset) found territories that the dataset
reports on their own and that had no WHEP polity. Each new page needed an external source for the
dates and extents it rests on. Fetched with the MediaWiki API
(`action=query&prop=extracts&explaintext=1`); the quotations below are verbatim from those extracts,
with section-heading markers removed.

## What it adds

### johor

- "Johor became a British protected state within the Unfederated Malay States in 1914, a position interrupted only by the Japanese occupation of Malaya."
  ([Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor))

### kedah

- "In 1896, Kedah along with Perlis and Setul were combined into the Siamese province of Monthon Syburi which lasted until it was transferred to the British by the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909."
  ([Kedah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kedah))

### kelantan

- "Under the terms of the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909, the Thais relinquished their claims over Kelantan, Terengganu, Kedah and Perlis to the British Empire, and Kelantan thus became one of the Unfederated Malay States with a British Adviser."
- "Kelantan became part of the Malayan Union in 1946 and then the Federation of Malaya on 1 February 1948, and together with other Malayan states attained independence on 31 August 1957."
  ([Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan))

### perlis

- "As with Kedah, the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909 forced Siam to relinquish its southern Malay vassal states of Kelantan, Trengganu, and Monthon Syburi (comprising Kedah, Perlis, and Satun (but Satun remained with Thailand)) to Great Britain."
  ([Perlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perlis))

### terengganu

- "The terms of the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909 saw power over Terengganu transferred from Siam to Britain."
- "A British advisor was appointed to the sultan in 1919, and Terengganu become one of the Unfederated Malay States."
  ([Terengganu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terengganu))

### perak

- "The Sultan of Perak then ceded to the British Dindings and Pangkor (the two now constitute Manjung District) so that the British could suppress pirate activity along the Perak coast where it became part of the Straits Settlements."
  ([Perak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perak))

### selangor

- "In 1896, largely through the coordination of the Resident Frank Swettenham, Selangor united with Negeri Sembilan, Perak and Pahang to form the Federated Malay States, with Kuala Lumpur as its capital."
- "In 1974, Selangor relinquished Kuala Lumpur to the federal government."
  ([Selangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selangor))

### negeri-sembilan

- "The domains of Sungai Ujong and Jelebu joined this confederation in 1895, forming the state of Negeri Sembilan in its modern borders."
- "Martin Lister became the new state's first Resident, and Negeri Sembilan became part of the Federated Malay States the same year."
  ([Negeri Sembilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negeri_Sembilan))

### pahang

- "In July 1895, Sultan Ahmad signed the Federation Agreement, which made Pahang, along with Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan, one of the Federated Malay States, a protectorate of the British Empire."
  ([Pahang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahang))

### penang

- "In 1800, Lieutenant-Governor George Leith secured a strip of hinterland across the Penang Strait, which was subsequently named Province Wellesley (now Seberang Perai)."
- "Penang was placed under British military administration until 1946, after which the Straits Settlements was abolished."
  ([Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang))

### malacca

- "Following the war, the British dissolved the Straits Settlements and placed Malacca under the Malayan Union in 1946, which was reconstituted as the Federation of Malaya in 1948."
  ([Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malacca))

### labuan

- "The proposal took effect from 1 January 1907."
- "Labuan then on 15 July 1946 joined the North Borneo Crown Colony, which in turn became the state of Sabah and Malaysia in 1963."
  ([Labuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labuan))

### straits-settlements-members

- "The Straits Settlements originally consisted of the four individual settlements of Penang, Singapore, Malacca and Dinding."
- "The island of Labuan, off the coast of Borneo, was also incorporated into the colony with effect from 1 January 1907, becoming a separate settlement within it in 1912."
  ([Straits Settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straits_Settlements))

### leopoldville

- "At the time of independence, the area now encompassing Kongo Central was part of the greater province of Léopoldville, along with the capital city of Kinshasa and the districts of Kwango, Kwilu and Mai-Ndombe."
  ([Kongo Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongo_Central))
- "It was formally established in 1919, and in 1933 was divided into the new provinces of Léopoldville and Lusambo."
- "The original four provinces had considerable autonomy, but in 1933 they were reorganized into six provinces, named after their capitals, and the central government assumed more control."
  ([Congo-Kasaï](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo-Kas%C3%A4%C3%AF))
- "Under the first constitution, the Loi Fondamentale, six provinces were provided for: Equateur, Kasai, Katanga, Kivu, Leopoldville, and Orientale."
- "In August 1962, 16 additional provinces were promulgated."
  ([Provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo))

### kwantung

- "The Kwantung Leased Territory (...) was a leased territory of the Empire of Japan in the Liaodong Peninsula from 1905 to 1945."
- "The leased area extended to the northern shore of Yadang Bay on the western side of the peninsula; on the eastern side it reached Pikou; Yevgeni Ivanovich Alekseyev, chief of Russian Pacific Fleet, became the head of this territory."
- "The area of the territory was 3,500 square kilometres (1,350 sq mi)."
- "After the foundation of Japanese-controlled Manchukuo in 1932, Japan regarded the sovereignty of the leased territory as transferred from China to Manchukuo."
  ([Kwantung Leased Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwantung_Leased_Territory))

### northern-nigeria

- "Lugard left the protectorate after some years, serving in Hong Kong, but was eventually returned to work in Nigeria, where in 1914 he sought the merger of the Northern Nigeria Protectorate with Southern Nigeria, creating the Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria."
- "The High Commissioner or Governor of Northern Nigeria, originally the High Commissioner of the Northern Nigeria Protectorate, after 1914 the Lieutenant Governor, Chief Commissioner, or Governor-General of the Northern Provinces of Nigeria, was effectively the viceroy of Northern Nigeria, exercising British suzerainty as representative of the Crown."
  ([Northern Region, Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Region,_Nigeria))

### southern-nigeria

- "In 1914, Southern Nigeria was joined with Northern Nigeria Protectorate to form the single colony of Nigeria."
  ([Southern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Nigeria_Protectorate))

### german-new-guinea

- "The mainland part of the territory, called Kaiser-Wilhelmsland, became a German protectorate in 1884."
- "The Bismarck Archipelago (New Britain, New Ireland and several smaller islands), and the North Solomon Islands were declared a German protectorate in 1885."
- "Following the outbreak of World War I in 1914, Kaiser-Wilhelmsland and nearby islands fell to Australian forces, while Japan occupied most of the remaining German possessions in the Pacific."
- "The mainland part of German New Guinea (Kaiser-Wilhelmsland), the Bismarck Archipelago and the North Solomon Islands are now part of Papua New Guinea."
  ([German New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_New_Guinea))

### gilbert-and-ellice

- "The Gilbert Islands and Ellice Islands (the colonial name of Tuvalu) were made British protectorates in 1892 and placed under the jurisdiction of the Western Pacific High Commission."
- "In 1916, the protectorate was made a Crown colony as the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony (GEIC)."
  ([Gilbert and Ellice Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_and_Ellice_Islands))

### bosnia

- "Bosnia and Herzegovina came under Austro-Hungarian administration in 1878, when the Congress of Berlin approved the occupation of the Bosnia Vilayet, which officially remained part of the Ottoman Empire."
- "In 1908, Austria-Hungary provoked the Bosnian crisis by formally annexing the occupied zone, establishing the Condominium of Bosnia and Herzegovina."
  ([Austro-Hungarian rule in Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_rule_in_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina))

### bohemia-moravia

- "The Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia was a partially annexed territory of Nazi Germany that was established on 16 March 1939 after the German occupation of the Czech lands."
- "After the Munich Agreement of September 1938, the German Reich had annexed the German-majority Sudetenland to Germany from Czechoslovakia in October 1938."
- "The state's existence came to an end with the surrender of Germany to the Allies in May 1945."
  ([Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectorate_of_Bohemia_and_Moravia))

### roc-retreat

- "Republic of China Government's retreat to Taiwan or the Great Retreat (Chinese: 大撤退) was an event where the remnants of the government of the Republic of China (ROC), alongside many refugees, retreated to the island of Taiwan (Formosa) beginning on December 7, 1949."
  ([Republic of China Government's retreat to Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retreat_of_the_government_of_the_Republic_of_China_to_Taiwan))
