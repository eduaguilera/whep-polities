# Eritrea — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-13)

## Why it was ingested

To establish the correct Eritrea chain with sourced dates for
territorial changes: Italian coastal presence (1885), colony
proclamation (1889/1890), British capture (1941), federation with
Ethiopia (1952), independence (1993).

## What it adds

Key dates from three Wikipedia articles:
- **Eritrea**: 1869 Assab purchase, 1882 government control,
  1885-02-05 Massawa occupation, 1889 Treaty of Wuchale,
  1993-05-24 independence referendum, 1993-05-28 UN admission.
- **Italian Eritrea**: 1890-01-01 colony proclamation,
  1889-05-02 Treaty of Wuchale, 1882-07-05 Assab, 1941-05-19
  British capture.
- **Federation of Ethiopia and Eritrea**: 1952-09-15 federation
  established, 1962-11-14 annexed as province.
