# Second-order dangling-reference gaps — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

After filling the 37 originally-dangling references, 8 second-order dangling references surfaced (predecessors referenced by the newly-created pages). This source documents the key dated facts for those 8 rows.

## What it adds

- **Oyo Empire**: peak c.1680 ("spanned over 150,000 square kilometres"); Ilorin secession 1817 (joined Sokoto); Dahomey revolt c.1823; Oyo-Ile capital destroyed 1835 by Ilorin forces; collapse by 1836.
- **Gobir**: Hausa state; Usman dan Fodio declared jihad 21 February 1804; capital Alkalawa fell 3 October 1808, Sarki Yunfa killed; independent state ended.
- **Colony of Natal**: "proclaimed a British colony on 4 May 1843 after the British government had annexed the Boer Republic of Natalia." Zululand annexed to Natal 1897 (not 1895 as WHEP CSV suggests).
- **Côte d'Ivoire 1889-1893** (CIV-1889-1893): French protectorate period preceding the 10 March 1893 colony decree.
- **Congo 1898-1900** (COG-1898-1900): French Congo administrative period preceding the 1900 CShapes boundary cut.
- **Kenya 1894-1902** (KEN-1894-1902): East Africa Protectorate established 1 July 1895 under Foreign Office; boundaries extended 1902 with Eastern Province of Uganda transfer.
- **Libya 1934-1943** (LBY-1934-1943): Unified Italian Libya 1934 (merger of Italian North Africa colonies); Allied invasion 1943 ending Italian control.
- **Morocco 1904-1956** (MOR-1904-1956): French-Spanish Entente Cordiale 1904 setting spheres of influence; Treaty of Fez 30 March 1912 establishing French Protectorate; French Protectorate ended 2 March 1956; Spanish Protectorate northern zone returned 7 April 1956.
