# The St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha Constitution Order 2009

## Source and scope

Order 4(a), p 2 renames the existing territory; constitutional preamble(d)–(e), p 12 recalls Ascension joining in 1922 and Tristan, Gough, Nightingale, Inaccessible in 1938; preamble(j) calls them separate territories forming a single grouping. The 1938 statement is retrospective statutory testimony, not the original Letters Patent. Subordinate legislation is a primary historical source for its own enactment.
