# Austria-Hungary (Wikipedia snapshot, 2026-04-11)

## Why it was ingested

Austria-Hungary is one of the most important European empires
1850–1918. The WHEP CSV tracks it with three rows (AUH-1800-1867,
AUH-1867-1908, AUH-1908-1918) whose splits correspond to the 1867
Ausgleich and the 1908 Bosnia annexation — both constitutional /
sovereignty events rather than territorial changes, which is
inconsistent with the WHEP polity-definition rule as written. This
source file provides the narrative dates needed to audit the three
polity rows and to file a proposal entry for the CSV audit findings.

## What it adds

Claims are tagged with the event date where Wikipedia gives one.
Quotes are short and verbatim from the 2026-04-11 snapshot.

<a id="1804-08-11"></a>
- **§1804-08-11 — Austrian Empire proclaimed.** Franz II assumed
  the new title Emperor of Austria in response to Napoleon's
  declaration of the French Empire. Marks the formal start of the
  "Austrian Empire" as a political entity; pre-1804 the Habsburg
  monarchy was the Archduchy of Austria + associated lands within
  the Holy Roman Empire.

<a id="1859"></a>
- **§1859 — Loss of Lombardy.** The Second Italian War of
  Independence (April–July 1859) ended with Austria ceding
  Lombardy to the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont via the Treaty of
  Zurich, a real territorial loss of the Austrian Empire within
  the AUH-1800-1867 row.

<a id="1866"></a>
- **§1866 — Austro-Prussian War and loss of Venetia.** The
  seven-week Austro-Prussian War (June–August 1866) ended with
  the Peace of Prague. Austria was excluded from German affairs
  (the peace settlement "settled the 'German question' in favor
  of a Lesser German Solution") and ceded Venetia to the Kingdom
  of Italy. A real territorial loss within the AUH-1800-1867 row.

<a id="1867"></a>
- **§1867 — Austro-Hungarian Compromise (Ausgleich).** The
  Compromise was formally established 30 March 1867 and
  promulgated 28 July 1867: "On 28 July, Franz Joseph, in his new
  capacity as King of Hungary, approved and promulgated the new
  laws, which officially gave birth to the Dual Monarchy."
  **This is a constitutional change, not a territorial change**
  — no territory was added or lost by the Ausgleich itself. The
  Austrian Empire simply became the dual state of Austria-Hungary
  under the same monarch (Franz Joseph I), with a divided
  parliament and shared ministries for foreign affairs, military,
  and finance.

<a id="1878"></a>
- **§1878 — Congress of Berlin mandate for Bosnia occupation.**
  Austria "occupied (but did not annex) the provinces of Bosnia
  and Herzegovina, a predominantly Slavic area. The occupation
  required 150,000 troops." Bosnia remained legally Ottoman until
  1908. Bosnia was part of the Austro-Hungarian customs union from
  1879 onward, however — meaning under WHEP's
  territorial-economic polity definition, Bosnia was already
  Austro-Hungarian *economically* from 1879, regardless of de
  jure sovereignty.

<a id="1908-10-06"></a>
- **§1908-10-06 — Formal annexation of Bosnia-Herzegovina.**
  Austria-Hungary converted the 1878 occupation into full
  annexation on this date, "replacing the occupation arrangement
  with full annexation under joint Cisleithanian-Transleithanian
  control." This is the trigger date for the WHEP AUH-1867-1908 →
  AUH-1908-1918 split, but it is a **de jure sovereignty change,
  not a territorial or economic change**. The territory had been
  part of the Austro-Hungarian customs union for 30 years.

<a id="1914-06-28"></a>
- **§1914-06-28 — Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand** in
  Sarajevo by Gavrilo Princip, triggering the chain of events
  leading to WWI.

<a id="1914-07-28"></a>
- **§1914-07-28 — Austria-Hungary declares war on Serbia.** The
  declaration started A-H's active participation in WWI.

<a id="1918-10-31"></a>
- **§1918-10-31 — Empire formally dissolved.** "Hungary's
  termination of union with Austria" effectively ended the dual
  monarchy. The component nations declared independence on a
  rolling basis from late October through November 1918.

<a id="1918-11-03"></a>
- **§1918-11-03 — Armistice of Villa Giusti.** Austria-Hungary's
  military signed the armistice, ending combat operations in
  WWI. This is the date the WHEP CSV uses as the end of the
  AUH-1908-1918 row (1918 without day precision).

<a id="1919-09-10"></a>
- **§1919-09-10 — Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye.** "Officially
  settled Austria's status as successor state." Defined the
  borders of the First Austrian Republic and formally recognized
  the loss of Bohemia/Moravia, Slovenia, Croatia, Slavonia,
  Galicia, Trentino, Trieste, Istria, and other Austrian
  Crownlands. Biger's Austria entry (p.47) uses "dismemberment in
  1919" rather than 1918.

<a id="1920-06-04"></a>
- **§1920-06-04 — Treaty of Trianon.** Officially settled
  Hungary's status as successor state. Hungary lost approximately
  two-thirds of its pre-war territory to Czechoslovakia, Romania
  (Transylvania), Yugoslavia, and Austria.

<a id="successor-states"></a>
- **§successor-states — Seven entities emerged from the
  dissolution:**
  1. **Republic of German-Austria** → First Austrian Republic
     (WHEP: AUT-1918-*)
  2. **First Hungarian Republic** / Kingdom of Hungary
     (WHEP: HUN-1918-*)
  3. **First Czechoslovak Republic**
     (WHEP: F51-1918-1938 or CZE)
  4. **Second Polish Republic** (gained Galicia from A-H)
     (WHEP: POL-1918-*)
  5. **Kingdom of Yugoslavia** (gained Slovenia, Croatia,
     Slavonia, Vojvodina, Bosnia; founded as Kingdom of Serbs,
     Croats and Slovenes December 1918)
     (WHEP: F248-1918-*)
  6. **Kingdom of Italy** (gained Trentino, Trieste, Istria,
     South Tyrol) (WHEP: ITA-1919-2025; no separate 1918–1919
     row)
  7. **Kingdom of Romania** (gained Transylvania, Banat,
     Bukovina) (WHEP: ROU-1918-*)

## Known limitations

1. **Wikipedia is a tertiary source.** For archival-grade dating
   of treaties and events, a history monograph or primary-source
   collection would be stronger. The dates here are cross-checked
   against the Austria-Hungary main article only and should be
   corroborated against a second source (Biger 1995 for boundary
   claims is the natural second source; both are already in the
   wiki).
2. **Date conflicts with other wiki snapshots.** The
   `wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11` source has "Bulgarian
   Declaration of Independence on 5 October" (1908) while this
   Austria-Hungary article gives the Bosnia annexation as
   1908-10-06 (one day later). These are two different events on
   adjacent days in the same week; they are not contradictory
   but citations should be careful to distinguish them.
3. **"Formal dissolution 1918-10-31" is the Austrian Empire
   side.** Hungary's formal dissolution (Hungarian Declaration
   of Independence from Austria) happened on 31 October 1918,
   but Austria-Hungary as a treaty-bound state did not legally
   cease to exist until Saint-Germain (September 1919) and
   Trianon (June 1920). The WHEP CSV uses 1918 as the end date,
   which is defensible but represents the earlier of several
   possible dissolution moments.
4. **The successor list here is comprehensive for trade and
   territorial purposes**, but does not distinguish the First
   Austrian Republic from the intermediate "Republic of
   German-Austria" (12 November 1918 — 21 October 1919), which
   is the entity that probably corresponds to WHEP's
   `AUT-1918-1919` row.
