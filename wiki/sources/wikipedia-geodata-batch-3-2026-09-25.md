# Wikipedia articles for the third geodata batch (accessed 2026-09-25)

## Why it was ingested

The third geodata batch (2026-09-25) gave polygons or rows to five territories that FAO 1952 reports
on their own: the Tangier Zone, Aden Colony, the Dodecanese, and Berlin as a whole city. Each page
needed an external source for the dates and extents it rests on. Fetched with the MediaWiki API
(`action=query&prop=extracts&explaintext=1`); the quotations below are verbatim from those extracts.

## What it adds

### tangier

- "The Tangier International Zone ... was a 382 km2 (147 sq mi) international zone centered on the
  city of Tangier, Morocco, which existed from 1925 until its reintegration into independent Morocco
  in 1956, with interruption during the Spanish occupation of Tangier (1940–1945)".
- "Later that year [1912], Article 7 of the Treaty Between France and Spain Regarding Morocco
  stipulated that Tangier would be granted a special status and defined its geographical
  boundaries."
- "the Tangier International Zone eventually started on 1 June 1925 under a Dahir of 15 May 1925."
- "Spanish troops occupied Tangier on 14 June 1940 ... Tangier was effectively annexed to the Spanish
  Protectorate of Morocco on 23 November 1940." The Anglo-French Agreement "entered into force on
  11 October 1945, when the Spanish government withdrew its military force".
  ([Tangier International Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangier_International_Zone))

### aden-colony

- "Aden Colony ... was a crown colony of the United Kingdom from 1937 to 1963 located in the southern
  part of modern-day Yemen. It consisted of the port city of Aden and also included the outlying
  islands of Kamaran, Perim and the Khuria Muria archipelago with a total area of 192 km2 (74 sq mi)."
- "Prior to 1937, Aden had been governed as part of British India (originally as the Aden Settlement
  subordinate to the Bombay Presidency, and then as a Chief Commissioner's province). On 1 April
  1937, Aden was separated from British India to become a Crown colony".
- "In 1963, Aden Colony was reconstituted as the State of Aden within the newly created Federation of
  South Arabia".
- "The Aden municipality, which covered the town, Tawali, Ma'alla and Crater, the Township authority
  of Sheikh Othman and finally Little Aden had been established in recent years as a separate body,
  covering the oil refinery and the workers' settlement." ([Aden Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aden_Colony))

### perim

- "It has a surface area of 13 km2 (5.0 sq mi)". ([Perim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perim))

### kuria-muria

- "In 1854 the sultan of Muscat ... presented the islands to Queen Victoria as a gift".
- "As a British possession until 1967, they were administered by the Governor of Aden until 1953, then
  by the British High Commissioner until 1963, and finally by the British Chief Political Resident of
  the Persian Gulf". On 30 November 1967 it was announced that "the islands would be returned to
  Muscat and Oman". ([Khuriya Muriya Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuria_Muria_Islands))

### dodecanese

- "The Italian Islands of the Aegean ... were an archipelago of fourteen islands (the Dodecanese,
  except Kastellorizo) in the southeastern Aegean Sea, that—together with the surrounding islets—were
  ruled by the Kingdom of Italy from 1912 to 1943 and the Italian Social Republic (under German
  occupation) from 1943 to 1945 ... until they were ceded to the Kingdom of Greece in 1947 under the
  Treaty of Paris".
- "Kastellorizo was temporarily occupied by France in 1915 and came under Italian control in 1921."
  ([Italian Islands of the Aegean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Islands_of_the_Aegean))

### berlin

- "With about two million inhabitants, West Berlin had the largest population of any city in Germany
  during the Cold War era." ([West Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Berlin))
- East Berlin, the Soviet sector, is the rest of the city; "In August 1961, the East German
  Government tried to stop the population exodus by separating West Berlin by the Berlin Wall."
  ([East Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Berlin))

### tuscany-and-lombardy-venetia

- "The Grand Duchy of Tuscany ... was an Italian monarchy located in Central Italy that existed, with
  interruptions, from 1569 to 1860"; "In December 1859 ... the Grand Duchy was joined to the Duchies of
  Modena and Parma to form the United Provinces of Central Italy, which were annexed by the Kingdom of
  Sardinia a few months later." ([Grand Duchy of Tuscany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Tuscany))
- "The Kingdom of Lombardy–Venetia ... was a constituent land (crown land) of the Austrian Empire from
  1815 to 1866"; "the region of Lombardy was ceded to France in 1859 ... which then immediately ceded
  it to the Kingdom of Sardinia." ([Kingdom of Lombardy–Venetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Lombardy%E2%80%93Venetia))

## Caveats

These are tertiary sources, used for dates and for the areas the polygons are compared with, not for
any value in the data. Aden Colony's "192 km2" is stated as including Kamaran, Perim and the Kuria
Muria Islands, but Perim alone is 13 km2 by the article above and the GADM outlines of the town's
districts alone exceed 150 km2, so the figure cannot be the sum of those parts; the page for the
colony compares its polygon with FAO 1952's own land total instead.
