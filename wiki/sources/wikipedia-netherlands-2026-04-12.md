# United Kingdom of the Netherlands (Wikipedia snapshot)

## Why it was ingested

Provide territorial-change dates for the NLD-1800-1830 polity page
and context for the Luxembourg and Belgium separations.

## What it adds

### 1815-creation
*Congress of Vienna creation.*

William I declared himself king on **16 March 1815**. The
constitution was "adopted 24 August 1815." The realm comprised
"17 provinces and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg which was
constitutionally distinct." United the former Dutch Republic,
Austrian Netherlands, and Prince-Bishopric of Liège.

### 1830-revolution
*Belgian Revolution.*

"The Belgian Revolution broke out on 25 August 1830, inspired by
the recent July Revolution in France." "Belgium was declared an
independent state on 4 October 1830." William I "launched the
Ten Days' Campaign" in August 1831, which "failed to defeat the
rebels" after "French intervened to support the Belgians."

### 1839-treaty
*Treaty of London.*

Signed **19 April 1839**. "The Dutch recognised Belgian
independence, in exchange for territorial concessions." This
treaty formally ended the United Kingdom as originally
constituted.

### area
*Area and population.*

68,095 km² total area. Population 5,563,119 (1817 census).

## Known limitations

Wikipedia is not an academic source. The article provides limited
detail on the internal constitutional structure. Biger 1995 may
have relevant NETHERLANDS and BELGIUM entries. The exact
territorial partition between Netherlands and Belgium in 1830/1839
needs more precise sourcing.
