# Wikipedia articles for the second geodata batch (accessed 2026-09-25)

## Why it was ingested

Seventeen polities were created on 2026-09-25 for reporting units that FAO 1952 and the LatAm
subnational panel report on their own, and each page needed an external source for the dates and
extents it rests on. Fetched with the MediaWiki API (`action=query&prop=extracts&explaintext=1`);
the quotations below are verbatim from those extracts.

## What it adds

### channel-islands

- "They are divided into two Crown Dependencies: the Bailiwick of Jersey, which is the largest of
  the islands; and the Bailiwick of Guernsey, consisting of Guernsey, Alderney, Sark, Herm and some
  smaller islands."
- "Although they are not part of the United Kingdom, the UK is responsible for the defence and
  international relations of the islands." ([Channel Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands))

### isle-of-man

- "a self-governing British Crown Dependency in the Irish Sea, between Great Britain and Ireland."
- "It has an area of around 572 square kilometres (221 sq mi)."
- "The Isle of Man is not part of the United Kingdom; however, the UK is responsible for its defence
  and external affairs." ([Isle of Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Man))

### svalbard

- "The treaty was signed on 9 February 1920 ... Japan was the last to ratify the treaty, on 2 April
  1925, and the treaty came into force on 14 August 1925."
- "In 1925, five years after the conclusion of the treaty, the Norwegian authorities officially
  renamed the islands 'Svalbard'." ([Svalbard Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svalbard_Treaty))

### jan-mayen

- "The Norwegian Meteorological Institute annexed the middle part of the island for Norway in 1922
  and the whole island in 1926 ..."
- "On 27 February 1930, the island was made de jure a part of the Kingdom of Norway."
  ([Jan Mayen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Mayen))

### kermadec

- "The islands are part of New Zealand. They are 33.6 km2 (13.0 sq mi) in total area and
  uninhabited, except for the permanently staffed Raoul Island Station."
- "On 1 August 1886, HMS Diamond annexed the islands for the United Kingdom. Letters patent issued
  on 18 January 1887, assigned the islands to the colony of New Zealand following a request from the
  colony's parliament." ([Kermadec Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermadec_Islands))

### gilbert-and-ellice

- "The Gilbert and Ellice Islands was a British territory in the southern Pacific Ocean from 1892
  to 1976, comprising the present-day independent countries of Kiribati and Tuvalu."
- "The islands became a Crown colony on 12 January 1916 by the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Order in
  Council, 1915. During the year 1916, the Union Islands (Tokelau) were also annexed to the Gilbert
  and Ellice Islands colony."
- "On 28 November 1919, Great Britain reasserted its claim to Christmas Island and annexed it to
  the colony."
- "Portions of the outlying Line Islands and Phoenix Islands were also added the colony."
- "In 1974, the Ellice Islands voted in favour of separation and the GEIC was split into separate
  colonies with effect from 1976."
  ([Gilbert and Ellice Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_and_Ellice_Islands))

### cook-niue-tokelau

- Cook Islands: "In 1901 the islands were included within the boundaries of the Colony of New
  Zealand by Order in Council ... The boundary change became effective on 11 June 1901"; "On 4
  August 1965, a constitution was promulgated" (self-government).
  ([Cook Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Islands))
- Niue: "Niue was brought within the boundaries of New Zealand on 11 June 1901 by the same Order and
  Proclamation as the Cook Islands." ([Niue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue))
- Tokelau: "New Zealand took over administration of Tokelau from the United Kingdom on 11 February
  1926"; "Tokelau formally became part of New Zealand on 1 January 1949."
  ([Tokelau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokelau))

### roc-provinces

- "As a result, the Republic of China in 1946 had 35 provinces."
- "The Beiyang government set up four more provinces out of Inner Mongolia and the surrounding
  areas (Chahar, Rehe, Ningxia, Suiyuan) and two others out of parts of historical Tibet (Chuanbian
  (later Xikang) out of Kham and Qinghai out of Amdo ...)."
  ([Administrative divisions of the Republic of China (1912-1949)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_China_(1912%E2%80%931949)))
- Xikang: "a nominal province formed by the Republic of China in 1939 ... The former territory of
  Xikang is now divided between the Tibet Autonomous Region (TAR) and Sichuan province."
  ([Xikang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xikang))
- Suiyuan: "The area Suiyuan covered is approximated by today's prefecture-level cities of Hohhot,
  Baotou, Wuhai, Ordos, Bayan Nur, and parts of Ulanqab, all part of Inner Mongolia."
  ([Suiyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suiyuan))
- Chahar: "In 1928, it became a province"; "In 1952, six years after becoming communist, the province
  was abolished and divided into parts of Inner Mongolia, Beijing Municipality and Hebei."
  ([Chahar Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chahar_Province))
- Rehe (Jehol): "a former Chinese special administrative region and province centered on the city
  of Rehe, now known as Chengde ... The second largest city was Chaoyang, followed by Chifeng."
  ([Rehe Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehe_Province))

### tibet

- "Tibet ... was a de facto independent state in East Asia that lasted from the collapse of the Qing
  dynasty in 1912 until its annexation by the People's Republic of China in 1951."
- "At that time, the government of Tibet controlled all of Ü-Tsang (Dbus-gtsang) and western Kham
  (Khams), roughly coincident with the borders of the Tibet Autonomous Region today. Eastern Kham,
  separated by the Yangtze River, was under the control of Chinese warlord Liu Wenhui."
- "the Tibetan Army was wholly inadequate to resist the People's Liberation Army (PLA) during the
  Chinese invasion of 1950." ([Tibet (1912-1951)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibet_(1912%E2%80%931951)))

### xinjiang

- "the Qing dynasty established Xinjiang ('new frontier') as a province in 1884"
- "In 1955, the status of Xinjiang was changed from a province to a 'Uygur Autonomous Region'."
  ([Xinjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinjiang))

### newfoundland

- "Until 1949, the Dominion of Newfoundland was a separate dominion in the British Empire."
  ([Newfoundland and Labrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newfoundland_and_Labrador))

### azores-madeira

- "The Azores, officially the Autonomous Region of the Azores, is an archipelago of Portugal ...
  together with Madeira, it is one of the two autonomous regions of Portugal."
  ([Azores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azores))
- "Madeira (740.7 km2 ...)"; "Porto Santo (42.5 km2 ...)". ([Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira))

### galapagos

- "They form the Galápagos Province of the Republic of Ecuador"; the islands were "officially named
  the Ecuador Archipelago ... following their annexation by Ecuador in 1832."
  ([Galápagos Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gal%C3%A1pagos_Islands))

### french-north-africa

- "a term that is often applied to the three territories that were controlled by France in the
  Maghreb region of North Africa during the colonial era, comprising Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia.
  ... French North Africa was never more than a term of convenience to refer to the three
  separately-governed territories." ([French North Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_North_Africa))

### korea

- "The division of Korea ... began at the end of World War II on 2 September 1945, with the
  establishment of a Soviet occupation zone in the north and a US occupation zone in the south.
  These zones developed into separate governments, named the Democratic People's Republic of Korea
  (North Korea) and the Republic of Korea (South Korea), which fought a war from 1950 to 1953."
  ([Division of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Korea))

### great-britain

- "Great Britain is an island ... consisting of the countries England, Scotland and Wales. With an
  area of 209,331 km2 (80,823 sq mi), it is the largest of the British Isles."
- "Politically, Great Britain refers to the whole of England, Scotland and Wales in combination,
  but not Northern Ireland; it includes islands, such as the Isle of Wight, Anglesey, the Isles of
  Scilly, the Hebrides and the island groups of Orkney and Shetland."
- "This territory, together with Northern Ireland, constitutes the United Kingdom."
  ([Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain))

### chile-regions

- "The administrative divisions of Chile were created in 1974 and limited to 13 regions ...
  Previously, Chile was divided into 25 provinces."
- "In December 2006, two new regions were created: the northern Arica and Parinacota Region, by
  taking out the two northernmost provinces from the Tarapacá Region; and Los Ríos Region in the
  south ..."; "In August 2017, the Ñuble Region was created from what was then the Ñuble Province
  of the Biobío Region ... It became operational in September 2018."
  ([Regions of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Chile))

## Known limitations

- Wikipedia is a secondary source. Every extent a polity page relies on is measured on its polygon
  and compared with the area FAO 1952 itself states for the unit, where FAO states one; these
  articles supply the dates and the membership of each unit, not the areas.
- The Republic of China articles give no areas for the 1947 provinces. The 22-province outline on
  [chn-p22-1939-1953](../polities/chn-p22-1939-1953.md) is checked against FAO's own stated figure
  instead.
