# Landes (departement)

## Summary

Landes is one of the ~83 original departements created by the French Revolutionary administrative reform of 1790, in the southwest of France along the Atlantic coast, with its prefecture at Mont-de-Marsan. It was formed mainly from the historic Gascon districts of Marsan, Chalosse, the Landes (the pine-forest/moorland region that gave it its name) and Born, plus a small southern portion of Guyenne. Unlike Alsace-Lorraine, Belfort, Corsica or the overseas departements -- the four categories the country's routing convention treats as departures from the standard 1790-start rule -- Landes was never annexed, detached, or administered outside Metropolitan France at any point in its history: it sits far from the eastern border affected by the 1871-1919 Alsace-Lorraine cession and is not an island or overseas territory. This entry is `type: subnational` because a departement is an administrative division of France, not a sovereign polity in its own right -- national sovereignty over this territory belongs throughout to the FRA national chain (FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025), and this row exists purely to give a home to any territorially-scoped data reported at the departement level, which the national rows are too large to represent.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id FRA-FRI13, admin_name FRI13) identified Landes as a French departement with no existing polity row -- the FRA national rows describe the whole country and cannot stand in for one of its 83-plus internal divisions. The decision text itself does not state a source table, value label, year range, or row count of pending data (contrast the exemplar MAN-1950-1955 page, which cites specific mitchell/fao1952 row counts): FRI13 reads as a NUTS3-style administrative code for Landes, so this row may exist to give a boundary/admin crosswalk a target rather than to receive an already-identified valued data series -- that gap in provenance is recorded as an open question below rather than papered over. What is independently checkable is that Landes has been continuously recognized as a distinct administrative unit since 1790 under INSEE's official geographic code (departement number 40), which is the kind of external administrative-history confirmation the page-requirements spec asks for in place of a trading-polity source like Federico-Tena (which does not cover French sub-national units at all).

Territory description: Landes departement, in the southwest of France on the Atlantic coast, in the modern region of Nouvelle-Aquitaine. Its prefecture is Mont-de-Marsan; it borders the departements of Gironde (north), Lot-et-Garonne and Gers (east), and Pyrenees-Atlantiques (south), with the Atlantic Ocean forming its entire western edge. It covers approximately 9,243 km2 in its modern extent (the third-largest departement in Metropolitan France by area), dominated by the Landes forest, the largest maritime pine forest in Europe. A reader can locate it on a modern map immediately southwest of Bordeaux, between the Gironde estuary area and the Basque Country.

## Decisions

### d-container-floor-1800

**Start year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790, to match the container floor**

The routing decision derived start_year=1790 from the country convention's default rule (Landes is one of the ~83 original 1790 Revolutionary departements, not one of the four excluded categories). But the polity table has no FRA row of any kind before FRA-1800-1871 -- the earliest national France entry in data/final/polities_database.csv starts in 1800. A container edge must fall inside both the member's own span and the container's own span, so an edge citing FRA-1800-1871 cannot cover 1790-1799, and no other container exists to cover that decade. Per the repository's container-floor policy (recent commit 'Enforce the container floor, and treat an absent page file as unwritten'), this entry's start_year is clamped to 1800, the earliest year a container exists for. The gap between the department's actual 1790 creation and this row's 1800 start is recorded as an open question rather than silently absorbed, since the ten years of difference are real administrative history this row does not capture.

### d-bespoke-subunit-code

**Chose the ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern with a self-assigned 3-letter subunit token**

FRA has no existing subnational rows, so there is no established subunit-token convention to follow for this country. Landes is an ordinary administrative departement, not a historical territory with its own name distinct from its administrative role (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so the bespoke-code path used for those three cases does not apply here. Following the dominant 57-row pattern instead, the code uses the ISO3 plus a self-chosen 3-letter token, LAN, loosely evoking the department's own name and short enough to read cleanly alongside ESP's two-letter provincial tokens; any subsequent French departement page should either match this token style or document its own departure.
