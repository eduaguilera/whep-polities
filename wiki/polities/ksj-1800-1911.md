# Kingdom of Kasanje

## Summary

The Kingdom of Kasanje was an Imbangala (Jaga) kingdom in the Kwango River valley in the interior of modern Angola. Founded c. 1620 by an Imbangala warrior band, Kasanje became a major hub of the trans-Atlantic slave trade, acting as a crucial intermediary between Portuguese Angola and the Lunda Empire in the far interior. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1911, when Portuguese forces fully subjugated the kingdom. Biger notes that "the Angolans achieved their independence from the Portuguese on 11 November 1975." The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). Kasanje occupied the Kwango valley between roughly 7°S and 10°S latitude, in the savanna plateau east of the Malanje highlands. Its territory bordered Portuguese-controlled areas to the west and the Lunda sphere to the east.
