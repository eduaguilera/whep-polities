# Afghanistan (1888-1893)

## Summary

Afghanistan (1888-1893) covers a brief transitional period within the British protectorate era, from a CShapes polygon boundary change on 31 December 1888 until the signing of the Durand Line Agreement on 12 November 1893. CShapes records identical areas for the 1886–1888 and 1888–1893 steps (both approximately 787,543 km² in geographic coordinates), suggesting this split reflects an administrative or diplomatic event rather than a genuine territorial change. The Durand Line Agreement of 1893 then produced a real territorial reduction, splitting the row into AFG-1893-1919. This row is a distinct WHEP entry because the CSV chain requires it as the link between AFG-1800-1888 and AFG-1893-1919.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records two time-steps sharing the same area (~78,754,262 square units in geographic coordinates): 1886-01-01 to 1888-12-30, and 1888-12-31 to 1893-11-11. Both are classified as protectorate status under British rule (owner=200). The subsequent 1893-11-12 step shows a reduced area (~62,567,277) reflecting the Durand Line cession. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 records Afghanistan as a protectorate under British rule (owner=200) continuously from 1886 through 1919, with a date-boundary at 1888-12-31 carrying identical area to the preceding step. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)
- The Durand Line with Pakistan was established by a treaty signed on 12 November 1893 and reaffirmed in 1905; a final treaty was signed on 22 November 1921. [biger-1995 §afghanistan](../sources/biger-1995.md#afghanistan)
- Afghanistan first found unity as a nation under the rule of Ahmad Shah Abdal in 1747. [biger-1995 §afghanistan](../sources/biger-1995.md#afghanistan)
- Afghanistan acquired a common boundary with China by the 1895 Anglo-Russian agreement separating the Wakhan strip from Russia. [biger-1995 §afghanistan](../sources/biger-1995.md#afghanistan)
- CShapes 2.0 population threshold for dependencies is 250,000; Afghanistan far exceeds this, confirming its inclusion. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

## Contradictions

CShapes records identical polygon areas for the 1886–1888 and 1888–1893 time-steps. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule (same area = same polity = one row), this raises the question of whether AFG-1800-1888 and AFG-1888-1893 should be merged into a single row. No documented rationale for the split has been found in `docs/` or `wiki/log.md`. This is flagged as a candidate oddity.

## Open questions

### oq-1888-split-rationale

The CShapes 1888-12-31 date boundary shows no area change between the two Afghanistan time-steps. No historical event on or around 31 December 1888 has been identified that would justify a WHEP row split. The next ingest should search for diplomatic correspondence or boundary commission records from this date. If no territorial event is found, a proposal to merge AFG-1800-1888 and AFG-1888-1893 should be filed.

### oq-protectorate-nature

Britain controlled Afghanistan's foreign affairs during this period while the Amir retained internal authority. The degree of sovereignty and whether this affects WHEP's territorial accounting (vs. attributing the territory to Britain) should be clarified in a future ingest.
