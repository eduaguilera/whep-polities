# Angola

## Summary

Angola is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of modern Angola from 1816 to the present. The start year 1816 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage for Portuguese colonial holdings in southwestern Africa, not any specific boundary event. Angola gained independence from Portugal on 11 November 1975. The row continues as a single WHEP polity because the territory has remained essentially stable since independence.

## Territorial extent

Angola's boundaries were established through a series of colonial treaties in the late 19th century. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Boundaries with neighbors (Congo, Zaire, Zambia, Namibia) were demarcated during the Scramble for Africa period. The exclave of Cabinda in the north is included in the Angolan territory.

## Decisions

### dec-polygon-single-vintage-proxy

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0, feature 540, vintage 1905 (~1,246,000 km²). A single polygon is used as a proxy for the full 1816–2025 span.

**Rationale for stable-border approximation (post-1905 data):** Angola's external boundaries were demarcated through colonial treaties by the early 1900s: the Congo/Belgian Congo border (1891 treaty), the southern border with German South-West Africa (Namibia) fixed 1886–1903, and the eastern border with Northern Rhodesia (Zambia) settled by ~1905. After those demarcations the external outline remained essentially unchanged through the colonial period and was inherited intact at independence in 1975. The 1905 polygon is therefore an accurate representation of Angola's territory for 1905–2025 data, and the approximation error for post-1905 data rows is minimal (negligible interior adjustments only).

**Pre-1905 data (1898–1904, ~7 rows):** These rows are served by a polygon that represents the boundaries *after* final treaty demarcation. In the 1898–1904 window most borders were already de-facto demarcated (Belgian Congo border 1891; Namibian border 1886–1903), so the 1905 polygon slightly over-represents the formally settled territory vs. the pre-treaty state. The magnitude of this over-representation is small — interior hinterland adjustments, not coastal or major boundary shifts — and the data magnitudes (maize ~300,000 t, sugar ~27,000 t, rice ~6,000 t) are consistent with the colonial Portuguese Angola footprint. No rematch is warranted; this is a documented minor approximation.

**polygon_status: assigned** — single polygon approximates a 209-year span. Acceptable for post-1905 data (error: negligible). Pre-1905 data (1898–1904) served under a slight over-representation of territory; direction and magnitude documented above.
