# Serbia within Yugoslavia (1945-2006)

## Summary

Serbia as a federal unit of Yugoslavia, from the People's Republic of Serbia (formalised in April 1945)
through the Socialist Republic of Serbia (1963) to the Republic of Serbia within the Federal Republic of
Yugoslavia (1992) and Serbia and Montenegro (2003), until Montenegro's independence in June 2006
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §serbia](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#serbia). "Within Serbia were the two autonomous provinces: Kosovo and Vojvodina"
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §serbia](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#serbia). Created on 2026-10-07 for issue 686.

**Why this entry exists.** The Serbian chain had no row between [ser-1918-1945](ser-1918-1945.md) and
[srb-2006-2008](srb-2006-2008.md), so `SER-1918-1945` named `SRB-2006-2008` as its successor, a 61-year
jump kept only because `scripts/write_iso3_successor_map.py` needed an edge to reach the Serbian chain
from modern `SRB` and `KOS`. This row replaces that stopgap with a true succession, and gives data that
reports Serbia alone in 1945-2005 a territory to land on. One row covers both the socialist and the FRY
years because Serbia's territory did not change in 2006 or 1992: only the federation around it did.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. CShapes 2.0 gwcode 340, the 2006-2008 step (feature year 2007, the same
step [srb-2006-2008](srb-2006-2008.md) uses). That step is Serbia with Vojvodina and Kosovo, whose
borders are those drawn for the Serbian federal unit in 1945 and unchanged until Kosovo's 2008
declaration [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). It measures
87,942 km² (Mollweide), against the 88,361 km² usually given for Serbia with Kosovo. CShapes has no
Serbian step for 1945-2006 because it codes Yugoslavia as one state then, so the feature year is out of
this row's span by necessity, not by lag.

The two rows never coexist, so sharing the polygon does not double-count anything. Kosovo under UN
administration (1999-2006) is not cut out; [scg-xk-1999-2006](scg-xk-1999-2006.md) carries Serbia and
Montenegro without it for the sources that report that way.
