# Namibia (1990-2025)

## Summary

Namibia (1990-2025) covers independent Namibia from 21 March 1990 to the present. On that date Namibia gained independence from South Africa after a prolonged guerrilla war led by SWAPO. SWAPO won the first multi-party elections and formed the government [biger-1995 §namibia](../sources/biger-1995.md#namibia). The territory is unchanged from the preceding mandate/colonial period.

## Territorial extent

Namibia is bounded by Angola and Zambia in the north, Botswana in the east, South Africa in the east and south, and the Atlantic Ocean. The Caprivi Strip extends northeast to the Zambia boundary. Area approximately 317,939 sq. miles (823,144 sq. km). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records status=independent, owner=NAM from 1990-03-21.
