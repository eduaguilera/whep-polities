# Zambia

## Summary

Zambia is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Zambia (formerly Northern Rhodesia) from 1964 to the present. Zambia became independent on 24 October 1964.

## Territorial extent

Zambia (area 290,586 sq. miles; 752,327 sq. km; population 9,000,000 [1994]) is bounded by Zaire, Tanzania, Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, and Angola. Capital: Lusaka. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Zambian independence from Britain was officially declared in 1964. The new government was led by Kenneth Kaunda and his United National Independence Party (UNIP), of Bemba origin" (p.518). At independence, the Lozi wished to maintain separate status granted by the colonial government (Barotse Agreement of 1964). [biger-1995 §zambia](../sources/biger-1995.md#zambia)
- The Zaire-Zambia boundary runs 1,200 miles (1,930 km). The central part of Africa "remained free of European occupation until the 1880s. The boundary of the Belgian Congo Free State extended as far as the Congo-Zambezi watershed" (p.517). Portuguese possessions were still confined to coastal areas. The Anglo-Belgian Agreement of 12 May 1894 established boundaries, though "this delimitation was imprecise" (p.517). [biger-1995 §zambia](../sources/biger-1995.md#zambia)
