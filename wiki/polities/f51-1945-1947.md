# Czechoslovakia (1945-1947)

## Summary

This row tracks Czechoslovakia for **3 years**, covering the restored
state after WWII
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Restored Czechoslovakia. Benes decrees expelled ~3 million Sudeten
Germans. Ruthenia (Subcarpathian Ukraine) ceded to the USSR (June 1945).
Borders largely restored to pre-Munich configuration except the Ruthenia
loss. Communist coup February 1948 (but this row ends 1947).

Predecessor: [f51-1938-1945](f51-1938-1945.md). Successor:
[f51-1947-1993](f51-1947-1993.md).
