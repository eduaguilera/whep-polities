# Eswatini

## Summary

Eswatini (formerly Swaziland) is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Swaziland from 1894 to the present. The 1894 start date reflects the South African Republic (Transvaal) assuming control over Swaziland. Swaziland became independent on 6 September 1968. The country was renamed Eswatini in 2018.

## Territorial extent

Eswatini is a small landlocked country in southern Africa, almost entirely surrounded by South Africa with a short boundary with Mozambique. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "The Kingdom of Swaziland was subject to expansionist interest from Transvaal throughout the nineteenth century. To prevent Afrikaner encroachment, Britain protected Swaziland's independence, which was recognized in agreements between Britain and Transvaal on 3 August 1881 and 27 February 1884" (p.392). [biger-1995 §swaziland](../sources/biger-1995.md#swaziland)
- "A further convention in 1894 gave the South African Republic powers of protection and administration for Swaziland. Britain assumed these powers when it annexed Transvaal in 1900" (p.392-393). In 1907, Swaziland came under the British High Commissioner for South Africa. The Kingdom of Swaziland became independent on 6 September 1968.
