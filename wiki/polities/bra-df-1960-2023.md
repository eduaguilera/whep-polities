# Distrito Federal (Brasília)

## Summary

The Distrito Federal (Brasília) is Brazil's federal district, created in 1960 when the national capital was relocated inland from Rio de Janeiro to the purpose-built city of Brasília. It is `type: subnational` because it is a first-order administrative division of the Brazilian federation (equivalent in status to a state, but without full statehood), never a sovereign entity in its own right -- national sovereignty rests continuously with Brazil (BRA-1909-2025) throughout this entry's span. The territory was excised from the state of Goiás specifically to house the new seat of federal government, following the constitutional mandate (dating back to 1891) to relocate the capital away from the coast; construction under President Juscelino Kubitschek culminated in the 21 April 1960 inauguration. This is a distinct place from the pre-1960 'Distrito Federal', which was not a separate territory carved from a state but rather the federal-capital status held by the city of Rio de Janeiro itself from 1889 until the 1960 transfer -- the two share a label but not a territory, which is why the routing decision this page implements treats them as two separate polities rather than one continuous entry.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Brazilian data (country: Brazil, iso3 BRA) reported under the admin-unit label 'Distrito Federal' for the years following the 1960 capital relocation to Brasília -- the routing decision does not state the exact row count or source dataset, since that detail lives in the upstream admin_unit extract rather than in the verdict passed to this page, and is flagged as an open question. Before this entry existed, any 'Distrito Federal' rows risked being matched either to the national BRA-1909-2025 row (losing the sub-national reporting granularity entirely) or, worse, conflated with the pre-1960 Distrito Federal (Rio de Janeiro), which is a different city and a different territory sharing only the administrative label. Both would be wrong: the routing reasoning found no name match or boundary-feature match tying this post-1960 data to any existing candidate polity, and the country convention's start-rule departures list does not cover this unit because its history is not a simple state carve-out but a label reused for two unrelated territories. What confirms this entity is distinct is straightforward administrative history rather than a specialist trading-polity source like Federico-Tena: the Brazilian constitution's own capital-relocation mandate and the 21 April 1960 transfer of federal-district status from Rio de Janeiro to a newly built, newly bounded territory around Brasília, carved out of Goiás -- a change in both government seat and physical territory occurring in the same year, which no existing polity row represents.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status.** Not yet assigned. No polygon is available in the GeoPackage for this period. The intended source, `geobr-ibge`, is already registered in sources.yaml specifically for Brazilian state-equivalent units (keyed by `abbrev_state`, with 'DF' as the Distrito Federal's code), but the underlying file (`data/geodata/geobr-ibge/states.gpkg`) has not been fetched into this checkout. GADM admin-1 data is present locally but this build's extract excludes Brazil entirely; cshapes carries Brazil only at the whole-country level. `polygon_status` is therefore `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `estimate` -- there is no wrong-territory polygon sitting in for the right one, there is simply no fetched file yet.

**Territory description.** The modern Distrito Federal is a roughly circular/polygonal administrative unit in central Brazil's Central-West region, entirely surrounding the city of Brasília, and bordered on all sides by the state of Goiás (with a short border touching Minas Gerais to the southeast in some administrative reckonings, though in practice it is a Goiás enclave). It sits on the Planalto Central at roughly 15.5-16.1°S, 47.3-48.3°W. Its area has been essentially stable since 1960 at approximately **5,760 km2** (roughly the size of Trinidad and Tobago, or about a fifth the size of the state of Sergipe) -- this figure is drawn from published IBGE territorial-area statistics for the unit, not measured from any geometry attached to this entry, since no geometry is attached. The boundary has not undergone the dramatic changes seen in some subnational units (no significant annexation or partition since 1960), which is why, once geobr-ibge is fetched, a single modern-vintage polygon should suffice for the whole 1960-2023 span without the proxy-rejection concerns that apply to units like post-Trianon Hungary.

## Predecessors and successors

This entry has no predecessor recorded yet: the territory and label 'Distrito Federal' existed before 1960, but denoted a different place (the city of Rio de Janeiro as federal capital, 1889-1960), which requires its own separate polity row not yet created (see open question oq-predecessor-pre1960-df). No successor is recorded either, since the Distrito Federal (Brasília) continues to exist past this entry's 2023 end_year without any administrative change -- the end_year likely needs to be extended rather than succeeded, per decision d-end-year-2023.

## Sourced claims

- Brazil's national capital relocated from Rio de Janeiro to the newly constructed city of Brasília on 21 April 1960, and the Distrito Federal was recreated at that time as the federal district surrounding the new capital, carved from the state of Goiás.
- IBGE (Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística) treats the Distrito Federal as a state-equivalent administrative unit and digitises its boundary at admin-1 level in the geobr package (id_column abbrev_state = 'DF'), the source this entry's polygon route points to but has not yet fetched locally.

## Decisions

### d-code-iso3-start-end

**Code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern**

BRA has no existing subnational rows, so this entry sets the country's precedent. I chose BRA-DF-1960-2023 rather than a bespoke code like the ALK/HYD/RYU examples, because 'Distrito Federal' is a standing administrative division type (a federal district), not a one-off historical territory with a proper name of its own the way Alaska Territory or Ryukyu are. DF is also IBGE's own two-letter abbreviation for the unit (abbrev_state column in geobr-ibge), so using it as the subunit code keeps this row consistent with how a future geobr-ibge fetch would key the feature.

### d-end-year-2023

**end_year set to 2023 per the routing decision, not left open to present**

The proposed verdict fixes end_year at 2023 even though the Distrito Federal (Brasília) has not been abolished and continues to exist today. I followed the verdict as given rather than extending to 2025 (the horizon used elsewhere in the table, e.g. BRA-1909-2025) because I have no visibility into why 2023 was chosen -- it may reflect the last year of ingested data for this unit rather than an administrative change. This is flagged as an open question below since it looks like it should probably extend further, matching the container's own 2025 end.

## Open questions

### oq-end-year-2023-vs-present

**Why does this entry end in 2023 rather than continuing to the present or to 2025?**

The Distrito Federal (Brasília) has not been abolished or restructured; it remains Brazil's federal district today. The routing verdict fixed end_year at 2023, matching neither the present day nor the container's own end_year of 2025 (BRA-1909-2025). This may simply reflect the last year covered by whatever admin-unit extract triggered this verdict, in which case end_year should be revised upward once later data is ingested, with a new container edge added to keep the span tiled. Left unresolved because I have no access to the underlying admin_unit extract's coverage.

### oq-predecessor-pre1960-df

**The pre-1960 Distrito Federal (Rio de Janeiro) sibling polity does not yet exist as a row to link as predecessor**

The routing reasoning explicitly notes that 'Distrito Federal' before 1960 denoted the federal-capital status of the city of Rio de Janeiro (1889-1960), and that this pre-1960 segment needs its own separate create_new proposal, which per the reasoning has already been recorded as a sibling verdict for the same admin_unit_id (BRA-DISTRITOFEDERAL). Until that entry is created and given a code, this page cannot list it in `predecessor`, leaving a gap in the historical chain immediately before 1960. Whoever authors that pre-1960 page should add this code (BRA-DF-1960-2023) to its own `successor` list, and this page's `predecessor` should be backfilled to point at it.

### oq-polygon-fetch-status

**geobr-ibge is registered but its local file (states.gpkg) does not exist yet, so no polygon can be assigned**

The polygon route is registered_source_unfetched: sources.yaml already designates geobr-ibge for Brazilian state-level units keyed by abbrev_state (DF), but data/geodata/geobr-ibge/states.gpkg is absent from this checkout. Once geobr-ibge is fetched, polygon_status should move from unassigned to assigned with polygon_feature_id set to the DF feature, and polygon_area_km2 populated from that geometry (expected ~5,760 km2, the modern Distrito Federal's area).
