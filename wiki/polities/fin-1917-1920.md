# Finland (1917-1920)

## Summary

Independent Finland from the declaration of independence (6 December 1917) to the Treaty of Tartu
(14 October 1920), which gave it Petsamo and an Arctic coast [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §finland](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#finland). Created on 2026-10-07
by splitting the old `FIN-1917-1940` row at 1920 (issue 686); the later years are
[fin-1920-1940](fin-1920-1940.md).

**Why this entry exists.** CShapes 2.0 draws Finland with Petsamo from 1917, three years before
Finland received it. Without this row, Finnish data for 1917-1919 was measured against a polygon
that included about 10,800 km² of Russian territory
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. Constructed (`build_fin_1917_1920` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): CShapes 2.0 gwcode 375's 1917-1940 step **minus Petsamo**.
Petsamo is taken from CShapes itself: the 1917-1940 step minus the 1940-2019 step leaves three
pieces, the 1940 cessions on the Karelian Isthmus and around Lake Ladoga (34,689 km²), Salla-Kuusamo
(7,937 km²), and one piece north of 68 N (10,837 km²), which is Petsamo. Only the northern piece is
removed. All figures are Mollweide areas.

**km² reasoning.** The result measures 376,316 km², against 387,153 km² for the CShapes step it is
cut from. The removed 10,837 km² is close to the ~10,500 km² usually given for Petsamo. No line was
drawn by hand: both boundaries are CShapes' own.
