# Turkiye (1914-1918)

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire / Turkiye for **5 years**, during
WWI [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The Ottoman Empire entered WWI in November 1914 on the side of the
Central Powers. Armenian Genocide (1915). Arab Revolt (1916). Armistice
of Mudros (30 October 1918). This is the Ottoman Empire's final
military phase before dissolution.

The CSV uses "Turkiye" naming and `TUR-` prefix, while the `OTT-*`
rows use "Ottoman Empire" naming. See
[log proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication](../log.md#proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication).

Predecessor: [tur-1913-1914](tur-1913-1914.md) -- WWI entry.
Successor: [tur-1918-1920](tur-1918-1920.md) -- post-WWI dissolution.

## Sourced claims

- **1914-11: Ottoman entry into WWI.** The Ottoman Empire entered WWI
  on the side of the Central Powers. This is the start-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1914](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1914).
- **1915: Armenian Genocide.** Systematic deportation and massacre of
  the Armenian population.
- **1916: Arab Revolt.** Arab provinces revolted against Ottoman rule,
  supported by the British.
- **1918-10-30: Armistice of Mudros.** The Ottoman Empire signed the
  armistice, ending its participation in WWI. This is the end-of-row
  event.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 640 (TUR), continuous
  from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
