# Gabon (1919-1960)

## Summary

The French colony of Gabon from the post-WWI territorial restoration on 28 June 1919 until independence on 17 August 1960. "On 17 August 1960 Gabon became independent" [biger-1995 §gabon](../sources/biger-1995.md#gabon). This is the longest and most stable phase of colonial Gabon, covering 41 years with no area change in CShapes. The 1919 start date corresponds to the restoration of Gabonese territory following the First World War: during 1912–1919 (the preceding row GAB-1912-1919), a portion of Gabon was transferred to Germany under the 1911 Agadir Crisis settlement; the territory reverted at 1919 with the Treaty of Versailles redistribution of former German Kamerun. CShapes records area 21.18 sq deg continuously from 1919-06-28 to 1960-08-16 (independence). This is a WHEP-distinct row because it begins with a genuine area change from the 18.42 sq deg of GAB-1912-1919.
