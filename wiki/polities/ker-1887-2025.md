# Kermadec Islands (New Zealand)

## Summary

The Kermadec Islands (Raoul, Macauley, Curtis, L'Esperance and their islets), about 1,000 km
north-east of New Zealand's North Island. "On 1 August 1886, HMS Diamond annexed the islands for the
United Kingdom", and "letters patent issued on 18 January 1887 assigned the islands to the colony of
New Zealand".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §kermadec](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#kermadec)

The row starts in 1887, when the islands joined New Zealand. `iso3` is `NA` so that the row cannot
enter New Zealand's iso family: it is an outlying dependency, and no label is routed to it except by
alias.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `New Zealand Kermadec Islands` on its own: a 1947 land-use table whose `use total`
is 3 thousand ha, i.e. **30 km²**. **2 rows** resolved to no polity. They could not go to
[nzl-1840-2025](nzl-1840-2025.md): Raoul Island lies **outside** its CShapes polygon, and an alias to NZL
would have filed a 30 km² land-use table as the whole country's for 1947.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: GADM 4.1 adm1 `NZL.10_1` ("Northern Islands"),
keeping only its parts east of the antimeridian. It measures **34 km²** (ESRI:54034) against FAO's 30
and the islands' stated "33.6 km2 ... in total area".
[gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

`NZL.10_1` also carries the Three Kings Islands (172.1° E, 34.2° S, the other 6 km² of the feature), which belong to
Northland, not to the Kermadecs; the envelope drops them. GADM's NZL was not in the repository's
extract and was added to `scripts/sources/gadm-4.1/fetch.sh` for this row, appended last so no
existing feature moved.

## Polygon status
