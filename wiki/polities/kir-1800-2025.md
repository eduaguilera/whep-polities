## Summary

Kiribati (pronounced "Kiribas") is an island state in the central Pacific Ocean, comprising 33 atolls and one island across the Gilbert, Phoenix, and Line Islands groups. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. The islands were under British administration as the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony; Kiribati became independent on 12 July 1979. Tuvalu (the Ellice Islands) separated earlier in 1978. CShapes records one time-step beginning 1999-09-14 (COW entry date), reflecting the population threshold delay for small Pacific states in COW. COW ccode=946 (stateabb=KIR); state-system entry 1999-09-14.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1979-polygon

CShapes records no polygon for Kiribati before 1999. The WHEP row starts in 1800. The pre-1999 (and especially pre-1979) polygon is back-projected. During the British colonial period, the Gilbert Islands were a separate administrative entity from 1916. Whether the polygon is consistent across the full 1800–2025 period should be verified.

### oq-ellice-islands-split

Tuvalu (the Ellice Islands) separated from the Gilbert Islands colony in 1975 (with separate administration) and became independent in 1978. The WHEP rows for KIR-1800-2025 and TUV-1800-2025 are both back-projected to 1800, which implies they were tracked separately from the database floor despite being jointly administered until the 1970s. The coverage logic here should be confirmed — did WHEP always use separate polygons for these territories, or were they merged before the split?
