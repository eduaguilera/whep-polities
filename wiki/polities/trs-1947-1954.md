# Free Territory of Trieste

## Summary

Established by Treaty of Paris (10 Feb 1947, in force 15 Sep 1947); terminated by the London Memorandum of Understanding (5 Oct 1954). Never placed under UN governance; de-facto split into Zone A (~222 km², Anglo-American) and Zone B (~515 km², Yugoslav), total ~737 km². FAO treated Trieste as a distinct reporting entity from Italy throughout 1937–1951 (43 rows). Post-1947 rows cannot route to ITA-1919-2025 (~300,000 km²) — the FTT was not Italian territory and the scale mismatch is ~400x. Pre-1947 Province of Trieste rows (~700 km²) also cannot route to all-Italy for the same scale reason.

## Territorial extent

No polygon found in CShapes 2.0 (0 hits for 'trieste'/'free territory'), Cliopatria v0.1.3 (0 hits), Paine-2024 (0 hits), or GADM 4.1. polygon_status=estimate; area 737 km² from Treaty of Paris documentation (Zone A ~222 km² + Zone B ~515 km²). Direction: narrow coastal strip at the north-eastern tip of the Adriatic, roughly 45.5–45.75°N, 13.5–13.9°E. A polygon could be constructed from the Morgan Line demarcation and Treaty of Paris Annex VI; see oq-trs-polygon-construction.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [ita-1919-2025](ita-1919-2025.md) — Italy held the Province of Trieste 1919–1947 (Treaty of Saint-Germain to Treaty of Paris).
- **Successor:** [ita-1919-2025](ita-1919-2025.md) — Zone A (~222 km²) transferred to Italy by London Agreement 1954 (formally confirmed Treaty of Osimo 1975). Zone B (~515 km²) went to Yugoslavia ([f248-1920-1991](f248-1920-1991.md)); post-1954 data routes to ITA-1919-2025.

## Contradictions

- **COW non-inclusion.** COW does not assign a state-system code to the FTT. WHEP includes it on data-coverage grounds (FAO separate reporting entity).
- **Formal vs. de-facto end.** Formal sovereignty over Zone A settled by Treaty of Osimo (1975); WHEP uses 1954 (de-facto termination, London Agreement).
