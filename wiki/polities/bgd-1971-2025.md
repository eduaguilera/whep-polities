# Bangladesh

## Summary

Bangladesh as tracked by WHEP from 1971, when civil war in East Pakistan led to the creation of the independent state of Bangladesh with the help of India. Previously East Pakistan (1947--1971), the territory was the eastern portion of Bengal, partitioned along religious lines in 1947.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Bangladesh is bounded by India and Myanmar, with coastline on the Bay of Bengal.
