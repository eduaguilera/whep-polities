# Canary Islands

## Summary

The Canary Islands are a Spanish archipelago of seven main islands off the northwest African coast. WHEP tracks them as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025 because they are administered as an autonomous community of Spain with distinct trade statistics. The polygon source is GADM 4.1, as CShapes 2.0 covers only sovereign states and their non-metropolitan dependencies meeting the population threshold; the Canary Islands are integral Spanish territory and do not appear as a separate CShapes polygon. The CSV assigns cow_code=20, but COW ccode 20 belongs to Canada — the Canary Islands have no independent COW entry because they are part of Spain (COW ccode=230). This is a data anomaly flagged in [Open questions](#oq-cow-code-mismatch) below.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from GADM 4.1. The seven main islands (Gran Canaria, Tenerife, Lanzarote, Fuerteventura, La Palma, La Gomera, El Hierro) plus smaller islets cover approximately 7,447 km². The archipelago lies 100–500 km west of Morocco. Boundaries are maritime throughout; no land borders. The Canary Islands are classified as an outermost region of the European Union, meaning EU law applies despite the Atlantic location.

## Predecessors and successors

The Canary Islands have been under continuous Spanish sovereignty since the late fifteenth century; no predecessor polity change occurred within the 1800–2025 window. The islands are an integral part of Spain (ESP-1800-2025) and not a colony.

- **Metropolitan state:** [esp-1800-2025](esp-1800-2025.md) — Spanish territory throughout; the Canary Islands row exists as a separate WHEP unit because distinct trade data is attributed to the islands.

## Sourced claims

- The Canary Islands appear in the Spanish boundary entries of Biger 1995 as integral Spanish territory; no separate boundary history entry exists because the islands have no land borders. [biger-1995 §spain](../sources/biger-1995.md#spain)
- CShapes 2.0 does not assign a separate polygon to the Canary Islands; they are subsumed in Spain's metropolitan polygon (CShapes Spain, cowcode=230). [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- COW ccode=20 refers to Canada, not the Canary Islands. The Canary Islands form part of Spain (COW stateabb=SPN, ccode=230, continuous from 1816). [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
- The WHEP CSV assigns polygon_source=GADM 4.1 and notes iso3_code=ICN, which is a non-standard code (ISO 3166-1 does not define ICN; Spain's ISO code is ESP). [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

The CSV assigns cow_code=20 to ICN-1800-2025. COW ccode=20 is Canada. The Canary Islands are not a COW state-system member — they are administered by Spain (COW 230). The csv cow_code field appears to use a WHEP-internal code that coincides with (but does not equal) the COW Canada code. This is misleading and should be clarified. See [oq-cow-code-mismatch](#oq-cow-code-mismatch).

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-mismatch

COW code collision. The CSV uses cow_code=20 for the Canary Islands, but COW ccode=20 is Canada. Whether this is a WHEP-internal identifier unrelated to COW, or a data entry error, is not documented. The next ingest should check the WHEP code-assignment documentation and, if no rationale exists, file a proposal to either remove the cow_code field for non-sovereign WHEP units or clarify its meaning.

### oq-polygon-provenance

GADM 4.1 polygon provenance. The CSV states polygon_source=GADM 4.1. It is not documented why GADM was used instead of Eurostat NUTS or another administrative source. The approximate area and geometry quality should be verified against an authoritative Spanish cartographic source.
