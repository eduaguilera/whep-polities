# Johor

## Summary

Johor, a sultanate under British protection since the 1885 treaty, with a General Adviser from 1914, when it joined the Unfederated Malay States, until the Malayan Union of 1 April 1946 (the handover year belongs to the incoming row, MYS-1946-1957). [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §johor](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#johor)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report Johor on its own; the label list and the harmonized dataset carry it as `british johor`, `british unfederated malay states: johor`. No row was the state: only its grouping (UMS-1909-1946) and British Malaya (GBM-1895-1946) existed. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
