# Saint Helena (Crown Colony)

## Summary

This row tracks **Saint Helena island** as a British Crown Colony, from
1834 (when the island transferred from the East India Company to direct
Crown administration) through to 1967 (the Saint Helena Act, which
granted the island a degree of self-government). The territory covered is
Saint Helena island itself (~122 km²), a volcanic South Atlantic island
located at approximately 15°58'S, 5°43'W, approximately 1,900 km west of
the African coast.

Ascension Island and Tristan da Cunha are geographically part of the same
British Overseas Territory today (ISO 3166-1: SHN) but were administered
separately throughout this period and are **not** covered by this polity.
Ascension was an Admiralty Station until 1922, then transferred to
Colonial Office and nominally administered from Saint Helena, but it was
always reported as a separate statistical entity. Tristan da Cunha was
a separate dependency. The FAO 1952 source label "St Helena" refers
specifically to Saint Helena island and its resident population data;
the 15 data rows (cattle, goats, horses, other hard fibers, poultry, etc.)
for years 1931–1951 scale to a small island (~122 km²), confirming they
cannot represent the broader three-island grouping (~411 km² total).

**Why this entry exists.** The FAO 1952 source records 15 rows of
island-level agricultural data for the label "St Helena" spanning
1931–1951. No WHEP polity existed for this territory; the label was
flagged `name_unresolved`, and the entity appeared only as a named
component inside the RAFR-1850-2021 aggregate ("Africa Other"), which
is not a valid routing target for island-level production data. This
row creates the granular polity needed to route the data correctly.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** GADM 4.1 ADM1 layer, feature SHN.2_1 (NAME_1 =
  "Saint Helena"), extracted from `data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_SHN.gpkg`.
- **Polygon method:** Single ADM1 island polygon from modern GADM 4.1.
  The GADM ADM0 for code SHN covers all three islands (Saint Helena +
  Ascension + Tristan da Cunha, total ~411 km²); the ADM1 Saint Helena
  feature isolates the island itself (~122–123 km²), which is the
  correct territorial scope for this polity.
- **Polygon_status: proxy.** No historical GIS source (CShapes 2.0,
  Cliopatria, Paine-2024) covers Saint Helena island for any vintage
  year. The GADM 4.1 modern island polygon is an appropriate proxy
  because island geography is unchanged since the colonial period;
  the coastline of a volcanic oceanic island does not shift with
  political events.
- **Area:** approximately 122–123 km² (Saint Helena island only).
- **Polygon_vintage_drift note:** The polygon date is 2024 and data
  spans 1931–1951, a ~73–93 year drift. For an island with fixed
  coastline this drift carries zero territorial risk; the proxy is
  faithful for the colonial period.
- **Confidence: high.** Island geography ensures the modern polygon
  is an exact territorial proxy for the colonial-era extent.

## Predecessors and Successors

- **Predecessor:** No WHEP predecessor row. Saint Helena was administered
  by the East India Company from 1659 until 1834. If WHEP sources contain
  data prior to 1834 attributed to "St Helena", a separate EIC-period
  polity row should be created (tentative code SHN-1659-1834 or
  SHN-1800-1834 for the WHEP-bounded period).
- **Successor:** No WHEP successor row defined yet. After the Saint Helena
  Act 1967, the territory became Saint Helena (with varying degrees of
  self-government), eventually part of the British Overseas Territory
  "Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha" (formal name from 2009).
  A post-1967 WHEP row (tentative code SHN-1967-2025) should be created
  if data sources report agricultural or production data for the island
  after 1967.

## Sourced Claims

- Saint Helena transferred from the East India Company to Crown
  administration on 22 April 1834 (Wikipedia, Saint Helena).
- The Saint Helena Act 1967 reorganised the island's constitution,
  providing for an elected council with greater self-governing powers
  (Wikipedia, Saint Helena).
- Saint Helena island area approximately 121–122 km² / 47 sq mi
  (Wikipedia, Saint Helena; GADM 4.1 computed area ~123 km²).
- Ascension Island transferred from Admiralty to Colonial Office in
  1922 but was administered as a dependency of Saint Helena rather
  than merged into it; it retained a separate statistical identity
  in FAO/colonial reporting (Wikipedia, Ascension Island).
- Tristan da Cunha was administered as a dependency of Saint Helena
  from 1938 (Wikipedia, Tristan da Cunha).
- FAO 1952 source contains 15 rows for "St Helena" spanning 1931–1951,
  covering livestock (cattle, goats, horses) and agricultural commodities
  (poultry, other hard fibers); data magnitudes consistent with a small
  (~122 km²) island economy.

## Decisions

- **Code SHN-1834-1967.** Prefix SHN matches the ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 for
  the broader Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha territory,
  signalling the primary island identity. Start year 1834 is the Crown
  Colony establishment date. End year 1967 is the Saint Helena Act,
  which is the first significant constitutional change.
- **Type: colonial.** Saint Helena was a Crown Colony throughout
  1834–1967, subordinate to the British Colonial Office.
- **Continent: Africa.** FAO/UN statistical practice assigns Saint Helena
  to the Africa region (Sub-Saharan Africa). The island is geographically
  in the South Atlantic but is conventionally grouped with Africa for
  statistical purposes.
- **Polygon: GADM 4.1 ADM1 Saint Helena feature (SHN.2_1), not ADM0.**
  The ADM0 SHN polygon covers all three islands (~411 km²) and would
  over-represent the territory by a factor of ~3.4 relative to Saint
  Helena island alone. The ADM1 feature provides the correct granularity.
- **Ascension and Tristan da Cunha excluded.** Both territories were
  reported as separate units in FAO/colonial statistics; folding them
  into this row would overstate territorial scope and potentially
  misroute data for those units.

## Open Questions

### oq-shn-pre-1834-polity

The East India Company administered Saint Helena from 1659 to 1834. If
any WHEP data source (e.g. trade data, commodity production) reports
figures for "St Helena" before 1834, a separate polity row for the
EIC period would be needed (e.g. SHN-1800-1834, using WHEP's 1800
temporal boundary, with the same island polygon).

### oq-shn-post-1967-polity

No WHEP row currently exists for Saint Helena after 1967. If data sources
report agricultural or production data for the island in the post-1967
period, a successor row (tentative code SHN-1967-2025) should be created.
The territorial scope and polygon would be the same as this row.

### oq-ascension-tristan-polities

Ascension Island and Tristan da Cunha appear as named administrative
areas in the GADM ADM1 layer (SHN.1_1 and SHN.3_1). If FAO or other
WHEP sources report data specifically for either territory, separate
polity rows should be created for them. They are not covered by
SHN-1834-1967.
