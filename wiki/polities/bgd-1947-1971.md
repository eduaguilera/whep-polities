# East Pakistan (1947-1971)

## Summary

This entry tracks "Bangladesh" as reported separately from "Pakistan" in
the pre-1961 data for 1953-1960. East Pakistan had its own agricultural
census and a distinct agricultural economy (rice, jute vs West Pakistan's
wheat, cotton). Previously routed to PAK-1949-1971, merging East
Pakistani data into the all-Pakistan polygon. 8 rows.
