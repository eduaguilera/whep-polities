# Mozambique (1975-2025)

## Summary

This row covers independent Mozambique from 25 June 1975 to the present. Mozambique gained independence from Portugal on that date, ending centuries of colonial rule. CShapes area unchanged from the 1891-1975 colonial period (66.97 degree-squared units).
