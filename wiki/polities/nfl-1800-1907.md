# Colony of Newfoundland

## Summary

Newfoundland and its mainland dependency of Labrador as a **British colony**,
before the 1907 elevation to Dominion status that
[nfl-1907-1949](nfl-1907-1949.md) already covers. The island was a British
possession throughout the nineteenth century; a representative assembly was
granted in 1832, responsible government in 1855, and the colony rejected
Confederation with Canada at the 1869 election. The coast of Labrador was
re-annexed to Newfoundland's jurisdiction in 1809 and administered from
St John's for the rest of the period.

This row exists because WHEP's Newfoundland chain began at 1907 while a source
reports the colony's trade from 1816. Federico-Tena's *List of trading polities*
carries `Newfoundland` with series running **1816–1938**; of the 336 rows that
produces in the intake table, **239 fall in 1816–1906**, before
`NFL-1907-1949` opens, and had nowhere in WHEP to land (measured 2026-08-17
against `data/final/polities_database.csv`). Newfoundland is also not covered by
the pre-Confederation umbrella `CAN-1800-1866`: the colony was never part of the
Province of Canada, and its accession to Canada is 1949, which is precisely why
`CAN-1886-1949` exists as a separate row.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, gwcode **21**, feature year **1886**.
  CShapes offers exactly ONE step for Newfoundland — 1886-01-01 to 1948-07-21 —
  so there is no alternative vintage to choose between, and 1886 lies INSIDE
  this row's span (1800–1907), unlike the 1920 vintage the Dominion row
  declares. The geometry is island plus Labrador, 398,416 km² — the same
  geometry `NFL-1907-1949` carries, since the source has only the one step.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

- **`polygon_area_km2` is left blank on purpose.** The 398,416 km² the Dominion
  row declares was measured from this same feature, so repeating it here would be
  a self-referential area — a number that cannot disagree with the polygon it
  came from, and therefore no evidence about the territory.

- **Why `proxy` and not `assigned`.** Two reservations, both about Labrador and
  neither about the island:
  1. CShapes' Labrador limit is the modern one — effectively the boundary the
     Judicial Committee of the Privy Council drew in **1927**, which settled a
     dispute with Quebec over the interior watershed. Before 1927 the colony's
     mainland claim was contested, and for the early decades of this row it was
     in practice a coastal jurisdiction rather than a delimited interior. The
     polygon therefore over-delimits (not necessarily over-states) the mainland
     part for the whole span.
  2. CShapes' coverage begins in 1886, so the eight decades 1800–1886 are
     represented by a later vintage regardless.

  The island itself (about 108,900 km² of the 398,416) is unambiguous and
  unchanged.

- **No split at 1886.** A second row for 1800–1886 would carry the identical
  geometry from the identical feature and assert a territorial change that did
  not occur; the reservation above is recorded as `proxy` instead.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none. The colony's existence predates this database's 1800
  floor.
- **Successor:** [nfl-1907-1949](nfl-1907-1949.md) — the same territory as a
  Dominion from 1907; that row's `predecessor` has been updated to point back
  here, where it was previously empty.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 codes Newfoundland as a separate unit (gwcode 21) for 1886–1948,
  reflecting that it was a distinct polity from Canada until the 1949 accession.
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)
- Federico-Tena reports Newfoundland as a trading polity from 1816, eight
  decades before WHEP's Newfoundland chain began; the source's start years are
  evidence of when its compilers could estimate, not of a political event, so
  the 1816 start is NOT taken as this row's start year.
  [federico-tena-2019 §known-limitations](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#known-limitations)
- The label `Newfoundland` was one of the Federico-Tena labels that route to
  nothing, and was classified there as a polity that "exists but starts later
  than the Federico-Tena window".
  [federico-tena-2019 §the-76-labels-that-never-route](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#the-76-labels-that-never-route)

## Decisions

- **[2026-08-17] Span opens at 1800, not 1816.** 1816 is a property of one
  source's series, not of the colony. This database's convention for entities
  already existing at its floor is to open at 1800, and 30 other rows do so.
- **[2026-08-17] `cow_code` left empty.** `NFL-1907-1949` carries 21, which is
  the **Gleditsch-Ward** code CShapes uses; COW's own state list does not
  include Newfoundland, and ccode 21 is unassigned there while 20 is Canada.
  Rather than propagate a doubtful code to a second row, this row leaves it
  blank. Whether the Dominion row's 21 should be removed is a separate question
  about that row and is not settled here.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1927-labrador

No source fetched into this repository offers Newfoundland's mainland
jurisdiction as it stood before the 1927 JCPC award. Until one is added, the
Labrador portion of this row's polygon is a back-projection of the settled
boundary.
