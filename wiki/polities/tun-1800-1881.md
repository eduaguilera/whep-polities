# Beylik of Tunis

## Summary

The Beylik of Tunis (1800-1881) tracks the autonomous Ottoman tributary state in modern Tunisia from the database start year to the establishment of the French protectorate. Tunisia was an Ottoman province from the sixteenth century; from the seventeenth century it became semi-autonomous under the Husainid dynasty (established 1705). Biger describes it as "a province of the Ottoman Empire in the nineteenth century, until French protection was imposed by force by a treaty signed on 12 May 1881" — the Treaty of Bardo, which ends this row. The row ends when TUN-1881-2025 begins. CShapes does not cover the pre-1886 period; the polygon is back-projected from the 1886 CShapes entry (area ~15.19 sq deg, ~164,000 km²).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (back-projected); the pre-1886 period is outside CShapes' 1886–2019 scope. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **Area.** CShapes records the same area (~15.19 sq deg) for all three Tunisia entries from 1886 to 2019. The same area is back-projected for 1800-1885. No territorial changes are known for this period.
- **COW.** TUN, 616.
- **Boundaries.** Tunisia is bounded by Algeria to the west, Libya to the east, and the Mediterranean to the north and east. No major boundary changes are recorded during this period.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** No predecessor row exists in the CSV (field is NA). Tunisia's territory prior to 1800 was also an Ottoman beylik; a coverage gap exists before 1800.
- **Successor:** [tun-1881-2025](tun-1881-2025.md) — French protectorate established by Treaty of Bardo, 12 May 1881. Same territory continues as a single WHEP row through independence (1956) to the present.

## Open questions

### oq-paine-2024-source

The CSV lists the polygon source as "Paine et al. (2024)". This source has not been ingested in the wiki. A future ingest should identify and create a source file for Paine et al. (2024) and update the sourced claims for this row accordingly.

### oq-husainid-boundaries

The Husainid dynasty (from 1705) and earlier Ottoman governance did not significantly alter Tunisia's external boundaries. However, the exact extent of Tunisian administrative authority over border regions (particularly near Algeria and Libya) in the 1800-1881 period is not documented in currently ingested sources. Biger does not give detailed pre-1881 boundary information for Tunisia.
