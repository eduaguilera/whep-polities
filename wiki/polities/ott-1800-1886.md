# Ottoman Empire (to 1886)

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire as a trade and production unit
from the WHEP database start year **1800** to **1886**, which is
where CShapes 2.0's temporal coverage begins [cshapes-2.0 §scope].
The 1800 start is the database floor, not a territorial event; the
1886 end is a **polygon-source boundary**, not a territorial event
either — this is the most important caveat on the page and is
discussed under *Territorial extent* and
[oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory].

Over the row's 87-year window the Ottoman Empire actually lost
substantial territory: Greek independence 1821–1830
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821], Serbian autonomy 1829
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1829], Muhammad Ali's seizure of
Syria and Arabia 1831–1833
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1831], and above all the Congress
of Berlin in 1878, which assigned Bosnia-Herzegovina to
Austro-Hungarian occupation, recognized Romania/Serbia/Montenegro
as independent, and gave Cyprus to Britain
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878]. Under a strict reading of
the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition], this row
should arguably be split further (e.g. at 1830 and at 1878); see
[oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory] and
[oq-further-splits-1830-1878].

Predecessor in the CSV: `NA` (pre-database). Successor:
[ott-1886-1908].

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (Ottoman Empire 1800-1802, 2.66M km2)`
  per the CSV (`polygon_source`). This is a single Cliopatria
  time-step — Cliopatria has 33 separate records for the Ottoman
  Empire [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §scope], but WHEP extracts only the
  1800-era snapshot and applies it across the full 1800–1886 row.
- **~2% temporal coverage, acknowledged in the repo.** From
  `docs/06_KNOWN_ISSUES_AND_DECISIONS.md`: *"Ottoman entries
  (OTT-1800-1886 etc.): Cliopatria polygon from one time-step
  applied across periods with significant territorial change (~2%
  temporal coverage)."* In other words: the polygon shows the
  1800 extent, but the row spans 87 years during which Greece,
  Serbia, Algeria, Tunisia (not yet), Bosnia, Bulgaria, and others
  leave or become autonomous. The polygon is literally correct
  for 1800 and literally wrong for 1885.
  [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §single-time-step]
- **No CShapes coverage.** CShapes 2.0 begins in 1886
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope]; the entire 1800–1886 window is outside
  its temporal scope. The row cannot be validated against
  CShapes-COW.
- **Post-1886.** The row ends exactly where CShapes begins. This
  is the load-bearing fact for [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory].
- **Modern concordance.** No ISO3 (OTT is a WHEP-internal code,
  not ISO). COW 640 [database] — the same code COW later
  assigns to Türkiye, consistent with COW's convention of
  treating the Ottoman Empire and Türkiye as a single state-system
  entity [cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion].

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — the WHEP database starts at 1800 by
  convention; whatever existed in 1799 is out of scope.
- **Successor:** [ott-1886-1908] — *Ottoman Empire (1886–1908)*,
  same polity under the WHEP polity-definition rule but a
  different polygon source. The split is at the CShapes temporal
  floor, not at a territorial event.

## Sourced claims

- **1821–1830: Greek War of Independence.** The uprising began
  6 March 1821 in the Danubian Principalities
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821]. Greece was formally
  recognized as independent in 1830 (the exact date not captured
  in the current Wikipedia snapshot — see
  [oq-1830-events]).
- **1829: Treaty of Adrianople.** Serbia gained autonomy from
  Ottoman rule [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1829].
- **1830: French invasion of Algeria.** "In 1830 military
  intervention by the French led to the conquest of Algiers,
  provoking the fall of Ottoman overlordship throughout the
  whole of Algeria" [biger-1995 §algeria]. Biger gives 1830 as
  a hard date for the loss of the Regency of Algiers (an
  Ottoman province since the 16th century, quasi-independent
  after 1671 per the same source). Full French control of the
  northern territory followed by 1847, integration with France
  in 1881, Sahara annexation 24 December 1902
  [biger-1995 §algeria]. Also corroborated by the
  `ALG-1830-1902` row in the database [database].
- **1831–1833: Muhammad Ali of Egypt seized Syria and Arabia.**
  "By the war's end in 1833" Egypt controlled Syrian territory
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1831]. Egypt nominally remained
  an Ottoman province but was de facto independent. Egypt is
  tracked as a separate WHEP row (`EGY-*`, not examined here).
- **1853–1856: Crimean War.** "Britain and France successfully
  defended the Ottoman Empire against Russia"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1853–1856]. No Ottoman
  territorial change resulted.
- **1877–1878: Russo-Turkish War.** Romania, Serbia, and
  Montenegro "each...formally proclaimed independence"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1877–1878].
- **1878: Congress of Berlin.** The most consequential territorial
  event in the row: "Austria-Hungary to occupy Bosnia and
  Herzegovina" and "Great Britain to take over Cyprus"; Bulgaria
  became autonomous under Ottoman suzerainty; Romania, Serbia,
  and Montenegro were formally recognized as independent
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878]. The `BOS-1878-1908` and
  `HZG-1878-1908` WHEP rows start at 1878 precisely because of
  this event [database].
- **COW state-system membership.** COW `statelist2024.csv` records a
  single continuous tenure row for the polity: `TUR, 640, Turkey,
  1816-01-01 → 2024-12-31` [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates].
  COW makes **no distinction** between the Ottoman Empire and
  modern Türkiye — the same ccode, stateabb, and (unbroken)
  state-system membership covers the entire 1816–2024 window.
  Under COW's model the Ottoman→Türkiye transition of 1922–1923
  is a regime change, not a polity change. This is consistent
  with WHEP's continuity rule in principle
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition], but WHEP
  splits the polity into multiple rows anyway (OTT-1800-1886 →
  OTT-1886-1908 → OTT-1908-1912 → TUR-1913-1914 → TUR-1914-1918
  → TUR-1918-1920 → TUR-1920-2025). The contrast is sharp: COW
  sees one continuous state for 209 years; WHEP sees seven rows
  for the same territory.
- **WHEP continuity model.** Under the WHEP polity definition
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition], a
  territorial-economic polity is split on substantial territorial
  change. The Ottoman Empire's 1800–1886 window contains several
  substantial changes (1830 Greece/Algeria, 1878 Berlin Congress)
  but the row is not split at them. The repo's 1886 split is
  triggered by polygon-source availability, not territorial
  change — see [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory].

## Open questions


### oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory

The row ends at
1886 because that is where CShapes 2.0 begins, not because of
a territorial event. Under the WHEP polity definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition], splits
should be driven by substantial territorial change. Either
the 1886 boundary is a pragmatic exception the rule needs to
permit explicitly (polygon-source transitions are allowed to
split a row even without a territorial change), or the split
is inconsistent with the rule and should be redrawn at 1878
or another real date. A `decision`-kind log entry is needed
to pick a position.

### oq-further-splits-1830-1878

If the WHEP rule *is* taken strictly, this row should probably
be further split at or near **1830** and **1878**. Biger 1995
now provides concrete anchor dates for both:

- **1830** — "In 1830 military intervention by the French led
  to the conquest of Algiers, provoking the fall of Ottoman
  overlordship throughout the whole of Algeria"
  [biger-1995 §algeria]. Greek independence in the same window
  (the War of Independence ran 1821–1829 per Biger via the
  GREECE entry snapshot in
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11]). Serbian autonomy between
  1815 and 1833 per [biger-1995 §bosnia]'s BOSNIA–SERBIA
  subsection. The 1830 cluster is therefore a real,
  academically-sourced territorial event large enough to
  justify a split under the WHEP rule.
- **1878** — "remained unchanged after the Congress of Berlin
  (1878), when Bosnia came under the influence of
  Austria-Hungary and Serbia was recognized as an independent
  state" [biger-1995 §bosnia]. Corroborates the
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878] claim.

Each "sub-split" would need its own polygon. Cliopatria has
33 Ottoman time-steps [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §scope], so the
material exists; the question is whether extracting more
time-steps is worth the pipeline complexity for this one
polity. With Biger in hand, the *historical* justification
for the splits is now solid; only the *pipeline cost* is
unresolved.

### oq-1830-events

**Partially resolved 2026-04-11** by
[log biger-ottoman-batch-ingest]. Biger 1995 directly
corroborates the **1830 French conquest of Algeria** with
multiple cross-referenced mentions ([biger-1995 §algeria],
pp.22/24/27/28) and states the Franco-Ottoman transition
explicitly: "provoking the fall of Ottoman overlordship
throughout the whole of Algeria" (p.22). Biger also confirms
the Ottoman-era preceding state ("Algeria became an Ottoman
province... After 1671 Algiers was a quasi-independent
backwater", p.22) and the full timeline of subsequent French
absorption (1847 full northern control, 1848 three
départements, 1881 integration, 1902 Sahara annexation). The
remaining gaps this question covered — the exact 1830 London
Protocol date for Greek independence and the 1830 Serbian
autonomy mechanism — Biger does not give verbatim, but puts
the Greek War of Independence in the range **1821–1829** (via
the GREECE entry snapshot p.247 already in
`wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11`). Full verbatim-date resolution
would need a GREECE or SERBIA focused read from Biger or a
dedicated source for the 1830 London Protocol.

### oq-muhammad-ali-egypt-1831

The 1831–1833 Muhammad Ali
Egyptian seizure of Syria is flagged in Sourced claims but
Egypt itself is tracked as a separate WHEP row. The
relationship between the Ottoman parent row and the Egyptian
child row during the Muhammad Ali crisis is not worked out;
cross-check against `EGY-*` pages once they exist.
