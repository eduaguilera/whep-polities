# Territory of Papua (1949-1975)

## Summary

The Territory of Papua -- the south-eastern quarter of New Guinea island plus
the Louisiade, D'Entrecasteaux and Trobriand groups -- from the 1949
administrative union with the Territory of New Guinea until Papua New Guinea's
independence in 1975.

The Papua and New Guinea Act 1949 put the two territories under ONE
administration, but it did not merge them. Papua remained an Australian
possession (a territory of the Commonwealth), while New Guinea remained a
United Nations Trust Territory administered by Australia under the 1946
trusteeship agreement. The two kept distinct legal status until 1975, and
statistical publications went on tabulating them separately.

This is a **component entry, not a sovereign or colonial state**. `type:
subnational` is deliberate and serves the same purpose as on
[man-1950-1955](man-1950-1955.md): the row must not compete with the combined
[png-1949-1975](png-1949-1975.md) in the matcher's family ranking. It exists to
carry the *reporting territory*.

**Why this entry exists.** `fao1952` reports `Papua` and `New Guinea` as two
separate units for 1950 and 1951. Both labels were routed to the combined
`PNG-1949-1975`, which receives the whole union. On that polity the two
territories' figures shared **9 (polity, source, year, item, unit, indicator)
keys**. A consumer that reduces duplicate keys with `mean()` published their
average, i.e. roughly half of either territory, as the value for the whole of
Papua and New Guinea. Examples:

| item (fao1952) | year | Papua | New Guinea | published mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| population total (1000) | 1951 | 369 | 1,103 | 736 |
| cropa (copra, 1000 t) | 1950 | 12.6 | 58.5 | 35.55 |
| natural rubber (1000 t) | 1951 | 2.6 | 0.1 | 1.35 |
| total tractors (number) | 1950 | 40 | 26 | 33 |

Routing each label to its own territory removes all 9 collisions. The combined
label `New Guinea and Papua` (land use, 1951) stays on `PNG-1949-1975`, which is
the territory it names.

The same fix applies to [tngu-1949-1975](tngu-1949-1975.md) for `New Guinea`.
The alias previously in force for `Papua` 1950-1951 argued that 11 rows did not
warrant a granular polity. That argument predates the collision measurement,
and it could not see that the rows were being averaged with New Guinea's.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `911` (Papua), vintage 1920 (the feature
dated 1920-12-17 to 1949-06-30), the same geometry
[tpap-1906-1949](tpap-1906-1949.md) uses (224,148 km2 in ESRI:54034; `polygon_area_km2` is left null because that figure is
the reused feature's own measurement, issue 195). See
[CShapes 2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md).

`polygon_status: proxy` rather than `assigned`, because CShapes stops coding
Papua separately on 1949-06-30 and codes only the combined unit (`910`,
461,689 km2) from 1 July 1949. The feature is therefore reused past its own end
date. The boundary it draws did not move: the Papua / New Guinea line of
1949-1975 is the pre-1949 line, and it survives today as the boundary between
the Papuan provinces and the New Guinea provinces of Papua New Guinea. The proxy
is a statement about the feature's dates, not about the shape.

**Scale check.** Papua's `feature 911` is 48.5% of the combined `910` polygon.
The two fao1952 labels divide the population the same way. Papua has 369
thousand of the 1,472 thousand in 1951 (25%), which fits the less populous
territory with the smaller share of land.

## Predecessors and successors

`predecessor` and `successor` are left empty on purpose, and the containment
edge to `PNG-1949-1975` carries the relation instead. The union polity already
names [tpap-1906-1949](tpap-1906-1949.md) as its predecessor and
[png-1975-2025](png-1975-2025.md) as its successor. Threading this component
into the same succession chain would give the ISO3 successor map two parallel
paths through 1949-1975 for one iso3. The containment edge is the relation that
is true.

## Decisions

### d-component-row-not-respan

Created as a new row rather than by extending `TPAP-1906-1949` to 1975. That
code embeds its span, and re-spanning it would change which polity every
existing citation names. It would also make the pre-1949 colony and the
post-1949 union component one row, which is the period split this database
draws at 1949 everywhere else in the PNG family.

### d-proxy-not-unassigned

The CShapes geometry is reused rather than leaving the polygon `unassigned`.
The territory's boundary is documented as unchanged, so an empty polygon would
discard a correct shape to avoid naming a feature past its coded end date.

## Open questions

### oq-other-sources-after-1949

Only `fao1952` is known to report Papua separately after 1949. Other sources
that do so (Australian territorial annual reports, or `mitchell` if it splits
the territories) are not ingested, so whether this row should receive more
data is not measured.
