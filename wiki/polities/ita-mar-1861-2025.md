# Marche (region of Italy)

## Summary

Marche is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni), running along the central Adriatic coast between Emilia-Romagna/San Marino to the north and Abruzzo to the south, with the Apennines forming its inland border toward Umbria and Tuscany. Its capital is Ancona. This entry is `type: subnational` because Marche has never been a sovereign state in this period: it was part of the Papal States until occupied by the Piedmontese army in 1860 and annexed to the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont by plebiscite that same year, becoming an administrative subdivision of the unified Kingdom of Italy, then the Republic, ever since. The entry exists to carry a *reporting territory* distinct from the national ITA rows: NUTS2-level regional statistical series (agricultural production, population, and similar data) report Marche specifically, a mid-sized, historically agrarian central region with its own economic profile, so routing this data to a national ITA row would misattribute a regional figure to the whole country. No prior polity row represented Marche on its own; the routed data would otherwise have fallen to the national chain, which contains but is not this region. Marche is a standard region case (unlike Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia, annexed in 1919, or Molise, split from Abruzzo in 1963), so per the country convention's default rule its start_year is set to the earliest national container's start, 1861, following the precedent already set for sibling entries ITA-CAL-1861-2025 and ITA-LOM-1861-2025.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a regional-level statistical series for Marche (NUTS2 code ITI3, per the routing decision's admin_name field), routed under the country convention for Italy's subnational regions (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json), following the same pattern already applied to sibling NUTS2 units including ITA-CAL and ITA-LOM. Previously, absent a polity row for Marche, this data had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows (ITA-1861-1866 through ITA-1919-2025) — which is wrong because those rows represent the whole of Italy (a multiple of Marche's area at any given date) and mixing a regional series into a national row would either overstate national totals if double-counted, or misattribute a distinctly regional figure to the nation as a whole. What confirms Marche is a distinct reporting entity rather than an artefact of routing convenience: it is Eurostat's own standard NUTS2 statistical division of Italy, used because Italy's own official statistics (ISTAT) report at the regional level as a first-class unit, separate from national totals — the same reasoning already applied to its NUTS2 siblings ITA-CAL and ITA-LOM.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The only registered source type that digitises country subdivisions at Marche's level (ADM1/NUTS2) is `gadm-4.1-adm1`, but the copy of that source present in this repository is an explicit curated subset of 81 countries that does not include Italy at all (zero ITA features). No other registered source (cshapes, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) covers modern Italian regional boundaries. A correct polygon almost certainly exists in GADM's full global release (a superset of the local subset), but it has not been fetched here, so `polygon_source` is set to the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` per the registered_source_unfetched route, and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` rather than `assigned`, exactly as done for the sibling entries ITA-CAL-1861-2025 and ITA-LOM-1861-2025. See open question oq-polygon-fetch-needed-mar.

**Territory description:** Marche covers approximately 9,400 km2, making it about 3% of Italy's total land area — a mid-sized, roughly rectangular region on the central Adriatic coast. A modern-map reader can locate it as the strip of central-eastern Italy between Emilia-Romagna/San Marino (north), Abruzzo (south), the Apennines and Umbria/Tuscany (west), and the Adriatic Sea (east); its main cities are Ancona (capital), Pesaro, and Ascoli Piceno. The region's own outer boundary has been stable since its 1860-61 annexation; administrative reorganizations in this period (such as the 2009 creation of the province of Fermo out of Ascoli Piceno province) redrew *provincial* boundaries within Marche, not the region's own outer boundary, so a single present-day regional outline should be a reasonably safe proxy back to 1861 once a source is fetched — a vintage-drift caveat that belongs on that future polygon assignment, not a reason to fabricate one now. No area figure here is measured from an attached geometry, since no polygon is yet assigned; the 9,400 km2 figure is Marche's well-documented modern administrative area, cited as territory context, not a polygon measurement.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first polity row created for Marche specifically, and it starts exactly at the region's incorporation into the unified Kingdom of Italy in 1861 (Marche having joined the Kingdom of Sardinia in 1860 via plebiscite after the Piedmontese army occupied the Papal States' eastern Marche provinces), so there is no distinct prior polity to chain from. No successor: the end_year of 2025 is open, matching the still-current status of Marche as a first-level administrative region of the Italian Republic; if a future administrative reorganization abolished or redrew Marche, a successor row would be added then. The four container edges (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) tile the whole span without gap or overlap, reflecting that Marche's own territory was unaffected by any of the national-level changes (Veneto 1866, Rome 1870, WWI settlement 1919) that motivate those national row boundaries.

## Sourced claims

- Marche is one of Italy's twenty regioni and corresponds to the Eurostat NUTS2 statistical unit ITI3, per the routing decision's admin_name field.
- Marche was part of the Papal States until the Piedmontese army occupied its eastern provinces in September 1860 during the unification campaign, followed by an annexation plebiscite later that year, so the region joined the Kingdom of Sardinia in 1860 and then the Kingdom of Italy at its proclamation in 1861.
- Marche's region-level (NUTS2) outline has been stable since unification, though its internal provincial boundaries have changed (e.g. the province of Fermo was created out of Ascoli Piceno in 2009), a distinction consistent with the reasoning already applied to Calabria (ITA-CAL-1861-2025) and Lombardia (ITA-LOM-1861-2025).

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-mar

**Code uses ITA-MAR, not the source's NUTS id ITI3**

The routing decision names this unit by its Eurostat NUTS2 code ITI3, but NUTS codes are a statistical classification, not a name a reader of this wiki would recognize, and they have been reassigned across NUTS revisions (Marche's own code has read ITE3 and ITI3 in different revisions). Following the dominant 57-of-66 pattern <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, the subunit token is set to MAR, a readable abbreviation of the region's own name (Marche), matching the precedent already set for sibling entries such as ITA-CAL-1861-2025 (CAL for Calabria) and ITA-LOM-1861-2025 (LOM for Lombardia) rather than reusing a codelist id that changes underneath the reader. iso3 stays ITA per the dominant pattern; polity_code is therefore ITA-MAR-1861-2025.

### d-start-year-1861-mar

**start_year set to 1861 per the country's default rule, matching the routing decision and the Calabria/Lombardia precedent**

The routing decision explicitly states that Marche is not one of the two enumerated departure cases (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia annexed 1919, Molise split from Abruzzo in 1963), so the country convention's default rule sets start_year to 1861, the start of the earliest container national row (ITA-1861-1866) whose territory includes Marche. This matches how ITA-CAL-1861-2025 and ITA-LOM-1861-2025 were already created under the same convention. Marche in fact joined the Kingdom of Sardinia in 1860 (via plebiscite after the Piedmontese invasion of the Papal States' eastern provinces), one year before the Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed, so 1861 slightly understates how early Marche left papal rule, but it is the correct start for this entry because 1861 is when the containing national row (and hence any statistical series routed here) begins, not the year of the plebiscite itself.

### d-container-tiling-mar

**Container edges use the four fine-grained ITA eras, not the coarse ITA-1861-1919 aggregate**

As with ITA-CAL-1861-2025 and ITA-LOM-1861-2025, the polity table carries both a fine partition of the Kingdom of Italy (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919) and a coarse aggregate row (ITA-1861-1919) covering the same span. Using the aggregate would double-count the interval against the fine rows if both are ever queried together. The four container edges here (1861-1866, 1866-1870, 1870-1919, 1919-2025) tile 1861-2025 with no gap and no overlap using the finer, non-aggregate rows, consistent with the sibling entries' precedent.

### d-polygon-route-unfetched-mar

**polygon_source is gadm-4.1-adm1 (the registered slug), not a route name, with polygon_status unassigned**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered in sources.yaml with id_column GID_1 and is the correct mechanism for a modern Italian ADM1 region, but the locally present gadm-4.1-adm1.gpkg is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Italy (0 ITA features), exactly as already documented for ITA-CAL-1861-2025, ITA-LOM-1861-2025, and the other Italian region siblings. Per the harness rule that registered_source_unfetched means the slug IS the source and polygon_status is unassigned, this entry sets polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1 (not 'new_source_needed' or the literal route name) and polygon_status: unassigned, with polygon_feature_id left null until the full/global GADM 4.1 ADM1 release is fetched and Marche's feature identified.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-fetch-needed-mar

**No fetched polygon source actually covers Marche yet**

The locally present gadm-4.1-adm1 file is an explicit 81-country subset that excludes Italy entirely (0 features for ITA), and no other registered source in sources.yaml digitises Italian ADM1 boundaries; cshapes/cshapes-europe are country-level only. The fix is to fetch the full/global GADM 4.1 ADM1 release (a superset of the local subset) and identify Marche's feature id within it, but this has not been confirmed and no id is guessed here. Until fetched, polygon_feature_id stays null and polygon_status stays unassigned. This mirrors the identical open question already recorded on ITA-CAL-1861-2025, ITA-LOM-1861-2025, and the other Italian region siblings, so a single future fetch of the global GADM layer would likely resolve all of them at once.

### oq-sibling-segmentation-conflict-mar

**This entry's single 1861-2025 span may conflict with how other, earlier-processed Italian regions were routed on a narrower start**

Some already-created Italian region siblings (e.g. ITA-ABR-1970-2025, ITA-LIG-1970-2025, ITA-EMR-1970-2025) start in 1970, not 1861, while others (ITA-CAL-1861-2025, ITA-LOM-1861-2025) start in 1861 under the same 'default rule' language. If the 1970-start entries reflect an intentional narrower reading of the country convention (e.g. because NUTS2/regional-level Italian statistics were only collected from 1970) rather than a routing-verdict inconsistency, then this entry's 1861 start could be the outlier, or vice versa. This should be checked against pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json and against whichever data series actually motivated this Marche row before more Italian regions are processed.

### oq-marche-outer-boundary-vintage-mar

**Marche's own outer boundary vintage relative to the 1948 region-formalization is unchecked**

Marche's provincial-level boundaries have changed over the 20th century (e.g. the province of Fermo was carved out of Ascoli Piceno only in 2009), but these are sub-region (provincial) boundary changes, not changes to the region's own outer boundary, which is assumed stable since the 1860 plebiscite by analogy with Calabria and Lombardia. This assumption has not been independently verified for Marche specifically (e.g. against ISTAT's historical administrative maps or against any 19th-century adjustment along the former papal frontier), and should be checked once a polygon source is fetched.
