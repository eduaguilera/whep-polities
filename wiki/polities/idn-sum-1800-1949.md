# Sumatra (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Sumatra, "one of the Sunda Islands of western Indonesia", "the largest island that is fully within Indonesian territory". One of the Outer Provinces ([idn-out-1800-1949](idn-out-1800-1949.md)). [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §sumatra](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#sumatra)

## Why this entry exists

The label `dutch sumatra` reports it alone for 1922-1948 (**9 (year, label) pairs**), beside `dutch east indies` and its other islands. No polity was this island before 1949; the 1949-1951 rows (IDN-JVM, IDN-BLB, IDN-OTH) group islands differently and begin in 1949. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: the eight GADM 4.1 adm1 provinces on the island (Aceh, North, West and South Sumatra, Riau, Jambi, Bengkulu, Lampung); the Riau Islands and Bangka-Belitung, separate residencies, are left out. **Proxy**: present-day GADM administrative boundaries stand for the colonial residencies, whose limits on the islands' coasts were the same ground.
