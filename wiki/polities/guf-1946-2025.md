# French Guiana (Overseas Department)

## Summary

French Guiana became a French overseas department (Département d'Outre-Mer, DOM) on 19 March 1946 under law 46-451, transitioning from colonial to departmental status. Its territory (~83,534 km² on the north-eastern coast of South America) and French sovereignty were entirely unchanged at this transition. This polity covers 1946–2025 (the WHEP database ceiling).

The predecessor polity [guf-1816-1946](guf-1816-1946.md) covers the colonial period. fao1952 data extends to 1951, providing the primary WHEP coverage for this row.

## Territorial Extent

Identical to [guf-1816-1946](guf-1816-1946.md): ~83,534–83,711 km², borders established by the 1900 arbitration award (Oyapock River / Brazil) and 1915 treaty (Maroni River / Suriname).
