# Sierra Leone (1895-1961)

## Summary

Sierra Leone (1895-1961) covers the British colonial period from 24 August 1895 -- when the boundary with French Guinea was adjusted, establishing the protectorate's final extent -- to 26 April 1961, the eve of independence. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=GBR (COW 200). The territory is unchanged throughout this period (area ~5.95).

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory bounded by Guinea, Liberia, and the Atlantic Ocean. Area ~5.95 (CShapes units). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).

## Open questions

### oq-sle-protectorate-1896

The Sierra Leone Protectorate was formally proclaimed on 31 August 1896, separate from the Colony of Sierra Leone (Freetown area). The CShapes boundary change at 1895 may reflect the Anglo-French agreement rather than the protectorate proclamation.
