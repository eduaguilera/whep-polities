# United States (1803-1848)

## Summary

This row tracks the United States from the **Louisiana Purchase
(1803)** to the **Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo (1848)** — a
**46-year row** of massive territorial expansion
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). During this
period the US grew from ~1.2M km2 to ~3.5M km2 through the
Louisiana Purchase (+2.1M km2), the acquisition of Florida
(Adams-Onis Treaty, 1819), the annexation of Texas (1845), and the
Oregon Treaty (1846).

The end-of-row event is the **Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo (1848)**
ending the Mexican-American War, which transferred ~1.4M km2
(California, Nevada, Utah, most of Arizona and New Mexico, parts of
Colorado and Wyoming) from Mexico to the United States. This is
another massive territorial change justifying the split under the
WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition).

Predecessor: [usa-1800-1803](usa-1800-1803.md). Successor:
[usa-1848-1867](usa-1848-1867.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon (since 2026-09-24).** `constructed/USA-1803-1848`: CShapes 2.0
  gwcode 2's 1886-1959 step (the contiguous United States, Great Lakes water
  included) **minus** Texas and the Mexican north (the Spanish side of the
  Adams-Onís line) and **minus** Oregon Country — i.e. the United States as it
  stood from the Adams-Onís Treaty (in force 1821) to the annexation of Texas
  (1845). It measures **4,761,396 km²** (EPSG:6933), 4,604,484 km² without the
  156,912 km² of Great Lakes water. `polygon_status: proxy`, because one
  configuration stands for a 45-year row. See
  [decision-polygon-constructed-adams-onis](#decision-polygon-constructed-adams-onis).
  The Cliopatria description below is the row's previous binding, kept as
  written; its reading of the 1842-1845 step was also wrong about which step
  was bound (the database carried the **1815-1819** step, 1,586,289 km²).
- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (United States of America
  1842-1845)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). A single
  Cliopatria time-step from 1842–1845 applied across all 46 years
  of the row. For the early part of the row (1803–1819), the
  polygon overstates the actual territory — it includes Florida
  (acquired 1819) and the full Louisiana Purchase extent that was
  not yet fully surveyed or settled. For the late part (1845–1848),
  it understates the territory — Oregon Treaty (1846) gains and
  Texas annexation (1845) may or may not be reflected in the
  1842–1845 Cliopatria polygon.
- **Area.** ~3.5M km2 by 1845 per CSV notes
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

### decision-polygon-constructed-adams-onis

**`polygon_source` moved `cliopatria` (`United States of America`, 1815) ->
`constructed` (`USA-1803-1848`) on 2026-09-24.** Written up by Claude (Claude
Code); open for review in the pull request that made it.

**Why.** PR 671 found [USA-1848-1867](usa-1848-1867.md)'s Cliopatria polygon
omitting about 4.37M km² of the trans-Mississippi West. This row had the same
defect, larger in proportion: Cliopatria's 1815-1819 step measures
**1,586,289 km²** and stops at the settled frontier, so it leaves out nearly all
of the **Louisiana Purchase — the event that opens the row**. Against the
census's 4,630,914 km² for 1820-1840 it was 34% of the territory.

**Which configuration, and why this one.** The row spans five acquisitions
(Louisiana 1803, West Florida 1810-1813, Red River basin 1818, Florida 1819/1821,
Texas 1845, Oregon 1846), and a row can carry one polygon. The Adams-Onís
configuration (1821-1845):

- holds for **24 of the 45 years**, longer than any other;
- is what the **1820, 1830 and 1840 censuses** measured — *Historical Statistics
  of the United States* series J 1-2
  ([Census Bureau 1975, ch. J](https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/1975/compendia/hist_stats_colonial-1970/hist_stats_colonial-1970p1-chJ.pdf))
  gives the same 1,788,006 sq mi (4,630,914 km², land and inland water, Great
  Lakes excluded) for all three;
- places no layer-B row at risk: none is routed to this row today.

**How it is built** (`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`,
`build_usa_1803_1848`), by subtraction from the contiguous US so that it nests
exactly between [USA-1800-1803](usa-1800-1803.md) and USA-1848-1867:

| removed | line used | source |
|---|---|---|
| Texas | the Sabine, 32°N and the Red River ARE the Texas outline | [GADM 4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md) adm1 |
| California, Nevada, Utah, Arizona, New Mexico | wholly on the Spanish side | GADM 4.1 adm1 |
| Colorado, Wyoming, Kansas, Oklahoma south/west of the line | 100°W from the Red River to the Arkansas, the Arkansas to its source, due north to 42°N | meridian + Natural Earth 10m Arkansas centreline (inside [Paine et al. 2024](../sources/paine-et-al-2024.md)) + parallel |
| Oregon Country (north of 42°N, west of the continental divide) | Washington, Oregon, Idaho whole; Montana's 11 Clark Fork/Kootenai counties and Wyoming's Teton, Sublette and Lincoln (north of 42°N) | GADM 4.1 adm1 + the adm2 layer of `gadm41_USA.gpkg` |

**Measured against the census** (EPSG:6933):

| | polygon | census (HSUS J 1-2) | residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| this row, without Great Lakes water | 4,604,484 | 4,630,914 | **-0.57%** |
| removed: Texas + Mexican Cession + Gadsden | 2,445,912 | 390,143 + 529,017 + 29,640 sq mi = 2,457,381 | -0.47% |
| removed: Oregon Country | 738,247 | 285,580 sq mi = 739,649 | -0.19% |
| added since USA-1800-1803: Louisiana + Floridas + Red River basin | 2,438,768 | 827,192 + 72,003 sq mi + 119,795 km² = 2,448,699 | -0.41% |

The row's -0.57% is exactly the offset the untouched CShapes contiguous US
carries against the census 1860 figure (7,783,059 without lakes vs 7,827,946),
so the construction adds no measurable error of its own; the two treaty
pieces, measured independently, both come within 0.5%. That the Oregon piece
lands at -0.19% is the evidence that the county approximation of the divide
is adequate.

**Approximations, stated.**

- **1803-1821 is overstated** by Florida and the West Florida strips (census
  72,003 sq mi = 186,487 km²) and by the Red River basin (119,795 km², British
  until 1818).
- **1845-1848 is understated** by Texas and Oregon (census 390,143 + 285,580 sq
  mi = 1,750,115 km², three years of the row). See
  [oq-texas-oregon-split](#oq-texas-oregon-split).
- **Oregon Country is excluded for 1818-1846**: it was held in joint occupation
  with Britain under the 1818 Convention, not as US territory, and the census
  counts it as an 1846 acquisition.
- The divide north of 42°N is the nearest county line; Sweetwater County's strip
  north of 42°N (Great Divide Basin) stays on the Louisiana side. Maine carries
  its post-1842 line. North Park (Colorado, west of the Arkansas-source meridian)
  is on the Spanish side, as the treaty's meridian puts it.
- [USA-TX-1845-2025](usa-tx-1845-2025.md) declares this row as its container for
  1845-1848; Texas lies wholly outside this polygon. The edge is history (Texas
  was a state from December 1845) and the polygon is the 1821-1845 one, so the
  two disagree for those three years by design.

## Open questions

### oq-texas-oregon-split

Opened 2026-09-24. The annexation of Texas (1845) and the Oregon Treaty (1846)
add about 1.75M km² (+38%) three years before this row ends — a larger change
than the Gadsden Purchase, and under
[decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition) a
candidate split (`USA-1803-1845` + `USA-1845-1848`). Not done here: it renames a
published code, is a structural change under the polity-autoimprove checklist,
and no layer-B row falls in 1845-1848 today, so the only consumer of the
difference is a per-km² series that has no data to divide.

### oq-polygon-pre-louisiana

**Answered 2026-09-24:** the row now carries the 1821-1845 configuration, which
overstates 1803-1821 by Florida and the Red River basin (about 306,000 km², 6% of
the polygon), no longer by the Cliopatria 1842-1845 extent. See
[decision-polygon-constructed-adams-onis](#decision-polygon-constructed-adams-onis).
The original question is kept below.

The Cliopatria 1842–1845 polygon is applied across the full row
including 1803–1819, when US territory was significantly smaller
(no Florida, partial Louisiana exploration). The polygon
overstates early-row territory. Not load-bearing if analyses
focus on the 1840s, but relevant for 1803–1819 sub-period work.
