# Atacama (region of Chile)

## Summary

Atacama is a first-order administrative region (Region III, later Region de Atacama) of Chile, on the country's northern desert coast between Antofagasta to the north and Coquimbo to the south. It was created by the 1974 Decreto Ley 575 regionalization reform and became operational in 1976, replacing the older province-based structure with the current region/province/comuna hierarchy. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chile held unbroken sovereignty over this territory throughout; the entry exists to carry a distinct *reporting territory* below the national level, not to claim statehood. It spans 1976-2025 (end open/exclusive, since the region is still current) because 1976 is the default start rule for Chilean regions created by DL 575 that are not among the three documented later-split departures (Arica y Parinacota, Los Rios, Nuble), and no earlier polity IS this specific region -- the existing CHL-* rows are national totals that merely contain it.

**Why this entry exists.** Data for Atacama-territory measures exist across 1900-2025, but no existing polity row is this specific sub-national reporting unit: the four CHL-* candidates in the polity table are national totals for all of Chile, which contain Atacama but are roughly 30x its area and cannot represent region-level series without conflating them with the rest of the country. The routing decision assigns this entry the post-1976 data plus a back-cast treatment for 1900-1975 territory-level reconstructions, whose method breakdown (30.7% interpolated_scaled, 23.7% luh2_calibrated_to_fao1961, 7.5% continuous_luh2_extrapolated, plus a continuous_iia_to_fao1990 segment) confirms these pre-1976 figures are dasymetric allocations onto the modern regional boundary rather than direct period observations naming an administrative predecessor -- so they back_cast onto this entry rather than matching a genuine historical polity. What confirms Atacama is a distinct, statistically-tracked unit rather than an arbitrary carve-out is that Chile's own national statistics institute (INE) and derived series (e.g. LUH2-calibrated regional agricultural data) report at the region level from 1976 onward, and DL 575 is itself the primary legal source establishing the region as a first-order administrative division with its own defined boundary, capital (Copiapo), and government from that date.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon has actually been pulled for this period: the routing decision names GADM 4.1 admin-1 (feature id `CHL.3_1`, expected) as the appropriate source -- a present-day admin-1 boundary is a low-vintage-risk proxy for the full 1976-2025 span because Atacama's regional boundaries have been stable since their 1976 creation -- but the GADM 4.1 layer cached locally in this repository is only an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 Chilean features present), so no feature can be fetched from disk today. `polygon_source` is therefore recorded as the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` with `polygon_status: unassigned`, distinct from `polygon_source: none`, because a specific already-registered source is intended -- it is only unfetched, not undecided.

**Territory description:** Atacama Region occupies Chile's Atacama Desert coast, from roughly 26 S to 29 S latitude, bordered by Antofagasta Region to the north, Coquimbo Region to the south, Argentina to the east, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Its capital is Copiapo. It comprises three provinces (Chanaral, Copiapo, Huasco) and covers roughly 75,000-76,000 km2 by present-day official Chilean government figures (INE), a figure not yet independently confirmed from an attached polygon since none has been fetched for this entry.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor is recorded: prior to 1976 there was no distinct Atacama-region administrative entity, only the pre-DL 575 province structure and the national government, and pre-1976 territory-level data is treated as a back-cast reconstruction rather than evidence of a genuine administrative predecessor (see why_this_entry_exists). No successor is recorded: the region remains current as of 2025, so end_year is open/exclusive per country convention rather than marking an actual dissolution or reorganization.

## Sourced claims

- Decreto Ley 575 of 1974 established Chile's current region/province/comuna administrative hierarchy, replacing the earlier province-based system, with the new regions becoming operational in 1976.
- Chile's national statistics institute (INE) reports agricultural and demographic series at the region level for Atacama from 1976 onward, distinct from national totals, which is the statistical basis for treating this as a separately-tracked reporting unit rather than folding it into the national CHL rows.

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Code follows the ISO3-SUBUNIT-years pattern, not a bespoke name**

Atacama is a first-order Chilean administrative region created by statute (DL 575, 1974) rather than a historical territory with a name and identity independent of the modern administrative map -- unlike the three bespoke-code cases (Alaska, Hyderabad, Ryukyu), which name pre-existing polities or occupation regimes. It therefore follows the dominant 57-of-66 pattern, CHL-AT-1976-2025, matching ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025 in structure. CHL currently has zero subnational rows, so this entry sets the country's precedent: other CHL regions created under the same 1976 reform should follow the identical CHL-<SUBUNIT>-1976-2025 shape unless they are one of the three documented later-split departures (Arica y Parinacota, Los Rios, Nuble).

### d-polygon-source-is-registered-slug-not-route

**polygon_source set to the registered GADM slug, status unassigned, not 'new_source_needed'**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: GADM 4.1 admin-1 is already a registered source in sources.yaml with id_column GID_1, and the modern Atacama boundary (expected GID_1 'CHL.3_1') would be a low-vintage-risk stand-in for the whole 1976-2025 span, since regional boundaries have been stable since creation. But the locally cached GADM 4.1 file is only an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely, so the feature cannot actually be pulled from disk today. Per the harness instructions, this makes polygon_source the slug itself (gadm-4.1-adm1) with polygon_status: unassigned -- not polygon_source: registered_source_unfetched (rejected by validate_declared_sources) and not new_source_needed (a suitable source already exists in principle, it just needs the global adm1 layer fetched).

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-feature-id-unverified

**CHL.3_1 is an expected, not confirmed, GADM feature ID for Atacama**

The routing decision's polygon_detail flags CHL.3_1 as 'unverified since absent locally' -- the locally cached GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer is an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile, so no feature has actually been pulled and inspected for this region. Fetching the full/global GADM 4.1 adm1 layer and confirming that CHL.3_1 is in fact Atacama (rather than a neighbouring region such as Antofagasta or Coquimbo, also created by the same 1976 reform) is required before polygon_status can move from unassigned to assigned. If the ID turns out to be wrong, the correct feature must be re-identified and this page's frontmatter corrected.

### oq-pre1976-data-routing-convention

**Whether pre-1976 Atacama-territory data should back_cast to this entry or match the national CHL rows, given sibling CHL-LI's differing precedent**

The routing_concerns note that pre-1976 data for the sibling unit CHL-LI (also created by DL 575) was recorded as 'matched' to the national polities CHL-1899-1902/CHL-1902-2025 for its pre-1976 years, rather than back_cast onto CHL-LI's own boundary. For Atacama, the routing reasoning instead treats 1900-1975 Atacama-territory data as reconstructions that should back_cast to this entry. This is a genuine inconsistency across two sibling Chilean regional entries created by the same 1974 reform: either CHL-LI's precedent should be revisited, or Atacama's routing here should instead match pre-1976 data to the national CHL rows. Left unresolved, future Chilean DL-575 regional entries will keep picking whichever convention their author prefers.

### oq-atacama-region-boundary-history-since-1976

**Whether Atacama's outer regional boundary has in fact been stable since 1976, or absorbed later comuna-level changes**

The polygon_reasoning asserts 'essentially no vintage risk' because Atacama's boundaries 'as created in 1976 have remained stable' through the present, but this claim has not been checked against Chile's comuna-level administrative history -- Chile has periodically created new comunas and adjusted provincial boundaries within regions over the 1976-2025 window. Whether any such changes affected Atacama's outer regional boundary specifically (as opposed to internal subdivisions only) has not been verified against a primary Chilean administrative-law source, and should be checked before treating a present-day GADM boundary as a safe proxy for the full 49-year span.
