# Eritrea (colony/administration, 1889-1952)

## Summary

This row covers **64 years** of Eritrea as a defined territorial
entity: Italian colony (1889-1941), British administration
(1941-1952). The territory was formally proclaimed as the Colony
of Eritrea on 1 January 1890, following the Treaty of Wuchale
(2 May 1889). CShapes area: 10.18 sq deg (~90,000 km²) — stable
throughout. Italy was defeated at Adwa (1896), limiting expansion
south. Britain captured Eritrea on 19 May 1941. The UN federated
Eritrea with Ethiopia on 15 September 1952, ending its existence
as a separate polity.

No WHEP split at 1941 because the territory did not change
(same 10.18 sq deg). The British capture is a regime change, not
a territorial change.

Predecessor: [eri-1885-1889](eri-1885-1889.md) — coastal strip.
Successor: NA (federated with Ethiopia 15 Sept 1952; became
Ethiopian province 14 Nov 1962). Territory reappears as
[eri-1993-2025](eri-1993-2025.md) after independence.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [eri-1885-1889](eri-1885-1889.md) — Italian
  coastal strip. Treaty of Wuchale (2 May 1889) expanded
  Italian control inland. Colony proclaimed 1 January 1890.
- **Successor:** NA. Federated with Ethiopia 15 September 1952
  (UN Resolution 390A). Eritrea was annexed as an Ethiopian
  province on 14 November 1962. The territory re-emerges as
  [eri-1993-2025](eri-1993-2025.md) after the 1993 independence
  referendum.
