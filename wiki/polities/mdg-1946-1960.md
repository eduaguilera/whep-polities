# Madagascar (1946-1960)

## Summary

Madagascar (1946-1960) covers the French colonial period from 27 October 1946, when Madagascar became a French overseas territory under the Fourth Republic constitution, to 25 June 1960, the eve of independence. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=FRA (COW 220).

## Territorial extent

Island territory in the Indian Ocean. No land boundaries. Area ~51.06 (CShapes units). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [MDG-1912-1946](mdg-1912-1946.md) -- French colony
- **Successor:** [MDG-1960-2025](mdg-1960-2025.md) -- independent Madagascar

## Sourced claims

- "Madagascar became a French protectorate of the French in 1885 and a colony in 1896." It became an independent republic in 1960 (p.368). [biger-1995 §madagascar](../sources/biger-1995.md#madagascar)
