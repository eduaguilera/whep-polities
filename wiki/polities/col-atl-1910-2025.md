# Atlántico (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Atlántico is one of Colombia's 32 current departments, on the Caribbean coast at the mouth of the Magdalena River, with Barranquilla as its capital and largest city. This entry captures it as type: subnational because Colombia's sovereignty over the territory never changes across the span — the national chain COL-1922-2025 already carries that — while Atlántico is tracked separately because production/trade data sources report statistics at the department level under the label "Atlántico" (or similar), a reporting unit distinct from and smaller than the national totals COL-1922-2025 would otherwise absorb them into. The department was constituted in 1910 by separation from the historic department of Bolívar, under the unitary departmental system established after the 1886 Constitution, and unlike several other Colombian units in this database (the former territorios nacionales, which only became departments in 1991), Atlántico has no such 1991 departure — it has existed continuously as a department since 1910 and continues to the present, so this entry spans the full 1910-2025 window with the default start rule and an open (2025) end.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures production/trade data rows reporting under a Colombian department-level label for "Atlántico" across a 1915-2023 data window (row count not independently re-tallied here; taken from the routing decision that proposed this polity). Previously, in the absence of any COL subnational entry, these rows would have been matched only to the national polity COL-1922-2025, which conflates department-level reporting with all-Colombia totals and would have made Atlántico's data invisible as a distinct series once merged into the national figure. No existing polity in this database — national or subnational — carries any name match or administrative boundary feature for Atlántico, and Colombia had zero existing subnational rows before this batch, so there was no competing entry to route to. Atlántico's distinctness as a reporting/administrative unit is confirmed by its formal, continuous existence as one of Colombia's constitutional departments since 1910, a status independently attested by standard treatments of Colombian administrative history (e.g. DANE's departmental gazetteer and general historical-geographic references), which is why it is treated here as a stable subnational unit rather than folded into the national total.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. GADM 4.1 admin-1 is the intended registered source, but the copy held in this repository is limited to an 81-country subset that does not include Colombia, so no adm1 feature for Atlántico can be fetched today (a registered_source_unfetched case, not a missing-source case). No other registered polygon source (CShapes, Cliopatria, Paine) carries Colombian department-level boundaries — they hold only a single country-level COL feature — so no proxy copy is possible either; there is nothing at this resolution to copy from.

Territory description: Atlántico is Colombia's smallest mainland department by area, roughly 3,300 km2, occupying the coastal strip at the delta of the Magdalena River on the Caribbean Sea, bordered by Bolívar department to the south and Magdalena department across the river to the east. Its capital, Barranquilla, is Colombia's fourth-largest city and principal Caribbean port. A reader can locate it on a modern map as the small, densely populated department directly north of Cartagena and Bolívar, wedged between the Magdalena's mouth and the sea. This description is drawn from general geographic reference, not from any polygon attached to this entry — no geometry has been assigned, so no measured km2 figure exists in this database for it yet.

## Predecessors and successors

Atlántico has no predecessor polity in this database: prior to 1910 its territory was part of Bolívar department (or an earlier intendencia arrangement), which is not separately represented here, and the routing decision treats 1910 as the department's own founding point rather than a split requiring a predecessor edge. It has no successor either — the department remains current as of 2025, so end_year is open-ended at the data window's edge and successor is empty, matching the pattern of sibling Colombian departments (Antioquia, Bolívar, Boyacá, Caldas, Cauca) proposed as single continuous spans with no back-cast segments.

## Sourced claims

- Atlántico was formally created as a department of Colombia in 1910, separated from the historic department of Bolívar, as part of the post-1886 unitary departmental system under the 1886 Constitution.
- Atlántico is not one of the seven territorios nacionales (Amazonas, Arauca, Casanare, Guainía, Guaviare, Putumayo, San Andrés) that were upgraded to departments only in 1991 under the new Constitution, so it does not carry a 1991 departure/back-cast segment the way those units do.
- The data window this polity was created to route (1915-2023) falls entirely within Atlántico's existence as a department, so no gap-filling predecessor entry is required before 1910.

## Decisions

### d-code-atl-not-iso-only

**Used the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern with subunit ATL, not a bespoke code**

Colombia has zero existing subnational rows, so there is no country-specific precedent to follow, but the table-wide dominant pattern (57 of 66 subnational rows) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, as in ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. Atlántico is an ordinary current administrative department with no historical name or identity distinct from its role as a Colombian department (unlike ALK, HYD, or RYU, which are historical territories with names of their own that outlived or predate their containing state's current form), so the bespoke-code exception does not apply. COL-ATL-1910-2025 was chosen: ISO3 COL, a 3-letter subunit abbreviation ATL for Atlántico (parallel to ESP's 2-letter province codes), and the years 1910 and 2025 matching start_year and end_year exactly, as required by the gate that checks the code's years against the frontmatter.

## Open questions

### oq-atl-founding-year

**Founding year of Atlántico department not independently verified**

The routing decision cites 1910 as the year Atlántico was constituted as a department, following the post-1886 unitary departmental reorganization of Colombia, but this was not checked against a primary Colombian legal source (e.g. the founding law/decree) from within this repository. If Atlántico's department status in fact dates to a different year (some general references cite 1910 as when it was separated from Bolívar department, others discuss earlier intendencia status), start_year and the code's start component would need to change together, since the gate requires the frontmatter years and the code's years to match exactly.

### oq-atl-polygon-source

**No department-level polygon is currently attachable for Atlántico**

The routing decision found this to be a registered_source_unfetched case: GADM 4.1 admin-1 is a registered polygon source generally, but the local repository copy is limited to an 81-country subset that excludes Colombia entirely, so no adm1 feature for Atlántico can be fetched from it today. No other registered source (CShapes, Cliopatria, Paine, etc.) carries Colombian department-level boundaries; those sources only have a single country-level COL polygon. Until the global GADM adm1 layer (or a Colombia DANE departmental boundary source) is fetched and registered, this page carries polygon_status: unassigned with no geometry, and no area figure can be given beyond a general sense of Atlántico's modern extent (roughly 3,300 km2, Colombia's smallest mainland department, on the Caribbean coast around Barranquilla) drawn from general geography rather than any GeoPackage feature.
