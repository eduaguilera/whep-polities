# Territoire de Belfort

## Summary

Territoire de Belfort is a small territorial collectivity in eastern France, functioning administratively as a département (INSEE code 90) though technically distinct in legal form. Its territory is the pre-1871 arrondissement of Belfort, in the historic Franche-Comté/Alsace border zone, centred on the city of Belfort. This entry is `type: subnational` because Territoire de Belfort is not and has never been a sovereign state: it is a sub-national administrative unit of France, and the existing FRA-* rows in the polity table are national totals for metropolitan/overseas France as a whole, none of which is a subnational match for this specific territory. The unit is created here because statistics (agricultural production series in mitchell-type sources) are collected and published at the level of this specific department, separately from national French totals, and no existing polity row captures that reporting territory. Its start_year of 1922 follows the country convention's explicit departure from the default département start rule (1790, the year of the Revolutionary département creation): Territoire de Belfort did not exist as a département-equivalent unit until 1922, having spent 1871-1922 as a residual arrondissement of the disestablished French Haut-Rhin. end_year is 2025, the open-end convention value, since the territory is current and unchanged in boundary since 1922.

**Why this entry exists.** **Input data captured:** French departmental agricultural/administrative statistics for Territoire de Belfort (nuts/admin unit FRA-FRC24, officially named "Territoire de Belfort" in nuts_code_names.csv), spanning back to 1862 in mitchell-type series, continuing through the present. The row count and exact source breakdown are drawn from the harness's routing evidence for unit_id FRA-FRC24; the key fact is that data exists from 1862, well before either the 1871 arrondissement or 1922 full-département administrative milestones.

**What it was previously matched to, and why that was wrong:** prior to this entry, rows for Territoire de Belfort would have had no correct subnational home among the three existing FRA-* candidates, all of which are national French totals. Routing this department-level series to a national FRA row would inflate the implied territory of the observation by roughly two orders of magnitude (609 km2 actual vs. metropolitan France's ~544,000 km2), the same kind of national/subnational mismatch this harness exists to correct.

**What confirms this entity is distinct:** Territoire de Belfort is a real, currently-existing French administrative unit with its own INSEE code (90) and its own statistical reporting; the country convention for France explicitly lists it as a named departure case from the default department start-year rule (1790), which is itself evidence that a domain expert (whoever authored `country_conventions.json`) identified Territoire de Belfort as institutionally distinct and dated its "present form" to 1922 rather than to the Revolutionary department creation used for all other French departments. The 1862-1921 data predating that date is treated as a back-cast under this entry's later identity, per the back_cast mechanism, rather than being read as evidence of an earlier separate polity.

**Territory description (for a reader without the geometry):** Territoire de Belfort is one of France's smallest departments by area, located in the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region in eastern France, at the gap between the Vosges mountains and the Jura, historically called the \"Trouée de Belfort\" (Belfort Gap) or \"Porte de Bourgogne\" — a strategic lowland corridor between France and the Rhine valley/Alsace, and between the Vosges and Jura mountain ranges. Its administrative centre and only sizeable town is Belfort. On a modern map it sits directly north of Montbéliard/the Doubs department, east of Haute-Saône, and immediately west of the Haut-Rhin department (Alsace) and the Swiss border near Basel/Jura canton. Its area is approximately **609 km2** (a well-documented, essentially unchanged figure since 1922, since the department's boundary has not been redrawn), making it the smallest metropolitan French department by area other than Paris. No geometry is attached to this entry yet, so this figure comes from external reference (INSEE/general knowledge of French department areas), not from a measured polygon — it is territory evidence, not a polygon-derived measurement, and should not be read as `polygon_area_km2` since no such column value is set.

**Why a GADM proxy was not simply substituted despite the boundary being present-day-stable:** the routing evidence notes that any France ADM1 layer available would be present-day, and using it for a unit whose distinctive shape actually does date stably to 1871/1922 would be a *small* approximation — but since the correct file is not currently present on disk in this repository (0 features returned for FRA in the local GADM 4.1 subset), there is nothing to attach even as an approximation, and inventing a placeholder without the source file would misrepresent what is actually available.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor row is created. Before 1871, the territory was simply part of the département of Haut-Rhin and is not separately routed. From 1871 to 1922 it existed as the arrondissement de Belfort, the residual French portion of Haut-Rhin left outside the German annexation of Alsace-Lorraine; that is a real administrative distinction but is not represented as a separate polity row here, since the routing decision treats 1862-1921 rows as back-cast onto this entry's later identity rather than as evidence of a genuinely separate contemporaneous reporting unit. No successor exists: Territoire de Belfort is still a current French territorial collectivity (technically sui generis since 1922, functioning as a département), so end_year is the open-end convention value 2025.

## Sourced claims

- mitchell and related French departmental series report agricultural/administrative statistics for Territoire de Belfort as early as 1862, predating both the 1871 creation of the arrondissement de Belfort and its 1922 elevation to full territorial-collectivity status.
- Territoire de Belfort was carved out in 1871 under the Treaty of Frankfurt as the part of the former département of Haut-Rhin that remained French after Alsace-Lorraine's annexation by the German Empire, administered initially as a residual arrondissement rather than a full département.
- In 1922 the arrondissement de Belfort was given full département-equivalent status (a territorial collectivity, sui generis, sometimes informally called the 90th département), the date this entry's country convention fixes as its start_year.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-no-subunit

**Code chosen as FRA-1922-2025, not FRA-<SUBUNIT>-1922-2025**

The dominant pattern for 57 of 66 subnational rows is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025), and FRA has zero existing subnational rows, so it sets its own precedent rather than following a sibling. I chose the minority <ISO3>-<start>-<end> pattern (6 existing rows, e.g. BDI-1922-1962) instead, because a stable, recognisable 2-letter or short subunit slug for Territoire de Belfort is not obvious from INSEE code 90 or from FRC24 (a NUTS/harness-internal admin id, not a public abbreviation), and inventing one risks colliding with a future FRA subnational code chosen under a real convention once more French departments are added. Using the bare ISO3-year pattern for this first FRA subnational entry is deliberately conservative until a country-wide French subunit-coding convention is set.

### d-container-single-edge

**Single container edge instead of splitting at any French national-era boundary**

The containment gate requires edges to tile start_year to end_year with no gap, but does not require splitting at container-internal era changes that don't affect the containing chain used here. FRA-1919-2025 is the polity table's own row for this span (interwar France through the present), so one edge covering 1922-2025 fully tiles this entry's span without leaving any year uncontained, and no additional edge is needed since the France row does not change codes across this window.

### oq-pre1922-method-split

**Whether 1862-1921 rows are genuinely back-cast or a separately-reported contemporaneous unit**

The routing_concerns field flags this directly: it assumes Territoire de Belfort's 1862-1921 data (filed while the territory was part of Haut-Rhin, then the residual arrondissement de Belfort after 1871) is a back-cast allocation onto the later 1922 department boundary, rather than genuinely separately-reported statistics scoped exactly to the historical arrondissement boundary. This has not been checked against the method column (observed vs. interpolated/scaled) split by year in the underlying series. If the pre-1922 data turns out to be mostly observed values scoped to the arrondissement's own boundary, a separate predecessor polity (covering 1871-1922, or 1862-1922) might be warranted instead of treating all pre-1922 rows as back-cast onto this entry.
