# Nigeria (1960-1961)

## Summary

A transition-year polity covering independent Nigeria between October 1960 (independence) and 1 October 1961, when the former British Northern Cameroons joined Nigeria, changing the boundary. "The Nigerians have been independent from British colonial rule in October 1960" [biger-1995 nigeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#nigeria). "On 1 January 1914, the two British protectorates were united to create a single state called the Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria" [biger-1995 nigeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#nigeria). The 1961 boundary change reflects the plebiscite results in British Cameroons: the northern part joined Nigeria on 1 June 1961. CShapes records the corresponding boundary shift.

This phase covers Nigeria before the incorporation of the Northern Cameroons.

## Open questions

### oq-nga-northern-cameroons-date

The precise date when Northern Cameroons was incorporated into Nigeria should be confirmed. The plebiscite was held on 11 February 1961 and the territory formally joined Nigeria on 1 June 1961, but the CShapes boundary change may use a different date.
