# Trinidad and Tobago

## Summary

Trinidad and Tobago is a country made up of two islands situated in the Caribbean Sea. Biger 1995 records its total land area as 1,980 sq. miles (5,126 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 1,300,000 in 1994; it has no land boundaries [biger-1995 &sect;trinidad-and-tobago](../sources/biger-1995.md#trinidad-and-tobago). When Columbus visited the islands in 1498, Trinidad was inhabited by the Arawak and Tobago by the Carib. The islands were neglected Spanish possessions until 1797, when they surrendered to a British naval expedition. WHEP tracks Trinidad and Tobago as a continuous row from 1800 because the island territory has remained constant.
