# Newfoundland (province of Canada)

## Summary

The province of Newfoundland (Newfoundland and Labrador since 2001) inside Canada. "Until 1949, the
Dominion of Newfoundland was a separate dominion in the British Empire"; it joined Canada on 31 March
1949, the date this repository already uses for Canada's enlargement (issue 15).
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §newfoundland](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#newfoundland)

The territory is the Dominion's, continued: island and Labrador. It is typed `subnational`, inside
[can-1949-2025](can-1949-2025.md), and follows [nfl-1907-1949](nfl-1907-1949.md).

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 prints Newfoundland as a sub-row of Canada: `Canada Newfoundland` and `Canada  Newfoundland`
(double space), population total, agricultural and by sex. Its 1937 rows already route to
[nfl-1907-1949](nfl-1907-1949.md) by alias (the pre-war columns are on post-war boundaries, which puts
Newfoundland beside Canada rather than inside a row that did not yet contain it). The **1951** rows,
**4**, resolved to no polity: the 1951 total, 361 thousand, is inside Canada's 14,009 thousand (the
1951 census counted Newfoundland), so Newfoundland is a part of Canada here, and an alias to
CAN-1949-2025 would put a part beside its whole under the same key.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** CShapes 2.0 gwcode 21 (Newfoundland, its one step 1886-1948),
398,423 km² (ESRI:54034), the same polygon [nfl-1907-1949](nfl-1907-1949.md) carries. The two rows
do not coexist (1907-1949 and 1949-2025, end years exclusive), so the polygon is never claimed twice.
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

CShapes draws Canada from 1948-07-22 with Newfoundland inside it, so this outline is the province's
ground within [can-1949-2025](can-1949-2025.md). No area is declared here: the IIA figure recorded on
the Dominion's row (110,679 km²) is the island alone, a different scope.
