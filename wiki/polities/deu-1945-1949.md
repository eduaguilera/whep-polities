# Allied-occupied Germany (1945-1949)

## Summary

Allied-occupied Germany covers the period from the unconditional surrender of Nazi Germany on 8 May 1945 to the establishment of the Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic in 1949. Germany was divided into four occupation zones administered by the United States, the United Kingdom, France, and the Soviet Union, with Berlin similarly partitioned.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [DEU-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) (Nazi Germany). Approximate — occupied Germany lost the territories east of the Oder-Neisse line to Poland and the USSR, making it ~25% smaller than the 1938-1945 polygon. A more accurate polygon would combine [F77-1949-1990](f77-1949-1990.md) (East Germany) and [F78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md) (West Germany).. The occupied zones collectively covered the territory of Germany within its 1937 borders minus the territories east of the Oder-Neisse line (transferred to Poland and the USSR). CShapes 2.0 tracks the FRG and GDR from 1949; a 1945-1949 polygon could use the combined FRG+GDR boundaries as an approximation.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Germany" for 1946-1948, covering agricultural production across the occupation zones. No sovereign German state existed, but the territory was a real economic unit with tracked agricultural output. Previously these 113 rows were unmatched.
