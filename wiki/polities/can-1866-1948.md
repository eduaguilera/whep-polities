# Canada (to 1948)

## Summary

This row tracks Canada for **83 years**, from pre-Confederation to the
eve of Newfoundland's entry
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Confederation 1 July 1867 united Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and New
Brunswick. Rapid westward expansion: Manitoba (1870), British Columbia
(1871), PEI (1873), Saskatchewan and Alberta (1905), Yukon Territory
(1898). Dominion status within the British Empire; Statute of
Westminster (1931) confirmed legislative independence. Row ends 1948;
Newfoundland joins 1949 (covered in successor row).
