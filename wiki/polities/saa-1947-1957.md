# Saar Protectorate

## Summary

This row covers the **Saar Protectorate** (French: *Protectorat de la
Sarre*), a distinct political and economic entity established under
French administration from **17 January 1947** — when the Saar Statute
separated the territory from the French occupation zone of Germany —
until **1 January 1957**, when the territory acceded to the Federal
Republic of Germany (West Germany) following the 1955 Saar referendum.
The Saar had its own constitution (1947), its own currency (Saar franc
tied to the French franc), a customs and monetary union with France, and
separate representation at the IOC and other international bodies. It was
not part of Germany during this period. The 1938–1945 Saar is part of
Nazi Germany (DEU-1938-1945), not this polity.

**Preferred resolution (for the next ingest):** add `gadm41_DEU.gpkg`
to `data/geodata/gadm-4.1/`, then assign
`polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1`, `polygon_feature_id: DEU.SAA` (or the
GADM internal GID), `polygon_method: modern_proxy`, and
`polygon_confidence: high`. The territory of the Saar Protectorate
corresponded to the modern Saarland state with borders fixed by the
1947 Saar Statute and essentially unchanged at accession in 1957. Area
approximately **2,570 km²**. The drift between the 1947–1957 historical
boundary and modern Saarland is negligible (boundary demarcation
corrections only; no substantive territorial change). Mark as ESTIMATE
not authoritative if used.

**Why this entry exists:** The Saar Protectorate was a legally distinct
political entity with its own constitution, parliament, government,
currency, customs union with France, and international representation
(including Olympic participation). It was not a part of Germany
(1945–1957) and should not be collapsed into DEU-1945-1949 or
F78-1949-1990 for any agricultural, trade, or population series
reported separately under "Saar" or "Saarland" during 1947–1957.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [DEU-1945-1949](deu-1945-1949.md) — The Saar was
  administered as part of the French occupation zone of Germany from
  May 1945. The Saar Statute (17 January 1947) formally separated it
  from the French zone and created the protectorate. On the WHEP chain
  the territory therefore flows from Allied-occupied Germany
  (DEU-1945-1949, covering both Western and Soviet zones) into this row
  when the Saar was administratively detached.
- **Successor:** [F78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md) — West Germany. The
  1955 Saar referendum rejected the proposed Saar Statute of the WEU;
  France and Germany agreed to political reintegration effective
  1 January 1957. Economic reintegration (customs and monetary union
  with West Germany) followed on 6 July 1959. For WHEP purposes the
  polity ends at 1957, the year of political reintegration.

## Contradictions

**End year 1957 vs 1959.** Some sources date the end of the Saar
Protectorate to the economic reintegration on 6 July 1959 (full customs
and monetary union with West Germany). The database uses **1957**
(political sovereignty transfer on 1 January 1957), consistent with the
WHEP rule that polity boundaries are defined by political/sovereignty
events rather than economic integration milestones. If data series
covering 1957–1959 are found labelled "Saar" they should be attributed
to F78-1949-1990 for those years.

## Decisions

- **Polygon unassigned.** All six available GIS sources lack this
  feature; GADM DEU is absent from the dataset. Preferred resolution:
  `gadm-4.1-adm1` Saarland as modern\_proxy once DEU file is acquired.
- **End year 1957.** Political accession date chosen over economic
  reintegration date of 1959. Consistent with WHEP sovereignty-event
  rule.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-missing

The Saar Protectorate has no polygon in any available source. Acquiring
`gadm41_DEU.gpkg` and assigning the GADM ADM1 Saarland feature as a
modern\_proxy (confidence: high; territory unchanged from 1947; ~0 km²
drift) would resolve this. Until then `polygon_status: missing`.

### oq-data-coverage-1947-1957

Identify which WHEP input datasets report separate Saar data for
1947–1957 (FAO, ILO, UN trade, etc.) and how many rows are involved.
The predecessor FAO alias on F78-1949-1990 explicitly notes the Saar
discrepancy but does not split it out. If meaningful Saar-specific data
exist, an alias or direct match should route them here.

**Partially answered (2026-07-24, assertion verification).** FAO's 1952
Production Yearbook **does** report the Saar separately, under the label
**"France Saar"** — reflecting the protectorate's customs and monetary union
with France — for at least 1948-1951: 118 rows covering butter, meat, poultry,
cattle/sheep/calf carcass weights, oats and rye. The magnitudes (oats ~17,000
ha, rye ~18,000 ha, meat ~5,000 t) match the Saar's small ~2,570 km² territory
and not metropolitan France, confirming the label denotes the Saar Protectorate
itself rather than France-including-Saar. Those rows now route here
(`france saar|fao1952|1948-1951`). Still to survey: ILO and UN trade.

The two were near-synonyms, but only `unassigned` is part of the documented
vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md) and only `unassigned` states the
commitment: **no polygon, with the reason documented.** Confirmed against
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` — the build attaches no geometry to this row
(`polygon_source: none`; `gadm41_DEU.gpkg`, which would supply Saarland as an
adm1 subunit, is not among the fetched sources — see the preferred resolution
under Territorial extent). This is a genuine gap, not a claim. `unassigned` is deliberately
preferred over a modern-borders guess; if a polygon is sourced later, the value
becomes `assigned`, `proxy` or `estimate` and checks A and C start applying.
