# Italian East Africa (1936-1941)

## Summary

**Africa Orientale Italiana (AOI)** was the Italian colonial aggregate created on 9 May 1936 when Mussolini proclaimed the Italian Empire following the fall of Addis Ababa. AOI combined Ethiopia (~1,000,000 km2), Eritrea (~125,000 km2), and Italian Somaliland (~500,000 km2). Total area: approximately 1,700,000 km2. AOI was dissolved by British forces in 1941; constituent territories reverted to pre-AOI configurations.

**Why this polity exists.** Data sources (particularly B.R. Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics*) occasionally report 1936-1941 Horn of Africa figures under labels "Ethiopia" or "Italian East Africa" that cover the full AOI territory. Routing such rows to ETH-1907-1936 (~1,000,000 km2) overstates territorial scope by ~70%.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: missing.** No single-feature polygon for AOI exists in CShapes 2.0. A composed-union polygon should be constructed by unioning CShapes features 530 (Ethiopia, vintage 1907) + 531 (Eritrea, vintage 1900) + 5200 (Italian Somaliland, vintage 1924). Approximate area: ~1,700,000 km2.

## Sourced claims

- **1936-05-09: AOI proclaimed.** Mussolini proclaimed the Italian Empire; Victor Emmanuel III named Emperor of Ethiopia. AOI created combining Ethiopia, Eritrea, and Italian Somaliland under a Governor-General.
- **1941-04-06: Fall of Addis Ababa.** British-led East Africa Campaign dissolved AOI de facto. Last Italian resistance at Gondar ended 1941-11-27.

## Contradictions

Mitchell labels some 1936-1941 series as "Ethiopia" without distinguishing AOI-aggregate from occupied-Ethiopian-territory figures. Magnitude comparison with adjacent years determines routing: if 1936-1941 value is ~70% larger than pre-1936 ETH series, route to AOI-1936-1941; if comparable, flag as occupation_disruption.

**The magnitude test has now been run, and it says these sources are NOT
reporting AOI-aggregate.** Assertion verification (2026-07-24) applied the
test above to two independent sources and both failed to show the ~70% step:

- **`iia`** keeps *Ethiopie* and *Erythrée* as separate country lines
  throughout 1934-1945 and never aggregates to AOI scope. The green-coffee
  series runs continuously across both the 1936 annexation and the 1941
  liberation (1934: 22,400 t; 1935: 19,800 t; 1936: 14,500 t; 1938: 16,300 t)
  with no step consistent with AOI's ~1.7M km² versus Ethiopia's ~1.0M km².
- **`fao1952`** reports *Eritrea* and *Somalia / Italian Somaliland* as
  separate labels with continuous production series spanning the whole
  occupation, never folding them under an "Ethiopia" umbrella.

So for these sources "Ethiopia" 1936-1941 means **Ethiopia proper**, and
routing it here overstates the territory by ~70%. There is no polity for
Ethiopia-proper during the occupation ([eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md)
ends at 1936, [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md) starts at 1941), so this is
a genuine coverage gap — see [oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941](#oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941).
Both assertions are quarantined pending that decision; this row remains
correct for any source that genuinely does report the aggregate.

## Decisions

- **New polity.** Created to receive aggregate data covering the full Italian East Africa territory (1936-1941). Type: aggregate (analogous to ANT-1961-2010).

## Open questions

### oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941

**No polity covers Ethiopia proper during the Italian occupation.** Raised
2026-07-24 by assertion verification (see [Contradictions](#contradictions)).
`ETH-1907-1936` ends at the annexation and `ETH-1941-1952` starts at the
liberation, so source data reporting Ethiopia-proper for 1936-1941 has
nowhere correct to go: routing it here folds a ~1.0M km² territory into a
~1.7M km² aggregate.

Two remedies were proposed by independent verifiers. The one that fits the
WHEP rule ("a sub-territory's data must NOT be matched up to its parent
aggregate; if no polity matches, create the granular polity") is a **new
polity**: *Ethiopia (Italian occupation, proper territory, 1936-1941)*,
iso3 `ETH`, predecessor `ETH-1907-1936`, successor `ETH-1941-1952`, covering
the historic highlands/heartland only (~1,000,000 km²), excluding Eritrea and
Italian Somaliland. The alternative — extending `ETH-1907-1936` or
`ETH-1941-1952` across the occupation — would misdate the territorial change.

Needs a human decision (new polity + chain re-dating). Until then the
affected assertions stay quarantined in
`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/quarantine.csv`.

## Polygon status consolidated (added 2026-07-28)

`polygon_status` changed from `missing` to **`unassigned`** in the
polygon_status vocabulary consolidation ([log lint-consolidate-polygon-status](../log.md#lint-consolidate-polygon-status)).

The two were near-synonyms, but only `unassigned` is part of the documented
vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md) and only `unassigned` states the
commitment: **no polygon, with the reason documented.** Confirmed against
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` — the build attaches no geometry to this row.
`polygon_source: constructed` is declared but there is no
`polygon_feature_id` and no entry for AOI-1936-1941 in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`, so nothing resolves; the
1,700,000 km2 in `polygon_area_km2` is a literature figure, not a measured
polygon. The union proposed in
[oq-aoi-polygon-construction](#oq-aoi-polygon-construction) (CShapes 530 + 531
+ 5200) is still the route to a real polygon; when it is built the value
becomes `assigned` and checks A and C start applying — including the area
comparison against that 1,700,000 km2 figure. Until then `unassigned` is the
honest state, and deliberately preferred over a modern-borders guess.
