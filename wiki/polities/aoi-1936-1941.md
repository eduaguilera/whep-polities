# Italian East Africa (1936-1941)

## Summary

> **`polygon_status` migrated missing -> unassigned (2026-07-29).** `missing` is a legacy value that predates the documented vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md#page-schema), which says not to use it in new pages. It was never migrated, so the manifest's `claims_polygon_status` set omitted it and consumers were told this row does not claim a polygon. This row has no geometry at all, which is exactly what `unassigned` means. See [lint-polygon-status-vocabulary](../log.md#lint-polygon-status-vocabulary). *(Superseded 2026-08-17: the polygon was built, so this row is now `assigned`. The note is kept because the migration it records still happened.)*


**Africa Orientale Italiana (AOI)** was the Italian colonial aggregate created on 9 May 1936 when Mussolini proclaimed the Italian Empire following the fall of Addis Ababa. AOI combined Ethiopia (~1,000,000 km2), Eritrea (~125,000 km2), and Italian Somaliland (~500,000 km2). Total area: approximately 1,700,000 km2. AOI was dissolved by British forces in 1941; constituent territories reverted to pre-AOI configurations.

**Why this polity exists.** Data sources (particularly B.R. Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics*) occasionally report 1936-1941 Horn of Africa figures under labels "Ethiopia" or "Italian East Africa" that cover the full AOI territory. Routing such rows to ETH-1907-1936 (~1,000,000 km2) overstates territorial scope by ~70%.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: assigned (2026-08-17).** No single-feature polygon for AOI exists in CShapes 2.0, so this row carries the composed union this page had specified from the start and nothing had built. `build_aoi_1936_1941` in [scripts/sources/constructed/build.py](../../scripts/sources/constructed/build.py) unions the CShapes 530 step 1907-1952 (Ethiopia) with the 531 step 1900-1941 (Eritrea) and with the constructed [ITS-1908-1960](its-1908-1960.md) (Italian Somaliland).

  | Member | Source | km² |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ethiopia | CShapes 530, step 1907-1952 | 1,127,556 |
  | Eritrea | CShapes 531, step 1900-1941 | 120,897 |
  | Italian Somaliland | constructed `ITS-1908-1960` (520 − 521) | 464,743 |
  | **union** | | **1,713,196** |

  The union equals the arithmetic sum because all three pairwise intersections measure **0.000 km²** — the three members tile the territory rather than overlapping it. 1,713,196 km² is **+0.78%** against the ~1,700,000 this page had declared, and **−0.70%** against the 1,725,330 km² usually quoted for AOI — so the polygon corroborates the declaration rather than being fitted to it.

  `polygon_area_km2` is deliberately **left at 1,700,000**, the independent estimate this page carried before the polygon existed, and is NOT rewritten to the 1,713,196 the geometry measures. `validate_polygons`' check A2 counts declarations that agree with their own polygon to within 0.1%, on the grounds that such a figure is no longer evidence about the territory; rewriting it would have pushed that count from 104 to 105 and turned a corroboration into a tautology. The measured figure lives in this table instead. Attached geometry measures 1,712,684 km² after `build_database`'s validity repair and simplification (−0.03% on the union), a 0.75% divergence from the declaration, which check A passes and reports.

  Two details are decisions, not defaults. **Ethiopia is taken at its 1907-1952 step, not 1952-1993**, because the later step (1,248,452 km²) is federated Ethiopia *including* Eritrea and using it would have counted Eritrea twice. **Italian Somaliland comes from `ITS-1908-1960`, not from cowcode 5200** as this page originally wrote, because 5200 is a CShapes *dependency* feature and the distribution registered in this repo is the sovereign-state one, which has no Italian Somaliland at all — its only Somali polygon (gwcode 520) begins at the 1960 union of the two Somalilands. `ITS-1908-1960` reconstructs the colonial boundary as 520 minus 521 and is documented there as bit-identical (symmetric difference 0.0 km²) to 5200, so the page's recipe is satisfied rather than substituted.

  **Why it was fixed:** this row declared `polygon_source: constructed` with no builder, so it shipped with **no geometry while receiving 10 layer-B rows** — matched but spatially unusable ([issue 155](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/155)).

- **Containment.** The union necessarily contains `ETH-1907-1936`, `ERI-1889-1952` and `ITS-1908-1960`, all three of which overlap 1936-1941, so AOI is now a container under `validate_spatial_containment` and is listed in its `LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` — the same relationship AOF-1895-1960, AEF-1910-1960 and MASG-1946-1963 already have with their members. What that does **not** settle is aggregation; see [oq-aoi-member-double-count](#oq-aoi-member-double-count).

## Predecessors and successors

AOI is a 5-year aggregate; constituent entities continue as separate polities before and after.

## Sourced claims

- **1936-05-09: AOI proclaimed.** Mussolini proclaimed the Italian Empire; Victor Emmanuel III named Emperor of Ethiopia. AOI created combining Ethiopia, Eritrea, and Italian Somaliland under a Governor-General.
- **1941-04-06: Fall of Addis Ababa.** British-led East Africa Campaign dissolved AOI de facto. Last Italian resistance at Gondar ended 1941-11-27.

## Contradictions

Mitchell labels some 1936-1941 series as "Ethiopia" without distinguishing AOI-aggregate from occupied-Ethiopian-territory figures. Magnitude comparison with adjacent years determines routing: if 1936-1941 value is ~70% larger than pre-1936 ETH series, route to AOI-1936-1941; if comparable, flag as occupation_disruption.

**The magnitude test has now been run, and it says these sources are NOT
reporting AOI-aggregate.** Assertion verification (2026-07-24) applied the
test above to two independent sources and both failed to show the ~70% step:

- **`iia`** keeps *Ethiopie* and *Erythrée* as separate country lines
  throughout 1934-1945 and never aggregates to AOI scope. The green-coffee
  series runs continuously across both the 1936 annexation and the 1941
  liberation (1934: 22,400 t; 1935: 19,800 t; 1936: 14,500 t; 1938: 16,300 t)
  with no step consistent with AOI's ~1.7M km² versus Ethiopia's ~1.0M km².
- **`fao1952`** reports *Eritrea* and *Somalia / Italian Somaliland* as
  separate labels with continuous production series spanning the whole
  occupation, never folding them under an "Ethiopia" umbrella.

So for these sources "Ethiopia" 1936-1941 means **Ethiopia proper**, and
routing it here overstates the territory by ~70%. There is no polity for
Ethiopia-proper during the occupation ([eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md)
ends at 1936, [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md) starts at 1941), so this is
a genuine coverage gap — see [oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941](#oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941).
Both assertions are quarantined pending that decision; this row remains
correct for any source that genuinely does report the aggregate.

## Decisions

- **New polity.** Created to receive aggregate data covering the full Italian East Africa territory (1936-1941). Type: aggregate (analogous to ANT-1961-2010).

## Open questions

### oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941

**No polity covers Ethiopia proper during the Italian occupation.** Raised
2026-07-24 by assertion verification (see [Contradictions](#contradictions)).
`ETH-1907-1936` ends at the annexation and `ETH-1941-1952` starts at the
liberation, so source data reporting Ethiopia-proper for 1936-1941 has
nowhere correct to go: routing it here folds a ~1.0M km² territory into a
~1.7M km² aggregate.

Two remedies were proposed by independent verifiers. The one that fits the
WHEP rule ("a sub-territory's data must NOT be matched up to its parent
aggregate; if no polity matches, create the granular polity") is a **new
polity**: *Ethiopia (Italian occupation, proper territory, 1936-1941)*,
iso3 `ETH`, predecessor `ETH-1907-1936`, successor `ETH-1941-1952`, covering
the historic highlands/heartland only (~1,000,000 km²), excluding Eritrea and
Italian Somaliland. The alternative — extending `ETH-1907-1936` or
`ETH-1941-1952` across the occupation — would misdate the territorial change.

Needs a human decision (new polity + chain re-dating). Until then the
affected assertions stay quarantined in
`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/quarantine.csv`.

### oq-aoi-member-double-count

**Does anything sum AOI together with its members?** Raised 2026-08-17 when the
polygon was attached. AOI's territory is now, geometrically, exactly
`ETH-1907-1936 ∪ ERI-1889-1952 ∪ ITS-1908-1960`, and all four rows are live over
1936-1941. Any consumer that sums or area-weights across all four counts the Horn
of Africa twice.

Nothing in this repo does that, and the [Contradictions](#contradictions) section
above already established that the two sources checked (`iia`, `fao1952`) report the
members separately and never fold them into an AOI umbrella — which is why the
assertions routing data here are quarantined. So the question is about downstream
aggregation, not about this geometry, and the geometry was attached rather than
withheld: 10 rows with no territory at all is strictly worse than 10 rows whose
territory a hypothetical double-sum would duplicate. Left open for whoever defines
aggregate-vs-member handling in the WHEP R package.
