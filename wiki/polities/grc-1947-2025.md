# Greece (1947-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Greece for **78 years**, from the Italian cession of the
Dodecanese Islands (Paris Peace Treaty, 10 February 1947, effective
15 September 1947) to the present. The row was split from GRC-1919-2025
at 1947 because the Dodecanese acquisition is a documented territorial
change of ~2,714 km² (~2% of metropolitan area) from
[ITAEG-1912-1947](itaeg-1912-1947.md).

Metropolitan borders have been stable since 1947. EU member since 1981.

Predecessor: [grc-1919-1947](grc-1919-1947.md) -- pre-Dodecanese Greece.
Successor: NA (still active).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0`, feature `cowcode=350`,
  `polygon_feature_year=1944` (CShapes feature start date 1944-10-13).
  **Polygon approximation (documented):** CShapes 2.0 does not record the
  1947 Dodecanese transfer. Both CShapes features for modern Greece
  (1919-11-27 and 1944-10-13) share identical geometry (~129,479 km²)
  that excludes the Dodecanese islands (~2,714 km²). The true post-1947
  area should be approximately 132,193 km²; the polygon understates
  actual territory by ~2,714 km² (~2%).
- **polygon_vintage_drift: approximation.** Direction: too small.
  Magnitude: ~2% (~2,714 km²). Corrected polygon construction is deferred
  to future work using GADM South Aegean sub-units (Dodecanese prefecture)
  or manual digitization of the island group.
- **Area.** ~129,479 km² (CShapes polygon; understates by ~2,714 km²
  ~2%). True post-1947 area ~132,193 km².
- **COW code 350**. COW lists Greece as continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Decisions

- **Split from GRC-1919-2025 at 1947.** The Dodecanese acquisition
  (~2,714 km², ~2%) is a documented territorial change under the WHEP
  territorial-change rule. This row represents post-Dodecanese Greece.
- **Polygon approximation.** CShapes 2.0 has no distinct post-1947 polygon
  for Greece; the same geometry (cowcode=350, both features 1919-11-27 and
  1944-10-13) is used throughout 1919-2019 in CShapes and excludes the
  Dodecanese. The 1944-10-13 feature is assigned here (nearest available
  vintage to 1947); `polygon_vintage_drift=approximation` is flagged.
  Rhodes (centroid ~28.2°E) is within the CShapes polygon boundary;
  Kastellorizo (centroid ~29.6°E) is not, confirming the Dodecanese
  exclusion. A corrected polygon can be constructed from GADM 4.1 South
  Aegean (Dodecanese prefecture) adm2 features when available.
- **ITAEG-1912-1947 successor.** The successor field of ITAEG-1912-1947
  was updated from GRC-1919-2025 to GRC-1947-2025, correctly representing
  the 1947 transfer.
- ★ **[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)** -- the territorial-
  change rule.
- ★ **[log decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority](../log.md#decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority)** -- the
  critical-stance rule.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-correction

The CShapes polygon understates post-1947 Greek territory by ~2,714 km²
(~2%) because CShapes does not record the Dodecanese transfer. A corrected
polygon should be constructed by:
1. Taking the CShapes 1944-10-13 base polygon.
2. Adding the Dodecanese island group using GADM 4.1 South Aegean
   (Dodecanese prefecture) adm2 features or manual digitization of
   the 12 principal islands (Rhodes, Kos, Leros, Patmos, Kalymnos,
   Kastellorizo, Nisyros, Tilos, Symi, Halki, Karpathos, Kassos).
3. Recording `polygon_source=cshapes-2.0+gadm-4.1-constructed`,
   `polygon_status=assigned`, `polygon_area_km2=~132193`.

Until then, `polygon_vintage_drift=approximation`, direction=too small,
magnitude=~2% is the documented status.
