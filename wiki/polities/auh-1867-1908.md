# Austria-Hungary (to 1908)

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as Austria-Hungary from **1867**
(Ausgleich) to **1908** (formal Bosnia annexation) [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
**Both endpoints are constitutional / sovereignty events, not
territorial changes**, which makes this the polity page with the
strongest rule-vs-CSV tension in the wiki so far.

- **1867 start (Ausgleich).** The Austrian Empire legally became
  the Dual Monarchy of Austria-Hungary on 30 March 1867
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1867](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1867), promulgated by
  Franz Joseph in his new capacity as King of Hungary on 28 July
  1867. **No territory was added or lost** — the same Habsburg
  domains that formed the Austrian Empire on 29 March 1867 formed
  the dual monarchy on 31 March 1867. Under the WHEP polity
  definition rule [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition), this is
  not a split trigger.
- **1908 end (formal Bosnia annexation).** Austria-Hungary
  formally annexed Bosnia-Herzegovina on **6 October 1908**
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1908-10-06](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1908-10-06). But
  Austria-Hungary had been **occupying** and **administering**
  Bosnia since 1878 [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1878),
  [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia), and (per standard historical fact,
  needing citation) Bosnia had been part of the Austro-Hungarian
  customs union since 1879. The 1908 event changed de jure
  sovereignty but not de facto control, trade regime, or
  economic integration. Under the strict WHEP rule, it is not a
  split trigger either.

Both issues are filed in [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Findings 3
and 4 for human review. **Under the strict WHEP rule the entire
period 1800–1918 (or 1804–1918) should arguably be a single
row.** The fact that there are three rows rather than one is a
CSV artifact, not a documented design decision.

Predecessor per CSV: [auh-1800-1867](auh-1800-1867.md). Successor per CSV:
[auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0 + CShapes-Europe` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes 2.0 starts in 1886 [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope), so
  for 1867–1885 the polygon is either a back-projection from
  1886 or a CShapes-Europe entry for pre-1886 European states.
  The CSV notes field says "CShapes 2.0 polygon (from 1886) is
  valid for this period" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), confirming that 1867–1885
  uses the 1886 polygon as a back-projection under CShapes's
  general "countries with no changes... backdated their borders
  to 1886" convention [cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding).
- **Territorial stability 1867–1908.** Austria-Hungary's borders
  were stable over this period. No major territorial transfers
  are cited in Biger's Austria or Hungary entries for 1867–1908
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). The 1878 Austro-Hungarian occupation
  of Bosnia changed administration but not legal sovereignty
  (which remained Ottoman until 1908)
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1878). The legal border
  of Austria-Hungary on 31 December 1907 was the same as its
  legal border on 1 January 1867. On a de facto / customs union
  basis, the effective economic border shifted in 1878 to
  include Bosnia-Herzegovina. The CShapes-COW Austria-Hungary
  polygon for 1886–1907 presumably represents the legal (not
  customs-union) extent, but this has not been verified and is
  flagged as [oq-1886-1907-polygon-scope](#oq-1886-1907-polygon-scope) below.
- **Modern concordance.** COW code 300 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). ISO3 AUH is
  a WHEP-internal code; no ISO code exists for Austria-Hungary
  historically.

## Sourced claims

- **1867: Ausgleich.** Austro-Hungarian Compromise formally
  established 30 March 1867, promulgated 28 July 1867 when
  "Franz Joseph, in his new capacity as King of Hungary,
  approved and promulgated the new laws, which officially gave
  birth to the Dual Monarchy"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1867](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1867). Biger: "In 1867
  the Habsburg Empire became the dual monarchy of
  Austria-Hungary, although it did not unite the Slav and
  German components strongly enough to prevent the growth of
  nationalist movements" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). The Ausgleich
  created shared institutions for foreign affairs, military,
  and finance; parliaments and domestic governance were
  separated.
- **1878: Austro-Hungarian occupation of Bosnia-Herzegovina.**
  Authorized by the Congress of Berlin. Austria-Hungary
  "occupied (but did not annex) the provinces of Bosnia and
  Herzegovina, a predominantly Slavic area. The occupation
  required 150,000 troops"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1878). Biger: "During
  the period of Austro-Hungarian rule (1878–1918)" — treating
  1878 as the start of effective Austro-Hungarian control over
  Bosnia without distinguishing 1878 occupation from 1908
  formal annexation [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia). **This matters for
  WHEP**: under the territorial-economic polity-definition rule
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition), Bosnia became part of
  the Austro-Hungarian polity in 1878, which would be a
  non-trivial expansion of the AUH row's effective territory.
  The CSV does not capture this as an event within the row.
- **1879 (unsourced, flagged): Austro-Hungarian customs union
  extended to Bosnia.** Standard historical fact that
  Austria-Hungary extended its customs union to cover
  Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1879, one year after the occupation.
  Not yet cited to a specific source in the wiki. Flagged as
  [oq-1879-bosnia-customs-union](#oq-1879-bosnia-customs-union).
- **1908-10-06: Formal annexation of Bosnia-Herzegovina.**
  "Austria-Hungary formally annexed Bosnia and Herzegovina,
  replacing the occupation arrangement with full annexation
  under joint Cisleithanian-Transleithanian control"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1908-10-06](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1908-10-06). This is
  the CSV's end-of-row event. **Under critical stance**: the
  1908 event transferred de jure sovereignty from Ottoman to
  Austro-Hungarian authority but did not change de facto
  control, which had been Austro-Hungarian since 1878. The
  Ottoman chain ([ott-1886-1908](ott-1886-1908.md) → [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md)) also splits
  at 1908, which is internally consistent under a "legal
  sovereignty transfers are splits" rule but not under the
  WHEP territorial-economic rule as written
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition). See
  [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 4.

## Open questions

### oq-1886-1907-polygon-scope

The CShapes-COW polygon for Austria-Hungary during 1886–1907
(the CShapes-covered portion of this row) presumably represents
the **legal** extent of Austria-Hungary, which would not include
Bosnia-Herzegovina (legally Ottoman until 1908). But the **de
facto** economic extent — including the customs union that
covered Bosnia from 1879 — was larger. Which extent does CShapes
represent for this period? A direct `ogrinfo -dialect SQLITE`
query on `cshapes2_full.gpkg` for `cowcode=300` during 1886–1907
compared against the `BOS-1878-1908` polygon would resolve this.
Related to [oq-bosnia-double-count](ott-1886-1908.md#oq-bosnia-double-count) on [ott-1886-1908](ott-1886-1908.md).

### oq-1879-bosnia-customs-union

Standard historical fact: Austria-Hungary extended its customs
union to cover Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1879, one year after the
occupation. Not yet cited to a specific source in the wiki. A
Biger or Wikipedia read for "Austro-Hungarian customs union
Bosnia" or a targeted economic-history source would resolve.
Load-bearing because it establishes 1879, not 1908, as the
effective economic-integration date for Bosnia from an
Austro-Hungarian perspective.

### oq-1867-ausgleich-exact-mechanism

Wikipedia gives 30 March 1867 as the date the Compromise was
formally established and 28 July 1867 as the promulgation date.
Biger treats the year 1867 as the boundary without distinguishing
the two dates. A more detailed source would clarify which date
corresponds to what legal act, and whether a single-day polity
split would be 30 March or 28 July. Low-priority unless a
human decides to un-split the row per audit Finding 3.

### oq-1866-real-turning-point

Biger treats the 1866 Austro-Prussian War as the real
territorial-political turning point, not 1867: "The Austria–Czech
boundary originates from the defeat of Austria at Hradec
Králové (Königgrätz) in 1866" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria), and "In the
Treaty of Versailles (1919), the boundaries of 1866 were
restored" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). Under a strict WHEP rule
reading, 1866 is a candidate split point that would fall on the
predecessor row [auh-1800-1867](auh-1800-1867.md), not this one. Linked to
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 3.
