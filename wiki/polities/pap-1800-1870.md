# Papal States

## Summary

This row tracks the Papal States for **71 years**, from the **1800**
database floor to the **1870** Italian capture of Rome
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV has
`notes = NA` and `verification_status = VERIFIED`. COW code 327.

At 1800, the Papal States were a significant central Italian state
extending across much of the Italian peninsula. Napoleon occupied
them (1798--1814); they were restored at the Congress of Vienna in
1815. Most territory was absorbed by the Kingdom of Italy in 1860
through plebiscites; Rome itself was captured on 20 September 1870
[wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome),
ending "more than one thousand years of Papal temporal power."

The Vatican City State (0.44 km²) was established by the Lateran
Treaty of 1929 but is not tracked as a WHEP row.

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [ita-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md) -- most Papal territory
absorbed into Italy 1860, Rome captured 20 September 1870.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (Papal States 1800-1802)` per the
  CSV [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This polygon
  represents the pre-Napoleonic extent and massively overstates the
  1860--1870 rump territory; see
  [oq-1860-rump-state](#oq-1860-rump-state).
- **Pre-1860 extent.** The Papal States at their largest covered a
  broad band of central Italy (Romagna, Marche, Umbria, Lazio). After
  Napoleon's fall, they were restored at the Congress of Vienna.
- **1860--1870 rump.** After 1860, the Papal States were reduced to
  Rome and Lazio only -- a fraction of the previous territory. The
  Cliopatria polygon does not reflect this reduction.
- **COW code 327** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  Check COW statelist for exact exit date -- the Papal States likely
  exit COW at 1870 when absorbed by Italy.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA (database floor at 1800). The Papal States
  existed long before 1800 but are truncated at the WHEP database
  start.
- **Successor:** [ita-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md) -- the Kingdom
  of Italy. Most Papal territory was absorbed in 1860 through
  plebiscites. Rome itself was captured on 20 September 1870, ending
  Papal temporal sovereignty. The absorption happened in two stages:
  most territory in 1860 (before this row's end date), Rome in 1870
  (the actual end-of-row event).

## Sourced claims

- **1860: Bulk absorption by Italy.** Most Papal States territory
  (Romagna, Marche, Umbria) was incorporated into the Kingdom of
  Italy through plebiscites. Biger confirms: "Plebiscites ratified
  the unification and established a monarchy"
  [biger-1995 §italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy). The Papal
  States continued as a rump state (Rome + Lazio only) from
  1860--1870.
- **1870-09-20: Capture of Rome.** Italy captured Rome, ending
  "more than one thousand years of Papal temporal power"
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome).
  This is the end-of-row event.
- **Napoleonic period.** Napoleon occupied the Papal States
  (1798--1814). They were restored at the Congress of Vienna in 1815.
  [biger-1995 §italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 327
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Contradictions

The CSV has the successor as ITA-1861-1919, but the absorption
happened in two stages: most territory in 1860 (before this row's
end date of 1870), Rome in 1870. The row's 1870 end date aligns
with the Rome capture, not the 1860 bulk absorption. This means
the Papal States continued as a rump state (Rome + Lazio only)
from 1860--1870, which is internally consistent but the Cliopatria
polygon from 1800--1802 massively overstates the 1860--1870 rump
territory.

## Open questions

### oq-1860-rump-state

The Papal States shrank dramatically in 1860 when most territory
went to Italy, but the CSV does not split at 1860 -- the row runs
through as one 71-year entity. The Cliopatria polygon from 1800--1802
massively overstates the 1860--1870 rump territory (Rome + Lazio
only). Under the WHEP territorial-change rule, 1860 is a candidate
mid-row split point. The CSV's choice to run through to 1870 is
defensible (the rump Papal State still existed as a sovereign entity
until 1870) but the polygon needs a temporal qualifier.

### oq-cow-327-exit-date

The exact COW exit date for the Papal States (code 327) needs
verification. It should be 1870 (capture of Rome) or possibly
20 September 1870 specifically. Cross-reference with COW statelist.
