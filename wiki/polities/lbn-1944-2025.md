# Lebanon

## Summary

Lebanon as tracked by WHEP from 1944, following independence from France. The country's boundaries were shaped by the post-WWI partition of Ottoman territory under the Sykes-Picot Agreement, with Lebanon falling within the French sphere. The Palestine--Lebanon boundary was demarcated by 1923.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Lebanon is bounded by Israel (49 mi; 79 km) and Syria.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [LBN-1920-1944](lbn-1920-1944.md) -- independence from French mandate

## Sourced claims

- The Palestine (Israel)--Lebanon boundary is based on an Anglo-French line stemming from the scramble for former Ottoman territory after World War I. [biger-1995 §lebanon](../sources/biger-1995.md#lebanon)
- The Sykes-Picot Agreement of May 1916 provided for a French sphere comprising the whole of present-day Lebanon. [biger-1995 §lebanon](../sources/biger-1995.md#lebanon)
- The Conference of San Remo (24 April 1920) established the mandates: Syria and Lebanon under France, Palestine and Mesopotamia under Britain. [biger-1995 §lebanon](../sources/biger-1995.md#lebanon)
