# India (1937-1947)

## Territorial extent

This row runs 1 April 1937 – 14 August 1947 and corresponds to a single,
unbroken CShapes-2.0 time-step for gwcode=750: `gwsdate=1937-04-01` to
`gwedate=1947-08-14` (record 569 of the shapefile on disk at
`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`), area 4,227,508 km²
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders).
Measuring that feature directly in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) gives
4,227,508.5 km² (`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`, feature
gwcode=750/gwsyear=1937), matching `polygon_area_km2` in the frontmatter
to five significant figures.

**Start boundary (1937-04-01):** the predecessor row `IND-1914-1937`
covers 1914–1931 and 1931–1937 CShapes time-steps at a constant
4,894,456 km²; the 1937 step drops to 4,227,508 km², a decrease of
~667,000 km². This is the Government of Burma Act 1935 taking effect
on 1 April 1937, which separated Burma from British India into its
own administration — the single largest territorial change touching
this row, but it falls exactly on the row's start date, not inside its
span, so it is correctly captured by the `IND-1914-1937 → IND-1937-1947`
predecessor boundary rather than requiring a further split of this row.
(Note: 1 April 1937 was also the effective date of the separate Aden
Settlement's transfer from Government of India to direct Colonial
Office administration, per general uncited history — Aden is not
separately broken out in the CShapes area figures checked here, and
no ingested source quantifies its area, so this is flagged only as an
uncited note, not built into the claim above.)

**End boundary (1947-08-14/15):** the successor row `IND-1947-1949`
begins where the next CShapes time-step (gwsyear=1947, area
3,046,196 km²) starts, i.e. the partition of British India into the
Dominions of India and Pakistan effective 15 August 1947 (Indian
Independence Act 1947). That is a genuine, large territorial change
(~1.18 million km² leaves the row) but again it lands on the row's end
date, so it is handled by the existing `IND-1937-1947 → IND-1947-1949`
split rather than requiring internal re-splitting of this row.

**Inside the span (1937–1947):** CShapes records no intermediate
time-step for gwcode=750 between 1937-04-01 and 1947-08-14 — the whole
decade is one polygon record, i.e. CShapes asserts constant borders
across World War II. This is corroborated by CShapes' own coding rule
that wartime territorial changes are excluded "unless they were made
permanent in treaties signed after the war"
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes):
the Japanese occupation of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands (1942–1945)
and the Japanese advance into Manipur/Kohima on India's northeastern
frontier (1944, repelled) were both wartime and reversed, so under
CShapes' own methodology they would not register as a change even if
they had been coded. No ingested source records a peacetime border
adjustment for British India between 1937 and 1947. **Conclusion: this
row's internal territory is constant, and no split is warranted inside
the span** — the only two real territorial changes touching this
polity (Burma 1937, Partition 1947) both fall exactly on its boundary
dates and are already handled by the predecessor/successor chain.

The polygon includes the princely-state envelope (British paramountcy
extended over, but did not administratively absorb, the princely
states); Hyderabad is separately tracked as `HYD-1724-1948`. Biger 1995
notes only that "India has been a single governmental entity for a
relatively short time dating from the consolidation of British colonial
rule in the first half of the nineteenth century"
[biger-1995 §india](../sources/biger-1995.md#india) — it does not give
a boundary history for the 1937–1947 window specifically, so the
Burma/Partition analysis above rests on CShapes rather than Biger.

  permanent in treaties signed after the war," which is why occupied

  population/coverage rules are about separate dependent *states*, not

  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies).

  India (ccode=750) as a state-system member at any point during
  1937–1947; its single tenure row for ccode 750 begins 1947-08-15

  1920 membership required "diplomatic recognition by Britain or
  France" and from 1920 required League/UN membership plus a
  population threshold — a colonial dependency meets neither
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion).
  This is why COW cannot be used to justify this row's start/end years;
  per the wiki's polity-definition rule, WHEP's 1937 start (Burma
  separation, a territorial event) and 1947 end (Partition, a
  territorial event) correctly differ from COW's 1947-08-15 statehood
  date, which marks a legal-status change with no bearing on the
  territory tracked here.
- Biger 1995's India entry gives no boundary detail specific to the
  1937–1947 window; its only substantive claim ("India has been a
  single governmental entity for a relatively short time...") is
  general background, not evidence for or against a split inside this
  span [biger-1995 §india](../sources/biger-1995.md#india).
- Uncited general history (option (b), not attributable to any
  ingested source): the Government of Burma Act 1935 separated Burma
  from British India effective 1 April 1937; the Aden Settlement was
  simultaneously transferred to direct Colonial Office rule the same
  day; the Indian Independence Act 1947 partitioned British India into
  the Dominions of India and Pakistan effective 15 August 1947. These
  are flagged as uncited claims only, not dressed up with a citation.

### open: whether mitchell's India covers the princely states

Recorded 2026-08-18 from the wiki notes queue. **Flagged, not resolved.**

Neither this page nor the assertion bundle addresses whether Mitchell's `India`
production series for 1937-1946 includes output from the **princely states** —
nominally under British paramountcy, not administratively absorbed, and inside
this row's polygon — or covers British-administered provinces only.

Pre-1947 Indian colonial agricultural statistics are frequently province-based and
may under-cover princely-state production. The observed magnitudes are **not
obviously inconsistent with either scope**, which is why this is recorded as an
open question rather than a finding: with a ~40% population difference between the
two readings, a scope error here would be large, but nothing in the data
distinguishes them.

Resolving it needs finer provenance on Mitchell's underlying colonial-yearbook
sources. Related: the raw IIA extract carries
`british india: princely states` and `british india: crown provinces` as separate
labels, so at least one source of the period did make the distinction — evidence
that the question is answerable in principle.

## Polygon corrected 2026-08-13 (issue 84)

> **Polygon rebuilt on 2026-08-13 to remove Portuguese India and French India (issue 84).**
>
> This row used to publish CShapes 2.0 gwcode 750 unchanged, and CShapes does not carve foreign
> enclaves out of a colonial power's outline. So it claimed the same ground as two other rows:
>
> ```
> before   this row n PTIND-1816-1961    3,719 km2    98.4% of Portuguese India
>          this row n FRIN-1816-1954       520 km2    95.2% of French India
> after    this row n PTIND-1816-1961       13.6 km2
>          this row n FRIN-1816-1954        47.2 km2
> ```
>
> **Neither enclave was ever British.** Portuguese India (Goa, Daman, Diu) was Portuguese until
> India annexed it in **December 1961**; French India (Pondicherry, Karikal, Mahe, Yanam) was
> French until the **October 1954** cession. This row ends in 1947, on 14 August, so both were foreign
> sovereign territory for the whole of its span and the subtraction needs no judgement call —
> which is exactly what could not be said of `IND-1949-2025`, and why that row is untouched.
>
> **Polygon:** `constructed` — `IND-1937-1947` in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`, which is
> `_cshapes2_step(750, 1937, 1947)` MINUS `build_ptind_1816_1961()` MINUS
> `build_frin_1816_1954()`. The subtrahends are the *same geometries those two rows publish*, not
> re-derivations, so the pairs cannot drift apart. The CShapes step is named by its **bounds**
> rather than by a feature year because `polygon_feature_year` is ambiguous for part of this
> chain — 1914 matches two steps of gwcode 750 — and the bound form reproduces the step this row
> was already bound to, so nothing but the enclaves moved.
>
> **`polygon_feature_year` is kept** at its old value even though `polygon_source` is now
> `constructed`. The `constructed` source has no `temporal` key in `scripts/sources.yaml`, so the
> year plays no part in resolving the feature — but it still records which CShapes vintage the
> polygon is built from, which is what `04_territory_basis.py` reads to decide whether a row's
> border is `measured` or merely `assumed_constant`. Dropping it to `null` would have reclassified
> this row as "polygon present but no recorded vintage year", which would be false.
>
> ```
> CShapes 750 `1937-1947` step    4,227,508 km2
> minus Portuguese India and French India   -4,246
> after the cut              4,223,262 km2
> published                  4,223,063 km2   (write-time simplification, -0.011%)
> ```
>
> **The residual 13.6 + 47.2 km² is a simplification artefact, not the recipe.** Measured on the
> builder's own geometry the enclaves are gone to 0.00 km²; `scripts/build_database.py` then
> applies a Douglas-Peucker simplification on write, which moves every boundary — including the
> two new holes — and puts a thin rim back. That same step is why the published area is 0.011%
> under the computed one. 61 km² is 0.001% of this row; it is recorded rather than described as
> zero.
>
> **What this does not fix:** `IND-1949-2025` still contains both enclaves in full (3,719 and 520
> km²), because that row spans 1949–2025 and therefore straddles both annexation dates — for part
> of it the enclaves genuinely were Indian. Correcting it means splitting the row at 1954 and
> 1961, a periodisation change to a major national family. **That split was considered and
> declined on 2026-08-13** — it would rename the modern-India polity code, which is referenced
> outside this repository, for a 0.13% correction, and Sikkim sits in the same polygon at
> 6,988 km² untouched. The overstatement is documented on
> [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md) instead.
