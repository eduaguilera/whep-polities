# Ethiopia (Italian occupation, proper territory, 1936-1941)

## Summary

A **5-year row** covering Ethiopia proper -- the historic highlands/heartland
(~1,000,000 km2) -- during the Italian occupation, 1936-1941. This is
EXPLICITLY NOT the Italian East Africa (AOI) aggregate: it excludes Eritrea
(~125,000 km2) and Italian Somaliland (~500,000 km2), which are covered by
[eri-1889-1952](eri-1889-1952.md) and [its-1908-1960](its-1908-1960.md)
respectively, and which together with this row compose
[aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md) (type=aggregate, ~1,700,000 km2).

**Approved by Catalin Covaci** (2026-07-27) as the remedy for open question
[oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md#oq-ethiopia-proper-1936-1941)
raised during assertion verification.

**Why this polity exists.** Assertion verification (2026-07-24) found that
two independent sources -- `iia` and `fao1952` -- keep *Ethiopie/Erythree/
Somaliland* (or equivalent labels) as separate country lines throughout
1934-1945, with continuous "Ethiopia" series showing no ~70% step across
either the 1936 annexation or the 1941 liberation (green coffee: 22,400 t
1934; 19,800 t 1935; 14,500 t 1936; 16,300 t 1938). This proves their
"Ethiopia" label means Ethiopia proper, not the AOI aggregate. Before this
polity existed there was nowhere correct to route that data: routing it to
AOI-1936-1941 would have overstated the territory by ~70%; routing it to
ETH-1907-1936 or ETH-1941-1952 would have misdated the territorial
interruption (those rows correctly end/start at the annexation/liberation).
See [aoi-1936-1941.md Contradictions](aoi-1936-1941.md#contradictions) for
the full magnitude-test writeup.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon: period_proxy from CShapes 2.0 feature 530 (Ethiopia, vintage
  1907)** -- the same feature used by predecessor
  [eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md). Ethiopia proper's borders were not
  redrawn by the occupation: [eth-1941-1952.md](eth-1941-1952.md) documents
  that CShapes feature 433 (ETH, dated 1941-05-05 to 1952-09-14) is
  geometrically identical to feature 530, and that its spatial intersection
  with Eritrea's 1941 feature (439) returns zero area. Since the geometry is
  confirmed identical immediately before (1907-1936) and immediately after
  (1941-1952) this row's period, and the occupation did not alter Ethiopia
  proper's external boundary on the ground (Italy administered AOI as a
  colonial super-structure over existing territorial units, not by redrawing
  the Ethiopian highlands border), the proxy carries negligible risk of
  territorial mismatch. No exact_historical CShapes feature exists for
  Ethiopia-proper-during-AOI specifically (Ethiopia had no independent
  international administration in these years), so period_proxy is the
  correct tier per the polygon-source hierarchy.
- **Excludes:** Eritrea (CShapes 531/439, ~90,000-125,000 km2) and Italian
  Somaliland (CShapes 5200, ~500,000 km2) -- both administered as part of
  AOI in this period but tracked as separate WHEP polities throughout.
- **Area:** ~1,000,000 km2 (identical to ETH-1907-1936 and ETH-1941-1952).
- **Confidence: high.** Based on independently verified geometric identity
  of the bracketing CShapes features, not a bare assumption of stability.

- **Successor:** [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md) -- begins at the

  Ethiopia-proper-only data here to that aggregate, and vice versa.

## Sourced claims

- **1936-05-09: AOI proclaimed**, absorbing Ethiopia administratively into
  Italian East Africa, but the highlands/heartland territory itself was not
  redrawn. Start-of-row event (predecessor row ends 1936-05-05 at the fall
  of Addis Ababa).
- **1941-05-05: Haile Selassie returns to Addis Ababa**; sovereignty
  restored. End-of-row event (successor row begins here).
- **iia** and **fao1952** report continuous "Ethiopia"-labeled series across
  1934-1945 with no step consistent with a ~1.7M km2 vs ~1.0M km2 territory
  change (green coffee: 22,400 t 1934; 19,800 t 1935; 14,500 t 1936; 16,300 t
  1938) -- confirming these sources mean Ethiopia proper throughout.
