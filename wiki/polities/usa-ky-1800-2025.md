# Kentucky (state of the United States)

## Summary

Kentucky is a present-day U.S. state occupying the region south of the Ohio River and east of the Mississippi River, admitted to the Union on June 1, 1792 as the 15th state after separating from Virginia's Kentucky County. This entry is `type: subnational` because Kentucky has never held sovereignty of its own — it is, and always has been, a constituent unit of the United States — and the entry exists to carry any statistical series (agricultural, demographic, or economic) that a data source reports at the state level rather than folding into national U.S. totals. Unlike Alaska (ALK-1867-1959), which earned a bespoke code because it passed through a distinct named administrative status (Territory of Alaska) before statehood, Kentucky has had one continuous administrative identity since 1792 and so follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end coding convention used for the large majority of subnational rows in this table. The entry's start_year (1800) is set later than Kentucky's actual admission year (1792) solely because no USA container era exists in the table before 1800; see the open question and decision notes for the resulting gap.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry is created ahead of any specific ingested data row, as part of the harness's systematic build-out of U.S. state-level subnational polities (following the same policy that created Alaska, and per the decision object supplied to this task, unit_id USA-KENTUCKY). No prior data extract or row count is attached to this specific creation task; the justification is structural, not data-driven: the harness's routing pass identified that no existing polity in the table represents Kentucky specifically (only the national USA-era chain and Alaska/candidate-name matches existed), meaning that any future data source reporting a "Kentucky" label at U.S.-state granularity would currently misroute to a national USA row roughly 75 times Kentucky's territory (the contiguous U.S. is about 8 million km2 against Kentucky's ~104,656 km2). Kentucky's statehood is confirmed by the U.S. National Archives' own admission-date records (June 1, 1792), and its status as a distinct, separately-administered unit of the federal system — rather than an undifferentiated slice of Virginia — is uncontested and requires no additional historian's source to establish, unlike ambiguous historical trading-territory cases.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `assigned` from `gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `USA.18_1`, the standard present-day GADM admin-1 boundary for the Commonwealth of Kentucky. This is a direct, non-proxy assignment: Kentucky's external borders (Ohio River to the north bordering Ohio, Indiana, and Illinois; Mississippi River to the west bordering Missouri; Virginia/West Virginia to the east; Tennessee to the south) have been essentially unchanged since admission in 1792, so the modern boundary is treated as valid for the entire 1800-2025 span with no reconstruction needed. Whether any 19th-century river-boundary shift occurred is flagged as an open question rather than assumed away.

**Territory description:** Kentucky occupies the region immediately south of the Ohio River in the east-central United States, bounded by the Mississippi River in its far-western Jackson Purchase area, Tennessee to the south, and the Appalachian ridges (Cumberland Mountains) forming its eastern border with Virginia and West Virginia. On a modern map this is the area containing Louisville, Lexington, and Frankfort (the state capital). Reported land area (U.S. Census Bureau) is approximately 104,656 km2. **This figure, and the identical figure measured from the attached GADM polygon, describe the same present-day shape; no independent 19th-century territorial figure has been checked against it.**

## Predecessors and successors

Kentucky has no predecessor or successor polity row: it was created directly from Virginia's Kentucky County by admission to the Union in 1792 (a jurisdictional transfer internal to the pre-1800 United States, not represented by any polity row in this table), and it continues without interruption to the present day as a U.S. state, so both predecessor and successor lists are empty. The entry spans the entire ingestible USA container chain from USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1792

**start_year set to 1800, not Kentucky's 1792 admission year**

Kentucky was admitted to the Union on June 1, 1792 (the 15th state, split from Virginia's Kentucky County), and the harness's default rule for U.S. states is to use the year of formal admission. However, the earliest USA container era present in the polity table is USA-1800-1803 (data/final/polities_database.csv). No container row exists that spans 1792-1800, and the containment gate rejects an edge falling outside either party's own span. Rather than fabricate a container edge into a non-existent pre-1800 USA era, this entry starts at 1800, the first year for which a valid container exists. The 1792-1800 gap for Kentucky specifically is recorded as an open question below; a proper fix would add an earlier USA container era or a dedicated one-off edge once the harness's container table is extended backward, but that is out of scope for this single-polity page.

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Code follows the ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end majority pattern**

Kentucky is an ordinary present-day U.S. state with a continuous, unbroken administrative existence and no distinct historical name of its own (unlike Alaska's 1867-1959 Territory period, which justified the bespoke ALK code). It therefore follows the dominant 57-of-66 pattern: USA-KY-1800-2025, using the standard two-letter state postal abbreviation KY already used implicitly elsewhere in this table's DZA/ESP examples for subunit codes.

## Open questions

### oq-1792-1800-gap

**Kentucky's actual statehood (1792) predates this entry's start_year (1800) by 8 years**

Kentucky was formally admitted to the Union on June 1, 1792, but the earliest USA container era available in the polity table (data/final/polities_database.csv) is USA-1800-1803. This entry's start_year was set to 1800 rather than 1792 purely to satisfy the containment-tiling gate, which rejects a container edge falling outside either party's own registered span. This means 1792-1800 Kentucky statehood is currently uncontained and unrepresented anywhere in the database. The correct fix is likely to add an earlier USA container era (e.g. USA-1792-1800 or extending USA-1800-1803 backward) rather than leave Kentucky's true admission date undocumented; until that happens, any data keyed to Kentucky 1792-1799 has nowhere valid to route.

### oq-polygon-vintage

**GADM 4.1 boundary reflects present-day Kentucky; no check for 19th-century boundary stability**

polygon_feature_id USA.18_1 (GADM 4.1 admin-1) is a modern boundary applied back to 1800. Kentucky's borders have been essentially stable since admission — the state was carved once from Virginia in 1792 and has not since ceded or gained territory to a neighboring state in a way that is commonly documented — but this has not been independently verified against a 19th-century boundary source (e.g. a historical GIS layer or Newberry Library atlas). If any 19th-century adjustment exists (river-boundary shifts with Ohio/Indiana/Illinois/Missouri along the Ohio and Mississippi rivers are common in this era), polygon_status should be revisited from 'assigned' to 'proxy'.
