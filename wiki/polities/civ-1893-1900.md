# Côte d'Ivoire (1893-1900)

## Summary

The French colony of Côte d'Ivoire from its formal proclamation as a distinct colony on 10 March 1893 to the CShapes-defined 1900 boundary cut. Previously the Ivory Coast coast had been a French protectorate administered from Grand-Bassam; the 1893 decree elevated it to a standalone colony. In 1895 it became part of the newly-federated French West Africa (AOF), while retaining its identity as a constituent colony.
