# Ottoman Empire (1886–1908)

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire as a territorial-economic unit
from **1886** to **1908**. Both boundaries are artefacts of data
availability more than historical events:

- **1886 start** is the CShapes 2.0 temporal floor
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope], not a territorial change. The actual
  territorial state of the empire in 1886 is the post-1878
  Congress of Berlin configuration — the Berlin settlement took
  effect eight years earlier, in 1878
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878]. WHEP carries a separate
  row [ott-1800-1886] for the Cliopatria-sourced pre-1886
  window. See [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory] on
  [ott-1800-1886] for the audit.
- **1908 end** is a real territorial event: on 5 October 1908
  Austria-Hungary formally annexed Bosnia and Herzegovina (which
  it had been occupying under a Congress of Berlin mandate since
  1878), Bulgaria declared independence, and Crete declared
  union with Greece [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908]. These
  three simultaneous events ended what remained of the 1878 de
  facto settlement. The WHEP split is here for a real reason.

Predecessor: [ott-1800-1886]. Successor: [ott-1908-1912].

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV (`polygon_source`).
  WHEP uses the COW variant of CShapes 2.0 — see
  `log.md 2026-04-11 decision-cshapes-is-cow-based`. CShapes
  records the Ottoman Empire under `cowcode=640` for this period;
  the polygon reflects the **post-1878** territorial
  configuration (Romania, Serbia, Montenegro, Bulgaria autonomous
  and carved out; Cyprus under British administration;
  Bosnia-Herzegovina nominally Ottoman but under Austro-Hungarian
  occupation) [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878].
- **The "Bosnia ambiguity" 1886–1908.** Under the Berlin
  settlement Bosnia was legally Ottoman but administratively
  Austro-Hungarian. The CShapes COW-based polygon for the
  Ottoman Empire in this window needs to be checked against the
  `BOS-1878-1908` and `HZG-1878-1908` WHEP rows to see whether
  Bosnia is counted in Ottoman territory here or already
  subtracted — see [oq-bosnia-double-count].
- **1886 "as-of" date interpretation.** Even though 1886 is not
  a territorial event, the row's polygon *as of 1886* is a
  correct representation of Ottoman borders *at 1886*. The
  problem is not the polygon itself but that it is also used
  as a proxy for the preceding row (`OTT-1800-1886`) via
  Cliopatria back-projection; see [ott-1800-1886].

## Sourced claims

- **1878 (inherited from predecessor row).** The Congress of
  Berlin reshaped Ottoman borders: Austria-Hungary gained the
  right to "occupy Bosnia and Herzegovina"; Britain took Cyprus;
  Romania, Serbia, and Montenegro were formally recognized as
  independent; Bulgaria became an autonomous principality under
  Ottoman suzerainty [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878].
  This is the *state* of the Ottoman Empire at the start of
  this row, not an event within it.
- **1881: French protectorate over Tunisia.** Not captured in
  the current Wikipedia snapshot but referenced in the
  `TUN-1800-1881` row's notes [database]: "Husainid dynasty
  from 1705. French protectorate (Treaty of Bardo) 1881."
  Tunisia was an autonomous Ottoman beylik before 1881 and thus
  a slice of Ottoman trade territory; its exit is not captured
  by a territorial event in *this* row's period (it fell
  before the row started) but is flagged here because the
  Cliopatria 1800 polygon for the predecessor row probably
  still includes it. See [oq-tunisia-boundary] and the
  predecessor page.
- **1882: British occupation of Egypt.** Same status — a
  territorial fact about Ottoman sovereignty that precedes this
  row's 1886 start, relevant for the predecessor row. Not
  captured in the current Wikipedia snapshot; see
  [ott-1800-1886] and open questions there.
- **1885: Bulgarian unification with Eastern Rumelia.** Also
  pre-1886 and thus not covered by this row, but materially
  affects what the CShapes 1886 polygon shows. Not captured in
  the current Wikipedia snapshot.
- **1908 (end of row): three simultaneous events.** "Bulgarian
  Declaration of Independence on 5 October"; Austria-Hungary
  formally annexed Bosnia-Herzegovina; Crete declared union
  with Greece [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908]. These are
  the events that trigger the split to [ott-1908-1912]. In
  territorial terms, the annexation of Bosnia formalized what
  had been de facto since 1878; the Bulgarian independence
  upgraded an autonomous principality to a sovereign state.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists a single continuous
  tenure for `TUR, 640` from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates]. COW makes no split at
  the 1886, 1908, 1912, or even 1923 transitions. The 1886
  boundary of this WHEP row has no counterpart in COW at all;
  the 1908 boundary is also not marked in COW. See the
  parallel discussion on [ott-1800-1886].
- **WHEP continuity model.** Under the WHEP polity definition
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition], the 1908
  end-of-row is a legitimate split (substantial territorial
  change — Bosnia annexation + Bulgaria independence + Cretan
  union). The 1886 start is the problematic boundary; see
  [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory] on [ott-1800-1886].

## Open questions


### oq-bosnia-double-count

During 1886–1908 Bosnia was
legally Ottoman but administratively Austro-Hungarian. WHEP
has a separate `BOS-1878-1908` row with its own polygon
[database]. Does the CShapes polygon for this row
(OTT-1886-1908) include or exclude Bosnia? If it includes it,
the same territory is counted twice (once here, once on
`BOS-1878-1908`); if it excludes it, the Ottoman polygon is
smaller than the Ottoman Empire's legal extent for the full
row's duration. Either answer is defensible but the page
should be explicit about which convention WHEP follows. A
direct `ogrinfo` query on `cshapes2_full.gpkg` row for
cowcode=640 during 1886–1908, compared against the
`BOS-1878-1908` polygon, would resolve this.

### oq-tunisia-boundary

Tunisia became a French protectorate
in 1881, before this row's 1886 start. The CShapes Ottoman
polygon for 1886–1908 should therefore exclude Tunisia.
Verify.
