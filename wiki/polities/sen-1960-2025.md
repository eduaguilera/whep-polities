# Senegal

## Summary

Senegal is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Senegal from 1960 to the present. Senegal became independent on 20 June 1960, initially as part of the Mali Federation, then as a separate state on 19 September 1960.

## Territorial extent

Senegal (area 75,951 sq. miles; 196,637 sq. km; population 7,900,000 [1994]) is bounded by Mauritania (305 miles; 813 km) in the north, Mali (260 miles; 419 km) in the east, Guinea (205 miles; 330 km) and Guinea-Bissau (210 miles; 338 km) in the south, and the Gambia (460 miles; 740 km) and the Atlantic Ocean in the west. Capital: Dakar. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
