# Gambia

## Summary

Gambia is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of The Gambia from 1800 to the present. The start year 1800 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage. Gambia gained independence from Britain on 18 February 1965.

## Territorial extent

Gambia is a narrow country along the Gambia River, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its short Atlantic coastline. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

- No predecessor (WHEP temporal boundary at 1800)

## Sourced claims

- Senegal is bounded by the Gambia (210 miles; 338 km) and Guinea-Bissau (205 miles; 330 km). "In 1982 the Senegambian (Senegal-Gambia) confederation was established after the Senegalese army saved Gambia's president from a military coup" (p.465). The confederation was dissolved in 1990. [biger-1995 §senegal](../sources/biger-1995.md#senegal)
- The Senegal-Gambia confederation "was created as a result of the close ethnic relations between the two countries, separated only by colonial borders: Gambia was a British colony but the same ethnic groups live in it" (p.465). [biger-1995 §senegal](../sources/biger-1995.md#senegal)

### oq-gambia-colonial-boundaries

Gambia colonial boundaries. The peculiar shape of Gambia (a narrow strip along the Gambia River inside Senegal) is a product of Franco-British colonial negotiations. The precise dates of boundary demarcation need investigation.
