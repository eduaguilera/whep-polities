# Gambia (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Gambia, a narrow West African territory along the Gambia River, covering 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. The British established a station at the mouth of the Gambia River in 1588 and founded Bathurst in 1826 [biger-1995 &sect;gambia](../sources/biger-1995.md#gambia); the colony became independent on 18 February 1965.

Unlike the island cases in this family, Gambia's boundary was *not* fixed across the whole span: Franco-British commissions demarcated the line with Senegal in 1895-1896, 1898-1899 and 1904-1905 (p.234), i.e. after the 1889 polygon vintage. The single row therefore carries one boundary for a period in which the line was still being surveyed — see [oq-gambia-colonial-boundaries](#oq-gambia-colonial-boundaries). The extent the demarcations settled is the same narrow river strip, so the error is a matter of metres along a surveyed line, not of scope.

## Territorial extent

Gambia is a narrow country along the Gambia River, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its short Atlantic coastline. Area 4,127 sq. miles (10,689 sq. km). The polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature 420 at its 1889 vintage, back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025.
