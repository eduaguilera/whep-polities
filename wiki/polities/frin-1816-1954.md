# French India (Établissements français dans l'Inde)

## Summary

This row tracks the **French Indian establishments** — the five scattered
enclaves on the Indian subcontinent that France retained or recovered after
the Napoleonic Wars: **Pondicherry** (Puducherry), **Karikal**, **Mahé**,
**Yanam** (Yanaon), and **Chandernagor** (Chandannagar). The collective
territory is known in French as *Établissements français dans l'Inde*. It
was formally administered as a colony/overseas territory of France from
**1816** (restoration to France by Britain following the Treaty of Paris,
1814–1815) until **1954**, when French civil administration was de facto
transferred to India following the Pondicherry Agreement of
1954-10-21. De jure sovereignty was transferred to India on
1962-08-16 (Treaty of Cession ratified).

The row exists because FAO/IIA yearbook **footnotes** for the 1960
agricultural data explicitly record that figures for **India exclude French
India** (Pondicherry et al.), which was still under nominal French
sovereignty at the time the 1960 survey was conducted. The territory
therefore requires its own WHEP entity so that the host India rows
(IND-1949-2025) are not inadvertently credited with French-administered
area or production. The footnote year is 1960; the enclave had been under
Indian civil administration since 1954, but formal French sovereignty
persisted until 1962.

**Chandernagor** occupies a special position: it voted in a 1949 referendum
to merge with India and was legally transferred to India on
1950-05-02 — several years before the other four establishments. A
separate WHEP row for Chandernagor alone is not created here; the present
row covers all five establishments as a single administrative unit for
the purpose of the IIA/FAO footnote reconciliation, with a note that
Chandernagor exited the entity in 1950 (see Open questions).

No WHEP predecessor row currently exists for earlier periods of French
India (the cliopatria dataset covers French India only to 1847).
Successor: [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** No faithful-vintage polygon has been located for
  French India in the 1816–1954 period. The sources queried are:
  - **CShapes 2.0**: does not list any French India or Pondicherry
    entry. CShapes 2.0 covers sovereign states and dependencies from
    1886 onward, but these small scattered enclaves are absent.
  - **Cliopatria** (`cliopatria_polities_only.geojson`): contains a
    "French India" series, but coverage ends at 1847 (`ToYear: 1847`).
    The latest feature spans 1846–1847 with area ~4,488 km². This is
    outside the WHEP period of interest (1860s onward and especially
    1953/1960 footnote years), so it cannot be used as a
    faithful-vintage polygon.
  - **Paine-2024** (`PCS.shp`): no matching feature found.
  - **GADM 4.1 adm1** (`gadm41_adm1.gpkg`): does not include India
    (IND not present in this partial extract). Puducherry (modern Union
    Territory) would in principle be a modern-proxy option, but the
    GADM file does not contain it.
- **Method.** No polygon assigned. `polygon_status: unassigned`.
- **Area.** The five establishments together cover approximately
  479–510 km² total (Pondicherry ~479 km², Karikal ~161 km², Mahé
  ~9 km², Yanam ~30 km², Chandernagor ~19 km², total ~698 km²
  by commonly cited figures). These are very small enclaves; no
  source polygon is available in the authorised WHEP geodata
  collection.
- **Confidence.** Low for polygon binding (none assigned). High for
  the historical facts.
- **Honest gap.** The absence of a polygon reflects a genuine gap in
  the available GIS sources for these tiny scattered colonial
  enclaves, not an error. An honest unassigned status is preferred
  over a false proxy. A future curator could construct a composed
  union from the modern Puducherry UT geometry (India GADM or
  OpenStreetMap) for the four remaining establishments plus a
  separate Chandernagor polygon.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** No WHEP row exists for French India before 1816
  (the Cliopatria series ends at 1847 and covers earlier, pre-modern
  phases; those are not directly linked here). The 1816 start reflects
  the post-Napoleonic restoration confirmed in the Treaty of Paris
  (1814) and Anglo-French conventions of 1815–1817 by which Britain
  returned Pondicherry, Karikal, Mahé, and Yanam to France. Karikal
  and Mahé were formally restituted in 1816–1817; Chandernagor in 1816;
  Yanam in 1817.
- **Successor.** [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md) — upon the de facto
  transfer of civil administration in October 1954 (and de jure in
  1962), the territory became part of India. Chandernagor had already
  joined India in 1950.

## Contradictions

- The spec cites the footnote year as 1960, by which time France had
  only nominal sovereignty (de facto transfer was 1954). The IIA/FAO
  footnote in 1960 likely carries over a boundary caveat from earlier
  editions rather than reflecting active French administration. The
  WHEP row end_year is set to 1954 (de facto), which is the
  economically meaningful date; the footnote caveat in 1960 data is
  a documentation lag.

## Decisions

- **Period.** `start_year: 1816` (Anglo-French restitution of the
  establishments, which is the start of the modern continuous French
  administrative period). `end_year: 1954` (de facto transfer of
  civil administration to India). The footnote year of 1960 post-dates
  effective French control; the row ends at 1954 to reflect the
  economically meaningful boundary.
- **Chandernagor not split.** Although Chandernagor merged with India
  in 1950, a separate WHEP row is not created at this stage. The IIA
  1953 footnote refers to "French India" as a unit; Chandernagor's
  early exit (1950) is documented here and in Open questions. A
  curator may split FRIN-1816-1950 (Chandernagor only) and
  FRIN-1816-1954 (the four remaining establishments) in future.
- **Polygon unassigned.** No faithful vintage polygon was found in any
  of the four authorised sources after direct OGR query on 2026-06-25.
  The cliopatria series ends in 1847; CShapes 2.0 and Paine-2024
  contain no matching feature; GADM 4.1 partial extract does not
  include India. Unassigned is the honest status.
- **iso3 = FRA.** The territory was a French colony/overseas
  territory throughout the period. ISO 3166 assigns no separate
  code to historical French India; FRA is the appropriate code.
- **cow = 220.** France (COW 220) is the sovereign power. The COW
  state system does not assign French India its own code.
- **continent = Asia.** Geographic location of the establishments is
  South Asia.
- **successor = IND-1949-2025.** India (post-constitution, 1949
  onward) absorbed all establishments by 1962; the 1949-2025 row is
  the appropriate successor.

## Open questions

### oq-chandernagor-split

Chandernagor left French India in 1950, four years before the
remaining establishments. The current row covers the whole entity
1816–1954. A more precise model would have:
- FRIN-1816-1950: Chandernagor only (area ~19 km²)
- FRIN-1816-1954: Pondicherry, Karikal, Mahé, Yanam (area ~680 km²)

However, since the IIA/FAO footnotes treat "French India" as a unit
and the data resolution does not distinguish sub-enclaves, the split
is deferred. Revisit if Chandernagor-specific agricultural data are
identified.

### oq-polygon-construction

A composed polygon for the four surviving establishments could be
constructed from:
1. Modern Puducherry Union Territory boundaries from OpenStreetMap or
   a full GADM India download (not present in the current partial
   extract).
2. A legacy Chandernagor town boundary from historical maps.
These sources are outside the four authorised WHEP geodata sources.
A future pipeline step could construct and bind such a polygon.

### oq-1953-footnote

The spec lists footnote years of both 1953 and 1960. The 1953 IIA
footnote likely has the same boundary caveat. Whether Chandernagor
is included or excluded from the 1953 India figures (given its 1950
legal transfer) is not confirmed. This should be checked against the
original IIA 1953 yearbook.

### oq-cliopatria-coverage

The Cliopatria series for French India covers 1673–1847 with 31
temporal features. Coverage ends well before the IIA/FAO reference
period. It is unclear why cliopatria does not continue the series
beyond 1847 (the French Indian establishments continued to exist).
This may reflect a Seshat modelling boundary or a data gap.
