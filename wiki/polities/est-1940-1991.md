# Estonian SSR

## Summary

Tracks Estonia under Soviet occupation and as a constituent republic of the USSR from **1940** to **1991**. The Soviet Union occupied Estonia in 1940 following the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact; after German occupation (1941-1944), Soviet control was re-established. Estonia restored independence in 1991 [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

The Estonian SSR had the same boundaries as interwar Estonia (minus Petsamo).~~ CShapes 2.0 tracks the USSR as one entity for this period; a polygon from [EST-1918-1940](est-1918-1940.md) would be a good approximation.

**Corrected 2026-09-24.** The struck sentence named the wrong territory. **Petsamo** (Pechenga) is on the Arctic coast and was **Finnish**, ceded by Finland to the USSR in 1944 (Moscow Armistice) and 1947 (Paris); it never had anything to do with Estonia. What the Estonian SSR lost was, in **January 1945**, about **2,200 km²** — roughly 5% of interwar Estonia — to the Russian SFSR: most of **Petseri** county (Pechory, Izborsk; about 1,350 km², to Pskov oblast) and the land east of the Narva river including **Ivangorod** (Jaanilinn, Narva's eastern suburb, to Leningrad oblast). So the boundary was the interwar one only for 1940-1944, and the interwar polygon attached here **overstates the 1945-1990 territory by that ~2,200 km²**. The row does not split at 1945; that missing era needs its own polygon and is listed in issue #686.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Estonia" for the Soviet period. The Estonian SSR maintained separate agricultural statistics as a union republic. Previously routed to F228 (USSR), merging Estonian data into the all-USSR polygon.

## Key dates

- **1940:** Soviet occupation and annexation.
- **1941-1944:** German occupation (within row window).
- **January 1945:** Petseri county and the land east of the Narva river (Ivangorod) transferred to the Russian SFSR, about 2,200 km² (added 2026-09-24).
- **1991:** Restoration of Estonian independence.
