# Saar Basin Territory (League of Nations)

## Summary

This row tracks the **Saar Basin Territory** under **League of Nations
administration**, from the entry into force of the Treaty of Versailles
(**10 January 1920**) until the formal return of the territory to Germany
following the **1935 plebiscite (1 March 1935)**. The governing commission
was an international body appointed by the League of Nations Council; France
held the coal mines in the territory as reparations for the destruction of
French mines during World War I, but sovereignty was vested in the League,
not France. The territory had its own distinct budget and administrative
apparatus, and its economic statistics were frequently reported separately
from Germany proper in interwar IIA yearbooks.

This row was created because **IIA yearbooks report Saar data separately
from Germany** in the 1929 edition (the spec footnote year), reflecting
the territory's distinct administrative and statistical identity during
1920–1935. The Saar is classified as a `separate_entity` host relative
to Germany in the WHEP footnote schema. It is **DISTINCT from
[SAA-1947-1957](saa-1947-1957.md)**, which covers the post-WWII Saar
Protectorate under French administration.

  2. **Cliopatria** (`cliopatria_polities_only.geojson`): no feature with
     `Name` containing "Saar". SKIPPED — feature absent.
  3. **Paine-2024** (`PCS.shp`): the dataset covers 46 African pre-colonial
     water-territory features only; no Saar feature. SKIPPED — out of scope.
  4. **GADM 4.1 ADM1** (`gadm41_adm1.gpkg`): no Germany (DEU) features
     present in the ADM1 layer (the country-specific `gadm41_DEU.gpkg` is
     not present in `data/geodata/gadm-4.1/`). SKIPPED — source file absent.
- **Polygon status: `unassigned`.** No eligible source covers this entity
  and period. This is an accepted WHEP pattern; the honest gap is documented
  here.
- **Preferred resolution.** Acquire `gadm41_DEU.gpkg` and assign the GADM
  ADM1 Saarland feature (`GID_1: DEU.Saarland_1` or equivalent) as a
  `modern_proxy`. The territory of the League of Nations Saar Basin
  corresponded closely to the modern German state of Saarland (~2,570 km²).
  Boundaries were fixed by Annex III of the Treaty of Versailles (1919) and
  virtually unchanged at the 1935 transfer; drift from the modern Saarland
  boundary is negligible. Mark method as `modern_proxy`, confidence `high`.
- **Area (not formally recorded).** Published references give the Saar
  Basin Territory an area of approximately **1,912 km²** (Treaty of
  Versailles Annex III). Note: the slightly larger figure (~2,570 km²) cited
  for the post-WWII Saar Protectorate reflects a somewhat broader boundary
  used in 1947. The 1920–1935 area is the Versailles-defined territory.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [DEU-1919-1920](deu-1919-1920.md) — the Saar Basin was
  ceded from Germany under Article 45–50 and Annex III of the Treaty of
  Versailles (signed 28 June 1919, in force 10 January 1920). Prior to that
  date the territory was part of the German Empire / transitional Weimar
  Germany (DEU-1919-1920). The WHEP chain flows from that transitional row
  into SAB-1920-1935 at the Versailles entry into force.
- **Successor:** [DEU-1920-1938](deu-1920-1938.md) — following the
  13 January 1935 plebiscite (90.7% vote to reunite with Germany) the Saar
  Basin was formally transferred back to Germany on **1 March 1935**. From
  that date the territory is covered under the Germany row. Note that
  DEU-1920-1938 acknowledges this return in its `oq-1935-saar-mid-row` open
  question.

## Sourced claims

- **Treaty of Versailles, Annex III (Articles 45–50), in force 10 January
  1920.** Transferred the Saar coal mines to France and placed the territory
  under a League of Nations governing commission for a 15-year period.
  [wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16.md)
- **Area approximately 1,912 km².** The Versailles-defined territory
  of the Saar Basin. Commonly cited in interwar reference works. (Not
  independently verified against a polygon in this session; recorded as
  an Open question.)
  [wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16.md)
- **Governing Commission.** The territory was governed by a five-member
  international commission appointed by the League of Nations Council, with
  France holding the coal mines (not sovereignty).
  [wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-colonial-mandates-2026-04-16.md)
- **Plebiscite, 13 January 1935.** Voters chose between union with Germany,
  union with France, or continuation of League of Nations administration.
  90.7% voted for return to Germany.
  [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md)
- **Transfer to Germany, 1 March 1935.** The formal end of League of
  Nations administration and the start of German sovereignty. This is the
  end-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md)
- **IIA 1929 yearbook footnote.** The International Institute of Agriculture
  1929 yearbook reports Saar data separately from Germany, the basis for
  this WHEP row as a `separate_entity`. This is the footnote year cited in
  the WHEP gap analysis.

## Contradictions

**Area: 1,912 km² vs. ~2,570 km².** The 1920 Versailles-defined Saar Basin
Territory (~1,912 km²) is smaller than the post-WWII Saar Protectorate
(~2,570 km²) covered by [SAA-1947-1957](saa-1947-1957.md). The post-WWII
boundaries were set by the 1947 Saar Statute and include some additional
territory. These are two distinct entities with similar but non-identical
geographic extents. This is not a data error; see Decisions below.

## Decisions

- **Polity code SAB-1920-1935.** The abbreviation SAB (Saar Basin) is used
  to distinguish this entity from SAA (post-WWII Saar Protectorate). ISO
  3166 has no code for the League of Nations Saar territory; the WHEP
  convention of using a sensible abbreviation applies. The code SAA is
  already reserved for the 1947–1957 protectorate.
- **Type: colonial.** The Saar was a League of Nations-administered
  territory with sovereignty formally vested in the League; it was not an
  independent state, nor simply a German province during this period. The
  `colonial` type follows the WHEP convention for dependent/administered
  territories.
- **Polygon unassigned.** No polygon source covers the 1920–1935 Saar
  Basin. GADM DEU is absent. The entity is left unassigned with a clear
  preferred resolution path.
- **Predecessor: DEU-1919-1920 (not DEU-1920-1938).** The territory was
  carved from Germany at Versailles entry into force (10 January 1920),
  which coincides with the DEU-1919-1920/DEU-1920-1938 boundary. The
  predecessor is therefore DEU-1919-1920 (the Versailles-transition row),
  and the successor is DEU-1920-1938 (Weimar/early Nazi Germany without
  the Saar, then with it after 1935).
- **Distinct from SAA-1947-1957.** The two Saar entries cover different
  historical periods and different legal frameworks (League of Nations
  trusteeship vs. French protectorate). They must not be merged.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-unassigned

The Saar Basin Territory has no polygon in any available source. The
preferred resolution is to acquire `gadm41_DEU.gpkg` and assign the GADM
ADM1 Saarland feature as a `modern_proxy` (method: modern_proxy, confidence:
high, drift from 1920 boundary: small but non-zero — the 1935 and 1947
boundaries differ). Until then `polygon_status: unassigned`.

### oq-area-verification

The ~1,912 km² figure for the 1920 Versailles-defined territory is drawn
from secondary sources and has not been verified against a georeferenced
polygon in this session. When a polygon is acquired, the area should be
recomputed from the actual geometry (EPSG:6933 equal-area projection).

### oq-iia-footnote-scope

The IIA 1929 yearbook footnote reports Saar data separately from Germany.
The scope of statistical series reported under "Saar" in IIA and other
interwar sources (production, trade, population) should be inventoried to
confirm which WHEP input data series need aliasing to this row.

### oq-cow-code

COW does not list the Saar Basin as a separate state. No COW code is
assigned. If another interwar sovereignty dataset (e.g., a revised COW
colonial supplement) assigns a code, `cow` should be updated.
