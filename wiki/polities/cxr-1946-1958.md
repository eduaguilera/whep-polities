# Christmas Island (under Singapore administration)

## Summary

This row tracks **Christmas Island** (Indian Ocean, ~139.7 km²) during the period of its administration as a dependency of the **Singapore Crown Colony**, from **1 January 1946** — when Christmas Island was transferred from the Straits Settlements to Singapore's jurisdiction upon dissolution of the Straits Settlements — until **1 October 1958**, when the island was transferred to Australian administration under the *Christmas Island Act 1958* (Cth).

Christmas Island is a geographically isolated volcanic island in the Indian Ocean at approximately 10.5°S, 105.6°E, roughly 1,400 km south of Singapore and 2,600 km northwest of Perth, Australia. Its economy during this colonial period was dominated by phosphate rock mining, which commenced in 1899 and continued under successive administrations.

**Why this entry exists.** The FAO Production Yearbook 1952 reports phosphate rock production for the label "Singapore Christmas Island" for years 1949–1951 (8 rows). No existing WHEP polity covers this territory: SGP-1946-1963 has a CShapes polygon covering only Singapore island (~544.72 km²), and Christmas Island is geographically separate ~1,400 km away. Folding the FAO data into SGP-1946-1963 would be a sub-territory mis-attribution.

## Decisions

- **Period.** 1946–1958. Straits Settlements dissolved 1 April 1946; island transferred to Australia 1 October 1958 (integer year 1958 per WHEP convention).
- **Polygon.** GADM 4.1 `gadm41_CXR.gpkg`, `GID_0=CXR`. Modern boundary used as period proxy; island geography unchanged.
- **polygon_status: assigned.** GADM polygon provides accurate geographic coverage; functionally exact for historical extent given fixed volcanic coastline.
- **Type: colonial.** Administered as colonial dependency of Singapore Crown Colony.
- **iso3: CXR.** Modern ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 used as standard territory identifier; no historical code exists.
- **cow: NA.** Not assigned a COW state-system code.
- **Sub-territory mis-attribution avoided.** FAO "Singapore Christmas Island" must not route to SGP-1946-1963. A dedicated CXR-1946-1958 entry is the only correct routing.
