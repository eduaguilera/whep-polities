# Tokelau


> **Polygon area declared 2026-08-06, and `polygon_status` lowered to `estimate` because it is one.**
>
> ```
> GADM 4.1 polygon as shipped   15.95 km²
> official land area            12 km²
> ratio                         1.33x
> ```
>
> `polygon_area_km2` now states the **official land area** rather than what the polygon measures.
> That is the field's purpose — it is the page's own figure, which `validate_polygons` check A
> compares the geometry against — and declaring the measured value instead would make the check
> self-referential and unable to detect anything.
>
> Because the divergence exceeds check A's 25% tolerance, `polygon_status` moves from `assigned` to
> **`estimate`**. That is not a workaround: `estimate` means the polygon approximates the territory,
> which is exactly the situation. Check A fails only for rows claiming `assigned`, and reports
> `estimate` rows as documented — so the disagreement stays visible in every run instead of being
> hidden behind a declared value chosen to agree with itself.
>
> **This is a class, not a defect in this row.** Comparing every micro-territory in the database
> against its official land area:
>
> ```
> TUV 1.39x   MHL 1.39x   TKL 1.33x   KIR 1.17x   NIU 1.01x   NRU 0.82x   MDV 0.58x
> ```
>
> The errors run **both directions**, from 42% under to 39% over, so this is not "GADM encloses
> atoll lagoons" — Maldives, the most lagoon-heavy of all, is 42% **short**. GADM's accuracy for
> territories of a few tens of km² is variable and unpredictable, and no single correction applies.
>
> **Any per-km² figure computed on this row is unusable.** At this magnitude the absolute
> uncertainty is comparable to the territory.
## Summary

Three atolls — Atafu, Nukunonu and Fakaofo — in the South Pacific, about 500 km north of Samoa. A
British protectorate from 1877, part of the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony from 1916, transferred
to New Zealand administration in 1925 and formally to New Zealand sovereignty in 1948. Still a
non-self-governing territory of New Zealand.

## Why this entry exists

**It was the only ISO3 code in whep_crops v1.0 with no polity family at any period** — 252 observed
FAOSTAT rows, 1961-2023, resolving to nothing. Every other one of the panel's 209 codes reached at
least one polity.

```
observed rows          252
years                  1961-2023
source                 FAOSTAT (all 252)
items                  4 (coconut, copra and related products)
```

Those rows were the largest single unresolved block in issue 155 after the successor-map work in
#170, #172 and #173 resolved 373 of 482 uncovered `(iso3, year)` pairs. Tokelau could not be
resolved by traversal, because traversal needs a row to walk from.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = TKL`.

```
GADM 4.1 measured      15.67 km²
official land area     12 km²
difference              ~4 km²
```

GADM is about 30% above the official figure, which for a territory of this size is the
low-magnitude regime rather than a defect: at a coastline resolution that resolves reef and lagoon
edges, three atolls measure larger than a land-area gazetteer reports. The same caveat is recorded
for `SMR-1800-2025` in `scripts/validate_stated_areas.py`, where FAO's smallest unit (1,000
hectares = 10 km²) cannot express San Marino's 61 km² at all. **Four km² of absolute uncertainty is
not usable as a denominator for anything**, and any per-km² figure computed on this row should be
read as an order of magnitude.

An area is nevertheless **declared**, because `validate_polygons`' area comparison is opt-in via
that field and a null leaves the row unchecked (issue 59) — the mechanism that hid four separate
defects this week, including Tuscany drawn as the Duchy of Massa.

## Span

`1800-2025`, a 226-year single-vintage row, which is normally the shape issue 22 warns about
("long single-vintage rows overstate early-period territory"). **It is safe here for a reason that
does not generalise:** the three atolls are the same three atolls throughout. There is no boundary
to have moved. Compare `COK-1800-2025` and `NIU-1800-2025`, which use the same span and the same
justification.

The administrative history — protectorate 1877, Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony 1916, New Zealand
administration 1925, New Zealand sovereignty 1948 — is a sequence of **sovereignty** changes, not
territorial ones. This database has no field for sovereignty, which is the same gap that caused
`GRL-1800-2025` and `ISL-1800-2025` to record Denmark under `successor:` until PR 135. So it is
recorded here in prose and deliberately not forced into a chain field.

## Predecessors and successors

Both empty, and deliberately. Tokelau's predecessors in the administrative sense are the British
protectorate and the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony, neither of which is a territorial
predecessor of these atolls — they governed them, they did not precede them. Putting either in
`predecessor:` would assert a chronology that did not happen, which is the error PR 135 removed from
Greenland and Iceland.

## Open questions

### oq-tkl-pre-1961-data

FAOSTAT begins in 1961 and no earlier source in this repo reports Tokelau. The span starts at 1800
for consistency with `COK` and `NIU` rather than because data reaches back that far; if a pre-1961
source is ingested, nothing about this row needs to change, which is the point of choosing the
constant-territory span.
