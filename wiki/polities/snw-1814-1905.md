# Sweden-Norway (1814-1905)

## Summary

This row tracks the Sweden-Norway personal union for **92 years**,
from the Treaty of Kiel to the dissolution of the union
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The union was
formed by the Treaty of Kiel (14 January 1814) after Denmark was forced
to cede Norway to Sweden. Norway declared independence on 7 June 1905
and the union was peacefully dissolved on 26 October 1905. The Swedish
king accepted Norwegian independence without war.

Predecessor: [swe-1809-1814](swe-1809-1814.md) -- Treaty of Kiel
(14 January 1814).
Successor: [SWE-1905-2025](swe-1905-2025.md) -- modern Sweden after
dissolution of the union. Norway continues as
[NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md).

  1905. No territorial changes to Sweden proper accompanied the
  dissolution.
