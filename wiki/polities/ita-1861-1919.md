# Italy (to 1919)

## Summary

This row tracks the Kingdom of Italy from its **1861**
proclamation to the **1919** Treaty of Saint-Germain [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
Unlike the long-running France and UK rows, this polity begins
at a real historical event — the unification of Italy on
17 March 1861 [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1861-unification](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1861-unification).

- **1861 start** is a real territorial event: the proclamation of
  the Kingdom of Italy from the merger of the Kingdom of
  Sardinia, the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, most of the Papal
  States, and several duchies. Population: 21,777,334; area:
  250,320 km² [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §area](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#area).
- **1919 end** is a real territorial event: the Treaty of
  Saint-Germain-en-Laye (10 September 1919) ceded Trentino,
  South Tyrol, Trieste, and Istria from Austria to Italy.
  CShapes records the polygon change at exactly 1919-09-10, with
  an area increase from ~31.25 to ~33.05 sq degrees
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).

The 59-year window includes two major mid-row territorial
acquisitions — Venetia (1866) and Rome (1870) — neither of
which triggers a CSV split. Under the WHEP territorial-change
rule, both are candidate split points.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0 + CShapes-Europe` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records Italy (`cowcode=325`, labeled
  "Italy/Sardinia") from 1886-01-01 to 1919-09-09 with area
  ~31.25 sq degrees (post-1870, with Rome but without
  Trentino/Trieste). At 1919-09-10 the area increases to ~33.05
  sq degrees [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Pre-1886 extent.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The
  1861–1866 polygon (without Venetia) and 1866–1870 polygon
  (with Venetia but without Rome) would need CShapes-Europe or
  another source.
- **1861 initial area.** 250,320 km² at unification
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §area](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#area). This excludes Venetia
  (gained 1866) and Rome/Lazio (gained 1870).
- **1866 Venetia gain.** The Vienna Peace Treaty of 3 October
  1866 ceded Venetia from Austria to Italy
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This substantially enlarged the kingdom
  during the row's window but is not a CSV split.
- **1870 Rome capture.** On 20 September 1870 Italy captured Rome
  and the remaining Papal States
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome). This completed
  Italian unification but is not a CSV split.

## Sourced claims

- **1861-03-17: Kingdom of Italy proclaimed.** Victor Emmanuel II
  of Sardinia declared King of Italy. Population: 21,777,334;
  area: 250,320 km²
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1861-unification](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1861-unification).
- **1866: Third Italian War of Independence.** Italy allied with
  Prussia against Austria; gained Venetia. Wikipedia gives
  20 June 1866 as the war start
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1866-venetia](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1866-venetia). Biger gives
  3 October 1866 as the Vienna Peace Treaty date for the cession
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This is a substantial territorial gain
  within the row; see [oq-1866-venetia-split-candidate](#oq-1866-venetia-split-candidate).
- **1870-09-20: Capture of Rome.** Italy took the Papal States,
  ending "more than one thousand years of Papal temporal power."
  Capital relocated from Florence to Rome
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome). Completes Italian
  unification. Another candidate mid-row split; see
  [oq-1870-rome-split-candidate](#oq-1870-rome-split-candidate).
- **1882-05-20: Triple Alliance.** Italy, Germany, and
  Austria-Hungary formed the Triple Alliance
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1882-triple-alliance](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1882-triple-alliance). Not a
  territorial event.
- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Italy annexed
  Trentino, South Tyrol, Trieste, and Istria from Austria.
  CShapes polygon change at this date (+1.80 sq degrees)
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1919-saint-germain](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1919-saint-germain). This is the
  CSV's end-of-row event.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists Italy (`ITA`,
  `ccode=325`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01
  to 2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). COW uses
  the Sardinian state as the continuator entity from 1816 — 45
  years before the 1861 unification. COW makes no split at 1861,
  1866, 1870, or 1919.

### oq-1866-venetia-split-candidate

The 1866 acquisition of Venetia from Austria is a substantial
territorial gain. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, it is
a candidate mid-row split:
- Real territorial event (Vienna Peace Treaty, 3 October 1866)
- Significant area gain (Venetia is ~18,000 km²)
- The gain happens in the first 5 years of the row

The CSV does not split at 1866. No documented rationale found.
Related to the general split-rule question across the whole
wiki (Ottoman 1886, Austrian 1867, German 1871, French 1860/
1871). Tier X — requires human split-policy decision.

### oq-1870-rome-split-candidate

The 1870 capture of Rome and the remaining Papal States is
another candidate mid-row split:
- Real territorial event (20 September 1870)
- Completed Italian unification
- Capital relocated from Florence to Rome

Smaller in km² than Venetia but symbolically decisive. The
predecessor `PAP-1800-1870` ends at 1870 per the CSV, so
WHEP already recognizes 1870 as a significant date from the
Papal perspective — but not from the Italian perspective. This
asymmetry is unexplained. Tier X.

### oq-cow-325-from-1816

COW starts Italy at `ccode=325` from 1816-01-01 — 45 years
before Italian unification. This means COW treats
Sardinia/Piedmont as the Italian continuator state. The WHEP
row starts at 1861 (unification). The predecessor
`SAR-1800-1860` presumably uses a different COW code or
none. Worth documenting the COW coding convention for the
Italian case.
