# Spanish West Africa (1912-1958)

## Summary

Spanish West Africa (1912-1958) covers the combined Spanish colonial territory on the Atlantic coast of Africa — comprising Río de Oro (the southern zone) and Saguia el-Hamra (the northern zone), later administratively unified as Spanish West Africa — from the 1912 Treaty of Fez (27 November 1912) until the territory was reorganized into the province of Spanish Sahara in 1958. The 1912 date reflects the Franco-Spanish Treaty of Fez, which also established the Spanish Protectorate in Morocco, causing CShapes to record a polygon change on 1912-11-27. The predecessor row SWA-1884-1912 covers the earlier colonial period. No COW code is assigned to Spanish West Africa as a dependent territory.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records Spanish West Africa with COW code 610 and area approximately 240,016 km² in the 1912-11-27 to 1958-10-09 step, which is larger than the preceding 1900–1912 step (approximately 156,010 km²), reflecting the addition of the Saguia el-Hamra zone defined in the Franco-Spanish 1912 agreement. The status is "protectorate" with owner=230 (Spain). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

The population threshold for CShapes dependencies is 250,000. Spanish West Africa's population was at or near this threshold during much of this period; its inclusion in CShapes confirms it met the criterion. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [SWA-1884-1912](swa-1884-1912.md) — Bonelli treaty (28 November 1884) established Spanish presence; earlier phase of Spanish West Africa
- **Successor:** [ESH-1958-1975](esh-1958-1975.md) — reorganized as Spanish Sahara (province) in 1958; Spain withdrew 14 November 1975

## Open questions

### oq-1912-treaty-details

The CShapes 1912-11-27 polygon change date matches the Treaty of Fez (27 November 1912), which established the French Protectorate in Morocco and a related Spanish protectorate zone. The specific clause that defined Spanish West Africa's expanded boundaries in 1912 has not been directly cited. A future ingest should consult the treaty text or a colonial boundary atlas to confirm what territory was added.

### oq-spanish-sahara-1958-reorganization

In 1958 Spain reorganized the territory as the Province of Spanish Sahara, separating the successor entity (ESH-1958-1975) from Spanish Morocco's southern zone (tracked as WHEP row SMO-1912-1956 and its post-1956 successor). A future ingest should confirm whether the southern zone of Spanish Morocco (CShapes cowcode=6021, 1956-04-07 to 1958-04-09) is tracked in the WHEP CSV.

### oq-biger-spanish-section

Biger 1995 has a standalone entry for Spain (p.455) and references to Spanish Sahara/Western Sahara boundaries. The next ingest should extract Spanish West Africa boundary details from Biger to complement the CShapes sourcing.
