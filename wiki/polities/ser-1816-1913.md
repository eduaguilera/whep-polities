# Serbia (1816-1913)

## Summary

This row tracks Serbia for **98 years**, from Serbian autonomy to the
Balkan Wars [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Serbia
was an Ottoman vassal that gained autonomy progressively: 1815-1817
Serbian Revolution, Congress of Berlin 1878 formal independence. Serbia
expanded significantly in the First Balkan War (1912-1913), gaining
Kosovo and parts of Macedonia. The 1913 end date aligns with the Second
Balkan War / Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913).

The CSV has `cow = NA` despite CShapes using `cowcode=345` and COW
listing Serbia/Yugoslavia under code 345. This is flagged as an open
question.

Predecessor: NA per CSV. Serbia was Ottoman territory before gaining
autonomy.
Successor: [ser-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md) and
[ser-1913-1915](ser-1913-1915.md) per CSV (dual successors).

## Sourced claims

- **1815-1817: Serbian Revolution.** Serbia gained autonomy from the
  Ottoman Empire through the First and Second Serbian Uprisings
  (1804-1815). The Principality of Serbia was established as an
  autonomous Ottoman vassal.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878)
- **1878: Congress of Berlin.** "Serbia... formally recognized as
  independent"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878).
  Serbia gained full independence alongside Romania and Montenegro.
  This is the COW start date (1878-07-13).
- **CShapes.** `cowcode=345` first appears at 1886-01-01 with area
  ~5.88 sq deg. Area grows to ~10.02 sq deg at 1913-08-10 (Balkan Wars
  gains)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1912-1913: Balkan Wars.** The First Balkan War (October 1912 -
  May 1913) saw Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece, and Montenegro defeat the
  Ottoman Empire. Serbia gained Kosovo and parts of Macedonia. The
  Second Balkan War (June-August 1913) saw Serbia gain further
  territory from Bulgaria. The Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913)
  concluded the wars.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 345 (YUG)
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
  COW uses code 345 for both Serbia and Yugoslavia, reflecting
  continuity. The CSV has `cow = NA`.

## Open questions

### oq-ottoman-predecessor

The CSV has `predecessor = NA` but Serbia was Ottoman territory before
gaining autonomy (1815-1817) and independence (1878). The predecessor
chain should reference Ottoman rows
([ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md)).

### oq-cow-code-na

The CSV has `cow = NA` but CShapes uses `cowcode=345` (Serbia) and COW
lists Serbia/Yugoslavia under code 345 from 1878-07-13. This appears
to be a CSV data gap.

### oq-dual-successor

The CSV lists both SER-1913-1918 and SER-1913-1915 as successors --
unusual. Most polities have a single successor. This may reflect
different aspects of Serbian continuity (e.g., Serbia proper vs.
Serbia in WWI context). Needs investigation when successor pages are
created.
