# Central African Republic (1919-1960)

## Summary

The French colony of Oubangui-Chari, covering the territory of the present-day Central African Republic, from a boundary change in 1919 until independence on 13 August 1960. "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were united in 1906" and were subsequently separated [biger-1995 central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). The Central African Republic constituted "a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad (Ubangi-Shari-Chad) when they were united in 1906 and became a section of French Equatorial Africa on 15 January 1910. On 30 July 1912 the boundaries of Oubangui-Chari were established in general terms" [biger-1995 central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). CShapes codes this polity as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
