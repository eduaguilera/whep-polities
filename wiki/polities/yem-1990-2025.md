# Yemen (1990-2025)

## Summary

Yemen (1990-2025) tracks the Republic of Yemen from unification in 1990 to the present. North Yemen (Yemen Arab Republic) and South Yemen (People's Democratic Republic of Yemen) merged on 22 May 1990 to form the Republic of Yemen. "In 1990 they merged to form the Yemen Republic." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen). This row was formed by merging YEM-1990-2000 and YEM-2000-2025, because CShapes records the same area (~11.50 sq deg) throughout with no change at year 2000. Yemen is bounded by Saudi Arabia and Oman (179 mi; 288 km), with coastline on the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, and Arabian Sea.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0; cowcode 678. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **CShapes entries for cowcode 678 (pre-unification).** Three entries from 1918-10-30 to 1990-05-21, all with identical area ~11.50 sq deg. No territorial change recorded between these time-steps (administrative/status changes only).
- **Unified Yemen.** CShapes cowcode for unified Yemen continues at 678 (same code as North Yemen). The area remains ~11.50 sq deg.
- **1992 Oman-Yemen boundary.** The boundary agreement with Oman was signed on 1 October 1992, demarcating the eastern border. This may cause a minor area change -- the next ingest should verify.
- **COW.** YEM, 678.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** The CSV predecessor field is NA. Historically the row succeeds both the Yemen Arab Republic (North Yemen) and the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen (South Yemen). These predecessor rows are not present in the CSV (only YEM-1990-2025 is tracked, with the North Yemen CShapes cowcode 678 used as the continuation).
- **Successor:** None (ongoing).

## Decisions

Merged YEM-1990-2000 + YEM-2000-2025 into YEM-1990-2025 (2026-04-13). CShapes records identical polygon areas (~11.50 sq deg) across both time-steps; no territorial change at year 2000 was documented. Note: the Treaty of Jeddah (12 June 2000) between Yemen and Saudi Arabia IS a real boundary treaty, but CShapes shows no area change. This merge is provisional and could be reversed if better polygon data shows a territorial change at 2000.

## Open questions

### oq-north-south-yemen-predecessors

The CSV predecessor field for this row is NA. The two pre-unification Yemeni states (North Yemen / YAR and South Yemen / PDRY) are not present as separate rows in the database. This is a coverage gap -- approximately 23 years of North and South Yemeni territorial history (1967-1990) is unaccounted for by explicit predecessor rows.
