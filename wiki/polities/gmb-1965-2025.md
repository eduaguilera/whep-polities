# Gambia

## Summary

Independent Gambia from 18 February 1965 to the present. "In 1982 the Senegambian (Senegal-Gambia) confederation was established after the Senegalese army saved Gambia's president from a military coup" (p.465). The confederation was dissolved in 1990 [biger-1995 &sect;senegal](../sources/biger-1995.md#senegal).

## Territorial extent

Gambia is a narrow country along the Gambia River, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its short Atlantic coastline. Area 4,127 sq. miles (10,689 sq. km). Polygon from CShapes 2.0.
