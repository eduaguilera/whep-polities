# Uganda (1910-1926)

## Summary

The Uganda Protectorate in its 1910-1926 configuration, following a boundary adjustment in June 1910. CShapes records area 22.97 sq deg for the period 1910-06-16 to 1926-09-26 (COW 500, status=protectorate, owner=Britain COW 200). "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894" [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda). The 1926 end date corresponds to a boundary change that reduced the area to 19.68 sq deg, matching the final colonial configuration that persisted until independence.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 22.97 sq deg for the period 1910-06-16 to 1926-09-26, status=protectorate, owner=Britain (COW 200).
