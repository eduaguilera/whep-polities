# Armenia (1991-2025)

## Summary

Independent Armenia from the dissolution of the USSR to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~30,000 km². Nagorno-Karabakh conflict with Azerbaijan dominated
post-independence politics. 2020 war and 2023 Azerbaijani takeover of
Karabakh. EU Eastern Partnership member.

Predecessor: [f228-1945-1991](f228-1945-1991.md). Successor: NA (still
active).

## Sourced claims

- **1991-09-21: Independence declared.** Armenia declared independence
  from the USSR.
- **1988-1994: Nagorno-Karabakh War.** First war with Azerbaijan over
  the Nagorno-Karabakh enclave.
- **2020: Second Nagorno-Karabakh War.** Azerbaijan recaptured
  significant territory.
- **2023: Azerbaijani takeover of Karabakh.** Azerbaijan took full
  control; ethnic Armenian population displaced.
- **Biger: Armenian statehood.** "Armenia was the first country in the
  world to proclaim Christianity its state religion (297–301)"
  [biger-1995 §armenia](../sources/biger-1995.md#armenia) (p.44).
- **Biger: 1918 independence.** "In 1918 an independent republic was
  proclaimed in former Russian Armenia, but was abolished by the
  Communists"
  [biger-1995 §armenia](../sources/biger-1995.md#armenia) (p.44).
- **Biger: Nagorno-Karabakh.** "With the eclipse of the USSR tension
  erupted between Armenia and Azerbaijan over the Nagorno-Karabakh
  Autonomous Oblast"
  [biger-1995 §armenia](../sources/biger-1995.md#armenia) (p.44).
