# Brazil (1903-1909)

## Summary

This row tracks Brazil from **1903** to **1909** — a **7-year
transitional row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is
post-Acre acquisition Brazil, during the Old Republic period
(Republica Velha). The 1909 split may relate to further boundary
treaties with neighboring states.

The Old Republic was characterized by the "politics of the
governors" (politica dos governadores) and the coffee-with-milk
(cafe com leite) alternation between Sao Paulo and Minas Gerais
elites. COW: BRA, ccode=140.

Predecessor: [bra-1800-1903](bra-1800-1903.md).
Successor: [bra-1909-2025](bra-1909-2025.md).

  ceded the Acre territory to Brazil. The post-treaty polygon is
  what this row represents.
- **1904-1906: Rodrigues Alves presidency.** Urban modernization of
  Rio de Janeiro. Oswaldo Cruz vaccination campaigns.
- **1906-1909: Afonso Pena presidency.** Continued Old Republic
  politics.
