# Senegal (colony, 1854-1886)

## Summary

The French colony of Senegal expanded significantly under Governor Louis Faidherbe, who arrived in 1854 and launched campaigns to extend French control beyond the coastal enclaves of Saint-Louis and Goree into the interior. Faidherbe established forts along the Senegal River, subjugated the Toucouleur Empire's western reaches, and laid the groundwork for French West Africa. The period ends in 1886 with the formation of broader French West African administrative structures.
