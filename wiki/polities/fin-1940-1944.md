# Finland (1940-1944)

## Summary

Finland between the Moscow Peace Treaty (12 March 1940), which took Karelia and Salla, and the
Moscow Armistice (19 September 1944), which took Petsamo [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §finland](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#finland). Created on 2026-10-07 by
splitting the old `FIN-1940-2025` row at 1944 (issue 686); the later years are
[fin-1944-2025](fin-1944-2025.md).

**Why this entry exists.** CShapes 2.0 moves Petsamo to the USSR in 1940, four years early. Finnish
data for 1940-1943 was therefore measured against a territory about 10,800 km² smaller than the one
Finland held. The 1941-1944 reoccupation of East Karelia during the Continuation War was a military
occupation, not a change of sovereignty, and is not drawn.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. Constructed (`build_fin_1940_1944` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): CShapes 2.0 gwcode 375's 1940-2019 step **plus Petsamo**.
Petsamo is the piece north of 68 N in the difference between CShapes' own 1917-1940 and 1940-2019
steps (see [fin-1917-1920](fin-1917-1920.md) for the three pieces of that difference).

**km² reasoning.** The result measures 344,696 km² (Mollweide), against 333,858 km² for the CShapes
step alone. The added 10,837 km² matches the ~10,500 km² usually given for Petsamo.

**Known overlap.** CShapes' Soviet steps from 1940 include Petsamo, so for 1940-1943 this row and the
USSR row both cover the 10,837 km². The overlap is the CShapes error that this split corrects on the
Finnish side only: the Soviet rows are CShapes features and are not rebuilt here.
