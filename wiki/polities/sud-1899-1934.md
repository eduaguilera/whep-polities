# Sudan (Anglo-Egyptian Condominium, to 1934)

## Summary

Anglo-Egyptian Sudan from the Anglo-Egyptian Agreement of 19 January 1899
that established the Condominium (joint British-Egyptian sovereignty,
de-facto British administration) through the administrative consolidation
phase ending around 1934, when Sudan entered its late-colonial political
development phase ([sud-1934-1956](sud-1934-1956.md)). Agricultural statistics
began to be compiled systematically in the early 20th century; pre-1961 input
data has Sudan wheat figures from 1903 onward.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 625, time-step 1914-1934, area 2,579,525 km².
CShapes records four overlapping time-steps for Condominium-era Sudan
(1899-1902, 1902-1910, 1910-1914, 1914-1934) reflecting minor administrative
boundary adjustments. The 1914-1934 polygon is the longest-stable and covers
most of the data window; areas across the four steps cluster around
2.5-2.6 million km² (modern Sudan + South Sudan combined, since partition
didn't happen until 2011).

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 agricultural input data has 98
Sudan rows (wheat and other items) spanning 1903-1933. Previously these
landed as `match_status: none` because no WHEP polity covered pre-1934 Sudan
— the [sud-1934-1956](sud-1934-1956.md) page explicitly named
`SUD-1910-1934` as a predecessor that never existed.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — database truncation. The Mahdist state (1881-1898)
  and earlier Ottoman-Egyptian Sudan are not in WHEP.
- **Successor:** [sud-1934-1956](sud-1934-1956.md) — late-colonial
  Anglo-Egyptian Sudan (advisory councils, path to independence).

## Open questions

### oq-1899-cutoff-vs-1881

Pre-1899 Sudan (Mahdist state, 1881-1898) is outside WHEP's current scope
but was a real and distinct political entity. If future work adds pre-1899
data, a separate MAH-1881-1898 or similar row would cover that.
