# Madagascar (1960-2025)

## Summary

Madagascar (1960-2025) covers independent Madagascar from 26 June 1960 to the present. On that date Madagascar gained independence from France, initially as the Malagasy Republic. The territory is unchanged from the preceding colonial period [biger-1995 §madagascar](../sources/biger-1995.md#madagascar).

## Territorial extent

Madagascar is an island country in the Indian Ocean, east of southeastern Africa. Area 226,658 sq. miles (586,818 sq. km). Capital: Antananarivo. No land boundaries. Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records status=independent from 1960-06-26.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [MDG-1946-1960](mdg-1946-1960.md) -- French colonial administration (overseas territory period, 1946-1960)
- **Successor:** none (ongoing)

## Sourced claims

- "Madagascar became a French protectorate of the French in 1885 and a colony in 1896." It became an independent republic in 1960 (p.368). [biger-1995 §madagascar](../sources/biger-1995.md#madagascar)
- CShapes records independence date as 1960-06-26 with same territorial extent as preceding period. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
