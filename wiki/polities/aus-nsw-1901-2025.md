# New South Wales (state of Australia)

## Summary

New South Wales as a state of the Commonwealth of Australia, from Federation on 1 January 1901 to the present. Before 1901 NSW was a self-governing British colony, already carried by the separate polity AUS-NSW-1800-1900 (span ending exclusive at 1900). At Federation, NSW ceded defence, customs, and other powers to the new Commonwealth government while retaining its own parliament, premier, and territory essentially unchanged from the colonial period. `type: subnational` is used, not `national`, because sovereignty from 1901 onward sits with the Commonwealth of Australia (AUS-1901-2025); this entry exists to carry the state-level reporting territory that Australian and international statistics (censuses, agricultural production, trade-by-state series) tabulate separately from both the national AUS totals and the other states.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Australian and international statistical series that report data at the New South Wales state level for years from 1901 onward -- state-level agricultural production, population, and trade figures that appear in sources such as Mitchell's historical statistics and FAO/IIA yearbooks under labels like "Australia, New South Wales" or "New South Wales" without any existing post-Federation polity to receive them; prior to this entry such rows would have routed to the national AUS-1901-2025 row, which is roughly ten times NSW's own territory and conflates six states' worth of production and population under one figure. The pre-Federation colonial entity (1800-1900) already has its own polity row, AUS-NSW-1800-1900, so this entry's boundary at 1901 is not arbitrary -- it is the Federation date itself, the point at which NSW's political status changed from sovereign colony to constituent state while its territory and administrative apparatus continued unbroken. That NSW is treated as a distinct reporting unit both before and after Federation, rather than folded into a single all-Australia figure, is standard practice in the historical-statistics literature (Mitchell reports Australian agricultural and demographic series by state throughout the twentieth century), which is the confirmation that this is a genuinely distinct, separately-tabulated entity and not a synthetic construct of this pipeline.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached in the GeoPackage for this period. The route determined for this polity is `registered_source_unfetched`: the correct source is `gadm-4.1-adm1` (GADM 4.1 admin-1 boundaries), which does publish Australian state-level features globally, but the copy of that dataset present in this repository is a curated subset of 81 countries that excludes Australia entirely (0 features for AUS on disk). This is an availability gap, not a naming or matching problem -- re-fetching the full global GADM 4.1 admin-1 release would resolve it directly, and vintage risk from using a present-day GADM boundary for the full 1901-2025 span is low, since the state boundary of NSW has been essentially unchanged since Federation (the last significant change was the 1911 excision of the Australian Capital Territory, itself already carried by its own entry AUS-ACT-1911-2025).

**Territory description:** New South Wales occupies the south-eastern part of mainland Australia, bordered by Queensland to the north, South Australia to the west, Victoria to the south, and the Tasman Sea to the east, with the Australian Capital Territory enclaved within it since 1911. Its modern extent, easily located on any current map of Australia, is approximately **800,642 km²** (the commonly cited official state area figure, excluding the ACT enclave), making it slightly smaller than France. This territory has been essentially stable since the ACT excision in 1911 and requires no proxy adjustment for the bulk of this entry's 1901-2025 span; the only boundary event inside the span is that 1911 excision itself, which this entry does not separately model (no sub-period split), leaving a small, known imprecision for 1901-1911 noted below.

## Predecessors and successors

Predecessor: AUS-NSW-1800-1900, the colonial polity representing NSW as a self-governing British colony, with span ending exclusive at 1900 (i.e., through calendar year 1899). This creates a one-year seam at calendar year 1900 in which neither polity's span covers the year -- Federation itself did not occur until 1 January 1901, so the gap reflects the true administrative transition point rather than an artefact to be papered over by re-spanning either polity. No successor: this entry remains current to the present (end_year 2025 per the open-end convention), and NSW continues as a state of Australia with no further change of status.

## Open questions

### oq-1911-act-excision-not-modelled

**The 1911 ACT excision is not modelled as a sub-period boundary change**

New South Wales's territory changed once within this entry's span: in 1911 the Australian Capital Territory was excised from NSW to form the federal territory, which now has its own entry, AUS-ACT-1911-2025. This entry does not split at 1911, so any polygon eventually attached using a present-day GADM NSW boundary will be a very slightly under-sized proxy for 1901-1911 (NSW's territory before the excision included the area that became the ACT, roughly 2,358 km² more than modern NSW). Given NSW's total area of ~800,642 km², this is under 0.3% of its territory and was judged not to warrant a separate pre-1911 polity row, but this should be revisited once a GADM feature is actually attached, since a boundary-vintage flag such as polygon_vintage_drift may be more honest than silently proxying the whole span with the post-1911 shape.
