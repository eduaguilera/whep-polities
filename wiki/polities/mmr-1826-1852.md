# Burma (1826-1852)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Burma (Konbaung dynasty) in the post-First Anglo-Burmese War period, 1826-1852. The First Anglo-Burmese War (1824-1826) ended with the Treaty of Yandabo, by which Burma ceded Arakan, Tenasserim, Assam, and Manipur to Britain. "After three Anglo-Burman wars, Burma became a province of India under the British" [biger-1995 §myanmar](../sources/biger-1995.md#myanmar). The database records approximately 553K km2 for this period, based on the Cliopatria polygon (Burma 1828-1833). The 1852 end date marks the Second Anglo-Burmese War, in which Britain seized Lower Burma (Pegu), causing a ~21% territorial reduction.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Cliopatria (Burma 1828-1833). The territory covered Upper Burma and parts of Lower Burma (excluding the British-controlled coastal strips of Arakan and Tenasserim). The Myanmar-Thailand boundary "follows mountain crests and thalwegs and has been demarcated. It runs 1,119.5 miles (1,800 km)" [biger-1995 §myanmar](../sources/biger-1995.md#myanmar). After 1852, Lower Burma (Pegu) was lost to Britain, reducing the kingdom to Upper Burma only.
