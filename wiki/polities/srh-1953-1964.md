# Southern Rhodesia (within Federation, 1953-1964)

## Summary

This polity covers Southern Rhodesia — the territory of present-day Zimbabwe — during the
period when it was a constituent territory of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland
(1 August 1953 to 21 January 1964). Southern Rhodesia retained its own internal legislature
and administration throughout the federation period; it was not merged into a unitary state.
Data labeled "zimbabwe" (ISO zwe) for 1953–1964 measures Southern Rhodesia alone
(~390,610 km²) and must be matched here, NOT to FRN-1953-1964, which represents the full
federation (~1,260,000 km²: Southern Rhodesia + Northern Rhodesia + Nyasaland). Routing
"zimbabwe"-labeled data to FRN would overstate the territorial unit by approximately 3×.

This granular polity was created to resolve a coverage gap in the ZWE family: ZWE-1900-1953
ends at 1953 and ZWE-1964-1980 starts at 1964, leaving 1953–1964 unrepresented at the
sub-federation level.

## Territorial extent

Southern Rhodesia — modern Zimbabwe — is a landlocked territory bounded by:
- Zambia (Zambezi River) to the north (795 km)
- Mozambique to the east (1,223 km)
- South Africa to the south (225 km)
- Botswana to the west (813 km)

Area: ~390,610 km² (150,873 sq. miles). Capital: Salisbury (now Harare).

The borders of Southern Rhodesia were stable from the 1900 BSAC partition of Northern and
Southern Rhodesia through to independence in 1980. No territorial changes occurred during the
federation period 1953–1964; the CShapes 2.0 polygon for cowcode=552 at vintage 1953 (or
1965) represents this territory accurately. The polygon is shared with ZWE-1900-1953 and
ZWE-1964-1980, both of which use CShapes cowcode=552.

**Polygon note**: CShapes 2.0 does not record cowcode=552 between 1953-08-01 and
1964-01-21 (that gap is covered by the federation polygon at cowcode=550). The polygon
for this polity is sourced from cowcode=552 at the 1953 vintage (last pre-federation
record), which is identical in extent to the pre- and post-federation polygons and
represents Southern Rhodesia's boundaries faithfully throughout the federation period.
Polygon confidence: HIGH — borders were stable and unchanged by federation entry or exit.

**Do NOT fold 'zimbabwe'-labeled data into FRN-1953-1964**: FRN covers the entire
federation (~1,260,000 km²). The ~3× area mismatch makes this routing analytically
incorrect. See [frn-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [ZWE-1900-1953](zwe-1900-1953.md) — Southern Rhodesia colony
  (British South Africa Company / self-governing colony) before federation.
- **Successor:** [ZWE-1964-1980](zwe-1964-1980.md) — Southern Rhodesia / Rhodesia after
  the federation dissolved (UDI 1965).
- **Federation aggregate:** [FRN-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md) — the full Federation of
  Rhodesia and Nyasaland; this polity is the granular Southern-Rhodesia-only component.

## Sourced claims

- "In 1923 Southern Rhodesia became a white-dominated, self-governing colony." The
  self-governing legislature continued to operate throughout the federation period.
  [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)
- Zimbabwe area: 150,873 sq. miles [390,610 sq. km.]. [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=552 (Southern Rhodesia / Zimbabwe) with an end date of
  1953-07-31 before the federation entry and a start date of 1964-01-22 after dissolution;
  the polygon extent is identical at both transitions, confirming stable borders.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (CShapes cowcode=550) covers ~1,260,000 km²,
  more than three times Southern Rhodesia's ~390,610 km². Routing sub-federation data to
  the federation aggregate would overstate the territorial unit by ~3×.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

None identified. The stable CShapes boundary record for cowcode=552 before and after the
federation period confirms that Southern Rhodesia's borders were unchanged by the federation.

## Decisions

- **Polygon reuse (1953 vintage)**: Because CShapes does not record cowcode=552 during
  1953–1964 (it records the federation as cowcode=550 instead), the polygon for this polity
  is taken from cowcode=552 at the 1953 vintage (polygon_feature_year=1953). This is
  appropriate because Southern Rhodesia's borders were stable and unchanged throughout
  the federation period. The polygon_vintage_drift (data year vs. polygon vintage) is at
  most 11 years (1964 data vs. 1953 polygon), which is acceptable given confirmed stable
  borders.
- **Code SRH**: A distinct code (SRH) is used rather than reusing ZWE with a sub-period,
  to make the sub-federation granularity explicit and avoid confusion with the ZWE family
  codes that represent the same territory under different administrative contexts.

## Open questions

### oq-srh-federation-data-scope

Some WHEP data sources for 1953–1964 may report at the federation level (covering all three
territories) rather than Southern Rhodesia alone. Such rows should be matched to
FRN-1953-1964, not this polity. The alias routing for "zimbabwe" (ISO zwe, 1953–1964) →
SRH-1953-1964 is year-ranged and handles the Zimbabwe-only data correctly; federation-level
data with different source labels routes to FRN-1953-1964 independently.
