# United Arab Emirates (1892-2025)

## Summary

Single row covering the southern Persian Gulf sheikhdoms from 1892, when their treaty relationships with Britain (dating back to 1820) were formalised as protectorates, through the Anglo-Ottoman Convention of 1913 — which secured Ottoman recognition of Kuwaiti and Trucial States autonomy — to independence as the United Arab Emirates on 2 December 1971 and on to 2025.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature 696 at its **1913** vintage, carried across the whole 1892-2025 span. CShapes classifies the 1913 feature as a protectorate under British rule. The territory corresponds to the seven emirates that later formed the UAE: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm al-Quwain, Ras al-Khaimah, and Fujairah. Two known limits of holding that one vintage for 133 years: the 1913 boundary change itself (see [oq-1913-boundary-change](#oq-1913-boundary-change)), and the Saudi-Abu Dhabi frontier, unsettled until the 1974 Treaty of Jeddah.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** No predecessor row (the chain begins with the 1892 protectorate treaties).
- **Successor:** No successor row; this row runs to the present.

Splits at 1913 and 1971 were once planned and never applied. 1913 is where the
CShapes feature changes, and 1971 is independence — a change of sovereignty over
the same seven emirates, not of extent. Both are recorded here rather than as
rows (2026-08-17, issue #25).

## Decisions

- **2026-08-17 (issue #25):** the body was written for planned splits at 1913 and
  1971 that were never applied, and asserted `ARE-1892-1913` and `ARE-1971-2025`
  as chain neighbours although neither row exists. Rewritten to describe the
  single 1892-2025 row. No row was created or deleted: creating them is a
  dating-and-scope decision, and nothing in the sources here shows the extent
  changing at either year.

## Open questions

### oq-1913-boundary-change

The 1913 start year corresponds to a CShapes polygon change, likely related to the Anglo-Ottoman Convention of 29 July 1913 which formalized boundaries in the Persian Gulf region. The specific territorial change should be documented.
