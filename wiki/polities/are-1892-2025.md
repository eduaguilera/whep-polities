# UAE (1913-1971)

## Summary

The UAE (1913-1971) covers the Trucial States under British protection from the Anglo-Ottoman Convention of 1913 -- which secured Ottoman recognition of Kuwaiti and Trucial States autonomy -- until independence on 2 December 1971. The Trucial States were a collection of sheikhdoms along the southern Persian Gulf coast that had treaty relationships with Britain dating back to 1820, formalized as protectorates in 1892. This row begins at 1913 due to a CShapes polygon change.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes classifies this as a protectorate under British rule. The territory corresponds to the seven emirates that would later form the UAE: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm al-Quwain, Ras al-Khaimah, and Fujairah.
