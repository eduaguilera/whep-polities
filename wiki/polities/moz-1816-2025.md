# Mozambique

## Summary

Mozambique is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Mozambique from 1816 to the present. The start year 1816 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage for Portuguese East Africa. Mozambique became independent on 25 June 1975.

## Territorial extent

Mozambique is a southern African country (area 308,642 sq. miles; 799,074 sq. km; population 16,600,000 [1994]). Bounded by Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia, Zimbabwe, South Africa, and Swaziland. Capital: Maputo. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- Portugal established trading posts along the coast of Mozambique in the sixteenth century and began exploring inland along the Zambezi River. A dispute arose with Britain over territorial claims in Delagoa Bay, submitted to President Mac-Mahon of France for arbitration. His award (1875) allocated the bay and adjacent land to Portugal (p.391). [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- Three concessionary companies were formed during 1888-1893 to develop resources within Mozambique. The last of the companies' concessions expired in 1941 and Mozambique remained an overseas province or colony of Portugal until its independence on 25 June 1975 (p.392).
