# Egypt (1967-1979)

## Summary

A **13-year row** covering Egypt with Sinai under Israeli occupation
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 1979 split is the
Egypt-Israel Peace Treaty (26 March 1979).

Major events: Sinai occupied by Israel (1967), Yom Kippur War (1973),
Camp David Accords (1978), Egypt-Israel Peace Treaty (1979).

Predecessor: [egy-1925-1967](egy-1925-1967.md). Successor:
[egy-1979-2025](egy-1979-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 per the CSV. Egypt minus the Sinai
  Peninsula (~61,000 km2 occupied by Israel). Effective area reduced
  from ~1M km2 to ~940,000 km2.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [egy-1925-1967](egy-1925-1967.md) -- Six-Day War
  (June 1967).
- **Successor:** [egy-1979-2025](egy-1979-2025.md) -- Egypt-Israel Peace
  Treaty (26 March 1979), Sinai returned.

## Sourced claims

- **1967-06: Sinai occupied.** Israel captured the Sinai Peninsula in
  the Six-Day War. Start-of-row territorial event.
- **1973-10-06: Yom Kippur War.** Egypt and Syria launched a surprise
  attack on Israel. Egypt crossed the Suez Canal, recaptured some
  territory, but the war ended in stalemate.
- **1978-09-17: Camp David Accords.** Framework for peace between Egypt
  and Israel, brokered by US President Carter.
- **1979-03-26: Egypt-Israel Peace Treaty.** Israel agreed to withdraw
  from Sinai in stages. End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
