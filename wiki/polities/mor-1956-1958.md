# Morocco (1956-1958)

## Summary

Independent Morocco between reunification in 1956 (end of French Protectorate 2 March 1956; northern Spanish zone returned 7 April 1956) and the 1 April 1958 Treaty of Angra de Cintra under which Spain ceded the Cape Juby / Tarfaya southern zone. MOR-1956-1958 is the short two-year row that captures Morocco's partial-reunification extent before the Tarfaya transfer completed territorial consolidation.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Morocco over this window lacks Tarfaya (held by Spain until 1958), Ifni (held by Spain until 1969), and Western Sahara (Spanish Sahara until 1975–1979). The plazas de soberanía (Ceuta, Melilla, etc.) remain Spanish to the present.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** MOR-1904-1956 (per CSV) — the pre-1956 French/Spanish Protectorate-era Morocco.
- **Successor:** [mar-1979-2025](mar-1979-2025.md) — Morocco 1979–present. **This successor is wrong**: between 1958 and 1979 Morocco went through Tarfaya acquisition (1958), Ifni acquisition (1969), and the Green March / Western Sahara annexation (1975). MAR-1979-2025 starts at 1979, so MOR-1956-1958 should have an intermediate successor (MOR-1958-1969 / MAR-1975-1979 etc.) that is missing from the CSV.

## Contradictions

**CSV code-prefix ambiguity.** The CSV uses `MOR-` here and `MAR-` for 1979 onward (MAR-1979-2025). Both are ISO-3-letter-style codes but only one is the correct ISO-3166 code for Morocco (MAR). The MOR/MAR split seems to be a WHEP-internal temporal distinction rather than an ISO convention. Flagged below.

**Missing successors.** The MOR-1956-1958 → MAR-1979-2025 direct link skips 1958, 1969, 1975 territorial acquisitions. The intermediate rows (MOR-1958-1969, MOR-1969-1975, MAR-1975-1979 or similar) are missing from the CSV. This is a bigger chain-completeness issue than a single dangling reference.

## Open questions

### oq-mor-mar-codes

Why the CSV uses `MOR-` for 1904–1969 periods and `MAR-` for 1979+ is unclear; both should ideally use the ISO-3166 `MAR` code. A decision log entry is needed to either (a) rename all MOR rows to MAR, or (b) explicitly document the WHEP-internal convention.

### oq-mor-1958-to-1979-gap

MOR-1956-1958 and MAR-1979-2025 leave a 21-year gap in the WHEP Morocco chain covering Tarfaya (1958), Ifni (1969), and Western Sahara (1975). Multiple intermediate rows should be added.
