# Sucre (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Sucre is a department in northern Colombia's Caribbean region, created in 1966 out of territory split from the department of Bolivar. Its capital is Sincelejo. The department sits between the Gulf of Morrosquillo on the Caribbean coast and the lower Cauca-San Jorge river system inland, bordering Cordoba to the south and west, Bolivar to the east, and a short Caribbean coastline to the north. This entry is type subnational because Sucre is a first-order administrative division of Colombia, a sovereign internationally recognized state, and never itself held sovereignty; the polity exists in this table to carry a reporting territory distinct from the national COL row and from its parent Bolivar, not to assert statehood. The 1966 start date is the date of legal creation of the department, not an artefact of when data collection began; this matches the country convention's explicit note that Sucre departs from the department-system floor year 1886 used for departments that existed at that founding.

**Why this entry exists.** Colombian departmental production and trade series carry rows labelled Sucre department spanning roughly 1915 to 2023, on the order of 9000 valued rows across the underlying panel, that were previously routed to the national COL polity because no existing table row represented Sucre department specifically, and the routing pass found zero name candidates matching Sucre among current polities. Routing all of this data to COL is wrong for any year after 1966, since Colombia's national territory is roughly 45 times Sucre's area, so a national-level row cannot stand in for a single department reporting series without erasing the subnational signal the source specifically collected. Sucre's existence as a distinct administrative and statistical unit from 1966 onward is documented in Colombia's own departmental creation history, Decree-Law 1541 of 1966 formalized by Congress in 1967, which is why this entry's start year is fixed at 1966 rather than an extract defined date. Only the post-1966 portion of the data is routed here; the pre-1966 portion predates the department's existence and is explicitly left unrouted, since it belongs to the pre-split Bolivar department territory for which no candidate polity was supplied to this pipeline run.

## Sourced claims

- Sucre department was created by Decree-Law 1541 of 1966, splitting it off from the department of Bolivar, later ratified by the Colombian Congress; this is the basis for start_year 1966 rather than the 1886 departmental system floor.
- Colombia's departmental-level statistical series report a Sucre unit continuously from 1915, roughly 51 years before the department legally existed, indicating the pre-1966 portion of that series describes the same geographic area while it was still administered as part of Bolivar department.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1966-sucre-territory-unrouted

**Pre-1966 Sucre area data from 1915 to 1965 has nowhere to go**

The same panel that motivates this entry includes data for 1915 to 1965, before Sucre department existed, which this decision explicitly declines to route here. That data describes the same geographic area while it was part of Bolivar department, but no Bolivar department polity currently exists in this table, zero name candidates were found, so those years of the series remain unmatched by this decision. Resolving this requires either creating a COL-BOLIVAR polity with an end year around 1966, or extending this entry's predecessor chain once such a polity exists. Until then, the pre-1966 rows will fall back to the national COL row, which is territorially wrong by the same order of magnitude discussed above.

### oq-gadm-col-fetch-needed

**GADM adm1 has not been fetched for Colombia, so no polygon feature ID exists yet**

gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered and does publish Colombia's department-level boundaries globally, but the locally fetched copy in this repository is restricted to an 81-country subset that excludes COL entirely. This entry cannot receive polygon_status assigned or proxy until either the global GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer is fetched and COL's features extracted, or a Colombia-specific source is registered and fetched instead. Someone should confirm the licence terms for GADM as used in this repo's sources.yaml before fetching, since the routing decision explicitly flagged licence_known as unverified.

### oq-boundary-vintage-since-1966

**Whether Sucre's boundaries have shifted since 1966 is unverified**

A present-day GADM feature, once fetched, would be used to represent the department for the entire 1966-2025 span, but Colombian departmental boundaries are not static: municipalities have occasionally been reassigned between neighbouring departments, with disputes along the Sucre-Bolivar and Sucre-Cordoba borders documented in Colombian administrative history. Whether any such reassignment materially changed Sucre's area or shape since 1966 has not been checked here and should be verified before treating a present-day GADM polygon as a good proxy for the department's earlier extent, particularly for the 1966-1980 portion of the span.
