# Djibouti

## Summary

Djibouti is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Djibouti from 1977 to the present. The Republic of Djibouti gained independence from France on 26 June 1977, becoming the last country in continental Africa to achieve independence.

## Territorial extent

Djibouti (area 8,958 sq. miles; 23,192 sq. km; population 410,000 [1994]) is bounded by Eritrea (68 miles; 110 km), the Gulf of Aden and Somalia (36 miles; 58 km), and Ethiopia (217 miles; 349 km). Capital: Djibouti. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Colonization efforts by the French only began in 1862, continuing until 1900" (p.191). France signed commercial treaties with local chiefs of the northeast African coast in 1862, and a French protectorate was established on the African coast of the Gulf of Aden in 1884-1885, named French Somaliland in 1896. [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
- "In 1977 it became the last country in continental Africa to achieve independence" (p.191). It was renamed from the French Territory of the Afars and Issas (since 5 July 1967) to the Republic of Djibouti. [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
- The boundary between Djibouti, Ethiopia, and Somalia tripoint is on Madaha Djalelo hill (1,614 feet; 492 m) at approximately 10°59'55"N and 42°58'05"E (p.192). [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
