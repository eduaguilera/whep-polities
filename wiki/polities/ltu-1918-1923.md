# Lithuania (interwar republic, 1918-1923)

## Summary

The Republic of Lithuania from the declaration of 16 February 1918 to the Klaipeda Revolt of January
1923, which brought the Klaipeda (Memel) Territory into the state [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §lithuania](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#lithuania). Created on
2026-10-07 by splitting the old `LTU-1918-1940` row at 1923 (issue 686); the later years are
[ltu-1923-1940](ltu-1923-1940.md). Independence was restored on 16 February 1918
[biger-1995 §latvia](../sources/biger-1995.md#latvia).

**Why this entry exists.** The pre-1961 input data reports Lithuania from 1919, and the old row
measured 1919-1922 against a polygon that included the Klaipeda Territory, which was German until
January 1920 and under Allied (French) administration until 1923. Juan's series show the step when it
was added: cattle 1,021,000 head in 1922 and 1,285,000 in 1923.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** proxy. Constructed (`build_ltu_1918_1923` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): CShapes 2.0 gwcode 368's 1922-1939 step **minus the Klaipeda
Territory**. CShapes and CShapes-Europe both draw the territory inside Lithuania from 1918 and inside no
German step, so neither can supply its outline. Cliopatria is the only fetched source that draws it: its
"Kingdom of Lithuania" at 1924 minus the same polity at 1920 is the territory
[cliopatria-v0.1.3](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md). Cliopatria's outline stops short of the Neman,
where CShapes runs the border, so it is swept south by up to 0.4 degrees to fill the strip; south of
the Neman between 21.2 E and 22.6 E lies East Prussia, which is not in the CShapes step, so the sweep
cannot take Lithuanian land there. A 3 km² piece left at the tip of the Curonian Spit belongs to the
territory and is dropped.

**km² reasoning (Mollweide).** The result measures 53,511 km². Lithuania with the territory is
55,725 km² in the same CShapes step, and the territory is stated as 2,848 km² including about 412 km²
of the Curonian Lagoon [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §lithuania](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#lithuania), which would give about 52,900 km². The removed area is
2,211 km², about 640 km² short, because Cliopatria's northern edge of the territory runs south of the
treaty line. The polygon therefore overstates this row by about 1.2%. That is why the status is
`proxy`, not `assigned`.

**What is not drawn.** The Vilnius region, held under the Soviet-Lithuanian treaty of 12 July 1920 and
seized by Poland in October 1920. CShapes' 1918-1920 step (83,688 km²) draws it; this row uses the
post-1920 extent for 1918-1920 as well, as the old row did.
