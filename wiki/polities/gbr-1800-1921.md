# United Kingdom (to 1921)

## Summary

This row tracks the United Kingdom as a territorial-economic unit
from **1800** (the WHEP database floor) to **1921** (Anglo-Irish
Treaty) [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Unlike France and the German/Austrian/Russian
empires, the UK's metropolitan territory was remarkably stable
during this 122-year window — the only significant metropolitan
territorial change is the 1921 loss of Ireland itself, which is
the CSV's end-of-row event.

- **1800 start** is the database floor. At 1800 the entity was
  still the Kingdom of Great Britain; it became the "United
  Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland" on **1 January 1801**
  via the Act of Union [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1801-union](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1801-union).
  The 1-year label anachronism (1800 vs 1801) is minor but
  comparable to the AUH 1800–1804 anachronism.
- **1921 end** is a real territorial event: the Anglo-Irish Treaty
  was signed on **6 December 1921** at 10 Downing Street
  [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1921-treaty](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1921-treaty). CShapes records the
  polygon split at exactly 1921-12-06 [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The
  Irish Free State formally came into existence on 6 December
  1922 [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1922-free-state](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1922-free-state). The CSV
  uses 1921 (treaty signing), not 1922 (formal establishment).

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [gbr-1921-2025](gbr-1921-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0 + CShapes-Europe` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records the UK (`cowcode=200`) from
  1886-01-01 to 1921-12-05 with area ~42.95 sq degrees
  (including all of Ireland). After 1922-12-06 the area drops
  to ~33.59 sq degrees (Ireland leaves) [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Transitional period 1921–1922.** CShapes has an intermediate
  row from 1921-12-06 to 1922-12-05 with the same 42.95 sq
  degree area — the UK still included all of Ireland on the map
  during the treaty ratification period. The area drop to 33.59
  happens at 1922-12-06 (one year after the treaty, when the
  Irish Free State constitution took effect).
- **Pre-1886 extent.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The
  CSV notes "CShapes-Europe" as a supplementary polygon source
  for the pre-1886 window [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **Metropolitan stability.** Unlike France (which gained
  Nice/Savoy in 1860 and lost Alsace-Lorraine in 1871), the UK's
  metropolitan territory was essentially unchanged from 1801
  to 1921. The main expansion was colonial (India, Africa,
  Southeast Asia, Oceania) — all tracked as separate WHEP rows.

- **Predecessor:** NA — the WHEP database floor is 1800. The

## Sourced claims

- **1801-01-01: Act of Union with Ireland.** "On 1 January 1801,
  the Great Britain and Ireland joined to form the United Kingdom
  of Great Britain and Ireland"
  [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1801-union](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1801-union). This happened within
  the first year of the WHEP row — the row's 1800 start precedes
  the union by 1 year.
- **1921-12-06: Anglo-Irish Treaty signed** at 10 Downing Street.
  Ratified by Dáil Éireann on 7 January 1922 by a vote of 64 to
  57 [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1921-treaty](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1921-treaty). Created the Irish
  Free State from 26 of Ireland's 32 counties. This is the
  CSV's end-of-row event.
- **1922-12-06: Irish Free State formally established** when the
  constitution became law via royal proclamation
  [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1922-free-state](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1922-free-state). Northern Ireland
  exercised its treaty option to opt out and remain in the UK.
- **1927: Name change.** "The United Kingdom changed its formal
  title to the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
  Ireland" [wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1927-name](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1927-name). Falls in the
  successor row, not this one.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists the UK (`UKG`,
  `ccode=200`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01
  to 2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). No break
  at 1921 or any other date. COW does not register the Irish
  partition as a state-system event for the UK (though Ireland
  enters COW separately as `IRE`, `ccode=205` at a later date).
- **CShapes polygon change at 1921/1922.** CShapes records the
  UK polygon at 42.95 sq degrees through 1922-12-05, then drops
  to 33.59 sq degrees at 1922-12-06 [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The
  ~9.36 sq degree drop represents Ireland leaving.

### oq-1921-vs-1922-end-date

The CSV ends this row at 1921 (Anglo-Irish Treaty signed
6 December 1921). But the Irish Free State was not formally
established until 6 December 1922. CShapes uses 1921-12-06
as the first split but retains the pre-partition polygon until
1922-12-06. The CSV's 1921 date is consistent with CShapes
but ambiguous: was the territorial change at treaty signing
(1921) or at formal constitutional establishment (1922)?
Compare with the AUH end-date question
([oq-1918-vs-1919-end-date](auh-1908-1918.md#oq-1918-vs-1919-end-date) on auh-1908-1918).

### oq-colonial-empire-scope

The UK's colonial empire (India, Africa, Southeast Asia,
Oceania) is tracked as separate WHEP rows. This page focuses
on metropolitan UK. However, the question of whether
metropolitan trade statistics include colonial trade needs
clarification for WHEP purposes. The FT data sources may
aggregate "United Kingdom" trade in ways that include colonial
territories. This is a data-scope question, not a territorial
one.

### oq-heligoland-1890

Heligoland was ceded by Britain to Germany in 1890 via the
Heligoland-Zanzibar Treaty. This is a minor metropolitan
territorial change (tiny island) but it is a real territorial
event within the row. Not yet sourced. A future Biger ingest
or a specific Wikipedia fetch would resolve it.
