# Argentina

## Summary

This row tracks Argentina from **1902** to **2025** — modern Argentina
with borders established by the 1902 British arbitral award and the
simultaneous General Arbitration Treaty with Chile. COW: ARG, ccode=160.

Replaces the post-1902 content of the former ARG-1800-2025 collapsed
row. Polygon binding unchanged: CShapes 2.0 gwcode=160 vintage-1902.

Split from ARG-1800-2025 on 2026-07-01 to restore the
autonomous-34-south-america three-period design.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `gwcode=160`, vintage **1902**.
  Unchanged from former ARG-1800-2025 polygon binding. Zero vintage
  drift at row opening.
- **Post-1902 minor adjustments.** Two border changes occurred after
  1902 but neither exceeds the polygon-split threshold:
  - **1984 Beagle Treaty** (Beagle Channel islands): a small strip of
    Tierra del Fuego / Beagle Channel islands transferred to Chile.
    Area change < 0.02% of total Argentine territory.
  - **1994 Laguna del Desierto** (Patagonian arbitral award): ~490 km²
    awarded to Argentina by IACHR arbitral tribunal, 21 October 1994.
    Area change < 0.02% of total Argentine territory (~2,780,000 km²).
  Both adjustments are documented here; neither warrants a further row
  split given their negligible magnitude.
- **COW.** ARG, ccode=160
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Sourced claims

- **Area: 1,068,298 sq mi (2,766,890 sq km).** Bounded by Bolivia
  (517 mi), Paraguay (1,165 mi), Brazil (761 mi), Uruguay (360 mi),
  Chile (3,201 mi), and the Atlantic Ocean. Capital: Buenos Aires
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
- **1816: Independence.** "Argentina gained independence in 1816"
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
- **Pre-1930 economic leadership.** "By 1930 it led the continent
  in gross national product per capita"
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
- **Post-1930 instability.** "sporadic and military intervention and
  dictatorship have been frequent"
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
  Military regime 1976–1983.
- **1983: Democracy restored.** "The restoration of democracy in
  1983 has paved the way for changes in civilian political
  organizations"
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
- **Argentina–Chile boundary.** "the longest South American boundary"
  at 3,201 mi (5,150 km). Last dispute (Laguna del Desierto) settled
  by arbitral tribunal 21 October 1994
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
- **Argentina–Uruguay boundary.** Peace convention of 27 August 1828
  placed boundary in the Uruguay River
  [biger-1995 §argentina](../sources/biger-1995.md#argentina).
