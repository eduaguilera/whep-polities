# Liberia

## Summary

Liberia is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the Republic of Liberia from 1847 to the present. Liberia declared independence on 26 July 1847, making it one of the oldest independent states in Africa.

## Territorial extent

Liberia is a West African country bounded by Sierra Leone, Guinea, and Ivory Coast, with a coastline on the Atlantic Ocean. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The Sierra Leone-Liberia boundary follows colonial-era Anglo-American demarcations. The boundary between Sierra Leone and Liberia was established through a series of agreements in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. [biger-1995 §sierra-leone](../sources/biger-1995.md#sierra-leone)
- The Ghana-Ivory Coast boundary context shows that the expansion of Britain and France in West Africa involved bilateral arrangements for allocating spheres of influence and demarcating common boundaries between 1889 and 1905 (p.243), which also affected Liberia's borders with its neighbors.
