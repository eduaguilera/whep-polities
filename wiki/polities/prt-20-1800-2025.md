# Azores

## Summary

The Azores, "an archipelago of Portugal, in the Atlantic Ocean, about 1,400 km (870 miles) west of
the Portuguese mainland". "The nine islands that compose the archipelago occupy a surface area of 2,346
km2". With [prt-30-1800-2025](prt-30-1800-2025.md) it makes up
[prt-azm-1800-2025](prt-azm-1800-2025.md).
