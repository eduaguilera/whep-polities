# Jordan

Independent Hashemite Kingdom from 1946-05-25 to present. CShapes area
~8.45 sq deg (unchanged from mandate period). Jordan is bounded by Syria
(233 mi; 375 km), Iraq (111 mi; 179 km), Saudi Arabia (461 mi; 742 km),
and Israel (165 mi; 266 km), with a small outlet to the Red Sea at Aqaba.
