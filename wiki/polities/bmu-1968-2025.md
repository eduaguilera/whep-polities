# Bermuda (self-governing British Overseas Territory)

## Summary

This row continues the Bermuda chain from the 1968 constitution (internal
self-government) to the 2025 database ceiling. The existing
BMU-1684-1968 row (British Crown Colony of Bermuda) ends in 1968, which
left FAOSTAT-era data from 1969 onward without a covering period; this
row closes that gap. The territorial extent is identical to the
predecessor: the ~54 km2 (GADM polygon 68 km2 including reef islets)
North Atlantic archipelago has no boundary history.

## Territorial extent

The Bermuda islands, unchanged from BMU-1684-1968.

- **Area:** **54 km²**, Bermuda's land area, which is also what its predecessor
  [bmu-1684-1968](bmu-1684-1968.md) declares for the same polygon.
- **Polygon status `proxy`, corrected 2026-08-25 (issue 570).** This page declared
  `polygon_area_km2: 68` with `polygon_status: assigned`, against a shipped polygon measuring
  **67.8 km²** — a rounded readout of its own geometry, which is the circularity issue 195 is about,
  and it escaped both checks that would normally catch it: check A skips any row whose claimed and
  measured areas are both under 200 km², and the self-referential arm A2 uses a 0.1% threshold that
  68-versus-67.8 (0.3%) just clears.
- **Why the polygon is 26% larger than the territory.** The outer coastline encloses Great Sound,
  Harrington Sound and Castle Harbour, so a ring drawn round the archipelago measures that water as
  well as the land. The polygon is therefore not exactly this territory, which is what `proxy` says
  and `assigned` did not — and it is why the predecessor row was already `proxy`. Two rows sharing one
  polygon now describe it the same way.
