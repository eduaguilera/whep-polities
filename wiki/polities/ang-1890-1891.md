# Angola (1890-1891)

## Summary

Portuguese West Africa (Angola) in a transitional territorial configuration from 20 August 1890 to 10 June 1891. The start date corresponds to a CShapes boundary change establishing an area of 86.19 sq deg (geographic coordinates), and the end date to the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 11 June 1891, which delimited the Angola-Rhodesia frontier and expanded the area to 111.93 sq deg. This is a WHEP-distinct row because both boundary events represent genuine territorial changes confirmed by CShapes area differences. "The Angolans achieved their independence from the Portuguese on 11 November 1975" [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). The CSV records this polity at the CShapes dependency level. Angola includes the Cabinda exclave in the north, separated from the main territory by a strip of Zairean territory [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). Bounded by Congo/Cabinda (125 mi; 201 km), Zaire (1,560 mi; 2,511 km), Zambia (690 mi; 1,110 km), Namibia (855 mi; 1,376 km), and the Atlantic Ocean [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). The 1890-1891 configuration represents a transitional extent before the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty expanded Portuguese control inland.
