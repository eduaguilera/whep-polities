# Saint Pierre and Miquelon

## Summary

Saint Pierre and Miquelon is a French overseas collectivity consisting of two small archipelagos (~242 km²) located off the south coast of Newfoundland, Canada. France has held the territory continuously since 1816, when it was restored by the Treaty of Paris (1814) and the Congress of Vienna (1815) after British occupation during the Napoleonic Wars. There has been no sovereignty break since 1816. The territory was administered as a French colony until 1976, then became a territorial collectivity and subsequently a collectivité d'outre-mer. Throughout WHEP's relevant period, the islands remained French-administered with a small resident population engaged primarily in fishing. This row was created to route 8 rows of fao1952 agricultural and land-use data labelled 'St Pierre and Miquelon' (years 1937–1951) to a dedicated polity. Routing to France (FRA-1919-2025) would be wrong because FRA's CShapes polygon covers metropolitan France in Europe; routing to RNAM-1850-2021 (North America Other aggregate) would dissolve the SPM-specific data into a residual aggregate.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** GADM 4.1 ADM0 (SPM). The two island groups (Île Saint-Pierre, Miquelon-Langlade archipelago) have had fixed, well-defined maritime/island boundaries since French reoccupation in 1816. Modern GADM boundaries are a faithful proxy for the entire 1816–2025 span.
- **Polygon method:** GADM 4.1 ADM0 direct assignment. No historical GIS source (CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, Paine-2024) covers this small French island territory.
- **Polygon status:** proxy. Modern GADM boundaries used as a period proxy; territory has been unchanged since 1816 given the stable, well-defined small-island geography.
- **Area:** approximately 242 km² (Île Saint-Pierre ~26 km², Miquelon-Langlade ~216 km²).
- **Confidence: high.** Island geography makes modern polygons a faithful proxy for historical extent. No territorial changes or annexations since 1816 restitution.

## Decisions

- **Code SPM-1816-2025.** Uses ISO3 code SPM (standard ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 for Saint Pierre and Miquelon). Start year 1816 reflects the formal restoration of French sovereignty; end year 2025 is WHEP's open temporal boundary.
- **Type: colonial.** The territory was a French colony through 1976 (covering the entire fao1952 data period 1937–1951) and subsequently an overseas collectivity. 'Colonial' is the appropriate WHEP type for the colonial era; the row spans the full continuous French administration from 1816.
- **Polygon: GADM 4.1 proxy.** No historical GIS source covers these small North Atlantic islands. GADM 4.1 modern island polygons are the appropriate proxy. Island geography ensures modern borders are identical to historical borders throughout the 1816–2025 span.
- **No routing to FRA or RNAM.** France's CShapes polygon covers metropolitan France in Europe (~544,000 km²); routing SPM data there would overstate territory ~2250x. RNAM is a residual aggregate, not a polity polygon; routing SPM-specific data there loses the geographic specificity of the source data.
