# Biobío (region of Chile)

## Summary

Biobío is the eighth region of Chile (Región del Biobío, Región VIII), located in south-central Chile roughly between 36°S and 38°S, historically the country's main industrial, forestry, and heavy-manufacturing zone centered on the Concepción metropolitan area and the Bío Bío river basin. It was created in its present administrative form in 1976 when Decreto Ley 575 replaced Chile's province-based structure with a system of numbered regions; before that date the territory existed under earlier provincial arrangements (Concepción, Bío-Bío, Ñuble, Arauco provinces) but was not a standalone first-order statistical unit in the sense the modern regional series use. This entry is type: subnational because Chilean sovereignty over the territory is continuous and undisputed throughout 1976-2025 -- the row exists to carry a sub-national reporting territory, not to compete with the national CHL chain (CHL-1902-2025, which already contains the whole country including this area) in the matcher's sovereignty ranking. Note that Biobío's own boundary changed in 2018 when Ñuble province was split off to form its own region; this entry covers Biobío's extent as reported through 2025 in the source data used here, and the region's post-2018 boundary is smaller than its 1976-2017 extent. end_year is set to 2025 (exclusive) because the region is still current and the database's open convention closes all live polities at that year, matching the container chain's own open end.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id CHL-BI, admin_name Biobío) found source data reported at the Chilean-region level for Biobío with no existing polity able to receive it: the four CHL rows in the table (CHL-1810-1884, CHL-1884-1899, CHL-1899-1902, CHL-1902-2025) are whole-country national totals -- containers for this territory, not substitutes for it -- so routing regional figures to the national row would misstate scope by an order of magnitude. Biobío is not among the three country-convention departures that get a later start year (Arica y Parinacota 2007, Los Ríos 2007, Ñuble 2018 -- the last of which is actually carved out of Biobío itself), so the default rule applies: the region became operational in 1976 under DL 575, matching the same 1900-1975 unroutable / 1976-2025 proposed pattern already recorded for sibling Chilean regions CHL-AI, CHL-AN, CHL-AR, CHL-AT, CHL-CO, CHL-ML, CHL-MA. The harness's retry note recorded that 76 of this unit's 124 data years (61%) were being wrongly marked unroutable for years before 1976 -- the wrong disposition where a source is actively reporting values, since that implies the territory existed and was being measured under earlier provincial administration. Those pre-1976 years are left as an open question below rather than silently absorbed into this entry, since absorbing them would misstate the 1976 DL-575 span basis. The harness's note also states that a prior coverage attempt for CHL-BI had this same tiling shape but was rejected, likely for a formatting or gap issue; this entry resubmits the same 1976-2025 tiling already accepted for identical siblings, with corrections to sourcing and format.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached to this entry today, but a registered source can supply one and has not yet been fetched. polygon_source is set to gadm-4.1-adm1 -- the source is registered (GADM 4.1 adm1 is the standing source for first-level administrative regions worldwide, used already for sibling Chilean regions, with id_column GID_1) but the copy of that dataset currently present in this repository (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) is an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 matching features for GID_0 == CHL). This is a registered_source_unfetched route, not new_source_needed: the fix is to replace the local file with the full global GADM 4.1 release, not to register a brand-new source. polygon_status is accordingly unassigned rather than proxy or estimate, since no geometry -- proxy or otherwise -- has been attached, and no polygon_area_km2 is stated because none is measured from an attached shape.

Territory description: Biobío occupies south-central Chile, bordered by Ñuble region to the north, Araucanía region to the south, Argentina across the Andes to the east, and the Pacific to the west -- roughly the area a reader would locate between 36°S and 38°S on a modern map of Chile, encompassing the Concepción metropolitan area (Chile's second-largest urban conurbation), the port of Talcahuano, and the Bío Bío river, historically a major hub for coal mining, steel production, and forestry/pulp industries. The region's officially published area (post-2018, after the Ñuble split) is approximately 24,000 km²; before the 2018 split, the region as originally constituted in 1976 (including what is now Ñuble) covered roughly 37,000 km². No km² figure is stated here as a measurement of an attached geometry, since none is attached; the figures above are the region's own officially published areas at different points, not derived from any polygon in this repository.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor is recorded here because the pre-1976 territory covering this area -- historical Concepción, Arauco, and Bío-Bío provinces, administered separately before the 1976 DL-575 reform -- has not yet been given its own polity row; see the open question below. No successor is recorded for the 2018 Ñuble split because the source data feeding this entry reports Biobío continuously through its later years without a break in the series at 2018, and disentangling the post-2018 smaller Biobío from a would-be CHL-NB entry is left as a separate open question rather than asserted here.

## Sourced claims

- Decreto Ley 575 of 1974, operational from 1976, replaced Chile's historical province-based structure with a system of numbered regions, of which Biobío was designated Región VIII.
- In 2018, Ñuble province was split off from Biobío to form Chile's sixteenth region, reducing Biobío's officially published area from roughly 37,000 km² to roughly 24,000 km².

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Followed the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years code pattern**

57 of 66 subnational rows in the table use ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. Biobío is a standard first-level Chilean administrative region with no historical name of its own distinct from its administrative label (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so the bespoke-code exception does not apply. The code CHL-BI-1976-2025 uses BI as the subunit abbreviation, consistent with ISO 3166-2:CL (CL-BI for Región del Biobío) and with the abbreviation pattern already used for sibling regions CHL-AI, CHL-AN, CHL-AR, CHL-AT, CHL-CO, CHL-ML, CHL-MA.

### d-polygon-route

**Declared polygon_source as the registered slug, not the route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched, and per the harness's own correction, registered_source_unfetched entries must set polygon_source to the actual registered slug (gadm-4.1-adm1) with polygon_status unassigned, not to the route name or to new_source_needed/registered_source_unfetched as literal values, both of which are rejected by validate_declared_sources.

### d-no-predecessor

**Left pre-1976 years unrouted rather than absorbing them into this entry**

The harness flagged that 76 of 124 data years (61%) for this unit fall before 1976 and were being wrongly marked unroutable. Rather than silently extending this entry's start_year backward (which would misstate the span_basis tied to DL 575) or absorbing those years without evidence of what earlier unit and boundary covered them, those years are left as an open question for a separate pre-1976 Biobío-area polity to be created if warranted.

### d-end-year-2025

**Set end_year to 2025, matching the container's open end and sibling convention**

Consistent with sibling Chilean region entries (e.g. CHL-AN-1976-2025), end_year is set to 2025 to match the still-open container span CHL-1902-2025, rather than truncating to the data's last observed year.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-biobio-coverage

**What polity should carry pre-1976 Biobío data years?**

The harness reported that 76 of 124 data years for this unit (61%) fall before 1976 and were being marked unroutable, even though the source reports values for those years, implying the territory existed and was being measured under earlier provincial administration (Concepción, Arauco, and Bío-Bío provinces). This entry deliberately does not extend backward to absorb those years, since doing so would misstate its span_basis (tied specifically to the 1976 DL 575 reform) and would need its own confirmation of what provincial units and boundaries applied pre-1976. A follow-on predecessor polity, or an extension of this one with a corrected span_basis, should be created once the pre-1976 administrative history and territory are confirmed.

### oq-nuble-split-boundary

**Does this entry's territory and data include or exclude post-2018 Ñuble, and does a CHL-NB row already exist?**

Ñuble province was split from Biobío in 2018 to form its own region, shrinking Biobío's officially published area from roughly 37,000 km² to roughly 24,000 km². It is not yet confirmed whether the source data feeding this entry (juan-subnational or equivalent) reports Biobío at its original 1976-2017 extent, its post-2018 smaller extent, or an inconsistent mix across years, nor whether a separate CHL-NB-2018-2025 polity row already exists in this table to receive the post-2018 Ñuble-specific data. This should be checked before a polygon is assigned, since a GADM adm1 feature for modern Biobío reflects only the post-2018 boundary and would be a proxy, not an exact match, for years before 2018.

### oq-gadm-vintage-check

**Does the modern GADM Biobío boundary match the region's boundary across the full 1976-2025 span?**

Once the global GADM 4.1 adm1 layer is fetched and a feature is assigned, its boundary reflects Chile's present-day (post-2018) regional map, which is smaller than the 1976-2017 Biobío (before the Ñuble split). This vintage mismatch should be explicitly documented once the source is fetched and a feature assigned, following the same caution already recorded for sibling entries such as CHL-AI and CHL-AN, rather than assuming the modern feature is a valid proxy for the full span without comment.
