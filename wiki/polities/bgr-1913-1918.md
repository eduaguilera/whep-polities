# Bulgaria (1913-1918)

## Summary

This row tracks Bulgaria for **6 years**, from the Second Balkan War
to the end of WWI
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Balkan Wars Bulgaria. Lost Southern Dobruja to Romania, most of
Macedonia to Serbia and Greece at the Treaty of Bucharest (10 August
1913). Joined the Central Powers in WWI (October 1915). Defeated,
armistice 29 September 1918.

Predecessor: [bgr-1878-1913](bgr-1878-1913.md) -- Balkan Wars
territorial losses.
Successor: [bgr-1918-1919](bgr-1918-1919.md) -- post-WWI Bulgaria.
