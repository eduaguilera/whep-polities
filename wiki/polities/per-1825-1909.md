## Summary

[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Peruvian
independence was effectively secured at the Battle of Ayacucho
(9 December 1824). The major territorial loss within this row is
the War of the Pacific (1879-83), in which Peru lost the Tarapaca
region to Chile.
