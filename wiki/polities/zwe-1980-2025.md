# Zimbabwe (1980-2025)

## Summary

Zimbabwe (1980-2025) covers independent Zimbabwe from April 1980 to the present. After ZAPU and ZANU won free elections in 1980, the Smith regime ended and Zimbabwe gained internationally recognized independence under black majority rule [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe). A merger agreement between ZAPU and ZANU was signed in December 1987 and ratified in 1988 [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe). The territory is unchanged from the preceding Rhodesian period.

## Territorial extent

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa bounded by Zambia (495 miles), Mozambique (760 miles), South Africa (140 miles), and Botswana (505 miles). Area 150,873 sq. miles (390,610 sq. km). Capital: Harare. Polygon source is CShapes 2.0, continuous from the preceding CShapes record (status=independent, area ~33.46).

## Open questions

### oq-zwe-1980-exact-date

The exact date of Zimbabwe's independence in April 1980 needs confirmation. CShapes uses 1965-11-11 (UDI) as the start of the "independent" record and does not split at 1980. The commonly cited date is 18 April 1980 (Lancaster House Agreement implementation).
