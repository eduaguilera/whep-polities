# Aruba

## Summary

An island 29 km off the Venezuelan coast, Dutch since 1636 and a **constituent country of the
Kingdom of the Netherlands** since 1986. Before that it was administered within the Netherlands
Antilles, and before 1954 within the colony of Curaçao and Dependencies.

## Why this entry exists

**WHEP publishes land-use hectares for Aruba and this database had no row to carry them**
(issue 185). Measured on whep `main`:

```
area 22   iso3 ABW   244 rows   1850-2022   source LUH2_grassland   unit ha
```

Aruba is not a FAOSTAT reporter. It enters WHEP through the **LUH2 land-use raster**, which
covers it as territory whether or not anyone files agricultural returns — so the rows are not a
join artefact, they are real land. They were invisible while WHEP folded its FABIO
Rest-of-World bucket; `eduaguilera/whep#628` stopped folding it, and each reporting area now
carries its own identity. Aruba had none.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = ABW`.

```
GADM 4.1 raw           181.94 km²
as published           181.79 km²
official land area     180 km²
ratio (published/official)  1.010x
```

A **1.1% agreement**, which is why `polygon_status` is `assigned` rather than `estimate`. That
is unusually good for a small territory: the same comparison across this database's
micro-territories runs from 42% under to 39% over (`MDV` 0.58x, `TUV` 1.39x, `MHL` 1.39x,
`TKL` 1.33x), documented on [tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md). Aruba is a single compact island
with no lagoon or reef fringe to disagree about, and at 180 km² it sits above the few-tens-of-km²
regime where GADM becomes unpredictable.

An area is **declared** rather than left null, because `validate_polygons`' area comparison is
opt-in via that field and a null leaves the row unchecked (issue 59).

## Span

`1800-2025`, a 226-year single-vintage row — normally the shape issue 22 warns about. **It is
safe here for a reason that does not generalise:** Aruba is one island whose coastline has not
moved. There is no boundary to have shifted. Compare [fro-1800-2025](fro-1800-2025.md),
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md) and `NIU-1800-2025`, which use the same span and the same
justification.

The administrative history — colony of Curaçao and Dependencies, Netherlands Antilles from 1954,
separate constituent country from 1986 — is a sequence of **sovereignty and administration**
changes, not territorial ones. This database has no field for sovereignty, which is the gap that
had `GRL-1800-2025` and `ISL-1800-2025` recording Denmark under `successor:` until PR 135. So it
is recorded here in prose and deliberately not forced into a chain field.

## No double claim with the Netherlands Antilles, and this was measured

Aruba **was** part of the Netherlands Antilles from 1954 to 1986, so a row spanning 1800-2025
looks like it must overlap the Antilles rows. It does not:

```
ABW ∩ ANT-1816-1961 = 0.0000 km²
ABW ∩ ANT-1961-2010 = 0.0000 km²
```

Both ANT rows bind the same `reporting-areas` feature, whose recipe in
`scripts/sources/reporting-areas/build.py` is `components: ["BES", "CUW", "SXM"]` — Bonaire/Sint
Eustatius/Saba, Curaçao and Sint Maarten. **Aruba is not one of the components.** So the
Antilles polygon already excludes it, and this row fills territory that was previously claimed
by nothing rather than duplicating territory claimed by something.

That the exclusion is *correct for 1954-1986* is a separate question this row does not settle:
during those years the Antilles genuinely included Aruba, and the polygon does not. The gap is
recorded as an open question below rather than fixed here, because changing the Antilles recipe
would move `ANT-1961-2010`'s published area and needs its own change.

## Predecessors and successors

Both empty, and deliberately — the same reasoning as [tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md). Aruba's
predecessors in the administrative sense are the colony of Curaçao and Dependencies and the
Netherlands Antilles, neither of which is a *territorial* predecessor of this island: they
governed it, they did not precede it. Putting either in `predecessor:` would assert a chronology
that did not happen, which is the error PR 135 removed from Greenland and Iceland.

## Contradictions

None identified. The GADM-versus-official difference is 1.1% and is discussed above.

## Open questions

### oq-abw-antilles-membership-1954-1986

`ANT-1961-2010`'s polygon excludes Aruba for its whole span, but Aruba was part of the
Netherlands Antilles until 1986. So for 1961-1986 the Antilles row understates its territory by
this island's 180 km², and any Antilles figure allocated by area is spread over 795 km² instead
of 975. Fixing it properly needs the Antilles split at 1986, which is a periodisation change to
an aggregate reporting row rather than anything about Aruba — filed as a question here so the
next person to touch `reporting-areas/build.py` sees it.

### oq-abw-pre-1986-data-attribution

WHEP's LUH2 rows for area 22 run back to 1850, but for 1954-1986 Aruba's *agricultural returns*
would have been filed under the Netherlands Antilles, not separately. LUH2 is a raster and does
not have that problem — it measures ground, not paperwork — so this row is the right target for
those rows. Any future **reported** (as opposed to modelled) Aruban series for 1954-1986 should
be checked for double counting against `ANT-1961-2010` before it is routed here.
