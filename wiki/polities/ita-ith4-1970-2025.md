# Friuli-Venezia Giulia (region of Italy)

## Summary

Friuli-Venezia Giulia (FVG) is a region in the extreme northeast of Italy, bordering Austria and Slovenia, comprising the historic areas of Friuli and Venezia Giulia (including the port city of Trieste). This is a `type: subnational` entry, not a national one: Italy has held sovereignty over the whole territory continuously since the 1919 post-WWI annexation from Austria-Hungary (with the Trieste area's status remaining formally contested between 1947 and 1975), and the entry exists solely to carry a reporting territory distinct from national Italian totals — the same purpose served by sibling entries such as ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 (Bolzano/South Tyrol) created earlier in this pipeline run. The span starts in 1970 rather than at annexation (1919) because that is when Italian regions became statistically/administratively distinct NUTS2-level reporting units under the regioni reform; from 1919 to 1969 the territory existed inside Italy but is carried by the national container ITA-1919-2025 rather than by a dedicated FVG row, since no source in this pipeline reports the region separately in that window. Pre-1919 data (1880-1918), from when the territory was Austro-Hungarian Küstenland/Venezia Giulia, cannot be routed to this or any Italian polity and is flagged as an open question rather than folded into this entry's span.

**Why this entry exists.** **Input data:** the routing decision covers unit `ITA-ITH4` ("FVG" / admin_name ITH4), country Italy, spanning 144 data years from 1880 to 2023 in the underlying source. **Previous match and why wrong:** without this entry, all post-1919 FVG-labelled data years would fall to the national container ITA-1919-2025, which is roughly 40x FVG's own territory (Italy's ~301,000 km2 vs FVG's ~7,932 km2) and would mix a regional reporting series into a national one, exactly the failure this pipeline's sibling entries (e.g. ITA-ITH1, ITA-ITC1) were created to avoid. **Confirmation of distinctness:** the routing decision explicitly follows the precedent already established for sibling Italian NUTS2 regions in this same pipeline run, and Italy's own 1970 regioni reform is independent external confirmation that the region became its own statistically-reported administrative unit at that date, separate from the national total and from its neighbouring regions.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. `polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1` names the registered source that should eventually supply this polygon — GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer does digitise Italian regions globally as GID_1 features — but the routing decision confirms the locally-present file at `data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg` is a subset covering only 81 countries and that Italy (ISO3 ITA) is absent from it (0 features present). The source is registered and the right one; it simply has not been fetched for Italy yet. `polygon_feature_id` is left null and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` until the full/global GADM 4.1 adm1 dataset (or an ITA-specific subset) is fetched and the FVG feature identified.

**Territory description:** Friuli-Venezia Giulia occupies Italy's northeastern corner, bordered by Austria to the north, Slovenia to the east, the Adriatic Sea to the south, and the Veneto region to the west. Its modern boundaries have been stable since the region's creation and cover approximately 7,932 km2 (per current Italian regional statistics, ISTAT), making it one of Italy's smaller regions. Locatable on a modern map as the area containing the cities of Trieste, Udine, Pordenone, and Gorizia. No figure here is derived from an attached polygon, since none is attached yet; the 7,932 km2 figure is the region's own well-known contemporary administrative area, cited as context for the reader, not a geometry measurement.

## Predecessors and successors

This entry has no predecessor polity: the 1880-1918 data years for this unit predate Italian sovereignty over the territory (then Austro-Hungarian Venezia Giulia/Küstenland) and are currently unrouted rather than carried by an earlier polity in this vocabulary — see open questions. It has no successor: the region remains a current Italian NUTS2 unit through end_year 2025, matching the open end_year convention applied to all still-current Italian subnational entries in this pipeline run (e.g. ITA-ITH1-1919-2025). The years 1919-1969, when the territory was part of Italy but before regions had distinct statistical identity, are carried by the national container ITA-1919-2025 rather than by any dedicated FVG-specific polity.

## Sourced claims

- Friuli-Venezia Giulia became one of Italy's five special-statute regions with its own regional government seated in Trieste following the 1963 statute, after the Trieste question was resolved by the 1954 London Memorandum and formally closed by the 1975 Treaty of Osimo with Yugoslavia.
- Mitchell-style and comparable historical statistical series report a unit labelled with an FVG/Venezia Giulia administrative name for data years 1880-1918 and again from some point after 1919, per the routing decision's underlying source rows for unit ITA-ITH4 spanning 144 data years 1880-2023, 39 of which (1880-1918) were marked unroutable to any existing Italian polity.

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Code follows the ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke name**

Friuli-Venezia Giulia (FVG) is a standard Italian administrative region created by the 1963/1970 special-statute reform, not a historical territory with an identity of its own independent of Italy the way Alaska or Hyderabad are. It has never been a distinct sovereign or quasi-sovereign entity, has no name that predates its function as an Italian region, and its predecessor territory (Venezia Giulia under Austria-Hungary, then contested/occupied 1919-1919) is not continuous with the modern administrative unit in the way the bespoke-code cases are. ITA has zero existing subnational codes, so there is no country-specific precedent to follow or depart from; the 57-of-66 dominant pattern (ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end) is used instead, giving ITA-ITH4-1970-2025, matching the sibling ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 (Bolzano/South Tyrol) already created in this same pipeline run.

### d-start-year-1970-not-1919

**Span starts in 1970, leaving 1880-1969 uncovered by this entry on purpose**

The routing decision explicitly proposes start_year 1970, matching the Italian regional-government reform (regioni) that gave FVG its own statistical/administrative identity as a NUTS2 unit, and treats 1919-1969 as covered by the national container ITA-1919-2025 (the region existed as territory but not yet as a distinct reporting unit). 1880-1918 data predates Italian sovereignty over this territory (it was Austro-Hungarian Venezia Giulia) and is unroutable to any Italian polity; that pre-1919 span is not this entry's problem to solve and is called out as an open question below rather than silently absorbed by widening this entry's start_year, which would misstate when FVG became a distinct Italian reporting unit.

## Open questions

### oq-1970-start-year-not-independently-verified

**1970 as FVG's statistical start year is asserted by analogy, not independently checked**

The routing reasoning states that 1970 is when Italian regions gained statistical/administrative identity as NUTS2 units, but explicitly flags that this date was asserted from the pattern already used for sibling regions (ITA-ITC1, ITH1) rather than independently re-verified for Friuli-Venezia Giulia specifically. FVG has an unusual administrative history relative to ordinary regions: it was granted special-statute autonomy earlier, with its regional government seated at Trieste established in 1963 following the resolution of the Trieste question (Free Territory of Trieste dissolved 1954, formally annexed 1975 per the Treaty of Osimo). It is possible FVG's own statistical reporting as a distinct unit began in 1963-1964 rather than 1970, which would mean this entry's start_year is a few years too late and some routed data years currently falling before 1970 could in fact belong to this polity rather than to the national container.

### oq-1880-1918-pre-italian-span-unrouted

**39 of 144 data years for this unit (1880-1918, 27%) remain unrouted with no polity**

The harness's own routing_concerns flag that 1880-1918 data years are marked unroutable because no Italian polity can carry data predating the 1919 annexation, but the source clearly reports measured values for this unit across those years, meaning some entity was being measured. That territory was Austrian Venezia Giulia / Küstenland (crownland administered from Trieste) inside Austria-Hungary. This wiki does not currently appear to contain an Austro-Hungarian crownland polity for the Trieste/Küstenland region (the routing_reasoning explicitly says this was 'asserted, not independently re-verified'). Whether such a polity should be created to absorb 1880-1918, or whether it already exists under a different code and this unit's pre-1919 years should be re-routed there, is unresolved by this entry and left for a follow-on routing pass.

### oq-trieste-special-status-1945-1975

**Trieste's disputed status (1945-1954 Allied occupation, 1954-1975 contested sovereignty) is not reflected in this entry's container edge**

The city of Trieste and its surrounding territory, now part of FVG, was under Allied military government as the Free Territory of Trieste from 1947 and was not definitively confirmed as Italian sovereign territory until the 1975 Treaty of Osimo (informally resolved by the 1954 London Memorandum). This entry's single container edge treats the whole 1970-2025 span as uniformly contained by ITA-1919-2025 with no acknowledgment that formal international recognition of Italian sovereignty over the Trieste zone postdates this entry's own 1970 start_year by five years. Whether this affects any routed data (e.g., statistics published for 'Zone A' or Trieste separately from FVG proper in the 1947-1975 window) has not been checked.
