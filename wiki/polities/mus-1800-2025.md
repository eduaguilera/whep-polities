# Mauritius

## Summary

Mauritius is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Mauritius from 1800 to the present. The start year 1800 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage. Mauritius has no land boundaries. It gained independence from Britain in 1968.

## Territorial extent

Mauritius is an island country in the Indian Ocean, east of Madagascar. Area 788 sq. miles (2,040 sq. km); population 1,100,000 (1994). Capital: Port Louis. No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "The island's first settlers were Arabs who arrived as traders in the tenth century but left shortly after. The Dutch discovered Mauritius in 1598 and introduced sugarcane, which accounts for almost 90% of the country's exports and 70% of the labor force" (p.380). [biger-1995 §mauritius](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritius)
- "The French gained control in 1715 and brought African slaves to work the extensive sugar plantations. The Creole language developed from a fusion of French and African elements. In 1810 the British captured Mauritius and began importing Indians as laborers. Independence was granted in 1968" (p.380). [biger-1995 §mauritius](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritius)
