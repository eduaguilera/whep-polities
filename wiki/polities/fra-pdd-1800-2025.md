# Puy-de-Dôme (département)

## Summary

Puy-de-Dôme is one of the original ~83 départements created by the French Revolutionary administrative reform effective 4 March 1790, carved from the former province of Auvergne and named for the Puy de Dôme volcano at its centre, with Clermont-Ferrand as its seat. It is a `type: subnational` entry rather than national, because throughout its existence it has sat inside successive French national containers -- it was never itself a sovereign state, and this row exists to give the département-level administrative tier its own polity code, distinct from the whole-country FRA rows that represent metropolitan France as a unit. Unlike Belfort (split off from Haut-Rhin in 1871), the Alsace-Lorraine départements (ceded and returned), Corsica (split into two départements in 1975), or the overseas départements (never part of metropolitan France at all), Puy-de-Dôme has never been split, merged, annexed, or reorganised since 1790 -- its boundaries as a département track directly onto the modern INSEE department coded 63.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry was proposed by the agent-harness routing pipeline for unit FRA-FRK14 ("Puy-de-Dôme"), reasoned purely from French administrative history rather than from a named, counted dataset: the routing decision gives no source name, row count, or year range for data specifically citing Puy-de-Dôme, only the observation that no existing polity in the table represents this territory -- the only rows touching it are the whole-country FRA national containers (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), which represent all of metropolitan France, not this one département. Before this entry, any data keyed to "Puy-de-Dôme" or its INSEE code 63 would have had nowhere finer than the national FRA row to land in. What confirms this is a distinct, addressable administrative entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision is that it is one of the original Revolutionary départements of 1790, a first-order administrative division of France with its own prefecture, statistical reporting, and continuous 235-year existence -- the same tier of unit as the ~95 other metropolitan départements, several of which (the pattern noted in the routing precedent, e.g. ESP-AS, ESP-CA analogues at the ISO3-subunit level) already have rows in this table for other countries.

**No FRA container exists for 1790-1800, so this page starts a decade after the département's true creation**

Puy-de-Dôme was created effective 4 March 1790, and the routing decision proposed start_year=1790 on that basis. But the polity table's earliest national FRA row is FRA-1800-1871, so no container edge could be written for 1790-1799 without pointing to a nonexistent or out-of-span container.
