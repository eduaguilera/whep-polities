# Syria and Lebanon (combined statistical unit)

> **Polygon now built (2026-07-29).** `polygon_feature_id` described a two-feature
> union in prose — "cowcode=652 row ... UNION cowcode=660 row ..." — which the
> single-id lookup cannot resolve, so every build attached nothing and this row sat in
> the polygon backlog. The union is now a builder in
> `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`: CShapes gwcodes 652 Syria (188,004 km²) and
> 660 Lebanon (10,209) at 1950 = 198,213 km², against the 195,632 recorded here from
> the vintages this page names (1.3%).
>
> Note for anyone reading the code: despite the `SYL` prefix this row is **not**
> Sylhet. It is the joint "Syria and Lebanon" reporting unit of the FAO 1952 yearbook.
> I initially misread it that way from the prefix alone and nearly filed the Syria and
> Lebanon gwcodes as a mis-binding.


Statistical aggregate polity created to route FAO data reported under the joint label "Syria and Lebanon". The FAO 1952 yearbook publishes tobacco production data (8 rows, covering 1949-1951) for the combined territory as a single reporting unit — a statistical artifact of the former French Mandate joint administration and the subsequent Syrian-Lebanese customs union (in effect until March 1950).

Polygon: composed union of CShapes 2.0 cowcode=652 (Syria, ~185,180 km²) and cowcode=660 (Lebanon, ~10,452 km²) = ~195,632 km². Both borders are stable 1944-1953.

Routing: data labelled "Syria and Lebanon" 1944-1953 -> SYL-1944-1953. After that period, route to SYR-1946-1967 and LBN-1944-2025 separately.
