# French Equatorial Africa (AEF)

## Summary

Afrique Équatoriale Française (AEF) was a federation of four French colonial territories in central Africa, governed as a single administrative unit from 15 January 1910 until the independence of its constituent territories in August–August 1960. The federation was administered from Brazzaville (Middle Congo) under a Governor-General. AEF was the federation-level reporting unit analogous to AOF (French West Africa) for its constituent colonies. This entry is the **federation-level aggregate** and carries only data reported at the AEF level; disaggregated data for individual territories should be routed to the constituent polity entries listed under [Constituent territories](#constituent-territories) below.

The federation was formally established on 15 January 1910 by reorganising earlier colonial possessions. Its four constituent territories were: Gabon (Colonie du Gabon), Middle Congo (Moyen-Congo), Ubangi-Shari (Oubangui-Chari, present-day Central African Republic), and Chad (Tchad). Prior to 1910, these territories had been administered under various earlier arrangements dating to the 1880s–1900s. Chad's formal colonial status as a colony (rather than occupied territory) was confirmed in 1920. The federation was dissolved when its constituent territories became independent republics between August and August 1960.

**Why composed_union:** CShapes 2.0 does not record a federation-level AEF entry. Unlike French West Africa (gwcode 430), which CShapes records for 1895–1904 before dropping the federation aggregate, AEF never appears as a unified CShapes feature — CShapes tracks only the four constituent territories individually throughout 1886–1960. There is no exact_historical GIS source for AEF as a unified polygon. The composed union of the four constituent CShapes territories is therefore the highest-quality achievable polygon.

**Vintage year 1938:** The 1938 vintage is chosen as the mid-point of the stable post-WWI boundary period (1919/1920–1960), consistent with the AOF-1895-1960 approach. Boundaries of the four territories were substantially stable from 1919/1920 until independence. The 1919–1960 polygons are used as the representative feature for the full 1910–1960 federation span.

**1910–1919 sub-period note:** For the first decade of the federation (1910–1919) the constituent territory boundaries differ slightly from the 1919–1960 stable polygons. CShapes records separate polygons for 1906–1912 (indices 364/368/372/380, union ~2,495,219 km²) and 1912–1919 (indices 365/369/373/381, union ~2,205,778 km²). The 1912–1919 reduction reflects the transfer of Neukamerun territories returned to German Cameroun under the 1911 Agadir Crisis treaty and restored to France after WWI. The 1919–1960 stable polygons are used for the full span; this is a proxy for the 1910–1919 period. The direction of drift is that the 1938-vintage polygon **overestimates** AEF territory for 1912–1919 by approximately +289,441 km² (the Neukamerun cession area).

**Historical area discrepancy:** Some secondary sources cite AEF area as ~2,953,000 km². That figure includes French Cameroun mandate (~437,000 km²), which was administered by France adjacent to AEF but was legally a separate League of Nations mandate / UN trust territory (not part of AEF proper). The CShapes 2,495,219 km² figure correctly represents only the four AEF constituent territories. The sum of modern territory areas for the four successors is ~2,516,652 km²; the CShapes union is ~0.9% smaller, reflecting minor boundary simplification at the colonial-era polygon level.

## Open questions

### oq-aef-1910-1919-boundary

The 1910–1919 sub-period used a different set of CShapes constituent polygons (indices 365/369/373/381 for 1912–1919, with union ~2,205,778 km²) due to the Neukamerun cession. The 1938-vintage polygon overstates territory for 1912–1919 by ~289,000 km². If data is reported specifically for 1910–1919, a sub-period polygon should be constructed. Current implementation uses the 1919–1960 stable polygon for the full 1910–1960 span.

### oq-aef-cow-code

AEF does not appear in the CShapes or CoW/GW state lists as a unified federation. If a gwcode or CoW code is identified in the literature for AEF as a whole, the cow_code field should be updated.

### oq-french-cameroun-exclusion

French Cameroun (League of Nations mandate / UN trust territory, CShapes gwcode 471) was administered adjacent to and sometimes informally associated with AEF but was legally separate. Any data rows labelled "French Cameroun" or "Cameroun" should be routed to a separate WHEP entry, not to AEF-1910-1960.
