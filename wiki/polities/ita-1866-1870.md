# Italy (1866–1870)

## Summary

This sub-period covers the Kingdom of Italy from the acquisition of
**Venetia** (Vienna Peace Treaty, 3 October 1866) to the capture of
**Rome** (20 September 1870). It was split from the former single row
`ITA-1861-1919` to correct a polygon-vintage overstatement: the CShapes
1886 polygon includes Rome/Lazio (~12,000 km²), which was not Italian
territory during 1866–1870.

Predecessor: [ita-1861-1866](ita-1861-1866.md).
Successor: [ita-1870-1919](ita-1870-1919.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-Europe, `cowcode=325`, polygon vintage 1886.
  This is an **ESTIMATE**: the 1886 polygon includes Rome/Lazio, which
  remained under Papal sovereignty until 20 September 1870. Applied to
  1866–1870, it **overstates** true Italian territory by approximately
  **12,000 km² (~4–5%)**.
- **True 1866–1870 extent.** Post-Venetia Italy: ~268,000 km². Includes
  Venetia (gained October 1866); excludes Rome/Lazio (Papal temporal
  sovereignty until 1870).
- **Polygon fix needed.** The proper polygon for this sub-period is a
  GIS-constructed feature subtracting the Lazio region from the 1886 CShapes
  polygon. No such polygon exists in CShapes 2.0 or CShapes-Europe. See
  [oq-gis-constructed-polygon](#oq-gis-constructed-polygon).

## Sourced claims

- **1866-10-03: Vienna Peace Treaty.** Italy gained Venetia from Austria
  following the Third Italian War of Independence
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This event begins this sub-period.
- **1870-09-20: Capture of Rome.** Italy took the Papal States, ending Papal
  temporal power. Capital relocated from Florence to Rome
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome). This event ends this sub-period.

## Decisions

- **Split from ITA-1861-1919 (2026-06-29).** The Rome (1870) acquisition is a
  genuine border-change event within the former single row. The 1886 CShapes
  polygon is an ESTIMATE for 1866–1870, overstating territory by ~12,000 km²
  (~4–5%). Documented here rather than silently applied.
- **Polygon status: estimate.** No alternative 1866–1870 polygon exists.
  The approximation is flagged and its direction/magnitude documented.

## Open questions

### oq-gis-constructed-polygon

A GIS-constructed polygon for 1866–1870 Italy could be derived by
subtracting the Lazio region from the CShapes 1886 polygon. Source
candidates: Papal States boundary from Cliopatria, CHGIS, or a dedicated
ecclesiastical-states dataset. This would reduce overstatement from ~4–5%
to near zero. Tier B — low priority (short 4-year sub-period).
