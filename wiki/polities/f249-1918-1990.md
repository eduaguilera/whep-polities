# Yemen (combined reporting: YAR and PDR Yemen)

## Summary

This row is a FAOSTAT reporting-area composite for pre-unification Yemen,
from North Yemen's independence (1918) to the 1990 unification. FAOSTAT
reporting area 249 "Yemen" carries a single series back to 1961 whose
territory is the WHOLE of present-day Yemen — the Yemen Arab Republic
(north; Mutawakkilite Kingdom to 1962) plus the south (Aden/Federation of
South Arabia under Britain to 1967, then the People's Democratic
Republic) — so its 1961-1989 years cannot be routed to any single
component without misstating the extent. The F-prefix follows the
F51/F228/F248 convention: F237/F249 = FAOSTAT area code.

## Territorial extent

The union of the northern and southern states equals the territory of the
post-1990 unified Republic of Yemen. Polygon: CShapes 2.0 cowcode 679 at
vintage 1990 (unified Yemen), 425,263 km2. `polygon_status: estimate`
because the vintage post-dates most of the span and the
Saudi-Yemeni border was only fully delimited in 2000 — the union extent
is nonetheless the correct order of magnitude and shape for routing.

## Predecessors and successors

- Successor: YEM-1990-2025 (unified Republic of Yemen).
- Components (partially modelled): MKY-1918-1962 (Mutawakkilite Kingdom,
  north). The southern chain (Aden Colony/Protectorate, Federation of
  South Arabia, PDR Yemen 1967-1990, CShapes cowcode 680) is not yet
  modelled; single-zone data must route to components, never here.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **249 "Yemen"**: 1961-1989 routes here; 1990
onward to YEM-1990-2025 (`pipelines/faostat-era-matching`, iso3 YEM;
shared boundary years route to the successor). Pre-1961 sources that
label the north alone ("Yemen" in FAO 1952 usage) route to
MKY-1918-1962, as already encoded in applied_aliases.csv.

## Decisions

- **Composite over components:** FAOSTAT's back-cast series covers both
  states; splitting it across YAR/PDRY would be an invention.
- **Span 1918-1990:** the two-polity configuration's lifetime on the
  northern side; southern components should be modelled separately if
  south-only data surfaces.
