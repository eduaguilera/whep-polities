# Korea (to 1945)

## Summary

The Korean peninsula as a single territorial-economic unit from 1800 to
liberation on 15 August 1945. This row spans two successive governance regimes
over the same territory:

- **Joseon dynasty / Korean Empire (1800-1910)** — independent monarchy. The
  Korean Empire was proclaimed in 1897; the preceding Joseon dynasty had the
  same borders.
- **Japanese Chōsen (1910-1945)** — annexed by Japan following the 1910
  Japan-Korea Treaty. Administered as the Chōsen Government-General. Territory
  unchanged from the Korean Empire.

Per the WHEP polity definition
([decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)),
the governance change in 1910 did not move the border, so both periods are one
row. The row ends at liberation; the subsequent U.S./Soviet occupation zones
are [kor-1945-1948](kor-1945-1948.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `constructed` source, feature `KOR-1800-1945`. Built by
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` as the **union of CShapes 2.0
gwcode 731 (North Korea) and gwcode 732 (South Korea)**, both using their
post-1945 polygons. CShapes 2.0 records no pre-1945 Korea feature — the
peninsula was non-sovereign (Japanese colony) and doesn't qualify under
Gleditsch-Ward state-system rules. The post-1945 union polygon (~219,400 km²)
is territorially identical to the unified peninsula under Joseon / Korean
Empire / Chōsen, which had stable borders at the 38th-parallel-irrelevant
peninsula outline throughout.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 agricultural input data reports
"Korea" (iso3c=NA because the input predates ISO 3166 assignments for united
Korea) for 578 rows across 1909-1960. Most rows (1909-1944) pre-date the
U.S./Soviet division. Without this page the matcher either returned
`match_status: none` or incorrectly routed rows to KOR-1945-1948 /
KOR-1948-2025. Routing rule added in `pipelines/pre1961-matching/match.R`:
country="Korea" + year < 1945 → `KOR` lookup → this row.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — database truncation at 1800. The Joseon dynasty
  predates the database.
- **Successor:** [kor-1945-1948](kor-1945-1948.md) — U.S. and Soviet occupation
  zones before the formal 1948 division into ROK (south) and DPRK (north).

## Sourced claims

- Joseon dynasty 1392-1897, Korean Empire 1897-1910, Japanese colonial Chōsen
  1910-1945 — one unchanging territorial unit. [biger-1995 §korea](../sources/biger-1995.md#korea)
- Peninsular area ~219,400 km² (union of modern NK and SK). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)
- Japanese annexation: Treaty of 22 August 1910. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- Liberation: 15 August 1945 (Japanese surrender). [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Open questions

### oq-korean-empire-vs-joseon-split

Whether to split this row at 1897 (proclamation of the Korean Empire) is a
judgment call. Territory didn't change; the regime did. Per the WHEP polity
rule that prefers territorial over constitutional events, keeping as one row.
Revisit if Joseon / Korean Empire data is ever needed separately.
