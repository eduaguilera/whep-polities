# Isère (department)

## Summary

Isère is one of the original départements created by the March 1790 Revolutionary reform, carved mainly from the former province of Dauphiné, with its préfecture at Grenoble. It is `type: subnational` because sovereignty over the territory always sits with the national FRA chain — this row exists only to carry statistics that were collected at the département level rather than for all of France, and it must not compete with the national rows in matching. The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, but no FRA container row exists before 1800 (the earliest is FRA-1800-1871), and a container edge cannot fall outside either party's span. Rather than leave 1790-1800 uncontained, this entry starts at 1800, the earliest year a container can legally cover it; the 1790-1800 decade is left as an open question below rather than silently dropped. Isère is not one of the four standard département exceptions (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corse, overseas), so its outline has been comparatively stable since 1790 aside from minor 19th-century canton adjustments.

**Why this entry exists.** The input is a France (unit_id FRA-FRK24, admin label 'FRK24') département-level series with continuous statistics from 1862 to 2023 routed by the harness's country-conventions matcher. Before this entry, an Isère-labelled row had no distinct polity to land on: the only FRA candidates in the table are FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919 and FRA-1919-2025, all national totals for their respective eras, and all three merely CONTAIN Isère rather than being identical to it — routing département-level data to a national row would silently aggregate one of ~90 subunits into a 100x-larger whole. Isère's distinctness as a statistical and administrative unit is confirmed by its continuous existence as a named département since the March 1790 Revolutionary reform (one of the original ~83), preserved through every subsequent French administrative regime referenced in the FRA container chain, and by French national statistical series (INSEE, and earlier state statistical yearbooks) that have always published at the département level rather than only nationally.

**Territory description:** Isère is a present-day French département in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region, in the southeast of France, with its préfecture at Grenoble. It borders Savoie and the Alps to the east, the Rhône valley to the west, and stretches from the Rhône lowlands up into the Belledonne and Vercors ranges. Its modern area is approximately **7,431 km²**. Département outlines of this kind have been essentially stable since their 1790 creation (unlike Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corse, or the overseas départements), so a GADM 4.1 present-day boundary would be a reasonable proxy back to 1800 once fetched, with the caveat that any 19th-century canton-level boundary adjustments are not reflected.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790 (the year Isère was created as a Revolutionary département), with span_basis noting this is the default-rule start since Isère is not one of the four listed exceptions. However, the FRA container chain's earliest row is FRA-1800-1871 — there is no FRA container row covering 1790-1800. The containment rule states that container edges must tile the whole polity span with no gap, and an edge is rejected if it falls outside either party's span, so a 1790-2025 span could not be legally containered for its first decade. Rather than leave a silent gap or invent a container edge referencing a row that doesn't exist, this entry starts at 1800, aligned with FRA-1800-1871, and the 1790-1800 decade for Isère is recorded as an open question rather than dropped.

## Open questions

### oq-1790-1800-gap

**Isère existed as a département from 1790, but this entry starts at 1800 for lack of a container**

Isère was created in March 1790, ten years before this entry's start_year. The gap exists purely because no FRA container row spans 1790-1800 in the current polity table (the earliest national FRA row is FRA-1800-1871). If a future ingest adds an earlier FRA container row (e.g. a FRA-1790-1800 or similar), this entry's start_year should be reconsidered and moved back to 1790, and any pre-1800 Isère-labelled data currently unroutable should be re-checked against it.

### oq-gadm-fra-fetch

**GADM France rows are not present in the locally cached gadm-4.1-adm1 file**

The registered gadm-4.1-adm1 source (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) is confirmed to contain 0 rows for France; it appears to be a curated subset of ~81 countries that omits France despite GADM publishing French département boundaries globally. Someone needs to fetch/add France's adm1 rows into this local file (or a fetch pipeline needs to include France) before polygon_feature_id can be assigned and polygon_status upgraded from unassigned to assigned. Until then, this and any other future FRA département-level entries will have the same gap.

### oq-department-boundary-vintage

**GADM 4.1 reflects present-day boundaries; 19th-century canton-level adjustments to Isère are not captured**

Once fetched, the GADM 4.1 polygon for Isère (expected GID_1 'FRA.35_1') would reflect the département's current outline, standing in for a span beginning in 1800. French départements have been broadly stable since creation, but minor boundary adjustments between neighbouring départements (canton transfers, communal reattachments) occurred through the 19th and 20th centuries. Whether any such adjustment affected Isère specifically, and how large an area it involved, has not been checked against any historical boundary source (e.g. IGN historical maps or Cassini-era cadastral records).
