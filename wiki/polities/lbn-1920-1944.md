# Lebanon (1920-1944)

## Summary

Lebanon (1920-1944) covers the French Mandate period, from the Conference of San Remo (24 April 1920) which established the mandates for former Ottoman territories, until Lebanese independence in 1944. Greater Lebanon was proclaimed on 1 September 1920 under French administration, carved from the former Ottoman provinces. The CShapes polygon reflects the mandate territory with France as the ruling power.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes classifies this as a mandate under French rule. The Palestine (Israel)--Lebanon boundary is based on an Anglo-French line established during the scramble for former Ottoman territory.

## Open questions

### oq-mandate-end-date

The database uses 1944 as the end year for the mandate, reflecting Lebanon's declaration of independence. France had recognized Lebanese independence on 22 November 1943, but French troops did not withdraw until 1946. The exact transition date used by CShapes should be confirmed.
