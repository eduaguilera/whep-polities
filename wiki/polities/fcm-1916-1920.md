# French-occupied Cameroon (1916-1920)

## Summary

The eastern part of German Kamerun under French military occupation, from the German surrender in
February 1916 to the start of the French mandate. British and French forces took the colony during
the First World War, divided it provisionally in 1916, and received it as two League of Nations
mandates. The Neukamerun strips that France had ceded to Germany in 1911 went back to French
Equatorial Africa.
[wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §french-cameroon](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#french-cameroon)

The next row, [fcm-1920-1960](fcm-1920-1960.md), begins in 1920, and German Kamerun
([gkm-1912-1916](gkm-1912-1916.md)) ends in 1916, so nothing covered the French zone in between.
British Cameroons ([bcm-1916-1961](bcm-1916-1961.md)) is its western neighbour in the same years.

## Why this entry exists

Mitchell's C5 (output of cocoa beans), note 2: "Data for Togoland are included with Cameroon to
1916. From 1917 to 1962 (1st line) statistics are for French Cameroon." (issue 688). With no French
row for 1917-1919, the three `cameroon` cacao rows for those years fell through to BCM-1916-1961,
**British** Cameroons, a different territory. The seam is continuous into the mandate
(2,700 t in 1919, 3,300 t in 1920 on FCM-1920-1960), which is the control that 1917-1919 are the
French series.

This row takes the `cameroon` (mitchell) rows for 1917-1919 through an alias: **3 rows**. The 1916
row is Kamerun plus Togoland by the same note, a territory no polity has, and stays where it is
(recorded as `cmr-cocoa-1900-1919-territory-mismatch`).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** CShapes 2.0 gwcode 471, French Cameroun, at 1920 (the 1919-1922
step): **423,069 km²** (ESRI:54034), the same outline as FCM-1920-1960.
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

- **Why `proxy`.** CShapes draws the mandate border from 1919; the 1916 provisional partition line
  and the final 1919 line were not the same, and CShapes has no feature for the occupation zone
  itself. The mandate outline is the closest drawn territory, and the difference between the two
  lines is a small share of this area.
- **Why not German Kamerun.** CShapes' Kamerun for 1916-1919 (gwcode 470, 799,953 km²) still
  includes Neukamerun and the British zone, almost twice this territory.
