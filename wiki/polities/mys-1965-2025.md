# Malaysia

## Summary

Malaysia as tracked by WHEP from 1965, when Singapore separated from the federation. The Federation of Malaysia was formed on 16 September 1963, incorporating Malaya, Sarawak, Sabah, and Singapore. Singapore's departure in 1965 reduced Malaysia to its current extent across Peninsular Malaysia and the Borneo states.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Malaysia consists of Peninsular Malaysia (bounded by Thailand) and East Malaysia on Borneo (bounded by Indonesia and Brunei).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [MYS-1963-1965](mys-1963-1965.md) -- Singapore's separation from Malaysia

## Sourced claims

- On 16 September 1963, Sarawak, Sabah, and Singapore joined the Independent Federation of Malaya, which became Malaysia. [biger-1995 §malaysia](../sources/biger-1995.md#malaysia)
- The Indonesia--Malaysia boundary on Borneo separated the Dutch Indonesian regions of East and West Kalimantan from the Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah. [biger-1995 §malaysia](../sources/biger-1995.md#malaysia)
