# Central African Republic

## Summary

Central African Republic is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the independent CAR from 1960 to the present. The CAR achieved independence from France on 13 August 1960.

## Territorial extent

The CAR is a landlocked country bounded by Chad (744 miles; 1,197 km) in the north, Sudan (724 miles; 1,165 km) in the east, Zaire (980 miles; 1,577 km) and Congo (290.5 miles; 467 km) in the south, and Cameroon (495 miles; 797 km) in the west. Capital: Bangui. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Open questions

### oq-car-sudan-boundary

CAR-Sudan boundary alignment. The boundary between CAR and Sudan was never formally agreed upon at independence, and there is no formal agreement regarding the alignment (p.147). The colonial boundary is accepted as the international boundary.
