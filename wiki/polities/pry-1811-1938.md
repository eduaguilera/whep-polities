# Paraguay (to 1938)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay from **1811** to **1938** — a **128-year
row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
Paraguayan independence was declared on 14-15 May 1811. Two major
wars shaped this row: the War of the Triple Alliance (1864-70),
in which Paraguay lost approximately 60% of its territory and most
of its male population, and the Chaco War (1932-35), in which
Paraguay gained territory from Bolivia. The 1938 split likely
corresponds to the final Chaco War settlement. COW: PRY, ccode=150.
