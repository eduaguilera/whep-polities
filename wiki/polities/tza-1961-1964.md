# Tanzania (1961-1964)

## Summary

This WHEP polity row covers the territory of mainland Tanganyika from independence in
December 1961 to the union with Zanzibar in April 1964 that created the United Republic
of Tanzania. The row is distinct from the preceding [tan-1922-1964](tan-1922-1964.md)
(British mandate/trusteeship) and the succeeding [tza-1964-2025](tza-1964-2025.md)
(Tanzania after the union) because CShapes records a territorial change at the union
date: the Zanzibar archipelago was added to the mainland, producing a measurably larger
polygon. The 1961 start date reflects Tanganyika's independence from British trusteeship
on 9 December 1961 under the Correlates of War state-system definition used by CShapes.

## Territorial extent

Mainland Tanganyika only; the Zanzibar islands were a separate British colony/protectorate
(and briefly independent sultanate) during this period and are not part of this row. The
polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=510, status=independent, 1961-12-09 to 1964-04-25,
area≈76.79 sq.deg.). The territory is bounded by Kenya and Uganda to the north, Rwanda,
Burundi, and the DRC (Zaire) to the west, Zambia, Malawi, and Mozambique to the south,
and the Indian Ocean to the east.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [tan-1922-1964](tan-1922-1964.md) — Tanganyika under British League
  of Nations mandate / UN trusteeship (1922–1964); covers the same mainland territory
  prior to independence.
- **Successor:** [tza-1964-2025](tza-1964-2025.md) — Tanzania (United Republic, mainland
  + Zanzibar) from 26 April 1964 onward.

## Contradictions

None identified between sources. CShapes and Biger agree on the 1961 independence date
for Tanganyika and the 1964 union date. The WHEP split at 1964 is driven by the CShapes
area change, consistent with the WHEP polity-definition rule.

## Open questions

### oq-zanzibar-area-increment

Zanzibar area increment size. The CShapes area change between the 1961–1964 row
(≈76.79 sq.deg.) and the 1964–2025 row (≈77.01 sq.deg.) is small (~0.22 sq.deg.).
This corresponds to the Zanzibar archipelago (~2,461 km²). The WHEP polity-definition
rule requires a "substantial" territorial change to justify a new row; whether this area
increment meets that threshold should be documented.
