# Dutch Guiana (to 1886)

## Summary

The Dutch colony of Suriname on the northeastern coast of South America before
1886, the year at which CShapes 2.0 coverage — and therefore WHEP's Suriname
chain — begins. The colony had been Dutch since the Treaty of Breda (1667). It
was occupied by Britain from 1799 to 1816 during the Napoleonic wars and
restored to the Netherlands by the Anglo-Dutch treaty of 1814, taking effect in
1816; the neighbouring Dutch colonies of Berbice, Demerara and Essequibo were
NOT restored and became British Guiana, which is why the western neighbour in
this period is [guy-1800-2025](guy-1800-2025.md). Slavery was abolished in 1863,
with indentured Indian and Javanese immigration from 1873 reshaping the
plantation economy inside an unchanged territory.

This row exists because WHEP's Suriname chain began at 1886 while a source
reports the colony's trade from 1800. Federico-Tena's *List of trading polities*
carries `Dutch Guayana` with series running **1800–1938**; of the 348 rows that
produces in the intake table, **188 fall in 1800–1885**, before
`SUR-1886-1954` opens (measured 2026-08-17 against
`data/final/polities_database.csv`). The label's failure to route at all is a
separate, spelling-level problem — *Guayana* against WHEP's *Guiana* — and is
not fixed here; even with an alias those 188 rows would have had no span to land
in.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, gwcode **115**, feature year **1886** — the
  earliest step CShapes offers for Suriname and the same binding
  `SUR-1886-1954` uses. 144,985 km².
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

- **`polygon_area_km2` is left blank on purpose.** 144,985 km² would be copied
  from the successor row, which measured it from this same geometry; declaring it
  here would add a number that agrees with itself and nothing else, which is what
  `validate_polygons`' self-referential-area check counts.

- **Why `proxy` and not `assigned`, where the successor row is `assigned`.**
  `sur-1886-1954` records that the CShapes geometry is the POST-settlement
  territory: the western boundary with British Guiana was settled by the 1899
  Paris arbitration (the New River Triangle, some 6,000–7,000 km²) and the
  southern boundary with Brazil by the 1906 treaty. That page can call the
  binding `assigned` because the data matched to it begins in 1909, after both
  settlements. THIS row covers 1800–1886, entirely before both, so the same
  geometry is a back-projection of boundaries that had not yet been drawn — the
  interior and the southwest in particular were undelimited. The magnitude of the
  reservation is bounded by the disputed areas themselves: the New River Triangle
  is about 4.8% of the declared area, and the Brazilian frontier dispute
  concerned the headwaters watershed rather than a large block.

- **No split at 1816.** The 1799–1816 British occupation interrupted
  SOVEREIGNTY, not territorial extent, and this schema has no field for
  sovereignty. A separate row for the occupation would assert a boundary change
  that did not happen. The occupation is recorded here instead, and a consumer
  attributing 1800–1816 trade to "the Netherlands" should read this paragraph
  first.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0's coverage of Suriname (gwcode 115) begins 1886-01-01, which is
  the reason WHEP's chain began there and not at an event in the colony's
  history. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- Federico-Tena reports `Dutch Guayana` from 1800; the source's early years are
  reconstructed, so the 1800 start is evidence of what its compilers could
  estimate rather than of a change in the colony.
  [federico-tena-2019 §known-limitations](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#known-limitations)
- The label was classified on the source page as an alias gap against
  `SUR-1886-1954`; the measurement recorded here shows the alias would close

  [federico-tena-2019 §the-76-labels-that-never-route](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#the-76-labels-that-never-route-32-of-them-do-not-exist)

## Decisions

- **[2026-08-17] One row for 1800–1886, not a chain of boundary steps.** No
  source fetched into this repository carries a pre-1886 Suriname boundary, so
  any interior step would be invented. A single row with a `proxy` binding and
  the disputed areas quantified above states what is actually known.
- **[2026-08-17] `cow_code` left empty**, though `SUR-1886-1954` carries 115.
  115 is the Gleditsch-Ward code CShapes uses; Suriname is not a COW
  state-system member before independence in 1975. The successor row's value is
  left as it is — that is a question about that row.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1899-western-boundary

Whether WHEP should represent the pre-1899 Dutch claim (including the New River
Triangle as claimed rather than as awarded) cannot be decided from a fetched
source here. If a historical GIS source covering nineteenth-century Guiana is
added, this row should be rebound to it.
