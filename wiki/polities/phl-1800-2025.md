# Philippines

## Summary

The Philippines as tracked by WHEP from 1800. An island state in the western Pacific Ocean with no land boundaries. The Philippines was a Spanish colony until 1898, then under American rule for nearly 50 years, becoming a fully independent republic in 1946 after Japanese occupation during WWII.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The Philippines is an archipelago of over 7,000 islands with no land boundaries.
