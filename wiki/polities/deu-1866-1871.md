# North German Confederation (1866-1871)

## Summary

This row covers **6 years** of the Prussian-led North German
Confederation, from the Austro-Prussian War (1866) through the
proclamation of the German Empire (18 January 1871). Prussia
annexed ~95,000 km² of territory in 1866 (Hanover, Hesse-Kassel,
Nassau, Frankfurt, Schleswig-Holstein) and formed the North German
Confederation with 22 northern states. The Franco-Prussian War
(1870-1871) brought the four southern German states into the fold
and annexed Alsace-Lorraine from France.

Predecessor: [deu-1800-1866](deu-1800-1866.md) — Kingdom of Prussia.
Successor: [deu-1871-1919](deu-1871-1919.md) — German Empire
proclaimed 18 January 1871.
