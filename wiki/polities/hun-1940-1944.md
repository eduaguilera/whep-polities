# Hungary (1940-1944)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **~4 years**, the peak-expansion WWII period
from the Second Vienna Award to the Soviet occupation and forced reversion.

The Second Vienna Award (30 August 1940) added northern Transylvania
(~43,492 km²) from Romania to Hungary. The April 1941 Yugoslav occupation
added the Backa and Baranya strips (~10,000 km²). At peak, Hungary
controlled ~172,000 km² — 58% larger than the Trianon-plus-First-Award
~109k km² baseline. From October 1944 Soviet forces began occupying
Hungary and stripping the annexations; the effective end of this regime is
placed at ~1944-10, when Soviet control made the enlarged borders
non-functional. The Treaty of Paris (10 February 1947) formally declared
the Vienna Awards null and void.

Predecessor: [hun-1938-1940](hun-1938-1940.md) — Second Vienna Award.
Successor: [hun-1944-1947](hun-1944-1947.md) — Soviet reversion.

## Territorial extent

  polygon has been found in CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, Paine et al.
  2024, or HistoGIS. The required polygon would show Hungary with
  northern Transylvania (Romanian territory, east of the Carpathian
  arc) and the Backa/Baranya strip (formerly Yugoslav).
- **Polygon status: missing.** Sources to check for a future polygon:
  Shepherd's *Historical Atlas of the 20th Century*; RECIT/IGN French
  historical atlas; Putzger historical atlas (German edition);
  Andree's *Historischer Handatlas*; Westermann *Grosser Atlas zur
  Weltgeschichte*. If found, replace `polygon_status: missing` with
  `polygon_status: assigned` and supply `polygon_source`,
  `polygon_feature_id`, `polygon_feature_year`.
- **Area approximation.** ~172,277 km²: Trianon base (~92,991 km²) +
  First Vienna Award (~11,830 km²) + Second Vienna Award northern
  Transylvania (~43,492 km²) + 1941 Yugoslav strips (~10,000 km²)
  + minor Subcarpathian Ruthenia areas (~13,964 km² occupied Mar 1939,
  already in CShapes 1938 polygon). The polygon_area_km2 field records
  this estimate as a planning figure; it carries ESTIMATE uncertainty.
- **Direction of approximation.** Any data point for 1940-1944 matched
  to the CShapes ~109k km² polygon would UNDERSTATE Hungary's territory
  by ~58%. Data attributable to northern Transylvania or Backa in this
  period should NOT be matched to this polity if they measure only the
  sub-territory; check source footnotes.
- **Data magnitude note.** The predecessor polity HUN-1938-1947's staple
  crop series (wheat ~1.82M t, maize ~1.81M t) showed no clear upward
  step at 1940-41, suggesting those source series may have excluded
  annexed territories. Researchers using production data for 1940-1944
  must verify whether the source publisher (FAO, USDA, national
  statistics) included or excluded northern Transylvania.

## Sourced claims

- **1940-08-30: Second Vienna Award.** "Hungary regained almost 40% of
  the area in the Vienna Award of 30 August 1940," specifically
  northern Transylvania (~43,492 km²) from Romania.
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria) (p.51)
- **1941-04: Yugoslav occupation.** Hungary occupied Backa and Baranya
  (~10,000 km²) following the German-led dismemberment of Yugoslavia.
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **1944-10: Soviet occupation begins.** Soviet forces crossed into
  eastern Hungary; the expanded borders became non-functional. This is
  the effective end-of-row event (exact date approximate; Treaty of
  Paris 1947-02-10 is the legal end date but the successor polity
  HUN-1944-1947 covers the transition period).
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 310, continuous.
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
