# Hungary (1940-1944)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **~4 years**, the peak-expansion WWII period
from the Second Vienna Award to the Soviet occupation and forced reversion.

The Second Vienna Award (30 August 1940) added northern Transylvania
(~43,492 km²) from Romania to Hungary. The April 1941 Yugoslav occupation
added the Backa and Baranya strips (~10,000 km²). At peak, Hungary
controlled ~172,000 km² — 58% larger than the Trianon-plus-First-Award
~109k km² baseline. From October 1944 Soviet forces began occupying
Hungary and stripping the annexations; the effective end of this regime is
placed at ~1944-10, when Soviet control made the enlarged borders
non-functional. The Treaty of Paris (10 February 1947) formally declared
the Vienna Awards null and void.

Predecessor: [hun-1938-1940](hun-1938-1940.md) — Second Vienna Award.
Successor: [hun-1944-1947](hun-1944-1947.md) — Soviet reversion.

  The gap (~4,785 km²) corresponds to the Backa and Baranya Yugoslav

  because it retains Yugoslavia's full pre-war footprint during the
  1941-1944 occupation period. The composed polygon therefore slightly
  understates the true extent.

## Sourced claims

- **1940-08-30: Second Vienna Award.** "Hungary regained almost 40% of
  the area in the Vienna Award of 30 August 1940," specifically
  northern Transylvania (~43,492 km²) from Romania.
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria) (p.51)
- **1941-04: Yugoslav occupation.** Hungary occupied Backa and Baranya
  (~10,000 km²) following the German-led dismemberment of Yugoslavia.
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **1944-10: Soviet occupation begins.** Soviet forces crossed into
  eastern Hungary; the expanded borders became non-functional. This is
  the effective end-of-row event (exact date approximate; Treaty of
  Paris 1947-02-10 is the legal end date but the successor polity
  HUN-1944-1947 covers the transition period).
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 310, continuous.
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
