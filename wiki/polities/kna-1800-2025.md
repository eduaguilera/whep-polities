# Saint Kitts and Nevis

## Summary

Saint Kitts and Nevis is a two-island federal state in the Caribbean Leeward Islands. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. The islands were under British colonial administration throughout the nineteenth century; before 1967 the colony included Anguilla (~91 km²) alongside Saint Kitts (~168 km²) and Nevis (~93 km²), making the combined colonial unit approximately 352 km². Anguilla first separated in 1967 (Revolution) and was formally separated as a distinct British territory in 1980. The islands became an associated state in 1967 and achieved full independence on 19 September 1983. CShapes 2.0 records one time-step from 1983-09-19 (independence) onward at constant area (~261 km²), confirming no post-independence territorial change. Biger (1995) confirms the island state's area and capital. COW ccode=60 (stateabb=SKN); state-system entry 1983-09-19.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=60). CShapes records one time-step: 1983-09-19 to 2019-12-31, status=independent, area=0.017 sq deg (~261 km² land area). Biger (1995) gives: "area: 104 sq. miles [269 sq. km.]." The pre-independence polygon (1800–1983) is back-projected from the CShapes independence-era polygon. [biger-1995 §saint-kitts-and-nevis](../sources/biger-1995.md#saint-kitts-and-nevis)

**Anguilla approximation (pre-1967 data):** Before 1967, the British colony comprised Saint Kitts, Nevis, AND Anguilla (~91 km² out of ~352 km² total, approximately 26% of the combined colonial territory). The current GADM-4.1 polygon covers only Saint Kitts and Nevis (~261 km²) and therefore EXCLUDES Anguilla. Consequently, any pre-1967 data matched to KNA-1800-2025 (e.g., FAO 1952 label "Br West Indies St Kitts-Nevis Anguilla", years 1949–1951) understates the spatial unit by roughly 26%. This is acknowledged as a polygon approximation; no separate WHEP polity exists for the St Kitts-Nevis-Anguilla colonial unit, and the magnitude difference is small enough that routing to KNA-1800-2025 is the practical resolution for agricultural statistics of this period. polygon_status remains assigned with this documented limitation.

**Polygon status:** assigned (GADM-4.1 modern borders). Pre-1967 data matched here carries a ~26% territory understatement due to exclusion of Anguilla.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-independence-polygon

The WHEP row starts in 1800 but CShapes coverage starts in 1983 (independence). The polygon for 1800–1983 is implicitly back-projected. The key territorial change is the inclusion of Anguilla before 1967: the colonial unit was "Saint Kitts-Nevis-Anguilla" (~352 km²) until Anguilla's 1967 separation, vs. the current post-1980 KNA polygon (~261 km²). This ~26% discrepancy is documented in the Territorial extent section. If a more precise pre-1967 polygon is needed, a composed-union of GADM KNA + GADM Anguilla (AIA) polygons would yield the correct colonial-era extent.
