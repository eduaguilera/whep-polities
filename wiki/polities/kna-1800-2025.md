# Saint Kitts and Nevis

## Summary

Saint Kitts and Nevis is a two-island federal state in the Caribbean Leeward Islands. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. The islands were under British colonial administration throughout the nineteenth century, became an associated state in 1967, and achieved full independence on 19 September 1983. CShapes 2.0 records one time-step from 1983-09-19 (independence) onward at constant area, confirming no territorial change. Biger (1995) confirms the island state's area and capital. COW ccode=60 (stateabb=SKN); state-system entry 1983-09-19.
