# Equatorial Guinea (1886-1968)

## Summary

Spanish Guinea from 1 January 1886 (the start of CShapes coverage) to independence on 12 October 1968. This row was formed by merging GNQ-1886-1900 and GNQ-1900-1968, because CShapes records area 2.19 sq deg throughout 1886-1968 with no change at the 1900 Franco-Spanish Convention step. "The Franco-Spanish Convention of 27 June 1900 ... Spanish Guinea became the independent republic of Equatorial Guinea on 12 October 1968" [biger-1995 §equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea). "The boundary was formulated in the Franco-German agreements of 1885 and 1894 that outlined the European powers' spheres of influence in Africa" [biger-1995 §equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea). The territory comprises the mainland enclave of Rio Muni (between Cameroon and Gabon) and the islands of Bioko (formerly Fernando Poo), Annobon, and the Corisco group.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 2.19 sq deg for both steps (1886-01-01 to 1900-06-26, and 1900-06-27 to 1968-10-11), status=colony, owner=Spain (COW 230). Equatorial Guinea is bounded by Cameroon (118 mi; 189 km) and Gabon [biger-1995 §equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea). The boundary between Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea stretches for 118 miles (189 km) from the Gulf of Guinea eastward. The current boundary approximates the colonial boundary that had separated the German protectorate of Kamerun and Spanish Guinea. "The French established themselves on the coast of central Africa in the late nineteenth century and Cameroon was established as a German protectorate (Kamerun) on 12 July 1884. France and Germany first established a boundary between their spheres of influence in central Africa on 24 December 1885" [biger-1995 §equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea).

## Open questions

### oq-gnq-1900-convention

The Franco-Spanish Convention of 27 June 1900 appears to be the key boundary event. The full terms of this convention and how they shaped the mainland territory (Rio Muni) versus the island (Bioko/Fernando Po) should be documented. If better polygon data (higher resolution than CShapes) reveals a real area change at 1900, the merge should be reversed.

### oq-gnq-pre-1886-predecessor

The CSV records predecessor=NA for this row, but Spanish presence in Equatorial Guinea (Fernando Poo) predates 1886. A pre-1886 row for Spanish Guinea may be needed to close the chain. The WHEP coverage goal requires territory to be accounted for before 1886.
