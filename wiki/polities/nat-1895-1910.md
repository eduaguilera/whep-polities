# Natal (1895-1910)

## Summary

The British Colony of Natal from the 1895 CShapes boundary cut (approximately, the start of the Zululand-annexation process) to its 31 May 1910 incorporation into the Union of South Africa. Over this 15-year window Natal doubled in size with the 1897 Zululand annexation; participated in the 1899–1902 Second Boer War; and joined the Orange Free State, Transvaal, and Cape Colony in the 1910 Union.
