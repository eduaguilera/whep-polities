# Natal (1895-1910)

## Summary

The British Colony of Natal from the administrative incorporation of Zululand under Natal (CShapes 2.0 date: 1895-04-23) to its 31 May 1910 incorporation into the Union of South Africa. Over this 15-year window Natal participated in the 1899-1902 Second Boer War and joined the Orange Free State, Transvaal, and Cape Colony in the 1910 Union.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature id 562, vintage 1895-04-23. This is the post-expansion Natal polygon (Natal proper + Zululand), area 72,516 km². The pre-expansion Natal polygon (same feature id 562, vintage 1886) covers 43,663 km². The difference (28,853 km²) is consistent with the Zululand annexation roughly doubling Natal's coastal territory.

### 1895 vs 1897: resolution

Wikipedia dates the Zululand annexation to Natal at 1897 (the formal Natal Act legislative date). CShapes 2.0 records the territorial change at 1895-04-23 (administrative incorporation). WHEP uses the CShapes date (1895) as the boundary-change year because:

- CShapes 2.0 is the polygon source and its feature timestamps define which polygon vintage is attached to each WHEP period.
- The CShapes feature for cowcode=562 transitions from the 43,663 km² polygon to the 72,516 km² polygon on 1895-04-23.
- Wikipedia's 1897 refers to the Natal Legislative Assembly's formal annexation act; the actual administrative transfer predates this.

The proposed rename to NAT-1843-1897 / NAT-1897-1910 is therefore **rejected**. The 1895 split is correct and kept.

### Polygon fix (2026-06-30)

**Bug fixed:** NAT-1895-1910 was previously assigned polygon_feature_year=1886 (the pre-expansion vintage, 43,663 km²) instead of the correct 1895 vintage (72,516 km²). This was a silent ~40% underrepresentation of territory. Corrected to polygon_feature_year=1895, polygon_area_km2=72516.
