# Portugal

## Summary

This row tracks Portugal as a single continuous territorial-economic unit
from **1800** to **2025** — a **226-year unbroken row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Despite major colonial
losses (Brazil independence 1822, African decolonization 1974-75),
the CSV does not split at any point because **metropolitan Portuguese
territory did not change substantially** during the entire period.
Portugal's European borders have been essentially stable since the
1297 Treaty of Alcanices with Spain — one of the oldest continuous
boundaries in Europe.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
splits trigger on substantial territorial change to the polity itself.
Colonial losses are tracked as separate WHEP rows, so they don't trigger
a split on the metropolitan row.

- **1800 start** is the database floor, not a territorial event.
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Portugal is still active.

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records
  Portugal (`cowcode=235`) as a single static polygon from 1886-01-01
  to 2019-12-31 with area ~9.64 sq degrees
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). No polygon
  changes at all — Portugal's borders are static across CShapes's entire
  coverage.
- **Pre-1886 extent.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The same
  polygon is likely back-projected given the extreme border stability.
- **Metropolitan area.** ~92,000 km² (metropolitan). Population ~10M
  (1994 per Biger context).
- **Metropolitan stability.** Portugal's European borders with Spain
  have not changed since the Congress of Vienna (1815) confirmed
  borders unchanged since the 1297 Treaty of Alcanices. The
  Spain-Portugal boundary is one of the oldest in Europe.

## Sourced claims

- **Metropolitan borders unchanged.** CShapes records a single static
  polygon for Portugal across its entire 1886-2019 coverage
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). No European
  territorial changes. The France-Spain boundary section in Biger
  confirms the Pyrenees unchanged since 1659; the Spain-Portugal
  boundary is one of the oldest in Europe (~1297)
  [biger-1995 §france-spain](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-spain).
- **1822: Brazil independence.** Brazil declared independence from
  Portugal on 7 September 1822. Brazil is tracked as a separate
  WHEP row.
- **1910: Republic replaces monarchy.** The Portuguese Republic was
  proclaimed on 5 October 1910, ending the constitutional monarchy.
  Not a territorial change — no WHEP split under the territorial-change
  rule.
- **1974: Carnation Revolution.** The Carnation Revolution of 25 April
  1974 ended the Estado Novo dictatorship. Not a territorial change —
  no WHEP split.
- **1974-75: African decolonization.** Guinea-Bissau (1974),
  Mozambique (1975), Cape Verde (1975), Sao Tome and Principe (1975),
  Angola (1975). Portugal's last major colonial losses. Each colony is
  tracked as a separate WHEP row.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists Portugal (`POR`,
  `ccode=235`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). No breaks.
- **Europe's oldest nation-state; Reconquista completed mid-13th century** (p.442). Only land boundary with Spain (755 miles) (p.442). First worldwide seaborne empire 15th-16th centuries (p.442). Spanish occupation 1578-1640 (p.442). 1926 fascist coup; Salazar dictatorship (p.443). 1974 Revolution of Carnations (p.443). [biger-1995 §portugal](../sources/biger-1995.md#portugal)
