# Romania (1947-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **78 years**, from the Treaty of Paris
(northern Transylvania restored) to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Modern Romania: ~238,000 km². Borders stable since 1947. Permanent
losses from 1940 remain: Bessarabia (now Moldova) and Northern Bukovina
(Ukraine), Southern Dobruja (Bulgaria). Communist period 1947-1989.
1989 Revolution (Ceausescu executed). EU member 2007.

Predecessor: [rou-1940-1947](rou-1940-1947.md) -- reduced Romania
during WWII and immediate post-war period.
Successor: NA (still active).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` cowcode=360, feature_year=1946.
  CShapes 1946 vintage used because it captures the post-Paris borders:
  northern Transylvania was de facto reoccupied by Soviet forces from
  August 1944 and the 1947 Treaty was formal confirmation of a boundary
  already reflected in the 1946 CShapes feature. No territorial
  difference between CShapes 1946 and post-1947 Romania. Modern GADM
  consistent at ~237,500 km². No polygon_vintage_drift.
- **Area.** ~238,000 km². Includes northern Transylvania (~43,000 km²)
  restored from Hungary; excludes Bessarabia, Northern Bukovina,
  Southern Dobruja.
- **COW code 360** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Romania as continuous to 2024-12-31.

## Split rationale

Predecessor ROU-1940-2025 used a CShapes 1940 polygon (~194,000 km²)
for the entire 1940-2025 span. This understated Romania by ~22% for
1947-2025 data (missing northern Transylvania ~43,000 km²). This page
uses the CShapes 1946 polygon (~238,000 km²) which correctly represents
Romania after the 1947 Treaty of Paris restoration. See
[rou-1940-1947](rou-1940-1947.md) for the earlier period.
