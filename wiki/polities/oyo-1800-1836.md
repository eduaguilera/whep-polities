# Oyo Empire

## Summary

The Oyo Empire from the 1800 database floor to its final collapse c.1836. At its 18th-century peak the empire spanned over 150,000 km² of modern southwestern Nigeria and southern Benin. Over the 1800–1836 window Oyo's decline accelerated: Ilorin seceded in 1817 (and joined the Sokoto Caliphate); Dahomey revolted c.1823; the Egba massacred Oyo administrators; the Old Oyo capital Oyo-Ile was destroyed in 1835 by Ilorin forces. By 1836 the empire as a political unit had ceased to function.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Heartland in northern Yorubaland; tributary reach into Dahomey and southern Hausa zones at peak; progressively shrinking over the 1800–1836 decline.
