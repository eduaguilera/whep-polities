# Gabon (to 1912)

## Summary

French Gabon from the 1839 Bouët-Willaumez treaty with King Denis (Antchoué Komé Rapontcombo) establishing French presence at the Gabon Estuary, through the 1849 founding of Libreville, Brazza's explorations of the 1870s–1880s, incorporation into French Congo (1886), separation as its own colony (1903), and inclusion in the AEF from 1910 — to the CShapes-defined 1912 boundary cut. CSV notes: "Start corrected from 1800 to 1839 (first French treaty with King Denis at Gabon Estuary). Mpongwe and Orungu coastal polities before 1839."

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes begins effective coverage from 1886; the 1839–1885 coastal-presence window is an extrapolation. The 1903 administrative separation from French Congo is within the polygon's coverage.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. Pre-1839 the area was organised as a collection of Mpongwe and Orungu coastal polities (not modelled as WHEP rows).
- **Successor:** [gab-1912-1919](gab-1912-1919.md) — Gabon 1912–1919.
