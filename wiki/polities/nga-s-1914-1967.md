# Southern Nigeria (Southern Provinces / Eastern and Western Regions with Lagos)

## Summary

Southern Nigeria after the 1914 amalgamation: the Southern Provinces, split in 1939 into the Eastern and Western Provinces (Regions from 1951) with the Colony of Lagos, without the Southern Cameroons. The territory was unchanged until the regions gave way to twelve states in 1967. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §southern-nigeria](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#southern-nigeria)

## Why this entry exists

The yearbooks report the Southern Provinces (`british nigeria: south`, 1914-1960, 56 pairs in the label list) beside `british nigeria: north`; SNI-1906-1913 stops at amalgamation and no row was the southern half after it. The Eastern and Western Regions alone (`british nigeria: east`, `: west`) stay unrouted: no fetched polygon draws the line between them. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
