# Papal States (Lazio, 1860-1870)

## Summary

The rump Papal States after the annexations of 1860: Rome and Lazio, held under French protection
until the Italian capture of Rome on 20 September 1870
[wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome).
"By 1860, much of the Papal States' territory had been conquered by the Kingdom of Sardinia ...
except Lazio, which remained under the pope's control" [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §papal-states](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#papal-states). Created on 2026-10-07 by
splitting the old `PAP-1800-1870` row at 1860 (issue 686).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried the pre-1860 extent (about 39,300 km²) to 1870, so for
1860-1869 it claimed the Romagna, the Marche and Umbria, which were Italian, and overlapped
[ita-1861-1866](ita-1861-1866.md) and [ita-1866-1870](ita-1866-1870.md) by about 27,000 km².

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. CShapes-Europe Id 327, the 1862-1870 step (feature year 1862). It
measures 11,864 km² (Mollweide), against 39,258 km² for the 1816-1860 step; Rome (12.5 E, 41.9 N) is
inside, Bologna, Perugia and Ancona are not. This matches the Italian rows, which use CShapes-Europe
325's own 1862 step for the same border.

CShapes-Europe also carries a one-year step for 1861 (30,097 km², with Perugia and Ancona still
papal). It contradicts the November 1860 plebiscites and every other source here, so it is not used:
the 1860-1861 years of this row use the 1862 step as well.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [pap-1800-1860](pap-1800-1860.md) -- the Papal States before the 1860 annexations.
- **Successors:** [ita-1870-1919](ita-1870-1919.md), which took Rome and Lazio in 1870, and
  [vat-1929-2025](vat-1929-2025.md), the Holy See's state from the Lateran Treaty (1929).

## Sourced claims

- **1860: annexations.** The Romagna, the Marche and Umbria went to Sardinia and then to the Kingdom of
  Italy; Lazio stayed papal [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §papal-states](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#papal-states).
- **1870-09-20: capture of Rome**, ending "more than one thousand years of Papal temporal power"
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome).
- **COW.** 327 [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Contradictions

- CShapes-Europe's 1861 step keeps the Marche and Umbria papal for one more year; see above.

## Decisions

- **Split from PAP-1800-1870 (2026-10-07, issue 686).** `end_year` is exclusive and the handover year
  goes to the incoming row, so 1860 belongs to this row.
