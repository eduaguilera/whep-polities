# Hautes-Alpes (département)

## Summary

Hautes-Alpes is a French département in the southeastern Alpine interior, one of the original départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform out of the southeastern portion of the historic province of Dauphiné, centred on Gap, Briançon, and Embrun. It is entered here as `type: subnational` because a département is a sub-unit of the sovereign French state, not a state in its own right: France's own sovereignty and national-scale statistics belong to the FRA-* national chain (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), and this row exists solely to carry data that a source tabulates at department scale for this specific unit. The routing decision found no existing polity match by name, confirmed this unit is not one of the four documented FRA departure cases (Territoire de Belfort, the Alsace-Lorraine trio, the Corsica 1793/1975 split, or an overseas département) that would require a non-default span, and so applied the country's default rule for an original Revolutionary département: creation-year start (1790) and an open end (2025), since the department is still current.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit FRA-FRL02 ("Hautes-Alpes") from the agent-harness routing pipeline. Before this entry, the unit had no polity match in the database at all — routing found no name match among existing rows, and Hautes-Alpes is not one of the four documented FRA departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, the Corsica split, or an overseas département) that would call for a different span rule than the country default. Routing it to the national FRA-1919-2025 row instead would have folded a ~5,500 km² administrative subdivision into a national total roughly 100 times larger in area, exactly the kind of scale mismatch that is itself evidence the data was previously unmatched or would have been mis-routed. What confirms this is a distinct, addressable reporting unit rather than an arbitrary carve-out is straightforward administrative continuity: it has been one of France's original 1790 départements for 235 years, treated as a first-class INSEE reporting geography throughout, which is exactly the persistent unit type the country's routing convention directs should get its own row absent one of the four listed exceptions. This mirrors the immediately preceding FRA-04-1790-2025 (Alpes-de-Haute-Provence) entry in every structural respect: same routing logic, same span basis, same unresolved container and polygon gaps.

Territory description for a reader without the geometry: Hautes-Alpes sits in the southeastern French Alps, within the Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur region, bordering Isère and Drôme to the north/northwest, Alpes-de-Haute-Provence to the south, Alpes-Maritimes to the southeast, and the Italian border (Piedmont) to the east; its administrative seat is Gap, with Briançon as the historic fortress town on the Italian frontier. ## Predecessors and successors

Hautes-Alpes has no predecessor or successor row in this table. It is one of the original départements created directly by the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform, formed from parts of the historic province of Dauphiné (specifically its southeastern Alpine districts around Gap, Briançon, and Embrun), which had been part of the Kingdom of France since 1349. It has not been split, merged, or renamed since 1790 and remains a current French département, so end_year is open at 2025. ### d-code-choice-fra-05

**Chose FRA-05-1790-2025, the INSEE department number, following the dominant table pattern and FRA precedent**

The dominant subnational pattern in the table (338 of 351 rows) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and existing FRA subnational rows already use INSEE department numbers as the subunit token (FRA-02, FRA-04, FRA-08, FRA-14, FRA-18, FRA-19, FRA-21 through FRA-28, and others). Hautes-Alpes is INSEE department 05, so FRA-05-1790-2025 both matches the cross-country default and the country-specific precedent already established for two dozen other French départements, including the immediately adjacent FRA-04-1790-2025 (Alpes-de-Haute-Provence), which this entry mirrors structurally since the routing decision, span basis, and unresolved container gap are identical. FRA-05 was checked against wiki/polities and is unused; no existing page or code collides with it.

### d-start-year-1790-not-1800

**Used 1790 (the department's actual creation) as start_year, per the routing decision's own span_basis, not 1800**

The routing decision explicitly derives start_year 1790 from Hautes-Alpes' creation as one of the original Revolutionary départements, unaffected by the four documented FRA departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica split, overseas départements), and leaves end_year open at 2025 since the department is still current. The schema requires the code's years to equal the frontmatter's years, so the code is FRA-05-1790-2025, not FRA-05-1800-2025. This produces the same 1790-1800 containment gap already flagged and left open on sibling entries FRA-04-1790-2025, and (per that page) FRA-PYRO-1790-2025 and FRA-HP-1790-2025 before it; it is recorded here as an open question rather than silently truncated or resolved unilaterally, so that a future FRA-1790-1800 container row (if added) can be applied identically across all of these entries at once rather than piecemeal.
