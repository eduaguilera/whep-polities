# Canada

## Summary

> **Boundary corrected 2026-08-05 — this row starts at the accession, not the referendum
> (issue 15).** It replaces `CAN-1948-2025`, which started in **1948** because CShapes
> dates its Canadian boundary change to `1948-07-22`, the second Newfoundland referendum.
> The accession was **31 March 1949**.
>
> The consequence was measurable, not cosmetic. This row's polygon includes Newfoundland,
> and `NFL-1907-1949` covers 1907-1948, so for calendar 1948 two live polities claimed
> the same 398,084 km² — **100.0% of Newfoundland**, measured on the shipped GeoPackage.
> Starting at 1949 removes the overlap exactly.
>
> It also removes a workaround. A year-scoped alias pinned `canada` 1948 to
> `CAN-1886-1948` *because* this row's start year was wrong — the alias was territorially
> correct while pointing at a polity whose own columns did not cover 1948. That alias now
> points at [can-1886-1949](can-1886-1949.md), which does cover it, so it is an ordinary
> alias rather than a compensation.
>
> And it fixes a dangling reference: the replaced page listed
> `predecessor: [CAN-1866-1948]`, **a code that has never existed**.

This row tracks Canada for **76 years**, modern Canada from 1949 to the
database truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Newfoundland joined Confederation 31 March 1949 -- the territorial event
triggering this row. Nunavut Territory created 1 April 1999. Area ~10M
km², second-largest country by area. Stable external borders throughout.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=20`.
- **Area.** ~9.98M km². Stable external borders since 1949.

## Sourced claims

- **Area: 3,851,791 sq mi (9,976,140 sq km).** "The largest country
  on the American continent." Capital: Ottawa
  [biger-1995 §canada](../sources/biger-1995.md#canada).
- **US boundary: longest in the world.** Canada–US boundary runs
  3,987 miles (6,416 km) in the south plus 1,538 miles (2,477 km)
  for the Alaska boundary — "The longest land boundary between any
  two countries in the world"
  [biger-1995 §canada](../sources/biger-1995.md#canada).
- **Boundary stability.** "Since 1905 the boundaries between Canada
  and the United States have been well maintained"
  [biger-1995 §canada](../sources/biger-1995.md#canada).
- **1949-03-31: Newfoundland joins.** Confederation via referendum.
  Start-of-row territorial event.
  [biger-1995 §canada](../sources/biger-1995.md#canada)
- **1999-04-01: Nunavut created.** Internal territorial reorganization
  (carved from NWT). No external border change.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
