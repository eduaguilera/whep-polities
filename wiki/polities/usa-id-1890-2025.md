# Idaho (state of the United States)

## Summary

Idaho, one of the fifty states of the United States, admitted to the Union on July 3, 1890 as the 43rd state, organized out of Idaho Territory. This entry is `type: subnational` because it does not compete with the national USA chain for sovereignty: Idaho has never been independently sovereign, and the row exists purely to carry state-level reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the state rather than the national level. The span 1890-2025 tracks continuous statehood, with no interruption or territorial change, through the two national USA polity eras spanning that period (USA-1867-1959 and USA-1959-2025).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Idaho as a distinct subnational reporting unit within the United States, created by the agent-harness pipeline processing the administrative unit `USA-IDAHO` ("Idaho", country United States of America). No prior polity row existed for Idaho at the state level -- the existing USA chain covers the country as a whole, so any Idaho-specific statistics would previously have had no state-level home and would either be absent from the database or folded into national USA totals, a mismatch whenever a source reports Idaho separately from all-U.S. figures. The routing decision names no specific source or row count (unlike the Manchuria exemplar), framing this instead as adding the state as a real, distinct administrative entity with no existing candidate polity -- consistent with a systematic pass creating one row per U.S. state, following the same pattern already used for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025). What confirms Idaho is a distinct reporting/administrative entity is uncontroversial: it is one of the fifty states, with its own government and long-standing use as a state-level unit in economic and demographic series (e.g., USDA, Census Bureau, Mitchell-style historical compilations).

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Idaho is a standard U.S. state with no distinct historical name of its own separate from statehood (unlike Alaska Territory, which carries the bespoke ALK-1867-1959 row for its pre-statehood territorial identity), so it follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows: ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The subunit token is ID, the USPS/ISO 3166-2:US code for Idaho, matching the two-letter convention already used for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025) and the Spanish provinces added in this same harness run. The full code is USA-ID-1890-2025.

### d-start-year-admission

**Start year is 1890 admission to the Union, per the country default rule**

The country convention's default rule for U.S. states is to start at formal admission to the Union unless the state is a departure case (Alaska, Hawaii, or an original 13 state). Idaho is not among the enumerated departures, and Idaho Territory (1863-1890) is not itself a polity row in this database, so 1890 is used directly rather than back-cast to the earlier territorial period. Data from 1879-1889, which predates statehood, is not covered by this entry's span; it would need separate back-cast routing if present in any source, which is left as an open question below.

### d-two-container-edges

**Two container edges tile 1890-2025 across the successive national USA rows**

Idaho's 1890-2025 span crosses exactly one boundary in the national USA polity chain: the 1959 transition from USA-1867-1959 to USA-1959-2025 (the year Alaska and Hawaii joined, expanding the union to fifty states). Two container edges, 1890-1959 and 1959-2025, tile the full span with no gap, following the same pattern as USA-AL-1819-2025's four edges (Idaho needs fewer because it enters the Union later, after three of the five national eras already dividing Alabama's history).

## Open questions

### oq-pre-statehood-data

**Pre-1890 Idaho Territory data has no polity home**

The routing decision's own reasoning notes that data as early as 1879 may exist for the geographic area now called Idaho, predating the 1890 admission date used as this entry's start_year. Idaho Territory (1863-1890) is not represented as its own polity row in this database, so any pre-1890 Idaho-labeled statistics currently have no state-level destination and would either be dropped, misrouted to the national USA chain, or require a new back-cast rule extending this entry's predecessor chain. This should be checked against the actual source data once ingestion runs against this new code, rather than assumed resolved by the page's existence.

### oq-boundary-stability-uncorroborated

**Claim of unchanged boundaries since 1890 is not independently sourced**

This page asserts that Idaho's external boundary has been stable since admission in 1890, following the same reasoning used for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025), but this has not been checked against a historical atlas or boundary-history source for Idaho specifically. Idaho's borders involved several adjustments during the territorial period (disputes with Montana Territory over the panhandle, and the Wyoming Territory boundary), and while these predate 1890, no source has been consulted to confirm no adjustment occurred after admission. If one is found, the GADM 4.1 modern polygon (feature USA.13_1) may not validly proxy the full 1890-2025 span.
