# Honduras

## Summary

Honduras is a Central American country situated between the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. Biger 1995 records its area as 43,277 sq. miles (112,044 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 5,240,000 in 1994, bounded by Guatemala, El Salvador, and Nicaragua [biger-1995 &sect;honduras](../sources/biger-1995.md#honduras). Columbus arrived in 1502, permanent Spanish settlement was established in 1522, and Honduras was part of Guatemala after 1570. The Central American colonies separated from Europe in 1821, and Honduras withdrew from the United Provinces of Central America in 1838. WHEP tracks Honduras as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory remained essentially constant.

## Territorial extent

Honduras has an area of 43,277 sq. miles (112,044 sq. km.) and is bounded by Guatemala (159 miles; 256 km.) to the west, El Salvador (213 miles; 342 km.) to the south, and Nicaragua (573 miles; 922 km.) to the southeast [biger-1995 &sect;honduras](../sources/biger-1995.md#honduras). - **Polygon approximation documented (2026-06-25):** The CShapes 2.0 polygon (feature 91, vintage 1886) is retained as an approximate proxy for the full 1800-2025 span. The 1886 polygon predates two unresolved border disputes: (1) Honduras-Nicaragua, settled by the 1906 King of Spain arbitration (confirmed by ICJ 1960); (2) Honduras-El Salvador, settled by the ICJ 1992 ruling. Both disputed zones are small relative to total Honduran area (~112,044 km2), estimated combined under 5-10% of territory. Staple production magnitudes (rice ~13 kt, maize ~174 kt, sugar ~18 kt) are consistent with whole-country Honduras, so no data-routing error is present. `polygon_status` changed from `assigned` to `approximate`. A potential future split at 1906 (HND-1800-1906 / HND-1906-2025) is deferred pending identification of a suitable post-1906 CShapes or Cliopatria polygon.
