# Honduras

## Summary

Honduras is a Central American country situated between the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. Biger 1995 records its area as 43,277 sq. miles (112,044 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 5,240,000 in 1994, bounded by Guatemala, El Salvador, and Nicaragua [biger-1995 &sect;honduras](../sources/biger-1995.md#honduras). Columbus arrived in 1502, permanent Spanish settlement was established in 1522, and Honduras was part of Guatemala after 1570. The Central American colonies separated from Europe in 1821, and Honduras withdrew from the United Provinces of Central America in 1838. WHEP tracks Honduras as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory remained essentially constant.
