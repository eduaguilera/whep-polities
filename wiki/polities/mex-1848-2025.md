# Mexico

## Summary

This row tracks Mexico from **1848** to **2025** — a **178-year row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The row begins
after the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo (2 February 1848). The
Gadsden Purchase (1854, -77K km2) is sub-threshold at -3.9%
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Territory has
been stable at ~1.96M km2 since 1854.

This is post-Mexican Cession Mexico. Major political events within
the row include the French intervention and Second Mexican Empire
under Maximilian (1862-1867), the Porfirio Díaz dictatorship
(1876-1911), the Mexican Revolution (1910-1920), and the
establishment of modern democratic Mexico. None of these triggered
CSV splits because borders remained stable.

Successor: NA (still active as of 2025).

## Sourced claims

- **1848-02-02: Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo.** Start-of-row event.
  Post-cession Mexico.
- **1854: Gadsden Purchase.** Mexico sold ~77,000 km2 to the US
  (La Mesilla). Sub-threshold territorial change (-3.9%)
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **1862-1867: French intervention.** France invaded Mexico;
  Maximilian I installed as Emperor (Second Mexican Empire,
  1864-1867). Executed 19 June 1867. Republic restored under
  Benito Juárez. No territorial change.
- **1876-1911: Porfiriato.** Porfirio Díaz ruled Mexico as
  dictator (with one interruption 1880-1884). Modernization
  and foreign investment but deep inequality.
- **1910-1920: Mexican Revolution.** Major civil conflict.
  Constitutional convention produced the 1917 Constitution
  (still in force). No border changes.
- **1960: Chamizal dispute resolved.** Minor border adjustment
  with the US along the Rio Grande near El Paso. Formally
  settled 1963, territory transferred 1967.
