# Kingdom of Rwanda

## Summary

The Kingdom of Rwanda is a WHEP polity row covering the pre-colonial Rwandan kingdom
from 1800 to 1890. The 1800 start date is a database truncation — the kingdom existed
continuously from at least the 15th century. The 1890 end year reflects the onset of
German East Africa's sphere of influence over Rwanda, which came under formal colonial
authority from approximately that date; Rwanda was subsequently consolidated as part of
German East Africa and later the Ruanda-Urundi Belgian mandate. The WHEP row uses a
Paine et al. (2024) polygon, as this pre-colonial entity predates CShapes' 1886 coverage.

## Territorial extent

The Kingdom of Rwanda occupied the territory of modern Rwanda — the Great Lakes highland
region of East-Central Africa, roughly bounded by the Virunga volcanoes to the north,
Lake Kivu to the west, the Rusizi River and Lake Tanganyika corridor to the southwest,
and the Akagera River to the east. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024); spatial
precision is approximate for the pre-colonial period.

## Sourced claims

- "Colonized by the Germans in the late nineteenth century, the region, along with
  neighboring Burundi, was conquered by the Belgians in World War I." (p.453).
  [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- "After World War I, German East Africa, consisting of Rwanda-Urundi and Tanganyika,
  was administered under League of Nations mandates with Belgium responsible for
  Ruanda-Urundi ... and Britain administering Tanganyika until its independence in 1961."
  (p.490). This confirms that the pre-colonial Rwandan kingdom was absorbed into German
  East Africa and then passed to Belgian administration.
  [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)
- "Ruanda-Urundi became independent as two separate nations on 1 July 1962." (p.457).
  The long chain from the pre-colonial kingdom through colonial period to independence
  is established by this lineage. [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- CShapes 2.0 does not record Rwanda as a separate unit before independence (cowcode 517
  enters only in 1962); the pre-colonial RWK row is outside CShapes' temporal scope
  (1886–2019) and CShapes' political scope (sovereign states and dependencies, not
  pre-colonial kingdoms). [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Open questions

### oq-german-incorporation-date

Precise date of German incorporation. The CSV end year of 1890 is approximate. Germany
established formal authority over Rwanda gradually between 1884 (Berlin Conference) and
1898 (first German resident in Kigali). The exact WHEP transition year needs confirmation
from a sourced account of German East Africa administration.

### oq-rwk-territory-overlap

Territory overlap with BDK-1800-1890. The Kingdom of Burundi (BDK) existed concurrently
with RWK. The two kingdoms shared the Ruanda-Urundi region. Whether the Paine et al.
polygons for RWK and BDK overlap or are cleanly partitioned needs spatial verification.
