# Ireland

## Summary

This row tracks Ireland as a territorial-economic unit from **1921**
(Anglo-Irish Treaty) to **2025** (database ceiling)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 105-year window
covers the Irish Free State era (1922–1937), Éire (1937–1949), and the
Republic of Ireland (from 1949).

The **1921 start** is a real territorial event under the WHEP
polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition):
the Anglo-Irish Treaty (signed 6 December 1921) partitioned Ireland,
creating the Irish Free State from 26 of Ireland's 32 counties, while 6
Ulster counties remained as Northern Ireland within the UK. Biger confirms
the border was "formed in 1920 when Ireland was partitioned" and stretches
224 miles (360 km)
[biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland). Britain recognized
the Irish Free State as "an autonomous dominion within the British Empire"
[biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland).

CShapes 2.0 records Ireland (cowcode=205) as a **single time-step** from
1922-12-06 to 2019-12-31 at **9.3516 sq degrees**
[cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The single
CShapes entry — with no area changes — confirms that Ireland's territory
has been stable since the 1922 Irish Free State constitution, and one WHEP
row for the entire 1921–2025 period is correct.

- **1921 start** matches the Anglo-Irish Treaty date (6 December 1921).
  CShapes uses 1922-12-06 (Irish Free State constitution effective);
  the CSV follows the treaty date. See
  [oq-1921-vs-1922-start-date](#oq-1921-vs-1922-start-date) for the
  open question on this discrepancy.
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Ireland is still active.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records
  Ireland (`cowcode=205`) from 1922-12-06 to 2019-12-31 at a constant
  area of **9.3516 sq degrees**
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). No area
  changes — the territory of the Irish Free State (later Republic of
  Ireland) has been geographically stable since 1922.
- **Northern Ireland not included.** The 1921 partition left 6 Ulster
  counties (Northern Ireland) within the UK. This WHEP row covers the
  26-county entity only. Northern Ireland is tracked as a separate
  subnational WHEP row `GBNIR-1921-2025`
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **The border.** Ireland–United Kingdom land boundary is 224 miles
  (360 km), formed in 1920 at partition
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland).
- **Pre-1922 gap.** CShapes begins Ireland's polygon at 1922-12-06.
  The period from the treaty date (1921-12-06) to the Free State
  constitution (1922-12-06) is a transitional year in which CShapes
  still attributes the territory to the UK polygon. WHEP's 1921 start
  is earlier than CShapes' first polygon entry.

## Predecessors and successors

Coverage goal: every km² must be accounted for.

- **Predecessor:** [gbr-1800-1921](gbr-1800-1921.md) — the Anglo-Irish
  Treaty split 26 of Ireland's 32 counties from the UK to form the Irish
  Free State. The GBR-1800-1921 row ends at 1921, the year of the treaty.
- **Successor:** NA — still active at 2025.

The Northern Ireland territory is tracked separately as
`GBNIR-1921-2025` (subnational, under GBR), which absorbed the 6 Ulster
counties that remained in the UK at partition.

## Sourced claims

- **1800 annexation.** "In 1800 Ireland was annexed to Britain."
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland). This is the
  history of the predecessor entity (GBR-1800-1921).
- **1916 Easter Rising and War of Independence.** "The Bloody Easter revolt
  broke out in Dublin, followed by a full-scale war of independence."
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland)
- **Irish Free State status.** "Britain recognized the Irish Free State as
  an autonomous dominion within the British Empire, but imposed partition
  and negotiated a separate status for Northern Ireland."
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland)
- **Partition border.** "The Ireland–United Kingdom boundary is 224 miles
  (360 km), formed in 1920 when Ireland was partitioned in an effort to
  balance the aspirations of Catholic Irish nationalists for a free
  independent Ireland, and Protestant Ulster unionists, who wanted to
  remain British."
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland)
- **Border never accepted.** "This border has never been accepted by the
  Republic of Ireland, which includes in its constitution a determination
  to reunite the island."
  [biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland)
- **CShapes single time-step, constant area.** CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=205)
  records one entry: independent/owner=205 from 1922-12-06 to
  2019-12-31, area 9.3516 sq degrees. No polygon changes — stable
  territory since the Irish Free State.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **COW state-system entry 1922-12-06.** COW lists Ireland (`IRE`,
  `ccode=205`) with a single tenure from 1922-12-06 to 2024-12-31.
  COW's entry date matches the Irish Free State constitution date, not
  the treaty signing date.
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)

## Contradictions

**Treaty date vs. Free State date for this row's start.** The CSV
uses 1921 as Ireland's start year (matching the Anglo-Irish Treaty,
6 December 1921). CShapes and COW both use 1922-12-06 (when the Irish
Free State constitution became law). The GBR-1800-1921 page notes
the same tension:
"CShapes records the polygon split at exactly 1921-12-06… The Irish
Free State formally came into existence on 6 December 1922"
[gbr-1800-1921](gbr-1800-1921.md). The database currently follows the
treaty date (1921), consistent with CShapes' intermediate row
(1921-12-06 to 1922-12-05 still attributed to UK polygon). This is a
WHEP date-precision question, not a territorial disagreement.
See [oq-1921-vs-1922-start-date](#oq-1921-vs-1922-start-date).

## Decisions

- **[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)** —
  the territorial-change rule. The 1921 split from GBR is justified
  (substantial territorial change — 26 counties leave the UK).

## Open questions

### oq-1921-vs-1922-start-date

**Treaty date (1921) vs. Free State date (1922) for this row's start.**
The CSV starts this row at 1921 (Anglo-Irish Treaty signed 6 December
1921). CShapes and COW both use 1922-12-06 (Irish Free State constitution
effective). The 1921–1922 year is a genuine transitional period with
legal ambiguity. Under WHEP's territorial-change criterion, the relevant
event is when the territory actually separated, which CShapes places at
1922-12-06. A future audit of FT trade statistics may reveal whether
Ireland had separate trade reporting from 1921 or from 1922 — that
empirical evidence would settle the row start date.

### oq-irish-constitutional-claim

**Irish constitutional claim to Northern Ireland (Articles 2–3, pre-1999).**
Biger notes the Republic of Ireland's constitution included "a
determination to reunite the island"
[biger-1995 §ireland](../sources/biger-1995.md#ireland). The Good Friday
Agreement (1998) removed this territorial claim from the constitution
(effective 1999). CShapes shows no polygon change at this event — the
claim was symbolic, not a de facto border adjustment. No WHEP split
is warranted, but the political history is relevant context.

### oq-emissions-data-overlap

Whether FT or FAOSTAT trade statistics for Ireland in the 1921–1922
transition period are attributed to the UK or to Ireland is not yet
confirmed. If Ireland's trade figures are reported under the UK umbrella
until 1922 (or later), the effective WHEP data start might be later than
1921. A data-sources audit would resolve this.
