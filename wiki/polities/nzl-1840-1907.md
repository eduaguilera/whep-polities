# New Zealand (colony)

## Summary

New Zealand (colony) tracks the British colonial period from the Treaty of Waitangi (1840) to the grant of dominion status (1907). Biger notes that New Zealand "underwent various waves of settlement before officially becoming part of the British Empire in the 1840s" and that "The 1840 Treaty of Waitangi between the British and most major Maori chiefs acknowledged, without really protecting, Maori land rights." The 1907 transition to Dominion of New Zealand is purely a sovereignty/status change — CShapes records identical area (~29.15 sq deg) before and after 1907-09-01. This means the WHEP split at 1907 is based on the status change rather than a territorial change; however, as the predecessor and successor rows have the same CShapes cowcode (920), this split is borderline under the WHEP polity definition and could potentially be merged. The current CSV treats it as a distinct row.

## Contradictions

The WHEP split at 1907 corresponds to the colony-to-dominion status change, not a territorial change. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule (same area = same WHEP polity), this split could be considered inconsistent — the CShapes area is identical before and after 1907-09-01. However, the current database maintains the split, possibly for compatibility with data sources that distinguish colonial and post-colonial periods. No explicit justification for maintaining the split has been found in `docs/` or `wiki/log.md`.

## Open questions

### oq-1907-split-justification

The NZL-1840-1907 / NZL-1907-2025 split at dominion status is a status change, not a territorial change. CShapes records the same area before and after 1907-09-01. Under the WHEP definition this should be a single continuous row. A human reviewer should decide whether to merge these rows or document a specific justification for keeping them separate (e.g., trade-data structure, source coverage differences for colonial vs. post-colonial periods).

### oq-pre-1886-polygon

The 1840-1885 period is outside CShapes' scope. The polygon is back-projected from the 1886 entry. The actual territorial extent during 1840-1885 may have differed from 1886 (particularly regarding Maori-controlled land vs. Crown land). A pre-1886 atlas source is needed to verify.
