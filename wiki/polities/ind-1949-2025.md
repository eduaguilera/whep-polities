# India

## Summary

CSV row for India from **1949** to **2025** — a **77-year row**
covering the Republic of India
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 750
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Key territorial changes within the row: Goa acquisition from
Portugal (1961), Sikkim accession (1975). Area ~3.3M km2.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature 750, step `1949-2019`. Published area **3,151,797 km²**
(equal-area `ESRI:54034`), against 3,152,040 km² on the raw step — the 0.008% gap is
`build_database.py`'s write-time simplification.

**That single step is CShapes' whole account of independent India.** Feature 750 has eight steps
between 1886 and 2019 and the last of them runs 1949-2019 unchanged, so the source encodes
**neither** the December 1961 annexation of Goa **nor** the 1975 accession of Sikkim. Measured on
every one of the eight steps:

```
CShapes 750, each step 1886-2019     ∩ GADM Goa     3,641 km²
CShapes 750, each step 1886-2019     ∩ GADM Sikkim  6,982 km²
```

So this polygon is India at its **terminal** extent for the whole 77-year row, and the
overstatement in the row's early years is not a binding error — there is no other step to bind to.
Quantified against the published geometry:

| overstated territory | km² | % of this row | wrong for | of the row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ptind-1816-1961](ptind-1816-1961.md) Portuguese India (Goa, Daman, Diu) | 3,719 | 0.118% | 1949-1961 | 13 of 77 years |
| [frin-1816-1954](frin-1816-1954.md) French India (Puducherry et al.) | 520 | 0.017% | 1949-1954 | 6 of 77 years |
| Sikkim (protectorate until 1975; **no WHEP polity**) | 6,988 | 0.222% | 1949-1975 | 27 of 77 years |

The first two are **double claims** — `PTIND-1816-1961` and `FRIN-1816-1954` publish the same
ground for the same years, so any area-weighted allocation counts it twice. Sikkim is not: no
polity claims it, so it is an overstatement of India only.

> **The five earlier `IND` periods do NOT have this problem any more (issue 84).**
> `IND-1800-1886` and `IND-1886-1893` … `IND-1947-1949` are now constructed as their own base
> geometry MINUS Portuguese India MINUS French India (and, for `IND-1800-1886`, minus Ceylon),
> because every one of them ends before the enclaves became Indian and the subtraction there needs
> no judgement. This row is the only `IND` period that straddles the annexation dates.

## The 1954/1961 split was considered and declined (issue 84, 2026-08-13)

Issue 84 left one question open after the five earlier periods were cut: whether this row should
be split at October 1954 (Pondicherry ceded) and December 1961 (Goa annexed) so that the enclaves
could be subtracted from the early part. **Decided: no split.** Three measured reasons, in order
of weight.

1. **The split would rename the modern-India row, and the rename is not local to this
   repository.** Splitting produces `IND-1949-1962` plus `IND-1962-2025`; the code `IND-1949-2025`
   would cease to exist. It is referenced by name outside this repository —
   `tests/testthat/test_build_cbs.R` in the consuming `whep` R package — and inside it by two
   alias rows that span the cut (`faostat` India 1961-2024 and `and-share` 1961-2009), by
   `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`, and by the `successor:` field of both enclave rows
   plus `KJM-1947-1972`. A 0.13% territorial correction is not worth breaking the identity of the
   flagship national series.

2. **Sikkim is nearly twice as large as Goa and gets no split, so splitting for Goa would be
   inconsistent.** 6,988 km² wrong for 27 years versus 3,719 km² wrong for 13. Being consistent
   would mean cutting this row at 1954, 1962 **and** 1975 — four rows for modern India — and the
   Sikkim cut would leave 6,988 km² attributed to no polity at all, since WHEP has no Sikkim row.

3. **No source supports the split geometrically.** CShapes stops at one step in 1949, so both
   halves would need constructed polygons; the post-1961 half would be the CShapes step
   unchanged, i.e. a new polity code created solely to carry an unchanged geometry.

**What is accepted, explicitly:** for 1949-1961 this row's polygon overstates India by 4,239 km²
(0.135%), of which every square kilometre is also claimed by a coexisting WHEP polity. Any spatial
use of this row before 1962 should treat that as a known ceiling. The corresponding note is on
[ptind-1816-1961](ptind-1816-1961.md) and [frin-1816-1954](frin-1816-1954.md) so the double claim
is readable from both ends.

**What this decision does not settle:** `validate_spatial_containment` cannot see the pair. This
row holds three contemporaneous polities of other families — `KJM-1947-1972` (legitimate: CShapes
assigns Kashmir by de facto control) plus the two enclaves — so it is exempted at container level
in `LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS`, which necessarily silences the two illegitimate ones. Pair-level
exemptions (issue 84's option 3) would express this properly and remain unbuilt; they would
surface nothing new today, because every other such pair is a single swallow and below the gate's
`>=3` threshold regardless.

## Open questions

### oq-goa-sikkim-mid-row-splits — CLOSED 2026-08-13 (issue 84)

Goa (1961) and Sikkim (1975) are territorial acquisitions within this row, and under a strict
WHEP polity-definition reading both are candidate mid-row split points. **Neither split is
taken**, and the reasoning is measured in "The 1954/1961 split was considered and declined"
above: 3,719 and 6,988 km² on a 3,151,797 km² row, against a rename of the modern-India polity
code that is referenced outside this repository. The consequence — that this row's polygon is
India at its terminal extent throughout, overstated by 0.135% before 1962 and 0.222% before
1975 — is now documented under "Territorial extent" rather than left implicit.
