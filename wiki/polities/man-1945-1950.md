# Manchuria (region, 1945-1950)

## Summary

The Manchuria region — the three northeastern provinces of China (Liaoning
[then also called Liaoning-Fengtien/Fengtian], Jilin/Kirin, and
Heilongjiang) — spanning Japan's surrender and Manchukuo's dissolution on
18 August 1945 through the proclamation of the People's Republic of China
and its consolidation of the northeast by 1950. This is a **subnational
region entry, not a sovereign state**: during this window the region was
contested and then held in succession by Republic of China / Kuomintang
forces, the Soviet occupying army (until early 1946), and the Chinese
Communist Party (from the 1945-46 race for Manchuria through the decisive
1948 Liaoshen/Liaoxi-Shenyang campaign), before formal incorporation into
the PRC. `type: subnational` is deliberate: this entry must not compete
with the national CHN chain (CHN-1945-1947 / CHN-1947-1949 / CHN-1949-1950)
in the matcher's family/sovereignty ranking — China held nominal and then
actual sovereignty throughout, but agricultural production statistics
report the Manchuria region separately from all-China totals, so a
dedicated regional entry is needed purely to route that reporting-territory
distinction correctly.

**Why this entry exists:** Mitchell's "China, Manchuria province of" data
label recurs continuously 1928-1952 at **regional** scale — e.g. wheat
production of roughly 300,000-1,400,000 tonnes/year, versus whole-China
wheat totals of roughly 18-20 million tonnes/year in the same era. For
1932-1945 this routes correctly to [man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md)
(Manchukuo). Before 1932 and after 1950 it falls back to the national CHN
row (Manchuria being ordinary Chinese territory outside the Manchukuo
period). But for 1945-1949 **no dedicated Manchuria-region polity existed**,
so the series was folding up into the national row
[chn-1947-1949](chn-1947-1949.md), overstating the reporting territory (a
national row absorbing what is actually a ~5-7% of national-output regional
series). See that page's open question
[oq-manchuria-region-1945-1950](chn-1947-1949.md#oq-manchuria-region-1945-1950)
and its data-quality note
[mitchell-manchuria-series-folded-up](chn-1947-1949.md#mitchell-manchuria-series-folded-up)
for the full discovery record. This entry closes that gap by extending
regional coverage one polity forward from Manchukuo, consistent with how
Manchukuo itself was created to hold the 1932-1945 span of the same label.

Approved by Catalin Covaci (2026-07-27).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `constructed` source, feature `MAN-1932-1945` reused as a
`period_proxy` (method) with `polygon_confidence: high`. Built by
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` as the union of three modern Chinese
provinces from GADM 4.1 admin-1 data:

- `CHN.11_1` Heilongjiang
- `CHN.17_1` Jilin
- `CHN.18_1` Liaoning

**Area:** **791,708 km2**, computed from the attached geometry in an
equal-area projection (ESRI:54034) — necessarily identical to
MAN-1932-1945, since the feature is reused. This is ~15x smaller than
contemporaneous whole-China territory, consistent with the regional scale of
the data this row receives (wheat ~300k-1.4M t/yr against whole-China totals
of ~18-20M t).

**Territorial difference from the reused Manchukuo (MAN-1932-1945) feature:**
Manchukuo additionally claimed Rehe/Jehol province (annexed 1933; roughly
modern western Liaoning + eastern Inner Mongolia), which the reused polygon
already **excludes** (see
[man-1932-1945#oq-rehe-annexation-1933](man-1932-1945.md#oq-rehe-annexation-1933)).
For this 1945-1950 entry that exclusion is actually correct, not merely
approximate: after Japan's surrender Jehol reverted to Chinese
administration outside the "three northeastern provinces" and was never
claimed as part of this entity. So the reused geometry — three provinces,
no Jehol — is the intended target territory here, unlike for Manchukuo
where it under-covers by ~10%. No ESTIMATE flag is applied for that reason;
`period_proxy` is used only because no GIS source digitises "Manchuria
1945-1950" as its own dated feature, not because the reused shape is a
stand-in for a different, larger territory.

Following period-source practice (e.g. Federico-Tena provincial breakdowns
for other regions), "Liaoning" is treated as continuous with its earlier
name "Fengtien"/"Fengtian" and its brief 1929-1945 name "Liaoning" under
various regimes; no province-boundary change materially affects the
1945-1950 window relative to the 1932-1945 union.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md) — Manchukuo, the
  Japanese puppet state occupying the same three provinces (plus Jehol),
  dissolved 18 August 1945.
- **Successor:** [chn-1950-2025](chn-1950-2025.md) — by 1950 the region is
  fully consolidated into the People's Republic of China and Mitchell's
  regional series, where it continues past 1950 (through 1952), is treated
  as folding into the national PRC total rather than warranting a further
  dedicated regional polity (no comparable magnitude/coverage discrepancy
  has been flagged for that later span; revisit if one is found).

## Open questions

### oq-mitchell-post1950-manchuria

Mitchell's "China, Manchuria province of" label continues past 1950 through
1952 while this entry ends at 1950. Current handling routes those
1950-1952 rows to CHN-1950-2025 (national fold-up), on the assumption that,
unlike 1945-1949, no magnitude/coverage mismatch has been separately
flagged for that tail. If a similar reporting-territory overstatement is
found for 1950-1952, extend this entry's end_year or create a follow-on
regional polity rather than re-opening this decision.
