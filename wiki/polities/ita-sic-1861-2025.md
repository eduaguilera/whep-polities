# Sicilia (region of Italy)

## Summary

Sicilia is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni) and, since 1946, one of five special-statute regions with an enhanced degree of legislative and fiscal autonomy (Regione Siciliana). It is the largest island in the Mediterranean, at Italy's southwestern extremity, together with several outlying archipelagos (Aeolian, Egadi, Pelagie, Ustica, Pantelleria). This entry is `type: subnational` because Sicily has not been sovereign in this period: it was annexed into the Kingdom of Italy at unification in 1861 (following the 1860 Expedition of the Thousand and a plebiscite) and has remained an administrative region of Italy ever since -- through the Kingdom, the Fascist period, and the post-1946 Republic, including its 1946 shift to special-statute status, which changed its degree of self-government but not its sovereignty or its territory. The entry exists to carry a *reporting territory* distinct from the national ITA rows: NUTS2-level regional statistics (Eurostat code ITG1) report Sicilia specifically, at roughly 1/12 the area and with a very different economic profile from national Italy-wide totals, so routing this data to a national ITA row would misattribute a regional figure to the whole country, exactly the same rationale documented for the sibling entry ITA-LOM-1861-2025 (Lombardia).

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a regional-level statistical series for Sicilia (Eurostat NUTS2 code ITG1), routed under the country convention for Italy's subnational regions in the same fashion as its already-created sibling ITA-LOM-1861-2025 (Lombardia) and ten further NUTS2 units named in that verdict (ITC1, ITC3, ITF1, ITF3-ITF6, ITG2, ITH1, ITH3). The routing decision does not give an exact row count for this specific unit but treats it as a standard "region of Italy" case, with data understood to span from the 19th century (per the country convention's default 1861 start rule) through the present, on the pattern of the Lombardia sibling's 1861-2023 series. Previously, absent a polity row for Sicilia specifically, this data had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows (ITA-1861-1866 through ITA-1919-2025) -- which is wrong because those rows represent the whole of Italy, roughly twelve times Sicilia's area at any given date, and mixing a regional series (e.g. Sicilian citrus or wheat production, or regional population) into a national row would either misattribute a distinctly regional figure to the whole country or, if double-counted alongside other regional rows, overstate national totals. What confirms Sicilia is a distinct reporting entity rather than an artefact of routing convenience: it is Eurostat's own standard NUTS2 statistical division of Italy (used because Italy's own ISTAT reports regional-level statistics as a first-class unit), and additionally Sicily has held its own special autonomous statute since 1946 (Regione Siciliana) with a separately elected regional assembly and distinct fiscal powers -- a stronger mark of institutional distinctness than most of its ordinary-statute NUTS2 siblings carry, even though that status difference is not treated as grounds to depart from the convention's 1861 start (see decisions).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. gadm-4.1-adm1 is the registered source type that fits Sicilia's admin level (ADM1/NUTS2) and, per public GADM documentation, already digitises Sicilia globally (expected GID_1 ITA.19_1), but the specific GADM file present in this repository's data/geodata is a curated 81-country subset that has zero features for Italy. No other registered source (cshapes, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) covers modern Italian regional boundaries either. Because the source type is registered and known, just not fetched in this repository, polygon_source is set to the slug gadm-4.1-adm1 (not `none`, not a route name) and polygon_status is `unassigned`. See open question oq-gadm-italy-fetch-needed.

**Territory description:** Sicilia is the largest island in the Mediterranean, at the southwestern tip of the Italian peninsula, separated from Calabria by the narrow Strait of Messina. Administratively the region also includes several offshore archipelagos: the Aeolian Islands (north, including Lipari, Vulcano, Stromboli), the Egadi Islands (west, near Trapani), the Pelagie Islands (south, including Lampedusa, closer to Tunisia than to Sicily), Ustica, and Pantelleria. Its capital is Palermo, with other major cities Catania, Messina, Syracuse, and Trapani. A modern-map reader can locate it as the triangular island forming the "toe-kicked" tip of Italy's boot, immediately north of Malta and Tunisia. The region covers approximately 25,700 km2 (the main island is about 25,460 km2, with the outlying archipelagos adding roughly 250 km2 more), making it around 8.5% of Italy's total land area -- comparable in scale to Lombardia (23,864 km2) among Italy's larger regions. The region's own outer boundary (the island's coastline plus its outlying archipelagos) has been stable since unification in 1861; the 1946 change was one of legal/administrative status (ordinary to special-statute region), not of territorial extent, so a single present-day regional outline should be a reasonably safe proxy back to 1861 once a source is fetched, subject to confirming which of the outlying archipelagos are bundled into the same ADM1 feature (see oq-islands-included).

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first polity row created for Sicilia specifically, and it starts exactly at the island's incorporation into the unified Kingdom of Italy in 1861 (following the 1860 Expedition of the Thousand and the subsequent plebiscite), so there is no distinct prior polity to chain from -- the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, of which Sicily was part before 1861, is outside this entry's span and is not itself a row in this table. No successor: end_year of 2025 is open, matching the still-current status of Sicilia as a special-statute region of the Italian Republic. The four container edges (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) tile the whole 1861-2025 span with no gap or overlap, since none of the national-level boundary changes those edges track (Veneto 1866, Rome 1870, WWI settlement 1919) affected Sicilia's own island territory; only its internal administrative *status* changed, in 1946, which does not require a container edge because sovereignty and containment within Italy did not change.

## Sourced claims

- Sicilia is one of Italy's twenty regioni and, since the 1946 royal legislative decree and the 1948 constitutional law implementing it, one of five special-statute regions (Regione Siciliana) with its own statute and greater legislative/fiscal autonomy than Italy's fourteen ordinary regions.
- The Kingdom of Italy annexed Sicily in 1860-1861 via the Expedition of the Thousand and a subsequent plebiscite, placing the island inside the Kingdom of Italy from unification (1861) onward, which is why this entry's span begins in 1861 rather than at a later national milestone (1866 Veneto, 1870 Rome).
- Eurostat's NUTS2 classification assigns Sicilia the code ITG1, the same statistical unit the routed regional data (agricultural/population series, 1861-2023 per the sibling Lombardia verdict's pattern) is keyed to.

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-sic

**Code uses ITA-SIC, not the source's NUTS id ITG1**

The routing decision names this unit by its Eurostat NUTS2 code ITG1, but a NUTS id is a statistical classification string, not a name a reader of this wiki would recognize on sight, and Sicilia's own NUTS code has changed across revisions. Following the dominant 57-of-66 <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern (already used for the sibling ITA-LOM-1861-2025), the subunit token is set to SIC, a readable abbreviation of the region's own name. iso3 stays ITA per the dominant pattern rather than adopting a bespoke code, since Sicilia is an ordinary administrative region with a name of its own but no distinct sovereign or quasi-sovereign history that would justify a bespoke token the way ALK or HYD do. polity_code is therefore ITA-SIC-1861-2025.

### d-start-year-1861-not-1946

**start_year set to 1861 despite Sicily's 1946 special-autonomy status**

The routing verdict itself flags a specific risk here: sibling verdict history for other Italian regions applied a 1946 split (tied to the postwar creation of autonomous regions) rather than the country convention's literal 1861 default, and asks whether Sicily's 1946 special-autonomy status (Regione Siciliana, one of Italy's five special-statute regions since the 1946 royal decree and 1948 constitutional law) is itself a documented reason to depart. It is not treated as one here: the convention's two enumerated departures are Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia (1919, tied to a literal territorial annexation) and Molise (1963, tied to a literal split of a pre-existing region into two). Sicily's 1946 change is a change in administrative *status* (ordinary region to special-statute region with a separate statute and greater fiscal/legislative autonomy) rather than a change in the island's own *territory* -- the island has been geographically the same reporting unit since it entered the Kingdom in 1861, which is the criterion the convention's default rule actually turns on. This entry therefore follows 1861, consistent with the same reasoning applied to Lombardia (ITA-LOM-1861-2025), and flags the 1946 status question as open below rather than silently resolving it against the enumerated-departures list.

### d-polygon-route-unfetched

**polygon_source set to the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1, not a route name or 'none'**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: GADM's adm1 layer is the registered source type for this admin level and already carries Sicilia (GID_1 ITA.19_1 in the standard GADM 4.1 release), but the copy of that source present in this repository's data/geodata is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Italy entirely. Per the instructions governing this route, the slug itself is the declared polygon_source (not new_source_needed, not none), and polygon_status is set to unassigned rather than proxy or assigned, since no feature has actually been fetched or measured. polygon_feature_id is left null because the exact GID_1 string, while plausible from public GADM documentation, cannot be confirmed without the file in hand.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-italy-fetch-needed

**The GADM file on disk still excludes Italy entirely**

data/geodata's local GADM 4.1 ADM1 layer is an explicit 81-country curated subset with zero ITA features, so despite gadm-4.1-adm1 being the correct registered source type for this admin level, no Sicilia feature can be attached until the global (non-subset) GADM 4.1 release is fetched, or an ISTAT official regional boundary file is ingested and mapped into the same pipeline. Until then polygon_source names the intended source but polygon_status stays unassigned. Whoever fetches it should also confirm the feature id (expected ITA.19_1 per public GADM documentation, unconfirmed here) and settle whether Sicilia's several offshore island groups (Aeolian, Egadi, Pelagie, Ustica, and Pantelleria) are bundled into the same ADM1 feature or split out, since that changes the measured area materially.

### oq-special-statute-1946

**Whether Sicily's 1946 special-autonomy status should have split this entry**

Per decision d-start-year-1861-not-1946, Sicily became a special-statute region in 1946 (Regione Siciliana), with its own statute, legislative competences, and fiscal arrangements distinct from Italy's fourteen ordinary-statute regions -- a status shared with only four other regions (Trentino-Alto Adige, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Valle d'Aosta, Sardegna). The convention's enumerated departures list only Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia (for a 1919 territorial reason, not the 1946 statute reason), so this entry ran the ordinary 1861 rule. If country_conventions.json is later revised to treat 1946 special-statute status as its own departure criterion (rather than only 1919 territorial annexation and 1963 splits), this entry, and likely also Sardegna's, would need re-scoping into pre/post-1946 rows. This should be checked against how any future Sardegna verdict is routed, since a consistent answer there would confirm or refute the approach taken here.

### oq-islands-included

**Whether the routed statistical data and the eventual polygon agree on which islands count as Sicilia**

Administrative Sicilia includes several offshore archipelagos (Aeolian Islands, Egadi Islands, Pelagie Islands including Lampedusa, Ustica, Pantelleria) that are geographically distant from the main island and sometimes tabulated separately in older statistical series. It has not been checked whether the source data routed to this entry (regional-level series for NUTS2 ITG1) includes or excludes these islands consistently across the 1861-2023 span, nor whether the eventual GADM or ISTAT polygon bundles them into a single ADM1 feature. A mismatch here would silently mis-state the territory description's km2 figure once a real polygon is attached.

### oq-data-coverage-vs-span

**Underlying data span vs. this entry's open end_year**

As with the Lombardia sibling entry, the routing decision does not confirm the exact upper bound of the routed data (Lombardia's equivalent data ran to 2023 while its span was left open to 2025); it should be confirmed whether Sicilia's data has the same two-year-or-so lag from a structural regional-statistics publication schedule, or whether 2024-2025 will populate directly. If the lag is structural it should be documented here rather than left implicit in an open-ended end_year.
