# Luba Empire

## Summary

The Luba Empire from the 1800 database floor to its absorption into the Congo Free State c.1885. Founded c.1585 in the Katanga region of modern DR Congo, the Luba polity was a powerful savanna empire controlling the Upemba depression and surrounding territory. Its dissolution was driven by two shocks: the mid-to-late-19th-century predation by East-African slave and ivory traders, and the 1889 succession dispute that split the unified state in two, after which the fragments were absorbed into the Congo Free State.

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Covers the Katanga region of modern DR Congo including the Upemba depression. The polygon is an approximate "area of Luba political authority" and reflects the mid-19th-century peak extent.
