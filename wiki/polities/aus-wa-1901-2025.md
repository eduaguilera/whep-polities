# Western Australia (state of Australia)

## Summary

Western Australia is the state occupying the western third of the Australian continent, formed when the colony of Western Australia became a founding state of the Commonwealth of Australia at Federation on 1 January 1901. This entry is `type: subnational` because Western Australia has never held sovereignty in its own right during this span — the Commonwealth (AUS-1901-2025) holds that — but it is a distinct, continuously-bounded statistical and administrative reporting unit within the Commonwealth, matching the treatment already given to the other Australian states (e.g. AUS-ACT-1911-2025) and matching the sibling convention noted in the routing decision for AUS-SOUTHAUSTRALIA/TASMANIA. Its predecessor is the colony of Western Australia (self-governing from 1890, existing as a separate British colony from 1829), tracked separately as AUWA-1829-1900. The state's external boundary has been essentially unchanged since Federation: no territory has been ceded, annexed, or split off, so the entry runs from 1901 to the present with a single open end_year.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Western Australia state-level statistics (population, production, trade) reported separately from national Australia (AUS-1901-2025) and from the pre-Federation colonial series (AUWA-1829-1900), for the 1901-2025 span during which WA has been a Commonwealth state. Data of this kind was previously routed to AUS-1901-2025, the whole-country row, which over-covers by roughly 12x (WA alone is about 2.53 million km2 against Australia's ~7.69 million km2) and conflates a single state's series with six other states' and two territories' figures. It was also not a match for AUWA-1829-1900, whose span ends in 1900 (exclusive) and which records the colonial administration under British constitutional arrangements, not the post-Federation state. That the unit is a distinct reporting entity is confirmed by the routing decision's own precedent: the country convention already treats other Australian states as separate polities (AUS-ACT-1911-2025 exists on this same basis), and Western Australia is a state of identical constitutional standing under the Commonwealth of Australia Constitution Act 1900.

**Territory description:** Western Australia occupies the western third of the Australian continent, bordered by South Australia and the Northern Territory to the east, with coastline on the Indian and Southern Oceans. It contains Perth (the state capital), the Kimberley, Pilbara, Goldfields, and South West regions. Its land area is approximately 2,527,013 km2 (Geoscience Australia's official state-area figure), making it the largest of the Australian states by a wide margin — this is an independently published figure, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry, since none is attached yet. The external boundary has been stable since 1901; no cession, annexation, or internal border change has occurred in this span.

### oq-1900-gap

**1900 is uncovered by any polity in the WA chain**

AUWA-1829-1900 has end_year=1900 (exclusive), so it carries data only through 1899, even though the colony of Western Australia continued to exist until Federation on 1 January 1901. This entry starts at 1901. That leaves calendar year 1900 unrouted for any Western-Australia-specific data, a structural artifact noted in the original routing decision. Resolving it requires revisiting AUWA-1829-1900's own end_year (to 1901 exclusive), which is out of scope for this page since it only authors AUS-WA-1901-2025.
