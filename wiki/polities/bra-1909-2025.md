# Brazil

## Summary

This row tracks Brazil from **1909** to **2025** — a **117-year row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is modern
Brazil. Territory stable at ~8.5M km2 (5th largest country by area).

Major political transitions within this row — none triggering a CSV
split because borders remained stable:

- **Old Republic** (Republica Velha, continuing to 1930)
- **Vargas Era** (1930-1945) — Getulio Vargas, authoritarian
  modernization, Estado Novo (1937-1945)
- **Democratic period** (1945-1964)
- **Military dictatorship** (1964-1985)
- **Re-democratization** (1985-present) — 1988 Constitution
- **Capital moved** from Rio de Janeiro to Brasilia (1960)

## Sourced claims

- **Area: 3,285,650 sq mi (8,509,833 sq km).** "The largest country
  of South America." Capital: Brasília. Bounded by 10 neighbors
  [biger-1995 §brazil](../sources/biger-1995.md#brazil).
- **1930: Vargas Revolution.** Getulio Vargas seized power, ending
  the Old Republic. Ruled 1930-1945 (and again 1951-1954).
- **1937-1945: Estado Novo.** Vargas's authoritarian corporatist
  regime.
- **1945: Democratization.** Vargas deposed; democratic elections
  resumed.
- **1960: Capital moved.** Capital transferred from Rio de Janeiro
  to the newly built Brasilia (inaugurated 21 April 1960).
- **1964-03-31: Military coup.** Beginning of 21 years of military
  dictatorship.
- **1985: Re-democratization.** Indirect election of civilian
  president Tancredo Neves (died before taking office; Jose Sarney
  succeeded).
- **1988: New Constitution.** The current Brazilian constitution,
  establishing the Federative Republic of Brazil.
