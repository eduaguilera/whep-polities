# Canada (Confederation era, to 1886)

## Summary

This row tracks Canada from 1866 (just before Confederation on 1 July
1867) through 1885, ending at the start year of the earliest CShapes 2.0
polygon vintage for Canada (gwcode 20, gwsyear 1886). Confederation united
Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and New Brunswick into the Dominion of Canada.
Rapid westward expansion followed: Rupert's Land and the North-Western
Territory transferred from the Hudson's Bay Company (1870, creating
Manitoba); British Columbia (1871); Prince Edward Island (1873); Yukon
Territory created (1898 falls in successor row); Alberta and Saskatchewan
(1905, successor row).

This polity was split from the former CAN-1866-1948 row (which spanned 82
years with a single 1886 polygon) to isolate the period of major territorial
change and reduce polygon vintage drift.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` gwcode 20, gwsyear 1886 (~9,553,560 km²),
used as a **proxy** (`polygon_status: proxy`). The 1886 polygon represents
Canada after the major westward acquisitions (Rupert's Land 1870, BC 1871,
PEI 1873) but before the Alberta/Saskatchewan split (1905). It is applied
here because no earlier period-accurate polygon is available in CShapes 2.0
or any other WHEP priority source (Cliopatria, CHGIS, Paine 2024 — none
cover British North America pre-1886).

**Known overstatement (approximation documented).** The 1886 polygon
represents ~9.55M km², but:

- At Confederation (1867–1869), Canada comprised only the four original
  provinces: Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and New Brunswick, totalling
  approximately **500,000 km²**. The 1886 proxy polygon overstates this
  by roughly **19×**.
- After the Rupert's Land/NWT transfer (1870) and BC accession (1871),
  Canada's administered territory expanded to roughly **8.5–9M km²** —
  close to the 1886 polygon area.
- From 1871 to 1885, the 1886 proxy polygon is a reasonable approximation
  (within ~10% of actual extent).

**Implication for agricultural data.** Pre-1961 agricultural data for
"Canada" in the 1866–1885 period comes almost entirely from the settled
eastern provinces (Ontario, Quebec, Maritimes) and, after 1871, from the
Pacific coast (British Columbia). The vast NWT wilderness had negligible
agricultural output. Spatial allocation of production values using the
1886 proxy polygon is therefore conservative (production density is
understated relative to the actually settled area), but the polygon
boundary error does not affect total production figures — only per-km²
density metrics.

A period-accurate polygon for 1866–1869 (four original provinces, ~500,000
km²) would require manual construction from GADM provincial boundaries or
the Newberry Library's historical North American atlas — flagged as a
future improvement but not blocking for production-series matching.

## Sourced claims

- **1867-07-01: Confederation.** British North America Act united Ontario,
  Quebec, Nova Scotia, and New Brunswick. Area ~500,000 km² at that point.
- **1870: Rupert's Land and NWT transferred.** Hudson's Bay Company
  territories acquired; Manitoba created. Canadian territory expands to
  roughly 8.5M km².
- **1871: British Columbia joins.**
- **1873: Prince Edward Island joins.**
- **1885: Canadian Pacific Railway completed.** Transcontinental rail link;
  landmark year coinciding with the CShapes polygon start.

## Decisions

- **Split from CAN-1866-1948.** The former 82-year row (1866–1948) was split
  at 1886 to separate the period of major territorial acquisition from the
  stable post-1886 period. CShapes 2.0 provides no polygon earlier than
  1886 for Canada, so this row uses the 1886 feature as a proxy. The proxy
  is a significant overstatement for 1866–1870 (19×) but reasonable for
  1871–1885 (within ~10%). Both approximations are documented above.
- **polygon_vintage_drift fix.** Splitting reduces the drift window for the
  CAN-1886-1948 successor row to zero (polygon year = row start year = 1886).

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1870-polygon

A period-accurate polygon for Confederation-era Canada (1867–1870, four
original provinces, ~500,000 km²) does not exist in any current WHEP source.
Possible future sources: Newberry Library historical atlas (North America
maps ~1867), GADM province-level composed union (ON + QC + NS + NB), or
manual digitisation from historical maps. Priority: low for agricultural
data (production density not affected); higher if spatial containment checks
are needed.

### oq-1866-start

The row start year is 1866 (carried from the former CAN-1866-1948), one year
before Confederation (1 July 1867). This likely reflects a CShapes or
database convention. The exact rationale should be verified.
