# Azores and Madeira (Portuguese Atlantic islands)

## Summary

Portugal's two Atlantic archipelagos taken together: the Azores, "an archipelago of Portugal ...
about 1,400 km west of the Portuguese mainland", which "together with Madeira ... is one of the two
autonomous regions of Portugal", and Madeira (Madeira island 740.7 km², Porto Santo 42.5 km²).
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §azores-madeira](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#azores-madeira)

One row for 1800-2025, typed `subnational`, inside [prt-1800-2025](prt-1800-2025.md), whose CShapes
polygon includes both archipelagos (Ponta Delgada and Funchal are inside it).

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports the islands as one unit, `Portugal Azores and Madeira` (the 1950 land-use table,
`use total` 310 thousand ha, i.e. **3,100 km²**, and 1949 sweet potatoes) and `Portugal: Azores and
Madeira` (sugar 1949-1951). **7 rows** resolved to no polity. They are a part of Portugal, and an
alias to PRT-1800-2025 would file the islands' land-use table and crops as the whole country's
(FAO's own Portugal land-use table is for 1948).
