# United Kingdom

## Summary

This row tracks the United Kingdom as a territorial-economic unit from
**1921** (Anglo-Irish Treaty) to **2025** (database ceiling)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 105-year window
covers the interwar period, WWII, post-war decolonization of the British
Empire (tracked as separate rows), and the 1927 name change to "United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland." Metropolitan borders
have been stable since the Irish partition.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
this row begins at a real territorial event — the Anglo-Irish Treaty
(signed 6 December 1921), which led to the secession of the Irish Free
State.

- **1921 start** is a real territorial event: Anglo-Irish Treaty signed
  6 December 1921. CShapes records a transitional row from 1921-12-06
  to 1922-12-05 still at 42.95 sq degrees; from 1922-12-06 onward the
  UK polygon drops to ~33.59 sq degrees (post-Irish Free State)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; the UK is still active.

UK = England + Scotland + Wales + Northern Ireland.

Predecessor: [gbr-1800-1921](gbr-1800-1921.md) — Anglo-Irish Treaty,
Irish Free State secession.
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Sourced claims

- **1921-12-06: Anglo-Irish Treaty.** Created the Irish Free State
  from 26 of Ireland's 32 counties. Northern Ireland opted to remain
  in the UK. Start-of-row event. Cross-referenced from
  [gbr-1800-1921](gbr-1800-1921.md).
- **1922-12-06: Irish Free State formally established.** CShapes
  polygon drops from 42.95 to 33.59 sq degrees at this date
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1927: Name change.** "The United Kingdom changed its formal title
  to the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland." Not a
  territorial event.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists the UK (`UKG`,
  `ccode=200`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31 — no break at 1921 or any other date
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Open questions

### oq-northern-ireland-status

Northern Ireland opted to stay in the UK under the Anglo-Irish Treaty.
Its status has been contested throughout the Troubles (1968-1998). The
Good Friday Agreement (1998) established power-sharing institutions
and affirmed that Northern Ireland's constitutional status can only
change by consent. Not a territorial change under the WHEP rule, but
relevant political context.
