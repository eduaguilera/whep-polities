# Western Australia (to 1900)

## Summary

The Colony of Western Australia (originally the Swan River Colony) from its founding on 12 August 1829 to the eve of Australian federation. Western Australia was the last of the six colonies to join federation, doing so reluctantly after negotiating concessions including promises of a transcontinental railway. The WHEP row ends on 31 December 1900 before the 1 January 1901 federation. The CSV uses the non-standard `AUWA` prefix to distinguish this colony from the federated `AUS`.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependency-level). Covers the western third of the Australian continent — roughly the modern state of Western Australia. The colony's eastern border with South Australia was set at the 129°E meridian in 1831 and has not changed.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in the WHEP chain. Nominally the area was part of New Holland and outside NSW's effective administration before 1829.
- **Successor:** [aus-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) — Commonwealth of Australia from 1 January 1901.
