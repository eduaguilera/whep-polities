# Equatorial Guinea

## Summary

Equatorial Guinea is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Equatorial Guinea from 1968 to the present. Equatorial Guinea gained independence from Spain on 12 October 1968.

## Territorial extent

Equatorial Guinea consists of a mainland portion (Rio Muni) and several islands including Bioko (formerly Fernando Po). It is bounded by Cameroon (118 miles; 189 km) and Gabon. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

## Sourced claims

- Cameroon is bounded by Equatorial Guinea (118 miles; 189 km). The Cameroon-Equatorial Guinea boundary follows the colonial boundary between German Kamerun and Spanish possessions established in the Franco-German protocols of 24 December 1885 and 4 February 1894. [biger-1995 §cameroon](../sources/biger-1995.md#cameroon)
- "A convention of 15 March 1894, when the boundary between Cameroon and French Congo in the area of Lake Chad, along the Chari River and 15'E, was established" (p.136). This defined the broader Central African colonial boundaries including Equatorial Guinea's eastern limits. [biger-1995 §cameroon](../sources/biger-1995.md#cameroon)
