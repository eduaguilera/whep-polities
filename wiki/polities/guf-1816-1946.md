# French Guiana (Colony)

## Summary

French Guiana is a territory on the north-eastern coast of South America, bounded by Brazil to the south and east, Suriname to the west, and the Atlantic Ocean to the north. Its total land area is approximately 83,534–84,000 km². France has held continuous sovereignty since 1667 (formally confirmed at the Treaty of Paris 1763 and the Congress of Vienna 1815). The 1816 start date is the WHEP temporal boundary aligned with Congress of Vienna restoration.

This polity covers the colonial period from 1816 to 1946, when French law 46-451 of 19 March 1946 transformed French Guiana from a colony (Guyane française) into a French overseas department (Département d'Outre-Mer, DOM). The successor polity is [guf-1946-2025](guf-1946-2025.md).

Created to cover 85 rows of IIA and fao1952 agricultural production data spanning 1909–1951 (cacao, sugar, livestock, land use, population ~37,000) labelled "french guiana" / "French Guiana", all with whep_code=None. Cannot fold into FRA-1800-1919 or FRA-1919-2025: those polities use CShapes metropolitan-France polygons (~544,000 km²) covering only continental Europe — French Guiana (~84,000 km²) is on a different continent, not contained in any metropolitan-France polygon. Routing to FRA would overstate territory ~6-7x and misplace the data geographically.

## Territorial extent

French Guiana's borders were established principally by the Treaty of Utrecht (1713, Oyapock River as eastern/Brazilian border), the arbitration award of 1 December 1900 (Swiss Federal Council, settling the Contesté franco-brésilien dispute between France and Brazil), and the treaty of 30 August 1915 (Maroni River as western/Suriname border). The territory has been geographically stable at ~83,534 km² since the 1900 arbitration.

## Open Questions

### oq-guf-1930-boundary

CShapes 2.0 feature 59 (1930-07-06 to 1946-03-18) yields ~13,068 km² — anomalously small relative to the actual ~83,534 km² territory. The July 1930 date does not correspond to any known boundary treaty affecting French Guiana. Possible explanations: (a) CShapes coding artefact reflecting a disputed-zone polygon variant; (b) boundary revision artefact unrelated to actual sovereignty extent. WHEP uses the 1886 feature (~83,711 km²) which matches GADM 4.1 and authoritative area figures. A future review should check the CShapes codebook for cowcode=120 feature 59 specifically.

### oq-guf-pre-1816-predecessor

The WHEP boundary is 1816; French Guiana has been continuously French since 1667 (with brief Portuguese and British occupations). No WHEP predecessor row exists. If pre-1816 data appears, a GUF-1667-1816 row may be needed.

### oq-guf-pre-1900-boundary

The Contesté franco-brésilien (interior jungle ~260,000 km² in dispute between France and Brazil, 1836–1900) was resolved by the 1900 Swiss arbitration in France's favour. WHEP data for French Guiana begins 1909, after the dispute resolution, so the stable post-1900 border applies. No split needed within this row.
