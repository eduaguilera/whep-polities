# Jan Mayen

## Summary

The volcanic island of Jan Mayen in the Norwegian Sea. The Norwegian Meteorological Institute
"annexed the middle part of the island for Norway in 1922 and the whole island in 1926", and "on 27
February 1930, the island was made de jure a part of the Kingdom of Norway".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §jan-mayen](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#jan-mayen)

The row starts in 1930, the de jure incorporation; the 1922 and 1926 annexations were made by the
institute rather than by the state, and no source in layer B reports the island before 1948.
`iso3` is `NA`, as on [svb-1925-2025](svb-1925-2025.md): the ISO code `SJM` covers both islands
together and neither row claims it.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `Norway Jan Mayen` on its own: a 1948 land-use table whose `use total` is 37
thousand ha, i.e. **370 km²**. **2 rows** resolved to no polity. The island lies outside
[nor-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md)'s mainland polygon; before this row it fell only in the
reporting-area aggregates.
