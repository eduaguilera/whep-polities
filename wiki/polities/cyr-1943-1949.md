# British Military Administration of Cyrenaica

## Summary

The eastern third of Libya under **British** military administration from the Axis defeat in
Cyrenaica (Tripoli fell 23 January 1943; the whole of Libya was in Allied hands by February) to
**1 June 1949**, when Britain granted Cyrenaica internal self-government and Emir Idris was
recognised as head of state. From that date the territory is
[cyr-1949-1951](cyr-1949-1951.md), the **Emirate** of Cyrenaica.

`end_year: 1949` is **exclusive**, so this row covers 1943-1948 inclusive and hands over to the
Emirate without gap or overlap.

## Why this entry exists

**The territory had an administration for six years and no row for it, and data had already
arrived for one of those years.** `TRP-1943-1951` and `FEZ-1943-1951` cover Tripolitania's and
Fezzan's whole occupation; `CYR-1949-1951` is the Emirate and starts in 1949, so 1943-1948
Cyrenaica was representable only as all-Libya (issue 198).

The gap was **not latent**. FAO's 1952 yearbook reports **four `Libya Cyrenaica` rows for 1948**,
and they were resolving to nothing at all:

```
r_fao_land_1952_3_9_use_agricultural_area_arable_land_orchards   1948    1,741 kha
r_fao_land_1952_3_9_use_forests_woodlands                        1948      155 kha
r_fao_land_1952_3_9_use_builton_wasteland                        1948   85,344 kha
r_fao_land_1952_3_9_use_total                                    1948   85,540 kha
```

That last row is **855,400 km²** — FAO's own stated area for Cyrenaica, the figure
`cyr-1949-1951`, `trp-1943-1951` and `fez-1943-1951` all measure their polygons against, and the
one `data/final/source_stated_areas.csv` carries at `fao,1952,1948,Libya Cyrenaica`. The database
was quoting a number in three pages that its own matcher could not route.

Routing those rows to `LBY-1943-1949` instead would have been worse than dropping them: Cyrenaica
is 837,876 km² of GADM's Libya of 1,616,070, so a Cyrenaica figure booked to the national row
**overstates the territory it describes by 1.93x** and double-counts against the
already-matched Tripolitania series.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** constructed — **bound to the existing `CYR-1949-1951` feature, unchanged**: the union
of 8 GADM 4.1 adm1 *shabiyat* (Al Butnan, Darnah, Al Jabal al Akhdar, Al Marj, Benghazi, Al
Wahat, Al Kufrah, Surt), **837,876 km²** against FAO 1952's **855,400** (**−2.0%**).

**No new geometry was built, deliberately.** The territory Britain administered from 1943 and the
territory the Emirate governed from 1949 are the same ground — only the polity changed — and
`build_cyr_1949_1951()`'s docstring said so before this row existed. Two rows sharing one
constructed feature is the established pattern for successive periods of one territory
(`MAN-1945-1950` reuses `MAN-1932-1945`'s feature the same way), and `validate_shared_polygons`
reports sharing only for rows that **coexist**; these two do not, because `end_year` is exclusive.

> **The partition of occupied Libya is unchanged and now holds for the whole occupation.**
>
> ```
> TRP-1943-1951   314,598 km²
> CYR-1943-1949   837,876   <- this row, same geometry as CYR-1949-1951
> CYR-1949-1951   837,876
> FEZ-1943-1951   463,596
> union         1,616,070 km²
> GADM Libya    1,616,070 km²   ratio 1.000000, both differences 0.0000 km²
> pairwise intersections            0.0000 km² (all pairs)
> ```
>
> Before this row the three-way partition held as a *territorial* partition from 1943 but only as
> a *span* partition from 1949: 1943-1948 had two of three sides. It is now both, throughout.
> `audit_family_shadowing`'s `PINNED_DISJOINT` re-measures `CYR-1943-1949` against Tripolitania and
> Fezzan on every run, alongside the three pairs it already held.

`polygon_status: proxy` for the same reason as the other three: modern *shabiyat* standing in for
historical territories whose boundaries are known to differ, with the Surt and Al Jufrah
placements a documented area-evidence judgement rather than a documentary one. The reasoning is
set out once on [cyr-1949-1951](cyr-1949-1951.md) and [trp-1943-1951](trp-1943-1951.md) and is
not repeated here, because it is the same decision about the same geometry.

## Why the code is `CYR-1943-1949`

Two different entities now sit under the `CYR` prefix — a British military administration and an
emirate. That is deliberate. Since PR 194 the family key is `rsplit("-", 2)[0]`, so `CYR` groups
these as **consecutive periods of one family**, which is what the continuity and period-gap checks
should see: the same territory, administered by two successive authorities, with no boundary
change between them. A distinct prefix (`CYRBMA-*`) would have split one territory into two
one-period families and made `validate_period_gaps` and `validate_chain_integrity` blind to the
handover they are there to check.

## Predecessors and successors

`predecessor: [LBY-1934-1943]` — Italian Libya, from which Britain took Cyrenaica in 1943. The
same predecessor as `TRP-1943-1951` and `FEZ-1943-1951`: the three territories entered occupation
together.

`successor: [CYR-1949-1951]` — the Emirate, from 1 June 1949. This is where the chain differs
from Tripolitania's and Fezzan's, which run straight to `LBY-1951-2025`, and the difference is
the history: Cyrenaica alone got a self-governing polity two and a half years before independence.

Both reverse edges were added: `LBY-1934-1943` now lists this row among its successors and
`CYR-1949-1951` lists it among its predecessors. `CYR-1949-1951` keeps `LBY-1943-1949` as a
predecessor too — the all-Libya aggregate row is not removed from the chain by the arrival of a
part-territory row.

`LBY-1943-1949` is **not** listed as a predecessor here. It is the contemporaneous all-Libya
aggregate over the same years, not an earlier polity, and treating a parallel aggregate as a
predecessor would put a chain edge where the relation is containment. That containment is
recorded where it belongs: `LBY-1943-1949` is now in `validate_spatial_containment`'s
container allowlist, holding TRP, FEZ and this row.

## Contradictions

- **COW code 620** is all-Libya. CShapes carries no separate code for Cyrenaica in any step from
  1912 to 2019, so 620 is inherited as the closest available identifier, exactly as `TRP`, `CYR`
  and `FEZ` inherit it. Flagged rather than resolved.
- **Overlap with `LBY-1943-1949`.** 1943-1948 is covered by both this row and the all-Libya
  aggregate. They are not in conflict — FAO 1952 publishes both an all-Libya series and
  per-territory series for the same years, and both are kept — but any spatial or area-weighted
  use must pick one level or double-count 837,876 km².

## Open questions

### oq-cyr-1943-start-year

1943 follows the other two occupation rows rather than a Cyrenaica-specific date. Cyrenaica was
in fact cleared earlier than Tripolitania — Benghazi fell 20 November 1942 and the British
Military Administration of Cyrenaica dates from then — so a 1942 start is defensible history. It
is not taken here because it would break the shared 1943 start of the three occupation rows and
change the code, and because no data earlier than 1948 has been observed for any of them. If a
1942 Cyrenaica figure appears, this is the row to move.

### oq-cyr-1943-1949-cyrenaica-label-source-scope

Separately from this row, the bare label **`Cyrenaica`** is aliased for source `mitchell`
(1949-1949) while the two rows carrying it in the consolidated layer-B data are **`fao1952`**
(1949: camels 14 thousand head, copper sulphate 8 t). Those two rows resolve to nothing today.
That is an alias-scope defect at 1949, not a coverage gap at 1943-1948, so it is recorded rather
than fixed here.
