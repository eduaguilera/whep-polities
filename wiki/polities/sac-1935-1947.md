# Saar (German incorporation, 1935-1947)

## Summary

This entry covers the **Saar** under direct German administration following the
**1935 plebiscite** (effective 1 March 1935) until the post-war Allied
occupation reorganised the territory as the **Saar Protectorate** (SAA-1947-1957)
in 1947. During this period the Saar was formally reintegrated into the German
Reich and governed as the **Saar** (*Saargebiet*, later *Gau Westmark*).

**Span**: 1 March 1935 – end of 1947 (transition to French-administered Saar
Protectorate, SAA-1947-1957).

**Predecessor**: SAB-1920-1935 (Saar Basin Territory, League of Nations
administration 1920–1935).

**Successor**: SAA-1947-1957 (Saar Protectorate, French administration).

## Why this polity exists

FAO 1952 production data contains rows labelled **"France Saar"** with
observation years including **1938**. The territory measured (~1,912 km²,
Versailles boundary) is the Saar under German administration at that time.
Folding this into DEU-1920-1938 (~476,000 km²) would overstate territory by
~250×. The correct match is this granular polity.

## Polygon status

**unassigned** — no historical GIS source (CShapes, Cliopatria, CHGIS, PAINE,
GHGIS) covers the 1935-1947 Saar as a distinct polygon feature.

**Area**: ~1,912 km² (Versailles boundary, 1920).

**Preferred resolution**: GADM ADM1 Saarland (gadm41_DEU.gpkg, GID_1 =
`DEU.14_1`) as `period_proxy`, confidence high. Note ~34% area overstatement
vs. Versailles boundary (~1,912 km² vs. modern Saarland ~2,569 km²). Once
gadm41_DEU.gpkg is acquired, bind with `polygon_status: proxy` and flag the
overstatement in this page. Document as ESTIMATE.

## Verification notes

- **`fao1952` Saar population 1937 = 821,000 reads as a TRUE TOTAL.**
  Verified 2026-07-24 (two independent agents). It matches the Saar's actual
  total population in the period (~812,000 at the 1935 plebiscite, rising
  toward ~900,000 by 1939), corroborating that the label's reporting
  territory is the Saar itself.

  Note this **contradicts** the same source's behaviour elsewhere, where the
  population series reads about one third of true totals (see
  [f248-1920-1947](f248-1920-1947.md#fao1952-population-series-is-not-total-population)).
  The FAO-1952 population series is therefore not uniformly one measure; the
  split between the two behaviours is unresolved and recorded in
  `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/source_conventions.csv`.
