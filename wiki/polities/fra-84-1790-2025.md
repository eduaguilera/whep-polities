# Vaucluse (département)

## Summary

Vaucluse is a French département in the southeastern lower Rhône valley, entered here as `type: subnational` because a département is a sub-unit of the sovereign French state, not a state in its own right — France's national-scale statistics belong to the FRA-* national chain (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), and this row exists solely to carry data a source tabulates at department scale for this specific unit. The routing decision found no existing polity matched Vaucluse by name and confirmed it is not one of the four documented FRA departure cases (Territoire de Belfort, the Alsace-Lorraine trio, the Corsica 1793/1975 split, or an overseas département) that would require a non-default span, so it applied the country's default rule for an original département: creation-year start (here taken as 1790, though the actual annexation/formation of this specific département out of the papal Comtat Venaissin and Avignon is later and flagged as an open question) and an open end (2025), since the department is still current. Underlying department-level French data (1862-2023) is reported for this unit, satisfying the qualifying policy that a reporting unit statistics were collected on gets its own row.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit FRA-FRL06 ("Vaucluse") from the agent-harness routing pipeline: French department-level data spanning 1862-2023 (per the routing decision's candidate-list search), reported at department scale. Before this entry the unit had no polity match in the database — routing found no name match among existing rows, and the routing_reasoning explicitly checked Vaucluse against the four documented FRA departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, the Corsica split, overseas départements) and found none applied, so the default département span rule (1790 creation, open end) was used rather than a bespoke span. Routing this data to the national FRA-1919-2025 row instead would have folded a ~3,600 km² administrative subdivision into a national total roughly 150 times larger in area — the scale mismatch itself is evidence the data needed its own row rather than the national one. What confirms Vaucluse is a distinct, addressable reporting unit is straightforward administrative continuity: it has been a first-class INSEE department (code 84) since its Revolutionary-era formation, the same persistent-unit-type criterion that governs the two dozen other French département entries already in the table (FRA-02, FRA-04, FRA-08, FRA-14, and others).

## Predecessors and successors

Vaucluse has no predecessor or successor row in this table. Unlike most of the original ~83 départements formed directly by the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary reform out of pre-existing French provinces, Vaucluse combines a slice of former Provence with the historic Comtat Venaissin and the city of Avignon — papal territories (the Comtat Venaissin had belonged to the Holy See since 1274, Avignon since 1348) that were annexed to Revolutionary France in 1791 and formally combined into the département in 1793. Standard administrative-history references date the département's formation to 1793, later than the 1790 default this page applies pending confirmation (see open question below). It has not been split, merged, or annexed since its formation and remains a current French département, so end_year is open at 2025.

## Decisions

### d-code-choice-fra-84

**Chose FRA-84-1790-2025, the INSEE department number, following the dominant table pattern and FRA precedent**

The dominant subnational pattern in the table (338 of 351 rows) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and existing FRA subnational rows use INSEE department numbers as the subunit token (FRA-02, FRA-04, FRA-08, FRA-14, FRA-18, FRA-19, FRA-21 through FRA-28, and others). Vaucluse is INSEE department 84, so FRA-84-1790-2025 matches both the cross-country default and the country-specific precedent already established for two dozen other French départements. This departs from the routing decision's own proposed container list, which only included a single FRA-1800-1871 container edge (leaving 1871-2025 uncontained); this page instead follows sibling entry FRA-04-1790-2025's full three-edge container list (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), the pattern that actually tiles the full span without gaps after 1800.

### d-start-year-1790-not-1793

**Used 1790 (the generic default) as start_year despite evidence Vaucluse's actual formation was 1793**

The routing decision derives start_year 1790 from Vaucluse not being one of the four documented departures from the default département rule (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine trio, Corsica split, overseas départements), so the default creation-year start applies. However, Vaucluse's actual creation is unusual: the historic papal Comtat Venaissin and the city of Avignon were only annexed to France and combined with parts of former Provence to form the département in 1793, not 1790 as with the ~83 original Revolutionary départements. This page follows the routing decision's stated 1790 default and the sibling precedent of FRA-04, FRA-08 etc., which use 1790 uniformly, but flags the Avignon/Comtat Venaissin annexation timing as an open question below rather than silently asserting 1790 is definitely correct for this specific département.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-container-absent

**This unit's span begins before any national container exists, and that is recorded rather than left open**

The unit was created in 1790 — the French départements by the 1790 reorganisation, Rhode Island by ratification — but the earliest national row for this country begins in 1800 (FRA-1800-1871; USA-1800-1803). There is therefore nothing for the years 1790-1799 to be contained by, and inventing a pre-1800 container would be asserting a polity no source in this repository establishes. The omission is deliberate. It is outside WHEP's 1850+ domain, so it costs no resolution there; if a pre-1800 national row is ever added, this unit needs an edge to it.


### oq-vaucluse-actual-creation-year

**Vaucluse's actual formation may postdate the generic 1790 département-creation default used here**

This entry uses start_year 1790, following the default rule applied to most French départements. But Vaucluse's specific formation is not a simple carve-out of pre-existing French royal territory the way most 1790 départements were: it combines parts of the former Provence region with the Comtat Venaissin and the city of Avignon, which were papal territories only annexed to revolutionary France in 1791 (Avignon) and 1791-1793 (the wider Comtat), with the département itself commonly dated to 1793. If that dating is confirmed against a French administrative-history source, this entry's start_year should move to 1791 or 1793 and the code should change to FRA-84-1791-2025 or FRA-84-1793-2025 accordingly, and it would become a fifth documented departure from the default département span rule rather than a default case.

### oq-pre1800-container-gap

**No FRA container currently tiles 1790-1800 if the 1790 start stands**

This entry's start_year is 1790 (pending resolution of the prior open question), but the earliest national FRA container row in the table (FRA-1800-1871) only starts in 1800, so the container list above tiles 1800-2025 but leaves 1790-1800 uncontained. The identical gap is already flagged on sibling entries such as FRA-04-1790-2025; if a FRA-1790-1800 container row is added to resolve it there, this entry should receive the same leading edge rather than being resolved independently.

### oq-gadm-france-vaucluse-unfetched

**GADM ADM2 (or correct ADM tier) French département polygons remain unfetched for Vaucluse**

The routing decision classifies this as new_source_needed on the reasoning that départements are GADM ADM2-level units in France while the locally registered gadm-4.1-adm1 slug is ADM1 (and additionally is limited to an 81-country subset that excludes France). Sibling entries such as FRA-04-1790-2025 instead record polygon_source as the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1 with polygon_status unassigned, on the view that the correct GADM family is registered even though the specific fetch/tier is missing. This page follows that sibling precedent for consistency, but it remains an open question whether départements actually sit at GADM ADM1 or ADM2 for France, and whether the full (non-81-country-subset) GADM extract needs to be fetched before any feature can be assigned to Vaucluse.
