# India (1914-1937)

## Summary

CSV row for India from **1914** to **1937** — a **24-year row**
covering British India through WWI and the interwar period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 750
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The 1937 end-of-row event: **Burma separated from India** under
the Government of India Act 1935 (effective 1 April 1937) — a
real territorial change satisfying the WHEP polity-definition rule.

## Territorial extent

This row is bound to CShapes 2.0, `cowcode=750`, `polygon_feature_year=1914`
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states).
Direct measurement of that feature from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`
(reprojected to ESRI:54034, equal-area) gives **4,893,939 km²**, matching the
successor page's independently-stated pre-Burma-separation figure of
4,894,456 km² for the same polygon (small residual is a coordinate-precision
artifact, not a different feature).

Direct inspection of the raw CShapes 2.0 distribution
(`data/geodata/cshapes2_full.gpkg`, gitignored, not the source-file citation
above but the underlying dataset it documents) shows `cowcode=750` recorded
across **three consecutive time-steps that overlap this row's span**:

| start | end | area (km², ESRI:54034) | capname |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1899-01-01 | 1914-07-02 | 4,819,795 | Calcutta |
| 1914-07-03 | 1931-02-12 | 4,894,456 | Calcutta |
| 1931-02-13 | 1937-03-31 | 4,894,456 | New Delhi (Delhi) |
| 1937-04-01 | 1947-08-14 | 4,227,508 | New Delhi (Delhi) *(successor row)* |

Two things follow directly from this table:

1. **The only real area change inside this bracket falls right at the row's
   1914 start**, not inside it: area rises from 4,819,795 km² to 4,894,456 km²
   at the 1899-01-01→1914-07-02 / 1914-07-03 boundary, i.e. exactly where WHEP
   also starts a new row. (Uncited general history, not from an ingested
   source: 3 July 1914 is the date of the Simla Convention between British
   India and Tibet, which fixed the McMahon Line and shifted the
   India–Tibet/Assam frontier — a plausible but not source-confirmed
   explanation for the jump; flagged as such rather than asserted as fact.)
2. **The 1931-02-13 CShapes time-step is not a territorial change.** The area
   is bit-identical on both sides (4,894,456 km²); the only field that
   changes is `capname`, from "Calcutta" to "New Delhi (Delhi)". This is a
   direct instance of the rule in `wiki/README.md` §"What a WHEP polity is":
   *"CShapes splits are not automatically WHEP splits… the test is whether
   `ST_Area(geom)` changes between CShapes time-steps. Same area = same WHEP
   polity = one row."* No split is warranted at 1931. (Uncited general
   history: 13 February 1931 is the date New Delhi was formally inaugurated
   as the seat of government of British India, which is consistent with —
   though not, from an ingested source, proven to be — the cause of this
   step in the CShapes coding.)

Cross-checking against COW: `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`
(the raw file the `cow-state-system-v2024` source documents) has exactly one
tenure row for India (`IND`, `ccode=750`): `1947-08-15 → 2024-12-31`. British
India never appears as a COW state-system member before independence, which
is consistent with COW's own inclusion criteria — diplomatic
recognition/League membership plus a population threshold that a colony by
definition cannot meet
[cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion).
This is expected, not a contradiction: per the WHEP polity-definition rule,
COW's state-system-membership gap says nothing about whether the *territory*
changed, and this row correctly does not use 1947 as an internal boundary.

**Bottom line: borders were effectively constant across the whole 1914–1937
span** (4,894,456 km² throughout), bookended by a frontier adjustment right
at the 1914 start and the well-documented Burma separation right at the 1937
end — both already reflected in the row boundaries, not inside them.

## Sourced claims

- The row's polygon is CShapes 2.0 `cowcode=750`, feature-year 1914
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states);
  measured area 4,893,939 km² (equal-area CRS ESRI:54034, from
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv) via
  `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`).
- CShapes 2.0's own coding-changes methodology restricts border-adjustment
  detection to transfers larger than 100×100 km and excludes wartime
  transient changes unless made permanent by postwar treaty
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes) —
  relevant context for why a sub-threshold administrative change (like the
  1931 capital move, see *Territorial extent*) would not register as a
  CShapes-coded territorial event even if it had been one.
- CShapes 2.0 codes India as a `colony` of Britain across the entire
  1914–1937 window with no change of `owner` or `status`
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)
  (direct read of `data/geodata/cshapes2_full.gpkg`, the raw file that
  source documents) — Crown rule is continuous through this row.
- COW's state-system list (`cow-state-system-v2024`) has no tenure row for
  India before 1947-08-15, consistent with its stated inclusion criteria
  (diplomatic recognition / League membership + population threshold)
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion).
  This is a definitional gap, not evidence against this WHEP row's 1914–1937
  span — see the WHEP polity-definition rule in `wiki/README.md`.
- Uncited general history (no ingested source covers this): the Simla
  Convention of 3 July 1914 fixed the McMahon Line between British India
  and Tibet; the transfer of the colonial capital to New Delhi was formally
  completed 13 February 1931; the Government of India Act 1935 (royal
  assent 24 July 1935) took effect 1 April 1937, introducing provincial
  autonomy and separating Burma from India on the same date. These three
  dates line up with the row's start, its one false-positive CShapes
  time-step, and its end, respectively, but are recorded here as general
  history, not as claims backed by an ingested source.
