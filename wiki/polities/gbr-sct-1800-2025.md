# Scotland

## Summary

Scotland, "the northern part of the island of Great Britain and adjacent islands, principally in
the archipelagos of the Hebrides and the Northern Isles". "On 1 May 1707, Scotland and England combined
to create the new Kingdom of Great Britain", so for the whole of this row it is one of the two parts
of [gbr-gb-1800-2025](gbr-gb-1800-2025.md) (Great Britain), the other being
[gbr-ew-1800-2025](gbr-ew-1800-2025.md) (England and Wales).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §scotland](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#scotland)

## Why this entry exists

The label `united kingdom: scotland` reports Scotland on its own, beside `united kingdom`,
`united kingdom: great britain` and `united kingdom: england, wales`, for 1921-1925 to 1951
(**18 (year, label) pairs**). No polity was Scotland: routing it to Great Britain or the United
Kingdom would put a part on its whole. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm1 `GBR.3_1` (Scotland) measures **78,957 km²** (ESRI:54034), against a stated land area
of 77,901 km² and a total of 80,231 km² with inland water. The boundary with England has not moved in
the span.

## Decisions

- **Span 1800-2025**: the territory is unchanged over the whole database period, so one row, under
  the polity-definition rule, although the labelled years are 1921-1951.
- **Container Great Britain**, not the United Kingdom directly: the UK row changes in 1921 and Great
  Britain does not, and Great Britain already carries the edges to both UK rows.
