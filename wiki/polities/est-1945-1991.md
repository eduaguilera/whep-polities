# Estonian SSR (1945-1991)

## Summary

The Estonian SSR after the transfer of most of Petseri county and the land east of the Narva river
(Ivangorod) to the Russian SFSR, until the restoration of independence in 1991 [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §estonia](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#estonia).
Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old `EST-1940-1991` row at 1945 (issue 686); the earlier years
are [est-1940-1945](est-1940-1945.md).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried the interwar polygon to 1991, so Estonian data for
1945-1990 was measured against a territory that still included about 2,200 km² of Pskov and Leningrad
oblasts. The pre-1961 input data reports Estonia for these years.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. CShapes 2.0 gwcode 366, the 1991-2019 step. The 1945 border is the one
Estonia kept at independence in 1991, so the modern step is the territory of this row; CShapes has no
Estonian step for 1940-1991 because it codes the USSR as one state then
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

**km² reasoning.** The step measures 45,770 km² (Mollweide), against 49,143 km² for the interwar step.
The 3,373 km² difference is larger than the ~2,200 km² transferred because the two CShapes steps also
draw Lake Peipus and the coast differently. Petseri (27.6 E, 57.8 N) and Ivangorod (28.2 E, 59.4 N)
fall inside the interwar step and outside this one.
