# Egypt (1899-1925)

## Summary

A **27-year row** covering the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium period through
Egyptian independence and slightly beyond
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Key events: Anglo-Egyptian Condominium over Sudan (1899), British
protectorate declared (1914), Egyptian independence (1922), 1923
constitution, Sudan crisis (1924). CShapes area ~137 sq deg
throughout (same polygon 1899-1925).

Predecessor: [egy-1800-1899](egy-1800-1899.md). Successor:
[egy-1925-1967](egy-1925-1967.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 per the CSV. Egypt plus nominal
  co-administration of Sudan via the Condominium.

- **Successor:** [egy-1925-1967](egy-1925-1967.md) -- Sudan separated

## Sourced claims

- **1899: Anglo-Egyptian Condominium.** Joint British-Egyptian
  administration of Sudan. Start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1914-12-18: British protectorate declared.** Britain formally
  detached Egypt from the Ottoman Empire at the outbreak of WWI.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1922-02-28: Unilateral Declaration of Egyptian Independence.** Britain
  ended its protectorate (declared 1914) and recognized Egypt as an
  independent kingdom, though with reserved matters (Suez Canal, Sudan,
  minority protections, imperial communications).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1923: Egyptian constitution promulgated.** First modern constitution.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1924: Sudan crisis.** Assassination of Sir Lee Stack; British ultimatum
  forced Egyptian withdrawal from Sudan. Triggered the 1925 territorial
  split in CShapes (area 137 → 90 sq deg).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
