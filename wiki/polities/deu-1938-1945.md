# Germany (1938-1945)

## Summary

This row tracks Nazi Germany for **8 years**, from the Anschluss with
Austria to total military defeat
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV has
`successor = NA` -- a gap, since territory continued through
occupation zones to West Germany (F78-1949-1990), East Germany
(F77-1949-1990), and eventually reunified Germany
([deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md)).

The row covers massive territorial expansion and contraction:
Anschluss (Austria, 13 March 1938), Sudetenland (Munich Agreement,
September 1938), rest of Czechoslovakia (March 1939), WWII conquests
1939-1942, then total contraction to unconditional surrender (8 May
1945). COW code 260 is continuous throughout
[cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

The 1945 end date marks complete military defeat and Allied
occupation -- Germany ceased to exist as a sovereign state.

Biger notes: "After World War II Germany was laid waste and occupied
by the Allied powers. They divided it into Western and Eastern zones"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

Predecessor: [deu-1920-1938](deu-1920-1938.md) -- Anschluss with
Austria (13 March 1938).
Successor: NA per CSV.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=260`. The polygon for this period must cover radical
  changes: pre-war Germany + Austria (~541,000 km²), wartime
  conquests, then collapse to nothing (occupied zones).
- **Area.** Starting at ~541,000 km² (Germany ~470,000 + Austria
  ~84,000 km² from the Anschluss). The Sudetenland added ~28,000 km²
  in October 1938. Wartime conquests 1939-1942 vastly expanded
  controlled territory but this was occupation, not sovereign
  incorporation in the sense the CSV tracks.
- **COW code 260** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Germany (`GMY`, `ccode=260`) as continuous from
  1816-01-01 -- no break at 1938 or 1945
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [deu-1920-1938](deu-1920-1938.md) -- the Weimar
  Republic / early Nazi period row. The 1938 split is the Anschluss
  with Austria (13 March 1938), a real territorial event adding
  ~84,000 km². Cross-reference with
  [aut-1919-2025](aut-1919-2025.md) which has a COW gap at
  1938-03-13.
- **Successor:** NA per CSV. This is a **gap** in the chain:
  Germany's territory did not vanish in 1945 -- it was divided into
  Allied occupation zones (American, British, French, Soviet) which
  became the Federal Republic of Germany (West, F78-1949-1990) and
  the German Democratic Republic (East, F77-1949-1990) in 1949. The
  chain eventually continues to [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md)
  (reunified Germany). The 1945-1949 occupation period and the
  1949-1990 FRG/GDR period are not yet in the wiki.

## Sourced claims

- **1938-03-13: Anschluss.** Austria absorbed into Nazi Germany.
  This is the start-of-row event. Cross-reference:
  [aut-1919-2025](aut-1919-2025.md) has a COW gap at 1938-03-13.
  ~84,000 km² added.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1938-09-30: Munich Agreement.** Germany gained the Sudetenland
  from Czechoslovakia (~28,000 km²). A mid-row territorial change
  with no CSV split.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1939-03-15: Occupation of rest of Czechoslovakia.** Germany
  occupied Bohemia and Moravia (Protectorate), Slovakia became a
  puppet state.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1939-09-01: Invasion of Poland.** Start of WWII in Europe.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1945-05-08: Unconditional surrender.** Germany ceased to exist
  as a sovereign state. Allied occupation began.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **Post-1945: division into zones.** Biger: "After World War II
  Germany was laid waste and occupied by the Allied powers. They
  divided it into Western and Eastern zones"
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 260, continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- no break at 1945
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Open questions

### oq-successor-chain-gap

The CSV has `successor = NA` for this row, but German territory
continued through Allied occupation zones (1945-1949) to West Germany
(F78-1949-1990) and East Germany (F77-1949-1990), eventually to
[deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) (reunified Germany). The chain has
two gaps: 1945-1949 (occupation, no sovereign German state) and
1949-1990 (FRG/GDR pages not yet created). This is a CSV coverage
gap, not a historical discontinuity.

### oq-territorial-expansion-1938-1942

Germany massively expanded 1938-1942 (Anschluss, Sudetenland,
Czechoslovakia, Poland, France, etc.) then contracted to total defeat.
The row covers both phases with a single polygon entry. CShapes
presumably records polygon changes at major annexation dates --
verify which territorial changes CShapes captures vs. treats as
military occupation.
