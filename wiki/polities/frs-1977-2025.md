# Djibouti (post-independence, 1977–2025)

## Summary

Independent Djibouti from its independence on 27 June 1977 to the present. The territory — formerly French Somaliland and then the French Territory of the Afars and Issas — became the last country in continental Africa to gain independence. WHEP codes this row as FRS-1977-2025, continuing the FRS polity-code chain from FRS-1884-1977 (the colonial period). The predecessor DJI-1886-2025 has been retired as a duplicate; the authoritative colonial-era row is [frs-1884-1977](frs-1884-1977.md). cow_code corrected from 987 (Micronesia — data error) to 522 (Djibouti) as part of the 2026-07-01 consolidation.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Area approximately 23,200 km² [8,958 sq. miles]. Bounded by Eritrea (110 km), Ethiopia (349 km), and Somalia (58 km); coastline along the Gulf of Aden and the Bab-el-Mandeb strait. [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti). CShapes assigns the territory to its full independence polygon from 1977 onward under cowcode=522.
