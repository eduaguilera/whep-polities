# Myanmar

## Summary

Myanmar (Burma) as tracked by WHEP from 1885, when Britain conquered Upper Burma and the kingdom was abolished on 1 January 1886. Burma became a province of India under the British, was occupied by Japan during WWII, and became independent in 1948. The country changed its name to Myanmar in 1989.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon source:** Cliopatria v0.1.3, Burma feature, 1967 time-step.

**Polygon status: `polygon_vintage_drift`.** The polygon vintage year (1967) is 82 years later than the polity start year (1885). This is a real extent question per WHEP rules, not a cosmetic issue. The 1967 Cliopatria snapshot is used as a single proxy across a 140-year span that includes three distinct territorial regimes:

1. **1885-1948 (British Burma):** The province of Burma under British India, separated from India in 1937 as a crown colony. The Shan States (eastern highlands, ~155,000 km2) and other Frontier Areas (Kachin, Chin, Kayah) were administered as semi-autonomous entities under indirect British rule, rather than being fully integrated into Ministerial Burma. Their inclusion in the polygon is approximate; the effective British administrative boundary was fluid in the hill-tract periphery. Additionally, the outer boundary with China in the northeast was not demarcated until 1960 (Sino-Burmese Boundary Treaty, signed 1 October 1960). For data years 1885-1960, the northeastern corner of the polygon represents an undemarcated limit rather than a surveyed boundary.

2. **1948-1989 (Union of Burma / Socialist Republic of the Union of Burma):** Independent state from 4 January 1948. The 1960 China border settlement is the major boundary change within this period (see above); adjustments were modest (minor rectifications to the 1941 Convention boundary). The Cliopatria 1967 snapshot broadly captures post-independence Burma but may not reflect the exact 1941/1960 conventional boundary along the China frontier.

3. **1989-2025 (Union of Myanmar):** Name change only; no territorial change.

**Direction and magnitude of approximation:** The main discrepancies for colonial-era (pre-1960) data are:
- Northeastern boundary with China: undemarcated until 1960; the 1967 polygon reflects the post-treaty line. Magnitude is minor (a few thousand km2 at most in disputed hill tracts).
- Shan States and Frontier Areas integration: the 1967 polygon includes these areas in full, which is appropriate for most data variables (the Shan States were de facto within the British Burma tariff/customs zone and later the Union). For data explicitly scoped to Ministerial Burma only (rare), the polygon overstates by approximately 155,000 km2 out of ~680,000 km2 total (~23%).

Myanmar is bounded by China (2,185 km), Laos (235 km), Thailand (1,800 km), Bangladesh (193 km), and India (1,463 km). [biger-1995 §myanmar](../sources/biger-1995.md#myanmar)

**Split recommendation:** The 140-year span and 82-year vintage drift are large enough that splitting into sub-periods would meaningfully reduce approximation. Recommended split points:
- **1885-1948** (British Burma): seek a Cliopatria or CShapes time-step for ~1920-1937. CShapes 2.0 covers Burma from 1886 as a British dependency (COW flag); a CShapes polygon for 1886-1937 would be preferable for the colonial period.
- **1948-2025** (independent Union of Burma / Myanmar): the Cliopatria 1967 snapshot is an adequate proxy for post-independence Burma; polygon_status could be upgraded to assigned for this sub-period. CShapes 2.0 covers Burma/Myanmar from 1937 onward and a CShapes polygon for 1948+ would be the preferred source.

Until a split is performed, the single 1967 Cliopatria polygon remains the best available proxy. Data consumers should be aware of the ~82-year vintage drift for colonial-period observations.

## Step-change note (rice paddy, MMR-1852-1885 to MMR-1885-2025)

The approximately 5x jump in rice paddy production at the 1885 boundary (from ~834,000 t in MMR-1852-1885 to ~4,015,500 t in MMR-1885-2025) is **not a routing error**. MMR-1852-1885 covers only the rump Konbaung kingdom (Upper Burma, ~437,000 km2, centred on Mandalay and the dry zone). MMR-1885-2025 incorporates Lower Burma -- the Irrawaddy delta rice bowl (Pegu division, Arakan, Tenasserim) which had been under British control since 1826/1852 and was the dominant rice-producing region. A 4-5x rice production jump when the delta is incorporated is geographically correct and requires no fix.

## Decisions

### dec-polygon-status-approximate

The polygon_status is set to **`polygon_vintage_drift`** (not `assigned`) because the 82-year vintage drift (1885 data, 1967 polygon) is above the WHEP threshold for treating as a real extent question. The 1967 Cliopatria snapshot is the best available single-polygon proxy, but the approximation must be disclosed. Future work should split into 1885-1948 / 1948-2025 sub-periods and source a CShapes 2.0 polygon for the post-1886 colonial period. (Recorded as `approximate` until 2026-07-28; see [Polygon status consolidated](#polygon-status-consolidated-added-2026-07-28).)

## Open questions

### oq-split-british-burma-1885-1948

A split at 1948 (British Burma vs independent Union of Burma) would reduce the vintage drift from 82 years (currently 1885 vs 1967) to 0 years for the post-1948 segment. CShapes 2.0 covers Burma as a British dependency from 1886 (COW coding), providing observed polygons for 1886-1948. This split should be prioritised in the next polygon ingest.

### oq-china-border-1960

The Sino-Burmese Boundary Treaty (1 October 1960) settled the northeast boundary, including rectification of the 1941 Panglong Convention line. For data years 1885-1959, the northeastern boundary used in the Cliopatria polygon (reflecting the post-1960 line) is approximately correct but slightly ahead of the actual demarcated border. Magnitude is minor (estimated <5,000 km2) and unlikely to affect production/trade data materially, but should be documented if a split polity is created for the colonial period.

### oq-shan-state-boundaries

Myanmar's eastern boundaries with Laos and Thailand pass through the Shan States. The Shan States (~155,000 km2) were administered under indirect British rule and their formal boundary with Thailand/French Indochina was defined in stages through the late 19th and early 20th centuries. For most WHEP data variables (production, trade flows), the Shan States are correctly included. For variables scoped to Ministerial Burma only, a researcher should apply a downward adjustment of approximately 23%.

## Polygon status consolidated (added 2026-07-28)

`polygon_status` changed from `approximate` to **`polygon_vintage_drift`** in the
polygon_status vocabulary consolidation
([log lint-consolidate-polygon-status](../log.md#lint-consolidate-polygon-status)).
`approximate` was a legacy near-synonym of `estimate` and was not one of the
values `scripts/validate_polygons.py` recognises, so this row's polygon claim
was invisible to check C.

`polygon_vintage_drift` was chosen over `estimate` because this page's own reason
is a vintage one, stated in those words above: a single 1967 Cliopatria snapshot
back-projected across a span starting in 1885, an 82-year drift. The geometry
itself is sound — it measures **675,971 km²** against the ~680,000 km² this page
cites for Burma/Myanmar — so the problem is not that the feature is approximate
but that its vintage does not represent most of the row's span. That is the same
use as [BRA-1800-1903](bra-1800-1903.md) (1890 polygon, row from 1800) and
[IDN-1800-1945](idn-1800-1945.md) (1900 polygon, row from 1800). Validator
behaviour is identical for `estimate` and `polygon_vintage_drift` (divergence
reported, not failed), so the label is chosen to name the cause. The recommended
1885-1948 / 1948-2025 split above remains the real fix.
