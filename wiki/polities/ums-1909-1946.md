# Unfederated Malay States (1909-1946)

## Summary

The Unfederated Malay States, "the collective term for five distinct British protected states situated
in the Malay Peninsula during the early to mid-twentieth century. These states were Johor, Kedah,
Kelantan, Perlis and Terengganu"; "each of them functioned as an individual protectorate under British
oversight and were not recognised as a single entity in international law". The four northern states
came under British protection by the 1909 Anglo-Siamese treaty. One of the three parts of
[gbm-1895-1946](gbm-1895-1946.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §unfederated-malay-states](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#unfederated-malay-states)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british unfederated malay states` reports the five states together for 1913 to 1937
(**5 (year, label) pairs**). No polity was the group. The individual states (`british johor`,
`british kedah`, ...) stay unrouted: no polygon source here draws them. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
