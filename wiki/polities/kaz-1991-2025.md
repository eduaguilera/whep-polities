# Kazakhstan (1991-2025)

## Summary

Independent Kazakhstan from the dissolution of the USSR to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~2,725,000 km² (9th largest country in the world). Last Soviet republic
to declare independence (16 December 1991). Oil and gas economy. Capital
moved from Almaty to Astana (1997).

Predecessor: [f228-1945-1991](f228-1945-1991.md). Successor: NA (still
active).

## Sourced claims

- **1991-12-16: Independence declared.** Kazakhstan was the last Soviet
  republic to declare independence.
  [biger-1995 §kazakhstan](../sources/biger-1995.md#kazakhstan)
- **1997: Capital moved.** Capital relocated from Almaty to Astana
  (renamed Nur-Sultan 2019-2022, reverted to Astana).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **Area and borders.** Biger: area 1,049,156 sq. miles (2,717,300
  sq. km.), pop. 17,200,000 (1994). Bounded by Russia (6,846 km.),
  China (1,533 km.), Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, and the
  Caspian Sea. Capital is Alma-Ata (p.333)
  [biger-1995 §kazakhstan](../sources/biger-1995.md#kazakhstan).
- **1991 independence.** "In 1991 Kazakhstan became an independent
  state within the framework of the Commonwealth of Independent
  States" (p.334). Full international border since Alma-Ata
  Declaration of 21 December 1991 (p.335)
  [biger-1995 §kazakhstan](../sources/biger-1995.md#kazakhstan).
- **Soviet-era boundary.** "The administrative boundary between
  Kazakhstan and Russia was modified during the 1960s but there
  have been no changes since" (p.335)
  [biger-1995 §kazakhstan](../sources/biger-1995.md#kazakhstan).
