# Côte d'Ivoire (1893-1902)


> **`end_year` moved 1900 -> 1902 on 2026-08-13 (issue 77): the years 1900 and 1901 belonged to no
> Ivorian polity, and the cause was a dropped CShapes step, not a wrong date.**
>
> `end_year` is **exclusive**, so this row covered 1893-1899 while
> [civ-1902-1932](civ-1902-1932.md) begins in 1902. **1900 and 1901 fell between them.** Issue 77
> called this "a two-year hole with no obvious event; most likely an error in one of the two end
> dates". Neither end date is wrong. CShapes 2.0 has a **third step** in between which WHEP never
> transcribed:
>
> | CShapes step | area (ESRI:54034) | capital | WHEP row |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1893-03-10 -> 1900-11-14 | 321,307.9 km² | Grand Bassam | this row |
> | **1900-11-15 -> 1902-03-19** | **321,307.9 km²** | **Bingerville** | **none — the hole** |
> | 1902-03-20 -> 1932-09-04 | 321,307.9 km² | Bingerville | `CIV-1902-1932` |
>
> **The missing step is a CAPITAL MOVE, not a territorial change** — Grand-Bassam to Bingerville on
> 15 November 1900 — and the three steps' geometries measure the same 321,307.9 km² to the digit. So
> absorbing it into this row costs no territorial precision, which is exactly the rule already used
> to merge `LAO-1893-1896` + `LAO-1896-1953` in April 2026: CShapes steps with identical areas and
> no documented boundary change are merged rather than kept apart. The 1902-03-20 step is likewise
> non-territorial (same area, same capital, same name).
>
> **The direction follows the event date.** The move happened on 15 November 1900, so ten and a half
> months of 1900 belong to the Grand-Bassam configuration; 1901 is territorially indistinguishable.
> Starting `CIV-1902-1932` in 1900 would have been equally defensible on geometry and worse on
> chronology.
>
> **This hole was genuinely latent, unlike the SEN and LAO holes fixed in the same pass.** The
> consolidated layer B holds no Côte d'Ivoire observation for 1900 or 1901; `mitchell`'s earliest
> Ivorian year is 1919, which routes correctly to `CIV-1902-1932`.

## Summary

The French colony of Côte d'Ivoire from its formal proclamation as a distinct colony on 10 March 1893 to the CShapes step boundary of 20 March 1902. Previously the Ivory Coast coast had been a French protectorate administered from Grand-Bassam; the 1893 decree elevated it to a standalone colony. In 1895 it became part of the newly-federated French West Africa (AOF), while retaining its identity as a constituent colony. The capital moved from Grand-Bassam to Bingerville on 15 November 1900, inside this span and with no change of territory.

ANSWERED 2026-08-13 (issue 77). The question asked what event the 1900 CShapes cut corresponds to and suggested it might be "a CShapes annual-grid artefact". It is neither an artefact nor a boundary change: CShapes 2.0 records the capital moving from Grand Bassam (-3.750, 5.200) to Bingerville (-3.900, 5.350) on 1900-11-15, with the polygon and its 321,307.9 km² unchanged. The 1902-03-20 cut has no visible cause in the shapefile columns at all (same name, capital and area), so it remains unexplained — but likewise non-territorial.
