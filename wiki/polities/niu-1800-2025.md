# Niue

## Summary

This row tracks Niue as a territorial-economic unit from the 1800 WHEP
database floor to the 2025 ceiling. The single raised-coral island in the
South Pacific passed through a British protectorate (1900), annexation to
New Zealand (1901), and free association with New Zealand (1974). As with
COK-1800-2025 and FRO-1800-2025, status changes did not alter the
territorial extent, so one period covers the whole span.

## Territorial extent

One island, ~261 km2 official land area; the GADM 4.1 adm0 polygon
measures 267 km2. No boundary changes over the span.

## Predecessors and successors

None. Status transitions (protectorate 1900, NZ annexation 1901, free
association 1974) did not alter the extent.
