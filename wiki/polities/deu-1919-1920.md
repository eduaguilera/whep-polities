# Germany (1919-1920)

## Summary

A **2-year transitional row** covering the immediate post-Versailles
period [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV has
`notes = NA` and `verification_status = VERIFIED`.

Germany lost ~65,000 km² of metropolitan territory at Versailles
(28 June 1919): Alsace-Lorraine to France, Eupen-Malmedy to Belgium,
Northern Schleswig to Denmark after plebiscite, West Prussia + Posen
to Poland, Memel to Lithuania, Saar under League administration.

The Weimar constitution was adopted 11 August 1919 but the WHEP row
runs to 1920, suggesting the split is at treaty ratification
(10 January 1920, Treaty of Versailles entered into force).

Predecessor: [deu-1871-1919](deu-1871-1919.md) -- Treaty of
Versailles (28 June 1919).
Successor: [deu-1920-1938](deu-1920-1938.md) -- the
Weimar Republic proper.

## Open questions

### oq-1919-vs-1920-split-date

What event triggers the split at 1920? The CSV has `start=1919`,
`end=1920`, duration=2 years. Candidate events:
- Treaty of Versailles signed: 28 June 1919
- Weimar constitution adopted: 11 August 1919
- Treaty of Versailles entered into force: 10 January 1920

No clear single event at "1920." The most likely candidate is the
Treaty of Versailles entering into force on 10 January 1920, which
would make the successor row DEU-1920-1938 start at the date the
Versailles territorial provisions became legally binding. But this
is undocumented in the CSV.
