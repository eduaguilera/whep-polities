# Panama

## Summary

Panama is a Central American country situated on the isthmus connecting Central and South America. Biger 1995 records that Panama proclaimed its independence on 3 November 1903, placing itself under the protective power of the United States, which administered the Panama Canal Zone [biger-1995 &sect;panama](../sources/biger-1995.md#panama). The boundary between Colombia and Panama runs for 140 miles (225 km.) between the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. WHEP tracks Panama from 1903 (independence from Colombia) as a continuous row.

## Territorial extent

The Colombia-Panama boundary runs for 140 miles (225 km.) mainly along water partings (p.172) [biger-1995 &sect;panama](../sources/biger-1995.md#panama). The Costa Rica-Panama boundary is 205 miles (330 km.) long (p.177). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
