# Austria-Hungary (1908-1918)

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as Austria-Hungary from **1908**
(formal Bosnia annexation) to **1918** (WWI dissolution)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Unlike the two predecessor rows, this row's **end
date is a real territorial event** — the dissolution of
Austria-Hungary into seven successor entities. The **start date
is the same rule-vs-CSV issue** flagged in
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 4 (1908 was a de jure
sovereignty transfer of Bosnia from Ottoman to Austro-Hungarian
authority but not a de facto territorial change —
Austria-Hungary had been administering and customs-unioning
Bosnia since 1878/1879).

The row covers the 10-year stretch from the peak of A-H's de
jure territorial extent to its complete dissolution. The
**real** territorial event of the period is 1918, not 1908.

Predecessor per CSV: [auh-1867-1908](auh-1867-1908.md). Successors: see
*Predecessors and successors* below — the current CSV successor
field is catastrophically incomplete per
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 1.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Full
  CShapes coverage (1886 onward includes this entire row)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The polygon represents the legal extent
  of Austria-Hungary 1908–1918, which includes
  Bosnia-Herzegovina following the formal annexation
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1908-10-06](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1908-10-06).
- **Pre-1908 extent vs post-1908 extent.** The *legal* territory
  changed between 31 December 1907 and 1 January 1909 (Bosnia
  moved from "Ottoman, administered by A-H" to "A-H proper").
  The *effective* territory did not change — Bosnia had been in
  the Austro-Hungarian customs union since 1879 per standard
  historical fact (unresolved citation; see
  [oq-1879-bosnia-customs-union] on [auh-1867-1908](auh-1867-1908.md)).
- **1914 WWI entry did not change the border.** World War I
  began with A-H's declaration of war on Serbia on 28 July 1914
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1914-07-28](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1914-07-28). During
  WWI A-H gained and lost wartime occupation zones (Serbia,
  Montenegro, Russian Poland briefly) but these are not coded
  in the polygon. Per [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes) "wartime
  territorial changes" are excluded unless made permanent in
  postwar treaties.
- **Modern concordance.** COW code 300 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). ISO3 AUH is
  a WHEP-internal code.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [auh-1867-1908](auh-1867-1908.md) per the CSV. See audit
  Finding 4 — the 1908 split is a de jure sovereignty event for
  Bosnia, which arguably should be a non-splitting event for
  A-H itself under the strict WHEP rule.

- **Successors:** the CSV currently lists
  `AUT-1918-2025; HUN-1918-1919; AUT-1918-1919` [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv) —
  **two unique entities (Austria and Hungary), with Austria
  duplicated**. This is catastrophically incomplete. See
  [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 1.

  The **actual** seven successor entities per
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §successor-states](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#successor-states):

  1. **First Austrian Republic** (preceded briefly by the
     Republic of German-Austria, November 1918 – October 1919).
     WHEP: [aut-1918-2025](aut-1918-2025.md), plus the orphan [aut-1918-1919](aut-1918-1919.md)
     which is a candidate duplicate per audit Finding 2.
  2. **First Hungarian Republic / Kingdom of Hungary.**
     WHEP: [hun-1918-1919](hun-1918-1919.md) and its successors through the
     Hungarian republic/kingdom chain.
  3. **First Czechoslovak Republic.**
     WHEP: [f51-1918-1938](f51-1918-1938.md). **Not currently linked in the CSV
     successor field** and its own `predecessor = NA`. Audit
     finding.
  4. **Kingdom of Yugoslavia** (initially Kingdom of Serbs,
     Croats and Slovenes, December 1918). Absorbed Slovenia,
     Croatia, Slavonia, Vojvodina, and Bosnia-Herzegovina from
     A-H. WHEP: [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md). **Not currently linked** and
     its own `predecessor = NA`.
  5. **Second Polish Republic** (gained Galicia from A-H).
     WHEP: [pol-1918-1919](pol-1918-1919.md). **Not currently linked** and its
     own `predecessor = NA`.
  6. **Kingdom of Romania** (gained Transylvania, Banat, and
     Bukovina from A-H). WHEP: ROU-1918-1919 exists but has
     `predecessor = ROU-1913-1918` (its own pre-WWI continuity)
     rather than AUH-1908-1918, so the territorial transfer of
     Transylvania from A-H to ROU is not captured in the
     predecessor/successor graph at all.
  7. **Kingdom of Italy** (gained Trentino, South Tyrol,
     Trieste, and Istria from A-H). WHEP: ITA-1919-2025, with
     no intermediate 1918-1919 row — the territorial transfer
     is absorbed into the modern Italy row without explicit
     graph linkage.

  **Consequence:** any WHEP analysis that walks the
  predecessor/successor graph backward from modern Czechia,
  Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia-Herzegovina,
  western/southern Poland, Transylvania, or northeastern Italy
  will hit a `predecessor: NA` wall and never reach the
  Austro-Hungarian parent. The single most consequential
  territorial event in early-20th-century Europe is invisible
  in the WHEP graph. Full details in
  [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 1.

## Sourced claims

- **1908-10-06: Formal annexation of Bosnia-Herzegovina.**
  "Austria-Hungary formally annexed Bosnia and Herzegovina,
  replacing the occupation arrangement with full annexation
  under joint Cisleithanian-Transleithanian control"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1908-10-06](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1908-10-06). Under
  critical stance, this is a de jure sovereignty change — not
  a de facto territorial change — and is the subject of
  [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 4.
- **1914-06-28: Sarajevo assassination.** "Assassination of
  Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo triggered the chain of
  events leading to World War I"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1914-06-28](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1914-06-28). Not a
  territorial event itself but the proximate cause of the 1914
  war declaration that led to A-H's dissolution.
- **1914-07-28: A-H declares war on Serbia.** "Austria-Hungary
  declared war on Serbia, initiating its World War I
  participation"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1914-07-28](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1914-07-28).
- **1918-10-31: Empire formally dissolved** following "Hungary's
  termination of union with Austria"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1918-10-31](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1918-10-31). Component
  nations declared independence on a rolling basis through
  late October and November 1918.
- **1918-11-03: Armistice of Villa Giusti.** A-H's military
  signed the armistice, ending combat operations in WWI
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1918-11-03](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1918-11-03). The WHEP
  CSV uses 1918 (year precision) as the end of this row
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye.** "Officially
  settled Austria's status as successor state"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1919-09-10](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1919-09-10). Biger
  corroborates with "The empire's dismemberment in 1919"
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria) and "In the Treaty of Versailles
  (1919), the boundaries of 1866 were restored and Austria was
  declared an independent republic" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria).
  Biger's use of "1919" as the dismemberment date is a
  date-precision disagreement with the CSV's 1918 (not a
  contradiction — they are picking different moments in the
  same dissolution sequence). See
  [oq-1918-vs-1919-end-date](#oq-1918-vs-1919-end-date).
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** "Officially settled
  Hungary's status as successor state"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1920-06-04](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1920-06-04). Hungary
  lost approximately two-thirds of its pre-war territory to
  Czechoslovakia, Romania (Transylvania), Yugoslavia, and
  Austria. This event post-dates the WHEP row's 1918 end but
  is the date when A-H's dissolution on the Hungarian side was
  legally finalized.

## Open questions

### oq-1918-vs-1919-end-date

The CSV ends this row in 1918; Biger says "dismemberment in
1919" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). Both are defensible depending on
which dissolution moment is picked:

- **1918-10-31** — Hungary terminates union (Wikipedia)
- **1918-11-03** — Villa Giusti armistice (Wikipedia)
- **1918-11-12** — Republic of German-Austria proclaimed
- **1919-09-10** — Treaty of Saint-Germain (Austrian side)
- **1920-06-04** — Treaty of Trianon (Hungarian side)

The CSV's 1918 end corresponds to one of the first three.
Biger's "1919" corresponds to Saint-Germain. Would be a
minor-precision improvement to pin the exact end date the CSV
is referring to. Current language in the audit proposal prefers
1918-11-03 (Villa Giusti armistice) as the most defensible
single-day end of Austria-Hungary's military and political
existence.

### oq-auh-successor-graph-reconstruction

Finding 1 of [log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) proposes rebuilding
the predecessor/successor graph to capture all seven A-H
successor entities. Until the human applies that proposal, this
row's successor field in the CSV is incomplete and the graph is
broken. No polity page can fix the CSV, but this row's
narrative successor list (above) should be kept in sync with
the actual historical dissolution regardless of the CSV
contents. Linked open question.

### oq-wartime-occupation-zones

During WWI, Austria-Hungary occupied substantial portions of
Serbia, Montenegro, Russian Poland, and (briefly) Romania. Per
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes), wartime occupations are not
coded unless made permanent in postwar treaties, so these are
correctly absent from the CShapes polygon. Worth documenting
as a known limitation for any analysis that needs wartime
trade-flow data.

### oq-trianon-hungary-territorial-loss

The Treaty of Trianon (1920-06-04) reduced Hungary to
approximately one-third of its pre-war territory. This is not
directly relevant to this AUH row (which ends in 1918) but is
load-bearing for the subsequent HUN-1918-1919 and HUN-1919-1920
rows, and for the Hungarian polity chain as a whole. Flagged
here for cross-referencing once a HUN polity page is created.
