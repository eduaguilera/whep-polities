# Azerbaijan (1991-2025)

## Summary

Independent Azerbaijan from the dissolution of the USSR to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~87,000 km². Oil-rich Caspian state. Nagorno-Karabakh conflict with
Armenia. 2020 and 2023 military operations recaptured Karabakh.

Predecessor: [f228-1945-1991](f228-1945-1991.md). Successor: NA (still
active).
