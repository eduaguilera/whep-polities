# Martinique

## Summary

French Caribbean island territory in the Lesser Antilles. Colonized by France in 1635 and continuously French (with brief British occupations 1794–1802 and 1809–1814). The 1816 start date reflects the Congress of Vienna settlement that confirmed French sovereignty. Martinique became a French overseas department (DOM) under the law of 19 March 1946, and was reclassified as a région d'outre-mer (ROM) in 2003 and a collectivité unique in 2015, while retaining full French sovereignty throughout. The entry covers the entire 1816–2025 WHEP reporting period as a single continuous unit; no internal political break justifies subdivision.

Created to cover WHEP `martinique` data series. CShapes 2.0 records Martinique under cowcode 66 as a French colonial dependency (status=colony, owner=220) with coverage from 1886 to 2019.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, colonial-dependency layer, cowcode=66, snapshot year=1886.
- **Method:** `period_proxy` — the 1886 CShapes snapshot is used to represent the full 1816–2025 period.
- **Rationale:** Martinique is a small island (~1128 km²) whose land boundaries have been geographically fixed since French settlement. No territorial changes occurred between 1816 and 2025; the 1886 polygon is therefore an accurate proxy for the entire WHEP span.
- **Coverage gaps:**
  - 1816–1885: CShapes does not cover this sub-period; territorial continuity with the 1886 snapshot is well-documented (same island, same French sovereignty since 1635).
  - 2020–2025: CShapes ends at 2019-12-31; the island remained a French DROM with unchanged boundaries.
- **Area:** CShapes computed ~1234 km² (EPSG:3857 projection); GADM 4.1 MTQ gives ~1201 km²; authoritative land area ~1128 km². CShapes and GADM slightly overestimate due to projection effects on a small island.
- **Alternative polygon available:** GADM 4.1 `gadm41_MTQ.gpkg` (GID_0=MTQ) provides a modern high-resolution alternative if CShapes is replaced.
- **Confidence:** high — island territory, no boundary ambiguity, multiple consistent sources.
- **ESTIMATE note:** The sub-periods 1816–1885 and 2020–2025 rely on territorial continuity inference, not a directly dated polygon snapshot. Not authoritative for those sub-periods individually.
