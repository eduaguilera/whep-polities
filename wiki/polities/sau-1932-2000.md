# Saudi Arabia (1932-2000)

## Summary

This row tracks Saudi Arabia for **69 years**, from the proclamation of
the Kingdom to the Yemen border settlement
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Kingdom of Saudi Arabia proclaimed 23 September 1932 (unification of
Hejaz and Nejd). Oil discovered 1938 (Dammam No. 7). ARAMCO concession.
Row ends 2000 with the Treaty of Jeddah settling the Saudi-Yemen border.
