# Angola (1905-1975)

## Summary

Portuguese Angola between the 1905 Victor Emmanuel III arbitration award (which fixed the final SE frontier with British Northern Rhodesia) and Angolan independence on 11 November 1975. Over this 70-year window Portugal consolidated interior occupation, built the Benguela Railway, and — after the 1961 Angolan War of Independence broke out — held the colony against MPLA/FNLA/UNITA insurgencies until the Alvor Agreement following the 1974 Carnation Revolution in Portugal.

## Open questions

### oq-ang-post-1975-chain

The post-1975 Angolan chain uses `AGO-*` codes while the colonial chain uses `ANG-*`. Whether the two chains should be unified or kept separate is a modelling decision. The `ang-1891-1905` page already points to `AGO-1816-2025` as one of its successors, suggesting the split is deliberate but produces a chain crossing.
