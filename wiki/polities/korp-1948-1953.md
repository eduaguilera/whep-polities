# Korea (whole peninsula, North and South, 1948-1953)

## Summary

The Korean peninsula taken as one unit after it had become two states. The Soviet and US
occupation zones of 1945 "developed into separate governments, named the Democratic People's
Republic of Korea (North Korea) and the Republic of Korea (South Korea), which fought a war from
1950 to 1953".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §korea](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#korea)

Both states have their own rows for these years, [kor-1948-2025](kor-1948-2025.md) and
[prk-1948-2025](prk-1948-2025.md), so this row is typed `aggregate`. It continues the
whole-peninsula series of [kor-1945-1948](kor-1945-1948.md) past 1948, for the one source that kept
reporting the peninsula as a whole.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `Korea` for 1949-1951 beside `Korea South` and `Korea North`, and its figures are
the whole: the 1949 nitrogen consumption, 108.9 thousand t, is South 98.9 plus North 10.0, and the
1951 population, 29,300 thousand, is the peninsula's (South alone is about 20,500). The 1949 land-use
table's `use total` is 20,189 thousand ha, i.e. **201,890 km²**.

**11 rows** (1949-1951) resolved to no polity: the label's only whole-peninsula row,
KOR-1945-1948, ends in 1948 (`year_uncovered`). Routing them to South Korea would have put the
peninsula's figures on half of it, beside `Korea South`'s own rows under the same key.

The label's 1934-1938 and 1937 rows are not touched: they route by name to
[kor-1800-1945](kor-1800-1945.md), the undivided peninsula, which is right.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: CShapes 2.0 gwcode 731 (122,203 km²) ∪ gwcode 732
(97,195 km²), both sampled at 1950. The union measures **219,398 km²** (ESRI:54034), against FAO's
stated 201,890 (0.92).
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)

Both CShapes features are single steps from 1948 to 2019, and between them they draw the whole
peninsula whichever line divides it (CShapes draws the 1953 armistice line from 1945), so the union
is the same ground in every year of the span.
