# Réunion (French Colony)

## Summary

Réunion is a volcanic island in the Indian Ocean, approximately 700 km east of Madagascar. Its land area is approximately 2,512 km² (authoritative) / 2,642 km² (CShapes 2.0 computed). Under continuous French sovereignty from the Congress of Vienna (1815) until the island became a French overseas department (DOM) on 19 March 1946. This WHEP polity covers 1816 (WHEP boundary / Congress of Vienna) to 1946.

Created to cover 62 rows of IIA agricultural production data (1909–1945) labelled "reunion". Federico-Tena (2019) records Réunion as a distinct trading polity: imports 1816–1938, exports 1850–1938. Cannot fold into France: ~550,000 km² (metropolitan France) vs ~2,512 km² — factor ~220x mismatch.
