# Chad

## Summary

Chad is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Chad from 1960 to the present. Chad achieved full independence from France on 11 August 1960.

## Territorial extent

Chad (area 495,753 sq. miles; 1,284,000 sq. km; population 6,100,000 [1994]) is bounded by Libya (656 miles; 1,055 km) in the north, Sudan (845 miles; 1,360 km) in the east, Central African Republic (744 miles; 1,197 km) and Cameroon (680 miles; 1,094 km) in the west, and Niger (54 miles; 87 km) and Nigeria. Capital: N'Djamena. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Chad is one of the poorest and least developed countries in Africa. Its history since independence from the French in 1960 has been dominated by a lack of common interests and understanding between the peoples of the south and those of the north" (p.148). [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- The CAR-Chad boundary was established as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were "united in 1906 and became a section of French Equatorial Africa on 15 January 1910. On 30 July 1912 the boundaries of Oubangui-Chari were established in general terms. In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated" (p.146). [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)
- The Cameroon-Chad boundary runs for 680 miles (1,094 km). The present boundary closely follows the delimitations of the Franco-German Convention of 18 April 1908, establishing the boundary between French and German possessions in central Africa (p.136). [biger-1995 §cameroon](../sources/biger-1995.md#cameroon)
