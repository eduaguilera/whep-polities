# South Sudan

## Summary

South Sudan is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the Republic of South Sudan from 2011 to the present. South Sudan gained independence from Sudan on 9 July 2011 following a January 2011 referendum. It is the world's newest country.

## Territorial extent

South Sudan is a landlocked country in east-central Africa bounded by Sudan, Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and the Central African Republic. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- Biger 1995 does not discuss South Sudan as a separate entity (it did not exist until 2011). However, the CAR-Sudan boundary section notes that "former British and French possessions in central Africa created administrative colonial boundaries that resulted in the present boundary between the Central African Republic and Sudan" (p.147), and that Sudan became independent on 1 January 1956. [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)
- The Kenya-Sudan boundary section notes that the boundary "runs from east to west along the 4 degrees 32'N parallel" and the 1914 border with the "Red Line" established administrative divisions relevant to the later North-South Sudan division (p.338-339). [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya)

### oq-south-sudan-boundaries

South Sudan boundary precision. The boundary between Sudan and South Sudan was determined by internal administrative lines during the colonial and post-colonial period. Precise demarcation remains disputed in several areas including the Abyei region.
