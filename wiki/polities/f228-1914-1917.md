# USSR (1914-1917)

## Summary

The CSV labels this row "USSR (1914-1917)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), but **the USSR
did not exist until 30 December 1922**. This polity is actually
the **Russian Empire** throughout the entire 1914-1917 window.
The label is an anachronism documented in
[log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
Per the critical-stance rule
[log decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority](../log.md#decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority),
the CSV label is preserved as-is and the mislabeling is
documented rather than silently corrected.

This row covers **4 years** of WWI-era Russia. Russia entered
WWI in August 1914, fighting on the Eastern Front with massive
casualties. The February Revolution (March 1917 Gregorian)
ended the Romanov dynasty — Tsar Nicholas II abdicated, and a
Provisional Government took power.

Predecessor per CSV: [f228-1905-1914](f228-1905-1914.md).
Successor per CSV: [f228-1917-1918](f228-1917-1918.md).

## Territorial extent

- **WWI Eastern Front (1914-1917).** Russia's western border
  was contested throughout this period. German and
  Austro-Hungarian forces occupied Russian Poland, Lithuania,
  and parts of Latvia and Belarus by 1915. These were de facto
  losses during the war, though not formalized by treaty until
  Brest-Litovsk (1918, next row).
- **No formal territorial changes** during this row's window.
  The territorial losses were formalized by treaty only in 1918.

## Sourced claims

- **1914-08: Russia enters WWI.** Russia mobilized in support
  of Serbia against Austria-Hungary, triggering the alliance
  system
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1917-03 (February Revolution).** Mass protests in Petrograd.
  Tsar Nicholas II abdicated on 15 March 1917 (Gregorian).
  The Provisional Government under Lvov, then Kerensky, took
  power
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).

## Contradictions

- **Label anachronism.** The CSV calls this row "USSR" but the
  entity is the Russian Empire (until March 1917) and then the
  Russian Provisional Government. The USSR was not established
  until 30 December 1922. See
  [log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
