# Algeria

## Summary

French colonial Algeria from the year after the conquest of Algiers to the 1886 CShapes baseline. This is the row for Algeria as *northern* Algeria only: the Saharan interior was not formally annexed until 24 December 1902, which is why the polygon here is a quarter the size of independent Algeria's. The row is the weakest in the Algerian chain on polygon fit, and the reason is stated precisely below rather than glossed.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `615`, time-step **1886**, `polygon_status: assigned`. Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **655,282 km²**. This covers the three northern departments (Algiers, Oran, Constantine) and the High Plateau, and does **not** include the Saharan interior.

**The Sahara exclusion is not an artefact — it is dated.** Biger gives the annexation to the day: "The Sahara region of Algeria became a military zone, formally annexed by France on 24 December 1902" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). The database's chain reflects that date exactly, and the geometry corroborates it: [DZA-1886-1902](dza-1886-1902.md) measures **655,282 km²** (the same 1886 polygon), while [DZA-1902-1919](dza-1902-1919.md) jumps to **2,442,683 km²** — a gain of 1,787,401 km², which is the Sahara. [DZA-1919-1962](dza-1919-1962.md) then measures 2,318,386 km². So the row boundary at 1902 is confirmed by a source date *and* by a 273% area jump in the attached geometries, independently of each other.

**The polygon overstates this row for its first two decades, and here is the sourced timeline.** Biger dates the conquest and its consolidation stage by stage:

- "In 1830 military intervention by the French led to the conquest of Algiers, provoking the fall of Ottoman overlordship throughout the whole of Algeria" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). Before that, Algeria "was under Turkish Ottoman rule" (Biger, p.28).
- "The French established themselves on the Mediterranean coast of Algeria in 1830" — coast, not interior.
- "by 1847 [the French] controlled the whole northern territory of Algeria" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).
- "[Northern Algeria] was divided into the three French districts in 1848" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).
- "[Algeria was] integrated with France in 1881" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).

So the 655,282 km² polygon describes the situation from about **1847-1848** onward, and overstates French-controlled territory for roughly **1831-1847** — the first 16 of this row's 55 years, i.e. 29% of the span. That is a real limitation of this row, stated here so that anyone using pre-1850 Algerian data knows the denominator is wrong for those years. In practice the row's data footprint begins later: **237 layer-B rows** route here (`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`), and they are post-1850 production series (wheat, oats, tobacco, livestock) from the northern agricultural zones, which the polygon covers correctly.

**Territorial changes inside 1831-1886: yes, and they are the progressive-occupation kind, not the treaty kind.** The 1830-1847 conquest was a continuous advance rather than a datable border shift, and the two datable events Biger gives inside the span — the 1848 creation of the three départements and the 1881 integration with France — are **administrative**, changing Algeria's constitutional relationship to France rather than its outer boundary. There is also one genuine boundary treaty inside the span: "on 18 March 1845 Morocco and France adopted the Morocco-Ottoman Empire boundary ... as their common international boundary in the Treaty of Lalla Marnia" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) — which *fixed* the western frontier rather than moving it, and only as far south as Teniet-al-Sassi, leaving the Saharan section undefined until the twentieth century.

**Should this row be split?** A defensible case exists for a split at **1847 or 1848** — the point at which the attached polygon starts describing reality — and none for a split anywhere else. It is not done here because no polygon exists for the pre-1847 extent from any source on disk: CShapes' coverage starts in 1886 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)), so a 1831-1847 row would be created only to carry `polygon_status: unassigned`, trading an overstated polygon for no polygon at all. Recorded as [oq-dza-pre-1847-extent](#oq-dza-pre-1847-extent) rather than acted on.

**Where the 1886 vintage comes from.** CShapes 2.0 begins in 1886, "the year that followed the Berlin conference on the partition of Africa" ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)) — so for an African polity the 1886 step is the earliest observation that exists, and this row uses it as the closest available rather than as a period measurement. CShapes also warns that for regions where borders were undefined — naming parts of Africa in the nineteenth century specifically — it inserts "a placeholder polygon that represents the borders as they were eventually defined", flagged with a dummy variable ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)). Whether feature 615's 1886 record carries that flag has not been checked against the raw gpkg; see the open question.

## Predecessors and successors

- **No predecessor.** The row starts at the conquest, and the Ottoman *Regency of Algiers* that preceded it ("After 1671 Algiers was a quasi-independent backwater", [biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) has no WHEP row.
- **Successor:** [DZA-1886-1902](dza-1886-1902.md) — same polygon and same measured area (655,282 km²); the 1886 boundary is the CShapes coverage floor rather than a territorial event, which is worth knowing because it means the 1831/1886 split carries no historical claim.
- Then [DZA-1902-1919](dza-1902-1919.md) (2,442,683 km², post-Sahara), [DZA-1919-1962](dza-1919-1962.md) (2,318,386 km²), [DZA-1962-2025](dza-1962-2025.md).

## Key dates

- 1830: French conquest of Algiers; Ottoman overlordship falls ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). Immediately precedes this row.
- 1845-03-18: Treaty of Lalla Marnia fixes the France(Algeria)-Morocco boundary as far south as Teniet-al-Sassi ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).
- 1847: French control extends over "the whole northern territory of Algeria" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). The year from which this row's polygon becomes accurate.
- 1848: Northern Algeria divided into three French départements ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).
- 1881: Algeria "integrated with France" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). Administrative, not territorial.
- 1886: CShapes coverage begins; end of this row for dataset rather than historical reasons.
- 1902-12-24: Sahara formally annexed ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). Falls in the successor-of-successor row.

## Sourced claims

- "In 1830 military intervention by the French led to the conquest of Algiers, provoking the fall of Ottoman overlordship throughout the whole of Algeria" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)).
- "by 1847 [the French] controlled the whole northern territory of Algeria" and "[Northern Algeria] was divided into the three French districts in 1848" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) — jointly the basis for the claim that this row's polygon is right from 1847-48 and overstated before.
- "[Algeria was] integrated with France in 1881" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) — in-span, administrative, no territorial content.
- "The Sahara region of Algeria became a military zone, formally annexed by France on 24 December 1902" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) — the reason this row and its successor exclude ~1.79M km² that later Algerian rows include.
- The Treaty of Lalla Marnia of 18 March 1845 fixed the Morocco-Algeria line "from the Mediterranean Sea to Teniet-al-Sassi" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)) — an in-span boundary *settlement* rather than a change, and it explicitly left the Saharan section of the frontier undefined.
- **COW has no Algerian member during this row.** `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv` gives `ccode=615` a single tenure `1962-07-05 → 2024-12-31` ([cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema) for columns) — independence, 76 years after this row ends. The frontmatter `cow: 615` is a code assignment for the Algerian family, not a claim of COW membership; colonial Algeria is absent from COW by design ([cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion)).
- CShapes' coverage "trac[es] international borders all the way back to 1886, the year that followed the Berlin conference on the partition of Africa" ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)) — so 1831-1885, 55 of this row's 55 years, is outside CShapes entirely and the 1886 polygon is used backwards.
- For nineteenth-century Africa CShapes may insert flagged "placeholder polygon[s] that represent the borders as they were eventually defined" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)) — a caveat that applies directly to a row like this one and is not yet checked. See [oq-dza-placeholder-flag](#oq-dza-placeholder-flag).
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): this row **655,282 km²**; [DZA-1886-1902](dza-1886-1902.md) **655,282 km²**; [DZA-1902-1919](dza-1902-1919.md) **2,442,683 km²**; [DZA-1919-1962](dza-1919-1962.md) **2,318,386 km²**. All ESRI:54034 from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`. The frontmatter's declared 655,481 km² differs from the measurement by 199 km² (0.03%), which is a simplification-stage difference, not a disagreement.
- Data footprint (this ingest): **237 layer-B rows** route to this code per `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`.

## Contradictions

- **Polygon extent vs. effective control, 1831-1847.** The attached geometry says 655,282 km² for the whole row; Biger says French control reached the whole northern territory only "by 1847" ([biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria)). These cannot both describe 1835. The database follows the polygon because it is the only geometry available for the period, and this page names the 1831-1847 window as the one where the denominator is wrong rather than leaving a reader to discover it. No competing polygon source exists on disk, so this is a recorded limitation rather than an unresolved dispute.

## Decisions

- **polygon_feature_year corrected from 1962 to 1886**: the 1962 CShapes polygon (post-independence, ~2.38M km²) was a severe vintage drift — it would have credited this row with the Sahara six decades before annexation. Replaced with the earliest CShapes entry for Algeria (feature year 1886), the same polygon [DZA-1886-1902](dza-1886-1902.md) uses. See issue `dza-1831-1886-polygon-vintage-drift-1962`.

## Open questions

### oq-dza-pre-1847-extent

Is there a source for French-controlled Algeria's extent between 1831 and 1847 that could support splitting this row at 1847? Cliopatria is already used elsewhere in this database for pre-CShapes European geometry and may have an Algeria feature; if it does, a `DZA-1831-1847` row with a real polygon would be strictly better than the current single overstated row. Not attempted here.

### oq-dza-placeholder-flag

Does CShapes feature 615's 1886 record carry the placeholder/undefined-border dummy variable ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders))? Nineteenth-century Africa is exactly the case CShapes names for that flag, and if it is set, this row's polygon is provisional rather than observed — a materially different claim from the one currently on the page. Checkable directly against `data/geodata/cshapes2_full.gpkg`.
