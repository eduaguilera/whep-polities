# British Togoland (1920–1957)

**Polity code**: BTL-1920-1957  
**Span**: 1920–1957  
**Type**: Colonial (League of Nations Mandate Class B / UN Trust Territory)  
**ISO3**: GHA (modern Ghana)  
**Continent**: Africa

## Territory

British Togoland was the western portion of the former German Togoland (Deutsches Schutzgebiet Togo), placed under British administration as a League of Nations Class B Mandate in 1922 (administered from 1920 under provisional arrangements). It lay immediately east of the Gold Coast (GHA-1898-1956) and west of French Togoland. Historical area: ~33,771 km².

The territory was administered as two sections:
- **Northern Section**: integrated with the Northern Territories of the Gold Coast for administrative purposes.
- **Southern Section**: administered separately.

### Why not other sources

- **Paine-2024** (`Ghana_BritishTogoland.shp`): This file contains a **LineString** geometry (boundary line), not a polygon. Not usable as polygon source.
- **CShapes 2.0**: Has no dedicated British Togoland polygon entry; only Gold Coast and Gold Coast + British Togoland combined entries.
- **Cliopatria**: No British Togoland entry.
- **GADM adm1**: Ghana ADM1 data was not available in the processed slice at time of entry creation.

The ~21% area discrepancy is documented here. The polygon represents the difference geometry at its 1957 vintage. Given the composed-difference method relies on CShapes Gold Coast polygons (which may include or exclude Togoland depending on vintage), users should treat area-dependent calculations with caution and reference the historical 33,771 km² figure.

## Why This Entry Was Created

FAO 1952 production data includes rows labeled "British Togoland" representing agricultural production statistics for the trust territory as a distinct reporting unit. This territory (~33,771 km²) is distinct from the Gold Coast Colony (~238,000 km²); routing its data to GHA-1898-1956 would understate the territory being measured by ~7× (wrong spatial unit). A dedicated polity entry is required to correctly match this sub-territory's data.
