# Estonian SSR (1940-1945)

## Era split (issue 686, 2026-10-07)

**This row was `EST-1940-1991` until 2026-10-07.** It now ends in 1945, when most of Petseri county and
the land east of the Narva river (Ivangorod) had been transferred to the Russian SFSR. The years
1945-1990 moved to [est-1945-1991](est-1945-1991.md).

**Polygon status:** assigned, unchanged. CShapes 2.0 gwcode 366, the 1918-1940 step (interwar
Estonia, 49,143 km², Mollweide), which is this row's territory for 1940-1944.

**Which year.** Wikipedia says the Soviet authorities transferred most of Petseri county "in 1944"
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §estonia](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#estonia). Other accounts date the two decrees to November 1944 (east of the Narva) and
January 1945 (Petseri). Estonia was under German occupation until autumn 1944, so the territory held
through 1944 was the interwar one. The split follows issue 686 and starts the new row in 1945.

## Summary

Tracks Estonia under Soviet occupation and as a constituent republic of the USSR from **1940** to **1991**. The Soviet Union occupied Estonia in 1940 following the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact; after German occupation (1941-1944), Soviet control was re-established. Estonia restored independence in 1991 [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

**Corrected 2026-09-24.** The struck sentence named the wrong territory. **Petsamo** (Pechenga) is on the Arctic coast and was **Finnish**, ceded by Finland to the USSR in 1944 (Moscow Armistice) and 1947 (Paris); it never had anything to do with Estonia. What the Estonian SSR lost was, in **January 1945**, about **2,200 km²** — roughly 5% of interwar Estonia — to the Russian SFSR: most of **Petseri** county (Pechory, Izborsk; about 1,350 km², to Pskov oblast) and the land east of the Narva river including **Ivangorod** (Jaanilinn, Narva's eastern suburb, to Leningrad oblast). So the boundary was the interwar one only for 1940-1944, and the interwar polygon attached here **overstates the 1945-1990 territory by that ~2,200 km²**. ~~The row does not split at 1945; that missing era needs its own polygon and is listed in issue #686.~~ Split on 2026-10-07; see the era-split section above.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Estonia" for the Soviet period. The Estonian SSR maintained separate agricultural statistics as a union republic. Previously routed to F228 (USSR), merging Estonian data into the all-USSR polygon.
