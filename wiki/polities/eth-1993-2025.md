# Ethiopia

## Summary

A **33-year row** covering post-Eritrea Ethiopia
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). ~1.1M km2. Federal
ethnic structure (ethnic federalism). Current terminal row.

Major events: Eritrean-Ethiopian War (1998-2000), EPRDF dominance, Abiy
Ahmed (2018), Tigray conflict (2020-2022).

Predecessor: [eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md). Successor: NA (current).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 per the CSV. Ethiopia minus Eritrea,
  ~1.1M km2.
- **1998-2000 border war with Eritrea.** Disputed territories along the
  border; Algiers Agreement (2000) established a boundary commission.
  Final border demarcation remained contested until the 2018 peace deal.

## Sourced claims

- **1995: FDRE constitution.** Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia
  established with ethnic-based regional states.
- **1998-2000: Eritrean-Ethiopian War.** Border war over Badme and other
  disputed areas. ~70,000-100,000 killed.
- **2000: Algiers Agreement.** Ceasefire and boundary commission
  established.
- **2018: Abiy Ahmed becomes PM.** Peace deal with Eritrea; Nobel Peace
  Prize 2019.
- **2020-2022: Tigray conflict.** Federal government vs TPLF; major
  humanitarian crisis. Pretoria Agreement (November 2022) ended
  hostilities.
- **Area: 471,800 sq mi (1,221,500 sq km).** Landlocked, bounded by
  Eritrea (651 mi), Djibouti (217 mi), Somalia (994 mi), Kenya
  (535 mi), Sudan (1,380 mi).
  [biger-1995 §ethiopia](../sources/biger-1995.md#ethiopia) (p.212)
- **1991: Eritrean independence acquiesced.** "In 1991, after 30 years
  of guerrilla and civil war, a new Ethiopian government acquiesced
  to Eritrean independence."
  [biger-1995 §ethiopia](../sources/biger-1995.md#ethiopia) (p.212)
- **Post-1991 decentralization.** "The new Ethiopian government is
  currently claiming to favor decentralization."
  [biger-1995 §ethiopia](../sources/biger-1995.md#ethiopia) (p.213)
