# Corrèze (département of France)

## Summary

Corrèze is a French département in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region, in the historic Limousin area of south-central France, bordered by Dordogne, Lot, Cantal, Puy-de-Dôme, Creuse, and Haute-Vienne. It is one of the original départements created by the Revolutionary administrative reform of 4 March 1790, carved substantially from the old province of Limousin, and its boundaries have been essentially stable since. This entry is type: subnational, not national, because France held sovereignty over this territory throughout its existence as a département: the row exists purely to carry a sub-national statistical reporting geography (a NUTS/INSEE département-level series), distinct from and much smaller than the national aggregates that already exist in this database (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), the same rationale already used for Spanish province rows such as ESP-AS-1833-2025. No existing FRA row could serve as a match_existing target because the only FRA candidates are the three national-total rows, and a département is not its country.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id FRA-FRI21, admin_name FRI21) captures valued statistical rows for the Corrèze département of France under a subnational data source that is not yet named in the routing record beyond "97.6% observed data" coverage; this entry follows the country convention already used for other adm1-level subnational France units in the same routing batch. Previously matched to: nothing usable -- the routing_reasoning states no existing polity matches Corrèze by name, since the only FRA candidates in the table are the three national-total rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), none of which qualifies as match_existing because a département is not its country; routing this data to a national row would collapse a sub-national administrative series into a country-level total. What confirms distinctness: Corrèze's status as one of the original 1790 Revolutionary départements, a stable and continuously governed unit of the French state with its own INSEE code (19) and continuous statistical reporting distinct from the national total -- the same evidentiary basis (a named, continuously-tabulated adm1 unit) already accepted for the Spanish NUTS3 province rows in this database.

## Predecessors and successors

Corrèze has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1790 the territory was part of the Ancien Régime province of Limousin (specifically its Bas-Limousin portion), which this database does not model as a distinct sub-kingdom polity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025 for its pre-1833 Principado predecessor. It has no successor either: Corrèze remains a current, active French département as of 2025 (part of the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region since the 2016 regional merger, which did not alter département boundaries), hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive).

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790**

The routing decision proposed the default 1790 start (the Revolutionary départements reform of 4 March 1790, of which Corrèze is one, carved substantially from the former province of Bas-Limousin). That date is historically correct for when the département was created, but this database's earliest national France row is FRA-1800-1871 -- there is no FRA row covering 1790-1800, and the container-edge rule requires every year of a subnational polity's span to fall inside a container edge that itself falls inside that container's own registered span (a 1790-1800 edge against FRA-1800-1871 would be rejected by the containment gate for extending outside the container's span). Rather than leave a 10-year gap uncontained, or invent a container edge the gate would reject, start_year was moved to 1800 to align with the earliest containable year. This mirrors the same constraint that would apply to any other French département entry until an earlier FRA national row is added to the table; if one is added later (e.g. FRA-1790-1800), this page's start_year and container should be revisited together, and the 1790-1800 gap noted here as an open question should close.

## Open questions

### oq-1790-1800-gap-uncovered

**Corrèze's own 1790-1800 existence is not represented by any row**

Corrèze was created in 1790, ten years before this entry's start_year. Because the polity table's earliest France national row is FRA-1800-1871, there is currently no container this page (or any French subnational page) can cite for 1790-1800 without violating the containment gate's within-span rule. This is a database-wide gap, not specific to Corrèze: every French département entry created under this same routing decision batch will hit the identical ten-year hole. Resolving it means either adding an earlier FRA national row (e.g. FRA-1790-1800) or deciding as a matter of policy that pre-1800 French subnational data is out of scope, and then revisiting start_year on this and sibling pages.

However the locally present gadm-4.1-adm1 file is an explicit 81-country subset that excludes France entirely (zero FRA rows), so the feature cannot be extracted from what's on disk. Until the full/global GADM 4.1 adm1 layer, or a France-specific extract, is added to the local geodata, polygon_status stays unassigned and polygon_feature_id stays null. Whoever fetches it should also check whether GADM's FRA.19_1 numbering genuinely corresponds to INSEE département code 19 (Corrèze) or is an independent internal GADM index that only coincidentally matches -- this page's polity_code assumes the former without having verified the raw GADM attribute table.

### oq-boundary-stability-unverified

**Corrèze's boundary stability across 1800-2025 is asserted from general knowledge, not checked in-repo**

This page treats Corrèze's boundary as essentially unchanged since its 1790 creation (no split, merger, or major reorganization, unlike a handful of French départements that were split, such as Corse into Corse-du-Sud/Haute-Corse in 1975, or the 1968 Seine reorganization into several Paris-region départements). That assumption is standard French administrative history but has not been verified against any source held in this repository, and if wrong it would mean a single polygon proxy across the full 1800-2025 span is invalid and the entry should instead be split at the reorganization date the way MAN-1932-1945 was proxied cautiously rather than assumed stable.
