# Greece

## Summary

This row tracks Greece for **107 years**, from the post-WWI settlements
to the present [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Modern Greece. The Treaty of Neuilly (1919) settled the Bulgarian
boundary. The Greco-Turkish War (1919-1922) and Treaty of Lausanne
(1923) resulted in a major population exchange with Turkey. WWII Axis
occupation (1941-1944). Greek Civil War (1946-1949). Military junta
(1967-1974). EU member since 1981. Metropolitan borders have been
stable since 1947, when Italy ceded the Dodecanese Islands to Greece.

Predecessor: [grc-1913-1919](grc-1913-1919.md) -- post-WWI settlements.
Successor: NA (still active).

## Sourced claims

- **1919: Treaty of Neuilly.** Settled the Bulgarian boundary
  (27 November 1919). Western Thrace transferred to Greece. This is
  the start-of-row event.
- **1923: Treaty of Lausanne.** Concluded the Greco-Turkish War
  (1919-1922). Major population exchange with Turkey (~1.5 million
  Greeks from Anatolia, ~500,000 Turks from Greece). Defined the
  modern Greek-Turkish border.
- **1947: Dodecanese Islands.** Italy ceded the Dodecanese Islands to
  Greece (Paris Peace Treaties, 10 February 1947). This is a
  territorial change within the row -- see
  [oq-dodecanese-1947](#oq-dodecanese-1947).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 350, continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
- **Area: 50,949 sq mi (131,938 sq km).** Bounded by Albania (175 mi),
  Macedonia (153 mi), Bulgaria (307 mi), Turkey (128 mi).
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece) (p.246)
- **1912-1913: First Balkan War boundary.** "As a result of the First
  Balkan War (1912--1913), it was forced to accept a boundary line
  with Bulgaria."
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece) (p.249)
- **1919-1922: Greco-Turkish War.** "after three years of war
  (1919--1922) with Greece, Turkey managed to eject the Greek army
  from Asia Minor and Eastern Thrace."
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece) (p.250)
- **Greece-Turkey boundary.** "runs 128 miles (206 km.) in the
  southeastern extremity of the Balkan Peninsula."
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece) (p.249)

Italy ceded the Dodecanese Islands to Greece in 1947 (Paris Peace
Treaties, 10 February 1947). This is a territorial change within
the row. Under the WHEP territorial-change rule, this could be a
candidate mid-row split point. The CSV does not split at 1947.
