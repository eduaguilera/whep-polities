# Greece (1830–1881)

## Summary

This row tracks early independent Greece for **51 years**, from the London
Protocol of independence (1830) to the Convention of Constantinople (2 July
1881), when the Ottoman Empire ceded Thessaly and part of Epirus (~13,000 km²)
to Greece [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Split from the former GRC-1830-1913 entry, which was superseded because the
1881 Thessaly cession constitutes a significant mid-span border change
(~28% area increase from ~47,000 km² to ~63,711 km²). Each period now has
its own polygon.

Predecessor: none (new state from Ottoman territory via Greek War of
Independence 1821–1829, London Protocol 1830).

Successor: [grc-1881-1913](grc-1881-1913.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** Cliopatria polygon (~47,000 km², vintage 1830). CShapes
  has no pre-1886 polygon for Greece; Cliopatria provides the correct early
  independent Greek state boundaries (Peloponnese, Attica, Boeotia, Cyclades).
- **1864 Ionian Islands gain.** Britain ceded the Ionian Islands (Corfu,
  Zakynthos, etc.) to Greece in 1864, adding ~2,300 km². The Cliopatria polygon
  does not capture this gain; it understates territory for 1864–1881 by
  approximately 5% (~2,300 km² / 47,000 km²). This approximation is documented
  here; direction: understatement; magnitude: ~5%.
- **Area.** ~47,000 km² at independence (Cliopatria); rises to ~49,300 km²
  after 1864 Ionian Islands gain (not reflected in polygon).
- **COW code 350** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none in WHEP — Greece was Ottoman territory before 1830.
  The Greek War of Independence (1821–1829) led to independence recognized by
  the London Protocol (1830). The Wikipedia Ottoman source records this:
  "Greek War of Independence begins" at 1821
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821).
  Coverage gap: Ottoman predecessor rows should be cross-referenced.
- **Successor:** [grc-1881-1913](grc-1881-1913.md) — Convention of
  Constantinople, 2 July 1881; Thessaly and part of Epirus ceded by the
  Ottoman Empire.

## Sourced claims

- **1821–1829: Greek War of Independence.** "Greek War of Independence begins"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821).
  Led to the creation of an independent Greek state recognized by the Great Powers.
- **1830: London Protocol.** Greece formally recognized as an independent state.
  This is the start-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)
- **1864: Ionian Islands.** Britain ceded the Ionian Islands to Greece,
  adding ~2,300 km². Mid-row territorial gain; polygon not updated; approximation
  documented above (~5% understatement for 1864–1881).
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)
- **1881: Thessaly cession.** Convention of Constantinople, 2 July 1881.
  Ottoman Empire ceded Thessaly and part of Epirus to Greece (~13,000 km²,
  ~28% area increase). This is the end-of-row event; see successor
  [grc-1881-1913](grc-1881-1913.md).
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)

The 1864 Ionian Islands gain (~2,300 km²) is not captured in the Cliopatria
polygon. If a composite polygon (Cliopatria base + Ionian Islands geometry) can
be built, consider splitting this row further at 1864 or updating the polygon
method to a composed union. For now, the approximation is documented.
