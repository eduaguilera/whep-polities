# Ñuble (region of Chile)

## Summary

Ñuble is a region (región) of Chile, the country's 16th and newest first-order administrative division, created by Law 21033, promulgated 6 September 2018 and in force from 6 September 2019, splitting the former Biobío region's northern half — the old Ñuble province — into its own region with its capital at Chillán. This entry is `type: subnational` because Ñuble is a sub-national administrative unit of a continuously sovereign Chilean state, not a distinct sovereign polity: Chile's national row (CHL-1902-2025) already carries sovereignty and treaty relations for this territory throughout. What this entry exists to carry instead is the reporting geography: production and trade statistics that a source tabulates at the region level only exist as "Ñuble" from 2018 onward, and routing them to national Chile would discard the sub-national resolution the source actually reports, while routing them to a notional "Biobío (2018-2025)" polity would be wrong because Biobío itself lost roughly a third of its population and land area at the same instant Ñuble was created. The span starts in 2018 (region creation) rather than at Chile's 1902 national start or at the underlying dataset's 1900 data floor, because before 2018 there was no "Ñuble region" for a data label to refer to — the territory existed, but as part of Biobío, and no candidate polity for pre-2018 Biobío was available to receive that portion of the data.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing input is a unit labelled "Ñuble" attributed to Chile with data reported for years 1900-2017 in the source series, none of which can legitimately be dated before the region's 2018 creation. The upstream routing decision explicitly rejected extending this entry back to 1900: doing so would misattribute pre-2018 figures to an administrative unit that did not exist, and the 1900-2017 portion is left unrouted here rather than forced onto this polity, since no Biobío-region polity exists among current candidates to absorb it (no name match, no iso3-sharing subnational candidate) and national CHL-1902-2025 contains but is not Ñuble, so it cannot correctly stand in for a sub-region series. That gap is intentional and recorded below as an open question, not silently absorbed. What confirms Ñuble is a distinct, real administrative entity — not a data artefact — is Chilean statute: Law 21033 (Diario Oficial, 6 September 2018) formally separates the provinces of Diguillín, Punilla and Itata (the former Ñuble province) from the Biobío region into the independent XVI Región de Ñuble, with its own regional government seated in Chillán, effective from the 2020 regional elections onward administratively and from 2019 formally. This is not a re-labelling of an existing statistical category but the creation of a new first-order region recognized by INE (Chile's national statistics institute) and by any post-2018 source that reports Chilean regions individually.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached from this entry. The routing decision names `gadm-4.1-adm1` as the correct registered source for this boundary — GADM's global admin-1 layer does digitize Chilean regions, including post-2018 splits — but the copy of that dataset cached in this repository (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) has been trimmed to 81 countries and Chile is not among them, so zero candidate features are locally available to select from. This is a fetch gap, not a genuine absence of source data, which is why `polygon_source` is set to the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` itself with `polygon_status: unassigned` rather than `polygon_source: none` — per the routing rubric, when the blocking issue is that a registered source hasn't been fetched locally, the slug is still the declared source. No existing CHL polygon in this repository can substitute: the other Chilean rows (cshapes-2.0-sourced) cover the whole country at various historical vintages and contain no sub-national region boundaries, and there is no combination of already-registered CHL features whose union or difference would yield Ñuble's boundary, so a `construct_from_registered` fallback does not apply either.

**Territory description:** Ñuble occupies the northern part of what was, until 2018, the Biobío region, in south-central Chile. It corresponds to the historical Ñuble province and comprises three current provinces — Diguillín, Punilla, and Itata — with its capital and largest city at Chillán. It borders the Maule region to the north, the (post-split) Biobío region to the south, Argentina (via the Andes) to the east, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Its area is approximately 13,178 km2 (INE's published regional figure), making it one of Chile's smaller regions by area; its 2017 census population was approximately 480,000. A reader can locate it on any modern map of Chile as the region immediately north of Concepción/Biobío and south of Maule/Linares.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor entry is listed: the territory Ñuble now occupies was part of the Biobío region before 2018, but no Biobío-region polity exists in this database to name as a predecessor, and asserting one without a matching row would create a dangling reference the containment gate cannot verify. Ñuble has no successor — it is Chile's current, ongoing regional configuration, so `end_year` is set to 2025 under the open-ended-current-unit convention, and there is no successor code to list.

## Sourced claims

- Law 21033, published in Chile's Diario Oficial on 6 September 2018, splits the provinces of Diguillín, Punilla and Itata from the Biobío region to form the new Región de Ñuble, Chile's sixteenth region, with Chillán as its capital.
- Chile's national statistics institute (INE) reports Ñuble's regional land area at approximately 13,178.5 km2 in its official regional geographic data, distinguishing it from the reduced post-split Biobío region.

## Decisions

### d-start-2018-not-1900

**Span set to 2018-2025, not extended back to the data's 1900 floor**

The routing decision found data labelled Ñuble for years back to 1900, but Ñuble did not exist as an administrative region until Law 21033 took effect in 2018-2019 — before that the territory was simply part of Biobío. Backdating this entry to 1900 would assert continuity of an administrative unit that is not historically accurate, and would misrepresent pre-2018 figures as belonging to a region that had not yet been carved out. Following the country's usual convention of matching a subnational entry's start_year to the unit's actual legal creation date (as with other post-1976-reform Chilean regions), 2018 was chosen as start_year and 1900-2017 was deliberately left unrouted pending a Biobío-region polity that does not currently exist in this repository.

### d-polygon-slug-not-none

**polygon_source set to the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1, not to none or a route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route was registered_source_unfetched, meaning GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer is the correct and existing source for this boundary but the locally cached copy has been trimmed to 81 countries and does not include Chile. Per instructions, a route name is never a valid polygon_source value, and registered_source_unfetched specifically means the slug itself IS the source, just not present in the cached file — so polygon_source is gadm-4.1-adm1 with polygon_status unassigned, and the missing-Chile problem is recorded as an open question rather than encoded as polygon_source: none, which would incorrectly imply no suitable source exists at all.

## Open questions

### oq-biobio-pre2018-gap

**No polity exists to receive Ñuble-territory data reported before 2018**

The routing input included data labelled Ñuble for years 1900-2017, which predate the region's creation and were left unrouted rather than forced onto this entry. That data plausibly belongs either to a Biobío-region polity spanning up to 2018 (which does not currently exist in this repository under any name surfaced to this routing pass) or, if no such regional-resolution row is warranted for that period, to national CHL-1902-2025 by default. Someone should confirm whether a Biobío subnational polity exists elsewhere in the repository under a different id, and if not, decide whether one should be created to absorb the pre-2018 portion of this series, or whether that portion should simply route to the national row.

### oq-gadm-chile-refetch

**GADM 4.1 admin-1 cache needs Chile added before a polygon can be assigned**

The locally cached gadm41_adm1.gpkg is trimmed to 81 countries and Chile is not one of them, so zero candidate Ñuble features are available even though GADM's full global release almost certainly digitizes this boundary (it is a real, government-defined administrative region as of 2018). Re-fetching or expanding the cached GADM extract to include Chile would let this entry move from polygon_status unassigned to assigned. Until then, no area figure can be attached to this polity, and the ~13,178 km2 INE figure cited above is a stated administrative area from Chilean government sources, not a polygon measurement.
