# Straits Settlements

## Summary

The Straits Settlements, "a group of British territories located in Southeast Asia. Originally
established in 1826 ..., the Straits Settlements came under control of the British Raj in 1858 and then
under direct British control as a Crown colony in 1867. In 1946 ... the colony was dissolved". They
"originally consisted of the four individual settlements of Penang, Singapore, Malacca and Dinding";
Labuan was added "with effect from 1 January 1907" and the Dindings "returned to Perak in February
1935". From 1895 they are one of the three parts of [gbm-1895-1946](gbm-1895-1946.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §straits-settlements](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#straits-settlements)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british straits settlements` reports the colony for 1909 to 1939 (**26 (year, label)
pairs**), beside `british federated malay states`, `british unfederated malay states` and `british
malaya`. No polity was the colony; British Malaya is the three units together. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** CShapes 2.0 `827` (Straits Settlements, one step 1886-1946): **3,601 km²** (ESRI:54034), exactly
the part of GBM-1895-1946's constructed union it contributes. **Proxy**: one CShapes step spans the
colony's changes (Labuan in 1907, the Dindings out in 1935), and before 1886 CShapes has no
Straits Settlements at all. Christmas Island and the Cocos Islands, attached to the colony, are not in
the CShapes feature; Christmas Island has its own row, [cxr-1888-1946](cxr-1888-1946.md).
