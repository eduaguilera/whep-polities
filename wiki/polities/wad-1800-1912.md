# Wadai Empire

## Summary

The Wadai Empire is a WHEP polity row covering the Sultanate of Wadai in eastern Chad
from 1800 to 1912. The 1800 start date is a database truncation — the sultanate was
founded c.1501 and was a major Islamic polity in the central Sahel and Lake Chad basin.
The 1912 end date marks the French military conquest of Wadai, when French colonial
forces defeated and exiled Sultan Doud Murra. The Wadai Empire preceded modern Chad in
the eastern Lake Chad region and is represented in WHEP as the pre-colonial sovereign
that France displaced. The polygon sources are Paine et al. (2024) and Cliopatria
(2 time-steps, 1636–1894).

## Territorial extent

Wadai's core territory was in eastern Chad (present-day Ouaddaï region), centred on the
capital Abeche (Abéché). At its height the empire controlled trade routes between the
Nile valley and the Lake Chad basin, exercising suzerainty over Darfur to the northeast
and Bornu to the west. Cliopatria records 2 time-steps for Wadai (covering 1636–1894),
reflecting the sultanate's territorial consolidation over this period. Spatial precision
for the pre-colonial period is approximately 25–100 km (Cliopatria limitation).

## Sourced claims

- Biger records Chad's colonial history: "Chad is one of the poorest and least developed
  countries in Africa" and notes that "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were
  separated" from French Equatorial Africa (p.146–148). The French conquest of Wadai in
  1912 is the event that brought eastern Chad under French control prior to this
  administrative reorganization. [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- Cliopatria v0.1.3 records Wadai as a polygon entity with 2 time-steps covering
  1636–1894, hand-traced from reference atlases. WHEP extracts one snapshot per polity
  row; the polygon applied to WAD-1800-1912 is a single time-step approximation valid
  within ~25–100 km spatial uncertainty.
  [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §scope](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md#scope)
- The CSV notes field states: "Pre-colonial empire in eastern Chad. Founded c.1501.
  Conquered by France 1912. Cliopatria: 2 time-steps 1636–1894." This is reproduced here
  as [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv) metadata pending a dedicated
  Wadai academic source.

## Contradictions

The Cliopatria time-step coverage ends at 1894, while the WHEP row continues to 1912.
This means the polygon for 1894–1912 is back-projected from the last Cliopatria
time-step, which may not accurately reflect French encroachment on Wadai's eastern
borders in the early 1900s. This is a known Cliopatria single-time-step limitation.
[cliopatria-v0.1.3 §single-time-step](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md#single-time-step)
