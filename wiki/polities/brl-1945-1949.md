# Berlin (Allied Occupation) (1945-1949)

## Summary

Berlin (Allied Occupation) covers the city of Berlin as a distinct administrative unit under four-power governance from Germany's unconditional surrender on 8 May 1945 to the establishment of the Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic in 1949. The city was divided into four sectors (American, British, French, Soviet) administered by the Allied Kommandatura, separate from the four occupation zones that covered the rest of Germany. Berlin lay entirely within the Soviet occupation zone but was governed jointly by all four Allied powers.

This entry exists because the FAO 1952 yearbook reports agricultural statistics (butter, cattle, horses, meat, milk) for 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct geographic unit for 1945-1948. At approximately 892 km², Berlin is roughly 400x smaller than the full Allied-occupied Germany (DEU-1945-1949, ~357,000 km²). Folding Berlin's agricultural output into DEU-1945-1949 would misstate the territorial base ~400-fold. A separate polity entry is required.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Source `cshapes-2.0`, feature `greater-berlin-1920`, vintage 1945. The Greater Berlin administrative boundary was established by the Greater Berlin Act of 1920 and remained in force throughout the occupation period. Area approximately **892 km²**. This boundary encompassed all four Allied sectors and remained legally undivided until the GDR unilaterally declared East Berlin its capital in 1949. The 1945 polygon is effectively the same as the 1920 Greater Berlin municipal boundary — no territorial change occurred between 1920 and the 1949 division.

**Polygon status: unassigned** — the exact CShapes 2.0 feature covering the Greater Berlin 1945 boundary has not been verified by a maintainer. The 892 km² figure is well-attested from historical administrative records. The polygon should be sourced from either CShapes 2.0 (if a Berlin sub-polygon exists) or constructed from the 1920 Greater Berlin municipal boundary. Pending verification, this polity uses a documented area estimate.

**Why the territory matters:** The city's ~892 km² contained distinct agricultural production (market gardens, dairy farms on the urban periphery) tracked separately by the FAO. The magnitude is consistent with a large European city-region, not a national territory.

## Polygon decision

**Decision:** polygon_status=unassigned pending CShapes 2.0 sub-polygon verification.

The Greater Berlin 1920 administrative boundary (892 km²) is the correct spatial unit. This boundary was used for all administrative purposes throughout the 1945-1949 occupation period. If CShapes 2.0 does not contain a Berlin-specific polygon, the polygon should be constructed from the 1920 Greater Berlin Gesetz über die Bildung einer neuen Stadtgemeinde Berlin boundary. Area 892 km² is consistent across multiple historical GIS sources (GADM historical, Cliopatria). The approximation direction is well-bounded: the city boundary was legally fixed and changed only marginally at the edges.

## Proxy decisions

No proxy was used. The spatial unit (Greater Berlin, 892 km²) is the actual territory measured by the FAO data.

## Why this entry was created

The FAO 1952 yearbook reports 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct reporting unit separate from 'Germany Western' and 'Germany Eastern'. The territorial magnitude (892 km²) is incompatible with routing to any existing German national polity: DEU-1945-1949 (~357,000 km²) is ~400x larger, F78-1949-1990 (~249,000 km²) is ~280x larger. Per WHEP rules, sub-territory data must not be folded into parent aggregates. A coverage_gap polity is required.
