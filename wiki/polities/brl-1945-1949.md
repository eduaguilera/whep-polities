# Berlin (Allied Occupation) (1945-1949)

## Summary

Berlin (Allied Occupation) covers the city of Berlin as a distinct administrative unit under four-power governance from Germany's unconditional surrender on 8 May 1945 to the establishment of the Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic in 1949. The city was divided into four sectors (American, British, French, Soviet) administered by the Allied Kommandatura, separate from the four occupation zones that covered the rest of Germany. Berlin lay entirely within the Soviet occupation zone but was governed jointly by all four Allied powers.

This entry exists because the FAO 1952 yearbook reports agricultural statistics (butter, cattle, horses, meat, milk) for 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct geographic unit for 1945-1948. At approximately 892 km², Berlin is roughly 400x smaller than the full Allied-occupied Germany (DEU-1945-1949, ~357,000 km²). Folding Berlin's agricultural output into DEU-1945-1949 would misstate the territorial base ~400-fold. A separate polity entry is required.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Source `gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `DEU.3_1` (Land Berlin), vintage 2022. The Greater Berlin administrative boundary was established by the Greater Berlin Act of 1920 and remained in force throughout the occupation period. Measured **891.9 km²** against this page's declared **892 km²** (0.01%). This boundary encompassed all four Allied sectors and remained legally undivided until the GDR unilaterally declared East Berlin its capital in 1949.

## Polygon decision

**Decision:** polygon_status=assigned on GADM 4.1 adm1 `DEU.3_1`, 2026-08-13.

The Greater Berlin 1920 administrative boundary (892 km²) is the correct spatial unit. This boundary was used for all administrative purposes throughout the 1945-1949 occupation period. CShapes 2.0 was checked and contains no Berlin-specific polygon, so the alternative this section named — the 1920 *Gesetz über die Bildung einer neuen Stadtgemeinde Berlin* boundary — is what was bound, in the form GADM already carries it as Land Berlin. The approximation direction is well-bounded: the city boundary was legally fixed and changed only marginally at the edges.

## Proxy decisions

No proxy was used. The spatial unit (Greater Berlin, 892 km²) is the actual territory measured by the FAO data.

## Why this entry was created

The FAO 1952 yearbook reports 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct reporting unit separate from 'Germany Western' and 'Germany Eastern'. The territorial magnitude (892 km²) is incompatible with routing to any existing German national polity: DEU-1945-1949 (~357,000 km²) is ~400x larger, F78-1949-1990 (~249,000 km²) is ~280x larger. Per WHEP rules, sub-territory data must not be folded into parent aggregates. A coverage_gap polity is required.
