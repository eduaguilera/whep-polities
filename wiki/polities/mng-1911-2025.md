# Mongolia

## Summary

Mongolia from the 1911 independence declaration following the Chinese Revolution. Under Qing dynasty rule from 1644 to 1911, Outer Mongolia declared independence in 1911. The Mongolian People's Republic was declared in 1924 with Soviet support. Area 604,829 sq. miles. Capital Ulan Bator. The CSV also has MNG-1911-1921 (Bogd Khanate) as a separate row; this page covers the modern continuous polity from 1911.

## Territorial extent

Mongolia is a landlocked country in East Asia. Area 604,829 sq. miles. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
