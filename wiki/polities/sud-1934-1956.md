# Sudan (1934-1956)

## Summary

Anglo-Egyptian Condominium of Sudan in its late colonial phase. This period covers WWII (Sudan Defence Force served with Allied forces in Eritrea, Libya, and Ethiopia), post-war political development, and the path to independence. Key milestones: Advisory Council for Northern Sudan (1944), Legislative Assembly (1948), self-government agreement (1953), independence 1 January 1956.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 625, time-step **1934-07-20 → 1955-12-31**,
area 2,486,943 km² per CShapes' own `area` field and 2,486,812 km² measured
on the equal-area projection. Selected by `polygon_feature_year: 1934`.

**Areas re-measured 2026-08-14.** The two measured figures above were 2,487,112 and
2,580,191 km²; against the committed GeoPackage in ESRI:54034 they are **2,486,812** and
**2,580,014** (−300 and −177). CShapes' own `area` field (2,486,943) is left alone: it is
the source's own number, not a measurement of what this repo publishes.

The 92,581 km² cession below still stands, and the distinction matters. That figure is the
difference of the two CShapes **source** steps; differencing the two **published**
geometries gives 93,202 km², because each has been simplified independently. Neither
number is the other's correction — they measure the source and the shipped polygon, which
is the same separation `validate_simplification_loss` exists to police.

**Corrected 2026-08-13 (issue 121).** This section already named the
1934-1955 step and its 2,486,943 km² area, but the frontmatter said
`polygon_feature_year: 1914` and claimed that the step above "matches the
frontmatter ... via CShapes' next-step preference". It does not. CShapes 625 has a step
`1914-04-21 → 1934-07-19`, so 1914 resolved to *that* step and the row
published **2,580,014 km² — byte-identical geometry to
[sud-1899-1934](sud-1899-1934.md)**, 93,079 km² (+3.7%) above the step this
page described. The declared recipe was right and the binding was wrong; the
vintage is now 1934, which is inside this row's span (1934–1955 inclusive).

**The 1934 step-down is a real cession, not simplification noise.** CShapes
dates the change to **20 July 1934**, the date of the Anglo-Italian agreement
that transferred the **Sarra Triangle** from Anglo-Egyptian Sudan to Italian
Libya. Differencing the two steps confirms it geographically, not just by date:
the change is **92,581 km² lost and 0 km² gained**, and the lost piece is a
single block bounded by **19.07°E–25.00°E, 19.51°N–22.00°N** — the far
north-west corner against Libya and Chad, closing on the 22nd parallel and the
25th meridian, which are the Sarra Triangle's own limits.

So the earlier reading of this gap as "−3.6%, within simplification and
boundary-vintage noise" (the `validate_polygon_period_fit` baseline note, and
issue 121's table) was wrong. Simplification does not remove territory only on
one side of a country and only in one contiguous block: the two steps differ
because the territory did, and for 22 years this row was publishing ground
Sudan had already ceded.

The successor [SUD-1956-2011](sud-1956-2011.md) is bound to CShapes'
`1956-01-01 → 2011-07-08` step, whose geometry is identical to the
1934-1955 one — independence changed no borders. So this row and its
successor now legitimately share geometry, while this row and its
predecessor no longer do.

**Why this entry exists:** Fills the 1934-1956 gap between
[sud-1899-1934](sud-1899-1934.md) and [SUD-1956-2011](sud-1956-2011.md).
Without this, 153 rows of Sudan data for 1935-1955 were unmatched.
