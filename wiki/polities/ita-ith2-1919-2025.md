# Trento (province of Italy)

## Summary

Trento is the southern of the two provinces making up the autonomous region of Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol, corresponding to the historic Italian-speaking (Welschtirol) part of the Austrian County of Tyrol around the Adige valley south of Bolzano. Before 1919 it belonged to Austria-Hungary; the Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye (1919) transferred it to Italy in the same act that transferred German-speaking South Tyrol/Bolzano to its north, over the Brenner Pass frontier line. From 1919 to 1927 the two territories were administered jointly as the region Venezia Tridentina; in 1927 they were split into the separate provinces of Trento and Bolzano, and from 1948 (and more fully from the 1972 Second Statute of Autonomy) each province, rather than the region, became the primary seat of self-government. This is a `type: subnational` entry, not a national one: Italy held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1919-2025, and the row exists to carry a reporting/administrative territory distinct from national Italian totals — the same convention already applied to its sibling province [ITA-ITH1-1919-2025](ita-ith1-1919-2025.md) (Bolzano/South Tyrol), which shares this entry's 1919 start-year departure from the general Italian container convention.

**Why this entry exists.** Unlike most entries in this pipeline, this row was NOT created because a data row asked for it — no source in this repository currently reports a distinct "Trento" or "ITH2" series (0 rows, no source, no year range). It was created for container-completeness: Italy's 20 modern regions are expected to tile the country container ITA-1919-2025, and Trentino-Alto Adige only had its Bolzano half represented by ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 — the Trento half of that region was entirely absent from the polity table, alongside Molise and Valle d'Aosta, which the agent-harness completeness pass (pipelines/agent-harness/author_for_completeness.py) also had to backfill. Before this entry, any hypothetical Trento-labelled data would have had nowhere correct to route except the national ITA row, fifteen-plus times too large a territory, or the regional container itself. What confirms Trento is a genuinely distinct administrative unit rather than a restatement of the region or of Bolzano: it is a real, still-existing Italian Autonomous Province (Provincia Autonoma di Trento) with its own elected government, its own statistical office, a distinct Eurostat NUTS code (ITH2, "Provincia Autonoma di Trento," per data/final/nuts_code_names.csv), and — since 1927 — an administrative identity separate from Bolzano's, even though both were folded into one region (Venezia Tridentina) from 1919-1927.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Assigned. `polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm2`, feature `ITA.17.2_1` — GADM 4.1's admin-2 layer, region 17 (Trentino-Alto Adige) sub-unit 2, the same layer and same region that supplies Bolzano's sibling feature `ITA.17.1_1` for [ITA-ITH1-1919-2025](ita-ith1-1919-2025.md). Applying this present-day provincial boundary across the full 1919-2025 span is an approximation: the boundary between what became Trento and Bolzano provinces only became an administrative division in 1927 (before that the two were one region, Venezia Tridentina), though the outer border with Austria at the Brenner line has been stable since 1919. **Territory:** the modern Autonomous Province of Trento, in the eastern Italian Alps, covering the upper and middle Adige valley south of Bolzano down to the Lombardy/Veneto border, including the Adamello-Brenta and Dolomiti di Brenta massifs and Lake Garda's northern tip, with provincial capital Trento. Its present-day area is approximately 6,200 km2 (a figure from general geographic reference, not measured from the attached GADM geometry, which has not been queried for this page).

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor polity is assigned: before 1919 this territory (Welschtirol) was part of Austria-Hungary's County of Tyrol, which is out of scope for this Italy-rooted subnational chain rather than a predecessor to be linked. No successor is assigned since the span runs to 2025 (present). If a future entry is created for the pre-1919 Austrian administration of Welschtirol, or for the 1919-1927 joint Venezia Tridentina region that briefly covered both this territory and Bolzano's, it should be linked here.

## Sourced claims

- The Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye (1919) transferred Trentino (Welschtirol) from Austria to Italy in the same settlement that transferred South Tyrol, fixing the Brenner Pass as the new frontier, per standard post-WWI treaty accounts.
- The provinces of Trento and Bolzano were formally split from the joint Venezia Tridentina regional administration in 1927, and the region's 1948 and 1972 (Second Statute of Autonomy) reforms progressively shifted self-government powers to the two provinces rather than the region — the same institutional history recorded on the Bolzano sibling page ita-ith1-1919-2025.md.
- Eurostat's NUTS attribute tables (data/final/nuts_code_names.csv) list ITH2 as "Provincia Autonoma di Trento" and ITH20 as "Trento," distinct from ITH1/ITH10 for Bolzano — confirming the two provinces are tracked as separate reporting units in the NUTS system this repository already ingests for other Italian regions.

## Decisions

### d-code-ith2-not-ith3

**Used NUTS code ITH2, correcting a typo on the sibling Bolzano page**

The Bolzano page (ita-ith1-1919-2025.md, decision d-start-year-1919-override and open question oq-bolzano-trento-joint-region) refers to an anticipated Trento sibling entry as "ITA-ITH3" twice. That is wrong: data/final/nuts_code_names.csv lists ITH1/ITH10 as Bolzano and ITH2/ITH20 as Trento — Trentino-Alto Adige is a two-province NUTS2 region (ITH1, ITH2), not three. This entry uses the correct code ITA-ITH2-1919-2025, following the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern with subunit taken from the routing decision's admin_name ("Trento") mapped to its actual NUTS2 code rather than the sibling page's mistaken guess. The Bolzano page's ITH3 references should be corrected in a follow-up edit to avoid misleading a future reader.

### d-no-source-claim

**Explicit that no source in this repository reports Trento as a distinct unit**

This entry was authored via the container-completeness path (pipelines/agent-harness/author_for_completeness.py), not from a routed data row: unit_id is "(none)" in the underlying decision, and routing_concerns states plainly that no source here reports this unit. The page is written to be explicit about that rather than implying, as a routing-driven entry would, that some dataset already carries a "Trento" series. The entry exists so that Italy's regional container tiles completely (Trentino-Alto Adige needs both its provinces represented, matching how Molise and Valle d'Aosta were also backfilled), not because of any statistical evidence of a distinct reporting unit beyond the NUTS classification itself.

## Open questions

### oq-joint-region-1919-1927

**Whether 1919-1927 should route to a joint Venezia Tridentina region row instead**

Mirroring open question oq-bolzano-trento-joint-region on the Bolzano sibling page: from 1919 to 1927 Trento and Bolzano were administered as one region (Venezia Tridentina), not as separate provinces. If any future source reports data for that period only at the joint regional level, it is unresolved whether it should route to this provincial entry (with the modern provincial boundary as a backward-projected approximation), to Bolzano's equivalent entry, or to a new joint 1919-1927 regional polity that both this entry and ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 would then need as a predecessor. No such source has been identified yet, which is itself part of why this entry currently has zero routed data rows.

### oq-gadm-feature-unverified

**GADM feature ITA.17.2_1 has not been independently confirmed against the local extract**

The routing decision supplied polygon_source gadm-4.1-adm2 and feature ITA.17.2_1 directly, by analogy with Bolzano's ITA.17.1_1 in the same admin-2 region (17 = Trentino-Alto Adige). This page accepts that assignment but has not independently re-verified that the local gadm-4.1-adm2 extract on disk actually contains ITA.17.2_1 with the expected Trento boundary (as opposed to, e.g., a different sub-unit ordering within region 17). The Bolzano page separately documents that the admin-1 GADM extract excludes Italy entirely (oq-gadm-italy-coverage); whether the admin-2 extract used here is complete for all of Italy's provinces, or only happens to include Trentino-Alto Adige's two units, is unconfirmed.

### oq-area-not-measured

**Stated ~6,200 km2 area is a reference figure, not measured from the attached polygon**

The territorial-extent section gives Trento province's approximate area (~6,200 km2) from general geographic knowledge, not from querying the GADM geometry attached as polygon_feature_id ITA.17.2_1. A future pass should compute the actual area of that feature in an equal-area projection and record it, both to confirm the feature is the right one (a materially different area would indicate a mismatched or malformed feature) and to give the page a properly measured figure as the exemplar convention requires.
