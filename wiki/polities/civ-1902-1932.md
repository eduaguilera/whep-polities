# Côte d'Ivoire (1902-1932)

## Summary

French colony of Côte d'Ivoire in its 1902-1932 configuration, covering the period 20 March 1902 to 4 September 1932. "The shape of Ivory Coast as a French colony was generally fixed by the French decree of 17 October 1899, breaking up the former French Sudan" [biger-1995 §guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#guinea). CShapes records area 26.34 sq deg for this period (COW 437, status=colony, owner=France COW 220). The 1932 end date corresponds to the dissolution of Upper Volta (Haute-Volta), whose territory was partly absorbed into Côte d'Ivoire, expanding the area to 38.45 sq deg. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area changes at both endpoints.
