# Ijebu Kingdom

## Summary

The Ijebu Kingdom from the 1800 database floor to the 1892 British Ijebu Expedition. Ijebu was a Yoruba kingdom founded c.15th century in southwestern Nigeria, a major trade power controlling access between the interior Yoruba cities and the Lagos lagoon. Its trade monopoly and high tariffs on European goods prompted the 1892 British punitive expedition.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Ijebu's heartland (Ijebu-Ode capital) extended south from the Yoruba interior to the Lagos lagoon coastline, controlling the major trade portage routes.
