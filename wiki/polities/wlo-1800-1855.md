# Kingdom of Waalo

## Summary

The Kingdom of Waalo was a Wolof kingdom at the mouth of the Senegal River (Saint-Louis area), one of the oldest Wolof states. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1855, when France conquered the kingdom under Governor Faidherbe. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). After its fall, the territory was incorporated into French West Africa and is eventually succeeded by modern Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom occupied the lower Senegal River delta and the area surrounding Saint-Louis. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). Waalo's location at the mouth of the Senegal River made it strategically important for both the trans-Saharan trade and European maritime trade.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none recorded in WHEP; the kingdom predates the 1800 database floor.
- **Successor:** [sen-1960-2025](sen-1960-2025.md) — modern Senegal (via French colonial incorporation from ~1855)
