# Nyasaland within Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland

## Summary

Nyasaland was a constituent territory of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland from 1 August 1953 to 6 July 1964, when it gained independence as Malawi. While politically subordinated within the Federation, Nyasaland retained a distinct territorial identity and continued to be administered as a separate unit. Statistical and agricultural data reported for "Malawi" or "Nyasaland" during this period (e.g. Mitchell historical statistics) refer exclusively to the ~118,483 km² Nyasaland territory, NOT to the full Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (~1,260,000 km²). Routing such data to FRN-1953-1964 would overstate territorial scope roughly 10x and is therefore prohibited. This polity exists to receive sub-territorial data for Nyasaland 1953-1964.

## Territorial extent

Area: 118,483 km² (CShapes 2.0, gwcode 553). The territory is geographically identical to MWI-1891-1953 (Nyasaland Protectorate) and to post-independence MWI-1964-2025 (Malawi). Nyasaland's boundaries were fully established by the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 1891 and the Anglo-German agreement of 1890 and did not change when it joined the Federation in 1953 or when the Federation dissolved in 1964.

**Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0 (cshapes2_full.gpkg), gwcode=553, gwsdate=1891-06-10, gwedate=1953-07-31. Method: exact_historical (boundary-stable proxy). Confidence: high.

**Polygon vintage drift:** The CShapes polygon for gwcode 553 has a feature year of 1891. This is used as a boundary-stable proxy for the 1953-1964 period because Nyasaland's boundaries were unchanged from 1891 through federation (1953) and into post-independence Malawi. The drift (~62 years between polygon vintage and period midpoint ~1958) does not indicate a territorial change; it reflects that CShapes records the entity under the pre-federation entry (gwsdate 1891, gwedate 1953). The polygon is geographically correct for 1953-1964.

**Why not FRN-1953-1964:** The Federation polygon (CShapes gwcode 550) covers ~1,260,000 km² encompassing Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, and Nyasaland. Nyasaland at ~118,483 km² represents roughly 9% of the Federation's area. Agricultural production data labelled "malawi" or "nyasaland" in Mitchell and other pre-independence sources is Nyasaland-scoped; assigning it to FRN would create a ~10x territorial overstatement. This polity is created to provide the correct ~118k km² match target.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 records gwcode 553 (Malawi/Nyasaland) as a British protectorate from 1891-06-10 to 1953-07-31, area 118,483 km². The territory is geographically identical to Nyasaland-within-Federation and to post-independence Malawi. [cshapes-2.0](../../data/geodata/cshapes2_full.gpkg)
- The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland existed from 1953-08-01 to 1964-01-21 (CShapes gwcode 550). Nyasaland was a constituent sub-territory retaining its own administration and boundary. [FRN-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md)
- Mitchell (British Historical Statistics / International Historical Statistics: Africa) reports agricultural data for "malawi" covering 1954-1960 (35 rows). Scale of values is consistent with a single territory of ~118k km², not the full Federation.

## Contradictions

None identified. The boundary stability of Nyasaland (1891-1964) is well-established; there were no annexations or cessions during the Federation period.

## Decisions

**2026-06-26 — polity created (sub-territorial gap):** MWI-1891-1953 ends 1953 and MWI-1964-2025 starts 1964, leaving a gap during the Federation period. Data labelled "malawi" or "nyasaland" for 1953-1964 is Nyasaland-scoped (~118k km²) and must not be routed to FRN-1953-1964 (~1.26M km²). This polity fills the gap and provides the correct territory match. CShapes gwcode 553 polygon (feature year 1891) is used as a boundary-stable proxy; polygon_vintage_drift is flagged and documented here.

**2026-06-26 — polygon assigned (boundary-stable proxy):** The CShapes 2.0 gwcode=553 polygon (1891 vintage) is the best available historical GIS source for Nyasaland boundaries. Boundaries were unchanged 1891-1964 (confirmed by fixed Anglo-Portuguese 1891 and Anglo-German 1890 treaty lines). Using as proxy for 1953-1964 is well-justified. No other polygon source (Cliopatria, CHGIS, Paine) records Nyasaland separately during the Federation period.

## Open questions

### oq-mwi-1953-cshapes-federation-gap

CShapes 2.0 records Nyasaland (gwcode 553) up to 1953-07-31 and does not have a separate entry for Nyasaland-within-Federation. The polygon used here (gwsdate 1891, gwedate 1953) is the boundary-stable proxy. A future version of CShapes or a dedicated historical atlas may provide an explicit 1953-1964 record; if so, update polygon_feature_year accordingly.
