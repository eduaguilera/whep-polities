# Italian Islands of the Aegean (Dodecanese)

## Summary

This row tracks the **Italian Islands of the Aegean** (Italian: *Isole
italiane dell'Egeo*), also commonly called the **Dodecanese**, from
Italian military occupation and formal cession by the Ottoman Empire
**(1912)** to transfer to Greece **(1947)**.

The row exists because the FAO/IIA yearbook footnotes for **1953** report
these islands' agricultural data as included in **Greece** (the host
polity in the spec), signalling that earlier series — when these islands
were a distinct Italian overseas possession — must be tracked under a
separate WHEP entity so the boundary change is recorded explicitly and
the 1953 Greek aggregate is not silently backdated to pre-1947 territory.

Italy seized the islands from the Ottoman Empire during the
Italo-Turkish War (1911–1912); formal cession was confirmed by the
Treaty of Lausanne (1912, Ouchy) for the main islands, and the Treaty of
London (1913) for the remaining Aegean islands. Italy administered the
territory as the **Possedimento delle Isole Egee** (from 1924 called
*Isole italiane dell'Egeo*), governed from Rhodes (Rodi). The group
comprises twelve larger islands and many smaller islets; the principal
islands are Rhodes (Rodi), Kos (Coo), Leros, Patmos, Kalymnos,
Kastellorizo, and others. After World War II Italy ceded the islands to
Greece by the Paris Peace Treaty of 1947 (effective 1947-09-15), and
Greece administered them as a formal part of its territory from 1947.
The 1953 footnote records that these islands are now included in the
Greek aggregate.

No WHEP predecessor for the specific islands under Ottoman rule exists
beyond the general [OTT-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) row. Successor is
[GRC-1919-2025](grc-1919-2025.md), which notes that Greek metropolitan
borders have been stable since 1947 when Italy ceded the Dodecanese.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [OTT-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) — the islands were
  part of the Ottoman Empire until the Italo-Turkish War. The OTT row
  ends at 1912 when, among other territorial losses, the Aegean islands
  were ceded. Note that the Ottoman predecessor row is approximate: the
  specific islands may have been covered by an earlier OTT row before
  1908; this should be reviewed against OTT-1886-1908 when that row
  exists.
- **Successor.** [GRC-1947-2025](grc-1947-2025.md) — upon the Paris
  Peace Treaty (1947), the islands were transferred to Greece. The
  GRC-1947-2025 row begins with the Dodecanese acquisition as its
  start-of-row event.

  [Cross-check: GRC-1919-2025 notes Dodecanese acquisition 1947.]

  assign a separate code to the Italian Aegean Islands.

## Decisions

- **polity_code ITAEG-1912-1947.** `ITAEG` is used as a mnemonic for
  "Italian Aegean" since no ISO 3166 code exists for this historical
  possession. The period 1912–1947 covers the full Italian administration
  from the Treaty of Ouchy to the Paris Peace Treaty.
- **type: colonial.** The Dodecanese was an Italian overseas possession,
  not a sovereign state or an internally self-governing territory; it was
  administered as a governor-general's territory directly under Rome.
- **iso3: ITA.** No separate ISO 3166 code exists; ITA (Italy) is
  assigned as the administering power, consistent with how other colonial
  dependencies are coded elsewhere in WHEP.
- **continent: Europe.** The Dodecanese islands are geographically in the
  eastern Aegean / Mediterranean, conventionally assigned to Europe.
- **polygon_status: unassigned.** All four primary polygon sources were
  queried and none contains a faithful historical feature for the Italian
  Islands of the Aegean. A constructed estimate is possible (using modern
  GADM South Aegean sub-units or a manual digitization of the island group)
  but exceeds the scope of this entry; documented here as an open question.
- **predecessor OTT-1908-1912.** The closest existing Ottoman row is
  OTT-1908-1912. The islands may technically trace back to an earlier OTT
  row (pre-1908), but OTT-1908-1912 is the terminal Ottoman predecessor
  in the current database.
- **successor GRC-1919-2025.** The GRC row already exists and is the
  correct successor since Greece absorbed the islands in 1947.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-construction

No existing polygon source covers the Italian Aegean Islands for 1912–1947.
A constructed polygon could be derived by:
1. Digitizing from historical maps of the Italian Aegean possession.
2. Using GADM 4.1 adm2-level features for the South Aegean
   (Dodecanese prefecture) as a modern proxy, if/when that layer is
   added to the WHEP geodata store.
3. Intersecting current Greek administrative boundaries with the
   known island list (Rhodes, Kos, Leros, Patmos, Kalymnos, Kastellorizo,
   Nisyros, Tilos, Symi, Halki, Karpathos, Kassos).

This is flagged as an honest gap; `polygon_status: unassigned` is
the correct WHEP policy until a faithful source is available.

### oq-kastellorizo

Kastellorizo (Castellorizo / Megisti) was occupied by France during WWI
(1915–1921) and only formally placed under Italian administration from 1921.
Whether the ITAEG period should start from 1921 for that specific island,
or whether the broader Italian administration start (1912) adequately
covers the group, should be reviewed if sub-island-level data ever arise.

### oq-ottoman-predecessor-row

The OTT-1908-1912 predecessor covers only a short window. The islands
were under Ottoman rule for centuries before 1908; the relevant Ottoman
predecessor for continuity may be OTT-1886-1908 or an earlier row if
created. This should be updated once those rows exist in the database.
