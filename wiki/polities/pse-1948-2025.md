# Palestine (West Bank and Gaza)

## Summary

This row tracks the West Bank and the Gaza Strip as a statistical
reporting unit from the end of the British Mandate (1948) to the 2025
database ceiling. It is a data-routing polity for the FAOSTAT "Palestine"
reporting area, whose scope is the occupied Palestinian territory (West
Bank including East Jerusalem, and Gaza), not Mandate Palestine — the
Mandate (1920-1948) is a separate entity (CShapes cowcode 665) and is NOT
covered by this row.

## Territorial extent

West Bank (~5,655 km2) plus Gaza Strip (~365 km2); the GADM 4.1 adm0
polygon measures 6,225 km2. The de-facto administration of parts of this
territory varied over the span (Jordanian/Egyptian 1948-1967, Israeli
occupation from 1967, Palestinian Authority areas from 1994); FAOSTAT
reports the statistical unit at this constant extent throughout.

## Predecessors and successors

Predecessor in the data sense is Mandate Palestine (CShapes 665, to
1948), not modelled as a WHEP polity yet; pre-1948 printed statistics
labelled "Palestine" must NOT route here (their territory includes
present-day Israel).

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **299 "Palestine"** reports 1961-2024 (32,534 data
rows observed). Routed here by `pipelines/faostat-era-matching` via iso3
PSE. The pre-1961 alias machinery must keep routing Mandate-era
"Palestine" labels elsewhere (see above).

## Verification notes

- **`fao1952`'s residual "Palestine" label means the non-Israel Arab
  remainder** (Jordanian West Bank + Egyptian Gaza), not the former Mandate
  as a whole. Verified 2026-07-24 by cross-checking the same source's
  "Israel" series, which is reported separately for 1949-1951 and routed to
  `ISR-1948-1967`. Two independent agents concurred. This partially answers
  [oq-mandate-palestine-polity](#oq-mandate-palestine-polity) for the
  post-1948 era.

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `PSE` (constant statistical extent
  over the FAOSTAT era).
- **Type `disputed`:** recognition status is contested; the row exists
  for data routing, not as a statement on statehood.
- **Start 1948:** end of the Mandate; distinguishes this unit from
  Mandate Palestine whose extent is ~4x larger.
