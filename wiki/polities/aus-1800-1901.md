# Australian Colonies (to 1901)

## Summary

Aggregate statistical entity for the six self-governing British colonies on the Australian continent before federation. Historical economic datasets (including FAO pre-1961 compilations and the Federico-Tena World Trade Historical Database) report aggregate "Australia" data for this period. The colonies federated as the Commonwealth of Australia on 1 January 1901.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `cowcode=900`, time-step **1901**, carried as a **proxy** (`polygon_status: proxy`). Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`, this row's polygon is **7,687,109 km²**.

**The polygon is a backdated copy, and that is why it is flagged `proxy`.** [AUS-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) binds CShapes feature 900 at time-step **1927** and measures **7,687,109 km² — the identical figure to the last square kilometre**. CShapes states its own method for this: changes were coded "in reverse chronological order", and for countries with no changes in the period "we simply backdated their borders to 1886" ([cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding)). So the 1901 time-step here is literally the same geometry as the later observation, not an independent 1901 measurement. CShapes' own limitation list makes the same point: for the 152 of 249 units with a single polygon across the whole window, "the '1886 polygon' is the same geometry as the latest observation" ([cshapes-2.0 §known-limitations](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#known-limitations), limitation 10).

**That backdating is nonetheless substantively defensible here, for one specific reason.** Federation did not change external boundaries: Biger records that "The Commonwealth of Australia was created on 1 January 1901 as a federation of six self-governing British colonies" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)) — a federation of existing colonies, so the outer coastline of the six colonies collectively *is* the Commonwealth's. Biger further notes Australia "has no land boundaries with other sovereign states except Papua New Guinea" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)), so there was no land frontier that could have shifted by treaty during this row. The one external change Biger dates in the vicinity — "In 1906 British New Guinea was transferred to the Commonwealth of Australia" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)) — falls **after** this row ends and is therefore not an in-span change. (WHEP tracks that territory separately; see [papng-1920-1949](papng-1920-1949.md) for the later combined Papua/New Guinea unit.)

**Territorial changes inside 1800-1901: internal, not external.** The colonies' *mutual* boundaries changed repeatedly during the span — Tasmania separated from New South Wales on 3 December 1825, Victoria on 1 July 1851, Queensland on 6 June 1859; South Australia was proclaimed 28 December 1836 and Western Australia (as the Swan River Colony) founded 12 August 1829 and renamed 6 February 1832 ([wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15 §what-it-adds](../sources/wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)). Every one of these is a *subdivision of the same continental landmass*, so none of them changes this row's aggregate extent, and **no in-span split of this row is warranted** on territorial grounds. What they do change is which sub-units exist to report separately, which is a data-scope question rather than a border question: individual CShapes polygons exist for [AUWA-1829-1900](auwa-1829-1900.md), [AUSA-1836-1900](ausa-1836-1900.md), [VIC-1851-1900](vic-1851-1900.md), [QUE-1859-1900](que-1859-1900.md), [TAS-1825-1900](tas-1825-1900.md) and [NSW-1800-1900](nsw-1800-1900.md), and a union of those polygons would be the geometrically preferable construction for this row.

The pre-1829 part of the span is the weakest: before Western Australia was founded in 1829 and South Australia in 1836, British claims did not cover the whole continent, so the 7.69M km² polygon overstates the colonised area for roughly the first three to four decades of the row. This is uncited general history rather than a sourced claim, and it matters little in practice because the data this row receives begins in 1850.

**Why this entry exists:** the pre-1961 input data reports "Australia" (iso=AUS) for 1850-1900. Previously this matched [AUWA-1829-1900](auwa-1829-1900.md) (Western Australia) because AUWA carried iso=AUS and had the longest duration — but Western Australia is only one of six colonies. Federico-Tena treat pre-federation Australia as a single aggregate trading polity from 1826, consistent with this row ([federico-tena-2019 §polity-granularity](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#polity-granularity)).

## Key dates

- 1788-02-07: Colony of New South Wales founded (First Fleet, Arthur Phillip) ([wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15 §what-it-adds](../sources/wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)). Predates this row.
- 1825-12-03: Van Diemen's Land proclaimed a separate colony (renamed Tasmania 1 January 1856).
- 1829-08-12: Swan River Colony founded; renamed Colony of Western Australia 6 February 1832.
- 1836-12-28: Colony of South Australia proclaimed at Glenelg.
- 1851-07-01: Victoria separated from NSW; gold rush.
- 1859-06-06: Queensland separated from NSW by Letters Patent.
- 1891-1898: Constitutional conventions.
- 1901-01-01: Federation as the Commonwealth of Australia — end-of-row event.

## Sourced claims

- "The Commonwealth of Australia was created on 1 January 1901 as a federation of six self-governing British colonies" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)) — the row's end boundary, and the reason federation is treated as a governance rather than a territorial event.
- Australia "has no land boundaries with other sovereign states except Papua New Guinea" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)) — so no in-span boundary-treaty change is possible for this row.
- "In 1906 British New Guinea was transferred to the Commonwealth of Australia" ([biger-1995 §australia](../sources/biger-1995.md#australia)) — out of span (post-1901), and hence not a reason to split this row.
- The six colonies' proclamation and separation dates listed under *Key dates* are from Wikipedia snapshots taken 2026-04-15 ([wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15 §what-it-adds](../sources/wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)). That source itself flags that these are "the conventional legal/ceremonial proclamation dates" and that inter-colonial territorial adjustments (NSW ceding land to South Australia, Queensland's Gulf of Carpentaria changes) are **not** captured in its single-year datapoints ([wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15 §known-limitations](../sources/wikipedia-australian-colonies-2026-04-15.md#known-limitations)) — so the claim "no in-span change to the aggregate" rests on those adjustments being internal, not on the source having enumerated them.
- **COW has no Australian member during this row at all.** `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv` gives `ccode=900` a single tenure `1920-01-10 → 2024-12-31` ([cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema) for columns). 1920-01-10 is the League of Nations date COW uses for dominions, and is the same acid-test date the CShapes paper names for the COW/GW split ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)). This row's `cow: NA` frontmatter is therefore correct and not an omission: pre-federation Australia is outside COW by design ([cow-state-system-v2024 §known-limitations](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#known-limitations), limitation 1).
- CShapes' polygon for this row is nonetheless dated 1901 even though CShapes' coverage starts 1886 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)) and Australia was a *dependency* until 1920 under COW coding; CShapes codes colonies and dominions as dependent territories subject to a 250,000-population threshold ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)), which Australia clears comfortably.
- Federico-Tena's database treats pre-federation Australia as one aggregate trading polity ([federico-tena-2019 §polity-granularity](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#polity-granularity)) — external corroboration that "Australia" as a single reporting unit before 1901 is the convention of the trade literature, not a WHEP invention.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): this row **7,687,109 km²**, [AUS-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) **7,687,109 km²** — bit-identical, confirming the backdating described above. The frontmatter `polygon_area_km2: 7686693` differs by 416 km² (0.005%) because it was computed at a different simplification stage; the measured figure is the authoritative one.

## Contradictions

- **Polygon vintage vs. row span.** The row covers 1800-1901 but binds a 1901 time-step, and CShapes has no observation at all before 1886 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)). There is no competing source that maps the pre-1886 colonial extent, so rather than pick a side the row is flagged `polygon_status: proxy` and the overstatement window (roughly 1800-1836, before South Australia's proclamation completed nominal continental coverage) is named explicitly above. See [oq-aus-colony-union-polygon](#oq-aus-colony-union-polygon).

## Open questions

### oq-aus-colony-union-polygon

The geometrically correct polygon for this aggregate row is the union of the six colony polygons already in the database (AUWA, AUSA, VIC, QUE, TAS, NSW) rather than a backdated Commonwealth outline, and the repo has the machinery for exactly that: a `constructed` builder in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`. Building it would also let the row's extent shrink correctly before 1829/1836. Not done here because this page's remit was documentation, and changing the polygon binding would move measured areas that other gates baseline.
