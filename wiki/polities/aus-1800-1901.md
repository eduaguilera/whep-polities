# Australian Colonies (to 1901)

## Summary

Aggregate statistical entity for the six self-governing British colonies on the Australian continent before federation. Historical economic datasets (including FAO pre-1961 compilations and the Federico-Tena World Trade Historical Database) report aggregate "Australia" data for this period. The colonies federated as the Commonwealth of Australia on 1 January 1901.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [AUS-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) (Commonwealth of Australia). Federation on 1 January 1901 did not change external boundaries — the six colonies collectively covered the same territory as the new Commonwealth.. The six colonies collectively covered the entire Australian continent and Tasmania. Individual CShapes polygons exist for [AUWA-1829-1900](auwa-1829-1900.md), [AUSA-1836-1900](ausa-1836-1900.md), [VIC-1851-1900](vic-1851-1900.md), [QUE-1859-1900](que-1859-1900.md), [TAS-1825-1900](tas-1825-1900.md), and [NSW-1800-1900](nsw-1800-1900.md). A union of these polygons would be the correct geometry.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Australia" (iso=AUS) for 1850-1900. Previously this matched [AUWA-1829-1900](auwa-1829-1900.md) (Western Australia) because AUWA carried iso=AUS and had the longest duration — but Western Australia is only one of six colonies. Federico-Tena (2019) treats pre-federation Australia as a single aggregate trading polity from 1826. This entry captures the aggregate data correctly.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Successor:** [AUS-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) — Commonwealth of Australia (federation 1 January 1901)

## Key dates

- 1788: Colony of New South Wales established (first European settlement)
- 1829: Swan River Colony (Western Australia) established
- 1851: Victoria separated from NSW; gold rush
- 1859: Queensland separated from NSW
- 1891-1898: Constitutional conventions
- 1901-01-01: Federation as Commonwealth of Australia
