# Manchukuo (1932-1945)

## Summary

The Japanese puppet state of Manchukuo ("State of Manchuria") from its
proclamation on 1 March 1932 — established after the Japanese Kwantung Army
seized Manchuria following the 18 September 1931 Mukden Incident — to its
dissolution on 18 August 1945 following Soviet invasion and Japanese surrender.
Officially claimed to be the restoration of the Qing dynasty under the last
emperor Puyi; recognised only by Japan, Italy, Nazi Germany, and a handful of
allied states. Agricultural production was reported separately from China
throughout this period in international compilations.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `constructed` source, feature `MAN-1932-1945`. Built by
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` as the union of three modern Chinese
provinces from GADM 4.1 admin-1 data:

- `CHN.11_1` Heilongjiang
- `CHN.17_1` Jilin
- `CHN.18_1` Liaoning

This approximates Manchukuo's core territory (the three "Manchurian"
provinces of the former Qing Empire). Historical Manchukuo also annexed the
Jehol (Rehe) region from Inner Mongolia in 1933; the 3-province polygon
captures ~90% of the state's land area and the overwhelming majority of its
population and agricultural output.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data has 146 rows labelled
"China, Manchuria Province of" spanning 1927-1952. The
`pipelines/pre1961-matching/match.R` has a `name_override` entry that maps
this name to `MAN` for 1932-1945 data, routing to this polity. Pre-1932 and
post-1945 data under that label falls back to `CHN` (per the `resolve_iso`
rule), since Manchuria was part of the Republic of China / PRC outside the
Manchukuo period.

## Open questions

### oq-rehe-annexation-1933

Rehe province (modern western Liaoning + eastern Inner Mongolia) was formally
annexed by Manchukuo in 1933 after a brief war. The constructed polygon
excludes it. Splitting GADM Inner Mongolia to include Jehol would be a
Phase-3 refinement if period-accurate Manchurian data becomes load-bearing.
