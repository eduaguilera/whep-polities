# Territory of New Guinea (1920–1949)

FAO 1952 'New Guinea' 1937-1949 routed here; 1950-1951 routed to PNG-1949-1975. CShapes feature 746, area 237,536 km², vintage 1920. Predecessor GNGU-1884-1914; successor PNG-1949-1975 (administrative union 1949-07-01).

## Verification notes

- **"New Guinea" means the Mandated Territory alone, not Papua + New Guinea**
  (2026-07-24, two independent agents). FAO 1952's population figure for this
  label is **1,006,000**, which matches 1941 Australian administrative figures
  for the Territory of New Guinea by itself (≈990,000–1,000,000) and excludes
  Papua's separately reported ~300,000. So the label is not a combined
  Australian-New-Guinea aggregate, and the routing to this row is correct.
