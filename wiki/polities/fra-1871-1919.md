# France (without Alsace-Lorraine, 1871-1919)

## Summary

This row tracks France as a territorial-economic unit from **1871**
(Treaty of Frankfurt) to **1919** (Treaty of Versailles). The defining
territorial characteristic is that France's metropolitan extent
**excludes Alsace-Lorraine**, which was ceded to Germany by the Treaty
of Frankfurt (10 May 1871) and not returned until the Treaty of
Versailles (28 June 1919).

- **1871 start** is a real territorial event: Treaty of Frankfurt
  (10 May 1871) ceded Alsace and parts of Lorraine to Germany.
  [biger-1995 §france-germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-germany)
  [wikipedia-france-2026-04-12 §1871-alsace-lorraine](../sources/wikipedia-france-2026-04-12.md#1871-alsace-lorraine).
- **1919 end** is a real territorial event: Treaty of Versailles
  (28 June 1919) returned Alsace-Lorraine to France. CShapes records
  the polygon change at 1919-06-28
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).

Predecessor: [fra-1800-1871](fra-1800-1871.md).
Successor: [fra-1919-2025](fra-1919-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

> **Correction (2026-07-29).** `polygon_area_km2` read `62.43`, which is CShapes'
> area in **square degrees** — the figure this page quotes below — written into a
> field denominated in km². It asserted that France without Alsace-Lorraine was
> smaller than Manhattan. Measured from the assigned polygon (CShapes feature 220
> at 1886-01-01) in the equal-area projection `ESRI:54034`, the territory is
> **532,305 km²**, which is the value now recorded. That is consistent with modern
> metropolitan France (~551,500 km²) less the ~14,500 km² of Alsace-Lorraine ceded
> in 1871, as this page's own successor note states.
>
> Found by comparing this repository's recorded areas against the same geometries
> measured independently by the WHEP R package (sf, rather than geopandas): 150
> rows carry an area, the median ratio between the two measurements is 0.9998, and
> this row diverged by a factor of 8,526.
>
> `scripts/validate_polygons.py` had not caught it because its area check skipped
> any row whose CLAIMED area was under 200 km², to keep projection noise on
> microstates out of the results — so a claim that was wrong by being far too
> small exempted itself. That filter now also considers the measured area.

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` feature 220, vintage 1886
  (polygon_status=assigned). CShapes records France (`cowcode=220`)
  at ~62.43 sq degrees from 1886-01-01 to 1919-06-27, reflecting
  post-1871 territory without Alsace-Lorraine
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Polygon vintage drift.** The polygon vintage is 1886 but the
  polity starts in 1871 — a drift of 15 years. Metropolitan borders
  were stable throughout 1871–1886; there is no known boundary change
  in that interval that would alter the polygon. The drift is not
  a concern for territorial representation.
- **Post-1871 extent.** The 1886 polygon correctly excludes
  Alsace-Lorraine, which is the defining territorial distinction of
  this polity versus FRA-1800-1871.

  1919-06-28 [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
