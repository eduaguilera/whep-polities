# Singapore (Straits Settlements)

## Summary

Singapore island before the 1946 Crown colony ([sgp-1946-1963](sgp-1946-1963.md)). "Singapore had
previously been established as a British colony since 1824, and had been governed as part of the
Straits Settlements since 1826"; "The colony was created when the Straits Settlements was dissolved
shortly after the Japanese occupation of Singapore ended in 1945." It sits inside
[sts-1826-1946](sts-1826-1946.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §singapore](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#singapore)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british singapore` runs from 1909 to 1960, beside `british straits settlements` and
`british malaya`. From 1946 it is SGP-1946-1963; its **24 (year, label) pairs before 1946** had no
polity. The iia alias `singapore` 1895-1945 routes to British Malaya ([gbm-1895-1946](gbm-1895-1946.md))
as a sub-territory; that is not copied, because this source reports British Malaya separately and the
island would be counted twice. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** CShapes 2.0 `830` (Singapore) at its 1953 step, the polygon SGP-1946-1963 uses: **545 km²**
(ESRI:54034). **Proxy**: CShapes has no Singapore step before 1946, and the island's reclaimed shoreline
grew later, so the post-war polygon stands in for the pre-war island.
