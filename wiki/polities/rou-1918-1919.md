# Romania (1918-1919)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **2 years**, the transitional "Greater
Romania" formation period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Romania gained massively from WWI: Transylvania and Banat from
Austria-Hungary, Bukovina from Austria, Bessarabia from Russia.
"Greater Romania" roughly doubled in size. Treaty of Trianon (4 June
1920) formalized the Hungarian territorial transfers. The 1919 end
date may align with Treaty of Saint-Germain (Austrian side) or an
intermediate boundary settlement.

Predecessor: [rou-1913-1918](rou-1913-1918.md) -- end of WWI.
Successor: [rou-1919-1920](rou-1919-1920.md) -- border settlement.

## Sourced claims

- **1918: Territorial gains.** Romania gained Transylvania, Banat, and
  parts of the Partium from Hungary; Bukovina from Austria; Bessarabia
  from Russia. This is the start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Formalized Austrian
  territorial cessions including Bukovina to Romania.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** Formalized the transfer of
  Transylvania and Banat from Hungary to Romania (ratified after the
  row ends).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
