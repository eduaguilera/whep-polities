# Sierra Leone (1961-2025)

## Summary

Sierra Leone (1961-2025) covers independent Sierra Leone from 27 April 1961 to the present. Sierra Leone became independent from British colonial rule on that date [biger-1995 §sierra-leone](../sources/biger-1995.md#sierra-leone). Political life has been highly unstable since independence, characterized by ethnic struggle between Temne and Mende, and the country became involved in the Liberian civil war from 1990 [biger-1995 §sierra-leone](../sources/biger-1995.md#sierra-leone). The territory is unchanged from the preceding colonial period.

## Territorial extent

Sierra Leone is a West African country bounded by Guinea, Liberia, and the Atlantic Ocean. Area 27,925 sq. miles (72,300 sq. km). Capital: Freetown. Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records status=independent from 1961-04-27.
