# Libya (1912-1919)

## Summary

Italian Libya from the Treaty of Ouchy (18 October 1912) ending the Italo-Turkish War — under which the Ottoman Empire ceded sovereignty over Tripolitania and Cyrenaica to Italy — to a 1919 CShapes boundary cut (aligned with post-WWI settlements and the 1919 Legge Fondamentale for Tripolitania). Italian effective control over the interior was minimal throughout this window; the Senussi-led resistance held most of Cyrenaica's interior and the 1918 Tripolitanian Republic briefly contested Italian control in Tripolitania.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Italian Tripolitania and Italian Cyrenaica were administratively separate colonies; the polygon records them as a composite.
