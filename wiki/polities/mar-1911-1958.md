## Scope correction (2026-07-24)

Flagged by assertion verification of `morocco|mitchell|1915-1957`
(`page_suspect`): the summary above describes this row as covering the **French
and Spanish protectorates together**, but the database maintains
[smo-1912-1956](smo-1912-1956.md) as a **separate polity** for the Spanish
protectorate, and the polygon attached here is the French zone.

Measured: this row **350,268 km²**, Spanish Morocco **52,792 km²** — they are
complementary, not nested, and together with the Tangier zone and Ifni they make
up the ~446,000 km² of Morocco proper. So the polygon is right and the prose is
wrong.

Read this row as **French Morocco**. The 1,283 layer-B rows matched here are
correctly the French-zone series; the 55 rows on SMO-1912-1956 are the Spanish
zone. Summing the two is legitimate and does **not** double-count — but reading
this row alone as "all of Morocco" understates by roughly 12%.
