# Kenya (1894-1902)

## Summary

The East Africa Protectorate under Imperial British East Africa Company / Foreign Office administration from the 1894 CShapes boundary event (Uganda Protectorate declared 1894, which transferred some frontier responsibilities) to the 1902 Colonial Office transfer and Uganda Eastern Province annexation. The East Africa Protectorate itself was formally proclaimed on 1 July 1895.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Covers the pre-1902 protectorate boundaries (before the eastward extension to absorb Uganda's Eastern Province).
