# Laos (1896-1953)

## Summary

Laos (1896-1953) covers the core of the French colonial period in Laos, from a CShapes polygon boundary adjustment on 15 January 1896 until Laotian independence on 22 October 1953. CShapes records identical areas for both the 1893–1896 and 1896–1953 time-steps (approximately 196,820 km²), indicating that the 1896 step reflects an administrative or diplomatic event rather than a genuine territorial change. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, same area means the same polity — yet the CSV maintains two rows (LAO-1893-1896 and LAO-1896-1953). This is a candidate for review. Laos was administered as part of French Indochina; its northern boundary with China was established by the 1885 Treaty of Tientsin.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records Laos as a French colony (owner=220) throughout this period. The polygon covers approximately 196,820 km² and is consistent across all CShapes time-steps from 1893 to 1953. The China–Laos boundary "was established between France and China by the Treaty of Peace and Commerce (Treaty of Tientsin) of 1885." [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)

## Sourced claims

- "In 1954 Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam were created as independent states and the French colonial boundary was adopted as the northern boundary." [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)
- CShapes records Laos with COW code 812; COW includes Laos as independent from 1953 onward. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)

## Contradictions

The WHEP CSV assigns end_year=1953 to LAO-1896-1953 and start_year=1954 to LAO-1954-2025. CShapes records the independence transition on 1953-10-23, with a new CShapes entry on 1953-10-23 (status=independent). There is a one-year discrepancy between CShapes (independence 1953) and the WHEP CSV end year (1953 = correct, but the successor start is 1954, creating a gap). This should be audited — the 1953 CShapes entry has status=independent, so the LAO-1954-2025 start may be off by one year.

## Open questions

### oq-1896-split-rationale

CShapes shows identical polygon areas for the 1893–1896 and 1896–1953 steps. No historical event on 15 January 1896 has been identified that would justify a WHEP row split. The January 1896 date may reflect a Franco-Siamese boundary treaty (the 1896 Anglo-French Agreement on Siam's buffer zones). The next ingest should confirm whether this involved any actual territorial transfer to Laos.

### oq-independence-year-gap

CShapes records Laotian independence on 1953-10-23, but the WHEP CSV successor LAO-1954-2025 starts in 1954. The one-year gap (1953–1954) is unaccounted for. A future audit should determine whether a row covering 1953-10-23 to 1953-12-31 is missing, or whether the WHEP convention is to round independence to the following calendar year.
