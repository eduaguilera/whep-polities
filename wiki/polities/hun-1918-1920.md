# Hungary (1918-1920)

Merges former HUN-1918-1919 and HUN-1919-1920. Spans Republic proclamation 1918-11-16 through Treaty of Trianon 1920-06-04 (the only real territorial event). Polygon: histogis-1860-habsburg, 325,421 km² (pre-Trianon Transleithania). Polygon vintage drift documented: data years 1918-1920 matched ~58-60 years ahead of 1860 vintage; drift is benign as Transleithanian borders were stable 1867-1920.

## CRITICAL: Trianon polygon straddle

This polity's polygon (~325,421 km²) is the pre-Trianon Transleithanian extent. The Treaty of Trianon (1920-06-04) reduced Hungary to ~93,000 km². Any data observation labelled "Hungary" or "1920" must be date-verified:

- **Pre-Trianon (before 1920-06-04)**: route here (HUN-1918-1920, polygon ~325k km²).
- **Post-Trianon (1920-06-04 or later)**: route to **HUN-1920-1938** (~93,000 km², CShapes 2.0 1920 feature).

Harvest-year 1920 observations (e.g. wheat, maize, sugar beet) almost certainly reflect post-Trianon conditions and must be routed to HUN-1920-1938. Matching them to this polity would overstate territory ~3.5×.
