# Van Diemen's Land (Tasmania) (to 1900)

## Summary

> **`polygon_status` migrated missing -> unassigned (2026-07-29).** `missing` is a legacy value that predates the documented vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md#page-schema), which says not to use it in new pages. It was never migrated, so the manifest's `claims_polygon_status` set omitted it and consumers were told this row does not claim a polygon. This row has no geometry at all, which is exactly what `unassigned` means. See [lint-polygon-status-vocabulary](../log.md#lint-polygon-status-vocabulary).


The British colony of Van Diemen's Land (renamed Tasmania on 1 January 1856) from its separation from New South Wales on 3 December 1825 to the eve of Australian federation. The island had been under NSW jurisdiction since the first British settlement at Risdon Cove in 1803; Governor Darling's 1825 proclamation made it a distinct colony. The WHEP row ends on 31 December 1900 before the 1 January 1901 federation.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependency-level), gwcode **906** at vintage **1886**, measuring **68,137 km²** in ESRI:54034. Covers the island of Tasmania and its offshore dependencies. Borders unchanged across the 1825–1900 window: Van Diemen's Land was the island from its 1825 separation onward, so the single CShapes step is the whole period's territory rather than one vintage of a moving border.

> **Binding added 2026-08-13 (issue 155).** This section said "Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependency-level)" while the frontmatter said `polygon_source: none` — the prose described a binding that was never written into the fields, so the build attached nothing. Measured: this row receives **195 layer-B rows**, the largest single block of the 632 rows still matched-but-spatially-unusable when the class was re-enumerated from the data on 2026-08-13 — and issue 155's own table does not list this polity at all, which is why the class has to be measured rather than read off the issue.
>
> The feature is the same dependency-level series its five sibling colonies already use — NSW 901, Western Australia 902, South Australia 903, Victoria 904, Queensland 905, all at `polygon_feature_year: 1886` — and CShapes carries exactly one Tasmania step, `906` spanning 1886-01-01 to 1900-12-31. CShapes' own stated `area` field for it is 68,137.77 km², and the geodesic measurement of the geometry is 68,137 km², against the island's modern land area of ~68,401 km² (−0.4%).
>
> `polygon_status: assigned` rather than `proxy`: the 1886 vintage is inside the row's period and Tasmania's boundary is a coastline, not a negotiated border, so there is no drift to document. `polygon_area_km2` is left `null` to match the five siblings — declaring the measured value back into the field would be the self-referential area that `validate_polygons` check A2 exists to flag.
