# Guinea (1894-1958)

## Summary

French Guinea, the colonial polity governing the territory of present-day Guinea, from 1894 until independence on 2 October 1958. Guinea was the first French African colony to vote for independence, rejecting Charles de Gaulle's proposed French Community in a 1958 referendum. "The Guineans gained their independence from the French in 1958" [biger-1995 guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#guinea). "France established themselves in Guinea in 1849 when they proclaimed the Boke region a French protectorate and then began its expansion eastward" [biger-1995 guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#guinea). CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
