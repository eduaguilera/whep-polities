# Ireland (within United Kingdom)

## Summary

Tracks Ireland as a distinct statistical unit within the United Kingdom from **1800** (Act of Union) to **1921** (Anglo-Irish Treaty and creation of the Irish Free State). Ireland maintained separate agricultural statistics and census records throughout this period, justifying a distinct WHEP row despite not being a sovereign state.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 composed union of the Republic of Ireland ADM0 polygon (ISO3=IRL, 26 counties, ~70,273 km²) and the Northern Ireland subunit (GBR ADM1 = Northern Ireland, 6 Ulster counties, ~14,148 km²), giving a whole-island polygon of **~84,421 km²** (32 counties). This is the correct extent for the 1800–1921 period, when Ireland was a single administrative unit within the United Kingdom.

**Why the composed union, not CShapes:** CShapes 2.0 tracks Ireland (cowcode=205) only from 1922-12-06, covering the 26-county Republic. The previous polygon (CShapes feature 205, vintage 1921) was copied from the post-partition IRL-1921-2025 row and understated the pre-1921 territory by ~20% (~70,273 km² vs ~84,421 km²). Ulster/Northern Ireland was Ireland's dominant flax-growing region — this understatement is economically material.

**Approximation note:** GADM 4.1 uses modern administrative boundaries. The Ireland–UK border has been stable since 1921 (224 miles / 360 km, formed 1920). The composed union boundary accurately represents the pre-partition whole island. Polygon confidence: **high**.

**Flax step-change diagnostic:** Post-1921 flax values are higher in IRL-1800-1921 than IRL-1921-2025, reflecting Northern Ireland's dominance in Irish flax production. Labels such as "ireland" appearing in post-1921 source data should be audited: if they cover the whole island, they may create a double-count risk against GBR-1921-2025.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — Act of Union 1800 merges Ireland into the UK.
- **Successor:** [irl-1921-2025](irl-1921-2025.md) — Irish Free State (26 counties). The 6 Ulster counties are tracked under GBR-1921-2025.

## Key dates

- **1800:** Act of Union merges Kingdom of Ireland into the United Kingdom.
- **1921:** Anglo-Irish Treaty; Ireland partitioned; Irish Free State established from 26 of 32 counties.

## Open questions

### oq-post-1921-ireland-labels

**Post-1921 "ireland" labels in IIA/JUAN sources.** If any source data labelled "ireland" after 1921 covers the whole island (including Northern Ireland), routing it to IRL-1921-2025 (26 counties) would produce a systematic overstatement and double-count risk against the GBR-1921-2025 UK polygon. An empirical audit of IIA and JUAN data series is needed to identify any post-1921 rows still aggregating the whole island.

## Polygon blocked (added 2026-07-24)

`polygon_status` set to **`unassigned`**: this page claimed `assigned` while the
build attached **no geometry**, because `polygon_feature_id` held the recipe
`gadm-IRL+GBR-NIR` rather than a resolvable id — and that recipe cannot
currently be executed.

- The fetched GADM 4.1 admin-1 file covers only **61 countries** and includes
  neither `GBR` nor `IRL`, so Northern Ireland cannot be taken from it.
- CShapes 2.0 cannot supply all-Ireland either: gwcode 205 (Ireland) begins in
  **1921** and covers the 26 counties only, while gwcode 200 (United Kingdom,
  1886-1921) covers Great Britain *and* all Ireland together with no way to
  separate the island — `UK(1886-1921) − UK(1921-2019)` yields the 26 counties
  that left, not the whole island.

This matters more than most of the backlog: **1,049 layer-B rows** match here,
the largest of any polity missing geometry. Two ways out — fetch GADM for `IRL`
and `GBR` and add a union builder to
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`, or use a historical GIS source that
carries pre-partition Ireland directly.

## Polygon RESOLVED (2026-07-24)

The blocker recorded above is closed. GADM 4.1 was fetched for `IRL` and `GBR`
(added to `scripts/sources/gadm-4.1/fetch.sh`), and `build_irl_1800_1921` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` now composes all-Ireland as
**GADM adm0 `IRL` (26 counties, 70,266 km²) ∪ adm1 `GBR.2_1` Northern Ireland
(14,167 km²) = 84,433 km²**, matching the ~84,421 km² all-island figure this
page already documented.

Modern borders are a sound proxy in this one case: the island's coastline is the
territorial boundary throughout 1800-1921, and the only internal change in the
period is the 1921 partition — which is precisely what the union reverses. The
1,049 layer-B rows matched here now have geometry.
