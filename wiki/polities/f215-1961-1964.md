# Tanganyika and Zanzibar (combined reporting)

## Summary

A **reporting unit, not a state.** Tanganyika (independent December 1961) and Zanzibar (a British
protectorate, independent December 1963) were separate until their union in April 1964. FAOSTAT
nevertheless reports them together as area **215 United Republic of Tanzania** from 1961, before
the union existed. This row names that combined ground so the data can be attributed to it,
following `F237-1954-1975` (Vietnam) and `F249-1918-1990` (Yemen). The name follows the F-convention:
`F` plus the FAOSTAT area code.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`, which routed area 215
1961-1963 to `TZA-1961-1964`, Tanganyika alone (938,880 km² in ESRI:54034; Zanzibar's 2,587 km²
outside it, intersection 0). FAOSTAT's series include Zanzibar (production pin
20260325T111448Z-4fffe):

```
cloves, t        1961     1962     1963     1964     1965
area 215        9,500    9,000   13,000   10,000   11,000
```

Cloves are grown in this ground almost only on Zanzibar and Pemba, so a Tanganyika-only series
would be near zero; instead it is at the same level before and after the 1964 union, with no
step. `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 215 1961-1963 here through
`manual_territory_routes`; 1964-2024 stays on `TZA-1964-2025`.

## `iso3: NA`, deliberately

Following `F206-2011-2025`: this row is reached by FAOSTAT area 215 through a manual route, not by
an ISO3 join, and giving it `TZA` would let an ISO3-keyed lookup for Tanzania return a row that
ends in 1964 alongside `TZA-1961-1964`.

## Predecessors and successors

None declared, following the convention recorded on `F206-2011-2025`: nothing names an aggregate as
its successor, and this row's members keep their own chains (`TZA-1961-1964` -> `TZA-1964-2025`;
`ZNZ-1890-1963` -> `TZA-1964-2025`).

## Contradictions

It contains both members by construction. Use either this row or `TZA-1961-1964` plus
`ZNZ-1890-1963`; summing all three double-counts. `ZNZ-1890-1963` ends at 1963 (exclusive), so for
1963 itself only this row and `TZA-1961-1964` are live.
