# Congo (1900-1906)

## Summary

French Congo (the later Moyen-Congo colony, modern Republic of the Congo / Congo-Brazzaville) during a short 1900–1906 CShapes boundary window. In 1903 French Congo was reorganised into separate colonies: Moyen-Congo, Gabon, Ubangi-Shari and (later) Chad, with Brazzaville as the federal capital. The 1906 boundary change anticipates the further administrative rationalisation that would culminate in the 1910 AEF federation.
