### the Chaco War falls inside this row's data span, not only at its end

Recorded 2026-08-18 from the wiki notes queue.

This page frames the **Chaco War (1932-1935)** as the reason for the 1938
predecessor/successor split, which is correct for the formal cession — the 1938
treaty. But the war also falls **inside this row's own observed data span**
(`bolivia|juan|1910-1937`), and during it Paraguay occupied most of the disputed
Gran Chaco.

So for 1932-1935 the territory under Bolivian *control* was materially smaller
than this row's polygon, three years before the boundary changed on paper. Whether
agricultural statistics for the disputed region were reported, reported partially,
or not reported at all during the occupation is not established here, and the
page previously offered no way for a reader to notice the question existed.

Recorded as a caveat on the 1932-1937 rows rather than as a defect: the Gran Chaco
is sparsely cultivated, so the quantities at stake are likely small — but "likely
small" is an assumption, not a measurement, and this row's data span covers the
years in question.
