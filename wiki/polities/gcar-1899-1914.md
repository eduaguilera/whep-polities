# German Caroline Islands (Karolinen, 1899-1914)

## Summary

This row covers the **Caroline Islands** (Karolinen) under **German colonial
administration**, from the German purchase of the archipelago from Spain in
1899 until Japan's military occupation in 1914.

Germany acquired the Caroline Islands (along with the Mariana Islands, except
Guam) from Spain under the **German-Spanish Treaty of 12 February 1899**, for
25 million pesetas, following Spain's defeat in the Spanish-American War of
1898. The Carolines were administered as part of the broader **German
Micronesia** colonial domain, placed under the jurisdiction of German New
Guinea (Kaiser-Wilhelms-Land).

The archipelago includes two distinct island groups that became separate modern
states: the **Palau Islands** (modern PLW) and the **Caroline Islands proper**
(modern FSM — Yap, Chuuk/Truk, Pohnpei/Ponape, and Kosrae). Under German
rule, both groups were administered together as Karolinen, with the main
administrative base at Koror (Palau).

Japan occupied the islands militarily beginning in **October 1914**, following
a German-Japanese naval engagement. German administration ceased, and Japan
subsequently received a League of Nations Class C mandate over the islands
in 1920 (see CAR-1920-1945).

**Why this entry exists.** IIA phosphate production data (item code 3006,
phosphate rock) labeled 'palau' (iso3c=plw) for years **1909-1914** records
output from the **Angaur phosphate mines** on Angaur Island (Palau), one of
the most commercially significant deposits in the Pacific at the time.
Mining on Angaur began in 1909 under a German commercial concession (Deutsche
Sudsee-Phosphat-Actiengesellschaft, DSPA). This data belongs to the German
colonial period and cannot correctly be attributed to ROCE-1850-2021
(Oceania Other aggregate) or to the later Japanese mandate entity.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: proxy.** No dedicated historical GIS polygon for the
  German Caroline Islands was found in CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, or Paine-2024.
  CShapes 2.0 feature ID 744 (German New Guinea) covers the Papua New Guinea
  mainland and nearby islands with an area of ~228,080 km2 -- this is the
  continental German New Guinea territory (Kaiser-Wilhelms-Land + the
  Bismarck Archipelago), not the Caroline Islands which lie ~2,000 km to
  the northeast. CShapes excludes sub-250k-population dependencies per its
  published scope (Schvitz et al. 2022, p.148); the German Carolines had
  a population of roughly 40,000-50,000, well below that threshold.
- **Proxy polygon used.** GADM 4.1 adm0 `PLW` (Palau) is used as a
  modern-border proxy for the Palau archipelago component of the German
  Carolines. Palau's land area is approximately **459 km2** across ~340
  islands. The IIA data specifically records Angaur phosphate production
  (Palau), so the PLW polygon is the geographically correct sub-unit.
  The full German Carolines also included the FSM-geographic islands
  (~702 km2); a composed PLW+FSM polygon would represent the full colonial
  territory (~1,161 km2 combined), but only Palau data is present in WHEP
  for this period.
- **ESTIMATE flag.** The polygon is a modern administrative boundary used
  as a period proxy. The Palau archipelago's extent has not changed
  materially since 1899; the modern PLW polygon is a reasonable
  approximation. Polygon_vintage_drift is ~125 years (1899 vs. GADM
  contemporary).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [DEU-1871-1919](deu-1871-1919.md) -- The German Empire
  under which the Caroline Islands were purchased from Spain in 1899 and
  administered. The Carolines became a German protectorate on
  12 February 1899; prior to this date (1885-1899) they were under Spanish
  sovereignty (not tracked separately in WHEP).
- **Successor:** [JPN-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md) -- Japan's military
  occupation began in October 1914. The interim occupation period
  (1914-1920) routes to JPN-1895-1945; from 1920 the formal League of
  Nations mandate routes to CAR-1920-1945.

## Decisions

- **GCAR-1899-1914 scoped to German Caroline Islands (Palau + FSM
  geographic extent) as a single colonial unit.** Germany administered
  Palau and the Caroline Islands together as Karolinen; splitting them
  at this administrative level is historically unsupported.
- **Polygon: GADM PLW proxy.** The WHEP data for this period is
  specifically Palau phosphate; CShapes 744 (German New Guinea) does NOT
  cover the Caroline Islands; PLW polygon is the most faithful available
  sub-unit.
- **Start year 1899.** Treaty of 12 February 1899 (German purchase from
  Spain). The German flag was raised over the Carolines on 12 October 1899;
  either date is defensible but 1899 is used for both events.
- **End year 1914.** Japan's military occupation began October 1914.
  The 1914 phosphate row (60,000 tonnes) is attributed to GCAR because
  the annual figure includes the German administration months and the
  occupation was not a formal sovereignty transfer at year-end.
- **Predecessor: DEU-1871-1919.** The German Empire administered the
  Carolines; no separate German Micronesia colonial-intermediate polity
  is needed in WHEP given the data coverage.
- **Successor: JPN-1895-1945.** For the 1914-1920 occupation gap,
  data rows are aliased to JPN-1895-1945; from 1920 onward they route
  to CAR-1920-1945 (League of Nations mandate).
- **iso3: NA.** No ISO 3166 code for German colonial territories.
- **cow: NA.** COW does not assign a state-system code to the German
  Caroline Islands (sub-250k-population dependency; excluded from CShapes).
- **polity_type: colonial.** Administrative colony without independent
  sovereignty.

## Open questions

### oq-spanish-predecessor

Palau and the Caroline Islands were under Spanish sovereignty from 1885-1899
(Spain asserted sovereignty following a dispute with Germany in 1885; Germany
accepted Spain's claim via the Pope's arbitration). No WHEP row covers the
Spanish colonial period (1885-1899). If pre-1899 data exists labeled
'palau' or 'caroline islands', a Spanish Caroline Islands polity would be
needed.

### oq-polygon-upgrade

A composed union of GADM PLW + GADM FSM would more accurately represent
the full German Caroline Islands territory (~1,161 km2). If the composer
pipeline is run, polygon_status can be upgraded to 'composed' and
polygon_area_km2 updated accordingly.

### oq-1919-occupation-precision

The 1914-1919 data rows (1915-1918, labeled 'palau') are currently aliased
to JPN-1895-1945 (Japanese Empire). Japan's military government over the
Carolines was a de facto occupation before the League mandate. If a separate
GCAR-1914-1920 row is created for the occupation period, these aliases should
be updated.
