# Two Sicilies

## Summary

The southern Italian peninsula (Naples + Sicily) from the 1800 database floor to the 1860–1861 Garibaldi campaign and annexation to the Kingdom of Sardinia. Strictly, the unified "Kingdom of the Two Sicilies" did not exist until 8 December 1816 — before that the territory was two *separate* kingdoms (Naples and Sicily) under the Bourbon dynasty. The 1806–1815 Napoleonic interruption created the French-client Kingdoms of Naples (under Joseph Bonaparte then Murat) and a distinct Bourbon-ruled Kingdom of Sicily under British protection. The unified Two Sicilies kingdom from 1816 collapsed under Garibaldi's Expedition of the Thousand in 1860–1861; King Francis II surrendered at Gaeta in February 1861.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Cliopatria (Kingdom of the Two Sicilies 1815–1819) per CSV. Core territory: mainland southern Italy south of the Papal States, plus the island of Sicily.

## Contradictions

**Start year is historically incorrect.** The Kingdom of the Two Sicilies did not exist until 8 December 1816. Before 1816 the WHEP polygon covers what were two separate states (Naples + Sicily), interrupted by the 1806–1815 Napoleonic-era Kingdom of Naples and Bourbon-held Kingdom of Sicily. CSV notes say "FIXED: merged duplicate 'Kingdom Two sicilies'" but do not address the 1800-vs-1816 start-year issue. This is a candidate CSV fix: either bump start_year to 1816 (and add a separate pre-1816 row or rows), or rename the row to something like "Naples + Sicily (to 1860)" and keep 1800 as a deliberate territorial aggregation.
