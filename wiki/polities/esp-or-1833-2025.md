# Ourense (province of Spain)

## Summary

Ourense is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, and today one of the four provinces making up the Galicia autonomous community in the northwestern corner of the Iberian Peninsula. It takes its name from its capital, the inland city of Ourense (historically Orense), and is Galicia's only landlocked province, occupying the southeastern portion of the region: bordered by Lugo province to the north, Pontevedra to the west, the Castile-and-Leon provinces of Zamora and Leon to the east, and Portugal (Braganca and Viana do Castelo districts) to the south, across the Mino/Minho river. This entry is type: subnational because Ourense is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated Ourense separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES113, for over a century and a half. Unlike the A Coruna case, no pre-existing database row matched this unit, so this is a genuine create_new following the same house convention already applied to A Coruna, Lugo and Madrid.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision captures valued statistical rows for unit ES113 ("Ourense"), country Spain, under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (ES112 Lugo, and onward through ES243), with data spanning at least 1865 onward but a system_start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform) and an open end_year of 2025 for still-current units; the routing decision does not give an exact row count but describes this as a standard province-level series. Previously matched to / why wrong: the routing_reasoning states plainly that "Ourense has no matching existing polity by name; the only ESP candidates are A Coruna, Madrid, and national Spain, none of which IS this province" -- so absent this entry, Ourense's data would have had no province-specific row to route to and would either be dropped or misrouted to the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, which is roughly 505,000 km2 against Ourense's ~7,273 km2, about 69x larger and incapable of representing province-level series without collapsing them into the national total. What confirms distinctness: sibling NUTS3 units for Spanish provinces (ES112, ES114, ES120, ES130, ES211-241) are consistently routed create_new in the same convention pass, confirming that Spanish provinces need individual polity rows rather than being folded into Galicia or into national Spain; Ourense's continuous separate tabulation by INE and Eurostat as ES113 is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-LU-1833-2025.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_feature pointing at mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro (the Spanish province numeric code), under which Ourense is conventionally cpro 32 -- but that source is flagged present_locally=False (the shapefile/geopackage has not been fetched into this repository) and its licence has not been checked, so the feature_id cannot be confirmed and this entry carries no geometry yet, exactly as ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-LU-1833-2025 do for the same reason. gadm-4.1-adm1 was not used because it models Spain at the 17-autonomous-community level, not the 50-province level, and Ourense province does not coincide with the Galicia autonomous community boundary (Galicia contains three other provinces: A Coruna, Lugo, Pontevedra), so an adm1 feature would be the wrong granularity entirely. Territory description: Ourense province occupies inland southeastern Galicia, Spain's only Galician province without a coastline. Its capital and largest city is Ourense, known historically for its Roman-era hot springs (the Burgas) and Roman bridge over the Mino river. The province is mountainous and rural, containing the Macizo Central Ourensan and the Ribeira Sacra wine region along the Sil and Mino river canyons. On a modern map it is bounded by Lugo province to the north, Pontevedra to the west, Zamora and Leon provinces (Castile and Leon) to the east, and Portugal to the south across the Mino/Minho river. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 7,273 km2 -- this is the province's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet. Provincial boundaries in Spain have been essentially unchanged since the 1833 reform, so once the IGN provinces layer is acquired, vintage risk across the full 1833-2025 span should be low, though this has not been independently confirmed for Ourense's specific external boundaries (see open questions).

## Predecessors and successors

Ourense has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory it now covers was organized under the Kingdom of Galicia's historic partido system, which this database does not model at sub-kingdom granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to ESP-CO-1833-2025, ESP-LU-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain, and of the Galicia autonomous community since 1981, as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention already established for the other Spanish NUTS/province rows.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-or-abbreviation

**Used ESP-OR-1833-2025, the two-letter abbreviation matching the ESP house convention**

The routing decision for unit ES113 (Ourense) found no existing polity row matching this territory -- unlike the ES111/A Coruna case, this is a genuine create_new. Following the pattern already established by the three existing ESP subnational rows (ESP-CO-1833-2025 for A Coruna, ESP-LU-1833-2025 for Lugo, ESP-MD-1833-2025 for Madrid), this entry uses OR, the conventional two-letter abbreviation for Ourense, rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES113) or the INE province numeric code (32). This keeps all four existing ESP subnational rows in one uniform <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> family, which is the dominant pattern for 52 of 61 subnational rows in the table, and lets a future gate or reader recognize the province-set by inspection without cross-referencing a separate NUTS-to-abbreviation table. The routing decision's own polygon_reasoning already anticipated this exact code ("ESP-OR-1833-2025, cpro=32 for Ourense"), so this page targets that anticipated code rather than inventing a different one.

### d-start-year-1833-not-1865

**Used the country convention start year 1833, not the data's own first year 1865**

The routing decision explicitly notes: 'the country convention gives a clear default start (1833) not tied to the data's own start year (1865)'. Ourense's earliest valued data rows begin in 1865, but the province itself was created in 1833 by the Javier de Burgos reform along with every other Spanish province, so start_year is set to the administrative creation date rather than the first year for which this pipeline happens to have data -- exactly the same reasoning already applied to the sibling ESP-CO, ESP-LU and ESP-MD entries, none of which tie start_year to their earliest data row either. Using the data's own start year would have made Ourense's span inconsistent with its three siblings for no principled reason, since all four provinces were created in the same 1833 reform.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-source-not-acquired

**mapspain-ign is registered but not fetched, so Ourense's cpro=32 feature is unconfirmed**

scripts/sources.yaml lists mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro, the Spanish province numeric code, under which Ourense is conventionally cpro 32 -- the routing decision's own polygon_reasoning names this feature id directly ("ESP-OR-1833-2025, cpro=32 for Ourense") but flags present_locally=False: the shapefile/geopackage has not been fetched into this repository, and its licence for redistribution has not been checked. Until that fetch happens and the cpro=32 feature is confirmed to be Ourense specifically -- distinct from Galicia's other three provinces (A Coruna, Lugo, Pontevedra), which share the same source -- this entry must carry polygon_status: unassigned and no geometry, exactly as ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-LU-1833-2025 do for the identical reason. Whoever acquires mapspain-ign should verify there is exactly one province-level feature for Ourense before assigning it here.

### oq-vintage-risk-unconfirmed

**Provincial boundary stability across 1833-2025 is asserted, not verified against a documented boundary history**

This page and its ESP siblings assert that Spanish provincial boundaries have been 'essentially stable since 1833', which is the standard justification for treating a single modern IGN polygon as valid across the whole 1833-2025 span once acquired. For Ourense specifically this has not been checked against any documented municipal-boundary reorganization within the province (Spain has periodically merged or split municipios, which can shift a province's internal but not external boundary, but external boundary changes -- e.g. any adjustment against Zamora or Pontevedra provinces, or the Portuguese border -- have not been positively ruled out for this province in particular). Until the mapspain-ign source is fetched and cross-checked against a historical boundary source, the 'low vintage risk' assessment carried over from the sibling entries remains an assumption rather than a confirmed fact for Ourense.

### oq-galicia-nuts2-not-modeled

**Galicia as an autonomous community / NUTS2 unit has no polity row of its own**

Ourense is one of four provinces (with A Coruna, Lugo, Pontevedra) making up the Galicia autonomous community, a NUTS2-level administrative and statistical unit created by Spain's 1978 Constitution and the 1981 Galician Statute of Autonomy. No polity row exists in this database for Galicia itself at that intermediate level -- data reported at NUTS2 Galicia granularity, if any exists in future ingests, would currently have no natural home between the four provincial rows and the national ESP-1800-2025 row. Whether that gap needs filling is a question for the ESP country convention generally, since it recurs identically for all four Galician provinces and for Spain's other 16 autonomous communities.
