# Serbia and Montenegro excluding Kosovo (1999-2006)

## Summary

The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (from 2003 the State Union of Serbia and Montenegro) **without
Kosovo**, for 1999-2006, the years in which Kosovo was under UN administration (UNSCR 1244, June
1999) and outside the federation's statistics. A **reporting-area row**: the polity `SCG-1992-2006`
keeps CShapes 2.0's coding, and this row records only what FAOSTAT area **186 Serbia and
Montenegro** covers.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`, which routed area 186
1992-2005 to `SCG-1992-2006`, Kosovo included. FAOSTAT's series step down in 1999 and then run on
without a step into the Kosovo-excluding successor areas (production pin 20260325T111448Z-4fffe;
all items below code 1700):

```
total harvested area, ha     1997        1998        1999        2000
area 186                3,418,824   3,399,640   3,093,609   3,024,891      -9% in 1999

cattle stocks, head          1998        1999        2000        2001
area 186                1,878,000   1,812,000   1,427,000   1,341,000      -21% in 2000

continuity at the split      2005 (186)            2006 (272 Serbia + 273 Montenegro)
harvested area, ha           2,995,656             2,949,846 + 32,510 = 2,982,356
```

The 1999 step alone could be the war. The level after it cannot: 2005's figure continues into
Serbia plus Montenegro in 2006 within 0.4%, and area 272 Serbia shows no step when Kosovo leaves
`SRB-2006-2008` in 2008 (see `SRB-XK-2006-2008`), so the series after 1999 is Kosovo-free throughout.
Livestock stocks step one year later than crops, consistent with stocks counted at the start of the
year. `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 186 1999-2005 here through
`manual_territory_routes`; 1992-1998 stays on `SCG-1992-2006`.
