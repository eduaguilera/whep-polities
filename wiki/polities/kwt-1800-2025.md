# Kuwait

## Summary

Kuwait as tracked by WHEP from 1800. Britain signed a secret treaty with the sheikh of Kuwait in 1899, and Kuwait fell firmly within Britain's sphere of influence until independence in 1961. Iraq invaded Kuwait on 2 August 1990, leading to the Gulf War.
