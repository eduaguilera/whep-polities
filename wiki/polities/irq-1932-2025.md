# Iraq

## Summary

Iraq as an independent state from 1932, when it became the first League of Nations mandate to graduate to independence. The 1932 CShapes polygon is the exact-vintage match for this polity (polygon_vintage_drift=0). Its boundaries with Iran, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, and Jordan were shaped by post-WWI arrangements.

## Territorial extent
