# Cabo Verde (to 1886)

## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Cabo Verde (Cape Verde), an archipelago in the Atlantic Ocean off the west coast of Africa. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. The islands were a Portuguese colony throughout this period. "The Portuguese arrived in the uninhabited archipelago in the fifteenth century and the islands later became a station in the Atlantic slave-trade" (p.144) [biger-1995 &sect;cape-verde](../sources/biger-1995.md#cape-verde).

## Territorial extent

Cape Verde Islands (area 1,557 sq. miles; 4,033 sq. km) located 386 miles (621 km) west of the coast of Africa. No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected).
