# Lithuanian SSR

## Summary

Tracks Lithuania under Soviet occupation and as a constituent republic of the USSR from **1940** to **1991**. The Soviet Union occupied Lithuania in 1940 following the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact; after German occupation (1941-1944), Soviet control was re-established. Lithuania restored independence in 1991 [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).
