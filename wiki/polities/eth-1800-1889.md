# Ethiopia (to 1889)

## Summary

This row tracks Ethiopia for **90 years**, from the database floor to the
accession of Menelik II
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes:
"Pre-Menelik Ethiopia. Zemene Mesafint (Era of Princes). Highland core
only: Gondar, Gojjam, Shewa, Tigray, Wello. ~240,000 km2"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The Zemene Mesafint (1769-1855) was an era of feudal fragmentation with
regional lords wielding real power and the emperor reduced to a figurehead.
Emperor Tewodros II reunified Ethiopia (1855), followed by Yohannes IV
(1872-1889). The highland core remained the only territory throughout.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor). Successor:
[eth-1889-1897](eth-1889-1897.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** Cliopatria (Ethiopian Empire 1769-1889, ~240,000
  km2) per the CSV notes
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Highland core only.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA (pre-database floor). The Solomonic dynasty had
  ruled since 1270, though the Zemene Mesafint reduced imperial power
  to a formality.
- **Successor:** [eth-1889-1897](eth-1889-1897.md) -- Menelik II
  accession (1889), beginning of expansion.

## Sourced claims

- **1769-1855: Zemene Mesafint (Era of Princes).** Feudal fragmentation;
  regional lords dominated the highland polities.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1855: Tewodros II reunifies Ethiopia.** Ended the Era of Princes.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1868: British expedition to Magdala.** Tewodros defeated and killed.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1872-1889: Yohannes IV.** Ruled from Tigray; defended against Egyptian
  incursions (1870s) and Mahdist Sudan.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1889: Yohannes IV dies at Battle of Metemma.** Menelik II of Shewa
  became emperor. End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
