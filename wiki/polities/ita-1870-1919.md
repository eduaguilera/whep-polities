# Italy (1870–1919)

## Summary

This sub-period covers the Kingdom of Italy from the capture of **Rome**
(20 September 1870) to the **Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye**
(10 September 1919), when Trentino, South Tyrol, Trieste, and Istria were
ceded from Austria to Italy. It was split from the former single row
`ITA-1861-1919` to correctly separate the polygon-vintage-faithful period
from the two earlier overstatement periods.

Predecessor: [ita-1866-1870](ita-1866-1870.md).
Successor: [ita-1919-2025](ita-1919-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-Europe, `cowcode=325`, polygon vintage 1886.
  For this sub-period the 1886 vintage is **faithful**: the polygon
  represents post-1870 Italy (Venetia + Rome included; Trentino/Trieste/
  Istria excluded). No territorial changes of the Italian mainland occurred
  between 1870 and 1886 or between 1886 and 1919 (colonial acquisitions
  in Africa are tracked separately; they do not alter the European polygon).
- **1870 extent.** Post-Rome Italy: ~286,000 km² (approximate; includes
  Venetia and Rome/Lazio). This matches the 1886 CShapes polygon well.
- **1919 end.** CShapes records the polygon change at 1919-09-10: area
  increases from ~31.25 to ~33.05 sq degrees (Trentino/South Tyrol/
  Trieste/Istria added). This event is captured in the successor row
  [ITA-1919-2025](ita-1919-2025.md).
- **priority_review: False.** The 1886 polygon is an appropriate
  representation of Italy's European territory throughout 1870–1919.

## Sourced claims

- **1870-09-20: Capture of Rome.** Italy took the Papal States and capital
  relocated from Florence to Rome
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome). Begins this sub-period.
- **1882-05-20: Triple Alliance.** Italy, Germany, and Austria-Hungary formed
  the Triple Alliance
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1882-triple-alliance](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1882-triple-alliance). Not a territorial event.
- **1911–1912: Libya conquest.** Italy seized Libya from the Ottoman Empire.
  Tracked separately; does not alter European polygon.
- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Italy annexed Trentino, South Tyrol,
  Trieste, and Istria from Austria. CShapes polygon change at this date
  (+1.80 sq degrees)
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1919-saint-germain](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1919-saint-germain). Ends this sub-period.
- **COW state-system.** COW lists Italy (`ITA`, `ccode=325`) with a single
  continuous tenure from 1816-01-01 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Decisions

- **Split from ITA-1861-1919 (2026-06-29).** This period is the
  polygon-faithful sub-period of the former ITA-1861-1919 row. The 1886
  CShapes polygon accurately represents Italian territory from 1870 onward;
  no polygon overstatement applies.
- **Polygon status: assigned (assumed_constant).** The 1886 polygon is
  appropriate for 1870–1919. No mid-period European territorial changes
  affect the polygon vintage quality.
- **priority_review: False.** No polygon issues remain for this sub-period.

## Open questions

### oq-colonial-libya-1911

Italy's 1911–1912 conquest of Libya is tracked on the Ottoman side
([ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md)) but the colonial extent is not yet
documented on this page. Future ingest should cross-reference events and
confirm the European polygon is unaffected.

### oq-cow-325-from-1816

COW starts Italy at `ccode=325` from 1816-01-01 — 45 years before Italian
unification. COW treats Sardinia/Piedmont as the Italian continuator state.
The WHEP row chain (SAR-1800-1860 → ITA-1861-1866 → ... → ITA-1870-1919)
uses 1861 as the unification break, with SAR-1800-1860 as the predecessor.
The COW coding convention differs; documented for cross-reference.
