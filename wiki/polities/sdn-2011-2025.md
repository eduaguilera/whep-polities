# Sudan

## Summary

Sudan is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Sudan (after the secession of South Sudan) from 2011 to the present. South Sudan gained independence on 9 July 2011 following a referendum.

## Territorial extent

Sudan is the northern portion of the former unified Sudan after South Sudan's secession in 2011. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

## Sourced claims

- The Central African Republic-Sudan boundary runs for 724 miles (1,165 km) between the boundary tripoint with Chad and the boundary tripoint with Zaire. It generally follows the watershed between the Congo River and the Nile drainage area (p.147). [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)
- "Sudan was under British rule from 1896 and from 1899 was administered as an Anglo-Egyptian condominium. The Central African Republic (then Oubangui-Chari) was a French protectorate. The boundary between the two was established in the Anglo-French Convention of 14 June 1898" (p.147). Sudan became an independent state on 1 January 1956. [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)

## Open questions

### oq-sudan-south-sudan-boundary

Sudan-South Sudan boundary. The boundary between Sudan and South Sudan was not precisely defined at the time of Biger's 1995 publication since South Sudan did not exist as a separate entity. The 2011 boundary largely follows internal administrative lines.
