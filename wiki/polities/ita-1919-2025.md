# Italy

## Summary

This row tracks Italy as a territorial-economic unit from **1919**
(Treaty of Saint-Germain) to **2025** (database ceiling)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 107-year window
spans the fascist period (1922-1943), WWII (Allied invasion, armistice
1943, liberation 1945), the transition from monarchy to republic (1946),
and the post-war democratic era. Post-WWII territorial adjustments
included losses of Istria/Dalmatia to Yugoslavia and 268 sq miles to
France (Treaty of Paris 1947, Biger).

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
this row begins at a real territorial event — the Treaty of
Saint-Germain-en-Laye (10 September 1919) gained Trentino, South Tyrol,
Trieste, and Istria from Austria.

- **1919 start** is a real territorial event: Treaty of Saint-Germain.
  CShapes records the polygon change at 1919-09-10, area increasing
  from ~31.25 to ~33.05 sq degrees
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Italy is still active.

Predecessor: [ita-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md) — Treaty of
Saint-Germain gained Trentino, South Tyrol, Trieste, Istria from
Austria.
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records
  Italy (`cowcode=325`) from 1919-09-10 at ~33.05 sq degrees
  (post-Saint-Germain, with Trentino/Trieste/Istria)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Post-WWII adjustments.** Italy lost Istria and parts of Dalmatia
  to Yugoslavia, and 268 sq miles to France, per the Treaty of Paris
  (1947). Biger documents both transfers:
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy),
  [biger-1995 §italy-slovenia](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy-slovenia).
  The Trieste dispute with Yugoslavia was not fully resolved until
  the Treaty of Osimo (1975).

## Sourced claims

- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Italy gained Trentino,
  South Tyrol, Trieste, and Istria from Austria. CShapes polygon
  change at this date (+1.80 sq degrees)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). Start-of-row

  [ita-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md).
- **1922-1943: Fascist period.** Mussolini's March on Rome (1922)
  to the Grand Council vote (25 July 1943). Not a territorial event
  under the WHEP rule.
- **1943: Armistice.** Italy signed an armistice with the Allies on
  8 September 1943. German occupation of northern Italy followed.
  Not a WHEP split — occupation does not change territory.
- **1946: Republic.** Institutional referendum of 2 June 1946 replaced
  the monarchy with a republic. Not a territorial event.
- **1947-02-10: Treaty of Paris.** Formalized WWII territorial losses:
  Istria and parts of Dalmatia to Yugoslavia; 268 sq miles to France
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
  These are real territorial changes within this row; see
  [oq-post-wwii-losses](#oq-post-wwii-losses).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists Italy (`ITA`,
  `ccode=325`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31 — no break at any date
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Open questions

### oq-post-wwii-losses

Italy lost Istria and parts of Dalmatia to Yugoslavia, and 268 sq
miles to France, per the Treaty of Paris (1947)
[biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy),
[biger-1995 §italy-slovenia](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy-slovenia).
These are real territorial changes within this row. Under the WHEP
rule, they are candidate split points — particularly the Istria loss,
which is substantial. The CSV does not split at 1947. Under a strict
reading of the WHEP rule, this could warrant a split (e.g.
ITA-1919-1947 and ITA-1947-2025). Tier X — requires human
split-policy decision.
