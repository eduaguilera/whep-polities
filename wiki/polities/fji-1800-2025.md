# Fiji

## Summary

Single WHEP row for Fiji spanning the whole 1800-2025 window, added to cover FAOSTAT/FABIO reporting code FJI. **520 layer-B rows** route here (`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`), the bulk of them colonial-era sugar and copra production. The row is undivided because the archipelago's extent did not change across the span: the only sovereignty events — British cession in 1874 and independence in 1970 — transferred the same islands.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `GID_0 = FJI`, carried as a **proxy** (`polygon_status: proxy`). Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **18,649 km²**. Biger gives Fiji as "some 332 islands (110 inhabited) in the South Pacific Ocean (area: 7,078 sq. miles [18,325 sq. km.])" ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)) — the measured polygon is **1.8% above** Biger's figure, a coastline/generalisation difference across 332 islands rather than a territorial discrepancy. Two independent numbers agreeing to under 2% is the strongest area corroboration available for this row.

**Why a modern polygon is not an approximation here.** Biger's decisive sentence for WHEP purposes is that Fiji "has no land boundaries" ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)). An archipelago with no land frontier has no boundary that a treaty could move; the only way its extent can change is by islands being added or removed wholesale. Neither happened in this span. Biger's entire boundary history for Fiji is three lines — first settlement ~3,500 years ago, independence from Britain in 1970, and post-independence ethnic tension between native Fijians (48%) and Indians (46%) — and names **no** boundary event at all ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)). For a boundary encyclopaedia to have nothing to say about a polity's boundaries is itself evidence that they did not move.

**Territorial changes inside the span: none found; the row should not be split.** The two events that a naive reading might treat as row boundaries are both sovereignty transfers over an unchanged territory, which under the WHEP polity definition do not open a new row when the reporting territory is constant ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)):

- **1874-10-10** — Deed of Cession; Fiji becomes a British Crown Colony. (Uncited general history: no source on disk states this date. It is given because it is the event a reader will look for, and it is marked plainly as uncited rather than dressed in a citation.)
- **1970-10-10** — independence from Britain, exactly 96 years later. Biger dates it to 1970 ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)); COW dates it to the day, `1970-10-10`, and `decolonization_events.csv` agrees.

The one caveat worth recording is not about extent but about **reporting scope**: Rotuma was administered separately for part of the colonial period, and the Fiji colony's statistical returns may or may not have included it. At roughly 47 km² — 0.25% of the archipelago — this cannot materially move any production series, and it is far below the 100 × 100 km threshold at which CShapes would record a change at all ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)).

**Why CShapes is not used.** CShapes 2.0 covers 1886-2019 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)), so 86 of this row's 225 years are outside it, and colonial Fiji sits near CShapes' dependency inclusion floor: dependencies are limited "to those units with a population greater than 250,000 during the sample period" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)), a threshold colonial Fiji only crossed in the twentieth century. GADM is used instead, and because the extent is constant it is a like-for-like stand-in rather than a temporal approximation.

## Predecessors and successors

- **No predecessor and no successor.** Both ends of the row are the WHEP window's own limits (1800 floor, 2025 ceiling), not missing chain links. The 1874 cession and 1970 independence deliberately do not split the row — see *Territorial extent*.

## Key dates

- 1874-10-10: Deed of Cession; British Crown Colony established. **Uncited** — no source on disk gives this date.
- 1970-10-10: independence from Britain ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji) for the year; `decolonization_events.csv` and COW `statelist2024.csv` for the day).
- 1987, 2000, 2006: coups. Governance events with no territorial content; listed so that a reader does not mistake their absence for an oversight.

## Sourced claims

- Fiji is "an independent archipelago of some 332 islands (110 inhabited) in the South Pacific Ocean (area: 7,078 sq. miles [18,325 sq. km.]; estimated population: 800,000 [1994])" and "has no land boundaries" ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)).
- Fiji "became independent from Britain in 1970" ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)).
- Biger records "significant ethnic tensions between native Fijians (48%) and Indians (46%)" ([biger-1995 §fiji](../sources/biger-1995.md#fiji)) — relevant to this row only because the Indo-Fijian population is a consequence of the indenture system that built the colonial sugar industry, which is what most of this row's 520 data rows measure.
- **COW `ccode=950` has a single tenure, `1970-10-10 → 2024-12-31`** — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). So COW covers only the last 55 of this row's 225 years, and the colonial 1874-1970 period is absent by design: dependencies that never meet COW's recognition criteria never appear ([cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion)). The frontmatter `cow: 950` is a code *assignment* for the modern state, not a claim of COW membership across the row.
- `decolonization_events.csv` records Fiji independence from Britain on 1970-10-10 — agreeing with COW to the day, which is a genuine two-source confirmation rather than a restatement, since the two files were compiled independently.
- CShapes limits dependency coding to units above 250,000 population ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)) and codes only territorial transfers larger than 100 × 100 km ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)) — both thresholds bear on why no CShapes polygon is bound here and why no CShapes evidence could settle the Rotuma question above.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): GADM `FJI` in ESRI:54034 = **18,649 km²**, versus Biger's 18,325 km² — agreement to 1.8%.
- Data footprint (this ingest): **520 layer-B rows** route to this code per `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`.

## Contradictions

- **Area, 18,649 vs 18,325 km².** GADM's measured polygon exceeds Biger's stated area by 324 km² (1.8%). The database follows the measured polygon because it is the geometry that actually receives data, and treats Biger's figure as corroboration of the right order of magnitude rather than a competing measurement. For a 332-island archipelago, disagreement at this level is expected from island-inclusion and coastline-resolution choices alone.

## Open questions

### oq-fji-rotuma-reporting-scope

Did colonial Fiji's agricultural returns include Rotuma, administered separately for part of the period? The territorial answer does not matter (47 km², 0.25% of the archipelago) but the *label* answer might, if a yearbook reported "Fiji" and "Rotuma" as two lines. Checkable with `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/08_source_stated_areas.py`: if any source states an area for its Fiji label, whether it is nearer 18,300 or 18,270 km² would settle it.
