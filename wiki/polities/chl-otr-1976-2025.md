# Chile: other regions (Arica y Parinacota, Tarapacá, Antofagasta, Atacama, Aysén, Magallanes)

## Summary

The ground that the LatAm subnational compilation files under **`Otras Regiones`** ("other
regions", unit `CHL-RESID`) for Chile: the four northern regions (Arica y Parinacota, Tarapacá,
Antofagasta, Atacama) and the two far-southern ones (Aysén and Magallanes), whose cereals,
potatoes and pulses the compilation reports as one residual rather than region by region.
[juan-subnational §composition-by-upstream](../sources/juan-subnational.md#composition-by-upstream)

It is typed `subnational` and sits inside [chl-1902-2025](chl-1902-2025.md), like the region rows
it is the union of: [chl-ap-2007-2025](chl-ap-2007-2025.md), [chl-ta-1976-2025](chl-ta-1976-2025.md),
[chl-an-1976-2025](chl-an-1976-2025.md), [chl-at-1976-2025](chl-at-1976-2025.md),
[chl-ai-1976-2025](chl-ai-1976-2025.md) and [chl-ma-1976-2025](chl-ma-1976-2025.md). The span
1976-2025 is the Chilean regions' own, the same as the regions' rows: the regions were "created in
1974" and replaced the 25 provinces, and the country convention dates them from 1976, when they
became operative.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §chile-regions](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#chile-regions)

## Why this entry exists

The routing ledger (`pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv`) had banked `CHL-RESID`
as `not_a_territory` on 2026-09-04: "a residual catch-all bucket, not a named territory". The
panel refutes that. A residual is a territory whenever the units named beside it are always the
same, because it is then always the same complement. Measured on the panel
(`whep_production_subnational.parquet`, 2026-09-25):

- `Otras Regiones` has **3,132 rows** (1,048 area, 1,048 production, 1,036 yield), 13 items
  (wheat, barley, maize, rye, oats, potatoes, sweet potatoes, dry beans, dry peas, chick peas,
  lentils; lupins and triticale later), 1935-2023.
- In **every one** of its 3,132 (year, item, indicator) cells the other Chilean units present are
  the same eight: Coquimbo, Valparaíso, Metropolitana, O'Higgins, Maule, Biobío, La Araucanía and
  Los Lagos. No other combination occurs.

So `Otras Regiones` is Chile minus those eight, which is the ground of this row. The ledger entry is
re-recorded as `create_new` with this code, and its coverage routes the data here (observed from
1976, `back_cast` for 1935-1975, the same disposition the eight named regions carry before 1976).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the union of the six regions' GADM 4.1 adm1
features, the same features their own rows carry. It measures **498,279 km²** (ESRI:54034) at source
and 494,771 as shipped. [gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

### Why Ñuble and Los Ríos are not in it

Chile has sixteen regions, and the eight named ones plus these six are fourteen. The other two,
Ñuble (split from Biobío, operational in September 2018) and Los Ríos (split from Los Lagos,
created in December 2006), are not in the residual: the panel keeps their crops inside Biobío and Los Lagos for the whole span. Measured on
wheat area, the largest item:

| | 2005 | 2006 | 2017 | 2018 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Biobío | 120,160 | 96,490 | 66,695 | 67,132 | 65,897 |
| Los Lagos | 31,591 | 35,205 | 22,521 | 23,694 | 19,154 |
| `Otras Regiones` | 1,134 | 63 | 44 | 49 | 50 |

If either split region had been moved into the residual when it was created, the residual would
step up in that year and the parent would step down. Neither happens: the residual is 44 ha in
2017 and 49 in 2018, when Biobío goes from 66,695 to 67,132; and it falls, not rises, in 2006-2007.
Its wheat after 2006 is 39-63 ha a year in every year. The panel's Ñuble and Los Ríos units carry
only livestock and land use. So the six regions above are the complement in every year,
and the 2007 and 2018 splits do not touch this row. (The 2007 Arica y Parinacota split is inside
it: both halves are here.)

## Polygon status

`assigned` — GADM 4.1 adm1 union of the six regions, 494,771 km² as shipped.

## Predecessors and successors

None. The regions were created together in 1976; before that the ground was the northern and
southern provinces of the pre-1974 system, which have no rows. The 1935-1975 panel rows reach this
row as `back_cast`, as the named regions' pre-1976 rows reach theirs.

## Data routing

- **Panel (`juan-subnational`)**: `Otras Regiones` 1976-2023 observed, 1935-1975 `back_cast` —
  **3,132 rows** that routed to no polity before.
- **Layer B**: none.

The six regions' own rows receive only the panel's livestock and land-use series; this row
receives only crops. The item sets are disjoint, so no (item, indicator, year) cell sits on both
this row and one of its parts (registered as `separate_series` in `polity_composition.csv`).

## Decisions

- **The ledger's `not_a_territory` is corrected**, for the reason under *Why this entry exists*:
  the named set is constant, so the residual is a fixed territory.
- **Six regions, not eight.** An earlier proposal (`unrouted_proposals.csv`, bucket b) named eight,
  including Ñuble and Los Ríos. The wheat series refute that; see above.

## Open questions

### oq-biobio-los-lagos-crops-include-the-split-regions

The same measurement says the panel's Biobío crop rows include Ñuble in 2018-2023 and its Los
Lagos crop rows include Los Ríos in 2007-2023, while those rows route to
[chl-bi-1976-2025](chl-bi-1976-2025.md) and [chl-ll-1976-2025](chl-ll-1976-2025.md), whose GADM
polygons are the post-split regions. That is a territory mismatch on those rows: Biobío plus Ñuble is
1.55x modern Biobío and Los Lagos plus Los Ríos 1.38x modern Los Lagos (GADM 4.1, ESRI:54034). It is
not changed here.
