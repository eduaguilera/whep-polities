# Comoros

## Summary

Comoros is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the independent Comoros Islands from 1975 to the present. The Comorians voted for independence from France in a referendum conducted in 1974, but only the people of the southernmost island of Mayotte voted to remain part of France.

## Territorial extent

Comoros is a republic of three islands in the Indian Ocean between the southeastern African mainland and Madagascar. Total area 863 sq. miles (2,235 sq. km). Capital: Moroni. No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
