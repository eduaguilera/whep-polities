# Egypt and Anglo-Egyptian Sudan

## Summary

A **composed-union** row holding agricultural data reported under **Egypt
with the Anglo-Egyptian Sudan folded in**. FAO and IIA yearbook **footnotes**
flag several Egyptian series as including Sudan (e.g. *"includes cattle
received from Anglo-Egyptian Sudan"*), so a figure labelled "Egypt" in those
series actually covers the combined Egypt + Sudan territory and must not be
mapped to Egypt alone. The two constituents are carried separately as
[EGY-1925-1967](egy-1925-1967.md) and [SUD-1934-1956](sud-1934-1956.md); this
row exists only for data that combines them.

Period 1934–1956 spans the overlap of the two constituent rows, ending at
Sudanese independence (1956-01-01), after which the two are always reported
apart.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `constructed` — union of CShapes 2.0 Egypt (gwcode 651)
  and Sudan (gwcode 625), built by `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`.
- **Method.** `composed_union` — no single external feature represents the
  combined entity; the polygon is the union of the two constituents.
- **Area.** ~3,485,332 km² (EPSG:6933), consistent with Egypt (~1.0M) plus
  Anglo-Egyptian Sudan (~2.5M).
- **Confidence.** High for the union geometry; the row's *use* depends on
  which data points the footnotes attach to (see Open questions).

## Predecessors and successors

Constituent rows EGY-1925-1967 and SUD-1934-1956 carry Egypt and Sudan
separately; this aggregate has no distinct predecessor/successor chain.

## Sourced claims

- FAO/IIA footnotes report Egyptian agricultural series as including the
  Anglo-Egyptian Sudan (footnote-territory extraction,
  `footnote_polity_proposals` action=composed_union, EGY ⊃ SUD).
- Sudan independence 1956-01-01 ends the combined-reporting period.

## Decisions

- **Composed-union, not a re-coding of Egypt.** Mapping combined figures to
  EGY-1925-1967 would understate the extent (Sudan omitted); a dedicated union
  row keeps the territory honest.
