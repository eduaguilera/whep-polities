# French Togoland (1920-1960)

## Summary

French Togoland was a League of Nations mandate (1920-1946) and then a United Nations trust territory (1946-1960) administered by France. It comprised the eastern portion of the former German colony of Togoland, partitioned after WWI. Independent as the Republic of Togo on 27 April 1960.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. French Togoland covered the eastern portion of former German Togoland (approximately modern Togo). CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE) may have this as a French mandate territory.

**Why this entry exists:** Created to fill the 1920-1960 gap in TGO coverage between German Togoland entries and [TGO-1960-2025](tgo-1960-2025.md). The pre-1961 input data reports "Togo" for 1933-1959. Without this entry, 76 rows were unmatched.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [GTO-1919-1922](gto-1919-1922.md) — German Togoland, partitioned after WWI
- **Successor:** [TGO-1960-2025](tgo-1960-2025.md) — independent Republic of Togo

## Key dates

- 1914: Anglo-French forces occupy German Togoland (26 August)
- 1920: League of Nations mandate assigned to France (eastern portion)
- 1946: Becomes UN trust territory
- 1956: Autonomous Republic of Togo within French Union
- 1960-04-27: Full independence as Republic of Togo
