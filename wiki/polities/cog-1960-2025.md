# Congo

## Summary

Congo (Brazzaville) is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the Republic of the Congo from 1960 to the present. Congo gained independence from France on 15 August 1960.

## Territorial extent

Congo is a central African country (area 131,977 sq. miles; 341,820 sq. km) with estimated population 2,700,000 (1994). Capital: Brazzaville. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

## Sourced claims

- "The Congolese gained their independence from the French in 1960" (p.175). [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire)
- "Congo became independent on 15 August 1960" (p.176). The boundary between Congo and Zaire runs for 1,498 miles (2,410 km). [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire)
- "In the Berlin Conference of 1884-1885 the Association, which changed its name to Congo Free State, was recognized as a governing power on the Congo River" (p.176). This relates to the Belgian side (Zaire), while French Congo was established through separate treaties. [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire)
- Independence from France in 1960 (p.175). Politics characterized by ethnic divisions between northern and southern peoples (p.175). [biger-1995 §congo](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo)

## Open questions

### oq-congo-brazzaville-naming

Congo naming convention. This row covers the Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), not the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa), which is covered under Zaire in Biger and has separate WHEP rows.
