# Yugoslavia (1920-1991)

## Summary

This row tracks Yugoslavia for **72 years**, from the Treaty of Trianon
era through the start of dissolution
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (renamed Kingdom of Yugoslavia in
1929). WWII Axis occupation and partition (1941-1945). Tito's communist
Yugoslavia 1945-1980. Post-Tito crisis. Yugoslav Wars began 1991.
Dissolution started June 1991 (Slovenia and Croatia declared
independence). This is a massive row covering very different political
configurations.

COW: YUG, 345, gap 1941-04-20 to 1944-10-20 (WWII occupation).

## Sourced claims

- **1929: Renamed Yugoslavia.** Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes
  renamed to Kingdom of Yugoslavia.
- **1941-04-06: Axis invasion.** Germany, Italy, Hungary, and Bulgaria
  invaded Yugoslavia.
- **1941-04-20: COW gap begins.** Yugoslavia ceased to exist as a
  sovereign state per COW.
- **1944-10-20: COW gap ends.** Liberation of Belgrade. Tito's partisan
  government recognized.
- **1945-1980: Tito's Yugoslavia.** Communist federation under Josip Broz
  Tito. Non-Aligned Movement. Broke with Stalin (1948).
- **1980-05-04: Tito's death.** Beginning of post-Tito political crisis.
- **1991-06-25: Slovenia and Croatia declare independence.** Start of
  Yugoslav dissolution. End-of-row event.

### oq-wwii-occupation-gap

COW shows a gap from 1941-04-20 to 1944-10-20. During this period
Yugoslavia was partitioned among Axis powers (Germany, Italy, Hungary,
Bulgaria) and puppet states (Independent State of Croatia, Serbia under
Nedic). The WHEP row spans this gap without splitting.
