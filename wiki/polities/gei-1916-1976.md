# Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony

## Summary

The British colony that comprised "the present-day independent countries of Kiribati and Tuvalu".
The Gilbert and Ellice protectorates of 1892 became a Crown colony "on 12 January 1916", with
"portions of the outlying Line Islands and Phoenix Islands" added later, and "the GEIC was split into
separate colonies with effect from 1976".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §gilbert-and-ellice](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#gilbert-and-ellice)

Its two successor territories already have rows, [kir-1800-2025](kir-1800-2025.md) and
[tuv-1800-2025](tuv-1800-2025.md), so this is typed `aggregate`: it exists for the data a source
reports on the colony as one unit.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports the colony as one unit under three spellings: `Gilbert and Ellice Islands` (copra
1949-1951, pigs 1950, population 1937 and 1951), `Gilbert and Ellice Is` (the 1949 land-use table,
`use total` 97 thousand ha, i.e. **970 km²**) and `Gilbert & Ellice Is- lands` (a hyphenation
artefact of the same label, population 1951). **10 rows** resolved to no polity. Routing them to
Kiribati alone would drop Tuvalu from a unit that includes it; no Kiribati-only or Tuvalu-only FAO
row exists for these years.

`Ocean Island` (Banaba) phosphate, which FAO prints separately, is **not** routed here: it is one
island of the colony (the Pacific Phosphate Company's mine, the colony's main revenue), and a
phosphate series is better kept on a row for that island than spread over 1,054 km². It stays unrouted and is listed as a follow-up.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: GADM 4.1 adm0 KIR (1,012 km²) ∪ TUV (42 km²) =
**1,054 km²** measured (ESRI:54034), against FAO's 970 (0.92).
[gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

`proxy` because the colony's extent moved inside the span and the polygon is its later, largest
extent: Fanning and Washington (Line Islands) from 1916, Christmas Island from 1919, the Phoenix
Islands from 1937, and Tokelau, which was in the colony in 1916-1926 and is not in the polygon.
All of these are coral islands of a few to a few hundred km²; the error is at most the Line and
Phoenix groups before they joined (a few hundred km² of the 1,054), in the direction of overstating
the early colony.

## Polygon status

`proxy` — GADM 4.1 KIR ∪ TUV, the colony's post-1937 extent.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor row: the 1892 protectorates have none, and in 1976 the colony's
territory is carried by the two existing rows [kir-1800-2025](kir-1800-2025.md) and
[tuv-1800-2025](tuv-1800-2025.md), which span the whole period.

## Data routing

`Gilbert and Ellice Islands` (6), `Gilbert and Ellice Is` (3), `Gilbert & Ellice Is- lands` (1),
all fao1952 1937-1951, by alias: **10 rows**, previously unrouted.

## Decisions

- **Span 1916-1976**, the Crown colony: FAO's rows are all 1937-1951.
- **Aggregate beside its parts, not registered in `polity_composition.csv`.** No source routes a
  row to Kiribati or Tuvalu alone in these years, so there is no double count to record, and
  Tuvalu's atolls ship at 90% of their area inside this union after the build's simplification,
  which the registry's containment check would read as a mis-binding.
