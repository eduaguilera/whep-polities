# Chocó (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Chocó is a department of Colombia on the northern Pacific coast, occupying the country's western lowlands between the Pacific Ocean and the Cordillera Occidental, bordering Panama to the north. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chocó is a first-order administrative division of the sovereign Colombian state, never an independent polity in its own right — sovereignty over this territory has rested with Colombia (and before that, Cauca/Gran Colombia) throughout the period covered. The span 1947-2025 reflects Chocó's formal elevation from intendencia to full department status in 1947 under Colombia's post-1886 unitary departmental system (Ley/decreto establishing the Departamento del Chocó), which is the administration's own founding date as a department rather than an artifact of when data collection happens to begin. A statistical source reports data for the Chocó territory back to 1915, predating departmental status; those earlier years are handled as back-cast coverage of this same entry rather than pushing start_year earlier, because the pre-1947 figures are reconstructions (interpolated_scaled/scaled methods) projecting national or regional totals onto Chocó's territory rather than direct measurements of a distinct Chocó administrative entity.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures data for Colombia's Chocó department: a source reports figures under an admin_name of "Chocó" for the period 1915-2023, per the routing decision's proposed span and back-cast years. Before this entry, that data had no dedicated department-level polity to route to and would otherwise have fallen back to the national COL row (COL-1922-2025), which is roughly 42x Chocó's actual departmental area (46,530 km² vs. Colombia's ~1,141,748 km²) — far too coarse to represent department-level statistics as if they were national totals. No prior mismatch is being corrected here since no existing polity previously absorbed this data incorrectly; this is a first-time creation for a department with no existing subnational COL precedent in the table (0 of 66 subnational rows are currently COL). The entity's distinctness as an administrative unit is confirmed by Colombia's own departmental system: Chocó has held first-order department status, with its own governor, assembly, and administrative apparatus, since its 1947 elevation from intendencia — a status distinct from, and senior to, the intendencia/municipio tiers below it.

**Territory description:** Chocó department covers Colombia's Pacific coastal lowlands and the Serranía del Baudó, bounded by Panama and the Caribbean-facing Urabá region to the north, Antioquia and Risaralda to the east across the Cordillera Occidental, and Valle del Cauca to the south. Its capital is Quibdó on the Atrato River. Chocó's present-day area is approximately 46,530 km², making it roughly Colombia's 6th-largest department by area but one of its least densely populated, characterized by tropical rainforest and some of the highest rainfall totals in the Americas. Since GADM vintage risk for Colombian departmental boundaries is assessed as low in the routing decision (boundaries have been largely stable since 1947), a modern GADM adm1 feature for Chocó, once fetched, should closely approximate the historical departmental territory across the full 1947-2025 span without material distortion.

After 2025 (the database's present horizon) Chocó continues as a department of Colombia with no successor change; there is no territorial discontinuity at either end of the span, only an administrative-status change at 1947.

## Open questions

### oq-1947-founding-date-unverified

**1947 department elevation date is asserted from general knowledge, not a primary source**

The routing_concerns explicitly flag that the exact year Chocó became a department (1947) is asserted from general knowledge of Colombian administrative history and has not been checked against a primary legal source (e.g. the enabling law/decreto that created the Departamento del Chocó). If the correct year differs, both start_year and the polity_code (COL-CHO-1947-2025) would need to change, and the container edge's start_year would need to match. This should be verified against a primary Colombian legislative source before the entry is treated as final.

### oq-pre1947-intendencia-not-modeled

**No polity exists yet for Chocó's pre-1947 intendencia-era administration**

Before 1947, Chocó existed as an intendencia (administered separately from, and at times within, Cauca department), and the 1915-1946 data currently routes to this entry as back_cast rather than to a matched polity, per the routing_reasoning. If a distinct intendencia-level polity is later added to the vocabulary for that period, the back_cast routing for 1915-1946 should be revisited and possibly re-pointed to that new polity instead of being carried by COL-CHO-1947-2025's back-cast coverage.
