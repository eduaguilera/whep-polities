# Cuba

## Summary

Cuba is the largest island in the Caribbean, situated 135 miles (217 km.) south of Florida. Biger 1995 records its area as 42,804 sq. miles (110,861 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 11,000,000 in 1994; it has no land boundaries [biger-1995 &sect;cuba](../sources/biger-1995.md#cuba). WHEP tracks Cuba as a continuous row from 1800 because its island territory has remained constant at approximately 109,000 sq. km. despite major political transitions: Spanish colony to 1898, US occupation 1899-1902, Republic with Platt Amendment 1902, full sovereignty 1934, and the Cuban Revolution of 1959 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
