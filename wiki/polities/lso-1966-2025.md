# Lesotho

## Summary

Lesotho is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Lesotho (formerly Basutoland) from 1966 to the present. Lesotho gained independence from Britain on 4 October 1966. It is entirely enclosed by South Africa.

## Territorial extent

Lesotho is a landlocked country entirely surrounded by South Africa. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The Kingdom of Swaziland entry notes that "Britain protected Swaziland's independence" from Transvaal expansion, similar to the British protection of Basutoland (Lesotho). Agreements between Britain and Transvaal on 3 August 1881 and 27 February 1884 established British responsibility over Swaziland (p.392), and the same pattern applied to Basutoland. [biger-1995 §swaziland](../sources/biger-1995.md#swaziland)
- The Mozambique-South Africa section confirms that the Cape of Good Hope was ceded to Britain by the Dutch in 1814, and Afrikaner farmers moved north. Natal was annexed by Britain in 1843, Transvaal gained independence in 1852, and "on 23 February 1854 it became the independent Boer Republic of Orange Free State" (p.392). Basutoland (Lesotho) was established as a British protectorate within this broader southern African context. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
