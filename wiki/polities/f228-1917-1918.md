# Russia (1917-1918)

## Summary

This row covers **2 years** of revolutionary upheaval. The polity
transitions from the Russian Provisional Government (early 1917)
to the RSFSR after the October Revolution. The Treaty of
Brest-Litovsk (3 March 1918) formalized massive territorial
losses — Poland, Finland, the Baltic states, Ukraine, and Belarus
were ceded or recognized as independent. The Russian Civil War
began.

Predecessor: [f228-1914-1917](f228-1914-1917.md).
Successor: [f228-1918-1920](f228-1918-1920.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (3 March 1918).** Russia ceded or
  recognized the independence of Poland, Finland, Estonia,
  Latvia, Lithuania, Ukraine, and Belarus. This was the single
  largest territorial loss in Russian history — roughly
  1.3 million km² and 50 million people. The treaty was later
  annulled after Germany's defeat in November 1918, but by then
  many of the separated territories had established independent
  states
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **Finland declared independence** on 6 December 1917, recognized
  by Soviet Russia on 4 January 1918.
