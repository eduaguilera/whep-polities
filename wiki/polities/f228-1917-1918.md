# USSR (1917-1918)

## Summary

The CSV labels this row "USSR (1917-1918)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), but **the USSR
did not exist until 30 December 1922**. This polity transitions
from the **Russian Provisional Government** (early 1917) to the
**RSFSR** (Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic) after
the October Revolution. The label is an anachronism documented in
[log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
Per the critical-stance rule
[log decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority](../log.md#decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority),
the CSV label is preserved as-is and the mislabeling is
documented rather than silently corrected.

This row covers **2 years** of revolutionary upheaval. The
October Revolution (7 November 1917 Gregorian) brought the
Bolsheviks to power. The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (3 March 1918)
formalized massive territorial losses — Poland, Finland, the
Baltic states, Ukraine, and Belarus were ceded or recognized as
independent. The Russian Civil War began.

Predecessor per CSV: [f228-1914-1917](f228-1914-1917.md).
Successor per CSV: [f228-1918-1920](f228-1918-1920.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (3 March 1918).** Russia ceded or
  recognized the independence of Poland, Finland, Estonia,
  Latvia, Lithuania, Ukraine, and Belarus. This was the single
  largest territorial loss in Russian history — roughly
  1.3 million km² and 50 million people. The treaty was later
  annulled after Germany's defeat in November 1918, but by then
  many of the separated territories had established independent
  states
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **Finland declared independence** on 6 December 1917, recognized
  by Soviet Russia on 4 January 1918.

  led by Lenin, seized power from the Provisional Government in

- **1918-03-03: Treaty of Brest-Litovsk.** Massive territorial
  concessions to the Central Powers. Russia lost approximately
  one-third of its European territory
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1917-12-06: Finnish independence.** Finland declared
  independence; recognized by Soviet Russia in January 1918
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).

## Contradictions

  entity is the Russian Provisional Government (until November
  1917) and then the RSFSR. The USSR was not established until
  30 December 1922. See
  [log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
