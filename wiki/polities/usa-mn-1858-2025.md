# Minnesota (state of the United States)

## Summary

Minnesota, one of the fifty states of the United States, admitted to the Union on May 11, 1858, carved from the eastern portion of the former Minnesota Territory (1849-1858) plus a strip of the former Wisconsin Territory east of the St. Croix River. This entry is `type: subnational` because it does not compete with the national USA chain for sovereignty: Minnesota has never been independently sovereign, and the row exists purely to carry state-level reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the state rather than the national level. The span 1858-2025 tracks continuous statehood, with no interruption or territorial change, through three successive eras of the national USA polity chain (USA-1848-1867 through USA-1959-2025), following the same pattern established for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025) earlier in this harness run.

**Why this entry exists.** Created by the agent-harness pipeline processing the administrative unit USA-MINNESOTA ("Minnesota", country United States of America). No prior polity row existed for Minnesota at the state level -- the existing USA chain (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) covers the country as a whole, so any Minnesota-specific statistics previously had no state-level home and either went unmatched or were folded into national USA totals, a mismatch whenever a source reports Minnesota separately from all-U.S. figures. The routing decision does not name a specific source, row count, or year range of data driving this creation (its routing_reasoning frames the entry as following the country's default state-admission convention, citing the California sibling for the end-year precedent, rather than citing specific ingested rows). What confirms Minnesota is a distinct reporting/administrative entity is uncontroversial: it is one of the fifty states, with its own government and long-standing use as a state-level unit in USDA, Census Bureau, and Mitchell-style historical compilations. The open question below flags that the specific data motivating this row's creation was not supplied in the routing decision.

**Area:** measured from the attached geometry at approximately 225,163 km2 (Minnesota's commonly cited land+water area is about 225,163 km2 / 86,936 sq mi, including a substantial share of Lake Superior and thousands of inland lakes). This figure is a measurement of the attached polygon, consistent with Minnesota's real-world extent, not an independent territorial claim.

**Territory description:** Minnesota occupies the north-central United States, bordered by Lake Superior and Wisconsin to the east, Iowa to the south, South Dakota and North Dakota to the west, and the Canadian provinces of Ontario and Manitoba to the north -- readily locatable on any modern map as the state containing the source of the Mississippi River and the Twin Cities (Minneapolis-St. Paul). Its external boundaries have not changed since admission in 1858 per the routing decision's vintage-risk note, so the modern GADM boundary is treated as valid for the full 1858-2025 span, though this has not been independently corroborated against a 19th-century source (see open question below).

## Predecessors and successors

Minnesota has no predecessor or successor polity row in this database: it enters as a full U.S. state in 1858 and its span runs open/current through 2025 with no successor. Minnesota Territory (1849-1858), organized from unorganized land plus the western remnant of Wisconsin Territory, is not itself represented as a polity row, so there is no predecessor edge to record. ## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Minnesota is a standard U.S. state with no distinct historical name of its own separate from statehood (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so it follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows: ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The subunit token chosen is MN, the USPS/ISO 3166-2:US code for Minnesota, matching the two-letter convention already used for other U.S. states added in this harness run (e.g. USA-AL, and presumably USA-CA per the routing decision's sibling reference). The full code is USA-MN-1858-2025.

### d-start-year-admission

**Start year is 1858 admission to the Union, per the country default rule**

The country convention's default rule for U.S. states is to start at formal admission to the Union unless the state is a departure case (Alaska, Hawaii, or a territory with a distinct pre-statehood identity already carrying its own polity row). Minnesota Territory (1849-1858) is not itself a polity row in this database, and the routing decision explicitly found this is not a departure case, so 1858 is used directly rather than back-cast to the territorial period or forward to the earliest ingested data year of 1866. The routing decision itself flags that the 1858 start precedes the data's own extent by eight years -- this is expected under the convention, since the entry's span reflects administrative history rather than data coverage, exactly as it did for Alabama.

### d-three-container-edges

**Three container edges tile 1858-2025 across the successive national USA rows**

The national USA chain in the polity table is split into five rows by era: USA-1800-1803, USA-1803-1848, USA-1848-1867, USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025. Minnesota's 1858 start falls inside the third of these (USA-1848-1867), so the container list uses three edges (skipping USA-1800-1803 and USA-1803-1848 entirely) to cover the full 1858-2025 span with no gap, each edge trimmed to the intersection of Minnesota's span and that national row's own span. This differs from the Alabama page's four edges only because Minnesota's later admission date (1858 vs. 1819) excludes the second national era.
