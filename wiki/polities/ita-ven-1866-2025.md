# Veneto (region of Italy)

## Summary

Veneto is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni), in the northeast of the country, bordering Austria to the north, Trentino-Alto Adige and Lombardy to the west, Emilia-Romagna to the south, and Friuli-Venezia Giulia to the east, with its capital at Venice. Unlike Lombardy, Piedmont, or the other regions that entered the Kingdom of Italy at unification in 1861, Veneto was still part of the Austrian Empire (as the Venetian half of the Kingdom of Lombardy-Venetia, under Austrian administration since 1815) when Italy unified. It was ceded to Italy only after the Third Italian War of Independence, formalized by the Treaty of Vienna (October 1866) and confirmed by a regional plebiscite the same month. This entry is `type: subnational`, not national: Italy (and, until 1866, Austria) held sovereignty throughout, and this row exists to carry a reporting territory distinct from the national ITA chain, matching the convention already used for other Italian NUTS2 regions (Lombardia ITC4, Bolzano/South Tyrol ITH1). Applying the country convention's default rule (start = earliest national container whose territory includes the region) to Veneto's actual annexation history yields 1866, not the generic 1861 used for regions already part of the Kingdom at unification.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit ITA-ITH3 (admin code ITH3, Eurostat NUTS2 code for Veneto) from the country-convention pipeline's Italian subnational routing pass (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json, pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv). Before this row existed, data for this reporting unit had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows, which is wrong for the same reason it was wrong for Lombardia: a NUTS2-level regional series (population, agricultural production) folded into a national total either double-counts against sibling regions or misattributes a regional figure (Veneto is roughly 1/6 of Italy's area) to the whole country. The routing decision additionally identifies a second, Veneto-specific error the naive convention would have produced: treating Veneto as available from 1861 like most other regions, when Veneto was still under Austrian rule until 1866 and so cannot be represented, even administratively, by an 1861-1866 Italian container. Data years 1861-1865, if present in the source, are routed as back_cast onto this same territory (then still Austrian) rather than left unroutable, since the source reports on the same physical territory across the annexation. What confirms Veneto is a genuinely distinct reporting/administrative unit rather than a restatement of national Italy: it is Eurostat's own NUTS2 statistical division (ITH3), used because ISTAT itself reports Italian regional statistics, including for Veneto, as a first-class unit separate from national totals — the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling NUTS2 regions created in this pipeline.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. `polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1` is recorded as the source of record (registered, correct source type for ADM1/NUTS2 boundaries) but `polygon_feature_id` is left null and `polygon_status` is `unassigned`, because the copy of gadm-4.1-adm1 present in this repository's local data is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Italy entirely (0 ITA features on disk), even though GADM's full global release does digitize Italian regions. This is the same gap already documented for the sibling Lombardia and Bolzano/South Tyrol entries (ita-lom-1861-2025.md, ita-ith1-1919-2025.md) and is not specific to Veneto; fixing it once at the source level (replacing the local extract or fetching Italy's admin-1 layer) would unblock this row along with the other pending Italian regions. **Territory description:** Veneto covers approximately 18,345 km2 in northeastern Italy, about 6.1% of Italy's total land area — a reader can locate it on a modern map as the region containing Venice, the Dolomites' southern foothills, and the lower Po and Adige valleys, bounded by Lake Garda and Lombardy to the west, the Austrian border and Trentino-Alto Adige to the north, and Friuli-Venezia Giulia to the east. The region's own outer boundary has been stable since the 1866 annexation drew it as a successor to the Austrian administrative division of Venetia; province-level reorganizations within Veneto in this period (e.g. changes to Venice and Padua's provincial boundaries) did not alter the region's own outline, so a single present-day regional polygon should be a reasonably safe proxy back to 1866 once a source is fetched — a vintage-drift caveat for that future assignment, not grounds to fabricate a polygon now. No area figure here is measured from an attached geometry; none is attached yet.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor is assigned: before 1866 this territory was the Venetian portion of Austria's Kingdom of Lombardy-Venetia, which is out of scope for this Italy-rooted subnational chain rather than a linkable predecessor polity — if a future entry is created for the pre-1866 Austrian administration of Venetia, it should be linked here as `predecessor`. No successor is assigned: the end_year of 2025 is open, matching Veneto's still-current status as a first-level region of the Italian Republic. The three container edges (ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) tile the whole 1866-2025 span with no gap or overlap, reflecting that none of the intervening national-level changes (capture of Rome 1870, WWI settlement 1919) altered Veneto's own regional boundary.

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-ven

**Code uses ITA-VEN, not the source's NUTS id ITH3**

The routing decision names this unit by its Eurostat NUTS2 code ITH3, but NUTS codes are a statistical classification unfamiliar to a wiki reader and have been reassigned across NUTS revisions. Following the dominant 57-of-66 <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern already used for Lombardia (ITA-LOM) and other Italian regions, the subunit token is set to VEN, a readable abbreviation of the region's own name, giving ITA-VEN-1866-2025. This is not a bespoke-code case: Veneto is a real, still-existing administrative region with a direct present-day successor (itself), unlike the dissolved-polity cases (Alaska Territory, Hyderabad State) that motivate the bespoke exception.

### d-start-year-1866-not-1861

**Start year set to 1866 per Veneto's actual annexation history, departing from the generic 1861 default**

The country convention's stated default rule sets a region's start at the earliest container whose territory includes it, with two explicit departures listed (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia at 1919, Molise at 1963). Veneto is not among those two explicitly enumerated departures, but applying the default rule correctly to Veneto's own history still yields 1866, not 1861, because Veneto was still Austrian territory during 1861-1866 and no Italian container from that window includes it. This is presented as a correct application of the general rule to this specific territory's history, not as inventing a third departure case, and mirrors how the routing decision itself reasoned about it. Confirmed against the routing_verdicts.csv proposal for ITA-ITH3, which states the same basis.

### d-container-tiling-1866

**Container edges use the three fine-grained ITA eras from 1866, not the coarse ITA-1861-1919 aggregate**

As with Lombardia (ita-lom-1861-2025.md), the polity table carries both a fine partition of the Kingdom of Italy (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919) and a coarse aggregate (ITA-1861-1919) covering the same span. Using the aggregate would double-count against the fine rows if both were ever queried together. Since Veneto's own span starts at 1866, only the ITA-1866-1870 and ITA-1870-1919 fine rows are needed to reach 1919, followed by ITA-1919-2025 to the present; these three edges tile 1866-2025 with no gap and no overlap.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-italy-coverage

**Local GADM 4.1 admin-1 extract excludes Italy entirely**

The repository's local gadm-4.1-adm1 dataset is a curated 81-country subset that does not include Italy at all (0 ITA features), even though GADM's global release does digitize Italian regions including Veneto. Until that local extract is replaced with full/global GADM admin-1 coverage (or the Italy layer is fetched separately), no `polygon_feature_id` can be assigned here and `polygon_status` must remain `unassigned`. This same gap blocks every other pending Italian subnational entry (Lombardia, Bolzano/South Tyrol, and presumably the remaining NUTS2 regions), so it is likely worth fixing once at the source level rather than per-entry.

### oq-pre1866-data-back-cast

**Whether any 1861-1865 data for this unit exists and how it should be treated**

The routing decision's reasoning mentions routing 1861-1865 data as a back_cast onto this same territory (then still under Austrian rule) rather than leaving it unroutable, on the grounds that a source reporting on this physical territory before the 1866 annexation is still reporting on it. This entry's own span starts at 1866, so if such pre-1866 rows exist in the underlying data they are not covered by this polity_code as written; whether they need a distinct predecessor row, a widened start_year, or a documented back_cast exception is unresolved and should be checked against what the actual routed dataset contains for this unit before further action.

### oq-sibling-1861-vs-1866-precedent

**Whether other Italian regions annexed after 1861 (if any) were given analogous departure treatment**

Veneto is being treated here as a case where the general start-year rule, correctly applied, departs from the common 1861 value used for regions already inside the Kingdom at unification — as distinct from the two explicitly enumerated departures (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia at 1919, Molise at 1963). It is not yet confirmed whether this 1866 reasoning was applied consistently to any other Italian region with a similarly late annexation date, or whether this entry is the first to make that distinction explicit. This should be checked once more Italian NUTS2 siblings are processed, since an inconsistency here would mean either this entry or an earlier sibling verdict needs revision.
