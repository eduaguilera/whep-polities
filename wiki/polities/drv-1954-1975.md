# Democratic Republic of Vietnam (North Vietnam)

## Summary

The Democratic Republic of Vietnam (Việt Nam Dân chủ Cộng hòa), commonly
called North Vietnam, was formally established in the north following the
Geneva Accords of 21 July 1954, which partitioned Vietnam at the 17th
Parallel. The DRV had existed as a proclaimed state since 1945, but the
Geneva partition consolidated its de facto territorial base north of the DMZ.
It absorbed the Republic of Vietnam after the fall of Saigon on 30 April 1975,
and unified Vietnam was proclaimed as the Socialist Republic of Vietnam on
2 July 1976. COW code 816.

The DRV occupied the northern half of the Indochina peninsula north of the
17th Parallel DMZ, encompassing the Red River Delta, the northern highlands,
and the coast up to the DMZ. Capital: Hanoi. Area approximately 158,000 km².

**Western statistical sources of the Cold War period referred to this state
as "North Vietnam" when they included it at all.** The label "Vietnam" or
"Viet Nam" in Mitchell and similar sources denotes the RVN (South Vietnam),
not the DRV. DRV data requiring routing should use "north vietnam" as the
alias label, not "viet nam".

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0, COW code 816, polygon vintage 1960. North Vietnam's
territory was defined by the 17th Parallel DMZ in the south (established by
the Geneva Accords 1954), with the Chinese border in the north, Laotian border
to the west, and Gulf of Tonkin coastline to the east. Area approximately
158,000 km².

The de jure boundary was stable 1954–1975, though the Ho Chi Minh Trail
corridors through Laos represented an informal extension of operational
control. No polygon split is required for the recognised boundary. Polygon
vintage 1960 is representative of the full 1954–1975 period (DMZ was fixed
at Geneva).

Note: CShapes assigns COW code 816 to both the DRV (1954–1975) and to unified
Vietnam (VNM-1975-2025) after 1976, but these are distinct CShapes features
with different geometries. The pre-1975 DRV polygon covers only northern
Vietnam; the post-1975 unified-Vietnam polygon covers the entire country.

**Why this entry exists:** To complete the Vietnam partition family alongside
RVN-1954-1975. Any data explicitly labelled "north vietnam" for 1954–1975
routes here rather than to VNM-1975-2025, and successorship/predecessorship
chains are correctly recorded.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [fid-1887-1954](fid-1887-1954.md) — French Indochina.
  The Geneva Accords of 21 July 1954 dissolved French Indochina and
  provisionally partitioned Vietnam, with the northern zone going to the DRV
  under Ho Chi Minh.
- **Successor:** [vnm-1975-2025](vnm-1975-2025.md) — unified Vietnam.
  After the fall of Saigon on 30 April 1975 the RVN ceased; the DRV renamed
  itself the Socialist Republic of Vietnam on 2 July 1976 and absorbed the
  south.

## Decisions

- Created 2026-06-24 to complete the Vietnam partition family (alongside
  RVN-1954-1975) and correctly record FID-1887-1954 successors and
  VNM-1975-2025 predecessors.
- Polygon chosen as CShapes 2.0 COW 816 vintage 1960 (northern-Vietnam-only
  geometry, representative of the stable 1954–1975 DRV extent).
- No polygon split required: the recognised de jure boundary was stable.

## Open questions

### oq-drv-1945-1954-predecessor

The DRV was proclaimed on 2 September 1945 and fought French forces until
the 1954 armistice. WHEP currently has no DRV-1945-1954 entry. If data for
this period under DRV sovereignty is ingested, a separate polity covering
1945–1954 (with FID-1887-1954 as the contemporaneous French Indochina entity)
should be created.

### oq-drv-cow-816-continuity

COW uses code 816 for both the DRV and unified Vietnam. WHEP treats these
as distinct polities (DRV-1954-1975 and VNM-1975-2025) because their
territorial extents differ substantially (north-only vs. whole country).
Pipeline code that joins on cow_code must account for this discontinuity.
