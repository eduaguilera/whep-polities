# Serbia (1913-1915)

## Summary

A **3-year row** in the CSV
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv) covering
post-Balkan Wars Serbia through the first phase of WWI. Serbia had
just gained Kosovo and part of Macedonia in the Balkan Wars
(1912-1913). The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in
Sarajevo (28 June 1914) triggered WWI. Austria-Hungary invaded
Serbia in July 1914. Serbia initially resisted successfully but was
overrun in October-December 1915 by joint Austro-Hungarian, German,
and Bulgarian forces. The Serbian government and army retreated
through Albania to Corfu.

Predecessor per CSV: [ser-1816-1913](ser-1816-1913.md).
Successor per CSV: [ser-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md).

## Territorial extent

- **1913: post-Balkan Wars expansion.** Serbia gained Kosovo and
  parts of Macedonia from the Ottoman Empire in the First Balkan
  War. The Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913) confirmed these
  gains. Serbia roughly doubled in area.
- **1914-1915: wartime contraction.** Austria-Hungary invaded in
  July 1914 but was repelled. By late 1915, a combined offensive
  by Austria-Hungary, Germany, and Bulgaria overran the country.
  Serbia was fully occupied by December 1915.
