# French Somaliland (1884–1977)

Authoritative WHEP row for colonial Djibouti. Polygon: CShapes 2.0 feature 522 (vintage 1900, ~23,192 km²), reassigned from retired DJI-1886-2025. Borders fixed by 1897 Franco-Ethiopian treaty. Successor: FRS-1977-2025.

## Known data-quality issues

### iia-coffee-is-ethiopian-transit-trade

**The IIA "green coffee" figures under this label are re-export, not
production.** Found 2026-07-24 by assertion verification of
`djibouti|iia|1929-1945` (two independent agents).

The series runs at a median of **~14,114 t/yr** — impossible for French
Somaliland, whose arid terrain supports essentially no coffee cultivation.
These are Ethiopian beans (grown around Harar and in southern Ethiopia)
moving through the port on the Djibouti–Addis Ababa railway, completed 1917;
France held a de facto monopoly on Ethiopia's export trade through Djibouti
until Assab opened in the mid-1950s.

**Double-count risk.** Summing this series alongside Ethiopia's own
production series counts the same coffee twice. Treat these rows as
entrepôt/transit trade attributable to the territory's *port function*, not
to its agriculture. The routing itself is correct — the source did record it
under this label — but any production aggregate must exclude it.

`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/source_conventions.csv`.
