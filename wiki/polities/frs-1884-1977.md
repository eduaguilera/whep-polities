# French Somaliland

## Summary

French Somaliland from the 1884 treaties with the Issa clan (and the contemporaneous founding of Djibouti city) to Djibouti's independence on 27 June 1977. French presence in the region pre-dates 1884 (Obock base acquired 1862), but the 1884 treaties are the conventional modern start date. In 1967 the territory was renamed *French Territory of the Afars and the Issas*; the 1977 independence produced the modern Republic of Djibouti.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Covers the approximate area of modern Djibouti. Borders were broadly stable from the 1897 Franco-Ethiopian treaty onward.
