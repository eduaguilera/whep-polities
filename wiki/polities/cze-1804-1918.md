# Czech Lands (Crownlands of Bohemia, 1804-1918)

## Overview

The Czech Lands designates the aggregate of the three historic crownlands — the **Kingdom of Bohemia**, the **Margraviate of Moravia**, and the **Duchy of Upper and Lower Silesia (Austrian Silesia)** — as they existed within the Habsburg monarchy from the proclamation of the Austrian Empire in 1804 to the dissolution of Austria-Hungary in October 1918. The crownlands formed a contiguous sub-territory of approximately 79,000 km² in the northwestern sector of the empire, administratively part of the Cisleithanian (Austrian) half after the Austro-Hungarian Compromise of 1867.

This WHEP entry exists to carry Czech-Republic-area economic and demographic data for the pre-independence period. It is a sub-imperial, non-sovereign unit: it was NOT an independent state, and the empire polygon (AUH-*) must not be used for it.

## Territorial Extent

The territory comprised:

| Crownland | German name | Computed area (ETRS89-LAEA) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------------|
| Kingdom of Bohemia | Königreich Böhmen | 51,973 km² |
| Margraviate of Moravia | Markgrafschaft Mähren | 22,203 km² |
| Duchy of Austrian Silesia | Herzogtum Ober- und Niederschlesien | 5,172 km² |
| **Union total** | | **79,349 km²** |

The modern Czech Republic covers approximately 78,866 km² (difference <1%); the minor discrepancy reflects post-1918 boundary settlements. The crownland perimeters were stable throughout 1804-1918 and do not require period subdivision for polygon purposes.

## Polygon Provenance

**Method**: `composed_union` — union of three individual crownland polygons from a single historical GIS source.

**Source**: Histogis 1860 Habsburg crownlands shapefile — *Anna Piechl / Peter Paul Marckhgott-Sanabria, "Austrian Empire Crownlands 1860"*, based on Floder (Österreich 1835, Woldan Collection), Andree & Scobel (Böhmen, Mähren und Österreichisch-Schlesien, 1905/1906, Rumsey Collection), Radefeld (Oesterreich 1849), and OpenStreetMap; published by Histogis (histogis.com). Quality rating: **yellow** (medium; spatially and historically checked by project historian).

**File**: `data/geodata/histogis-1860-habsburg/crownlands_1860/austrian_empire_adm2_crownlands_1860.shp`

**Feature IDs used**:
- `id=7` — Königreich Böhmen (Kingdom of Bohemia), dated 1815-06-09 to 1867-12-21
- `id=15` — Markgrafschaft Mähren (Margraviate of Moravia), dated 1815-06-09 to 1867-12-21
- `id=5` — Herzogtum Ober- und Niederschlesien (Austrian Silesia), dated 1815-06-09 to 1867-12-21

**Why this source**: CShapes 2.0 covers only sovereign states and colonial dependencies, and carries no sub-imperial Habsburg features. Cliopatria/Seshat covers the Kingdom of Bohemia only through 1526, then jumps to Czechoslovakia 1919; it has no entry for the Habsburg crownlands 1804-1918. The Histogis crownlands shapefile is the only available vector source with individual crownland-level polygons for this period.

**Temporal proxy note**: The Histogis features are dated 1815-1867 (end of the Austrian Empire period before the Compromise). They are used as a period proxy for the full 1804-1918 span because the crownland borders were administratively stable throughout — no documented boundary change affected the Bohemian, Moravian, or Silesian crownland perimeters between 1804 and 1918. The 1804-1815 gap (between empire proclamation and the shapefile start date) involves identical territory. The post-1867 Cisleithanian period maintained the same crownland units with unchanged external boundaries.

**Area cross-check**: 79,349 km² (composed union) vs. ~78,866 km² modern Czech Republic = +483 km² (+0.6%). Difference attributable to minor post-1918 border adjustments (Hlučín/Hultschin region to Czechoslovakia from Germany; small Cieszyn adjustments). Direction: slight overestimate of historical territory relative to modern Czech Republic.

**ESTIMATE — not authoritative** for precise sub-crownland boundary questions; suitable for country-level economic-historical aggregation.

## Predecessor / Successor

- **Predecessor**: `AUH-1800-1859` (Austrian Empire) — the Czech Lands were part of the Habsburg monarchy before and throughout the 1804-1918 period; the immediate macro-entity entry is AUH-1800-1859 for the period starting 1804.
- **Successor**: `F51-1918-1938` (Czechoslovakia 1918-1938) — the Czech crownlands became the Czech-speaking core of the newly proclaimed Czechoslovak Republic on 28 October 1918.

## Why This Entry Was Created

WHEP carries Czech Republic (CZE) economic data from the pre-independence period. Without this entry, data for Bohemia, Moravia, and Austrian Silesia between 1804 and 1918 would be incorrectly assigned to the whole AUH empire polygon (~675,000 km²) or left unmatched. This entry provides the correct ~79,000 km² sub-territory for aggregating Czech-area GDP, population, and trade data under the iso3 code CZE.
