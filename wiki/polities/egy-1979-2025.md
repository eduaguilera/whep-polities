# Egypt

## Summary

A **47-year row** covering post-Camp-David Egypt with full Sinai returned
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is the current
terminal row of the main Egyptian chain.

Sinai withdrawal completed 25 April 1982. Mubarak era (1981-2011), 2011
revolution, brief Morsi presidency, Sisi regime from 2014. ~1M km2.

Predecessor: [egy-1967-1979](egy-1967-1979.md). Successor: NA (current).
