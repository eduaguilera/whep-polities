# Chad (1900-1912)

## Summary

French Chad (Territoire Militaire du Tchad) in its initial configuration from 1900 to 1912. France defeated the Wadai Empire and established military control over the Chad basin by 1900. "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were united in 1906" [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated" [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad). The 1912 end date corresponds to a CShapes boundary change associated with the reorganisation of French Equatorial Africa and the redistribution of territory following the Franco-German Convention of 1911.

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. Chad (area: 495,753 sq mi [1,284,000 sq km]) [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad). The territory in this period was the Territoire Militaire du Tchad, bounded by French West Africa to the west, Libya to the north, Sudan to the east, and Oubangui-Chari/Cameroon to the south.
