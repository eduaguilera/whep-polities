# Virginia (state of the United States)

## Summary

Virginia, one of the original 13 U.S. states, ratified the Constitution on 1788-06-25 as the 10th state and has remained a continuous state of the United States ever since -- through the 1861-65 secession and readmission (Virginia joined the Confederacy in 1861, was readmitted to the Union in 1870), the 1863 loss of its northwestern counties to the new state of West Virginia, and to the present. `type: subnational` because Virginia never held independent sovereignty during this span; it is a constituent state of a sovereign United States, and the row exists to carry a reporting geography distinct from and smaller than the national USA totals. `start_year` is 1800, not Virginia's actual 1788 admission, because the USA national container chain in this database begins at USA-1800-1803 -- see the container-floor decision below, which documents this as the same defect the harness's commit 11e8ca1 fixed for eleven other original-13 states.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: routing decision unit_id USA-VIRGINIA, admin_name VIRGINIA, country United States of America, proposing polygon_route registered_source_feature against gadm-4.1-adm1 feature USA.47_1. No row count or year range is given in the decision payload itself, but the unit is treated as a still-current state (open end_year 2025). Previously matched to: nothing -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly that Virginia has no name match to any existing polity and is not identical to the USA national rows, which are national totals a state is contained by, not equivalent to. What confirms distinctness: Virginia's continuous existence as one of the original 13 states, admitted 1788-06-25 as the 10th state to ratify the Constitution, is a matter of settled constitutional history, not something this entry needs to newly establish; the reason a dedicated row is needed is that USA national totals (e.g. USA-1867-1959) aggregate all states together, and any statistical source reporting at the state level (as many US historical statistical series do) needs a state-level polity to route to, exactly the reasoning already applied to Alaska (ALK-1867-1959) and to Spain's provinces (ESP-AS-1833-2025 and siblings).

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: assigned. polygon_source gadm-4.1-adm1, feature USA.47_1, the modern Virginia ADM1 boundary (distinct from USA.49_1 West Virginia, which split off in 1863). Measured area of the attached geometry is approximately 110,787 km2 -- this is a measurement of the polygon, not independent evidence about the historical territory, and it understates the true 1800-1863 territory of Virginia, which included what is now West Virginia (an additional ~62,700 km2, for a combined historical area of roughly 173,500 km2 before 1863). This vintage mismatch is documented rather than resolved here (see decisions and open questions): no pre-1863 Virginia boundary is digitised in this repo's registered sources, so the modern boundary is used as the best available proxy for the whole 1800-2025 span, understating Virginia's own actual territory for the pre-1863 portion of it. Territory description: Virginia sits in the U.S. Mid-Atlantic/Upper South, bordered by Maryland and the District of Columbia to the north/northeast, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, North Carolina and Tennessee to the south, Kentucky to the west, and West Virginia to the northwest. Its capital is Richmond; major cities include Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and the Northern Virginia suburbs of Washington, D.C. (Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax). On a modern map it is easily located as the state directly south of Washington, D.C. and Maryland along the Chesapeake Bay.

## Predecessors and successors

Virginia has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1800 (this entry's start_year) and indeed before that back to 1607, the territory was the English/British Colony and Dominion of Virginia and then, from 1776, the independent Commonwealth of Virginia prior to ratifying the Constitution in 1788. This database's USA national chain does not extend before 1800 and has no separate colonial-Virginia row, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row (see open question on the colonial gap). It has no successor: Virginia remains a current U.S. state as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the treatment of other still-current subnational rows such as ESP-AS-1833-2025.

## Sourced claims

- Virginia ratified the U.S. Constitution on 1788-06-25, becoming the 10th state to do so -- a settled constitutional-history fact, checkable against any standard account of the ratification sequence (e.g. National Archives records of state ratification dates).
- West Virginia separated from Virginia in 1863 during the Civil War, reducing Virginia's territory by roughly 62,700 km2 -- checkable against the admission date of West Virginia as the 35th state (1863-06-20) and against GADM's separate USA.49_1 feature for West Virginia versus USA.47_1 for Virginia.
- GADM 4.1 ADM1 feature USA.47_1 is the correct, unambiguous match for present-day Virginia and is distinct from USA.49_1 (West Virginia) -- checkable directly in the GADM 4.1 attribute table shipped in data/geodata/gadm-4.1/.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-truncated-to-container-floor

**start_year is 1800, not Virginia's 1788 ratification, because no container exists before 1800**

Stage 1's routing_reasoning proposed start_year 1788, the date Virginia ratified the U.S. Constitution and became the 10th state (1788-06-25). That date is correct as a historical fact but cannot be used as this page's start_year: the USA national container chain in the polity table begins at USA-1800-1803, and a subnational unit cannot be contained by a national row that does not yet exist in the chain. This is exactly the defect the harness's own commit 11e8ca1 ("Enforce the container floor") describes: eleven states -- Delaware 1787, Connecticut/Georgia/Maryland/Massachusetts 1788, Kentucky 1792 -- were proposed with pre-1800 starts against the same USA-1800-1803 floor, and the fix was to truncate to 1800 and say so explicitly rather than let the ledger and the page disagree silently. Virginia is a further instance of the identical situation and gets the identical treatment: start_year 1800, container floor stated as the reason, and Virginia's actual 1788 admission preserved in prose (Summary and Predecessors sections) as the historical fact it is, distinct from the database's start_year. Adding an earlier USA era row to the chain is out of scope for this page -- it would need to be a decision made once for the whole USA chain, not per-state -- and is flagged as an open question below rather than solved here.

### d-polygon-vintage-risk-west-virginia-and-earlier

**polygon_status is assigned despite a boundary that did not exist until 1863**

The proposed feature USA.47_1 (GADM 4.1 ADM1 'Virginia') reflects the present-day Virginia boundary, which excludes West Virginia (separated 1863) and has not otherwise changed since. For the 1800-1863 portion of this page's span, Virginia's actual territory included present-day West Virginia, making the attached polygon (~110,787 km2) smaller than the true historical territory (~173,500 km2) for roughly 63 of the 225 years covered. polygon_status is still recorded as assigned rather than proxy because GADM ADM1 is the correct registered source for a present-day US state and no alternative digitised boundary for pre-1863 Virginia exists in this repo's registered sources; the under-coverage is documented here and in Territorial extent with km2 numbers rather than left implicit, per the polygon proxy rules' requirement to state what a correct polygon would look like when one is not used.

## Open questions

### oq-usa-chain-missing-pre-1800-era

**Does the USA national container chain need an era earlier than 1800 for original-13-states entries?**

Virginia (ratified 1788), and per the harness's own commit log Delaware (1787), Connecticut/Georgia/Maryland/Massachusetts (1788), and Kentucky (1792), all have documented admission dates earlier than USA-1800-1803, the earliest row in the current national chain. Every one of these states has had its start_year truncated to 1800 for lack of an earlier container. If this database is meant to model founding-era state admissions precisely, the chain is missing an era (e.g. USA-1776-1800 or USA-1788-1800) that would let these six-plus states carry their real ratification years instead of a uniform 1800 floor. If the database does not intend to model the 1776-1800 period at state granularity, this is not a gap needing a fix, but that should be a stated decision rather than an artefact of container tiling. This entry alone cannot resolve it since it is one of at least seven affected units.

### oq-west-virginia-boundary-pre-1863

**Should a separate polygon be constructed for Virginia's 1800-1863 territory including West Virginia?**

The attached GADM 4.1 ADM1 feature USA.47_1 reflects modern Virginia, post-1863, which excludes the ~62,700 km2 that became West Virginia. For 1800-1863 (63 of this page's 225 years), the true Virginia territory was roughly 173,500 km2, not the 110,787 km2 the attached polygon measures -- an understatement of about 36%. No constructed union polygon exists yet for old-boundary Virginia. Whether this repo's proxy tolerance treats this understatement as acceptable for a subnational US state, or whether it warrants a constructed pre-1863 feature the way constructed/build.py handles other historical unions, is unresolved.

### oq-virginia-colonial-predecessor

**Should Virginia have a predecessor row for the colonial/Commonwealth period before 1800?**

Virginia existed as the Colony and Dominion of Virginia (English/British, from 1607) and then as an independent Commonwealth (from 1776) well before the 1800 start_year this page uses. This database has no USA-era row before 1800 and no separate colonial Virginia row either, so predecessor is left empty. If a colonial-era entity (e.g. under a future GBR colonial chain, analogous to F228 rows for Russia) is ever added for British North America, it should predecessor-link to this entry; until then the 1607-1800 span of Virginia's own existence is undocumented in this database's chain, a bigger gap than the twelve-year container-floor truncation above since it spans nearly two centuries.
