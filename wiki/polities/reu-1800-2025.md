# Réunion

## Summary

This row tracks Réunion as a territorial-economic unit from the 1800 WHEP
database floor to the 2025 ceiling. The Indian Ocean island (called
Île Bourbon before 1848) was a French colony through 1946 and has been an
overseas department since. One island, no boundary changes: a single
period covers the span.

## Territorial extent

~2,512 km2 official; the GADM 4.1 adm0 polygon measures 2,520 km2.
CShapes 2.0 also carries the island as a French colony feature
(cowcode 585, 1886-2019), consistent in extent.

## Predecessors and successors

None. The 1946 colony-to-department transition did not alter the extent.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **182 "Réunion"** reports 1961-2006 (13,083 data
rows observed; FAOSTAT folds the French overseas departments into France
from 2007). Routed here by `pipelines/faostat-era-matching` via iso3 REU.

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `REU` (modern extent, valid for the
  whole span).
- **Single period:** created to close the FAOSTAT-era gap (area 182
  previously had no polity family).
