# Serbia (1913-1918)

## Summary

A **6-year row** in the CSV
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv) overlapping with
SER-1913-1915. This is an unusual CSV structure with **two
contemporaneous SER rows** for the 1913-1915 period. The 1913-1918
row likely represents Serbia's continued legal existence during the
occupation period — the Serbian government-in-exile operated from
Corfu (1916-1918) and the Serbian army fought on the Salonika Front.
The 1918 end date aligns with the formation of the Kingdom of Serbs,
Croats and Slovenes (Yugoslavia) on 1 December 1918.

Predecessor per CSV: [ser-1816-1913](ser-1816-1913.md) AND
[ser-1913-1915](ser-1913-1915.md) — **dual predecessor**, unusual.

Successor per CSV: NA. **But historically Serbia became the core of
the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (Yugoslavia, F248).** The
CSV chain is broken here — [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) has
`predecessor = NA` instead of referencing this row.

## Territorial extent

- **1913: post-Balkan Wars.** Same as SER-1913-1915 — Serbia at its
  expanded borders including Kosovo and parts of Macedonia.
- **1915-1918: occupied / government-in-exile.** Serbia was fully
  occupied by Central Powers from late 1915. The government
  operated from Corfu. Serbian forces fought on the Salonika
  (Thessaloniki) Front alongside Allied forces.
- **1918: liberation and dissolution into Yugoslavia.** Allied
  breakthrough on the Salonika Front (September 1918). Serbia
  liberated by November 1918. On 1 December 1918, the Kingdom
  of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes was proclaimed, absorbing Serbia.

## Open questions

### oq-dual-predecessor

Unusual CSV structure: this row has two predecessors
(SER-1816-1913 and SER-1913-1915) and overlaps temporally with
SER-1913-1915 (both start in 1913). This may reflect different
aspects of Serbian continuity — the 1913-1915 row as a
territorial/military entity, and the 1913-1918 row as the
continuous legal/political entity including the government-in-exile
period. Needs investigation.

### oq-yugoslavia-successor-gap

The CSV has `successor = NA` for this row, but historically Serbia
became the core of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes
(F248-1918-1919). The [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) page
already documents this gap: its CSV `predecessor = NA` is flagged
as a bug (see oq-predecessor-csv-bug on that page). This is a
reciprocal gap — both the SER and F248 sides of the chain are
broken in the CSV.
