# Basilicata (region of Italy)

## Summary

Basilicata is one of Italy's twenty ordinary-statute administrative regions, occupying the instep of the Italian peninsula between Campania, Puglia and Calabria, with its capital at Potenza and a second province at Matera. This entry captures a NUTS2-level statistical series (unit id ITA-ITF5) reported continuously for this territory from 1861 (Italian unification) to the present, at 97.8% observed coverage -- not a residual category or a national total mislabeled as regional. type is set to subnational, not national, because Basilicata was never a sovereign state: it is a reporting/administrative subdivision of the Kingdom of Italy and its successor Italian Republic throughout, and the two national ITA container rows (ITA-1861-1919, then ITA-1919-2025) hold sovereignty over it for the entire span. The entry exists because no existing polity IS Basilicata -- the ITA-* rows in this database are national aggregates and cannot correctly receive a series that is specifically regional -- and because the country convention's default start-year rule (region start_year = start_year of the earliest national container whose territory includes it, absent an explicit departure) puts this region's start at 1861 rather than at the 1970 date used for several sibling Italian regions created earlier in this same pipeline run.

**Why this entry exists.** **Input data captured:** a NUTS2-level regional series for unit ITA-ITF5 (Basilicata), reported continuously from 1861 to the present with 97.8% observed/non-blank coverage -- i.e., not a sparse or residual category. Row counts and exact source table are held in the routing pipeline's ledger for unit_id ITA-ITF5 rather than restated here.

**Previously matched to, and why wrong:** before this routing decision, data for this territory had no dedicated regional polity to land on -- the only ITA-coded rows in the database (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1861-1919, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) are national totals for the whole Kingdom of Italy / Italian Republic, none of which IS Basilicata specifically. Routing a regional series to a national row would either double-count it against whatever national totals already include Basilicata, or silently misrepresent a Potenza/Matera-scale series as describing the entire country.

**Confirmation the entity is distinct:** the routing decision's own coverage statistic (97.8% observed across the claimed 1861-2025 span) is direct evidence that Basilicata was tabulated as its own reporting unit continuously, not merely swept up inside national figures or reported only from some later administrative-reform date. This is corroborated structurally by the two sibling regional rows already in the table before this decision (ITA-LIG-1970-2025 for Liguria, ITA-PIE-1970-2025 for Piemonte), which establish that this database already treats Italian NUTS2 regions as legitimate independent polities distinct from the national ITA chain -- Basilicata simply follows that same precedent, differing only in its start year per the country convention's default rule rather than the 1970 truncation used for those and other sibling regions (see the open decision on that point below).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is available in the local GeoPackage for this period, though a source is registered in principle. polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: gadm-4.1-adm1 is a registered source in sources.yaml with the id_column (GID_1) needed to look up Italian ADM1 regions, and it is the mechanism already used elsewhere in this table for equivalent regional lookups -- but the local copy of gadm_410-levels.gpkg is a subset covering only 81 countries, and Italy is not among them, so the Basilicata feature cannot be located on disk today. polygon_source is therefore recorded as the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1 (per the routing rule: when the route is registered_source_unfetched, the slug itself is the source), polygon_feature_id is null, and polygon_status is unassigned pending a re-fetch of the global (non-subset) GADM file.

**Territory description, in terms locatable on a modern map:** Basilicata is the modern Italian region occupying the arch of the peninsula's instep between Campania to the west, Puglia to the east and north-east, and Calabria to the south -- a small strip of coastline on the Gulf of Taranto (Ionian Sea) and a shorter strip on the Tyrrhenian side near Maratea. It comprises two provinces, Potenza (the regional capital, inland and mountainous -- part of the southern Apennines) and Matera (the Sassi di Matera UNESCO site sits in this province). The modern region's administrative area is approximately **9,995 km2**, a figure from present-day Italian regional statistics (ISTAT), not measured from any polygon attached to this entry -- no polygon is attached yet, per the status above. Basilicata's own administrative boundaries have been essentially stable since unification (province of Potenza established 1861, province of Matera split off from it only in 1927, both remaining wholly inside the same regional footprint used today), so once a GADM feature is fetched, the modern ADM1 boundary should be a reasonable proxy across the full 1861-2025 span with no dramatic-territory-change caveat of the kind that rules out proxying (unlike, e.g., post-Trianon Hungary or post-1918 Poland).

## Predecessors and successors

Basilicata has no predecessor polity in this database: it is created here as a full-span (1861-2025) region, not as a continuation of an earlier subnational entry, because no prior page existed for this territory under any code. It has no successor either -- the region is still current, administered today as one of Italy's ordinary-statute regions with its capital at Potenza, and the entry's end_year of 2025 is an open span reflecting that currency rather than a dissolution. Nationally, the region sits within two successive container eras: ITA-1861-1919 (the Kingdom of Italy before the WWI territorial settlement) and ITA-1919-2025 (the Italian state from the postwar settlement to the present) -- Basilicata's own borders were untouched by the WWI adjustments, which concerned the north-eastern frontier, so the region's continuity across that container boundary is a fact about the national chain, not about Basilicata's own territory changing.

## Decisions

### d-full-span-not-1970-truncation

**Basilicata gets the full 1861-2025 span, departing from sibling ITF/ITC/ITH regions that start at 1970**

Sibling Italian NUTS2 rows created earlier in this pipeline (ITC1, ITC3, ITC4, ITF1, ITF3, ITF4, ITF6, ITG1, ITG2, ITH1, ITH3) all begin at 1970, with pre-1970 years for those regions left matched to the national ITA rows. This entry instead follows the country convention's stated default start rule verbatim: a region's start_year equals the start_year of the earliest national container whose territory includes it, unless the region appears in the departures list (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia at 1919, Molise at 1963). Basilicata is not in that departures list, and the routing decision for ITF5 explicitly derives 1861 from the rule rather than from any sibling precedent. The routing_concerns field for this same decision flags that this may mean the DEPARTURES list is incomplete (missing an implicit 1970 departure that should apply to all non-listed regions, matching the sibling pattern) rather than that Basilicata is a genuine exception. I did not resolve that tension here -- it is recorded as an open question below -- because re-deriving the departures list is a country-convention change outside the scope of authoring one page, and the routing decision as given already commits to the 1861 span with 97.8% data coverage back to that year (not a residual/national-total series), which is affirmative evidence for treating Basilicata's own data as real regional reporting all the way back to unification.

## Open questions

### oq-departures-list-completeness

**Does the country convention's DEPARTURES list omit an implicit 1970 departure shared by all NUTS2 regions?**

Eleven sibling Italian regional entries created under this same pipeline (ITC1, ITC3, ITC4, ITF1, ITF3, ITF4, ITF6, ITG1, ITG2, ITH1, ITH3) were all given start_year 1970, with earlier years routed to the national ITA containers instead of the region. Basilicata (ITF5) is the only one so far assigned the full 1861-2025 span under the default rule, because it is not named in the convention's explicit departures list (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia 1919, Molise 1963). If 1970 is in fact the correct start for ordinary (non-special-statute) Italian regions -- e.g. because Italy's ordinary regional governments were not constituted until 1970-1971 regardless of data continuity -- then the departures list is missing a general 1970 entry and this page's 1861 start is wrong; Basilicata's data may be continuously observed back to 1861 while still statistically belonging to the national total before regional government existed. Resolving this requires checking whether the sibling regions' 1970 truncation was itself a considered decision (i.e., whether their own data also extends pre-1970 with similar 97%+ coverage) or an artifact of the extracts used for them. If the sibling decision is authoritative, this page's span and container should be re-aligned to 1970-2025 with pre-1970 data returned to the ITA national containers.

### oq-gadm-italy-not-in-local-subset

**GADM 4.1 ADM1 polygon for Basilicata cannot be fetched from the local 81-country subset**

The registered source gadm-4.1-adm1 is the correct mechanism for locating an Italian NUTS2/ADM1 boundary (it is already used for other regions in this table), but the local copy of gadm_410-levels.gpkg on disk in this repository only covers 81 countries and Italy is not among them. This is why polygon_status is unassigned rather than assigned or proxy: the source and feature (Basilicata, ISTAT region code 17 / GADM GID_1 likely 'ITA.13_1' or similar) are known in principle but not present in the local file. Someone needs to re-fetch the global (not the 81-country subset) gadm_410-levels.gpkg, locate the Basilicata ADM1 feature, and record its GID_1 as polygon_feature_id before this can move to assigned. Until then no area figure is available for this territory beyond its well-known modern extent (~9,995 km2, Basilicata's current administrative area), which is stated in Territorial extent as background locating information, not as a polygon measurement.
