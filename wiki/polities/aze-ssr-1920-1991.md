# Azerbaijan SSR (1920-1991)

## Summary

The Azerbaijan Soviet Socialist Republic (Azerbaijan SSR) was a
constituent union republic of the USSR from 1920 to 1991, covering
the South Caucasus territory of modern Azerbaijan (~87,000 km²).
The Red Army conquered Azerbaijan on 28 April 1920. From 1922 to
1936 it was subsumed within the Transcaucasian Soviet Federative
Socialist Republic (TSFSR) before becoming a direct constituent
republic of the USSR. It declared independence on 30 August 1991
(re-affirmed by the Constitutional Act of 18 October 1991); the USSR
"officially ceased to exist" on 26 December 1991
[wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §independence-1991](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#independence-1991)
— this page previously said 25 December, which the source does not support.

~86,000 km² measured (87,000 declared), including the Nakhchivan exclave;
Nagorno-Karabakh was an autonomous oblast within the Azerbaijan SSR.

Predecessor: [f228-1920-1921](f228-1920-1921.md). Successor:
[aze-1991-2025](aze-1991-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `cowcode=373`, vintage 1991.
  This is the modern Azerbaijan polygon, used as a proxy for the
  entire 1920-1991 period. Azerbaijan SSR borders were largely
  coterminous with modern Azerbaijan; the main difference is that
  Nagorno-Karabakh was an autonomous oblast within the Azerbaijan
  SSR but is captured in the same polygon footprint.
- **Area.** ~87,000 km². This approximates the 1920-1991 territorial
  extent well; only minor boundary demarcation shifts occurred
  internally within the USSR.
- **Polygon status: proxy.** CShapes 2.0 does not carry sub-republic
  polygons for the Soviet period (it records only the USSR aggregate
  at cowcode=365). The 1991 Azerbaijan CShapes polygon (cowcode=373)
  is used as a period proxy. The polygon_vintage_drift is
  approximately 71 years (1920 to 1991 vintage), but the territorial
  extent is a reasonable approximation because Azerbaijan SSR borders
  remained essentially stable from 1936 onward. The main
  approximation concern is the 1920-1936 TSFSR period, when
  Azerbaijan, Armenia, and Georgia were federated — but the
  individual republic boundaries within the TSFSR were maintained
  throughout, so the polygon remains valid.
- **Direction of approximation.** Slight understatement possible
  for very early years (1920-1922) before Soviet borders fully
  consolidated; slight overstatement possible if Nagorno-Karabakh
  administrative transfers are considered, but these are sub-1%
  variations on ~87,000 km².

## Sourced claims

- **1920-04-28: Soviet conquest.** "The Azerbaijan SSR was established on
  28 April 1920 after the surrender of the government of the Azerbaijan
  Democratic Republic to local Bolsheviks ... and the invasion of the
  Bolshevik 11th Red Army."
  [wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §establishment-1920](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#establishment-1920)
- **1922-03-12: Transcaucasian SFSR.** "On 12 March 1922 the leaders of
  Azerbaijan, Armenian, and Georgian Soviet Socialist Republics established
  a union known as the Transcaucasian Soviet Federal Socialist Republic
  (TSFSR)."
  [wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §tsfsr-1922](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#tsfsr-1922)
- **1936-12-05: Direct union republic.** The TSFSR was dissolved and
  Azerbaijan, Armenia and Georgia "were elevated to union republics
  individually" by the constitution "adopted on 5 December 1936". The
  Azerbaijan SSR article itself dates this only to "December 1936"; the day
  comes from the constitution that effected it.
  [wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §tsfsr-dissolution-1936](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#tsfsr-dissolution-1936)
- **1991: independence — two dates, both real.** The 30 August 1991
  declaration on restoring state independence is the date this page and the
  successor row use; Azerbaijan's own commemorated date is the
  Constitutional Act of 18 October 1991, and the USSR ceased to exist on
  26 December 1991. See [Contradictions](#contradictions) — the row ends in
  1991 on any reading.
  [wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §independence-1991](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#independence-1991)
- **Territory: ~86,000 km², measured.** The attached CShapes polygon
  (cowcode 373, vintage 1991) measures **86,014 km²** in an equal-area
  projection, against the `polygon_area_km2: 87000` declared in
  frontmatter — 1.1% apart, well inside the proxy tolerance. No ingested
  source states an area for the Azerbaijan SSR, so this is the only
  defensible number on the page; the "approximately 86,600 km²" previously
  asserted here (the modern-Azerbaijan figure) had no source behind it.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **The polygon includes both autonomous units.** Nakhichevan "was formed
  on 16 March 1921 and became a part of the Azerbaijan SSR proper on
  9 February 1924"; the Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast was "created on
  July 7, 1923" with an area at formation of "4,161 km2". Both sit inside
  the 1991 footprint, so neither is missing from the ~87,000 km².
  [wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §autonomous-units](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#autonomous-units)

## Contradictions

**Independence date: 30 August vs 18 October 1991.** The re-ingested source
gives both, in different articles: "Azerbaijan declared independence from
the USSR on 30 August 1991" (History of Azerbaijan) against "the Supreme
Council of Azerbaijan adopted a Declaration of Independence on 18 October
1991 ... The country celebrates its Day of Restoration of Independence on
18 October" (Azerbaijan). These are two legal steps, not two accounts of
one: the August declaration restored independence in principle, the October
Constitutional Act enacted it. This page keeps 30 August 1991 in the
narrative because that is what the successor row
[aze-1991-2025](aze-1991-2025.md) was built on, and because the WHEP row
boundary is the year 1991 either way — no data routing depends on the
choice. Resolve it properly if a series ever needs sub-annual attribution.
[wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13 §independence-1991](../sources/wikipedia-azerbaijan-ssr-2026-08-13.md#independence-1991)

**Area 86,600 vs ~87,000 km².** The earlier text asserted "approximately
86,600 km²" against a `polygon_area_km2` of 87,000 and cited a source file
that did not exist. Neither figure is now attributable to an external
source: 86,600 km² is the modern Azerbaijan figure and appears only in
Wikipedia infoboxes, which the plaintext ingest does not capture. The page
now claims only the measured polygon area.
