# Gilbert and Ellice Islands Protectorate

## Summary

The British protectorate over the Gilbert and Ellice Islands, declared in 1892, made the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony in 1916 (GEI-1916-1976). [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §gilbert-and-ellice](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#gilbert-and-ellice)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report the islands for 1909-1915 (`british gilbert and ellice islands`, 4 pairs); GEI-1916-1976 starts at the Crown colony and no row covered the protectorate. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** GADM 4.1 adm0 Tuvalu (the Ellice Islands, 42 km2) plus the parts of adm0 Kiribati inside 169-178 E: the sixteen Gilbert Islands and Banaba (Ocean Island, annexed to the protectorate in 1900). The Line and Phoenix Islands, which GEI-1916-1976's outline (all of KIR + TUV) holds, lie east of 180 and were added to the colony from 1916, so they are left out. GADM's Gilbert outlines include reef flats and measure more than the stated land area. Measured: **404 km2** (ESRI:54034).
