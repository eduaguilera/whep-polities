# Valladolid (province of Spain)

## Summary

Valladolid is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, and since 1983 also a constituent province of the Autonomous Community of Castile and Leon -- though unlike Asturias, Cantabria or Madrid, Castile and Leon is a nine-province community (Avila, Burgos, Leon, Palencia, Salamanca, Segovia, Soria, Valladolid, Zamora), so the province and community boundaries do not coincide at the community level, only at the province level within it. Valladolid sits at the geographic and administrative heart of Castile and Leon: the city of Valladolid is the community's seat of government (its Cortes and Junta), and briefly hosted the Spanish royal court in the 16th and early 17th centuries. This entry is type: subnational because Valladolid is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025, distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated Valladolid separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES418, for over a century and a half.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id ESP-ES418) captures valued statistical rows for unit ES418 ("Valladolid"), country Spain, under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (A Coruna/ES111, Madrid/ES-MD, Asturias/ES120, Cantabria, Lugo, Ourense and Pontevedra), following the system default start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform, applied because Valladolid is not one of the Canary Islands departure units that take a different start rule) and an open end_year of 2025 for still-current units. Previously matched to: nothing specific -- the routing_reasoning states "no existing polity matches Valladolid by name or territory," so the fallback in the absence of a dedicated row would have been the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, which would collapse a province-level series into a country-level one, the same reason rejected for every other Spanish province row created so far. What confirms distinctness: Valladolid's status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES418) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since the 1833 reform is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the six existing ESP subnational rows -- continuous, separately-tabulated provincial statistics distinguish it from the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, which at roughly 505,000 km2 is about 62x the area of Valladolid province (~8,110 km2).

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_feature pointing at mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro (the Spanish INE province numeric code), under which Valladolid's cpro is 47, giving the feature reference recorded here as polygon_feature_id "47." The polygon_reasoning flags that sources.yaml marks mapspain-ign present_locally=False even though seven rows in this repository already derive from it (Asturias, Cantabria, A Coruna, Lugo, Madrid, Ourense, Pontevedra) -- that flag conflicts with the established fact that the source has already been used repeatedly, so it is recorded as a concern to verify rather than treated as a hard blocker, matching the same unresolved discrepancy noted on the six prior Spanish province pages. gadm-4.1-adm1 was checked and returns zero name matches for Valladolid (it models Spain at the 17-autonomous-community level; Castile and Leon is one community covering nine provinces, and adm1 does not carry province-level attribution), so it was correctly ruled out rather than substituted.

Territory description: Valladolid province occupies the northern Spanish plateau (the Duero river basin) at the center of Castile and Leon, bordered by Palencia and Burgos to the north and east, Segovia and Avila to the south, Salamanca and Zamora to the west, and Leon to the northwest. Its capital and largest city, also named Valladolid, sits at the confluence of the Pisuerga and Esgueva rivers. On a modern map the province is the flat, wheat-growing heart of Castile, roughly midway between Madrid and the Galician coast. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 8,110 km2 -- this is the province's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet. Provincial boundaries in Spain have been essentially unchanged since the 1833 reform, so once the IGN provinces layer is confirmed accessible, vintage risk across the full 1833-2025 span should be low, matching the assessment already made for the six sibling province pages.

## Predecessors and successors

Valladolid has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory it now covers was organized under the Antiguo Regimen's own partido/corregimiento structure (Valladolid had been a seat of the Real Chancilleria and, briefly, the royal court, under the historic Kingdom of Castile), distinct in administrative form from the 1833 province even though the city and rough territorial core persisted, and this database does not model sub-kingdom Castilian divisions at that granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to the six existing ESP subnational rows. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain (and, since 1983, a constituent province of the Autonomous Community of Castile and Leon) as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention established for the other Spanish NUTS3 province rows.

## Sourced claims

- INE (Instituto Nacional de Estadistica) and Eurostat both report Valladolid as a NUTS3 statistical unit (ES418) with continuous province-level data collection since the 19th century, consistent with its creation as one of the 50 provinces in the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform.
- The routing decision for unit ESP-ES418 records that Valladolid is one of the original provinces created simultaneously by the 1833 reform, not part of the 1927 Canary Islands split, so its span follows the country default 1833-2025 rather than a departure start year.
- The polygon_reasoning for this decision records that mapspain-ign's id_column is cpro (INE province numeric codes) and that Valladolid's INE province code is 47, while flagging that sources.yaml's present_locally=False for mapspain-ign conflicts with the fact that seven prior rows in this repository already derive from that same source.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-va-abbreviation

**Used the two-letter abbreviation VA rather than the province numeric code, matching the ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern for Spanish provinces already established by ESP-AS, ESP-CA, ESP-CO, ESP-LU, ESP-MD, ESP-OR and ESP-PO-1833-2025 is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short letter abbreviation of the unit name rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES418) or the INE province numeric code (47). This page follows that convention exactly, minting ESP-VA-1833-2025 rather than a numeric-coded alternative, so that the sibling set of Spanish province rows stays internally consistent and any future province entry can be predicted from the same rule without a lookup table. VA was chosen because it is both the modern ISO 3166-2 subdivision code (ES-VA) and the everyday INE/postal abbreviation for the province, reducing ambiguity for a future author matching a new data source against this row. It also avoids collision with VLL, VLD or other longer forms sometimes seen in older gazetteer sources.

## Open questions

### oq-mapspain-ign-present-locally-flag

**mapspain-ign is flagged present_locally=False despite seven existing rows already using it**

The polygon_reasoning for this routing decision explicitly notes that sources.yaml's present_locally flag for mapspain-ign says False, yet seven Spanish province rows (Asturias, Cantabria, A Coruna, Lugo, Madrid, Ourense, Pontevedra) already cite this exact source with cpro id_column matches. Either the flag is stale metadata that should be corrected once the provinces.gpkg file is confirmed on disk, or the seven prior rows were created with a feature reference that was never actually verified against a locally-present file, in which case all eight rows (including this one) share an unverified polygon precondition. This should be resolved by checking whether provinces.gpkg is actually present in the data directory, and if not, whether the prior seven rows' polygon_status should be revisited rather than left as unassigned indefinitely.
