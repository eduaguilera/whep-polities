# Mali

## Summary

Mali is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Mali from 1960 to the present. Mali gained independence on 22 September 1960 after a brief Mali Federation with Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Mali is a landlocked West African country (area 478,767 sq. miles; 1,240,000 sq. km; population 8,750,000 [1994]). Bounded by Algeria, Niger, Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Guinea, Senegal, and Mauritania. Capital: Bamako. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The Malians gained their independence from the French in 1960 after a short-lived federation with the Senegalese called the Mali Federation. Containing only two states with major cultural and historical differences made the federation highly unstable and caused it to collapse (p.374). [biger-1995 §mali](../sources/biger-1995.md#mali)
- Both Mali and Niger were parts of the French possession in western Africa organized in 1895 as French West Africa. Mali was organized as the French colony of French Sudan in 1891, although they gained control of the southern part of today's Mauritania along the Senegal River during the late eighteenth century (p.375).
