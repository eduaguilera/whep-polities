# Senegal (1886-1959)

## Summary

Senegal (1886-1959) covers the French colonial territory of Senegal from 1 January 1886 — the CShapes 2.0 temporal baseline, coinciding with the post-Berlin-Conference colonial partition — to 3 April 1959, when CShapes records the colony ending. This period encompasses Senegal's role within French West Africa (Afrique Occidentale Française, AOF), which France federated from 16 June 1895. CShapes records the entire period as status=colony, owner=FRA (COW 220), with a constant area (~16.43 CShapes units), indicating no territorial boundary changes throughout this 73-year colonial span.

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory bounded by Mauritania (north), Mali (east), Guinea and Guinea-Bissau (south), the Atlantic Ocean (west), and with The Gambia as a near-enclave running along the Gambia River. CShapes 2.0 area ~16.43 (constant throughout the colonial period). Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

Note: Several predecessor entities for portions of this territory exist in the WHEP database (CAY-1800-1886, BND-1800-1887, FTT-1800-1862, WLO-1800-1855, SNE-1800-1887, JOL-1800-1890, SLM-1800-1887), reflecting pre-colonial polities absorbed by French expansion in the 1880s.

## Open questions

### oq-sen-1959-mali-federation-gap

CShapes records the Senegal colony ending 1959-04-03 and independence starting 1960-08-20, leaving a gap of roughly 16 months. This corresponds to the Mali Federation (April 1959 – August 1960), in which Senegal and French Sudan federated. The WHEP database has no row for this transition entity. The SEN-1886-1959 end date follows the CShapes colony end; the SEN-1960-2025 predecessor field in the CSV lists SEN-1886-1959 directly, implying no gap row. Whether a SEN-1959-1960 Mali Federation row is needed, or whether the dates should be adjusted, requires a human decision.
