# Piauí

## Summary

Piauí is a state of Brazil in the country's Northeast Region, a narrow wedge of territory between Maranhão to the west, Ceará, Pernambuco and Bahia to the east and south, and a short Atlantic coastline near Parnaíba to the north. This entry is `type: subnational` because Piauí has never been a sovereign state: it is a constituent unit of Brazil throughout, first as an Imperial province (Piauí was separated from Maranhão as its own captaincy/province in the colonial period, well before this entry's 1889 start) and then as a state of the Republic. The 1889 start_year marks the Proclamation of the Republic, which converted all Imperial provinces into states of the new federation -- the same default-case logic already applied to the other BRA subnational rows (Paraná, Pernambuco, Alagoas, Amazonas, Bahia, Ceará, Espírito Santo, Goiás, Maranhão, Mato Grosso, Minas Gerais, Pará, Paraíba), none of which had an administrative status change later than 1889 that would justify a later start, and Piauí is not among the six documented departure cases (Acre, Amapá, DF, Mato Grosso do Sul, Rondônia, Roraima, Tocantins). The entry is created to receive statistical rows reported at the Piauí-state level rather than the Brazil-national level, which the national BRA rows cannot correctly represent since they are roughly 34x Piauí's territory.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: statistical rows reported at the Piauí-state level for Brazil, per the routing decision's span (1889-2025 by convention; the underlying data extent per the routing reasoning runs from 1900 to 2023, inside this span). Previously matched to: the national BRA rows (BRA-1800-1903, BRA-1903-1909, BRA-1909-2025), which is wrong because those rows cover the whole of Brazil -- roughly 34x Piauí's ~251,529 km² -- so any state-level series routed there is silently aggregated at national scale, both inflating national totals with a component that was already reported separately and losing the state-level resolution the source actually provides. Confirmation the entity is distinct: Piauí is one of Brazil's 26 states plus the Federal District, each an IBGE-recognized administrative and statistical reporting unit with its own state government since the 1889 Republic; it is not a Federico-Tena trading polity (that dataset does not carve up Brazil internally), but IBGE itself -- Brazil's national statistics agency -- publishes state-level series for Piauí as a first-class reporting unit, which is the operative confirmation here, parallel to the geobr-ibge source already used for the other existing BRA-<SUBUNIT> rows.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon feature is attached because the registered source, geobr-ibge (IBGE's Brazilian state boundaries, id_column=abbrev_state, value 'PI' for Piauí), is registered in scripts/sources.yaml but not present locally -- data/geodata/geobr-ibge/states.gpkg does not exist on disk, so no feature can be selected or measured in this pass, per the routing decision's `registered_source_unfetched` route. `polygon_source` is therefore set to the registered slug `geobr-ibge` itself rather than `none`, since the correct source is known and only needs fetching; `polygon_status` is `unassigned` accordingly, and `polygon_feature_id` and `polygon_area_km2` are left blank rather than guessed.

**Territory a reader can locate today:** Piauí sits in Brazil's Northeast Region, wedged between Maranhão to the west and Ceará/Pernambuco/Bahia to the east and south, with a short Atlantic coastline around the delta city of Parnaíba in the north. Its capital is Teresina, on the Parnaíba River which forms most of the state's eastern border. The interior is largely semi-arid sertão and the Serra da Capivara, a UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site, sits in its southeast. The present-day IBGE-mapped state covers approximately 251,529 km², Brazil's 7th-largest state by area -- this figure is IBGE's official published territory statistic, not one measured from any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is attached yet. Once geobr-ibge is fetched this can be cross-checked against the actual feature's measured area.

Piauí's boundaries have been comparatively stable since the colonial era relative to states like Paraná or Acre; no major territorial cession or annexation affecting Piauí post-1889 is documented here, though this has not been independently verified against a historical boundary source (see open question below).

## Sourced claims

- Piauí was elevated from a captaincy subordinate to Maranhão to its own captaincy in 1718, and became a province of the Empire of Brazil at independence in 1822; it was converted into a state of the newly proclaimed Republic on 15 November 1889, the event this entry's start_year encodes.
- IBGE publishes Piauí's present-day territory as approximately 251,529 km², the 7th-largest of Brazil's 26 states, with Teresina as its capital -- the only planned state capital in Brazil not located on the coast.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-piaui

**Followed the dominant BRA subnational code pattern**

Used ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end (BRA-PIAUI-1889-2025) rather than a bespoke code, since Piauí is not a historical territory with an identity separate from its status as a Brazilian federative unit -- it is one of the original Imperial provinces converted to a state at the 1889 Proclamation of the Republic, exactly the default case the routing decision describes (Piauí is not among the six documented departure cases: Acre, Amapá, DF, Mato Grosso do Sul, Rondônia, Roraima, Tocantins). This matches the existing BRA-<SUBUNIT> rows (BRA-PARANA-1889-2025, BRA-ALAGOAS-1889-2025, BRA-BAHIA-1889-2025, BRA-PERNAMBUCO-1889-2025, etc.), all sharing the same 1889 start convention for provinces not among the departure cases.

### d-container-three-edges-piaui

**Split the container into three national-era edges instead of one**

The routing decision's proposed container_code was BRA-1909-2025 alone, but Piauí's proposed start_year is 1889, and the polity table shows the national BRA chain segmented into BRA-1800-1903, BRA-1903-1909, and BRA-1909-2025 for exactly this window. A single container edge of 1909-2025 would leave 1889-1909 -- twenty years, including the entire First Republic founding period -- outside any container span, which the containment gate rejects. Three edges, one per national era, tile 1889-2025 with no gap; Piauí's own province/state boundary is not documented as having moved at either 1903 or 1909, so all three edges share the same practical territory, only the containing national polygon era differs. This mirrors the identical fix already applied on BRA-PARANA-1889-2025.

### oq-boundary-vintage-piaui

**Present-day IBGE boundary stands in for an 1889-2025 span without independent confirmation of stability**

Unlike Paraná (Contestado War cession to Santa Catarina, documented) or Acre (annexed 1903), no specific territorial change affecting Piauí after 1889 is confirmed here -- this entry simply asserts comparative stability without having checked a historical boundary source against the modern IBGE shape. If Piauí's border with Maranhão, Ceará, Pernambuco, or Bahia moved at any point in this span (several 19th/20th century inter-state boundary disputes exist in Brazilian Northeast history, e.g. with Ceará and Pernambuco over disputed frontier areas), the present-day polygon would be a proxy of unknown quality rather than an exact match. Flagged for whoever assigns the polygon once geobr-ibge is fetched.
