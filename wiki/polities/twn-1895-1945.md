# Japanese Taiwan (1895–1945)

Taiwan (Formosa) under Japanese colonial administration, from the Treaty of Shimonoseki (17 April 1895) — by which Qing China ceded the island to Japan — until Japan's surrender and the end of World War II (15 August 1945). Administered as the **Taiwan Sōtokufu** (Taiwan Governor-General's Office) under the Government-General of Taiwan. Capital: Taipei (Taihoku).

This polity covers the 'china taiwan' WHEP data series for 1895–1945. It is a distinct entity from the Japanese Empire sovereign polity (JPN-1895-1945), which covers the full imperial homeland and should not be used as a territorial proxy for Taiwan.

## Territorial extent

Taiwan island plus the Penghu (Pescadores) archipelago, totalling approximately **36,152 km²**. The boundaries remained stable throughout the colonial period.
