# Formosa (Japanese Taiwan)

## Summary

This row tracks Taiwan (Formosa) as a Japanese colony for **50 years**, from the cession of the island by Qing China (Treaty of Shimonoseki, 17 April 1895) to Japanese surrender and ROC retrocession (25 October 1945). Administered as Taiwan Governor-General's Office. Area ~36,000 km² — Taiwan island plus the Pescadores (Penghu). Territorial extent is essentially identical to modern Taiwan (ROC).

The row was created to eliminate the 49-year overlap between the original TWN-1896-2025 and the Japanese colonial period, and to correctly route pre-1945 agricultural data (label "China, Taiwan Province of", iso TWN, years 1896–1944) to this colonial-period polity rather than to the post-WWII ROC state.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 feature cowcode=713, vintage year 1949. The island outline was fixed at Treaty of Shimonoseki (1895) and has not changed in extent — the modern polygon (36,193 km²) faithfully represents the colonial-period territory. No polygon_vintage_drift concern: the island boundary did not move between 1895 and 1949.
- **Area.** ~36,000 km² (Taiwan main island + Pescadores/Penghu). Biger 1995 records 13,900 sq mi [35,987 km²]; "no land boundaries" [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan) (p.487).
- **Relationship to JPN-1895-1945.** Taiwan is a sub-territory of the Japanese Empire. The empire polygon (~658,000 km²) is an aggregate and must NOT absorb TWN data. Per the WHEP sub-territory rule, data labeled "Taiwan" / "Formosa" measures ~36,000 km² and belongs here, not to JPN-1895-1945.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [chn-1800-1895](chn-1800-1895.md) — Qing China held Taiwan until cession at Treaty of Shimonoseki (17 April 1895).
- **Successor:** [twn-1945-2025](twn-1945-2025.md) — Republic of China took administration on 25 October 1945 (Retrocession Day) following Japan's formal surrender (2 September 1945).

## Sourced claims

- **1895-04-17: Treaty of Shimonoseki.** Japan acquired Taiwan and the Pescadores from Qing China. Start-of-row event. [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **1895-1945: Taiwan Governor-General's Office.** Japan administered Taiwan as a colonial possession; Japanese agricultural and trade statistics record Taiwan separately from the Home Islands. [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **1945-08-15: Japan's surrender.** WWII ends; Taiwan transferred from Japanese to ROC administration. [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **1945-10-25: Retrocession Day.** ROC formally assumed control of Taiwan. Start year of successor row TWN-1945-2025. [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **Area: 35,987 km².** Island plus Pescadores; no land boundaries. [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan) (p.487).
