# Territory of Papua and New Guinea (1949-1975)

## Summary

The Territory of Papua and New Guinea was an Australian-administered territory formed in 1949 by the administrative union of the Territory of Papua (an Australian external territory) and the Territory of New Guinea (a UN Trust Territory formerly under League of Nations mandate). Australia governed the combined territory until independence on 16 September 1975.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0, feature ID 910, polygon vintage 1949. The polygon covers the eastern half of New Guinea island plus the surrounding island groups (Bismarck Archipelago, Bougainville, etc.), corresponding to modern Papua New Guinea (~462,840 km²). This is composed of the former Territory of Papua (~224,148 km²) and Territory of New Guinea (~237,536 km²) polygons. Polygon status: assigned. Polygon confidence: high — CShapes 2.0 records this Australian-administered territory explicitly at the 1949 administrative-union date.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data has "Papua New Guinea" for 1958-1960. Previously these 3 rows were unmatched because PNG-1975-2025 starts at independence. This entry covers the Australian-administered UN trust territory period.
