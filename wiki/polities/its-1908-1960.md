# Italian Somaliland (1908-1960)

## Summary

Italian Somaliland was an Italian colonial possession in the Horn of Africa, covering the territory of what would become the southern part of Somalia. The WHEP row ITS-1908-1960 starts in 1908, when Italy purchased the remaining Benadir ports and concessions from the Sultan of Zanzibar/Oman, consolidating full administrative control; it ends in 1960 when Italian Somaliland merged with British Somaliland on 1 July 1960 to form the independent Somali Republic. "The independent Somali Democratic Republic was formed in 1958 [de facto 1960 when British and Italian Somaliland merged]" [biger-1995 §somalia](../sources/biger-1995.md#somalia). CShapes records Italian Somaliland (cowcode 5200) across multiple time-steps from 1889 to 1960, with a territorial expansion in 1924 (Jubaland/Oltregiuba transfer from British Kenya) and a status change to occupied territory during British military administration (1941–1950), followed by UN trusteeship (1950–1960). The 1908 WHEP start date reflects the completion of Italian direct control rather than CShapes administrative categories.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source: `constructed` — CShapes 2.0 gwcode 520 @1960 minus gwcode 521 (British Somaliland), measuring 464,743 km². See [Polygon binding: composed difference (2026-07-28)](#polygon-binding-composed-difference-2026-07-28) below. Biger's Somalia area of 246,201 sq. miles (637,414 sq. km.) refers to the post-1960 republic, i.e. both Somalilands; capital: Mogadishu [biger-1995 §somalia](../sources/biger-1995.md#somalia).

CShapes records Italian Somaliland (cowcode 5200) with the following time-steps:

- 1889-11-19 to 1924-06-30: status=colony, owner=Italy (325), area ~30.26 sq deg
- 1924-07-01 to 1941-03-19: status=colony, owner=Italy (325), area ~37.94 sq deg (Jubaland added)
- 1941-03-20 to 1950-03-31: status=occupied, owner=United Kingdom (200), area ~37.94 sq deg
- 1950-04-01 to 1960-06-30: status=mandate, owner=Italy (325), area ~37.94 sq deg

[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

The area increase in 1924 reflects the transfer of Jubaland (Oltre Giuba / Trans-Juba) from British Kenya to Italy under the Treaty of London (1924). The occupied and mandate phases do not alter territory. The WHEP row uses the expanded post-1924 extent (~464,743 km²), which persists to independence.

**Polygon decision (2026-07-01, superseded by the 2026-07-28 section below):** CShapes 2.0's dependencies distribution has six features for cowcode 5200 (FIDs 779–784). The post-1924 extent (start 1924-07-01, b_def=1, ~464,743 km²) is the target territory for ITS-1908-1960: it is the earliest definitive-border extent capturing the full post-Jubaland colony, and it covers the dominant period of the 103 data rows (1924–1959). The occupation/mandate features have identical geometry but a different sovereign (UK/UN). The pre-1924 features (vintage 1889–1924, ~370,338 km²) understate territory by the Jubaland margin (~94,000 km²). Note that FIDs are row indices in a distribution that is not a registered polygon source, so they were never resolvable binding keys — the 2026-07-28 composed difference reproduces the same geometry from the sovereign-state distribution instead.

**Approximation note:** Data rows for 1920–1924 (pre-Jubaland transfer) are matched to the post-1924 polygon, which overstates territory by ~94,000 km² (~20%) relative to the actual pre-1924 extent. This approximation is documented here; a row split at 1924 (oq-its-1924-split) would resolve it exactly.

The Kenya-Somalia boundary was established in 1891 by an Anglo-Italian treaty delimiting respective spheres of influence (p.337–338) [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya).

## Contradictions

The CShapes start for Italian Somaliland is 1889-11-19, but the WHEP row starts in 1908. The CSV has separate rows ISM-1889-1924 and ISM-1924-1960 (with COW=325) alongside ITS-1908-1960 (with COW=NA). These rows appear to overlap in time (ISM-1889-1924 and ITS-1908-1960 both cover 1908–1924; ISM-1924-1960 and ITS-1908-1960 both cover 1924–1960). This is a potential duplication that requires audit — see [oq-its-predecessor-chain](#oq-its-predecessor-chain).

Biger gives the formation date of the Somali Democratic Republic as 1958 (p.471), with an editorial note in the biger-1995 source file that the de facto date is 1960 (merger of the two Somalilands). The CSV end year 1960 follows the de facto territorial merger, which is consistent with the WHEP polity-definition rule (territorial change drives row boundaries).

## Open questions

### oq-its-predecessor-chain

The CSV contains both ITS-1908-1960 (COW=NA) and ISM-1889-1924 / ISM-1924-1960 (COW=325, both with predecessor=NA and no successor field linking to SOM-1960-2025). These three rows appear to cover overlapping territory and time ranges. Either ITS-1908-1960 is the correct single row (replacing both ISM rows) or ISM rows are the correct representation and ITS is a duplicate. The ITS predecessor field is NA, leaving the 1889–1908 period unaccounted. A chain audit is needed: if ITS-1908-1960 is the canonical row, ISM-1889-1924 and ISM-1924-1960 may need to be reviewed or their relationship clarified.

### oq-its-1924-split

CShapes records a real area change at 1924-07-01 (Jubaland transfer, ~30.26 → ~37.94 sq deg). Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, a genuine territorial change of this magnitude (roughly 25% area increase) should trigger a row split. The current row ITS-1908-1960 spans this event without a split. A split into ITS-1908-1924 and ITS-1924-1960 may be warranted; the ISM rows in the CSV are already split at 1924, which is consistent with the CShapes territorial change. This is a candidate CSV correction.

### oq-its-1941-british-occupation

CShapes records Italian Somaliland as "occupied" (owner=UK) from 1941-03-20 to 1950-03-31 (British East Africa Campaign conquest), then as "mandate" (owner=Italy) from 1950-04-01 (UN Trust Territory of Somalia). The WHEP row ITS-1908-1960 spans these status changes without a split. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, no territorial change occurred, so no split is required — but the occupied and mandate phases are historically distinct and may warrant notation.

## Polygon binding: composed difference (2026-07-28)

**Current state:** `polygon_source: constructed`, `polygon_feature_id:
ITS-1908-1960`, `polygon_status: assigned`, **464,743 km²** (measured from the
built geometry in ESRI:54034).

The polygon is built by `build_its_1908_1960()` in
[scripts/sources/constructed/build.py](../../scripts/sources/constructed/build.py)
as a **difference**:

```
CShapes 2.0 gwcode 520 @1960 (Somalia, 636,242 km²)
  MINUS gwcode 521 @1960 (British Somaliland, 171,499 km²)
  =                        464,743 km²
```

### Why the subtraction, and why CShapes cannot supply the territory directly

The registered `cshapes-2.0` source is the **sovereign-state** distribution
(keyed on Gleditsch-Ward codes). Its only Somali feature is gwcode 520, which
starts on **1960-07-01** — so it is the whole of *independent Somalia*, i.e.
the former Italian Somaliland **plus** British Somaliland, which united on that
date. There is no pre-1960 Italian Somaliland feature in it.

British Somaliland, however, *is* a separate G-W code (521, protectorate
1886–1960) and its polygon is entirely contained in 520 — the intersection
equals 521 exactly, to the square metre. So the difference is not an
approximation of the colonial boundary; it *is* the colonial boundary, recovered
by removing the British share.

**Verification.** The composed geometry is bit-identical — symmetric difference
**0.0 km²** — to CShapes 2.0's own `Italian Somaliland` dependency feature
(cowcode 5200, time-steps from 1924-07-01 onward, 464,743 km²), which exists
only in the dependencies=TRUE distribution and is therefore not reachable
through a registered polygon source. That exact match is why the status is
`assigned` rather than `estimate`: the geometry is a sourced colonial boundary
reconstructed losslessly, not an inference.

**External check.** Britannica gives Italian Somaliland as 178,218 sq. miles
(**461,585 km²**); the measured 464,743 km² is within **0.7%** of that. The
~500,000 km² figure sometimes quoted belongs to the enlarged **Somalia
Governorate** of Italian East Africa (1936–1941), which annexed the Ogaden from
conquered Ethiopia — a wartime extent this row does not represent. Biger's
637,414 km² for "Somalia" is the post-union republic, not the colony.

**Residual approximation (unchanged, and the reason for oq-its-1924-split).**
464,743 km² is the **post-1924** extent, after the Jubaland / Oltre Giuba
transfer from British Kenya (Treaty of London, 1924). The 1889–1924 extent was
**370,338 km²**. This row spans 1908–1960, so the polygon overstates its first
16 years by ~94,400 km² (~20%). The row's 103 layer-B data rows run 1924–1959,
so the dominant period is exact; a split at 1924 would close the remainder.
