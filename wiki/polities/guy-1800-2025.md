# Guyana (to 1886)

## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Guyana (formerly British Guiana), a territory on the northern coast of South America. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. "The first European settlers arrived in northern South America from the Netherlands in 1581" (p.261). "Between 1781 and 1814 the colonies of Berbice, Demerara and Essequibo were ruled alternately by the Dutch and British" (p.261). "The British purchased the colonies from Holland and in 1831 the three colonies were united into the colony of British Guiana" (p.261) [biger-1995 &sect;guyana](../sources/biger-1995.md#guyana).

## Territorial extent

The Guyana-Venezuela boundary runs for 462 miles (743 km.) from the Atlantic coast to the tripoint with Brazil on Mount Roraima [biger-1995 &sect;guyana](../sources/biger-1995.md#guyana). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected).
