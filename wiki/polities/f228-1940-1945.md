# USSR (1940-1945)

## Summary

The CSV labels this row "USSR (1940-1945)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Unlike the
preceding F228 rows from 1905-1922, **this label is correct** —
the USSR existed from 30 December 1922 onward. However, this
page is part of the F228 chain whose earlier rows are
mislabeled; see
[log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism)
for the full audit.

This row covers **6 years** of the WWII period. Germany invaded
the USSR on 22 June 1941 (Operation Barbarossa). The Soviet
Union suffered catastrophic losses before turning the tide at
Stalingrad (1942-1943) and advancing westward to Berlin by May
1945. The Yalta and Potsdam conferences shaped the postwar
order. By 1945 the USSR had reached its **maximum territorial
extent**, gaining Karelia from Finland, Konigsberg/Kaliningrad
from Germany, the Kuril Islands and South Sakhalin from Japan,
and establishing a sphere of influence over all of Eastern
Europe.

Predecessor per CSV: [f228-1921-1940](f228-1921-1940.md).
Successor per CSV: [f228-1945-1991](f228-1945-1991.md).

## Territorial extent

- **1940 start: post-annexation USSR.** The row begins with the
  USSR at its expanded 1940 borders — including the Baltic
  states, Bessarabia, Northern Bukovina, and the Karelian
  Isthmus (gained in the Winter War).
- **1941-1944: German occupation.** Germany occupied vast
  territories including Ukraine, Belarus, the Baltic states,
  and western Russia to the gates of Moscow, Leningrad, and
  Stalingrad. The USSR recovered all occupied territory by
  late 1944.
- **1944-1945: Soviet territorial gains.**
  - **Karelia:** Confirmed Soviet after the Moscow Armistice
    with Finland (19 September 1944). Petsamo also ceded.
  - **Konigsberg/Kaliningrad:** Taken from Germany; formally
    transferred at Potsdam (August 1945).
  - **Kuril Islands and South Sakhalin:** Taken from Japan
    (August-September 1945). South Sakhalin had been lost to
    Japan in 1905 (Treaty of Portsmouth) — this was a reversal
    40 years later.
  - **Carpathian Ruthenia (Transcarpathia):** Ceded by
    Czechoslovakia to the USSR (1945).
- By 1945 the USSR was at its **maximum territorial extent**.

## Sourced claims

- **1941-06-22: Operation Barbarossa.** Germany invaded the USSR
  with approximately 3.8 million troops. The largest military
  invasion in history
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1942-1943: Battle of Stalingrad.** Turning point of WWII
  on the Eastern Front. German Sixth Army encircled and
  destroyed
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1945-02: Yalta Conference.** Roosevelt, Churchill, and
  Stalin agreed on postwar spheres of influence and the
  structure of the United Nations
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1945-07/08: Potsdam Conference.** Formalized the postwar
  settlement including German partition and Soviet gains in
  East Prussia
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1945-08/09: Soviet invasion of Manchuria and Kuril
  Islands.** USSR declared war on Japan (8 August 1945),
  seizing the Kuril Islands and South Sakhalin
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
