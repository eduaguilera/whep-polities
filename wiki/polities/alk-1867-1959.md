# Territory of Alaska

## Summary

This row tracks **Alaska** from the **Alaska Purchase (1867-10-18)**,
when the United States acquired the territory from the Russian Empire
for $7.2 million, to **Alaskan statehood (1959-01-03)**, when Alaska
was admitted as the 49th state
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). During 1867–1884
Alaska had no formal civilian government; it was administered as a
customs district and then a military district. The Organic Act of 1884
established a civil government. Full territorial status (with an
elected legislature) came with the Second Organic Act of 1912. Alaska
remained a non-contiguous US territory until statehood, which was
granted by the Alaska Statehood Act signed 1958-07-07 with statehood
effective 1959-01-03.

Alaska occupies the northwestern corner of North America, bordering
Canada's Yukon Territory and British Columbia. Its land area of
approximately 1,518,800 km2 makes it by far the largest US state/
territory (roughly 2.4 times the size of Texas). The territory was
sparsely populated throughout this period; the 1950 census counted
approximately 128,000 inhabitants. Economic activity centred on fur
trapping, fishing (especially salmon canning), gold mining, and, from
the 1940s onward, military installations and oil exploration.

This polity is created to absorb FAO (1952) agricultural data
labelled "Alaska" for 1937–1951 (cattle, milk, irrigation, tractors,
horses). Routing this sub-territory data to the parent polity
USA-1867-1959 (~9.4M km2) would violate WHEP sub-territory rules and
risk double-counting with US-aggregate data. The parallel entry for
Territory of Hawaii (HAWI-1898-1959) confirms the pattern for US
territorial sub-units.

No COW system membership exists for Alaska as a separate entity
(COW treats it as part of the United States, ccode=2); the `cow`
field is left null.

Predecessor: [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) (Russian Empire, which held Alaska as Russian America until 1867).
Successor: [usa-1959-2025](usa-1959-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `GADM level-1` (Global Administrative Areas),
  feature `USA.2_1` (Alaska state boundary), sourced from the
  GADM 4.1 release (2022), polygon_feature_year 2020
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **Method.** `constructed_estimate` (modern_proxy). CShapes 2.0
  does not include sub-national US territories; no exact historical
  GIS source for Alaska's territorial boundary has been identified in
  CShapes, Cliopatria, CHGIS, or Paine. The modern Alaska state
  boundary (GADM level-1) closely approximates the territorial
  boundary because Alaska's external land borders with Canada were
  settled by the Alaska Boundary Tribunal of 1903 and have not
  changed since. The coastal boundary (international waters) and
  the Canada boundary are identical between the 1903 ruling and the
  present day.
- **Area.** ~1,518,800 km2 (GADM projected). This is consistent
  with the widely cited land area of Alaska (~1,518,800 km2). The
  parent polity USA-1867-1959 covers ~9.4M km2; Alaska represents
  approximately 16% of that total, confirming the sub-territory
  relationship.
- **Confidence.** Medium. The modern GADM polygon is an ESTIMATE
  because it is not a dedicated historical GIS source. However,
  the territorial borders have been stable since 1903 and the
  approximation error is minimal (the pre-1903 boundary dispute
  affected only a narrow coastal strip in the southeast panhandle).
- **Polygon approximation note.** The Alaska Boundary Tribunal
  (October 1903) resolved the disputed Alaskan Panhandle boundary
  with Canada. The GADM polygon reflects the post-1903 boundary.
  For data years 1867–1903 (outside the FAO 1952 coverage window),
  the polygon slightly overstates the southeastern panhandle extent;
  the area effect is small (the disputed strip was a few thousand
  km2 at most, <0.5% of total territory).
- **Steps not required.** Step 1 (exact_historical via CShapes/
  Cliopatria): no feature found for Alaska as a sub-national US
  territory. Step 2 (composed_union): no relevant component
  polygons identified that would improve on the GADM boundary.
  Step 3 (period_proxy): no adjacent-period historical polygon.
  Step 4 (constructed_estimate/modern proxy): applied — GADM
  level-1 Alaska state boundary used.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) — the
  Russian Empire (1856–1905), which administered Alaska as Russian
  America (Русская Америка). The Alaska Purchase (Treaty of Cession,
  1867-03-30, ratified 1867-10-18) transferred sovereignty to the
  United States for $7.2 million (~2 cents per acre). No separate
  WHEP polity for "Russian America" exists; the predecessor is the
  Russian Empire row that spans the sale year.
- **Successor.** [usa-1959-2025](usa-1959-2025.md) — upon
  admission to statehood on 1959-01-03, Alaska's territory was
  absorbed into the United States. Subsequent Alaska data are
  covered under USA-1959-2025 (with the understanding that state-
  level data would need a future ALK-1959-2025 row if required).

## Decisions

- **Polity code.** `ALK-1867-1959` chosen. ALK is not an ISO 3166
  alpha-3 code (Alaska is sub-national); it is a WHEP-internal
  code following the pattern of HAWI-1898-1959 for Hawaii. ISO
  3166-2 uses `US-AK` for the state; WHEP uses a standalone code
  for the territory period.
- **Polygon selection.** GADM level-1 Alaska state boundary used as
  a constructed_estimate. This is the only readily available GIS
  polygon covering Alaska's full extent. CShapes 2.0 covers
  sovereign states and colonial dependencies; Alaska as a US
  territory is not a separate CShapes entry. Cliopatria does not
  include US sub-national territories. The modern-borders
  approximation is documented and flagged; the error is minimal
  given border stability since 1903.
- **iso3.** Assigned `USA` because Alaska was a US territory
  throughout this period. No separate ISO 3166 code exists for
  historical Alaska.
- **continent.** Assigned `North America` (Alaska is geographically
  part of the North American continent, unlike Hawaii which is
  assigned `Oceania`).
- **cow.** Left null. COW assigns Alaska to the United States
  (ccode=2); there is no separate COW entry for the Territory of
  Alaska. This is consistent with CShapes' treatment.
- **Sub-territory rule.** This polity is created specifically to
  prevent sub-territory data from being routed to the parent polity
  USA-1867-1959. Alaska agricultural data (~1.5M km2) must not be
  merged with US-aggregate data (~9.4M km2) which would inflate
  the per-km2 statistics and risk double-counting.

## Open questions

### oq-russian-america-predecessor

The predecessor is set to [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md)
(Russian Empire 1856-1905), which is the closest existing WHEP row
covering the period when Alaska was Russian America. A dedicated
"Russian America" polity (covering only the North American territory)
does not exist in the database. If one is created in the future,
the predecessor field here should be updated.

### oq-gadm-polygon-validation

The GADM level-1 Alaska polygon has not been validated against a
historical map of the 1867–1903 territorial boundary. The pre-1903
panhandle boundary differed from the post-1903 line; the error is
estimated at <0.5% of total area. A future improvement would be to
use a digitized historical map of the 1867 territorial boundary,
but this is not required for the FAO 1952 data (1937–1951 period
falls fully within the post-1903 stable boundary).

### oq-post-1959-alaska-data

FAO and other sources may report Alaska data after 1959 statehood,
labelled "Alaska" rather than "USA". A future ALK-1959-2025 polity
(or routing alias to USA-1959-2025) would be needed for that period.
Out of scope for this issue.
