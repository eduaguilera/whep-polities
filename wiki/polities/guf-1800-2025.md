# French Guiana

## Summary

This row tracks French Guiana as a territorial-economic unit from the
1800 WHEP database floor to the 2025 ceiling. A French colony through
1946 and an overseas department (département d'outre-mer) since, it has
remained under continuous French administration with a stable coastal
extent; the disputed inland border with Brazil was settled by the 1900
Swiss arbitration, well before FAOSTAT-era data.

## Territorial extent

~83,534 km2 official; the GADM 4.1 adm0 polygon measures 83,670 km2.
Stable over the data-bearing period.

## Predecessors and successors

None modelled. The 1946 colony-to-department transition did not alter the
extent.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **69 "French Guiana"** reports 1961-2006 (8,934
data rows observed; FAOSTAT folds the French overseas departments into
France from 2007). Routed here by `pipelines/faostat-era-matching` via
iso3 GUF. Data from 2007 onward arrives under FAOSTAT area 68 "France".

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `GUF` (modern extent; the 1900
  arbitration predates all data years).
- **Single period:** created to close the FAOSTAT-era gap (area 69
  previously had no polity family).

## Open questions

### oq-post-2006-france-attribution

FAOSTAT merges GUF into France from 2007; whether WHEP should re-split
French overseas departments out of the France reporting area is a
data-side question, not a polity question.
