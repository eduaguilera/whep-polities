# Bhutan

## Summary

Bhutan as tracked by WHEP from 1800. A Himalayan kingdom bounded by India and China (Tibet), Bhutan maintained its independence while accepting British guidance in foreign affairs from 1865 (Treaty of Sinchula) until full sovereignty was recognized by India in 1949.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. India is bounded by Bhutan for 376 miles (605 km). Bhutan also shares a boundary with China to the north.
