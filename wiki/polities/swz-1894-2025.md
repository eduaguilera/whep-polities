# Eswatini (1894-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Swaziland (now Eswatini) from the 1894 convention giving the South African Republic powers of protection and administration, through British administration from 1902, independence on 6 September 1968, and on to 2025. CShapes records three sub-periods (1895-1902 under SAR/Transvaal, 1902-1968 under Britain, 1968- independent) with identical area (1.55 degree-squared units); they are merged into this one row because none of them is a territorial change.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature 572 at its 1902 vintage.
- **Area.** ~17,364 sq km. CShapes area = 1.55 throughout, colonial period and after — which is why the ruler changes do not split the row.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** SWK-1800-1894 (Swazi Kingdom), which lists this row as its successor. CShapes itself starts at 1895; the row starts at the 1894 convention. This page's `predecessor:` frontmatter is empty, so the link is one-sided — a chain-symmetry cleanup, not part of issue #25, and left alone here.
- **Successor:** No successor row; this row runs to the present.

A split at 1968 was once planned and never applied: independence changed the
ruler, not the boundary, and CShapes gives the same 1.55 area on both sides of it
(2026-08-17, issue #25).
