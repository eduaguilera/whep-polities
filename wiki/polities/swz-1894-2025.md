## Summary

This row covers colonial Swaziland (now Eswatini) from 1895 (CShapes start, shortly after the 1894 convention giving the South African Republic control) to 6 September 1968 (independence). CShapes records two sub-periods (1895-1902 under SAR/Transvaal, 1902-1968 under Britain) with identical area (1.55 degree-squared units), merged here because there is no territorial change.
