# Malawi

## Summary

Malawi is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Malawi (formerly Nyasaland) from 1964 to the present. Nyasaland became independent as Malawi on 6 July 1964.

## Territorial extent

Malawi is a landlocked east African country (area 45,747 sq. miles; 118,484 sq. km; population 9,700,000 [1994]). Bounded by Tanzania (295 miles; 475 km) in the north, Mozambique (975 miles; 1,569 km) in the east, south and southwest, and Zambia (520 miles; 837 km) in the west. Capital: Lilongwe. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Malawi, which was a protectorate of the British, became independent in 1964 under the leadership of Dr. Hastings Kamuzu Banda" (p.369). Banda's regime is known as one of the most conservative governments in Africa. [biger-1995 §malawi](../sources/biger-1995.md#malawi)
- The Malawi-Mozambique boundary "is delimited in accordance with the Anglo-Portuguese treaty of 11 June 1891. It extends from the Tanzanian boundary tripoint with Zambia" (p.369). The boundary is demarcated throughout by beacons or natural features such as rivers, lakes, and drainage divides along 975 miles (1,569 km). [biger-1995 §malawi](../sources/biger-1995.md#malawi)

## Open questions

### oq-lake-malawi-boundary

Lake Malawi boundary dispute. Tanzania claims that the boundary in Lake Nyasa (Lake Malawi) should follow the median line, while Malawi claims the entire lake based on the Anglo-German agreement of 1890.
