# Emilia-Romagna (region of Italy)

## Summary

Emilia-Romagna is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni), spanning north-central Italy from the Po valley south to the Apennines and the Adriatic coast, bordering Lombardy and Veneto to the north, Tuscany and Marche to the south, and the Adriatic Sea to the east, with its capital at Bologna. Unlike Veneto or Trentino-Alto Adige, Emilia-Romagna's territory was part of the Kingdom of Italy from unification (the Papal Legations of Bologna, Ferrara, Forlì and Ravenna, and the Duchies of Modena and Parma, were annexed in 1859-1860, before the Kingdom's 1861 proclamation) — its late start year is not an annexation question but an administrative-implementation one. The Italian Constitution of 1948 established ordinary-statute regions on paper, but they were not actually constituted with elected councils and functioning administrations until 1970, so Emilia-Romagna has no distinct regional-level statistical identity before that year even though its territory had long been settled within Italy. This entry is `type: subnational`, not national: Italy held sovereignty throughout the full 1861-2025 span, and this row exists only to carry a reporting territory distinct from the national ITA chain from 1970 onward, matching the convention used for the other Italian NUTS2 regions already created in this pipeline (Veneto ITA-VEN, Bolzano/South Tyrol ITA-ITH1, Trentino ITA-ITH4).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit ITA-ITH5 (admin code ITH5, Eurostat NUTS2 code for Emilia-Romagna) from the country-convention pipeline's Italian subnational routing pass (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json, pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv), with a proposed span of 1970-2025 and container ITA-1919-2025. Before this row existed, any regional-level data for this reporting unit (population, agricultural production, or other NUTS2-scale statistics collected by ISTAT or Eurostat) had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows, which would either double-count against sibling regions if summed, or misattribute a regional figure (Emilia-Romagna is roughly 1/13 of Italy's area) to the whole country. The routing decision applies the country convention's default start rule (regional council elections operative from 1970) with no departure, unlike Veneto (1866, annexation-driven) or Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia (1919, also annexation-driven) — Emilia-Romagna's territory was already Italian well before 1861, so its late start reflects only when regional-level administration and statistics began, not when the territory itself joined Italy. What confirms Emilia-Romagna is a genuinely distinct reporting/administrative unit rather than a restatement of national Italy: it is Eurostat's own NUTS2 statistical division (ITH5) and a constitutionally-established Italian region with its own elected council and administration since 1970, the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling NUTS2 regions created in this pipeline (Veneto, Bolzano/South Tyrol, Trentino).

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-emr

**Code uses ITA-EMR, following the dominant subunit pattern**

The routing decision names this unit only by its Eurostat NUTS2 admin code ITH5, a statistical classification that has been reassigned across NUTS revisions and is not self-explanatory to a wiki reader. Following the dominant 57-of-66 <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern already applied to sibling Italian NUTS2 regions (ITA-VEN for Veneto, ITA-ITH1 for Bolzano/South Tyrol, ITA-ITH4 for Trentino), the subunit token is set to EMR, a readable abbreviation of Emilia-Romagna's own name, giving ITA-EMR-1970-2025. This is not a bespoke-code case: Emilia-Romagna is a real, still-existing administrative region with a direct present-day successor (itself), unlike the dissolved-polity cases (Alaska Territory, Hyderabad State) that motivate the bespoke exception. It departs from the sibling precedent only in abbreviation choice (EMR rather than reusing the NUTS digit ITH5), for readability.

### d-single-container-edge-1970

**Single container edge (ITA-1919-2025) is sufficient; no fine-grained pre-1919 edges needed**

Unlike Veneto (annexed 1866, requiring three fine-grained ITA edges from 1866 to tile the whole span) or other regions whose start predates 1919, Emilia-Romagna's own span starts at 1970, which falls entirely inside the single national container ITA-1919-2025 (1919-2025). No earlier ITA era (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919) needs to be cited as a container edge, because this entry makes no claim about the region before 1970: the routing decision's default country-convention start rule (1970, the year of the first regional council elections under the ordinary-statute regime) applies with no departure, unlike Veneto's or Trentino-Alto Adige's annexation-driven exceptions. One edge, ITA-1919-2025, tiles the whole 1970-2025 span with no gap.

### d-polygon-source-none-not-new-source-needed

**polygon_source set to none, not to the rejected route names**

The routing decision's polygon_route is new_source_needed and its polygon_source field was left blank/rejected by validate_declared_sources when a route name or placeholder was substituted for a real slug. Per the harness's own instruction, new_source_needed does not correspond to any registered slug today (the routing_reasoning explains that gadm-4.1-adm1's local extract is a curated 81-country subset with 0 ITA features, and no other registered source carries Italian sub-national boundaries), so polygon_source is set to the literal none and polygon_status to unassigned, with the ISTAT Confini amministrativi / full global GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer as the candidate future source recorded in the open questions instead of being fabricated as a value in this field.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-italy-coverage

**Local GADM 4.1 admin-1 extract excludes Italy entirely, same gap as sibling regions**

The repository's local gadm-4.1-adm1 dataset is a curated 81-country subset that does not include Italy at all (0 ITA features), even though GADM's global release does digitize Italian regions including Emilia-Romagna. Until that local extract is replaced with full/global GADM admin-1 coverage, or ISTAT's own Confini amministrativi region boundaries are fetched and registered as a source, no polygon_feature_id can be assigned here and polygon_status must remain unassigned. This same gap already blocks Veneto, Trentino-Alto Adige, and Bolzano/South Tyrol's entries, so it is likely worth fixing once at the source level rather than per-region; whether it is fixed via a global GADM re-fetch or an ISTAT-specific registered source is unresolved.

### oq-pre1970-data-treatment

**Whether any pre-1970 Emilia-Romagna-labelled data exists and how it should be treated**

This entry's span starts at 1970 per the country convention's default rule for ordinary-statute regions (first regional council elections). It is not yet confirmed whether the underlying juan-subnational dataset (or any other source) contains rows labelled for this territory before 1970 that would need a different treatment (back_cast onto the same physical territory under the earlier provincial/prefectural administration, or routing to the national ITA chain instead). This should be checked against the actual routed dataset for unit ITA-ITH5 before assuming 1970 is a clean lower bound with no pre-existing data to reconcile.

### oq-emr-boundary-stability

**Whether Emilia-Romagna's provincial boundaries within the region shifted after 1970 in ways affecting a future polygon proxy**

Emilia-Romagna's outer regional boundary is assumed stable since the region's 1970 creation, matching the constitutional NUTS2 unit, but internal provincial reorganizations (e.g., the creation of the province of Rimini in 1992, split from Forlì) are known to have occurred within this period. Whether any of these internal changes altered the region's own outer boundary (as opposed to only internal provincial lines) has not been checked, and matters for whether a single present-day regional polygon, once fetched, would be a safe proxy for the full 1970-2025 span or would need a vintage caveat for an earlier sub-period.
