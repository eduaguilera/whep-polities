# Basutoland (1868-1886)

## Summary

Basutoland became a British protectorate on 12 March 1868 after King Moshoeshoe I requested British protection from encroaching Boer settlers and the Orange Free State. From 1871 to 1884, Basutoland was annexed to the Cape Colony, but following the Gun War (1880-1881) it was restored to direct British control as a Crown Colony in 1884. The period ends in 1886 when administrative arrangements were formalised.
