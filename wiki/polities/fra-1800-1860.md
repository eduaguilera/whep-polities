# France (before Savoy and Nice, to 1860)

## Summary

This row tracks France as a territorial-economic unit from **1800**
(the WHEP database floor) to **1860** (Treaty of Turin). The window spans
the Consulate and First Empire, the Bourbon Restoration, the July
Monarchy, the Second Republic and the first eight years of the Second
Empire. Its territory is post-1815 France **with** Alsace-Lorraine and
**without** Savoy and the County of Nice, which were Sardinian until 1860.

- **1800 start** is the database floor, not a territorial event.
  At 1800 France was the French Republic (Consulate period under
  Napoleon Bonaparte).
- **1860 end** is a real territorial event: by the Treaty of Turin
  (24 March 1860) the Kingdom of Sardinia ceded Savoy and Nice to France
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy)
  [wikipedia-france-2026-04-12 §1860-nice-savoy](../sources/wikipedia-france-2026-04-12.md#1860-nice-savoy).
  Measured on CShapes-Europe, France grows by **13,631 km² (+2.55%)**
  at that step, which is the same order as the 1871 loss of
  Alsace-Lorraine that ends the next row.

**Why this entry exists.** Until 2026-09-25 one row, `FRA-1800-1871`, ran
from 1800 to 1871 on the 1816 polygon, so the 166 layer-B rows for
1860-1870 (juan and mitchell `france`) were measured against a territory
13,631 km² smaller than the one they report, and the three départements
created in 1860 (Savoie, Haute-Savoie, Alpes-Maritimes) sat in a container
whose polygon did not contain them. That row was split at 1860 into this
row and [fra-1860-1871](fra-1860-1871.md). This row keeps the 1816 polygon,
which is exactly the pre-1860 territory, and receives the 147 layer-B rows
for 1850-1859.

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [fra-1860-1871](fra-1860-1871.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes-Europe` feature 220, the step dated
  1816-1860 (polygon_status=assigned). This is the polygon the former
  `FRA-1800-1871` row carried; after the split it is bound only to the
  years it was drawn for.
- **What the polygon contains, measured.** Areas are planar in the
  equal-area projection `ESRI:54034`, against GADM 4.1 départements and
  arrondissements:
  - Haute-Savoie: **0** of 4,881 km² inside the polygon.
  - Savoie: **437** of 6,307 km² inside, in two slivers of 228 and 182
    km² along the Rhône and Guiers. The 1815-1860 border followed those
    rivers, which are also the modern Savoie-Ain-Isère boundary, so the
    slivers are CShapes-Europe's generalised line, not territory.
  - Arrondissement of Nice (the former County of Nice): **623** of 3,078
    km² inside, the same generalisation along the Var.
  - Arrondissement of Grasse: 920 of 1,242 km² inside. Grasse belonged
    to Var until 1860 and was French before the annexation, so it is
    correctly in this polygon.
  - Alsace-Lorraine is inside, which is what separates the 1800-1871
    rows from [fra-1871-1919](fra-1871-1919.md).
- **Area.** `polygon_area_km2` 532,812 is CShapes-Europe's own stated
  `Area` for this step; the published polygon measures 533,999 km²
  (+0.22%) and the source step 534,004 km². The CShapes figure is declared, as for the CAP split, rather
  than a number read off the geometry.
- **Who holds Savoy and Nice before 1860.** The Kingdom of Sardinia,
  [sar-1800-1860](sar-1800-1860.md). Its Cliopatria 1815 polygon holds
  12,804 of the 13,631 km² that France gains in 1860 (93.9%); the 827
  km² it misses are edge generalisation in four pieces of 105-359 km².
  That polygon also reaches about 6,046 km² into this row's published territory
  (Dauphiné and upper Provence, lon 5.5-7.2 E); that is a Cliopatria
  boundary error inherited from before this split and is not changed by
  it.
- **Napoleonic years.** The 1816 polygon is post-Congress-of-Vienna
  France. It does not represent the First Empire's annexations
  (1804-1814), and the 1814 First Treaty of Paris briefly left France part
  of Savoy (Chambéry, Annecy) until the Second Treaty of Paris (1815).
  See [oq-pre-1816-database-floor](#oq-pre-1816-database-floor).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA. The WHEP database floor is 1800.
- **Successor:** [fra-1860-1871](fra-1860-1871.md). The split at 1860 is
  the Treaty of Turin (24 March 1860). Under this repository's
  convention `end_year` is exclusive and a handover year belongs to the
  incoming polity, so 1860 itself is a year of FRA-1860-1871.

## Sourced claims

- **1815: Congress of Vienna.** France was "restored to its 1792
  boundaries" after the Napoleonic Wars
  [wikipedia-france-2026-04-12 §1815](../sources/wikipedia-france-2026-04-12.md#1815).
  All Napoleonic European conquests were lost.
- **1860-03-24: Treaty of Turin, Nice and Savoy annexed.** Biger:
  "the historic Italian region of Savoy and the town of Nice were
  given to France"
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
  End-of-row event. The convention of 7 March 1861 then "created a
  boundary that departed from the details of the originally projected
  alignment" (same section); CShapes-Europe starts its enlarged France
  step in 1861.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists France (`FRN`,
  `ccode=220`) from 1816-01-01 onward
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Contradictions

- **Size of the 1860 gain.** Earlier versions of this page, of
  fra-1800-1919 and of the wikipedia-france source note gave the gain as
  8,261 km², citing an internal rationale file, not a source. It is
  contradicted by measurement: CShapes-Europe's 1861 France step is a
  strict superset of its 1816 step (0 km² of the old polygon outside the
  new) and the difference is **13,631 km²**, of which 4,733 km² lies in
  modern Haute-Savoie, 5,745 km² in Savoie and 2,623 km² in
  Alpes-Maritimes. The two Savoy départements alone cover 11,188 km² in
  GADM. This page follows the measurement.

## Decisions

- ★ **1860 split applied (2026-09-25).** The former `FRA-1800-1871` was
  split into this row and [fra-1860-1871](fra-1860-1871.md). This
  resolves [oq-1860-nice-savoy-split-candidate](#oq-1860-nice-savoy-split-candidate).
  The reasons the old page gave for not splitting do not survive
  measurement: the gain is 2.55% of the territory, not 1.5%, and
  CShapes-Europe already ships the post-1860 polygon (step 1861-1870),
  so no constructed polygon is needed.
- ★ **Polygon kept.** The CShapes-Europe 1816 polygon is exactly this
  row's territory; nothing was replaced.
- ★ **Code.** Recoded from `FRA-1800-1871` to `FRA-1800-1860` rather
  than keeping the old code as a superseded row, the way the 2026-09-24
  recodes (VEN, ECU, FRA-84, ADE) were done: every reference was
  rewritten except the historical logs.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1816-database-floor

Data routed to this polity for years 1800-1815 falls before the
CShapes-Europe polygon vintage of 1816. The Napoleonic period had
highly variable French extent (Empire vs. Consulate). The 1816 polygon
represents post-Congress of Vienna France, which is approximately
correct for the Consulate period at 1800 but does not capture the
First Empire's peak extent (1810). No layer-B row for France falls
before 1850 today, so this has no data consequence yet.

Resolved 2026-09-25: split applied, see Decisions above.
