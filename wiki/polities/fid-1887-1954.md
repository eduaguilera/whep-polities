# French Indochina


> **Polygon replaced 2026-08-05: the previous one contained New Caledonia, Réunion and Kerguelen.**
>
> The attached Cliopatria feature had **15 parts spanning 55.1°E to 168.0°E, 49.8°S to 39.6°N**, so
> a containment test placed Nouméa, Saint-Denis and Port-aux-Français inside French Indochina.
> **All 35 steps of that feature carry them** — checked one by one in the raw GeoJSON — so no
> choice of `polygon_feature_year` could fix it. Cliopatria's "French Indochina" is a record of
> French possessions east of Africa, not of Indochina.
>
> Now built by `build_fid_1887_1954()` in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`: the 1920 step
> clipped to a mainland envelope. Regenerable, and the recipe is checked rather than described.
>
> ```
> dropped 11 parts, 36,992 km²   New Caledonia 20,278 · Kerguelen 8,939 · Réunion 3,513
>                                Loyalty Is. ~2,700 · 681 km² at 39.4°N = the French
>                                concession at Tianjin
> kept    4 parts, 753,049 km²   Hanoi ✓ Saigon ✓ Phnom Penh ✓ Vientiane ✓ Guangzhouwan ✓
>                                Nouméa ✗ Réunion ✗ Kerguelen ✗ Tianjin ✗
> ```
>
> **Guangzhouwan is deliberately kept.** At 110.4°E, 21.2°N it falls inside the envelope, and it
> was leased to France and administered from Indochina — unlike the Tianjin concession, which was
> not.
>
> **Vintage moved 1900 → 1920.** Clipped areas by step: 1895-97 = 659,948; 1900-04 = 662,141;
> 1905-07 = 700,369; **1908-1939 = 753,213**; 1940-45 = 2,473 (Japanese occupation); 1946-55 =
> 751,021. The territory was essentially complete after the 1907 Franco-Siamese treaty and stable
> until 1940, so the plateau represents a 1887-1954 row better than the 1900 step did.
>
> **The clip did not cure the invalidity, and the repair mattered.** Every individual part is
> valid; two kept parts abut along a *line*, which a multipolygon may not do. The two candidate
> repairs are not equivalent:
>
> ```
> union       753,049.331 km²   area-preserving; merges two adjacent pieces
> make_valid  752,992.664 km²   DROPS the 56.7 km² part entirely
> ```
>
> Union is used, and the builder **asserts** the area is preserved — so if a future Cliopatria
> vintage makes those parts genuinely overlap, it raises rather than quietly changing the area.
> `make_valid` would have deleted real territory to satisfy a predicate.
>
> **Why no gate had caught any of this.** Measured area was 699,133 km² against ~740,000 cited —
> 5% low, which reads as ordinary vintage drift. The three wrong territories added ~28,000 km²
> while the 1900 step fell short of the later extent, so **the two errors partly cancelled**. An
> area comparison is structurally the wrong instrument; only a containment test finds it. The new
> figure is 1.8% high, and now declared, so `validate_polygons` can check it.


The Indochinese Union (Union indochinoise) as a single territorial-economic
unit from its formal federation on 17 October 1887 to dissolution at the
Geneva Accords on 21 July 1954. Comprised Cochinchina (colony), Annam and
Tonkin (protectorates, merged into French Vietnam), Cambodia (protectorate
from 1863), Laos (protectorate from 1893), and Kwangchow Wan (leased from
China 1898-1946). Administered from Hanoi with a unified statistical office;
agricultural data for "Indochina" in the pre-1961 dataset refers to the
federation as a whole, not a member territory.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cliopatria` feature `"French Indochina"`, time-step covering
1900-1904 (area ~699,133 km² in Cliopatria's equal-area projection). The
polygon approximates the federation at its stable early-20th-century extent
including Vietnam (Cochinchina + Annam + Tonkin), Cambodia, and Laos, but
excludes Kwangchow Wan (the tiny leased Chinese enclave at Zhanjiang).
Cliopatria records 31 time-steps for "French Indochina" 1860-1957; the 1900
polygon was chosen as the central representative of the stable federation
era. Not using CShapes 2.0 because CShapes records the member territories
as separate features (gwcode 811 Cambodia, 812 Laos, 815 Vietnam) without a
federation-level aggregate.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Indochina" as a
single country for 262 rows spanning 1866-1948. Previously these rows had
`match_status: none` because no WHEP polity existed with iso3=FID. The
`name_override` entry `"Indochina" → "FID"` in
`pipelines/pre1961-matching/match.R` was already in place — just missing
the target.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — the federation absorbed pre-existing French
  territorial acquisitions (Cochinchina 1862, Cambodia 1863) which aren't
  separately tracked in WHEP.
- **Successor:** Vietnam (north and south), Cambodia, Laos as independent
  states from 1954. The WHEP successor chain uses [khm-1953-2025](khm-1953-2025.md)
  (Cambodia), [lao-1954-2025](lao-1954-2025.md) (Laos), and the relevant VNM
  rows.
