# Jersey (Bailiwick of Jersey)

## Summary

Jersey, "officially the Bailiwick of Jersey, ... an autonomous and self-governing British Crown
Dependency in Northwestern Europe 14 miles (23 km) off the Cotentin Peninsula". The main island is
"45 square miles (120 km2)", and "Jersey's territory also includes some surrounding uninhabited islands
and rocks". It is one half of [chi-1800-2025](chi-1800-2025.md), the Channel Islands, the other being
[ggy-1800-2025](ggy-1800-2025.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §jersey](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#jersey)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british jersey` reports Jersey alone for 1928-1932 to 1960 (**30 (year, label)
pairs**), beside `british guernsey`. Only the joint Channel Islands row existed, and routing each
bailiwick to it would average two different islands' values under one key. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm0 `JEY` measures **125 km²** (ESRI:54034), against the main island's stated 120 km²;
the rest is the islets and GADM's intertidal coastline.
