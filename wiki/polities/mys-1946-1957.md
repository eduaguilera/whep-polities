# Malayan Union / Federation of Malaya (1946-1957)

## Summary

The British-administered Malayan Union (1 April 1946 – 1 February 1948) and its successor the Federation of Malaya (1 February 1948 – 31 August 1957). The Malayan Union combined the Federated and Unfederated Malay States with Penang and Malacca under a single crown-colony-style administration; widespread Malay opposition led by UMNO over citizenship provisions and the reduction of sultans' powers forced its dissolution after less than two years, replaced by the Federation of Malaya (restoring the sultans' protected-state status). The Federation achieved independence on 31 August 1957 as `MYS-1957-1963` (the following WHEP row, which precedes the 1963 formation of Malaysia).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Covers peninsular Malaya (the nine Malay states plus the Straits Settlements of Penang and Malacca, but excluding Singapore which remained a separate crown colony).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. The 1946 start corresponds to the Malayan Union proclamation; prior to that the territory was administered as separate federated/unfederated states and a crown colony, not yet modelled as a single WHEP row.
- **Successor:** [mys-1957-1963](mys-1957-1963.md) — Federation of Malaya independent 1957–1963.

## Contradictions

**Polity-type compression.** MYS-1946-1957 compresses the Malayan Union (a crown-colony entity) and the Federation of Malaya (a British protected state / later independent federation) into a single WHEP row. These are distinct legal configurations but the territory is broadly stable, so the compression is consistent with the WHEP territorial-economic-unit rule.
