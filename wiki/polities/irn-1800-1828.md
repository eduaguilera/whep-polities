# Persia (Qajar to 1828)

## Summary

> **Correction (2026-07-29): this row was drawn as the United States.**
> `polygon_feature_id` was literally `"United States of America"` with
> `polygon_feature_year: 1815`, so the polygon sat in North America — centroid lon
> -81.2, lat 37.8, against Iran's lon 54, lat 33 — and did not overlap
> `IRN-1828-2025` at all.
>
> Rebound to Cliopatria `"Qajar Dynasty"` at 1800, which selects that source's
> 1800-1804 time-step: **1,743,611 km²**. That is larger than the successor
> `IRN-1828-2025` (~1,621,564 km²) because Persia had not yet ceded its Caucasus
> territories under the treaties of Gulistan (1813) and Turkmenchay (1828) — which is
> the event this row ends on. Cliopatria's own 1828-1855 Qajar step measures
> 1,621,696 km², within 0.01% of the successor's polygon, which confirms the two rows
> now describe one continuous territory.
>
> Why no area check caught it: the USA in 1815 measured ~1,586,287 km² and Iran
> ~1,621,564 — a ratio of 0.98. Magnitude cannot detect position. It was found by
> checking that consecutive periods of one family overlap in space
> ([validate_spatial_containment](../../scripts/validate_spatial_containment.py)), and
> the eight wrong-country polygons found earlier were all caught only because their
> sizes were absurd. See issue 46.


This row tracks Qajar Persia for **29 years**, from the database floor
to the Treaty of Turkmenchay
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes: "Qajar
Iran including South Caucasus (modern Azerbaijan Armenia eastern
Georgia). Lost to Russia via Treaty of Gulistan (1813) and Treaty of
Turkmenchay (1828) ~170000+ km2. Cliopatria has Qajar boundaries"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The Qajar dynasty ruled Iran from 1789. At the start of this row, Persia
controlled the South Caucasus (modern Azerbaijan, Armenia, eastern
Georgia). Two wars with Russia resulted in massive territorial losses:
the Treaty of Gulistan (1813) and the Treaty of Turkmenchay (1828) cost
~170,000+ km2. Cross-ref [f228-1800-1856](f228-1800-1856.md) for the
Russian side of the Caucasus conquest. The 1828 end date aligns with
Turkmenchay.

## Open questions

### oq-gulistan-mid-row-split

The Treaty of Gulistan (1813) was a major territorial loss (~50,000+ km2
of Caucasus territory). Under a strict reading of the WHEP
polity-definition rule, this could be a mid-row split candidate. The CSV
does not split at 1813, combining both Russo-Persian Wars into a single
row ending at 1828.

### oq-cliopatria-qajar-coverage

The CSV specifies "Cliopatria has Qajar boundaries" but only a single
time-step (1800--1804) is noted. The Qajar polygon at 1800--1804 includes
the South Caucasus, which was progressively lost by 1828. The polygon
overstates Qajar territory for the 1813--1828 portion of the row.
