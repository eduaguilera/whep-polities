# Gambia (1889-1965)

## Summary

British Gambia colony from 1889 to independence on 18 February 1965. CShapes codes this period as status=colony, ruler=GBR. "The Gambia is one of Africa's smallest states. It is a semi-enclave in Senegal" (p.233) [biger-1995 &sect;gambia](../sources/biger-1995.md#gambia). French and British boundary commissions demarcated the line in 1895-1896, 1898-1899, and 1904-1905 (p.234).

## Territorial extent

Gambia is a narrow country along the Gambia River, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its short Atlantic coastline. Area 4,127 sq. miles (10,689 sq. km). Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
