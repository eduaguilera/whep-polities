# Pyrénées-Orientales

## Summary

Pyrénées-Orientales is one of the original French départements, created by the Revolutionary administrative reform of 4 March 1790 out of the former province of Roussillon (itself annexed from the Crown of Aragon under the 1659 Treaty of the Pyrenees) together with a small adjoining strip of Languedoc. It occupies the far southern edge of France on the Mediterranean coast and the Spanish border, with Perpignan as its seat, and corresponds today to INSEE département code 66. This entry is `type: subnational` because a département is a sub-unit of the French state, not a sovereign polity in its own right — France's own sovereignty and national-level statistics belong to the FRA-* national chain (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-2025 and other eras), while this row exists purely to carry data reported at département-level for Pyrénées-Orientales specifically. It is not one of the four department-level exceptions the country's routing convention calls out for a later start (Belfort, the Alsace-Lorraine trio, Corse's 1793/1975 split, or the overseas départements), so its start_year follows the default: the département's actual creation in 1790. It has been continuously in existence since, with no split, merger, or renaming, and remains current, so its end_year is open at 2025.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Mitchell-derived and related département-level statistical series for Pyrénées-Orientales (INSEE code 66) spanning roughly 1862-2023, with the routing decision reporting 97.6% of those data years as directly observed rather than back-cast, i.e. this is a genuine sub-national reporting unit with its own continuous data trail, not a reconstruction. Before this entry existed, no polity row represented the département itself — only the national FRA-* chain did, so any data tabulated specifically for Pyrénées-Orientales had nowhere correct to go and would have been either dropped or mis-routed to a national-scale row roughly 140 times larger in surface area than the département (metropolitan France is about 543,940 km2 against the département's ~4,116 km2). That mismatch in scale is itself evidence the data was previously unmatched or mismatched: a series reporting on a ~4,100 km2 unit cannot correctly be filed under an entry meant to represent the whole of France. What confirms this is a distinct, addressable reporting unit rather than an arbitrary carve-out is that Pyrénées-Orientales has held continuous, unchanged administrative status as one of France's original 1790 départements for 235 years, with INSEE and equivalent national statistical series treating it as a first-class reporting geography throughout — exactly the kind of persistent administrative/statistical unit this table's country convention for France directs should get its own row by default, since it is not one of the four listed exceptions requiring different treatment.

Territory description for a reader without the geometry: Pyrénées-Orientales sits at the southeastern tip of mainland France, bordering Spain (Girona province, Catalonia) to the south, Andorra and Ariège to the west, Aude to the north, and the Mediterranean Sea to the east; its administrative seat is Perpignan. It corresponds to the historical region of (French) Roussillon plus a strip of Languedoc's Fenouillèdes district, and to the modern INSEE département numbered 66. Its present-day area is approximately **4,116 km2**, a figure drawn from standard national statistical references for the current department rather than measured from any polygon attached to this entry (none is attached). Because French départements are described in the repository's polygon proxy rules as comparatively stable since their 1790 creation, this area figure is a reasonable order-of-magnitude expectation for the whole 1790-2025 span, but it is not verified against a period-appropriate polygon and should not be treated as authoritative until GADM's France coverage is fetched and a feature is actually assigned.

### d-code-choice-fra-pyro

**Chose FRA-PYRO-1790-2025 after FRA-PO-1800-2025 was found already held**

The routing decision proposed container FRA-1800-1871 with span 1800-2025 for this unit, but downstream code assignment for a first attempt used FRA-PO-1800-2025 and that code is already occupied by an unrelated built-table entry for a different territory. Per instructions to choose a free code and change nothing else, this page uses FRA-PYRO-1790-2025: ISO3 FRA, a four-letter subunit abbreviation PYRO (Pyrénées-Orientales) that avoids collision with the taken PO, and the department's true creation year 1790 (not 1800) as start_year, matching the routing_reasoning's own span_basis text which cites the 4 March 1790 reform. The dominant subnational pattern in the table is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, which this follows.

### d-start-year-1790-not-1800

**Used 1790 as start_year, matching the department's actual creation, despite the proposed container starting in 1800**

The routing_reasoning explicitly derives start_year 1790 from the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary decree creating the original ~83 départements, and routing_concerns flags that this start precedes the earliest available FRA container (FRA-1800-1871, itself starting 1800). Since the schema requires the years in the polity code to equal the years in the frontmatter, and the department demonstrably existed from 1790, this page keeps 1790 as the true start_year and documents the 1790-1800 containment gap as an open question rather than silently truncating the department's history to fit the container inventory. If a pre-1800 FRA container is added later, the container list here should gain a leading edge for 1790-1800.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1800-container-gap

**No FRA container is registered for 1790-1800, leaving this polity's earliest decade uncontained**

This page's start_year is 1790, matching the département's actual creation by the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary reform, but the earliest FRA container row available in the polity table (FRA-1800-1871) itself starts in 1800. The container list above therefore only tiles 1800-2025, leaving 1790-1800 (the Revolutionary/Directory/early Consulate period) uncontained. Either a FRA-1790-1800 (or similarly named) container needs to be added to the table to cover the Revolutionary decade, or this entry's start_year should be revised to 1800 to match the existing container inventory at the cost of losing the department's true founding date. Left unresolved pending a decision on whether the containment gate treats this as a hard failure.
