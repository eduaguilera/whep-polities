# Hong Kong (with Kowloon, 1860-1898)

## Summary

Hong Kong after the Convention of Peking (24 October 1860), which ceded "the part of Kowloon south of
Boundary Street, together with Stonecutters Island" [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §hong-kong](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#hong-kong), and before the 1898 lease of
the New Territories. Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old `HKG-1842-2025` row (issue 686).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried the modern SAR back to 1842, about 12 times this era's
extent. No data reaches these years.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** proxy. Constructed (`build_hkg_1860_1898` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): [hkg-1842-1860](hkg-1842-1860.md)'s island, plus the GADM 4.1
districts Yau Tsim Mong, Sham Shui Po and Kowloon City [gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md) cut at
22.3245 N, the latitude of Boundary Street. Stonecutters Island (22.32 N) falls south of the cut.

**km² reasoning (Mollweide).** 98.0 km², 14.0 km² more than the island. The 1860 cession is usually
given as about 10 km² including Stonecutters Island; the excess is reclaimed land along today's Kowloon
coast and the cut being a straight line where Boundary Street is not quite straight. Hence `proxy`.
