# Libya (1943-1949)

## Summary

Libya under Allied military occupation from the 1943 defeat of Axis forces in North Africa to 1949, when the UN General Assembly passed the resolution that would lead to independence on 24 December 1951. Tripolitania and Cyrenaica were under British military administration; Fezzan was under French military administration. CSV status: CShapes ruler="ITA", status="occupied" — but in practice Italian sovereignty ended in 1943 and was formally renounced in the 1947 Treaty of Peace with Italy.

## Data scope and FAO 1952 label structure

FAO 1952 reports Libya-level data under two distinct label patterns:

- **Plain "Libya"** — all-Libya aggregate, covering Tripolitania + Cyrenaica + Fezzan combined (~1,617,537 km²). These rows route to LBY-1943-1949 (1943–1949 data years) and LBY-1950-1951 (1950–1951 data years).
- **"Libya Tripolitania"** / **"Tripolitania"** — western province only (~330–400k km²). Routes to TRP-1943-1951 via existing alias.
- **"Libya Cyrenaica"** — eastern province only (~850–900k km²). Routes to CYR-1949-1951 via existing alias.
- **"Libya Fezzan"** — southern province only. No separate WHEP polity assigned as of 2026-06-30.

The plain "Libya" label is unambiguously all-Libya scope. There is no double-count between LBY-1943-1949 (plain "Libya") and TRP-1943-1951 (explicit "Libya Tripolitania") because FAO used distinct labels for each level of reporting.

## Contradictions

**CShapes ruler attribution.** CShapes records ruler=ITA for this window (per CSV notes: `status=occupied, ruler=ITA`) but the factual position is that Italian sovereignty ended in 1943 with the Allied invasion and was formally renounced in 1947. A more faithful coding would be ruler=GBR (Tripolitania/Cyrenaica) / FRA (Fezzan) / UNO (from 1949). Flagged below.

**Possible double-count concern (resolved).** The 15 FAO 1952 rows matched to LBY-1943-1949 carry the plain "Libya" label (all-Libya scope). "Libya Tripolitania" data already routes to TRP-1943-1951 via its own alias. FAO used mutually exclusive labels: plain "Libya" = aggregate; qualified "Libya Tripolitania" / "Libya Cyrenaica" = sub-provincial. No double-count exists. Livestock magnitude confirms all-Libya scope: plain "Libya" 1949 horses+mules+asses = 43k+109k = 152k heads vs Tripolitania-only explicit FAO figure of 33k heads (2026-06-30 investigation).

## Open questions

### oq-lby-1943-livestock-magnitude

FAO 1952 plain "Libya" 1949 horses+mules+asses = 152k heads; explicit Tripolitania-only figure = 33k. The provincial sub-totals for Cyrenaica and Fezzan are not separately reported in FAO 1952 for this commodity, so the relationship between 152k (all-Libya) and the ~33k Tripolitania figure implies Cyrenaica+Fezzan contribution of ~119k. This is internally consistent but worth flagging: if Fezzan had negligible livestock, Cyrenaica would account for ~78% of Libya's horse/mule/ass stock, which seems plausible given its larger territory (~53% of Libya) but should be checked against any available Cyrenaica-specific source.
