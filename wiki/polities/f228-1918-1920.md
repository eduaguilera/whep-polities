# USSR (1918-1920)

## Summary

The CSV labels this row "USSR (1918-1920)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), but **the USSR
did not exist until 30 December 1922**. This polity is actually
the **RSFSR** (Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic)
throughout the entire 1918-1920 window. The label is an
anachronism documented in
[log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
Per the critical-stance rule
[log decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority](../log.md#decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority),
the CSV label is preserved as-is and the mislabeling is
documented rather than silently corrected.

This row covers **3 years** of the Russian Civil War. The Red
Army fought White armies across the former empire. Allied
intervention (British, French, American, Japanese) supported
the Whites. The Polish-Soviet War (1919-1921) overlapped with
this period.

Predecessor per CSV: [f228-1917-1918](f228-1917-1918.md).
Successor per CSV: [f228-1920-1921](f228-1920-1921.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Civil War territory was fluid.** The RSFSR's effective
  control expanded and contracted with the front lines of the
  Civil War. White forces controlled Siberia (Kolchak), southern
  Russia (Denikin, Wrangel), and the northwest (Yudenich) at
  various points.
- **Allied intervention zones.** British forces in Archangel and
  Murmansk; Japanese forces in the Russian Far East; French
  forces in Odessa. These were temporary occupations, mostly
  withdrawn by 1920.
- **Polish-Soviet War (1919-1921).** Poland invaded Ukraine in
  April 1920; the Red Army counterattacked to the gates of
  Warsaw before being repulsed in August 1920 (Battle of
  Warsaw). The territorial settlement came with the Treaty of
  Riga (March 1921, next row).

## Sourced claims

- **1918-1920: Russian Civil War.** Multi-front conflict between
  the Bolshevik Red Army and various White forces, national
  independence movements, and foreign interventions
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1920: Polish-Soviet War climax.** The Battle of Warsaw
  (August 1920) halted the Soviet westward advance
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).

## Contradictions

  entity is the RSFSR. The USSR was not established until
