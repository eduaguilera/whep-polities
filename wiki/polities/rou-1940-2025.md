# Romania (1940-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **86 years**, from WWII to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Reduced Romania post-1940 losses. Regained northern Transylvania from
Hungary after WWII (1947 Treaty of Paris) but did not regain Bessarabia
(remained Soviet, now Moldova) or Southern Dobruja (remained Bulgarian).
Communist period 1947-1989. 1989 Revolution (Ceausescu executed). EU
member 2007. Modern Romania ~238,000 km².

Predecessor: [rou-1920-1940](rou-1920-1940.md) -- three territorial
losses in 1940.
Successor: NA (still active).

## Sourced claims

- **1940: Territorial losses.** Romania lost ~100,000 km² in three
  events. This is the start-of-row event.
- **1941-1944: Axis member.** Romania joined the Axis, fought on the
  Eastern Front. King Michael's coup (23 August 1944) switched Romania
  to the Allied side.
- **1947-02-10: Treaty of Paris.** Northern Transylvania returned from
  Hungary. Bessarabia and Northern Bukovina confirmed as Soviet.
- **1947-1989: Communist period.** People's Republic / Socialist
  Republic of Romania. Ceausescu era from 1965.
- **1989-12-25: Revolution.** Nicolae Ceausescu executed. End of
  communist rule.
- **2007-01-01: EU accession.** Romania joined the European Union.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 360, continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
- **Biger 1995: area and borders.** Romania: 91,699 sq mi [237,400 sq km];
  bounded by Ukraine, Moldava, Black Sea, Bulgaria, Serbia, Hungary
  [biger-1995 §romania](../sources/biger-1995.md#romania) (p.447).
- **Biger 1995: 1944 Soviet occupation.** "In 1944 Romania was occupied
  by Soviet forces" [biger-1995 §romania](../sources/biger-1995.md#romania) (p.448).
