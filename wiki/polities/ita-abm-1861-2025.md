# Abruzzi e Molise

## Summary

The Abruzzi and Molise taken together: the statistical compartment and then the region "Abruzzi e Molise" from Italian unification until the constitutional law of 27 December 1963 separated Molise, and since then the union of the two regions Abruzzo and Molise. `type: subnational`: Italy held this ground throughout.

**Why this entry exists.** The Mediterranean subnational panel ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)) reports a unit labelled `ITF1`. In NUTS that code is Abruzzo alone, and the unit was routed to ITA-ABR-1970-2025 (10,792 km2), with its 1861-1969 years back-cast onto it. The unit's landuse block says it is larger: the per-year sum of its land-cover classes is a **constant 15,234.96 km2 in every one of its 141 landuse years**. Abruzzo plus Molise (ITA-MOL-1963-2025, 4,436) is 15,228 in the published table (+0.04%). The panel has no `ITF2` (Molise) unit, so Molise's data was riding on Abruzzo's row. The Molise page already asked for this row: its predecessor discussion names the joint region as missing. Claude created it on 2026-09-24 while correcting the Southern-Europe alias defects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned, constructed. The union of GADM 4.1 adm1 Abruzzo (ITA.1_1) and Molise (ITA.12_1) ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)), built by `build_ita_abm_1861_2025()`. Those are the very features ITA-ABR-1970-2025 and ITA-MOL-1963-2025 bind, so the union cannot drift from its parts. Measured in ESRI:54034: 15,216 km2 against the panel's 15,234.96 (+0.12%); Abruzzo alone is 10,784, 0.71 of the panel total.

**Historical extent.** The outer limit of the compartment is essentially that of the four Abruzzese provinces and Molise since 1861. Internal changes (the 1927 province of Pescara, the 1970 province of Isernia) moved nothing across it. Small adjustments along the Campania and Lazio borders are not measured here and are within the polygon's agreement with the panel.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. From 1861 to 1963 this was the region itself, so the split of 1963 could be written as a succession into Abruzzo and Molise; it is not, because the panel keeps reporting the union after 1963 and this row therefore has to continue as a reporting aggregate to 2025. ITA-ABR-1970-2025 and ITA-MOL-1963-2025 are its parts, coexisting with it.

- GADM 4.1 adm1 Abruzzo (ITA.1_1) and Molise (ITA.12_1) are 10,784 and 4,433 km2 in ESRI:54034 ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)).

## Decisions

### d-observed-from-1861

**All of the unit's years, 1861-2023, are observation of this territory**

Abruzzi e Molise existed as a compartment from 1861, so the pre-1970 years are not a reconstruction onto a boundary that did not exist yet -- they are this territory reported as itself. `juan-subnational` `ITF1` routes 1861-2023 here, and its former `back_cast` onto Abruzzo is removed.

### d-panel-slugs-only

**The official NUTS slugs keep ITF1 = Abruzzo**

The `eurostat-nuts2013`, `eurostat-nuts2016` and `jrc-nuts2016` rows for `ITF1` are left on ITA-ABR-1970-2025, because in those code systems ITF1 is Abruzzo.

## Open questions

### oq-chain-after-1963

**Should the 1963 split be modelled as a chain as well?**

A purist table would end the region in 1963 and make Abruzzo and Molise its successors. Doing so would leave the panel's 1963-2023 union with no polity, so this row continues as a reporting aggregate. If a source ever reports Abruzzi e Molise only up to 1963, splitting this row would be the cleaner model.
