# Outer Provinces of the Dutch East Indies

## Summary

The Outer Provinces (Buitengewesten): the Dutch East Indies outside Java and Madura, western New Guinea included. Sumatra, Bali and Lombok, which the list also reports alone, have rows inside it. [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §java](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#java)

## Why this entry exists

The label `dutch east indies: other islands` reports it alone for 1909-1948 (**43 (year, label) pairs**), beside `dutch east indies` and its other islands. No polity was this island before 1949; the 1949-1951 rows (IDN-JVM, IDN-BLB, IDN-OTH) group islands differently and begin in 1949. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: every GADM 4.1 adm1 province of Indonesia but the six on Java island (Madura is in Jawa Timur). **Proxy**: present-day GADM administrative boundaries stand for the colonial residencies, whose limits on the islands' coasts were the same ground.
