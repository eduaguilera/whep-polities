# West Germany

## Summary

This row tracks the Federal Republic of Germany (Bundesrepublik
Deutschland, FRG) for **42 years**, from its founding on 23 May 1949
to German reunification on 3 October 1990
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The FRG was established in the Western occupation zones (American,
British, French) of defeated Nazi Germany. Biger notes that the
occupation zones "matured into the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
and German Democratic Republic (GDR), respectively"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). COW code 260
is the same code used for all DEU rows -- COW treats the FRG as the
German continuator state.

Predecessor: [wzo-1938-1949](wzo-1938-1949.md) -- Western Germany /
Western Occupation Zones (1938–1949). WZO-1938-1949 was created to fill
the date gap between the end of DEU-1938-1945 and the formal founding
of the FRG on 23 May 1949, symmetric with SBZ-1938-1949 for the
eastern counterpart.

Successor: [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German reunification
3 October 1990. Biger: "in 1990 the German Democratic Republic was
integrated into the Federal Republic"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=260`, polygon from 1949 onward at the reduced post-WWII
  West German territory (excluding the Soviet zone / GDR and
  territories east of the Oder-Neisse line).
- **Area.** Approximately 249,000 km² -- the pre-war German territory
  minus the GDR (~108,000 km²) and the eastern territories ceded to
  Poland and the USSR.
- **COW code 260** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Germany (`GMY`, `ccode=260`) as continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- COW treats FRG as the continuator
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

The FAO 1952 yearbook reports German data under the label "Germany
Western" for the years 1937–1951. The alias for F78-1949-1990 covers
only 1949–1951 (5 rows), from the FRG's formal founding on 23 May 1949.
Earlier years (1937–1937 → DEU-1920-1938; 1938–1948 → WZO-1938-1949)
are routed by year-ranged alias rows in `applied_aliases.csv` to their
respective polities.

**Why the split?** The FRG did not legally exist until 23 May 1949.
Routing 1937–1948 FAO data to F78-1949-1990 would assign it to a
polity before its founding. WZO-1938-1949 was created to receive the
1938–1948 rows; it carries the same CShapes gwcode=260 polygon
(~248,000 km²) used here, so the geographic scope is identical.

**Why WZO and not DEU-1938-1945 for 1938–1948?** The FAO "Germany
Western" label explicitly identifies the western-zone geographic area
(~248,000 km²), not the full Reich (~470,000 km²). Routing to
DEU-1938-1945 would overstate territory ~2×.
