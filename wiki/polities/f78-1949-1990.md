# West Germany

## Summary

This row tracks the Federal Republic of Germany (Bundesrepublik
Deutschland, FRG) for **42 years**, from its founding on 23 May 1949
to German reunification on 3 October 1990
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The FRG was established in the Western occupation zones (American,
British, French) of defeated Nazi Germany. Biger notes that the
occupation zones "matured into the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
and German Democratic Republic (GDR), respectively"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). COW code 260
is the same code used for all DEU rows -- COW treats the FRG as the
German continuator state.

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but historically the FRG descended from the
Western occupation zones of defeated Nazi Germany. The chain gap from
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) (which has `successor=NA`) to this
row reflects the 1945-1949 Allied occupation period with no sovereign
German state.

Successor: [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German reunification
3 October 1990. Biger: "in 1990 the German Democratic Republic was
integrated into the Federal Republic"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

## Data aliases

| alias-id | original_name | common_name | source | rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| alias-fao1952-germany-western | Germany Western | West Germany | FAO 1952 yearbook | 254 |

The FAO 1952 yearbook reports German data under the label "Germany
Western" for the years 1937–1951. This is a retrospective western-zone
attribution: the FAO applied the western-zone frame to pre-war and
occupation-period statistics that were originally collected for the
whole Reich or for the combined occupation zones. The alias routes all
254 rows with `polity_name = "Germany Western"` (iso3c = NA) to
F78-1949-1990 via the lookup-key prefix "F78" in the pre-1961 matching
pipeline.

### polygon_vintage_drift — 1937–1948

The CShapes 2.0 polygon for F78-1949-1990 has vintage 1949 (cowcode =
260, ~249,000 km²). Rows attributed to years 1937–1948 therefore carry
a polygon_vintage_drift in two opposite directions:

- **1937–1944 (pre-war / wartime):** the 1949 polygon *understates*
  the actual territorial extent. Pre-war Greater Germany (after the
  Anschluss and annexations) reached approximately 471,000 km² —
  roughly 90 % larger than the FRG polygon. The FAO retrospective
  attribution implicitly strips these territories and presents only the
  western-zone-equivalent statistics, but the spatial mismatch is real
  and users should treat any area-normalised indicators for these years
  as approximations. No split is warranted: DEU-1938-1945 covers the
  pre-war/wartime period and should receive data for the full Reich;
  F78-1949-1990 is the correct target only for FAO series explicitly
  labelled as western-zone retrospectives.

- **1945–1948 (Allied occupation):** the 1949 polygon *marginally
  overstates* the strict occupation-zone boundary. The Saar Protectorate
  was under French administration from 1947 and did not accede to the
  FRG until 1 January 1957 (~2,570 km², <2 % of FRG area). For the
  254-row FAO series this discrepancy is below the threshold for a
  split; it is documented here for transparency.

No polity split is required. F78-1949-1990 is already the granular
western-Germany entry. The drift is documented above and in the
pipeline match-rule comment.

## Open questions

### oq-predecessor-occupation-gap

The 1945-1949 Allied occupation period is not captured as a WHEP row.
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) has `successor=NA` and this row has
`predecessor=NA`. The ~4 years of four-power occupation (with no
sovereign German state) are a genuine gap in the chain. This is a
deliberate omission (occupation =/= sovereignty) but should be
explicitly documented so the territorial continuity is clear:
DEU-1938-1945 territory --> occupation zones --> F78-1949-1990 + F77-1949-1990.
