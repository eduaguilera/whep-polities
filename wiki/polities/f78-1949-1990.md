# West Germany

## Summary

This row tracks the Federal Republic of Germany (Bundesrepublik
Deutschland, FRG) for **42 years**, from its founding on 23 May 1949
to German reunification on 3 October 1990
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The FRG was established in the Western occupation zones (American,
British, French) of defeated Nazi Germany. Biger notes that the
occupation zones "matured into the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
and German Democratic Republic (GDR), respectively"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). COW code 260
is the same code used for all DEU rows -- COW treats the FRG as the
German continuator state.

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but historically the FRG descended from the
Western occupation zones of defeated Nazi Germany. The chain gap from
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) (which has `successor=NA`) to this
row reflects the 1945-1949 Allied occupation period with no sovereign
German state.

Successor: [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German reunification
3 October 1990. Biger: "in 1990 the German Democratic Republic was
integrated into the Federal Republic"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=260`, polygon from 1949 onward at the reduced post-WWII
  West German territory (excluding the Soviet zone / GDR and
  territories east of the Oder-Neisse line).
- **Area.** Approximately 249,000 km² -- the pre-war German territory
  minus the GDR (~108,000 km²) and the eastern territories ceded to
  Poland and the USSR.
- **COW code 260** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Germany (`GMY`, `ccode=260`) as continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- COW treats FRG as the continuator
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA per CSV. The FRG was established from the
  Western Allied occupation zones of defeated Nazi Germany
  ([deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md)). The 1945-1949 occupation
  period had no sovereign German state -- this is a chain gap.
- **Successor:** [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German
  reunification 3 October 1990. The GDR acceded to the Federal
  Republic under Article 23 of the Basic Law.

## Sourced claims

- **1949-05-23: FRG founded.** The Basic Law (Grundgesetz) came into
  force, establishing the Federal Republic of Germany in the Western
  zones. This is the start-of-row event.
- **FRG and GDR formation from occupation zones.** Biger: the
  occupation zones "matured into the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
  and German Democratic Republic (GDR), respectively"
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).
- **1990-10-03: German reunification.** Biger: "in 1990 the German
  Democratic Republic was integrated into the Federal Republic"
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). This is the
  end-of-row event.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 260, continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- no break for FRG/GDR
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
- **CShapes.** `cowcode=260` polygon from 1949 onward at the reduced
  West German territory
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).

## Open questions

### oq-predecessor-occupation-gap

The 1945-1949 Allied occupation period is not captured as a WHEP row.
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) has `successor=NA` and this row has
`predecessor=NA`. The ~4 years of four-power occupation (with no
sovereign German state) are a genuine gap in the chain. This is a
deliberate omission (occupation =/= sovereignty) but should be
explicitly documented so the territorial continuity is clear:
DEU-1938-1945 territory --> occupation zones --> F78-1949-1990 + F77-1949-1990.
