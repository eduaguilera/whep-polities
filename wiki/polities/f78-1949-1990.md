# West Germany

## Summary

This row tracks the Federal Republic of Germany (Bundesrepublik
Deutschland, FRG) for **42 years**, from its founding on 23 May 1949
to German reunification on 3 October 1990
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The FRG was established in the Western occupation zones (American,
British, French) of defeated Nazi Germany. Biger notes that the
occupation zones "matured into the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
and German Democratic Republic (GDR), respectively"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). COW code 260
is the same code used for all DEU rows -- COW treats the FRG as the
German continuator state.

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but historically the FRG descended from the
Western occupation zones of defeated Nazi Germany. The chain gap from
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) (which has `successor=NA`) to this
row reflects the 1945-1949 Allied occupation period with no sovereign
German state.

Successor: [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German reunification
3 October 1990. Biger: "in 1990 the German Democratic Republic was
integrated into the Federal Republic"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

## Predecessors and successors

## Data aliases

| alias-fao1952-germany-western | Germany Western | West Germany | FAO 1952 yearbook | 1937 | 1951 | 45 |

The FAO 1952 yearbook reports German data under the label "Germany
Western" for the years 1937–1951 (45 rows). This is a retrospective
western-zone attribution: the FAO applied the western-zone frame to
pre-war and occupation-period statistics that were originally collected
for the whole Reich or for the combined occupation zones. The alias
routes all 45 rows with `original_name = "Germany Western"` (source =
fao1952) to F78-1949-1990 across the full year range 1937–1951.

**Why F78-1949-1990 and not DEU-1938-1945?** The FAO "Germany Western"
label explicitly identifies the western-zone geographic area
(~248,000 km²), not the full Reich (~470,000 km²). Routing to
DEU-1938-1945 would overstate territory ~2×. The CShapes 2.0 polygon
for F78-1949-1990 (~249,000 km²) matches the western-zone area; the
polygon_vintage_drift is temporal only (see below) — the geographic
scope is correct for all years 1937–1951.

### polygon_vintage_drift — 1937–1948

The CShapes 2.0 polygon for F78-1949-1990 has vintage 1949 (cowcode =
260, ~249,000 km²). Rows attributed to years 1937–1948 carry a
polygon_vintage_drift, but this is **temporal drift only — the
geographic scope is correct**:

- **Geographic scope (resolved).** The FAO "Germany Western"
  designation refers to the US/UK/French occupation zones geographic
  area throughout 1937–1951. The 1949 CShapes polygon (~249,000 km²)
  represents this same geographic area. The data was never meant to
  cover Greater Germany or the Soviet zone. Routing to F78-1949-1990 is
  correct; the spatial extent matches the data being reported.

- **1937–1944 (pre-war / wartime):** the FAO retrospectively applied
  the western-zone frame to statistics originally collected for the
  whole Reich. The 1949 polygon correctly delimits the geographic area
  of interest (the future western zones). Users should note that these
  statistics are FAO's western-zone re-attribution of Reich-era data,
  not contemporaneous sub-national records. Area-normalised indicators
  for these years should be treated as approximations.

- **1945–1948 (Allied occupation):** the 1949 polygon marginally
  overstates the strict occupation-zone boundary. The Saar Protectorate
  was under French administration from 1947 and did not accede to the
  FRG until 1 January 1957 (~2,570 km², <2 % of FRG area). This
  discrepancy is below the threshold for a split.

No polity split is required. F78-1949-1990 is the correct granular
western-Germany entry for all FAO "Germany Western" rows 1937–1951.
The alias year range has been extended from 1949–1951 to 1937–1951 in
`applied_aliases.csv`. The temporal drift is documented here; the
routing is resolved.

## Open questions

### oq-predecessor-occupation-gap

The 1945-1949 Allied occupation period is not captured as a WHEP row.
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) has `successor=NA` and this row has
`predecessor=NA`. The ~4 years of four-power occupation (with no
sovereign German state) are a genuine gap in the chain. This is a
deliberate omission (occupation =/= sovereignty) but should be
explicitly documented so the territorial continuity is clear:
DEU-1938-1945 territory --> occupation zones --> F78-1949-1990 + F77-1949-1990.
