# Chile (1884-1899)

## Summary

Post-War-of-the-Pacific Chile. This polity covers Chile from **1884**
(effective control of annexed Atacama and Tarapacá provinces following
the 1883 Treaty of Ancón and 1884 Truce of Valparaíso) to **1899**.
Chile's territory expanded by ~240,000 km² compared to the pre-war
period, incorporating the northern provinces of Antofagasta (from Bolivia)
and Tarapacá (from Peru), which contained valuable nitrate and copper
deposits.

Predecessor: [chl-1810-1884](chl-1810-1884.md).
Successor: [chl-1899-1902](chl-1899-1902.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1883: Treaty of Ancón.** Signed October 1883; Peru ceded Tarapacá
  to Chile. Chilean administration effective from 1884.
- **1884: Truce of Valparaíso.** Bolivia ceded Antofagasta province
  to Chilean administration (formalized as permanent cession in the
  1904 Treaty of Peace and Friendship).
- **Nitrate boom.** The annexed provinces contained the world's largest
  nitrate deposits, driving significant economic growth in Chile through
  the late 19th century.

## Polygon decision

CHL-1884-1899 retains the existing CShapes gwcode=155 vintage-1886
polygon. The 2-year vintage drift (1884 start vs 1886 polygon) is
acceptable — no territorial change occurred 1884–1886. The polygon
correctly reflects Chile's post-conquest extent including Atacama and
Tarapacá provinces.
