# Hungary (1938-1947)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **9 years**, the Vienna Awards and WWII period.

Vienna Awards expanded Hungary: First Vienna Award (2 Nov 1938, +~11,830 km² of southern Slovakia), Second Vienna Award (30 Aug 1940, +~43,492 km² of northern Transylvania), Yugoslav occupation (Apr 1941, +~10,000 km²). At peak (1941-1944) Hungary controlled ~172,000 km². Treaty of Paris (10 Feb 1947) restored Trianon borders.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, cowcode=310, feature 1938-11-02 to 1947-02-09.
- **Area.** ~108,785 km² (ETRS89 LAEA equal-area): Trianon (~92,991 km²) plus First Vienna Award southern Slovakia (~11,830 km²).
- **Polygon approximation — UNDERSTATED for 1940-1944.** CShapes has only one polygon for this entire span; it does not capture the Second Vienna Award or Yugoslav annexation:
  - 1938-11-02 to 1940-08-29: polygon accurate (~109k km²).
  - 1940-08-30 to ~1944-10: polygon understates by ~63,000 km² (~58%); Second Vienna Award added northern Transylvania (~43,492 km²) and Yugoslav occupation added ~10,000 km², making peak territory ~172,000 km².
  - 1944-1947-02-09: territory reverted toward Trianon as Soviet occupation stripped annexations.
  - **Direction:** Hungary was LARGER than the polygon, not smaller.
  - **No expanded polygon available** in CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, or other WHEP sources. Option A split (see open question) requires sourcing a ~172k km² polygon from a WWII-era historical atlas.
- **Data magnitudes.** Staple crops (wheat ~1.82M t, maize ~1.81M t, sugar ~354k t) are consistent with Trianon-scale Hungary and show no clear upward step at 1940-41, suggesting source series may have excluded annexed territories.
