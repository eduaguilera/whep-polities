# Hungary (1938-1947)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **10 years**, the Vienna Awards and WWII
period [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Vienna Awards expanded Hungary: First Vienna Award (2 Nov 1938, southern
Slovakia), Second Vienna Award (30 Aug 1940, northern Transylvania from
Romania). Joined Axis. Soviet occupation 1944-45. Treaty of Paris
(10 Feb 1947) restored pre-1938 Trianon borders.

Predecessor: [hun-1920-1938](hun-1920-1938.md) -- interwar Horthy period.
Successor: [hun-1947-2025](hun-1947-2025.md) -- post-WWII Hungary.
