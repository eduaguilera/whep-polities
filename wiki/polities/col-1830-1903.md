# Colombia (to 1903)

## Summary

This row tracks Colombia from **1830** to **1903** — a **74-year row**. It covers the Colombian rump state after the dissolution of Gran Colombia, successively known as the Republic of New Granada (1831-1858), the Granadine Confederation (1858-1863), the United States of Colombia (1863-1886), and the Republic of Colombia (1886 onward). The end-of-row event is the separation of Panama in November 1903.

Predecessor: [col-1800-1830](col-1800-1830.md).
Successor: [col-1903-1922](col-1903-1922.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-2.0, cowcode=100, feature vintage 1886-01-01 to 1903-11-02. This is the pre-Panama-separation Colombia polygon, covering modern Colombia plus Panama (~1,167,415 km²).
- **Area.** ~1,167,415 km² (Colombia + Panama), per CShapes-2.0 cowcode=100 vintage 1886. This is the correct order of magnitude for the Republic of New Granada and its successors — NOT the ~2.85M km² Gran Colombia footprint that included Venezuela and Ecuador.
- **Polygon vintage note.** The CShapes-2.0 feature covers 1886-1903 (the Republic of Colombia period). The earlier sub-periods 1830-1886 (Republic of New Granada / Granadine Confederation / United States of Colombia) had approximately the same core territory (~1.14-1.42M km² per Cliopatria's 'Republic of New Granada' 1834-1858 and 'United States of Colombia' 1863-1865 features). The CShapes-2.0 1886 vintage is used as a single polygon for the full 1830-1903 span; any territorial variation within this period is minor relative to the corrected magnitude. The polygon_vintage_drift for the 1830-1886 sub-period is an acknowledged approximation.
- **Previous polygon error.** The prior polygon was Cliopatria 'Gran Colombia' feature year 1830, area ~2,846,354 km². That polygon includes Venezuela and Ecuador (which seceded in 1830-1831) and overstated the post-1831 Colombian territory by approximately 2.4x. This error has been corrected.
- **COW.** NA — COW does not code Colombia before 1886.

## Sourced claims

- **1830-1831: Post-Gran Colombia.** Venezuela and Ecuador seceded from Gran Colombia, leaving the Colombian rump state with roughly modern Colombia + Panama (~1.14-1.22M km²).
- **1831-1858: Republic of New Granada.** Official name of the Colombian state after Gran Colombia's dissolution.
- **1858-1863: Granadine Confederation.** Federal reorganization.
- **1863-1886: United States of Colombia.** Federal republic period.
- **1886: Republic of Colombia.** Centralized constitution adopted. CShapes-2.0 records the first Colombia entry from 1886-01-01.
- **1903-11-03: Panama separation.** Panama declared independence from Colombia, supported by the United States. End-of-row event.
