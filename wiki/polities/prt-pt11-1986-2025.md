# Norte (NUTS II region of Portugal)

## Summary

Norte is one of Portugal's NUTS II statistical regions, covering the northern part of the country -- the districts of Viana do Castelo, Braga, Porto, Vila Real and Braganca, plus part of Aveiro district, with Porto as its principal city. It was created in 1986 when Portugal, in preparation for EU accession, adopted the European Union's Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics (NUTS) framework, which divides the country into statistical regions for data collection and EU regional-policy purposes. `type: subnational` because Norte is not itself a sovereign state or the national polity -- Portugal (PRT-1800-2025) holds sovereignty throughout -- but is instead a defined statistical/administrative reporting unit beneath it, alongside sibling NUTS II regions such as Centro, Lisboa, Alentejo, Algarve, and the autonomous regions of the Azores and Madeira. This entry exists specifically to carry the region's own statistical series separately from the national total, because Norte cannot BE the national polity in the years it reports without double-counting against Portugal's other NUTS regions, each of which is (or will be) its own row.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures reporting-unit data labelled PT11/Norte for Portugal's Norte NUTS II region, with the routing decision indicating a source series spanning 1870-2020 (151 total data years), of which the years 1986-2025 (this entry's span) are routed here as 'proposed' rather than 'unrouted'. This data was previously at risk of being matched wholesale to the national polity row PRT-1800-2025, which is wrong for two reasons: that row is already claimed by other subnational units (e.g. PTFA/Algarve) for overlapping years, and a subnational region cannot BE the national total in the same years without double-counting the same underlying economic activity across multiple rows. The entity is confirmed distinct by its own formal statistical definition: Eurostat's NUTS classification defines Norte (code PT11) as a discrete NUTS II region with its own boundary, separate from all other Portuguese NUTS II regions (Centro, Lisboa, Alentejo, Algarve, Acores, Madeira), and this same create_new split (pre-1989 unroutable / 1989+ proposed) has already been recorded for sibling units PT15, PT16, PT17, PT18 and PTAV in the same batch, confirming Norte belongs to a systematic and already-validated set of distinct regional reporting units rather than being an isolated one-off classification.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The registered source `gadm-4.1-adm1` is the intended route (`polygon_route: registered_source_unfetched`), but the locally cached copy of GADM 4.1 admin-1 data is restricted to a set of 81 countries that does not include Portugal, so no feature can be located on disk today. `polygon_status` is therefore `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `assigned` -- this is a genuine gap, not a placeholder.

**Territory description:** Norte occupies the northern third of mainland Portugal, bordering Spain's Galicia region to the north and east, the Atlantic coast to the west, and Portugal's Centro region to the south. It comprises the districts of Viana do Castelo, Braga, Porto, Vila Real and Braganca in full, plus the northern part of Aveiro district (Aveiro itself is split between Norte and Centro at the finer NUTS III level). Its largest city and the region's namesake economic center is Porto. By area, Norte covers roughly 21,300 km2 (Eurostat's published figure for NUTS2 region PT11), making it one of the largest and most populous of Portugal's NUTS II regions alongside Lisboa. No polygon is attached to this entry yet, so no measured-geometry figure exists here; the 21,300 km2 stated above is Eurostat's own published regional area statistic, not a measurement of any polygon in this GeoPackage.

Constructing a polygon later would most plausibly be a union of the five whole districts plus the Norte-side portion of Aveiro from a fetched GADM Portugal adm1/adm2 layer, or alternatively a direct fetch of Eurostat GISCO's official NUTS2021 boundary files, which would avoid the Aveiro-split approximation problem entirely but whose licence terms have not yet been verified for this repository's use.

## Predecessors and successors

Norte has no predecessor polity row: the pre-1986 years during which its territory was measured (1870-1988 per the routing decision) are currently unrouted, not attributed to any earlier-named polity, because NUTS II Norte is a 1986 Eurostat statistical construct with no single historical administrative precursor of the same name or boundary -- northern Portugal was instead governed through separate distritos (Viana do Castelo, Braga, Porto, Vila Real, Braganca, part of Aveiro) with no unified reporting tier until NUTS. It has no successor: Norte remains an active NUTS II region as of 2025, so end_year is the open-ended present rather than a transition to a different polity.

## Sourced claims

- Portugal adopted the EU's NUTS (Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics) regional classification system in 1986, coinciding with its accession to the European Economic Community, per the country convention document governing this batch of PRT NUTS entries.
- Eurostat publishes an official area figure for NUTS2 region PT11 (Norte) of approximately 21,300 km2, derived from its GISCO geographic reference dataset for Portuguese NUTS regions.
- GADM 4.1's locally cached admin-1 layer is restricted to a fixed set of 81 countries and does not currently include Portugal, per the polygon_reasoning recorded in the routing decision for this unit.
- Two independent data sources (mitchell-style series referenced in the routing decision and Eurostat regional statistics) report figures under the PT11/Norte label starting around 1986-1989, distinct from Portugal's national total series.

## Decisions

### d-dominant-pattern-followed

**Followed the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end code pattern**

Portugal has no existing subnational precedent in the table (0 prior PRT subnational rows), so this entry sets the country's convention. Rather than inventing something bespoke, I followed the pattern used by 57 of 66 existing subnational rows (e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025, DZA-CVD-1902-1919): <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>. Norte is not a historical territory with a name of its own (unlike ALK or HYD) -- it is a EU statistical construct (NUTS II) introduced in 1986, so the bespoke-code pattern used for named historical territories does not apply. PT11 is Eurostat's own NUTS code for Norte, making it a natural, checkable subunit token, analogous to using ISO 3166-2 codes for Spanish provinces.

### d-start-year-1986-not-1989

**Used start_year 1986 per country convention default, not 1989**

The routing decision notes tension between the country convention's stated default start_year of 1986 (when the EU NUTS framework was introduced) and the sibling coverage split's boundary of 1989 (first year data is actually routed as 'proposed' rather than 'unrouted'). I chose 1986 because that is what the country convention document defines as the applicable default for all Portuguese NUTS units in this batch, and using a different year for Norte alone would break consistency with sibling entries (PT15, PT16, PT17, PT18, PTAV, etc.) that are expected to also use 1986. The open question below flags that this should be reconciled across the batch rather than resolved unilaterally by this single entry.

## Open questions

### oq-nuts-ii-geometry-needs-eurostat-gisco

**A union of whole districts is not an acceptable proxy for this NUTS II boundary, measured**

GADM's adm1 layer for Portugal is its 18 mainland DISTRICTS, and NUTS II boundaries cut across them, so a union of whole districts under-covers this region. Measured against the official NUTS II area:

| region | district union | official | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norte | 18,156 km² | 21,278 km² | −14.7% |
| Centro | 27,397 km² | 28,199 km² | −2.8% |
| Alentejo | 23,740 km² | 31,605 km² | −24.9% |

For Norte that is -14.7%. Attaching it would publish a polygon missing a large share of the territory while `polygon_status` claimed it was real, so the geometry is left unassigned. The correct source is the Eurostat GISCO NUTS geometry at the matching vintage — the same release whose attribute table already supplies data/final/nuts_code_names.csv — which needs registering as a source and fetching. Algarve is the one exception and is already assigned: NUTS II Algarve is coterminous with the district of Faro.


### oq-1986-vs-1989-start-year

**Should start_year be 1986 (NUTS introduction) or 1989 (first reported data)?**

The country convention document states a default start_year of 1986 for Portuguese NUTS regions, but the sibling-verdicts coverage split (used for PT15, PT16, PT17, PT18, PTAV and others in the same batch) treats 1986-1988 as 'unroutable' and only begins the 'proposed' polity coverage at 1989. This page uses 1986 to match the stated convention default and to keep Norte consistent with how the convention document is written, but if the sibling entries were actually built with start_year 1989, this entry will be inconsistent with them. This needs to be reconciled across the whole NUTS batch by checking what start_year each already-created sibling PRT-PTxx page actually used, and this page's start_year corrected to match if 1989 turns out to be the operative convention in practice.

### oq-1870-1988-data-unrouted

**119 of 151 data years (1870-1988) remain unrouted for this reporting unit**

The routing decision explicitly flags that 79% of PRT-PT11's data years (1870-1988) are marked unroutable because they predate the 1986 NUTS system, yet the source clearly reports values for the region across that whole span, meaning some territory was being measured under an earlier administrative name. This page only covers 1986-2025 and does not resolve where the pre-1986 Norte-area data (roughly 116 years of measured values) should be routed -- to the national PRT-1800-2025 row (risking double-counting with other pre-1989 subnational claims already on that row, per the routing_reasoning), to an earlier named administrative predecessor of the Norte region (e.g. a historical Minho/Douro Litoral distrito grouping), or left genuinely unroutable. This is unresolved and should be revisited once the full batch of Portuguese NUTS entries is complete and the pre-1986 territorial history of northern Portugal's districts is worked out.

### oq-gadm-portugal-not-fetched

**GADM 4.1 adm1 source is not fetched for Portugal; construction method for Norte is undecided**

polygon_status is 'unassigned' because gadm-4.1-adm1 is a registered source but the local copy is restricted to 81 countries, none of which is Portugal, so no feature can be assigned yet. Even once fetched, Norte is not a single GADM adm1 feature -- it would need to be constructed as a union of Portuguese distritos (Viana do Castelo, Braga, Porto, Vila Real, Braganca, plus part of Aveiro), and Aveiro is split between Norte and Centro at NUTS III level, so a pure distrito union can only approximate the true Eurostat/GISCO NUTS II boundary. Whether to fetch GADM and construct an approximation, or instead fetch Eurostat GISCO's NUTS2021 boundaries directly (a cleaner match but with unverified licence terms), is unresolved and should be decided before polygon_status can move past unassigned.
