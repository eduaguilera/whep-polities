# Malaya and Singapore


> **`type` corrected `national` -> `aggregate` on 2026-08-05 (issue 44).** "Malaya and Singapore" is a JOINT REPORTING UNIT, the same kind of row as EGYSUD-1934-1956, F237-1954-1975 and BLX-1850-1999, all of which are typed `aggregate`.
>
> This is not cosmetic. `matchlib.pick_by_year` resolves a label to an ISO family and
> then prefers `polity_type == "national"`. Eight polities carry `iso3_code = MYS` and
> **all eight were typed `national`**, so the preference discriminated nothing and the
> winner was decided by family ORDER. FAOSTAT area 131 "Malaysia" for 1961 resolved to
> `BNB-1881-1963` — British North Borneo, 72,557 km² — instead of the federation.
## Summary

A **composed-union** row for FAO series that report the **Federation of Malaya
figure as including Singapore** (footnotes for 1949–1960: *"Includes Federation
of Malaya and Singapore"* / *"Including Singapore"*). Singapore was a separate
Crown Colony, so a "Malaya" figure that folds it in covers the combined
territory and must not map to Malaya alone. Constituents:
[MYS-1946-1957](mys-1946-1957.md) / [MYS-1957-1963](mys-1957-1963.md) and
[SGP-1946-1963](sgp-1946-1963.md).

Period 1946–1963 spans the post-war Crown Colony era to the formation of
Malaysia (1963-09-16), after which Singapore is part of Malaysia (then
independent 1965).
