# Afghanistan (1893-1919)

## Summary

Afghanistan (1893-1919) covers the Durand Line era, from the signing of the Durand Line Agreement on 12 November 1893 -- which defined the boundary between Afghanistan and British India -- until the Treaty of Rawalpindi in 1919, which ended the Third Anglo-Afghan War and granted Afghanistan full sovereignty over its foreign affairs. During this period Afghanistan was effectively a British protectorate, with Britain controlling its external relations while the Amir retained internal authority.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes classifies this as a protectorate under British rule. The Durand Line established the eastern boundary, while the Afghanistan--Iran boundary was settled between 1872 and 1935. The Wakhan Corridor was created by the 1895 Anglo-Russian agreement.
