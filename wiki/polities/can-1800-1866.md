# Canada (pre-Confederation)

## Summary

Canada (pre-Confederation) covers British North America from the start of the WHEP time window (1800) until 1866, just before Confederation (1 July 1867) united the Province of Canada, Nova Scotia, and New Brunswick into the Dominion of Canada. During this period the territory was administered as several separate British colonies, including Upper Canada, Lower Canada, and the Hudson's Bay Company territories. The database uses 1866 as the end year because the CShapes polygon changes at Confederation.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The territory encompassed the British colonies in North America. The Canada--United States boundary is "the longest land boundary between any two countries in the world" at 3,987 miles (6,416 km) in the south, plus the Alaska boundary of 1,538 miles (2,477 km).

## Open questions

### oq-multiple-colonies

Pre-Confederation British North America consisted of several distinct colonies (Upper/Lower Canada, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, etc.) with their own governance structures. Whether the WHEP database should track these as a single entity or multiple rows is an open question.
