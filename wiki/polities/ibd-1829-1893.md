# Ibadan City-State

## Summary

The Ibadan city-state from its founding as a war camp for Yoruba refugees c.1829 (after the collapse of the Oyo Empire) to the 15 August 1893 British protectorate treaty signed by Baale Fijabi with Lagos acting Governor G.C. Denton. During the intervening six decades Ibadan emerged as the dominant Yoruba military power, controlling an extensive tributary empire across northern and eastern Yorubaland.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Covers Ibadan and its tributary reach across northern Yorubaland. The polygon should be read as "area of Ibadan military hegemony" rather than a surveyed border — tributary control waxed and waned with 19th-century Yoruba warfare.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor (CSV):** OYO-1800-1836 (Oyo Empire) — Ibadan inherited the Oyo sphere of military dominance in Yorubaland.
- **Successor:** [sni-1899-1906](sni-1899-1906.md) — Southern Nigeria Protectorate (1899–1906). Ibadan is one of three predecessors of SNI-1899-1906 alongside BKN (Benin) and IJB (Ijebu).
