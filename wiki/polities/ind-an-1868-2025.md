# Andaman and Nicobar Islands

## Summary

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands, "a union territory of India comprising 836 islands", occupying "a
total land area of approximately 8,249 km2". "The islands became part of the British Raj in 1868.
During the Second World War, the islands were invaded by the Empire of Japan". It sits inside the India
rows, [ind-1800-1886](ind-1800-1886.md) to [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md) (for 1937-1947 inside
[inu-1937-1947](inu-1937-1947.md), the part of British India that became the Dominion).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §andaman-and-nicobar](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#andaman-and-nicobar)

## Why this entry exists

The labels `british andaman and nicobar islands` (1909-1945, 33 pairs) and `india: andaman and
nicobar islands` (1934-1938, 1 pair) report the islands alone: **34 (year, label) pairs**. No polity
was the islands, and routing them to British India would put a part on its whole. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm1 `IND.1_1` measures **7,611 km²** (ESRI:54034), 8% under the stated 8,249 km²
(GADM's simplified coastline of many small islands). The island group has not changed.

## Polygon status

`assigned` — GADM 4.1 adm1 `IND.1_1`.

None: the territory is continuous through the span.

## Data routing

`british andaman and nicobar islands` 1909-1945 and `india: andaman and nicobar islands`
1934-1938 (whep-normalize): 34 pairs, by alias. Nothing in layer B routes here: its labels for this ground are the whole-country ones.

## Decisions

- **Not registered as a part of the India rows in `polity_composition.csv`**: CShapes' India
  polygons cover only 78% of GADM's islands, so a geometric containment check would fail on
  coastline detail; the container edges above carry the relation.
- **Starts 1868**, the year the quoted source gives for the islands joining British India. The
  1942-1945 Japanese occupation does not split the row: the territory did not change.
