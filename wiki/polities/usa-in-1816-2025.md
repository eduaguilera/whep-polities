# Indiana (state of the United States)

## Summary

This entry represents Indiana, a state of the United States, from its admission to the Union on December 11, 1816 to the present (2025). `type: subnational` because Indiana is a constituent state within the sovereign United States, not an independent polity — sovereignty and the national data chain remain with the USA rows (USA-1803-1848 through USA-1959-2025); this row exists to give state-level statistical series their own reporting territory, distinct from national USA totals. No prior polity row in the table represented Indiana specifically: the only USA-family candidates were national totals (which are roughly ninety times Indiana's land area) and the Territory of Alaska (an unrelated, non-contiguous U.S. territory). GADM 4.1 confirms Indiana as a real, distinct administrative unit (feature USA.15_1), and this entry mirrors the already-established precedent of USA-CALIFORNIA in this table: an ordinary, non-original, non-Alaska/Hawaii state admitted well after 1800, coded and dated the same way.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Indiana-specific statistical reporting that, prior to this harness decision, had no dedicated polity row and would otherwise be matched to the national USA rows, which cover the entire United States (about 9.8 million km2 versus Indiana's 94,326 km2 — roughly 100x over-broad as a container for state-level figures). No specific dataset rows have yet been confirmed as routed to this code (see open question below); this page is created by the harness's routing decision (unit_id USA-INDIANA, verdict create_new) ahead of, and to enable, that matching. What confirms Indiana is a distinct entity worth its own row: it is one of the fifty United States, with its own government, statistical agencies, and GADM-recognized administrative boundary (USA.15_1) separate from every neighboring state and from the national aggregate — the same standard already applied to sibling entries such as California and Alaska in this table.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `USA.15_1` (Indiana), `polygon_status: assigned` — a direct, non-proxy match, since GADM's present-day Indiana boundary is the same boundary the state has held since admission in 1816. This is a registered source already used for sibling U.S. state entries in this table (e.g. `ALK-1867-1959` uses the same GADM release for Alaska).

**Area:** approximately 94,326 km2 (36,420 sq mi), the state's well-known modern land+water area, consistent with the measured area of the attached GADM geometry. Indiana lies in the American Midwest, bordered by Lake Michigan and the state of Michigan to the north, Ohio to the east, Kentucky to the south (across the Ohio River), and Illinois to the west — a shape and location any reader can locate on a modern U.S. map without difficulty.

**Why the polygon is a direct assignment, not a proxy:** Indiana's borders were set at or shortly after admission (the Ohio River line with Kentucky, the survey line with Ohio, the 1816 ten-mile northern extension to include a Lake Michigan shoreline, and the later-fixed line with Illinois) and have not changed materially since. There is no analogous case here to the Manchuria-style proxy problem, where the reporting unit and the available geometry diverge — the unit this row is created to hold (the state of Indiana) and the geometry available (modern Indiana) are the same territory throughout the row's span.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: before December 11, 1816, the territory that became Indiana was part of Indiana Territory (organized 1800, itself carved from the Northwest Territory), which is not separately represented in this polity table and is not this row's predecessor since the table currently has no pre-statehood Indiana Territory entry. No successor: Indiana remains a current U.S. state and this row's end_year (2025) is the table's open-ended present, matching the convention used for other still-existing subnational U.S. entries.

## Sourced claims

- Indiana was admitted to the Union as the 19th state on December 11, 1816, having been organized as Indiana Territory in 1800 from the Northwest Territory.
- Indiana's modern land area is approximately 94,326 km2 (36,420 sq mi), per standard U.S. Census Bureau state-area figures.
- GADM 4.1 admin-1 data records Indiana as feature USA.15_1 with boundaries consistent with the state's current legal boundary, which has been stable since the resolution of the 1816 Michigan boundary extension.

Indiana is an ordinary U.S. state admitted through the standard territorial process, with no distinct historical name or contested-sovereignty period of its own (unlike Alaska Territory 1867-1959, coded bespoke as ALK-1867-1959, or historical named territories like Hyderabad or the Ryukyus). It therefore follows the dominant 57-of-66 subnational pattern, <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, giving USA-IN-1816-2025, rather than a bespoke code. ## Open questions

### oq-boundary-vintage

**Modern GADM boundary used as proxy back to 1816 admission**

The attached polygon (gadm-4.1-adm1, feature USA.15_1) reflects Indiana's present-day boundary. Indiana's external state lines were essentially fixed at admission in 1816 (the boundary with Illinois, Ohio, Michigan, and Kentucky along the Ohio River and the Michigan cession of 1816), so the vintage risk is low, but no historical-boundary source has been checked against the 1816-1850 window specifically to confirm there was no later adjustment (e.g. minor river-boundary shifts with Kentucky/Illinois that have occurred elsewhere along the Ohio River due to channel migration).
