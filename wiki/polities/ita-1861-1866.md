# Italy (1861–1866)

## Summary

This sub-period covers the Kingdom of Italy from its proclamation on
**17 March 1861** to the acquisition of Venetia at the **Vienna Peace
Treaty of 3 October 1866**. It was split from the former single row
`ITA-1861-1919` to correct a polygon-vintage overstatement: the CShapes
1886 polygon includes both Venetia (~18,000 km²) and Rome/Lazio
(~12,000 km²), neither of which was Italian territory during 1861–1866.
The split was right; the polygon it was given was not. Since 2026-08-13
this row uses CShapes-Europe's **own 1862–1866 step**, which excludes both.

Predecessors: [sar-1800-1860](sar-1800-1860.md), [pap-1800-1870](pap-1800-1870.md),
TWO-1800-1860, TUS-1800-1860, DMO-1800-1860, DPA-1800-1860.
Successor: [ita-1866-1870](ita-1866-1870.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-Europe, `Id=325`, polygon vintage **1862**,
  which selects that source's `From=1862, To=1866` step. Areas, all on the
  equal-area projection: **247,918 km² as published** in
  `polities_database.gpkg`, 247,820 km² for the source step before the build's
  validity repair (`polygon_feature_index.csv`), 247,268 km² in the source's
  own `Area` field. The three agree to 0.3%; none of the spread is
  territorial.
- **True 1861 extent.** At proclamation, Italy had 250,320 km²
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §area](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#area).
  Regions included: Piedmont, Lombardy, Liguria, Tuscany, most of the
  Papal States (except Rome/Lazio), Emilia-Romagna, Sicily, Sardinia, Umbria.
  The bound step is **1.0% below** that figure — the closest any polygon in
  this database gets to it.
- **What is excluded.** Venetia (Austrian Lombardo-Venetia, ceded 1866);
  Rome and Lazio (Papal temporal sovereignty until 1870). The 1862–1866
  step excludes both, which is why its capital field reads **Turin** —
  Italy's capital 1861–1865 — while the 1871+ steps read Rome.
- **Why the vintage is 1862 and not 1861.** CShapes-Europe has no step
  covering 1861 or 1866: it drops the transition years and resumes at 1862.
  1862 is inside this row's span (1861–1865 inclusive), so no proxy or
  out-of-span vintage is involved.
- **What this row used to carry, and why it was wrong.** Until 2026-08-13 the
  frontmatter said `polygon_feature_year: 1886`, `polygon_status: estimate`,
  and this section stated that "no such polygon exists in CShapes 2.0 or
  CShapes-Europe (Italy has a single backdated polygon)". That was false.
  CShapes-Europe — the source this row already named — offers five steps for
  Id 325 (1862–1866, 1867–1870, 1871–1885, 1886–1918, 1919–2023), and the
  first of them is exactly the pre-Venetia, pre-Rome Italy this row needs.
  The row therefore published 284,847 km² — **36,929 km² more than the step
  it should have used (+14.9% of it), and +13.8% above the 250,320 km²
  external figure** — and published geometry byte-identical to
  `ITA-1866-1870` and `ITA-1870-1919`. Issue 121, signals A and B of
  `validate_polygon_period_fit`.

## Sourced claims

- **1861-03-17: Kingdom of Italy proclaimed.** Victor Emmanuel II of Sardinia
  declared King of Italy. Population: 21,777,334; area: 250,320 km²
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1861-unification](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1861-unification).
- **1866: Third Italian War of Independence.** Italy allied with Prussia against
  Austria; gained Venetia by the Vienna Peace Treaty of 3 October 1866
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This event ends this sub-period.

## Decisions

- **Split from ITA-1861-1919 (2026-06-29).** The Venetia (1866) and Rome
  (1870) acquisitions are genuine border-change events within the former
  single row. That reasoning stands.
- **Rebound 1886 → 1862 vintage, `estimate` → `assigned` (2026-08-13).**
  The overstatement the split was created to document did not need
  documenting; it needed a different step of the same feature. Measured on the
  published GeoPackage — before: 284,847 km²; after: 247,918 km². External
  check: 250,320 km² at proclamation, so the error fell from +13.8% to −1.0%.
  `polygon_status` is now `assigned` because the polygon is a real
  contemporaneous boundary, not an approximation.
- **No declared `polygon_area_km2`.** Left null deliberately. The only figure
  available to declare would be copied off the geometry, which
  `validate_polygons` classes as self-referential; the binding is instead
  guarded relationally by `validate_polygon_period_fit`.
- **Constructed polygon no longer needed.** The former
  `oq-gis-constructed-polygon` proposed subtracting Venetia and Lazio from
  the 1886 feature to get "near zero" error. CShapes-Europe already ships
  that geometry, so the open question is closed rather than answered.
