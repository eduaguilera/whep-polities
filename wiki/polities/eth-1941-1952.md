# Ethiopia (1941-1952)

## Summary

An **11-year row** covering restored Ethiopian sovereignty after British liberation from Italian occupation. Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941. Row ends with the federation of Eritrea with Ethiopia (1952).

Predecessor: [eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md). Successor: [eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md). Occupation period: [aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, feature 530, vintage 1907 (proxy). The 34-year drift is benign: Ethiopian borders were identical pre-invasion (1907-1936) and post-liberation (1941-1952). The CShapes 1907 polygon correctly represents the territory for this period.
- **polygon_status: proxy** -- no CShapes vintage exists for the 1941-1952 period specifically; the 1907 vintage is used as a documented approximation.
- Approximate area: ~1,000,000 km2.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md) -- sovereignty interrupted by Italian occupation 1936-1941.
- **Successor:** [eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md) -- Eritrea federated with Ethiopia (1952), expanding territory by ~125,000 km2.

## Sourced claims

- **1941-04-06: British forces enter Addis Ababa.** East Africa Campaign defeated Italian forces. AOI dissolved de facto.
- **1941-05-05: Haile Selassie returns.** Sovereignty restored. Start-of-row event.
- **1941-11-27: Last Italian resistance (Gondar) surrenders.** AOI completely dissolved.
- **1952: Eritrea federated with Ethiopia.** UN General Assembly Resolution 390 (V). End-of-row event; see ETH-1952-1993.

## Decisions

- **Split from ETH-1907-1952.** The old 46-year row was split at 1936 and 1941. This row covers restored sovereignty only.
- **Polygon proxy.** CShapes 1907 vintage used as proxy; drift is benign (borders identical). Documented per wiki page standards.
