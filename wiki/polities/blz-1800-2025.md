# Belize

## Summary

Belize (formerly British Honduras) is a Central American country on the Caribbean coast, bordered by Guatemala to the west and south and Mexico to the north. Biger 1995 shows Belize on the regional map of Central America with its capital at Belmopan, noting that in 1821 the Central American colonies separated from Spain and in 1823 Honduras, Guatemala, Nicaragua and Belize combined to form the United Provinces of Central America [biger-1995 &sect;belize](../sources/biger-1995.md#belize). WHEP tracks Belize as a continuous row from 1800, covering the period as a British settlement (later colony) through independence in 1981, because its territory remained essentially constant throughout.

## Territorial extent

Belize's territory has remained constant at approximately 22,966 sq. km. throughout the WHEP period. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Guatemala has maintained a longstanding territorial claim to Belize, but the boundaries shown in Biger's 1995 map reflect the internationally recognized borders.
