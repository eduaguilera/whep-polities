# South Seas Islands under Japan (Nan'yō Guntō, 1914-1945)

## Summary

The Micronesian islands north of the equator that Germany held until 1914: the Caroline Islands,
Palau, the Marshall Islands and the Northern Mariana Islands (Guam, American since 1898, was never
part of them). The Japanese navy occupied them in October 1914, civil administration was built up
from 1920, and Japan held them as the League of Nations Class C "Mandate for the German Possessions
in the Pacific Ocean Lying North of the Equator" until its defeat in 1944-1945.
[wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13 §mandate-conferral](../sources/wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13.md#mandate-conferral)
The island groups administered were the same throughout: the six districts of 1922 (Saipan,
Palau, Yap, Truk, Ponape, Jaluit) cover all four modern successor territories.
[wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13 §administration](../sources/wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13.md#administration)

This row is the **whole mandate as one reporting unit**. It is typed `aggregate` because one of its
parts, the Carolines ([car-1920-1945](car-1920-1945.md)), has a row of its own, and the other island
groups have rows for other eras ([mhl-1874-2025](mhl-1874-2025.md)). This is the pattern of
[gct-1919-1956](gct-1919-1956.md) and [papng-1920-1949](papng-1920-1949.md): a combined unit beside
polities that also exist separately.

## Why this entry exists

Issue 687 matched every layer-B iia series cell by cell against the raw IIA extract
(`harmonized_data.xlsx`), on the exact `(unit, year or period, value)` triple.

- **`micronesia (federated states of)`** (eggs, raw centrifugal sugar, tobacco area and production;
  1928-1942, 27 rows) is raw **`japanese south pacific`** in every cell. That is the mandate total,
  not the Carolines. The biggest series, cane sugar (1934-1938 average 63,900 t; 1937 74,500 t), is
  the Nan'yō Kōhatsu industry on **Saipan, Tinian and Rota**, in the Marianas. Sugar was more than
  60% of the mandate's revenue by the early 1930s.
  [wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13 §economy](../sources/wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13.md#economy)
  These rows had been routed to [car-1920-1945](car-1920-1945.md), whose polygon (modern FSM,
  702 km²) contains none of the Marianas and none of Palau.
- **`palau`** phosphate (item `p`, 1909-1932) is raw **`japanese south pacific: palau-angaur`** in
  14 of 14 cells (one of them, 1913, is a zero, which matches anything), the Angaur mine in Palau. The 1909-1914 years stay on
  [gcar-1899-1914](gcar-1899-1914.md), whose polygon is Palau. The 1915-1932 years had been routed to
  CAR-1920-1945 (partly by back-cast), whose polygon does not contain Angaur. This row contains it.

So this row takes the `micronesia (federated states of)` label for 1920-1945 and `palau` for
1915-1932 (8 rows). Palau is a part of this territory, so the phosphate sits inside the polygon it is
attributed to. The raw extract has no other phosphate series for the mandate in those years, so
nothing is counted twice.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** The polygon is `constructed`: the union of the GADM 4.1 country
outlines of the four successor territories, FSM (the Carolines east of Palau), MHL (the Marshalls),
MNP (the Northern Marianas) and PLW (Palau). It measures **2,063 km²** (ESRI:54034).
[gadm-4.1 §What it adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

- **Why a proxy and not `assigned`.** GADM draws modern coastlines. They include reef and lagoon
  area that land-area figures leave out, and the Trust Territory page records about 1,791 km² of
  land for the same islands. The extra ~270 km² (15%) is coastline convention, not territory: no
  island group was added or lost between 1914 and 1947.
- **Why this composition.** These are exactly the island groups the mandate held, and the same four
  make up [ttpi-1947-1994](ttpi-1947-1994.md), which is built from the same members. So this row and
  its successor sit on the same ground.
- **Why no historical source.** CShapes 2.0 codes Japan's colonies (Korea, Taiwan, Karafuto) but not
  the mandate, and Cliopatria has no feature for Micronesia. Modern outlines are the only source that
  separates these islands at all.

## Polygon status

`proxy` — constructed union, 2,063 km², modern coastlines. See *Territorial extent*.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [gcar-1899-1914](gcar-1899-1914.md), the German Carolines, the German colonial row
  this database carries for these islands. German New Guinea's Marshall and Mariana districts have
  no rows of their own.
- **Successor:** [ttpi-1947-1994](ttpi-1947-1994.md), the UN Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands,
  from the 18 July 1947 trusteeship agreement.
  [wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13 §end-of-mandate](../sources/wikipedia-nanyo-2026-08-13.md#end-of-mandate)
  The 1945-1947 US military government has no row.

## Key dates

- October 1914: the Japanese navy occupies German Micronesia north of the equator.
- 1920: League of Nations Class C mandate conferred.
- April 1922: civil administration in six districts, and a Governor of the South Seas.
- March 1932: the Nan'yō-chō (South Seas Government) takes over from the navy.
- 1944-1945: US forces take the islands.
- 18 July 1947: the mandate is revoked and the Trust Territory established.

## Decisions

- **Span 1914-1945.** Starts with the 1914 occupation, not the 1920 mandate, because the territory
  and the administering power were the same from 1914, and the `palau` phosphate for 1915-1919 is
  from this territory. Ends in 1945, as [car-1920-1945](car-1920-1945.md) does.
- **Type `aggregate`.** It coexists with the Carolines row, which is one of its parts.
- **Scope of the reroute.** Only the two iia labels issue 687 fingerprinted. The fao1952
  `Pacific Islands (U S Trust)` 1934-1938 sugar is the same territory under a post-war name and is
  tracked in issue 684. It is not moved here.
