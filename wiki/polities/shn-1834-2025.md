# Saint Helena island

## Territory and evidence

This is Saint Helena island alone, excluding Ascension and the Tristan da Cunha group. Its former 1967 end was a constitutional/status boundary, not evidence that the island ceased to exist as a reporting territory. The 2009 constitution distinguishes the three territories within their common grouping. [Constitution 2009](../sources/st-helena-constitution-2009.md).

The retained 1834 start records the existing catalogue coverage, not a newly asserted coastline change. Earlier island continuity is outside this correction. No separate post 1967 island is created. ISO3 SHN belongs to the composite grouping and is no longer attached to this island-only row.

GADM 4.1 ADM 1 SHN.2_1 is a modern coastline proxy; measured area 122.661178 km² in ESRI:54034. It does not prove an exact nineteenth-century shoreline. Historical island-level aliases remain on this identity.

The legacy fao1952 alias 'St Helena & Dependencies' 1937–1951 remains unresolved source research: its small population and lack of duplicate cells do not establish an island-only footprint. This change preserves that prior routing rather than asserting it is verified; the original table's coverage and retrospective conventions require a separate appraisal in [issue743](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/743). Modern FAOSTAT 187 and FishStat 654 explicitly name the composite and must not route here.

## Revision

Replaces `SHN-1834-1967` for the same land footprint with corrected temporal coverage. Refs [issue742](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/742). Science decision: source-defined reporting extent and supported annual validity, not a statehood-date substitution.

The pre-existing catalogue reference area 123 km² (unchanged, original source attribution not independently reverified in this correction) is retained separately from the measured 122.661178 km² geometry. The existing stated-area ledger also records an IIA 122 km² figure and a conflicting FAO 1952 figure 330 km²; the latter is part of the unresolved historical scope question, not evidence for enlarging this island polygon.
