# Hungary (1918-1919)

Formerly coded HUN-1918-1920; end_year shortened to 1919 on 2026-06-30 to fix Trianon polygon straddle (issue hun-1918-1920-trianon-polygon-straddle). Spans Republic proclamation 1918-11-16 through end-1919. Polygon: histogis-1860-habsburg, 325,421 km² (pre-Trianon Transleithania). Polygon vintage drift (~58-60 years ahead of 1860 vintage) is benign: Transleithanian borders were stable 1867-1920.

## Trianon boundary: why end_year=1919

The Treaty of Trianon (4 June 1920) reduced Hungary from ~325,000 km² to ~93,000 km² — a 72% territorial loss. Annual agricultural data spanning 1918-1920 includes harvest-year 1920 observations (wheat n=2, maize n=2, sugar beet n=3). The sugar beet n=3 confirms a 1920-harvest observation is present.

Harvest-year 1920 data reflects the post-Trianon rump state (~93,000 km²). Matching it to this polity's 325,421 km² polygon would overstate territory ~3.5×. Ending this polity at 1919 ensures year-1920 data routes automatically to HUN-1920-1938 (CShapes 2.0, ~93,000 km²).
