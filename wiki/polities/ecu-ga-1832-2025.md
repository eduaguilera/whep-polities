# Galápagos Islands (Ecuador)

## Summary

The Galápagos archipelago, 900 km west of the South American mainland. The islands "form the
Galápagos Province of the Republic of Ecuador", and were "officially named the Ecuador Archipelago
... following their annexation by Ecuador in 1832".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §galapagos](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#galapagos)

One row for 1832-2025, typed `subnational`, inside the Ecuador rows
([ecu-1830-1942](ecu-1830-1942.md), [ecu-1942-2025](ecu-1942-2025.md)), whose CShapes polygons include
the islands.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `Ecuador  Galapagos Islands` (double space) in its 1947 land-use table: `use total`
768 thousand ha, i.e. **7,680 km²**. **2 rows** resolved to no polity. They are a part of Ecuador,
and an alias to Ecuador would file a 7,680 km² land-use table as the whole country's for 1947
(FAO's own Ecuador table is for 1949).
