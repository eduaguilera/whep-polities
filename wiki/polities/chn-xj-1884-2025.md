# Sinkiang (Xinjiang)

## Summary

Xinjiang, north-western China: "the Qing dynasty established Xinjiang ('new frontier') as a province
in 1884", and "in 1955, the status of Xinjiang was changed from a province to a 'Uygur Autonomous
Region'".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §xinjiang](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#xinjiang)
The 1955 change was of status, not of ground, so one row runs 1884-2025. It is typed
`subnational`, inside each China row of the span.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `China Sinkiang` as one of the five parts of `China` in its 1947 land-use table
(`use total` 171,193 thousand ha, i.e. **1,711,930 km²**; arable 994 thousand ha); see
[chn-p22-1939-1953](chn-p22-1939-1953.md) for the whole family. **2 rows** resolved to no polity.

`fao1952-china-whole-and-five-parts` warns that routing this label to the China row would take the
double-count on that row from 63% to 87% of the whole, because `China` is already there. That is
why it had been left unrouted. A row of its own does not do that: the whole and the part are on
different polities, and the pair is registered as nested (`sum_risk`) in `polity_composition.csv`.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** GADM 4.1 adm1 `CHN.28_1` (Xinjiang Uygur), **1,599,332 km²** measured
(ESRI:54034), against FAO's 1,711,930 (0.93). GADM files the Aksai Chin and other disputed ground
under separate `Z0*` codes, which are not included.
[gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

`proxy` because the outline is modern and the row runs from 1884. 97.0-99.7% of it lies inside the
China rows' CShapes polygons across the span; the rest is border slivers.
