# United Kingdom of the Netherlands

## Summary

This row tracks the Netherlands entity from **1800** (the WHEP
database floor) to **1830** (Belgian Revolution)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The polity_name "United Kingdom of the
Netherlands" is strictly valid only from **16 March 1815** (when
William I declared himself king) to **4 October 1830** (Belgian
independence declaration). The 1800–1815 window is pre-United
Kingdom and belongs to the Batavian Republic (1795–1806) and the
Kingdom of Holland (1806–1810), both French client states, followed
by direct French annexation (1810–1813) and the brief Sovereign
Principality (1813–1815). The 15-year label anachronism is
comparable to the AUH 1800–1804 issue.

- **1800 start** is the database floor. The CSV has `predecessor: NA`.
- **1830 end** is a real territorial event: the Belgian Revolution
  broke out on 25 August 1830 and Belgium was declared independent
  on 4 October 1830
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1830-revolution](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1830-revolution).
  The territory lost was approximately 42% of the United Kingdom's
  area [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The CSV notes: "United Kingdom of Netherlands (1815-1830) including
Belgium and Luxembourg. ~71K km². Belgian Revolution 1830 led to
-42% territory loss" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [nld-1830-2025](nld-1830-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (Netherlands 1815-1819)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). A single Cliopatria time-step applied across
  30 years [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §single-time-step](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md#single-time-step). The polygon
  represents the post-Congress-of-Vienna configuration including
  Belgium and Luxembourg. ~71K km² (68,095 km² per Wikipedia).
- **No CShapes coverage.** CShapes 2.0 starts in 1886
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Pre-1815 extent.** The 1800–1815 period covers entities
  (Batavian Republic, Kingdom of Holland, French annexation) with
  very different territorial configurations. The Cliopatria polygon
  for the United Kingdom (1815–1819) does not apply to the pre-1815
  period. This is a known polygon mismatch.
- **Area.** 68,095 km² total, population 5,563,119 in 1817
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §area](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#area).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — the WHEP database floor is 1800. The entity
  at 1800 was the Batavian Republic (French client state since 1795),
  which became the Kingdom of Holland (1806–1810), then direct French
  territory (1810–1813), then the Sovereign Principality (1813–1815),
  before becoming the United Kingdom of the Netherlands in 1815.
- **Successor:** [nld-1830-2025](nld-1830-2025.md) — the rump Netherlands after
  the Belgian secession. Also: `BEL-1831-2025` (Belgium) and
  `LUX-1839-2025` ([lux-1839-2025](lux-1839-2025.md)) are successor entities for the
  lost territory. The 1830 split produced three successor states from
  the original United Kingdom.

## Sourced claims

- **1815-03-16: William I declares himself king.** The constitution
  was "adopted 24 August 1815." The realm comprised "17 provinces
  and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg which was constitutionally
  distinct"
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1815-creation](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1815-creation).
- **1830-08-25: Belgian Revolution.** "The Belgian Revolution broke
  out on 25 August 1830, inspired by the recent July Revolution in
  France"
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1830-revolution](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1830-revolution). End-of-row
  event.
- **1830-10-04: Belgian independence declared.** "Belgium was declared
  an independent state on 4 October 1830"
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1830-revolution](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1830-revolution).
- **1831: Ten Days' Campaign.** William I "launched the Ten Days'
  Campaign" in August 1831, which "failed to defeat the rebels" after
  "French intervened to support the Belgians"
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1830-revolution](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1830-revolution).
- **1839-04-19: Treaty of London.** "The Dutch recognised Belgian
  independence, in exchange for territorial concessions"
  [wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12 §1839-treaty](../sources/wikipedia-netherlands-2026-04-12.md#1839-treaty). This is the
  same treaty that created the modern Grand Duchy of Luxembourg
  [biger-1995 §luxembourg](../sources/biger-1995.md#luxembourg) (see [lux-1839-2025](lux-1839-2025.md)).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists the Netherlands
  (`NTH`, `ccode=210`) from 1816-01-01 to 1940-07-14 (German
  occupation), then 1945-06-26 onward
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). COW makes no split at
  1830 (Belgian Revolution). The CSV has `cow_code = NA` for this
  row — the COW code 210 should apply.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1815-entities

The 1800–1815 window covers the Batavian Republic (1795–1806),
Kingdom of Holland (1806–1810), French annexation (1810–1813),
and the Sovereign Principality (1813–1815). None of these is the
"United Kingdom of the Netherlands." Under a strict reading of
the WHEP rule, the 1815 formation of the United Kingdom is a
major territorial event (merger of Dutch Republic + Austrian
Netherlands + Liège) and should arguably be a mid-row split or
start date. The CSV uses 1800 (database floor) instead.

Same omission pattern as the Russian Empire pre-1886 rows.

### oq-three-way-succession

The 1830 dissolution produced three successor entities:
NLD-1830-2025 (rump Netherlands), BEL-1831-2025 (Belgium),
and LUX-1839-2025 (Luxembourg, partition finalized 1839). The
CSV's `successor` field only lists `NLD-1830-2025`. Belgium
and Luxembourg are not listed as successors in the CSV despite
clearly inheriting territory. This is a coverage gap.
