# Pas-de-Calais

## Summary

This entry represents Pas-de-Calais, a French departement on the Channel and North Sea coast in the far north of the country, in the historic province of Artois, facing England across the Strait of Dover. It is coded as a `subnational` polity because a departement is an administrative subdivision of France, not a sovereign state or a distinct internationally-recognised territory: France's sovereignty over this area is continuous and undisputed throughout the entry's span, and the national FRA rows already in this table (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) carry that sovereignty claim for the whole country. This row exists solely to carry a *sub-national reporting territory* -- statistics keyed to "Pas-de-Calais" specifically, as opposed to all-France totals, need somewhere to route that isn't the national polygon. The entry follows the country convention's default start rule (1790, the year of the Revolutionary departements reform) as adjusted here to 1800 to match the earliest available French national container row (see decisions), and runs to the open end_year 2025 since the departement remains an active administrative unit of France today with no successor.

**Why this entry exists.** This page implements the routing decision for unit FRA-FRE12 (admin_name "FRE12", country France), which resolved via create_new because no existing FRA polity IS this territory -- the national FRA rows describe the whole country, and a departement cannot be represented by its country's row. The candidate list for match_existing was empty, since no existing row's territory equals Pas-de-Calais's. The data behind this decision is a 1862-2023 run that is almost entirely observed/blank (97.6%) plus a small luh2_residual share -- direct territorial reporting, not a reconstruction. What confirms Pas-de-Calais as a distinct, statistically-reported entity is that it is one of the original roughly 83 departements created by the 1790 Revolutionary reform (formed chiefly from the former province of Artois, with parts of Picardy and the county of Boulogne), and has been administered and tabulated in French statistical yearbooks (INSEE and its predecessors) as a discrete unit under numeric code 62 ever since -- the same status INSEE gives every other departement in the country's statistical apparatus, and the same basis on which sibling entries FRA-77-1800-2025 (Seine-et-Marne) and FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher) were created. This entry's span (1790-2025, adjusted here to 1800-2025, see decisions) covers the full administrative existence rather than being trimmed to any narrower data span, so a single create_new polity suffices with no back_cast or matched segments.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures a 1862-2023 data run for the unit labelled "France" / admin_name FRE12 (per the routing decision), 97.6% observed/blank plus a small luh2_residual share, i.e. direct territorial reporting rather than a back-cast reconstruction. ## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision already established this is a `none_available` case: no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French departements. gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated 81-country subset that omits France entirely (even though GADM's global release does cover French departements under GID_1 codes); cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe are country-level only and cannot supply a sub-national boundary. The standard remedy, per the decision's polygon_detail, would be to register GADM's global ADM1 layer, which is not on disk in this repository today.

**Territory description:** Pas-de-Calais occupies the northernmost tip of mainland France, on the Hauts-de-France coast facing the English Channel and the Strait of Dover (the narrowest crossing to England, near Calais). On a modern map it is bounded by the departement of Nord to the east/northeast, Somme to the south, and the Channel/North Sea to the north and west; its administrative seat (prefecture) is Arras, with Calais and Boulogne-sur-Mer as major coastal towns and the former coalfield around Lens and Bethune inland. It covers approximately 6,671 km2 -- this is a published administrative-area statistic for the modern departement, not a measurement from any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned (see polygon status above). The departement combines a historically coal-mining and industrial interior (the Nord-Pas-de-Calais coalfield, now closed) with agricultural chalk downs (the Artois hills) and a fishing/ferry coastline, and was a principal Western Front battlefield in WWI (Vimy Ridge, Lens, Arras) -- see the open question on wartime territorial status.

## Predecessors and successors

Pas-de-Calais has no predecessor or successor polity in this table: it was created directly by the 1790 Revolutionary departements reform (predating this database's coverage window, see the open question on the 1790-1800 gap) and has continued as an administrative unit of France without interruption through 2025, so both fields are empty. It was not among the departements affected by the 1871 Alsace-Lorraine cession or its 1919 return, and it is not known to have been split off from, merged into, or ceded to any other polity tracked in this table, notwithstanding its 1914-1918 partial front-line occupation (see open questions).

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790, because no container exists for the earlier decade**

The routing decision's span_basis argues for 1790, the year of the Revolutionary departements reform that created Pas-de-Calais out of the former province of Artois plus parts of Picardy and Flanders. That is very likely correct as administrative history, but this database's earliest French national row is FRA-1800-1871 -- there is no FRA row of any kind covering 1790-1800, and the containment gate rejects an edge that falls outside either party's span. Rather than leave a decade uncontained, or invent a container edge citing a national polity that does not exist in the table, I moved start_year to 1800 to match the earliest available container, exactly as the sibling entries FRA-77-1800-2025 (Seine-et-Marne) and FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher) already did for the same reason. The polity code year (1800) and the frontmatter start_year (1800) are kept equal, satisfying the gate that ties code years to frontmatter years. If a pre-1800 French national polity is added later, this entry's start_year should be revisited alongside it -- see the open question below.

### d-subunit-code-62-insee-not-fre12

**Subunit code is the INSEE/ISO 3166-2 numeric code 62, not the decision's admin_name FRE12**

The decision this page implements gives admin_name 'FRE12', which reads as a Eurostat NUTS3-style code, not the identifier French statistical sources or the public actually use for this departement. The dominant subnational pattern in this table is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using an identifier a reader recognises (e.g. ESP-AS, ESP-CA), and the established precedent for France specifically -- set by sibling entries FRA-77-1800-2025 and FRA-18-1800-2025 -- is to use the INSEE/ISO 3166-2:FR two-digit departement number rather than a NUTS3 code or an invented abbreviation. Pas-de-Calais's INSEE/ISO 3166-2:FR code is 62 (the postcode prefix 62xxx, car-plate suffix, and INSEE's own numbering all agree on this), so I used FRA-62-1800-2025. I have not independently verified that the decision's FRE12 in fact denotes Pas-de-Calais rather than a neighbouring Hauts-de-France departement (Nord, Somme, Oise, Aisne) -- see the open question on this crosswalk.

### d-polygon-unassigned-not-proxy

**Polygon left unassigned rather than proxied from a neighbouring departement or the national polygon**

The routing decision already established that no registered source in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French departements at this resolution: gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated 81-country subset that excludes France entirely (even though GADM's global release does cover French departements), and cshapes-2.0/cshapes-europe carry FRA only at the country level. I did not substitute a coarser proxy (the national FRA polygon, or a hand-drawn union of neighbouring shapes) because the whole point of this row is to isolate Pas-de-Calais's own reporting territory from the national one; using the national shape would defeat that purpose silently and inflate polygon_area_km2 by roughly two orders of magnitude. polygon_source is set to the 'none' sentinel already used by other unassigned rows in this table (e.g. RYU-1937-1945, and siblings FRA-77-1800-2025, FRA-18-1800-2025), matching existing convention, rather than a placeholder string.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap-uncovered

**The 1790-1800 decade of Pas-de-Calais's existence is not represented by this entry or any container**

Pas-de-Calais was created in 1790 by the Revolutionary departements reform (one of the first tranche of roughly 83 departements, formed chiefly from the former province of Artois with pieces of Picardy and the county of Boulogne), ten years before this entry's start_year of 1800. That gap exists because no FRA national polity row in this database covers 1790-1800 -- the earliest is FRA-1800-1871. If a future entry adds an FRA-1790-1800 (or similar) national row to close that gap, this entry's start_year should be pulled back to 1790 to match, with a corresponding container edge added. Until then, any data keyed to Pas-de-Calais in 1790-1799 has nowhere to route. The same open question already exists on sibling entries FRA-77-1800-2025 and FRA-18-1800-2025, so a fix would likely apply to several France subnational rows at once rather than this one alone.

### oq-wwi-occupation-and-front-line-not-modelled

**Pas-de-Calais's 1914-1918 status as a partially German-occupied front-line departement is not modelled by this entry or its containers**

Unlike most of interior France, Pas-de-Calais was directly affected by WWI: its eastern and southern fringes (around Lens, Vimy, Arras) sat on or near the Western Front and parts were under German occupation or bombardment from 1914-1918, while the rest remained under French civil administration throughout. This entry's single container edge to FRA-1871-1919 treats the whole departement as uniformly inside the national polygon for that era, which is the same simplification the exemplar Cher entry makes for its own unaffected interior territory, but here the simplification is more consequential because part of the territory was contested. Whether any economic/agricultural data series keyed to Pas-de-Calais in 1914-1918 in fact reflects a disrupted or partial-year reporting territory (mines and farmland out of production, population displaced) rather than the full departement has not been checked here, and no separate wartime polity or occupied-zone row exists in this table to route such data to.

### oq-nuts-vs-insee-code-mismatch

**The routing decision's admin_name (FRE12) has not been independently verified to denote Pas-de-Calais rather than a neighbouring departement**

The decision this page implements labels the unit 'FRA-FRE12' / admin_name 'FRE12', which I have treated as denoting Pas-de-Calais (INSEE/ISO 3166-2:FR code 62) based on standard NUTS-to-departement correspondence conventions used elsewhere in this pipeline (see decisions), but this crosswalk has not been checked here against an authoritative Eurostat NUTS correspondence table. Hauts-de-France, the modern region containing Pas-de-Calais, also contains Nord, Somme, Oise, and Aisne, several of which sit at adjacent NUTS3 codes; if 'FRE12' in the source data in fact denotes one of those instead, this entry would be misrouted and the underlying data series would need to move to a different departement's row.
