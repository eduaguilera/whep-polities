# Sarawak (Brooke Raj / Crown Colony)

## Summary

Sarawak occupies the northwest coast of Borneo island (now the Malaysian state of Sarawak), from its establishment as the Brooke Raj in 1841 through its incorporation into Malaysia in 1963. Two administrative phases: Brooke Raj (1841-1946), a personal dynasty under the White Rajahs (James Brooke from 1841; recognised as a British protectorate 1888); Crown Colony of Sarawak (1946-1963), ceded to the British Crown by the third White Rajah Charles Vyner Brooke on 1 July 1946. CShapes 2.0 gwcode=824. Area ~124,276 km².

This polity is distinct from and must NOT be confused with British North Borneo (BNB-1881-1963, ~72,620 km², northeast Borneo / modern Sabah). The FAO 1952 dataset reported "British Borneo Sarawak" as a distinct sub-entry, treating North Borneo, Sarawak, and Brunei as peers; each routes to its own polity.

- Representative polygon year used: 1889 (FID 621), covering the full defined-boundary Brooke Raj and most of the span.
- Method: exact_historical. Territory was stable — no boundary changes between 1888 and 1963. All three CShapes rows record the same polygon and area.
- Proxy for 1841-1888 (pre-protectorate): The 1889 polygon is used; Brooke territory was approximately the same, expanding incrementally from the initial 1841 Kuching cession but reaching close to final extent by mid-century. No quantified boundary change documented for WHEP purposes.
- Confidence: High.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: none in WHEP. Before 1841, the territory was governed by the Brunei Sultanate (BRN-1816-1888 in WHEP). A specific predecessor row is not created here; users requiring pre-1841 coverage should route to BRN-1816-1888.
- Successor: [mys-1963-1965](mys-1963-1965.md) — Sarawak joined Malaysia on 16 September 1963.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 gwcode=824, capital Kuching (110.33E, 1.53N):
  - FID 620: protectorate from 1888-06-14.
  - FID 621: boundary defined from 1889-06-20 to 1946-06-30.
  - FID 622: Crown Colony from 1946-07-01 to 1963-09-15.
- Area: 124,276 km² (all three CShapes rows identical; consistent with published figure of ~124,450 km² for modern Sarawak).
- James Brooke received Kuching from the Sultan of Brunei in 1841 as reward for suppressing a rebellion; he became the first White Rajah. Britain recognised Sarawak as a protectorate in 1888.
- Charles Vyner Brooke ceded Sarawak to the British Crown on 1 July 1946, establishing the Crown Colony of Sarawak.
- Sarawak and North Borneo (Sabah) both joined the Federation of Malaysia on 16 September 1963.

## Decisions

- Code BSW: mnemonic for Brooke Sarawak / British Sarawak; no ISO collision.
- Start year 1841: the founding of the Brooke Raj by James Brooke is the appropriate WHEP entry point, matching the Federico Tena polities reference (Sarawak 1841-1938). CShapes begins only at 1888 (British protectorate); the 1889 polygon serves as proxy for 1841-1888.
- Single row 1841-1963: no polygon boundary changes across the full span; the Brooke/Crown Colony administrative transition at 1946 does not alter the territorial extent.
- Polygon year 1889 chosen as representative: earliest fully-defined boundary; identical in area to all subsequent CShapes rows.
- iso3: MYS (modern successor state).
- cow: 824 (CShapes 2.0 gwcode for Sarawak). Note: the issue description incorrectly cited gwcode 821, which is the Federated Malay States (part of British Malaya / GBM-1895-1946); the correct gwcode confirmed from CShapes 2.0 is 824.
- Alias "British Borneo Sarawak" -> BSW-1841-1963 added for FAO 1952 data (years 1937-1951).

## Open questions

### oq-bsw-pre-1841-brunei
Before 1841, the Sarawak territory was part of the Brunei Sultanate. WHEP polity BRN-1816-1888 covers Brunei (greater) for this period, though it does not encompass the full northwest Borneo coast that Brunei nominally controlled. No action required for current data coverage.

### oq-bsw-japanese-occupation-1941-1945
Sarawak was occupied by Japan from December 1941 to September 1945. No separate Japanese occupation polygon in CShapes for Sarawak. Current row spans the occupation; data from 1942-1945 should be interpreted in context.
