# Qatar

## Summary

Qatar as tracked by WHEP from 1800. A small peninsula in the Persian Gulf, Qatar became a British protectorate in 1916 and gained independence in 1971. The Qatar--Saudi Arabia boundary was historically defined by the Blue Line of the 1914 Anglo-Turkish Convention.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Qatar shares a short (25 mi; 40 km) land boundary with Saudi Arabia.
