# Northern Ireland

## Summary

Northern Ireland, "a part of the United Kingdom in the north-east of the island of Ireland".
"Northern Ireland was created in 1921, when Ireland was partitioned by the Government of Ireland Act
1920, creating a devolved government for the six northeastern counties." It sits inside
[gbr-1921-2025](gbr-1921-2025.md); before 1921 its six counties were part of
[irl-1800-1921](irl-1800-1921.md), Ireland within the United Kingdom.
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §northern-ireland](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#northern-ireland)

## Why this entry exists

The label `united kingdom: north ireland` reports the six counties for 1909-1913 to 1951 (**31
(year, label) pairs**). No polity was Northern Ireland. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm1 `GBR.2_1` measures **14,167 km²** (ESRI:54034). The boundary is the 1921 county
boundary and has not moved since.

## Data routing

`united kingdom: north ireland` (whep-normalize): 1921-1951 by alias; the 1909-1913 average by a
`back_cast` alias (the six counties had no boundary of their own before 1921, so a pre-war value can
only be the later territory stated backwards). 31 pairs. Nothing in layer B routes here: its labels for this ground are the whole-country ones.

## Decisions

- **Starts in 1921**, at partition, not 1800: before that there was no Northern Ireland, and the
  six counties were inside Ireland. The pre-1921 label years are `back_cast`, which the alias
  disposition exists for.
