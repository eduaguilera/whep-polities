# Ethiopia (1907-1952)

## Summary

A **46-year row** -- the longest in the Ethiopian chain
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Covers late Menelik
II, the Italian invasion and occupation (1935-1941), liberation, and the
federation with Eritrea (1952).

Major events: Italian invasion (1935), Italian East Africa (1936-1941),
British liberation (1941), Eritrea federated with Ethiopia (1952).

Predecessor: [eth-1902-1907](eth-1902-1907.md). Successor:
[eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 per the CSV. Pre-1935 borders stable
  from the boundary-treaty era. During Italian occupation (1936-1941),
  Ethiopia ceased to exist as an independent state. Post-1941,
  pre-federation borders restored.
- The 1952 federation with Eritrea added ~125,000 km2.

## Sourced claims

- **1913: Menelik II dies.** Succeeded by Lij Iyasu, then Empress Zewditu
  with Ras Tafari (later Haile Selassie) as regent.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1930: Haile Selassie crowned Emperor.**.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1935-10-03: Italian invasion.** Mussolini's Italy invaded from Eritrea
  and Italian Somaliland.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1936-05-05: Fall of Addis Ababa.** Italy proclaimed Italian East
  Africa (combining Ethiopia, Eritrea, Italian Somaliland).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1936-1941: Italian occupation.** Ethiopia occupied; Haile Selassie
  in exile. WHEP treats this as a continuity break within the row.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1941: British liberation.** Combined British-Ethiopian forces defeated
  Italy. Haile Selassie restored.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1952: Eritrea federated with Ethiopia.** UN General Assembly Resolution
  390 (V) established the federation. End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

## Open questions

### oq-italian-occupation-continuity

The Italian occupation (1936-1941) interrupted Ethiopian sovereignty for
5 years. CShapes may code Ethiopia as non-existent during this period.
The CSV treats the entire 1907-1952 span as a single row, implying
continuity through the occupation. This should be verified against
CShapes coding rules.
