# Equatorial Guinea (1900-1968)

## Summary

Spanish Guinea, the colonial polity governing the territory of present-day Equatorial Guinea, from a boundary change in 1900 until independence on 12 October 1968. "The Franco-Spanish Convention of 27 June 1900, German Kamerun became the French mandate of Cameroon following World War I. It achieved independence on 1 January 1960. Spanish Guinea became the independent republic of Equatorial Guinea on 12 October 1968" [biger-1995 equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea). The boundary between Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea was formulated in the Franco-German agreements of 1885 and 1894 [biger-1995 equatorial-guinea](../sources/biger-1995.md#equatorial-guinea). CShapes codes it as a Spanish colony (ruler=ESP).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). The boundary between Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea stretches for 118 miles (189 km) from the Gulf of Guinea eastward (p.137). The current boundary approximates the colonial boundary that had separated the German protectorate of Kamerun and Spanish Guinea (p.138).
