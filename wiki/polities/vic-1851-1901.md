# Victoria (to 1900)

## Summary

The Colony of Victoria from its separation from New South Wales on 1 July 1851 to the eve of Australian federation. Victoria was carved from the southeastern part of NSW and became one of the most prosperous pre-federation Australian colonies on the back of the 1850s gold rush. The WHEP row ends on 31 December 1900 immediately before the 1 January 1901 federation.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependency-level). The colony's borders — the modern boundaries of the state of Victoria — were fixed at separation from NSW in 1851 and have not since changed. The CSV uses `iso3=VIC` as a WHEP-internal code.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [nsw-1800-1900](nsw-1800-1901.md) — Colony of New South Wales, from which Victoria was separated on 1 July 1851. The NSW chain does not record a formal WHEP row split at 1851; the territorial loss is absorbed into the NSW-1800-1901 single row.
- **Successor:** [aus-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) — Commonwealth of Australia from 1 January 1901.

### d-end-year-1901-not-1900

**The colony existed through all of 1900, so its exclusive `end_year` is 1901**

The Commonwealth of Australia was proclaimed on 1 January 1901, so this colony existed for every day of 1900 and ceased on the last of it. With `end_year` exclusive that is 1901, and the row previously said 1900 -- covering only to the end of 1899 and leaving 1900 with no colony at all.

The error was visible inside this table before it was visible in the data: the aggregate row [AUS-1800-1901](aus-1800-1901.md) already ends in 1901 and the successor states all begin in 1901, so the six colonies were the only rows dating federation to 1900. What surfaced it was the panel -- six Australian units report 1900 and those rows resolved to nothing, because the colony alias stopped at 1899 and the state alias began at 1901. Corrected 2026-09-17.
