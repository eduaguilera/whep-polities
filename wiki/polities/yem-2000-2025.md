# Yemen

## Summary

Yemen as tracked by WHEP from 2000 (a successor row to YEM-1990-2000). North and South Yemen merged in 1990 to form the Republic of Yemen. Yemen was twice occupied by the Turks, with the second occupation lasting from 1872 to 1918. The Oman--Yemen boundary was settled by a 1992 agreement.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Yemen is bounded by Saudi Arabia and Oman (179 mi; 288 km), with coastline on the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, and Arabian Sea.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [YEM-1990-2000](yem-1990-2000.md) -- continuation of unified Yemen

## Sourced claims

- Yemen was twice occupied by the Turks, the second time from 1872 to 1918. [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen)
- In 1967 a republican government took control in the north and in south Yemen the Yemen Democratic People's Republic was formed; in 1990 they merged to form the Yemen Republic. [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen)
- The Oman--Yemen boundary agreement was signed on 1 October 1992, demarcating the boundary and providing for the rights of nomadic peoples. [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen)
