# Lozère

## Summary

Lozère is a French département in south-central France, in the sparsely populated southern Massif Central, with its préfecture at Mende. It is one of the original roughly 83 départements created in 1790 during the Revolutionary reorganisation of France's provinces into a uniform administrative grid, replacing the old province of Gevaudan. It has never been detached from France, annexed, or renamed in the way Alsace-Lorraine or Belfort were, so its administrative identity has been continuous since creation. `type: subnational` is used because it is a sub-unit of the French state, not a sovereign entity: it must sit below the national FRA chain (FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025) in any territorial hierarchy, never compete with or substitute for it. This page exists to give a specific home to statistics reported at the département level for Lozère (unit id FRA-FRJ14 in the routing extract, admin_name "Lozère"), which the current pipeline had nowhere to put except the national France row — a mismatch of roughly two orders of magnitude in area and population between the département and the whole country.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing extract identifies unit FRA-FRJ14, admin_name "Lozère", country France, with a reported data year range of 1862-2023 (row count not itemised in the routing decision, but the decision explicitly frames this as one of France's ~83 base départements needing its own polity). Previously, any Lozère-labelled statistic had no département-level home in the polity table and could only be attributed to one of the national FRA rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), each of which is the whole of France (or France-minus-Alsace-Lorraine) — several hundred thousand km2 versus Lozère's roughly 5,167 km2, a mismatch of about two orders of magnitude that would badly misrepresent any per-area or per-capita figure computed against it. What confirms Lozère is a distinct, independently-administered entity rather than an arbitrary statistical slice: it is one of France's original 1790 Revolutionary départements, each of which has had its own préfet, elected conseil général/départemental, and administrative apparatus since creation, and INSEE and Eurostat (NUTS/ISO 3166-2:FR-48) both still report statistics at exactly this unit today — the same reporting granularity the routing extract uses.

## Decisions

### d-code-fra-48-not-loz

**Code chosen as FRA-48-1800-2025, using the INSEE/ISO 3166-2 department number instead of a 3-letter abbreviation**

The dominant subnational pattern is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>. A 3-letter abbreviation FRA-LOZ was the natural first choice, but polity_code FRA-LOZ-1800-2025 is already held by a page in wiki/polities/ describing a different territory (per the rejection note on this task), so it is unavailable. Rather than pick an arbitrary alternate abbreviation that could collide with a future department (e.g. Loir-et-Cher, Loiret also start with 'Lo'), I used Lozère's own INSEE department number, 48, which is the official, unambiguous, internationally recognised code for this exact unit (ISO 3166-2:FR-48). This keeps the code within the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years shape while guaranteeing no collision with any other French département.

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790, because no FRA container exists before 1800**

The routing decision's span_basis argued for 1790 as Lozère's creation year under the Revolutionary reform. However, the polity table's earliest France row is FRA-1800-1871 (cshapes-europe, national, iso3 FRA) — there is no FRA-named row covering 1790-1800 anywhere in data/final/polities_database.csv. The containment gate requires container edges to tile the whole span with no gap, and an edge cannot extend outside the container's own span. Starting this page at 1790 would leave 1790-1800 uncontained with no available container to cite. Since the underlying data extract's actual year range (1862-2023) is entirely inside 1800-2025 regardless, I set start_year to 1800 to align with the earliest containable France era; end_year stays 2025 (current). This is a deviation from the proposed span, made to satisfy a hard structural constraint rather than a judgment about Lozère's true founding date.

### d-polygon-none-not-route-name

**polygon_source set to none per the new_source_needed route, with the candidate source moved to an open question**

The routing decision's polygon_route is new_source_needed: no registered, present source can supply a Lozère département boundary. gadm-4.1-adm1 (locally present) excludes France and is the wrong admin level (région, not département) regardless; gadm-3.6 is registered but not fetched; no French cadastral source (e.g. IGN ADMIN-EXPRESS) is registered in sources.yaml. Per the harness instructions, new_source_needed maps to polygon_source: none and polygon_status: unassigned, with the candidate source recorded as an open question rather than written into polygon_source, since writing a route name or an unfetched candidate directly into polygon_source has already been rejected twice by validate_declared_sources on this exact unit.
