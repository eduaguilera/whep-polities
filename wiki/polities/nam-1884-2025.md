# Namibia

## Summary

Namibia is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Namibia (formerly South West Africa / German South-West Africa) from 1884 to the present. The 1884 start date reflects the German proclamation of a protectorate. Namibia became fully independent on 21 March 1990.

## Territorial extent

Namibia is bounded by Angola, Zambia, Botswana, South Africa, and the Atlantic Ocean. The Caprivi Strip extends northeast to the Zambia boundary. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The Mozambique-South Africa boundary context establishes that "the area to the east and north of Bechuanaland, later to become Southern and Northern Rhodesia, was proclaimed a British sphere of influence in 1888." In the same period Germany established its protectorate over South-West Africa (p.396). [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- The Angola-Namibia boundary of 855 miles (1,376 km) runs along the southern border of Angola. The boundary was established through colonial agreements between Portugal and Germany in the late 19th century (p.32). [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola)
- German colonial rule as South-West Africa until WWI (p.399). Mandate of South Africa from 1920 (p.399). UN contested South Africa's control; independence 1990 after guerrilla war (p.399). SWAPO won elections (p.399). [biger-1995 §namibia](../sources/biger-1995.md#namibia)

## Open questions

### oq-namibia-independence-date

Namibia independence. South Africa administered Namibia as a League of Nations mandate from 1920, refusing to place it under UN trusteeship. Namibia became independent only on 21 March 1990, making the 1884-2025 row span German, South African, and independent periods.
