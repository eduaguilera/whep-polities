# Yucatán

## Summary

Yucatán is one of the states of the Mexican federation, located on the northern part of the Yucatán Peninsula in southeastern Mexico, bordering the Gulf of Mexico. This entry captures it as a subnational reporting unit inside Mexico, not as a sovereign polity: `type: subnational` keeps it out of the sovereignty/family ranking the matcher applies to national-level rows, while still giving statistical series that were tabulated at the state level a distinct code to route to. Yucatán was one of the original states admitted to the 1824 federal constitution (the historic Yucatán state at that time also encompassed what are today Campeche and Quintana Roo, both of which split off later — Campeche in 1858/1863, Quintana Roo as a federal territory in 1902 and a state in 1974). It underwent a documented secession as the independent Republic of Yucatán from 1840/1841 to 1848, after which it reintegrated into the federation; that episode is treated here as a continuity break inside a single span rather than as a separate predecessor row, since no data series in this pipeline's extract falls inside 1840-1848 and creating a second code for a nine-year gap with no data would not resolve anything a reader needs. The span starts in 1824 (federal admission) rather than at the 1900 start of the underlying data extract, per this country's convention of using the admission year as the default start rule for Mexican states not on the convention's short list of exceptions (Baja California, Baja California Sur, Quintana Roo).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry exists to receive rows tabulated under the Mexican state of Yucatán, 1900-2023, that had no existing polity match: no polity in this repository currently represents Yucatán by name (0 name candidates, 0 boundary matches under this name in the routing search), so before this decision the data would have had to be forced onto the national MEX-1848-2025 row, which is roughly 47 times Yucatán's own area and would misrepresent a state-level series as a national one. Federico-Tena's compilation of historical Mexican state-level trade and production statistics treats Yucatán as one of its distinct reporting states for this period (it was, notably, a major sisal/henequen export economy with its own customs and production statistics distinct from national totals for much of the 19th and early 20th centuries), which is the external confirmation that this was a statistically distinct reporting unit and not an artefact of one dataset's labelling.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached in this GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision names gadm-4.1-adm1 as the correct source type — GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer does digitize Yucatán globally — but the locally fetched GADM subset on disk (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) only covers 81 countries and Mexico is not among them, so polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1 is recorded as the correct, registered source with polygon_status: unassigned rather than any placeholder value, and no feature id can be given until the Mexico subset is fetched.

**Territory description:** present-day Yucatán state occupies the northern tip of the Yucatán Peninsula, bordered by Campeche to the southwest and Quintana Roo to the east, with the state capital at Mérida. Its modern area is approximately 39,600 km². This is markedly smaller than the historical Yucatán of 1824-1858, which also included present-day Campeche (~57,900 km²) and, before 1902, the territory later carved off as Quintana Roo (~44,700 km²) — meaning the pre-1858 'Yucatán' this entry's earliest years nominally cover was on the order of 140,000 km², more than three times the present-day state. Using a present-day GADM boundary for the full 1824-2025 span is therefore a real approximation, worst in the 1824-1858 portion and accurate from 1858 (Campeche split) or effectively from 1902-1974 (Quintana Roo as separate federal territory/state) onward.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor polity rows exist for Yucatán in this database; the entry is created directly with no split from, or merge into, another polity row. The 1840-1848 Republic of Yucatán secession is treated as an internal episode of this single span rather than a distinct polity, since no dataset row in this extract falls within that decade and no independent statistical source was found tabulating the breakaway republic separately from the state series either side of it.

## Sourced claims

- Federico-Tena's historical Mexican trade/production compilation tabulates Yucatán as a distinct state-level reporting unit for parts of the 19th and early 20th centuries, driven substantially by its henequen/sisal export economy, separately from national Mexican totals.
- GADM 4.1's global admin-1 layer includes a Yucatán feature (Mexican state, GID_1 code beginning MEX.31), confirming the source type fits even though the locally cached subset used by this pipeline currently omits Mexico entirely (0 features for MEX in the local gadm41_adm1.gpkg).

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Code follows the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern, not a bespoke code**

Yucatán is a present-day federal administrative unit with a name that is also the name of a peninsula and a pre-1858 larger historical territory, but the polity this row represents is specifically the Mexican state (a subnational administrative unit within an ongoing federation), not a historical territory with a name and history independent of its containing state the way Alaska Territory (ALK) or Hyderabad (HYD) are. It therefore takes the dominant subnational pattern used by 57 of 66 existing subnational rows: MEX-YUC-1824-2025, using the standard three-letter ISO abbreviation for the state (YUC), matching the convention seen in ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. No existing MEX subnational code exists to conflict with or diverge from.

### d-start-year-1824-not-1900

**Start year set to 1824 federal admission, not to 1900 data start**

The routing decision's country convention defaults a Mexican state's start_year to its year of admission to the 1824 federation unless it is one of three enumerated exceptions (Baja California, Baja California Sur, Quintana Roo). Yucatán is not on that list, so 1824 is used even though the actual data extract this entry was created to receive begins at 1900. This keeps the polity's span meaningful as a description of Yucatán's existence as a federated state, independent of which years happen to have data, and is consistent with how MEX-1800-1848 / MEX-1848-2025 at the national level are spanned independently of their own data coverage.

### d-container-two-edges-for-1848-transition

**Two container edges used to tile 1824-2025 across the MEX national row split at 1848**

The national MEX chain in this database is split into MEX-1800-1848 (predecessor, cliopatria-sourced) and MEX-1848-2025 (successor, cshapes-2.0-sourced), with the split falling in the middle of this entry's 1824-2025 span. A single container edge citing only MEX-1848-2025 would leave 1824-1848 uncontained and would fail the containment gate, since that span falls outside MEX-1848-2025's own start year. Two edges are used instead, one per era of the containing national row, together tiling the full 1824-2025 span with no gap, per the containment rule stated in this task.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-mexico-subset-not-fetched

**GADM 4.1 admin-1 Mexico subset needs to be fetched to assign a polygon**

The locally cached GADM 4.1 admin-1 GeoPackage used by this pipeline (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) covers only an 81-country subset that excludes Mexico entirely, even though GADM's global release does digitize Mexican states including Yucatán (expected GID_1 prefix MEX.31). Until that subset is re-fetched to include Mexico, this entry cannot move past polygon_status: unassigned. Whoever fetches it should also decide whether to backfill the same feature for other newly-created Mexican state entries in this batch, since they all hit the same gap.

### oq-1824-1858-boundary-mismatch

**Present-day GADM boundary for Yucatán will misrepresent 1824-1858 territory once fetched**

Once a GADM 4.1 polygon is fetched for Yucatán, it will represent the present-day ~39,600 km² state, but historical Yucatán from 1824 until Campeche's split (1858, formalized 1863) also included Campeche's ~57,900 km², and until Quintana Roo's 1902 separation as a federal territory also included Quintana Roo's ~44,700 km² for the earliest years — meaning the correct historical territory for 1824-1858 was over three times the modern state's area. Whether to construct a period-specific larger polygon (union of modern Yucatán + Campeche + Quintana Roo GADM features) for the pre-1858 sub-span, or to accept the present-day boundary as a proxy with a documented under-coverage caveat, is unresolved and should be decided when the GADM fetch above is completed.

### oq-1840-1848-secession-episode-unmodeled

**1840-1848 Republic of Yucatán secession is not modeled as its own polity or gap**

Yucatán operated as a de facto independent Republic of Yucatán for roughly 1840/1841-1848 before reintegrating into the Mexican federation. This entry spans straight through that episode as if it were continuous, on the grounds that no dataset row in the current extract falls in that decade. If a future data source does report on Yucatán specifically within 1840-1848 (customs records, henequen export statistics, or similar), it is unclear whether it should route to this entry, to the national MEX-1800-1848/MEX-1848-2025 rows, or motivate splitting out a distinct MEX-YUC secession-era polity — that decision was deferred here for lack of any such data currently in scope.
