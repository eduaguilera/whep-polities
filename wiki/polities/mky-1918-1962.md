# Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen (1918-1962)

## Summary

The Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen (also known as the Yemen Imamate or North Yemen) was an independent monarchy established after the withdrawal of Ottoman forces in November 1918. "Yemen was twice occupied by the Turks, the second time from 1872 to 1918." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen). The kingdom was ruled by the Mutawakkil imams of the Zaidi Shia tradition from Sanaa, controlling the highlands and coastal strip (Tihama) of the Arabian Peninsula's southwestern corner. This polity is a distinct WHEP row because it is the only internationally-recognized Yemeni state for the entire period 1918-1962, and it is the entity measured by IIA coffee production data labelled 'yemen' for 1922-1945. The kingdom ended with the 1962 republican revolution, which overthrew Imam Muhammad al-Badr and established the Yemen Arab Republic.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0; cowcode 679 (North Yemen), polygon_feature_year 1920. CShapes records cowcode 679 for the pre-republican North Yemeni state, with entries covering 1918-10-30 to 1990-05-21. The polygon area is approximately 195,000-200,000 km², corresponding to North Yemen's highlands and coastal strip along the Red Sea (Tihama plain). The polygon covers the Yemeni highlands and Tihama coast.

**Polygon vintage drift note:** CShapes uses the same 679 polygon time-step from 1918 through 1990 (no territorial change recorded). The 1920 polygon is used as the best available vintage for the 1918-1962 Mutawakkilite Kingdom period. The 1934 Treaty of Taif with Saudi Arabia settled the Saudi-Yemeni border and formalized Saudi control over the Asir and Najran regions; however, CShapes records no polygon change at this event. This is a known limitation: the pre-1934 territory was larger than the post-1934 territory, and the 1920 polygon may represent the post-Taif boundary backdated to 1918. See [oq-polygon-1934-taif](#oq-polygon-1934-taif).

**Why this entry exists:** The IIA source reports 21 rows of coffee (green) production under the label 'yemen' for 1922-1945. The only previous Yemen polity in the database was YEM-1990-2025 (unified Republic of Yemen, 1990-present), whose span is entirely disjoint from the data years (gap of 45+ years). The Mutawakkilite Kingdom was the principal coffee-exporting territory during this period, producing the famed Yemeni mocha coffee exported through the port of Hudaydah (and historically Mocha/Al-Makha). The territory was fully independent of any external empire in 1922-1945.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** Ottoman Yemen (no WHEP row yet; Ottoman Empire controlled Yemen 1872-1918). When a WHEP row for Ottoman Yemen is created, it should be linked here. See [oq-ottoman-yemen-predecessor](#oq-ottoman-yemen-predecessor).
- **Successor:** [YEM-1990-2025](yem-1990-2025.md) — the Republic of Yemen (1990-present) represents unified North+South Yemen. The successor field points to YEM-1990-2025 as the furthest downstream existing polity pending creation of YAR-1962-1990.

## Sourced claims

- "Yemen was twice occupied by the Turks, the second time from 1872 to 1918." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen) (p.414) — establishing 1918 as the start of independent North Yemen.
- "In 1967, a republican government took control in the north and in south Yemen the Yemen Democratic People's Republic was formed." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen) (pp.414-415) — the 1962 revolution overthrew the Mutawakkilite monarchy; Biger's reference to the "republican government" is to its successor state.
- COW state system (v2024) lists cowcode 679 (North Yemen) from 1918 to 1990, confirming this entity's independence is recognized by the standard state system dataset. [cow-state-system-v2024 §scope](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#scope)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode 679 with polygon entries covering 1918-10-30 to 1990-05-21, all at the same area (~11.50 sq deg). [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- The 1934 Treaty of Taif (signed 20 May 1934) between Saudi Arabia and North Yemen settled the Saudi-Yemeni border, with Saudi Arabia retaining Asir and Najran. This is a real territorial event not captured in CShapes. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

- **CShapes cowcode.** CShapes uses cowcode 679 for North Yemen (pre-1990) but transitions to 678 (the same code used for unified Yemen) after unification. This wiki page binds to cowcode 679 (North Yemen) for the 1918-1962 period, distinct from YEM-1990-2025 which uses cowcode 678.
- **1934 Taif Treaty area change.** The Treaty of Taif (1934) ceded the Asir region to Saudi Arabia. CShapes records no polygon change at this event (same area throughout 1918-1990). Either CShapes uses a post-Taif polygon backdated to 1918, or the dataset silently omits this boundary change. Biger 1995 pp.462-464 (ARABIA-YEMEN section) may clarify; the next ingest should check.

## Decisions

- Created MKY-1918-1962 (2026-06-26) to cover IIA coffee production data ('yemen', 1922-1945) that had no valid routing target in the previous database. The unified Yemen polity (YEM-1990-2025) starts 45+ years after the data period and represents a different, merged political entity.

## Open questions

### oq-taif-1934-split

The 1934 Treaty of Taif between Saudi Arabia and North Yemen settled the border and formalized Saudi control over the Asir and Najran regions. This is a real territorial cession that may justify a WHEP polity split at 1934 (MKY-1918-1934 and MKY-1934-1962). However, CShapes records no polygon change at this event, so no alternative polygon is currently available to represent the pre-1934 territory. If Cliopatria, Biger, or another source provides a pre-1934 North Yemen polygon, the split should be made. Until then, the single 1918-1962 row with the CShapes 1920 polygon is used as an approximation.

### oq-yar-1962-1990

The Yemen Arab Republic (YAR, cowcode 679, 1962-1990) is the direct successor state to the Mutawakkilite Kingdom. It is not yet present as a separate WHEP row. A YAR-1962-1990 row should be created linking MKY-1918-1962 -> YAR-1962-1990 -> YEM-1990-2025 to complete the North Yemen chain. The current successor field points directly to YEM-1990-2025, which skips the republican North Yemen period.

### oq-ottoman-yemen-predecessor

The Ottoman Empire controlled most of what became North Yemen from 1872 to 1918. A WHEP row for Ottoman Yemen (as a sub-territory, not the whole Ottoman Empire) would complete the territorial chain before 1918. Until that row exists, the predecessor field for MKY-1918-1962 is empty, representing a coverage gap before 1918.

### oq-polygon-1934-taif

CShapes 2.0 records the same polygon area for cowcode 679 throughout 1918-1990, implying no territorial change at the 1934 Treaty of Taif. This is inconsistent with the historical record — the treaty formally ceded the Asir and Najran to Saudi Arabia (~100,000+ km²). Either CShapes uses a post-Taif polygon backdated to 1918, or the dataset silently omits this boundary change. The next ingest should check Biger 1995 pp.462-464 (ARABIA-YEMEN section) and verify whether the CShapes polygon coordinates match the post-1934 boundary.
