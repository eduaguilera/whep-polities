# United States (1848-1867)

## Summary

This row tracks the United States from the **Treaty of Guadalupe
Hidalgo (1848)** to the **Alaska Purchase (1867)** — a **20-year
row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This
period begins with the massive Mexican Cession (+1.4M km2:
California, Nevada, Utah, most of Arizona, New Mexico, parts of
Colorado and Wyoming), includes the Gadsden Purchase (1854), and
spans the entire Civil War (1861–1865). Territory reaches ~4.3M km2
pre-Alaska.

The end-of-row event is the **Alaska Purchase (1867)** from Russia,
adding ~1.7M km2 of territory. This is the same event documented
from the Russian side in
[f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) — "Russia sold Alaska to the
United States for $7.2 million"
[f228-1856-1905 §oq-alaska-sale-split](f228-1856-1905.md#oq-alaska-sale-split).

Predecessor: [usa-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md). Successor:
[usa-1867-1959](usa-1867-1959.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon (since 2026-09-24).** `CShapes 2.0` gwcode 2, the step
  **1886-01-01 / 1959-01-02** — the contiguous United States without Alaska
  (CShapes gwcode 3) or Hawaii (gwcode 4), US share of the Great Lakes
  included. **7,939,971 km²** (EPSG:6933), 7,783,059 km² without the 156,912 km²
  of Great Lakes water, against the census's 7,827,946 km² for 1860
  (*Historical Statistics of the United States* J 1-2: 3,022,387 sq mi), -0.57%.
  It **includes the Gadsden Purchase** (census 29,640 sq mi = 76,767 km²), so
  1848-1853 is overstated by about 1.0%. See
  [decision-polygon-cshapes-contiguous](#decision-polygon-cshapes-contiguous).
  The Cliopatria description below is the row's previous binding, kept as
  written; it was also wrong about which step was bound (the database carried
  the **1849-1852** step, 3,469,207 km², not 1859-1860).
- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (United States of America
  1859-1860)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). A single
  Cliopatria time-step from 1859–1860 applied across all 20 years.
  This polygon captures the post-Mexican Cession, post-Gadsden
  Purchase configuration — a good temporal fit for 1854–1867 but
  slightly overstates 1848–1854 (Gadsden Purchase not yet acquired).
- **Area.** ~4.3M km2 pre-Alaska per CSV notes
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

### decision-polygon-cshapes-contiguous

**`polygon_source` moved `cliopatria` (`United States of America`, 1849) ->
`cshapes-2.0` (gwcode 2, `polygon_feature_year: 1886`) on 2026-09-24.** Written
up by Claude (Claude Code); open for review in the pull request that made it.

**Why.** PR 671 (issue 655) measured Cliopatria's 1849 polygon against CShapes'
contiguous US and found it missing **4,519,604 km²**, 4,366,867 of it one piece
centred near 108.5°W 40.3°N: the Mexican Cession, Oregon and the unorganised
Louisiana interior, all US territory by 1848. Cliopatria draws the United
States by settler-frontier extent and fills the West in step by step
(5,724,208 km² by 1866, 7,948,373 by 1868). So the row published **3,469,207
km²** for a country the census measured at 7,751,179 km² in 1850 (2,992,747 sq
mi), and the 1867 seam published a 2.72x step of which only Alaska is history.
That step was baselined as an ARTEFACT in `validate_source_change_steps.py` and
is now resolved (removed from the baseline).

**Why CShapes' 1886 step and not a constructed polygon.** The contiguous border
this row needs was complete in 1853 and unchanged until Chamizal (1963), so
CShapes' first gwcode 2 step — which excludes Alaska and Hawaii, both separate
CShapes units — is exactly the 1853-1867 extent. It has no earlier step, so
`validate_polygon_period_fit` has no in-span alternative to prefer, and binding
it directly avoids a constructed copy of a source feature. The successor
[USA-1867-1959](usa-1867-1959.md) is this same feature united with Alaska
(CShapes' 1959-01-03 step, 9,446,212 km²), so the 1867 seam is now
7,939,971 -> 9,446,212 km², +1,506,241 km² = CShapes' Territory of Alaska, and
nothing else: **1.19x, all of it the purchase**.

**The Gadsden Purchase is documented, not split.** It was signed on 1853-12-30
and ratified in 1854, inside this row. Measured against the row:

| | km² | share of polygon |
|---|---|---|
| Gadsden Purchase (census 29,640 sq mi) | 76,767 | **0.97%** |
| years it is overstated | 1848-1853 | 6 of 20 |
| layer-B rows in those years | 4 of 46 (mitchell 1850-1853) | |

A split would rename this published code and create a six-year row for a
change of under 1% — below any threshold this table applies to other
territorial adjustments (the 1842 Webster-Ashburton line, the 1818 Red River
cession and the 1872 San Juan arbitration are all unsplit). The overstatement
is one-directional and stated here; a consumer dividing 1850-1853 output by
this polygon's area understates density by about 1%.

**Nesting.** [USA-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md) and
[USA-1800-1803](usa-1800-1803.md) are now constructed by subtraction from this
same feature, so the three pre-1867 rows nest exactly and the 1848 step
(4,761,396 -> 7,939,971 km², 1.67x) is Texas, Oregon, the Mexican Cession and
(because this polygon carries it) the Gadsden strip: census 1,234,380 sq mi =
3,197,030 km² against a measured difference of 3,178,575 km² (-0.58%).

## Open questions

### oq-civil-war-cow-continuity

COW records no break for the United States during the Civil War
(1861–1865). The Confederacy is not a separate COW entity. Under
the WHEP rule, the Civil War is not a territorial-change split
event (Union territory was contested but not permanently ceded).
The CSV does not split here. Consistent, but worth documenting
for trade-data analysis: Civil War years may have anomalous trade
patterns.

### oq-gadsden-purchase-polygon

**Answered 2026-09-24:** the row now carries CShapes' contiguous US, which
includes the Gadsden territory for the whole row; 1848-1853 is overstated by
76,767 km² (0.97%), and a split was declined. See
[decision-polygon-cshapes-contiguous](#decision-polygon-cshapes-contiguous). The
original question is kept below.

The Gadsden Purchase (1854, ~77,000 km2 from Mexico) is a minor
territorial acquisition within this row. The Cliopatria 1859–1860
polygon post-dates the purchase so it should include the Gadsden
territory. Not verified against Cliopatria geometry.
