# Saudi Arabia (1924-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Saudi Arabia for **101 years**, from Ibn Saud's conquest of
the Hejaz in 1924 to 2025
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The proclamation of the
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on 23 September 1932 falls inside it: it united the
Hejaz and Nejd under one name, both already under Ibn Saud since 1925, so it
renamed the state rather than moving a boundary.

Pre-unification Saudi entities: the Emirate of Nejd under Ibn Saud (from
1902) and the Kingdom of Hejaz (1916-1925, established after the Arab
Revolt ended Ottoman rule). Ibn Saud conquered the Hejaz in 1924-1925,
uniting it with Nejd. The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia was proclaimed on
23 September 1932 -- an event inside the row, not its end.

The CSV notes are unusually rich for this row: "Pre-1924 Arabian interior
has no polygon source. CShapes starts Saudi at 1927." This means CShapes
coverage only begins partway through the row. See
[oq-notes-preservation](#oq-notes-preservation).

Predecessor: [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) per CSV -- note this is a
very indirect predecessor. The Ottoman Empire controlled the Hejaz coast;
the Arab Revolt of 1916 ended Ottoman rule; the Hashemite Kingdom of
Hejaz existed 1916-1925; Ibn Saud conquered the Hejaz from the
Hashemites in 1924-1925. See
[oq-pre-1924-hejaz](#oq-pre-1924-hejaz).

Successor: none; this row runs to the present. A split at 1932 into a
`SAU-1932-2000` row was once planned and never applied.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) per CSV -- a very
  indirect predecessor. The chain of control over the Hejaz coast runs:
  Ottoman Empire (until 1916 Arab Revolt) then Hashemite Kingdom of
  Hejaz (1916-1925) then Ibn Saud's conquest (1924-1925). There is a
  coverage gap between OTT-1908-1912 and this row; see
  [oq-pre-1924-hejaz](#oq-pre-1924-hejaz).
- **Successor:** No successor row; this row runs to the present.

A split at 1932 was once planned and never applied. The proclamation of the
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on 23 September 1932 renamed a state that already held
both the Hejaz and Nejd, so it is not a territorial boundary; a
`SAU-1932-2000` row would also have needed a defence of its 2000 end year, which
no source here supplies (2026-08-17, issue #25). The boundary changes that *did*
happen after 1932 — the 1934 Treaty of Taif with Yemen, the 1965 exchange with
Jordan, the 1974 Treaty of Jeddah with Abu Dhabi — are inside this row and are
not reflected in its single polygon; see
[oq-post-1932-boundary-changes](#oq-post-1932-boundary-changes).

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1924-hejaz

The Hejaz was Ottoman territory until the 1916 Arab Revolt, then
Hashemite from 1916 to 1925 (Kingdom of Hejaz under Sharif Hussein and
then his son Ali). The CSV predecessor for this row is
[ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md), which ends in 1912 -- 12 years before
this row starts. The intervening period (1912-1924) is a coverage gap:
the Hejaz was part of the Ottoman Empire until 1916 (under TUR-coded
rows), then the independent Hashemite Kingdom of Hejaz (1916-1925, not
in the CSV at all). This is a significant territorial-chain gap.

### oq-post-1932-boundary-changes

This row carries one polygon (CShapes feature 670, vintage 1935) for 1924-2025,
but the Saudi frontier moved several times inside that span: the Treaty of Taif
with Yemen (1934) settled the Asir/Najran/Jizan line, the 1965 exchange with
Jordan traded Aqaba-corridor land, and the Treaty of Jeddah (1974, revisited
2000) settled the frontier with Abu Dhabi. Whether any of these is large enough
to warrant a split is undecided here — the polygon sources would have to be
checked for corresponding features first. The `2000` in the never-created
`SAU-1932-2000` code was probably a nod to the 2000 Saudi-Yemen treaty, but no
document in the repo says so.

### oq-notes-preservation

The CSV notes for this row are unusually rich and worth citing directly:
"Pre-1924 Arabian interior has no polygon source. CShapes starts Saudi
at 1927." These notes document an important limitation of the polygon
data -- the first three years of this row (1924-1927) lack CShapes
polygon coverage, and the pre-1924 Arabian interior is entirely
unrepresented in the polygon sources.
