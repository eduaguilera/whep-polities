# Finland (1920-1940)

## Era split (issue 686, 2026-10-07)

**This row was `FIN-1917-1940` until 2026-10-07.** It now starts in 1920, the year of the Treaty of
Tartu (14 October 1920), under which Finland received Petsamo and its Arctic coast
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §finland](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#finland). The years 1917-1919 moved to [fin-1917-1920](fin-1917-1920.md).

**Polygon status:** assigned, unchanged. CShapes 2.0 gwcode 375, the 1917-1940 step
(387,153 km², Mollweide). That step already draws Petsamo as Finnish: the difference between it
and the 1940-2019 step contains a 10,837 km² piece north of 68 N (28.4-31.8 E, 68.1-69.8 N), which
is Petsamo. So the polygon is right for 1920-1940 and was wrong for 1917-1919, when Petsamo was
Russian. The earlier frontmatter said `polygon_feature_year: 1917`; it is now 1920, which resolves
to the same CShapes step.

**Why this split exists.** Finnish data for 1917-1919 was measured against a territory 10,837 km²
(2.8%) larger than the one Finland held. The predecessor/successor chain now runs
[fin-1809-1917](fin-1809-1917.md) -> [fin-1917-1920](fin-1917-1920.md) -> this row ->
[fin-1940-1944](fin-1940-1944.md).

## Summary

This row tracks Finland for **20 years**, from the Treaty of Tartu (1920) to the
Winter War territorial losses
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Finland declared independence from Russia on 6 December 1917. Previously
part of the Russian Empire as the Grand Duchy of Finland since 1809
(cross-ref the F228 chain and [swe-1800-1809](swe-1800-1809.md) which
lost Finland to Russia). Finnish Civil War 1918. Treaty of Tartu (1920)
confirmed borders with Soviet Russia. The Winter War (November 1939 --
March 1940) ended with the Moscow Peace Treaty (12 March 1940), in which
Finland lost Karelia and other territories to the USSR (~10% of its
area). The 1940 end date aligns with this treaty.

Predecessor: [fin-1917-1920](fin-1917-1920.md). Successor: [fin-1940-1944](fin-1940-1944.md).

## Open questions

### oq-russian-predecessor

Finland was part of the F228 chain (Russian Empire) as the Grand Duchy
of Finland from 1809. The CSV lists `predecessor = NA`, which is
expected for a newly independent state but means the predecessor graph
cannot trace back through Russia to Sweden
([swe-1800-1809](swe-1800-1809.md)).

### oq-winter-war-losses

The Winter War resulted in significant territorial loss (~10% of
Finland's area, including Karelia, Salla, and parts of the Rybachi
Peninsula). The 1940 row break aligns with the Moscow Peace Treaty.
