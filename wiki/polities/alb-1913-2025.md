# Albania (1913-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Albania for **113 years**, a continuous row from
independence to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Albania declared independence from the Ottoman Empire on 28 November
1912. The 1913 start date reflects the Treaty of London (May 1913) which
recognized Albanian independence. Borders established by the Conference
of Ambassadors (1913). Small Balkan state ~28,748 km². Occupied by Italy
1939-1943. Communist regime under Enver Hoxha 1944-1985 (extreme
isolationism). Democratic transition 1991. EU candidate.

Predecessor: NA per CSV. Successor: NA (still active).

## Sourced claims

- **Area: 11,101 sq mi (28,752 sq km).** Bounded by Serbia (178 mi),
  Macedonia (94 mi), Greece (175 mi), and the Adriatic Sea. Capital:
  Tirana
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **No pre-20th century statehood.** "The Albanians never formed a
  state of their own until the twentieth century"
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1878: Albanian nationalism.** Albanian League for the Defense of
  the Rights of the Albanian People established
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1912-11-28: Independence declared.** Albania declared independence
  from the Ottoman Empire.
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania)
- **1913: Creation as independent state.** "The creation of Albania in
  1913 was more the result of Italian and Austro-Hungarian efforts"
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
  Start-of-row event. Treaty of London (May 1913) recognized
  independence.
- **1921: Boundary confirmed.** Conference of Ambassadors confirmed
  Albanian boundaries in November 1921
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1926-07-30: Final boundary treaty.** Albania–Greece boundary
  finalized
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1924–1928: Zog regime.** "In 1924 Ahmed Zog seized power…
  proclaimed Albania a republic and declared himself president. In
  1928 he proclaimed Albania a monarchy"
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1939: Italian conquest.** "in 1939 Mussolini conquered the
  country"
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
- **1943: German occupation.** Following Italian armistice.
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania)
- **1944–1985: Hoxha regime.** "Following World War II a Communist
  regime headed by Enver Hoxha was established in Albania"
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania).
  Extreme isolationism, broke with USSR (1961) and China (1978).
- **1991: Democratic transition.** Multi-party elections.
  [biger-1995 §albania](../sources/biger-1995.md#albania)

## Open questions

### oq-ottoman-predecessor

Albania was part of the Ottoman Empire before independence. The CSV
lists `predecessor = NA`. The Ottoman chain pages
([ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md)) should ideally reference Albania as
a successor territory.

### oq-italian-occupation-1939

Italy occupied Albania 1939-1943 but there is no WHEP split during the
Italian occupation period. COW may show a gap. Worth checking whether
CShapes maintains a separate polygon during occupation.
