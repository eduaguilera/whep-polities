# Yugoslavia (1919-1920)

## Summary

This row tracks Yugoslavia for **2 years** -- a short transitional row
during the post-WWI border settlement period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (later renamed Yugoslavia in
1929). Borders were being settled by a series of post-WWI treaties:
Treaty of Saint-Germain (September 1919, Austrian territories), Treaty of
Neuilly (November 1919, Bulgarian border), Treaty of Trianon (June 1920,
Hungarian territories including Croatia, Slavonia, Vojvodina). CShapes
shows cowcode=345 (Yugoslavia/Serbia) growing from ~21.4 to ~28.7 sq deg
during this period.

The CSV lists `predecessor = NA` for this row -- this is a **CSV bug**.
The predecessor should link to at least
[f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) and arguably also to
[ser-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md). See
[oq-predecessor-csv-bug](#oq-predecessor-csv-bug).

Predecessor: [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) (despite CSV saying NA).

## Sourced claims

- **September 1919: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Settled borders with the
  new Republic of Austria, confirming Yugoslav acquisition of Slovenian
  and Dalmatian territories from the former Austria-Hungary.
- **November 1919: Treaty of Neuilly.** Settled the Bulgarian border,
  confirming Yugoslav control of parts of Macedonia.
- **June 1920: Treaty of Trianon.** Settled the boundary with Hungary,
  confirming Yugoslavia's acquisition of Croatia, Slavonia, Vojvodina,
  and parts of the Banat. CShapes records growth to ~28.7 sq deg.
