# Cabo Verde

## Summary

Independent Cabo Verde (Cape Verde) from 5 July 1975 to the present. Cape Verde gained independence from Portugal in 1975. "Following independence in 1975 the PAICV dominated the country and converted it into a single-party state" (p.144-145). In 1990 Cape Verde became a multi-party state [biger-1995 &sect;cape-verde](../sources/biger-1995.md#cape-verde).
