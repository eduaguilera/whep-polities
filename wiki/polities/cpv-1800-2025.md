# Cabo Verde

## Summary

Cabo Verde (Cape Verde) is a WHEP polity row covering the Cape Verde Islands from 1800 to the present. The start year 1800 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage. Cape Verde gained independence from Portugal in 1975. The islands have no land boundaries.

## Territorial extent

Cape Verde Islands (area 1,557 sq. miles; 4,033 sq. km) are located 386 miles (621 km) west of the coast of Africa. The archipelago consists of 10 islands and 5 islets. Capital: Praia, on the island of Sao Tiago. No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

- No predecessor (WHEP temporal boundary at 1800)

## Sourced claims

- "The Portuguese arrived in the uninhabited archipelago in the fifteenth century and the islands later became a station in the Atlantic slave-trade" (p.144). [biger-1995 §cape-verde](../sources/biger-1995.md#cape-verde)
- "Following independence in 1975 the PAICV (Partido Africano da Independencia de Cabo Verde), the party that emerged from the Cape Verde liberation movement, dominated the country and converted it into a single-party state" (p.144-145). In 1990 Cape Verde became a multi-party state. [biger-1995 §cape-verde](../sources/biger-1995.md#cape-verde)
- Negotiations over unification with Guinea-Bissau were begun after independence, "but a 1980 coup in Guinea-Bissau put an end to Cape Verdean domination and unification plans were halted" (p.145). [biger-1995 §cape-verde](../sources/biger-1995.md#cape-verde)
