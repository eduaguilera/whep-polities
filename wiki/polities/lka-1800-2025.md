# Sri Lanka

## Summary

Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon) as tracked by WHEP from 1800. An island state in the Indian Ocean south of India, Sri Lanka has no land boundaries. It was a British colony from 1815 and gained independence in 1948, becoming a republic in 1972.
