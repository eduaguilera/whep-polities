# Illinois (state of the United States)

## Summary

Illinois is a U.S. state in the American Midwest, admitted to the Union on December 3, 1818, carved from the Illinois Territory (itself split from the Indiana Territory in 1809). This entry covers the state from admission to the present. `type: subnational` because Illinois is a constituent state of a sovereign country, not itself sovereign — statehood is what distinguishes it from the federal territorial period that preceded it, which is why the span starts at 1818 rather than at the earliest available data year. The state's external boundary has been stable since admission apart from a minor 1818 adjustment that shifted the northern line north of the Indiana-style baseline to secure a Lake Michigan shoreline and the future site of Chicago — a deliberate congressional move, not a later territorial change, so it predates and is absorbed into this entry's start year rather than being a within-span boundary event.

**Why this entry exists.** USDA NASS agricultural extracts (via `mitchell` and related USDA-derived sources) report Illinois separately from national US totals starting in the mid-1860s, with the routing verdict for this batch showing an empty candidate list — no existing USA-* polity represents a sub-national US state, since all prior USA rows (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) are national-total rows spanning the whole country. Data previously had no home for state-level rows and would otherwise have been misrouted to a national container that is roughly 60x Illinois's area. Illinois's distinctness as a reporting and administrative unit is not in question — it is one of the 50 states of a federal union, with its own government, statutes, and continuous existence as a corporate polity since 1818; the only judgment call here is the start year, resolved by the country convention's default rule (span begins at admission to the Union) rather than by the data's own start year, which only reflects when USDA NASS began publishing state-level series.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm1` source, feature `USA.14_1`, matched directly by name ("Illinois") in the already-registered GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer. `polygon_status: proxy` rather than `assigned` because the feature reflects present-day (2025) state boundaries applied back to 1818; this is a low-risk proxy since Illinois's external boundary has been essentially unchanged since admission (the 1818 northern-boundary shift to include the Chicago area and Lake Michigan frontage was decided before/at admission and is therefore already inside this entry's start year, not a within-span change) — no Illinois river-boundary or county-line dispute has altered the state's outline at the scale this polygon is used for.

**Territory description:** Illinois occupies the section of the American Midwest bounded by Lake Michigan and Wisconsin to the north, the Mississippi River (and Iowa/Missouri) to the west, the Ohio River (and Kentucky) to the south, and Indiana to the east — readily located on a modern map as the state containing Chicago, Springfield (the capital), and the Illinois/Mississippi River confluence region. The GADM feature's measured area is 149,995 km2 (labelled here as the geometry's own measurement, not an independent territorial claim); this is consistent with the commonly cited figure for the state (~149,995 km2 / 57,914 sq mi), so no material under- or over-coverage is expected relative to the reporting unit the routed data describes.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor polity rows are declared. Before 1818, the area was part of the Illinois Territory (an unincorporated federal territory, not itself represented as a WHEP polity), so there is no predecessor row to link — the territorial phase is out of scope for this subnational-state entry, which begins at statehood by design. There is no successor: Illinois continues to the present (end_year 2025, open/current per convention) with no scheduled dissolution or reorganization.

## Sourced claims

- Illinois was admitted as the 21st state of the Union on December 3, 1818, per the U.S. Congress's enabling act and admission resolution.
- USDA NASS state-level agricultural statistics (as compiled in `mitchell`) report Illinois as a distinct reporting unit separate from the U.S. national total beginning in the mid-1860s, the data this entry was created to host.
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer records Illinois's present-day land area as 149,995 km2 under feature ID USA.14_1.

## Open questions

### oq-1818-boundary-detail

**Whether the 1818 northern-boundary shift needs its own polygon vintage note**

Illinois's enabling act moved the state's northern boundary north of the Indiana/Ohio baseline (41°N) to roughly 42°30'N specifically to give the state a Lake Michigan shoreline and include the future Chicago area. This is treated here as already folded into the 1818 start year rather than as a within-span boundary change, but the GADM 4.1 feature used as proxy reflects only the final, present-day boundary. If any pre-1818 or transitional-era data is ever routed to this entry (unlikely given the 1818 start), the polygon would overstate 1818-era territory by the northern strip; this has not been checked against any data source and is left open.

### oq-usda-nass-first-year

**Exact first year of separate Illinois-level reporting in the routed sources is not confirmed**

The routing decision states USDA NASS state-level series exist from around the mid-1860s (data feeding into `mitchell`), but the precise first data year for Illinois specifically was not verified against the raw extract in this session — only that the candidate list was empty and no existing USA-* row was appropriate. Whether the earliest Illinois-labelled row actually predates 1818 admission (which would indicate a data-quality issue rather than a span question) has not been checked.
