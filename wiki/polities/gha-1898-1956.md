# Ghana (1898-1956)

## Summary

The British Gold Coast colony in its expanded configuration, from 1898 (after the incorporation of Ashanti territory and northern territories) until 1956 (when British Togoland joined following a plebiscite, leading to the 1957 boundary that became independent Ghana). "The Ghanaians were the first people in sub-Saharan Africa to become independent from colonial rule when they gained independence from the British in 1957" [biger-1995 ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). "Gold Coast became an independent state on 6 March 1957, adopting the name Ghana. Ivory Coast gained independence on 7 August 1960" [biger-1995 ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). The 1898 start date marks the incorporation of the Northern Territories and Ashanti after the Fourth Anglo-Ashanti War (1896). CShapes records the boundary change.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Ghana: area 92,098 sq mi [238,533 sq km]. Bounded by Ivory Coast (415 mi; 668 km), Burkina Faso (341 mi; 549 km), and Togo (545 mi; 877 km) [biger-1995 ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). The British established the Gold Coast colony on 13 January 1886; northern territories were made part of the colony in 1897 and the kingdom of Ashanti was formally annexed in 1901 (p.243-244).
