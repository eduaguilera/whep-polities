# Yugoslavia (1918-1919)

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as Yugoslavia from **1918** to
**1919** — a 2-year transitional row [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (later renamed
Yugoslavia in 1929) was proclaimed on 1 December 1918. It
absorbed substantial Austria-Hungary territory: Slovenia,
Croatia, Slavonia, Vojvodina, and Bosnia-Herzegovina
[wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §successor-states](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#successor-states).

COW treats this entity under code 345, which is **Serbian
continuity** from 1878 — there is no break in the COW record at
1918. Serbia was already independent (since the Congress of
Berlin, 1878), and the new kingdom was formed by the merger of
Serbia with the former A-H South Slavic territories. See
[oq-serbia-continuity](#oq-serbia-continuity).

The CSV lists `predecessor = NA` for this row — this is a
**CSV bug**. The territory came from both
[auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md) (for the A-H territories) and the
pre-existing Serbian state (complex multi-predecessor
situation). See [oq-predecessor-csv-bug](#oq-predecessor-csv-bug).

Predecessor per CSV: NA (**bug** — should reference at least
[auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md)). Successor: [f248-1919-1920](f248-1919-1920.md), eventually reaching
[f248-1920-1991](f248-1920-1991.md) (interwar and WWII-era Yugoslavia).

## Open questions

### oq-predecessor-csv-bug

The CSV lists `predecessor = NA` for this row. This is a bug.
Yugoslavia/Kingdom of SCS was formed by merging the pre-existing
Kingdom of Serbia with former Austria-Hungary territories
(Slovenia, Croatia, Slavonia, Vojvodina, Bosnia-Herzegovina).
The predecessor field should reference at least
[auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md), and arguably also the Serbian
polity chain. This is a complex multi-predecessor situation.
Filed as part of
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 1.

### oq-serbia-continuity

COW treats Yugoslavia (code 345) as Serbian continuity from
1878, with no break at 1918. This is a defensible coding
decision (Serbia was the core state that absorbed others) but
raises questions for WHEP: should the 1918 formation of the
Kingdom of SCS be treated as a new polity (territorial change
from ~5 sq deg Serbia to ~17+ sq deg Yugoslavia is massive) or
as a continuation of Serbia? The COW no-break coding and the
WHEP CSV's use of F248 (a WHEP-internal code, not Serbia's SER
code) suggest WHEP already treats this as a distinct entity
from Serbia, despite COW continuity.
