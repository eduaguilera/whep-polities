# Pakistan (territory within British India)

## Summary

This entry tracks "Pakistan" as reported separately from "India" in the
pre-1961 data for 1940-1946. Same principle as Finland/Ireland: the data
compiler extracted Pakistan-territory statistics from British India
records. Previously routed to IND (British India), merging
Pakistan-specific data into the all-India polygon. 10 rows.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status.** Not yet assigned. No proxy copied because
  post-1947 Pakistan lost East Pakistan (Bangladesh) in 1971, and
  British India included territories that did not become Pakistan. The
  territory covered is the provinces of Punjab (west), Sindh, NWFP, and
  Baluchistan -- roughly modern Pakistan minus Bangladesh.
- **Why this entry exists.** The pre-1961 data reports "Pakistan"
  separately from "India" for 1940-1946. Same principle as
  Finland/Ireland: data compiler extracted Pakistan-territory statistics
  from British India records. Previously routed to IND (British India),
  merging Pakistan-specific data into the all-India polygon. 10 rows.
