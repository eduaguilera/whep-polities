# Ghana (to 1888)

## Summary

The British Gold Coast in its earliest tracked configuration, from 1821 (when the British Crown took over the forts from the African Company of Merchants) to 1888, when the CShapes boundary changed following territorial expansion inland. CShapes records area 5.58 sq deg for the period 1886-01-01 to 1888-11-30 (the earliest CShapes record for COW 452), status=colony, owner=Britain (COW 200). Prior to 1886, no CShapes polygon exists; the 1821-1886 extent uses the Paine et al. (2024) polygon for the Ashanti region. "The Ghanaians were the first people in sub-Saharan Africa to become independent from colonial rule when they gained independence from the British in 1957" [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 5.58 sq deg for the period 1886-01-01 to 1888-11-30, status=colony, owner=Britain (COW 200). This represents only the coastal Gold Coast Colony; the interior was still under Ashanti and other indigenous control. The database notes that the start was corrected from 1800 to 1821 because the British Crown took over the forts at that date, while the Ashanti Empire dominated the region in 1800. Bounded by Ivory Coast (415 mi; 668 km), Burkina Faso (341 mi; 549 km), Togo (545 mi; 877 km) [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana).
