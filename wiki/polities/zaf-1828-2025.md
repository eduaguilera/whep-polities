## Data caveats

For 1852-1910, the input data reports "South Africa" alongside separate
"Cape Province", "Natal", and "Cape of Good Hope" entries. The "South
Africa" rows for this pre-Union period may be aggregates that include
Cape and Natal -- risk of double-counting when summing all entries.
After 1910 (Union of South Africa), all subnational entries correctly
route to this polity.

**Alias: "Union of S Africa" (FAO 1952, 1949-1951).** This label is a
name-alias for the Union of South Africa with no territory mismatch.
Routed to ZAF-1828-2025 with high confidence. No polygon change needed;
CShapes 560 (vintage 1910) is appropriate for Union-period data.

**Alias: "cape natal" (Mitchell, 1918-1957).** This label represents a
combined Cape+Natal provincial sub-total. Routed to ZAF-1828-2025 at
medium confidence. Caveat: this provincial aggregate may undercount
national cattle figures since it excludes Transvaal and Orange Free
State provinces. Users summing Mitchell "cape natal" with other
provincial entries for the same years should be aware of potential
double-counting against the national total.
