# Northern Nigeria (1899-1904)

## Summary

The British Protectorate of Northern Nigeria in its initial 1899-1904 configuration, established on 1 January 1900 when the Royal Niger Company's charter was revoked and the British government assumed direct control. "The most populated African country" was Nigeria [biger-1995 §nigeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#nigeria). CShapes does not have a direct record for COW 385 in this date range (CShapes coverage for NNI begins at 1905), so this is a pre-CShapes-start period tracked via the dependencies dataset. The territory was carved from the former Sokoto Caliphate, Bornu, Nupe, and Igala kingdoms.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. The protectorate encompassed the vast northern interior of modern Nigeria, bounded roughly by the Niger-Benue confluence to the south and Lake Chad to the northeast. The boundaries with French West Africa (Niger, Chad) and German Kamerun were being delimited during this period. The CShapes earliest record for COW 385 (beginning 1905) shows area 59.89 sq deg for independent Nigeria.
