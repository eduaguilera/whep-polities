# Niger

## Summary

Niger is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Niger from 1960 to the present. Niger gained independence on 3 August 1960.

## Territorial extent

Niger is a landlocked country in West Africa. The Algeria-Niger boundary runs across a sparsely populated tract of the Sahara for 594 miles (956 km). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
