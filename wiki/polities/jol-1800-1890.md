# Kingdom of Jolof

## Summary

The Kingdom of Jolof (Diolof) was a Wolof kingdom in the interior of Senegal, the rump of the former Jolof Empire that had fragmented c. 1550 when its vassal states (Cayor, Baol, Waalo, Sine, Saloum) gained independence. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1890, when France conquered the kingdom. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). After its fall, the territory was incorporated into French West Africa and is eventually succeeded by modern Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom occupied the interior of Senegal, roughly between the Senegal River to the north and the peanut basin to the south. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). By the 19th century, Jolof was a reduced inland kingdom, having lost its coastal vassals centuries earlier.
