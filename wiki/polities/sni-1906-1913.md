# Southern Nigeria (1906-1913)


> **Polygon corrected 2026-08-05 (issue 51): it was the PRE-merger territory.**
>
> This row and [sni-1899-1906](sni-1899-1906.md) both carried
> `polygon_feature_year: 1899`, so both resolved to the same CShapes step and had
> byte-identical geometry — leaving the 1906 split with no spatial consequence at all,
> though the split exists *because* of a territorial change.
>
> **CShapes models the merger exactly; the row simply pointed at the wrong step.** gwcode
> 4783 has two:
>
> ```
> row 680   1899-1906   130,337 km²    Southern Nigeria Protectorate alone
> row 681   1906-1913   202,103 km²    after Lagos Colony merged in   (+71,766 km²)
> ```
>
> The Lagos Colony merged into the Southern Nigeria Protectorate on 16 February 1906,
> forming the Colony and Protectorate of Southern Nigeria and bringing Lagos and the
> western Yoruba territories with it. 71,766 km² is that addition.
>
> `polygon_feature_year: 1906` selects step 681 unambiguously — only one of the two
> candidates *starts* in 1906, so `find_feature`'s tie-break has a single exact match and
> the binding does not depend on shapefile row order (contrast issue 99, where two steps
> started in the queried year and order decided).
>
> **Why nothing caught it:** neither SNI row declared a `polygon_area_km2`, so
> `validate_polygons`' area check — which is opt-in via that field — had nothing to compare
> (issue 59). An area is now recorded here. `validate_succession_geography` did see the
> symptom, and this was found while triaging its baseline.
## Summary

Southern Nigeria (1906-1913) tracks the Protectorate of Southern Nigeria from February 1906 — when the Lagos Colony was merged into Southern Nigeria, causing the CShapes area to expand — until amalgamation with Northern Nigeria to form the unified Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria on 1 January 1914. CShapes records cowcode 4783 (Southern Nigeria) with a territorial change at 1906-02-16: area increases from ~10.65 to ~16.53 sq deg, reflecting the incorporation of the Lagos Colony. The WHEP row begins in 1906, corresponding to this CShapes area change. The predecessor row SNI-1899-1906 covers the earlier Southern Nigeria period.

## Open questions

### oq-lagos-colony-merger

CShapes records a territorial change for Southern Nigeria at 1906-02-16 (area ~10.65 → ~16.53 sq deg). This corresponds to the merger of Lagos Colony into the Protectorate of Southern Nigeria. The exact date and terms of this merger are not documented in currently ingested sources. The predecessor row SNI-1899-1906 should have Lagos Colony as an additional predecessor; whether a separate Lagos Colony WHEP row exists or should exist is an open question.

### oq-sni-predecessors-check

The CSV lists SNI-1906-1913 predecessors as SNI-1899-1906 only, but the 1906 area change reflects the incorporation of the Lagos Colony (a separate territorial entity). The Lagos Colony does not appear to have a WHEP row — this may be a coverage gap. A future ingest should verify whether the Lagos Colony should have its own row in the 1800-1906 period.
