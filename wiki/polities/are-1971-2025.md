# United Arab Emirates

## Summary

The United Arab Emirates as tracked by WHEP from 1971, when six emirates federated on 2 December 1971, with Ras al Khaimah joining on 10 February 1972. Previously known as the Trucial States under British protection since 1892, the UAE's boundaries with Oman and Saudi Arabia remain complex, including the Musandam exclave.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The UAE is bounded by Oman (336 miles; 540 km) and Saudi Arabia, with coastline on the Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [ARE-1913-1971](are-1913-1971.md) -- federation of Trucial States into independent UAE

## Sourced claims

- The UAE has been independent since 2 December 1971; six emirates united in 1968 and Ras al Khaimah joined on 10 February 1972. [biger-1995 §uae](../sources/biger-1995.md#uae)
- The emirates were known as the Trucial States from 1892, after Britain imposed its protection on the coastal states. [biger-1995 §uae](../sources/biger-1995.md#uae)
- The Oman--UAE boundary includes the Musandam peninsula exclave of Oman, totaling 336 miles (540 km). [biger-1995 §oman](../sources/biger-1995.md#oman)

## Open questions

### oq-oman-uae-enclaves

The Oman--UAE boundary includes several enclaves (Musandam, Madha, Nahwa). Biger notes large sections of these boundaries are not delimited and differences of opinion as to exact positions often arise. The next ingest should clarify how WHEP handles these enclaves.
