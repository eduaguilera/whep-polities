# Sierra Leone (1889-1895)

## Summary

Sierra Leone (1889-1895) covers the British colony of Sierra Leone from 10 August 1889 — following the Anglo-French boundary agreement that reduced the colony's northern and eastern extent — to 23 August 1895, when a further boundary adjustment established the territory's final colonial limits. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=GBR (COW 200). The area is ~5.95 CShapes units, smaller than the preceding period but equal to the subsequent SLE-1895-1961 period, indicating the 1895 boundary adjustment did not change the polygon area (only the precise demarcation).

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory bounded by French Guinea (north and east), Liberia (south-east), and the Atlantic Ocean (west). CShapes 2.0 area ~5.95 (from 1889-08-10 to 1895-08-23). Same area as SLE-1895-1961. Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Predecessors and successors

- **Successor:** [SLE-1895-1961](sle-1895-1961.md) — Sierra Leone after 1895 boundary (same area; protectorate proclaimed 1896)

## Contradictions

The CShapes boundary change at 1895-08-24 does not change the polygon area (SLE-1889-1895 and SLE-1895-1961 both have area ~5.95). Under the WHEP polity-definition rule (split only on territorial change), SLE-1889-1895 and SLE-1895-1961 could be merged into a single row. The current two-row structure follows the CShapes time-step rather than a genuine territorial change. This may be a chain-architecture candidate for review.

## Open questions

### oq-sle-1895-boundary-merge-candidate

The CShapes area for SLE-1889-1895 and SLE-1895-1961 is identical (~5.95). The WHEP polity-definition rule specifies that same area = same WHEP polity = one row. The current split at 1895-08-24 follows a CShapes administrative time-step (possibly the formal Anglo-French Convention ratification date) without a territorial change. A human should review whether these two rows should be merged into SLE-1889-1961.
