# Switzerland

## Summary

This row tracks Switzerland as a single continuous territorial-economic
unit from **1800** to **2025** — a **226-year unbroken row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Switzerland's borders
are extremely stable. Biger confirms that "Since 1815 the northern
boundary of Switzerland has never been changed"
[biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).
The France-Switzerland boundary "dates from the Second Treaty of Paris
(20 May 1814)." Switzerland was occupied by Napoleon in 1799 and
"reestablished as a totally independent state" in 1815 at the Congress
of Vienna, with perpetual neutrality guaranteed since 1815.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
no splits are warranted because metropolitan borders have been
essentially unchanged since 1815.

- **1800 start** is the database floor, not a territorial event.
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Switzerland is still active.

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records
  Switzerland (`cowcode=225`) as a single static polygon from 1886-01-01
  to 2019-12-31 with area ~4.89 sq degrees
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). No polygon
  changes at all — Switzerland's borders are static across CShapes's
  entire coverage.
- **Pre-1886 extent.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The same
  polygon is likely back-projected given the extreme border stability
  since 1815.
- **Metropolitan area.** ~41,285 km². Population ~7M (1994).
- **Minor boundary changes.** Dappes valley exchange with France on
  8 December 1862 — a minor adjustment not reflected in CShapes or
  triggering a WHEP split
  [biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).

## Sourced claims

- **1799-1815: Napoleonic period.** Switzerland was occupied by Napoleon
  in 1799 and reorganized as the Helvetic Republic (1798-1803), then
  the Act of Mediation (1803-1815). Switzerland was "reestablished as
  a totally independent state" in 1815 at the Congress of Vienna
  [biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).
- **1815: Congress of Vienna.** Perpetual neutrality guaranteed.
  Northern boundary confirmed and "has never been changed" since 1815
  [biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).
- **1814: France-Switzerland boundary.** The boundary "dates from the
  Second Treaty of Paris (20 May 1814)"
  [biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).
- **1862: Dappes valley exchange.** Minor boundary exchange with France
  on 8 December 1862
  [biger-1995 §france-switzerland](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-switzerland).
  Not a substantial territorial change.
- **Static CShapes polygon.** CShapes records a single static polygon
  for Switzerland across its entire 1886-2019 coverage
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). No polygon changes.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists Switzerland (`SWI`,
  `ccode=225`) with a single continuous tenure from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). No breaks —
  consistent with perpetual neutrality (no WWII occupation gap unlike
  Belgium or France).
