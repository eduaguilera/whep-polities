# Netherlands

## Summary

This row tracks the Netherlands as a territorial-economic unit from
**1830** (Belgian secession) to **2025**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The **1830 start
is a real territorial event** -- the Belgian Revolution of August 1830
split the United Kingdom of the Netherlands, reducing its territory by
approximately 42% (from ~71K km² to ~41K km²). The rump Netherlands
retained the northern provinces and continued as a sovereign state.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
this row begins at a real territorial event (loss of Belgium), not the
database floor.

- **1830 start** is a real territorial event (Belgian secession).
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; the Netherlands is still active.

Predecessor: [nld-1800-1830](nld-1800-1830.md) (United Kingdom of the
Netherlands). The Belgian secession split the United Kingdom into
Belgium, rump Netherlands, and Luxembourg.
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (Netherlands 1834-1839)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Area ~41K km²,
  roughly 58% of the former United Kingdom of the Netherlands.
- **Post-1886 coverage.** CShapes 2.0 records the Netherlands
  (`cowcode=210`) from 1886 onward
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Metropolitan stability.** The Netherlands' European borders have
  been essentially stable since the Treaty of London (1839), which
  formalized the Belgian separation and established the modern
  Dutch-Belgian boundary.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [nld-1800-1830](nld-1800-1830.md) -- the United
  Kingdom of the Netherlands. The Belgian Revolution (August 1830)
  stripped the southern provinces. Territory reduced by ~42%.
- **Successor:** NA -- the Netherlands is still active at the database
  end year of 2025.

## Sourced claims

- **1830: Belgian Revolution.** The Belgian Revolution of 25 August
  1830 created Belgium from the southern provinces of the United
  Kingdom of the Netherlands, reducing the Netherlands to the
  northern provinces (~41K km²).
- **1839: Treaty of London.** Signed 19 April 1839, this treaty
  formalized the Dutch-Belgian boundary. The Netherlands recognized
  Belgian independence. Luxembourg was separated as a distinct entity.
- **1890: Luxembourg personal union dissolved.** Luxembourg had been
  in personal union with the Netherlands since 1815. When William III
  died in 1890, the union dissolved because Luxembourg's Salic law
  excluded his daughter Wilhelmina from the Luxembourg throne.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists the Netherlands (`NTH`,
  `ccode=210`) from 1816-01-01 to 1940-07-14 (German occupation),
  then 1945-06-26 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
  COW makes no split at 1830 -- it treats the Netherlands as
  continuous across the Belgian secession. The CSV has `cow_code = NA`,
  which is an omission -- the COW code 210 should apply. See
  [oq-cow-code-na](#oq-cow-code-na).

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-na

The CSV has `cow_code = NA` for NLD-1830-2025. COW code 210
(Netherlands, `NTH`) applies continuously from 1816-01-01 onward
(with a WWII occupation gap). This is an omission in the CSV, not
a genuine absence from COW. Same pattern as
[nld-1800-1830](nld-1800-1830.md) and the Russian Empire pre-1886
rows.
