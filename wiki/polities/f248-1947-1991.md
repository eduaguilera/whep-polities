# Yugoslavia (1947-1991)

## Summary

Split from F248-1920-1991. SFRY (Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) period, from the 1947-02-10 Paris Peace Treaty (Istria/Zadar acquisition from Italy) to the start of dissolution in 1991. The polygon is the same CShapes 1920 geometry used for F248-1920-1947, carrying `polygon_status=estimate` because the ~4,165 km² acquisition is not reflected.

COW: YUG, 345.

Predecessor: [f248-1920-1947](f248-1920-1947.md).
Successors: [mkd-1991-2025](mkd-1991-2025.md), [f248-1991-1992](f248-1991-1992.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0`, `cowcode=345`, `polygon_feature_year=1920`. This is the pre-1947 boundary geometry reused for the post-1947 period.
- **Area.** ~255,000 km² (polygon). Actual post-1947 territory ~259,165 km² (~255,000 + ~4,165 km² acquired).
- **Polygon status.** `estimate` — direction: UNDERSTATE. The 1920 CShapes geometry omits the ~4,165 km² acquired at the 1947 Paris Peace Treaty (Istria ~3,600 km², Zadar ~50 km², Free Territory of Trieste Zone B ~515 km²). This is a ~1.7% underestimate of the actual post-1947 territory.

## Polygon decision

CShapes 2.0 has no distinct post-1947 Yugoslavia polygon. Query of `cshapes2_full.gpkg` confirms `cowcode=345` uses identical geometry (~28.72 sq deg, ~255,000 km²) from 1920-06-04 to 1991-11-19 without any 1947 or 1954 boundary update. This is consistent with CShapes' documented exclusion of territorial transfers under 10,000 km² (Schvitz et al. 2022); the Yugoslav acquisition totals ~4,165 km² which falls below that threshold.

No composed polygon was built because no authoritative 1947 Istrian boundary GIS layer was available in the project's priority stack (CShapes/Cliopatria/CHGIS/Paine/GHGIS).

**Open improvement:** A composed polygon (1920 CShapes base + GADM Croatia/Slovenia Istrian boundary overlay) would eliminate the ~1.7% underestimate and could be built if an authoritative 1947 Istrian line becomes available. This would upgrade `polygon_status` from `estimate` to `assigned` and change `polygon_feature_year` from 1920 to 1947.

## Sourced claims

- **1947-02-10: Paris Peace Treaty.** Italy ceded Istria (~3,600 km²), Zadar (~50 km²), and associated islands to Yugoslavia. Start-of-row event.
- **1954: London Memorandum.** Free Territory of Trieste Zone B (~515 km²) de facto administered by Yugoslavia (formal cession 1975 Treaty of Osimo). Minor additional acquisition not reflected in polygon.
- **1980-05-04: Tito's death.** Beginning of post-Tito political crisis.
- **1991-06-25: Slovenia and Croatia declare independence.** Start of Yugoslav dissolution. End-of-row event.

## Decisions

- **Split from F248-1920-1991 (2026-07-01).** The original 1920-1991 row spanned the 1947 Paris Peace Treaty border change. Split at 1947-02-10 to give each sub-period its own polygon entry. The post-1947 period carries `polygon_status=estimate` because no distinct post-1947 CShapes geometry exists and the ~4,165 km² acquisition is below CShapes' 10,000 km² threshold.
