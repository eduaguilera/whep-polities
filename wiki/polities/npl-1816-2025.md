# Nepal

## Summary

Nepal as tracked by WHEP from 1816, following the Anglo-Nepalese War (1814--1815) and the Treaty of Sugauli, which established Nepal's modern boundaries along the Kali and Mechi rivers. Nepal successfully resisted British colonization and remained relatively closed to international influence until the 1950s.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Cliopatria. Nepal is bounded by China (768 mi; 1,236 km) in the north and India (1,050 mi; 1,690 km) in the east, south and west. Approximately 149,000 km2.
