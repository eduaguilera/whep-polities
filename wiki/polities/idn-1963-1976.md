# Indonesia (1963-1976)

## Summary

Indonesia from the **1963-05-01** hand-over of western New Guinea (UNTEA to
Indonesian administration) to the **1976-07-17** formal annexation of East Timor
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 850
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). `end_year` is exclusive, so the
row covers 1963-1975.

Two events sit inside the span and neither moves a border: the Act of Free Choice
(August 1969), which confirmed the West New Guinea transfer that had already
happened, and Konfrontasi (1963-1966), which transferred no territory in either
source.

## Territorial extent

This row's polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature gwcode `850`, time-step **`1969-09-17 →
1976-07-16`**, measuring **1,877,243 km²** in the shapefile
(`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`, ESRI:54034, measured directly — not a
wiki-source citation) and **1,876,795 km²** in the committed
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` after simplification.

That polygon is exactly Indonesia-with-western-New-Guinea, and "exactly" is
measured, not asserted: the union of feature 850's `1949-12-29` step (1,466,882 km²)
and feature 851 (410,361 km²) differs from it by **0.0 km²** of symmetric
difference. So the geometry this row needs from 1963 onward already exists in the
source; what carries a 1969 stamp is the source's *vintage*, not the territory.
Its successor `IDN-1976-2002` is 1,891,777 km² in the same CRS, ~14,982 km² more,
consistent with East Timor (~14,874-14,919 km² by most estimates).

**Why the row starts six years before its polygon's vintage.** Administration of
western New Guinea passed from the Netherlands to UNTEA on 1962-08-15 and from
UNTEA to Indonesia on **1963-05-01** — a date CShapes itself records, as the third
of three geometrically identical time-steps for feature 851 (measurement in
[nng-1949-1963](nng-1949-1963.md#the-1963-vs-1969-question-settled-2026-08-14)).
Biger 1995 gives the same year
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). The 1969-09-17 stamp on
feature 850 is the Act of Free Choice, i.e. the *sovereignty* date, which under
[log decision-row-boundary-dating](../log.md#decision-row-boundary-dating) cannot set
a WHEP row boundary. The row therefore starts 1963 and keeps the 1969 vintage in
`polygon_feature_year`, which is the field that records what the source says.

The 1975 invasion and the 1976 formal annexation of East Timor are treated as one
event at 1976 for row purposes, following CShapes' own coding of the change
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). That choice
is unchanged by this row's re-dating, and is a case where the de facto date (December
1975) and the source's date (1976-07-17) are 19 months apart rather than six years;
see *Open questions*.

## Sourced claims

- Indonesia "has expanded to include ... formerly western New Guinea (1963) and the
  former Portuguese colony of East Timor"
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) — Biger gives East
  Timor's addition as "1955" in the printed text, almost certainly a typo for
  **1975**.
- "The East Timorese long resisted Indonesian rule. The Central Moluccas also
  attempted to establish an independent state but the revolt was quelled"
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) — the Moluccas (RMS)
  revolt predates this row (1950), but the sentence is Biger's framing of ongoing
  internal resistance during the period.
- The Portuguese colony of East Timor was invaded by Indonesia in 1975
  [biger-1995 §timor-leste](../sources/biger-1995.md#timor-leste).
- CShapes codes territorial changes to dependent/annexed territory as one of three
  change types, including "transfers of sovereignty over dependent territories"
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes) — the
  category both the West New Guinea and the East Timor changes fall into, and the
  reason its stamps sit on the treaty/recognition dates.
- CShapes 2.0 is distributed in a COW-coded and a GW-coded version
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states). **The file
  on disk is the GW-coded one**: `CShapes-2.0.shp`'s only state-identifier columns
  are `gwcode`, `gwsdate`, `gwedate` and their year/month/day parts, with no
  `cowcode` column at all (direct read of the shapefile). The page this one
  descends from asserted the opposite and inferred that its 1969 vintage "reflects
  COW's dating"; corrected here, because the inference was load-bearing for the
  dating question this row settles.
- Per the COW state-system list (`statelist2024.csv`, columns per
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema)),
  Indonesia (`ccode=850`) has a single continuous tenure entry, 1949-12-27 to
  2024-12-31, with no COW-coded exit or re-entry inside this row's span — any
  territorial change here is a border change, not a change of statehood.

## Open questions

### oq-east-timor-1975-vs-1976

The same convention applied to East Timor would ask whether this row should end at
the **December 1975 invasion** rather than the **1976-07-17 formal annexation**:
de facto control preceded de jure annexation by about 19 months, and
[log decision-row-boundary-dating](../log.md#decision-row-boundary-dating) prefers the
de facto date. It was left alone here because issue 23 is about the West New Guinea
boundary and because one year of one row is a smaller error than the six this PR
moves; deciding it needs a source on when Indonesian statistics began covering the
territory. Flagged rather than changed.

## Renamed from `IDN-1969-1976` (2026-08-14)

Same polygon binding, same sources, same prose lineage; `start_year` moved from
1969 to 1963 and the code followed it, absorbing what used to be the last six years
of `IDN-1949-1969`. `IDN-1969-1976` is retired with a redirect here. Issue 23.

## Status corrected to `draft` (2026-07-24)

This page was flagged **`wiki_status: reviewed`** while containing **no source
citations at all** — its territorial content was a stub. The `reviewed` label is
supposed to mean a human checked the claims against sources, so carrying it here
was misleading: it invited both readers and the verification pipeline to treat
unsourced assertions as settled.

An audit found **10 of 72 `reviewed` pages** in this state — the whole IND chain,
the whole IDN chain, and CHN-1914-1921. All ten are downgraded to `draft`, which
is what they actually are. Nothing about the polity's data or polygon changed;
only the honesty of the label.

To earn `reviewed` this page needs its **Territorial extent** and **Sourced
claims** sections filled from real sources (Biger 1995, CShapes 2.0, the COW
state-system list) with inline citations, and the specific territorial changes
inside its span identified. Until then the assertion-verification pipeline treats
it as an unverified draft and corroborates its claims independently — see
[log decision-reviewed-status-audit](../log.md#decision-reviewed-status-audit).
