# Turkey (Anatolia, to 1913)

## Summary

This entry tracks "Turkey" as reported in Mitchell's Historical
Statistics and FAOSTAT for present-day borders retroactively. Pamuk
confirms that these sources report Turkey for modern Anatolian borders.
Previously routed to OTT (Ottoman Empire), which attributed Anatolian
agricultural data to an empire spanning Arabia, the Levant, and North
Africa. 30 rows, 1880-1912.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status.** Unassigned. The Ottoman Empire polygon (CShapes-2.0
  cowcode 640, vintage 1886, ~2.92M km²) was mistakenly recorded in the
  database as assigned but does NOT represent this polity's territory.
  That polygon covers the full Ottoman Empire including Arabia, the
  Levant, the Balkans, and North Africa, overstating Anatolian extent by
  approximately 3.7×. It has been cleared (polygon_source=none).
- **Recommended proxy.** CShapes-2.0 cowcode 640 at vintage 1923
  (~779,964 km²) — the post-WWI Turkish Republic boundary — is a
  reasonable ESTIMATE for Anatolian + Eastern Thrace extent during
  1800–1913. Caveats: (1) Eastern Thrace was lost 1913–1923 and not
  recovered until the Treaty of Lausanne (1923), so the 1923 boundary
  slightly overstates Ottoman Anatolia; (2) minor border changes occurred
  in eastern Anatolia (Kars/Ardahan region). The same polygon is used by
  [TUR-1920-2025](tur-1920-2025.md). Until a purpose-built historical
  Anatolian polygon is sourced, this proxy should be applied with
  polygon_status=estimate and a note on the approximation direction.
- **Why this entry exists.** Mitchell's Historical Statistics and FAOSTAT
  report "Turkey" for present-day borders retroactively (Pamuk confirms).
  Previously routed to OTT (Ottoman Empire), which attributed Anatolian
  agricultural data to an empire spanning Arabia, the Levant, and North
  Africa. 30 rows, 1880-1912.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-source

A purpose-built polygon covering Anatolia + Eastern Thrace (~780,000 km²)
is needed for the 1800–1913 period. The CShapes-2.0 vintage-1923 Turkey
polygon (~779,964 km²) is available as a documented ESTIMATE (see
Territorial extent above) but should be confirmed against Ottoman
administrative maps before assigning. Historical boundary datasets
(Cliopatria, CHGIS, GHGIS) or constructed Anatolian boundaries may
provide a more accurate pre-WWI extent.
