# Turkey (Anatolia, to 1913)

## Summary

This entry tracks "Turkey" as reported in Mitchell's Historical
Statistics and FAOSTAT for present-day borders retroactively. Pamuk
confirms that these sources report Turkey for modern Anatolian borders.
Previously routed to OTT (Ottoman Empire), which attributed Anatolian
agricultural data to an empire spanning Arabia, the Levant, and North
Africa. 30 rows, 1880-1912.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status.** ESTIMATE. CShapes-2.0 cowcode 640 vintage 1923
  (~779,964 km²) — the post-WWI Turkish Republic boundary — is assigned
  as a proxy for Anatolian + Eastern Thrace extent during 1800–1913.
  This is the same polygon used by [TUR-1920-2025](tur-1920-2025.md).
- **Approximation direction.** The 1923 boundary slightly *overstates*
  pre-1913 Ottoman Anatolian extent. Eastern Thrace was lost in the
  First Balkan War (1912–1913) and not recovered until the Treaty of
  Lausanne (1923), meaning the pre-1913 Ottoman administration of Eastern
  Thrace is included in the 1923 polygon but was not part of the rump
  state during 1913–1923. The overstatement is modest (Eastern Thrace
  ~23,764 km²) relative to the total (~780k km²), roughly 3%.
  Additionally, minor border changes occurred in eastern Anatolia
  (Kars/Ardahan region, ceded to Russia 1878, recovered 1921) which
  are captured within the 1923 boundary.
- **Magnitude check.** ~779,964 km² vs Ottoman Empire full extent
  ~2.92M km² at its 1886 peak. This polity correctly represents
  Anatolian-only extent at ~27% of the empire, consistent with Mitchell
  and FAOSTAT reporting for present-day Turkish borders (Pamuk confirms).
  Data values (cotton seed, silk-worm cocoons) are a small fraction of
  empire-wide totals, confirming sub-territory scope.
- **Why this entry exists.** Mitchell's Historical Statistics and FAOSTAT
  report "Turkey" for present-day borders retroactively (Pamuk confirms).
  Previously routed to OTT (Ottoman Empire), which attributed Anatolian
  agricultural data to an empire spanning Arabia, the Levant, and North
  Africa. 30 rows, 1880-1912.
- **Pending improvement.** A purpose-built polygon covering Anatolia
  (~780,000 km²) for the pre-1913 period would improve accuracy. Sources
  to check: Cliopatria, CHGIS, GHGIS, or constructed Ottoman
  administrative boundaries. Until then, polygon_status=estimate.
