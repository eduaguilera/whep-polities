# Gibraltar

## Summary

A headland at the southern tip of Iberia, British since the 1713 Treaty of Utrecht.
Spain claims it; this database records *de facto* administration.

At **6.8 km²** this is the second-smallest polity here, after
[vat-1929-2025](vat-1929-2025.md). `polygon_confidence` is `low` for that reason and not because
the outline is doubtful: any per-km² figure computed on 6.8 km² carries absolute uncertainty
comparable to the territory, exactly as recorded for the Holy See.

## Why this entry exists

**FAOSTAT reports land-use data for this territory and this database had no row for it**
(issue 209). Measured on the `faostat-landuse` pin:

```
FAOSTAT area 82   196 rows   1961-2025
```

Downstream those rows resolved to `ROW-1850-2025` — an aggregate with no territory and no
geometry — so their land was published under an identity that describes nowhere. `eduaguilera/whep`
promoted each folded reporting area to its own numeric code in whep#628 but left the *territorial*
identity behind: all 62 still carried `polity_code = "ROW-1850-2025"`. This row is what whep#717
can give it instead.

### Already inside the statistical buckets, and that is not a new overlap

```
GIB ∩ REUR-1850-2025     6.2 km²   86.0%
GIB ∩ ROW-1850-2025      4.1 km²   56.4%
```

The same relationship every other bucket component with its own polity already has — `FRO`, `NCL`,
`GLP`, `MTQ`, and the `ATF`/`SGS`/`WLF` added in #201. The buckets are in
`validate_spatial_containment`'s `LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` for exactly this reason. What changes is
that data reported against this territory can now reach it.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = GIB`.

```
GADM 4.1 measured      7.2 km²
official land area     6.8 km²
ratio                  1.059x
```

The declared area is the **official land area**, not what the polygon measures, so
`validate_polygons` check A compares the geometry against something independent of it — the point
issue 195 is about, where 104 of 200 declared areas agree with their own geometry and so cannot
disagree with it.

`polygon_status: estimate` because at this magnitude GADM's divergence from an official
gazetteer is the low-magnitude regime rather than a defect: the same comparison across this
database's micro-territories runs from 42% under to 39% over, documented on
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md).

## Contradictions

`ESP-*` does **not** contain Gibraltar — measured at 0 km² against every live Spanish period — so
the enclave is not double-claimed, unlike the `VAT`/`ITA` and `SMR`/`ITA` pairs that #197 tracks.
GADM cuts Gibraltar out of Spain, where CShapes-derived national polygons generally do not cut out
enclaves.
