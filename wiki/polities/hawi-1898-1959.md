# Territory of Hawaii

## Summary

This row tracks **Hawaii** from the **formal annexation by the United
States (1898-07-07, Newlands Resolution)** to **statehood as the 50th
state (1959-08-21)**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Hawaii had been an
independent kingdom until the 1893 overthrow of Queen Liliuokalani; a
short-lived Republic of Hawaii (1894–1898) preceded annexation. As a
US territory, Hawaii had a non-voting delegate in Congress, a
presidentially-appointed governor, and a territorial legislature.
Statehood was granted by the Hawaii Admission Act signed 1959-03-18,
with statehood effective 1959-08-21.

The archipelago consists of eight main islands in the North Pacific,
with a total land area of approximately 16,636 km2. Honolulu (O'ahu)
served as the territorial capital and the seat of Pearl Harbor naval
base, whose attack on 1941-12-07 brought the United States into the
Second World War.

No WHEP predecessor row for the Kingdom of Hawaii (c. 1810–1893) or
the Republic of Hawaii (1894–1898) currently exists in the database;
the predecessor field is left blank pending creation of those rows.
Successor: [usa-1959-2025](usa-1959-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` (Schvitz et al. 2022), feature
  `cowcode=4`, "Hawaii", dated `1898-07-06` to `1959-08-20`
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is an
  **exact_historical** polygon: CShapes 2.0 includes colonial
  dependencies and explicitly records Hawaii as a US colony
  (status="colony", owner=cowcode 2) for precisely this period.
  The polygon covers all islands of the Hawaiian archipelago.
- **Method.** `exact_historical` — the CShapes 2.0 feature matches
  the entity, period, and geographic extent with no proxy needed.
- **Area.** ~16,686 km2 (projected to EPSG:6933 equal-area). This
  is consistent with published land area of the state of Hawaii
  (~16,636 km2); the small difference reflects projection rounding.
- **Confidence.** High. The CShapes 2.0 dataset is the authoritative
  GIS source for sovereign states and colonial dependencies from 1886
  onward. The Hawaii feature covers the annexation-to-statehood window
  exactly.
- **No proxy needed.** Steps 2 (composed_union), 3 (period_proxy),
  and 4 (modern_proxy / constructed_estimate) were not required.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** No WHEP row for the **Kingdom of Hawaii** (unified
  c. 1810 under Kamehameha I; overthrown 1893) or the **Republic of
  Hawaii** (1894–1898) currently exists. These should be created as
  separate entries; when created, HAWI-1898-1959 should be updated to
  reference them. The Kingdom of Hawaii was recognized as sovereign by
  the United States and several European powers.
- **Successor.** [usa-1959-2025](usa-1959-2025.md) — upon admission
  to statehood in 1959, Hawaii's territory was absorbed into the
  United States. The HAWI row ends at statehood; subsequent Hawaii
  data are covered under USA-1959-2025.

## Contradictions

### con-continental-us-misrouting-resolved

**Resolved 2026-06-29.** When iso3=USA was set, HAWI appeared in the
iso_fam['USA'] bucket. The layer-B pipeline's pick_by_year() selected
HAWI (earlier CSV row) over USA-1867-1959 for years 1898-1959,
misrouting ~6,945 continental-US rows from three sources:

| source  | rows misrouted | example magnitude |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| juan    | ~6,195        | maize ~67.9 Mt (year-aggregated) |
| mitchell| ~621          | wheat ~7.3 Mt |
| iia     | ~248          | various crops |

Realistic HAWI agricultural magnitudes are orders of magnitude smaller
(sugarcane ~1-3 Mt, pineapple ~0.3-0.7 Mt for peak years; total
arable ~0.1 Mha). The fix: iso3 cleared in polities_database.csv;
source+year-restricted aliases added in applied_aliases.csv for
juan/mitchell "united states of america" → USA-1867-1959. The polygon
(CShapes 2.0 cowcode=4, 16,686 km2) is unchanged and correct.

## Decisions

- **Polygon selection.** CShapes 2.0 cowcode=4 is an exact match
  for the Territory of Hawaii; no proxy or estimate was required.
  The polygon_feature_id follows the cowcode convention used
  elsewhere in the WHEP database (e.g., USA-1959-2025 uses `2`).
- **iso3.** Cleared to empty (was `USA`). Although Hawaii was a US
  territory and modern ISO 3166 assigns the islands to the United
  States, setting iso3=USA placed HAWI in the iso_fam['USA'] bucket
  alongside USA-1867-1959 and ALK-1867-1959. The layer-B matching
  pipeline's fam_for_code() expands any alias target to its full ISO
  family; because HAWI appeared earlier in CSV row order (row 275 vs.
  row 622 for USA-1867-1959), pick_by_year() selected HAWI for all
  years 1898-1959, routing ~6,945 continental-US data rows (Juan
  ~6,195, Mitchell ~621, IIA ~248) labeled "united states of america"
  to HAWI instead of USA-1867-1959. This produced impossibly large
  staple values (maize ~67.9 Mt, wheat ~7.3 Mt) for a 16,686 km2
  island group. Clearing iso3 removes HAWI from iso_fam['USA'] so the
  existing year-ranged aliases for "united states of america" correctly
  route to USA-1867-1959. Data labeled "hawaii" continues to route
  correctly via the existing blanket alias hawaii → HAWI-1898-1959.
  Two belt-and-suspenders source-restricted aliases were also added in
  applied_aliases.csv (juan and mitchell, 1898-1959 → USA-1867-1959).
- **continent.** Assigned `Oceania` following the geographic
  classification of the Hawaiian archipelago. The USA rows use
  `North America`; Hawaii's geographic placement in the Pacific
  warrants the `Oceania` assignment consistent with its island
  character.
- **Predecessor blank.** Kingdom of Hawaii and Republic of Hawaii
  are not yet in the database. Rather than leave a dangling
  reference, the predecessor field is left empty pending those
  entries. This is documented here as an open question.

## Open questions

### oq-predecessor-kingdom

The **Kingdom of Hawaii** (c. 1810–1893) and **Republic of Hawaii**
(1894–1898) predate this row. CShapes 2.0 does not cover them (its
scope begins 1886 and Hawaii only appears as a US colony from 1898).
Cliopatria should be checked for a Kingdom of Hawaii entry. If found,
the predecessor field here should be updated.

### oq-continent-classification

Assigning `Oceania` vs. `North America` is a judgment call.
Geographically Hawaii is in Oceania / Polynesia; politically it was
administered from Washington D.C. The USA rows use `North America`.
Review for consistency with how other Pacific territories are coded
in WHEP.
