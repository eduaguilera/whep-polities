# South Africa (Union and Republic)

## Summary

The Union of South Africa, formed on 31 May 1910 by the unification of the Cape Colony, Colony of Natal, Transvaal Colony, and Orange River Colony. Declared a republic on 31 May 1961 and left the Commonwealth. Apartheid regime 1948–1994. Democratic transition under Mandela 1994. Still active as of 2025. Area ~1.22M km².

**Previous code ZAF-1828-2025 retired 2026-07-01.** That entry incorrectly set start_year=1828, predating the Union by 82 years and creating a direct territorial conflict with the concurrent polity CAP-1800-1910 (~601k km²). The 1828 start had no documentary basis; the correct start is 31 May 1910.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 cowcode=560, vintage 1910, area ~1,221,037 km². Covers the full Union of South Africa (Cape Province + Natal + Transvaal + Orange Free State) from 1910. South West Africa (Namibia) administered separately under League of Nations mandate and tracked under NAM-1920-1990.

## Data routing

### dec-natal-label-1910-1957

Data labelled **'natal'** in Mitchell sources for years **1910–1957** is routed to ZAF-1910-2025.

**Basis:** The Colony of Natal was absorbed into the Union of South Africa on 31 May 1910 as Natal Province. The polities NAT-1843-1895 and NAT-1895-1910 end in 1910 and have no coverage of post-Union years. Mitchell's data for 'natal' 1910–1957 records provincial figures within South Africa.

**Approximation:** Natal Province (~94,361 km²) represents approximately 6% of ZAF's total area (~1,221,037 km²). Routing provincial data to the national polity overstates territorial scope roughly 13×. This is a known sub-territory approximation — no Natal-within-ZAF granular polity currently exists in WHEP.

**Alias added:** `natal` 1910–1957 → ZAF-1910-2025 in `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/applied_aliases.csv` (2026-07-02).
