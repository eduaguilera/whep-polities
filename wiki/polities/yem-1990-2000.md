# Yemen (1990-2000)

## Summary

Yemen (1990-2000) tracks the Republic of Yemen from unification in 1990 to the WHEP database split point at 2000. North Yemen (Yemen Arab Republic) and South Yemen (People's Democratic Republic of Yemen) merged on 22 May 1990 to form the Republic of Yemen. Biger notes: "In 1990 they merged to form the Yemen Republic." The WHEP split at year 2000 does not correspond to a known territorial change — CShapes records the same area (~11.50 sq deg) for all Yemen entries from 1918 to 1990, and the CShapes record for unified Yemen covers 1990-05-22 onwards at the same area. The 2000 split is likely a data-source cutoff artifact rather than a territorial event; see [oq-yem-2000-split-reason](#oq-yem-2000-split-reason).

## Open questions

### oq-yem-2000-split-reason

The WHEP split at year 2000 (from YEM-1990-2000 to YEM-2000-2025) does not correspond to a known territorial change. CShapes records the same area for unified Yemen before and after 2000. The split may be a data-source cutoff artifact (e.g., from FAOSTAT or M49 dataset boundaries) rather than a territorial event. The next ingest should check whether any boundary change occurred around 2000, particularly in relation to the Saudi Arabia-Yemen border agreements.

### oq-north-south-yemen-predecessors

The CSV predecessor field for this row is NA. The two pre-unification Yemeni states (North Yemen / YAR and South Yemen / PDRY) are not present as separate rows in the database. This is a coverage gap — approximately 23 years of North and South Yemeni territorial history (1967-1990) is unaccounted for by explicit predecessor rows.
