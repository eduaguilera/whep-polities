# Java (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Java, "one of the Greater Sunda Islands", "the core of the colonial Dutch East Indies". Madura, off its north-east coast, has its own row ([idn-mad-1800-1949](idn-mad-1800-1949.md)). [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §java](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#java)

## Why this entry exists

The label `dutch java` reports it alone for 1909-1948 (**46 (year, label) pairs**), beside `dutch east indies` and its other islands. No polity was this island before 1949; the 1949-1951 rows (IDN-JVM, IDN-BLB, IDN-OTH) group islands differently and begin in 1949. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
