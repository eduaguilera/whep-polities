# Seine-et-Marne

## Summary

This entry represents Seine-et-Marne, a French département in the Île-de-France region, southeast of Paris, bordering Paris's outer suburbs and extending into rural Brie country toward Champagne. It is coded as a `subnational` polity because a département is an administrative subdivision of France, not a sovereign state or a distinct internationally-recognised territory -- France's sovereignty over this area is continuous and undisputed throughout the entry's span, and the national FRA rows in this table already carry that sovereignty claim for the whole country. This row exists solely to carry a *sub-national reporting territory*: statistics keyed to "Seine-et-Marne" or its INSEE code 77 specifically, as opposed to all-France totals, need somewhere to route that isn't the national polygon. The entry follows the country convention's default start rule (1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform) as adjusted here to 1800 to match the earliest available French national container row (see decisions), and runs to the open end_year 2025 since the département remains an active administrative unit of France today with no successor.

**Why this entry exists.** This page implements the routing decision for unit FRA-FR102 (admin_name "FR102", country France), which resolved via create_new because no existing FRA polity IS this territory -- the national FRA rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) describe the whole country, and a département cannot be represented by its country's row. The unit's data spans 1862-2023 and falls entirely within the proposed 1790-2025 administrative existence (adjusted here to 1800-2025, see decisions), so a single create_new polity suffices with no back_cast or matched segments. Before this decision the data for this unit was, by construction of the routing pipeline, unmatched rather than misrouted -- match_existing was excluded outright since no existing row's territory equals Seine-et-Marne's. What confirms Seine-et-Marne as a distinct, statistically-reported entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision is that it is one of the original ~83 départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary reform and has been administered, and presumably tabulated in French statistical yearbooks (INSEE and its predecessors), as a discrete unit under the numeric code 77 ever since -- the same status that INSEE gives every other département in the country's statistical apparatus.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision already established this is a `new_source_needed` case: no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements. gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated subset that omits France entirely (even though GADM's global release does cover French départements); cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe are country-level only and cannot supply a sub-national boundary; mapspain-ign is specific to Spain. The standard remedy would be to register GADM's global admin-2 layer or French IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS product, neither of which is on disk in this repository today.

**Territory description:** Seine-et-Marne is the largest département by area in the Île-de-France region, occupying the southeastern portion of metropolitan Paris's surrounding region. On a modern map it is bounded by the départements of Seine-Saint-Denis, Val-de-Marne and Essonne to the west, Yonne and Aube to the south and east, and Marne and Aisne to the northeast; its administrative seat (préfecture) is Melun. It covers approximately 5,915 km2 -- this figure is a published administrative-area statistic for the modern département, not a measurement from any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned (see polygon status above). The département includes eastern Paris exurbs (Marne-la-Vallée, home to Disneyland Paris) as well as substantial agricultural land in the Brie plateau.

## Predecessors and successors

Seine-et-Marne has no predecessor or successor polity in this table: it was created directly by the 1790 Revolutionary départements reform (predating this database's coverage, see the open question on the 1790-1800 gap) and has continued as an administrative unit of France without interruption through 2025, so both fields are empty. It is not known to have been split off from, merged into, or ceded to any other polity tracked here.

## Sourced claims

- INSEE and the ISO 3166-2:FR standard both identify Seine-et-Marne with the numeric code 77, distinct from any letter-based ISO 3166-2 subdivision code scheme used for other countries in this table.
- The routing decision for unit FRA-FR102 states Seine-et-Marne is not among the 4 listed departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, overseas départements) in the country convention, so the default 1790 start rule applies to it rather than a bespoke span.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790, because no container exists for the earlier decade**

The routing decision's span_basis argues for 1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform that created Seine-et-Marne. That is very likely correct as administrative history, but this database's earliest French national row is FRA-1800-1871 -- there is no FRA row of any kind covering 1790-1800, and the containment gate rejects an edge that falls outside either party's span. Rather than leave a decade uncontained, or invent a container edge citing a national polity that does not exist in the table, I moved start_year to 1800 to match the earliest available container. The polity code year (1800) and the frontmatter start_year (1800) are kept equal, satisfying the gate that ties code years to frontmatter years. If a pre-1800 French national polity is added later, this entry's start_year should be revisited alongside it -- see the open question below.

### d-subunit-code-77-not-abbreviation

**Subunit code is the INSEE/ISO 3166-2 numeric code 77, not a letter abbreviation**

The dominant pattern for subnational rows is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and the exemplar subunit codes shown (ESP-AS, ESP-CA) are ISO 3166-2 letter codes. France's départements do not have ISO 3166-2 letter codes in general use; the standard identifier for Seine-et-Marne, used by INSEE, La Poste, and ISO 3166-2:FR alike, is the two-digit number 77. I used FRA-77-1800-2025 rather than inventing a non-standard letter abbreviation, since 77 is the identifier a reader would actually recognise on a map, car plate, or postcode prefix (77xxx).

### d-polygon-unassigned-not-proxy

**Polygon left unassigned rather than proxied from a neighbouring département or the national polygon**

The routing decision already worked out that no registered source in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at the resolution this entry needs -- gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated subset that excludes FRA, and cshapes-europe/cshapes-2.0 are country-level only. I did not attempt a coarser proxy (e.g. the national FRA polygon, or a hand-drawn union) because the whole point of this row is to isolate Seine-et-Marne's own reporting territory from the national one; substituting the national shape would defeat that purpose silently. polygon_source is set to the 'none' sentinel used by other unassigned rows in this table (e.g. RYU-1937-1945, BLZ-1886-1970) rather than a placeholder string, matching existing convention.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap-uncovered

**The 1790-1800 decade of Seine-et-Marne's existence is not represented by this entry or any container**

Seine-et-Marne was created in 1790 by the Revolutionary départements reform, ten years before this entry's start_year of 1800. That gap exists because no FRA national polity row in this database covers 1790-1800 -- the earliest is FRA-1800-1871. If a future entry adds an FRA-1790-1800 (or similar) national row to close that gap, this entry's start_year should be pulled back to 1790 to match, with a corresponding container edge added. Until then, any data keyed to Seine-et-Marne in 1790-1799 (unlikely for this pipeline's sources but possible in older gazetteers) has nowhere to route.

### oq-departement-boundary-stability-unverified

**Seine-et-Marne's boundary stability across 1800-2025 is asserted from general knowledge, not checked against a source**

The routing decision's span_basis claims Seine-et-Marne has never been split, merged, or annexed since 1790, based on 'general knowledge of French administrative history' rather than a cited source. Département boundaries in the Paris region in particular were reorganised in 1968 (creation of the petite couronne départements Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis, Val-de-Marne, and the reduction of the old Seine-et-Oise), and Seine-et-Marne itself lost some communes near that reorganisation. Whether any of this touched Seine-et-Marne's own perimeter (as opposed to its neighbours') has not been verified here -- when a polygon source is finally registered, its boundary vintage should be checked against a specific year, not assumed identical to the present-day shape for the whole 1800-2025 span.

### oq-data-span-vs-administrative-span

**The routed data only covers 1862-2023, not the full 1800-2025 administrative span this entry claims**

The routing decision notes the unit's full data span is 1862-2023, well inside the proposed 1790-2025 administrative existence. This entry's frontmatter nonetheless claims the full administrative span (1800-2025) rather than being trimmed to the data span, following this database's convention of representing administrative existence rather than data coverage. Whether that convention is right for a case with a 160+ year gap between administrative start and first data point is worth revisiting if other département entries in this pipeline take a different approach (e.g. trimming start_year to the first data year instead).
