# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

## Summary

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is an island nation in the Windward Islands, southern Caribbean, comprising the main island of Saint Vincent and a chain of smaller islands (the Grenadines). WHEP tracks this row from 1833 (succeeding VCT-1800-1833) to 2025. The 1833 start year likely reflects the abolition of slavery in British colonies (Slavery Abolition Act 1833). Saint Vincent became an associated state in 1969 and fully independent on 27 October 1979. Biger (1995) confirms area and the Caribbean location. Note: the CSV assigns cow_code=58, but COW ccode=57 is St. Vincent and Grenadines, and CShapes cowcode=58 is Antigua & Barbuda; the correct COW code for St. Vincent is 57. See [Contradictions](#contradictions).

## Territorial extent

## Contradictions

**cow_code mismatch.** The CSV assigns cow_code=58 to VCT-1833-2025. COW ccode=57 is St. Vincent and the Grenadines; COW ccode=58 does not appear in the v2024 statelist (St. Vincent's correct code is 57). CShapes cowcode=58 is Antigua & Barbuda. The WHEP database may therefore be using Antigua & Barbuda's polygon for Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. The correct cow_code should be 57. This mirrors the LCA-1838-2025 cow_code error (where LCA is assigned 57, which should be VCT's code).

It appears the WHEP CSV has the COW codes for LCA (Saint Lucia) and VCT (Saint Vincent) swapped: LCA has cow=57 (which is SVG/St. Vincent's real code) and VCT has cow=58 (which is not a valid COW code for either island). The correct mapping is LCA=56, VCT=57.

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-swap-with-lca

The CSV cow_codes for LCA-1838-2025 (cow=57) and VCT-1833-2025 (cow=58) appear to be incorrect. COW assigns SLU/ccode=56 to St. Lucia and SVG/ccode=57 to St. Vincent. The WHEP codes are shifted by one. A proposal should be filed to correct both rows: LCA cow_code → 56, VCT cow_code → 57. The associated polygons should also be re-verified to ensure the correct geometry is in the database.

### oq-1833-split-rationale

The split between VCT-1800-1833 and VCT-1833-2025 at 1833 is not documented (notes=NA). The date corresponds to the Slavery Abolition Act 1833. Whether this is a WHEP-qualifying territorial change should be established with a sourced rationale.
