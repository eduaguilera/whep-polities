# Dominion of Newfoundland

## Summary

This row tracks the **Dominion of Newfoundland** from its elevation to
full Dominion status within the British Empire **(1907)** to its
accession to the Canadian Confederation **(1949-03-31)**.

The row was created because FAO/IIA yearbook **footnotes** for 1953 and
1960 report Newfoundland's data as a **separate entity** (action:
`composed_union/separate_entity`) relative to the host Canada: pre-1949
agricultural or demographic figures were reported independently for
Newfoundland before it was absorbed into Canada, necessitating a
distinct WHEP entity for that period.

Newfoundland (including Labrador on the mainland) was one of the
original British North American colonies. It rejected Confederation with
Canada at the 1869 referendum, remaining a separate British colony.
Responsible government was granted in 1855; Dominion status was conferred
in 1907 alongside the other self-governing Dominions (Canada, Australia,
New Zealand, South Africa). Newfoundland suffered severe fiscal distress
during the Great Depression; in 1934 the British government suspended
representative government and replaced it with the appointed Commission of
Government (six commissioners). A 1948 referendum produced a narrow vote
in favour of Confederation (52.3 %), and Newfoundland became Canada's
tenth province on 1949-03-31.

The capital throughout this period was St. John's, Newfoundland.
Successor: [can-1948-2025](can-1948-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` (Schvitz et al. 2022), feature
  `gwcode=21`, "Newfoundland", dated `1886-01-01` to `1948-07-21`.
  Feature FID=9, area ~398,416 km².
- **Method.** `period_proxy` — the CShapes 2.0 feature predates the
  Dominion period slightly (coverage begins 1886, Dominion status 1907)
  but covers the same territorial extent without change: Newfoundland
  island plus Labrador. The territory was fixed at the time of responsible
  government (1855) and the 1927 Privy Council ruling confirmed the
  Labrador boundary with Quebec. No territorial change occurred between
  1886 and 1948. The feature therefore faithfully represents the Dominion
  period 1907–1949.
- **Coverage gap.** CShapes ends this feature on 1948-07-21; Newfoundland
  actually joined Canada on 1949-03-31. The CShapes end date reflects
  when Newfoundland disappears as a separate state in the Gleditsch-Ward
  system, but the actual accession date was 31 March 1949. The polygon
  geometry is unchanged for this final gap period.
- **Area.** ~398,416 km² (as recorded in CShapes 2.0). This encompasses
  the island of Newfoundland (~108,860 km²) plus Labrador (~294,330 km²),
  consistent with the modern Province of Newfoundland and Labrador
  (~405,212 km² including inland waters). The slight difference reflects
  projection and water-body treatment in CShapes.
- **Confidence.** High for geometry; medium for the 1886–1907 pre-Dominion
  portion (not applicable here since WHEP period starts 1907). The CShapes
  feature is the standard authoritative source for this territory.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** No WHEP row for the pre-Dominion Colony of Newfoundland
  (responsible government 1855–1907) currently exists. That period is not
  required for the FAO/IIA footnote context but could be added as a
  separate entry if production/trade data for that period are needed.
- **Successor.** [can-1948-2025](can-1948-2025.md) — upon Confederation
  on 1949-03-31, Newfoundland's territory was absorbed into Canada. The
  NFL row ends at 1949; subsequent data for Newfoundland are covered under
  CAN-1948-2025 (which explicitly documents Newfoundland's accession as
  the territorial event triggering that row).

## Sourced claims

- **1907: Dominion status.** Newfoundland was granted Dominion status at
  the 1907 Colonial Conference, joining Canada, Australia, New Zealand,
  and South Africa as self-governing Dominions of the British Empire.
  [cshapes-2.0 feature gwcode=21]
- **1927: Labrador boundary confirmed.** The Judicial Committee of the
  Privy Council ruled on the Newfoundland–Quebec boundary in Labrador,
  confirming ~294,330 km² of Labrador as part of Newfoundland.
- **1934: Commission of Government.** Responsible government was
  suspended; Newfoundland was administered by a commission appointed by
  the UK Government until 1949.
- **1948 referendum.** Two referenda held in June and July 1948; the
  second produced 52.3 % in favour of Confederation with Canada vs.
  47.7 % for responsible government.
- **1949-03-31: Confederation.** Newfoundland Act (UK, 1949) and Terms of
  Union (Canada-Newfoundland, 1948) brought Newfoundland into Canada as
  the tenth province.
  [can-1948-2025 predecessor note]
- **CShapes feature.** CShapes 2.0 assigns gwcode=21 to Newfoundland,
  coverage 1886-01-01 to 1948-07-21, area=398,415.72 km², capital
  St. John's.
  [cshapes-2.0 feature gwcode=21, FID=9]

## Contradictions

- **CShapes end date (1948-07-21) vs. actual accession (1949-03-31).**
  CShapes ends the Newfoundland feature more than eight months before the
  actual Confederation date. This is a known convention in the
  Gleditsch-Ward state system, where the end date represents the last day
  of independence within the coding rules, not necessarily the exact
  political date. WHEP end_year is set to 1949 to match the true
  accession.

## Decisions

- **polity_code NFL-1907-1949.** `NFL` is the standard three-letter
  abbreviation for Newfoundland (used in ISO 3166-2:CA and broadly in
  bibliographic contexts). The Dominion period 1907–1949 defines the
  WHEP span; the pre-1907 colonial period is excluded from this row.
- **iso3 = CAN.** No separate ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code exists for
  historical Newfoundland; it is now part of Canada. CAN is assigned as
  the sovereign successor state.
- **cow = 21.** CShapes 2.0 uses gwcode=21 for Newfoundland, consistent
  with the Correlates of War / Gleditsch-Ward coding.
- **polygon_feature_id = "21".** The CShapes gwcode (not the FID) is used
  as the feature identifier, following the convention in other WHEP pages
  (e.g., Hawaii uses cowcode=4, Canada uses cowcode=20). The build script
  matches on gwcode.
- **polygon_feature_year = 1920.** A year well within both the CShapes
  coverage (1886–1948) and the Dominion period (1907–1949), ensuring the
  temporal match succeeds in build_database.py.
- **start_year = 1907.** Dominion status conferred at the 1907 Colonial
  Conference. The CShapes feature begins 1886 (responsible-government
  colony), but the WHEP entity is the Dominion, so start_year follows the
  political event.
- **end_year = 1949.** Confederation date 1949-03-31, not the CShapes
  coding end of 1948-07-21.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-dominion-colony

The Colony of Newfoundland (responsible government 1855–1907) pre-dates
this row. CShapes 2.0 covers it under the same gwcode=21 feature (from
1886). If WHEP data for the colonial period are needed, a separate
NFL-1855-1907 (or NFL-1886-1907) row should be created referencing the
same CShapes feature.

### oq-commission-of-government-1934-1949

From 1934 to 1949, Newfoundland was governed not as a self-governing
Dominion but under a Commission of Government (appointed by London).
Some sources treat this as a distinct constitutional status. The current
row spans 1907–1949 as a single polity; if the 1934–1949 commission
period warrants a separate entity, a row split at 1934 should be
considered.

### oq-labrador-boundary

The 1927 Privy Council ruling confirmed Labrador as part of Newfoundland,
but Quebec contested this boundary until 2001. The CShapes polygon
reflects the post-1927 settled boundary. Whether pre-1927 data should
use a different polygon (with uncertain Labrador extent) is an open
question for precise territorial matching.

## iso3 corrected to `NFL` (2026-07-24)

Carried `iso3: CAN`. Newfoundland was a **separate self-governing Dominion until
31 March 1949**, not part of Canada — that is the entire reason this row exists
and the reason [can-1886-1948](can-1886-1948.md) explicitly excludes it.

Sharing Canada's ISO code placed this row in Canada's matching family, where —
both being `national` and overlapping 1907-1948 — the winner was decided by
family ordering rather than by anything meaningful. Canada-labelled data could
have landed on Newfoundland (a 25x smaller territory) at any time. Flagged by
`scripts/audit_family_shadowing.py`.

`NFL` is not an ISO 3166 code; it is used here as a WHEP-local identifier for an
entity that predates the modern code list, which is what the `iso3` field is for
in such cases. No data is currently matched to this row.
