# Bogd Khanate of Mongolia

## Summary

The Bogd Khanate of Mongolia (1911-1921) covers the period from the
declaration of independence from Qing China in December 1911 to the
1921 Soviet-backed revolution. The Bogd Khan (Jebtsundamba Khutuktu VIII)
was enthroned as theocratic ruler. De facto independent but with
contested sovereignty -- China attempted to reassert control in 1919-1920.
CShapes does not cover this period (its Mongolia entry starts 1921-03-13),
and the CSV uses the earliest CShapes polygon back-projected to 1911.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (earliest Mongolia polygon),
  back-projected from 1921 start.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **Area.** ~184.4 sq deg (~1,566,500 km2 per Biger).
- **Pre-CShapes.** The 1911-1921 polygon is an approximation;
  the Bogd Khanate's actual territorial control was contested,
  particularly during the Chinese occupation of 1919-1920.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** Qing China (no direct CSV row for Outer Mongolia
  under Qing). At 1911 this territory was part of the Qing Empire.
- **Successor:** [MNG-1921-2025](mng-1921-2025.md) -- Soviet-backed
  revolution of March 1921. Mongolian People's Republic declared 1924.

## Open questions

### oq-bogd-khanate-sovereignty

The Bogd Khanate's sovereignty was contested. China attempted to reassert
control multiple times, and the 1915 Treaty of Kyakhta established
Mongolia as autonomous under Chinese suzerainty. A future ingest should
clarify whether WHEP should track this as fully independent or as an
autonomous entity under Chinese suzerainty.

### oq-1919-chinese-occupation

Chinese forces occupied Urga in 1919-1920, effectively ending Bogd
Khanate autonomy until the Soviet-backed liberation in 1921. This
period may warrant separate treatment or at minimum documentation.
