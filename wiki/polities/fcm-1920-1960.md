# French Cameroon (1920-1960)

## Summary

French Cameroon (Cameroun français) was a League of Nations mandate (1920-1946) and then a United Nations trust territory (1946-1960) administered by France. It comprised approximately 80% of the former German colony of Kamerun, the eastern and larger portion after the post-WWI partition. Independent as the Republic of Cameroun on 1 January 1960.

**1920–1922 gap:** CShapes FID 354 (identical boundary, status=occupied, 1919-1922) covers the period before formal League mandate. The boundary reflects the same partition line as FID 355.

**Boundary notes (Milner-Simon Agreement, 1922):** The eastern/larger share of former German Kamerun was assigned to France under the Milner-Simon Agreement (July 1922). A minor adjustment in 1931 transferred a small area of the Wouri enclave to French administration; this is reflected in the CShapes boundary. The [BCA-1919-1961](bca-1919-1961.md) (British Cameroon) covered the western strip (~20% of former Kamerun, ~87,000 km²), consistent with the ~423,069 km² vs ~510,000 km² total.

**Is the source's "Cameroon" French-only or French+British?** Independently
corroborated as **French Cameroun alone** (2026-07-24, assertion verification of
`cameroon|mitchell|1920-1959`, 308 rows): the series is built on cacao and
coffee, and colonial economic history (Britannica; cocoa-history literature)
establishes that cacao had no real economic weight in the British Cameroons
mandate zone while being central to French Cameroun's economy. A combined
French+British figure would therefore not look like this series. The magnitudes
also show no discontinuity at the 1920-1922 mandate transition or at 1960
independence. This was previously assumed from the iso/label alone; it now rests
on external evidence rather than on this page's own assertion.

## Key dates

- 1916: Anglo-French forces complete conquest of German Kamerun
- 1920: League of Nations mandate assigned to France (4/5 of territory)
- 1922-07-20: Milner-Simon Agreement formally partitions Kamerun; CShapes FID 355 begins
- 1931: Minor Wouri enclave adjustment documented in CShapes boundary
- 1946: Becomes UN trust territory
- 1955-1958: UPC (Union des Populations du Cameroun) insurrection
- 1960-01-01: Independence as Republic of Cameroun
