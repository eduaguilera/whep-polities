# French Cameroon (1920-1960)

## Summary

French Cameroon (Cameroun français) was a League of Nations mandate (1920-1946) and then a United Nations trust territory (1946-1960) administered by France. It comprised approximately 80% of the former German colony of Kamerun, the eastern and larger portion after the post-WWI partition. Independent as the Republic of Cameroun on 1 January 1960.
