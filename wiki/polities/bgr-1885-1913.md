# Bulgaria (1885-1913)

## Era split (issue 686, 2026-10-07)

**This row was `BGR-1878-1913` until 2026-10-07.** It now starts in 1885, the year Bulgaria united with
Eastern Rumelia (proclaimed 6 September 1885, recognised in the Tophane Agreement of 24 March 1886)
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §bulgaria](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#bulgaria). The years 1878-1884 moved to [bgr-1878-1885](bgr-1878-1885.md).

**Polygon status:** assigned, unchanged. CShapes-Europe Id 355, the 1886-1912 step (93,278 km²,
Mollweide), which includes Eastern Rumelia. The previous principality step (1879-1885) measures
57,594 km², so the unification added about 35,700 km². That is the figure this page's open question
`oq-1885-eastern-rumelia` asked for, and the question is resolved by the split.

The page text below still says the polygon is CShapes 2.0's; the frontmatter has bound
CShapes-Europe since before this split, and that binding is what the database uses.

## Summary

This row tracks Bulgaria for **28 years**, from the union with Eastern Rumelia
to the Second Balkan War
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Bulgaria was an autonomous principality under Ottoman suzerainty from
1878 (Congress of Berlin) until full independence on 5 October 1908 --
the same date as the OTT-1886-1908 to OTT-1908-1912 split. Eastern
Rumelia unified with Bulgaria in 1885. Bulgaria became an independent
kingdom in 1908. The 1913 end date aligns with the Second Balkan War
(Treaty of Bucharest, 10 August 1913), when Bulgaria lost territory
to Serbia, Greece, and Romania.

Cross-reference: the Bosnia double-count resolution on
[ott-1886-1908](ott-1886-1908.md) proved that Bulgaria's 10.28 sq deg
independence polygon (`cowcode=355`) matches the Ottoman area drop at
1908-10-05 exactly. See
[log autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count](../log.md#autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count).

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but Bulgaria was Ottoman territory.
The Congress of Berlin (1878) created an autonomous Bulgarian
principality.
Successor: [bgr-1913-1918](bgr-1913-1918.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1878: Congress of Berlin.** "Bulgaria became an autonomous
  principality under Ottoman suzerainty"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878). Romania, Serbia, and
  Montenegro were formally recognized as independent at the same
  congress. Bulgaria was given a lesser status (autonomous, not
  independent). This is the start-of-row event.
- **1885: Eastern Rumelia unification.** The autonomous Ottoman
  province of Eastern Rumelia unified with the Principality of
  Bulgaria. This was a significant territorial gain within the row
  (~35,000 km²) with no CSV split.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878)
- **1908-10-05: Declaration of independence.** Bulgaria declared full
  independence from the Ottoman Empire on 5 October 1908 -- the same
  date as Austria-Hungary's formal annexation of Bosnia and Crete's
  union with Greece
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1908). CShapes confirms:
  `cowcode=355` first appears at 1908-10-05 with area 10.28 sq deg,
  matching the Ottoman polygon's area drop at the same date
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). See
  [log autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count](../log.md#autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count).
- **1912-1913: Balkan Wars.** The First Balkan War (October 1912 -
  May 1913) saw Bulgaria, Greece, Serbia, and Montenegro defeat the
  Ottoman Empire. The Second Balkan War (June-August 1913) saw
  Bulgaria defeated by its former allies. Treaty of Bucharest
  (10 August 1913): Bulgaria lost Southern Dobruja to Romania, parts
  of Macedonia to Serbia and Greece.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 355
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

### oq-1885-eastern-rumelia

**Resolved 2026-10-07 (issue 686):** the row was split at 1885; see the era-split section above.

Eastern Rumelia unified with Bulgaria in 1885 -- a significant
territorial gain (~35,000 km²) within the row with no CSV split.

split point (specific date, large area gain). The unification was
initially opposed by the Great Powers but eventually accepted.
