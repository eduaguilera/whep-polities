# Syrian Arab Republic

## Summary

Syria as tracked by WHEP from 1967, reflecting the territorial change caused by Israel's occupation of the Golan Heights during the Six-Day War. Syria gained independence from France in 1946. Its borders were shaped by the post-WWI Sykes-Picot Agreement and the Conference of San Remo (1920), which placed Syria under French mandate.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Syria is bounded by Turkey, Iraq (376 mi; 605 km), Jordan (233 mi; 375 km), Israel (47 mi; 76 km), and Lebanon.

## Predecessors and successors
