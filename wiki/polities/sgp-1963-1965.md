# Singapore (State of Singapore in Malaysia)

## Summary

This row covers Singapore as a constituent state of the Federation of
Malaysia, from the September 1963 merger to the August 1965 separation.
It bridges the existing chain SGP-1946-1963 (Crown Colony) to
SGP-1965-2025 (independent republic): without it, FAOSTAT Singapore data
for 1964 had no covering period.

Singapore's statistics remained separately reported during the Malaysia
years, so this is a genuine sub-national reporting polity — its data must
NOT be folded up into MYS-1963-1965, per the sub-territory rule.

## Territorial extent

Singapore Island and islets, identical to predecessor and successor. The
CShapes 2.0 colony feature (cowcode 830, vintage 1962) measures 547 km2;
the island's extent did not change during the federation years.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: SGP-1946-1963 (Singapore, Crown Colony).
- Successor: SGP-1965-2025 (Republic of Singapore).

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **200 "Singapore"** reports 1961-2024 continuously
with no discontinuity across 1963-1965, i.e. always the island alone.
1961-1963 routes to SGP-1946-1963, 1964 here, 1965 onward to
SGP-1965-2025 (`pipelines/faostat-era-matching`, iso3 SGP; shared
boundary years route to the successor).

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 cowcode 830 at vintage 1962 (the colony
  feature; CShapes has no separate 1963-1965 Singapore feature because it
  tracks it inside Malaysia). One-year vintage drift on an unchanged
  island: extent is exact in practice.
