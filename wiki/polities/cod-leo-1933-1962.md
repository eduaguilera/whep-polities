# Leopoldville Province

## Summary

The Leopoldville province of the Belgian Congo, named for its capital from the 1933 reorganisation into six provinces, and one of the six provinces of the Loi Fondamentale at independence (30 June 1960), until the August 1962 split into provincettes. It held the capital and the districts of Bas-Congo, Kwango, Kwilu and Lac Leopold II. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §leopoldville](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#leopoldville)
