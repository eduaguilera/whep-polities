### mitchell's Algeria is the civil departments here too, not the full colony

Recorded 2026-08-18. **A `suspect_wiki_pages` finding described this row's data as
covering "the full colony including post-Sahara-annexation departments". Measured,
it does not** — and the same denominator problem already documented on
`dza-1902-1919` carries through this whole span.

Mitchell's `algeria` area series across the 1919 polity boundary:

| series | 1902-1918 | 1920-1960 |
|---|---|---|
| wheat (ha) | 1,387,000 (n=17) | 1,624,000 (n=40) |
| barley (ha) | 1,344,000 (n=17) | 1,239,000 (n=40) |

**No step.** Combined with the finding on `dza-1902-1919` — where the series also
shows no step at the **1902** annexation of the Southern Territories — Mitchell's
Algeria runs on **one constant reporting basis across 1867-1960**, and the 1902
test identifies that basis as the three civil departments (Alger, Oran,
Constantine), which IIA states as **575,511 km²**.

So the reporting territory did not widen when the colony did, and it did not widen
at this row's start either. Wheat area of 1.6M ha is northern-Algerian cropland;
the Sahara contributed no measurable cultivation.

**The consequence is a denominator error on 1,535 rows.** Any per-km² intensity
computed from this row's polygon, which includes the Sahara, understates by
roughly the same **4.2x** already recorded for the predecessor row. There is no
polity for the civil departments to route the data to — that is case 2 of issue
#400, which this finding extends from 397 rows to **1,932**.
