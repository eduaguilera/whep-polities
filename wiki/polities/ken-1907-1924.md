# Kenya (1907-1924)

## Summary

British East Africa Protectorate / Kenya Colony in its 1907-1924 configuration. "Kenya was declared a British protectorate in 1895" [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya). CShapes records two boundary changes within this period: at 1907-01-01 the area remained 62.84 sq deg, then at 1907-12-06 it dropped to 51.69 sq deg following the transfer of the Eastern Province from the Uganda Protectorate to the East Africa Protectorate and an associated boundary adjustment. The area remained at 51.69 sq deg until 1924-06-30, when a further boundary change reduced it to 44.02 sq deg. The 1907-1924 period thus covers the protectorate at its post-transfer extent. The 1920 transition from protectorate to colony is within this row (CShapes codes status=protectorate to 1920-07-22, then colony from 1920-07-23).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 51.69 sq deg for most of this period (from 1907-12-06 to 1924-06-30), status=protectorate then colony, owner=Britain (COW 200). The area reflects the transfer of the former Eastern Province of Uganda to Kenya's administration. Bounded by Sudan (144 miles; 232 km), Ethiopia, Somalia, the Indian Ocean, Tanzania, and Uganda [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya).

## Decisions

### decision-polygon-feature-year-1908

**`polygon_feature_year` moved `1907` -> `1908` on 2026-08-13 (issue 100).** CShapes
breaks gwcode 501 on **1907-12-06** — the Eastern Province transfer this page already
documents — so the year 1907 contained TWO steps and both started in 1907:

| step | dates | area |
|---|---|---|
| pre-transfer | 1907-01-01 – 1907-12-05 | **772,550 km²** (62.84 sq deg) |
| post-transfer | 1907-12-06 – 1914-04-20 | **635,868 km²** (51.69 sq deg) |

Two candidates starting in the queried year is exactly the tie `find_feature` cannot
break, so the binding fell through to shapefile row order and took the **pre-transfer**
772,550 km² polygon. That contradicts this page's own Summary and Territorial extent,
both of which say the row "covers the protectorate at its **post-transfer** extent" and
that CShapes "records area 51.69 sq deg for most of this period (from 1907-12-06 to
1924-06-30)". So the row was carrying a polygon **136,682 km² (21%) too large** for
16 of its 17 years, not merely a fragile one.

`1908` falls inside the post-transfer step and inside no other, which pins the binding by
the data instead of by row order. The row spans 1907-1923, so the year stays inside the
row's own period and raises no out-of-span vintage question.

## Open questions

### oq-ken-1907-eastern-province-transfer

The 1907-12-06 boundary change in CShapes should be precisely attributed. It corresponds to the transfer of the Eastern Province from the Uganda Protectorate to the East Africa Protectorate, but the specific Order in Council should be identified.

### oq-ken-1924-jubaland-transfer

The 1924-07-01 boundary change corresponds to the transfer of Jubaland (Trans-Juba) to Italy under the 1924 Anglo-Italian Agreement. This should be confirmed with a primary source.
