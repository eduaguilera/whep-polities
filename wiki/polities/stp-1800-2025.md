# Sao Tome and Principe

## Summary

São Tomé and Príncipe is a two-island republic in the Gulf of Guinea, approximately 250 km off the coast of Gabon. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. Under Portuguese colonial administration throughout the nineteenth and most of the twentieth century, it became independent on 12 July 1975. CShapes 2.0 is the polygon source (cowcode=403 for Equatorial Guinea, but see [Contradictions](#contradictions) for the cow_code mismatch). The CSV assigns cow_code=411; however, COW ccode=411 corresponds to Equatorial Guinea, not São Tomé and Príncipe (COW ccode=403 for STP). Biger (1995) confirms the island state's area and independence date.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Area: 372 sq. miles [964 sq. km.]. São Tomé island (~859 km²) and Príncipe island (~136 km²) plus several small islets. No land boundaries; entirely maritime. The islands lie in the Gulf of Guinea approximately 250 km from Gabon. [biger-1995 §sao-tome-and-principe](../sources/biger-1995.md#sao-tome-and-principe). CShapes records three time-steps for cowcode=411 (which CShapes labels as Equatorial Guinea), all showing an identical area of 2.19 sq deg — indicating no territorial change. The CShapes polygon used by WHEP for STP-1800-2025 needs verification: the csv states polygon_source=CShapes 2.0 but the CShapes cowcode mapping may be incorrect.

## Predecessors and successors

São Tomé and Príncipe was settled by the Portuguese in the 1480s and remained under Portuguese control throughout the 1800–1975 period. It became independent without territorial change.

- **Predecessor:** None listed in CSV. Portuguese colonial administration throughout.
- **Successor:** None; STP-1800-2025 is the terminal row.

## Contradictions

**cow_code mismatch.** The CSV assigns cow_code=411 to STP-1800-2025. COW ccode=411 is Equatorial Guinea. São Tomé and Príncipe's COW code is 403 (stateabb=STP). CShapes cowcode=411 is also Equatorial Guinea (Bight of Biafra territory). The WHEP polygon for STP may actually be the Equatorial Guinea polygon. The correct cow_code for STP-1800-2025 should be 403. This requires verification that the polygon in the database is actually the São Tomé polygon and not the Equatorial Guinea polygon.

## Polygon RESOLVED (2026-07-24)

Previously `unassigned`: São Tomé and Príncipe is absent from CShapes 2.0
entirely, and the fetched GADM adm0 file did not include it. `STP` has now been
added to `scripts/sources/gadm-4.1/fetch.sh`, so this row is bound to GADM 4.1
adm0 `STP` (**1,002 km²**) as a `proxy`.

Modern borders are appropriate here — the territory is two islands whose
coastline has not changed — but the status stays `proxy` rather than `assigned`
because the polygon is a present-day feature standing in for the full
1800-2025 span, not a period-accurate one.

## What the old binding cost downstream, and what now stops it returning (2026-08-05)

The consequence of the pre-2026-07-24 binding was measured in the consumer, and it
is larger than a wrong polygon on one page. While this row carried CShapes feature
411, it and both `GNQ` rows held **one** polygon, so the ground inside it was claimed
twice. Intersecting the 205 live real polities of WHEP's embedded copy for 2015
against the 0.5 degree grid, **451 of 67,691 cells claim more territory than they
contain**, 12.72 Mha in excess, worst ratio **2.0000×** — cell (10.25, 1.75), inside
mainland Rio Muni, handed in full to Equatorial Guinea and to São Tomé and Príncipe
alike. Eleven other cross-family pairs did the same thing in that copy. It stayed
invisible because the consumer's crosswalk renormalises each cell's polity fractions
to sum to 1, which absorbs the overlap into a plausible-looking split.

None of this repository's other checks could see it: the area check had no recorded
`polygon_area_km2` to compare against, the containment check reports a polygon holding
three or more contemporaneous polities and two identical polygons hold one each, and
the period-fit and determinism checks both confirm that `411@1900` resolves cleanly —
it was simply the wrong country. `scripts/validate_shared_polygons.py` now asks the
question that settles it, and pins this pair by name, so the binding cannot return
without a named failure. Filed as eduaguilera/whep#514.

**The `cow_code` half of the same confusion is still open.** CShapes labels cowcode
411 as Equatorial Guinea, and this page took 411 for its polygon *and* for its
`cow_code`. The polygon is fixed; the `cow_code` still reads 411, which
`scripts/validate_cow_codes.py` carries as a baselined collision with
`GNQ-1886-1968`. The COW state system entry cited above gives `ccode=403` for
`stateabb=STP`, so the value is answerable from a source this repository has already
ingested — see [oq-cow-code-and-polygon-verification](#oq-cow-code-and-polygon-verification).
It is deliberately not changed here, because `cow_code` is an identity field a
consumer resolves against and correcting it re-fingerprints the published contract,
which is a separate change from adding a gate.
