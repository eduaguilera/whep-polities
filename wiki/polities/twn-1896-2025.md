# Taiwan

## Summary

This row tracks Taiwan for **130 years**, a single continuous row
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Japanese colony (1895-1945) following Treaty of Shimonoseki. After WWII,
administered by Republic of China. ROC government retreated to Taiwan
(1949) after losing Chinese Civil War. Unique political status -- not
universally recognized as sovereign. Area ~36,000 km².

## Territorial extent

## Sourced claims

- **1895: Treaty of Shimonoseki.** Qing China ceded Taiwan to Japan.
  Row starts 1896.
  [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **1945: Japanese surrender.** Taiwan transferred to ROC administration.
  [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **1949: ROC retreat.** Kuomintang government relocated to Taiwan after
  Communist victory on the mainland.
  [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan)
- **Biger 1995: island, no land boundaries.** Taiwan: 13,900 sq mi
  [35,987 sq km]; population 21,000,000; "has no land boundaries"
  [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan) (p.487).
