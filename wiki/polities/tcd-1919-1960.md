# Chad (1919-1960)


> **`start_year` moved 1920 -> 1919 on 2026-08-13 (issue 77): the year 1919 belonged to no
> Chadian polity.**
>
> `end_year` is **exclusive**, so [tcd-1912-1919](tcd-1912-1919.md) covers 1912-1918 while this row
> began in 1920. **1919 fell between them**, and `validate_period_gaps` carried the pair
> `("TCD-1912-1919", "TCD-1920-1960")` in its baseline as an accepted hole pending a historical
> answer.
>
> **The AEF siblings settled it, and they are unanimous.** The same post-Versailles boundary
> restoration of 28 June 1919 reorganised four French Equatorial African territories at once, and
> in CShapes 2.0 each of the other three has a single step running 1919-06-28 to independence,
> transcribed in WHEP as a row that STARTS IN 1919:
>
> | family | CShapes step | WHEP row |
> |---|---|---|
> | Gabon | 1919-06-28 -> 1960-08-16 | `GAB-1919-1960` |
> | Central African Republic | 1919-06-28 -> 1960-08-12 | `CAF-1919-1960` |
> | Congo | 1919-06-28 -> 1960-08-14 | `COG-1919-1960` |
> | **Chad** | 1919-06-28 -> **1920-03-16**, then 1920-03-17 -> 1960-08-10 | ~~`TCD-1920-1960`~~ |
>
> Chad is the only one of the four whose CShapes record is **split again** at 1920-03-17, the
> separation of the Territoire du Tchad from Oubangui-Chari. WHEP transcribed the second half of
> that split and **dropped the first**, which is the entire reason Chad alone has a 1919 hole. So
> this was not a historical question about 1919 at all — it was one missing CShapes step.
>
> **Nothing territorial is lost by absorbing it.** Measured in ESRI:54034, the dropped step
> (1919-06-28 -> 1920-03-16) and the step this row already carried (1920-03-17 -> 1960-08-10) are
> both **1,271,888 km²** — identical to the digit. The restoration, not the administrative
> separation, is where the geometry changes: [tcd-1912-1919](tcd-1912-1919.md) carries the reduced
> 1,220,971 km² of the 1911 Neukamerun cession.
>
> **That is also why 1919 goes to THIS row rather than to the earlier one.** Extending
> `TCD-1912-1919` to cover 1919 would have published the 1911 cession dip for a year in which it
> had already been reversed, and that page states explicitly that it is "the only row that should
> carry" the dip. `polygon_feature_year` is therefore `1919`, binding the restored step, matching
> the three siblings.
>
> `polygon_area_km2` stays **null**, and the first draft of this change got that wrong. Declaring
> 1,271,888 km² here would have been an area measured from this row's own polygon, and
> `validate_polygons` check A2 caps how many of those the repo tolerates — the declaration pushed it
> from 104 to 105 and failed the gate, correctly: "a declared area within 0.1% of its own polygon is
> not evidence about the territory". An area belongs here only when a yearbook or gazetteer states
> one.
>
> **1919 is latent, unlike the SEN and LAO gaps fixed in the same pass**: the consolidated layer B
> holds no Chad observation for 1919 (`mitchell` has Chad only from 1953). The issue's claim that
> all four gaps were latent was right here and wrong for the other two.

## Summary

Chad (1919-1960) covers the French colonial territory of Chad from 28 June 1919 — the post-Versailles boundary restoration, when CShapes returns Chad to its full extent — to 10 August 1960, the eve of independence. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=FRA (COW 220). Two CShapes steps fall inside the span, 1919-06-28 to 1920-03-16 and 1920-03-17 to 1960-08-10, split by the administrative reorganization of French Equatorial Africa: "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated." (p.146, cited in biger-1995). Both steps measure 1,271,888 km², so the row is territorially uniform and the 1920 separation is administrative only; see the note above for why the row now starts in 1919 rather than 1920.

## Territorial extent

Landlocked central African territory bounded by Libya (north), Sudan (east), Central African Republic (south), Cameroon (south-west), Nigeria (west), and Niger (north-west). CShapes 2.0 area ~107.30 (from 1919-06-28 to 1960-08-10, unchanged across the 1920-03-17 split: 1,271,888 km² measured in ESRI:54034 on both steps). The preceding TCD-1912-1919 period had area ~103.11, indicating boundary additions in mid-1919 (specifically after the Treaty of Versailles/Cameroon partition). Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Sourced claims

- "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated." (p.146) This administrative separation falls INSIDE this row, at the 1920-03-17 CShapes step boundary; it does not change the territory (both steps measure 1,271,888 km²) and it is no longer the event that starts the row. [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- "Chad is one of the poorest and least developed countries in Africa." (p.148) [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were together." (pp.146–147) This was the administrative unit preceding the 1920 separation. [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=483, status=colony, owner=220 (France) across two steps inside this row, 1919-06-28 to 1920-03-16 and 1920-03-17 to 1960-08-10, both area ~107.30. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Contradictions

The Biger source states separation occurred "In 1920" (p.146) without a specific date. CShapes gives 1920-03-17 as the precise date of the step boundary. This is not a contradiction but a precision difference, and since 2026-08-13 it is no longer the row boundary either: the row starts at the 1919-06-28 territorial restoration, and the 1920 separation sits inside it as an administrative change with no area effect.

## Decisions

- **tcd-1919-start-not-1920** (2026-08-13, issue 77) — Absorb the dropped CShapes step 1919-06-28 -> 1920-03-16 into this row rather than into `TCD-1912-1919`, and rename `TCD-1920-1960` -> `TCD-1919-1960`. Evidence: (a) all three AEF siblings — `GAB-1919-1960`, `CAF-1919-1960`, `COG-1919-1960` — already start in 1919 on the same 28 June 1919 event; (b) the dropped step and the step already carried measure the same 1,271,888 km², so absorbing it costs no territorial precision; (c) the reduced 1,220,971 km² of the 1911 Neukamerun cession belongs to `TCD-1912-1919` alone, so extending that row into 1919 would have published a cession already reversed.

## Open questions

### oq-tcd-1919-area-increase

CShapes records an area increase from ~103.11 (TCD-1912-1919) to ~107.30 at 1919-06-28. The additional territory likely came from the partition of German Kamerun following WWI, where parts of the former German colony were assigned to France and incorporated into French Equatorial Africa. The specific treaty provision and transferred districts should be identified. The same increase, on the same date, applies to Gabon, CAR and Congo, so the answer is one event covering four families.
