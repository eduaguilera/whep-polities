# La Araucanía (region of Chile)

## Summary

La Araucanía is one of Chile's present-day first-order administrative regions, located in south-central Chile roughly 550-700 km south of Santiago, bordering the Biobío region to the north, Los Ríos region to the south, Argentina to the east, and the Pacific to the west. It corresponds historically to the core of the Araucanía (Mapuche territory) incorporated into the Chilean state through the 19th-century "Pacification of Araucanía" campaigns, and to the area formerly organized under the provinces of Cautín and Malleco before Chile's 1974 regionalization reform (DL 575) replaced the province system with the current region/province/commune hierarchy, effective 1976. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chile has held continuous sovereignty over the area throughout the covered period — the entry exists to carry a *regional reporting geography* used by statistical sources, not a claim of separate statehood. The 1976 start date marks when La Araucanía began existing as an operationally distinct administrative unit with its own boundary; data describing the same physical territory before 1976 is attributed instead to the national polity CHL-1902-2025, since pre-1976 sources are reconstructing/allocating national totals onto this territory rather than reporting on a then-existing bounded unit.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures reporting data on the territory now known as La Araucanía, a current Chilean administrative region, covering roughly 1900-2023 per the source material examined during routing, though the region only became an operationally distinct administrative unit in 1976. The pre-1976 portion of that data run was previously implicitly routed as if it applied to the modern regional boundary; that was wrong because no bounded "La Araucanía" administrative unit existed before 1976 to be measured — data from that period is source material reconstructing/allocating national Chilean totals down to the territory the region would later occupy, which is why it is back-cast onto the national polity CHL-1902-2025 rather than folded into this entry's own span. What confirms the post-1976 unit is distinct: DL 575 (1974) and its 1976 implementation created La Araucanía as one of Chile's named first-order regions with its own governor, budget, and statistical reporting apparatus (INE compiles region-level statistics for it), the same convention already applied to the sibling entry CHL-LI and consistent with the country-wide rule that Chilean subnational polities begin at the 1976 regionalization unless a specific unit's boundary or existence differs from that default."

No area figure has been measured from an attached geometry for this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned; the 31,842 km² figure is the region's own officially stated area, not a geometry-derived measurement.

## Decisions

### d-region-start-1976

**Region span begins 1976, not the full 1900-2023 data run**

The routing decision found data on this territory back to 1900, but La Araucanía only became an operational administrative region in 1976 under DL 575 (the 1974 regionalization reform). Following the country convention set for CHL region-level polities (matching the CHL-LI sibling pattern), pre-1976 years are back_cast onto the national polity CHL-1902-2025 rather than opening this entry earlier, because the source is allocating/reconstructing national totals down to this territory rather than reporting on a then-existing distinct administrative unit. Opening the span at 1900 would misrepresent the region as having existed as a bounded administrative entity for 76 years before it did.

### d-code-pattern

**Code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT pattern**

CHL-AR-1976-2025 follows the majority pattern (<ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>) used by 57 of 66 subnational rows, e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025, DZA-CVD-1902-1919. La Araucanía is an ordinary administrative region without the kind of standalone historical identity (like Alaska Territory or Hyderabad) that would justify a bespoke code, so the bespoke-code exception does not apply here.

### d-polygon-unassigned

**Polygon left unassigned pending a full GADM 4.1 fetch**

The routing decision (polygon_route: registered_source_unfetched) identified gadm-4.1-adm1 as the correct registered source for Chilean adm1 regions, but the copy present in this repository's data/geodata/gadm-4.1/ directory is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 features for CHL). Per the harness rule, registered_source_unfetched means the slug itself is the declared polygon_source and polygon_status is set to unassigned, rather than writing new_source_needed (which validate_declared_sources rejects). No polygon_feature_id can be set until the full/global GADM 4.1 Chile ADM1 layer is fetched into the local geodata directory.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-provincial-identity

**Did a distinct La Araucanía-area province exist before 1976 under Chile's earlier province system?**

Chile's pre-1974 administrative system organized the country into provinces (not the current 16-region scheme), and the area now called La Araucanía roughly overlapped the historical Cautín and Malleco provinces. If those provinces were themselves distinctly tabulated in early-20th-century statistical sources, a longer back-cast attribution to this specific territory (rather than lumping all pre-1976 data into the national CHL-1902-2025 polity) might be justified, or an earlier polity span might be more accurate than treating 1900-1975 purely as national back-cast. This has not been checked against Chilean historical administrative-boundary sources (e.g. INE historical province gazetteers) in this session.

### oq-gadm-fetch-and-vintage

**GADM 4.1 Chile ADM1 layer needs to be fetched, and its vintage against 1976 boundaries confirmed**

The routing decision flags that GADM 4.1 reflects present-day (post-2018) Chilean region boundaries, and asserts vintage risk is 'low' because no known border changes have occurred to La Araucanía since 1976 — but this claim was not verified against an authoritative source in this session (e.g. a check of INE or BCN records of any boundary adjustments, especially any commune reassignments between La Araucanía and neighboring regions Biobío or Los Ríos). Once the full GADM 4.1 dataset (or a fresh shapefile fetch) is obtained and added to data/geodata/gadm-4.1/, this should be confirmed before polygon_status moves from unassigned to assigned.

### oq-sibling-disposition-inconsistency

**This verdict's back_cast treatment departs from the 'unrostable' disposition given to sibling units CHL-AI, CHL-AN**

The routing_concerns explicitly note that sibling Chilean regions (CHL-AI, CHL-AN, and others) were designated 'unroutable' for their pre-1976 years by the same harness pass, while this entry (La Araucanía) instead back-casts pre-1976 data onto the national polity. Whoever maintains the country convention for CHL should reconcile whether back_cast or unroutable is the correct default for pre-regionalization region-level data across all Chilean subnational entries, since applying different rules to siblings created in the same pass produces inconsistent database rows for structurally identical situations.
