## Sourced claims

- New Zealand: area 103,562 sq. miles (268,122 sq. km.), population
  3,400,000 (1994), capital Wellington, no land boundaries.
  [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand) (p.404)
- New Zealand became part of the British Empire in the 1840s; the
  1840 Treaty of Waitangi between the British and most major Maori
  chiefs acknowledged Maori land rights.
  [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand) (p.404)
- New Zealand joined the South Pacific Commission as a founder
  member in 1947 and administered the UN Trust Territory of Western
  Samoa until its independence in 1962.
  [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand) (p.404)
