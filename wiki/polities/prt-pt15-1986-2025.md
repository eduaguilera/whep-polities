# Algarve (NUTS II region of Portugal)

## Summary

This entry captures Algarve, Portugal's southernmost NUTS II statistical region (NUTS code PT15), covering the historic Algarve province in the far south of mainland Portugal — the area corresponding closely to the modern Faro district, bordered by Spain's Andalusia to the east, the Atlantic to the south, and the Alentejo region to the north. The entry is `type: subnational` because Algarve has never been a sovereign or quasi-sovereign state: it is a statistical/administrative subdivision of Portugal used for EU regional-policy and national-accounts reporting, and it must not compete with the national PRT chain for routing purposes. The routing decision that motivates this page found a data series (source unit PRT-PT15) reporting Algarve-specific figures for which no existing polity row provided a correct geographic match, and 0 name or boundary candidates existed for a distinct Algarve row. Per the country's NUTS-era convention for Portugal, entries created for NUTS regions default to a 1986 start (the year Portugal's NUTS statistical regions were first defined following 1986 EU accession) unless a documented departure applies; no departure was found for Algarve specifically (unlike AML or Aveiro, which do have departures), so this entry starts in 1986 and runs to the present (2025, exclusive), i.e. it remains current.

**Why this entry exists.** **Input data:** the routing decision covers source unit PRT-PT15 (admin_name "PT15"), a Portuguese NUTS II region, with a continuous reported series from 1870 to the present under a 100%-observed method; the routing proposal being implemented here covers the NUTS-era portion, 1986-2025.

**Previous match and why it was wrong:** the routing decision records 0 name candidates and 0 boundary matches against existing polities — the data was previously unrouted/unmatched rather than misrouted to a wrong existing row, since no PRT subnational entry existed to receive it. Routing it to the national PRT row would conflate a NUTS II sub-region (roughly 5% of mainland Portugal's ~89,000 km2) with the whole country, which is exactly the kind of territory-size mismatch this pipeline exists to correct.

**Confirmation of distinctness:** Algarve is a codified EU/Eurostat NUTS II statistical region (PT15) with its own INE (Portuguese national statistics institute) regional accounts, distinct from the other six mainland/island NUTS II regions of Portugal (Norte, Centro, Área Metropolitana de Lisboa, Alentejo, Açores, Madeira) — its statistical distinctness as a reporting unit, independent of any question of sovereignty, is what qualifies it for its own polity row under the reporting-unit creation policy used elsewhere in this table (e.g. issue 400, cited for the MAN-1950-1955 exemplar).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The registered source that would normally supply Portuguese first-level administrative polygons, gadm-4.1-adm1, is a subset covering only 81 countries and does not include Portugal, so no registered_source_unfetched fallback exists either — this is a genuine new-source gap (candidate: Eurostat/GISCO NUTS vector data, unregistered and unlicensed as of this session; see open question oq-polygon-source). polygon_source is therefore `none` and polygon_status `unassigned`.

**Territory description:** Algarve is the southernmost region of mainland Portugal, corresponding closely to the historic Faro district: it stretches roughly 150 km east-west along Portugal's south coast, from the Guadiana river (Spanish border) in the east to Cape St. Vincent in the west, bounded by the Atlantic to the south and the Serra do Caldeirão/Alentejo region to the north. Its modern NUTS III/municipal composition (16 municipalities including Faro, Portimão, Loulé, Lagos, Tavira) covers approximately **4,996 km2** — a figure drawn from published Eurostat/INE regional statistics for the modern Algarve NUTS II region, not measured from any geometry attached to this entry, since no polygon is attached.

## Predecessors and successors

Algarve as a NUTS II statistical region has no predecessor or successor polity row: it is not created by the dissolution of an earlier unit nor does it end in a merger or split within the data window. Before 1986 the same territory was administered as the historic Faro district and, further back, under regional governorships of the Kingdom/Republic of Portugal — continuous Portuguese sovereignty throughout, which is why no predecessor edge is asserted; the 1986 start reflects when the NUTS statistical framework began classifying it, not a change of territory or administration. It remains a current NUTS II region as of 2025, so no successor is asserted either.

## Decisions

### d-code-choice

**Code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern using the NUTS code as subunit token**

The dominant pattern for 57 of 66 subnational rows is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>. Algarve's NUTS II code is PT15, but 'PT' duplicates the ISO3 prefix awkwardly, so the subunit token used here is 'PT15' verbatim (matching how the source unit_id and admin_name identify it) giving PRT-PT15-1986-2025. This is deliberately NOT PRT-ALG-1986-2025 (which already has an orphan page in wiki/polities/ whose row never made it into the built table) and not a bespoke code, since Algarve is a standard administrative/statistical NUTS region rather than a historical territory with its own name distinct from Portugal's regional geography, so the bespoke pattern (3 of 66 rows) is not warranted.

## Open questions

### oq-geometry-held-by-the-district

**This NUTS unit carries no geometry because a district row already holds the same ground**

NUTS II Algarve is coterminous with the district of Faro, so PRT-FA-1835-2025 and this row are the same ground, not a container and a member. Two live rows declaring one source feature is what validate_shared_polygons refuses, and it refuses it for a real reason: both would be counted as separate territory. Until Portugal's two layers are given separate depths — districts inside NUTS II, with edges to match — the district row carries the boundary and this row states that it does, rather than both claiming the same feature.


### oq-pre1986-basis

**Pre-1986 back-cast years (1870-1985) are not yet routed to this entry**

The routing decision's reasoning proposes back-casting the 1870-1985 portion of the source series onto this same polity, on the basis that the region existed under earlier Portuguese administration and NUTS is merely its current statistical label. That back-cast has NOT been implemented in this page's start_year (set to 1986, the NUTS-era start per country convention) because no cross-check was done against Portugal's historic district boundaries (Faro district, the historic administrative predecessor covering roughly the same area) to confirm territorial continuity back to 1870. If that check is done and confirms continuity, start_year should move to 1870 with an added container edge for the pre-1986 sovereign chain (Kingdom of Portugal / First Republic / Estado Novo eras) and the pre-1986 rows re-routed here as back_cast; until then those rows remain unrouted by this entry.

### oq-polygon-source

**No registered polygon source covers Algarve; GISCO NUTS is the candidate but is unregistered and unlicensed here**

gadm-4.1-adm1, the registered source that would normally carry Portuguese district/region polygons, holds only a subset of 81 countries and Portugal is not among them, so this is effectively a new-source gap rather than an unfetched-but-registered one. The best real-world candidate is Eurostat/GISCO's NUTS2021 vector dataset (or an earlier NUTS vintage matching pre-2016 boundaries, since NUTS Algarve's exact extent has been revised across NUTS vintages 1988/2003/2013/2016/2021), but it is not registered in sources.yaml and its licence has not been verified in this session. polygon_source is therefore 'none' and polygon_status 'unassigned' until that source is registered and fetched.

### oq-nuts-vintage

**Which NUTS vintage boundary would apply is unresolved**

NUTS region boundaries for Portugal have been revised across multiple vintages since the classification's 1988 introduction (and 1986 EU accession); Algarve/PT15's exact municipality composition in NUTS2021 may not exactly match the region as delineated when Portuguese regional statistics began reporting it in the 1980s-90s. Whichever polygon source is eventually registered, the correct NUTS vintage for a 1986-2025 span needs to be chosen deliberately rather than defaulting to the latest (2021) vintage.
