# Central African Republic (1912-1919)

## Summary

The French colonial territory of Oubangui-Chari in its 1912–1919 reduced configuration, covering the period 30 March 1912 to 27 June 1919. "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were united in 1906" [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). CShapes records a genuine area reduction at 1912-03-30 (from 50.60 sq deg to 40.57 sq deg), corresponding to the reorganisation of French Equatorial Africa which detached territory northward (into what became French Chad). The area then returns to 50.60 sq deg at 1919-06-28, marking the boundary re-adjustment at the end of the First World War that redistributed former German Kamerun territory. This is a WHEP-distinct row because both boundary events represent genuine territorial changes.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 40.57 sq deg for the period 1912-03-30 to 1919-06-27, status=colony, owner=France (COW 220). The pre-1912 (1906–1912) area was 50.60 sq deg and the post-1919 area returns to 50.60 sq deg, indicating that territory was temporarily reassigned during this phase. Bounded by Chad to the north, Sudan to the east, Congo and Zaire to the south, and Cameroon to the west. The area corresponds to the basin of the Oubangui (Ubangi) River [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic).

## Open questions

### oq-caf-1912-reorganisation

The 30 March 1912 boundary change corresponds to a reorganisation of French Equatorial Africa. The specific administrative decree that caused the area to drop from 50.60 to 40.57 sq deg should be identified — likely related to the creation or expansion of Tchad (Chad) as a separate administrative territory.

The 27 June 1919 end date corresponds to the post-WWI redistribution of former German Kamerun territory (Treaty of Versailles, 28 June 1919, effective the following day). The specific boundary changes affecting CAR at this date should be documented with a primary or secondary source.
