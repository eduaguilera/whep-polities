# Austria (1918-1919)

## Summary

Austria (1918-1919) represents the brief post-World War I transition period between the dissolution of Austria-Hungary and the establishment of the Treaty of Saint-Germain boundaries (signed September 1919). During this period the German-Austrian rump state declared itself a republic (12 November 1918) and sought union with Germany (Anschluss), which the peace treaties prohibited. The WHEP database tracks this as a distinct row because CShapes records a territorial change between the Austro-Hungarian boundaries and the post-treaty Austrian boundaries.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The territory shrank dramatically from the multinational Austro-Hungarian Empire to the small Alpine republic, losing Bohemia, Moravia, Galicia, and the South Tyrol.

**1918 was not a clean bridge — the label tracked a moving footprint (added 2026-08-18).** This page
frames 1918-1919 as a two-year bridge between Cisleithania and Saint-Germain Austria. The IIA
magnitudes for calendar-year 1918 are internally inconsistent with EITHER territory:

```
sugar   1918      8,266 t   against a 124,200 t median for rump Austria 1919-1945   → far too LOW
wine    1918    117,703 t   against a  35,000 t median for rump Austria 1919-1945   → 3x too HIGH
```

Both readings point the same way once you place them geographically. Sugar collapses because the
Bohemian and Moravian beet belt was already reporting separately after Czechoslovak independence on 28
October 1918 — the same belt whose departure makes the Cisleithania-to-rump step so steep on the
predecessor row. Wine is far above the rump baseline because South Tyrol's vineyards were still being
counted under Austrian claim until the Italian occupation was completed during 1919.

So for this one calendar year the label is neither Cisleithania nor the final republic: it is a
not-yet-settled administrative footprint that had already lost its sugar and not yet lost its wine.
`austria|iia|1918-1918` is 4 values and cannot carry a verdict either way, but the year should not be
read as belonging to either neighbouring territory.
