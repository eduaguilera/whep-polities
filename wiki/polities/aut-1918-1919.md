# Austria (1918-1919)

## Summary

Austria (1918-1919) represents the brief post-World War I transition period between the dissolution of Austria-Hungary and the establishment of the Treaty of Saint-Germain boundaries (signed September 1919). During this period the German-Austrian rump state declared itself a republic (12 November 1918) and sought union with Germany (Anschluss), which the peace treaties prohibited. The WHEP database tracks this as a distinct row because CShapes records a territorial change between the Austro-Hungarian boundaries and the post-treaty Austrian boundaries.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The territory shrank dramatically from the multinational Austro-Hungarian Empire to the small Alpine republic, losing Bohemia, Moravia, Galicia, and the South Tyrol.
