# Russian SFSR (1917-1991)

## Summary

Tracks the **Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR)** as
a distinct statistical unit from the October Revolution (November 1917)
through the dissolution of the USSR (26 December 1991). This polity was
created to receive IIA data labeled **'russian federation'** with
**iso=rus** for the pre-1991 period (confirmed year range 1906–1960, ~133
rows). Routing those rows to any F228 polity (Russian Empire ~21 M km²;
USSR ~22.4 M km²) would overstate territory by approximately 25–30%,
making this a genuine coverage gap, not a labelling error.

From October 1917 to 30 December 1922 the RSFSR was the sovereign
Bolshevik state (overlapping in time with the F228 chain polities
F228-1917-1918, F228-1918-1920, F228-1920-1921, and partially
F228-1921-1940). From 30 December 1922 to 26 December 1991 the RSFSR
was the largest constituent republic of the USSR (~76% of its land
area). IIA retrojects the modern iso code 'rus' back to data that was
collected or reported for the territory of what is now the Russian
Federation.

Predecessor: [f228-1914-1917](f228-1914-1917.md) (Russian Empire)
or alternatively [f228-1917-1918](f228-1917-1918.md) for the
revolutionary transition period.
Successor: [rus-1991-2014](rus-1991-2014.md) (Russian Federation).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Proxied from CShapes 2.0, `gwcode=365`,
`polygon_feature_year=1991` (i.e. the same polygon as
[rus-1991-2014](rus-1991-2014.md)). The RSFSR's territory within the
USSR was approximately 17.1 M km² throughout 1922–1991, closely matching
the post-1991 Russian Federation. The 1991 CShapes feature is the best
available published GIS approximation for the RSFSR's internal borders.
This is a **proxy** polygon, not an exact match to any year-specific
RSFSR boundary.

**Approximation caveats:**

- **1917–1922 (RSFSR as sovereign state):** Territory was highly
  fluid. Post-Brest-Litovsk (March 1918) the RSFSR had lost much of
  western Russia; the Civil War front shifted repeatedly until 1920.
  After the Treaty of Riga (March 1921) the RSFSR consolidated to a
  territory broadly similar to later Soviet-era boundaries minus the
  other Soviet republics. The 1991 proxy overstates slightly for 1918–1921
  (Brest-Litovsk losses not recovered until end of Civil War).
- **1922–1954:** The Crimea was part of the RSFSR until its transfer
  to the Ukrainian SSR in February 1954. The 1991 polygon does not
  include Crimea. For data years 1922–1954 the proxy therefore slightly
  understates RSFSR territory by ~27,000 km².
- **1954–1991:** The 1991 proxy (without Crimea) matches the RSFSR's
  administrative territory closely.

**Why this entry exists:** IIA assigns modern national iso codes
retroactively. Data labeled 'russian federation' (iso=rus) in the IIA
for years 1917–1991 measures production or economic activity within the
territory of what is now Russia — not the full Russian Empire or the full
USSR. The F228 chain polities cover the aggregate empire/state territory;
they are not the correct routing target for sub-republican data.

**Area comparison:**
- Russian Empire 1906–1917: ~21.0 M km²
- Full USSR 1945–1991: ~22.4 M km²
- RSFSR within USSR: ~17.1 M km² (~76% of USSR)
- Modern Russian Federation: ~17.1 M km²

Routing 'russian federation' data to F228-1945-1991 (USSR) would
overstate territory by ~31%; routing to F228-1905-1914 (Russian Empire)
would overstate by ~23%.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor (sovereign period):** [f228-1914-1917](f228-1914-1917.md)
  — Russian Empire. The October Revolution (November 1917) ended the
  Empire; the Bolshevik RSFSR replaced it.
- **Predecessor (chain context):** This polity is a sub-territory entry
  that runs in parallel with the F228 sovereign chain during 1917–1991;
  it is not a successor of F228-1945-1991.
- **Successor:** [rus-1991-2014](rus-1991-2014.md) — dissolution of the
  USSR (26 December 1991). The RSFSR was renamed the Russian Federation
  and became an independent state.

## Sourced claims

- **RSFSR area ~17.1 M km².** The Russian Soviet Federative Socialist
  Republic covered approximately 17,075,400 km², making it the largest
  constituent republic of the USSR and larger than any other sovereign
  state.
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md)
- **USSR total area ~22.4 M km².** The Union of Soviet Socialist
  Republics covered approximately 22,402,200 km².
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md)
- **RSFSR share ~76% of USSR.** 17.1 M / 22.4 M ≈ 76.3%.
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md)
- **Crimea transfer 1954.** Crimea Oblast was transferred from the RSFSR
  to the Ukrainian SSR by decree on 19 February 1954.
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md)
- **IIA iso=rus retroactive coding.** The International Institute of
  Agriculture (IIA) and successor datasets assign modern national iso
  codes retroactively; 'rus' refers to the territory of the present-day
  Russian Federation, not to the full empire or USSR.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

**Overlap with F228 sovereign chain:** The F228 polities
(F228-1917-1918, F228-1918-1920, F228-1920-1921, F228-1921-1940)
cover the same calendar years as this polity's start period (1917–1940)
but at a different territorial scope (sovereign state / USSR aggregate
vs. RSFSR sub-republic). This is intentional: F228 polities receive data
for the full empire/USSR aggregate; RSFSR-1917-1991 receives IIA data
retroactively coded to Russian core territory. Both polities coexist.

## Decisions

- **Created to fix issue russian-federation-iia-sub-territory-coverage-gap:**
  IIA data labeled 'russian federation' (iso=rus, ~133 rows, 1906–1960
  confirmed) had no valid WHEP polity. Routing to F228 aggregate polities
  was rejected because it overstates territory by 23–31%.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1917-russian-core

For IIA rows with label 'russian federation' (iso=rus) in years
**1906–1916** (before the RSFSR existed), the correct routing is unclear:
the data measures Russian core territory (~17 M km²) but no WHEP polity
exists at that granularity. The F228-1905-1914 and F228-1914-1917
polities cover the full Russian Empire (~21 M km²) — a ~23% territorial
overstatement. Options:

1. Create a separate 'Russian core (pre-1917)' polity (small territorial
   mismatch vs. empire but same data label).
2. Route 1906–1916 rows to F228-1905-1914 / F228-1914-1917 with a
   documented approximation note (23% overstatement).
3. Leave unmatched and flag as coverage_gap until the data source
   footnotes clarify what territory the pre-1917 IIA 'russia' data
   actually measures.

Resolution depends on whether the IIA 1906–1916 data was collected
at empire scale or Russian-core scale. A magnitude comparison against
known empire-level totals would answer this.

### oq-polygon-rsfsr-exact

A more accurate RSFSR polygon for specific sub-periods (particularly
1922–1954 to include Crimea, and 1917–1921 for the Civil War era) could
be sourced from Cliopatria (check for 'Russian SFSR' features) or a
composed GIS approach using CShapes USSR minus other SSR polygons.
Until then the 1991 proxy is used.

## Polygon caveat (added 2026-07-24) — needs a decision

`scripts/validate_polygons.py` found the attached geometry measures
**21,824,142 km²** while this page states **17,098,242 km²** (28% divergence).
Both numbers are right about different things, which is the problem:

- ~17.1M km² is the **Russian SFSR** — the republic this row is about.
- ~21.8M km² is the **whole Soviet Union**, which is what CShapes gwcode 365
  returns for these years. CShapes models the USSR as a single state and has no
  feature for any constituent republic.

So the polygon is not an approximation of the RSFSR; it is **a different
entity** — the union of which the RSFSR was one part (albeit ~77% of its area).
`polygon_status: proxy` understates that. Data matched here is attributed to a
territory 28% larger than the republic, and it overlaps the
[f228-*](f228-1945-1991.md) chain, which already covers the USSR itself.

**Open decision.** Three options, none free:

1. **`polygon_status: unassigned`** with this reason recorded — honest, and
   consistent with the rule preferring a documented gap to a misleading
   polygon. Costs the row its geometry.
2. **Composed polygon** — the USSR minus the other fourteen republics, built in
   `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` from a source that has republic
   boundaries. Correct, but needs such a source.
3. **Retire this row** if the `F228-*` chain already covers every data label
   that would route here, since the two overlap in both time and territory.

Until this is settled, treat any RSFSR-matched aggregate as covering the whole
USSR, not the republic.
