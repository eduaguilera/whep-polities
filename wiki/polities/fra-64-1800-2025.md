# Pyrénées-Atlantiques

## Summary

Pyrénées-Atlantiques is a French département in the far south-west, on the Atlantic coast and the Spanish border, with its préfecture at Pau. It covers roughly 7,645 km² and includes the historic Béarn and part of the French Basque Country (Pays Basque). It was created in the 1790 Revolutionary reorganisation of France into départements under the name Basses-Pyrénées, and was renamed Pyrénées-Atlantiques in 1969 without (per available evidence) any boundary change. This entry is `type: subnational`: the département has never been a sovereign polity, only ever a first-level administrative subdivision of France, and this row exists purely to carry a reporting territory distinct from the national FRA total, not to claim any independent statehood. It was created as a `create_new` case because no existing polity row -- name-based or GADM-boundary-based -- matched the reporting unit labelled 'FRA-FRI15' in the source routing decision, and because 97.6% of the rows attributed to that label are genuinely observed (not blank placeholders or imputed), which is the signal that distinguishes a real sub-national reporting series from a residual bucket that happens to share a label.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures mitchell-sourced agricultural production data for the unit the source labels 'FRA-FRI15' (admin_name FRI15, France), with data beginning in 1862 and continuing to the present; 97.6% of its rows are observed rather than blank or imputed, which the routing decision treats as confirmation this is a genuine standing reporting territory and not a national-total residual. Before this entry was created, the routing search found zero name-based candidates and zero GADM-boundary-match candidates among existing polities -- there was no existing FRA département-level row for it to have been (mis)matched to, so there is no prior mismatch to correct here; the entry fills a genuine absence rather than fixing a wrong routing. What confirms the entity is distinct enough to warrant its own row rather than folding into FRA-1919-2025 is structural, not from an external trading-polity source like Federico-Tena (this is a modern administrative unit, not a historical trading polity): French départements are the level at which INSEE and predecessor statistical services have published agricultural and demographic series since the 19th century, and Pyrénées-Atlantiques/Basses-Pyrénées is one of the original 1790 départements, continuously distinct from its neighbours (e.g. Hautes-Pyrénées, Landes) for the entire span.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_reasoning establishes that none of the registered sources can supply this boundary even in principle: cshapes-europe and cshapes-2.0 are national-level (one feature per country per period), so there is nothing at sub-country granularity to union or difference; gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered but the locally-present file is an 81-country subset that excludes France entirely, so even though GADM's global adm1/adm2 layers do cover French départements in the wider world, nothing executable is present on disk. The proposed remedy is `new_source_needed`: either the global GADM 4.1 adm2 layer or France's own IGN ADMIN-EXPRESS-COG dataset, neither of which is registered yet.

**Territory description:** Pyrénées-Atlantiques is département 64 of France, in the extreme south-west: it fronts the Bay of Biscay at Biarritz and Bayonne in the west, borders Spain (Navarre and Aragón) along the Pyrenees to the south, and borders the départements of Landes and Hautes-Pyrénées to the north and east. Its préfecture is Pau. It comprises the historic province of Béarn in the east and the French Basque Country (Pyrénées-Atlantiques' Basque cantons: Bayonne, Biarritz, Saint-Jean-de-Luz, Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port) in the west. Its area today is approximately **7,645 km²** (a commonly cited modern reference figure for the département, not measured from any polygon attached to this entry, since none exists yet). Under its earlier name, Basses-Pyrénées, the same territory was assembled in 1790 primarily from the pre-Revolutionary province of Béarn together with Basque territories (Labourd, Basse-Navarre, part of Soule) that had previously been distinct pays; the 1969 rename to Pyrénées-Atlantiques is reported (per the routing decision) as nominal only, with no accompanying boundary change.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Start year set to 1800, not the routing decision's proposed 1790**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, reasoning that Pyrénées-Atlantiques (as Basses-Pyrénées) is an original 1790 Revolutionary département and none of the four documented exceptions (Territoire de Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine départements, Corse split, overseas départements) apply. That reasoning about the département's founding date is correct on its own terms, but it does not by itself satisfy the containment gate: container edges must tile the whole span with no gap, and the earliest FRA row in the polity table is FRA-1800-1871 (start_year 1800) -- there is no FRA container row covering 1790-1800. Using start_year 1790 would leave a decade uncontained and the entry would be rejected. This page therefore starts the code and frontmatter at 1800, the earliest year any FRA container actually exists for, and records the 1790-1800 gap as an open question rather than silently dropping it.

## Open questions

### oq-1790-1800-gap

**The 1790-1800 span is neither in this entry nor in any FRA container row**

Basses-Pyrénées was created in the 1790 Revolutionary reform, ten years before this entry's start_year of 1800. The polity table's earliest FRA row (FRA-1800-1871) also starts in 1800, so the gap is not specific to this entry -- it appears to be an unaddressed edge of the whole FRA chain. Either a new FRA container row for 1790-1800 needs to be created, or the mitchell/fao data (which only starts in 1862 per the routing note) never actually needs this decade covered, in which case the true start_year for reporting purposes might reasonably be later than 1800 too. Left open because resolving it means editing the national FRA chain, out of scope for a single département page.

### oq-polygon-source-not-yet-built

**No polygon exists yet for this département in any registered source**

The routing decision found no registered source (cshapes-europe, cshapes-2.0, gadm-4.1-adm1) carries French département-level (adm2) boundaries; the locally-present GADM adm1 subset excludes FRA entirely. A real boundary source (global GADM adm2, or France's own IGN ADMIN-EXPRESS-COG) exists but is not registered/fetched here. Until it is, this entry has polygon_status: unassigned and no polygon_feature_id. Whether the eventual boundary should be built via the constructed/build.py union pipeline or via a newly registered adm2 source is unresolved.

### oq-1969-rename-continuity

**Whether the 1969 Basses-Pyrénées to Pyrénées-Atlantiques rename changed any boundary**

The span_basis in the routing decision asserts the 1969 rename was purely nominal (same boundary, no territorial change), but this page has not independently verified that against IGN or INSEE historical boundary records -- it is taken on the routing note's word. If the rename in fact coincided with any commune transfer (as sometimes happened in French departmental reorganisations), the polygon proxy assumption used once a boundary source exists would need re-checking.
