# Panama (1903-1979, before Canal Zone return)

## Summary

Panama as an independent republic from its separation from Colombia on 3 November 1903 until the 1979 return of the Panama Canal Zone under the 1977 Torrijos-Carter Treaties. During this period the US-administered Canal Zone (~1,432 km²) was a de facto exclave within Panamanian territory, tracked separately as CZN-1903-1979.

Prior to independence, Panama was the Panamá Department of Colombia; that territory is covered by COL-1830-1903 (and before 1830 by the Gran Colombia / New Granada chain). PAN-1903-1979 begins at independence and does NOT extend back to 1800.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0, feature ID 95, vintage 1903. The polygon covers the Republic of Panama (~75,517 km²) excluding the US-administered Canal Zone strip. Polygon vintage year matches polity start year exactly — zero vintage drift.

The Colombia-Panama boundary runs for ~225 km mainly along water partings (Biger 1995, p.172). A boundary treaty formalising the line established in June 1855 was signed on 20 August 1924. The Costa Rica-Panama boundary is ~330 km long (Biger 1995, p.177).

FAO 1952 yearbook reports Panama and the Panama Canal Zone as two separate concurrent entries. The land-area ratio of 51.8× in the FAO data matches the actual area ratio of Panama (~75,517 km²) to Canal Zone (~1,432 km²) at 52.7×, confirming that the "panama" FAO label maps to PAN-1903-1979 (not to the Canal Zone) for data years 1916-1960.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [col-1830-1903](col-1830-1903.md) — Panama was the Panamá Department of Colombia until independence on 3 November 1903. Panama was created as an autonomous province in Colombia in 1855, with boundaries similar to those existing today (Biger 1995, p.172).
- **Successor:** [pan-1979-2025](pan-1979-2025.md) — Panama 1979–present (post-Canal-Zone-return).

- The 1903 Hay-Bunau-Varilla Treaty granted the US rights "as if it were sovereign" over a ~16 × 80 km canal zone. [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §panama](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)

- Torrijos-Carter Treaties signed 7 September 1977; the surrounding territory was returned first, in 1979. Full canal handover followed on 31 December 1999. [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §panama](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
