# Northern Rhodesia (1911-1953)

British colonial territory in south-central Africa, created on 17 August 1911 by the administrative merger of Northeastern Rhodesia (BSA Company territory, 1900–1911) and Northwestern Rhodesia (BSA Company territory, 1900–1911) under a single Administrator. Administered by the British South Africa Company until 1924, then directly by the British Crown as a protectorate. On 1 August 1953 it was absorbed into the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland.

## WHEP Coverage

Covers WHEP data for Zambia-territory observations for the period 1907–1953 (observations pre-dating 1911 are attributed here as the closest administrative predecessor on the same territory).

## Predecessor and Successor

- Successor: **FRN-1953-1964** — Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (1953–1964).

## Territorial Extent

Area approximately **751,924 km²**, closely matching modern Zambia (752,618 km²). Borders were largely stable throughout the colonial period; no significant boundary changes occurred between 1911 and 1953.
