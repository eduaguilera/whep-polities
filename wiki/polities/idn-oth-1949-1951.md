# Other islands (within Indonesia, 1949-1951)

## Summary

This WHEP polity covers **all of Indonesia except Java/Madura and Bali/Lombok**, as the third reporting unit used by the **FAO 1952 dataset** for the period 1949–1951. The FAO source disaggregated Indonesia into three distinct island-group sub-units: "Indonesia Java and Madura" (`IDN-JVM-1949-1951`, ~132,000 km²), "Indonesia Bali and Lombok" (`IDN-BLB-1949-1951`, ~10,505 km²), and "Indonesia Other islands" (this polity, ~1,757,495 km²). This three-part structure reflects a sub-national agricultural census framework used in the immediate post-independence period.

"Other islands" comprises **Sumatra, Kalimantan (Indonesian Borneo), Sulawesi, the Maluku archipelago, West and East Nusa Tenggara (excluding Lombok), and the Papua region** — the vast majority of Indonesia's land area, but a smaller share of its agricultural output (the FAO data shows lower rice paddy and cassava yields than Java, reflecting the less-intensively farmed outer islands).

Routing this data to `IDN-1949-1969` (full Indonesia, ~1,900,000 km²) would overstate the represented territory by a factor of ~1.08 (8% overstatement by area); more critically it would **double-count** with the separately-matched Java/Madura and Bali/Lombok entries, which is the primary reason a dedicated polity is required.

The area is computed as Indonesia total (~1,900,000 km²) minus Java+Madura (~132,000 km²) minus Bali+Lombok (~10,505 km²).

**Polygon provenance**: GADM 4.1 sub-national Indonesia level-1 features for all provinces **not** covered by IDN-JVM-1949-1951 and IDN-BLB-1949-1951: specifically, the Sumatra provinces (Aceh, North Sumatra, West Sumatra, Riau, Riau Islands, Jambi, South Sumatra, Bengkulu, Lampung, Bangka-Belitung), the five Kalimantan provinces, the six Sulawesi provinces (North, Central, South, Southeast Sulawesi, Gorontalo, West Sulawesi), Maluku, North Maluku, East Nusa Tenggara, Papua, and West Papua. The province of West Nusa Tenggara (IDN.20_1) is **not** included here because the BLB polity already uses the full NTB province polygon as a proxy for Bali+Lombok (an acknowledged approximation documented on that wiki page); Sumbawa's area is thus counted toward the BLB polygon but belongs conceptually to "Other islands". This is recorded as an open question (see below).

All island physical extents are stable geographic features; modern GADM province boundaries are a valid proxy for 1949–1951 boundaries.

**Polygon status: proxy** — modern GADM boundaries used for geographically stable island extents; no period-specific historical GIS available for Indonesian sub-national provinces.

**Polygon confidence: high** — island/province boundaries are fixed physical geography; the main approximation is the NTB/Sumbawa exclusion (see open question).

## Predecessors and successors

## Decisions

- **Coverage gap**: Created to fix the third panel of the FAO 1952 Indonesia sub-national reporting structure. Issues `indonesia-java-madura-missing-polity` and `indonesia-bali-lombok-coverage-gap` were resolved by IDN-JVM and IDN-BLB; this polity resolves `indonesia-other-islands-missing-polity`.
- **No routing to IDN-1949-1969**: Even though the 8% area overstatement would be minor on its own, routing is prohibited because it would double-count with the already-matched JVM and BLB sub-national entries in the same dataset.
- **NTB/Sumbawa handling**: The BLB polygon uses the full West NTB province (IDN.20_1), which includes Sumbawa (~15,400 km²) as well as Lombok (~4,725 km²). Sumbawa belongs geographically to "Other islands" but is not separately extractable from the GADM province without sub-provincial processing. The OTH area estimate of ~1,757,495 km² therefore slightly understates the true "Other islands" territory by ~15,400 km² (Sumbawa). This is a ~0.9% discrepancy and does not materially affect data routing.

## Open questions

The exact GADM 4.1 GID_1 codes for all "Other islands" provinces should be verified against the GADM shapefile. (West Nusa Tenggara / IDN.20_1 excluded because BLB uses the full NTB province polygon.)

### oq-sumbawa-double-attribution

Sumbawa island (~15,400 km²) is part of West Nusa Tenggara province (IDN.20_1), which BLB uses as its polygon proxy. Sumbawa therefore falls in the BLB polygon but belongs geographically and agriculturally to "Other islands". The area discrepancy is ~0.9% and unlikely to affect data values materially, but if sub-provincial polygon extraction is performed for BLB (to limit it to Lombok only), OTH should be updated to add the Sumbawa portion of NTB.

### oq-fao-reporting-years

The exact year range for which "Indonesia Other islands" appears as a distinct FAO label should be confirmed against the raw FAO 1952 source file. The 15 currently-unmatched rows span 1949–1951; if the label also appears in earlier colonial-period FAO data (pre-1949), a predecessor entry may be warranted.
