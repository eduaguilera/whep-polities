# Botswana

## Summary

Botswana is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Botswana (formerly Bechuanaland) from 1966 to the present. Bechuanaland gained independence from Britain on 30 September 1966.

## Territorial extent

Botswana is a landlocked country in southern Africa bounded by Namibia, Zimbabwe, South Africa, and Zambia. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The Mozambique-South Africa boundary context establishes that "the area to the east and north of Bechuanaland, later to become Southern and Northern Rhodesia, was proclaimed a British sphere of influence in 1888" and Cecil Rhodes's British South Africa Company was granted a charter to operate in the area along the Zambezi River (p.396). [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- The Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 11 June 1891 established the boundary between Mozambique and Southern Rhodesia, with Bechuanaland forming part of the broader British sphere in southern Africa. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- Botswana gained independence in 1966; became multi-party democracy (p.87). British ruled Tswana indirectly through rural committees (p.87). Area 224,606 sq mi; capital Gaborone. [biger-1995 §botswana](../sources/biger-1995.md#botswana)
