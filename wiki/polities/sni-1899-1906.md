# Southern Nigeria (1899-1906)

## Summary

The British territories of Southern Nigeria in their pre-amalgamation configuration, 1899-1906. This covers the period before the Colony and Protectorate of Lagos and the Niger Coast Protectorate were merged into the Colony and Protectorate of Southern Nigeria on 1 January 1906. The territory was carved from the former kingdoms of Benin, the Ijebu, and other Yoruba and Igbo polities. "The Nigerians have been independent from British colonial rule" since 1960 [biger-1995 §nigeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#nigeria). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. The territory encompassed the southern coastal regions of modern Nigeria, including Lagos, the Niger Delta, and the Yoruba hinterland. Bounded by Northern Nigeria to the north, German Kamerun to the east, and the Gulf of Guinea to the south.
