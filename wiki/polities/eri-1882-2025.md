# Eritrea

## Summary

Eritrea covers the territory from Italian colonial establishment through independence. The start year 1882 reflects early Italian presence on the Red Sea coast; the territory was formally proclaimed the colony of Eritrea in 1889. It was federated with Ethiopia in 1952, annexed as a province in 1962, and gained independence after a 1993 referendum following a 30-year liberation war.

## Territorial extent

Eritrea is located in the Horn of Africa on the Red Sea coast. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
