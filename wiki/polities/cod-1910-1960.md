# DR Congo (1910-1960)

## Summary

Belgian Congo, the colonial polity governing the territory of the present-day Democratic Republic of the Congo, from 1910 (when the Belgian state formally took over from King Leopold's Congo Free State) until independence on 30 June 1960. "In the Berlin Conference of 1884-1885 the Association, which changed its name to Congo Free State, was recognized as a governing power on the Congo River" [biger-1995 congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). The Congo Free State was submitted to Belgium in 1895, "which accepted it only in 1908, when it became Belgian Congo" [biger-1995 congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). CShapes codes it as a Belgian colony (ruler=BEL).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature 490, vintage 1910. The Congo-Zaire boundary runs for 1,498 miles (2,410 km) [biger-1995 congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). The 1910 start date reflects a boundary change recorded in CShapes after the transition from Congo Free State to Belgian Congo.

**Polygon validity for full 1910-1960 span:** Belgian Congo's external borders were fixed by a series of bilateral treaties concluded before 1910 (with France, Portugal, and the UK). No annexations or cessions occurred between 1910 and independence in 1960. The CShapes 2.0 feature 490 (vintage 1910) is therefore a valid spatial representation for the entire 1910-1960 span. No polygon split is required.

## Contradictions

None identified. Biger gives 1908 for the formal Belgian takeover; CShapes uses 1910 as the boundary-change year. The discrepancy may reflect the date of boundary demarcation vs. administrative transfer.

## Decisions

### dec-cod-alias-fao1952

The FAO 1952 dataset labels this territory as "Belgian Congo" and "Belgian Congo (fao1952)" across 180 rows covering years 1937-1951, which falls entirely within the 1910-1960 span. Both label variants are aliased to COD-1910-1960. No alternative routing was needed: no granular sub-polity exists for any part of this territory in this period, and the FAO data magnitudes are consistent with a single colony-level observation.

## Open questions

### oq-cod-1908-vs-1910

Biger indicates Belgium formally accepted the Congo Free State in 1908. CShapes marks the boundary change at 1910. The reason for this two-year discrepancy (possibly a boundary commission completing work in 1910) should be clarified.
