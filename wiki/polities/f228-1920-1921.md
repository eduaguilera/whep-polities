# RSFSR (1920-1921)

## Summary

This row covers **2 years** at the end of the Russian Civil War
under the RSFSR. The Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921) settled the
border with Poland. The Treaty of Tartu with Finland (1920) and
recognition of the Baltic states formalized the post-WWI
territorial settlement.

Predecessor: [f228-1918-1920](f228-1918-1920.md).
Successor: [f228-1921-1940](f228-1921-1940.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` gwcode 365 per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), the step
  **1920-02-02 / 1920-09-01**, measured **21,506,736 km²**
  [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md).
- **Which step, and why the year could not say.** CShapes cuts gwcode 365's 1920
  into four steps that this row could match — `1918-11-11/1920-02-01`
  (21,405,134 km²), `1920-02-02/1920-09-01` (21,506,736 km²),
  `1920-09-02/1920-10-27` (21,700,852 km²) and `1920-10-28/1921-03-17`
  (21,656,484 km²) — and this row covers 1920 only, so three candidates *start*
  in 1920 and every one of them contains it. No `polygon_feature_year` can name
  one, so the binding was decided by shapefile row order until 2026-08-17 (issue
  100). It is now pinned by `polygon_feature_date: 1920-02-02`, resolved by day in
  `build_database.find_feature`. **The geometry did not change**; the spread across
  the four candidates is only 1.01×, which is precisely why a silent re-fetch swap
  would have gone unnoticed.
- **Why this step and not one of the other three.** It is in force for **213 of
  the row's 366 days** — a majority — against 32, 56 and 65 for the others. (The
  determinism baseline had justified the same pick as "the step in force at the
  row's start"; measured, that is not right: on 1920-01-01 the step in force is the
  21,405,134 km² one. The choice is defensible on duration, not on the row's first
  day.)
- **Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921).** Settled the
  Polish-Soviet border. Poland gained western Belarus and
  western Ukraine (the "Kresy" territories). This formalized
  Russia's loss of territory east of the Curzon Line.
- **Treaty of Tartu with Finland (14 October 1920).** Confirmed
  Finnish independence and settled the Russo-Finnish border.
  Finland gained Petsamo (access to the Arctic Ocean).
- **Baltic states recognized.** Estonia (Treaty of Tartu,
  2 February 1920), Latvia (Latvian-Soviet Peace Treaty,
  11 August 1920), Lithuania (Soviet-Lithuanian Peace Treaty,
  12 July 1920) all had their independence recognized by
  Soviet Russia during this period.
