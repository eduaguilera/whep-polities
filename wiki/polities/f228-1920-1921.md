# USSR (1920-1921)

## Summary

The CSV labels this row "USSR (1920-1921)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), but **the USSR
did not exist until 30 December 1922**. This polity is actually
the **RSFSR** (Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic)
throughout the entire 1920-1921 window. The label is an
anachronism documented in
[log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
Per the critical-stance rule
[log decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority](../log.md#decision-csv-is-evidence-not-authority),
the CSV label is preserved as-is and the mislabeling is
documented rather than silently corrected.

This row covers **2 years** at the end of the Russian Civil War.
The Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921) settled the border with
Poland. The Treaty of Tartu with Finland (1920) and recognition
of the Baltic states formalized the post-WWI territorial
settlement.

Predecessor per CSV: [f228-1918-1920](f228-1918-1920.md).
Successor per CSV: [f228-1921-1940](f228-1921-1940.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921).** Settled the
  Polish-Soviet border. Poland gained western Belarus and
  western Ukraine (the "Kresy" territories). This formalized
  Russia's loss of territory east of the Curzon Line.
- **Treaty of Tartu with Finland (14 October 1920).** Confirmed
  Finnish independence and settled the Russo-Finnish border.
  Finland gained Petsamo (access to the Arctic Ocean).
- **Baltic states recognized.** Estonia (Treaty of Tartu,
  2 February 1920), Latvia (Latvian-Soviet Peace Treaty,
  11 August 1920), Lithuania (Soviet-Lithuanian Peace Treaty,
  12 July 1920) all had their independence recognized by
  Soviet Russia during this period.

## Sourced claims

- **1921-03-18: Treaty of Riga.** Ended the Polish-Soviet War.
  The border was drawn well east of the Curzon Line, giving
  Poland significant territories in western Belarus and Ukraine
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1920: Baltic peace treaties.** Soviet Russia recognized the
  independence of Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania through
  separate bilateral treaties
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
- **1920-10-14: Treaty of Tartu (Finland).** Finland's borders
  confirmed, including Petsamo
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).

## Contradictions

- **Label anachronism.** The CSV calls this row "USSR" but the
  entity is the RSFSR. The USSR was not established until
  30 December 1922. See
  [log proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism](../log.md#proposal-f228-ussr-anachronism).
