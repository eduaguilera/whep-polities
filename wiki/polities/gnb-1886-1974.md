# Guinea-Bissau (1886-1974)

## Summary

This row covers Portuguese Guinea (Guinea-Bissau) during the colonial period from 1886 (CShapes start for dependency data) to 10 September 1974 (Portuguese recognition of independence). CShapes records three sub-periods (1886-1886, 1886-1942, 1942-1974) all with identical area (2.76 degree-squared units), merged here because there is no territorial change. The entire colonial period was under Portuguese rule.
