# Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba

## Summary

The three Caribbean islands that became **special municipalities of the Netherlands** on 10 October
2010, when the Netherlands Antilles was dissolved: Bonaire, off the Venezuelan coast, and Sint Eustatius
and Saba in the Leeward Islands, some 800 km to the north-east. They are grouped under the single ISO
3166-1 code `BES` and are published as one unit by UN WPP and by GADM, which is why this is one row
rather than three.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-08-24 to close issue 554. Before this page the BES islands had **no polity row at all** —
`grep -c '^BES' site/polities.csv` returned 0 — so a third of the Netherlands Antilles' successor
territory was absent from the database entirely.

The downstream cost is measured on that issue: FAOSTAT/FABIO area code 151 carries food in every year
1961–2010 with no population denominator, because neither population source WHEP reads publishes `ANT`.
UN WPP 2024 publishes `CUW` 156.882k, `SXM` 33.798k and `BES` 20.561k for 2010 instead — a sum of
211.24k — reachable only by walking `successor`, which had no edge to walk.

**The composition was already encoded here.** `scripts/sources/reporting-areas/build.py` builds
`ANT-1961-2010`'s polygon as the union of `BES`, `CUW` and `SXM`. This page and `SXM-2010-2025` make the
two members that had no polity into rows; the successor list on the federation's page now states what the
polygon was always composed from.

## Territorial extent

`polygon_status: estimate`, bound to the GADM 4.1 adm0 outline for `BES`, which is a **three-part
multipolygon** — the islands are not contiguous and lie roughly 800 km apart.

The outline measures **324.9 km² geodesic** (323.7 km² projected). The reference land area is about
**328 km²** — Bonaire 288, Sint Eustatius 21, Saba 13 — so the polygon agrees to **0.99×**. That is
unusually good for a small-island GADM outline: issue 570 records six such territories running 5–26%
large, and this one does not, which is consistent with that issue's finding that the oversize comes from
enclosed water rather than from the source being coarse — these three islands have none.

`polygon_area_km2` is declared as **325**, the polygon's own measurement, so the declared figure and the
geometry agree. The status is `estimate` rather than `assigned` to match `CUW-2010-2025` and
`SXM-2010-2025`: all three are present-day GADM outlines standing for a territory whose boundaries have
not moved since 2010, but none has been checked against a cadastral source.

## Chain

Predecessor `ANT-1961-2010`, at the dissolution of 10 October 2010. No successor: the row is live.

Note that these islands are **special municipalities of the Netherlands** rather than a constituent
country, unlike Curaçao and Sint Maarten. They are modelled as their own polity anyway, because the data
requires a destination and `NLD-1830-2025` would send any per-capita consumer to the Netherlands'
17 million people. The type is `territory`, not `national`.
