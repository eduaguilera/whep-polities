# Kingdom of Saloum

## Summary

The Kingdom of Saloum was a Serer kingdom in the Sine-Saloum region of Senegal (paired with the Kingdom of Sine), founded c. 14th century. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1887, when France established a protectorate over the kingdom. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). After its incorporation, the territory became part of French West Africa and is eventually succeeded by modern Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom occupied the Saloum River basin in the Sine-Saloum region of Senegal, south of the Kingdom of Sine. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). The territory included river flats and the Saloum Delta area.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none recorded in WHEP; the kingdom predates the 1800 database floor.
- **Successor:** [sen-1960-2025](sen-1960-2025.md) — modern Senegal (via French colonial incorporation from ~1887)

## Sourced claims

- Serer kingdom in Sine-Saloum region, Senegal (paired with Sine). Founded c. 14th century. French protectorate 1887. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
