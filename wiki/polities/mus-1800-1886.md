# Mauritius (to 1886)

## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Mauritius, an island country in the Indian Ocean east of Madagascar. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. "In 1810 the British captured Mauritius and began importing Indians as laborers" (p.380) [biger-1995 &sect;mauritius](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritius).

## Territorial extent

Mauritius: area 788 sq. miles (2,040 sq. km). No land boundaries. Capital: Port Louis. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected).
