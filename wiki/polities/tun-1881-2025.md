# Tunisia

## Summary

Tunisia from the establishment of the French protectorate (Treaty of
Bardo, 12 May 1881) to the present. Independent since 20 March 1956.
CShapes records three entries for Tunisia (COW 616) across this period,
all with the same area (~15.19 sq deg, ~164,000 km2) -- the 1956 splits
are administrative (independence date), not territorial. This justifies
a single continuous WHEP row. The legacy sub-row TUN-1886-1955 has
been deleted as redundant.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). Three CShapes
  entries (1886-1955, 1956-01-01 to 1956-03-19, 1956-03-20 to 2019),
  all with identical area ~15.19 sq deg. No real territorial change.
- **Pre-1886.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The same polygon
  is back-projected to 1881.
- **COW.** TUN, 616.
- **Area.** ~164,000 km2.

## Predecessors and successors

  French protectorate established by Treaty of Bardo, 12 May 1881.
- **Successor:** NA -- still active as of 2025.

## Open questions

### oq-1956-independence-exact-date

CShapes has two entries at 1956: one ending 1956-03-19, one starting
1956-03-20. This suggests 20 March 1956 as the independence date, which
matches the historical record. No territorial change accompanies this --
it is purely a sovereignty transition.

### oq-tun-1800-1881-page

The predecessor row TUN-1800-1881 (Beylik of Tunis) exists in the CSV
but has no wiki page yet. A future ingest should create one.
