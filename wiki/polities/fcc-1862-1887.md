French Cochinchina was a French colony in southern Vietnam, established following the Treaty of Saigon (5 June 1862) in which the Nguyen dynasty ceded three eastern provinces to France. A second treaty in 1874 transferred the remaining three western provinces. Cochinchina was incorporated into French Indochina upon the latter's creation on 17 October 1887.
