# Antofagasta (region of Chile)

## Summary

Antofagasta is the second region of Chile (Región de Antofagasta, Región II), a hyper-arid strip of the Atacama Desert in Chile's north, stretching from roughly 21°S to 26°S between the Andes and the Pacific. It was created in its present administrative form in 1976 when Decreto Ley 575 replaced the country's old province-based structure with a system of numbered regions; before that date the territory existed as the province of Antofagasta but was not a standalone first-order statistical unit in the sense the modern regional series use. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chilean sovereignty over the territory is continuous and undisputed throughout 1976-2025 -- the row exists to carry a sub-national reporting territory, not to compete with the national CHL chain (CHL-1902-2025, which already contains the whole country including this area) in the matcher's sovereignty ranking. The region's economy is dominated by copper mining (Chuquicamata, Escondida) and, historically, nitrate (saltpeter) extraction, and it holds one of the driest inhabited climates on Earth. end_year is set to 2025 (exclusive) because the region is still current and the database's open convention closes all live polities at that year, matching the container chain's own open end.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id CHL-AN, admin_name "Antofagasta") found source data reported at the Chilean-region level for Antofagasta with no existing polity able to receive it: the four CHL rows in the table (CHL-1810-1884, CHL-1884-1899, CHL-1899-1902, CHL-1902-2025) are whole-country national totals -- containers for this territory, not substitutes for it -- so routing regional figures to the national row would misstate scope by roughly an order of magnitude. Antofagasta is not among the three country-convention departures (Arica y Parinacota 2007, Los Ríos 2007, Ñuble 2018), so the default rule applies: the region became operational in 1976 under DL 575, and this is a newly created polity with that start year rather than a re-span of an existing one. The harness's retry note recorded that 76 of this unit's 124 data years (61%) were being wrongly marked `unroutable` for years before 1976 -- the wrong disposition where a source is actively reporting values, since that implies the territory existed and was being measured under an earlier administration (the historical province of Antofagasta, itself formed from territory annexed from Bolivia and Peru after the War of the Pacific, 1879-1883/1904). Those pre-1976 years are left as an open question below rather than silently absorbed into this entry, since absorbing them would misstate the 1976 DL-575 span basis. This entry is structurally identical to the sibling verdicts already recorded for other Chilean regions (CHL-AI, CHL-AR, CHL-AT, CHL-CO, CHL-BI, CHL-ML, CHL-MA), all create_new with the same 1900-1975 unroutable / 1976-2025 proposed pattern; the harness's own note additionally flagged that a previous pass of this exact case had wrongly truncated the proposed segment to 2023 (the data's last year) instead of the convention's open 2025, which this entry corrects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached to this entry today, but a registered source can supply one and has not yet been fetched. `polygon_source` is set to `gadm-4.1-adm1` -- the source is registered (GADM 4.1 adm1 is the standing source for first-level administrative regions worldwide, used already for sibling Chilean regions, with id_column GID_1) but the copy of that dataset currently present in this repository (`data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg`) is an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 matching features for `GID_0 == "CHL"`). This is a `registered_source_unfetched` route, not `new_source_needed`: the fix is to replace the local file with the full global GADM 4.1 release, not to register a brand-new source. `polygon_status` is accordingly `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `estimate`, since no geometry -- proxy or otherwise -- has been attached, and no `polygon_area_km2` is stated because none is measured from an attached shape.

**Territory description:** Antofagasta occupies Chile's second-northernmost region, bordered by Bolivia and Argentina to the east across the Andes, the Pacific to the west, Tarapacá region to the north, and Atacama region to the south -- roughly the area a reader would locate between 21°S and 26°S on a modern map of Chile, encompassing the port city of Antofagasta and the mining centers of Calama and Chuquicamata. The region's officially published area is approximately 126,049 km², making it Chile's third-largest region by area and one of the driest inhabited places on Earth (the region contains the hyper-arid core of the Atacama Desert). No km² figure is stated here as a measurement of the (currently absent) attached geometry, since none is attached; the 126,049 km² figure is the region's own officially published area, not derived from any polygon in this repository.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor is recorded here because the pre-1976 territory covering this area -- the historical province of Antofagasta, itself formed from land annexed from Bolivia (definitively by the 1904 Treaty of Peace and Friendship) and administered as a province before the 1976 DL-575 reform -- has not yet been given its own polity row; see the open question below. No successor is recorded because the region remains current through the database's 2025 (exclusive) horizon, with no reorganization since 1976 changing its boundaries or administrative status.

## Sourced claims

- Antofagasta's officially published area is approximately 126,049 km², Chile's third-largest region by area, and the territory was annexed from Bolivia after the War of the Pacific (1879-1883), formalized by the 1904 Treaty of Peace and Friendship between Chile and Bolivia.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Followed the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years code pattern**

57 of 66 subnational rows in the table use <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>. Antofagasta is a standard first-level Chilean administrative region with no historical name of its own distinct from its administrative label (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so the bespoke-code exception does not apply. The code CHL-AN-1976-2025 uses AN as the subunit abbreviation, consistent with ISO 3166-2:CL (CL-AN for Región de Antofagasta) and with the abbreviation pattern already used for sibling regions CHL-AI, CHL-AR, CHL-AT, CHL-CO, CHL-BI, CHL-ML, CHL-MA.

### d-polygon-route

**Declared polygon_source as the registered slug, not the route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched, and per the harness's own correction, registered_source_unfetched entries must set polygon_source to the actual registered slug (gadm-4.1-adm1) with polygon_status unassigned, not to the route name or to new_source_needed/registered_source_unfetched as literal values, both of which are rejected by validate_declared_sources.

### d-no-predecessor

**Left pre-1976 years unrouted rather than absorbing them into this entry**

The harness flagged that 76 of 124 data years (61%) for this unit fall before 1976 and were being wrongly marked unroutable. Rather than silently extending this entry's start_year backward (which would misstate the span_basis tied to DL 575) or absorbing those years without evidence of what earlier unit and boundary covered them, those years are left as an open question for a separate pre-1976 Antofagasta province polity to be created if warranted.

### d-end-year-2025

**Set end_year to 2025, not 2023, matching the container's open end**

The routing decision's own concerns note flagged that a prior version of this case wrongly ran the proposed segment only to 2023 (the data's last year) instead of the country convention's open end_year of 2025. This entry uses 2025 to match the still-open container span CHL-1902-2025 and the treatment of sibling Chilean region entries.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-antofagasta-coverage

**What polity should carry pre-1976 Antofagasta data years?**

The harness reported that 76 of 124 data years for this unit (61%) fall before 1976 and were being marked unroutable, even though the source reports values for those years, implying the territory existed and was being measured under an earlier administrative name (the historical province of Antofagasta, part of the territory annexed from Bolivia after the War of the Pacific and formalized by the 1904 treaty). This entry deliberately does not extend backward to absorb those years, since doing so would misstate its span_basis (tied specifically to the 1976 DL 575 reform) and would need its own confirmation of what unit and boundary applied pre-1976, including whether the 1879-1904 transitional period should be split from the settled post-1904 provincial era. A follow-on predecessor polity, or an extension of this one with a corrected span_basis, should be created once the pre-1976 administrative history and territory are confirmed.

### oq-gadm-vintage-check

**Does the modern GADM Antofagasta boundary match the region's boundary across 1976-2025?**

Once the global GADM 4.1 adm1 layer is fetched and a feature is assigned, its boundary reflects Chile's present-day (post-2018) regional map. Antofagasta itself was not part of the 2007 Arica y Parinacota split or the 2018 Ñuble creation, both of which affected other regions, so vintage risk here is lower than for Tarapacá-adjacent regions; but this should be explicitly checked rather than assumed once the source is fetched, since GADM vintage risk has been documented as a live issue for other Chilean sibling entries (e.g. CHL-AI).
