# Egypt (to 1899)

## Summary

This row tracks Egypt for **100 years**, from the database floor to the
Anglo-Egyptian Condominium over Sudan
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes: "Khedivate
of Egypt including Sudan (conquered 1820-24) and Equatoria. Cliopatria
polygon represents mid-century extent (~1.83M km2). Egypt lost Sudan to
Mahdist revolt by 1885. Pre-1820: Egypt proper only (~1M km2)"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The row spans Ottoman province, Muhammad Ali dynasty, and the Khedivate.
Suez Canal opened 1869. British occupation 1882. The 1899 split marks the
Anglo-Egyptian Condominium agreement over Sudan.

**Overlap warning:** [egy-1800-1922](egy-1800-1922.md) covers the same
start year and extends to 1922. See
[oq-egy-overlap](#oq-egy-overlap).

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor). Successor:
[egy-1899-1925](egy-1899-1925.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** Cliopatria (Muhammad Ali dynasty 1848-1864, ~1.83M
  km2) per the CSV notes
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This represents
  mid-century maximum extent including Sudan.
- **Pre-1820 vs post-1820.** Before the Sudan conquest (1820-24), Egypt
  proper was ~1M km2. After the Mahdist revolt (1885), Sudan was
  effectively lost, reducing the polity back to Egypt proper.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA (pre-database floor). Egypt was an Ottoman province.
- **Successor:** [egy-1899-1925](egy-1899-1925.md) -- Anglo-Egyptian
  Condominium established over Sudan (1899).

## Sourced claims

- **1805: Muhammad Ali rises to power.** Founded a dynasty that ruled
  Egypt until the 1952 revolution.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1820-1824: Conquest of Sudan.** Egypt expanded southward to ~1.83M km2.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1869: Suez Canal opened.** Major geopolitical event increasing
  European interest in Egypt.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1882: British occupation of Egypt.** Following the Urabi revolt,
  Britain occupied Egypt while it remained nominally Ottoman.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1885: Sudan lost to Mahdist revolt.** The fall of Khartoum and death
  of General Gordon. Egypt's effective territory reduced.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1899: Anglo-Egyptian Condominium.** Agreement between Britain and
  Egypt establishing joint administration over Sudan. End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
