# Romania (1920-1940)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **21 years**, Greater Romania at maximum
extent [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Greater Romania ~295,000 km². Lost territory in 1940 via three
simultaneous events: Second Vienna Award (northern Transylvania to
Hungary), Soviet ultimatum (Bessarabia and Northern Bukovina to USSR),
Treaty of Craiova (Southern Dobruja to Bulgaria).

Predecessor: [rou-1919-1920](rou-1919-1920.md) -- border settlement.
Successor: [rou-1940-2025](rou-1940-2025.md) -- reduced Romania.

## Sourced claims

- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** Formalized Transylvania and Banat
  as Romanian territory. This is the start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1940-06-26: Soviet ultimatum.** USSR demanded and received
  Bessarabia and Northern Bukovina (~50,000 km²).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1940-08-30: Second Vienna Award.** Northern Transylvania returned to
  Hungary (~43,000 km²).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1940-09-07: Treaty of Craiova.** Southern Dobruja returned to
  Bulgaria (~7,700 km²). This is the end-of-row event (last of the
  three 1940 losses).
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
