# Paraguay (Chaco War period)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay during the Chaco War (1932–1935) and the interregnum to the Buenos Aires peace treaty (1938-07-21). The CShapes COW-based dataset records Paraguay's territorial change at the treaty date (1938-07-21), not at the 1935 ceasefire. The correct pre-treaty CShapes polygon is the one with start=1886-01-01, end=1938-07-20 (cowcode=150, ~293,510 km²), which faithfully represents Paraguay's borders throughout 1932–1938-07-20.

## Territorial extent

- Polygon source: CShapes 2.0, cowcode=150, CShapes row spanning 1886-01-01–1938-07-20 (pre-treaty polygon).
- Area: ~293,510 km² (equal-area projected; pre-Chaco-War-treaty extent).
- No polygon vintage drift: polygon_feature_year=1886 reflects when CShapes first recorded this polygon, not a territorial change date. The polygon is valid 1886-01-01 to 1938-07-20, exactly covering the 1932–1938 polity span.
- The ~105,000 km² difference between pre- and post-treaty polygons (~293,510 vs ~398,810 km²) represents territory formally awarded to Paraguay by the 1938 Buenos Aires treaty. Any observed step-change in data between PRY-1932-1938 and PRY-1938-2025 is expected and consistent with this expansion signal.
- Known approximation (data territory smaller than polygon): During 1932–1938, Paraguay's productive economic zone was concentrated in the settled eastern region (Región Oriental, ~159,827 km²). The Gran Chaco (~133,683 km² under Paraguayan jurisdiction) was sparsely populated scrubland with negligible agricultural or commercial output. Production and trade data labeled "paraguay" almost certainly reflect eastern Paraguay only (~160,000 km²). Direction: polygon OVERstates the measured economic territory by approximately 1.8×. This cannot be corrected without a sub-national polygon for eastern Paraguay; documented here as a known limitation.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: pry-1870-1932. Split at 1932 = outbreak of Chaco War.
- Successor: pry-1938-2025. Split at 1938 = final Chaco War settlement (Buenos Aires treaty, 1938-07-21).

## Sourced claims

- 1932-06-15: Chaco War begins (Battle of Laguna Chuquisaca). [cow-state-system-v2024]
- 1935-06-12: Ceasefire; Paraguay in de facto control of most disputed Chaco territory.
- 1938-07-21: Buenos Aires peace treaty finalizes borders; Paraguay retains ~75% of Gran Chaco. [cow-state-system-v2024]
- CShapes 2.0 wartime rule: "we also excluded wartime territorial changes, unless they were made permanent in treaties signed after the war" (Schvitz et al. 2022, p.151); the date recorded is the postwar treaty date. [cshapes-2.0]
- CShapes cowcode=150 has two polygon entries: (1) 1886-01-01 to 1938-07-20, area ~293,510 km²; (2) 1938-07-21 to 2019-12-31, area ~398,810 km². [cshapes-2.0]
- Biger 1995: Paraguay total area 157,107 sq mi [406,752 sq km] (modern area). [biger-1995 §paraguay]

- 2026-07-01: Corrected polygon assignment from CShapes 1938 polygon (post-treaty, ~399k km²) to CShapes pre-treaty polygon (1886 vintage, valid 1886-01-01–1938-07-20, ~293,510 km²). The pre-treaty polygon exactly matches this polity's span and correctly represents Paraguay's borders at Chaco War onset. No polygon vintage drift: CShapes records the treaty date as the change date, so the 1886 polygon is valid through 1938-07-20. See issue pry-1932-1938-polygon-vintage-drift.
- 2026-07-01: Confirmed no further polygon change needed (issue pry-1932-1938-polygon-vintage-chaco-war). polygon_feature_year=1886 is not vintage drift — it is the CShapes first-record date for a polygon valid through 1938-07-20. Data-territory approximation documented: polygon (~293,510 km²) overstates measured economic territory (~160,000 km² eastern Paraguay) by ~1.8× due to negligible Chaco agricultural output. This approximation applies equally to PRY-1938-2025 post-treaty (~398,810 km²).
