# Paraguay (Chaco War period)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay during the Chaco War (1932–1935) and the interregnum to the Buenos Aires peace treaty (1938-07-21). The CShapes COW-based dataset records Paraguay's territorial change at the treaty date (1938-07-21), not at the 1935 ceasefire, consistent with the CShapes wartime rule ("wartime territorial changes excluded unless made permanent in postwar treaties"). The correct pre-treaty CShapes polygon is the one with `start=1886-01-01, end=1938-07-20` (cowcode=150, ~293,510 km²), which faithfully represents Paraguay's borders throughout 1932–1938-07-20. Predecessor: pry-1870-1932. Successor: pry-1938-2025.

## Territorial extent

- Polygon source: CShapes 2.0, cowcode=150, CShapes row spanning 1886-01-01–1938-07-20 (pre-treaty polygon).
- Area: ~293,510 km² (equal-area projected; pre-Chaco-War-treaty extent; Paraguay already held the eastern Gran Chaco before 1932).
- The CShapes dataset has two polygons for Paraguay (cowcode=150): one valid 1886-01-01 to 1938-07-20 (~293,510 km²) and one valid 1938-07-21 onward (~398,810 km²). The pre-treaty polygon is the correct match for this polity span (1932–1938).
- No vintage drift: the 1886–1938-07-20 CShapes polygon exactly covers the 1932–1938 polity span. Borders were stable from 1870 to 1932 (identical extent to PRY-1870-1932), and the Chaco War involved disputed territory that CShapes correctly assigns to Paraguay's 1886 baseline (eastern Chaco already Paraguayan; western Chaco added at treaty).
- The ~105,000 km² difference between the pre- and post-treaty polygons (~293,510 vs ~398,810 km²) represents the territory formally awarded to Paraguay by the 1938 Buenos Aires treaty. This territory (Gran Chaco Boreal scrubland) had negligible agricultural production, so data magnitudes are unaffected.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: pry-1870-1932. Split at 1932 = outbreak of Chaco War.
- Successor: pry-1938-2025. Split at 1938 = final Chaco War settlement (Buenos Aires treaty, 1938-07-21).

## Sourced claims

- 1932-06-15: Chaco War begins (Battle of Laguna Chuquisaca). [cow-state-system-v2024]
- 1935-06-12: Ceasefire; Paraguay in de facto control of most disputed Chaco territory.
- 1938-07-21: Buenos Aires peace treaty finalizes borders; Paraguay retains ~75% of Gran Chaco. [cow-state-system-v2024]
- CShapes 2.0 wartime rule: "we also excluded wartime territorial changes, unless they were made permanent in treaties signed after the war" (Schvitz et al. 2022, p.151); the date recorded is the postwar treaty date. [cshapes-2.0]
- CShapes cowcode=150 has two polygon entries: (1) 1886-01-01 to 1938-07-20, area ~293,510 km²; (2) 1938-07-21 to 2019-12-31, area ~398,810 km². [cshapes-2.0]
- Biger 1995: Paraguay total area 157,107 sq mi [406,752 sq km] (modern area). [biger-1995 §paraguay]
