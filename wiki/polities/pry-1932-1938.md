# Paraguay (Chaco War period)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay during and immediately after the Chaco War (1932–1935) through final border settlement (1938). Paraguay gained the bulk of the Gran Chaco from Bolivia; the final extent was codified in the 1938 Buenos Aires peace treaty. CShapes 1938 polygon (cowcode=150, ~406,752 km²) represents post-Chaco borders. Predecessor: pry-1870-1932. Successor: pry-1938-2025.
