# Syria (1922-1945)

## Summary

French Mandate of Syria from the formal establishment of the League of Nations mandate in December 1922 to the end of 1945. "The Conference of San Remo (24 April 1920) established the mandates: Syria and Lebanon under France, Palestine and Mesopotamia under Britain" [biger-1995 §syria](../sources/biger-1995.md#syria). CShapes records area 18.57 sq deg for the period 1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31 (COW 652, status=mandate, owner=France COW 220). Syria formally gained independence on 17 April 1946 when French troops withdrew.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 18.57 sq deg for the period 1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31, status=mandate, owner=France (COW 220). The territory corresponds to the inland area east of Lebanon and north of Palestine/Transjordan. "The Golan Heights ... have been Israeli-occupied Syrian territory since 1967" [biger-1995 §syria](../sources/biger-1995.md#syria). The mandate borders were drawn by the Anglo-French agreements following WWI.

## Open questions

### oq-syr-mandate-internal-divisions

France divided the mandate territory into several sub-states (State of Damascus, State of Aleppo, Alawite State, Jabal Druze). The CShapes polygon treats Syria as a single entity; the internal administrative divisions may be relevant for understanding boundary events.

### oq-syr-1920-1922-gap

The database starts at 1922, but the San Remo Conference was in April 1920. CShapes shows a record from 1920-12-24 to 1922-12-01 (status=mandate). Why does the WHEP row start at 1922 rather than 1920?
