# Haute-Loire

## Summary

Haute-Loire is a département in south-central France (Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region), with its préfecture at Le Puy-en-Velay. It was created in 1790 as one of the original 83 Revolutionary départements, formed mainly from the eastern part of the former province of Auvergne (the Velay and part of the Gévaudan). This entry captures it as an ordinary interior département with no distinctive administrative history of its own: it was not among the four documented departures the country convention lists for FRA subnational rows (Belfort, which split from Haut-Rhin in 1871-1922; the Alsace-Lorraine départements, annexed 1871-1919; Corsica, split into two départements in 1975; and the overseas départements, created 1946). Because Haute-Loire tracks the default rule, type: subnational is used to keep it out of the national FRA family/sovereignty ranking while still carrying département-level statistical series that the national FRA rows are too coarse to host. The entry starts in 1800 rather than 1790 (the département's actual creation year) because 1800 is the earliest year for which any FRA polity row exists in this database to serve as a container -- the routing decision's proposed 1790 start cannot be tiled by a container edge and is addressed in decisions below.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a France-country statistical extract carries a "FRK13" (NUTS/INSEE-style) admin unit labelled Haute-Loire, with values reported across roughly 1862-2023 (per the routing decision's admin_name FRK13). No row count was supplied in the routing payload beyond the reasoning text, but the unit is a standard, single, first-level administrative division (INSEE département code 43). Previously this data would have been routed to the national row FRA-1919-2025 or FRA-1800-1919, which cover the whole of metropolitan and (post-1919) reunified France -- territory roughly 130-200x larger than a single département, discarding the sub-national resolution the source data actually carries. Historical/administrative confirmation of distinctness: Haute-Loire has been a continuously named, continuously bounded first-level administrative unit of France since the 1790 Revolutionary reorganization, with its own INSEE code (43) and préfecture (Le Puy-en-Velay), used as the unit of account in French national statistics (INSEE, historical agricultural and population censuses) independently of any other département -- it is not a subdivision alias of a neighbouring unit or of the national total.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Start year set to 1800, not the routing decision's proposed 1790**

The routing decision (proposed.start_year: 1790) followed the country convention's default rule for a département not among the four listed FRA departures, reasoning from Haute-Loire's actual 1790 creation as a Revolutionary département. This page instead starts the entry at 1800, because the containment gate requires container edges to tile the entry's whole span with no gap, and every existing FRA polity row (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1800-1919, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) begins at 1800 -- there is no FRA container available for 1790-1800. Rather than leave a decade uncontained or invent a container row outside this task's scope, the span was trimmed to 1800-2025, tiled exactly by FRA-1800-1871 -> FRA-1871-1919 -> FRA-1919-2025. The administrative-history justification for an early start still holds for 1790-1800, but is left as an open question rather than acted on here, since creating or extending a national FRA container is a separate change with its own consequences for every other FRA subnational row.
