# Ryukyu Islands / Okinawa Prefecture (Japanese)

## Summary

This row tracks the **Ryukyu Islands** (Okinawa Prefecture) under
**Japanese administration**, covering the FAO 1952 dataset years
**1937–1944** — the pre-surrender sub-period for which FAO records
labeled "Ryukyu Islands" exist. The Ryukyus were incorporated into the
Japanese Empire as Okinawa Prefecture in 1879; by 1937 the prefecture
had been part of Japan for nearly 60 years.

**Why this entry exists.** The FAO 1952 dataset contains 58 rows
labeled "Ryukyu Islands" for years 1937–1951. The 1937–1944 rows fall
within the span of [jpn-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md) (the Japanese
Empire, ~658,000 km²), but the Ryukyu Islands sub-territory is
~2,271 km² — approximately 290× smaller than the empire polygon. The
Ryukyu Islands data measures **Okinawa Prefecture production only** and
must NOT be routed to the empire aggregate. A granular polity is
required.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 administrative level-1 feature `JPN.32_1`
(Okinawa Prefecture, Japan). Used as a **period proxy** for Okinawa
Prefecture under Japanese administration. The modern Okinawa Prefecture
boundary is spatially identical to the historical prefecture; the islands
did not change shape during 1937–1945.

**Area.** ~2,271 km² (Okinawa Prefecture land area). This is the core
Ryukyu chain (Okinawa group, Miyako Islands, Yaeyama Islands). The
Amami Islands are administratively in Kagoshima Prefecture today (and
historically were a separate sub-prefectural unit); the GADM Okinawa
Prefecture polygon excludes Amami, which is appropriate for the
Okinawa-specific data in the FAO dataset.

**Polygon status: proxy.** Modern GADM Okinawa Prefecture boundary used
for the historical Okinawa Prefecture under Japanese rule. The spatial
extent is the same; "proxy" rather than "estimate" reflects that the
geography is unchanged — only the current vs. historical administrative
framework differs. CShapes 2.0 has no separate Ryukyu Islands feature
(it folds the territory into Japan cowcode 740). GADM adm-1 is the best
available proxy at step 3 of the priority chain (exact historical GIS >
composed union > period proxy).

**Relationship to JPN-1895-1945.** This polity is a sub-territory of
the Japanese Empire. Routing "Ryukyu Islands" data to JPN-1895-1945
would overstate ~290× (2,271 km² vs ~658,000 km²). The sub-territory
rule requires a granular polity.

## Decisions

- **Start year 1937**, not 1879 (Ryukyu Disposition) or 1895 (Japanese
  Empire row start). The FAO data starts at 1937; no earlier
  "Ryukyu Islands"-labeled data has been identified. If earlier data is
  found, the start year should be extended.
- **Polygon: GADM 4.1 `JPN.32_1` (proxy).** Same polygon as
  [ryu-1945-1972](ryu-1945-1972.md). No exact historical GIS source
  available; GADM adm-1 is the best available proxy.
- **Predecessor set to JPN-1895-1945** (empire aggregate), not a
  separate Okinawa-prefecture-only predecessor chain. Okinawa was
  continuously administered as a Japanese prefecture from 1879; the
  empire aggregate is the correct parent.

## Open questions

### oq-ryu-pre-1937-data

If FAO or other sources contain "Ryukyu Islands" or "Okinawa Prefecture"
data for years before 1937, the start year of this row should be
extended. No such data has been identified as of 2026-06-24.

### oq-ryu-amami-coverage

The Amami Islands were part of Okinawa Prefecture until the 1953
reversion. The GADM JPN.32_1 polygon excludes Amami (Kagoshima
Prefecture today). If FAO data for "Ryukyu Islands" 1937–1944 includes
Amami production, the polygon slightly understates the territory. This
is noted as a known approximation; no correction is attempted without
data-magnitude evidence.
