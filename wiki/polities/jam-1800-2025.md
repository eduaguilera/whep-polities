# Jamaica

## Summary

Jamaica is an island country in the Caribbean Sea with no land boundaries. Biger 1995 focuses on international land boundaries and therefore provides only brief coverage of island states without land boundaries [biger-1995 &sect;jamaica](../sources/biger-1995.md#jamaica). Jamaica was a Spanish colony from 1509 until captured by Britain in 1655, and remained a British colony until independence on 6 August 1962. WHEP tracks Jamaica as a continuous row from 1800 because its island territory has remained constant throughout.
