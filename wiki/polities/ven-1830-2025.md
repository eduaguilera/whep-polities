# Venezuela

## Summary

This row tracks Venezuela from **1830** to **2025** — a **195-year
continuous row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW: VEN, ccode=101.

> **Start corrected 1821 -> 1830, and the code with it (was `VEN-1821-2025`), on 2026-09-24.**
> The row used to begin in 1821 while its own text said Venezuelan independence from Gran
> Colombia was "formalized in 1830". For 1821-1829 it therefore overlapped
> [COL-1800-1830](col-1800-1830.md) (Gran Colombia), whose Cliopatria polygon contains
> **902,110 km²** of this row's 910,634 km² — the same ground claimed twice, by a union and by
> one of its own members. Venezuela was a department of Gran Colombia from the 1819 Congress of
> Angostura (Carabobo, 1821, completed its liberation from Spain) until the Congress of Valencia
> separated it in 1830, and the 1830 constitution made the secession formal. With
> `end_year` exclusive, the first year it is a state of its own is 1830. No alias routed any
> pre-1830 year here, so no data moves.

Predecessor: [col-1800-1830](col-1800-1830.md) — Gran Colombia, from which Venezuela seceded in 1830.
Successor: NA (ongoing).
