# Gabon

## Summary

Independent Gabon from 17 August 1960. Area 103,346 sq. miles. Capital Libreville. Bounded by Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, and Congo.

## Territorial extent

Gabon is located in Central Africa on the Atlantic coast. Bounded by Equatorial Guinea and Cameroon to the north and Congo to the east and south. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
