# Burundi

## Summary

Burundi is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Burundi from 1962 to the present. Ruanda-Urundi became two separate nations on 1 July 1962, with Ruanda renamed the Republic of Rwanda and Urundi becoming independent as Burundi.

## Territorial extent

Burundi is a landlocked Central African country bounded by Uganda, Tanzania, Zaire (now DRC), and Rwanda. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

- "Colonized by the Germans in the late nineteenth century, the region, along with neighboring Burundi, was conquered by the Belgians in World War I" (p.453). Catholic missionaries eventually persuaded the colonial authorities to transfer their support to the majority Hutu. [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- Rwanda (area 10,169 sq. miles) is bounded by Uganda (105 miles; 169 km) in the north, Tanzania (135 miles; 217 km) in the east, Burundi (180 miles; 290 km) in the south, and Zaire (135 miles; 217 km) in the west. The Zaire entry confirms Burundi borders Zaire with 145 miles (233 km). [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)

### oq-hutu-tutsi-boundary-impact

Ethnic conflict and boundary impact. Biger notes the 1963 Tutsi refugee invasion from Uganda and the 1994 civil war that sent hundreds of thousands of Rwandan refugees into Zaire. Whether any of these events affected Burundi's territorial extent needs investigation.
