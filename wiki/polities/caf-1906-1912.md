# Central African Republic (1906-1912)

## Summary

The French colonial territory of Oubangui-Chari-Tchad in its 1906-1912 configuration, covering the period 11 February 1906 to 29 March 1912. "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were united in 1906" [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). CShapes records area 50.60 sq deg for this period (COW 482, status=colony, owner=France COW 220). The 1912 end date corresponds to the reorganisation of French Equatorial Africa that reduced the territory's area to 40.57 sq deg, detaching the northern portion that became separate Chad. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area changes at both endpoints.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 50.60 sq deg for the period 1906-02-11 to 1912-03-29, status=colony, owner=France (COW 220). The successor CAF-1912-1919 shows a reduced area of 40.57 sq deg after the northern territory was reorganised into Chad. Bounded by Chad to the north, Sudan to the east, Congo and Zaire to the south, and Cameroon to the west. The combined Oubangui-Chari-Tchad territory was part of French Equatorial Africa (AEF).
