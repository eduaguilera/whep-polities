# Bali (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Bali, "the westernmost of the Lesser Sunda Islands", "East of Java and west of Lombok", with Nusa Penida and its neighbours. One of the Outer Provinces ([idn-out-1800-1949](idn-out-1800-1949.md)). [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §bali](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#bali)

## Why this entry exists

The label `dutch bali` reports it alone for 1934-1938 to 1948-1950 (**7 (year, label) pairs**), beside `dutch east indies` and its other islands. No polity was this island before 1949; the 1949-1951 rows (IDN-JVM, IDN-BLB, IDN-OTH) group islands differently and begin in 1949. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
