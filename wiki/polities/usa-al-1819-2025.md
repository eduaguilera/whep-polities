# Alabama (state of the United States)

## Summary

Alabama, one of the fifty states of the United States, admitted to the Union in 1819 out of the eastern part of the former Mississippi Territory. This entry is `type: subnational` because it does not compete with the national USA chain for sovereignty: Alabama has never been independently sovereign, and the row exists purely to carry state-level reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the state rather than the national level. The span 1819-2025 tracks continuous statehood, with no interruption or territorial change, through four successive eras of the national USA polity chain (USA-1803-1848 through USA-1959-2025).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Alabama as a distinct subnational reporting unit within the United States, created by the agent-harness pipeline processing the administrative unit `USA-ALABAMA` ("Alabama", country USA). No prior polity row existed for Alabama at the state level -- the existing USA chain (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) covers the country as a whole, so any Alabama-specific statistics would previously have had no state-level home and would either be absent from the database or folded into national USA totals, which is a mismatch whenever a source reports Alabama separately from all-U.S. figures. The routing decision does not name a specific source, row count, or year range of data driving this creation (unlike, e.g., the Manchuria exemplar, which cites 20 rows across two named sources) -- its routing_reasoning instead frames this as adding the state as a real, distinct administrative entity with no existing candidate polity, consistent with a systematic pass creating one row per U.S. state. What confirms Alabama is a distinct reporting/administrative entity is uncontroversial: it is one of the fifty states, with its own government and long-standing use as a state-level reporting unit in economic and demographic series (e.g., USDA, Census Bureau, and Mitchell-style historical compilations). The open question below flags that the specific data motivating this row's creation was not supplied in the routing decision and should be traced.

**Area:** measured from the attached geometry at approximately 135,767 km2 (Alabama's commonly cited land+water area is about 135,767 km2 / 52,420 sq mi). This is a measurement of the attached polygon, consistent with Alabama's real-world extent, not an independent territorial claim.

**Territory description:** Alabama occupies the southeastern United States, bordered by Tennessee to the north, Georgia to the east, Florida and a short Gulf of Mexico coastline to the south, and Mississippi to the west -- readily locatable on any modern map of the U.S. as the state directly north of Mobile Bay. Its external boundaries have not changed since admission in 1819 (no subsequent territorial cessions or annexations), so the modern GADM boundary is treated as valid for the full 1819-2025 span, per the routing decision's vintage-risk assessment ("Low ... Alabama's borders have been fixed since admission to the Union"). This claim of boundary stability since 1819 has not been independently corroborated against a historical source in authoring this page; see the open question on vintage risk.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Alabama is a standard U.S. state with no distinct historical name of its own (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so it follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows: ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The subunit token chosen is AL, the USPS/ISO 3166-2:US code for Alabama, matching the two-letter convention already used for the Spanish provinces added in this same harness run (esp-as, esp-ca, esp-lu, esp-or, esp-po). The full code is USA-AL-1819-2025.

### d-start-year-admission

**Start year is 1819 admission to the Union, per the country default rule**

The country convention's default rule for U.S. states is to start at formal admission to the Union unless the state is a departure case (Alaska, Hawaii, or a territory with a distinct pre-statehood identity already carrying its own polity row). Alabama Territory (1817-1819) is not itself a polity row in this database, and the routing decision explicitly found no departure reason, so 1819 is used directly rather than back-cast to an earlier territorial period.

### d-four-container-edges

**Four container edges tile 1819-2025 across the successive national USA rows**

The national USA chain in the polity table is split into five rows by era: USA-1800-1803, USA-1803-1848, USA-1848-1867, USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025. Alabama's 1819 start falls inside the second of these, so the container list uses four edges (skipping USA-1800-1803 entirely) to cover the full 1819-2025 span with no gap, each edge trimmed to the intersection of Alabama's span and that national row's own span.
