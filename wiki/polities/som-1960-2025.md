# Somalia

## Summary

Somalia is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of unified Somalia from 1960 to the present. British Somaliland became independent on 26 June 1960, merging with Italian Somaliland to form a united Somalia on 1 July 1960.

## Territorial extent

Somalia is located in the Horn of Africa, bounded by Djibouti, Ethiopia, and Kenya. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "France signed commercial treaties with local chiefs of the northeast African coast in 1862, and a French protectorate was established on the African coast of the Gulf of Aden in 1884-1885, which was named French Somaliland in 1896" (p.191). Italy occupied the southwestern coast of the Red Sea in 1889. [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
- The Djibouti-Somalia boundary runs for 36 miles (58 km) in a straight line following the colonial boundary between the earlier British and French possessions in Africa's eastern projection. "In 1960, Italian Somaliland united with British Somaliland and became independent as Somalia" (p.192). [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
- The Kenya-Somalia boundary runs for 424 miles (682 km). The boundary history notes that "from 1889 Italy made various claims to the Indian Ocean coast of Somalia" and in 1891 an Anglo-Italian treaty established respective spheres of influence (p.337-338). [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya)
- **Somali Democratic Republic formed 1958** (p.471). Unification aim encouraged wars with Ethiopia 1963, 1977-1978 (p.471). Siad Barre fell 1990; de facto division; Somaliland self-proclaimed 1991 (p.472). [biger-1995 §somalia](../sources/biger-1995.md#somalia)

### oq-somaliland-de-facto

Somaliland de facto independence. The former British Somaliland declared independence from Somalia in 1991 but is not internationally recognized. The WHEP row treats Somalia as a single continuous polity.
