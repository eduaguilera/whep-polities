# Nièvre

## Summary

This entry represents Nièvre, a French département in the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region, in the historic province of Nivernais, centred on the Loire valley town of Nevers. It is coded as a `subnational` polity because a département is an administrative subdivision of France, not a sovereign state or a distinct internationally-recognised territory -- France's sovereignty over this area is continuous and undisputed throughout the entry's span, and the national FRA rows in this table already carry that sovereignty claim for the whole country. This row exists solely to carry a *sub-national reporting territory*: statistics keyed to "Nièvre" or its Eurostat NUTS3 code FRC12 specifically, as opposed to all-France totals, need somewhere to route that isn't the national polygon. The entry follows the country convention's default start rule (1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform) as adjusted here to 1800 to match the earliest available French national container row (see decisions), and runs to the open end_year 2025 since the département remains an active administrative unit of France today with no successor.

**Why this entry exists.** This page implements the routing decision for unit FRA-FRC12 (admin_name "FRC12", country France), which resolved via create_new because no existing FRA polity IS this territory -- the national FRA rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) describe the whole country, and a département cannot be represented by its country's row. Before this decision the data for this unit was, by construction of the routing pipeline, unmatched rather than misrouted -- match_existing was excluded outright since no existing row's territory equals Nièvre's. What confirms Nièvre as a distinct, statistically-reported entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision is that it is one of the original ~83 départements created by the March 1790 Revolutionary reform and has been administered, and tabulated in French statistical yearbooks (INSEE and its predecessors, and Eurostat's NUTS3 series under FRC12) as a discrete unit under the numeric code 58 ever since -- the same status that INSEE gives every other département in the country's statistical apparatus. This entry's span (1790-2025, adjusted here to 1800-2025, see decisions) covers the full administrative existence rather than being trimmed to any narrower data span, so a single create_new polity suffices with no back_cast or matched segments.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision already established this is a `new_source_needed` case: no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements. gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated subset that omits France entirely (even though GADM's global release does cover French départements); cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe are country-level only and cannot supply a sub-national boundary. The standard remedy would be to register GADM's global admin-2 layer or French IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS product, neither of which is on disk in this repository today.

**Territory description:** Nièvre occupies the western part of the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region, in the historic province of Nivernais. On a modern map it is bounded by Loiret to the northwest, Yonne to the north, Côte-d'Or to the east, Saône-et-Loire to the southeast, Allier to the south, and Cher to the west; its administrative seat (préfecture) is Nevers, on the Loire. It covers approximately 6,817 km2 -- this figure is a published administrative-area statistic for the modern département, not a measurement from any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned (see polygon status above). The département straddles the Loire valley in the west and the wooded, hilly Morvan massif in the east, and its economy has historically combined cereal farming and cattle grazing with iron and metallurgical industry (notably at Nevers and Fourchambault) and canal transport (Canal du Nivernais, Canal latéral à la Loire).

## Predecessors and successors

Nièvre has no predecessor or successor polity in this table: it was created directly by the March 1790 Revolutionary départements reform (predating this database's coverage, see the open question on the 1790-1800 gap) and has continued as an administrative unit of France without interruption through 2025, so both fields are empty. It is not known to have been split off from, merged into, or ceded to any other polity tracked here, and it was not among the départements affected by the 1871 Alsace-Lorraine cession or its 1919 return, being located in interior Burgundy far from that frontier.

- The routing decision for unit FRA-FRC12 states Nièvre is not among the 4 listed departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corse, overseas départements) in the country convention, so the default 1790 start rule applies to it rather than a bespoke span.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790, because no container exists for the earlier decade**

The routing decision's span_basis argues for 1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform that created Nièvre. That is very likely correct as administrative history, but this database's earliest French national row is FRA-1800-1871 -- there is no FRA row of any kind covering 1790-1800, and the containment gate rejects an edge that falls outside either party's span. Rather than leave a decade uncontained, or invent a container edge citing a national polity that does not exist in the table, I moved start_year to 1800 to match the earliest available container, exactly as sibling entries FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher) and FRA-77-1800-2025 (Seine-et-Marne) already did for the same reason. The polity code year (1800) and the frontmatter start_year (1800) are kept equal, satisfying the gate that ties code years to frontmatter years. If a pre-1800 French national polity is added later, this entry's start_year should be revisited alongside it -- see the open question below.

### d-subunit-code-58-not-frc12

**Subunit code is the INSEE/ISO 3166-2 numeric code 58, not the routing decision's admin_name FRC12**

The dominant pattern for subnational rows is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and the routing decision this page implements labels the unit 'FRC12', a Eurostat NUTS3 statistical code for a Bourgogne-Franche-Comté département. France's départements do not have letter-based ISO 3166-2 codes in general public use, and NUTS3 codes are a Eurostat statistical construct rather than how French sources or the public identify a département. The standard identifier for Nièvre, used by INSEE, La Poste, and ISO 3166-2:FR alike, is the two-digit number 58. I used FRA-58-1800-2025 rather than the NUTS3 code, matching the convention already set by sibling entries FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher, from NUTS3 FRB01) and FRA-21-1800-2025 (Côte-d'Or, from NUTS3 FRC11), since 58 is the identifier a reader would actually recognise on a map, car plate, or postcode prefix (58xxx).

### d-polygon-unassigned-not-proxy

**Polygon left unassigned rather than proxied from a neighbouring département or the national polygon**

The routing decision already worked out that no registered source in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at the resolution this entry needs -- gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated subset that excludes FRA entirely, and cshapes-europe/cshapes-2.0 are country-level only. I did not attempt a coarser proxy (e.g. the national FRA polygon, or a hand-drawn union of neighbouring départements) because the whole point of this row is to isolate Nièvre's own reporting territory from the national one; substituting the national shape would defeat that purpose silently. polygon_source is set to the 'none' sentinel used by other unassigned rows in this table (e.g. sibling FRA-18-1800-2025, FRA-21-1800-2025) rather than a placeholder string, matching existing convention.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap-uncovered

**The 1790-1800 decade of Nièvre's existence is not represented by this entry or any container**

Nièvre was created in March 1790 by the Revolutionary départements reform, carved from the former province of Nivernais, ten years before this entry's start_year of 1800. That gap exists because no FRA national polity row in this database covers 1790-1800 -- the earliest is FRA-1800-1871. If a future entry adds an FRA-1790-1800 (or similar) national row to close that gap, this entry's start_year should be pulled back to 1790 to match, with a corresponding container edge added. Until then, any data keyed to Nièvre in 1790-1799 has nowhere to route. The same open question already exists on sibling entries FRA-18-1800-2025 and FRA-21-1800-2025, so a fix would likely apply to several France subnational rows at once rather than this one alone.

### oq-departement-boundary-stability-unverified

**Nièvre's boundary stability across 1800-2025 is asserted from general knowledge, not checked against a source**

The routing decision's span_basis claims Nièvre is not among the four departure cases (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corse, overseas départements) listed in the country convention, and so follows the default 1790 rule with an unbroken existence, but this is general knowledge of French administrative geography rather than a cited source. Whether Nièvre's perimeter has been fully stable since 1790 -- including any minor commune-level boundary adjustments with neighbouring Cher, Allier, Saône-et-Loire, Yonne, and Loiret -- has not been verified here. When a polygon source is finally registered (see polygon status), its boundary vintage should be checked against a specific year rather than assumed identical to the present-day shape for the whole 1800-2025 span.

### oq-nuts-vs-insee-code-mismatch

**The routing decision's admin_name (FRC12, a NUTS3 code) does not match the INSEE code (58) used as this entry's subunit identifier**

The decision this page implements labels the unit 'FRC12', a Eurostat NUTS3 statistical code for a Bourgogne-Franche-Comté département, while this entry uses INSEE/ISO 3166-2:FR numeric code 58 as its polity_code subunit (see decisions). I have treated FRC12 and INSEE 58 as denoting the same territory (Nièvre) based on standard NUTS-to-département crosswalks, but this crosswalk has not been independently verified against an authoritative Eurostat correspondence table within this pipeline run. If the source data's FRC12 rows in fact refer to a different Bourgogne-Franche-Comté département (the region also contains Côte-d'Or, Doubs, Haute-Saône, Jura, Saône-et-Loire, Territoire de Belfort, and Yonne, several of which are NUTS3-adjacent in code order), this entry would be misrouted.
