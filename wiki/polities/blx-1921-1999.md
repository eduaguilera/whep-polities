# Belgium-Luxembourg Economic Union

> **RETIRED 2026-08-05 — duplicate of [blx-1850-1999](blx-1850-1999.md) (issue 40).**
>
> Both rows exist for the same purpose: to receive series that report Belgium and Luxembourg
> as one unit. Measured, they are the same territory:
>
> ```
> BLX-1850-1999   33,344 km²      BEL + LUX = 33,283 km²
> BLX-1921-1999   33,305 km²      the two overlap by 32,925 km² = 98.7%
> ```
>
> `BLX-1850-1999` is kept, on three grounds:
>
> | | `BLX-1921-1999` (retired) | `BLX-1850-1999` (kept) |
> |---|---|---|
> | observed rows | **0** | **156,557** via FAOSTAT area 15 |
> | iso3 | `BEL` — **wrong**, this row is not Belgium | `BLX`, a local code for the unit |
> | convention | one-off | matches nine sibling reporting-area rows (`ROW`, `RAFR`, `REUR`, `RASI`, `RLAM`, `RNAM`, `ROCE`, `ANT-1961-2010`) |
>
> The wrong `iso3` was not cosmetic: it put this row in **Belgium's** ISO family, where it
> tied with `BEL-1831-2025` at a 1.085× area ratio, so `matchlib.pick_by_year`'s national
> preference discriminated nothing and family order decided whether data labelled "Belgium"
> reached Belgium or the union. Retyping it `aggregate` (#116) demoted it below Belgium;
> retiring it removes the family tie altogether.
>
> **A correction I owe this page.** I first argued the opposite — that `BLX-1850-1999`'s 1850
> start was "asserting something no source supports", since the BLEU was founded in 1921.
> That was wrong, and reading the page would have prevented it: `BLX-1850-1999` states it is
> "not a country row; it is a data reporting unit", and its 1850-1999 span "follows the
> current WHEP primary-production and land-footprint rows". It never claimed the union
> existed in 1850. All nine reporting-area rows use exactly that convention.
>
> **What is kept from this page:** the historical fact below that the BLEU began in **1921**,
> which the surviving reporting-area row does not record. That is the one thing this row knew
> that the other does not.


> **`type` corrected `national` -> `aggregate` on 2026-08-05 (issues 115, 44).** The Belgium-Luxembourg Economic Union is an aggregate over two states, and THIS ROW'S OWN SIBLING says so: BLX-1850-1999 ("Belgium-Luxembourg") is already typed `aggregate`. One union, two rows, two different types. Typed `national` with iso3 `BEL`, this row tied with BEL-1831-2025 at a 1.085x area ratio, so family order decided whether "Belgium" data reached Belgium or the union.
>
> `matchlib.pick_by_year` resolves a label to an ISO family and then prefers
> `polity_type == "national"`. When both candidates are national the preference
> discriminates nothing and the winner falls to family order, which is arbitrary.
## Summary

A **composed-union** row for series that report **Belgium and Luxembourg as a
single unit** — the Belgium-Luxembourg Economic Union (BLEU, 1921 onward), a
customs and monetary union under which much interwar and post-war trade and
production data was published jointly. FAO/IIA **footnotes** flag Luxembourg as
included under Belgium. A figure so labelled covers both and must not map to
Belgium alone. Constituents: [BEL-1831-2025](bel-1831-2025.md) and
[LUX-1839-2025](lux-1839-2025.md).

Period 1921–1999 spans BLEU from its founding to the eve of the euro /
single-market dissolution of the separate union; pre-1961 WHEP data is the
primary consumer.
