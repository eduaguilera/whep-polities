# Jordan

## Summary

Jordan as tracked by WHEP from 1918, following the end of Ottoman rule. The area had no specific regional identity until Britain established Transjordan in 1922, carved from the British mandate over Palestine. Jordan annexed the West Bank after the 1948 war but lost it to Israel in 1967.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Jordan is bounded by Syria (233 mi; 375 km), Iraq (111 mi; 179 km), Saudi Arabia (461 mi; 742 km), and Israel (165 mi; 266 km), with a small outlet to the Red Sea at Aqaba.
