## Summary

French Congo (Moyen-Congo) in its 1906-1912 configuration, part of French Equatorial Africa (AEF). "Congo became independent on 15 August 1960" [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). CShapes records this as a distinct boundary period between the COG-1900-1906 and COG-1912-1919 rows. The boundary changes at 1906 and 1912 correspond to administrative reorganisations within AEF, particularly the creation and restructuring of constituent territories. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area changes at both endpoints.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. The territory of French Congo (Moyen-Congo) was bounded by Gabon, Cameroon, the Congo Free State / Belgian Congo, and Oubangui-Chari. Gabon is bounded by Congo for 1,183 miles (1,903 km) [biger-1995 §gabon](../sources/biger-1995.md#gabon).

### oq-cog-1906-1912-reorganisation
