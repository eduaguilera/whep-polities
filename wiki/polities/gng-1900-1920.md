# German New Guinea (Kaiser-Wilhelmsland, Bismarck Archipelago, North Solomons)

## Summary

The 'Old Protectorate' of German New Guinea: Kaiser-Wilhelmsland (a protectorate from 1884), the Bismarck Archipelago and the North Solomons (Bougainville and Buka), from the 1899-1900 partition of the Solomons until the 1920 League mandate (TNGU-1920-1949); under Australian military occupation from 1914. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §german-new-guinea](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#german-new-guinea)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report German New Guinea for 1909-1917 (`german new guinea`, 9 pairs); no row covered it before the 1920 mandate. The family-extension route to Papua (TPAP) was withdrawn in PR #707. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** CShapes 2.0 feature 912 at its 1920-1949 step, TNGU-1920-1949's polygon: the mandate was exactly this territory. CShapes' own 1900-1920 steps for 912 measure 228,300 km2 and leave out Bougainville and Buka (about 9,500 km2), which were German until 1914, so the later step is the better outline (polygon vintage is allowed to differ from the row's years). The island possessions administered from Rabaul (the Carolines, Palau, the Marianas, the Marshalls, Nauru) are not part of this row: the label list reports them separately. Measured: **237,536 km2** (ESRI:54034).
