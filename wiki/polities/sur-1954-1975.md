# Surinam (Autonomous) (SUR-1954-1975)

## Summary

On 25 November 1954, the Charter for the Kingdom of the Netherlands came into effect, granting Suriname autonomy as a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands. Suriname gained full independence on 25 November 1975, at which point this polity ends and SUR-1975-2025 begins.

## Territorial extent

The territory is ~144,985 km², identical to the colonial predecessor SUR-1886-1954 and the independent successor SUR-1975-2025. CShapes 2.0 feature FID 56 records this period from 1954-12-15 to 1975-11-24. The territory was fully stable throughout this period — no boundary changes occurred between colonial autonomy and independence.
