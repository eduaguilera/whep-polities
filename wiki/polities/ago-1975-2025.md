# Angola (independent, 1975–2025)

## Summary

Independent Angola from 11 November 1975 (independence from Portugal under the Alvor Agreement) to the current database ceiling. The People's Republic of Angola (later renamed Republic of Angola in 1992) inherited the territorial boundaries of Portuguese Angola intact; no external border changes occurred at independence or subsequently. The Cabinda exclave in the north is included.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 feature 540 at the 1975 vintage (~1,246,700 km²). Boundaries unchanged from colonial period. Polygon confidence: high. Pre-1975 data must NOT be routed here; route to ANG-1905-1975 (1905-1975) or earlier ANG-* polities.
