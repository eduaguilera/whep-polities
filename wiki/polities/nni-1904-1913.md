# Northern Nigeria (1904-1913)


> **A spurious predecessor was removed on 2026-08-05, and how it survived is worth recording.**
>
> This page listed `NWR-1900-1905` — **Northwestern Rhodesia**, in present-day Zambia — as a
> partial predecessor of Northern Nigeria. Measured centroids are 26.52°E, 14.90°S against
> 8.42°E, 10.55°N: about **3,467 km** apart, sharing no boundary at any date. The codes were
> almost certainly confused for their initials.
>
> **It survived because the gate written to catch it could not see it.**
> `validate_succession_geography` exists to flag succession links whose polygons do not touch,
> and its docstring names this very pair. But its `parse_links()` split chain fields on `,`
> alone, while `build_database.py` joins multiple values with `"; "` — so
> `predecessor: [NNI-1899-1904, NWR-1900-1905]` became one unresolvable token, which the loop
> skipped **silently**. The check failed *open*: 86 fields contain a semicolon, hiding 145 of
> 1,054 links, 13.8% of its own input. Fixed the same day, together with making an unresolvable
> token a hard failure rather than a skip.
>
> **And the error had been laundered into prose that cited the database as its source.** The
> Predecessors section read *"CSV notes NWR-1900-1905 as a predecessor to NNI-1904-1913,
> suggesting some territory transferred"* — but the CSV is built FROM this page's frontmatter, so
> the page was citing itself as evidence, and two further paragraphs then explained a CShapes
> area change as "separation of territory that became Northwestern Rhodesia", which is
> geographically impossible. All three passages are corrected below.
## Summary

Northern Nigeria (1904-1913) tracks the British Protectorate of Northern Nigeria from the consolidation of British control in 1904 until amalgamation with Southern Nigeria to form the Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria on 1 January 1914. The protectorate was established by Frederick Lugard following the conquest of the Sokoto Caliphate in 1903. CShapes records cowcode 4784 (Northern Nigeria) with a territorial change at 1904-04-08: area drops from ~60.16 to ~54.59 sq deg, an administrative boundary adjustment of undocumented cause. The WHEP row begins in 1904, corresponding to this CShapes area change. The predecessor row NNI-1899-1904 covers the earlier period of Northern Nigeria.

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-mismatch

The CSV records cow=385 for NNI-1904-1913, but CShapes 2.0 uses cowcode 4784 for Northern Nigeria (a dependency-mode entry). COW code 385 is the code for Nigeria as an independent state (from 1960). The use of 385 for colonial Northern Nigeria in the CSV may be a legacy assignment or a COW colonial-period code. This should be verified against the COW state-system and colonial/dependency lists.

### oq-1904-area-change

CShapes shows a Northern Nigeria area change at 1904-04-08 (from ~60.16 to ~54.59 sq deg). The nature of this territorial change is not documented in currently ingested sources. It is
NOT a separation of territory to Northwestern Rhodesia — that reading came from a spurious
predecessor link removed on 2026-08-05, and the two territories are ~3,467 km apart. A British colonial records source would clarify this.
