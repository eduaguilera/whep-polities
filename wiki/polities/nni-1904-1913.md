# Northern Nigeria (1904-1913)

## Summary

Northern Nigeria (1904-1913) tracks the British Protectorate of Northern Nigeria from the consolidation of British control in 1904 until amalgamation with Southern Nigeria to form the Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria on 1 January 1914. The protectorate was established by Frederick Lugard following the conquest of the Sokoto Caliphate in 1903. CShapes records cowcode 4784 (Northern Nigeria) with a territorial change at 1904-04-08: area drops from ~60.16 to ~54.59 sq deg, reflecting the separation of territory that became Northwestern Rhodesia or an administrative boundary adjustment. The WHEP row begins in 1904, corresponding to this CShapes area change. The predecessor row NNI-1899-1904 covers the earlier period of Northern Nigeria.

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-mismatch

The CSV records cow=385 for NNI-1904-1913, but CShapes 2.0 uses cowcode 4784 for Northern Nigeria (a dependency-mode entry). COW code 385 is the code for Nigeria as an independent state (from 1960). The use of 385 for colonial Northern Nigeria in the CSV may be a legacy assignment or a COW colonial-period code. This should be verified against the COW state-system and colonial/dependency lists.

### oq-1904-area-change

CShapes shows a Northern Nigeria area change at 1904-04-08 (from ~60.16 to ~54.59 sq deg). The nature of this territorial change — whether it reflects a boundary adjustment, separation of territory to Northwestern Rhodesia, or an administrative reorganization — is not documented in currently ingested sources. A British colonial records source would clarify this.
