# Iceland


> **`successor:` cleared 2026-08-05 — the field was being used for a relation it does not express.**
>
> It listed the three Denmark periods plus `GRL-1800-2025`. None of those is a successor:
>
> * This row spans 1800-2025 continuously. **A row covering the whole window has no temporal
>   successor inside it** — there is no year at which something takes over from it.
> * The direction was backwards for Denmark regardless. Iceland became independent **of** Denmark (sovereign in personal union 1918, republic 1944), so if anything Denmark is a *predecessor* of an Icelandic state — never a successor to it.
> * `GRL-1800-2025` as a successor is meaningless: Greenland and Iceland are two separate North Atlantic territories, and neither ever took over from the other.
>
> What the Denmark links actually encoded is **sovereignty**, which this schema has no field for.
> That relation is documented in prose below ("Relationship to DNK chain") and is not lost by
> clearing the list; putting it in `successor:` made it look like a chronology.
>
> Measured consequences of the misuse, all now gone: **8** of the disjoint-geography links baselined
> in PR 132 (Denmark does not touch either island — correct, and never a succession question), and
> the `GRL <-> ISL` cycle in the succession graph, where each row named the other as its successor.
## Summary

This row tracks Iceland as a territorial-economic unit for **226 years**,
from the **1800** WHEP database floor to the **2025** database ceiling
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Iceland is an island in
the North Atlantic with no land boundaries (area: 102,962 km²)
[biger-1995 §iceland](../sources/biger-1995.md#iceland). Over this period
Iceland's political status changed substantially — Danish colony (pre-1918),
sovereign state in personal union with Denmark (1918–1944), then fully
independent republic (from 1944) — but its territory never changed.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
the test for a row split is territorial change, not regime change or legal
status. CShapes 2.0 records **four time-steps** for Iceland (cowcode=395):
colonial status under Denmark to 1918-11-30, colonial/union status
1918-12-01 to 1942-04-21, occupied status 1942-04-22 to 1944-06-16, and
independent from 1944-06-17 — yet **all four carry the same
`ST_Area(geom)` of 19.5614 sq degrees**
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states).
Same area across all CShapes time-steps confirms that one row for the
entire WHEP window is correct: no territorial change occurred.

- **1800 start** is the database floor.
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Iceland is still active.
- **1944-06-17** is when Iceland became fully independent (Icelandic
  republic proclaimed) [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
  COW's state-system entry date for Iceland is 1944-06-17, after which
  it appears as `ICE`, `ccode=395`. The pre-independence period is
  absent from COW entirely.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes records
  Iceland (`cowcode=395`) from 1886-01-01 onward at a constant area of
  **19.5614 sq degrees** across all four time-steps
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). This is a
  backdated polygon: CShapes started from the earliest observation (1886)
  and backdated unchanged borders — Iceland's borders were fixed by
  geography (it is an island) and never adjusted
  [cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding).
- **Pre-1886 extent.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. Iceland's
  island geography means the pre-1886 polygon is identical to the
  post-1886 one — no land boundaries could have changed. The CSV notes
  `CShapes 2.0` as the polygon source; pre-1886 is implicitly
  back-projected from the constant geometry.
- **No area changes.** Of CShapes' 249 political units, 152 have a
  single polygon for the entire period — Iceland is among those with
  multiple time-steps but no geometry change, meaning CShapes' own
  methodology produced identical polygons for each administrative entry
  [cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding).

## Sourced claims

- **Island geography; no land boundaries.** "Iceland (Island) is an
  island in the North Atlantic (area: 39,769 sq. miles [102,962 sq. km.]).
  It has no land boundaries."
  [biger-1995 §iceland](../sources/biger-1995.md#iceland)
- **Norse settlement from 872; Danish sovereignty.** "First discovered in
  the eighth century by Celtic monks; colonized by Norse Vikings from 872.
  Passed under Norwegian then Danish sovereignty."
  [biger-1995 §iceland](../sources/biger-1995.md#iceland)
- **Independence in 1944.** "Total independence in 1944 as a result of
  World War II." [biger-1995 §iceland](../sources/biger-1995.md#iceland)
- **CShapes time-steps (all same area).** CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=395)
  records four entries: (1) colony/owner=390 from 1886-01-01 to
  1918-11-30, area 19.5614; (2) colony/owner=390 from 1918-12-01 to
  1942-04-21, area 19.5614; (3) occupied/owner=2 from 1942-04-22 to
  1944-06-16, area 19.5614; (4) independent/owner=395 from 1944-06-17
  to 2019-12-31, area 19.5614. Constant area across all entries confirms
  no territorial change.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **COW state-system entry 1944-06-17.** COW lists Iceland (`ICE`,
  `ccode=395`) with a single tenure from 1944-06-17 to 2024-12-31.
  Pre-independence Iceland is absent from COW (did not meet the
  diplomatic-recognition and population criteria while a Danish colony).
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
- **1942 occupation.** CShapes records a status change to "occupied /
  owner=2 (USA)" from 1942-04-22 to 1944-06-16. This reflects the
  Allied occupation during WWII. No area change occurred — consistent
  with WHEP's single-row treatment.
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)

## Open questions

### oq-1918-union-status

**1918 Danish-Icelandic Act of Union.** CShapes records a status change
at 1918-12-01 (second colony entry), which corresponds to the Act of
Union 1918 under which Iceland became a sovereign kingdom in personal
union with Denmark. Whether this administrative change had any economic
impact on WHEP trade statistics (i.e., whether Iceland had distinct FT
trade data from 1918 onward) is not yet sourced. If it did, a
sub-row split at 1918 might be warranted on data-availability grounds
rather than territorial grounds.

### oq-pre-1886-polygon

Pre-1886 polygon provenance. The 1800–1885 window is fully outside
CShapes' scope. Iceland's island geography makes the polygon stable
by definition, but the formal sourcing for the pre-1886 geometry is
not documented in the CSV (no "CShapes-Europe" or Cliopatria reference
for Iceland). A future audit should confirm that the same CShapes
geometry is being used back-projected to 1800.
