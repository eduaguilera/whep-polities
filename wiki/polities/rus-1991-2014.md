# Russia (1991-2014)

## Summary

Post-Soviet Russian Federation from the dissolution of the USSR to the
annexation of Crimea
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~17.1 million km². CIS formation (December 1991). First Chechen War
(1994-1996). Second Chechen War (1999-2009). Putin presidency from
2000. Row ends 2014 when Russia annexed Crimea from Ukraine —
territorial change triggers WHEP split.

Predecessor: [f228-1945-1991](f228-1945-1991.md). Successor:
[rus-2014-2025](rus-2014-2025.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1991-12-26: USSR dissolved.** Start-of-row event. Russia emerged as
  the principal successor state.
- **1991-12: CIS formation.** Commonwealth of Independent States
  established at Belavezha Accords.
- **1994-1996: First Chechen War.** Russian military campaign in
  Chechnya.
- **1999-2009: Second Chechen War.** Resumed conflict in Chechnya.
- **2000: Putin presidency.** Vladimir Putin became president.
- **2014-03: Crimea annexation.** End-of-row event. Russia annexed
  Crimea from Ukraine following a disputed referendum.
