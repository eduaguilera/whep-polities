# Liechtenstein

## Summary

Principality of Liechtenstein. The County of Vaduz became a separate territory in 1342 and was united with Schellenberg in 1719 to form the principality. Liechtenstein gained political independence after 1806 and joined the German Federation from 1815 to 1867. It is located between Austria and Switzerland with an area of 62 sq. miles.
