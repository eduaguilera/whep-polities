# Kingdom of Sine

## Summary

The Kingdom of Sine was a Serer kingdom in the Sine-Saloum region of Senegal, paired with the Kingdom of Saloum. Founded c. 14th century, it was one of the oldest Serer polities. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1887, when France established a protectorate. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). After its incorporation, the territory became part of French West Africa and is eventually succeeded by modern Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom occupied the Sine River basin in the Sine-Saloum region of Senegal, north of the Kingdom of Saloum. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none recorded in WHEP; the kingdom predates the 1800 database floor.
- **Successor:** [sen-1960-2025](sen-1960-2025.md) — modern Senegal (via French colonial incorporation from ~1887)
