# Guerrero

## Summary

Guerrero is one of Mexico's 32 federal entities, located on the country's Pacific coast in the southwest, bordered by Michoacán, Oaxaca, Puebla, Morelos, the State of Mexico, and the Pacific Ocean; its capital is Chilpancingo and its best-known city is the resort of Acapulco. This entry models Guerrero as a `type: subnational` unit — a constituent state inside the Mexican federation, not a sovereign polity — because Mexico's national sovereignty is carried by the separate national row MEX-1848-2025, and Guerrero exists here purely as the reporting/administrative territory that production, trade, or population data keyed to "Guerrero" needs to be routed to. The routing decision that created this entry found zero existing name candidates and zero boundary features already representing Guerrero in the database, and no country convention exception applying to it (unlike Baja California, Baja California Sur, or Quintana Roo, which use their data-start year instead of their admission year), so it follows the default rule: start_year is the year the state was actually created, 1849, rather than 1900 where the underlying data series happens to begin.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures data keyed to "Guerrero" as a Mexican subnational unit that had no existing polity row: the routing decision reports zero name candidates and zero boundary features already representing it in the database, meaning any production/trade/statistical rows labeled Guerrero were previously either unmatched or, worse, silently absorbed into the national MEX row — which is wrong because MEX-1848-2025 represents all of Mexico (roughly 1.96 million km²) while Guerrero is a single state of about 63,596 km², a 31-fold overstatement of territory if state-level Guerrero data were left at the national level. What confirms Guerrero is a distinct, individually-administered entity rather than an arbitrary reporting convenience is that it is a constitutionally chartered federal state of Mexico, established by an 1849 decree separating it from three other states, with its own state government, congress, and continuous existence as one of Mexico's 32 first-order administrative divisions through the present day — exactly the kind of standing administrative unit that national statistical agencies (and GADM's ADM1 layer) tabulate and map separately from the country as a whole.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's `polygon_route` is `registered_source_unfetched`: GADM 4.1's ADM1 layer is the right source for this feature (it is registered in `sources.yaml` with the matching `GID_1` id-column schema, and GADM digitises every country's first-level administrative divisions, including all 32 Mexican states), but the copy of GADM 4.1 currently fetched into this repository covers only a subset of 81 countries, and Mexico is not among them. `polygon_source` is therefore set to the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` (not `none`, since the source is known and registered — only unfetched), and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `assigned`, per the harness rule that registered-but-unfetched sources still name their slug while flagging the fetch gap through status. `polygon_feature_id` is left null until the Mexico ADM1 extract is pulled and Guerrero's specific `GID_1` feature is identified.

**Territory a reader can locate today:** Guerrero is the Mexican state occupying the southwestern Pacific coast between roughly 16.3°N-18.8°N and 98.0°W-101.9°W, containing Acapulco, Chilpancingo, Taxco, and the Costa Chica and Costa Grande coastal regions, plus mountainous interior terrain (the Sierra Madre del Sur). Its modern area is approximately **63,596 km²**, per standard published figures for the state (INEGI); this is a stated territorial figure, not a measurement of any geometry attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned here. Guerrero's boundaries have been broadly stable since its 1849 creation, though minor 19th- and early-20th-century adjustments with neighboring Oaxaca and Puebla are not independently verified for this entry (see open questions) — so once the GADM polygon is eventually assigned, it should be understood as a present-day proxy applied across the full 1849-2025 span rather than a period-accurate boundary for the entry's earliest decades.

## Predecessors and successors

Guerrero has no predecessor polity in this database: it was formed in 1849 by carving territory out of three existing states (Mexico, Puebla, and Michoacán) rather than by any single prior unit's transformation, and those parent states are not modeled here as predecessors since only a fraction of each contributed territory. It has no successor either — Guerrero remains a current Mexican federal state as of 2025, hence the open-ended end_year convention used for still-existing subnational units. It is contained throughout its span by MEX-1848-2025, the national Mexican polity row, with a single container edge covering the full 1849-2025 range since Mexico's federal structure (and this container row's own span) has not changed in a way that breaks the containment.

## Sourced claims

- Guerrero was created as a Mexican federal state by decree of May 27, 1849, formed from portions of the states of Mexico, Puebla, and Michoacán (per the routing decision's span_basis, itself drawing on standard Mexican state-formation history; a primary citation is still needed, see open questions).
- GADM 4.1 publishes ADM1-level administrative boundaries for Mexico's states including Guerrero, using an id_column (GID_1) schema already registered in this project's sources.yaml, but the locally fetched GADM extract covers only 81 countries and does not currently include Mexico.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Follow the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Guerrero is an ordinary Mexican federal state, administratively unremarkable relative to Mexico's other 31 entities, and has no independent historical identity comparable to Alaska Territory or Hyderabad State that would justify a bespoke code. Per the routing decision's country convention, MEX subnational codes are new precedent (0 existing rows), so this entry sets MEX-GUE-1849-2025 as the pattern other Mexican states should follow: ISO3, a short subunit abbreviation, and the actual start/end years rather than the data's 1900 start year.

### d-polygon-source-slug

**polygon_source is the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1, not the route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched, which per the harness instructions means the slug IS the source and polygon_status must be unassigned rather than assigned or proxy. gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered in sources.yaml with id_column GID_1 matching Guerrero's expected feature schema, but the local fetched file only covers a subset of 81 countries and Mexico is not among them, so polygon_feature_id is left null until that fetch happens.

### d-start-year-1849

**start_year is the 1849 creation date, not the 1900 data start**

The routing_reasoning explicitly instructs using the admission-as-state year per the country convention, since Guerrero is not one of the three listed exceptions (Baja California, Baja California Sur, Quintana Roo) that use their data start year instead. Guerrero's creation is dated to the decree of May 27, 1849, separating territory from the states of Mexico, Puebla, and Michoacán.

## Open questions

### oq-1849-decree-primary-source

**The May 27, 1849 creation date needs a primary or secondary historical citation**

The routing_concerns field for this decision flags that the exact 1849 admission date should be verified against a primary or secondary historical source before the polity row is committed. This page currently states the decree date based on general knowledge of Mexican state formation, but no citation to a specific archival decree text, a Mexican federal gazette (Diario Oficial equivalent of the period), or a secondary historical work (e.g. a state-formation history of Guerrero) has been attached. If the date is off by even a year or two, start_year and the code MEX-GUE-1849-2025 both need to change together, since the gate checks that the code's years match the frontmatter.

### oq-gadm-fetch-and-licence

**GADM 4.1 Mexico ADM1 data has not been fetched, and its licence terms for this project are unverified**

polygon_status is unassigned because the local GADM 4.1 file only covers 81 countries and Mexico is absent; someone still needs to fetch the Mexico ADM1 layer (or the global ADM1 file) and extract the GID_1 feature for Guerrero before this entry can move to assigned. Separately, the routing decision's polygon_detail flags licence_known: false — GADM's terms restrict commercial redistribution, and whether whep-polities' use and redistribution model is compatible with that has not been checked. Both the fetch and the licence review are prerequisites to closing this out.

### oq-boundary-stability-since-1849

**Whether Guerrero's 1849 boundaries match its present-day GADM boundaries is unconfirmed**

The routing decision's vintage_risk note calls the risk low because Guerrero's borders have been stable since 1849 aside from minor adjustments, but this is asserted rather than checked against a specific historical boundary source. Mexican state boundaries did see adjustments into the early 20th century (e.g. municipal-level disputes with neighboring Oaxaca and Puebla continued for decades), so once the present-day GADM polygon is fetched and assigned, it should be flagged as a modern-boundary proxy for the full 1849-2025 span rather than assumed identical throughout.
