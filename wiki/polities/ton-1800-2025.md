# Tonga

## Summary

Tonga is a Polynesian kingdom comprising 171 islands (36 inhabited) in the South Pacific. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. Tonga is unique among Pacific island states in that it was never colonized — it was a British protected state (1900–1970) but retained its indigenous monarchy and sovereignty. Full independence was formally achieved on 4 June 1970. Biger (1995) confirms Tonga is a kingdom. CShapes records one time-step beginning 1999-09-14 (COW batch entry for small Pacific states). COW ccode=955 (stateabb=TON); state-system entry 1999-09-14.

## Open questions

### oq-british-protection-period

Tonga was under a British Treaty of Friendship and Protection from 1900 to 1970. During this period, Tonga had its own indigenous sovereignty but limited external relations. CShapes has no pre-1999 polygon for Tonga. Whether the WHEP polygon for the 1800–1999 period is a back-projection from the CShapes entry and whether territorial changes occurred during the protection period (1900–1970) should be confirmed.
