# British India (to 1886)

## Summary

British India **before the annexation of Upper Burma** (1 January 1886) — the East
India Company's territories to 1858, then Crown rule.

This row exists because the previous single row 1800-1893 was bound to a CShapes
polygon that already included Upper Burma, overstating British India before 1886
by roughly 440,000 km².

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** constructed — Cliopatria v0.1.3 `British Raj` @ **1880**, MINUS Ceylon,
  Portuguese India and French India. **4,135,466 km² declared, 4,135,440 measured.**
  Cliopatria is the base because **CShapes 2.0 has no India feature before 1886-01-01** — the
  polygon priority stack working as intended, not source-mixing.

> **Ceylon was removed on 2026-08-10 (issue 84), and it was 65,600 km².**
>
> Cliopatria's `British Raj` feature includes Ceylon, so this row — the only `IND` period whose
> base geometry is Cliopatria rather than CShapes 750 — geometrically contained the whole of
> [lka-1800-2025](lka-1800-2025.md):
>
> ```
> IND-1800-1886 ∩ LKA-1800-2025    65,600 km²   99.5% of Ceylon
> every other IND period ∩ LKA          0 km²
> ```
>
> Ceylon was a **separate Crown Colony from 1802**, administered from Colombo and answering to
> the Colonial Office rather than the India Office. It was never part of British India, and
> every CShapes-bound `IND` period says so already.
>
> **The declared area agreed with the geometry because both included Ceylon.** The page declared
> 4,209,917 and the polygon measured 4,209,869 — a 0.001% match that read as confirmation and
> was two errors cancelling. Exactly the shape of `IDN-OTH-1949-1951`'s West Papua (PR 182), and
> the reason a declared figure agreeing with its own geometry proves nothing on its own.
>
> ```
> Cliopatria British Raj @1880    4,209,877 km²   (after a 0.0009% validity repair)
> minus Ceylon (envelope clip)       72,834
> minus Portuguese India              1,305
> minus French India                    312
> published                       4,135,440 km²   declared 4,135,466 -> 0.001%
> ```
>
> **The Ceylon removal is 72,834 km², not 65,600, and the difference is the point.** Subtracting
> CShapes 780's *outline* left **12 fragments totalling 7,995 km², every one of them on Sri Lanka**
> (79.9-81.9°E, 6.6-9.7°N) and still attributed to British India — because Cliopatria's Ceylon is
> about 8,000 km² larger than CShapes', so cutting one source's island out of another source's
> continent leaves the coastal disagreement behind. The same class of residue as `IDN-OTH`'s West
> Papua rim (PR 182), where the fix was to cut GADM with GADM; here there is no Cliopatria Ceylon
> to cut with, since Cliopatria's `British Ceylon` series **ends at 1814** — folding Ceylon into
> `British Raj` from then on is precisely the error.
>
> So the clip is Ceylon's **bounding box**, which removes the island and every coastal
> disagreement in one operation. That is only safe where the box holds nothing else, and it was
> checked rather than assumed:
>
> ```
> Ceylon envelope        lon 79.70..81.89, lat 5.92..9.82
> India's nearest land   0.443° away (~49 km, the Palk Strait)
> other live polities with land inside the box   NONE
> ```
>
> Result: `IND-1800-1886 ∩ LKA-1800-2025` is now **0.0 km²**, from 65,600.
>
> The two enclaves are subtracted too, and for this span that is unambiguous: Portuguese India
> was Portuguese until December 1961 and French India French until October 1954, so both were
> foreign throughout 1800-1886. Each subtrahend is the **same geometry the other row publishes**
> — CShapes 780 @1948 for Ceylon, and the `PTIND`/`FRIN` builders — so the pairs cannot drift.
>
> **Still unfixed, and deliberately, in two places.**
>
> ~~The CShapes-bound periods contain Portuguese India (3,719 km²) and French India (520 km²). They
> cannot be cut the same way, because `IND-1949-2025` spans both sides of 1954 and 1961 — Goa and
> Pondicherry really did become Indian.~~ **Revised 2026-08-13:** that was true of `IND-1949-2025`
> alone, and it was written as though it applied to all six. The five periods
> `IND-1886-1893` … `IND-1947-1949` all end in or before 1949 — five years before the Pondicherry
> cession and twelve before Goa — so for them the subtraction needed no judgement either, and they
> now do it: each is `constructed` as its own CShapes 750 step MINUS both enclaves. Only
> `IND-1949-2025` still contains them, and it does need the row split at 1954 and 1961, a
> periodisation change to a major national family, which stays on issue 84.
>
> And on *this* row, thin rims of both enclaves survive:
>
> ```
> IND-1800-1886 ∩ PTIND-1816-1961    8.3 km²
> IND-1800-1886 ∩ FRIN-1816-1954    40.2 km²
> ```
>
> Same coastline-disagreement mechanism as Ceylon's, and **the envelope trick cannot be reused**:
> Pondicherry, Karikal, Mahe, Yanam, Goa, Daman and Diu are enclaves *surrounded by* Indian
> territory, so any box or buffer around them deletes genuine Indian land — trading a 48 km²
> double claim for a comparable amount of wrongly removed territory. 48 km² is 0.001% of this row,
> and a visible double claim beats an invisible deletion.
>
> Building the difference also needed the **source repaired first**: Cliopatria's `British Raj`
> @1880 self-intersects in Balochistan (63.83°E, 29.47°N), and GEOS aborts a set operation on it
> rather than returning something wrong. `build.py`'s new `_valid()` helper repairs it under a
> 0.1% area budget — the actual cost is **0.0009%**.
- **`polygon_status: polygon_vintage_drift`**, deliberately. Cliopatria's `British Raj`
  series itself begins in **1859**, so for **1800-1859 the polygon is a
  back-projection**, and the territorial expansions inside that window — the
  Anglo-Maratha Wars (1803-1818), the 1816 Sugauli cession, the 1826 Treaty of
  Yandabo (Arakan, Manipur, Assam, Tenasserim), the 1843 conquest of Sindh, the
  1845-1849 Anglo-Sikh Wars — are **not** represented.

> **The back-projection is now measured, 2026-08-17 (issue 11), and it is a factor of 4.3
> in 1800.** It was previously described here as "real and unquantified", and the sentence
> claiming there is "no East India Company feature in either source" was wrong.
>
> Cliopatria **does** carry a Company-era British India extent: not under `British Raj`
> but under the polity named **`British Empire`** (the direct-rule member of the
> `(British Empire)` container), which has features from **1794 to 1858**. Clipping each
> one to the subcontinent (lon 66-101°E, lat 5-37°N) and removing the Ceylon envelope,
> against the **same clip** applied to the polygon this row actually publishes:
>
> ```
> year   Cliopatria extent    share of the 1880 back-projection
> 1800         916,568 km2     23%     (the published polygon is 4.32x too large)
> 1805       1,506,345         38%
> 1815       1,672,850         42%
> 1820       2,067,599         52%
> 1830       2,300,176         58%
> 1840       2,720,405         69%
> 1845       2,600,852         66%
> 1850       3,578,744         90%
> 1855       3,439,866         87%
> 1858       2,968,667         75%     (the 1857-58 rebellion)
> 1859       3,937,388         99%     British Raj series begins
> 1880       3,961,111        100%     = the 4,135,466 published, box-clipped
> ```
>
> The 174,355 km² between 3,961,111 and the published 4,135,466 is territory outside the
> measuring box (chiefly Baluchistan west of 66°E); the box is applied to **both** sides,
> so the shares are internally consistent.
>
> **The overstatement costs nothing today, and that is why the row is not being split.**
> WHEP's consolidated layer B begins at **1885**. Its earliest India observation is a
> single row for **1885**, and that is also the only row in the whole corpus matched to
> this polity:
>
> ```
> rows matched to IND-1800-1886        1        year 1885
> earliest India row in layer B     1885
> earliest row in layer B, any country   1850
> ```
>
> So no datum is matched to the 1800-1859 back-projection, and the one datum this row
> does carry sits five years from the polygon's 1880 vintage, where the drift is under
> 1%. Re-periodising 1800-1886 would move exactly one observation, by nothing.
>
> **Two reasons it would also make the row worse, not better.**
>
> 1. **The pre-1859 feature is not scope-comparable to `British Raj`.** Point tests on
>    the source: `British Empire` @1856 excludes **Hyderabad** (~214,000 km²) and
>    **Delhi** — the latter still inside Cliopatria's `Mughal Empire`, which runs to
>    1858 — while `British Raj` @1859 contains both. WHEP's British India includes the
>    princely states, as the 1880 base does. Splitting on this series would therefore
>    publish a spurious ~590,000 km² step at 1859 that is a **change of source coding,
>    not of territory**.
> 2. **The feature double-counts its own area, entirely inside India.** Summing the
>    feature's parts and comparing against their union, in the same box:
>
>    ```
>    year   sum of parts   union       overlap
>    1815      1,749,754   1,745,600     4,154   (0.2%)
>    1820      2,818,367   2,140,350   678,017  (24.1%)
>    1840      3,482,542   2,793,239   689,303  (19.8%)
>    1856      4,002,074   3,489,869   512,204  (12.8%)
>    ```
>
>    `build.py`'s `_valid()` repair budget is 0.1%; repairing these features moves
>    **4.3% to 23.8%** of their area, so the builder **refuses them outright** rather
>    than guessing. The union is still a defensible extent, but a source that stores a
>    quarter of British India twice is not one to periodise a major national family on.
>
> **The composed-difference idea the issue called "most promising" is both unnecessary
> and worse.** Unnecessary because a direct extent series exists; worse because
> Cliopatria's own independent-polity dates in this region are unreliable — its
> `Talpur Dynasty` (Sindh) runs to **1863**, twenty years after the 1843 conquest, and
> its `Sikh Empire` ends in **1841**, eight years before the Punjab annexation. A
> subcontinent-minus-independents difference would inherit both errors as British or
> non-British territory respectively.
>
> **What would reopen this:** any ingest of India data **before 1885**. At that point the
> back-projection stops being free and the split has to be paid for — most likely from a
> source outside Cliopatria, since neither of the two objections above is fixable
> downstream.

## Sourced claims

- **1858: Crown rule.** The Government of India Act transferred authority from the
  East India Company to the Crown after the 1857 rebellion; Cliopatria's "British
  Raj" series begins in 1859 accordingly
  [cliopatria-v0.1.3](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md).
- **1886-01-01: Upper Burma annexed.** Following the Third Anglo-Burmese War of
  November 1885, Upper Burma was annexed and administered as part of British
  India. CShapes 2.0's India coverage begins on this exact date
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1893-11-12: Durand Line.** The Afghan frontier agreement; CShapes records a
  +167,089 km² change on precisely this date
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).
- **COW state-system membership.** ccode 750
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
