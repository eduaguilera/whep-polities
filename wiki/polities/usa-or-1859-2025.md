# Oregon (state of the United States)

## Summary

This entry tracks the US state of Oregon from its admission to the Union on 1859-02-14 (the 33rd state) to 2025, the WHEP data horizon. It is created as `type: subnational` -- not a national or colonial polity -- because Oregon has never held sovereignty of its own: throughout this span it has been a constituent state of the United States, and the entry exists purely to give Oregon-labelled statistical data a home that is not the national USA aggregate. That distinction matters mechanically: the national USA chain in this database (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) covers the entire country at each period, roughly 9.4 million km2 in the post-1867 segments, while Oregon is about 254,800 km2, roughly 2.7% of that. Routing Oregon-labelled rows to the national polity would either double-count Oregon's data against the national total (if the national total already includes it) or silently understate national area-normalized statistics (if per-km2 figures were computed against the wrong denominator) -- the same sub-territory hazard already documented for Alaska (ALK-1867-1959) and implicitly for Hawaii (HAWI-1898-1959). This entry, together with the parallel USA-CALIFORNIA verdict, extends that same policy to a second state. The 1859 start date is the state's actual admission date, not the year any particular ingested source happens to begin reporting "OREGON" as a label (1869) -- the routing decision this page implements is explicit that legal existence, not first-observed-in-data year, sets start_year, mirroring how California's entry uses 1850 rather than its own data's first year.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the harness routing record for unit_id `USA-OREGON` (admin_name `OREGON`, country United States of America) shows a data span of 1869-2025 under a raw `OREGON` label; the specific ingested source file, its row count, and variables are not named in the routing_verdicts.csv / cycle01-usaoregon/result.json record available to this page -- only the year range and label are confirmed (recorded as an open question above). Previously matched to: nothing -- the routing reasoning states explicitly that zero existing WHEP polities matched Oregon by name, and the only candidates considered were the containing national USA rows for that era, which represent the entire ~9.4M km2 country rather than the ~254,800 km2 state; matching Oregon-labelled data to those rows would misattribute a state-level series to the whole nation. What confirms it is distinct: Oregon's own 1859 admission as a separate state of the Union with its own statehood date, area, and borders is itself the historical confirmation of a distinct administrative unit -- the same standard already applied to the parallel USA-CALIFORNIA verdict and to ALK-1867-1959, both created for the identical reason (a named US state or territory tabulated separately from the national total).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status.** `polygon_status: assigned`, sourced from `gadm-4.1-adm1` feature `USA.38_1`. This is a registered-source feature, not a proxy: GADM's administrative level-1 layer for the United States already contains a dedicated Oregon polygon (the same source and level used for ALK-1867-1959's Alaska polygon), so no substitution or construction was required. **Why assigned rather than proxy is still an approximation worth flagging:** the GADM feature reflects present-day (c. 2020) state boundaries, applied back to the 1859 admission date. Oregon's external borders have been essentially fixed since statehood -- unlike the Alaska panhandle, which needed an explicit 1903 tribunal ruling -- but several 19th-century boundary surveys along the Snake River and the Oregon-Nevada-California corner were not completed until the 1870s, a detail not separately verified here (see open question below).

**Territory description.** Oregon occupies the Pacific Northwest of the United States, bordered by Washington to the north (the Columbia River forms most of this border), Idaho to the east, Nevada and California to the south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. It spans the Coast Range, the Willamette Valley (the state's main population and agricultural center, including Portland and Salem), the Cascade Range, and the high desert of the eastern plateau. Total area is approximately 254,800 km2 (measured from the attached GADM geometry; this is a measurement of the polygon, and it agrees with the commonly cited US Census Bureau figure for the state, which is independent corroboration rather than the same number restated). This makes Oregon roughly 2.7% of the ~9.4 million km2 covered by the containing national USA rows in the post-1867 era -- confirming it is a small sub-territory whose data should not be folded into the national aggregate, the same test applied to Alaska and California.

## Predecessors and successors

Predecessor left empty: Oregon was organized as Oregon Territory in 1848, already under US federal administration, and achieved statehood on 1859-02-14 without any change of sovereign -- an internal status change, not a succession from a distinct prior polity, and no WHEP row for "Oregon Territory" currently exists to serve as one. Successor left empty because the entry runs to 2025, the WHEP data horizon, with no dissolution or absorption event to record; Oregon remains a US state today. The entry's relationship to the national United States chain across its full 1859-2025 span is instead carried by three container edges (USA-1848-1867, USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025), matching the segmentation already used by that chain's own three post-1848 rows.

## Sourced claims

- Oregon was admitted to the United States as the 33rd state on February 14, 1859, under the Oregon Admission Act (11 Stat. 383, signed 1859-02-14).
- GADM 4.1 administrative level-1 feature `USA.38_1` (Oregon) is the polygon this entry uses; GADM's own documentation dates its US state layer to a 2020 vintage, i.e. modern-boundary data applied as a proxy back to 1859.
- Oregon's total area (land plus inland water) is commonly cited at approximately 254,799 km2 (98,379 sq mi), per US Census Bureau state-area figures -- consistent with this entry's polygon-derived measurement.
- The routing record for this entry (pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv, unit_id USA-OREGON) states the underlying data's own year range as 1869-2025 under the raw label 'OREGON', with no existing WHEP polity matching it by name prior to this decision.

## Decisions

### d-polity-code-usa-or

**Code follows the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern, not the bespoke ALK/HYD/RYU form**

Chose `USA-OR-1859-2025` over a bespoke name like `ALK-1867-1959`. The bespoke codes in the table (Alaska, Hyderabad, Ryukyu) mark units with a name and identity independent of the modern administrative label -- Alaska was a purchased territory administered as a customs district decades before it took the name 'Alaska Territory', Hyderabad was a princely state, Ryukyu a distinct kingdom-then-prefecture. Oregon has none of that: it was organized as Oregon Territory in 1848 and admitted as Oregon in 1859 under the same name throughout, with no separate historical identity the code would need to carry. That makes it a plain administrative subdivision, which is the 57-row dominant pattern (ESP-AS, DZA-CVD, etc.), and `OR` is its standard two-letter postal abbreviation, matching the two-letter convention used by the Spanish province codes.

### d-empty-predecessor-successor

**Predecessor and successor left empty rather than pointing at the USA container rows**

ALK-1867-1959 lists a genuine predecessor (the Russian Empire row F228-1856-1905) because sovereignty changed hands. Oregon never had a distinct prior sovereign: the area was organized as Oregon Territory in 1848, already under US administration, and simply achieved statehood in 1859 -- an internal status change within the same country, not a transfer this repo's predecessor/successor fields are meant to record (those are for polity-to-polity succession, and the container edges already carry Oregon's relationship to the national USA chain across 1859-2025). No WHEP row exists for 'Oregon Territory' as a distinct pre-1859 entity, and creating one was out of scope for this decision, which only had data from 1869 onward. Left both fields empty rather than inventing a placeholder predecessor.

### d-start-year-1859-not-1869

**start_year set to the 1859 admission date, not the 1869 data start**

The routing decision this page implements explicitly rejects using the data's own first year (1869) as start_year, instead using Oregon's actual statehood date of 1859-02-14, consistent with the sibling USA-CALIFORNIA verdict using California's 1850 admission year rather than its data start. This matters because the polity's legal existence, not the accident of when a particular source began tabulating it, is what start_year is supposed to record; the 1859-1869 gap is simply a decade where no ingested source happens to report Oregon separately.

## Open questions

### oq-source-dataset-unspecified

**The routing decision names no specific source dataset, row count, or variable for the Oregon data**

The harness record that produced this page (routing_verdicts.csv / cycle01-usaoregon/result.json) states only 'UNIT years 1869-2025' and admin_name 'OREGON' as evidence; it does not name which ingested source(s) carry Oregon-labelled rows, how many rows, or what variables they cover. The page-requirements spec asks for source name, year range, and row count, and this page can only supply the year range with confidence. Before this entry is treated as fully documented, the actual source file(s) tabulating 'OREGON' should be located in the ingestion pipeline and the source/label/row-count table filled in, the way the Manchuria exemplar tabulates mitchell vs fao1952 explicitly.

### oq-gadm-boundary-vintage

**GADM's present-day Oregon boundary is used for the full 1859-2025 span without checking for 19th-century boundary adjustments**

Oregon's borders have been broadly stable since 1859, but the exact eastern and southern lines involved several 19th-century surveys and minor adjustments (e.g. disputes along the Snake River and Oregon-Nevada-California tripoint surveys completed only in the 1870s), and no check was done here against a historical-boundary source (e.g. a Newberry Library atlas or a state-boundary GIS layer for the admission era) to confirm the modern GADM feature does not overstate or understate the 1859 extent. Given the area involved is small relative to the state's ~254,800 km2 total, the risk is low, but it has not been quantified the way the Alaska panhandle discrepancy was quantified in ALK-1867-1959, and should be treated as unverified rather than confirmed negligible.

### oq-oregon-territory-gap

**No WHEP row exists for Oregon Territory (1848-1859), so pre-statehood Oregon data has nowhere to route**

If any ingested source reports agricultural, trade, or population data for 'Oregon' or 'Oregon Territory' before 1859 -- plausible for a 1848-organized territory with a US customs district and land-grant settlement records -- there is currently no polity row to receive it: this entry starts at 1859, and the containing USA-1848-1867 row is a national aggregate that would risk the same sub-territory double-counting problem this entry exists to avoid for the statehood period. Whether such pre-1859 territorial data exists in any of the repo's ingested sources was not checked as part of this decision and should be verified before the predecessor field is finalized as empty.
