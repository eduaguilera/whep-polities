# Malaysia (1957-1963)

## Summary

The Federation of Malaya in its initial independent configuration, from 31 August 1957 to 15 September 1963. CShapes records area 10.71 sq deg for this period (COW 820, status=independent). "On 16 September 1963 they both joined the Independent Federation of Malaya, which became Malaysia" [biger-1995 §malaysia](../sources/biger-1995.md#malaysia) -- referring to the inclusion of Sabah and Sarawak (and initially Singapore), which more than doubled the territory to 26.79 sq deg.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 10.71 sq deg for the period 1957-08-31 to 1963-09-15, status=independent, owner=Malaysia (COW 820). This represents only Peninsular Malaya (the former Malayan Union / Federation of Malaya). The Indonesia-Malaysia boundary on Borneo separated "the Dutch Indonesian regions of East and West Kalimantan from the Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah" [biger-1995 §malaysia](../sources/biger-1995.md#malaysia).
