# United States (original)

## Summary

This row tracks the United States from the **1800** database floor
to **1803** — a **4-year row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
It covers the original USA territory east of the Mississippi River:
the 13 original states plus early admitted states and territories
(Ohio, Kentucky, Tennessee, Vermont). The CSV notes estimate
~1.2M km2 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The end-of-row event is the **Louisiana Purchase (1803)** from
France, which approximately doubled US territory by adding ~2.1M km2
west of the Mississippi. This is a massive territorial change that
fully justifies the split under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition).

Predecessor: NA (database floor). Successor:
[usa-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (United States of America
  1800-1802)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). A single
  Cliopatria time-step covering 1800–1802, applied across the
  full 4-year row. Given the row is only 4 years and the polygon
  time-step aligns closely, this is a good temporal fit.
- **Coverage.** Original 13 states plus Vermont (1791), Kentucky
  (1792), Tennessee (1796), and Ohio (admitted 1803). Territory
  east of the Mississippi, south of the Great Lakes, north of
  Spanish Florida. Approximate area ~1.2M km2
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
