# Belgian Congo and Ruanda-Urundi

## Summary

A **composed-union** row for colonial agricultural returns that report the
**Belgian Congo with Ruanda-Urundi folded in**. Belgium administered
Ruanda-Urundi (a League of Nations, later UN, mandate) jointly with the Congo,
and FAO/IIA **footnotes** flag combined reporting. A figure labelled "Belgian
Congo" in those series covers the combined territory and must not map to the
Congo alone. Constituents are carried separately as
[COD-1910-1960](cod-1910-1960.md) and [RWB-1922-1962](rwb-1922-1962.md).

Period 1922–1960 spans the mandate era to Congolese independence
(1960-06-30).
