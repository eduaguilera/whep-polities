# Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha

## Territory and evidence

The composite comprises Saint Helena island, Ascension, and Tristan da Cunha with Gough, Nightingale and Inaccessible. The 2009 Order records the latter islands becoming dependencies on 12 January 1938 and preserves the group under its new name; it is retrospective evidence for 1938. Constitutional changes do not split this footprint. [Original Order](../sources/st-helena-constitution-2009.md).

FAOSTAT 187 explicitly names the three components; country area is 39,000 ha in 1980, 1981 and 2021, incompatible with Saint Helena island alone. This FAO series is mirrored by UNdata and is not independent measurement. [Series](../sources/faostat-area187-country-area.md). FishStat 654 also names the composite and has trade records 1980–2023. [FishStat](../sources/fishstat-trade-2025-1.md). The labels define intended coverage, not completeness of each island's observed or estimated trade.

GADM 4.1 ADM 0 SHN measures 411.227121 km² in ESRI:54034, comprising Ascension 99.630825, Saint Helena 122.661178 and Tristan group 188.935117 km². It is a modern coastline proxy, not a marine jurisdiction or an exact historical area. The difference from FAO 390 km² remains a source/geometry-definition difference, not something to force into equality.

Separate constituent records remain live, with containment edges. Aggregate and constituent observations must not be summed without source-specific deduplication. No political containment in the UK land polygon is asserted. The FAOSTAT mapping remains a no-observations registry route for this inventory; it must not claim land-use rows were scanned.
