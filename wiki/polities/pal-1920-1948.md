# Palestine (1920-1948)

## Summary

The British Mandate of Palestine from 1920 to 1948. "The country was under Ottoman rule until World War I, after which the League of Nations awarded a mandate to Great Britain" [biger-1995 §israel](../sources/biger-1995.md#israel). "The Conference of San Remo (24 April 1920) established the mandates: Syria and Lebanon under France, Palestine and Mesopotamia under Britain" [biger-1995 §syria](../sources/biger-1995.md#syria). The mandate ended on 14 May 1948 with the declaration of the State of Israel. "In 1922 Transjordan was carved from the British mandate over Palestine" [biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan), reducing the mandate's eastern boundary. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. The territory corresponds to the area between the Jordan River / Dead Sea and the Mediterranean, plus the Negev desert to the south. After the 1922 separation of Transjordan, the mandate covered only the territory west of the Jordan River. "The Palestine (Israel)-Lebanon boundary is based on an Anglo-French line that stemmed from the scramble for former Ottoman territory in the wake of World War I" [biger-1995 §lebanon](../sources/biger-1995.md#lebanon).
