# Ethiopia (1907-1936)

## Summary

A **29-year row** covering late Menelik II, Lij Iyasu, Empress Zewditu, and the reign of Haile Selassie up to the Italian invasion. Row ends when Italy occupied Addis Ababa (1936-05-05) and proclaimed Italian East Africa. Sovereignty restored 1941; see [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md).

Predecessor: [eth-1902-1907](eth-1902-1907.md). Successor: [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md) (post-liberation). Occupation period: [aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, feature 530, vintage 1907. Pre-invasion borders stable through this period.
- Approximate area: ~1,000,000 km2.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [eth-1902-1907](eth-1902-1907.md).
- **Successor:** [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md) -- sovereignty restored after British liberation.
- **Occupation aggregate:** [aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md) -- Italian East Africa 1936-1941.

## Sourced claims

- **1913: Menelik II dies.** Succeeded by Lij Iyasu, then Empress Zewditu with Ras Tafari as regent.
- **1930: Haile Selassie crowned Emperor.**
- **1935-10-03: Italian invasion.** Mussolini's Italy invaded from Eritrea and Italian Somaliland.
- **1936-05-05: Fall of Addis Ababa.** End-of-row event. Italy proclaimed Italian East Africa (AOI) combining Ethiopia, Eritrea, and Italian Somaliland.

## Decisions

- **Split from ETH-1907-1952.** The old 46-year row was split at 1936 and 1941 to properly route data from the Italian occupation period. See [aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md) and [eth-1941-1952](eth-1941-1952.md).
