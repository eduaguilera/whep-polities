# North Dakota (state of the United States)

## Summary

North Dakota is one of the two states carved from the northern half of Dakota Territory when it split and both halves were admitted to the Union on the same day, 2 November 1889 (by tradition the papers were shuffled so neither state could claim to be admitted first). `type: subnational` because this is a constituent U.S. state, not a sovereign polity: national-level USA rows (USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025) already carry U.S. sovereignty across this span, and marking this row subnational keeps it from competing with those rows in family/sovereignty ranking while still letting state-level statistical series (USDA crop and production reports keyed to "NORTH DAKOTA") route to a polity whose boundary actually matches the reporting unit. The boundary has been stable since 1889: no county or state-line changes have altered the state's outer perimeter, so a single polygon and a single row cover the whole 1889-2025 span without a proxy substitution.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry exists to receive USDA-sourced production/crop series labeled "NORTH DAKOTA" that were previously unmatched or misrouted to the national USA row. USDA reports observations for the state (and for the pre-statehood Dakota Territory years under the same label) from 1879 through 2025, spanning back_cast years 1879-1888 before formal statehood. Routing this data to USA-1867-1959 or USA-1959-2025 (the national containers) discards the state-level granularity the source actually reports at, and overstates the reporting territory by roughly 25x (the continental U.S. vs. one state). No prior polity row in this table carried the North Dakota reporting unit distinctly; Dakota Territory's 1861 organization and 1889 split into North and South Dakota is standard U.S. administrative history (U.S. Statutes at Large, Enabling Act of 1889), confirming this was a distinct, separately-administered unit from admission onward, not merely a reporting convenience.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Copied from the registered source feature `USA.35_1` in `gadm-4.1-adm1` (present-day GADM 4.1 admin-1 boundary for North Dakota). This is an `assigned` polygon, not a proxy: North Dakota's state boundary has not changed since 1889, so a present-day administrative boundary source is a direct fit for the entire 1889-2025 span, unlike cases where modern boundaries stand in for pre-modern polities of different extent.

**Territory description:** North Dakota occupies the northern part of the U.S. Great Plains, bordered by Canada (Manitoba and Saskatchewan) to the north, Minnesota to the east across the Red River, South Dakota to the south, and Montana to the west. Its outline is almost entirely straight surveyed lines except the Red River eastern border -- a shape readily located on any modern U.S. map as the rectangular state directly south of the Canadian prairie provinces.

**Area:** The attached GADM polygon measures approximately 183,108 km2 (this is a measurement of the attached geometry, not an independently sourced territory figure). This is consistent with the commonly cited land+water area of North Dakota (~183,272 km2 per U.S. Census Bureau), supporting that the geometry is a faithful representation of the state rather than an artifact of GADM's admin-1 simplification.

## Predecessors and successors

North Dakota has no predecessor or successor row in the current table. Before 1889 the territory was the northern portion of Dakota Territory (organized 1861), which has no polity row of its own yet -- see the open question below rather than a fabricated predecessor edge. After 1889 there is no successor: the state persists to the open end_year 2025 with the same boundary and no administrative supersession.

## Sourced claims

- USDA reports crop and production series under the label "NORTH DAKOTA" from 1879 through 2025, including years before formal statehood (Dakota Territory era, 1879-1888).
- North Dakota and South Dakota were both admitted to the Union on 2 November 1889 under the Enabling Act of 1889, splitting the organized Dakota Territory (established 1861) into two states.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not ALK's bespoke form**

The proposed decision used a bespoke-flavored reasoning (comparing to ALK-1867-1959), but North Dakota is not a historical territory with a name distinct from the modern state -- it is simply a U.S. state with no name change across its span, matching 57 of 66 subnational rows in the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern (e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025). I chose USA-ND-1889-2025 over the bespoke style ALK uses, since ALK-1867-1959 (Territory of Alaska) named a genuinely distinct historical entity (a territory, not a state) whereas Dakota Territory's northern half became statehood-named North Dakota with no separate identity worth a bespoke code.

### d-dropped-predecessor-claim

**Dropped the predecessor claim from the previous rejected answer**

The rejected page declared predecessor: [USA-1867-1959], but that code is a container-chain entry (the national United States row for that era), not a distinct predecessor polity with its own successor field to reciprocate. Declaring it as a predecessor conflated containment with succession and produced the one-directional edge that validate_chain_integrity rejected. This page instead leaves predecessor and successor empty, and records the Dakota Territory relationship as an open question rather than a false edge, since no Dakota Territory polity row currently exists in the table to point to.

### d-two-container-eras

**Split the container into two edges to tile 1889-2025 with no gap**

USA-1867-1959 alone would leave 1959-2025 uncontained, and USA-1959-2025 alone would leave 1889-1959 uncontained. Following the tiling requirement, this page declares two container edges, each trimmed to fall within both this polity's own span (1889-2025) and the container's own span (so the first edge starts at 1889, not 1867, and ends at 1959; the second starts at 1959 and runs to 2025), avoiding the containment gate's rejection of edges extending outside either party's span.

## Open questions

### oq-dakota-territory-predecessor

**No Dakota Territory polity row exists to serve as a true predecessor**

USDA production data for North Dakota extends back to 1879, a decade before statehood, under the Dakota Territory administration (organized 1861, split 1889). This page does not declare a predecessor edge because no Dakota Territory row currently exists in the polity table -- creating one would require deciding whether it should be a single Dakota Territory polity later split into two successors (ND and SD), or two separate proxy entries. Until that decision is made elsewhere, the pre-1889 USDA rows for "NORTH DAKOTA" are back_cast onto this row's start_year=1889 span rather than gapped out, which likely needs revisiting once/if a Dakota Territory entry is created.

### oq-territory-boundary-clean-split

**Whether pre-1889 territorial data maps cleanly onto the modern North Dakota boundary**

The routing decision behind this page assumed the USDA "NORTH DAKOTA" label for 1879-1888 corresponds cleanly to the modern state's boundary and not to some other division of Dakota Territory (e.g. county-level reporting that predates the formal North/South split line). No independent check was performed confirming that pre-1889 reporting geography exactly matches the post-1889 state boundary now carried by this polygon; if it does not, the earliest back_cast years could be misattributed by area.
