# Bragança (district of Portugal)

## Summary

Bragança is one of continental Portugal's 18 administrative districts, located in the far northeast in the historical region of Trás-os-Montes, bordering Spain. It was established as part of the 1835 Mouzinho da Silveira/Costa Cabral liberal reform that created Portugal's modern district system, replacing the old ancien-régime comarcas, and it has persisted as a stable administrative unit ever since — it is not a modern EU NUTS-nomenclature invention, unlike neighbouring entries such as Norte or Centro whose statistical identity only dates to 1979-1988. This entry is `type: subnational` because Portugal (PRT-1800-2025) holds sovereignty throughout; what this row exists to carry is a district-level statistical reporting territory that a data source measured separately from the national total and from Portugal's other 17 continental districts. The span 1835-2025 reflects the district's founding date and the open end_year of its national container, since a subnational unit cannot outlive the container it sits inside.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures data routed under unit_id PRT-PTBG / admin_name PT_BG ("Bragança"), a district-level statistical reporting unit for Portugal. The upstream routing decision found this unit distinct from all sibling Portuguese districts (including PT_FA/Faro) and from the national total PRT-1800-2025, which cannot be claimed for district-level years without double-claiming the same territory across every other Portuguese district simultaneously. The routing decision itself, however, proposed start_year 1989 by treating Bragança as if it followed the same NUTS-region convention branch as Norte, Centro and Algarve; the Portugal country convention explicitly distinguishes traditional district names (1835 floor) from EU NUTS-nomenclature region names (1979/1986-89 floor), and Bragança is a traditional district, not a NUTS-named region, so this page corrects the span to start at 1835. Historically, Bragança has been administered as a distinct district since that 1835 reform, which is itself the confirming source that this was a distinct, continuously-existing administrative entity rather than a artefact of modern EU statistical nomenclature.

## Decisions

### d-district-not-nuts-start-1835

**Start year set to 1835 (district reform), not 1986/1989 as the routing decision proposed**

The upstream routing decision proposed start_year 1989 (with a note that 1986 is the stated convention default), treating PT_BG (Bragança) as if it were a NUTS-nomenclature region like Norte or Centro. But the country convention for Portugal (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json) explicitly branches on vocabulary: 'traditional district names (ending in a city/province name matching one of the 18 continental districts, plus Açores/Madeira) follow the district rule' with start_year 1835, while only names matching EU NUTS nomenclature (Norte, Centro, Lisboa e Vale do Tejo, Alentejo, Algarve, and the two autonomous regions) follow the 1979/NUTS rule. Bragança is one of the 18 continental districts created by the 1835 reform, not a NUTS-named region, so the convention's own rule assigns it 1835, not 1986/1989. The three already-created sibling rows in the database (PRT-ALG-1986-2025, PRT-CENTRO-1986-2025, PRT-PT11-1986-2025) are all NUTS-named regions (Algarve, Centro, Norte) and correctly use 1986 under the NUTS branch; they are not precedent for a district-named unit like Bragança, and the routing decision's premise that sibling verdicts already settled on 1989/1986 for this unit was a misapplication of that precedent to the wrong vocabulary.

### d-code-pattern-subunit

**Followed the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end code pattern**

Chose PRT-BG-1835-2025 over a bespoke code because Bragança is an ordinary administrative district, not a historical territory with its own name independent of the current state (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad State). The dominant pattern (57 of 66 subnational rows, e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025) fits: ISO3 PRT, subunit code BG for Bragança, start_year 1835 and end_year 2025 matching the frontmatter exactly as the code-consistency gate requires.

### oq-1998-nuts-revision-boundary

**Did the 1998 NUTS III revision (Alto Trás-os-Montes) redraw Bragança's boundary?**

Bragança district corresponds closely to the NUTS III subregion historically called Alto Trás-os-Montes and later renamed/reconfigured as Terras de Trás-os-Montes in Portugal's post-1998/2002 NUTS revisions. It is not yet confirmed whether the district's own administrative boundary (the 1835 unit this entry actually tracks) has been redrawn at any point 1835-2025, or whether only the overlapping NUTS statistical layer changed while the district line stayed fixed. A present-day GADM feature would carry today's boundary; if the district line moved, using it as a proxy for the full 1835-2025 span would need a vintage-risk note the way MAN-1950-1955 documents its own proxy under-coverage.

### oq-data-coverage-vs-span

**What years does the actual routed data cover for this unit, and does it now fall entirely inside 1835-2025?**

The routing decision's harness concern reported 109 of 144 data years (76%) as unroutable under the previously proposed 1989 start, because most measured years for PT_BG predate 1989. Moving start_year to 1835 should resolve that mismatch since 1835 is well before any plausible data start for a 19th/20th-century district series, but this has not been re-run against the actual source rows for PT_BG to confirm zero remaining unroutable years. If any data years still fall outside 1835-2025, that would indicate either a different territory should have received them or the span needs further adjustment.
