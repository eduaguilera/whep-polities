# Madagascar

## Summary

Madagascar is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Madagascar from 1882 to the present. The start year 1882 reflects the beginning of French involvement. Madagascar became a French protectorate in 1885 and a colony in 1896. It became independent on 26 June 1960.

## Territorial extent

Madagascar is an island country in the Indian Ocean, east of the southeastern coast of Africa. Area 226,658 sq. miles (586,818 sq. km); population 13,000,000 (1994). Capital: Antananarivo. No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
