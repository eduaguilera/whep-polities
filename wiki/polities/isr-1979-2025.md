# Israel

## Summary

Israel as tracked by WHEP from 1979, reflecting the Camp David Accords and the return of the Sinai Peninsula to Egypt following the 1979 peace treaty. The state was proclaimed on 14 May 1948. Israel's boundaries remain contested, with the Golan Heights occupied since 1967 and the West Bank under dispute.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Israel is bounded by Lebanon (49 mi; 79 km), Syria (47 mi; 76 km), Jordan (148 mi; 238 km), and Egypt (165 mi; 266 km).
