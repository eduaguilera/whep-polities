# Marshall Islands


> **Polygon area declared 2026-08-06, and `polygon_status` lowered to `estimate` because it is one.**
>
> ```
> GADM 4.1 adm0, unsimplified    300.28 km²
> the same polygon as shipped    299.99 km²   (-0.1%)
> official land area             181 km²
> shipped / official             1.66x
> ```
>
> `polygon_area_km2` states the **official land area**, not what the polygon measures. That is the
> field's meaning ([wiki/README.md](../README.md)): it is a figure about the *territory*, which is
> what makes `validate_polygons` check A a test rather than a tautology.
>
> **The shipped figure here was WRONG until 2026-08-13 (issue 71).** It read `252.35 km²`,
> `ratio 1.39x`, because `build_database.py` thinned every geometry with
> `SimplifyPreserveTopology(0.01)` — about 1.1 km at the equator — before writing it, and the
> Marshall Islands' 1,156 islands and islets are almost all smaller than that. The build lost 16% of
> the polygon; GADM never did. The source polygon has always measured 300.28 km², so the real ratio
> is **1.66x**, not 1.39x — the largest overshoot in the whole micro-territory class. The build now
> carries an area budget and `scripts/validate_simplification_loss.py` asserts that no shipped
> polygon moves more than 5% from its source.
>
> `polygon_status` stays **`estimate`** and the reason still holds, more strongly than before: at
> 1.66x the divergence is far outside check A's 25% tolerance, so `estimate` — the polygon
> approximates the territory — is the honest value, and check A reports it in every run instead of
> failing on it.
>
> **This is a class, not a defect in this row**, and the class was re-measured after the
> simplification fix: 33 of the 38 `gadm-4.1-adm0` micro-territories that declare an area measure
> **more** than their declared land area, and this row is the extreme. The full table and the
> reversed conclusion are on [MDV-1800-2025](mdv-1800-2025.md), kept in one place so it cannot go
> stale in six.
>
> **Any per-km² figure computed on this row is unusable.** At this magnitude the absolute
> uncertainty is comparable to the territory.
## Summary

The Marshall Islands is a Pacific island nation comprising 29 atolls and 5 islands in the central Pacific. WHEP tracks it from 1874 to 2025. The 1874 start year likely reflects early German commercial/administrative contact; Germany formally established a protectorate in 1885. The islands subsequently passed through German (1885–1914), Japanese mandate (1919–1944), and U.S. Trust Territory (1944–1986) administration before becoming a freely associated state in 1986 and entering the COW system in 1991 (UN membership). CShapes records one time-step beginning 1991-09-17 (UN membership/COW entry). COW ccode=983 (stateabb=MSI); state-system entry 1991-09-17.

## Open questions

### oq-1874-start-year

The WHEP start year 1874 is not explained in the CSV (notes=NA). Germany established a formal protectorate over the Marshall Islands in 1885, not 1874. The 1874 date may reflect early German trading-station presence or a data entry artifact. The rationale should be identified and documented.

### oq-pre-1991-polygon

CShapes records no Marshall Islands polygon before 1991. The polygon for 1874–1991 is back-projected and covers multiple administrative regimes (German, Japanese, American). Whether a single polygon is appropriate for this entire period, or whether separate polygons should be used for the different colonial phases, should be assessed.
