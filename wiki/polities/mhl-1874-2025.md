# Marshall Islands

## Summary

The Marshall Islands is a Pacific island nation comprising 29 atolls and 5 islands in the central Pacific. WHEP tracks it from 1874 to 2025. The 1874 start year likely reflects early German commercial/administrative contact; Germany formally established a protectorate in 1885. The islands subsequently passed through German (1885–1914), Japanese mandate (1919–1944), and U.S. Trust Territory (1944–1986) administration before becoming a freely associated state in 1986 and entering the COW system in 1991 (UN membership). CShapes records one time-step beginning 1991-09-17 (UN membership/COW entry). COW ccode=983 (stateabb=MSI); state-system entry 1991-09-17.

## Open questions

### oq-1874-start-year

The WHEP start year 1874 is not explained in the CSV (notes=NA). Germany established a formal protectorate over the Marshall Islands in 1885, not 1874. The 1874 date may reflect early German trading-station presence or a data entry artifact. The rationale should be identified and documented.

### oq-pre-1991-polygon

CShapes records no Marshall Islands polygon before 1991. The polygon for 1874–1991 is back-projected and covers multiple administrative regimes (German, Japanese, American). Whether a single polygon is appropriate for this entire period, or whether separate polygons should be used for the different colonial phases, should be assessed.
