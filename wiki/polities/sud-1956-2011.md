# Sudan (1956-2011)

## Summary

Sudan (1956-2011) covers the period from Sudanese independence on 1 January 1956 until the secession of South Sudan on 9 July 2011. Sudan was the largest country in Africa during this period (area: 967,500 sq mi). The country experienced persistent civil war between the predominantly Arab-Muslim north and the Christian/animist south, leading ultimately to the Comprehensive Peace Agreement (2005) and the independence referendum in South Sudan (2011).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Sudan during this period encompassed the territory of both modern-day Sudan and South Sudan. It was bounded by Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Zaire/DRC, the Central African Republic, Chad, and Libya.
