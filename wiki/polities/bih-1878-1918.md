# Bosnia and Herzegovina (Austro-Hungarian administration)

## Summary

Bosnia and Herzegovina under Austro-Hungarian rule: occupied in 1878 under the Congress of Berlin while nominally Ottoman, annexed in 1908 as a condominium of Austria and Hungary, until the Dual Monarchy's collapse in 1918. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §bosnia](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#bosnia)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report Bosnia and Herzegovina for 1911 and 1913 (`bosnia and herzegovina`, 2 pairs); no row was the territory before 1992. The rows come from post-war yearbooks (iia_1921/1925) but the boundary is the same either way. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** CShapes 2.0 feature 346 (Bosnia-Herzegovina, 1992): the 1878 occupation zone's boundaries are those of the modern state, which kept them through Yugoslavia. The Sandzak of Novi Pazar, garrisoned by Austria-Hungary until 1908, was not part of the administered territory and is not drawn. Measured: **51,535 km2** (ESRI:54034). Share of the polygon inside each container: AUH-1866-1908 99%, OTT-1800-1886 95%, AUH-1908-1918 100%.

## Polygon status

`proxy` -- CShapes 2.0 feature 346 at its 1992 step; the outline is a stand-in for the historical boundary, as described above.
