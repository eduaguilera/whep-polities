# Nicaragua

## Summary

Nicaragua is a Central American country bounded by Honduras to the north, the Caribbean Sea to the east, Costa Rica to the south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Biger 1995 records its area as 50,054 sq. miles (129,640 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 4,200,000 in 1994 [biger-1995 &sect;nicaragua](../sources/biger-1995.md#nicaragua). Nicaragua was discovered by Columbus in 1502, became independent in 1821, briefly joined the Mexican Empire, then formed a political union with Costa Rica, Honduras, and El Salvador in 1823 before gaining full independence in 1838. WHEP tracks Nicaragua as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory remained essentially constant.

## Territorial extent

Nicaragua has an area of 50,054 sq. miles (129,640 sq. km.) [biger-1995 &sect;nicaragua](../sources/biger-1995.md#nicaragua). It is bounded by Honduras (573 miles; 922 km.) to the north and Costa Rica (192 miles; 309 km.) to the south. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

**The 1894 Mosquito Coast reincorporation is missing from this page, and it costs exactly one row (added 2026-08-18).**
This row treats Nicaragua's territory as constant from 1800, but in 1894 Zelaya annexed the formerly
British-protected Miskito Reserve on the Caribbean coast — roughly a fifth of national area. Statistics
reported as "Nicaragua" before 1894 would exclude it.

Measured: `mitchell|nicaragua` runs 1893-1960, 146 rows, of which **1** predates 1894. So the reporting
territory is not strictly constant across the span, and the exposure is a single 1893 row. Recorded
rather than split: a one-row polity is not worth creating, and `nicaragua|mitchell|1893-1960` is
`best_available` for this reason among others.
