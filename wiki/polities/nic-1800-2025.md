# Nicaragua

## Summary

Nicaragua is a Central American country bounded by Honduras to the north, the Caribbean Sea to the east, Costa Rica to the south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Biger 1995 records its area as 50,054 sq. miles (129,640 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 4,200,000 in 1994 [biger-1995 &sect;nicaragua](../sources/biger-1995.md#nicaragua). Nicaragua was discovered by Columbus in 1502, became independent in 1821, briefly joined the Mexican Empire, then formed a political union with Costa Rica, Honduras, and El Salvador in 1823 before gaining full independence in 1838. WHEP tracks Nicaragua as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory remained essentially constant.
