# Ottoman Empire (1908–1912)

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire as a territorial-economic unit
from **1908** to **1912** — a short, four-year terminal row that
captures the brief interval between the formal Austro-Hungarian
annexation of Bosnia (with the related Bulgarian independence and
Cretan union with Greece in the same year) and the outbreak of
the First Balkan War. Both boundaries are real territorial events,
making this the clearest application of the WHEP polity-definition
rule [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition) among the
three Ottoman rows.

- **1908 start:** Austria-Hungary formally annexed Bosnia and
  Herzegovina on 5 October 1908; Bulgaria declared independence
  the same day; Crete declared union with Greece
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1908).
- **1912 end:** The Italo-Turkish War ended with the Treaty of
  Ouchy; Libya passed to Italy
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1911–1912](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1911-1912). Weeks later the
  First Balkan War began, which would cost the Ottoman Empire
  "83 percent of their European territory and almost 70 percent
  of their European population"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1912–1913](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1912-1913).

Predecessor: [ott-1886-1908](ott-1886-1908.md). Successor chain forks: the CSV
lists `TUR-1913-1914; SAU-1924-1932` as successors [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
The absence of an intermediate `OTT-1912-1918` row is worth
noting — see [oq-1912-1920-gap](#oq-1912-1920-gap).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV (`polygon_source`),
  COW variant [log decision-cshapes-is-cow-based](../log.md#decision-cshapes-is-cow-based).
  The polygon reflects the **post-Bosnia-annexation** configuration:
  Bosnia-Herzegovina is no longer counted in Ottoman territory,
  and Libya is still in (for part of the row — the annexation to
  Italy happens mid-row in October 1912).
- **Mid-row territorial change.** The Italo-Turkish War (1911 →
  October 1912) falls *inside* this row's window. The Ottoman
  Empire lost Libya and the Dodecanese in November 1912
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1911–1912](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1911-1912). A strict
  application of the WHEP polity-definition rule would say this
  is a substantial territorial change and should trigger a
  split within 1908–1912. The row is short enough (4 years)
  that the repo appears to have chosen not to. See
  [oq-libya-mid-row-change](#oq-libya-mid-row-change).
- **Row ends before the Balkan Wars.** The Balkan Wars began in
  October 1912 and ran into 1913; the Ottoman Empire lost
  almost all of its remaining European territory
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1912–1913](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1912-1913). These losses are
  *not* represented in this row — it ends at 1912 — nor in any
  obvious OTT successor. The repo transitions directly from
  OTT-1908-1912 to TUR-1913-1914. See [oq-1912-1920-gap](#oq-1912-1920-gap).

## Sourced claims

- **5 October 1908 (start of row): three simultaneous events.**
  "Bulgarian Declaration of Independence on 5 October"; formal
  Austro-Hungarian annexation of Bosnia-Herzegovina; Cretan
  union with Greece [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1908).
- **1911–1912: Italo-Turkish War and Ottoman loss of Libya.**
  Biger gives this event in two phases: **1911 invasion and
  initial Italian claim** — "In 1911, the Cyrenaica, Fezzan
  and Tripolitania regions of northern central Africa were
  passed from Ottoman to Italian control"
  [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya) (LIBYA–CHAD, p.360) and
  "[Italy] established itself in Libya in 1911"
  [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya) (LIBYA–SUDAN, p.361); then **1912 formal
  treaty** — "In 1912 Italy captured Libya and proceeded to
  destroy its tribal foundation" [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya) (LIBYA
  main, p.359). Wikipedia corroborates: "Italian forces took
  those areas on 5 November" [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1911–1912](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1911-1912). Biger's two-phase framing is consistent with
  1911 = Italian invasion (September) and annexation
  declaration (5 November 1911), 1912 = Treaty of Ouchy
  (18 October 1912) ending the Italo-Turkish War. **This event
  falls inside this row's window**, starting in September 1911
  and concluding in October 1912 — a few weeks before the row
  itself ends.
- **1910-05-19 Franco-Ottoman boundary convention.**
  Immediately preceding the Italo-Turkish War, France and the
  Ottoman Empire formally delimited the present-day
  Libya–Tunisia boundary: "on 19 May 1910, a Franco-Turkish
  convention delimited most of the present-day Libya–Tunisia
  boundary, which was then demarcated with pillars by a joint
  commission during 1910–1911" [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya) (p.361).
  Load-bearing for the Ottoman continuity argument: the
  Ottoman Empire was still formally delimiting territorial
  boundaries with European powers **less than two years**
  before losing Libya. This weakens the "just polygon
  availability" framing of the 1911–1912 transition — it was
  a real, rapid territorial change from a previously-stable
  border.
- **1912–1913 (after row): First and Second Balkan Wars.** "loss
  of 83 percent of their European territory and almost 70
  percent of their European population"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1912–1913](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1912-1913). These losses
  start in October 1912 (within the row's window) but are
  attributed to the next WHEP row in the chain (TUR-1913-1914),
  which is the next row in time but is coded as a separate
  polity.
- **COW state-system membership.** Still one continuous
  tenure for `TUR, 640, 1816-01-01 → 2024-12-31`
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). COW does not mark any
  of the boundaries WHEP uses (1886, 1908, 1912).

## Open questions


### oq-libya-mid-row-change

**Partially resolved 2026-04-11** by
[log biger-ottoman-batch-ingest](../log.md#biger-ottoman-batch-ingest). Biger 1995 gives a clean
two-phase framing: Italy "established itself in Libya in 1911"
(LIBYA–SUDAN, p.361) and "In 1912 Italy captured Libya"
(LIBYA main, p.359) [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya). The invasion began
September 1911; the annexation declaration was 5 November 1911;
the Treaty of Ouchy ending the war was signed 18 October 1912.
So the Libya transfer is a real, rapid, >1.5 million km²
territorial change *within* this row's 1908–1912 window —
larger than the entire Ottoman Empire's contemporary European
territory, and vastly above any plausible "substantial
change" threshold under the WHEP polity-definition rule.

This means: either (a) WHEP needs an explicit "end-of-row
grace period" convention for territorial changes in the final
months of a row, (b) the row should have been split at
September 1911 or 18 October 1912, or (c) WHEP needs to
permit polygon-source constraints to override the territorial
rule (a separate general question also flagged under
[oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory](#oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory) on [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md)).
Biger's two-phase framing rules out option (c) as the
*historical* justification — the Libya change is clearly
substantial. The remaining question is a repo-wide rule the
user has to decide.

Biger's corroborating detail: the 1910-05-19 Franco-Ottoman
boundary convention (LIBYA–TUNISIA, p.361) shows the Ottoman
side of this border was *still being formally delimited on the
ground* by a joint commission in 1910–1911, which makes the
case for a real rapid transition stronger.

### oq-1912-1920-gap

There is no OTT row between 1912 and
the TUR rows that start in 1913. The Balkan Wars, WWI, and
the Turkish War of Independence are represented entirely
under `TUR-*` codes, not `OTT-*` codes. This may be a
reasonable editorial choice (modern Türkiye starts in 1913)
or it may obscure the continuity. The choice should be
recorded in a `decision`-kind log entry explaining why the
rename from OTT to TUR happens at 1913 rather than at 1922
(abolition of the sultanate, per
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1922](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1922)) or 1923 (proclamation
of the Republic of Türkiye). Under the polity-definition
rule, a rename is not a split, so OTT-1908-1912 and
TUR-1913-1914 are the same polity — but they have different
codes.
