# Illes Balears (province of Spain)

## Summary

Illes Balears (the Balearic Islands: Mallorca, Menorca, Eivissa/Ibiza and Formentera, plus minor islets) is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, and since 1983 also coextensive with the Balearic Islands autonomous community -- one of the small set of Spanish provinces (with Asturias, Cantabria, Murcia, La Rioja, Madrid, Navarre and the Canary archipelago) where the 1833 province boundary and the post-Franco autonomous community boundary are identical. This entry is type: subnational because Illes Balears is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated the Balearics separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES53, for over a century and a half. Why this entry exists: input data is the routing decision for unit_id ESP-ES53, capturing valued statistical rows for the Balearic Islands under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (A Coruna/ES111, Madrid, Asturias/ES120), following the system default start_year of 1833 (not a Canary-Islands-style departure case) and an open end_year of 2025 for a still-current unit. Previously matched to nothing -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly that no existing polity represents the Balearic Islands, and the fallback in the absence of this row would have been the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, collapsing a province-level series into a country-level one, rejected for the same reason as for Asturias, A Coruna and Madrid. What confirms distinctness: Illes Balears' status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES53) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since the 1833 reform is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling ESP province rows.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Copied/registered from mapspain-ign feature cpro=07 (registered_source_feature route), the same IGN current-day province boundary layer already used for ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025, keyed by INE province numeric code (Illes Balears = 07). polygon_status is set to proxy rather than assigned because, as with the mainland sibling rows, this is a current-day administrative boundary reused across the full 1833-2025 span on the reasoning that Spanish provincial boundaries have been essentially stable since the 1833 reform -- valid for mainland provinces with contiguous land borders, but not independently verified here for an archipelago (see open question on archipelago vintage risk below). The routing decision's polygon_reasoning also flags an unresolved discrepancy: sources.yaml marks mapspain-ign as present_locally=False, while this session's own usage table shows seven ESP provinces already resolved through it -- flagged rather than silently assumed either way (see open question below). Territory description: Illes Balears occupies the Balearic archipelago in the western Mediterranean, roughly 90-300 km east of the Spanish mainland (nearest point near Valencia/Denia), comprising Mallorca (the largest island, capital Palma), Menorca, Eivissa (Ibiza) and Formentera, plus minor islets such as Cabrera to the south of Mallorca. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 4,992 km2 -- this is the region's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon. On a modern map it is the same outline as the Balearic Islands autonomous community, since (unlike Galicia's four provinces) Illes Balears was never subdivided among multiple provinces after 1833 -- it has always been a single province covering the whole archipelago.

## Predecessors and successors

Illes Balears has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the archipelago was organized under the Antiguo Regimen's own governmental structure (historically part of the Crown of Aragon and later a captaincy general), distinct in administrative form from the 1833 province even though the territory and name persisted, and this database does not model sub-kingdom Balearic divisions at that granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain (and, since 1983, also the Balearic Islands autonomous community) as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention established for the other Spanish NUTS3 units.

## Sourced claims

- INE and Eurostat both report Illes Balears as a NUTS3 statistical unit (ES53) with continuous province-level data collection since the 19th century, consistent with its creation as one of the 50 provinces in the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform.
- The routing decision for unit ESP-ES53 records that no existing polity represented the Balearic Islands specifically, that it is not a departure case like the Canary Islands (which split later), and that the default 1833 start rule therefore applies, matching the treatment given to every other ESP sibling unit routed this session.
- The polygon_reasoning for this decision records that mapspain-ign's id_column is cpro (INE province numeric codes), that Illes Balears' INE province code is 07, and that seven ESP provinces have already been resolved through this same source in this session despite sources.yaml marking it present_locally=False -- a discrepancy the decision explicitly flags rather than silently resolving.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-as-abbreviation

**Used the letter code ES53 pattern's short form rather than the province numeric code, matching ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern for Spanish provinces already established by ESP-AS-1833-2025 (Asturias), ESP-CO-1833-2025 (A Coruna) and ESP-MD-1833-2025 (Madrid) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short letter code rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES53) or the INE province numeric code (07). This page mints ESP-IB-1833-2025 using IB, the modern ISO 3166-2 subdivision code (ES-IB) and INE's own two-letter abbreviation for Illes Balears, rather than PM (from the historic 'Baleares'/'Palma de Mallorca' provincial naming still seen in some older statistical series) or the digit code 07, so a future author can predict the code from the ISO subdivision list without a lookup table. This departs slightly from the routing decision's own container_code label (which used the country-level ESP-1800-2025 verbatim, matching) but follows precedent for the subunit token itself, which the routing decision left unspecified beyond the NUTS id ES53.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-source-not-acquired

**The mapspain-ign polygon source's local presence is disputed between sources.yaml and this session's usage table**

The routing decision's polygon_reasoning flags a direct contradiction: sources.yaml marks mapspain-ign as present_locally=False, yet the repository's own usage table shows seven ESP provinces already resolved through it in this session, including entries created moments before this one. This page proceeds on the assumption that the source is in fact available (as the majority of evidence suggests) and cites feature id 07 (Illes Balears' INE province code) accordingly, but if sources.yaml is correct and the other resolutions were premature, this page's polygon_status should be downgraded from proxy/registered_source_feature to unassigned until provinces.gpkg is confirmed on disk. A future author should re-check whether ESP-33-1833-2025 (Asturias, cpro 33) and the other six sibling rows actually carry working geometry before trusting this one.

### oq-archipelago-vintage-risk

**Unlike mainland provinces, Illes Balears' 1833 boundary is an archipelago whose administrative composition (islands + islets) may not be captured identically by a current-day IGN polygon**

The vintage-risk argument used for mainland provinces (essentially unchanged boundaries since 1833, so low risk despite the ~190-year gap) rests on contiguous land borders that rarely move. Illes Balears is an archipelago (Mallorca, Menorca, Eivissa/Ibiza, Formentera, plus minor islets such as Cabrera, which was only constituted as its own municipality/national park in 1991-actions that could affect how mapspain-ign's cpro=07 polygon draws the unit's edge, e.g. whether Cabrera's surrounding waters or islet boundary are included consistently across the full 1833-2025 span. This has not been checked against IGN's actual feature definition and should be verified once the source is confirmed present locally (see oq-polygon-source-not-acquired).
