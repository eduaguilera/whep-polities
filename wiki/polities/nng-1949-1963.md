# Netherlands New Guinea (1949-1963)

## Summary

The western half of New Guinea, **retained by the Netherlands when the rest of
the Dutch East Indies became independent Indonesia in December 1949**, and
administered as a separate Dutch territory until it was transferred to Indonesia.

## Why this entry exists

Found 2026-07-24 while documenting [idn-1949-1969](idn-1949-1969.md). Three
things pointed the same way:

1. The layer-B data carries a **"Netherlands New Guinea"** label (3 `fao1952`
   rows, 1949-1951) that was **unmatched** — no polity existed for it.
2. The arithmetic in the Indonesian chain has a hole of exactly this size:
   CShapes gwcode 850 measures **1,877,243 km²** for the Dutch East Indies but
   only **1,466,882 km²** for independent Indonesia 1949-1969. The difference,
   **410,361 km²**, is precisely CShapes gwcode 851.
3. CShapes carries that territory as its **own entity** (gwcode 851) with three
   time-steps — 1949-1962, 1962-1963, 1963-1969 — tracking Dutch administration,
   the UNTEA interregnum, and Indonesian administration.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 gwcode **851**, feature year 1955, **410,361 km²** —
  an exact historical feature for this entity, not a proxy.
- **Territory constant** across the row: the same landmass throughout; only the
  administering power changed.
- `type: colonial` — a Dutch overseas territory, not a sovereign state; it has no
  COW code of its own.

## Sourced claims

- **1949-12: Indonesian independence excludes western New Guinea.** The Dutch
  retained the territory at the transfer of sovereignty, which is why CShapes
  splits gwcode 851 off from 850 at exactly this date
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1962: New York Agreement, UNTEA interregnum.** Administration passed to a UN
  temporary authority; CShapes records a 1962-1963 time-step accordingly
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1963: transfer to Indonesian administration.** Biger 1995 dates the handover
  to 1963, which is this row's end year
  [biger-1995](../sources/biger-1995.md).

## Open questions

### oq-1963-to-1969-attribution

**The sources disagree by six years about when this territory becomes
Indonesian.** Biger 1995 dates the handover to **1963** (the UNTEA-to-Indonesia
transfer under the 1962 New York Agreement); CShapes keeps gwcode 851 as a
distinct feature until **1969** and only enlarges Indonesia's own polygon at
1969-09-17, the Act of Free Choice.

This row follows Biger and ends at 1963. The consequence is that for **1963-1969**
Indonesia administered a territory that [idn-1949-1969](idn-1949-1969.md)'s
polygon excludes, so Indonesian data in that window may cover 410,361 km² more
than the row it is matched to — about 28% of that row's area. No polity currently
holds those six years. Resolving it means either extending this row to 1969, or
splitting `IDN-1949-1969` at 1963. Needs a decision.
