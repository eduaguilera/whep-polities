# Netherlands New Guinea (1949-1963)

## Summary

The western half of New Guinea, **retained by the Netherlands when the rest of
the Dutch East Indies became independent Indonesia in December 1949**, and
administered as a separate Dutch territory until it was transferred to Indonesia.

## Why this entry exists

Found 2026-07-24 while documenting the Indonesian chain (then
`IDN-1949-1969`, now [idn-1949-1963](idn-1949-1963.md)). Three things pointed
the same way:

1. The layer-B data carries a **"Netherlands New Guinea"** label (3 `fao1952`
   rows, 1949-1951) that was **unmatched** — no polity existed for it.
2. The arithmetic in the Indonesian chain has a hole of exactly this size:
   CShapes gwcode 850 measures **1,877,243 km²** for the Dutch East Indies but
   only **1,466,882 km²** for independent Indonesia 1949-1969. The difference,
   **410,361 km²**, is precisely CShapes gwcode 851.
3. CShapes carries that territory as its **own entity** (gwcode 851) with three
   time-steps — 1949-1962, 1962-1963, 1963-1969 — tracking Dutch administration,
   the UNTEA interregnum, and Indonesian administration.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 gwcode **851**, feature year 1955, **410,361 km²** —
  an exact historical feature for this entity, not a proxy.
- **Territory constant** across the row: the same landmass throughout. The only
  change inside the span is the administering power (Dutch until 1962-08-14, then
  the UNTEA interregnum), which under the WHEP definition is not a row boundary —
  which is why the three CShapes time-steps for feature 851 are one WHEP row.
- `type: colonial` — a Dutch overseas territory, not a sovereign state; it has no
  COW code of its own.

## Sourced claims

- **1949-12: Indonesian independence excludes western New Guinea.** The Dutch
  retained the territory at the transfer of sovereignty, which is why CShapes
  splits gwcode 851 off from 850 at exactly this date
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1962: New York Agreement, UNTEA interregnum.** Administration passed to a UN
  temporary authority; CShapes records a 1962-1963 time-step accordingly
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **1963-05-01: transfer to Indonesian administration.** Biger 1995 dates the
  handover to 1963 [biger-1995](../sources/biger-1995.md), and CShapes' own change
  log for gwcode 851 opens a time-step on exactly **1963-05-01** (direct read of
  `data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`, not a wiki-source citation). This is
  this row's end year, and `end_year` is exclusive, so the row covers 1949-1962.

## The 1963-vs-1969 question, settled (2026-08-14)

This row was extended to `end_year: 1969` on 2026-07-24 on the reading that
Biger (1963) and CShapes (1969) disagree about when the territory became
Indonesian, and that following one source end-to-end was safer than splitting the
difference. **Measuring CShapes rather than paraphrasing it dissolves the
disagreement**, and `end_year` is back to 1963 — the year the code has said all
along.

What the shapefile actually contains for gwcode **851** (measured in ESRI:54034):

| time-step | km² |
|---|---|
| 1949-12-29 → 1962-08-14 | 410,361 |
| 1962-08-15 → 1963-04-30 | 410,361 |
| 1963-05-01 → 1969-09-16 | 410,361 |

Three steps, **geometrically identical** (`shapely.equals` is True across them,
symmetric difference 0 km²). So CShapes does **not** date the transfer to 1969: it
opens a step on **1963-05-01**, the day Indonesia took over from UNTEA, which is
Biger's date to the month. The only thing 1969-09-16/17 marks in this file is when
feature 851 stops existing as a separate *sovereignty* unit — the Act of Free
Choice. Under the WHEP definition, sovereignty recognition is not a territorial
event and cannot set a row boundary.

And the objection that no 1963 polygon exists for Indonesia is false, also by
measurement: gwcode 850's `1969-09-17` step (1,877,243.324 km²) is *exactly* the
union of its `1949-12-29` step (1,466,882 km²) and feature 851 (410,361 km²) —
symmetric difference **0.0 km²**. The geometry the enlarged Indonesia needs from
1963 is already in the source; only the source's stamp on it says 1969.

Consequences, all of them dating changes rather than geometry changes:

- this row ends 1963 (covers 1949-1962) and its successor is
  [idn-1963-1976](idn-1963-1976.md);
- `IDN-1949-1969` becomes [idn-1949-1963](idn-1949-1963.md) and `IDN-1969-1976`
  becomes [idn-1963-1976](idn-1963-1976.md), both retired with redirects;
- the 410,361 km² this row held for 1963-1968 — with **zero data rows** attached in
  that window, its only data being 3 `fao1952` rows for 1949-1951 — moves to
  Indonesia, which is who administered and reported it. Indonesian data for
  1963-1968 previously sat on a polygon 21.9% smaller than Indonesia's actual
  territory (410,361 / 1,877,243).
