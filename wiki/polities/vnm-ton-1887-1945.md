# Tonkin (within French Indochina, 1887-1945)

## Summary

Tonkin (Bắc Kỳ) was the northern of the three Vietnamese territories of French Indochina: the Red
River delta around Hanoi and Haiphong, and the mountains between it and China. France made it a
protectorate by the 1883-1884 Treaties of Huế, and from 1887 it was one of the members of the
Indochinese Union, beside Annam, Cochinchina, Cambodia and (from 1893) Laos. The French
protectorate ended with the Japanese coup of 9 March 1945.

This row is `subnational`: it is a part of [vnm-1887-1954](vnm-1887-1954.md), which is the three
Vietnamese territories together, and it exists so that data reported for Tonkin alone can sit on
Tonkin's ground instead of on all of Vietnam.

## Why this entry exists

Issue 687 matched every layer-B iia series cell by cell against the raw IIA extract
(`harmonized_data.xlsx`), on the exact `(unit, year or period, value)` triple. The iia label
`viet nam` (273 rows, 1925-1944, all routed to VNM-1887-1954) is not Vietnam. For nine items, **every
cell** in both the area and the production series is raw **`french tonkin`**:

| item | ha cells | t cells | matching `french tonkin` |
|---|---|---|---|
| coffee, green | 16 | 14 | 30 of 30 |
| cotton lint | 17 | 17 | 34 of 34 |
| cotton seed | 17 | 15 | 32 of 32 |
| groundnuts, with shell | 17 | 16 | 33 of 33 |
| jute | 13 | 10 | 23 of 23 |
| other berries (a mis-coded item) | 17 | 7 | 24 of 24 |
| sesame seed | 9 | 10 | 19 of 19 |
| tea | 17 | 12 | 29 of 29 |
| tobacco, unmanufactured | 16 | 16 | 32 of 32 |
| beans, dry | 1 | 1 | 2 of 2 |

These 258 rows are moved here by item-scoped corrections in
`data/final/source_label_item_corrections.csv`. An alias cannot do it, because the same label and
years also carry series that are not Tonkin's:

- silk-worm cocoons and raw centrifugal sugar match **`french cochinchina`** most often (3 of 6 and
  4 of 7 cells), with the rest unmatched. They stay on VNM-1887-1954, which contains Cochinchina.
- castor beans: the production cell matches `french tonkin` and the area cell does not, so the item
  is left where it is.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: the union of the 25 GADM 4.1 admin-1 provinces of
Vietnam north of Thanh Hóa (Hà Giang, Cao Bằng, Lào Cai, Lai Châu, Bắc Kạn, Tuyên Quang, Lạng Sơn,
Yên Bái, Điện Biên, Thái Nguyên, Vĩnh Phúc, Bắc Giang, Phú Thọ, Quảng Ninh, Sơn La, Bắc Ninh, Hà Nội,
Hải Dương, Hưng Yên, Hải Phòng, Hoà Bình, Hà Nam, Thái Bình, Nam Định, Ninh Bình). It measures
**115,912 km²** (ESRI:54034).
[gadm-4.1 §What it adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

- **The southern line.** Tonkin's border with Annam ran between Ninh Bình and Thanh Hóa, and the
  modern provinces still follow it. Thanh Hóa, Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh were Annam, so they are left out.
  This is why the North Vietnam polygon (CShapes 816 at 1954-1975, 155,654 km², cut at the 17th
  parallel) cannot be used: it holds about 40,000 km² of northern Annam as well.
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)
- **The north-west.** Lai Châu and Điện Biên (the Sip Song Chau Tai), occupied by France in
  1888-1889, were administered from Tonkin, so they are included.
- **Against the stated area.** The area usually quoted for Tonkin in French colonial statistics is
  about 115,700 km², 0.2% below this union. That figure has not been re-checked against a scan
  here, so it corroborates the composition and is not relied on.
- **Why `proxy`.** The provinces are modern. The frontier with China is the 1887-1895 convention
  line, which the modern border follows with small later adjustments, and internal province lines
  inside Tonkin do not matter for the union.

## Polygon status

`proxy` — constructed union of modern provinces, 115,912 km². See *Territorial extent*.

**No declared area.** `polygon_area_km2` is left empty: the 115,700 km² colonial figure has not been
checked against a scan, and declaring the polygon's own 115,912 km² would compare it with itself.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1887 Tonkin was a French protectorate outside any row this database carries
for Vietnam; after 1945 its ground is in the Democratic Republic of Vietnam
([drv-1954-1975](drv-1954-1975.md)) once that row begins. The row is a sub-unit, so chain links run
through its container.

## Decisions

- **Span 1887-1945.** The row starts with the Indochinese Union (1887), which is when its container
  begins, rather than with the 1884 protectorate, so that it is inside its container for its whole
  span. No layer-B row is earlier than 1925. It ends with the 1945 coup.
- **Only the fingerprinted items move.** The corrections name the ten items above. Any new item
  under iia `viet nam` stays on VNM-1887-1954 until it is checked.
- **Cochinchina after 1887 is not created.** Two series match `french cochinchina` at 50-57%, which
  is not enough to move them.
