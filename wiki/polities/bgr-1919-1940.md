# Bulgaria (1919-1940)

## Summary

This row tracks Bulgaria for **22 years**, the interwar period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Neuilly Bulgaria. Lost Western Thrace to Greece, western border
territories to Yugoslavia. ~103,000 km². Interwar revisionist politics.
End date 1940: Treaty of Craiova (7 September 1940) -- Romania returned
Southern Dobruja to Bulgaria.

Predecessor: [bgr-1918-1919](bgr-1918-1919.md) -- Treaty of Neuilly.
