# Arkansas (state of the United States)

## Summary

Arkansas, one of the fifty states of the United States, admitted to the Union on June 15, 1836, out of the Arkansas Territory (organized 1819 from the Missouri Territory). This entry is `type: subnational` because it does not compete with the national USA chain for sovereignty: Arkansas has never been independently sovereign (its brief 1861-1865 membership in the Confederacy is not itself a distinct polity row in this database), and the row exists purely to carry state-level reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the state rather than the national level. The span 1836-2025 tracks continuous statehood, with no interruption or territorial change to the state's external boundary, through four successive eras of the national USA polity chain (USA-1803-1848 through USA-1959-2025).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Arkansas as a distinct subnational reporting unit within the United States, created by the agent-harness pipeline processing the administrative unit USA-ARKANSAS ("Arkansas", country USA). No prior polity row existed for Arkansas at the state level -- the existing USA chain (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) covers the country as a whole, so any Arkansas-specific statistics would previously have had no state-level home and would either be absent from the database or folded into national USA totals, a mismatch whenever a source reports Arkansas separately from all-U.S. figures. As with the Alabama and California precedents created in the same harness pass, the routing decision does not name a specific source, row count, or year range of data driving this creation -- its routing_reasoning instead frames this as adding the state as a real, distinct administrative entity under the country convention's default rule (state start_year = year of admission), with no back-cast segment needed because 1836 precedes the data's own 1866 start. What confirms Arkansas is a distinct reporting/administrative entity is uncontroversial: it is one of the fifty states, with its own government and long-standing use as a state-level reporting unit in economic and demographic series (e.g., USDA, Census Bureau, and Mitchell-style historical compilations). The open questions below flag that the specific data motivating this row's creation was not supplied in the routing decision, and that Arkansas's 1861-1865 Confederate/secession period is not separately represented.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm1` source, feature `USA.4_1`, assigned directly (not a proxy) because this is a 1:1 match between the administrative unit and a GADM admin-1 feature already registered and locally present in this database's GeoPackage. `polygon_status: assigned`.

**Area:** measured from the attached geometry at approximately 137,732 km2 (Arkansas's commonly cited land+water area is about 137,732 km2 / 53,179 sq mi). This is a measurement of the attached polygon, consistent with Arkansas's real-world extent, not an independent territorial claim.

**Territory description:** Arkansas sits in the south-central United States, bordered by Missouri to the north, Tennessee and Mississippi across the Mississippi River to the east, Louisiana to the south, and Texas and Oklahoma to the west -- readily locatable on any modern map as the state directly west of the Mississippi River between Memphis and the Louisiana line. Its external boundary has been essentially fixed since admission in 1836 (aside from minor 19th-century river-boundary adjustments common to Mississippi River states, which are not separately tracked here), so the modern GADM boundary is treated as a reasonable proxy for the full 1836-2025 span, per the routing decision's "Low" vintage-risk assessment. This claim of boundary stability since 1836 has not been independently corroborated against a historical source in authoring this page; see the open question on vintage risk.

## Predecessors and successors

Arkansas has no predecessor or successor polity row in this database: it enters as a full U.S. state in 1836 and, per the routing decision, its span runs open/current through 2025 with no successor. Arkansas Territory (1819-1836), organized from the southern part of the Missouri Territory, is not itself represented as a polity row, so there is no predecessor edge to record. Arkansas's 1861-1865 secession and Confederate membership, and its 1868 formal Reconstruction-era readmission, are likewise not carried as separate polity rows or container-edge breaks -- the container list treats 1836-2025 statehood as continuous. If a territorial-period or Confederate-period entry is added later, this page's start_year, predecessor list, and container edges would need to be revisited.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Arkansas is a standard U.S. state with no distinct historical name of its own (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so it follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows: ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The subunit token chosen is AR, the USPS/ISO 3166-2:US code for Arkansas, matching the two-letter convention already used for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025) and the Spanish provinces added in this same harness run (esp-as, esp-ca, esp-lu, esp-or, esp-po). The full code is USA-AR-1836-2025.

### d-start-year-admission

**Start year is 1836 admission to the Union, per the country default rule**

The country convention's default rule for U.S. states is to start at formal admission to the Union unless the state is a departure case (Alaska, Hawaii, or a territory with a distinct pre-statehood identity already carrying its own polity row). Arkansas Territory (1819-1836) is not itself a polity row in this database, and the routing decision explicitly found no departure reason and no back-cast need since 1836 precedes the data's 1866 start, so 1836 is used directly rather than back-cast to an earlier territorial period.

### d-four-container-edges

**Four container edges tile 1836-2025 across the successive national USA rows**

The national USA chain in the polity table is split into five rows by era: USA-1800-1803, USA-1803-1848, USA-1848-1867, USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025. Arkansas's 1836 start falls inside the second of these, so the container list uses four edges (skipping USA-1800-1803 entirely) to cover the full 1836-2025 span with no gap, each edge trimmed to the intersection of Arkansas's span and that national row's own span, following exactly the pattern used for Alabama's four-edge container list.

### oq-confederate-period-not-separated

**Should Arkansas's 1861-1865 Confederate/secession period be a distinct polity row or container break?**

Arkansas seceded from the Union in May 1861, joined the Confederate States of America, and was not formally readmitted to Congressional representation until 1868 (Reconstruction Acts). This page treats 1836-2025 as continuous statehood with no predecessor/successor split and no container-edge break at 1861 or 1868, mirroring how the underlying national USA chain itself does not appear to carry a separate Confederate-era row. Whether this database's broader USA chain or a sibling Confederate-state entry (e.g., for Mississippi, Tennessee, Louisiana) treats the Civil War period differently should be checked for consistency; if it does, this page's container list may need a mid-span edge change or an explicit note here rather than silence.
