# Namibia (1966-1990)

## Summary

Namibia (1966-1990) covers the territory of South West Africa / Namibia from 27 October 1966 -- when the UN General Assembly revoked South Africa's mandate -- to 20 March 1990, the eve of Namibian independence. Despite the UN revocation, South Africa continued to administer the territory. CShapes records this period as status=colony, owner=ZAF (COW 560). A prolonged guerrilla war led by SWAPO eventually forced the transition to independence [biger-1995 §namibia](../sources/biger-1995.md#namibia).

## Territorial extent

Same territory as preceding mandate period. Bounded by Angola and Zambia (north), Botswana (east), South Africa (east and south), Atlantic Ocean (west). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Area ~72.31 (CShapes units), unchanged from NAM-1920-1966.

## Open questions

### oq-nam-un-revocation

The UN revoked the mandate on 27 October 1966, but South Africa rejected this. The legal status of South African administration 1966-1990 (illegal occupation vs. continued mandate) varies by source.
