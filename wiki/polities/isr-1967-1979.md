# Israel (1967-1979)

## Summary

Israel (1967-1979) covers the period from the Six-Day War (June 1967) -- which dramatically expanded Israeli-controlled territory -- until the Camp David Accords and the Egypt-Israel Peace Treaty (1979), which led to Israel's withdrawal from the Sinai Peninsula. During this period Israel occupied the Sinai, the Gaza Strip, the West Bank, East Jerusalem, and the Golan Heights. The CShapes polygon changes at 1967 (territorial expansion) and at 1979 (Sinai return).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Following the Six-Day War, Israel controlled territory far beyond the 1949 armistice lines, including the entire Sinai Peninsula, the Gaza Strip, the West Bank including East Jerusalem, and the Golan Heights.

## Sourced claims

- "On 6 October 1973, frustrated by the loss of territory, Syria and Egypt attacked Israel (the Yom Kippur War), but by the time the war had ended, Israel had occupied an additional salient extending toward Damascus." [biger-1995 §israel](../sources/biger-1995.md#israel)
- After the Six-Day War, "Israel refuted any future validity of the 1949 armistice lines and of demilitarized zones. It insisted on a definitive peace treaty that would establish a mutually agreed delimitation of territorial sovereignty with demarcated boundaries."
