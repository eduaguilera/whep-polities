# Uganda (1894-1902)

## Summary

Uganda (1894-1902) covers the British Protectorate of Uganda from 8 June 1894 — when CShapes records the protectorate starting — to 14 May 1902, when a boundary transfer reduced the territory. CShapes records this as status=protectorate, owner=GBR (COW 200). The protectorate at this stage had a larger area (~30.99 CShapes units) than subsequent periods, reflecting territory that was later transferred to the East Africa Protectorate (present-day Kenya). The Biger source confirms "In 1894 the British government was persuaded to make Uganda a protectorate." (p.490)

## Territorial extent

East African interior territory at this stage extending further east and south than later Uganda. Bounded by the Sudan (north), German East Africa/Tanganyika (south and east), and the Belgian Congo (west). CShapes 2.0 area ~30.99 (from 1894-06-08 to 1902-05-14). The area reduction to ~21.86 at 1902-05-15 reflects the transfer of the Eastern Province (Rudolf Province, later Northern Frontier District) to the East Africa Protectorate. Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Sourced claims

- "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894. Britain was anxious to protect its trade through the vital Suez Canal and future markets in Africa." (p.490) [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda)
- "In 1894 the British government was persuaded to make Uganda a protectorate." (p.490) [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda)
- "In 1886, the British and German spheres of influence in East Africa lay respectively to the north and south of a line which ran from Vanga, on the coast of the Indian Ocean, to the eastern shore of Lake Victoria." (p.490) This 1886 partition established the framework within which the Uganda Protectorate was declared in 1894. [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=500, status=protectorate, owner=200 (UK), from 1894-06-08 to 1902-05-14, area ~30.99. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Contradictions

Biger (p.490) associates Uganda primarily with the Kingdom of Buganda ("largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms") — this appears to be a Biger error, conflating Buganda with Burundi. The Uganda Protectorate was centred on Buganda, not Burundi (which was part of German East Africa). This apparent error in Biger is flagged but does not affect the territorial or temporal data used for this WHEP row.

## Open questions

### oq-uga-biger-burundi-error

Biger (p.490) states the Uganda Protectorate "largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi." This is likely a transcription or editing error in Biger: the Uganda Protectorate was centred on Buganda, not Burundi. Burundi was part of German East Africa. This apparent error should be noted when citing Biger on Uganda but does not affect the polygon or date data drawn from CShapes.

### oq-uga-1902-eastern-province-transfer

CShapes records a large area reduction at 1902-05-15 (~30.99 to ~21.86). This corresponds to the Uganda Order in Council 1902, which transferred the Eastern Province to the East Africa Protectorate. The specific districts transferred and the rationale for the transfer should be confirmed from colonial administration sources.
