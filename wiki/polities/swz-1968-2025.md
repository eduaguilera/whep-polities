# Eswatini (1968-2025)

## Summary

This row covers independent Eswatini (formerly Swaziland) from 6 September 1968 to the present. The Kingdom of Swaziland gained independence from Britain on that date. The country was renamed Eswatini in 2018. CShapes area unchanged from the colonial period (1.55 degree-squared units).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0.
- **Area.** ~17,364 sq km. Small landlocked country in southern Africa, bounded by South Africa and Mozambique. CShapes area = 1.55, unchanged from the colonial period.
