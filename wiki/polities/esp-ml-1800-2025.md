# Melilla

## Summary

Melilla, "an autonomous city of Spain on the North African coast", with "an area of 12.3 km2". "After
the expansion of Melilla's perimetre to its current limits in the wake of the Treaty of Wad Ras (1860)"
it grew to its present extent; "Prior to the passing of its Statute of Autonomy in 1995, Melilla was a
regular municipality of the province of Málaga." With [esp-ce-1800-2025](esp-ce-1800-2025.md) it
makes up [cem-1800-2025](cem-1800-2025.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §melilla](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#melilla)

## Why this entry exists

The label `spain: melilla` reports Melilla alone for 1928-1932 to 1952 (**15 (year, label)
pairs**). Only the joint Ceuta-and-Melilla row existed. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: the Melilla parts of GADM 4.1 adm1 `ESP.7_1`, inside 3.00-2.90 W, 35.25-35.33 N:
**7.6 km²** (ESRI:54034), well under the stated 12.3 km². GADM draws the city small, so the polygon
understates the territory; **proxy** for that reason and because the perimeter before 1860 was
smaller still.
