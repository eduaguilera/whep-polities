# Germany (1920-1938)

## Summary

This row tracks Germany for **19 years**, covering the Weimar Republic
(1920--1933) and the early Nazi period (1933--1938)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV has
`notes = NA` and `verification_status = VERIFIED`.

Metropolitan borders were relatively stable during this period. The
Saar Basin was under League of Nations administration until the 1935
plebiscite (returned to Germany); the Rhineland was remilitarized in
1936 (not a territorial change). The **1938 end date** is the
Anschluss with Austria (13 March 1938) -- a real territorial change
in which Austria was absorbed into Germany.

Biger notes: "The 1920s' Weimar Republic lacked domestic legitimacy"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

Predecessor: [deu-1919-1920](deu-1919-1920.md) -- transitional
post-Versailles row.
Successor: [deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) --
Germany after the Anschluss.

## Sourced claims

- **1920--1933: Weimar Republic.** The Weimar Republic period.
  Biger: "The 1920s' Weimar Republic lacked domestic legitimacy"
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).
- **1935-01-13: Saar plebiscite.** The Saar Basin, under League of
  Nations administration since 1920, voted to return to Germany. The
  formal transfer occurred on 1 March 1935. This is a territorial
  change within the row (~1,900 km² gained) but does not trigger a
  CSV split.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1936-03-07: Rhineland remilitarization.** Germany remilitarized
  the Rhineland in violation of the Treaty of Versailles. This was
  a military/political event, not a territorial change (the Rhineland
  was already German sovereign territory).
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1938-03-13: Anschluss.** Austria was absorbed into Nazi Germany.
  This is the end-of-row event -- a real territorial change adding
  approximately 84,000 km². CShapes should show the area increase at
  this date. Cross-reference: [aut-1919-2025](aut-1919-2025.md) has a
  COW gap at 1938-03-13.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 260, continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- no break at 1920 or 1938
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
