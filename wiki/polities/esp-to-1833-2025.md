# Toledo (province of Spain)

## Summary

Toledo is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province. Unlike Asturias or Cantabria, Toledo does not correspond to an autonomous community of its own: since 1982 it has been one of five provinces (with Ciudad Real, Cuenca, Guadalajara and Albacete) making up Castilla-La Mancha, so the province boundary and the modern administrative geography diverge above the province level even though the province itself has been stable since 1833. This entry is type: subnational because Toledo is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because INE and Eurostat have tabulated Toledo separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES425, since the province's creation.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id ESP-ES425) captures valued statistical rows for unit ES425 ("Toledo"), country Spain, with data years 1865-2021 (per routing_concerns), all falling inside the proposed 1833-2025 span with no coverage gap. This follows the same country convention applied to sibling ES1xx/ES4xx NUTS3 Spanish units, using the system default start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform, since Toledo is not one of the Canary Islands departure units) and the open end_year of 2025 for still-current units. Previously matched to: nothing -- the routing_reasoning states plainly "Toledo is a Spanish province with no existing polity representing it"; absent this row, ES425 data would have fallen back to the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, collapsing province-level series into a country-level one, which was rejected for the same reason it was rejected for the sibling provinces already routed. What confirms distinctness: Toledo's status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES425) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since 1833 is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025 -- continuous, separately-tabulated provincial statistics distinguish it from the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_feature pointing at mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro (the Spanish INE province numeric code); Toledo's conventional INE province code is 45, so the anticipated feature would be ESP-45-1833-2025 once the provinces.gpkg source file is fetched onto disk. polygon_detail flags this as low vintage_risk since provincial boundaries have been essentially stable since 1833, but the source is registered without being present_locally, exactly the same unmet precondition already recorded for ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. Territory description: Toledo province sits in central Spain, south of Madrid province, within the modern autonomous community of Castilla-La Mancha, bordered by Madrid and Avila to the north, Ciudad Real to the south, Cuenca to the east, and Caceres to the west. Its capital, the city of Toledo, sits on the Tagus river, which crosses the province west to east; the province is largely the northern part of the historic La Mancha plateau plus the Montes de Toledo range in the southwest. On a modern map its outline corresponds to the present-day Provincia de Toledo. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 15,370 km2 -- this is the province's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet.

## Predecessors and successors

Toledo has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory was organized under the Antiguo Regimen as part of the historic Kingdom of Toledo / Castilla la Nueva jurisdiction, a structure this database does not model at that granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row, matching the treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention established for the other Spanish NUTS3 units.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-as-abbreviation

**Used the two-letter abbreviation TO rather than the province numeric code, matching the ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern for Spanish provinces already established by ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CA-1833-2025 and siblings is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short letter abbreviation of the unit name rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES425) or the INE province numeric code (45). This page follows that convention exactly, minting ESP-TO-1833-2025 rather than a numeric-coded alternative, so that the sibling set of Spanish province rows stays internally consistent and any future province entry can be predicted from the same rule without a lookup table. TO was chosen as the modern ISO 3166-2 subdivision code (ES-TO) and the everyday abbreviation for the province, reducing ambiguity for a future author matching a new data source against this row.

### oq-pre-1833-territory-unmodeled

**The pre-1833 Kingdom of Toledo / Castilla la Nueva jurisdiction has no distinct predecessor polity in this database**

Before the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform, the territory now covered by Toledo province was organized under earlier Antiguo Regimen jurisdictions (historically associated with the Kingdom of Toledo within Castilla la Nueva) that do not correspond cleanly to the 1833 province's administrative form. This database does not model that earlier structure as a distinct polity row, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row, mirroring the treatment recorded for ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. Whether any pre-1833 Castilian jurisdiction should eventually be modeled as its own polity is a live open question for the ESP country convention generally, not specific to this entry.
