# Tuscany

## Summary

The Grand Duchy of Tuscany from the 1800 database floor to its 1860 annexation to the Kingdom of Sardinia (the precursor state of unified Italy). The row spans three distinct regimes compressed into a single WHEP polity: the 1801–1807 Napoleonic Kingdom of Etruria under the Bourbon-Parma, direct French annexation 1808–1814 under Elisa Bonaparte, and the 1814–1859 restored Habsburg-Lorraine grand duchy (Ferdinand III, then Leopold II). The 1859 revolution deposed Leopold II; a 1860 plebiscite with ~95% support for union annexed Tuscany to Sardinia.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Cliopatria (Grand Duchy of Tuscany 1800–1802) per CSV. Core territory corresponds to modern Tuscany (Firenze / Siena / Livorno / Pisa / Arezzo / Grosseto). The 1801–1807 and 1808–1814 French-regime configurations had different administrative boundaries that are not individually captured by this single-row WHEP model.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. The 1800 start is a database floor; the Medici-then-Lorraine Grand Duchy of Tuscany was founded in 1569.
- **Successor:** [ita-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md) — Kingdom of Italy. Tuscany is one of six WHEP predecessors of ITA-1861-1919 (SAR, TWO, PAP, TUS, DMO, DPA).

## Contradictions

**WHEP-polity rule compression.** Under README §what-a-whep-polity-is (territorial-economic unit), the 1801–1807 Etruria, 1808–1814 French annexation, and 1814–1859 Habsburg restoration are all tracked in one row because the *territory* is roughly stable, not because legal continuity held. This is an acceptable compression per the rule.

## Open questions

### oq-tus-1808-1814-france

During 1808–1814 Tuscany was formally part of France (départements of Arno, Ombrone, Méditerranée). Whether this should interrupt the TUS row or be absorbed (per the territorial-economic-unit rule) is a judgment call; the current compression is explicitly justified on that rule but could be reopened.
