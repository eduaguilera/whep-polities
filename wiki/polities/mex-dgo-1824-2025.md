# Durango (state)

## Summary

Durango is one of the 31 states of Mexico, in the north-central part of the country on the eastern slope of the Sierra Madre Occidental, bordering Chihuahua, Coahuila, Zacatecas, Sinaloa and Nayarit. It was erected as a state of the federation by the 1824 Constitution of the United Mexican States, having previously been the Intendancy of Durango under New Spain (part of the Provincias Internas). Its territory has been broadly stable since 1824 — unlike Baja California, Baja California Sur or Quintana Roo, Durango was not carved out of, or split from, another modern state, and no historical source records a later boundary reorganization separating it from a combined Durango-Chihuahua province, though this has not been independently verified beyond the general 1824 founding date. `type: subnational` because sovereignty over this territory has always rested with the Mexican national state (colonial Spain, then independent Mexico); this row exists only to carry a reporting/administrative unit below that level, mirroring how other Mexican states (e.g. Baja California Sur) are represented in this database.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Mexican agricultural/production data reported at the state level for Durango starting in 1900 (per the routing decision's dataset), which prior to this entry had no state-specific row to attach to — only the two national MEX rows (MEX-1800-1848 and MEX-1848-2025) existed in the table, so any Durango-labelled series would have had to be misrouted to the whole-country row, diluting a state-scale figure into a national one roughly 19x larger in area. No Federico-Tena or other historical trading-polity source was consulted for this specific unit (Durango is a domestic administrative division, not a historical trading entity), but the 1824 Constitution of the United Mexican States is itself the confirming historical source that Durango was constituted and administered as a distinct state from that date, distinct from both the national government and from neighbouring states such as Chihuahua and Coahuila, which is why it needs its own polity row rather than being folded into MEX.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached in the GeoPackage for this period. The route is `registered_source_unfetched`: GADM 4.1 admin-1 (`gadm-4.1-adm1`) is already a registered source in `scripts/sources.yaml` and is used elsewhere in this repository for first-level administrative units, and it does digitise Durango as feature `MEX.10_1` in its full global release — but the copy of that source fetched locally into this repo is an 81-country subset that does not include Mexico, so the feature cannot be extracted today. Fetching the full global `gadm41_adm1.gpkg` (or a Mexico-specific extract) and merging it into the local geodata would make `MEX.10_1` available directly, with no union/difference construction needed since Durango is a native GADM adm1 polygon. `polygon_feature_id: MEX.10_1` is recorded now so that step is a straight extraction rather than a fresh lookup. **Territory description:** the modern state of Durango covers roughly 123,000 km² of north-central Mexico, centered on the state capital Victoria de Durango, spanning from the Sierra Madre Occidental in the west across an arid central plateau (part of the Bolsón de Mapimí) to its eastern border with Coahuila. Any figure eventually measured from the GADM geometry once fetched will be a measurement of that polygon, not independent evidence about the historical extent of the 1824-1900 state, which has not been separately verified for boundary drift.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor row: prior to 1824 this territory's population and production were reported, if at all, under the colonial Intendancy of Durango / the broader Provincias Internas, which are not separately modelled in this database, so `predecessor` is empty rather than pointing to an unmodelled unit. No successor row: Durango remains a current Mexican state with no dissolution or absorption, so `successor` is empty and `end_year` is set to 2025 following the country convention of an open end_year for still-current subnational units, matching the pattern used for other current Mexican states such as Baja California Sur.

## Sourced claims

- The 1824 Constitution of the United Mexican States (Constitución Federal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos) lists Durango among the original states of the federation, formed from the former colonial Intendancy of Durango.
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer digitises Durango as a native feature with GID_1 code MEX.10_1, using present-day state boundaries, in its full global release (the locally fetched copy in this repo is restricted to an 81-country subset that excludes Mexico).

## Decisions

### d-follow-default-1824-start

**Start year set to 1824 (state founding), not 1900 (data start)**

The default rule for Mexican states used in this pipeline sets the span's start_year to the year of admission to the federation as a state, rather than to the earliest year with data. Durango was among the original states erected by the 1824 Constitution, so start_year is 1824 even though the production/trade data being routed to this polity only begins at 1900. Durango is not one of the three documented departures from this default (Baja California, Baja California Sur, Quintana Roo, all of which were organized as states much later than 1824), so no special-casing was applied. The container edges were split at 1848 to align with the two existing national MEX rows (MEX-1800-1848 and MEX-1848-2025) so the full 1824-2025 span is tiled without a gap on either side of the Guadalupe Hidalgo cession.

## Open questions

### oq-durango-polygon-fetch

**GADM adm1 fetch needed to assign a polygon**

The locally fetched gadm-4.1-adm1 file (data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg) is an 81-country subset that excludes Mexico entirely, so feature MEX.10_1 (Durango) cannot be extracted until the full global GADM 4.1 admin-1 release, or a Mexico-specific extract of it, is fetched and merged into that file. Until then polygon_status stays `unassigned` and polygon_area_km2 is null. Once fetched, the resulting present-day boundary should be flagged in this page as a stated approximation for the pre-1900 (and arguably pre-1950) portion of the span, since no historical boundary source has been checked for 19th-century reorganizations of Durango's territory.

### oq-durango-possible-early-split

**Unverified possibility of an early split from a combined Durango-Chihuahua unit**

The routing decision that created this entry flags, without resolving, the possibility that Durango's territory was reorganized after 1824 — specifically that it may have been split out from a combined Durango-Chihuahua colonial-era province rather than existing at its current extent from the 1824 founding date. No source was found in this pass to confirm or refute that. If a 19th-century territorial reorganization is later documented, start_year and/or the container basis text here may need revision, and the GADM present-day polygon (once fetched) would be an even weaker proxy for the earliest decades than currently stated.
