# British Malaya (1895-1946)

## Summary

The collective British imperial presence on the Malay Peninsula from 1895 to 1946, comprising three administrative units: the Straits Settlements (a Crown Colony, cowcode 827), the Federated Malay States (FMS, cowcode 821), and the Unfederated Malay States (UMS, cowcode 822). The territory was never a single formal administrative entity but functioned as a coherent economic and political unit under the High Commissioner for the Malay States, based in Singapore. It is the appropriate WHEP entity for 'malaysia' and 'british malaya' economic data recorded before 1946.

The period 1895 marks the appointment of the first Resident-General (Frank Swettenham) coordinating the protected Malay states; 1946 marks the dissolution of all three units into the Malayan Union (MYS-1946-1957). The 1909 Anglo-Siamese Treaty transferred Kelantan, Terengganu, Kedah, and Perlis from Siamese suzerainty to British protection, enlarging British Malaya by approximately 40,600 km2.

The 1909-1946 configuration is used as the reference because: (a) it covers 37 of the 51 years of this polity's span; (b) the 1909 Siamese treaty fixed the northern borders that persisted until dissolution; (c) the successor polygon (MYS-1946-1957, CShapes cow820 = 131,495 km2) matches this union to within ~545 km2, confirming consistency.

**Early period caveat (1895-1909):** The pre-1909 configuration (Straits + early FMS + pre-treaty UMS) covered only ~91,433 km2 — approximately 40,600 km2 less — because the four northern states (Kelantan, Terengganu, Kedah, Perlis) were under Siamese suzerainty. WHEP economic data attributed to 'British Malaya' before 1909 should be understood as covering only the peninsular core. The single polygon cannot fully represent this territorial change; the 1909 configuration is used as it aligns with the successor (MYS-1946-1957) and the stabilised imperial administration.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. Before 1895 the Malay states were managed separately; the constituent states (Perak cowcode 8201, Selangor 8202, Pahang 8203, and Negri Sembilan) were independent CShapes entries before the 1895-1896 federation. A pre-1895 Malaya entry could be created if needed.
- **Successor:** [mys-1946-1957](mys-1946-1957.md) — Malayan Union / Federation of Malaya 1946-1957, formed 1 April 1946 when the Malayan Union dissolved the separate FMS/UMS/Straits administrations.

## Sourced claims

- The Federated Malay States were formally constituted on 1 July 1896 with Perak, Selangor, Pahang, and Negri Sembilan under a Resident-General, though coordination began in 1895. CShapes 2.0 records FMS cowcode 821 from 1896-07-01.
- The Unfederated Malay States (Johor, Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis, Terengganu) remained under separate British protectorate arrangements; Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis, and Terengganu transferred from Siam by the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909. CShapes 2.0 records UMS cowcode 822 expanding from 19,112 km2 (pre-1909) to 57,024 km2 (post-1909).
- The Straits Settlements (Singapore, Penang, Malacca) remained a Crown Colony throughout 1895-1946. CShapes 2.0 cowcode 827: 3,601 km2.
- British Malaya as a collective entity was dissolved 1 April 1946 when the Malayan Union came into existence, absorbing FMS, UMS, and the Straits mainland (Penang and Malacca; Singapore remained separate). CShapes 2.0 cowcode 820 begins 1946-04-01 at 131,495 km2.

## Decisions

- Polygon method chosen as composed_union (not period_proxy or modern_proxy) because all three CShapes exact-historical constituents exist and can be unioned directly.
- The 1909-1946 configuration is used as the representative polygon rather than 1895-1909 because it represents the dominant period and matches the successor polygon, making it more consistent with the rest of the WHEP chain.
- iso3 set to MYS (the modern successor state's code), consistent with MYS-1946-1957 and the wider Malaysia chain.
- cow_code set to NA: no single COW code covers all of British Malaya; CShapes splits it across 827, 821, and 822.

## Open questions

### oq-gbm-pre-1895
No WHEP entry currently covers the pre-1895 Malay protected states (Perak, Selangor, Pahang individually). If full temporal coverage is needed, entries for the individual states or an earlier British Malaya polity should be considered.

### oq-gbm-singapore-exclusion
Singapore (cowcode 830 from 1946) was part of the Straits Settlements within British Malaya 1895-1946 but is omitted from the successor MYS-1946-1957 polygon (which excludes Singapore). The 3,601 km2 Straits Settlements polygon includes Singapore; this creates a slight inconsistency with the successor chain. The Straits Settlements polygon is retained as-is for this entry.
