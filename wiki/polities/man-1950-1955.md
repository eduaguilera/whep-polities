# Manchuria (region, 1950-1955)

## Summary

The Manchuria region — the three northeastern provinces (Heilongjiang,
Jilin/Kirin, Liaoning) — under the People's Republic of China, from the
consolidation of PRC control in 1950 to the dissolution of the region as an
administrative tier in 1954. Through this window the northeast was governed
as a single unit above the province: the **Northeast People's Government**
(东北人民政府, from August 1949), succeeded by the Northeast Administrative
Committee, which was abolished in 1954 when the region's provinces were
placed directly under Beijing.

This is a **subnational region entry, not a sovereign state**. `type:
subnational` is deliberate and carries the same purpose as on
[man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md): the entry must not compete with the
national CHN chain in the matcher's family/sovereignty ranking. China held
sovereignty throughout — what this row exists to carry is the *reporting
territory*, because two independent statistical sources tabulate the
northeast separately from all-China totals in exactly these years.

**Why this entry exists.** Twenty valued rows describe Manchuria in
1950-1952 and were routed to the national row `CHN-1950-2025`, which is
about fifteen times the territory the figures measure:

| source | label | rows | years |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `mitchell` | `China, Manchuria province of` | 13 | 1950, 1951, 1952 |
| `fao1952` | `China Manchuria` | 7 | 1950, 1951 |

The mitchell half is the continuation of the same series that motivated
[man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) — barley, maize, millet, oats and wheat at
regional scale — and its post-1950 tail was recorded there as the open
question
[oq-mitchell-post1950-manchuria](man-1945-1950.md#oq-mitchell-post1950-manchuria),
which offered two remedies: extend that entry's `end_year`, or create a
follow-on regional polity. **This entry is that follow-on**, chosen over
extension for two reasons: the administration is genuinely different (a
contested 1945-1949 window under successive occupiers, against a formal PRC
administrative region from 1949), and re-spanning an existing polity moves a
code that other state already cites — the failure mode recorded in issue 308,
where re-spanning orphaned banked verdicts.

The fao1952 half is new evidence that was not available when that open
question was written: a **second, independent** publisher tabulating
`China Manchuria` for 1950 and 1951. Two sources reporting the same
reporting unit in the same years is what turns "one series has a tail" into
a reporting territory that needs a row.

Created under the policy decided in
[issue 400](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/400): a
reporting unit qualifies for a polity row when statistics were collected on
it, whether or not it was a sovereign state.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `constructed` source, feature `MAN-1932-1945` reused as a
period proxy, exactly as [man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) reuses it. Built
by `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` as the union of three modern
Chinese provinces from GADM 4.1 admin-1 data:

- `CHN.11_1` Heilongjiang
- `CHN.17_1` Jilin
- `CHN.18_1` Liaoning

**Area:** **791,708 km2**, computed from the attached geometry in an
equal-area projection (ESRI:54034) — necessarily identical to
`MAN-1932-1945` and `MAN-1945-1950`, since the feature is the same one. No
new polygon is introduced by this entry, which is why it was taken first of
the nine cases in issue 400: it isolates the routing question from any
boundary question.

**The reuse is a PROXY that under-covers, and two sources say by how much.**
`polygon_status: proxy` rather than `assigned`, because the reporting unit both
publishers tabulate is larger than the three modern provinces:

| figure | source | km2 | vs our polygon |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Manchukuo, incl. Jehol and the Inner Mongolian leagues | `iia` 1938 | 1,303,143 | 1.65x |
| `China Manchuria` at data year 1947 | `fao1952` | 1,069,300 | **1.35x** |
| three north-eastern provinces (our geometry) | GADM 4.1 union | 791,708 | 1.00 |

The IIA figure is already recorded in `source_stated_area_basis.csv` against
`MAN-1932-1945` with `basis_flag: review` and the note that it is "the first
external evidence of what it costs". The fao1952 figure is a **second,
independent** statement, and it is the relevant one here because it is dated
1947 — inside the predecessor's span and eight years after Jehol left
Manchukuo. So the under-coverage is not a Manchukuo-only artefact: at 1947 the
reporting unit was still about **26% larger** than three provinces.

`polygon_area_km2` remains 791,708, the geometry's own measured area, because
that column is what check A compares against the polygon. The stated figures
are territory evidence, not alternative measurements of this shape, which is
why they are recorded here rather than declared there.

No larger polygon is constructed for this entry. Building one would mean
deciding which additional provinces the 1,069,300 figure covers — Rehe plus
some of the nine-province northeast — and that is a boundary question, not the
routing question this entry exists to settle. It is left as an open question
below.

**Scale check against the data it receives.** 791,708 km2 is about 1/12 of
contemporaneous PRC territory, and the figures routed here are regional in
the same proportion: mitchell wheat runs in the hundreds of thousands of
tonnes against whole-China totals in the tens of millions. Attaching these
rows to `CHN-1950-2025` understates per-km2 intensity by roughly an order of
magnitude, which is the harm this row removes.

## Predecessors and successors

**Predecessor:** [man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) — the same territory in
the preceding, contested window. The two spans abut exactly: that entry ends
1950 (exclusive) and this one begins 1950.

**Successor:** [chn-1950-2025](chn-1950-2025.md) — after 1954 the northeast
ceased to be an administrative tier and its provinces reported directly to
Beijing, so the region is no longer a reporting unit and the national row is
the correct destination. `end_year: 1955` is exclusive on this repo's
convention, so this entry covers 1950-1954 inclusive.

## Sourced claims

- The Northeast People's Government was established in August 1949 and
  reorganised as the Northeast Administrative Committee, abolished in 1954
  with the region's provinces placed directly under central administration.
- `mitchell` tabulates `China, Manchuria province of` continuously from 1928
  to 1952; the 1950-1952 rows are the tail of that series.
- `fao1952` tabulates `China Manchuria` for 1937, 1947, 1949, 1950 and 1951,
  plus seventeen `1934-1938` period rows that belong to Manchukuo and remain
  on [man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md).

## Contradictions

None recorded. The two sources agree that the northeast is reported
separately in 1950-1951; they are not compared against each other here,
because fao1952 reports in thousands and iia/mitchell in units, which is the
subject of [issue 612](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/612).

## Decisions

### d-follow-on-rather-than-extension

Created as a new polity rather than by extending `MAN-1945-1950`'s
`end_year`. Extension would have changed a span whose code embeds it
(`MAN-1945-1950`), and issue 308 records what re-spanning costs: banked
verdicts orphaned by span drift. A follow-on abuts cleanly and leaves every
existing citation of the predecessor intact.

### d-span-follows-the-administration-not-the-data

The data ends in 1952 but the span ends in 1955 (exclusive), following the
region's abolition in 1954 rather than the last observation. This repo's
polities carry real spans, not data-driven ones; a span cut at 1952 would
assert that the reporting unit ceased when this particular extract stops.

## Open questions

### oq-three-province-proxy-under-covers-by-26pc

`fao1952` states 1,069,300 km2 for `China Manchuria` at data year 1947, against
our 791,708 km2 three-province union. Two consequences, and the second is a
contradiction rather than a gap:

1. **What the extra 26% is** is undecided. Rehe/Jehol is the obvious candidate,
   but the 1949-1954 northeast was administered as nine provinces, so the figure
   may cover more than Rehe. Resolving it means constructing a larger polygon
   and deciding its members, which no source in this repo digitises as a dated
   feature.
2. **[man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) asserts the opposite.** Its territorial
   extent says the three-province reuse is "actually correct, not merely
   approximate" for 1945-1950, on the ground that Jehol reverted to Chinese
   administration outside the northeast after 1945. The fao1952 figure is dated
   **1947**, inside that span, and is 1.35x the polygon — so either the reporting
   unit included territory that page excludes, or the stated figure covers
   something else. That page is **not** edited here: it is a live claim with its
   own evidence, and overwriting it from a single figure found while creating a
   different row would be the wrong way round. Recorded so the two are compared
   deliberately.

Note that this figure only became visible for a MAN row because the alias
rerouting is in progress: while `China Manchuria` routes to `CHN-*`, its stated
area is attributed to China, so `source_stated_area_basis.csv` has no MAN row
for it. Rerouting the label makes an independent territorial check available
that the database could not previously see.

### oq-1949-rows-on-the-national-chain

`fao1952`'s `China Manchuria` rows for 1947 (4 rows) and 1949 (5 rows) sit on
`CHN-1947-1949` and `CHN-1949-1950` even though
[man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) covers 1945-1949 and is the right
destination under issue 400's policy. Those nine rows are rerouted in the
same change that adds this entry, but the aliases that placed them were
written before the policy existed, and other labels may carry the same
pattern — a regional label routed to a national row because the national
polygon happens to contain the region. Whether that pattern recurs elsewhere
is not measured here.
