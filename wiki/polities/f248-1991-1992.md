# Yugoslavia (1991-1992)

## Summary

Transitional row during active Yugoslav dissolution
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Two-year dissolution period. Slovenia and Croatia declared independence
June 1991. Ten-Day War (Slovenia). Croatian War of Independence began.
Bosnia war began 1992. By end of 1992, Yugoslavia as a unified state
had ceased to exist — replaced by successor states and the rump Federal
Republic of Yugoslavia (Serbia and Montenegro).

Predecessor: [f248-1920-1991](f248-1920-1991.md). Successor:
[svn-1992-2025](svn-1992-2025.md), [hrv-1992-2025](hrv-1992-2025.md),
[bih-1992-2025](bih-1992-2025.md), [scg-1992-2006](scg-1992-2006.md),
[mkd-1991-2025](mkd-1991-2025.md).
