# Côte d'Ivoire (1933-1947)

## Summary

The enlarged French colony of Côte d'Ivoire during the period 10 August 1933 to 3 January 1947, when the colony incorporated territory from the dissolved Upper Volta colony. "Cote d'Ivoire and Mali were both units within French West Africa, which was established in 1895" [biger-1995 §ivory-coast](../sources/biger-1995.md#ivory-coast). CShapes records the area as 38.45 sq deg for this period — substantially larger than the standard 26.34 sq deg of the earlier (1902–1932) and later (1947–1960) Ivory Coast — reflecting the absorption of Upper Volta territory by French decree on 5 September 1932 (effective January 1933 in CShapes). The territory reverted to approximately the standard Ivory Coast boundary on 4 January 1947 when Upper Volta was reconstituted. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area change at both endpoints is genuine.

## Territorial extent

Standard Ivory Coast area (26.34 sq deg) bounded by Mali (331 mi), Burkina Faso (360 mi; 584 km), Ghana (415 mi; 668 km), Liberia (445 mi; 716 km), Guinea (379 mi; 610 km) [biger-1995 §ivory-coast](../sources/biger-1995.md#ivory-coast). The additional ~12 sq deg represents Upper Volta territory absorbed between 1932 and 1947.

## Contradictions

The CSV start year for this row is 1933, but CShapes records the enlarged area from 5 September 1932. The standard WHEP methodology uses CShapes boundary dates; the 1933 CSV start may reflect rounding to a year rather than the precise CShapes step date of 1932-09-05. This discrepancy should be reviewed — see [oq-civ-1932-vs-1933-start](#oq-civ-1932-vs-1933-start).

## Open questions

### oq-civ-1932-vs-1933-start

CShapes records the enlarged Ivory Coast territory from 1932-09-05 (corresponding to the French decree of 5 September 1932 that dissolved Upper Volta and distributed its territory). The CSV start year is 1933. Whether the WHEP row should be CIV-1932-1947 (matching the CShapes step date) or CIV-1933-1947 (matching the CSV) should be resolved. The CSV predecessor is CIV-1902-1932, suggesting the row split was intended at 1932, not 1933.

### oq-civ-1933-admin-event

CShapes records a second step within the enlarged period (1933-08-10 to 1947-01-03) with no area change relative to the prior step (1932-09-05 to 1933-08-09). The administrative event of 10 August 1933 should be identified.
