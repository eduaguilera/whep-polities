# Lithuania (interwar republic, 1923-1940)

## Era split (issue 686, 2026-10-07)

**This row was `LTU-1918-1940` until 2026-10-07.** It now starts in 1923, the year Lithuania took the
Klaipeda (Memel) Territory: "The Klaipeda Revolt took place in January 1923", and the Klaipeda
Convention followed in May 1924 [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §lithuania](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#lithuania). The years 1918-1922 moved to
[ltu-1918-1923](ltu-1918-1923.md).

**Polygon status:** assigned, unchanged. CShapes 2.0 gwcode 368, the 1922-1939 step (frontmatter year now 1923, which resolves to the same step), which includes
the Klaipeda Territory. CShapes draws the territory inside Lithuania from 1918 and inside no German
step, which is three to five years early; that is why the earlier era needed its own row.

**The data agrees.** In juan's Lithuanian series, cattle rise from 1,021,000 head (1922) to 1,285,000
(1923) and barley area from 173,900 ha to 196,000 ha between 1922 and 1924, the step the territory
should produce.

**What is still not split: 1939.** In March 1939 Lithuania ceded the Klaipeda Territory to Germany
[wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §lithuania](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#lithuania), and in October 1939 the USSR transferred the Vilnius region to it (about
6,900 km²). CShapes draws neither change. Cliopatria removes the territory in 1939 but does not add
Vilnius. The modern CShapes step minus this one is about 9,100 km², which also contains the strips
transferred only in 1940. No fetched source draws the October 1939 line, so the one year 1939-1940
stays on this polygon. It is roughly 4,000 km² too small (Vilnius gained, Klaipeda lost).

## Summary

The Republic of Lithuania as an independent state from its restoration of
independence on 16 February 1918 to Soviet annexation on 3 August 1940.
Independence was contested through the Lithuanian Wars of Independence
(1918-1920) against Soviet Russia, the Bermontians, and Poland. The
Soviet-Lithuanian Peace Treaty of 12 July 1920 recognised Lithuania including
the Vilnius region, but Poland seized Vilnius in October 1920 and held it until
1939 — so the effective interwar capital was Kaunas. The Klaipėda (Memel) region
was added in 1923 and lost to Nazi Germany in 1939.

time-steps for 1918-1940:

|---|---:|---|
| 1918-1920 | 83,882 km² | Max extent briefly holding Vilnius region per the 1920 Soviet-Lithuanian treaty |

The 1922-1939 polygon is the best match for the 1920-1939 data window since
agricultural statistics from the republic used the Kaunas-era boundaries.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 agricultural input data reports
"Lithuania" for 1919-1939, consistent with the interwar republic's own
statistical office (Kaunas). The previous wiki chain
([LTU-1800-1918](ltu-1800-1918.md) → [LTU-1940-1991](ltu-1940-1991.md)) left a
gap for this period, causing 404+ input rows to fail matching.
