# Ceuta

## Summary

Ceuta, "an autonomous city of Spain on the North African coast". "In 1580, it was annexed by the
Hispanic Monarchy, and the city chose to remain under Spanish authority after 1640. Ceuta belonged to
the Spanish province of Cádiz as a regular municipality until ... 1995". "It has an area of 18.5
km2". With [esp-ml-1800-2025](esp-ml-1800-2025.md) it makes up
[cem-1800-2025](cem-1800-2025.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §ceuta](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#ceuta)

## Why this entry exists

The label `spain: ceuta` reports Ceuta alone for 1928-1932 to 1952 (**15 (year, label) pairs**),
beside `spain: melilla` and the joint `spain: ceuta, melilla`. Only the joint row existed. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed`: the Ceuta parts of GADM 4.1 adm1 `ESP.7_1` ("Ceuta y Melilla", one feature for both
cities), inside 5.40-5.25 W, 35.85-35.94 N: **20.8 km²** (ESRI:54034) against the stated 18.5 km².
Perejil islet (0.17 km²), which GADM files in the same feature, and the plazas menores are left out.
**Proxy** before 1860: "The subsequent 1860 Treaty of Wad Ras expanded the borders of the Spanish
plazas de soberanía", so the modern perimeter overstates the pre-1860 city; the labelled years are all
later.
