# Zulu Kingdom

## Summary

The Zulu Kingdom from its consolidation under Shaka in 1816 to the British victory at the Battle of Ulundi on 4 July 1879. Shaka's military reforms and political consolidation transformed the Zulu chiefdom into the dominant power in southeastern Africa, driving the *mfecane* migrations that reshaped southern African polities. The Anglo-Zulu War of 1879 ended Zulu independence.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Heartland in modern KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Area ~25,464 km² per CSV notes. Cliopatria 0.1.3 records 5 time-steps for ZUL spanning 1820–1894.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. 1816 is the foundation year under Shaka; the Mthethwa alliance preceded but is not modelled.
- **Successors:** [nat-1843-1895](nat-1843-1895.md) — Natal Colony (British); and [zaf-1828-2025](zaf-1828-2025.md) — South Africa. Zulu territory was annexed to Natal in 1887, and Natal later joined the Union of South Africa in 1910.

## Sourced claims

- "Shaka assumed leadership of the entire Mthethwa alliance" c.1816, consolidating the Zulu Kingdom through military reorganisation and incorporation of neighbouring clans. [wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15 §zulu](../sources/wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- The Anglo-Zulu War culminated in "the Siege of Ulundi, the Zulus' capital city, and the subsequent defeat of the Zulu Kingdom" in 1879. [wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15 §zulu](../sources/wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- Cliopatria 0.1.3 records 5 time-steps for ZUL 1820–1894, extending beyond 1879 to include the post-Ulundi Zulu rump and Zululand. [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §coverage](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md)

## Contradictions

**End-year vs Cliopatria.** Cliopatria extends the ZUL polity to 1894 (Zululand annexation to Natal in 1887 is the more canonical date). WHEP's 1879 end_year follows the Battle of Ulundi — the end of *independent* Zulu statehood — rather than the end of *distinct administrative Zulu territory*. Flagged below.
