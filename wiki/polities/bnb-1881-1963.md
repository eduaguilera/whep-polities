## Summary

British North Borneo covers the northeast portion of Borneo island (now the Malaysian state of Sabah), from its establishment as a Chartered Company territory in 1881 through its incorporation into Malaysia in 1963. Three administrative phases: Chartered Company (1881-1888), British Protectorate (1888-1946), Crown Colony (1946-1963). CShapes 2.0 cowcode=823. Area ~72,620 km².

The FAO 1952 dataset reported "British Borneo North Borneo" as a distinct sub-entry. This polity must NOT be routed to British Malaya (MYS-1946-1957, Malay Peninsula ~132,000 km²) or Brunei (BRN-1888-2025).
