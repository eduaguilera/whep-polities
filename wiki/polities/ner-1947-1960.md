# Niger (1947-1960)

## Summary

The Colony of Niger in its final phase as part of French West Africa, from the post-WWII reorganisation (1946 French Union) to independence. Niger became an overseas territory of France in 1946 with representation in the French parliament, gained internal autonomy as a republic within the French Community in 1958, and achieved full independence on 3 August 1960.
