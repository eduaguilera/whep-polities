# Zimbabwe (1891-1900)

## Summary

This WHEP polity row covers the BSAC-administered territory of Rhodesia from 1891 to
1900. The 1891 start date corresponds to CShapes' first record of cowcode=552 as a
British colony (1891-06-11), with a substantially larger area (~88.16 sq.deg.) than
subsequent records. This larger area reflects that in this period the BSAC administered
both Southern Rhodesia (modern Zimbabwe) and Northern Rhodesia (modern Zambia) as a
single administrative unit. The 1900 end date marks CShapes' territorial change for this
cowcode (area drops to ~33.46 sq.deg.), corresponding to the formal administrative
separation of Northern Rhodesia, which became a separate protectorate leaving only
Southern Rhodesia under this polity.

## Territorial extent

The area recorded by CShapes for the 1891–1900 period is approximately 88.16 sq.deg.,
compared to 33.46 sq.deg. for the 1900–1953 period. This large area reflects the BSAC
administration encompassing both what would become Southern Rhodesia (Zimbabwe) and
Northern Rhodesia (Zambia). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0
(cowcode=552, status=colony, owner=200, start=1891-06-11, end=1900-01-28).

Zimbabwe is a landlocked southern African country (area: 150,873 sq. miles
[390,610 sq. km.]) bounded by Zambia, Mozambique, South Africa, and Botswana.
Capital: Harare (then Fort Salisbury/Salisbury). [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)

## Sourced claims

- "In 1889, Cecil Rhodes, who had secured mineral concessions in Matabeleland, was
  chartered and received many of the powers of the British government." (p.395). The BSAC
  charter granted in 1889 covers the administrative entity that becomes cowcode=552 in
  CShapes. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- "In 1893, the British South Africa Company seized Matabeleland" (p.395) — the conquest
  of the Ndebele Kingdom established BSAC control over the full territory covered by this
  row. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- "From 1891 operations extended north of the Zambezi River." (p.395). This confirms that
  the 1891–1900 row's large polygon (encompassing both sides of the Zambezi, i.e. both
  Rhodesias) reflects the actual administrative reality. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=552, status=colony, owner=200 for 1891-06-11 to
  1900-01-28, with area≈88.16 sq.deg. (BSAC-administered Rhodesia including Northern
  Rhodesia). The following record (1900-01-29 to 1953-07-31) has area≈33.46 sq.deg.,
  confirming the Northern Rhodesia separation. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Contradictions

The CShapes start date for this polygon (1891-06-11) differs from the preceding row
(ZWE-1890-1891) which runs from 1889-01-01. The 1891-06-11 date likely corresponds to
a specific administrative act or charter event. The exact event behind this CShapes
coding date has not been confirmed from Biger or another source.
