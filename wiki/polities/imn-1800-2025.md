# Isle of Man

## Summary

The Isle of Man in the Irish Sea, "a self-governing British Crown Dependency"; it "is not part of
the United Kingdom", which is responsible for its defence and external affairs. Its area is given as
"around 572 square kilometres".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §isle-of-man](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#isle-of-man)

One row for 1800-2025, typed `territory`: the island's extent and its status as a Crown dependency
did not change in the period.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `United Kingdom Isle of Man` on its own: population 50 thousand (1937) and 55
thousand (1951), and a 1950 land-use table whose `use total` is 57 thousand ha, i.e. **570 km²**.
**6 rows** resolved to no polity. The island lies **outside** the polygon of
[gbr-1921-2025](gbr-1921-2025.md) (CShapes 200 does not draw it; Douglas falls in no polygon), and it
is not part of the United Kingdom, so an alias to GBR would have filed a Crown dependency's figures
as the UK's.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm0 `IMN`, **580 km²** at source and 571 as shipped
(ESRI:54034), against FAO's 570 and the usually quoted 572.
[gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

GADM's IMN was not in the repository's extract; it was added to `scripts/sources/gadm-4.1/fetch.sh`
for this row, appended last so no existing feature moved.
