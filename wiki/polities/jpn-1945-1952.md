# Japan (occupied)

## Summary

This row tracks Japan under Allied occupation for **8 years**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes: "US
occupation (SCAP). All colonies stripped. Treaty of San Francisco
(1952-04-28) restores sovereignty"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers (SCAP, General MacArthur)
governed Japan. All colonial territories (Taiwan, Korea, South Sakhalin,
Pacific islands) were stripped. The Treaty of San Francisco (28 April
1952) restored Japanese sovereignty and ends this row.

Predecessor: [jpn-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md). Successor:
[jpn-1952-2025](jpn-1952-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, feature 740 (1886 vintage): home
  islands only. This polygon was also used by JPN-1800-1895 and
  JPN-1952-2025, and accurately represents post-surrender Japan with all
  colonies stripped (Taiwan, Korea, South Sakhalin, Pacific mandates all
  ceded).
- **Area.** ~377,657 km² (home islands); matches the stripped-empire
  territory that defines this row.
- **Polygon vintage drift.** The polygon vintage is 1886 while this row
  spans 1945-1952. This is not a territorial mismatch: the home-island
  boundaries did not change between 1886 and 1952.
- **Ryukyu Islands excluded.** The Ryukyu Islands (Okinawa Prefecture,
  ~2,271 km²) are NOT included in this polity. US military government
  of the Ryukyus was established separately from SCAP's administration
  of the Japanese home islands. "Ryukyu Islands" data from the FAO
  1952 dataset routes to [ryu-1945-1972](ryu-1945-1972.md), not here.
  Routing "Ryukyu Islands" to JPN-1945-1952 would use a polygon
  (~377,000 km²) that is ~166× larger than the actual sub-territory.
- **Routing alias.** IIA rows labelled 'japan' or 'jpn' dated 1945-1952
  route here via applied_aliases.csv (added 2026-06-24; previously
  omitted from the resolver's resolved_family, leaving these rows
  unmatched).
- **COW code NA** in the CSV. See
  [jpn-1952-2025](jpn-1952-2025.md#oq-cow-code-na).

## Sourced claims

- **Island state geography.** Japan consists of four large islands and
  many smaller islands, total area 145,870 sq mi (377,657 sq km). No
  land boundaries.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan) (p.329)
- **1945-08-15: Japan surrenders.** Start-of-row event. "Japan's
  participation in World War II was ended by the destruction of Hiroshima
  and Nagasaki by US atomic bombs in 1945."
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan) (p.329)
- **1945-09-02: Instrument of Surrender signed.** Formal ceremony aboard
  USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1946-11-03: New constitution promulgated.** Took effect 3 May 1947.
  Renounced war (Article 9).
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1951-09-08: Treaty of San Francisco signed.** Peace treaty with 48
  nations.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1952-04-28: Treaty of San Francisco enters into force.** Sovereignty
  restored. End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
