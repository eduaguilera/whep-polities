# Hungary (1919-1920)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **2 years**, a transitional period
covering the Hungarian Soviet Republic and the Treaty of Trianon
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Hungarian Soviet Republic (March-August 1919), then Romanian
occupation, then restoration under Horthy regency. Treaty of Trianon
signed 4 June 1920 -- Hungary lost ~72% of pre-war territory (CShapes:
from ~30 to ~11 sq degrees). This is one of the most devastating
territorial losses in 20th-century European history. The 1920 end date
aligns with Trianon.

Predecessor: [hun-1918-1919](hun-1918-1919.md) -- post-A-H dissolution.
Successor: [hun-1920-1938](hun-1920-1938.md) -- interwar Horthy Hungary.

## Sourced claims

- **1919: Hungarian Soviet Republic.** Bela Kun's communist government
  (March-August 1919). Overthrown after Romanian invasion.
- **1919-08: Romanian occupation.** Romanian forces occupied Budapest
  (August 1919). Withdrew November 1919.
- **1920-03-01: Horthy regency.** Admiral Miklos Horthy became regent
  of Hungary, establishing an authoritarian conservative regime.
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** Hungary lost ~72% of pre-war
  territory. Transylvania to Romania, Slovakia to Czechoslovakia,
  Croatia/Slavonia/Vojvodina to Yugoslavia, Burgenland to Austria.
  CShapes records the area drop from ~30 to ~11 sq deg on this date.
  This is the end-of-row event.

  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
