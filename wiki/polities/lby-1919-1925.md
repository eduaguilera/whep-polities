# Libya (1919-1925)

## Summary

Italian Libya in the immediate post-WWI period, from the 1919 Treaty of Versailles settlements (and the short-lived 1918–1922 Tripolitanian Republic) to the 1925 CShapes boundary cut. Italian effective control was weakest in this period — the Tripolitanian and Cyrenaican populations retained substantial autonomy under Libyan leaders (the Senussi in Cyrenaica; the Tripoli notables); the 1922 Fascist accession in Italy led to the renewed "pacification" campaign from 1923.
