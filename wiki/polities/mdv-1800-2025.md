# Maldives


> **Polygon area declared 2026-08-06. The figures and the conclusion in this note were
> RE-MEASURED AND REVERSED on 2026-08-13 (issue 71) — see "what was wrong" below.**
>
> ```
> GADM 4.1 adm0, unsimplified    299.68 km²
> the same polygon as shipped    299.68 km²   (-0.0%)
> official land area             298 km²
> shipped / official             1.006x

>
> `polygon_area_km2` states the **official land area**, not what the polygon measures. That is the
> field's meaning ([wiki/README.md](../README.md)): it is a figure about the *territory*, which is
> what makes `validate_polygons` check A a test rather than a tautology — declaring the measured
> value instead is exactly what check A2 counts and refuses to let grow.
>
> **WHAT WAS WRONG.** Until 2026-08-10 this note read `GADM 4.1 polygon as shipped 172.59 km²`,
> `ratio 0.58x`, and concluded that GADM understates the Maldives by 42%. It does not.
> `build_database.py` applied `SimplifyPreserveTopology(0.01)` — 0.01° is about 1.1 km at the
> equator — to every geometry before writing it, and the Maldives is 791 atolls almost all smaller
> than that, so the build itself deleted 42% of the country. The source polygon always measured
> 299.68. The 42% was ours, not GADM's.
>
> That is issue 71 in one row: `polygon_area_km2` was compared against the geometry this repository
> *ships*, so this page could declare 299.68 — the truth about the polygon — and fail check A on a
> **correct** polygon, or declare 172.59 and understate the country by 42%. It took the second
> option. The build now carries an area budget (`SIMPLIFY_MAX_AREA_CHANGE`: try the tolerance,
> measure what it costs, keep the original if it costs too much) and
> `scripts/validate_simplification_loss.py` fails if any shipped polygon moves more than 5% from
> the source area recorded in `data/final/polygon_feature_index.csv`. Measured across all 735
> shipped polygons, the largest movement is 2.1%.
>
> `polygon_status` stays **`estimate`**, but NOT for the reason first given here. The old reason was
> "the divergence exceeds check A's 25% tolerance", and at 0.6% it plainly does not. `estimate` is
> kept because the declared figure is an official land-area statistic and the polygon is GADM's
> coastal footprint — two different measurements of the Maldives that happen to agree closely, not
> one verified against itself. Whether an `assigned` claim is now warranted is a territorial
> judgement about whether GADM's footprint *is* the territory, and is left open.
>

## Summary

The Maldives is an archipelago of approximately 1,200 low-lying coral islands in the Indian Ocean, southwest of Sri Lanka. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. The islands were under nominal Sultanate authority with a British protectorate from 1887 to 1965, when full independence was achieved on 26 July 1965. The WHEP row spans the full period because CShapes records no area change across all three time-steps (protectorate and independence eras all show identical area), confirming no territorial change. COW ccode=781 (stateabb=MAD); state-system entry 1965-07-26.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=781). CShapes records three time-steps: 1887-12-16 to 1965-05-25 (protectorate, owner=200/Britain), 1965-05-26 to 1965-07-25 (transitional protectorate, owner=200), and 1965-07-26 to 2019-12-31 (independent, owner=781). All three time-steps show identical area=0.00274 sq deg (~300 km² total land area across 26 atolls). Constant area across all time-steps confirms no territorial change — the WHEP single-row approach is correct. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
