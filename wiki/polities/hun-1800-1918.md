# Kingdom of Hungary (Transleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Kingdom of Hungary from **1800** to **1918**. Initially within the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Transleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich. The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from **HistoGIS 1860 (ACDH-CH, Austrian Academy of Sciences)**. Built by dissolving 5 Transleithanian crownlands from the [Austrian Empire Crownlands 1860 shapefile](https://histogis.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/shp-samples): Kingdom of Hungary, Grand Principality of Transylvania (merged into Hungary 1867), Kingdom of Croatia-Slavonia, Voivodeship of Serbia & Banat of Temeschwar (abolished 1860, merged into Hungary), Military Frontier (dissolved 1881, merged into Croatia-Slavonia). Measured area ≈ **325,421 km²** (ETRS89 LAEA), matching Wikipedia's 1910 figure of 325,411 km² to within 0.01%. Source shapefile under open academic license. Assembly script: `scripts/sources/histogis-1860-habsburg/build.py`. Output GeoPackage: `data/geodata/histogis-1860-habsburg/habsburg_cisleithania_transleithania.gpkg` (gitignored).

**Why the post-1918 polygon was not used:** post-Trianon Hungary ([HUN-1918-1919](hun-1918-1919.md), ~93,000 km²) lost ~71% of Transleithanian territory at the Treaty of Trianon (1920) — Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia-Slavonia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia all went to successor states. Transleithania covered ~325,000 km² (modern Hungary, Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia, Burgenland).

**Data territory confirmed:** Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics* defines "Hungary" pre-1918 as **Transleithania** — the Lands of the Holy Hungarian Crown including Croatia-Slavonia, Slovakia, Transylvania, etc. (~325,000 km²). The wheat production values in the data (~1.5-2.3M tonnes in the 1850s-1870s) are consistent with this much larger territory (modern Hungary produces ~5M tonnes with intensive agriculture from only ~93k km²). This confirms the polygon rejection was correct: the post-Trianon Hungary polygon represents only ~29% of the data's territory.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Hungary" separately from "Austria" throughout 1851-1917. Mitchell confirms this follows Transleithania boundaries. Hungary had its own parliament (Diet/Országgyűlés), its own agricultural administration, and its own statistical office. Previously, the pipeline merged Hungarian data into the Austria-Hungary polygon.
