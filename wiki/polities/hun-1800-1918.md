# Kingdom of Hungary (Transleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Kingdom of Hungary from **1800** to **1918**. Initially within the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Transleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich. The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from **HistoGIS 1860 (ACDH-CH, Austrian Academy of Sciences)**. Built by dissolving 5 Transleithanian crownlands from the [Austrian Empire Crownlands 1860 shapefile](https://histogis.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/shp-samples): Kingdom of Hungary, Grand Principality of Transylvania (merged into Hungary 1867), Kingdom of Croatia-Slavonia, Voivodeship of Serbia & Banat of Temeschwar (abolished 1860, merged into Hungary), Military Frontier (dissolved 1881, merged into Croatia-Slavonia). Measured area ≈ **325,421 km²** (ETRS89 LAEA), matching Wikipedia's 1910 figure of 325,411 km² to within 0.01%. Source shapefile under open academic license. Assembly script: `scripts/sources/histogis-1860-habsburg/build.py`. Output GeoPackage: `data/geodata/histogis-1860-habsburg/habsburg_cisleithania_transleithania.gpkg` (gitignored).

**Why the post-1918 polygon was not used:** post-Trianon Hungary ([HUN-1918-1920](hun-1918-1920.md), ~93,000 km²) lost ~71% of Transleithanian territory at the Treaty of Trianon (1920) — Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia-Slavonia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia all went to successor states. Transleithania covered ~325,000 km² (modern Hungary, Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia, Burgenland).

**Data territory confirmed from 1868, not from 1851 — corrected 2026-08-18.** Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics* defines "Hungary" pre-1918 as **Transleithania** — the Lands of the Holy Hungarian Crown including Croatia-Slavonia, Slovakia, Transylvania, etc. (~325,000 km²). The wheat production values in the data (~1.5-2.3M tonnes in the 1850s-1870s) are consistent with this much larger territory (modern Hungary produces ~5M tonnes with intensive agriculture from only ~93k km²). This confirms the polygon rejection was correct: the post-Trianon Hungary polygon represents only ~29% of the data's territory.

**The pre-1868 tail is not Transleithania, and this page used to claim it was.** From 1849 to 1867/68 the
territory the polygon covers was not one administrative unit: Croatia-Slavonia was attached to Austria
rather than Hungary, and the Voivodeship of Serbia and Banat of Temeschwar was a separate autonomous
crownland under direct rule from Vienna. Both returned to Hungary only with the 1867 Ausgleich —
Croatia-Slavonia's reintegration completing in 1868 — which is the same event this page's own Key dates
section marks as the point Hungary *becomes* Transleithania. So "throughout 1851-1917" was inconsistent
with the rest of the page.

**Measured, and the exposure is small.** Of 1,003 `juan` rows for this label in 1851-1917, **29** fall
before 1868, in the years 1851, 1853, 1854, 1857 and 1859. The data then has an eight-year hole:
1860-1867 are absent entirely, so the gap straddles exactly the transition and no row sits on the
ambiguous boundary itself. The remaining 974 rows are 1868 or later and are unaffected.

**Not rerouted.** No narrower polity exists for pre-1868 Hungary-without-Croatia-Slavonia-and-Vojvodina,
so those 29 rows stay here and the verdict is `best_available` rather than `verified_equal` for the whole
span. Creating a row for a nineteen-year administrative arrangement covered by five data years is
probably not worth it, but that is a judgement rather than a measurement — recorded as
`oq-pre-1868-scope` below.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Hungary" separately from "Austria" throughout 1851-1917. Mitchell confirms this follows Transleithania boundaries. Hungary had its own parliament (Diet/Országgyűlés), its own agricultural administration, and its own statistical office. Previously, the pipeline merged Hungarian data into the Austria-Hungary polygon.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1868-scope

Twenty-nine of this row's 1,003 `juan` rows (years 1851, 1853, 1854, 1857, 1859) predate the 1867/68
Ausgleich, when Croatia-Slavonia sat under Austria and the Voivodeship of Serbia and Banat of Temeschwar
was a separate crownland — so for those five years "Hungary" denotes a narrower territory than this
row's 325,421 km². Does that warrant a separate pre-1868 polity?

Against: five data years, 29 rows, and the polygon source (HistoGIS 1860) is itself a 1860 snapshot of
the crownlands this row dissolves together, so a narrower polygon would have to be rebuilt from the same
shapefile with two crownlands excluded. For: the difference is not marginal — Croatia-Slavonia plus
Vojvodina and the Banat are a substantial share of the 325,421 km², so those five years are overstated
by well more than a rounding error.
