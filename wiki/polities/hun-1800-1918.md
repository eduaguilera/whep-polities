# Kingdom of Hungary (Transleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Kingdom of Hungary from **1800** to **1918**. Initially within the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Transleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich. The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. **Proxy deliberately not copied** because post-Trianon Hungary ([HUN-1918-1919](hun-1918-1919.md), ~93,000 km²) lost ~71% of Transleithanian territory at the Treaty of Trianon (1920) — Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia-Slavonia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia all went to successor states. Using the post-1918 polygon would be severely misleading. Transleithania covered ~325,000 km² (modern Hungary, Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia, Burgenland). A purpose-built polygon should be sourced from historical administrative boundary datasets.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Hungary" separately from "Austria" throughout 1851-1917. Hungary had its own parliament (Diet/Országgyűlés), its own agricultural administration, and its own statistical office. Previously, the pipeline merged Hungarian data into the Austria-Hungary polygon.
