# Aysén (region of Chile)

## Summary

Aysén is the eleventh region of Chile (Región de Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo), a sparsely populated territory in Chilean Patagonia between the Andes and the Pacific, roughly from 43°S to 49°S. It was created in its present form in 1976 when Decreto Ley 575 replaced the country's old provincial structure with thirteen (now sixteen) numbered regions; before that date the area existed as the provinces of Aysén (within a larger administrative grouping) but was not a standalone first-order unit in the sense the modern statistical series use. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chile's sovereignty over the area is continuous and undisputed throughout 1976-2025 — the entry exists to carry a sub-national reporting territory, not to compete with the national CHL row in the matcher's sovereignty ranking. The region's economy is dominated by sheep and cattle ranching, salmon aquaculture, and forestry, and its population remains the second-smallest of any Chilean region. end_year is set to 2025 (exclusive) because the region is still current and the database's open convention closes all live polities at that year.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id CHL-AI, admin_name "Aysen") found source data reported at the Chilean-region level for Aysén with no existing polity able to receive it: the only CHL rows in the table are whole-country national totals, which are containers for this territory, not substitutes for it — matching regional figures to the national row would duplicate the same category of conflict already flagged for another Chilean region (O'Higgins). Under this country's convention, Chilean regions default to a 1976 start year (the date DL 575 became operational) unless a unit-specific departure is documented; no departure was found for Aysén, so the default applies and this is a newly created polity rather than a re-span of an existing one. The harness's own retry note recorded that 76 of 124 data years for this unit (61%) were being marked `unroutable` for years before 1976, which is the wrong disposition for years where a source is actively reporting values — those pre-1976 years belong to whatever earlier administrative unit covered this territory (the historical Aysén province), not to no polity at all, and are flagged below as an open question rather than silently absorbed by this entry, since absorbing them here would misstate this row's own start-year basis.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached to this entry today, but a registered source can supply one and has not yet been fetched. `polygon_source` is set to `gadm-4.1-adm1` — the source is registered (GADM adm1 is the standing source for first-level administrative regions worldwide, licence-known, free for non-commercial use) but the copy of that dataset currently present in this repository is an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 matching features for `GID_0 == "CHL"`). This is a `registered_source_unfetched` route, not `new_source_needed`: the fix is to replace the local file with the full global GADM 4.1 release, not to register a new source. `polygon_status` is accordingly `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `estimate`, since no geometry — proxy or otherwise — has been attached.

**Territory description:** Aysén occupies the central section of Chilean Patagonia, bordered by the Andes and Argentina to the east, the Pacific and its fjords to the west, Los Lagos region to the north, and Magallanes region to the south — roughly the area a reader would locate between 43°15'S and 49°15'S on a modern map of Chile. The region's official area is approximately 108,494 km², making it Chile's second-largest region by area despite one of its smallest populations. No km² figure is stated here as a measurement of the (currently absent) attached geometry, since none is attached; the 108,494 km² figure is the region's own officially published area, not derived from any polygon in this repository.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-aysen-coverage

**What polity should carry pre-1976 Aysén data years?**

The harness reported that 76 of 124 data years for this unit (61%) fall before 1976 and were being marked unroutable, even though the source reports values for those years, implying the territory existed and was being measured under an earlier administrative name (likely the historical Aysén province, part of a larger administrative agrupación before DL 575). This entry deliberately does not extend backward to absorb those years, since doing so would misstate its span_basis (tied specifically to the 1976 DL 575 reform) and would need its own confirmation of what unit and boundary applied pre-1976. A follow-on predecessor polity, or an extension of this one with a corrected span_basis, should be created once the pre-1976 administrative history and territory are confirmed.

### oq-gadm-vintage-check

**Does the modern GADM Aysén boundary match the region's boundary across 1976-2025?**

Once gadm-4.1-adm1 is fetched globally and a feature is attached, the geometry will reflect present-day administrative boundaries. Aysén's boundary has been broadly stable since 1976 with no major reported splits, but this has not been verified against Chilean regional boundary legislation for the full 1976-2025 span (e.g., any commune-level reassignments between Aysén and neighboring Los Lagos or Magallanes regions), so the eventual polygon_status of 'assigned' should not be applied without that check.
