# Réunion (French Overseas Department)

## Summary

Réunion is a volcanic island in the Indian Ocean, approximately 700 km east of Madagascar, with a land area of approximately 2,512 km². On 19 March 1946, French law 46-451 transformed Réunion from a colonial territory into a French overseas department (département d'outre-mer, DOM). Despite this status change, the island's territory and administrative geography remained identical: same island, same boundaries, no territorial acquisition or cession.

This polity covers Réunion from 1946 (DOM transition) onward. The FAO reports Réunion data as a distinct territorial unit throughout this period — folding it into metropolitan France (FRA, ~550,000 km²) would overstate ~220x; the island's agricultural production belongs to the island's economy, not France's.

Created to route 47 rows of FAO 1952 agricultural data (cattle, cassava, carcass weights, etc.) for years 1949–1951.
