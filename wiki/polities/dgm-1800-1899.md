# Kingdom of Dagbon (Dagomba)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Dagbon (also known as Dagomba or the Dagomba Kingdom) was a pre-colonial state in the northern savanna of modern Ghana, ruled by the Ya-Na paramount chief based at Yendi. Founded c. 1400s, Dagbon was one of the largest and most organised kingdoms of the West African interior. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1899, when German colonial forces established a protectorate. Following Germany's defeat in World War I, the region was transferred to British administration and incorporated into the Gold Coast. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). Dagbon occupied the savanna zone of northern Ghana, centred on Yendi and extending to Tamale. Its territory bordered the Mossi kingdoms to the northwest and the coastal kingdoms to the south.

## Open questions

### oq-german-protectorate-date

The end year 1899 corresponds to German protectorate establishment. However, the transition from nominal independence to German control occurred over several years (1897–1899). The exact date of effective German sovereignty needs sourcing.

### oq-deeper-sourcing

Biger covers Ghana only at the post-colonial level. A specialist source on the Dagomba kingdom (e.g., Staniland 1975 *The Lions of Dagbon*; Pellow & Chazan or regional histories) is needed to source territorial boundaries and political history.
