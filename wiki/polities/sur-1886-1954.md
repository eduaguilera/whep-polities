# Dutch Guiana (SUR-1886-1954)

## Summary

Dutch Guiana was the colonial name for the territory on the northeastern coast of South America administered by the Netherlands from 1886 until 25 November 1954, when the Charter for the Kingdom of the Netherlands granted Suriname autonomous status. This polity covers the colonial period only. The 84 Mitchell rice-paddy data rows (1909-1960) for this period route here via year-ranged aliases.

## Territorial extent

The territory is ~144,985 km² as measured from CShapes 2.0 feature FID 55. The 1886 polygon vintage predates two significant boundary settlements: the 1899 Paris arbitration (western boundary with British Guiana, resolving the New River Triangle dispute of ~6,000-7,000 km²) and the 1906 Brazil-Surinam treaty (southern boundary). However, the CShapes geometry represents the post-settlement stable territory — the final settled borders — not the pre-arbitration extent. WHEP data begins 1909, after both settlements, so there is no territorial inconsistency for the data matched to this polity. No further internal split of SUR-1886-1954 is needed.

The polygon_vintage_drift for data from 1909-1954 is at most 23-68 years (relative to the 1886 vintage year), but since the territory was stable after 1906, this drift is purely temporal and does not indicate a spatial mismatch.

## Polygon decision

CShapes 2.0 has three distinct features for COW 115 (Surinam):
- FID 55: 1886-01-01 to 1954-12-14, colonial period, ~144,985 km² — assigned to this polity (SUR-1886-1954)
- FID 56: 1954-12-15 to 1975-11-24, autonomous period — assigned to SUR-1954-1975
- FID 57: 1975-11-25 to 2019-12-31, independent — assigned to SUR-1975-2025

All three CShapes features share identical geometry (same area ~144,985 km², EPSG:6933), confirming territorial stability across all three periods.
