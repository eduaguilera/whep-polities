# Cameroon (1960-1961)

## Summary

A transition-year polity covering independent Cameroon between 1 January 1960 (independence of French Cameroon) and 1 October 1961 (reunification with the southern part of British Cameroons). "French Cameroon became independent on 1 January 1960 while Oubangui-Chari became independent as the Central African Republic on 13 August 1960" [biger-1995 cameroon](../sources/biger-1995.md#cameroon). The territory expanded on 1 October 1961 when the southern British Cameroons joined, creating the Federal Republic of Cameroon. "The two parts of Cameroon became federated in 1961 and were unified in 1972" [biger-1995 cameroon](../sources/biger-1995.md#cameroon). CShapes records a boundary change at this date.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. Covers only the former French Cameroon territory. The 1961 successor polity expands to include the former British Southern Cameroons.
