# German Empire (1871-1919)

## Summary

This row covers **49 years** of the German Empire (Kaiserreich),
from the proclamation at Versailles (18 January 1871) through the
Treaty of Versailles (28 June 1919). The Empire united the North
German Confederation with the four southern German states and
annexed Alsace-Lorraine from France. It became the dominant
European land power, acquired a colonial empire at the Berlin
Conference (1884-1885), entered WWI (1914), and collapsed with
the Kaiser's abdication (9 November 1918). The Treaty of
Versailles stripped ~65,000 km² of metropolitan territory and
the entire colonial empire.

Predecessor: [deu-1866-1871](deu-1866-1871.md) — Prussia/North
German Confederation.
Successor: [deu-1919-1920](deu-1919-1920.md) — post-Versailles
transitional row.

  German Empire, adding Bavaria, Wurttemberg, Baden,

  Germany lost Alsace-Lorraine, Eupen-Malmedy, Northern Schleswig,
  West Prussia, Posen, Upper Silesia (partial), Memel, Saar
  (administered), and all colonies. Total metropolitan loss:
  ~65,000 km²
  [wikipedia-german-empire-2026-04-11 §1919-06-28](../sources/wikipedia-german-empire-2026-04-11.md#1919-06-28).
