# Kingdom of Benin

## Summary

The Kingdom of Benin from the 1800 database floor to the British Punitive Expedition of 1897. This is the Edo kingdom of modern southern Nigeria (Edo State), **not** the modern Republic of Benin (ex-Dahomey). The Oba dynasty was established c.1180 by Oranmiyan; the 19th-century kingdom was famed for its bronzes and brass plaques. Following a British delegation ambush in January 1897, a British punitive force captured Benin City in February 1897, deposing Oba Ovonramwen and looting the palace bronzes.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Covers the core Edo-speaking area of modern Edo State, Nigeria, and adjacent portions of Delta and Ondo States. The kingdom's 19th-century extent was reduced from its 17th-century peak; by the time of conquest it was effectively a city-state with tributary dependencies.
