# Nigeria (1914-1960)

## Summary

This row tracks colonial Nigeria for **47 years**. The Colony and
Protectorate of Nigeria was formed on 1 January 1914 by amalgamating
the Northern and Southern Nigeria protectorates. Nigeria gained
independence on 1 October 1960.

CShapes shows the same area (71.1 sq deg) for 1914-1960 and 1960-1961,
with the territorial change only occurring in 1961 when Northern
Cameroons joined Nigeria (area increases to 74.9 sq deg). The end year
was corrected from 1961 to 1960 to eliminate the umbrella overlap with
NGA-1960-1961 and place the chain boundary at independence.
