# Syria (French Mandate, 1920-1922)

## Summary

Following the San Remo Conference of April 1920, France received a League of Nations mandate over Syria. French forces defeated King Faisal's Arab Kingdom of Syria at the Battle of Maysalun on 24 July 1920 and occupied Damascus. In 1922, the League of Nations formally approved the mandate, and France subdivided the territory into several autonomous states.
