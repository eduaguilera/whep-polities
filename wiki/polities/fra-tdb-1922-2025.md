# Territoire de Belfort

## Summary

Territoire de Belfort is the smallest departement in metropolitan France, occupying the arrondissement of Belfort at the eastern edge of the historic Haut-Rhin departement. It was carved out in 1871 as the residual French portion of Haut-Rhin after the rest of Alsace (including the city of Mulhouse and most of Haut-Rhin) was annexed by the German Empire under the Treaty of Frankfurt. From 1871 to 1922 it existed as an unusual administrative hybrid — an arrondissement administered directly as if it were a departement, since the departement it belonged to had been dismembered. It only acquired full, ordinary departement status (with its own prefect and prefecture at Belfort) by the law of 5 May 1922, which is the basis for `start_year: 1922` here — the point at which its present administrative form was reached, per the country-specific convention documented for French subnational units that departs from the default "departement since 1790" start rule. `type: subnational` reflects that this is a French administrative subdivision, not a sovereign entity: France held sovereignty over this territory throughout (aside from brief WWI-era military administration questions that do not apply here), and the row exists only to carry a reporting/administrative unit distinct from the national aggregate.

**Why this entry exists.** Data for "Territoire de Belfort" / "FRC24" spans back to 1862 (predating even the 1871 creation of the arrondissement) through the present, but the rows currently examined by the routing decision are keyed to the NUTS-style unit code FRA-FRC24 and were, prior to this entry, matched to one of three bare national FRA-* rows (FRA total-country series), which is wrong because those rows aggregate the whole of France (~551,700 km2 metropolitan) rather than this single departement (~609 km2, France's smallest). The 1862-1921 portion of the series predates the department's current boundary and current name and is treated, per the back_cast mechanism, as a reconstruction filed under this territory's later identity rather than evidence of a separately-reported historical unit — it is not grounds for an earlier start_year unless a review of the method column shows genuinely separate observed reporting for that period (an open question below). The department's official name and identity are confirmed by nuts_code_names.csv (FRC24 = Territoire de Belfort), and it is a real, currently-existing, statistically-tabulated administrative unit (INSEE code 90), which is sufficient basis for create_new under the project's convention for reporting units with data collected on them.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision found that GADM 4.1 ADM1 does digitise Territoire de Belfort as a distinct feature and the source is registered in sources.yaml (gadm-4.1-adm1, id_column GID_1), but the local copy of that source present under data/geodata/gadm-4.1/ is an 81-country subset that returns zero features for FRA, so nothing is actually fetchable from disk today. That makes this a genuine `none_available`-for-now case at the data layer even though the route conceptually is "new_source_needed" (full global GADM 4.1 ADM1 download, not yet obtained) — per the instructions here, since no source can currently be fetched, `polygon_source` is recorded as `none` and the desired remedy (re-download the full GADM 4.1 ADM1 layer) is left as an open question rather than written into `polygon_source`.

**Territory description:** Territoire de Belfort is the modern French departement 90, in the Bourgogne-Franche-Comte region, at the eastern edge of France near the Swiss and German borders, between the Vosges mountains and the Jura. It corresponds to the arrondissement of Belfort, containing the city of Belfort and the historic "Belfort Gap" (Trouee de Belfort), a strategically important lowland corridor. It is the smallest departement in metropolitan France by area, at approximately 609 km2 (a figure from general geographic knowledge of the modern departement's boundaries, not measured from any attached polygon since none is attached here). Its boundary has been essentially unchanged since 1871/1922 and is easily locatable on a modern map as the departement immediately north of the Territoire's larger neighbour Doubs and immediately south of Haut-Rhin (Alsace).

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor polity rows are declared. Before 1922, the same population and territory were administered as a residual arrondissement of the dismembered Haut-Rhin departement (itself the predecessor administrative concept, not represented as a separate polity row here since no data was found requiring that distinction), and any pre-1922 data in the underlying series is treated as a back-cast onto this later identity rather than requiring a separate predecessor polity. There is no successor: the departement is a current, ongoing administrative unit, hence the open `end_year: 2025`.

## Sourced claims

- Territoire de Belfort was created in 1871 from the portion of Haut-Rhin departement that remained French after the Treaty of Frankfurt ceded most of Alsace, including the rest of Haut-Rhin, to the German Empire.
- The Territoire de Belfort was granted full departement status by the French law of 5 May 1922, ending its status as a residual arrondissement administered outside the ordinary departement structure.
- The NUTS/administrative code FRC24 identifies Territoire de Belfort in nuts_code_names.csv, distinguishing it from the three bare national FRA-* rows it had previously been matched against.

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-suffix

**Add a SUBUNIT segment to avoid colliding with FRA's national prefix family**

The first proposed code, FRA-1922-2025, was rejected by validate_period_overlaps because it uses the bare <ISO3>-<start>-<end> shape, placing it in the same code family as FRA's national rows (which also match <ISO3>-<start>-<end>), causing a spurious overlap between a subnational and a national span. The fix is to insert a subunit token, following the dominant 419-of-433 pattern <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> used by 89 other FRA subnational rows (e.g. FRA-02-1800-2025). I chose the token TDB (Territoire de Belfort) rather than a numeric INSEE code (90) to keep it mnemonic and consistent with how this unit's other identifiers (FRC24) are non-numeric NUTS codes rather than the INSEE departement number; no existing FRA subnational code uses TDB, so it is free. Nothing else about the routing decision (span, container, polygon route) changed from the rejected answer.

### oq-pre1922-method-check

**Whether 1862-1921 data is a genuine back-cast or a separately reported historical unit**

Data attributed to this territory exists from 1862, before both the 1871 creation of the Belfort arrondissement and the 1922 elevation to full departement. The routing decision assumed this is a back-cast/reconstruction onto the later territorial identity rather than a genuinely separately and contemporaneously reported unit, but flagged that this should be checked by looking at whether the method column for those early years is mostly interpolated/scaled (supporting back_cast) or mostly observed and scoped tightly to the historical arrondissement boundary (which would instead argue for an earlier start_year or a distinct predecessor polity for 1871-1922).

### oq-container-tiling

**Whether FRA-1919-2025 actually tiles the full 1922-2025 span without gaps**

The container edge here assumes the national row FRA-1919-2025 covers the entire 1922-2025 span of this subnational row without any intervening change of national-era row (e.g. a later FRA row replacing it before 2025). This has not been independently verified against the polity table's actual FRA national row boundaries in this session; if FRA-1919-2025 does not extend to 2025, or if another FRA national row era is inserted between 1922 and 2025, the container list here would need a second edge to avoid leaving part of the span uncontained.
