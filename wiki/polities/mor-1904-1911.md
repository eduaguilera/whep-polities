# Morocco (1904-1911)

## Summary

The Alaouite Sultanate of Morocco between the **1904 Entente Cordiale** and the
start of the protectorate chain in 1911. Morocco was still a sovereign state in
these years — the Franco-British and Franco-Spanish agreements of 1904 were
agreements *between third parties* about spheres of influence, not a transfer of
Moroccan sovereignty, which happened with the **Treaty of Fez of 30 March 1912**
[biger-1995 §morocco](../sources/biger-1995.md#morocco). The period contains the
Algeciras Conference (1906), the deposition of Abd al-Aziz (1908) and the Agadir
crisis (1911).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon.** Cliopatria `Morocco`, time-step **1905-1911**, measured
  **359,089 km²** (ESRI:54009). This is the same Cliopatria series that
  [mor-1800-1904](mor-1800-1904.md) binds, so the family's territorial basis is
  continuous across the 1904 boundary: the preceding step 1890-1904 measures
  359,471 km², i.e. the sultanate's extent is flat to **0.106%** across the split.
  The 1904 boundary is therefore a *diplomatic* split, not a territorial one,
  and it is drawn where it is because the neighbouring rows already draw it there.
- **Not the French zone.** [mar-1911-1958](mar-1911-1958.md) is French Morocco
  (CShapes feature 600, 350,268 km², see its 2026-07-24 scope correction) and
  [smo-1912-1956](smo-1912-1956.md) is the Spanish zone (52,792 km²). This row
  precedes the partition and covers the **whole sultanate**, which is why its area
  sits between the French zone alone and the French + Spanish sum.

**Why this entry exists.** The years 1904-1910 were held by `MOR-1904-1956` until
that row was superseded on 2026-06-24 as a duplicate of `MAR-1911-1958`
([log morocco-mar-mor-double-count](../log.md#morocco-mar-mor-double-count)). The
duplication was real for **1911-1956**, but `MOR-1904-1956` also covered
**1904-1910**, which `MAR-1911-1958` does not — so retiring the whole row removed
seven years no other live polity claims. Nothing detected it, because
`validate_period_gaps` compares rows *within* a prefix and the hole falls
*between* the `MOR` and `MAR` families. It surfaced instead in
`data/final/iso3_successor_map.csv`, where modern `MAR` resolved 1850-1903 and
then nothing until its own rows begin in 1911 (issue 82). Two observed layer-B
rows land in the hole (IIA `morocco`, 1909 and 1910), and any ISO3-keyed consumer
asking for `MAR` in 1904-1910 got an empty answer with no error.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [mor-1800-1904](mor-1800-1904.md) — same sultanate, same
  Cliopatria series; the boundary is the 1904 Entente Cordiale.
- **Successor:** [mar-1911-1958](mar-1911-1958.md) — the protectorate chain. The
  handover year is taken from `MAR-1911-1958`'s existing start rather than
  asserted here; see the open question below.

## Sourced claims

- **1904: Entente Cordiale.** Recognised French preponderance in Morocco and set
  the stage for the 1912 protectorate; it did not end Moroccan sovereignty.
  [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §spanish-morocco](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- **1912-03-30: Treaty of Fez.** French protectorate established, Spanish zone in
  the north — the actual end of the sovereign sultanate.
  [biger-1995 §morocco](../sources/biger-1995.md#morocco)
- **Cliopatria treats 1905-1911 as the last sovereign-Morocco step**, then has no
  Morocco feature at all until 1956-1957 — independently consistent with the
  protectorate reading of 1912-1955.
  [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §scope](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md#scope)
- ISO3 `MOR` is a WHEP-local code, not ISO 3166-1; CoW does not list Morocco for
  1904-1955. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

**Cliopatria's step runs to 1911 inclusive; this row ends at 1911 (exclusive).**
Cliopatria draws sovereign Morocco through 1911 and the Treaty of Fez is 1912, so
on the sources alone this row would end in 1912. It ends in 1911 because
`MAR-1911-1958` starts there, and creating an overlap to satisfy a source is
worse than deferring to the neighbouring row. The one year in dispute — 1911 — is
`MAR-1911-1958`'s own unexplained start year, already recorded there as
`oq-1911-start`.

## Open questions

### oq-mor-1911-vs-1912

Whether the sovereign/protectorate boundary belongs at 1911 or 1912. Resolving
`oq-1911-start` on [mar-1911-1958](mar-1911-1958.md) to 1912 would move this
row's `end_year` to 1912 with it (and rename it `MOR-1904-1912`); the Cliopatria
step and the Treaty of Fez both point that way. Not decided here, because the
evidence for what 1911 was meant to mark sits on the other page and the fix for
the coverage hole must not depend on settling it.

### oq-mor-siba

The *Bled al-makhzen* / *Bled es-siba* problem carried by
[mor-1800-1904](mor-1800-1904.md) applies unchanged here: the fixed-border
Cliopatria polygon is the *makhzen*'s notional extent, and effective control in
the Rif, Atlas and pre-Saharan zones was smaller — more so in these years, not
less, since the 1907-1908 succession war (*hiba* of Abd al-Hafid) left large
areas outside any central authority.
