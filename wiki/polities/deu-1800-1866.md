# Prussia (to 1866)

## Summary

This row covers **67 years** of the Kingdom of Prussia from the
database floor (1800) through the Austro-Prussian War (1866).
Prussia was the largest and most powerful German state, leading the
Zollverein customs union from 1834. The row ends at 1866 when
Prussia annexed Hanover, Hesse-Kassel, Nassau, Frankfurt, and
Schleswig-Holstein (~95,000 km²) after defeating Austria, and
formed the North German Confederation.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor).
Successor: [deu-1866-1871](deu-1866-1871.md) — Austro-Prussian War
(1866), massive territorial expansion.

### juan-germany-label-is-not-prussia

**The `juan` / Mitchell-IHS series labelled "germany" does not track Prussia's
borders, so this row is the wrong scope for it.** Found 2026-08-18 while clearing
the wiki notes queue; measured on `consolidated_layer_b.parquet`.

If the 1850-1865 figures were Prussia alone, they would jump twice: at the **1866**
annexations (Schleswig-Holstein, Hanover, Hesse-Kassel, Nassau, Frankfurt, roughly
+34% of Prussian territory) and again at **1871**, when unification added the
southern states. Measured medians show neither — only ordinary growth:

| staple | 1850-1865 | 1867-1870 | 1872-1880 |
|---|---|---|---|
| rye (t) | 5,470,500 | 6,277,000 | 6,581,000 |
| wheat (t) | 2,205,000 | 2,530,500 | 2,982,000 |
| potatoes (t) | 14,076,500 | 20,221,500 | 22,816,000 |
| oats (t) | 3,831,000 | 3,959,500 | 4,326,000 |

Rye rises **15%** across the whole 1850-1880 span and wheat 35%, where doubling
the territory would have to show up as a step. The 1850-1865 level is already on
the same scale as the confirmed German-Empire figures for 1872-1880, so the series
is compiled on a **fixed, larger-than-Prussia basis** approximating the eventual
~541,000 km² Empire throughout.

**Affected rows:** 116 `juan` rows route here, and a further 37 to
`DEU-1866-1871` (North German Confederation, which excluded Bavaria, Württemberg
and Baden) — same mismatch, same reason.

**Why this is not rerouted.** No polity exists for "the German lands as a
statistical aggregate before 1871". The DEU family runs Prussia →
North German Confederation → Empire, each a narrower territory than the series
describes, so there is no correct target and inventing one is a decision about
whether a pre-unification statistical aggregate is a polity in this database's
sense. Compare `RWB-1922-1962` in issue #355, blocked the same way: the smaller
label has nowhere better to go.

Until then, `juan` German figures for 1850-1870 **overstate this row's territory**,
in the same direction and for a similar reason as the boundary-vintage mismatch
already recorded for `czechoslovakia|juan|1938-1944`: coherent numbers compiled on
a territory other than the one the row represents.
