# Serbia excluding Kosovo (2006-2008)

## Summary

Serbia **without Kosovo** for 2006-2008, between Montenegro's separation (June 2006) and Kosovo's
declaration of independence (February 2008), the years in which `SRB-2006-2008` includes Kosovo.
Kosovo was under UN administration throughout (UNSCR 1244) and outside Serbia's statistics. A
**reporting-area row**: the polity `SRB-2006-2008` keeps CShapes 2.0's coding; this row records
only what FAOSTAT area **272 Serbia** covers.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`, which routed area 272
2006-2007 to `SRB-2006-2008` (87,993 km² in ESRI:54034, Kosovo's 10,735 km² inside it) and 2008-2024
to `SRB-2008-2025` (without Kosovo). If the two routes were right, the series would drop in 2008.
It does not (production pin 20260325T111448Z-4fffe; all items below code 1700):

```
total harvested area, ha   2006        2007        2008        2009
area 272              2,949,846   3,012,785   2,987,659   3,012,204     -0.8% in 2008
```

and it continues area 186 Serbia and Montenegro's series, which already excludes Kosovo from 1999
(see `SCG-XK-1999-2006`). So 2006-2007 is Serbia net of Kosovo, the same ground as 2008 onward, and
`pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 272 2006-2007 here through
`manual_territory_routes`.
