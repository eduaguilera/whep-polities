# FAOSTAT Country area, country 187, item 6600

## Source and scope

UNdata update 22 January 2024, retrieved 3 November 2026: 1980, 1981 and 2021 each 39.00 thousand ha under Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha. This is a mirror of FAOSTAT, not independent measurement. Combined with the explicit label it contradicts an island-only 123 km² target; the number does not certify completeness of every commodity series.
