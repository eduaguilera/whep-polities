# Wikipedia: League of Nations Mandates and Late Colonial Africa

Snapshot of three Wikipedia articles consulted on 2026-04-16 for creating WHEP polity entries to fill coverage gaps in the pre-1961 agricultural data matching pipeline.

## Articles consulted

- **French Cameroun** — League of Nations mandate (1920) then UN trust territory (1946) over eastern portion of German Kamerun. Independent 1 January 1960. Capital: Yaoundé.
- **French Togoland** — League of Nations mandate (1920) then UN trust territory (1946) over eastern portion of German Togoland. Independent 27 April 1960. Capital: Lomé.
- **Colony of Niger** — French colony within French West Africa. Overseas territory 1946, autonomous republic 1958, independent 3 August 1960. Capital: Niamey.
