# African colonial-period — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

To cite dated colonial-era boundary and administrative events for the CShapes-sourced WHEP rows representing short-duration African colonial configurations (ANG-*, CIV-*, COD-*, COG-*, GAB-*, KEN-*, LBY-*).

## What it adds

- **Portuguese Angola**: "Agreements concluded with the Congo Free State, the German Empire and France in 1885–1886 fixed the limits of the province." Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 1891 delimited the SE border; "arbitration award of King Victor Emmanuel III of Italy in 1905" set a further boundary. Independence 11 November 1975 per Alvor Agreement.
- **Congo Free State**: Leopold II announced the "Congo Free State" name on 29 May 1885 (after Berlin Conference); formally replaced the International Association of the Congo on 1 August 1885. Stairs Expedition to Katanga 1891. Treaty with the UK 1894 (eastern strip for Lado Enclave). Belgian Parliament annexation 15 November 1908 (Belgian Congo).
- **French Equatorial Africa (AEF)**: AEF established in 1910 containing Gabon, French Congo, Ubangi-Shari, French Chad. AEF divided into four colonies in 1908. Dar al Kuti annexed 1912. Federation became unitary in 1934.
- **Libreville / Gabon 1839**: French admiral Édouard Bouët-Willaumez "negotiated a trade and protection treaty with the local Mpongwé ruler, Antchoué Komé Rapontcombo (known to the French as King Denis), in 1839." Foundation of French presence at Gabon Estuary.
- **French West Africa (AOF)**: Federation "originally created in 1895 as a union of Senegal, French Sudan, French Guinea and Ivory Coast only." Dahomey added 1904. 1895 date is the AOF federation formation.
- **East Africa Protectorate (Kenya)**: Protectorate proclaimed 1 July 1895. 1902: governance shifted to the Colonial Office and the protectorate boundaries "were extended to include what was previously the Eastern Province of Uganda". Capital moved from Mombasa to Nairobi in 1905. 23 July 1920: inland areas annexed as Colony of Kenya.
- **Italian Libya → Libyan independence**: "From 1943 to 1951, Tripolitania and Cyrenaica were under British military administration, while the French controlled Fezzan." UN General Assembly resolution (1949) declared Libya should become independent before 1 January 1952. Libya declared independence as the United Kingdom of Libya on 24 December 1951.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia snapshots, not peer-reviewed. CShapes 2.0 time-steps define the WHEP row boundaries (1886, 1890, 1891, 1894, 1900, 1906, 1912, 1943, 1951, etc.) based on cartographic-area changes rather than the political events reported here — there can be small offsets between the CShapes cut and the political event (treaty signing vs ratification vs administrative reorganisation).
