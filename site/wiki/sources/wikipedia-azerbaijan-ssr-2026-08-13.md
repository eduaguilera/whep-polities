# Azerbaijan SSR and its autonomous units — Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-13)

## Why it was ingested

## What it adds

### establishment-1920

- "The Azerbaijan SSR was established on 28 April 1920 after the surrender
  of the government of the Azerbaijan Democratic Republic to local
  Bolsheviks led by Mirza Davud Huseynov and Nariman Narimanov and the
  invasion of the Bolshevik 11th Red Army."
- "Azerbaijan SSR was created on 28 April 1920 following the Red Army
  invasion of Azerbaijan."
- "On 28 April 1920, Temporary Revolutionary Committee took control over
  the country, and formed a government named Council of People's
  Commissars of Azerbaijan SSR."

### tsfsr-1922

- "On 12 March 1922 the leaders of Azerbaijan, Armenian, and Georgian
  Soviet Socialist Republics established a union known as the
  Transcaucasian Soviet Federal Socialist Republic (TSFSR)."
- "In December 1922 TSFSR agreed to join the union with Russia, Ukraine,
  and Belarus, thus creating the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics."
- "the first two years of the Azerbaijani SSR were as an nominally
  independent country until incorporation into the Transcaucasian SFSR,
  along with the Armenian SSR and the Georgian SSR."

### tsfsr-dissolution-1936

- "In December 1936, the Transcaucasian Union was dismantled when the
  leaders in the Union Council found themselves unable to come to
  agreement over several issues. Azerbaijan, Armenia, and Georgia then
  each became a republic of the Soviet Union directly."
  (Azerbaijan SSR article — month and year only, no day.)
- The day comes from the constitution that did it:
  "The 1936 Constitution of the Soviet Union, also known as the Stalin
  Constitution, was the communist state constitution of the USSR adopted
  on 5 December 1936." — and, in the same article, "The Transcaucasian
  Socialist Federative Soviet Republic, one of the four republics to sign
  the Treaty on the Creation of the USSR, was dissolved and its
  constituent republics, the Georgian Soviet Socialist Republic, Armenian
  Soviet Socialist Republic and Azerbaijani Soviet Socialist Republic,
  were elevated to union republics individually."
  ([1936 Constitution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936_Constitution_of_the_Soviet_Union))

### independence-1991

The two articles consulted give **two different dates**, and both are
real events at different legal levels:

- "Azerbaijan declared independence from the USSR on 30 August 1991 and
  became part of the Commonwealth of Independent States."
  ([History of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Azerbaijan))
- "the Supreme Council of Azerbaijan adopted a Declaration of Independence
  on 18 October 1991 which was affirmed by a nationwide referendum in
  December, while the Soviet Union officially ceased to exist on
  26 December. The country celebrates its Day of Restoration of
  Independence on 18 October."
  ([Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan))
- "On 5 February 1991, Azerbaijan SSR was renamed the Republic of
  Azerbaijan according to the Decision No.16-XII of the Supreme Soviet of
  Azerbaijan ... remained in the USSR for the period before the
  declaration of independence in October 1991."
- "The final dissolution of the Soviet Union took place on 26 December
  1991."

### autonomous-units

- Nakhichevan: "an autonomous republic within the Azerbaijan SSR, itself a
  republic within the Soviet Union. It was formed on 16 March 1921 and
  became a part of the Azerbaijan SSR proper on 9 February 1924."
  ([Nakhichevan ASSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakhchivan_Autonomous_Soviet_Socialist_Republic))
- Nagorno-Karabakh: "an autonomous oblast within the Azerbaijan Soviet
  Socialist Republic that was created on July 7, 1923"; "At the time of
  its formation, area of the oblast was 4,161 km2 (1,607 sq mi)."
  ([Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh_Autonomous_Oblast))

## Known limitations

- **No area figure.** None of these articles states the area of the
  Azerbaijan SSR in prose; the modern-Azerbaijan figure lives in the
  infobox, which the plaintext extract does not return. The polity page's
  "~86,600 km²" therefore cannot be sourced here, and the page now cites
  the measured polygon instead. Only the NKAO figure (4,161 km² at
  formation, 1923) comes from this snapshot.
- **1991 is genuinely ambiguous in the source.** 30 August 1991 (Supreme
  Soviet declaration on restoring state independence) and 18 October 1991
  (Constitutional Act, and the date Azerbaijan itself commemorates) are
  both stated, in different articles. Neither changes the WHEP row, which
  ends in 1991 either way.
- Wikipedia is a secondary source. Dates that would move a WHEP row
  boundary should be checked against the legal instruments themselves.
- The Azerbaijan SSR article says the republic was "one of the constituent
  republics of the Soviet Union between 1922 and 1991" in its lead while
  dating the republic's creation to 28 April 1920 in the body; the 1920
  date is the one the WHEP row uses, on the WHEP rule that the territory,
  not the legal status, defines the row.
