# Europe + Asia gap-fillers — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

To provide externally-sourced dates for five pre-federation / interwar / decolonisation-era rows that were dangling references from elsewhere in the WHEP CSV.

## What it adds

- **Grand Duchy of Tuscany**: dissolved 1801, replaced by Kingdom of Etruria under House of Bourbon-Parma; annexed to France 1808 (Elisa Bonaparte as Grand Duchess); restored to Habsburg-Lorraine under Ferdinand III at Congress of Vienna 1814; 1859 revolution deposed Grand Duke; 1860 referendum (~95% for union) and formal annexation to Sardinia.
- **Kingdom of the Two Sicilies**: formally established 8 December 1816 (Ferdinand IV → Ferdinand I, Treaty of Casalanza) uniting Naples and Sicily; before 1816 they were separate Kingdoms of Naples and Sicily. Garibaldi's Expedition of the Thousand 1860; King Francis II surrendered at Gaeta February 1861; Proclamation of Kingdom of Italy 17 March 1861.
- **Konbaung Burma**: Bodawpaya acquired Arakan 1784, Manipur 1814, Assam 1817. First Anglo-Burmese War 1824–1826 ended with Treaty of Yandabo; Burma ceded Arakan, Manipur, Assam and Tenasserim plus a £1M indemnity.
- **Kingdom of Montenegro**: gained territory post-Balkan Wars (Sandžak split with Serbia 30 May 1913); Austria-Hungary occupied Montenegro 15 January 1916 – October 1918; Podgorica Assembly 26 November 1918 deposed King Nikola I and unified Montenegro with Serbia.
- **Malayan Union / Federation of Malaya**: Malayan Union established 1 April 1946 (Federated + Unfederated Malay States + Penang + Malacca); dissolved and replaced by Federation of Malaya 1 February 1948; independence 31 August 1957.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia snapshots, not peer-reviewed. For Two Sicilies, the WHEP start year of 1800 is **historically incorrect** — the unified Kingdom of the Two Sicilies did not exist until 8 December 1816. Before 1816 the territory was two separate polities (Naples + Sicily). This is an input to a CSV-fix recommendation on [two-1800-1860](../polities/two-1800-1860.md).
