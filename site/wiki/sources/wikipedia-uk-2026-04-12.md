# History of the United Kingdom / Anglo-Irish Treaty

## Why it was ingested

Provide territorial-change dates for the GBR-1800-1921 polity
page. Initial Wikipedia baseline for a draft page.

## What it adds

### 1801-union
*Act of Union with Ireland.*

"On 1 January 1801, the Great Britain and Ireland joined to form
the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland." Ireland sent
approximately 100 MPs to the House of Commons and 28
representative peers to the House of Lords.

### 1921-treaty
*Anglo-Irish Treaty.*

The Anglo-Irish Treaty was signed on **6 December 1921** at
10 Downing Street, London. Ratified by the Dáil on
**7 January 1922** by a vote of 64 to 57. The treaty created
the Irish Free State from 26 counties.

### 1922-free-state
*Irish Free State establishment.*

The Irish Free State formally came into existence on
**6 December 1922** when the constitution became law via royal
proclamation. Article 12 of the treaty provided Northern Ireland
"an option to opt out of the Irish Free State within one month
of the Treaty coming into effect," which "was exercised by the
Parliament of Northern Ireland."

### 1927-name
*Name change.*

"In 1927, the United Kingdom changed its formal title to the
United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland."

## Known limitations

Wikipedia is not an academic source. The History of the United
Kingdom article provides sparse coverage of territorial changes
to the UK mainland between 1801 and 1921 (the period was
remarkably stable for metropolitan borders). Heligoland (ceded
to Germany 1890), Cyprus (1878 from Ottoman Empire), and other
minor changes are not covered. Biger 1995's GREAT BRITAIN entry
(not yet ingested) will provide exact boundary dates.
