# Self-ref / chain-overlap — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

To cite dated events for the six WHEP rows whose codes are dangling references but that largely represent "same-polity-before-an-administrative-event" cases (LCA-1800-1838, VCT-1800-1833, PAN-1800-1979, FRS-1884-1977, SMO-1912-1956, MOR-1956-1958).

## What it adds

- **Saint Lucia**: island changed hands "a dozen times" between French and British before "the British secured it in 1814, as part of the Treaty of Paris, ending the Napoleonic Wars." Slavery abolished in British colonies 1834; "Full freedom was duly granted by the British in 1838" after the four-year apprenticeship. 1838 in WHEP is not a territorial change but the end-of-apprenticeship administrative marker.
- **Saint Vincent**: British control confirmed by the Treaty of Paris (1763); Second Carib War 1795–1797; slavery abolished 1834, apprenticeship ended 1838. The WHEP 1833 boundary aligns with the eve of slavery abolition rather than a specific territorial event; may be a CShapes-era administrative marker.
- **Panama**: independence from Colombia 3 November 1903; Hay-Bunau-Varilla Treaty 1903 granted the US rights "as if it were sovereign" in a ~16×80 km canal zone "in perpetuity"; Torrijos-Carter Treaties signed 7 September 1977; Canal Zone territory returned 1979; full canal handover 31 December 1999.
- **French Somaliland**: treaties signed with the Issa clan 1884 (Obock base 1862, Djibouti founded 1884); territory renamed *French Territory of the Afars and the Issas* in 1967; Djibouti independence 27 June 1977.
- **Spanish Protectorate in Morocco**: "established on 27 November 1912 by a treaty between France and Spain." Northern zone became part of independent Morocco on 7 April 1956; southern zone (Cape Juby/Tarfaya) ceded via the Treaty of Angra de Cintra on 1 April 1958.
- **Morocco 1956–1958**: French Protectorate ended in 1956 (March 2 conventional date); Spanish Protectorate northern zone 7 April 1956; the Tarfaya transfer 1 April 1958 is the obvious terminus for a "Morocco 1956-1958" WHEP row.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia snapshots, not peer-reviewed. The 1833 and 1838 WHEP cuts for Saint Vincent and Saint Lucia are unusual — they align with the emancipation timeline rather than a territorial or sovereignty change. Flagged on individual pages.
