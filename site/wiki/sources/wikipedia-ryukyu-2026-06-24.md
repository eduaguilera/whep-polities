# Ryukyu Islands — Wikipedia (accessed 2026-06-24)

## Why it was ingested

Needed to establish dates and legal framework for the two new Ryukyu
Islands polities (RYU-1937-1945 and RYU-1945-1972) created to resolve
the FAO 1952 coverage gap for data labeled "Ryukyu Islands".

## What it adds

- Start/end dates for US Military Government of the Ryukyu Islands
  (USMGRI, 1945–1950) and US Civil Administration of the Ryukyu Islands
  (USCAR, 1950–1972).
- San Francisco Peace Treaty (1951) Article 3 designation of Ryukyu
  Islands as US trust territory.
- Amami Islands reversion to Japan (1953-12-25), distinguishing the
  Amami sub-territory from the main Ryukyu chain.
- Okinawa Reversion Agreement (signed 1971-06-17, in force 1972-05-15).
- Geographic area of the Okinawa Prefecture / Ryukyu Islands core chain:
  ~2,271 km².

## Known limitations

- Wikipedia is a secondary source; all date claims should be verified
  against primary sources (San Francisco Peace Treaty text, USCAR
  administrative records) if precise start/end dates matter for matching.
- The 2,271 km² figure is for the modern Okinawa Prefecture (GADM 4.1
  JPN.38_1) and excludes the Amami Islands (Kagoshima Prefecture today).
  If FAO data for "Ryukyu Islands" pre-1953 included Amami production,
  the polygon understates the territory for those years.
- The Wikipedia article conflates USMGRI and USCAR periods in some
  passages; dates here are taken from the specific sub-articles on each
  administrative body.
