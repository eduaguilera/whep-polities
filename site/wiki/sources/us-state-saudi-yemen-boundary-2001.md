# Saudi Arabia/Yemen boundary guidance, 2001

## Territorial effect and precision

Guidance Bulletin No. 21, **5 February 2001**, PDF p 2, Background, estimates
Yemen's acquisition under the 2000 treaty at **35,000 km²** and describes a
small Saudi acquisition near Jabal ath Tha'ar. This is an estimate without a
demonstrated calculation or uncertainty interval, not a measured polygon-area
target. It corroborates territorial change; its magnitude does not validate
CShapes' different measured area.

Read with the caveats on pp 1–2: the eastern section had not previously been
delimited; no reference datum for the turning points had been released;
representation was indefinite and not necessarily authoritative. The final
survey/demarcation remained pending. The stated acquisition must not be
interpreted as a precisely measured transfer across an already agreed border.
The three-page original includes a small-scale explanatory map.
