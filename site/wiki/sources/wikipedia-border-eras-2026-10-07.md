# Wikipedia articles for the border eras of issue 686 (accessed 2026-10-07)

## Why it was ingested

Issue 686 listed fifteen live rows whose single polygon ran across a documented change in their
borders, plus the missing Serbian rows between 1945 and 2006. Splitting those rows needed a cited date
and a stated extent for every new boundary. The articles below were fetched with the MediaWiki API
(`action=query&prop=extracts&explaintext=1`) on 2026-10-07. The quotations are verbatim from those
extracts.

## What it adds

### finland

- Treaty of Tartu (Finland-Russia): "The Treaty of Tartu ... was signed on 14 October 1920 between
  Finland and Soviet Russia" and "Finland additionally received Petsamo, with its ice-free harbour on
  the Arctic Ocean".
- Petsamo Province: "This panhandle used to give Finland access to the Arctic Ocean, until it was
  annexed by the Soviet Union in 1944."
- Moscow Armistice: "signed between Finland on one side and the Soviet Union and United Kingdom on the
  other side on 19 September 1944" and "The new armistice also handed all of Petsamo to the Soviet
  Union, and Finland was further compelled to lease Porkkala to the Soviet Union for a period of fifty
  years (the area was returned to Finnish control in 1956)."

Used by: fin-1917-1920, fin-1920-1940, fin-1940-1944, fin-1944-2025.

### bulgaria

- Bulgarian unification: "The Unification of Bulgaria ... was the act of unification of the
  Principality of Bulgaria and the province of Eastern Rumelia in the autumn of 1885", proclaimed on
  6 September 1885, and on 24 March 1886 "the Ottoman Empire and Bulgaria signed the Tophane
  Agreement, which recognized the Prince of Bulgaria as Governor-General of the autonomous Ottoman
  Province Eastern Rumelia."

Used by: bgr-1878-1885, bgr-1885-1913.

### papal-states

- Papal States: "By 1860, much of the Papal States' territory had been conquered by the Kingdom of
  Sardinia and was incorporated into the Kingdom of Italy in 1861, except Lazio, which remained under
  the pope's control."

Used by: pap-1800-1860, pap-1860-1870.

### lithuania

- Klaipeda Revolt: "The Klaipeda Revolt took place in January 1923 in the Klaipeda Region (also known
  as the Memel Territory or Memelland)", "After prolonged negotiations, a formal international
  agreement, the Klaipeda Convention, was signed in May 1924", and "The Klaipeda Region covered
  2,848 km2 (1,100 sq mi), which included the Curonian Lagoon of approximately 412 km2".
- Klaipeda Region: "In March 1939, Lithuania acquiesced to Nazi demands and transferred the Klaipeda
  Region to Germany."

Used by: ltu-1918-1923, ltu-1923-1940.

### austria

- Treaty of Zurich (1859): the treaty between France and Austria "reaffirmed the terms of the
  preliminary peace ... and ceded Lombardy to France".
- Treaty of Vienna (1866): "The treaty confirmed the terms of 12 August Armistice of Cormons,
  resulting in the transfer of Venetia and most of Friuli to the French Empire, who then gave the
  region to Italy after the consent of the inhabitants through a referendum."

Used by: aut-1800-1859, aut-1859-1866, aut-1866-1918.

### italy

- Treaty of Rapallo (1920): "The treaty, signed on 12 November 1920 in Rapallo, Italy, generally
  redeemed the promises of territorial gains in the former Austrian Littoral by awarding Italy
  territories generally corresponding to the peninsula of Istria and the former Princely County of
  Gorizia and Gradisca, with the addition of the Snezhnik Plateau" and "in Dalmatia, Italy received
  the city of Zadar and several islands."
- Treaty of Paris between Italy and the Allied Powers: "signed on 10 February 1947"; its territorial
  changes include the "Transfer of the Adriatic islands of Cres, Losinj, Lastovo and Palagruza; of
  Istria south of the river Mirna; of the exclave territory of Zadar in Dalmatia; of the city of
  Rijeka and the region known as the Julian March to the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia"
  and the "Transfer to France of Briga and Tenda, and minor revisions of the Franco-Italian border
  including Mont Cenis plateau, Mont Thabor, and Mont Chaberton".

Used by: ita-1919-1947, ita-1947-2025.

### china

- Treaty of Aigun: "an 1858 treaty between the Russian Empire and Qing dynasty of China" and "in 1860
  the Sino-Russian Convention of Peking affirmed Russian gains under the Treaty of Aigun and also
  ceded Primorye and the Ussuri region to the Russians."
- Convention of Peking: "It granted Russia the right to the Ussuri krai, a part of the modern day
  Primorye".

Used by: chn-1800-1860, chn-1860-1895.

### hong-kong

- Treaty of Nanking: signed "on 29 August 1842"; "The Qing government agreed to make Hong Kong Island
  a crown colony, ceding it to the Queen Victoria of Great Britain, in perpetuity".
- Kowloon: "The part of Kowloon south of Boundary Street, together with Stonecutters Island, was ceded
  by Qing China to the United Kingdom under the Convention of Peking of 1860."
- Convention for the Extension of Hong Kong Territory: "signed between Qing China and the United
  Kingdom in Peking on 9 June 1898, leasing to the United Kingdom for 99 years, at no charge, the New
  Territories (as the area became known) and New Kowloon".

Used by: hkg-1842-1860, hkg-1860-1898, hkg-1898-2025.

### estonia

- Petseri County: "Soviet authorities transferred most of Petseri county from the Estonian SSR to the
  Russian SFSR in 1944" and "Since 1944, however, most of the county has been administered as
  Pechorsky District of Pskov Oblast".

Used by: est-1940-1945, est-1945-1991.

### var

- Arrondissement of Grasse: "The arrondissement of Grasse was created in 1800 as part of the
  department Var." and "In 1860 it became part of the department Alpes-Maritimes."

Used by: fra-83-1790-1860, fra-83-1860-2025.

### serbia

- Socialist Republic of Serbia: "The process was formalised in April 1945, when the provisional
  People's Assembly of Serbia was created, also appointing the first People's Government of Serbia",
  "Within Serbia were the two autonomous provinces: Kosovo and Vojvodina", and "In that form, it
  lasted until the constitutional reforms from 1990 to 1992, when it was reconstituted, as the
  Republic of Serbia within the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia."

Used by: ser-1945-2006.

## Known limitations

- Wikipedia gives the Petseri transfer as 1944. Other accounts date the decrees to November 1944
  (the land east of the Narva) and January 1945 (Petseri). The Estonian split follows issue 686 and
  puts the boundary at 1945. The page says so.
- These articles give dates and named territories, not outlines. Every polygon used with them comes
  from a fetched geodata source, and each page says which one.
