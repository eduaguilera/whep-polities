# Saudi-Yemen boundary treaty, 2000

## Dates and scope

Concluded at Jeddah on **12 June 2000**; entered into force on **4 July 2000**
by exchange of instruments of ratification. The registration cover is printed
p 203 (PDF p 236); Article 5 is printed p 230 (PDF p 263). Registered on
9 October 2006; the treaty-series DOI publication date is 16 September 2010,
not the date of the territorial agreement. Arabic is the authentic text;
the cited Article 5 is the UN English translation.

Article 2, printed pp 228–229, specifies the boundary sections. Article 3
requires detailed surveyed maps and provides for signed maps to become part
of the treaty. This supports a real boundary agreement, not precise validation
of every vertex in CShapes or a January 1 statistical transition.

The annual 2000 polity split is an explicit representation convention. CShapes
2.0 GW 670/678 changes on the signing date; the treaty's legal effective date
is later in the same year. Neither source establishes the territory used by
every item-level annual observation during that transition year.
