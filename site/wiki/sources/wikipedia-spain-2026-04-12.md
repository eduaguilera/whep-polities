# History of Spain / Spanish-American War

## Why it was ingested

Provide territorial-change dates for the ESP-1800-2025 polity page.
Initial Wikipedia baseline covering the Peninsular War, Latin American
independence movements, and the 1898 Spanish-American War.

## What it adds

### 1808-peninsular-war
*Napoleonic invasion and Peninsular War.*

"Napoleon called on Ferdinand to come to Bayonne" where Charles IV
abdicated and Joseph Bonaparte was designated king (1808). The
Peninsular War lasted 1808–1814; Spain regained independence in 1814.
Not a permanent territorial change for metropolitan Spain.

### colonial-losses
*Loss of American colonies (1810s–1820s).*

| Territory | Date | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Argentina | 1816 | "Argentina declared its independence in 1816" |
| Chile | 1817 | Lost "permanently in 1817" at Battle of Chacabuco |
| Colombia | 1819 | "Battle of Boyacá in 1819, ending Spanish rule" |

| Peru | 1824 | "Battle of Ayacucho...signified the end of the Spanish Empire on the American mainland" |
| Cuba, Puerto Rico | Retained | Until 1898 |

### 1898-treaty
*Spanish-American War and Treaty of Paris.*

War: April 21 to August 13, 1898. Treaty of Paris signed
**10 December 1898**. Spain ceded Puerto Rico, Guam, and the
Philippines to the United States, and Cuba gained independence
(under US protectorate). US paid "$20 million to Spain to cover
infrastructure owned by Spain."

## Known limitations

Wikipedia is not an academic source. Metropolitan Spanish territorial
changes are not covered (Spain's European borders were essentially
stable post-1815). The Carlist Wars (1833–1876) had no territorial
impact on Spain's external borders. Biger 1995's SPAIN entry would
provide exact boundary dates.
