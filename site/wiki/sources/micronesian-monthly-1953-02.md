# Yap Grows Up

## Source and scope

Micronesian Monthly, February 1953, Vol. II No. 3, printed p. 16 (PDF p. 17). Announces transfer of the Woleai islands from Palau to Yap on 1 April 1953 and lists the islands. This is a contemporaneous planned-transfer announcement, not the signed order. The 1980 Code corroborates the resulting district extent. Original scan preserved by Habele; university handle 10524/65437 currently redirects to a collection.
