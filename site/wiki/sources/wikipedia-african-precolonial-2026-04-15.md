# African precolonial states — Wikipedia snapshots (2026-04-15)

## Why it was ingested

To provide externally-sourced founding and terminal dates for the eleven sub-Saharan African precolonial rows in the WHEP CSV whose polygons come from Paine et al. (2024). Satisfies wiki rule 1b (each polity page must cite at least one external source beyond `[database]`).

## What it adds

Key dated claims per polity (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-04-15):

- **Sokoto Caliphate**: jihad launched by Usman dan Fodio on 21 February 1804; population 10–20 million by 1837; over 30 emirates at peak; defeated at Burmi 27 July 1903; last Vizier conceded 13 March 1903.
- **Bornu Empire**: continuation of Kanem-Bornu founded c.700, the Bornu phase following the 1380 westward move after Sayfawa dynasty expelled from Kanem; conquered by Rabih az-Zubayr in 1893 ("first time the empire came under foreign domination"); Rabih killed at Battle of Kousséri 22 April 1900; partitioned between British and French colonial administrations by 1902.
- **Ibadan**: founded c.1829 as a war camp for Oyo/Ife/Ijebu refugees; British protectorate via treaty signed by Baale Fijabi with Governor G.C. Denton on 15 August 1893.
- **Ijebu Kingdom**: 15th-century founding (c.1400s); British launched Ijebu Expedition in 1892 against trade monopoly, using Maxim guns; capital captured 1892.
- **Kingdom of Benin** (Edo, southern Nigeria — NOT modern Republic of Benin): Oba dynasty established c.1180 by Oranmiyan; British Punitive Expedition captured Benin City in 1897.
- **Egba / Abeokuta**: founded c.1830 after collapse of Oyo Empire; Egba United Government recognized; lost autonomy to British colonial structure by 1914 (Alake recognized as paramount ruler, Egba incorporated into Southern Nigeria Protectorate).
- **Nupe Kingdom / Bida Emirate**: Nupe established mid-15th century in the Niger–Kaduna basin; subjugated by Fulani/Sokoto in early 19th century; British Niger Company captured Bida in 1897, installing a puppet ruler.
- **Igala Kingdom**: roughly triangular ~14,000 km² territory at the Niger–Benue confluence, in modern Kogi State, Nigeria; subjugation date by Royal Niger Company not clearly specified on Wikipedia (WHEP CSV asserts 1901; flagged as `oq`).
- **Luba Empire**: founded c.1585 in Katanga; "split in two by a succession dispute in 1889, ending the unified state, and later joined the Congo Free State."
- **Lunda Empire**: founded c.1665 (first Mwane-a-Yamvu title); "at the start of the colonial era (1884), the Lunda heartland was divided between Portuguese Angola, King Leopold II's Congo Free State and the British in North-Western Rhodesia."
- **Zulu Kingdom**: founded c.1816 under Shaka after he assumed leadership of the Mthethwa alliance; defeated at Battle of Ulundi 1879 (Anglo-Zulu War).
- **Swazi Kingdom**: Ndvungunye acceded 1780; Sobhuza I's reign 1815–1839 consolidated the clans; London Convention 1894 placed Swaziland as protected state of the South African Republic.

## Known limitations

Wikipedia snapshots, not peer-reviewed. Founding dates for African precolonial states typically mark dynastic events and are contested; the 1800 start year on WHEP rows is a database floor, not a founding date. Terminal dates match conventional historiography (1879 Ulundi, 1892 Ijebu Expedition, 1893 Rabih, 1894 London Convention, 1897 Benin Punitive Expedition, 1897 Bida, 1903 Sokoto). Paine et al. (2024) polygon provenance should be verified separately.
