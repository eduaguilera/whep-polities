# El Salvador

## Summary

El Salvador is the smallest Central American country, bounded by Guatemala to the west and Honduras to the north and east, with the Pacific Ocean to the south. Biger 1995 shows El Salvador on the regional boundary map (p.264) and notes that the Central American colonies separated from Europe in 1821 [biger-1995 &sect;el-salvador](../sources/biger-1995.md#el-salvador). WHEP tracks El Salvador from 1821 (independence from Spain) as a continuous row because its territory has remained essentially constant since independence.

## Territorial extent

El Salvador is bounded by Guatemala to the west and Honduras (213 miles; 342 km.) to the north and east, with the Pacific Ocean to the south [biger-1995 &sect;honduras](../sources/biger-1995.md#honduras). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
