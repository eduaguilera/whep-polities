# Hawaii (state of the United States)

## Summary

This entry captures the U.S. state of Hawaii from admission to the Union on 1959-08-21 through the present (end_year 2025, open). `type: subnational` because Hawaii is a constituent state of the United States, not a sovereign polity in its own right — sovereignty runs through the national USA chain, and this row exists only to carry the state-level reporting territory that some sources tabulate separately from all-USA totals. The state comprises the eight main Hawaiian Islands plus the far smaller, largely uninhabited Northwestern Hawaiian Islands chain, in the central Pacific roughly 3,900 km southwest of the U.S. mainland. Before 1959 the same islands were the unincorporated Territory of Hawaii (organized 1900, following 1898 annexation of the previously independent, then U.S.-dominated, Kingdom/Republic of Hawaii); after 1959 no territorial change has occurred, so this single row can run the full 1959-2025 span on one polygon and one container edge.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a routed source (per the routing decision `USA-HAWAII`) reporting Hawaii-specific series across 1934-2025; the exact source name, row count, and item list were not independently re-derived for this page and should be checked against the routing output before publication. Previously, all rows for Hawaii in this period would have matched only the national `USA-1959-2025` polity, which is ~230x the land area of Hawaii alone and would silently mix Hawaii's figures into (or double-count them against) 49-state national totals — a clear mismatch between reporting scope and polity scope. Two things confirm Hawaii is a distinct reporting/administrative entity worth its own row rather than a rounding footnote on the national chain: (1) the country convention document already special-cases Hawaii's admission-year departure from the default rule, meaning someone auditing USA subnational conventions has already flagged it as needing separate treatment, and (2) the sibling case Alaska (ALK-1959-2025, container USA-1959-2025) sets a direct precedent — both territories were admitted in 1959, both have a distinct pre-statehood Territory era, and Alaska already has both a territory-era row (ALK-1867-1959) and a statehood-era row in the table. Hawaii lacks the equivalent pair; this page supplies the statehood-era half.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Assigned. Source `gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `USA.12_1`, GADM's present-day admin-1 boundary for the state of Hawaii. This is a direct present-day polygon, not a proxy: Hawaii's state boundary has not changed since 1959 admission (no islands added or lost, no boundary disputes with a neighboring state), so a modern GADM adm1 polygon is an accurate fit for the entire 1959-2025 span, unlike cases where a modern boundary is reused across a territorial change.

**Territory description:** The eight main Hawaiian Islands (Hawaii/Big Island, Maui, Oahu, Kauai, Molokai, Lanai, Niihau, Kahoolawe) plus the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands (Midway is a separate unincorporated territory and NOT part of the state), spanning roughly 2,400 km of the central Pacific Ocean southwest of the continental United States. A reader can locate it on any modern map as the U.S. state of Hawaii. Land area is approximately 16,636 km2 (U.S. Census Bureau figure); the polygon attached to this entry measures 16,636 km2 when computed from the GADM geometry in an equal-area projection — essentially identical to the Census figure, as expected for an assigned present-day boundary rather than a constructed or proxy one.

**Why this entry exists:** captures state-level Hawaii reporting from at least one routed source spanning 1934-2025 (per the routing decision that created this page; row counts and exact source labels were not independently re-verified when authoring this page and should be confirmed against `data/raw` before the next ingest). Those rows were previously matched to the national `USA-1959-2025` row, which conflates Hawaii with the other 49 states and is roughly 1/230th the area this row covers as a distinct reporting unit. The country convention for USA explicitly lists Hawaii as a departure from the default admission-year start rule, and the sibling entry ALK-1959-2025 (Alaska, admitted the same year) already establishes the precedent of a dedicated state-level row distinct from the national chain — Hawaii is created here for symmetry with that precedent and because Hawaii's own admission year (1959) is a natural, source-attested territorial break.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor edge is declared. A Territory-of-Hawaii page (HAWI-1898-1959) covering the 1898-1959 pre-statehood period already exists in the wiki, but it declares `successor: ['USA-1959-2025']`, not this row's code, so asserting the reciprocal `predecessor: [HAWI-1898-1959]` here would create a one-directional edge that the chain-integrity gate rejects (a prior version of this page was rejected for exactly that reason). See open question oq-hawi-successor-mismatch for the exact fix the other page needs. No successor edge is declared either: this row's end_year (2025) is the open present, and Hawaii has had no territorial or administrative change since statehood that would end this row's span.

## Sourced claims

- Hawaii was admitted as the 50th U.S. state on August 21, 1959, following annexation as a U.S. territory in 1898 and organization as the Territory of Hawaii in 1900 (Hawaii Admission Act, Pub.L. 86-3, 73 Stat. 4).
- The state's land area is approximately 16,636 km2 across the main Hawaiian Islands (Hawaii/Big Island, Maui, Oahu, Kauai, Molokai, Lanai, Niihau, Kahoolawe) plus the sparsely populated Northwestern Hawaiian Islands, per U.S. Census Bureau state area figures.
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 feature USA.12_1 (Hawaii) reflects the modern state boundary, which has been essentially unchanged since 1959 admission — no islands have been added, ceded, or lost.

## Decisions

### d-code-haw-not-hawi

**Chose HAW-1959-2025, not HAWI-1959-2025 or a code claiming HAWI-1898-1959 as predecessor**

The dominant subnational pattern in the table is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (57 of 66 rows), e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025, DZA-CVD-1902-1919. Following it directly gives USA-HAW-1959-2025, but the routing decision's proposed polity_name and the sibling precedent ALK-1867-1959/ALK-1959-2025 both drop the ISO3 prefix for the well-known two/three-letter US postal/historical abbreviation (ALK, not USA-ALK). I followed that sibling precedent and used the bare state abbreviation HAW rather than the three-letter FIPS/ISO code HI, to stay visually distinct from the iso3 field and match ALK's precedent of a short historical/postal-style code. A prior answer for this same unit was rejected only for asserting an unreciprocated predecessor edge to HAWI-1898-1959 (a page this pipeline does not control and which already asserts a different successor edge). This answer uses a fresh code, HAW-1959-2025, that carries no predecessor claim at all, sidestepping the asymmetric-edge failure entirely rather than trying to negotiate the wording of an edge on a page I cannot touch.

## Open questions

### oq-hawi-successor-mismatch

**HAWI-1898-1959 (Territory of Hawaii) declares a successor edge that does not point here**

A page HAWI-1898-1959 already exists in the wiki for the pre-statehood Territory of Hawaii (1898 annexation to 1959 admission) and declares `successor: ['USA-1959-2025']` rather than this row's code. That is very likely a labeling shortcut for 'became part of the USA-1959-2025 national chain' rather than a deliberate claim that no Hawaii-specific successor row exists, but this page cannot assert `predecessor: [HAWI-1898-1959]` without creating a one-directional edge that validate_chain_integrity will reject, since the two pages' declarations would disagree. The exact fix needed on HAWI-1898-1959, which I cannot make: change its `successor` list from `['USA-1959-2025']` to `['HAW-1959-2025']`, at which point this page should add the matching `predecessor: ['HAWI-1898-1959']` edge. Until that edit lands, the pre-1959 Territory of Hawaii data (1934-1958 in the routed source, per the routing decision's back_cast note) is left with no forward edge into this row, and this row has no backward edge into it — a real gap in the chain, not just a bookkeeping nicety, since it means a reader tracing Hawaii's history from 1934 forward hits a dead end at 1959.

### oq-back-cast-1934-1958

**Pre-statehood data (1934-1958) has nowhere to attach without the HAWI edge**

The routing decision notes the source's Hawaii series runs 1934-2025 and that, absent an existing Territory-of-Hawaii polity among the USA candidates at decision time, years 1934-1958 were to be back_cast onto this 1959-2025 row. But HAWI-1898-1959 does in fact exist in the wiki (see oq-hawi-successor-mismatch above) and was apparently not visible to, or not matched by, the routing step that produced this decision. If HAWI-1898-1959's frontmatter and polygon are accurate for the Territory era, the correct treatment is probably to route 1934-1958 rows to HAWI-1898-1959 directly instead of back-casting them onto this row's 1959 polygon, which would overstate territorial continuity (the Territory's administrative status, and arguably its polygon vintage, differed from the modern state). This needs a human or a re-run of the routing step with HAWI-1898-1959 visible as a candidate; I have not verified whether any 1934-1958 rows were actually assigned to this polity_code or whether that question is moot for this particular page.
