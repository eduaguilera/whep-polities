# Creuse (département)

## Summary

Creuse is a French département in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region of west-central France, historically part of the Marche and northern Limousin provinces, with its prefecture at Guéret. It is one of the original ~83 départements created by the National Constituent Assembly's decree of 4 March 1790, formed principally from the old province of Marche, and its boundaries have not been fundamentally redrawn since — it was not affected by the four events this pipeline treats as departures from the default 1790 start (the 1871-1919 loss/return of Alsace-Lorraine, the 1871 creation of the Territoire de Belfort, the 1975 split of Corsica, or the creation of the overseas départements). `type: subnational` because Creuse never held sovereignty; it has always been an administrative subdivision of the French state, nested since 1800 inside the FRA-* national rows already present in this table. The entry exists to give an as-yet-unrepresented French département its own row so that département-level statistics reported under the name "Creuse" have somewhere specific to route to instead of being absorbed into the national FRA total.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: this entry is created purely from the routing decision for unit_id FRA-FRI22 (admin_name "FRI22", country France), which found zero existing name-candidate polities for Creuse and zero matching GADM features in the locally cached source. No specific dated data rows (source, row count, year range) are cited in the routing decision itself — the task record shows the routing/matching stage identified an administrative unit needing a new home, not a specific data ingest batch, so this page cannot cite a row count the way a data-driven entry could; that gap is itself worth flagging (see open questions).

Previously matched to: nothing — the routing_reasoning states explicitly that 0 name candidates were found, meaning no existing polity (least of all the national FRA-* rows) was silently absorbing Creuse-labelled data before this entry was created. The problem this entry solves is prospective (giving Creuse somewhere to route to) rather than corrective (fixing a bad prior match).

What confirms distinctness: Creuse is one of France's départements, the country's standard first-tier administrative subdivision since 1790, each with its own INSEE code, prefecture, and statistical reporting identity (population censuses, agricultural surveys, etc. are all published at département granularity by INSEE and predecessor bodies). Département-level administrative and statistical distinctness has never been in question for any of the original Revolutionary départements.

Territory description: Creuse occupies roughly the northeastern quarter of the historical province of Marche in west-central France, today part of the Nouvelle-Aquitaine administrative region. It borders the départements of Haute-Vienne (west), Corrèze (south), Puy-de-Dôme (east), Allier (northeast), Cher (north) and Indre (northwest). Its area is approximately 5,565 km² (INSEE's published figure for the modern département), making it one of metropolitan France's larger but most sparsely populated départements; the prefecture is Guéret. This figure comes from INSEE's official département area table, not from any geometry attached to this entry — no polygon is attached yet, so there is no measured-area figure to report here.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-clamped-to-1800

**Start year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, citing Creuse's creation as one of the ~83 Revolutionary départements on 4 March 1790. The France container chain in the polity table, however, has no row earlier than FRA-1800-1871 (1800-1871). Per the container-floor rule this pipeline enforces (see repo commit 'Enforce the container floor, and treat an absent page file as unwritten'), a member cannot be containered before its earliest available container edge. Extending Creuse back to 1790 would leave 1790-1800 uncontained, which the containment gate rejects. start_year was therefore clamped to 1800 to match the container floor, sacrificing ten years of pre-container history rather than leaving a gap. If a pre-1800 France container row is added later, this entry's start_year should be revisited.

### d-code-uses-insee-department-number

**Polity code uses the INSEE département number, not a name abbreviation**

The dominant subnational pattern is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (57 of 66 rows), e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025. French départements have no established alpha-abbreviation precedent in this table (zero existing FRA subnational rows) but do have a stable, universally recognized two-digit INSEE code. Creuse's INSEE code is 23, giving FRA-23-1800-2025 — keeping the ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end shape while using an identifier a reader can actually look up.

### oq-pre-1800-span-orphaned

**1790-1800 is not represented by any polity row**

Creuse existed as a Revolutionary département from March 1790, but the FRA container chain in the polity table starts at 1800 (FRA-1800-1871), so this entry's start_year was clamped to 1800 (see decision d-start-year-clamped-to-1800). Whether a pre-1800 France national row should be added to close this gap is unresolved and affects every French subnational entry that will follow this one, not just Creuse.

### oq-department-boundary-stability-unverified

**Boundary stability since 1790 is asserted, not verified against a source**

The routing decision's span_basis claims Creuse 'has retained its present form since' 1790, which is why a present-day GADM polygon was judged low-vintage-risk once fetched. This entry inherits that claim but has not independently checked it against a French administrative-history source (e.g. INSEE's boundary-change record) — minor canton reassignments between Creuse and its neighbours over two centuries are plausible and would matter once a polygon is attached.
