# Romania (1919-1920)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **2 years**, a transitional border-settling
period [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Greater Romania borders being formalized. Treaty of Saint-Germain
(September 1919, Bukovina from Austria). Treaty of Trianon (June 1920,
Transylvania from Hungary). By end of row, Greater Romania at maximum
extent.

Predecessor: [rou-1918-1919](rou-1918-1919.md) -- Greater Romania
formation.
Successor: [rou-1920-1940](rou-1920-1940.md) -- Greater Romania at full
extent.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=360`, the step **1919-09-10 / 1919-11-26**, measured
  **141,247 km²**.
- **Which step, and why the year could not say.** CShapes cuts gwcode 360's
  1919 into three steps — `1918-11-01/1919-09-09` (128,499 km²),
  `1919-09-10/1919-11-26` (141,247 km²) and `1919-11-27/1920-06-03`
  (148,934 km²) — and this row covers 1919 only, so *every* candidate
  contains 1919 and no `polygon_feature_year` can name one of them. The
  binding was therefore decided by shapefile row order until 2026-08-17
  (issue 100). It is now pinned by `polygon_feature_date: 1919-09-10`,
  which `build_database.find_feature` resolves by day and which falls inside
  exactly one of the three spans. **The geometry did not change**: 1919-09-10
  is the start date of the step row order was already returning, chosen
  because it is this row's own start-of-row event (Treaty of Saint-Germain,
  see Sourced claims).
- **Area.** Approaching ~295,000 km² as treaties formalize the
  territorial gains.
- **COW code 360** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Romania as continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Sourced claims

- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain.** Formalized Austrian
  territorial cessions including Bukovina to Romania. This is the
  start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** Formalized the transfer of
  Transylvania and Banat from Hungary to Romania. This is the
  end-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
