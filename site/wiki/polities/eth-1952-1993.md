# Ethiopia (1952-1993)

## Summary

A **42-year row** covering Ethiopia including Eritrea
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Eritrea was federated
(1952), then annexed (1962), triggering a 30-year independence war. The
Derg military regime (1974-1991) replaced Haile Selassie. Eritrean
independence (1993) is the end-of-row event.

Predecessor: [eth-1907-1952](eth-1907-1952.md). Successor:
[eth-1993-2025](eth-1993-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

## Sourced claims

- **1952: Eritrea federated with Ethiopia.** Start-of-row event. Eritrea
  retained its own parliament and autonomy.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1962: Eritrea annexed.** Haile Selassie dissolved the Eritrean
  parliament and annexed Eritrea as a province. Eritrean armed resistance
  began.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1974-09-12: Derg revolution.** Military junta (the Derg) deposed
  Haile Selassie. Mengistu Haile Mariam emerged as leader by 1977.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1977-1978: Ogaden War.** Somalia invaded the Ogaden; Ethiopia
  repelled the invasion with Soviet and Cuban support.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1983-1985: Ethiopian famine.** Major humanitarian crisis.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1991-05-28: EPRDF captures Addis Ababa.** Mengistu fled; the Derg
  collapsed.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1993-05-24: Eritrean independence.** Following a referendum (99.8%
  for independence). End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
