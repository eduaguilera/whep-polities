# British Military Administration of Tripolitania

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** constructed — a union of GADM 4.1 adm1 *shabiyat*. **9 units, 314,598 km²** against
FAO 1952's **353,000 km²** for this reporting unit (-10.9%).

> **Occupied Libya's three territories are now a partition, asserted rather than asserted-at.**
>
> ```
> TRP-1943-1951   314,598 km²
> CYR-1949-1951   837,876
> FEZ-1943-1951   463,596
> union         1,616,070 km²
> GADM Libya    1,616,070 km²   ratio 1.000000, both differences 0.0000 km²
> pairwise intersections            0.0000 km² (all three)
> ```
>
> That property is the point of building them this way: per-territory data sums to the national
> total, so `TRP` + `CYR` + `FEZ` is usable alongside `LBY`. `audit_family_shadowing`'s
> `PINNED_DISJOINT` re-measures the three pairs on every run.

### No source in this repo draws these territories

Checked directly rather than assumed: **CShapes has only gwcode 620 (Libya entire)** for every
step from 1912 to 2019, and Cliopatria's nearest features are `Ottoman Tripolitania` (1840-1911)
and an **ancient** `Cyrenaica` (−331 to −92). So the three are composed from modern GADM
*shabiyat*, and the assignment of two of those units is a judgement.

### The judgement, and the evidence for it

The historical boundaries do not follow the modern *shabiyat*, and the disagreement sits in
exactly two units: **Surt** (78,659 km², the Sirte basin, historically the
Tripolitania/Cyrenaica frontier) and **Al Jufrah** (111,245 km², the Jufra oases, between
Tripolitania's southern reach and Fezzan's northern one). The other 20 are unambiguous by
geography. All four placements were measured against FAO 1952's own areas — Tripolitania
353,000, Cyrenaica 855,400, Fezzan 551,100, which sum to **exactly** its Libya of 1,759,500:

```
placement                        TRP        CYR        FEZ      worst
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->TRP      -10.9%      -2.0%     -15.9%     -15.9%   <- chosen
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->FEZ      -42.4%      -2.0%      +4.3%     -42.4%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->FEZ      -20.1%     -11.2%      +4.3%     -20.1%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->TRP      +11.4%     -11.2%     -15.9%     -15.9%
```

**The reason is stronger than "smallest worst error".** GADM's Libya measures **1,616,058 km²
geodesically against the accepted 1,759,540 — it is 8.2% short**, and that is real rather than a
projection artefact: checked in three equal-area projections and on the WGS84 ellipsoid, GADM's
bounds match Libya's accepted extent to 0.01°, so nothing is missing geographically, and the same
measurement gives Indonesia 0.992x. **If the whole is 8.2% under FAO, every part should be under
too.** Only the chosen placement achieves that; the others put a part *above* FAO while the whole
is below, which is internally inconsistent whatever their average error.

**What is still a guess:** whether the 1943-1951 administrators drew Sirte into Cyrenaica. The
1934 Italian provinces put Sirte in Misurata, i.e. Tripolitania, which argues for the third or
fourth placement — and both are refused on the consistency test above. So this is an
area-evidence decision over a documentary one, and it is recorded as such rather than presented
as settled.

**`polygon_status: proxy`** for that reason: modern *shabiyat* standing in for historical
territories whose boundaries are known to differ. Check A reports `proxy` rows as documented
rather than failing them, so the -10.9% disagreement with FAO stays visible in every run.

## Summary

This row tracks **British Military Administration (BMA) Tripolitania**
from the 1943 Allied defeat of Axis forces in North Africa — when
British forces took control of the western Libyan province from
Italian rule — through **24 December 1951**, when Tripolitania merged
with Cyrenaica and Fezzan to form the **United Kingdom of Libya**.

The row exists because FAO yearbook data (fao1952) reports 37 rows of
agricultural statistics (livestock counts, carcass weights) separately
for **"Libya Tripolitania"** covering 1948–1951, and an additional 2
rows for **"Tripolitania"** covering 1949–1951. These labels identify
Tripolitania as a distinct enumeration unit, not the whole of Libya.
Routing this data to either LBY-1943-1949 (all-Libya, ~1.6 M km²) or
LBY-1951-2025 (unified Libya, ~1.76 M km²) would overstate the
territorial scope by approximately 4×. The parallel is exact: the
eastern counterpart **CYR-1949-1951** (Emirate of Cyrenaica, ~850–
900 k km²) was created for the same reason.

Tripolitania is the western province of modern Libya, bounded by
Tunisia and Algeria to the west, Niger and Chad to the south, the
Cyrenaica (Syrte basin) to the east, and the Mediterranean coast to
the north. Under British military administration from 1943, it was
one of three Libyan provinces (Tripolitania, Cyrenaica, Fezzan)
administered under the 1949 UN trusteeship framework leading to
independence. At independence on 24 December 1951, Tripolitania
became a federated province of the United Kingdom of Libya under
King Idris I.

Predecessor: [lby-1934-1943](lby-1934-1943.md) (Italian Libya
1934–1943). Successor: [lby-1951-2025](lby-1951-2025.md) (Kingdom/
Republic of Libya).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** No faithful-vintage polygon has been located
  for BMA Tripolitania as a distinct sub-Libyan territory.
  - **CShapes 2.0** (`gwcode=620`, "Libya", 1943 to 1951-12-23,
    area ≈ 1,617,537 km²) covers all three Libyan provinces together
    (Tripolitania, Cyrenaica, Fezzan). The CShapes feature is not a
    faithful proxy: it represents a territory roughly 4–5 times larger
    than Tripolitania alone (~330,000–400,000 km²).
  - **Cliopatria** has no modern (1943–1951) Tripolitania entry.
  - **GADM 4.1 adm1** does not contain Libya administrative units in
    the installed dataset (zero features returned for GID_0='LBY').
  - **Paine 2024** covers water bodies, not polities.
- **Method.** No polygon could be bound. `polygon_status: unassigned`
  is the honest and accepted WHEP outcome for this case, consistent
  with the treatment of the parallel CYR-1949-1951 entry.
- **Area estimate (contextual only, not bound).** Tripolitania's
  historical land area is commonly cited at approximately 330,000–
  400,000 km² (the northwestern quarter of modern Libya, including
  the Tripolitanian coastal lowlands (Jefara plain), Jebel Nafusa
  highlands, and the Fezzan-adjacent desert regions). This is roughly
  one-fifth to one-quarter of the total Libyan area (~1.76 M km²).
  This figure is not used for `polygon_area_km2` because it lacks a
  source-file feature to anchor it.
- **Confidence.** N/A (unassigned).

**Why this entry exists.** FAO 1952 yearbook data (fao1952) includes
37 rows for "Libya Tripolitania" and 2 rows for "Tripolitania" with
years 1948–1951. Both labels identify the western Libyan province as
a distinct agricultural enumeration unit, separate from "Libya
Cyrenaica" (8 rows, 1948–1950) and from any all-Libya aggregate.
No existing WHEP polity matches this sub-territorial scope: the
closest candidates, LBY-1943-1949 (~1.6 M km²) and LBY-1951-2025
(~1.76 M km²), are all-Libya entries.

## Contradictions

- **CShapes covers all-Libya, not Tripolitania.** The CShapes
  1943–1951 Libya polygon (≈1.6 M km²) cannot be used as a proxy
  for BMA Tripolitania (≈330–400 k km²) without overstating the
  territory by approximately 4–5×. This is the primary reason for
  the unassigned polygon status.
- **COW code.** CShapes assigns gwcode=620 to "Libya" throughout the
  1943–2019 window, with no separate COW code for Tripolitania. The
  WHEP polity therefore inherits cow=620 as the closest available
  identifier, with the caveat that 620 represents all of Libya rather
  than Tripolitania specifically.
- **Overlap with LBY-1943-1949.** The span 1943–1949 is also covered
  by LBY-1943-1949 (all-Libya aggregate). These two polities are not
  in conflict: LBY-1943-1949 captures all-Libya data; TRP-1943-1951
  captures data enumerated specifically for the Tripolitania province.
  Data routed to TRP must not be double-counted with LBY aggregates.

## Decisions

- **Period 1943–1951.** BMA Tripolitania began with British military
  occupation of the province in 1943 and ended at Libyan independence
  on 24 December 1951. `start_year=1943`, `end_year=1951` captures
  this window. The FAO data only covers 1948–1951, but the
  administrative period begins in 1943, consistent with the treatment
  of LBY-1943-1949.
- **polity_code TRP-1943-1951.** `TRP` is the natural three-letter
  abbreviation for Tripolitania (no ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code exists
  for the historical province). No collision found in
  `/wiki/polities/` for any TRP-prefixed file.
- **iso3 LBY.** No separate ISO3 code exists for historical
  Tripolitania; LBY is assigned as the modern sovereign successor.
- **cow 620.** Inherited from CShapes Libya coding. Flagged as
  approximate (see Contradictions).
- **polygon_status unassigned.** No faithful polygon found after
  checking all four canonical sources (CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria,
  Paine 2024, GADM 4.1 adm1). This is the honest gap; consistent
  with the parallel CYR-1949-1951 treatment.
- **Predecessor LBY-1934-1943.** The last Italian Libya row directly
  precedes the BMA period. LBY-1943-1949 is kept as a separate
  aggregate polity.

### oq-trp-fezzan — RESOLVED 2026-08-10

FAO 1952 does enumerate Fezzan separately (`Libya Fezzan`, stated area 551,100 km²), so the row
this question asked for now exists as **[fez-1943-1951](fez-1943-1951.md)** — coded `FEZ` rather
than the `FZN` guessed here, for consistency with `TRP` and `CYR`; nothing referenced `FZN`.

The estimates above were close: Tripolitania is **314,598 km²** and Cyrenaica **837,876**, both
just under the ranges given, and Fezzan takes the remaining **463,596**. The three are now an
exact partition of GADM's Libya.
