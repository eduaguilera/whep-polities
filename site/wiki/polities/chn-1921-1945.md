# China (1921-1945)

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as China from **1921** to **1945** --
a **25-year row** covering the Warlord Era, the Nanjing Decade, and
the Second Sino-Japanese War [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
COW code 710 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Key periods within this row:
- **1921-1928**: Warlord Era continues; Northern Expedition (1926-1928)
  under Chiang Kai-shek reunifies much of China nominally.
- **1928-1937**: Nanjing Decade -- the Nationalist government exercises
  varying degrees of control; Chinese Communist Party grows in strength.
- **1937-1945**: Second Sino-Japanese War / WWII in Asia. Japan
  occupied large portions of eastern China (Manchuria from 1931,
  full-scale invasion from 1937). Japanese surrender 15 August 1945.

The end-of-row event is the Japanese surrender (1945), after which
China recovered Taiwan and other occupied territories.

Predecessor per CSV: [chn-1914-1921](chn-1914-1921.md). Successor per
CSV: [chn-1945-1947](chn-1945-1947.md).
