# Greece (to 1913)

## Summary

This row tracks Greece for **84 years**, from independence to the
Balkan Wars [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

CSV notes: "Start date corrected from 1800 to 1830 (Greek
independence). Greece was Ottoman territory before 1830. Polygon
back-projected from CShapes 1886 (63711 km2); Cliopatria has more
accurate 1830-era boundaries (~47000 km2)."

Greece expanded significantly during this period: 1864 Ionian Islands
from Britain, 1881 Thessaly from the Ottoman Empire, 1913
Macedonia/Epirus/Crete from the Balkan Wars. The 1913 end date
aligns with the Second Balkan War (Treaty of Bucharest, 10 August
1913).

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but Greece was Ottoman territory before
1830. The Greek War of Independence (1821-1829) led to independence.
The existing Wikipedia Ottoman source covers this: "Greek War of
Independence begins" at 1821
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821).

Successor: [grc-1913-1919](grc-1913-1919.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1821-1829: Greek War of Independence.** "Greek War of
  Independence begins"
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821). Led to the creation of
  an independent Greek state recognized by the Great Powers.
- **1830: London Protocol.** Greece formally recognized as an
  independent state. This is the start-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)
- **1864: Ionian Islands.** Britain ceded the Ionian Islands
  (Corfu, Zakynthos, etc.) to Greece. A mid-row territorial gain
  with no CSV split.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)
- **1881: Thessaly.** The Ottoman Empire ceded Thessaly and part of
  Epirus to Greece (Convention of Constantinople, 2 July 1881).
  A significant mid-row territorial gain (~13,000 km²) with no CSV
  split.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)
- **1913: Balkan Wars.** The First Balkan War (1912-1913) and Second
  Balkan War (1913) resulted in Greece gaining Macedonia, Epirus,
  and Crete. Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913). This is the
  end-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1821](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1821)

  (63711 km2); Cliopatria has more accurate 1830-era boundaries

## Open questions

### oq-ottoman-predecessor

The CSV has `predecessor = NA` but Greece was clearly Ottoman
territory before 1830. The Greek War of Independence (1821-1829)
is well-documented. This is a coverage gap in the CSV's predecessor
chain -- the Ottoman rows ([ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md) and
predecessors) should be cross-referenced as the territorial source.

### oq-territorial-expansion

Greece nearly doubled in size 1830-1913 with no mid-row splits:
1864 Ionian Islands, 1881 Thessaly (~13,000 km²), 1913 Balkan Wars
gains. Under the WHEP territorial-change rule, 1881 Thessaly is a
candidate mid-row split point (significant area gain from a treaty
with a specific date). The CSV does not split at any of these dates.
