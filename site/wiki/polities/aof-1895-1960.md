# French West Africa (AOF)

## Summary

Afrique Occidentale Française (AOF) was a federation of eight French colonial territories in West Africa, governed as a single administrative and statistical unit from 16 June 1895 until the independence of its constituent territories in 1958–1960. The federation was administered from Dakar (Senegal) under a Governor-General. AOF as a whole was the reporting unit for the fao1952 dataset rows labelled "French West Africa" (150 rows, 1937–1951) and "French W Africa" (12 rows, 1949–1951). This entry is the **federation-level aggregate** and carries only data reported at the AOF level; disaggregated data for individual territories should be routed to the constituent polity entries listed under [Constituent territories](#constituent-territories) below.

The federation was originally constituted in 1895 from Senegal, French Sudan, French Guinea, and Ivory Coast. Dahomey (present-day Benin) was added formally in 1904, Mauritania and Niger followed. Upper Volta was created in 1919, dissolved in 1932 (its territory absorbed by Ivory Coast, French Sudan, and Niger), and reconstituted on 4 September 1947. French Togoland (FTO-1920-1960) was a separate League of Nations mandate and later UN trust territory administered by France adjacent to AOF, not formally part of the federation, though Federico-Tena's series included its trade from 1922 onward.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Constructed polygon; feature `AOF-1895-1960` in `data/geodata/constructed/constructed.geojson`. Built as the geographic union of CShapes 2.0 (GW/COW dependencies layer) constituent territory polygons at vintage year 1938 (gwcodes 432 Mali/French Sudan, 433 Senegal, 434 Benin/Dahomey, 435 Mauritania, 436 Niger, 437 Cote d'Ivoire, 438 Guinea). Combined area approximately 4,774,865 km².

**Why not CShapes 2.0 gwcode 430 directly:** CShapes 2.0 records AOF under gwcode 430 for only four time-steps covering 1895-06-16 to 1904-12-31. The federation continued until 1960 but CShapes drops the federation-level aggregate after 1904, tracking only individual constituent territories. The 1938 vintage was chosen because: (a) the fao1952 data starts at 1937; (b) at 1938 all constituent territories have stable CShapes polygons; (c) Upper Volta had been dissolved in 1932 and its territory was already absorbed into adjacent colonies in the CShapes 1938 representation.

**Vintage drift note:** The polygon vintage is 1938 while the polity spans 1895–1960. Territorial boundaries of constituent units shifted during this period: Upper Volta was dissolved (1932) and reconstituted (1947), and some boundary adjustments occurred in the early colonial period. For data years 1937–1947 the 1938 polygon is a close fit; for 1948–1951 the re-creation of Upper Volta (1947-01-04, gwcode 439) means the 1938 polygon slightly under-represents AOF's spatial footprint by approximately 272,339 km² (Upper Volta's area in CShapes). This is documented but does not affect the aggregate statistics materially — the fao1952 series reports "French West Africa" as a single unit regardless.

**CShapes early-period areas (for cross-reference):**
- 1895–1898: ~5,621,749 km² (gwcode 430; includes pre-AEF Saharan territories)
- 1898–1902: ~5,585,922 km²
- 1902–1904: ~3,799,635 km² (after AEF territories transferred eastward)

## Open questions

### oq-aof-polygon-construction

The constructed polygon has been added to `data/geodata/constructed/constructed.geojson` (gitignored). The provenance note reads: "Union of CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE) gwcodes 432 (Mali/French Sudan), 433 (Senegal), 434 (Benin/Dahomey), 435 (Mauritania), 436 (Niger), 437 (Cote d'Ivoire incl. dissolved Upper Volta), and 438 (Guinea) at 1938, representing AOF's territorial extent at the start of the fao1952 reporting window." The polygon must be incorporated into the polities_polygons.gpkg during the next pipeline run.

### oq-french-sudan-pre1960

No WHEP entry exists for French Sudan (the territory that became Mali) before 1960. CShapes gwcode 432 records it for 1932-09-05 to 1944-07-04 and likely other time-steps. Creating MLI-1890-1960 or similar would allow routing of any fao1952 data specifically attributed to "French Sudan" or "Sudan français" to a distinct polity rather than the AOF aggregate.

### oq-mauritania-pre1960

No WHEP entry exists for Mauritania before 1960. CShapes gwcode 435 records it from 1920-01-12. Creating MRT-1920-1960 would fill this gap.

### oq-fto-inclusion

French Togoland (FTO-1920-1960) was not formally part of AOF (it was a separate League of Nations mandate / UN trust territory) but Federico-Tena explicitly states its trade was included in the AOF series from 1922. fao1952 data labelled "French West Africa" may or may not include French Togoland figures. Disambiguating whether fao1952 "French West Africa" includes or excludes Togoland requires checking the original FAO 1952 yearbook.
