# Democratic Republic of the Congo (1894-1910)

## Summary

The Congo Free State and early Belgian Congo in its expanded configuration from 14 August 1894 to 15 June 1910. "In the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 the Association, which changed its name to Congo Free State, was recognized as a governing power on the Congo River" [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). CShapes records a territorial expansion from 181.23 sq deg (the 1891–1894 configuration) to 194.22 sq deg at 1894-08-14, reflecting the addition of territory along the northeast frontier (the Lado Enclave or similar boundary adjustments). Belgian Congo became independent on 30 June 1960 [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area expands at 1894 and contracts at 1910 (to 189.79 sq deg), both genuine territorial changes.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 194.22 sq deg for the period 1894-08-14 to 1910-06-15, status=colony, owner=Belgium (COW 211). Two CShapes steps have identical area (1894-08-14 to 1908-10-17, and 1908-10-18 to 1910-06-15), indicating the 18 October 1908 event — transfer from the Congo Free State (personal property of King Leopold II) to the Belgian state — was an administrative change without territorial effect. Under WHEP methodology, same-area CShapes steps are collapsed into one row. The territory covers the vast Congo River basin in central Africa. "The boundary between Congo and Zaire runs for 1,498 miles (2,410 km.)" [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire).

## Open questions

### oq-cod-1894-expansion-cause

The 14 August 1894 boundary expansion (from 181.23 to 194.22 sq deg) needs a historical source. This likely corresponds to the Anglo-Congolese Agreement of 12 May 1894 (or its effective date), which transferred the Lado Enclave (Uganda/Nile corridor) and other territory from British to Congo Free State control. A primary or secondary source should confirm this.

### oq-cod-1910-reduction-cause

The 16 June 1910 boundary reduction (from 194.22 to 189.79 sq deg) needs a historical source. This likely corresponds to a post-Kamerun or internal administrative boundary adjustment in French Equatorial Africa. The treaty or decree should be identified.

### oq-cod-cow-code-absent

The CSV has cow=NA for all pre-independence COD rows. CShapes assigns cowcode=490 throughout. Whether WHEP should use 490 as the COW code for the colonial period should be resolved as a policy question.
