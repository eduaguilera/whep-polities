# Greater Berlin (1938-1945)

## Summary

Greater Berlin (1938-1945) covers the city of Berlin as a distinct municipal administrative unit within Nazi Germany from the enactment of the Greater Berlin Act's boundary in force under the Third Reich through Germany's unconditional surrender on 8 May 1945. The boundary remained legally unchanged from 1920 to 1945. This entry exists because the FAO 1952 yearbook reports agricultural statistics for 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct geographic reporting unit for 1938-1939. At ~892 km², Greater Berlin is roughly 700x smaller than DEU-1938-1945 (~600,000 km²); sub-territory rules prohibit folding Berlin's output into the national polity.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Source `gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `DEU.3_1` (Land Berlin), vintage 2022. Measured **891.9 km²** in ESRI:54034 against this page's declared **892 km²** — 0.01%. The Greater Berlin administrative boundary was established by the 1920 Act and remained legally unchanged throughout the Nazi period and the subsequent occupation, and the modern federal state of Berlin is that same perimeter, which is why a modern-vintage feature is not an approximation here.

## Polygon decision

polygon_status=assigned on GADM 4.1 adm1 `DEU.3_1`. The 1920 boundary is the correct spatial unit;
legally fixed and unchanged from 1920 to 1945, and GADM's Land Berlin is that perimeter. Same feature
as [brl-1945-1949](brl-1945-1949.md), deliberately: the two rows are the same city under different
governance, so byte-identical geometry across the 1945 boundary is the correct outcome and not the
duplicated-binding error `validate_polygon_period_fit` signal B looks for — GADM offers no
alternative step, because it is not a temporal source.

## Why this entry was created

FAO 1952 reports 'Germany Berlin' as a distinct unit for 1938-1939. DEU-1938-1945 (~600,000 km²) is ~700x larger. Sub-territory rules require a separate polity. Same city boundary already covered by BRL-1945-1949 for 1945-1949; this entry extends that coverage back to 1938.
