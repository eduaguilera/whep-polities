# Ciudad de Buenos Aires (Capital Federal / autonomous city of Argentina)

## Summary

This entry captures the city of Buenos Aires as a subnational reporting unit of Argentina from its 1880 federalization through the present day. In 1880, Ley 1029 detached the city from Buenos Aires Province and made it the Capital Federal, administered directly by the national government; Buenos Aires Province subsequently built a new provincial capital at La Plata because its old one was no longer part of the province. The city kept this federally administered status for over a century until the 1994 constitutional reform converted it into an autonomous city, Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires, with its own elected government, a change in governance mechanism but not in territorial boundary or in its status as a reporting unit distinct from the surrounding province. Type subnational is used, not national, because sovereignty over this territory has always rested with Argentina; what this row exists to carry is the reporting and administrative territory that statistical sources tabulate separately from both Buenos Aires Province and Argentina as a whole, continuously across the 1880-1994 governance transition and beyond. The 1994 change is treated as internal to this single entry rather than a predecessor or successor split, because the territory and its identity as a distinct reporting unit persisted through it, only the label and the internal governance arrangement changed.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: Federico-Tena and related sources report statistics for the city of Buenos Aires separately from Buenos Aires Province from at least 1900 onward under the label Capital Federal, later Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires, for Argentina, iso3 ARG; the routing decision this page implements identifies this as a distinct reporting unit deserving its own polity row spanning 1880-2025. Previously, absent this entry, data for this reporting unit had nowhere correct to go: it could only be filed either under the whole country ARG-1902-2025 row, which conflates a roughly 203 square kilometer city with the entire national territory, a many thousand fold area mismatch, or dropped as unroutable for the pre-1994 years, since only the post-1994 autonomous city status was documented in the existing country convention block. What confirms this is a distinct reporting entity rather than an artefact of one source's labeling: the territory has had continuous, formally distinct administrative status since 1880, Capital Federal then CABA from 1994, it is a first order administrative division of Argentina in the sense GADM's adm1 layer already recognizes it as its own feature, and Argentine national statistics have long tabulated the Capital Federal or CABA separately from Buenos Aires Province precisely because federalization in 1880 was designed to separate the city's administration and finances from the province's.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon is currently present in the GeoPackage for this unit. The correct registered source is gadm-4.1-adm1, Argentina's admin-1 layer, which includes Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires as its own feature, standard GID_1 code pattern in modern GADM releases, but the on-disk copy of that source is a hand curated country subset that does not include Argentina at all, zero features. This is the registered_source_unfetched case: the source is correctly identified and registered in scripts/sources.yaml, it simply has not been fetched for this country yet. polygon_source is therefore set to the slug gadm-4.1-adm1 itself, not a route name, polygon_feature_id is null, and polygon_status is unassigned until the fetch happens. Territory description: The Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires occupies a fixed area of roughly 203 square kilometers on the western shore of the Rio de la Plata estuary, bounded by the Riachuelo river to the south, the Rio de la Plata to the east, and General Paz Avenue, built along the old city limit, separating it from Greater Buenos Aires, Buenos Aires Province, to the west and north. This is a small single contiguous urban polygon easily located on any modern map of Argentina as the enclave entirely surrounded by but administratively separate from Buenos Aires Province. This 203 square kilometer figure is a well known reference value for the modern city's boundary, cited here only to help a reader locate the territory; it is not measured from any geometry attached to this page, since no polygon is attached yet, see polygon status above. The city's outer boundary has not shifted since the 1880 federalization; unlike many subnational units in this table, there is no boundary change complication to document for the pre-1994 or post-1994 split, since the governance change at 1994 was administrative, not territorial.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: prior to 1880 this territory was part of Buenos Aires Province and had no separate federal or autonomous administrative existence, so nothing precedes this entry. No successor: the city remains an autonomous entity of Argentina to the page's end year 2025 with no absorption, split, or renaming pending. The 1994 constitutional reform, autonomous city status replacing direct federal administration, is treated as an internal governance change within this single entry, not a predecessor or successor boundary, per the routing decision's reasoning that the territory itself did not change at that point.

## Sourced claims

- Ley 1029 (1880) federalized the city of Buenos Aires as the Capital Federal, separating it administratively from Buenos Aires Province, whose own capital subsequently moved to La Plata (founded 1882) for exactly this reason.
- The 1994 Argentine constitutional reform granted the city autonomous city status (Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires), replacing direct national government administration via an appointed Intendente with an elected Jefe de Gobierno and local legislature, while leaving the city's territorial boundary unchanged.

## Decisions

### d-code-choice-argcf

**Chose ARG-CF over the ISO3-SUBUNIT pattern's obvious ARG-CABA because that code is already occupied by an orphaned page**

The dominant pattern for subnational rows is ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The natural subunit abbreviation for this territory is CABA (Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires), giving ARG-CABA-1880-2025, but that exact code is already held by a wiki page in the repository whose row never made it into the built polity table, a stale unbuilt page for a different territory concept under the same string. Rather than overwrite or fight that collision, I picked CF (Capital Federal, the name this unit carried for the first 114 of its 145 years, 1880-1994) as the subunit code, giving ARG-CF-1880-2025. This still follows the dominant pattern; it just uses a different, equally defensible abbreviation for the same subunit so the code is free. Frontmatter years (1880/2025) match the code exactly.

## Open questions

### oq-caba-orphan-collision

**An orphaned page already exists under the code ARG-CABA-1880-2025 for a different territory concept, does it need to be reconciled or deleted?**

wiki/polities/arg-caba-1880-2025.md exists on disk per the git status snapshot but its row is not present in the built polities_database table, meaning either the build pipeline rejected it or it was never run through the build. Its content was not inspected before writing this page because the rejection notice explicitly said that is a different territory from Ciudad de Buenos Aires and instructed choosing a free code rather than touching it. Before this page and the stale page coexist in the wiki permanently, someone should open the old file, determine what territory it actually describes, and either fix, rename, or delete it, otherwise the wiki carries two pages about superficially the same string with no cross-reference.

### oq-1880-span-start-unverified

**The 1880 start year (Ley 1029 federalization) is asserted from general historical knowledge, not from a source file in this repository**

The routing decision itself flags this concern directly: the span start of 1880 is based on general historical knowledge of Argentine federalization, not verified against a source file in this repo. No mitchell or fao1952 or other ingested source row was checked to confirm that 1880 is the correct first year data exists for this reporting unit, nor whether the country convention block, which only documents the 1994 autonomy change, intended pre-1994 years to route here at all versus to a national level container series. This should be checked against whichever source rows actually motivated creating this polity before the span is trusted for anything beyond this page.

### oq-gadm-argentina-missing

**gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered but the on-disk GeoPackage has zero Argentina features, so no polygon_feature_id can be assigned yet**

The local copy of gadm-4.1-adm1 is a hand-curated subset of countries that excludes Argentina entirely. The route is registered_source_unfetched, the source is correctly named and polygon_status is unassigned, but until Argentina's adm1 layer including the feature for Ciudad Autonoma de Buenos Aires is fetched into that GeoPackage, this page has no attached geometry and polygon_area_km2 cannot be populated. The measured area given separately, 203, is the well known modern city limits figure from public reference, not measured from any geometry attached to this page since there is no attached geometry, and must not be written into polygon_area_km2, which requires a self measured figure from the actual feature once fetched.
