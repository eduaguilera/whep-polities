# Cambodia (1904-1907)

## Summary

Cambodia (1904-1907) covers a brief territorial transition within French Indochina. CShapes records a polygon change on 18 February 1904, when Cambodia's area increased from approximately 108,315 km² to approximately 123,927 km² — reflecting the transfer of the Stung Treng region from Laos to Cambodia. A further and larger territorial increase occurred on 23 March 1907, when the Battambang and Siem Reap provinces were returned from Siam (Thailand) to French Cambodia, increasing the area to approximately 151,293 km². The 1904-1907 row is thus a distinct WHEP entry because two real territorial changes bracket it. Cambodia had no COW code during this colonial period; it was administered as part of French Indochina.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records Cambodia as a colony under French rule (owner=220) throughout this period. The area change from the 1893–1904 step to the 1904–1907 step represents the acquisition of Stung Treng from Laos. The subsequent 1907–1953 step reflects the return of Battambang and Siem Reap from Siam under the Franco-Siamese Treaty of 23 March 1907.
