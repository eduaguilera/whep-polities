# Israeli national statistics area (1967-2025)

## Summary

The territory that **Israel's national statistics** cover from 1967: the area within the 1949
armistice lines, together with East Jerusalem and the Golan Heights. It excludes the rest of the
West Bank, the Gaza Strip and (1967-1982) Sinai. This is a **reporting-area row**. It describes
what a statistical series covers and makes **no statement about sovereignty or borders**; the
polity rows `ISR-1967-1979` and `ISR-1979-2025` follow CShapes 2.0's coding, and `PSE-1948-2025`
records the West Bank and Gaza. That polity-level question is recorded in
`scripts/validate_coexisting_overlaps.py` as open (class 1 of issue 143) and is not decided here.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`. FAOSTAT area **105
Israel** 1967-2024 was routed to `ISR-1967-1979` (polygon 88,667 km² in ESRI:54034, including Sinai,
the West Bank and Gaza) and `ISR-1979-2025` (28,118 km², including the West Bank and Gaza).
FAOSTAT's series do not follow that ground (production pin 20260325T111448Z-4fffe):

| harvested area, ha | 1965 | 1966 | 1967 | 1968 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| olives | 11,000 | 11,400 | 11,000 | 10,900 |
| grapes | 11,600 | 11,400 | 11,000 | 10,800 |
| all items below code 1700 | 309,874 | 288,073 | 316,025 | 318,169 |

FAOSTAT carries area **299 Palestine** separately from 1991, and its first olive figure, 81,872 ha
in 1994, is about seven times area 105's; had area 105 absorbed that ground in 1967, its olive area
could not have stayed flat. `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 105 1967-2024 here through
`manual_territory_routes`; 1961-1966 stays on `ISR-1948-1967`, whose ground is the same apart from
East Jerusalem and the Golan Heights.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** proxy, constructed by `build_isr_ra_1967_2025()`: CShapes 666's 1979-2019
step **minus** GADM 4.1's Palestine feature (West Bank and Gaza Strip). See
[CShapes 2.0's border coding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders) and
[GADM 4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md).

```

minus GADM Palestine                      6,220 km²   (42 km² of it lies outside the CShapes step)
= this polygon                           21,898 km²
published in polities_database.gpkg     21,961 km²   after build_database simplification (-0.5% vs declared)
of which outside the 1948-1967 step       1,310 km²   Golan Heights 1,112, and Green-Line slivers
declared                                 22,072 km²   Israel CBS total area, incl. East Jerusalem
                                                      and the Golan Heights (-0.8%)
```

`proxy` because of one known gap: **GADM draws East Jerusalem inside the West Bank**, so the polygon
omits it (about 70 km²) although the statistics include it. Point tests: the Old City and Sheikh
Jarrah fall outside the polygon, West Jerusalem and Katzrin inside. The remaining ~200 km² of small
pieces are where CShapes' and GADM's lines do not coincide, the largest about 40 km² each.

## Predecessors and successors

None declared: this is a reporting area inside `ISR-1967-1979` and then `ISR-1979-2025`, recorded
as two containment edges, not a link in the succession chain.

## Contradictions

It lies inside both containers by construction. Use either the polity row, or this row plus the
separately reported territories; summing both levels double-counts.

## Open questions

- Whether Israeli statistics covered the Golan Heights from 1967 or only from 1981 is not
  established here; the FAOSTAT series shows no step at either date, and the Golan's cropland is
  small against the national total.
- Agriculture of Israeli localities in the West Bank may or may not be inside the national series;
  it is not separable in FAOSTAT and is not modelled by this polygon.
