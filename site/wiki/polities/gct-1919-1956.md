# Gold Coast and British Togoland (1919-1956)

## Summary

> **Polygon ATTACHED 2026-08-06, and the blocker was the `type` field.**
>
> The recipe had been verified and deliberately left unregistered since 2026-07-29 because attaching
> it made `scripts/audit_family_shadowing.py` fail: this row and [btl-1920-1957](btl-1920-1957.md)

> `aggregate` from the start specifically because of this row's history. `aggregate` is the type that
> says "this is a reporting unit over others", which makes the coexistence describable instead of a
> collision.
>
> **The union is corroborated three independent ways**, which is unusually strong for a composed
> polygon:

The combined British administrative unit of the Gold Coast Colony plus British Togoland mandate, administered jointly from 1919 (when the League of Nations mandate was assigned following WWI) until 1956 (when a UN plebiscite on 9 May 1956 resulted in British Togoland voting to merge into the Gold Coast ahead of independence). The FAO used this combined territory as a single reporting unit throughout 1938-1951, publishing 32 rows of livestock and crop data under the label "Gold Coast and Br Togoland". This entry was created to route that data correctly rather than misrepresenting the unit as the Gold Coast Colony alone (~213,314 km²), which would understate territory by ~26,742 km² (~12%) and exclude British Togoland's production.

British Togoland was the western portion of the former German colony of Togoland, partitioned between Britain and France following WWI. Britain administered it as a League of Nations Class B mandate (1920-1946) and then a UN Trust Territory (1946-1956) attached to the Gold Coast administration.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Composed union of two CShapes 2.0 features:
- Gold Coast Colony: CShapes cowcode=452, row start=1898-06-14 / end=1956-03-05, area ~213,314 km²
- British Togoland mandate: CShapes cowcode=462, row start=1922-07-20 / end=1955-12-31, area ~26,742 km²
- **Union area: ~240,056 km²**

This composed-union polygon is validated: CShapes encodes the post-plebiscite Gold Coast (row start=1956-03-06 / end=1957-03-05, cowcode=452) at exactly 240,056 km², which is the same geometry, confirming that the union of the two component features produces the correct combined territory.

**Period note:** The FAO data runs 1938-1951. The Gold Coast polygon is stable from 1898 to 1956; British Togoland's polygon is stable from 1922 to 1955. The composed union is valid for the full FAO reporting window.

**Why this entry exists:** Routing "Gold Coast and Br Togoland" to GHA-1898-1956 (Gold Coast Colony only, ~213,314 km²) would understate the reporting unit by ~26,742 km² (~12%) and exclude British Togoland's agricultural production. The FAO explicitly treated this as a joint reporting unit, requiring a matching polity.

## Decisions

- **Polity scope:** FAO data measured the combined British administrative unit (Gold Coast + British Togoland), not Gold Coast alone. Routing to GHA-1898-1956 would misrepresent the reporting unit.
- **Polygon method:** Composed union of two CShapes features; validated against CShapes 1956 Ghana polygon.
- **Date span 1919-1956:** Start 1919 (mandate assignment post-Versailles); end 1956 (plebiscite integration). FAO data 1938-1951 falls within this window.

## Open questions

### oq-gct-pre-1922-polygon

British Togoland's CShapes polygon is only coded from 1922-07-20 (formal partition date). For data years 1919-1921 the British Togoland area was still part of the jointly occupied/administered German Togoland territory. If data exists for 1919-1921 a separate assessment is needed; for the FAO 1938-1951 window this is not relevant.
