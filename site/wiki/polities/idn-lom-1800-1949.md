# Lombok (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Lombok, "an island in West Nusa Tenggara province", "with the Lombok Strait separating it from Bali to the west and the Alas Strait between it and Sumbawa to the east", about 4,567 km2. One of the Outer Provinces ([idn-out-1800-1949](idn-out-1800-1949.md)). [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §lombok](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#lombok)
