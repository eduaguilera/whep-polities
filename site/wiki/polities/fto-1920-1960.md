French Togoland was a League of Nations mandate (1920-1946) and then a United Nations trust territory (1946-1960) administered by France. It comprised the eastern portion of the former German colony of Togoland, partitioned after WWI. Independent as the Republic of Togo on 27 April 1960.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. French Togoland covered the eastern portion of former German Togoland (approximately modern Togo). CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE) may have this as a French mandate territory.

## Key dates

All dates below are from
[wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §french-togoland](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#french-togoland)
unless marked otherwise.

- 1914: Anglo-French forces occupy German Togoland (French landing at Little
  Popo 6 August; invasion 26 August; surrender at Kamina 27 August)
- 1916: Togoland divided into French and British administrative zones
- 1922: League of Nations mandate to France over the eastern portion — the
  source dates the mandate "from 1922 to 1946"; this row starts at 1920 and
  its first two years are the joint-occupation period, as the
  `polygon_vintage_note` records
- 1946: Becomes UN trust territory, still administered by French
  commissioners
- 1955: Autonomous republic within the French Union "by statute", with the
  constitution approved in a 1956 referendum (this line previously read
  "1956")
- 1960-04-27: Full independence as Republic of Togo
