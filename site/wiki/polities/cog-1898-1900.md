## Summary

French Congo (later Moyen-Congo) during a brief 1898–1900 CShapes window. The period falls between the late-1890s concessionary-company reorganisations and the 1903 administrative creation of Moyen-Congo as a distinct colony. No single named political event on the Wikipedia French Equatorial Africa article anchors the 1898 start or the 1900 end.
