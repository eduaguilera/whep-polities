# Igala Kingdom

## Summary

The Igala Kingdom from the 1800 database floor to its nominal incorporation into British colonial Nigeria c.1901 per the CSV. The kingdom occupies a roughly triangular ~14,000 km² territory at the confluence of the Niger and Benue rivers in modern Kogi State, Nigeria, with the Attah as paramount ruler.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The Wikipedia article describes the territory as "a triangular area of about 14,000 km² in the angle formed by the Benue and Niger rivers."

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. The 1800 start is a database floor; the kingdom dates to c.16th century per CSV notes.
- **Successor:** [nni-1899-1904](nni-1899-1904.md) — Northern Nigeria Protectorate (1899–1904). IGL is one of four predecessors of NNI-1899-1904.

## Sourced claims

- The Igala Kingdom occupies "a triangular area of about 14,000 km² (5,400 sq mi) in the angle formed by the Benue and Niger rivers", now within Kogi State in central Nigeria. [wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15 §igala](../sources/wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- The Wikipedia Igala-people article mentions British colonial interference (e.g., later deposition of Attah Ameh Oboni) but does not give a specific 1901 incorporation date; CSV assertion of 1901 incorporation by the Royal Niger Company is from CSV notes (`research`). [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Contradictions

The 1901 end year is asserted by CSV notes ("Came under British Royal Niger Company 1901") without a directly sourced Wikipedia date. The Royal Niger Company lost its charter on 31 December 1899, so the 1901 incorporation would have been under the new Northern Nigeria Protectorate administration, not the RNC itself. Flagged as open question.
