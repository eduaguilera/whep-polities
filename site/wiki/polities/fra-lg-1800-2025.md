# Lot-et-Garonne (département of France)

## Summary

Lot-et-Garonne is a French département in the southwest interior of the country, in the modern Nouvelle-Aquitaine region, with its prefecture at Agen. It was one of the original 83 départements created by the Revolutionary administrative reform of 4 March 1790, assembled principally from the Ancien Régime Agenais together with fragments of the neighbouring Condomois, Bazadais and Bruilhois. This entry is type: subnational, not national, because France held sovereignty over this territory throughout its existence as a département -- the row exists purely to carry a statistical/administrative reporting geography smaller than, and nested inside, the national FRA chain, exactly as the existing ESP province rows (ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CA-1833-2025) carry Spanish provinces beneath the national ESP row. No FRA-coded polity previously represented this specific département: the three existing FRA-* rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) are national totals for successive eras defined by the Alsace-Lorraine boundary changes, and while they contain Lot-et-Garonne they are roughly 100x its area and cannot substitute for a department-level series. Although the department's own founding predates 1800, this entry's start_year is set to 1800 rather than the routing decision's proposed 1790, because no container row of any kind exists in the database for 1790-1800 (see decisions); the true 1790 founding date is preserved here as historical fact even though the row itself cannot represent it.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision for unit_id FRA-FRI14 (admin_name "FRI14", country France) captures valued statistical rows for Lot-et-Garonne; the decision's own span_basis fixes the era as 1790 (department creation) to an open 2025 end, under the FRA country convention's default-start rule (departments not among the four listed departure cases -- Belfort 1922, Alsace-Lorraine 1919, Corsica split 1975, overseas 1946 -- take the Revolutionary 1790 default rather than the extract's own 1862 data start, which the routing_reasoning explicitly flags must not be used as the span). Previously matched to: nothing dedicated -- the routing_reasoning states plainly that "no existing FRA polity IS this département," identifying the three FRA-* candidates (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) as national totals across different Alsace-Lorraine eras that merely contain, rather than are, this territory; routing this unit's data to any of them would collapse a department-level series into a country-level one. What confirms distinctness: Lot-et-Garonne is one of metropolitan France's 96 départements, each a first-order INSEE and Eurostat NUTS3 statistical division (code FR612) with continuous department-level data collection since the 1790 creation, the same class of evidence (a persistent, separately-tabulated sub-national statistical unit) already accepted for the ESP province rows in this database.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Start year set to 1800, not the routing decision's proposed 1790, because no FRA container row exists before 1800**

The routing decision (unit_id FRA-FRI14) proposes start_year 1790, reasoning that Lot-et-Garonne is an original Revolutionary département created 4 March 1790 and is not one of the four listed departure cases (Belfort 1922, Alsace-Lorraine 1919, Corsica split 1975, overseas 1946) that would justify a later start. That reasoning about the department's own founding date is accepted. But the containment gate requires every year of a polity's span to fall inside at least one container edge, and the container edge in turn must fall inside the container polity's own span (an edge outside either party's span is rejected). The earliest FRA row in the database, FRA-1800-1871, itself starts in 1800 -- there is no FRA-* row of any kind covering 1790-1800, national or otherwise (checked against data/final/polities_database.csv, whose earliest FRA start year across all four rows, including the superseded FRA-1800-1919, is 1800). Setting start_year to the proposed 1790 would therefore leave a 10-year gap this entry cannot container, which the gate would reject. Rather than invent an ungrounded container edge for 1790-1800, this page uses 1800 as start_year, matching the earliest year the national chain actually covers, and records the true founding date (1790) in the summary and sourced claims as historical fact even though it precedes what the row's own start_year and container can represent. This is the same kind of floor effect noted generically in the routing_concerns field ("downstream logic should resolve containment per-year, not statically") -- here it bites concretely at the very first year.

### d-code-subunit-letters-lg

**Used a two-letter mnemonic (LG) for the subunit code rather than the INSEE department number (47)**

The dominant subnational code pattern (57 of 66 rows) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and the existing ESP sibling rows (ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CA-1833-2025, etc.) establish the house convention of a short letter abbreviation rather than a numeric administrative code, explicitly choosing readability over the official numbering scheme (ESP-AS-1833-2025's own decisions section rejected a Spanish province-number code for the same reason). France has zero existing subnational rows, so this entry sets the FRA precedent rather than following one. Lot-et-Garonne's INSEE department number is 47, which could have served as the subunit token (FRA-47-1800-2025), but following the ESP precedent instead mints FRA-LG-1800-2025 using the everyday abbreviation, so that any future French département entry (Gironde, Dordogne, etc.) can be predicted from the same letter-based rule rather than requiring a lookup between two competing numbering conventions for this country.

### oq-pre-1790-territory-unmodeled

**The pre-1790 territory (parts of the Agenais, Condomois and other Ancien Régime provinces) has no predecessor row, and the 1790 founding date itself falls outside this entry's own start_year**

Lot-et-Garonne was assembled in 1790 largely from the Agenais and parts of the Condomois, Bazadais and Bruilhois -- pre-Revolutionary provincial fragments that this database does not model as distinct polities, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row. This is sharper than the analogous case for ESP-AS-1833-2025 (which at least can cite a documented Principado de Asturias) because here the gap is compounded by d-start-year-1800-not-1790: the entry's own start_year (1800) already postdates the department's actual creation (1790) by a decade for containment reasons, so even the 1790-1800 window of the department's own existence is currently unrepresented by any row, predecessor or otherwise. If a future FRA country-convention decision extends the national chain earlier than 1800, this entry's start_year should be revisited down to 1790 to close that gap rather than leaving it as a permanent floor effect.

### oq-boundary-stability-not-verified

**Minor 19th-century boundary adjustments to Lot-et-Garonne's arrondissements are not checked against the modern outline**

French département boundaries have been largely stable since 1790, but Lot-et-Garonne specifically underwent minor arrondissement-level adjustments in the 19th century (e.g., changes affecting the Nerac and Villeneuve-sur-Lot arrondissements) that have not been checked against whether they moved the department's own outer boundary versus purely internal subdivisions. Until the gadm-4.1-adm1 France features are fetched (see oq-polygon-source-fra-missing-from-local-gadm) and a specific feature is examined, vintage risk across the full 1800-2025 span is asserted by analogy to the general stability of French départements rather than confirmed for this specific unit.
