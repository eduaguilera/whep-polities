# Saint Lucia (to 1838)

## Summary

Saint Lucia from the 1800 database floor to the 1 August 1838 end of the post-abolition apprenticeship period. Over the 1800–1814 window Saint Lucia was contested between French and British forces during the Napoleonic Wars; Britain secured the island definitively by the Treaty of Paris (1814). Slavery was abolished in British colonies in 1834, followed by a four-year "apprenticeship" that ended on 1 August 1838.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from GADM 4.1 or Natural Earth per CSV `polygon_source`. The island's physical boundary has not changed over the period. No CShapes coverage — CShapes' temporal coverage starts in 1886.
