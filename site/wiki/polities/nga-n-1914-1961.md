# Northern Nigeria (Northern Provinces / Northern Region)

## Summary

The Northern Provinces of Nigeria after the 1 January 1914 amalgamation (under a Lieutenant Governor, later Chief Commissioner), from 1951 the Northern Region, until the Northern Cameroons joined the region on 1 June 1961. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §northern-nigeria](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#northern-nigeria)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA and FAO yearbooks report the Northern Provinces/Region (`british nigeria: north`, 1914-1960, 56 pairs in the label list); NNI-1904-1913 stops at amalgamation and no row was the northern half after it. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
