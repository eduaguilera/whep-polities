# Pakistan (territory within British India, 1937–1947)

## Summary

This entry covers the territory that became West Pakistan as reported separately from "India" in the pre-1961 FAO dataset for 1937–1946. The data compiler extracted Pakistan-territory agricultural statistics from British India records, labelled "Pakistan", before formal independence (15 August 1947). The polity uses the 1947 CShapes polygon for West Pakistan as a proxy (Punjab west, Sindh, NWFP, Baluchistan — roughly modern Pakistan). Data: ~59 rows, years 1937–1946.

The prior polity code PAK-1940-1947 (start_year=1940) was too late: 19 fao1952 rows labelled "Pakistan" for 1937–1939 were left unmatched. These are FAO retroactive territorial attributions for the future-Pakistan sub-territory; routing them to IND would misstate scope ~5x (~881625 km² vs ~4.2M km²) in violation of WHEP sub-territory rules. The corrected code is PAK-1937-1947.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status.** Assigned (CShapes 2.0, feature 770, vintage 1947). The polygon represents the full West Pakistan territory at partition: Punjab (west), Sindh, NWFP, and Baluchistan — approximately 881,625 km² (excludes East Pakistan / East Bengal, which is tracked under BGD entries).
- **Polygon approximation.** The 1947 CShapes polygon is the correct proxy for 1937–1946 data because this territory had been under full British Indian administration since ~1893 (the Durand Line treaty establishing the western boundary). The constituent provinces were annexed at different dates — Sindh in 1843, Punjab in 1849, Baluchistan piecemeal through the 1870s–1890s — but by 1893 the full eventual Pakistan territory was under British India control. Because this polity's data covers 1937–1946, the 1947 polygon represents the territory without meaningful drift; the polygon vintage (1947) is at most 10 years after the earliest data year (1937). **Polygon_vintage_drift: max 10 years** (1937→1947).
- **No pre-1937 data.** The before_1961 dataset contains no "Pakistan"-labelled rows before 1937. If pre-1843 or pre-1849 data for these territories were to be added in future, they would require separate polity entries (e.g., Sindh-pre-1843, Sikh Empire 1799–1849) because the 1947 polygon would overstate territory by ~340,000 km² before 1850.
- **East Pakistan exclusion.** This polity does NOT cover East Pakistan (East Bengal). The data label "Pakistan" in 1937–1946 refers to the western provinces only. East Bengal data, if present, routes to BGD-1947-1971 (after 1947) or to the British India entry.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None assigned. The territory was part of British India (no separate WHEP British India polity currently exists for this exact province set). Data labelled "Pakistan" before independence is attributed here rather than to IND.
- **Successor:** [PAK-1947-1949](pak-1947-1949.md) — Pakistani independence 15 August 1947.

## Sourced claims

- The Durand Line, Pakistan's western boundary with Afghanistan, was established by treaty 12 November 1893, completing British India's control of the territory that became Pakistan. [biger-1995 §afghanistan]
- Sindh was annexed by the British East India Company in 1843 following the Battle of Hyderabad. [wikipedia-sindh]
- Punjab was annexed by the British East India Company in 1849 following the Second Anglo-Sikh War. [wikipedia-punjab]
- Baluchistan was brought under British suzerainty piecemeal through the 1870s–1890s (Quetta occupied 1876; British Baluchistan formally constituted 1887; treaties with Kalat throughout this period). [wikipedia-baluchistan]
- CShapes 2.0 records Pakistan (COW code 770) as independent from 1947-08-15 with an area of approximately 861,625 km² (first CShapes step). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes]
- The before_1961 FAO dataset contains 282 rows labelled "Pakistan" spanning 1937–1960; rows for 1937–1946 (pre-independence) are attributed to this polity. [data/external/before_1961.csv]

## Decisions

### dec-start-year-correction-2026-06-30

The polity code was corrected from PAK-1800-1947 (start_year=1800) to PAK-1940-1947 (start_year=1940). The 1800 start was an artifact of the polity creation pipeline; the FAO before_1961 dataset contains no "Pakistan"-labelled data before 1940. No split at 1843/1849/1893 is warranted because no data exists for those sub-periods.

### dec-start-year-extension-1937-2026-07-01

The polity code was further corrected from PAK-1940-1947 (start_year=1940) to PAK-1937-1947 (start_year=1937). The 1940 start (Lahore Declaration year) left 19 fao1952 rows labelled "Pakistan" for 1937–1939 unmatched. These are FAO retroactive territorial attributions for the future-Pakistan sub-territory (~881625 km²). Routing them to IND-1937-1947 (~4.2M km²) would misstate scope ~5x in violation of WHEP sub-territory rules. The 1947 CShapes polygon remains the proxy; polygon_vintage_drift is now max 10 years (1937→1947), which is acceptable given stable British India administration of this territory since 1893.

## Open questions

### oq-east-pakistan-1937-1946

The before_1961 data label "Pakistan" for 1937–1946 may or may not include East Bengal / East Pakistan territory in the production figures. If the source records (FAO working papers for South Asia pre-1947) show that the Pakistan-territory extractions included East Bengal, then the polygon proxy should be the full 1947 Pakistan polygon (~942,826 km² post-1949 Kashmir allocation, or a composed union of West + East Pakistan). This should be verified against the original FAO source methodology before ingesting population data.
