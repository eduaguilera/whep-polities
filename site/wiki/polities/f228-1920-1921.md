# RSFSR (1920-1921)

## Summary

This row covers **2 years** at the end of the Russian Civil War
under the RSFSR. The Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921) settled the
border with Poland. The Treaty of Tartu with Finland (1920) and
recognition of the Baltic states formalized the post-WWI
territorial settlement.

Predecessor: [f228-1918-1920](f228-1918-1920.md).
Successor: [f228-1921-1940](f228-1921-1940.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Treaty of Riga (18 March 1921).** Settled the
  Polish-Soviet border. Poland gained western Belarus and
  western Ukraine (the "Kresy" territories). This formalized
  Russia's loss of territory east of the Curzon Line.
- **Treaty of Tartu with Finland (14 October 1920).** Confirmed
  Finnish independence and settled the Russo-Finnish border.
  Finland gained Petsamo (access to the Arctic Ocean).
- **Baltic states recognized.** Estonia (Treaty of Tartu,
  2 February 1920), Latvia (Latvian-Soviet Peace Treaty,
  11 August 1920), Lithuania (Soviet-Lithuanian Peace Treaty,
  12 July 1920) all had their independence recognized by
  Soviet Russia during this period.
