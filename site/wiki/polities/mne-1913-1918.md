# Montenegro (1913-1918)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Montenegro at its post-Balkan-Wars maximum extent, from the 30 May 1913 Sandžak partition with Serbia to the 26 November 1918 Podgorica Assembly's deposition of King Nikola I and unification with Serbia. Austria-Hungary occupied Montenegro from 15 January 1916 to October 1918; the Podgorica Assembly's deposition came in the immediate post-war reorganisation that produced the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes.
