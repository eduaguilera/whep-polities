# Costa Rica

## Summary

Costa Rica is a Central American country situated between the Caribbean Sea and the Pacific Ocean, bounded by Nicaragua to the north and Panama to the south. Biger 1995 records its area as 19,730 sq. miles (51,100 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 3,500,000 in 1994 [biger-1995 &sect;costa-rica](../sources/biger-1995.md#costa-rica). Costa Rica became independent from Spain on 15 September 1821. WHEP tracks it as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory remained essentially constant across the transition from Spanish colony to independent state.

## Territorial extent

Costa Rica is bounded by Nicaragua (192 miles; 309 km.) to the north and Panama (205 miles; 330 km.) to the south [biger-1995 &sect;costa-rica](../sources/biger-1995.md#costa-rica). The Costa Rica-Nicaragua boundary was confirmed by the Canas-Jerez Treaty of 15 April 1858 (p.177). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
