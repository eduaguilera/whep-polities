# Eastern Germany / Soviet Occupation Zone (1938–1949)

## Summary

This polity captures the eastern sub-territory of Germany as recorded
by the FAO 1952 production yearbook under the label **"Germany Eastern"**
for the years 1938–1948. The span straddles two historical phases:

- **1938–1944** — the eastern regions of the Third Reich
  (Gaue east of the Elbe: East Prussia, Pomerania, Brandenburg-East,
  Silesia, Saxony, and related territories). FAO applied the later
  Soviet-zone frame retrospectively to pre-war data, as it did
  symmetrically for "Germany Western" (see [f78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md)).
- **1945–1949** — the **Soviet Occupation Zone** (Sowjetische
  Besatzungszone, SBZ), the portion of defeated Germany administered
  by the USSR from unconditional surrender (8 May 1945) until the
  proclamation of the German Democratic Republic (7 October 1949).

The SBZ was the direct territorial predecessor of East Germany
([F77-1949-1990](f77-1949-1990.md)). It covered approximately
**109,272 km²**, roughly 30 % of Allied-occupied Germany
(≈357,000 km²). Routing "Germany Eastern" data to the aggregate
polities DEU-1938-1945 (~580,000 km²) or DEU-1945-1949 (~357,000 km²)
would overstate territorial scope by a factor of 5× and 3×
respectively, violating the sub-territory rule.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `CShapes 2.0` gwcode = **265** (GME), polygon vintage
**1949**, area **≈ 109,272 km²**. CShapes records the Soviet
occupation zone boundary at the 1949 time-step (the same polygon
it assigns to F77-1949-1990 East Germany). This is the most
authoritative available vector for the SBZ boundary.

**Period proxy for 1938–1944 (approximation flag):** The 1949
CShapes polygon is used as a period proxy for the 1938–1944 phase.
This introduces a documented mismatch:

- The pre-war eastern Gaue did *not* coincide with the post-war SBZ
  boundary. The Oder-Neisse territories (East Prussia, most of
  Silesia, eastern Pomerania) were part of the Third Reich's eastern
  extent but were ceded to Poland and the USSR in 1945 and are
  **outside** the SBZ polygon.
- The SBZ polygon therefore *understates* the actual eastern-German
  territory for 1938–1944. Pre-war eastern Germany east of the Elbe
  was roughly 180,000–200,000 km² (including the territories later
  ceded), compared to the SBZ's ~109,272 km².
- Accordingly, production values attributed to "Germany Eastern"
  for 1938–1944 using this polygon carry an implicit territorial
  approximation of approximately −40 % relative to actual pre-war
  eastern-German extent. Area-normalised indicators for those years
  should be treated as approximations.
- No finer-grained historical polygon covering pre-war eastern
  Germany (excluding the ceded territories) at the SBZ boundary is
  available in CShapes, Cliopatria, CHGIS, or GHGIS for this
  sub-territory. A split at 1944/1945 (with a separate ~180,000 km²
  pre-war eastern-Germany polygon) is theoretically warranted but
  no source polygon is available; this single polity with the
  approximation documented here is the best achievable resolution.

**Polygon status:** `assigned` (CShapes 2.0 gwcode 265, vintage 1949;
period proxy for 1938–1944 with ~−40 % area approximation documented above).

## Why this entry was created

The FAO 1952 agricultural yearbook distinguishes **"Germany Eastern"**
and **"Germany Western"** for the period 1938–1951, applying the
post-war occupation-zone frame retrospectively to wartime and pre-war
data. The "Germany Western" label was already routed to F78-1949-1990
(see [f78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md)). No matching polity existed
for the eastern counterpart. The existing aggregate polities:

| Polity | Area | Overstatement vs SBZ |
|---|---|---|
| DEU-1938-1945 (Nazi Germany incl. Austria) | ~580,000 km² | ~5× |
| DEU-1945-1949 (all Allied-occupied Germany) | ~357,000 km² | ~3× |
| F77-1949-1990 (East Germany/GDR) | ~108,000 km² | ~1× (correct from 1949) |

The existing "germany eastern" alias already routed to F77-1949-1990
for 1949–1990 (correct). This polity fills the 1938–1948 gap: the FAO
data for those years must route to a granular eastern-zone entity, not
to any full-Germany aggregate.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [DEU-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) — Nazi Germany
  (the Third Reich). The eastern Gaue that FAO labels "Germany Eastern"
  were sovereign German territory under this polity until 8 May 1945.
- **Successor:** [F77-1949-1990](f77-1949-1990.md) — East Germany
  (GDR). The SBZ became the GDR on 7 October 1949. The boundary is
  identical; F77 uses the same CShapes gwcode 265 polygon.

## Key dates

- 1938 — FAO retrospective series begins for "Germany Eastern"
- 1945-05-08 — Unconditional surrender; SBZ formally established under
  Soviet Military Administration (SMAD)
- 1945-07-17–08-02 — Potsdam Conference; Oder-Neisse line confirmed,
  ceded territories transferred to Poland/USSR
- 1949-10-07 — GDR proclaimed; SBZ becomes F77-1949-1990

## Decisions

- ★ **Sub-territory rule** — "Germany Eastern" measures ~109,000 km²
  of eastern Germany, not the full Reich (~580,000 km²) or all
  occupied Germany (~357,000 km²). Folding into any aggregate DEU
  polity overstates territorial scope 3–5×.
- ★ **Period proxy** — The SBZ polygon (1949 vintage) is used for
  1938–1944 because no sub-national eastern-Germany polygon exists
  for the pre-war period at this boundary. The approximation (~−40 %
  area understatement vs. actual 1938–1944 eastern extent) is
  documented here and accepted as the best available resolution.
- ★ **Single polity, not a split** — A formal split at 1944/1945
  (pre-war eastern Gaue vs. SBZ) would require a ~180,000 km²
  pre-war polygon that does not exist in any accessible GIS source.
  The single polity with the approximation note is preferred over
  an unsourced constructed polygon.
