# German Togoland (1884-1920)

## Summary

Togoland (Deutsch-Togo, the German Togoland protectorate) as a single undivided territory
on the Gulf of Guinea, from the German colonial acquisitions of 1884-1885 until the
British/French partition. Germany acquired Togoland in the period of the Berlin Conference,
15 November 1884 - 26 February 1885
[wikipedia-german-empire-2026-04-11 §1884-1885](../sources/wikipedia-german-empire-2026-04-11.md#1884-1885);
"the German protectorate of Togoland was invaded by French and British forces" on 26 August
1914 and fell after five days
[wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §french-togoland](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#french-togoland);
the territory was then "divided into French and British administrative zones in 1916" and
"following the war, Togoland formally became a League of Nations mandate divided for
administrative purposes between France and the United Kingdom"
[wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §french-togoland](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#french-togoland).

**Why this entry exists.** [btl-1920-1957](btl-1920-1957.md) and
[fto-1920-1960](fto-1920-1960.md) — the two halves of the partition — both declared
`predecessor: GTO-1919-1922`, **a code that named no row in the database**. It was one of the
dead chain targets baselined in `validate_chain_integrity` (issue 135) and the reason the TGO
family had no answer for anything before 1920 (issue 171). This row is the entity those two
pages meant. It is **not** spanned 1919-1922: a predecessor in this database ends exactly
where its successors begin, and both mandate rows begin in 1920, so this row runs to 1920 and
starts at the German acquisition rather than at the transitional years alone. Both mandate
pages now point here and this row names both of them, so the relation is symmetric.

The row receives **no data**. The pre-1961 input reports "Togo" from 1933, inside
[fto-1920-1960](fto-1920-1960.md)'s span; nothing in the current inputs is labelled for the
German colony. It exists to make the chain traversable, which is what the ISO3 successor map
and every consumer walking `predecessor` need.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: `proxy`.** `constructed/GTO-1884-1920`, built by
  `build_gto_1884_1920()` in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`.
- **`polygon_area_km2` is deliberately null.** The only area figure this row could state is the
  one measured off its own geometry, and `validate_polygons`' check A2 counts exactly that as a
  self-referential declaration -- a comparison that cannot fail. The measured value is recorded
  in `polygon_method` instead, where it reads as a measurement rather than as evidence about the
  territory. The ~87,200 km2 external figure is not declared either, because it is uncited here;
  see [oq-german-togoland-area-figure](#oq-german-togoland-area-figure).
- **The composition.** French Togoland (CShapes 2.0 gwcode 461, the 1922-1960 mandate step,
  **57,094 km²**) **∪** British Togoland (the `BTL-1920-1957` constructed difference —
  CShapes gwcode 452 at 1960 minus 452 at 1950 — **26,630 km²**). The two mandates are
  disjoint and their union measures **83,724.5 km²**, exactly their sum.
- **Why a union of the successors rather than a source feature.** No source in the priority
  stack carries Deutsch-Togoland as a feature of its own: CShapes 2.0 begins its Togo
  coverage with the 1922 mandate step (gwcodes 461 and 462) and Cliopatria has no Togoland
  polity. The two mandates ARE the partition of this territory — "larger eastern part to
  France, western part to Britain"
  [biger-1995 §togo](../sources/biger-1995.md#togo) — so re-joining them is the only
  composition the repository's own sources support.
- **Km² reasoning for `proxy` rather than `assigned`.** 83,724 km² sits **4% below** the
  ~87,200 km² figure [fto-1920-1960](fto-1920-1960.md) records for the German colony. The
  gap is not a missing piece: both mandates are present in the union. It is that each
  CShapes mandate outline is individually a little tight — British Togoland alone measures
  21% under the ~33,771 km² historical figure, which
  [btl-1920-1957](btl-1920-1957.md) documents and accepts. The outline is therefore the
  **1922 mandate boundary pair**, not the 1884-1914 colonial boundary (the Milner-Simon
  line was drawn after the war), and `proxy` is the honest label for a geometry taken from
  a later period. Lomé, the German colonial capital, is inside it — it is inside gwcode 461,
  as fto-1920-1960.md's point-in-polygon check records.
- **What the polygon does not represent.** The German colony's boundaries with the Gold
  Coast, Dahomey and the Volta interior moved during German rule (the northern hinterland
  was delimited by Anglo-German and Franco-German agreements in the 1890s). This single
  outline is used for the whole 1884-1920 span; a row this long with one outline is a
  vintage compromise, recorded as [oq-german-era-boundary-steps](#oq-german-era-boundary-steps).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none. This is a genuine **chain start**: WHEP's coverage of this territory
  begins with the German protectorate, and the precolonial polities of the Togo interior are
  not modelled here (the nearest precolonial rows, `ASH-1800-1896` and `DGM-1800-1899`, are
  Ashanti and Dahomey — neighbours, not predecessors).
- **Successors:** [btl-1920-1957](btl-1920-1957.md) — British Togoland, the western portion,
  which "joined Ghana at independence in 1957"
  [biger-1995 §togo](../sources/biger-1995.md#togo) — and
  [fto-1920-1960](fto-1920-1960.md) — French Togoland, the larger eastern portion, which
  became the independent Togolese Republic in April 1960
  [biger-1995 §togo](../sources/biger-1995.md#togo).

## Contradictions

**The span this row carries is not the span the two mandate pages named.** Both asked for
`GTO-1919-1922`, i.e. only the transitional years between the Milner-Simon boundary
declaration (10 July 1919) and the League mandates (20 July 1922). Three things argue for
1884-1920 instead:

1. A predecessor here **ends exactly where its successor begins** — the convention
   `validate_chain_integrity` documents and the one used to repair nine typo'd edges in issue
   135. `BTL-1920-1957` and `FTO-1920-1960` both begin in **1920**, not 1922, because both
   pages date the start of the separate British and French administrations to 1920 and treat
   1920-1921 as the pre-partition joint-occupation years.
2. A 1919-1922 row would **overlap both of its own successors** by two years, which
   `validate_period_overlaps` reports and which asserts that the undivided colony and its two
   partition halves existed simultaneously.
3. The years 1884-1918 would still have no row, so the TGO chain would still start in 1919
   and the gap issue 171 is about would remain.

The transitional period is real history and is not lost: it is the last two years of this
row, and it is described here and in both mandate pages' key-date tables.

**Whether the occupation years belong under a German name.** 1914-1920 was Anglo-French
military occupation, not German rule, so the name "German Togoland" is strictly accurate only
to 1914. The row is nevertheless one territorial identity across 1884-1920 — the same
undivided Togoland, under one administration at a time, until it was cut in two — and every
page in this family already refers to it as "former German Togoland". Splitting 1914-1920 into
its own row would create a six-year polity with no data, no separate polygon available and no
consumer, so it is not done; the occupation is stated in the summary instead.

## Open questions

### oq-german-era-boundary-steps

One outline for 36 years. The German colony's northern hinterland boundaries with the Gold
Coast and Dahomey were fixed by Anglo-German and Franco-German agreements during the 1890s,
so the 1884 territory was smaller than the 1914 one, and the polygon used here is a
reconstruction of the 1922 partition rather than either. If a source carrying Deutsch-Togoland
directly is ingested (a German colonial atlas, or a Cliopatria release that adds it), this row
should be rebound to it and possibly split at the 1890s delimitations.

### oq-german-togoland-area-figure

The ~87,200 km² comparison figure is recorded on
[fto-1920-1960](fto-1920-1960.md) without a citation to an ingested source. It agrees with
the commonly published area of the German protectorate, but it should be traced to a citable
source before it is used as anything more than a sanity check on the 4% gap documented above.
