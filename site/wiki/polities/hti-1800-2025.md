# Haiti

## Summary

Haiti occupies the western region of the Caribbean island of Hispaniola. Biger 1995 records its area as 10,718 sq. miles (27,750 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 6,600,000 in 1994 [biger-1995 &sect;haiti](../sources/biger-1995.md#haiti). In 1804 Haiti earned the distinction of being the first black nation to acquire independence and abolish slavery. It is bounded by the Dominican Republic (222 miles; 358 km.) to the east, and by the Atlantic Ocean and Caribbean Sea on its other sides. WHEP tracks Haiti as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory on the western portion of Hispaniola remained constant despite numerous political transitions.

## Territorial extent

Haiti's area is 10,718 sq. miles (27,750 sq. km.), about three-quarters mountainous terrain [biger-1995 &sect;haiti](../sources/biger-1995.md#haiti).
