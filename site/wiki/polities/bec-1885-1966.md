# Bechuanaland Protectorate (1885-1966)

## Summary

The Bechuanaland Protectorate was a British protectorate in southern Africa, distinct from the adjacent Crown Colony of British Bechuanaland (BBE-1885-1895), covering the territory of modern Botswana. The protectorate was declared on 31 March 1885 when Britain extended protection over the Tswana chiefdoms north of the Molopo River in response to Boer and German encroachment. It remained a protectorate — never formally annexed as a Crown Colony — until independence on 30 September 1966, when it became the Republic of Botswana. "Boundary with South Africa (pp.89–90): covers Bechuanaland Protectorate establishment" [biger-1995 §botswana](../sources/biger-1995.md#botswana). The WHEP row covers 1885–1966; the start year corresponds to the protectorate proclamation, the end to independence. "Botswana gained independence in 1966; became multi-party democracy" [biger-1995 §botswana](../sources/biger-1995.md#botswana).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Botswana is a landlocked southern African country (area: 224,606 sq. miles [581,730 sq. km.]) bounded by Namibia (845 miles; 1,360 km.), Zambia at a junction point and Zimbabwe (505 miles; 813 km.), and South Africa (1,144 miles; 1,840 km.); capital: Gaborone [biger-1995 §botswana](../sources/biger-1995.md#botswana). Desert covers more than three quarters of the territory (the Kalahari).

CShapes records "British Bechuanaland" (cowcode 5612, area ~10.62 sq deg) for 1886–1895, but this is the Crown Colony of British Bechuanaland south of the Molopo River (later incorporated into the Cape Colony), not the Protectorate. CShapes does not separately record the Protectorate as an independent time-step entry under its own cowcode; the BEC polygon is drawn from the dependencies=TRUE layer [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). The protectorate territory remained territorially stable throughout 1885–1966 — no boundary changes are recorded by CShapes for this entity over the colonial period.

## Open questions

### oq-bec-pre-1885-predecessor

The Bechuanaland Protectorate (1885) replaced independent Tswana chiefdoms (Bangwaketse, Bakwena, Bangwato, and others). The WHEP chain has no pre-1885 row for this territory, creating a coverage gap from 1800 to 1885. Biger records that Boundary with South Africa covers Bechuanaland Protectorate establishment (pp.89–90) but does not detail the pre-protectorate polities. A pre-colonial Tswana row or rows would be needed to close the coverage gap.

### oq-bec-cshapes-polygon-scope

The BEC-1885-1966 polygon is from CShapes dependencies=TRUE, but CShapes records only the Crown Colony of British Bechuanaland (cowcode 5612, south of the Molopo) for the 1886–1895 period — not the Protectorate. The exact polygon used for BEC needs verification: it should cover the Protectorate territory north of the Molopo, not the Crown Colony territory that was absorbed into the Cape Colony in 1895.
