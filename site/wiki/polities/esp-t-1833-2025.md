# Tarragona (province of Spain)

## Summary

Tarragona is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, occupying the southwestern coastal strip of Catalonia. This entry is type: subnational because Tarragona is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated Tarragona separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES514, for over a century and a half. Unlike Asturias or Cantabria, Tarragona is one of four provinces making up the autonomous community of Catalonia (alongside Barcelona, Girona and Lleida), so its 1833 province boundary and its post-1979 sub-autonomous-community boundary are identical, but the province does not equal the whole autonomous community the way Asturias and Cantabria's single-province communities do. The routing decision that created this row found that no existing ESP polity in the database -- neither A Coruna (a different province), Madrid (the capital region), nor the national ESP-1800-2025 total -- represents Tarragona specifically, so a dedicated province-level row was required, following the same default start-year rule (1833, since Tarragona is not one of the Canary Islands departure units) and open end_year (2025) convention already used across the ESP sibling set.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id ESP-ES514) captures valued statistical rows for unit ES514 ("Tarragona"), country Spain, under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (A Coruna/ES111, Madrid/ES-MD, Asturias/ES120, Cantabria/ES130, and onward through the ES112-ES243 range), following a system default start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform, applied because Tarragona is not one of the Canary Islands departure units that take a different start) and an open end_year of 2025 for still-current units. Previously matched to: nothing distinct -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly that the three existing ESP candidates (A Coruna, Madrid, the national total) are each a different territory and none IS Tarragona, so absent this row the fallback would have been the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, collapsing a province-level series (roughly 6,300 km2) into a country-level one (roughly 505,000 km2, about 80x larger), the same failure mode already avoided for A Coruna and Madrid. What confirms distinctness: Tarragona's status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES514) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since the 1833 reform is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the other ESP province rows -- continuous, separately-tabulated provincial statistics distinguish it from both the national aggregate and its Catalan sibling provinces (Barcelona, Girona, Lleida).

## Predecessors and successors

Tarragona has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory was governed under Catalonia's Antiguo Regimen corregimiento structure, distinct in administrative form from the 1833 province despite continuity of the city and rough territory, and this database does not model that earlier structure at that granularity -- the same treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention already established for the other Spanish NUTS3 units.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-t-abbreviation

**Used the single-letter abbreviation T rather than the province numeric code, matching the ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern for Spanish provinces already established by ESP-CO-1833-2025 (A Coruna), ESP-MD-1833-2025 (Madrid), ESP-AS-1833-2025 (Asturias) and ESP-CA-1833-2025 (Cantabria) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short abbreviation of the unit name rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES514) or the INE province numeric code (43). This page follows that convention, minting ESP-T-1833-2025 rather than a numeric-coded alternative, so the sibling set of Spanish province rows stays internally consistent. T is the vehicle-plate/INE-adjacent single-letter abbreviation historically used for Tarragona in Spanish statistical series distinct from Toledo (TO) and Teruel (TE), which already occupy two-letter slots in this database (ESP-TO-1833-2025, ESP-TE-1833-2025 per the existing wiki file listing), so a single-letter code avoids collision while still being unambiguous to a future author cross-checking against ISO 3166-2:ES-T.

Whoever next touches mapspain-ign should confirm the file's on-disk state before assigning this feature.

### oq-pre-1833-territory-unmodeled

**The pre-1833 Corregimiento/Principality-era territory around Tarragona has no distinct predecessor polity in this database**

Before the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform, the area now covered by Tarragona province was part of the Principality of Catalonia's corregimiento structure (the Corregiment de Tarragona among others), an Antiguo Regimen jurisdiction whose boundaries do not correspond cleanly to the 1833 province even though the city and rough territory persisted across the transition. This database does not model that earlier structure as a distinct polity row, so predecessor is left empty here, mirroring ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025. Whether any pre-1833 Catalan corregimiento should eventually be modeled as its own polity is an open question for the ESP country convention generally, and is worth flagging here because Tarragona, like Asturias, carries a documented pre-1833 administrative identity that a future author might mistake for grounds to add a predecessor without checking the bespoke-code precedent (ALK, HYD, RYU) first.
