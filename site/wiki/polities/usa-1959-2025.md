# United States of America

## Summary

This row tracks the modern United States from **1959** (admission
of Alaska and Hawaii as the 49th and 50th states) to **2025** — a
**67-year row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
Territory is stable at ~9.8M km2. This is the only USA row in the
CSV with `cow_code = 2`; the four predecessor rows have
`cow_code = NA` despite COW recording continuous US membership from
1816.

Predecessor: [usa-1867-1959](usa-1867-1959.md). Successor: NA
(still active at 2025).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  records the United States (`cowcode=2`) from 1886 onward
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The
  polygon for the post-1959 period should reflect the 50-state
  configuration including Alaska and Hawaii.
- **Area.** ~9.8M km2. Territory has been stable since 1959 with
  no acquisitions or cessions.
- **Which 1959 step.** CShapes splits 1959 for gwcode 2 in two: Alaska
  is admitted on 1959-01-03 and Hawaii on 1959-08-21, so the year
  contains a 49-state step (**9,446,212 km²**, 1959-01-03 to
  1959-08-20) and the 50-state step this row wants (**9,462,898 km²**,
  1959-08-21 onward). `polygon_feature_year: 1960` selects the latter
  unambiguously; see the decision below.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Successor:** NA — the United States is still active at the

### decision-polygon-feature-year-1960

**`polygon_feature_year` moved `1959` -> `1960` on 2026-08-13 (issue 100).** Three CShapes
steps for gwcode 2 contain the year 1959 (pre-Alaska 7,939,971 km²; Alaska-only
9,446,212 km²; 50 states 9,462,898 km²) and two of them *start* in 1959, which is the tie
`build_database.find_feature` cannot break. The binding therefore fell through to
shapefile row order and took the **49-state** step — 16,686 km² short, and about the size
of Hawaii (16,635 km² of land), which is what it was missing. This page's own Territorial
extent says the polygon "should reflect the 50-state configuration including Alaska and
Hawaii", so the pick was wrong against the documented intent, not merely non-reproducible.

`1960` falls inside the 50-state step (1959-08-21 – 2019-12-31) and inside no other, so
the choice is now made by the data. The row covers 1959-2024, so the year stays inside
the row's own period.

## Open questions

### oq-notes-na

Resolved — `notes = NA` is the default CSV state for rows without
substantive notes. Not a data quality issue; low priority.

### oq-cow-code-predecessor-rows

The four predecessor USA rows (1800–1803, 1803–1848, 1848–1867,
1867–1959) all have `cow_code = NA` in the CSV. COW records
continuous US membership from 1816. The correct COW code for all
rows from 1816 onward is 2. This is the same pipeline omission
noted on F228 pre-1886 rows — COW codes were likely populated
from CShapes 2.0 (starts 1886) and not backfilled.
