# Los Lagos and Los Ríos (Los Lagos region before the 2007 split)

## Summary

The ground of Chile's Región de Los Lagos as it was from 1976 until Los Ríos was split off: modern
Los Lagos ([chl-ll-1976-2025](chl-ll-1976-2025.md)) plus modern Los Ríos
([chl-lr-2007-2025](chl-lr-2007-2025.md)). Chile's regions were "created in 1974", replacing the 25
provinces, and the country convention dates them from 1976; "In December 2006, two new regions were
created: ... Los Ríos Region in the south", which the country convention dates from 2007.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §chile-regions](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#chile-regions)

It is typed `subnational` and sits inside [chl-1902-2025](chl-1902-2025.md). The span 1976-2025 is
not the region's administrative life (that ended in 2007) but the span over which the LatAm
subnational panel reports a series on this ground: it keeps Los Lagos' crops on the pre-split region
in every year, including after 2007. It is the same kind of row as
[chl-otr-1976-2025](chl-otr-1976-2025.md) and [chl-binb-1976-2025](chl-binb-1976-2025.md).

## Why this entry exists

The panel's `CHL-LL` unit (`whep_production_subnational.parquet`, measured 2026-09-25) files three
kinds of series under one id and one name (`Los Lagos`), and they are two territories:

| indicator | rows | territory | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| area, production, yield (19 crops) | 4,528 (2,512 from 1976, 2,016 back-cast 1935-1975) | **Los Lagos + Los Ríos** | the panel's `CHL-LR` unit has no crop row in any year; Los Lagos' share of Chile's crop area does not step at the split (0.098, 0.095, 0.105 in 2006-2008) |
| landuse (7 classes) | 868 | modern Los Lagos | the five disjoint classes sum to 42,302 km² in every year, 0.88x [chl-ll-1976-2025](chl-ll-1976-2025.md)'s polygon and 0.64x this one |
| livestock_stock | 311 | modern Los Lagos | Los Ríos carries its own cattle and sheep series from 1906 |

So the crop rows were on 0.73x their territory while they routed to CHL-LL-1976-2025. PR #703 added an
optional `indicator` scope to the alias registry for exactly this unit shape; this row is the
territory the crop scope routes to. The measurements are recorded on
[chl-ll-1976-2025 §oq-los-lagos-unit-is-two-territories](chl-ll-1976-2025.md#oq-los-lagos-unit-is-two-territories).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the union of GADM 4.1 adm1 `CHL.9_1` (Los Lagos) and
`CHL.10_1` (Los Ríos), the same features the two parts' own rows carry, so whole and parts come from
one source and tile exactly. It measures **66,527 km²** at source (ESRI:54034) and 66,448 km² as
shipped in `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`, against 48,248 for modern Los Lagos and 18,183 for
Los Ríos as shipped (sum 66,431, 0.9997x the whole; the difference is each outline being simplified
on its own). [gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

**km² reasoning.** Chile publishes roughly 65,000 km² for Los Lagos before the split and roughly
48,000 after it (see [chl-ll-1976-2025](chl-ll-1976-2025.md)); the union is 1.38x modern Los Lagos,
the factor by which the crop rows were mis-sized. No boundary of the two regions moved between 1976
and 2007 other than the split itself, so the modern GADM outlines are the region's own ground, not a
proxy for it.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1976 the ground was the Valdivia, Osorno, Llanquihue and Chiloé provinces of
the pre-1974 system, which have no rows; the panel's 1935-1975 crop rows reach this row as
`back_cast`, as Los Lagos' own pre-1976 landuse and livestock rows reach chl-ll-1976-2025. The 2007
split is not a succession here: the series this row carries continues on the whole.

## Data routing

- **Panel, crops.** `CHL-LL` (`whep-lab-latam`) and `Los Lagos` (`juan-subnational`), indicators
  `area`, `production`, `yield`: 1976-2023 observed, 1900-1975 `back_cast` (data from 1935).
  **4,528 rows**, all moved from CHL-LL-1976-2025.
- **Panel, landuse and livestock.** The same labels scoped to `landuse` and `livestock_stock` stay on
  CHL-LL-1976-2025. `Los Lagos` under `juan-subnational` had no rule before; it is written here, split
  the same way, so that a caller keying the panel by name does not fall back to the name route and put
  the crops back on the post-split region.
- **Layer B**: none.

Whole and parts receive disjoint indicators: crops here, landuse and livestock on the parts, and the
panel's `CHL-LR` carries no crop row. No (item, indicator, year) cell is on both this row and one of
its parts, so nothing is averaged or double-counted (registered as `separate_series` in
`polity_composition.csv`).

**Consumer.** WHEP reads the `indicator` column only from eduaguilera/whep#1294; a resolver without it
would see five rules for one label and years. That PR must merge before WHEP re-syncs past this row.

## Decisions

- **Code `CHL-LLLR`**, the two parts' abbreviations joined (LL + LR), because the row names their
  union and neither part's code may be reused.
- **Span 1976-2025, not 1976-2007.** The row carries a statistical series on this ground, which does
  not end at the split; ending it in 2007 would leave 2007-2023 crop rows with no territory of their
  own, the defect this row exists to remove.
- **Crops only.** Landuse and livestock are measured on modern Los Lagos and stay there.

## Open questions

None specific to this row. Whether the panel should report Los Ríos' crops separately from 2007 is a
