# Brazil (to 1903)

## Summary

Brazil from the **database floor (1800)** to the **Treaty of Petrópolis
(17 November 1903)** — Portuguese Brazil at 1800, independent from 7 September
1822, Empire until 1889, then the First Republic.

This row exists because the 1903 treaty was a real territorial change: Bolivia
ceded **Acre (~125,568 km² as CShapes measures the boundary shift)**, so a single
1800-2025 Brazil row would attribute post-Acre territory to pre-Acre data.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 gwcode 140, feature year **1890** (the 1886-1903
  time-step), **8,311,721 km²** — Brazil *before* the Acre acquisition.
- **`polygon_status: polygon_vintage_drift`**, deliberately. The polygon is
  accurate for 1886-1903 but this row starts at **1800**, and CShapes coverage
  does not extend before 1886. Brazil's borders in the earlier decades derive
  from the 1750 Treaty of Madrid and 1777 Treaty of San Ildefonso lines and were
  not identical to the 1886 boundary — most notably the Amazonian frontiers were
  unsettled. So for 1800-1886 the polygon is a **back-projection**, and data in
  those years is matched to a slightly later boundary. Direction of error is not
  uniform and is not quantified here.

## Sourced claims

- **1808: Portuguese court relocates.** The Portuguese royal family fled to
  Brazil following Napoleon's invasion of Portugal; Rio de Janeiro became the de
  facto capital of the Portuguese Empire.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1822-09-07: Brazilian independence.** Dom Pedro I proclaimed independence
  from Portugal (the "Cry of Ipiranga"); the Empire of Brazil was established.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1889-11-15: Republic proclaimed.** The Empire of Brazil (1822-1889) ended;
  the First Brazilian Republic followed.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1903-11-17: Treaty of Petrópolis.** Bolivia ceded the Acre territory to
  Brazil during the rubber boom — the largest single territorial acquisition of
  Brazil's independent history. CShapes records the resulting boundary change as
  a jump from 8,311,721 km² to 8,437,289 km² (**+125,568 km²**).
  [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md)
- **COW state-system membership.** BRA, ccode 140
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).
