# Uganda (1902-1910)

## Summary

The Uganda Protectorate in its 1902-1910 configuration, following the transfer of the Eastern Province to the East Africa Protectorate (Kenya). CShapes records area 21.86 sq deg for the period 1902-05-15 to 1910-06-15 (COW 500, status=protectorate, owner=Britain COW 200). "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894" [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda). The 1902 start reflects the Order in Council that transferred the Eastern Province, and the 1910 end corresponds to a further boundary adjustment.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 21.86 sq deg for the period 1902-05-15 to 1910-06-15, status=protectorate, owner=Britain (COW 200). This is smaller than the previous UGA-1894-1902 area (31.00 sq deg) due to the transfer of the Eastern Province to Kenya. Bounded by Sudan to the north, Kenya to the east, Lake Victoria and Tanganyika to the south, and Congo to the west.

## Contradictions

Biger states "Uganda became independent on 8 September 1967" (p.490), but the correct date is 9 October 1962. This is likely a typographical error in Biger.

### oq-uga-1902-eastern-province-transfer

### oq-uga-1910-boundary-adjustment

The 1910-06-16 boundary change (area increases from 21.86 to 22.97 sq deg) needs identification. It may relate to the transfer of the Rudolf Province or another border adjustment with Kenya.
