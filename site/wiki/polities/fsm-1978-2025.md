# Micronesia

## Territory and evidence

The four-district reporting territory comprises Yap, Chuuk (Truk), Pohnpei (Ponape) and Kosrae. Order 3027 defines that grouping and its interim legislative/fiscal jurisdiction effective 1 October 1978, while retaining specified territory-wide arrangements. This supports a territorial-economic unit before UN membership, not a claim of sovereignty in 1978. [Order 3027](../sources/ttc-1980.md).

The 1991 start was a COW/state-system date, not a land-footprint change. FishStat explicitly names these four states and reports them from 1988. Neither the Marshall Islands, Palau nor Northern Marianas belongs in this row. [FishStat](../sources/fishstat-trade-2025-1.md).

GADM 4.1 ADM 0 FSM contains four ADM 1 states named Chuuk, Kosrae, Pohnpei, Yap. It is a modern coastline proxy for the historically defined islands, not an exact 1978 shoreline or marine jurisdiction. Measured area 774.856986 km² in ESRI:54034; unchanged geometry from the former record. Historical charts were not found with sufficient island detail to replace it. Modern reef/coastline generalization remains an uncertainty.

FAOSTAT area 145 still begins 1991 in the observed-year map. FishStat's earlier data must be tested against a coherent regenerated consumer snapshot. The old code is migrated through the published rename table.
