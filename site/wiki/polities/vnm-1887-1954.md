# Vietnam (French Indochina)

## Summary

The Vietnam component of French Indochina — comprising Cochinchina
(colony, ceded 1862), Annam (protectorate, 1884), and Tonkin
(protectorate, 1884) — as a distinct territorial unit from the
federation's founding on 17 October 1887 until the Geneva Accords on
21 July 1954. This polity captures data labeled "Viet Nam" or
"Vietnam" in IIA and Mitchell that refers specifically to the Vietnam
territory (~326k km²), as opposed to the full Indochinese federation
FID-1887-1954 (~699k km²).

This polity is **not** the same as FID-1887-1954 (French Indochina),
which additionally covers Cambodia and Laos. The IIA dataset reports
trade data for "viet nam" separately from "indochina", and Mitchell
rice-paddy figures show "viet nam" at ~2.1–2.4 M ha versus "indochina"
at ~5.5–6 M ha for the same period — consistent with Vietnam being
approximately 47% of FID's total area.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` feature `gwcode=815`
("Vietnam (Annam/Cochin China/Tonkin)"), time-step
1893-10-03 to 1954-04-30 (area ~326,024 km² in Mollweide equal-area
projection). CShapes records two time-steps for this feature: the
earlier one (1887-10-17 to 1893-10-02, ~381,936 km²) predates the
incorporation of Laos into the Indochinese federation and includes some
territory later assigned to Laos. The 1893-1954 feature is used here
as the representative polygon because it reflects the stable colonial-
era extent of the three Vietnamese territories after the Laos
protectorate was fully incorporated.

**Why this entry exists:** The IIA dataset (58 rows with year=NA,
plus 215 year-bearing rows 1930–1943) labels its Vietnam trade data
as "viet nam" with iso3c=VNM, measuring only the Vietnam portion of
French Indochina. Routing these rows to FID-1887-1954 (the full
federation at ~699k km²) would overstate territorial coverage by
approximately 2×. CShapes 2.0 records the Vietnam territories as a
separate feature (gwcode=815) for the entire colonial period, making
it possible to assign a correct polygon without constructing an
estimate.

**Relationship to FID-1887-1954:** FID-1887-1954 remains correct for
data labeled "Indochina" or reporting the federation as a whole.
VNM-1887-1954 covers only the three Vietnamese territories
(Cochinchina, Annam, Tonkin) within the federation. The two polities
are spatially disjoint subsets of different granularity — not
duplicates.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** FCC-1862-1887 (French Cochinchina) — the initial
  French foothold before the full Indochinese federation was
  established. Annam and Tonkin protectorates are technically the
  predecessors too, but are not separately tracked in WHEP; FCC
  represents the pre-federation French presence in Vietnam.
- **Successor:** VNM-1975-2025 (Vietnam, unified from 1975). The
  1954–1975 period (post-Geneva, pre-unification) is handled by
  South Vietnam (SVI) and Democratic Republic of Vietnam polities
  when data allows the split; data labeled generically "Vietnam"
  or "Viet Nam" for 1954–1975 maps to SVI per the existing
  normalise_iso() rule in match.R.

## Decisions

- Created 2026-06-24 to fix territorial overstatement: previously,
  "viet nam" IIA/Mitchell rows were being routed to FID-1887-1954
  (~699k km²) via the alias `viet nam (1887-1954) → FID-1887-1954`,
  overstating territory ~2×. VNM-1887-1954 uses the CShapes gwcode=815
  polygon (~326k km²), matching the actual extent of the data.
- The 58 IIA year=NA rows could not be routed by the old year-bounded
  alias; the new source-scoped alias `viet nam [source=iia] →
  VNM-1887-1954` covers them.
- Polygon vintage: the post-Laos-transfer feature is used rather than the
  1887 feature because the 1887 polygon includes territories later assigned
  to Laos. **Corrected 2026-08-05 (issue 99): `polygon_feature_year` was
  `1893`, which did not select that feature.** CShapes gwcode 815 has three
  steps containing 1893 — `1887-1893`, `1893-1893` and `1893-1954` — and two
  of them *start* in 1893, so `find_feature`'s tie-break ("prefer the feature
  whose start-year equals the queried year") could not single one out and
  returned whichever the shapefile lists first. It returned a
  **379,848 km²** pre-transfer step, against the 326,024 km² recorded here —
  a 16% error, on a row that does declare an area.

  The break is 1893-10-03, the Franco-Siamese treaty moving the left bank of
  the Mekong to Laos. `polygon_feature_year: 1894` falls inside exactly one
  candidate span and selects the intended 324,216 km² extent. The decision
  above was already right; only the year that expresses it was wrong.

  Corroboration that 324,216 is the intended figure: `F237-1954-1975`, the
  same territory a year later, measures 324,094 km², and Vietnam's actual
  area is ~331,000 km².

  Vintage drift is now explicit rather than "small": 1887-1893 was genuinely
  the larger 379,848 km² configuration, so those six years are understated by
  17% by this binding. That costs nothing today — the earliest `viet nam`
  observation is **1930** — but it is a real limit of the single-polygon
  choice, and splitting the row would be the fix if pre-1894 data ever
  arrives.
- The existing alias `viet nam (1887-1954) → FID-1887-1954` in
  applied_aliases.csv is updated to point to VNM-1887-1954 since all
  "viet nam" data in the IIA and Mitchell sources refers to the Vietnam
  territory, not the full federation.

## Open questions

### oq-year-na-iia-dating

The 58 IIA rows with year=NA cannot be assigned to specific years.
IIA operated 1909–1945, so the data is from the French Indochina era,
but the absence of year metadata prevents time-series placement. These
rows are matched to VNM-1887-1954 but will appear as undated in
visualisations. A future data-improvement pass should recover year
information from the original IIA publications if possible.

### oq-1887-1893-polygon-choice

The 1887–1893 CShapes feature (~381,936 km²) is not used because it
includes territory later assigned to Laos. However, a handful of
data rows may date to 1887–1893; they will be matched to the 1893
polygon (drift of up to 6 years). This is acceptable given the
coarse temporal resolution of the IIA and Mitchell data.
