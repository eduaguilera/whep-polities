# Latvia (Livonia and Courland)

## Summary

Tracks the Latvian lands as Baltic governorates of the Russian Empire from **1800** to **1918**. The territory comprised the Governorate of Livonia (southern part) and the Governorate of Courland, with distinct administrative identity within Imperial Russia [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [LVA-1918-1940](lva-1918-1940.md) (interwar Latvia). The combined Livonia (southern part) and Courland governorates corresponded closely to interwar Latvia's territory.. Livonia (southern part, centred on Riga) and Courland covered modern Latvia. CShapes 2.0 tracks Latvia from 1918 independence.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Latvia" for 1913. Latvia's Baltic governorates were distinct administrative units in the Russian Empire. Previously routed to F228 (Russian Empire).
