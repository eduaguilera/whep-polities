# Israel (1948-1967)

## Summary

Israel (1948-1967) tracks the State of Israel from its declaration of independence on 14 May 1948 until the Six-Day War of June 1967. During this period Israel was bounded by armistice lines agreed with Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, and Syria in 1949 following the first Arab-Israeli war. The row ends when the Six-Day War dramatically expanded Israeli-controlled territory (the CShapes polygon area increases from ~1.96 to ~8.32 sq deg on 1967-06-10), creating the distinct successor row ISR-1967-1979. Biger notes that the country was under Ottoman rule until World War I, after which the League of Nations awarded a mandate to Great Britain; the British Mandate ended at independence in 1948.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0; cowcode 666.
- **CShapes entry.** Single time-step: 1948-05-14 to 1967-06-09; area ~1.96 sq deg (~20,770 km²).
- **Armistice lines.** The 1949 armistice lines — the "Green Line" — formed the de facto borders during this period. These were not internationally recognized international boundaries.
- **Pre-1948.** The predecessor British Mandate territory (PAL-1920-1948) was substantially larger, including what became Jordan's West Bank.
- [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [pal-1920-1948](pal-1920-1948.md) — British Mandate for Palestine ended 14 May 1948; Israel declared independence. The rump western portion became Israel; the eastern portion (West Bank) was administered by Jordan from 1948. Significant territorial coverage gap between PAL-1920-1948 and ISR-1948-1967 — Jordan's annexation of the West Bank is not explicitly covered by a WHEP row in this period.
- **Successor:** [isr-1967-1979](isr-1967-1979.md) — Six-Day War (5–10 June 1967) expands territory to include Sinai, Gaza, West Bank, East Jerusalem, and Golan Heights.

## Contradictions

The CSV predecessor field for ISR-1948-1967 is listed as NA, but historically the territory came from the British Mandate for Palestine (PAL-1920-1948). This appears to be a missing predecessor link in the CSV rather than a deliberate design choice.

## Open questions

### oq-west-bank-coverage

The West Bank was administered by Jordan (annexed 1950, until 1967 occupation) but no WHEP row explicitly covers this territory during 1948-1967. PAL-1920-1948 ends in 1948, and ISR-1948-1967 covers only the territory within the Green Line. The next ingest should investigate whether a JOR-coded row explicitly covers the West Bank for this period, or whether this is a genuine coverage gap.

### oq-1949-armistice-lines

The 1949 armistice agreements established the Green Line with Egypt (February 1949), Lebanon (March 1949), Jordan (April 1949), and Syria (July 1949). Biger does not provide specific detail on these armistice boundaries. A dedicated historical-atlas source (e.g., Biger's earlier work on Palestine) would strengthen this page.
