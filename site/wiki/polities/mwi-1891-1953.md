# Nyasaland (1891-1953)

## Summary

Nyasaland (initially styled the British Central Africa Protectorate, 1891-1907; renamed Nyasaland Protectorate from 1907) was a British dependency in south-central Africa covering the territory of present-day Malawi. The protectorate was formally established on 14 May 1891 following the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 11 June 1891 which delimited boundaries with Portuguese East Africa. CShapes 2.0 records the territory under gwcode 553 (Gleditsch-Ward / COW code for Malawi) as a British protectorate from 1891-06-10 to 1953-07-31, area 118,483 km². On 1 August 1953 Nyasaland was merged into the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (FRN-1953-1964); it re-emerged as independent Malawi on 6 July 1964 (MWI-1964-2025).

## Territorial extent

Area: 118,483 km² (CShapes 2.0, gwcode 553, feature year 1891). Landlocked territory bounded by German East Africa / Tanganyika (north), Mozambique (east, south, and southwest), and Northern Rhodesia / Zambia (west). The boundaries were fixed by the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 1891 and the Anglo-German agreement of 1890 (Lake Nyasa northern boundary). No significant territorial change occurred during the protectorate period 1891-1953; the footprint is identical to modern Malawi.
