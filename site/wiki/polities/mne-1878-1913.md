# Montenegro (to 1913)

## Summary

This row tracks independent Montenegro for **36 years**, from the
Congress of Berlin to the Balkan Wars
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Montenegro gained international recognition as an independent state at
the Congress of Berlin (1878), alongside Serbia, Romania, and Bulgaria.
The Wikipedia Ottoman source covers this: "Montenegro were formally
recognized as independent"
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878).

Montenegro was a small Balkan state (~9,475 km² at independence) that
expanded in the Balkan Wars (1912-1913). The 1913 end date aligns with
the Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913), the same end date used for
Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece, and Romania's pre-Balkan-Wars rows.

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- but Montenegro gained independence from
Ottoman suzerainty at the Congress of Berlin (1878).

Successor: [mne-1913-1915](mne-1913-1915.md).

  Bucharest (10 August 1913) concluded the Second Balkan War. This
  is the end-of-row event.
