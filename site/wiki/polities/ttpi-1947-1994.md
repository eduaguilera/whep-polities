# Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands (1947-1994)

> **`iso3: PCI` assigned 2026-08-13 (issue 209), and no new polity was created for it.**
>
> FAOSTAT reports **area 164, "Pacific Islands Trust Territory"** — 489 rows, **1961-1990** — and
> that is this row. The trusteeship is what the label names, its span sits inside 1947-1994, and the
> data stops in 1990 because the trusteeship terminated for the Marianas, the Marshalls and the FSM
> in 1986 and reporting moved to the successors; **area 163, the Northern Marianas, begins in 1991**,
> the year after 164 ends.
>
> So the tenth territory in that issue needed an **identifier**, not an entity. Creating a
> `PCI-1961-1990` row would have duplicated this one and put two live polities on the same ground for
> thirty years — the defect class `eduaguilera/whep#514` reports.
>
> `PCI` is FAOSTAT's own ISO3 for the area and is not an ISO 3166 code: no ISO code exists for a
> dissolved trust territory, so this is a **local** identifier in the sense
> `validate_local_iso_codes` documents, like `AUH` for Austria-Hungary or `OTT` for the Ottoman
> Empire.
>
> **What it means for containment:** with `MNP-1986-2025` created in the same change, this row again
> holds three contemporaneous polities of other families and returns to
> `validate_spatial_containment`'s `LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` — it was removed on 2026-08-10 when finer
> simplification pushed the Marshall Islands below the 90% threshold.

The **Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands (TTPI)** was a United Nations
strategic trust territory administered by the United States from 1947 to
1994. It comprised the former Japanese-mandated Micronesian islands:
the **Caroline Islands** (now the Federated States of Micronesia and
Palau), the **Marshall Islands**, and the **Northern Mariana Islands**.
The territory did not include Guam, which was a separate US territory
under direct American sovereignty throughout this period.

The trust was created by the UN Security Council on **18 July 1947**
(Resolution 21), replacing the Japanese South Seas Mandate (Nan'yo-cho,
1920-1945) that had ended with Japan's defeat in World War II.

The trust territory was progressively terminated as constituent districts
achieved self-determination:
- **Northern Mariana Islands** — Covenant with the US signed 15 February
  1975; became a US Commonwealth (CNMI) effective 1 January 1978.
- **Marshall Islands** — Compact of Free Association entered into force
  21 October 1986; UN trusteeship terminated by SC Res. 683 (22 December 1990).
- **Federated States of Micronesia** — Compact of Free Association entered
  into force 3 November 1986; UN trusteeship terminated by SC Res. 683
  (22 December 1990).
- **Palau** — Compact of Free Association entered into force 1 October 1994;
  UN trusteeship formally terminated by SC Res. 956 (10 November 1994).

**Why this entry exists.** The FAO 1952 yearbook (*fao1952*) reports 18 rows
of agricultural data (cattle, cassava, poultry, sweet potatoes) for the label
"Pacific Is us trust" for years 1949-1951. The FAO reported this territory as
a single administrative unit. No existing WHEP polity covers this aggregate:
CAR-1920-1945 ends in 1945, FSM-1991-2025 starts in 1991, MHL-1874-2025 has
Marshall-Islands-only scope, and PLW-1991-2025 begins in 1991.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: proxy (composed union of modern GADM polygons).**

No historical GIS polygon for the TTPI as a unified entity was found in
CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria v0.1.3, or Paine et al. 2024.

**Proxy polygon used.** GADM 4.1 adm0 composed union of four successor territories:

| Component | GADM adm0 code | Successor polity | Land area (km²) |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Caroline Islands (FSM extent) | FSM | FSM-1991-2025 | ~702 |
| Marshall Islands | MHL | MHL-1874-2025 | ~181 |
| Northern Mariana Islands | MNP | (no WHEP polity) | ~464 |
| Palau | PLW | PLW-1991-2025 | ~444 |
| **Union total** | | | **~1,791** |

**ESTIMATE flag.** Polygon is a composed union of modern (post-1991) boundaries;
vintage drift ~40-45 years for 1949-1951 data. Land territory is stable (same
islands), so drift reflects administrative-status change only, not genuine
extent change.

**Scope exclusions:** Guam excluded (separate US territory, FAO reported
separately). Wake Island excluded (US military jurisdiction, not TTPI).
