# Benin (1895-1898)

## Summary

The initial French colonial territory in the area of present-day Benin, from 1895 to 13 June 1898. This row represents the small coastal Dahomey colony (area 2.97 sq deg in CShapes geographic coordinates) before the major territorial expansion that occurred on 14 June 1898 — the date on which the area jumps to 9.57 sq deg as France consolidated its inland boundaries with neighbouring colonies. "The colony of Benin was created from the Etablissements dues Golfe de Guinee (the name given to the French protectorates along the Bight of Benin in 1883), and was renamed Dahomey one year later. A French decree of March 1901 specified a boundary" [biger-1995 §benin](../sources/biger-1995.md#benin). "The Beninese were ruled by the French from the beginning of the twentieth century, at which time the country was called Dahomey after the great kingdom that controlled the area in precolonial times" [biger-1995 §benin](../sources/biger-1995.md#benin). This is a WHEP-distinct row because CShapes records a real area change at 1898-06-14.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 2.97 sq deg for two time-steps: 1895-01-01 to 1897-07-22 and 1897-07-23 to 1898-06-13, both status=colony, owner=France (COW 220). The two CShapes steps have identical areas, indicating only an administrative (non-territorial) event on 23 July 1897 — under WHEP methodology these are collapsed into one row (1895–1898). The territory covers primarily the coastal region later known as French Dahomey, bounded by Togo (400 mi; 644 km), Nigeria (480 mi; 773 km), and the Bight of Benin coastline [biger-1995 §benin](../sources/biger-1995.md#benin).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [dhy-1800-1894](dhy-1800-1894.md) — Kingdom of Dahomey, conquered by France in the Second Franco-Dahomean War of 1894; French protectorate established 1894, colony from 1895
- **Successor:** [ben-1898-1960](ben-1898-1960.md) — expanded French Dahomey after the 14 June 1898 boundary consolidation brought inland territories under French sovereignty

## Contradictions

Biger (p.74) states the colony was "created from the Etablissements dues Golfe de Guinee" renamed in 1884, but the CShapes start date for this polity is 1895-01-01. The gap between 1883/1884 and 1895 in CShapes may reflect CShapes coverage limitations for early colonial periods. Biger's account implies French presence predating the 1895 CShapes start.

## Open questions

### oq-ben-1897-admin-event

CShapes records a coding change on 23 July 1897 for Benin, but the area does not change (2.97 sq deg throughout). The event behind this coding step — likely an administrative reclassification — should be identified. Under WHEP methodology this does not trigger a new polity row, but it may be historically significant.

### oq-ben-1895-start-predating-cshapes

Biger describes French protectorates in the Bight of Benin from 1883 and a rename to Dahomey in 1884. CShapes coverage begins at 1895. Whether a pre-1895 WHEP row for French Dahomey should exist (predating CShapes) is an open question dependent on whether territorial data for 1884–1894 can be established from Biger or another source.
