# Valparaíso (region of Chile)

## Summary

Valparaíso is one of Chile's sixteen first-order administrative regions (regiones), covering the central coastal strip of the country immediately north-west of the Santiago Metropolitan Region, including the port city of Valparaíso, the resort city of Viña del Mar, and Chile's Pacific island territories (Juan Fernández archipelago and Easter Island/Rapa Nui, both administratively attached to this region). The region was created in its current form by Decreto Ley 573 and 575 of 1974, which restructured Chile's territory into regions in place of the older province system; the new structure became operative in 1976, which is why this entry's `start_year` is 1976 rather than the date of the decree itself. `type: subnational` is used because Valparaíso has never been a sovereign or quasi-sovereign entity of its own — sovereignty belongs to the Chilean state throughout — but statistical sources (production, trade, demographic) report figures at the regional level distinct from the national CHL totals, which is what this row exists to carry. The region has not been affected by any of Chile's later regional splits (the 2007 creation of Los Ríos/Arica y Parinacota, or the 2018 creation of Ñuble from Biobío), so its 1976 boundary is understood to persist unchanged through 2025.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures rows in the agent-harness routing pipeline labelled `Valparaíso` under `country: Chile`, filed originally against the national CHL row despite describing a first-order administrative subdivision roughly 1/20th the size of the country. The routing decision (unit_id CHL-VS) found no existing polity representing this region and no name/boundary candidate close enough to reuse, so a new subnational polity was proposed rather than continuing to fold the data into the national total. The 1976 start year follows the country-wide convention set for Chile's DL 573/575 regions (as opposed to the two documented exceptions, Los Ríos/Arica y Parinacota in 2007 and Ñuble in 2018, which get later start years because they were carved out later). What confirms this is a genuinely distinct reporting unit rather than a relabeling of the national total is that Chile's own statistical apparatus (INE) and international compilers report agricultural, demographic and economic data by región as a standing convention going back to the 1976 reorganization, not merely as an ad hoc breakdown — Valparaíso appears as its own line in Chilean regional statistics throughout this period. Data years 1900-1975 that were filed under the same "Valparaíso" label are explicitly NOT covered by this entry, because they predate the 1976 regional system entirely and most plausibly refer to the pre-existing Valparaíso Province, a different administrative unit with different (and currently unresearched) boundaries — see open question below.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision found that `gadm-4.1-adm1` is the correct registered source in principle (global GADM carries Chile's regions as GID_1 admin-1 features) but the copy of that source present in this repository is restricted to a subset of 81 countries that does not include Chile (0 features for CHL). This is `polygon_route: registered_source_unfetched` — the source is registered and the right answer, it simply has not been fetched for this country — so `polygon_status` is `unassigned` and `polygon_source` is set to the slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` itself (not a route name, not a placeholder), per the instruction that an unfetched-but-registered source's slug IS the source.

**Territory description:** Valparaíso Región occupies the central Chilean coast between roughly 32°S and 33.5°S, bordered by the Coquimbo Región to the north, the Santiago Metropolitan Región and O'Higgins Región to the east and south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Its mainland extent is small and easily located on a modern map — it runs from the resort city of Viña del Mar and the port of Valparaíso inland to Los Andes and San Felipe — but the *administrative* region also includes two isolated Pacific territories: the Juan Fernández archipelago (~670 km west of the mainland) and Easter Island/Rapa Nui (~3,700 km west, in the South Pacific). Continental Valparaíso is approximately 16,400 km², but the administrative region including Easter Island and Juan Fernández is closer to 17,700 km² — no measurement is offered here from an attached geometry, since none is yet assigned; these figures are drawn from published Chilean regional statistics (INE), not from any polygon in this repository.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first entry created for this reporting unit, since pre-1976 "Valparaíso" data is deliberately excluded from this span (see open question) rather than treated as a continuation. No successor: the region is still current as of the 2025 open-end convention used throughout this table, and none of Chile's subsequent regional splits (2007, 2018) touched Valparaíso's boundary, so there is no reason to expect a successor entry unless a future split occurs.

## Sourced claims

- Decreto Ley 573 (1974) and Decreto Ley 575 (1974) restructured Chile into regions, replacing the prior provincial system, with the new regional division becoming operative in 1976.
- Chile's Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (INE) reports Región de Valparaíso as including the Isla de Pascua (Easter Island) and Juan Fernández provinces as part of its administrative territory, distinct from the mainland core around the cities of Valparaíso and Viña del Mar.

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Polity code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern**

Chile has no existing subnational codes, so this entry sets the country's precedent. Rather than inventing a bespoke code, CHL-VS-1976-2025 follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 existing subnational rows (e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025, DZA-CVD-1902-1919): ISO3, a short subunit abbreviation (VS for Valparaíso, chosen to be memorable and non-colliding with other Chilean regions likely to be added later, e.g. VA for Valparaíso would collide with Valle/Ñuble style abbreviations), then start and end years. Valparaíso Región is an ordinary first-order administrative division created by a generic 1974 reorganization decree, not a historical territory with a name and identity of its own (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad State), so the bespoke-code pattern used for those 3 outlier rows does not apply here.

### d-polygon-source-slug-not-route

**polygon_source set to the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1, not a route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched with proposed polygon_source gadm-4.1-adm1. Per the instructions, when the route is registered_source_unfetched the slug IS the source and polygon_status must be unassigned (not the route name itself, and not new_source_needed, which was explicitly rejected by validate_declared_sources in an earlier draft of this pipeline). This entry therefore declares polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1 with polygon_feature_id left null and polygon_status: unassigned, reflecting that the source exists and is registered but its Chile-covering data has not yet been fetched into the local copy of GADM 4.1.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-valparaiso-province

**Does pre-1976 data filed under 'Valparaíso' belong to a distinct predecessor polity?**

The routing decision explicitly flags that data years 1900-1975 filed under the label 'Valparaíso' were excluded from this entry because they predate the 1976 regionalization system, but it does not resolve what those rows actually measure. Chile had a pre-1976 provincial system (with a Valparaíso Province) whose boundaries are not researched here and may differ meaningfully from the modern región — e.g. old Valparaíso Province may not have included Easter Island or Juan Fernández under the same administrative umbrella, or may have had different mainland extent. Someone needs to research Chile's pre-1976 provincial boundaries and decide whether to (a) create a CHL-VS-<start>-1976 predecessor polity, (b) route the pre-1976 rows to the national CHL total instead, or (c) find evidence the provincial and modern regional boundaries were similar enough to extend this entry's start_year backward. Until resolved, those rows remain unrouted or misrouted.

### oq-no-polygon-fetched

**No polygon is available for this or any Chilean región at present**

Because the locally present copy of gadm-4.1-adm1 excludes Chile entirely (0 features), this entry — and every future Chilean subnational entry — will have polygon_status: unassigned until someone fetches the full global GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer (or a Chile-specific admin1 source such as the Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional's regional boundary files) into the repository's data store. This is a blocking gap for any area-based validation of this entry and likely several siblings (Chile's other 15 regions) that this pipeline will also want to create.

### oq-easter-island-separate-reporting

**Should Easter Island / Juan Fernández be split into their own entries?**

Easter Island (Rapa Nui) is administratively part of Valparaíso Región but is geographically, demographically, and economically entirely disjoint from the mainland — it has its own indigenous governance structures and is sometimes reported separately in tourism, population, or trade statistics. If any source in this pipeline's data reports Easter Island figures distinctly from mainland Valparaíso figures, those rows would be misrouted here rather than to a dedicated Easter Island entry. This has not been checked against the data currently being routed.
