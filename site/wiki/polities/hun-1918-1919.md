# Hungary (1918-1919)

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as Hungary from **1918** to
**1919** — a 2-year transitional row covering the chaotic
period between the dissolution of Austria-Hungary and the
Treaty of Trianon [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is a successor
state of [auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md), one of the seven entities
that emerged from the dissolution of Austria-Hungary in 1918
[wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §successor-states](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#successor-states).

The Republic of Hungary was proclaimed 16 November 1918 (COW
start date). The defining territorial event — the Treaty of
Trianon (4 June 1920), in which Hungary lost ~72% of its
pre-war territory — falls on a successor row, not this one.
This row covers the period of maximum territorial extent of the
newly independent Hungary before the Trianon losses were
imposed. See [oq-trianon-date-chain](#oq-trianon-date-chain).

Predecessor per CSV: [auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md). Successor:
[hun-1919-1920](hun-1919-1920.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Full CShapes coverage from 1918
  onward [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Shrinking territory within the row window.** CShapes records
  Hungary from 1918-11-03 at ~37.6 sq deg (the full Kingdom of
  Hungary territory), shrinking to ~31.7 at 1918-12-01, then
  ~30.2 at 1919-09-10 (Treaty of Saint-Germain settling the
  Austrian side). The drastic reduction to ~11.0 sq deg comes
  at 1920-06-04 (Treaty of Trianon), which falls on the
  successor row.
- **COW continuity.** COW code HUN, 310, from 1918-11-16 to
  2024-12-31 — continuous with no break at any point
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md). Unlike Austria (which
  has a 1938-1955 gap), Hungary's COW entry is unbroken.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md) — Hungary inherited
  the Transleithanian (Hungarian) half of Austria-Hungary,
  initially at its full pre-war extent.
- **Successor:** [hun-1919-1920](hun-1919-1920.md) — the chain continues
  through the Trianon period. Eventually reaches HUN-1920-1938
  (post-Trianon Horthy-era Hungary).

## Sourced claims

- **1918-11-16: Republic of Hungary proclaimed.** COW records
  Hungary as entering the state system on this date
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md). CShapes uses 1918-11-03
  (A-H armistice date) as the start.
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** "Officially settled
  Hungary's status as successor state"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1920-06-04](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1920-06-04).
  Hungary lost approximately two-thirds of its pre-war
  territory to Czechoslovakia, Romania (Transylvania),
  Yugoslavia, and Austria. This event post-dates this row but
  is the defining event for the Hungarian polity chain.

## Open questions

### oq-trianon-date-chain

The Treaty of Trianon (4 June 1920) — in which Hungary lost
~72% of its pre-war territory — falls on a successor row
(HUN-1919-1920 or HUN-1920-1938), not on this one. The chain
splits need examining: does the 1919 end of this row correspond
to any real territorial event, or is it an arbitrary split?
The CShapes data shows gradual shrinkage during 1918-1919 but
the catastrophic Trianon reduction only at 1920-06-04. Under
the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition), the 1919
split point may not correspond to a territorial event.
