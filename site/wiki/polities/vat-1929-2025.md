# Holy See (Vatican City State)

## Summary

A 0.49 km² walled enclave within Rome, sovereign since the **Lateran Treaty of 11 February 1929**.
The smallest polity in this database by two orders of magnitude.

## Why this entry exists

**WHEP publishes land-use hectares for the Holy See and this database had no row to carry them**
(issue 185). Measured on whep `main`:

```
area 94   iso3 VAT   268 rows   1850-2022   source LUH2_grassland   unit ha
```

The Holy See is not a FAOSTAT reporter. It enters WHEP through the **LUH2 land-use raster**, which
covers it as territory whether or not anyone files returns. They were invisible while WHEP folded
its FABIO Rest-of-World bucket; `eduaguilera/whep#628` stopped folding it.

## Span starts at 1929, and the 1850-1929 rows are answered by other polities

The data begins in 1850; this row does not. **1929 is when the Vatican City State came into
existence**, and back-dating a sovereign row to 1850 would assert an entity that did not exist —
the error issue 22 is about, in its most extreme form.

The territory itself is covered for the whole period, by rows that already exist:

```
1850-1870   PAP-1800-1870    Papal States        (Rome was papal territory)
1870-1929   ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025        (Rome annexed 1870; the "Roman Question")
1929-2025   VAT-1929-2025    this row
```

Between 1870 and 1929 the popes held **no** temporal sovereignty — the Roman Question, unresolved
until Lateran. So the honest answer for those 59 years is that the ground was Italian, and it is
Italy's rows that carry it. That is not a gap; `write_iso3_successor_map.py` resolves `VAT` for
pre-1929 years by walking back from this row.

## Territorial extent, and why `polygon_status` is `estimate`

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = VAT`.

```
GADM 4.1 raw           0.5267 km²
as published           0.5265 km²
official land area     0.49 km²
ratio (published/official)  1.075x
```

> **This row is why PR 189 exists.** Built before that change, the published geometry measured
> **0.2746 km² — 48% of the enclave deleted**, the worst simplification loss in the database.
> `SimplifyPreserveTopology(0.01)` thins at 0.01° ≈ 1.1 km, and the Vatican is about 1 km across,
> so the tolerance was wider than the territory. The simplification budget added in 189 now picks
> a tolerance scaled to the feature (here ×0.01, costing 0.04%), which is what makes the figure
> above comparable to anything.
>
> It was invisible to the gates by construction: check A skips rows where **both** the claimed and
> the measured area are under `--min-km2 200`, and 0.49 against 0.27 is two numbers that agree
> with each other about nothing.

The 7.5% agreement is good in relative terms and **`polygon_status` is still `estimate`, not
`assigned`.** That is not hedging. At 0.5 km² the absolute disagreement is 0.037 km² — 37,000 m²,
about five football pitches — and a coastline-or-wall vertex placed a few metres out moves the
figure by a percent. Calling that `assigned` would claim a precision the source does not have at
this magnitude. `estimate` means the polygon approximates the territory, which is exactly the
situation; check A reports `estimate` rows as documented rather than failing them.

**Any per-km² figure computed on this row is meaningless**, and more so than for the
micro-territories discussed on [tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md), which are 20-100× larger. WHEP's
268 LUH2 grassland rows for this area are hectares of modelled land cover on a 0.49 km² enclave
that is almost entirely buildings, courtyards and gardens; they should be read as "the raster's
cell happens to overlap here", not as agriculture.

An area is nevertheless **declared** rather than left null, because `validate_polygons`' area
comparison is opt-in via that field and a null leaves the row unchecked (issue 59).

## Contradictions

### It is an enclave, so Italy's polygon contains it

Measured:

```
VAT ∩ ITA-1919-2025 = 0.5267 km²  —  100% of VAT
VAT ∩ ITA-1870-1919 = 0.5267 km²
VAT ∩ PAP-1800-1870 = 0.5267 km²
```

For 1929-2025 that is a **real double claim**: `ITA-1919-2025` is live, non-aggregate, and its
polygon covers the whole enclave, so 0.53 km² of ground is claimed by two coexisting rows. It is
recorded rather than fixed, for two reasons.

First, it is **the same class as issue 84** — British India's polygon containing Ceylon, Portuguese
India and (measured in PR 184) French India — and the same class as the `IDN-OTH`/`NNG` overlap
fixed in PR 182. A national polygon taken from a source that does not cut out enclaves will
contain them. Solving it here would mean giving Italy a constructed polygon with the Vatican
subtracted, **and splitting `ITA-1919-2025` at 1929**, since before Lateran the enclave really was
Italian. That is a periodisation change to a major national family and does not belong in the
change that creates this row.

Second, the magnitude: 0.53 km² against Italy's 300,310 km² is **0.00018%**. Any area-weighted
allocation double-counts by that fraction. It is worth recording exactly, and it is not worth
restructuring Italy for on its own.

The 1870-1929 and 1850-1870 intersections above are **not** contradictions — they are correct.
Those years are before this row starts, and the ground genuinely was Italian and papal
respectively.

## Predecessors and successors

`predecessor: [PAP-1800-1870]` — the Papal States are the Holy See's territorial predecessor, and
`PAP-1800-1870` gains the matching `successor:` entry so the edge is readable from both ends
(the graph is only 55% symmetric, and a one-directional edge is invisible to any traversal that
reads the other field — `validate_chain_integrity` signal F).

**The edge is not year-adjacent, and that is deliberate.** This database's usual convention is
that a predecessor ends exactly where its row begins; `PAP` ends 1870 and this row starts 1929.
The 59-year interval is the Roman Question, during which there was no papal state to be the
predecessor of anything. Inserting a bridging row for it would invent an entity; leaving the edge
non-adjacent records the real discontinuity.

`ITA-1919-2025` is deliberately **not** listed as a predecessor even though it held the ground
until 1929. It is live and runs to the 2025 ceiling, so the symmetric `successor:` entry it would
need trips `validate_chain_integrity` signal D (a live entity has nothing after it) — and more
importantly, Italy did not *become* the Vatican. It ceded a piece of Rome.

## Open questions

### oq-vat-italy-enclave-subtraction

Whether `ITA-1919-2025` should be split at 1929 and given a constructed polygon with this enclave
removed. Doing so would close the 0.53 km² double claim above and bring Italy in line with how
`IDN-OTH-1949-1951` was fixed in PR 182. It would also move Italy's published area and needs the
same treatment for San Marino, which is the identical case at 61 km² and 124× larger — so the two
should be settled together, under issue 84's heading rather than piecemeal.

### oq-vat-luh2-plausibility

WHEP reports 268 rows of LUH2 grassland for this area, 1850-2022 — including years before the
state existed. Since LUH2 is a raster it will report land cover for the cell regardless, but the
values should be checked against the enclave's actual 0.49 km²: if the modelled grassland exceeds
the territory, the rows are a cell-overlap artefact rather than a measurement, which is the shape
`build_polycell_support()`'s overlap warning exists to surface.
