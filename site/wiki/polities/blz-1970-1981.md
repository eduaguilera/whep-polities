# Belize (pre-independence)

## Summary

British Honduras renamed Belize in 1973 while still a British colony. This row covers the transition period from the capital relocation to Belmopan (1970) through independence on 21 September 1981. Internal self-government was in place from 1964; full independence was delayed by the Guatemalan territorial claim. No data rows are currently matched to this entry.
