# Oceania Other

## Summary

WHEP aggregate reporting-area row for residual Oceanian and Pacific territories in WHEP harmonization tables.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** derived WHEP `reporting-areas` source.
- **Components:** WHEP Oceania Other component territories from `regions_full.csv`, with Canton and Enderbury proxied by Kiribati and Johnston/Midway/Wake proxied by US Minor Outlying Islands.
- **Proxy note:** This is a residual reporting-area polygon, not a country polygon.

## Sourced Claims

- `inst/extdata/regions.csv` in WHEP records `ROCE`, area code 906, as Oceania Other.
- `inst/extdata/harmonization/regions_full.csv` lists the component territories grouped under `ROCE`.
