# Sokoto Caliphate

## Summary

The Sokoto Caliphate, 1804–1903. Founded by Usman dan Fodio's Fulani jihad against the Sultanate of Gobir declared on 21 February 1804, it became the largest state in 19th-century sub-Saharan Africa (population 10–20 million by 1837, comprising over 30 tributary emirates across modern northern Nigeria, Niger, Cameroon, and Burkina Faso). Defeated by British forces in March–July 1903; incorporated into the Northern Nigeria Protectorate.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Core heartland on the middle Niger and Sokoto river systems; tributary emirates (Kano, Katsina, Zaria, Adamawa, Ilorin, etc.) extended the effective frontier southward to the edge of the Yoruba kingdoms and eastward to the Bornu frontier. Area ~792,706 km² per the CSV notes. Cliopatria 0.1.3 records 6 time-steps for the Sokoto polity.
