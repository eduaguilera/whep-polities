# Jammu and Kashmir (disputed territory)

## Summary

This row tracks **Jammu and Kashmir** as a distinct statistical entity from
**Indian independence and the First Kashmir War (1947)** to the
**Simla Agreement (1972-07-02)**, which formalized the Line of Control
separating Indian-administered Jammu and Kashmir from Pakistani-administered
Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.

This row exists because FAO/IIA yearbook **footnotes** for the years
**1953 and 1960** report data for India and Pakistan that explicitly
**exclude** Jammu and Kashmir, treating the disputed territory as neither
fully attributable to India nor to Pakistan. The footnotes identify the
territory as a boundary ambiguity requiring separate accounting. Without a
distinct WHEP polity for this entity, the FAO/IIA observations for 1953
and 1960 cannot be correctly attributed — they would otherwise be silently
lost or double-counted across the India and Pakistan rows.

The Princely State of Jammu and Kashmir was created in 1846 under the Treaty
of Amritsar and governed by the Dogra dynasty under British suzerainty. At
British Indian independence (1947-08-15), the Maharaja Hari Singh initially
sought independence. Following a Pakistani-backed tribal invasion in October
1947, Hari Singh signed the Instrument of Accession to India on
1947-10-26. Pakistan disputed this accession, leading to the First Kashmir
War (1947–1949). The UN-brokered ceasefire (1949-01-01) left India in
control of approximately two-thirds of the territory (including the Kashmir
Valley, Jammu, and Ladakh) and Pakistan in control of Azad Kashmir and
Gilgit-Baltistan. The Line of Control was not formally demarcated until the
Simla Agreement (1972-07-02), which converted the ceasefire line into a
bilateral boundary.

No ISO 3166 code exists for the disputed territory as a whole; the field is
left blank. COW does not assign this disputed territory a sovereign state
code; the `cow` field references CShapes gwcode 7506 (Jammu and Kashmir,
the Indian-administered portion) for polygon binding purposes only.
Successors: [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md) and
[pak-1949-1971](pak-1949-1971.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` (Schvitz et al. 2022), feature
  `gwcode=7506`, "Jammu and Kashmir", dated `1947` to `1949`. This is the
  **Indian-administered portion** of the disputed territory (~105,844 km²).
  CShapes 2.0 also records a second feature `gwcode=7708`, "Kashmir (North,
  Azad)" covering Pakistani-administered Azad Kashmir (~81,689 km²) for the
  same 1947–1949 period.
- **Method.** `period_proxy` — the CShapes features only cover 1947–1949,
  while this polity row runs 1947–1972. After the 1949 ceasefire the
  territory was absorbed into the CShapes India (gwcode=750) and Pakistan
  (gwcode=770) polygons respectively. The gwcode=7506 feature is the best
  available historical polygon for the Indian-administered zone.
- **Limitation.** The polygon bound here (`gwcode=7506`) represents only the
  Indian-administered portion (~105,844 km²). The full disputed territory
  (Indian-administered J&K + Azad Kashmir + Gilgit-Baltistan) would be
  approximately 187,533 km² (105,844 + 81,689). A composed union of both
  CShapes features would be more accurate but requires construction outside
  the standard binding; this is noted as an open question.
- **Area.** ~105,844 km² (CShapes 2.0 `area` field, gwcode=7506, projected).
  This represents the Indian-administered portion only.
- **Confidence.** Medium. The polygon covers only 1947–1949 and only the
  Indian-administered zone. It is an acceptable proxy for the location and
  rough scale of the territory but does not capture the full extent or the
  post-1949 situation.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** The Princely State of Jammu and Kashmir (1846–1947)
  predates partition. CShapes 2.0 does not cover it; Cliopatria records
  "Kashmir and Jammu" from 1846 to 1947. No WHEP row currently exists for
  the pre-partition Princely State; predecessor field is left blank pending
  creation of that row.
- **Successors.**
  - [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md) — Indian-administered Jammu and
    Kashmir became a state of India; the IND row absorbs this territory from
    the WHEP perspective after the Simla Agreement.
  - [pak-1949-1971](pak-1949-1971.md) — Pakistani-administered Azad Kashmir
    and Gilgit-Baltistan were de facto part of Pakistan's territory covered
    by the PAK row. Successor to PAK-1949-1971 chains to PAK-1971-2025.

## Contradictions

- CShapes 2.0 places this territory under two separate gwcodes (7506 and
  7708) for the 1947–1949 period, then absorbs it into the India (750) and
  Pakistan (770) polygons from 1949 onward. This means there is no single
  CShapes feature spanning 1953 or 1960 (the footnote years), which are the
  primary reason this row exists. The polygon is necessarily a proxy.
- The COW state system does not recognize Jammu and Kashmir as an independent
  state; no COW ccode exists for it. The `cow: 7506` value here is a
  reference to the CShapes non-state gwcode used for polygon lookup only,
  not a COW state-system membership.

## Decisions

- **Polity code.** `KJM-1947-1972` — KJM (Kashmir-Jammu) following the
  convention that ABBR is a sensible 3-letter abbreviation when no ISO3 fits.
  ISO3 is left blank since no international standard code applies to the
  disputed territory as a whole.
- **Period.** Start 1947 (Instrument of Accession / First Kashmir War);
  end 1972 (Simla Agreement, which converted the ceasefire line to the Line
  of Control). Both FAO/IIA footnote years (1953, 1960) fall within this
  window.
- **Consolidation.** The spec calls for consolidating "3 footnote Kashmir
  rows" into one disputed-territory polity. This single KJM row covers the
  full disputed territory (both Indian- and Pakistani-administered zones) as
  the statistical entity that FAO/IIA footnotes treat as distinct from both
  India and Pakistan.
- **Polygon selection.** gwcode=7506 (Jammu and Kashmir, Indian-administered)
  is used as the primary polygon binding because it is the larger portion and
  the one explicitly named in CShapes. The gwcode=7708 portion is documented
  here but cannot be bound simultaneously under the current one-feature-per-
  polity schema. This is an acknowledged limitation.
- **Type.** `disputed` — the territory is neither a recognized sovereign
  state nor a standard colonial dependency, but a disputed princely-state
  accession outcome.
- **Continent.** `Asia` — standard geographic classification for South Asia.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-composed-union

The full disputed territory requires a union of CShapes gwcode=7506 (~105,844
km²) and gwcode=7708 (~81,689 km²) for a total of ~187,533 km². The current
binding (gwcode=7506 only) represents approximately 56% of the full disputed
extent. A constructed composed-union polygon should be built if the build
pipeline supports it, and `polygon_area_km2` updated to ~187,533. Until then,
the 105,844 km² figure should be understood as a partial proxy.

### oq-predecessor-princely-state

The Princely State of Jammu and Kashmir (1846–1947) is recorded in Cliopatria
as "Kashmir and Jammu" (1846–1947 in multiple slices). A WHEP predecessor
row should be created for this entity. When created, KJM-1947-1972's
predecessor field should be updated.

### oq-post-1972-continuation

After the Simla Agreement (1972), the disputed territory continued to be
administered separately by India (as Jammu and Kashmir state, later Union
Territory from 2019) and Pakistan (Azad Kashmir + Gilgit-Baltistan). If
post-1972 data sources also footnote-exclude J&K from India or Pakistan, a
continuation row (e.g., KJM-1972-2025) may be needed. This should be
reviewed when later FAO/IIA data are ingested.

### oq-cow-code

CShapes 2.0 assigns gwcode=7506 to Jammu and Kashmir and gwcode=7708 to
Azad Kashmir, but COW proper does not list either as a system member. The
`cow` field here references gwcode=7506 purely for polygon lookup. If a
future revision of COW or the WHEP schema requires a valid COW state-system
membership code, this field should be set to null and the polygon binding
updated to use an alternative id scheme.
