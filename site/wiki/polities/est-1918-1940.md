# Estonia (interwar republic)

## Summary

The Republic of Estonia as an independent state from its declaration of
independence on 24 February 1918 to Soviet annexation on 6 August 1940. Estonia
declared independence immediately after the German evacuation and Russian
Revolution, fought a War of Independence against both Soviet Russia and the
Baltische Landeswehr, and was recognised by the Soviet Union in the
[Treaty of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Tartu_(Russian%E2%80%93Estonian)) (2 February 1920) [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). Agricultural statistics were compiled by the
Estonian State Statistical Central Bureau throughout the period.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 366, time-step 1918-1940, area 49,295 km². The
1920 Treaty of Tartu defined boundaries (including the Petseri/Pechory area and
the trans-Narva Ingrian region) that remained stable until 1940. The treaty
territory is slightly larger than modern Estonia (~45,000 km²) because Petseri
and Narva areas were transferred to the Russian SFSR in 1944/1945.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 agricultural input data reports
"Estonia" for 1919-1939, consistent with the interwar republic's own statistical
office. The previous wiki chain
([EST-1800-1918](est-1800-1918.md) → [EST-1940-1991](est-1940-1991.md)) left a
gap for this period, causing 427+ input rows to fail matching. This page closes
the chain.
