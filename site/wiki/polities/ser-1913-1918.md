# Serbia (1913-1918)

## Summary

A **6-year row** covering post-Balkan Wars Serbia through WWI.
Serbia gained Kosovo and parts of Macedonia in 1913. Occupied by
Central Powers from late 1915; government-in-exile on Corfu
(1916-1918); Serbian army fought on the Salonika Front. CShapes shows
no area change at 1915 (10.02 sq deg throughout), confirming
occupation was not a territorial change. The 1918 end date aligns with
the formation of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (Yugoslavia)
on 1 December 1918.

Predecessor: [ser-1816-1913](ser-1816-1913.md) -- pre-Balkan Wars Serbia.
Successor: [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) -- Yugoslavia.

## Territorial extent

- **1913: post-Balkan Wars.** Serbia at its
  expanded borders including Kosovo and parts of Macedonia.
- **1915-1918: occupied / government-in-exile.** Serbia was fully
  occupied by Central Powers from late 1915. The government
  operated from Corfu. Serbian forces fought on the Salonika
  (Thessaloniki) Front alongside Allied forces.
- **1918: liberation and dissolution into Yugoslavia.** Allied
  breakthrough on the Salonika Front (September 1918). Serbia
  liberated by November 1918. On 1 December 1918, the Kingdom
  of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes was proclaimed, absorbing Serbia.
