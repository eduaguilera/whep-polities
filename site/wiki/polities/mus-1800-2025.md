# Mauritius (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Mauritius, an island country in the Indian Ocean east of Madagascar, covering 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. "In 1810 the British captured Mauritius and began importing Indians as laborers" (p.380) [biger-1995 &sect;mauritius](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritius); Mauritius became independent on 12 March 1968. Neither transition moved a boundary, so one row carries the whole span.

## Territorial extent

Mauritius: area 788 sq. miles (2,040 sq. km). No land boundaries. Capital: Port Louis. The polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature 590 at its 1886 vintage, back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025; the boundary being the coastline, the back-projection carries no territorial error. What it does not settle is the Chagos Archipelago — see [oq-mauritius-dependencies](#oq-mauritius-dependencies).
