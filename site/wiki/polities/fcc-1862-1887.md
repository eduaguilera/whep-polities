# French Cochinchina (1862-1887)

## Summary

> **`polygon_status` migrated missing -> unassigned (2026-07-29).** `missing` is a legacy value that predates the documented vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md#page-schema), which says not to use it in new pages. It was never migrated, so the manifest's `claims_polygon_status` set omitted it and consumers were told this row does not claim a polygon. This row had no geometry at all, which is exactly what `unassigned` means. See [lint-polygon-status-vocabulary](../log.md#lint-polygon-status-vocabulary). **Superseded 2026-08-17 (issue 155):** a polygon was constructed and the status is now `proxy` — see Territorial extent.


French Cochinchina was a French colony in southern Vietnam, established following the Treaty of Saigon (5 June 1862) in which the Nguyen dynasty ceded three eastern provinces to France. A second treaty in 1874 transferred the remaining three western provinces. Cochinchina was incorporated into French Indochina upon the latter's creation on 17 October 1887.

The three dropped parts are 49.6 km² at 107.5 °E/12.4 °N, 84.9 km² near Nha Trang (109.4 °E) and

**Why the obvious candidate is not used.** Cliopatria's `French Indochina` is the whole federation
at 753,049 km² — 11× this row — and its own successor [fid-1887-1954](fid-1887-1954.md) already
uses it. CShapes 2.0's earliest Indochina-region feature is gwcode 815 from 1887, i.e. it starts
where this row ends.

**Vintage caveat, which is why this is `proxy`.** France took the three eastern provinces in 1862
and the three western ones in 1867, but Cliopatria models the whole loss at its 1859 step break
(the campaign began 1858). So this polygon is the **six-province** Cochinchina for the row's whole
1862–1887 span and overstates 1862–1867. Twenty of the 25 years are six-province, and no source
here can split the halves: Cliopatria's 1868–1869 step measures 234,478 km², i.e. it records no
further reduction at 1867. See oq-fcc-1862-1867-extent.

**`polygon_area_km2` is left null, deliberately.** This page has never declared an area, so the
only number available to put there is what the polygon itself measures — and a declared area copied
off its own polygon is precisely the tautology `validate_polygons`' check A2 counts and pins a
ceiling on. The external ~65,000 km² is stated above as the check instead. `GTO-1884-1920` is the
existing precedent for a constructed `proxy` row carrying no area.

## Open questions

### oq-fcc-1862-1867-extent

The polygon is the six-province Cochinchina for the whole 1862–1887 span, but France held only the
three eastern provinces (Biên Hòa, Gia Định, Định Tường) from 1862 to 1867. Cliopatria puts the
entire loss at its 1859 step break and records no further reduction at 1867 (1859–1867 measures
234,392 km² and 1868–1869 measures 234,478 km²), so the source cannot split the two halves and
neither can GADM, which has no VNM file. Splitting this row at 1867, or clipping the polygon to the
eastern three provinces for the first five years, needs a source that models the 1862 line. Five of
the row's 25 years are affected, and the layer-B rows this row receives are the `indochina` labels
for 1866–1886, only one of which (1866) falls inside those five.
