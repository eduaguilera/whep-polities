# Estonia (Governorate)

## Summary

Tracks Estonia as a Baltic governorate of the Russian Empire from **1800** to **1918**. The Governorate of Estonia (Estland) was one of the Baltic provinces with distinct administrative and statistical identity within Imperial Russia [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [EST-1918-1940](est-1918-1940.md) (interwar Estonia). The Governorate of Estonia had essentially the same territory as the interwar republic.. The Governorate of Estonia covered the northern part of modern Estonia (centred on Reval/Tallinn). CShapes 2.0 tracks Estonia from 1918 independence.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Estonia" separately for 1908-1917. Estonia was a distinct administrative unit (Baltic governorate) within the Russian Empire with its own statistical identity. Previously routed to F228 (Russian Empire), merging Estonian data into the all-Russia polygon.
