# Suriname

## Summary

Suriname is a country on the northern coast of South America. Biger 1995 references Suriname in the context of the Guyana-Venezuela boundary discussion and as one of Brazil's northern neighbors (371 miles; 597 km.) [biger-1995 &sect;suriname](../sources/biger-1995.md#suriname). Suriname was Dutch territory from the mid-seventeenth century, and in the late eighteenth century Suriname and Berbice (now part of Guyana) disputed ownership of land west of the Courantyne River. WHEP tracks this row from 1975 (independence from the Netherlands), with a predecessor row SUR-1954-1975 covering the late colonial period.

## Territorial extent
