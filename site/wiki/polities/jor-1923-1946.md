# Jordan (1923-1946)

## Summary

The Emirate of Transjordan under British mandate, from the 1923 boundary revision that fixed its eastern extent to independence in 1946. The row's start is a genuine territorial event — the emirate roughly doubled in area — and its end is a sovereignty event over an unchanged territory. Between them the borders held: CShapes records no further change, and the measured geometry is identical to the successor row's.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `663` (dependencies=TRUE; status mandate, ruler GBR), time-step **1925**, `polygon_status: assigned`. Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **89,270 km²**. Jordan's conventional area today is 89,342 km², so the polygon is within 0.1% of the modern figure — which is the expected result, since Transjordan's 1923 borders are essentially Jordan's present ones minus the West Bank.

**The 1923 doubling is real and measurable.** [JOR-1920-1923](jor-1920-1923.md) binds the same CShapes feature at time-step **1920** and measures **45,194 km²**; this row measures **89,270 km²** — a gain of **44,076 km², i.e. 98%**. The previous version of this page described the change in square degrees ("4.26 to 8.45 sq deg"), which is a unit that varies with latitude and cannot be compared across polities; the equal-area figures above replace it. At 44,076 km² the change is far above CShapes' 100 × 100 km coding threshold ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)), so it is a genuinely observed CShapes change and not an artefact.

**The 1925 vintage covers 1923-1946 with no in-span change, and the successor proves it.** [JOR-1946-2025](jor-1946-2025.md) binds feature 663 at time-step **1946** and measures **89,270 km² — identical to this row to the square kilometre**. So CShapes coded a *new time-step* at 1946 (independence, a status change) while the *geometry* stayed byte-identical: the transition from mandate to kingdom moved no border. That is exactly the pattern the WHEP polity definition anticipates, and it is why the 1946 row boundary is justified on reporting-scope grounds rather than territorial ones ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)).

**On the 1925 vintage vs. the 1923 row start.** CShapes' step for this feature is dated 1925, not 1923, so the first two years of the row use a polygon vintage-stamped after them. The previous version of this page said the 1925 step had "the same area" as 1923 and merged them on that basis; the merge is right, but the reason is worth stating precisely — CShapes coded a 1925 step for a *status* reason (the mandate's administrative reorganisation) with no geometric consequence, so binding the 1925 geometry to a row starting in 1923 asserts nothing false about 1923-1925. The row's years follow the event and the vintage field keeps recording the source, per [log decision-row-boundary-dating](../log.md#decision-row-boundary-dating).

**Territorial changes inside 1923-1946: none. The row should not be split.** Biger's Transjordan material dates only two things in the vicinity, and both are outside this span: "In 1922 Transjordan was carved from the British mandate over Palestine" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)) — the year before this row starts — and "Following the 1948 war with Israel, Transjordan had ... annexed the West Bank" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)) — two years after it ends, and therefore a change to the *successor* row, not this one. Between 1923 and 1946 Biger names nothing, CShapes codes nothing, and COW records nothing. The 1928 Anglo-Transjordanian Agreement (a grant of partial self-government) is constitutional, not territorial.

**One thing Biger's framing implies that is worth flagging for the data pipeline.** Because Transjordan was "carved from the British mandate over Palestine" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)), a 1920s or 1930s source reporting "Palestine" may or may not include Transjordan. That is a label-scope risk on the Palestine side rather than a territorial question here, but any assertion routing interwar "Palestine" data needs to establish which side of the 1922 carve-out it means. Recorded as [oq-jor-palestine-label-scope](#oq-jor-palestine-label-scope).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [JOR-1920-1923](jor-1920-1923.md) — Transjordan before the boundary revision, measured **45,194 km²**. The boundary between the rows is the 98% expansion described above.
- **Successor:** [JOR-1946-2025](jor-1946-2025.md) — the independent Hashemite Kingdom, measured **89,270 km²**, identical geometry. The row boundary is a status change, not a territorial one; the West Bank annexation that *does* change Jordan's extent falls inside the successor's span (1948-1949), not at its start.

## Key dates

- 1922: Transjordan carved from the British mandate for Palestine ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)). Predates this row.
- 1923-09-29: boundary revision; the emirate's measured area rises from 45,194 to 89,270 km². Start-of-row event. **The day-precision date is uncited** — the year and the fact of the revision are supported by the CShapes step and the area measurement, but no source on disk gives 29 September.
- 1928: Anglo-Transjordanian Agreement grants partial self-government. **Uncited.** Constitutional, not territorial.
- 1946-03-22: Treaty of London; Britain recognises Transjordan's independence. **This is the date COW uses** for `ccode=663`'s tenure start.
- 1946-05-25: Transjordan formally proclaimed a kingdom under Abdullah I. **Uncited.** See *Contradictions* — this and the Treaty of London date compete for "independence".
- 1948-1949: West Bank annexation ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)). Falls in the successor row.

## Sourced claims

- "In 1922 Transjordan was carved from the British mandate over Palestine" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)).
- "Since the area of what is now Jordan never developed ... no specific regional identity until its establishment after World War I by Britain" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)) — Biger's judgement that the entity is a twentieth-century British creation, which is why this row's chain has no pre-1920 predecessor.
- "Following the 1948 war with Israel, Transjordan had ... annexed the West Bank" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)) — out of span, and the reason this row's extent must not be confused with post-1948 Jordan's.
- **COW records `ccode=663` as a single tenure `1946-03-22 → 2024-12-31`** — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). So COW's Jordan begins on the day of the Treaty of London and covers **none** of this row's mandate span; the frontmatter `cow: 663` is a code assignment for the Jordanian family, not a membership claim for 1923-1946, mandates being absent from COW by design ([cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion)).
- CShapes codes "international mandates" as one of its four dependency categories ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)) — the category that makes a polygon available for this row at all, since no independent state existed here to be coded.
- CShapes codes border adjustments only above 100 × 100 km ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)); the 1923 change is 44,076 km², so it is genuinely observed. The corollary is that any in-span change below 10,000 km² — 11% of the emirate — would be invisible to CShapes, which bounds the stability claim.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): this row **89,270 km²**; [JOR-1920-1923](jor-1920-1923.md) **45,194 km²**; [JOR-1946-2025](jor-1946-2025.md) **89,270 km²**. All ESRI:54034 from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`. These replace the square-degree figures the page previously carried, which are not comparable across latitudes.
- Data footprint (this ingest): **73 layer-B rows** route to this code per `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`.

## Contradictions

- **Which date is Transjordan's independence: 1946-03-22 or 1946-05-25?** COW uses **1946-03-22**, the Treaty of London, for `ccode=663`'s tenure start (`statelist2024.csv`). The conventional Jordanian date is **1946-05-25**, when the Hashemite Kingdom was proclaimed and which Jordan observes as Independence Day. Both are defensible: the treaty is when Britain recognised independence, the proclamation is when the kingdom constituted itself. The database's row boundary is the *year* 1946 either way, so nothing downstream turns on it, and both dates are recorded rather than one being silently chosen. Note that COW's day values are sometimes placeholders ([cow-state-system-v2024 §known-limitations](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#known-limitations), limitation 3) — but 22 March is not a January-1 placeholder, so in this case COW's date is a real one.
- **1923 vs 1925 for the boundary revision.** This row starts 1923 while its CShapes polygon is stamped 1925. As set out under *Territorial extent*, the two CShapes steps carry the same geometry, so the discrepancy is one of vintage labelling rather than substance. Recorded here rather than resolved, since resolving it would need the raw CShapes change log.

## Open questions

### oq-jor-palestine-label-scope

Does an interwar source reporting "Palestine" include Transjordan? Biger's "carved from the British mandate over Palestine" ([biger-1995 §jordan](../sources/biger-1995.md#jordan)) means both readings existed in the 1920s administrative record. If any of this row's 73 data rows arrived via a "Palestine"-labelled series, the routing needs checking; conversely, a Palestine series that silently includes Transjordan would double-count against this row. Checkable against the label lexicon (`data/final/source_label_lexicon.csv`) and the stated areas in `data/final/source_stated_areas.csv` — a Palestine label stating ~26,000 km² excludes Transjordan, one stating ~115,000 km² includes it.
