# Zanzibar Protectorate

## Summary

The Zanzibar Protectorate consisted of the islands of Unguja (Zanzibar Island, ~1,660 km²) and Pemba (~980 km²), together approximately 2,650 km² in the Indian Ocean off the East African coast. Britain declared a formal protectorate over the Sultanate of Zanzibar on 4 November 1890, following the Heligoland–Zanzibar Treaty (1 July 1890) by which Germany renounced its claims to the territory in exchange for Heligoland Island. The Sultan retained nominal sovereignty but effective administration was British. Zanzibar gained independence on 10 December 1963, and united with mainland Tanganyika on 26 April 1964 to form Tanzania. "Zanzibar was a British colony 1860–1963." [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)

## Territorial extent

Zanzibar and Pemba are coral and volcanic islands lying roughly 35–50 km off the Tanzanian coast. Total combined area approximately 2,650 km²:

- Unguja (Zanzibar Island): ~1,660 km²
- Pemba Island: ~980 km²

This is the territory reported as "Zanzibar and Pemba" in the FAO 1952 agricultural survey (fao1952 source, 71 rows, 1937–1951). The territory is entirely separate from mainland Tanganyika (~942,000 km²); routing Zanzibar data to TAN-1922-1964 would overstate territorial extent by a factor of ~350.

**Polygon status: unassigned.** CShapes 2.0 covers dependent territories with population ≥250,000. Zanzibar's population in this period was approximately 240,000–300,000 (borderline threshold). It is uncertain whether CShapes 2.0 includes a Zanzibar feature; the feature ID is not yet confirmed. If CShapes 2.0 does not include Zanzibar, GADM (administrative level 0 for Tanzania sub-units) provides island polygons for Unguja and Pemba which could be combined as a composed union. As an island territory with stable coastlines, a constructed estimate from GADM or OpenStreetMap coastal data would closely approximate the historical extent (islands do not have land-border ambiguity). The ~2,650 km² figure is well-established in historical sources.

**Polygon drift note.** The FAO data covers 1937–1951, well within the 1890–1963 span. Island borders are fixed; no polygon vintage drift issue applies.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** The Sultanate of Zanzibar (Omani/Arab sultanate, pre-1890) is not modelled in WHEP. The 1890 start year marks the formal British protectorate declaration.
- **Successor:** [tza-1964-2025](tza-1964-2025.md) — Tanzania, formed 26 April 1964 by union of Zanzibar and Tanganyika.

Note: The union on 26 April 1964 means Zanzibar's end year is 1964 by merger date, but the proposed code ZNZ-1890-1963 uses 1963 reflecting independence (10 December 1963). The period between independence and union (December 1963 – April 1964) is a short gap. See open question below.

## Sourced claims

- "The country [Tanzania] was formed in 1964 as a result of the union of mainland Tanganyika" and the former sultanate of Zanzibar (independent 1963). [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)
- "Zanzibar was a British colony 1860–1963." [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)
- Heligoland–Zanzibar Treaty of 1 July 1890: Germany ceded claims to Zanzibar and Pemba to Britain in exchange for Heligoland. Britain declared a formal protectorate on 4 November 1890.
- FAO 1952 agricultural survey (fao1952) reports 71 rows of data for "Zanzibar and Pemba" covering years 1937–1951, including cattle, cassava, and carcass weight data.
- Zanzibar Revolution of 12 January 1964 overthrew the Sultan and established the People's Republic of Zanzibar, which subsequently merged with Tanganyika.

## Contradictions

**Biger 1895 dates.** Biger states "Zanzibar was a British colony 1860–1963," using 1860 as a start. This refers to informal British influence following the 1861 Sultanate succession crisis, but formal protectorate status dates to 1890 (Heligoland–Zanzibar Treaty). WHEP uses 1890 as the start year for the formal protectorate. The 1860 date in Biger likely reflects decisive British influence over the Sultanate, not formal protectorate declaration.

## Decisions

- Polity created to route fao1952 "Zanzibar and Pemba" data (1937–1951, 71 rows). Territory ~2,650 km² is categorically distinct from mainland Tanganyika (~942,000 km²); no routing to TAN-1922-1964 is appropriate.
- Polygon status set to unassigned pending CShapes 2.0 feature ID confirmation. GADM composed union of Unguja + Pemba is the recommended fallback. Island territory with stable coastlines means any modern coastal polygon is a close proxy for historical extent.
