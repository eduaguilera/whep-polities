# Hyderabad State

## Summary

This row tracks **Hyderabad State** from **1724**, when Qamar-ud-Din Khan
(Nizam-ul-Mulk) established effective autonomy from the Mughal Empire after
the Battle of Shakarkhedla, to **1948**, when India's military Operation Polo
(17–18 September 1948) forcibly annexed the state and it was absorbed into the
Indian Union.

The row exists because FAO/IIA yearbook **footnotes** for the 1953 reference
year report agricultural data for this territory as **included in India** — the
Hyderabad State data were folded into the all-India figures after annexation,
requiring a distinct WHEP entity to account for the territory's pre-annexation
period and its boundary change relative to the IND rows.

Hyderabad was the largest and most populous of the British-era princely states
in the Indian subcontinent, covering the Deccan plateau in south-central India.
Its rulers bore the title Nizam. Under British paramountcy (formalized
progressively from the 1798 Subsidiary Alliance), the state retained internal
autonomy while ceding control of external affairs. At independence of British
India in 1947, the then-reigning Nizam, Mir Osman Ali Khan, attempted to
maintain independence or seek UN recognition. The Indian government concluded
negotiations, then launched Operation Polo in September 1948; the Nizam signed
the Instrument of Accession on 23 November 1948, formally merging Hyderabad
into India.

Post-annexation, Hyderabad State continued as an administrative unit until the
States Reorganisation Act of 1956 dissolved it, dividing its territory among
Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra. The WHEP row ends at annexation
(1948); subsequent data for this territory are covered under
[ind-1947-1949](ind-1947-1949.md) and [ind-1949-2025](ind-1949-2025.md).

No WHEP predecessor row currently exists for the earlier Asaf Jahi dynasty
(from 1724) or for any Mughal-period predecessor; the predecessor field is left
blank pending creation of those entries.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria` (Seshat Global History Databank), feature
  `Name="Hyderabad State"`, FID=14865, temporal slice `FromYear=1947,
  ToYear=1947`. The feature captures the territorial extent of Hyderabad State
  in 1947, immediately prior to and during the accession crisis, which is the
  last stable year before annexation.
- **Method.** `period_proxy` — the polygon covers the final year (1947) of the
  state's independent existence, used as a proxy for the entire 1724–1948
  period. The core territorial extent of Hyderabad State was broadly stable
  from the late 18th century onward; there were no major boundary changes after
  the 1798 Subsidiary Alliance fixed the state's limits. The 1947 polygon is
  therefore a faithful representation of the polity's extent throughout the
  modern data period.
- **Area.** ~211,298 km² (pre-computed `Area` field in the Cliopatria dataset,
  WGS84). This is consistent with historically documented estimates of
  ~214,000 km² for Hyderabad State. The bounding box spans approximately
  74.8°–81.5°E, 15.2°–20.7°N, covering the Deccan Plateau region.
- **Confidence.** Medium-high. The Cliopatria dataset's Hyderabad State feature
  is a direct named match for this entity. The 1947 temporal slice is the best
  available period-proxy within the cliopatria dataset (the dataset has a gap
  from 1799 to 1946 for this entity). The area matches published historical
  figures within ~1%.
- **Cliopatria gap note.** The cliopatria dataset contains Hyderabad State
  features from 1727 to 1798, then jumps directly to 1947. For the WHEP
  polity's start year of 1724, the nearest available polygon is the 1947
  feature; earlier features (1727–1798) show a larger territory (~261,000 km²)
  reflecting the pre-Subsidiary Alliance extent. The 1947 feature (~211,000
  km²) is the more relevant proxy for the British-paramountcy period
  (post-1798) when production data are available.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** No WHEP row for the Asaf Jahi state prior to 1724, the
  Mughal subah of Hyderabad, or any earlier entity currently exists. The
  cliopatria dataset has Hyderabad State features back to 1727, suggesting
  future WHEP entries could be created for the pre-British period. The
  predecessor field is left empty pending those entries.
- **Successor.** [ind-1947-1949](ind-1947-1949.md) — upon annexation in
  September 1948, Hyderabad's territory was absorbed into the Indian Union.
  The IND rows cover the successor state. After 1956 the territory was
  reorganized into three Indian states (Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra)
  but that is a sub-national reorganization within the IND polity.

## Sourced claims

- **Autonomy date (1724).** Nizam-ul-Mulk's appointment as Viceroy of the
  Deccan and his defeat of rivals at the Battle of Shakarkhedla (1724) is
  the conventional founding date of effective Hyderabad State autonomy.
  [Cliopatria FID=14865 Wikipedia field: "Hyderabad State"]
- **British Subsidiary Alliance (1798).** The 1798 treaty with the British
  East India Company formalized British paramountcy, ceding external affairs
  while preserving internal autonomy. This date marks the transition to the
  princely-state era relevant for agricultural data.
- **Operation Polo (1948-09-17/18).** Indian military operation that ended
  Hyderabad State's independence. The Nizam signed the Instrument of Accession
  on 1948-11-23. [end_year=1948]
- **FAO/IIA 1953 footnote.** The 1953 IIA yearbook footnote reports Hyderabad's
  agricultural data as included in India figures, confirming that post-1948
  data were folded into the all-India series. This is the direct provenance for
  the WHEP composed_union entry.
- **Area.** The Cliopatria `Area` field records 211,298 km² for the 1947
  feature. Historical sources commonly cite ~214,000 km²; the difference is
  within normal GIS boundary variation.
- **COW/ISO.** Hyderabad was never a COW system member (princely states under
  British paramountcy were not counted as sovereign). The COW code 750 (India)
  is used following the WHEP convention for colonial/dependent entities.

## Contradictions

- The cliopatria dataset's earlier Hyderabad State features (1727–1798) record
  a territory of ~261,000 km² — significantly larger than the 1947 figure of
  ~211,000 km². This reflects genuine territorial reduction following the 1798
  Subsidiary Alliance and other losses. Using the 1947 polygon as a proxy
  for the full 1724–1948 period therefore slightly underestimates the
  state's extent in its early decades. This is documented as an accepted
  approximation; no single polygon covers the full range.

## Decisions

- **Polygon selection.** The cliopatria `Name="Hyderabad State"` FID=14865
  (1947-1947) was chosen as the best available proxy. CShapes 2.0 does not
  include Hyderabad State (it covers only sovereign states, not British
  princely states). Paine-2024 did not return a Hyderabad match. GADM 4.1
  adm1 could proxy via Telangana/Andhra Pradesh modern states, but this would
  be a highly imprecise modern overlay, less faithful than the historical
  Cliopatria polygon.
- **Start year 1724.** The conventional date of Nizam-ul-Mulk's effective
  autonomy. An alternative start year of 1798 (Subsidiary Alliance) could be
  justified for the British paramountcy period specifically; however 1724 is
  the polity's accepted founding date in historical literature and in the
  cliopatria dataset.
- **End year 1948.** Operation Polo concluded 17–18 September 1948. The
  Instrument of Accession was signed 23 November 1948. The convention here
  is to end the row at the year of effective annexation (1948).
- **type: colonial.** Used to indicate a dependent/sub-sovereign entity
  (British princely state under paramountcy). The `national` type is reserved
  for fully sovereign states; `colonial` is the closest available type for
  a nominally autonomous but paramountcy-constrained princely state.
- **polygon_feature_year: 1947.** The cliopatria FID=14865 feature covers
  FromYear=1947, ToYear=1947. Setting `polygon_feature_year=1947` ensures
  the build's temporal match succeeds (1947 ∈ [1947, 1947]).

## Open questions

### oq-cliopatria-gap-1799-1946

The cliopatria dataset has no Hyderabad State feature between 1799 and 1947.
This likely reflects a gap in the Seshat data compilation rather than a
genuine territorial discontinuity. If cliopatria is updated with intervening
features, the polygon binding should be reviewed to use a feature whose
temporal range more directly overlaps the WHEP data period (1800s–1940s).

### oq-start-year

The start year of 1724 reflects political autonomy. If WHEP data coverage for
Hyderabad only begins in the British-paramountcy era (post-1798 or later),
a start year of 1798 or 1800 may be more appropriate for data-alignment
purposes. This should be reviewed when the production/trade data are mapped
to this polity.

### oq-predecessor

The Asaf Jahi dynasty as Mughal viceroys before 1724 is not covered by any
WHEP row. Cliopatria has Hyderabad State features back to 1727; earlier
features could support a pre-1724 row if needed.

### oq-type

The `colonial` type is an approximation for a princely state. Review whether
a dedicated `princely_state` or `dependent` type exists or should be created
in the WHEP taxonomy.

## Type corrected to `subnational` (2026-07-24)

Was typed `colonial`. Hyderabad was a **princely state under British suzerainty**
— a component of the territory British India administered, not a colony in its
own right — so `subnational` is the accurate description, matching how other
components are typed (e.g. [ser-1918-1945](ser-1918-1945.md) for Serbia within
Yugoslavia).

This also removes a real matching hazard. Sharing `iso3_code: IND` and spanning
**1724-1948**, this row competed with the whole India chain in the matcher's
year-containment step, and at equal type rank the winner was decided by family
ordering rather than by anything meaningful. It was caught when
`IND-1800-1893` was split: with the old row superseded, **36 rows of data labelled
plainly "india" (1885-1892) began routing to Hyderabad** — attributing
all-India figures to a single princely state. Ranking `subnational` below
`national` closes that path, the same failure mode as
[alk-1867-1959](alk-1867-1959.md) (Territory of Alaska shadowing the United
States) fixed earlier the same day.

No data is labelled "Hyderabad" in the layer-B corpus, so this row currently
receives nothing directly.
