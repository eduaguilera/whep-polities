# Konbaung Burma (to 1826)

## Summary

The Konbaung Dynasty at its peak from the 1800 database floor to the Treaty of Yandabo (24 February 1826) ending the First Anglo-Burmese War. Under King Bodawpaya the dynasty expanded westward, acquiring Arakan (1784, before the WHEP window floor), Manipur (1814), and Assam (1817). CSV records the territorial extent at ~798,000 km². The Treaty of Yandabo ceded Arakan, Tenasserim, Assam, and Manipur to British India, shrinking Burma by roughly 31% per CSV notes.

Polygon from Cliopatria (Burma 1822–1824) per CSV. The polygon captures Konbaung's maximum extent including Arakan, Assam, Manipur, and Tenasserim — territory lost in 1826.

## Open questions

### oq-mmr-peripheral-polities

The ceded territories (Arakan, Manipur, Assam) had their own pre-Konbaung polities that are not modelled in WHEP. Assam (the Ahom kingdom) and Manipur (a distinct Meitei polity) in particular had centuries of sovereignty before Konbaung conquest.
