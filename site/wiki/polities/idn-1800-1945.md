# Dutch East Indies (1800-1945)

## Summary

The Dutch East Indies from the WHEP database floor (1800) to the Japanese
surrender and Indonesian declaration of independence (August 1945).

**This row merges the former `IDN-1800-1889` and `IDN-1889-1945`.** Those two rows
were bound to the *same* CShapes feature geometry (1,877,243 km² on both sides of
1889) and no territorial event separated them: CShapes breaks at `1889/06/19 →
`1889/06/20` for a Gleditsch-Ward **state-system status** change, not a boundary
change, and the "1889 Anglo-Dutch boundary treaty" the old page cited **does not
exist** — the Anglo-Dutch Borneo convention was 1891. Under the WHEP rule that
rows split on *territorial* change, the pair was redundant.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 gwcode 850, feature year 1900, **1,877,243 km²** —
  identical across every CShapes time-step from 1886 to 1945.
- **`polygon_status: polygon_vintage_drift`**, because CShapes coverage begins in
  **1886** while this row starts at 1800. The 1800-1886 portion is a
  back-projection, and Dutch control was in fact still consolidating: the Aceh War
  began in 1873 and outer-island "pacification" campaigns ran into the early
  1900s. Early-period data is therefore matched to the territory the Netherlands
  eventually held rather than what it administered at the time. Stated rather
  than implied.
- **Includes western New Guinea**, which was retained by the Netherlands after
  1949 and is held from that year by
  [nng-1949-1963](nng-1949-1963.md) — the 410,361 km² difference between this
  row's area and independent Indonesia's 1,466,882 km².

## Sourced claims

- **1824: Anglo-Dutch Treaty of London.** Divided the archipelago into Dutch and
  British spheres, the boundary underlying this territory
  [biger-1995](../sources/biger-1995.md).
- **1873: Aceh War begins.** Dutch authority over the outer islands was still
  being established well after this row's start
  [biger-1995](../sources/biger-1995.md).
- **1891: Anglo-Dutch Borneo convention.** The actual boundary agreement, three
  years *after* the spurious 1889 split point
  [biger-1995](../sources/biger-1995.md).
- **1945-08-17: Indonesian declaration of independence.** End-of-row event
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
- **CShapes coverage.** gwcode 850, geometry constant 1886-1945
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
