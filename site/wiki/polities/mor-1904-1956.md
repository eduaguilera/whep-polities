# Morocco (1904-1956)

## Summary

Morocco under French-Spanish sphere-of-influence arrangements from the 1904 Entente Cordiale (which set out French and Spanish spheres) to the 1956 end of both protectorates and the reunification under independent Morocco. The Treaty of Fez (30 March 1912) formally established the French Protectorate; the 27 November 1912 Franco-Spanish convention established the Spanish Protectorate. The French Protectorate ended on 2 March 1956; the Spanish northern zone was returned on 7 April 1956.

## Contradictions

**Parallel SMO chain.** WHEP has both MOR-1904-1956 (implicitly covering the full Moroccan territory) and SMO-1912-1956 (Spanish Protectorate zone). Since 1912 the territory has been administratively bifurcated (French + Spanish + plazas de soberanía + Ifni + Western Sahara), so a single MOR row for 1904–1956 overstates territorial continuity. ## Open questions

### oq-mor-smo-overlap

The MOR-1904-1956 and SMO-1912-1956 rows overlap spatially and temporally. A cleaner model would either (a) shrink MOR-1904-1956 to the French zone only (renaming it MOR-FRA-1912-1956 or similar), or (b) remove SMO as a separate row and treat Spanish Morocco as a subnational annotation on MOR.
