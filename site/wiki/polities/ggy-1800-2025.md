# Guernsey (Bailiwick of Guernsey)

## Summary

The Bailiwick of Guernsey, "a self-governing British Crown Dependency off the coast of Normandy,
France, comprising several islands of the Channel Islands", with "a total land area of 78 square
kilometres". It comprises "three separate jurisdictions: Alderney, Guernsey (incorporating Herm), and
Sark". With [jey-1800-2025](jey-1800-2025.md) it makes up [chi-1800-2025](chi-1800-2025.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §guernsey](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#guernsey)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british guernsey` reports the bailiwick for 1928-1932 to 1960 (**27 (year, label)
pairs**). Only the joint Channel Islands row existed. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** GADM 4.1 adm0 `GGY` (Guernsey with Alderney, Sark and Herm) measures **88.5 km²** (ESRI:54034),
against the stated 78 km² of land; GADM's feature carries the intertidal coast.
