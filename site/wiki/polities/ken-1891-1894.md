# Kenya (1891-1894)

## Summary

The pre-Protectorate East Africa under Imperial British East Africa Company administration from a 1891 CShapes boundary cut to 1894 (preceding the 1 July 1895 formal proclamation of the East Africa Protectorate). The IBEAC had obtained a royal charter in 1888 and was the effective administrator of the territory through the 1890s; the 1895 Foreign Office transfer (modelled in [ken-1894-1902](ken-1894-1902.md)) ended company rule.
