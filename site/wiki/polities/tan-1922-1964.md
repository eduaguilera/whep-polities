# Tanzania (1922-1964)

## Summary

Tanganyika Territory under the League of Nations Mandate (from 1922) and later UN Trusteeship (from 1946), administered by Britain until independence on 9 December 1961 and union with Zanzibar on 26 April 1964. "The country was formed in 1964 as a result of the union of mainland Tanganyika" and the former sultanate of Zanzibar [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania). Tanganyika was a British mandate then trustee territory; independent in 1961 [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
