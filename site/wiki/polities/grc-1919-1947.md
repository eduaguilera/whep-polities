# Greece (1919-1947)

## Summary

This row tracks Greece for **28 years**, from the post-WWI settlements
(Treaty of Neuilly, 27 November 1919) to the Italian cession of the
Dodecanese Islands (Paris Peace Treaty, 10 February 1947, effective
15 September 1947). The row was split from GRC-1919-2025 at 1947
because the Dodecanese acquisition is a documented territorial change:
~2,714 km² (~2% of metropolitan area) transferred from
[ITAEG-1912-1947](itaeg-1912-1947.md) to Greece.

Predecessor: [grc-1913-1919](grc-1913-1919.md) -- post-WWI settlements.
Successor: [grc-1947-2025](grc-1947-2025.md) -- post-Dodecanese Greece.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0`, feature `cowcode=350`,
  `polygon_feature_year=1919` (CShapes feature start date 1919-11-27).
  This feature correctly excludes the Dodecanese Islands, which were
  under Italian sovereignty (ITAEG) throughout this period. No
  approximation is needed: the polygon faithfully represents the
  territorial scope of GRC-1919-1947.
- **Area.** ~129,479 km² (CShapes 2.0, cowcode=350, 1919-11-27 feature).
  Excludes Dodecanese (~2,714 km²). Does not include Western Thrace until
  post-Lausanne settlement (confirmed by Treaty of Lausanne 1923).
- **COW code 350**. COW lists Greece as continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [grc-1913-1919](grc-1913-1919.md) -- Treaty of Neuilly
  (27 November 1919) settled the Bulgarian boundary and transferred Western
  Thrace to Greece; this is the start-of-row event.
- **Successor:** [grc-1947-2025](grc-1947-2025.md) -- Italian cession of
  the Dodecanese (Paris Peace Treaty, 1947) expanded Greek territory by
  ~2,714 km² (~2%); this territorial change justifies the split.

## Sourced claims

- **1919: Treaty of Neuilly.** Settled the Bulgarian boundary
  (27 November 1919). Western Thrace transferred to Greece. Start of row.
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece)
- **1923: Treaty of Lausanne.** Concluded the Greco-Turkish War
  (1919-1922). Major population exchange with Turkey (~1.5 million
  Greeks from Anatolia, ~500,000 Turks from Greece). Defined the
  modern Greek-Turkish border.
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece)
- **1941-1944: WWII Axis occupation.** Greece occupied by Germany, Italy,
  and Bulgaria. Occupation does not alter WHEP territorial extent for
  the recognised Greek state.
- **1946-1947: Greek Civil War begins.** Territorial scope unaffected.
- **1947: Dodecanese cession.** Italy ceded the Dodecanese Islands to
  Greece (Paris Peace Treaties, 10 February 1947, effective 15 September
  1947). This territorial change is the end-of-row event and the reason
  for the split.
  [biger-1995 §greece](../sources/biger-1995.md#greece)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 350, continuous
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Decisions

- **Split from GRC-1919-2025 at 1947.** The Dodecanese acquisition
  (~2,714 km², ~2% of metropolitan area) is a documented territorial
  change under the WHEP territorial-change rule. The polygon for this
  pre-1947 row (CShapes 1919-11-27 feature, ~129,479 km²) correctly
  excludes the Dodecanese and requires no approximation.
- **CShapes 2.0 polygon selection.** CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=350) has two
  features for modern Greece: 1919-11-27 and 1944-10-13. Both share
  identical geometry (~129,479 km²); CShapes does not record the
  Dodecanese transfer. The 1919-11-27 feature is used for this row.
  Because both CShapes features exclude the Dodecanese, this polygon is
  geometrically correct for the pre-1947 period (no drift).

  change rule.
