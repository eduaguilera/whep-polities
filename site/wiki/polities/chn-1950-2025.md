# China (PRC)


> **Polygon corrected 2026-08-05 (issues 51, 59): it was 1.81 million km² too small, for
> seventy-five years.**
>
> `polygon_feature_year` was `1949`, which selects CShapes gwcode 710's **1949-1950** step —
> the People's Republic at the moment of its founding, before Tibet. This row covers
> **1950-2024**.
>
> ```
> row 550   1949-1950    7,557,665 km²   <- was bound here
> row 551   1950-2019    9,369,260 km²   <- correct
> China's actual area   ~9,596,960 km²   (the correct step is within 2.4%)
> ```
>
> So the row understated China by **21%** — about the area of Tibet plus a margin — across
> the entire period for which FAOSTAT reports Chinese agriculture. On absolute area this is
> the largest polygon error found in this database.
>
> **How it was found, which is the part worth keeping.** Not by an area check: this row
> declared no `polygon_area_km2`, so `validate_polygons`' comparison — opt-in via that field
> — never looked at it (issue 59, the fourth defect that blind spot hid this session). It was
> found by a *relational* signal instead: `CHN-1949-1950` and `CHN-1950-2025` had
> **byte-identical geometry** because they shared the same `polygon_feature_year`, while
> CShapes offered a differently-sized step covering 1950. That is the same signature as
> `SNI-1906-1913` in issue 51.
>
> Identical geometry alone is *not* a defect — 115 consecutive pairs in this database share
> geometry and most are correct, because decolonisation usually preserves borders. The
> discriminating condition is identical geometry **plus a differently-sized step available
> for the later row**, which narrows 115 to 8.
>
> `polygon_feature_year: 1950` resolves unambiguously: both steps contain 1950 but only row
> 551 *starts* in it, so `find_feature`'s exact-start rule has a single match and the binding
> does not depend on shapefile order.
## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as China (PRC) from **1950** to
**2025** -- a **76-year row** covering the People's Republic of China
from territorial consolidation (Tibet, Hainan) to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code **NA** in
the CSV [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv), but should
be **710** -- see [oq-cow-code-na](#oq-cow-code-na).

Key periods within this row:
- **1950-1953**: Korean War, Tibet incorporation, land reform.
- **1958-1962**: Great Leap Forward.
- **1966-1976**: Cultural Revolution.
- **1978+**: Deng Xiaoping's reform and opening-up policy.
- **1997**: Hong Kong returned from the United Kingdom (end of
  99-year New Territories lease + retrocession of Hong Kong Island
  and Kowloon).
- **1999**: Macau returned from Portugal.

The 1997 and 1999 returns are real territorial gains but do not
correspond to CSV splits -- the row runs continuously to 2025. Under
the WHEP polity-definition rule, these are minor additions to an
already-massive polity (Hong Kong ~1,100 km², Macau ~33 km²) and
do not meet any reasonable threshold for a split.

Predecessor per CSV: [chn-1949-1950](chn-1949-1950.md). Successor per
CSV: NA (current).

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-na

The CSV has `cow_code = NA` for this row
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW `statelist2024.csv`
records China as `CHN, ccode=710` continuously from 1860-01-01 to
2024-12-31. All earlier CHN rows in the CSV correctly use `cow_code = 710`.
The NA on this row is an omission. **Proposed fix:** set `cow_code = 710`.

### oq-taiwan-status

The ROC/PRC territorial dispute is unresolved. After 1949, the ROC
controlled Taiwan while the PRC controlled the mainland. The CSV
tracks CHN rows as the mainland entity. Taiwan (TWN) may have its
own row in the CSV. The territorial overlap question -- whether
"China" in 1950-2025 includes Taiwan in its polygon -- depends on
the CShapes polygon definition. CShapes typically assigns Taiwan to
the ROC/TWN entity post-1949. This affects territorial coverage
accounting for the China chain.
