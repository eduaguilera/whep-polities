# New Zealand

## Summary

New Zealand from the Treaty of Waitangi (1840) to the present. This row was formed by merging NZL-1840-1907 and NZL-1907-2025, because CShapes records identical area (~29.15 sq deg) before and after the 1907-09-01 transition from colony to dominion. "New Zealand underwent various waves of settlement before officially becoming part of the British Empire in the 1840s" and "The 1840 Treaty of Waitangi between the British and most major Maori chiefs acknowledged, without really protecting, Maori land rights." [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand). New Zealand gained dominion status in 1907 and full sovereignty under the Statute of Westminster (adopted 1947). New Zealand joined the South Pacific Commission as a founder member in 1947 and administered the UN Trust Territory of Western Samoa until its independence in 1962 [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0; cowcode 920. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- **CShapes entries.** The earliest CShapes entry for cowcode 920 begins 1886-01-01 with area ~29.15 sq deg. The transition from colony to dominion is marked by a CShapes date split at 1907-09-01, but the area remains identical (~29.15 sq deg) across the split and all subsequent entries through 2019.
- **Pre-1886.** CShapes does not cover before 1886. The polygon is back-projected from the 1886 CShapes entry for the 1840-1885 period.
- **Area.** ~103,562 sq miles (268,122 sq km) -- an island state with no land boundaries. [biger-1995 §new-zealand](../sources/biger-1995.md#new-zealand)
- **Boundaries.** No land boundaries (island state).
- **Polygon vintage approximation (pre-1907).** The polygon is vintaged at 1907. For production/trade data matched in 1850-1906, this is a back-projection: New Zealand's main-island geography (North, South, Stewart Islands) was territorially stable from the 1860s onward with no cessions or annexations of the main islands. The approximation is physically minor for this period. Direction: no systematic over- or under-count for mainland NZ data before 1907.
- **Polygon under-representation after 1901-1926 (administered Pacific territories).** The CShapes polygon covers mainland New Zealand (~268,122 sq km) only. It does NOT include NZ-administered Pacific territories acquired after 1901: Cook Islands (1901, ~240 sq km land), Niue (1901, ~260 sq km), Western Samoa (League mandate 1920, ~2,842 sq km), and Tokelau (1926, ~12 sq km). These territories total ~3,354 sq km (~1.25% of mainland NZ). Their omission creates a minor spatial under-representation of NZ's administered area after 1926. For production and trade data whose geographic scope is mainland NZ alone this has no effect. For data rows explicitly referencing NZ-administered territories, the polygon under-represents the spatial extent but the magnitude is negligible for most economic aggregates.

## Decisions

Merged NZL-1840-1907 + NZL-1907-2025 into NZL-1840-2025 (2026-04-13). CShapes records identical polygon areas (~29.15 sq deg) across the 1907 colony-to-dominion transition; no territorial change at the 1907-09-01 split point.

**Cook Islands trade rows (2026-06-25).** The Federico-Tena dataset includes 21 rows labelled "trade with cook islands" for 1928-1938 under the New Zealand country entry. These represent NZ-perspective bilateral trade statistics (NZ as the reporting polity trading with its administered Cook Islands territory), not Cook Islands domestic statistics. They are correctly assigned to NZL-1840-2025. The Cook Islands had been under NZ administration since 1901 and did not maintain independent trade statistics in this period; UK Colonial Statistical Yearbooks record Cook Islands data as part of British Settlements Oceania only up to 1901. No re-routing required.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1886-polygon

The 1840-1885 period is outside CShapes' scope. The polygon is back-projected from the 1886 entry. The actual territorial extent during 1840-1885 may have differed from 1886 (particularly regarding Maori-controlled land vs. Crown land). A pre-1886 atlas source is needed to verify.

### oq-dominion-date

Biger does not mention the 1907 dominion transition specifically; further sources should confirm the exact date of New Zealand's transition from colony to dominion.

### oq-pacific-territory-polygons

No separate WHEP polity rows currently exist for Cook Islands (post-1901), Niue (post-1901), or Tokelau (post-1926) under NZ administration. If future data ingest includes production or trade data specifically attributed to these territories (not NZ-perspective bilateral trade), dedicated polity rows with their own polygons should be created. The Federico-Tena "British Settlements Oceania" entry covers Cook Islands 1893-1901 only (before NZ annexation).
