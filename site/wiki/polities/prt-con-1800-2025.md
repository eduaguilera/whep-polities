# Mainland Portugal

## Summary

Continental Portugal, which "comprises the bulk of the Portuguese Republic, namely that part on the
Iberian Peninsula ..., having approximately 95% of the total population and 96.6% of the country's
land". "Continental Portugal is divided into 18 districts", and it is
[prt-1800-2025](prt-1800-2025.md) less the islands, [prt-azm-1800-2025](prt-azm-1800-2025.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §mainland-portugal](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#mainland-portugal)

## Why this entry exists

The label `portugal: mainland` reports the continent alone for 1909-1913 to 1945 (**37 (year,
label) pairs**), beside `portugal` and `portugal: madeira`. Routing it to Portugal would put the
continent on the whole it is 96.6% of. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the 18 district features of GADM 4.1 adm1 (every `PRT` adm1 feature but the Azores
and Madeira). The union measures **88,756 km²** (ESRI:54034). The existing district rows
(`PRT-AV-1835-2025` and siblings) are its parts.

## Predecessors and successors

None: the territory is continuous through the span.

## Data routing

`portugal: mainland` (whep-normalize), 1909-1945: 37 pairs, by alias. Nothing in layer B routes here: its labels for this ground are the whole-country ones.

## Decisions

- **Typed `aggregate`, with no container edge**: the eighteen district rows (`PRT-AV-1835-2025` and
  siblings) and the post-1986 NUTS regions already sit inside Portugal, and this row is exactly their
  union. Declared as a second member of Portugal it would make every district overlap a sibling;
  as an aggregate it is what it is, the districts summed.
- **Span 1800-2025**: the only change to the continental frontier in the span is the 1801 cession
  of Olivenza (about 430 km²) to Spain, a century before the first labelled year, so one row with the
  modern districts stands for the whole span.
