# Laos (1893-1953)

## Summary

Laos (1893-1953) covers the French colonial period in Laos, from the establishment of French control in 1893 until Laotian independence on 22 October 1953. This row was formed by merging LAO-1893-1896 and LAO-1896-1953, because CShapes records identical areas for both the 1893-1896 and 1896-1953 time-steps (approximately 196,820 km²), indicating that the 1896 step reflects an administrative or diplomatic event rather than a genuine territorial change. Laos was administered as part of French Indochina; its northern boundary with China was established by the 1885 Treaty of Tientsin.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records Laos as a French colony (owner=220) throughout this period. The polygon covers approximately 196,820 km² and is consistent across all CShapes time-steps from 1893 to 1953. The China-Laos boundary "was established between France and China by the Treaty of Peace and Commerce (Treaty of Tientsin) of 1885." [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None (CShapes coverage of French Laos begins 1893)
- **Successor:** [LAO-1954-2025](lao-1954-2025.md) — Laotian independence (23 October 1953 in CShapes, 1954 in WHEP CSV)

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 records Laos as a French colony from 1893 through 1953, with a date-boundary at 1896-01-15 carrying identical area (~196,820 km²) to the preceding step; independence is recorded from 1953-10-23. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)
- The China-Laos boundary was established between France and China by the Treaty of Peace and Commerce (Treaty of Tientsin) of 1885. [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)
- "In 1954 Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam were created as independent states and the French colonial boundary was adopted as the northern boundary." [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)
- CShapes records Laos with COW code 812; COW includes Laos as independent from 1953 onward. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states)

## Contradictions

The WHEP CSV assigns end_year=1953 to LAO-1893-1953 and start_year=1954 to LAO-1954-2025. CShapes records the independence transition on 1953-10-23, with a new CShapes entry on 1953-10-23 (status=independent). There is a one-year discrepancy between CShapes (independence 1953) and the WHEP CSV successor start (1954, creating a gap). This should be audited.

## Decisions

Merged LAO-1893-1896 + LAO-1896-1953 into LAO-1893-1953 (2026-04-13). CShapes records identical polygon areas for the 1893-1896 and 1896-1953 time-steps; no territorial change at the 1896-01-15 split point was documented.

## Open questions

### oq-independence-year-gap

CShapes records Laotian independence on 1953-10-23, but the WHEP CSV successor LAO-1954-2025 starts in 1954. The one-year gap (1953-1954) is unaccounted for. A future audit should determine whether a row covering 1953-10-23 to 1953-12-31 is missing, or whether the WHEP convention is to round independence to the following calendar year.
