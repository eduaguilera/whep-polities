# Serbia (including Kosovo)

## Summary

> **Kept in preference to the duplicate `SER-2006-2008`, retired 2026-08-05 (issue 43).**
> Both rows were live over 2006-2007. This one is correct on every axis: real ISO3,
> correct COW, a polygon binding that resolves to Serbia rather than to Serbia and
> Montenegro, a declared area within 0.3% of the geometry, and all 68,875 observed
> FAOSTAT rows for area 272. See [ser-2006-2008](ser-2006-2008.md) for the comparison.

This row covers the Republic of Serbia between the dissolution of the
State Union of Serbia and Montenegro (June 2006) and Kosovo's declaration
of independence (February 2008). During this window Serbia's reporting
extent still included Kosovo, making it ~12% larger than the successor
SRB-2008-2025 — a genuine territorial period, not a status footnote.
Without it, FAOSTAT Serbia data for 2006-2007 had no covering period.

## Territorial extent

Serbia proper plus Kosovo. CShapes 2.0 **gwcode 340** carries this exact
configuration for 2006-2008; at vintage 2007 the polygon measures 87,745 km2
recorded, 87,993 km2 measured on the shipped GeoPackage (0.3%). Serbia without
Kosovo is 77,183 km2 in the same source.

(Corrected 2026-08-05: this section previously said "cowcode 345". The
`polygon_feature_id` field has always been 340 and is right — `sources.yaml` gives
CShapes 2.0 an `id_column` of `gwcode`, and in Gleditsch-Ward 340 is Serbia while
**345 is Yugoslavia**. The retired duplicate `SER-2006-2008` was bound to 345 and so
drew Serbia and Montenegro; see issue 43.)

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **272 "Serbia"** reports from 2006: 2006-2007
routes here, 2008 onward to SRB-2008-2025
(`pipelines/faostat-era-matching`, iso3 SRB; shared boundary years route
to the successor). Whether FAOSTAT's post-2008 Serbia series still
includes Kosovo de facto is a data question (FAOSTAT has no separate
Kosovo area); see open question.

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 cowcode 345 at vintage 2007 — exact historical
  GIS for this configuration (highest-priority polygon source).

## Open questions

### oq-kosovo-in-post-2008-series

FAOSTAT carries no Kosovo reporting area; if Serbia's post-2008 numbers
in some domains still cover Kosovo, the SRB-2008-2025 polygon understates
that data's territory. Needs a data-magnitude check (e.g. wheat area
around 2008) before any routing change.
