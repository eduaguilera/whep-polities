# Eritrea (Italian coastal, 1885-1889)

## Summary

This row covers **5 years** of early Italian colonial presence on
the Red Sea coast. Italy occupied Massawa in February 1885 after
Egyptian withdrawal. The territory was a narrow coastal strip of
approximately 1.59 sq deg (~14,000 km²) according to CShapes. The
row ends with the Treaty of Wuchale (2 May 1889), which recognized
Italian possession of inland territories, and the formal
proclamation of the Colony of Eritrea (1 January 1890).

Predecessor: NA (Ottoman/Egyptian coastal control).
Successor: [eri-1889-1952](eri-1889-1952.md) — Treaty of Wuchale,
6x territorial expansion.
