# Côte d'Ivoire (1947-1960)

## Summary

The French colony of Ivory Coast in its final colonial configuration, from the reconstitution of Upper Volta on 4 September 1947 (which detached territory previously administered as part of Ivory Coast) until independence on 7 August 1960. "The Cote d'Ivoire gained independence from the French in August 1960" [biger-1995 ivory-coast](../sources/biger-1995.md#ivory-coast). During the decolonization period, "the Baule were the most active ethnic group in politics" and Felix Houphouet-Boigny founded the PDCI in 1946, leading the country to peaceful independence [biger-1995 ivory-coast](../sources/biger-1995.md#ivory-coast). CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).

## Territorial extent

The 1947 start date reflects the detachment of Upper Volta territory, which had been administered as part of Ivory Coast since 1932.
