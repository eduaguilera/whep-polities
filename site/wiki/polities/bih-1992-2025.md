# Bosnia and Herzegovina (1992-2025)

## Summary

Independent Bosnia and Herzegovina from Yugoslav dissolution to the
present [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~51,000 km². Bosnian War 1992-1995. Dayton Agreement established
two-entity structure (Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika
Srpska). Stable borders.

Predecessor: [f248-1991-1992](f248-1991-1992.md). Successor: NA (still
active).

## Sourced claims

- **1992-03-01: Independence declared.** Bosnia and Herzegovina declared
  independence following referendum.
  [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia)
- **1992-1995: Bosnian War.** Devastating conflict among Bosniaks,
  Serbs, and Croats.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1995-12-14: Dayton Agreement.** Ended the Bosnian War. Established
  the current two-entity political structure.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **Biger: Ottoman conquest.** "[Ottoman] conquest of the country
  (1461–1483) and its incorporation into the empire"
  [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia) (p.85).
- **Biger: 1878 Austro-Hungarian rule.** "the great Balkan crisis of
  1875–1878, following which Bosnia-Herzegovina was put under
  Austro-Hungarian administration"
  [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia) (p.85).
- **Biger: Bosnia-Serbia boundary.** "The boundary between Bosnia and
  Serbia runs for 328 miles (527 km)"
  [biger-1995 §bosnia](../sources/biger-1995.md#bosnia) (p.87).
