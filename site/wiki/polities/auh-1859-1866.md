# Austrian Empire (1859-1866)

## Summary

This row covers **8 years** between the loss of Lombardy (1859)
and the loss of Venetia (1866). The Austro-Prussian War (1866)
resulted in Austria's exclusion from German affairs and the loss
of Venetia (~25,000 km²) to Italy. Biger calls 1866 "the real
territorial-political turning point."

Predecessor: [auh-1800-1859](auh-1800-1859.md) — loss of Lombardy.
Successor: [auh-1866-1908](auh-1866-1908.md) — loss of Venetia
and Austro-Hungarian Compromise (1867).
