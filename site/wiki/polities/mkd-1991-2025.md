# North Macedonia (1991-2025)

## Summary

Independent North Macedonia (formerly Macedonia) from Yugoslav
dissolution to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

~26,000 km². Peaceful secession from Yugoslavia. Long-running name
dispute with Greece resolved 2019 (Prespa Agreement — renamed from
"Macedonia" to "North Macedonia"). Stable borders.

Predecessor: [f248-1920-1991](f248-1920-1991.md) and
[f248-1991-1992](f248-1991-1992.md) (dual predecessor — declared
independence September 1991 during the transitional period). Successor:
NA (still active).

## Sourced claims

- **1991-09-08: Independence declared.** Macedonia declared independence
  from Yugoslavia via referendum. Peaceful secession.
  [biger-1995 §macedonia](../sources/biger-1995.md#macedonia)
- **2001: Insurgency.** Brief ethnic Albanian insurgency. Ohrid
  Framework Agreement.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **2019-02-12: Prespa Agreement.** Name dispute with Greece resolved.
  Country renamed from "Macedonia" to "North Macedonia."
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **Biger: Ottoman to Serbian.** "Macedonia was a part of the Ottoman
  Empire until 1912, but became part of Serbia in 1913 as a result
  of the Balkan War"
  [biger-1995 §macedonia](../sources/biger-1995.md#macedonia) (p.367).
- **Biger: Yugoslav republic.** "the Communist regime in Yugoslavia
  under Tito established Macedonia as one of the constituent
  republics of Yugoslavia"
  [biger-1995 §macedonia](../sources/biger-1995.md#macedonia) (p.367).
- **Biger: Greek name dispute.** "the new state had problems gaining
  international recognition, with the Greeks officially objecting to
  the use of the name Macedonia"
  [biger-1995 §macedonia](../sources/biger-1995.md#macedonia) (p.367).
