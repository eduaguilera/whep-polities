# Mauritania (1960-1975)

## Summary

Independent Mauritania in its initial 1960-1975 territorial configuration. "The Mauritanians became independent from colonial rule by the French in November 1960" [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania). CShapes records area 89.91 sq deg for the period 1960-11-28 to 1975-11-13 (COW 435, status=independent). The 1975 end date corresponds to the partition of Spanish Sahara (Western Sahara) between Mauritania and Morocco, which increased Mauritania's area to 98.49 sq deg. "In 1976 Spain handed its Spanish Sahara territory over to Morocco and Mauritania" [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 89.91 sq deg for the period 1960-11-28 to 1975-11-13, status=independent, owner=Mauritania (COW 435). Mauritania: area 395,956 sq mi [1,025,130 sq km] [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania). The 1975 expansion reflects Mauritania's acquisition of the southern third of Spanish Sahara (Tiris al-Gharbiyya).
