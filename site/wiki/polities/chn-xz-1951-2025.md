# Tibet (within the People's Republic of China)

## Summary

Tibet after "Central Tibet was annexed by the People's Republic of China (PRC) after the government of Tibet signed the Seventeen Point Agreement which the 14th Dalai Lama ratified on 24 October 1951". The de facto independent period is [tib-1913-1950](tib-1913-1950.md), on the same polygon. [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §tibet](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#tibet)

## Why this entry exists

The label `tibet` runs from 1934-1938 to 1960; its **8 (year, label) pairs from 1951** had no polity, because TIB-1913-1950 ends at the annexation. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
