# Saint Vincent (to 1833)

## Summary

Saint Vincent from the 1800 database floor to the 1833 WHEP boundary (immediately before the 1834 abolition of slavery in the British West Indies). The British had consolidated control after the Second Carib War of 1795–1797 (deporting c.5,000 Black Caribs / Garifuna to Roatán / Honduras / Belize); the Treaty of Paris 1763 had already confirmed British possession after the Seven Years' War. The 1833 WHEP end year does not match a specific territorial or sovereignty event in the Wikipedia article.

## Contradictions

**1833 is not a named political event on the Wikipedia article.** The British Slavery Abolition Act 1833 (royal assent 28 August 1833, effective 1 August 1834) may be the intended referent, but this is a labour-system change rather than a territorial or sovereignty change. Under the WHEP territorial-economic-unit rule, VCT-1800-1833 and VCT-1833-2025 should arguably be one row.
