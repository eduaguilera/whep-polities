# Aden Settlement (within British India)

## Summary

The port of Aden under British India, before it became [adc-1937-1967](adc-1937-1967.md). "For
nearly a century following the 1839 capture of the port of Aden by forces of the East India Company
..., the town and its immediately surrounding area were under British rule and known as the Aden
Settlement. This area became a dependency (a semi-exclave) of the distant Bombay Presidency". From
1932 to 1937 it was the Chief Commissioner's Province of Aden, and "The Aden Protectorate remained
unaffected by this change": the hinterland is [ade-1839-1967](ade-1839-1967.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §aden-settlement](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#aden-settlement)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british aden` runs from 1909 to 1960 beside `british aden protectorate` (1937-1960).
From 1937 it is the colony, ADC-1937-1967; its **32 (year, label) pairs before 1937** had no polity,
because ADC begins at the 1937 separation from British India although the port did not move. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** The polygon is ADC-1937-1967's constructed feature (GADM 4.1 YEM districts of Aden, Little Aden,
Perim and the Kuria Muria islands, **296 km²**, ESRI:54034). **Proxy**: the settlement is the same
port, but Little Aden and the islands were attached to it at different dates, so the polygon may
overstate the settlement in its early decades; the labelled years are all 1909-1936. The colleague
also lists `british perim` separately, so for those years the polygon's Perim (13 km²) is counted in
two labels' territory.
