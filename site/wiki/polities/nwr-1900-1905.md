# Northwestern Rhodesia (1900-1905)

## Summary

Northwestern Rhodesia was a territory administered by the British South Africa Company (BSAC) from 1900 to 1905 in what is now the northwestern part of Zambia. The territory was created when the BSAC's operations in Northern Rhodesia were administratively split between Northwestern Rhodesia (Barotseland and surrounding areas) and Northeastern Rhodesia. In 1911, the two were merged into Northern Rhodesia. The 1905 end date in the database corresponds to a CShapes boundary change. "In 1923 Southern Rhodesia became a white-dominated, self-governing colony" [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe), reflecting the broader Rhodesian colonial trajectory.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. The territory encompassed the western and northwestern portions of modern Zambia, including Barotseland (the Lozi kingdom's territory under the Lochner Concession of 1890). The eastern boundary with Northeastern Rhodesia roughly followed the Kafue River system.
