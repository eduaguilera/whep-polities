# Hungary (1944-1947)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **~3 years**, from the Soviet occupation and
forced reversion of annexations through the Treaty of Paris.

From October 1944 Soviet forces occupied Hungary and the enlarged borders
(post-Second Vienna Award) became non-functional. The territory contracted
progressively back toward the Trianon baseline. The Treaty of Paris
(10 February 1947) formally declared the Vienna Awards null and void and
reinstated the Trianon boundary. This polity covers the transition from
the collapsed WWII-expanded Hungary to the restored Trianon Hungary; it
is succeeded immediately by HUN-1947-2025.

Predecessor: [hun-1940-1944](hun-1940-1944.md) — Soviet occupation reversion.
Successor: [hun-1947-2025](hun-1947-2025.md) — Treaty of Paris restores Trianon.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, cowcode=310, polygon vintage
  1947-02-10. This is the post-Treaty-of-Paris Trianon boundary.
- **Area.** ~93,033 km² (Trianon borders). Bounds: Slovakia (420 mi),
  Ukraine (64 mi), Romania (275 mi), Serbia (103 mi), Croatia (204 mi),
  Slovenia (63 mi), Austria (227 mi).
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.266)
- **Polygon accuracy note.** The CShapes 1947 polygon represents the
  legal post-treaty boundary, not an intermediate state during the
  1944-1947 transition. The actual border during Oct 1944 - Feb 1947
  was in flux (Soviet military control, provisional government, armistice
  terms). The 1947 polygon is the best available and is used as the
  assigned polygon for the entire sub-period; any data for 1944-1946
  is matched to a polygon that understates the legal uncertainty of
  that transition period. The approximation is modest (~0%) once
  reversion is complete but may be directionally off by a few percent
  for 1944-1945.
- **Split rationale.** This polity is the third of three created by
  splitting HUN-1938-1947. The predecessor polity HUN-1940-1944 covered
  the expanded territory; this polity covers the reversion period.

## Sourced claims

- **1944-10: Soviet occupation begins.** Soviet forces entered eastern
  Hungary; the Second Vienna Award annexations (northern Transylvania,
  Backa) became non-functional. This is the start-of-row event.
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **1947-02-10: Treaty of Paris.** "the Treaty of Peace with Hungary,
  signed on 10 February 1947, declared the Vienna Award null and void
  and the Trianon boundary was reinstated."
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.268)
- **Area: 35,934 sq mi (93,033 sq km).** Post-Trianon Hungary, borders
  restored by Treaty of Paris.
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (p.266)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 310, continuous.
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
