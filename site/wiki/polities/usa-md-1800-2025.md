# Maryland (state of the United States)

## Summary

Maryland, one of the thirteen original U.S. states, ratifying the Constitution on 28 April 1788, as a subnational reporting unit within the United States. `type: subnational` because Maryland never held sovereignty independent of the federal union -- it is a constituent state, and this row exists purely to give juan-subnational's state-level Maryland data (previously absorbed into the country-wide USA-* national rows) a container of its own size, distinct from the roughly 3.8-million-km2 national territory those rows represent. The entry's start_year is 1800, not Maryland's actual 1788 admission: the polity table's earliest national USA row (USA-1800-1803) itself only begins in 1800, and the containment gate rejects any edge whose span falls outside its container's own recorded span, so 1800 is the earliest year any USA state-level row can currently be containered against. This mirrors the identical compromise already made for Delaware (USA-DE-1800-2025) and Connecticut (USA-CT-1800-2025), both also original-13 states.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: juan-subnational rows for Maryland ("Maryland" / USA subnational label), part of the same batch that produced usa-al, usa-ar, usa-az, usa-co, usa-ct and usa-de in this session; the routing decision's own reasoning cites the underlying extract's data years as running from 1866, though the exact row count for Maryland specifically was not inspected in authoring this page (see open questions). Previously matched to: the country-wide USA national rows (USA-1867-1959, USA-1959-2025, etc.), which is wrong because those rows represent the entire United States (~3.8 million km2 at any given era) rather than the ~32,131 km2 Maryland actually reports on -- a roughly 120x territory mismatch. Confirmation of distinctness: Maryland is a constitutionally and administratively distinct U.S. state with its own state government, and juan-subnational tabulates it as a separate reporting unit from both the national total and from neighboring states, which is the same evidentiary bar already applied to create the sibling state-level entries in this batch.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. `polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `USA.21_1`, matching Maryland's present-day state boundary. Maryland's boundaries were substantially settled before this entry's 1800 start -- the Mason-Dixon line with Pennsylvania (1767), the Potomac River boundary with Virginia, and the 1791 cession of land for the District of Columbia all predate statehood-era reporting -- so the modern GADM boundary is a low-risk proxy for the full 1800-2025 span, the same reasoning used for the other original-13 and long-settled Eastern states in this batch (cf. usa-de, usa-ct).

**Territory description:** the modern U.S. state of Maryland, on the mid-Atlantic coast surrounding the Chesapeake Bay, bordered by Pennsylvania to the north, Delaware and the Atlantic Ocean to the east, Virginia and West Virginia to the south and west (along the Potomac River), and wrapping around the District of Columbia on three sides. It includes the Eastern Shore portion of the Delmarva Peninsula. **Area measured from the attached GADM polygon: approximately 32,131 km2** (this is a measurement of the geometry, not an independent historical figure -- no separately sourced historical km2 for Maryland was consulted for this page).

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor: Maryland has been a single, continuously governed state since before this entry's 1800 start (predecessor list empty because no distinct prior polity precedes it in the table -- it is simply the earliest era the containing national chain supports) and it remains a current U.S. state, so successor is empty and end_year is the open convention value 2025.

## Sourced claims

- Maryland was the seventh of the thirteen original states to ratify the U.S. Constitution, doing so on 28 April 1788, which is the historical basis (though not the effective start_year, see decisions) for Maryland's statehood.
- Maryland ceded land along the Potomac in 1791 to help form the District of Columbia, a boundary change that predates this entry's 1800 start and is already reflected in the modern GADM state boundary used as this entry's polygon.
- juan-subnational is the sole source assigning Maryland-level (state-of-the-United-States) rows in this pipeline's data, distinguishing this entry from the country-wide USA-* national rows that the data was previously (mis)matched to before this page's creation.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1788

**start_year set to 1800, departing from the proposed 1788 ratification year**

The routing decision that motivated this page proposed start_year=1788, Maryland's ratification date (28 April 1788, the seventh of the original 13). But the polity table's earliest USA national row is USA-1800-1803 ("United States (original)"), and the container-tiling rule this harness enforces rejects any container edge whose span falls outside the container's own recorded span (1800-1803 at the earliest). There is no national USA row covering 1788-1800 to attach an edge to, so a 1788 start would leave a 12-year gap the containment gate cannot pass. Rather than invent an unbacked container row, this page follows the precedent already set for Delaware (USA-DE-1800-2025, ratified 1787) and Connecticut (USA-CT-1800-2025, ratified 1788): the earliest available container bounds the earliest state row, not the state's own admission history. If USA gets an earlier national row in a future ingest (e.g. USA-1787-1800 covering the Confederation-to-Constitution period), Maryland's start_year should be revisited alongside every other original-13 state page that made the same compromise.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap

**Is there a case for a USA-1787-1800 (or similar) national row that would let original-13 states start at their true admission years?**

Maryland (ratified 1788) joins Delaware (1787) and Connecticut (1788) in having its subnational start_year pinned to 1800 purely because that is where the polity table's national USA chain begins (USA-1800-1803). If a future ingest adds an earlier national USA row -- covering the Confederation period or the 1787-1800 span -- every original-13 state page, including this one, should be re-spanned to its actual ratification year rather than the shared 1800 floor. Until then, the 12 years 1788-1800 of Maryland's actual statehood are simply not represented by any polity row in this database.

### oq-juan-subnational-coverage

**What does the juan-subnational source actually cover for Maryland, and does it justify the full 1800-2025 span?**

This entry's sole listed source is juan-subnational, and the page was authored to create a container/routing home for that source's Maryland rows, not because a specific row count was inspected here. It is unconfirmed whether juan-subnational has continuous Maryland coverage across 1800-2025 or only a subset of years (the routing decision's own reasoning described the underlying extract's data years as running from 1866 for this batch, echoing the same flag raised on the sibling Delaware and Connecticut pages). If the source is sparse before 1866, that should be noted rather than implying dense reporting back to 1800.

### oq-dc-boundary-interaction

**Does the modern Maryland GADM polygon reflect the 1791 cession of land to form the District of Columbia?**

Maryland ceded roughly 70 square miles (about 180 km2) of its territory in 1791 to help form the federal District of Columbia -- a cession that predates this entry's 1800 start, so the modern GADM boundary (which already excludes DC) should be the correct shape for the whole 1800-2025 span. But no specific check was made here confirming GADM 4.1's Maryland feature draws the DC boundary consistently with the 1791/1846 cession history (Virginia's portion of the original DC federal district was retroceded in 1846; Maryland's portion was not). This is flagged rather than resolved.
