# Niger (1947-1960)

## Summary

The Colony of Niger in its final phase as part of French West Africa, from the post-WWII reorganisation (1946 French Union) to independence. Niger became an overseas territory of France in 1946 with representation in the French parliament, gained internal autonomy as a republic within the French Community in 1958, and achieved full independence on 3 August 1960.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from CShapes 2.0 (cshapes2_full.gpkg), cowcode 436, feature_year 1947. Method: exact_historical. The geometry is pixel-identical to the NER-1922-1947 core polygon (~1,182,017 km²). What the 1947-01-04 step removed is **the Dori and Fada N'Gourma cercles, ceded back to a reconstituted Upper Volta** — not Tibesti, which had already gone to Chad in 1931; the correction and its measurement are on [ner-1922-1947](ner-1922-1947.md#territorial-extent) and in [wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §colony-of-niger](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#colony-of-niger). "While there were minor border changes after 1947, the modern borders of Niger were roughly established with this change", so the territory equals modern Niger.

**Why this entry exists:** Fills the 1947-1960 gap between [NER-1922-1947](ner-1922-1947.md) and [NER-1960-2025](ner-1960-2025.md). Without this, 76 rows of Niger data for 1948-1959 were unmatched.
