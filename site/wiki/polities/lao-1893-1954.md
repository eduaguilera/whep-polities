## Summary

Laos (1893-1954) covers the French colonial period in Laos, from the establishment of French control in 1893 through the year of independence, 1953 (Franco-Lao Treaty, 22 October 1953); the exclusive `end_year: 1954` means the row's last covered year is 1953. This row was formed by merging LAO-1893-1896 and LAO-1896-1953, because CShapes records identical areas for both the 1893-1896 and 1896-1953 time-steps (approximately 196,820 km²), indicating that the 1896 step reflects an administrative or diplomatic event rather than a genuine territorial change. Laos was administered as part of French Indochina; its northern boundary with China was established by the 1885 Treaty of Tientsin.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records Laos as a French colony (owner=220) throughout this period. The polygon covers approximately 196,820 km² and is consistent across all CShapes time-steps from 1893 to 1953. The China-Laos boundary "was established between France and China by the Treaty of Peace and Commerce (Treaty of Tientsin) of 1885." [biger-1995 §laos](../sources/biger-1995.md#laos)

## Approximation notes

### polygon-vintage-drift-1936-1951

Data labelled "indochina laos" in source fao1952 covers years 1936-1951 (59 rows). These are routed to LAO-1893-1954 (coded `LAO-1893-1953` before 2026-08-13), whose CShapes 2.0 polygon has a vintage year of 1896 — a drift of 40-55 years. This drift is acceptable as an approximation because Laos borders were stable after the 1907 Franco-Siamese Treaty (which settled the Mekong boundary with Siam). The CShapes polygon area (~196,820 km²) remained constant across all CShapes time-steps from 1893 to 1953, confirming no territorial change occurred during the data years. The polygon should NOT be replaced or updated; the drift is documented here as a known limitation. If sub-period production or area data requires greater precision, a future audit could verify against the original 1907 treaty boundary description.
