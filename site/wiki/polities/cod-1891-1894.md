# Democratic Republic of the Congo (1891-1894)

## Summary

The Congo Free State in its 1891-1894 territorial configuration, a personal possession of King Leopold II of Belgium. "In the Berlin Conference of 1884-1885 the Association, which changed its name to Congo Free State, was recognized as a governing power on the Congo River" [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). CShapes records this as a distinct boundary period. The 1891 start date and 1894 end date correspond to CShapes boundary changes reflecting territorial adjustments as the Free State's borders were progressively delimited with neighbouring colonial powers. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area changes at both endpoints.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period in the database. The Congo Free State's vast territory in central Africa was progressively delimited through treaties with France (to the west), Portugal (to the southwest), and Britain (to the southeast). "The boundary between Congo and Zaire runs for 1,498 miles (2,410 km.)" [biger-1995 §congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire).
