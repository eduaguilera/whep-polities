# Angola

## Summary

Angola is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of modern Angola from 1816 to the present. The start year 1816 reflects the beginning of WHEP temporal coverage for Portuguese colonial holdings in southwestern Africa, not any specific boundary event. Angola gained independence from Portugal on 11 November 1975. The row continues as a single WHEP polity because the territory has remained essentially stable since independence.

## Territorial extent

Angola's boundaries were established through a series of colonial treaties in the late 19th century. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Boundaries with neighbors (Congo, Zaire, Zambia, Namibia) were demarcated during the Scramble for Africa period. The exclave of Cabinda in the north is included in the Angolan territory.
