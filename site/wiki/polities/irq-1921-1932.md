# Iraq (British Mandate for Mesopotamia)

## Summary

Iraq under the British mandate, from the installation of the Hashemite monarchy in 1921 to independence in 1932. The row exists because the mandate is a distinct reporting unit with a distinct legal status, not because its territory differed from independent Iraq's — the geometry is identical either side of 1932. The one substantive territorial question inside the span is the status of the Mosul vilayet, disputed with Turkey until 1926, and it is documented rather than glossed below.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `645`, time-step **1932**, `polygon_status: assigned`. Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **436,195 km²**. Iraq's conventional area is 438,317 km², so the polygon is within 0.5%.

**The polygon is the successor's, used backwards, and that is measurable.** [IRQ-1932-2025](irq-1932-2025.md) binds the same feature at the same 1932 time-step and measures **436,195 km² — identical to the square kilometre**. CShapes has **no pre-1932 time-step for Iraq at all**: the mandate is not separately coded, so the independence-year polygon is the earliest that exists. CShapes' own method explains why — where a unit has no earlier coded change, borders are simply backdated ([cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding)), and its coverage in any case begins only in 1886 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)). So this row's polygon asserts "Iraq's 1932 borders" and is used as a stand-in for 1921-1931, not as an observation of them.

**Whether that stand-in is safe turns on Mosul, and the answer is: yes for extent, with a 1921-1926 caveat on control.** Iraq was assembled from "three former Ottoman provinces" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — Basra, Baghdad and Mosul. The Mosul vilayet, roughly 100,000 km² or **23% of this row's area**, was claimed by Turkey and its status was not settled until the 1926 Ankara/Frontier Treaty; throughout the dispute it was under British mandate administration, not Turkish control. So the 1932 polygon describes the territory Britain *administered* for the whole row, and overstates the territory whose sovereignty was *undisputed* for the first five years (1921-1926). That distinction matters for interpretation but not for data routing: agricultural output recorded for mandate Iraq in the 1920s was collected by the mandate administration, which is the same body that administered Mosul.

CShapes' own coding rule points the same way: disputed territories are not coded separately but assigned "based on [a state's] de facto control over the region" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)). Under that rule Mosul belongs to mandate Iraq from 1921, because Britain held it de facto — so CShapes would have drawn this polygon the same way even had it coded a 1921 step.

**Territorial changes inside 1921-1932: one dispute resolved, no boundary moved.** The 1926 settlement confirmed the existing line rather than shifting it, so under the WHEP rule there is no in-span territorial change and **the row should not be split**. Biger names two Iraqi boundary events and both are far outside this span: the state's creation "as a British mandate after World War I" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)), which is this row's premise, and the Iraq-Kuwait boundary demarcation, which "was only carried out after the defeat of Iraq by United Nations sponsored forces in the Gulf War of 1991" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — 59 years later. That last point deserves emphasis for anyone using this row: **the Iraq-Kuwait line was undemarcated throughout the mandate period**, so any statement that this polygon's southern boundary is the 1920s legal boundary is stronger than the sources support.

## Predecessors and successors

- **No predecessor.** The row starts at the mandate's establishment, and the Ottoman provinces that preceded it have no WHEP row of their own; Ottoman-era data routes to the Ottoman chain ([ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) and its successors).
- **Successor:** [IRQ-1932-2025](irq-1932-2025.md) — independent Iraq, **identical polygon and identical measured area (436,195 km²)**. The 1932 row boundary is a sovereignty change over unchanged territory, which is a reporting-scope boundary rather than a territorial one ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)).

## Key dates

- 1920-04-25: San Remo conference assigns the Mesopotamia mandate to Britain. **Uncited** — no source on disk gives this date.
- 1921-08-23: Faisal I enthroned as King of Iraq. **Uncited.** Start of row.
- 1922-1925: Mosul vilayet disputed with Turkey; under British mandate administration throughout. The dispute is implied by Biger's "three former Ottoman provinces" framing ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) but the dates are **uncited**.
- 1926-06-05: Ankara/Frontier Treaty settles the Mosul question in Iraq's favour. **Uncited.**
- 1932-10-03: Iraq admitted to the League of Nations; independence. **This is the exact date COW uses** for `ccode=645`. End of row.

## Sourced claims

- "Iraq as a modern state came only into being as a British mandate after World War I" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — the row's premise.
- Iraq was created from "three former Ottoman provinces" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — Basra, Baghdad and Mosul; the naming of the three is conventional history, Biger gives the count.
- "In 1932 Iraq was the first mandate to graduate to independence" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — the end-of-row event, and Biger's "first" is a useful cross-check on the WHEP mandate chains: no other mandate row should end earlier for independence reasons.
- The Iraq-Kuwait boundary demarcation "was only carried out after the defeat of Iraq by United Nations sponsored forces in the Gulf War of 1991" ([biger-1995 §iraq](../sources/biger-1995.md#iraq)) — so this row's southern boundary was legally undemarcated for its entire span, and the CShapes line there is a later reconstruction.
- **COW records `ccode=645` as a single tenure `1932-10-03 → 2024-12-31`** — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). The start is the League of Nations admission date, consistent with COW's post-1920 criterion of League or UN membership ([cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion)). It also means COW covers **none** of this row: the frontmatter `cow: 645` is a code assignment for the Iraqi family, not a membership claim for the mandate years. Because 1932-10-03 is a specific day rather than a January-1 placeholder ([cow-state-system-v2024 §known-limitations](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#known-limitations), limitation 3), it corroborates this row's end year to the day.
- CShapes codes "international mandates" as one of four dependency categories ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)) — yet it still has no pre-1932 step for Iraq, so the mandate's availability as a category did not translate into an observation here.
- CShapes assigns disputed territory "based on [de facto] control over the region" rather than coding it separately ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)) — the rule under which Mosul is part of this row's polygon for the whole span.
- For areas where borders were undefined — CShapes names the Middle East in the nineteenth century specifically — it inserts flagged "placeholder polygon[s] that represent the borders as they were eventually defined" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)). Given that the Iraq-Kuwait line was undemarcated until 1991, whether feature 645's record carries that flag is a live question; see [oq-irq-placeholder-flag](#oq-irq-placeholder-flag).
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): this row **436,195 km²**; [IRQ-1932-2025](irq-1932-2025.md) **436,195 km²** — identical, confirming that the two rows share one geometry and that the 1932 boundary carries no territorial claim. ESRI:54034 from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`.
- Data footprint (this ingest): **45 layer-B rows** route to this code per `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`.

## Contradictions

- **Sovereignty over Mosul, 1921-1926.** The attached polygon includes the Mosul vilayet (~100,000 km², 23% of the row) for the whole span; Turkey contested Iraqi sovereignty over it until 1926. These are reconcilable rather than contradictory once *administration* is distinguished from *sovereignty*: Britain administered Mosul as part of mandate Iraq throughout, and CShapes' de facto-control rule ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)) resolves the case the same way the database does. Recorded so that a reader treating this polygon as a map of *undisputed* Iraqi territory in 1923 is warned.
