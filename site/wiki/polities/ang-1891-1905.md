## Summary

Portuguese West Africa (Angola) in its expanded territorial configuration from 11 June 1891 to 29 May 1905. The 1891 start date corresponds to a CShapes boundary change: the area jumped from 86.19 sq deg (the 1890–1891 configuration) to 111.93 sq deg following the Anglo-Portuguese Treaty of 11 June 1891, which delimited the Angola–Rhodesia frontier and confirmed Portuguese sovereignty over the interior hinterland. The 1905 end date marks another boundary reduction (to 103.81 sq deg), corresponding to the delimitation of the Congo–Angola border. "The Angolans achieved their independence from the Portuguese on 11 November 1975" [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). This row is a distinct WHEP polity because the territorial area changed at both endpoints; same-area CShapes steps are collapsed into single rows per WHEP methodology.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 111.93 sq deg (geographic coordinates) for the period 1891-06-11 to 1905-05-29, status=colony, owner=Portugal (COW 235). Angola includes the Cabinda exclave in the north, separated from the main territory by a strip of territory along the Congo River [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). Bounded by Congo/Cabinda (125 mi; 201 km), Zaire (1,560 mi; 2,511 km), Zambia (690 mi; 1,110 km), Namibia (855 mi; 1,376 km), and the Atlantic Ocean [biger-1995 §angola](../sources/biger-1995.md#angola). The 1891 configuration represents the post-Anglo-Portuguese Treaty extent; the reduction at 1905 reflects a border adjustment along the northeast frontier.
