# Indonesia excluding East Timor (1976-2002)

## Summary

Indonesia's territory **without East Timor**, for the years 1976-2002 in which `IDN-1976-2002`
includes it. A **reporting-area row**: it records what FAOSTAT area **101 Indonesia** covers in
those years, and takes no position on East Timor's status, which `IDN-1976-2002` and
`TLS-1800-2025` record at the polity level.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`. Area 101 1976-2001
was routed to `IDN-1976-2002`, whose polygon includes East Timor, while FAOSTAT area **176
Timor-Leste** reports East Timor separately for **every** year 1961-2024 (for example harvested
area 92,515 ha in 1976 and 220,767 ha in 2001, summing items below code 1700). Measured in
ESRI:54034, `TLS-1800-2025` (14,978 km²) overlaps `IDN-1976-2002` by 14,951 km², so the same
ground was claimed by two areas.

Area 101 shows no step when East Timor leaves Indonesia: total harvested area 31,720,793 ha in 2001,
31,969,266 ha in 2002 (+0.8%). East Timor's share would be about 0.7%, too small to read off a
break by itself; the decisive evidence is that FAOSTAT carries East Timor under its own code
throughout. `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 101 1976-2001 here through
`manual_territory_routes`.
