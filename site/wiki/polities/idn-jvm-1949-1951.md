## Summary

This WHEP polity covers the islands of **Java** and **Madura** as a combined reporting unit as used by the **FAO 1952 dataset** for the period 1949–1951. The FAO source disaggregated Indonesia into distinct island-group sub-units — "Indonesia Java and Madura", "Indonesia Bali and Lombok", and "Indonesia Other islands" — reflecting a sub-national agricultural census structure used in the immediate post-independence period. This entry prevents incorrect routing of Java+Madura agricultural production data to the whole-Indonesia polity `IDN-1949-1963`, which covers approximately 1,900,000 km² (a ~14× territorial overstatement).

Java is the most densely settled island of Indonesia and its agricultural and economic core. Madura is a small island immediately to the northeast of Java (3,354 km²) and was administratively linked with Java as a single provincial unit. Together they total approximately **132,000 km²**.
