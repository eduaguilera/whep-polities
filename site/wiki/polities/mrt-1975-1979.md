# Mauritania (1975-1979)

## Summary

This WHEP polity row covers Mauritania during the period when it occupied a portion of
Western Sahara, from November 1975 to August 1979. The 1975 start date corresponds to
the Madrid Accords (14 November 1975) and the Spanish withdrawal from Western Sahara,
after which Morocco and Mauritania partitioned the territory between them. CShapes
records the area increase for cowcode=435: from≈89.91 sq.deg. (1960–1975) to≈98.49
sq.deg. (1975–1979), reflecting the addition of the southern Western Sahara zone
(Tiris al-Gharbiyya / Río de Oro). The 1979 end date marks Mauritania's renunciation of
its Western Sahara claim following military pressure from the Polisario Front and the
Algiers Agreement (5 August 1979), after which Morocco occupied the full territory.

## Territorial extent

Mauritania's core territory (area: 395,956 sq. miles [1,025,130 sq. km.]; population:
2,280,000 [1994]) plus the southern zone of Western Sahara (Tiris al-Gharbiyya),
approximately 270,000 sq. km. CShapes area for this period is≈98.49 sq.deg. compared
to the base Mauritania area of≈89.91 sq.deg., an increase of≈8.58 sq.deg. Capital:
Nouakchott. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=435, status=independent,
owner=435, start=1975-11-14, end=1979-08-04).

## Sourced claims

- "In 1976 Spain handed its Spanish Sahara territory over to Morocco and Mauritania, and
  these two states arranged a partition along a straight line from 23 degrees N to the
  24 degrees N parallel with the Atlantic Ocean, north of Dakhla." (p.380). This
  establishes the partition event and the specific boundary agreed upon.
  [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania)
- "February 1976 saw Spain hand the territory of Western Sahara, formerly Spanish
  Sahara, to Morocco and Mauritania, which arranged a partition. The status of Western
  Sahara has not been resolved." (p.391). [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=435 with status=independent and owner=435 for
  1975-11-14 to 1979-08-04, area≈98.49 sq.deg. — visibly larger than the preceding
  record (area≈89.91 sq.deg.) and the following record (area≈89.91 sq.deg.) after
  renunciation. This area change justifies the WHEP row split at 1975 and 1979.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- "The Mauritanians became independent from colonial rule by the French in November
  1960." (p.378). [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania)

## Contradictions

Biger (p.380) gives the Spanish handover as "1976" (February 1976), while CShapes
records the start date of the enlarged Mauritanian polygon as 1975-11-14 (the date of
the Madrid Accords, before the formal transfer). WHEP follows CShapes (1975-11-14)
for the start date, since the Madrid Accords are the territorial commitment event. This
is a definitional difference, not a factual contradiction.
