# Toscana (region of Italy)

## Summary

Toscana (Tuscany) is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions, occupying the historic core of central Italy between the Apennines and the Tyrrhenian coast, with its capital at Florence. Unlike Emilia-Romagna, Veneto, or the two 1919-annexation regions already created in this pipeline, Tuscany's territory joined the Kingdom of Italy at the moment of its own proclamation: the Grand Duchy of Tuscany was annexed by plebiscite in March 1860 and became part of the Kingdom of Sardinia, which was renamed the Kingdom of Italy in 1861. Tuscany's start_year of 1861 is thus the earliest possible one: the region has been part of unified Italy for the entire national span. This entry is type: subnational, not national: Italy held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1861-2025, and this row exists only to carry a reporting territory distinct from the national ITA chain, for the same reason as the sibling NUTS2 regions already created in this pipeline (Veneto, Bolzano/South Tyrol, Trentino, Emilia-Romagna).

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit ITA-ITI1 (admin code ITI1, Eurostat NUTS2 code for Toscana) from the country-convention pipeline's Italian subnational routing pass (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json, pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv), with data spanning 1861-2023 and a proposed span of 1861-2025, containered by ITA-1861-1866 at the start. Before this row existed, any region-level data for Toscana had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows, which are roughly 13x Tuscany's area and would misattribute a regional figure to the whole country, or double-count against sibling regions if summed. No existing polity IS Tuscany: the only candidates the routing pass considered were the national Italy rows, which contain Tuscany but are not it. The routing decision applied the country convention's default start rule (start_year equals the earliest container national row's start) with no departure, unlike Veneto or the two 1919 regions, whose late starts are annexation-driven. What confirms Tuscany is a genuinely distinct reporting/administrative unit rather than a restatement of national Italy: it is Eurostat's own NUTS2 statistical division (ITI1) and a constitutionally-established Italian region with its own elected council and administration, the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling NUTS2 regions created in this pipeline. Unlike those siblings, Tuscany's data span (1861-2023) reaches back to unification itself, which is why this entry's proposed span runs the region's full administrative existence rather than starting only at the 1970 constitution of ordinary-statute regions; the routing note flags this as a judgment call to check for consistency with sibling create_new verdicts.

Tuscany's outer boundary has been essentially stable since the Grand Duchy's 1860 annexation, so a single present-day regional polygon, once a source is fetched or registered, should be a safe proxy for the entire 1861-2025 span. ## Decisions

### d-full-span-not-1970-start

**Proposed span runs the full 1861-2025 national era rather than starting at the 1970 ordinary-statute regional constitution**

Sibling NUTS2 regions created earlier in this pipeline (Emilia-Romagna, Veneto post-1866, Bolzano/Trentino post-1919) generally start at the point their own administrative or annexation history begins, which for regions already inside Italy at unification (Emilia-Romagna) meant 1970, the year ordinary-statute regions were actually constituted with elected councils. Toscana's routing verdict instead proposed start_year=1861, following the country convention's default start rule (earliest container national row) rather than the 1970 regional-constitution date, because the routed data itself spans back to 1861. This departs from the Emilia-Romagna precedent and is flagged as a judgment call; it is followed here because reversing it would strand the 1861-1969 data with no home, and the routing decision explicitly commits to treating this span as continuously proposed rather than back_cast/unroutable.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1970-consistency

**Is the 1861 start consistent with how sibling regions handled their pre-1970 (pre-NUTS) years?**

The routing decision for this unit explicitly raises this as unresolved: Emilia-Romagna's create_new verdict starts that region's span in 1970 (when ordinary-statute regions were actually constituted with elected councils), treating 1861-1969 data as belonging to the national ITA chain rather than to the region. Toscana's verdict instead proposes 1861 as the start, using the country convention's default start rule directly. If the intended convention for regions that existed within Italy since unification is uniformly 1970-unless-annexation-driven, this entry's span is inconsistent with Emilia-Romagna's and should be reconciled, either by trimming Toscana's start to 1970 and creating a separate pre-1970 back-cast entry, or by revisiting the Emilia-Romagna precedent. This has not been checked against the other Italian regional pages in this batch beyond noting the discrepancy.

### oq-no-polygon-source

**No registered polygon source covers any Italian sub-national region, including this one**

As with every other Italian NUTS2 region created in this pipeline, no registered source in the repository (cshapes-europe, cshapes-2.0, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) carries Italian admin-1 boundaries, and the locally present gadm-4.1-adm1 file excludes Italy from its 81-country subset. Until the full global GADM 4.1 ADM1 release is fetched, or ISTAT's official Confini amministrativi region boundaries are registered as a source, this entry (and its four already-created siblings) cannot move past polygon_status: unassigned. This blocks any area-based verification of this page's stated ~22,990 km2 territory description.
