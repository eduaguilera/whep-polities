# Sweden (1814-1905)

## Summary

Sweden as a distinct statistical unit during the Sweden-Norway personal union (1814-1905). Despite the union, Sweden and Norway maintained entirely separate governments, parliaments (Riksdag / Storting), budgets, and statistical offices. Agricultural data was compiled separately for each country throughout the union period. The union entity is tracked as [SNW-1814-1905](snw-1814-1905.md) with the combined CShapes polygon.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm0` feature `SWE`, used as a **proxy** (`polygon_status: proxy`). Measured directly from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg` in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034), this row's polygon is **449,316 km²** — within 0.2% of the 449,964 km² Biger gives for Sweden's area ([biger-1995 §sweden](../sources/biger-1995.md#sweden)), which is the expected agreement since both describe present-day Sweden.

**Why a modern proxy rather than a period polygon.** CShapes 2.0 begins in 1886 ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)), so 1814-1885 is outside its coverage entirely, and the CShapes-Europe extension's only feature for cowcode 380 in this window is the **combined union** territory: measured at **765,829 km²**, i.e. Sweden plus Norway (Norway's own row [NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md) measures 320,132 km², and 449,316 + 320,132 = 769,448 km², within 0.5% of the union polygon). That combined polygon is bound to [SNW-1814-1905](snw-1814-1905.md); using it for Sweden-only data would double-count Norway on the choropleth.

**Was Sweden's territory constant across 1814-1905? Yes, and here is how that is known.** Two independent checks agree:

1. **Boundary history.** Biger's Sweden entry records the loss of Finland in 1808 and the compensating union with Norway "lasting until 1905", and then jumps to the twentieth century with no intervening boundary event ([biger-1995 §sweden](../sources/biger-1995.md#sweden)). His Norway entry likewise records only the 1397 Union of Kalmar and the 1905 "peaceful separation from Sweden" ([biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)). Neither entry names any Swedish land-boundary change between 1814 and 1905. Sweden's land boundaries in the period were with Norway (internal to the union) and with the Grand Duchy of Finland, whose line was fixed by the 1809 Treaty of Fredrikshamn — before this row starts.
2. **State-system continuity.** COW records Sweden (`ccode=380`) as a **single uninterrupted tenure, 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31**, in `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv` ([cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema) for the column meanings). There is no COW exit or re-entry at 1814, at 1905, or anywhere between. Norway (`ccode=385`) by contrast enters COW only at **1905-06-07** — the day Norway declared the union dissolved. So COW treats the union as a Norwegian, not a Swedish, discontinuity, which is exactly the asymmetry that justifies Sweden having a row across the union while Norway's separate COW existence starts at its end.

**No in-span split is warranted.** The 1866 Riksdag reform (bicameral parliament replacing the Estates) is a constitutional change with no territorial content, and under the WHEP polity definition regime change alone does not open a row. The row's two boundaries — 1814 (Norway acquired under the Treaty of Kiel) and 1905 (union dissolved) — are both *union* events rather than changes to Sweden's own territory; they exist as row boundaries because they change which statistical unit "Sweden" sits inside, not because Swedish borders moved.

**Why this entry exists separately from SNW-1814-1905.** The pre-1961 agricultural input data reports "Sweden" and "Norway" as separate countries throughout the union period — consistent with the two kingdoms keeping separate statistical offices. Matching Swedish-only data to the union polygon would incorrectly colour Norway on the choropleth map. This entry captures Sweden-only data; Norway data matches [NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [SWE-1809-1814](swe-1809-1814.md) — Sweden after loss of Finland (1809), before union with Norway. Same GADM proxy polygon, so the same 449,316 km² measurement; the predecessor boundary is a union event, not a border change.
- **Successor:** [SWE-1905-2025](swe-1905-2025.md) — Sweden after dissolution of the union (7 June 1905). This row switches to CShapes 2.0 feature 380 (vintage 1905), measured at **445,909 km²** — 0.8% below the GADM proxy used here, which is a coastline/generalisation difference between the two polygon sources rather than a territorial change.

## Key dates

- 1814-01-14: Treaty of Kiel — Denmark cedes Norway to Sweden.
- 1814-08-14: Convention of Moss — Norway accepts personal union.
- 1866: Riksdag reformed (bicameral parliament replaces Estates). No territorial content.
- 1905-06-07: Norway declares union dissolved; COW's Norway tenure starts this day (`statelist2024.csv`).
- 1905-10-26: Formal dissolution; Sweden accepts.

## Sourced claims

- Sweden suffered "loss of Finland in 1808, compensated by union with Norway (lasting until 1905)" ([biger-1995 §sweden](../sources/biger-1995.md#sweden)). The Finnish loss predates this row; the union defines it.
- Biger gives Sweden's area as 173,732 sq. miles (449,964 km²) ([biger-1995 §sweden](../sources/biger-1995.md#sweden)) and Norway's as 149,469 sq. miles (386,975 km²) ([biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)). Both are 1994 figures for the modern states, not period measurements, and Biger's Norway figure includes Svalbard and Jan Mayen, which is why it exceeds this database's 320,132 km² CShapes measurement for [NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md).
- The "Kingdom of Norway [was] proclaimed in 1905 after peaceful separation from Sweden" ([biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)) — the end-of-row event, and Biger's characterisation ("peaceful") is the reason there is no wartime-border complication of the kind CShapes excludes ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)).
- COW `ccode=380` has one tenure row, `1816-01-01 → 2024-12-31`, and `ccode=385` (Norway) has `1905-06-07 → 1940-04-30` then `1945-05-07 → 2024-12-31` — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). COW's 1816 Swedish start is a January-1 value and should not be read as a January-1 event ([cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates) documents this convention).
- CShapes 2.0 traces borders only "from 1886 through 2019" ([cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)), and pre-1886 European coverage comes from the separate CShapes-Europe extension ([cshapes-2.0 §known-limitations](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#known-limitations), limitation 2) — which is why 72 of this row's 92 years have no CShapes-proper polygon available at all and a modern proxy is used.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): GADM `SWE` = **449,316 km²**; CShapes-Europe union feature 380 (vintage 1816, bound to SNW) = **765,829 km²**; [NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md) = **320,132 km²**; [SWE-1905-2025](swe-1905-2025.md) = **445,909 km²**. All in ESRI:54034 from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`.

## Contradictions

- **Whether "Sweden" in the union period is one unit or two.** COW gives Sweden a continuous 1816-2024 tenure and starts Norway only in 1905, which reads the union as Swedish sovereignty over Norway. Biger describes it as a union Norway "separated" from peacefully in 1905, which reads it as two kingdoms. WHEP follows the data: the pre-1961 sources report Sweden and Norway as separate reporting units throughout, so the database carries both this row and [NOR-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md), plus [SNW-1814-1905](snw-1814-1905.md) for sources that report the union as one. This is a reporting-scope decision, not a claim that one source is wrong.
