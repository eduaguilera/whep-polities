# Sierra Leone (1886-1889)

## Summary

Sierra Leone (1886-1889) covers the British colony of Sierra Leone from 1 January 1886 — the CShapes 2.0 temporal baseline — to 9 August 1889, when a boundary agreement with France reduced the colony's extent. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=GBR (COW 200). The area at this period (~6.85 CShapes units) is slightly larger than the subsequent period, reflecting the pre-1889 boundary with French Guinea before the Anglo-French agreement took effect.

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory centered on Freetown, bounded by French Guinea (north and east), Liberia (south-east), and the Atlantic Ocean (west). CShapes 2.0 area ~6.85 for the 1886-01-01 to 1889-08-09 period. The territorial reduction at 1889-08-10 (to ~5.95) reflects the Anglo-French boundary agreement that established the border with French Guinea. Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
