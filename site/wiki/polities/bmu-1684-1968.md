# British Crown Colony of Bermuda

## Summary

WHEP polity for Bermuda as a British Crown Colony, covering the period from Crown Colony status formalised in 1684 to the Bermuda Constitution Order 1968, which granted internal self-government. Created to cover FAO 1952 data (41 rows, 1937–1951, label "Bermuda") and IIA data (17 rows, 1930–1945, label "bermuda") reporting agricultural statistics for this small North Atlantic island territory (~54 km²). Bermuda is not covered by any GBR polity in WHEP; British GBR entries cover Great Britain only.

## Territorial Extent

Bermuda is an archipelago of approximately 181 small coral islands in the North Atlantic Ocean (~64.8°W, 32.3°N), of which roughly 20 are inhabited. Total land area approximately 54 km². The territory has been under continuous British control since 1612 (English settlement) and formally became a Crown Colony administered by the Colonial Office in 1684 when the Somers Isles Company charter was revoked.

The territory's boundaries were geographically fixed (island group, no land borders) throughout the entire 1684–1968 period. No territorial changes occurred: the polygon is stable for the full span.

- **Polygon source:** GADM 4.1 adm0 (`GID_0 = BMU`).
- **Polygon status:** proxy — modern GADM boundary used for the colonial period. Given that Bermuda is an island group with fixed geography, the current polygon is an exact match for the historical extent; no drift is expected.
- **Polygon confidence:** high — island territory with fixed geographic extent throughout the polity span; modern administrative boundary equals historical Crown Colony boundary.
- **Polygon area:** ~54 km² (consistent with widely cited figure for Bermuda's land area).
- **Polygon vintage drift:** none expected; island boundaries are stable since British settlement.

## Why This Polity Was Created

FAO 1952 data (source `fao1952`) reports 41 rows of agricultural statistics for "Bermuda" spanning 1937–1951, including cattle, horses/mules/asses, milk, tractors, and other meat. IIA data reports 17 rows for "bermuda" (lowercase) spanning 1930–1945, including hen eggs. These rows cannot be matched to any existing WHEP polity:

- GBR polities in WHEP cover Great Britain; Crown Colonies are distinct administrative entities.
- No Caribbean or Atlantic colonial aggregate polity exists that would include Bermuda.
- The data magnitudes (~54 km² island) are consistent only with Bermuda itself, not any larger territory.
