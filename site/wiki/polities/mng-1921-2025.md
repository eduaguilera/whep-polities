# Mongolia

## Summary

Mongolia from the 1921 Soviet-backed revolution to the present. After
the Bogd Khanate period (1911-1921), Soviet and Mongolian forces
expelled Chinese troops in March 1921. The Mongolian People's Republic
was formally declared in 1924 with Soviet support. CShapes records a
single polygon for Mongolia (COW 712) from 1921-03-13 to 2019-12-31,
with no territorial changes. Area ~184.4 sq deg (~1,566,500 km2).
Mongolia transitioned to democracy in 1990.
