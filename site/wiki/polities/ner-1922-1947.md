# Colonial Niger (1922-1947)

## Summary

The Colonie du Niger was formally established on 13 October 1922 when France reconstituted the territoire militaire du Niger (carved out of Upper Senegal-Niger in 1911) as a full civilian colony within French West Africa (AOF). The colony was administered from Niamey and remained part of AOF until the postwar French Union reforms of 1946-1947, after which it is tracked as [NER-1947-1960](ner-1947-1960.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from CShapes 2.0 (cshapes2_full.gpkg), cowcode 436, feature_year 1926 (row index 307: 1905-01-01 to 1926-12-27). Method: **exact_historical** — CShapes 2.0 includes Niger explicitly as a colonial dependency across four successive records spanning 1905-1960. The 1926 snapshot is used as the reference year because it falls squarely within the 1922-1932 sub-period and the geometry is identical to the 1926-1932 and 1947-1960 snapshots (all at 1,182,017 km²).

**Territorial note (1932-1947 sub-period):** Between 1932-09-05 and 1947-01-03, CShapes records Niger at 1,259,559 km² (+77,542 km²). **That increment is the eastern Upper Volta cercles of Dori and Fada N'Gourma, not Tibesti** — this page said Tibesti until 2026-08-13 and was wrong twice over. The source puts Tibesti's transfer in the opposite direction and six years earlier: "In 1931, Tibesti Cercle ceded to Chad Colony in French Equatorial Africa, and in 1932, the colony of French Upper Volta was divided amongst its neighbors, with the Cercles of Dori and Fada N'gourma ceded to Niger Colony", returning to a reconstituted Upper Volta in 1947 [wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §colony-of-niger](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#colony-of-niger). The geometry agrees: differencing CShapes row 309 against row 307 puts the extra 77,542 km² at **1.32°W–2.39°E, 10.94°–15.08°N** — the south-western corner, adjacent to Burkina Faso — whereas Tibesti lies near 16–19°E, 20–22°N, beyond Niger's easternmost CShapes vertex (15.997°E) in every one of its six steps. The core polygon (1,182,017 km²) remains the polity-level representative area; the 1932-1947 expansion is documented here and does not alter the exact_historical classification.

**Why this entry exists:** Fills the 1922-1947 gap in the Niger colonial chain. Niger WHEP data for 1922-1946 was previously unmatched. The existing entry [NER-1947-1960](ner-1947-1960.md) references NER-1932-1947 as predecessor (a code that was never created); NER-1922-1947 subsumes that anticipated entry and extends coverage back to the colony's formal creation in 1922. Note: [NER-1947-1960](ner-1947-1960.md) should be updated so that its `predecessor` field points to NER-1922-1947 instead of NER-1932-1947.

## Key dates

Dates below are from
[wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13 §colony-of-niger](../sources/wikipedia-french-africa-mandates-2026-08-13.md#colony-of-niger)
except the CShapes step dates, which are from the dataset.

- 1900-07-23 — Zinder Military Territory formed; administratively part of Senegambia and Niger (1902-1904) then Upper Senegal and Niger (1904-1911)
- 1910-06-22 — renamed Niger Military Territory, then Gao cercle ceded to French Sudan on 1911-06-21
- 1922-10-13 — the civilian Colony of Niger takes control (formal constitution as Colonie du Niger)
- 1931 — **Tibesti cercle ceded away, to Chad Colony in French Equatorial Africa**
- 1932-09-05 (CShapes step) — Upper Volta dissolved; the cercles of **Dori and Fada N'Gourma** are ceded **to** Niger, +77,542 km²
- 1946-12-31 — the military territories of N'Guigmi and Agadez ceded to Niger Colony, leaving only Bilma under military rule (civilian only from 1956)
- 1947-01-04 (CShapes step) — Upper Volta reconstituted and Dori and Fada N'Gourma ceded back to it; Niger simultaneously reconstituted as an overseas territory under the French Union reforms → succeeded by [NER-1947-1960](ner-1947-1960.md)
