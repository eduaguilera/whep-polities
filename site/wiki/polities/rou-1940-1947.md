# Romania (1940-1947)


> **Polygon BUILT 2026-08-05 (issues 12, 100, 106). Re-measured 2026-08-17 at
> 193,854 km² against the 194,000 declared here — 0.08%.**
>
> `constructed` recipe: CShapes gwcode 360 at 1941 (the 1940-2019 step, 237,379 km² —
> post-war Romania, already without Bessarabia, Northern Bukovina and Southern Dobruja but
> *with* Northern Transylvania) **minus** an eight-county proxy for the Second Vienna Award
> holding. See `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`.
>
> **Why it could not be done with a `polygon_feature_year`.** The 194,000 km² this page
> records is unreachable from CShapes alone: no feature offers it, and the step the row was
> previously bound to spans exactly `{1940}` while both neighbours also contain 1940, so no
> year selects it — the one case in issue 100 that no year edit can make deterministic. It
> carried 245,035 km², 26% above its own declared figure.
>
> **The proxy, stated rather than implied.** Northern Transylvania is approximated by eight
> modern Romanian counties (Satu Mare, Maramureș, Sălaj, Bistrița-Năsăud, Cluj, Mureș,
> Harghita, Covasna) totalling 43,685 km² against the award's 43,104 — **+1.3%**. Bihor is
> excluded: the award took only its northern part, and GADM carries Bihor as one 7,548 km²
> county, so including it whole would overshoot by 24%. `polygon_status` is therefore
> `proxy`, not `assigned`.
>
> That the subtraction lands within **146 km²** of a figure derived independently from
> history is the strongest available check on the proxy — the two were arrived at by
> unrelated routes.


This row tracks Romania for **7 years**, from the three 1940 territorial
losses until the 1947 Treaty of Paris formally restored northern
Transylvania [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Romania lost approximately 100,000 km² in 1940 via three simultaneous
events: Soviet ultimatum (Bessarabia + Northern Bukovina, ~50,000 km²),
Second Vienna Award (northern Transylvania to Hungary, ~43,000 km²), and
Treaty of Craiova (Southern Dobruja to Bulgaria, ~7,700 km²). Resulting
territory: ~194,000 km².

Romania joined the Axis, fought on the Eastern Front, then switched to
the Allied side via King Michael's coup (23 August 1944). Northern
Transylvania was de facto reoccupied by Soviet forces from August 1944;
formally restored by the Treaty of Paris (10 February 1947).

Predecessor: [rou-1920-1940](rou-1920-1940.md) -- three 1940 territorial
losses.
Successor: [rou-1947-2025](rou-1947-2025.md) -- northern Transylvania
restored.

## Split rationale

ROU-1940-2025 was a single span using a CShapes 1940 polygon (~194,000
km²). This understated Romania's territory for 1947-2025 by ~22%
(missing northern Transylvania ~43,000 km²). The 1947 Treaty of Paris is
a documented boundary change qualifying for a polity split under WHEP
rules. This page covers the 1940-1947 period; [rou-1947-2025](rou-1947-2025.md)
covers 1947-2025 with the correct ~238,000 km² polygon.

## Polygon caveat (added 2026-07-24, SUPERSEDED 2026-08-05, verified 2026-08-17)

> **Historical record only.** The 26% overstatement described below was removed by the
> `constructed` builder documented under *Territorial extent*. Re-measured 2026-08-17 from
> the published GeoPackage: **193,829 km²**, i.e. **0.08% below** the declared 194,000 —
> not 245,035 / +26%. `polygon_status` is `proxy`, not `estimate`. The diagnosis of *why*
> CShapes cannot supply this row's territory still stands, and is exactly why the
> replacement subtracts a GADM county proxy instead of selecting a CShapes vintage.

`polygon_status` downgraded from `assigned` to **`estimate`** by
`scripts/validate_polygons.py`, which found the attached geometry measures
**245,035 km²** against this page's stated **194,000 km²** (a 26% divergence).

The stated figure is the historically correct one: after the 1940 cessions —
Bessarabia and northern Bukovina to the USSR, northern Transylvania to Hungary
(Second Vienna Award), southern Dobruja to Bulgaria — Romania held roughly
195,000 km². **CShapes 2.0 does not model the 1940-44 Hungarian occupation of
northern Transylvania**, so no exact feature exists for this row's territory:
its 1940-1940 feature (245,066 km²) is pre-cession and its 1940-2019 feature
(237,379 km²) is the post-1947 restored state.

The attached polygon therefore **overstates this row's territory by ~26%**,
entirely in northern Transylvania and the ceded eastern provinces. Resolving it
needs a composed polygon or an alternative historical GIS source.
