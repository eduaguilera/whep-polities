# Van Diemen's Land (Tasmania) (to 1900)

## Summary

The British colony of Van Diemen's Land (renamed Tasmania on 1 January 1856) from its separation from New South Wales on 3 December 1825 to the eve of Australian federation. The island had been under NSW jurisdiction since the first British settlement at Risdon Cove in 1803; Governor Darling's 1825 proclamation made it a distinct colony. The WHEP row ends on 31 December 1900 before the 1 January 1901 federation.
