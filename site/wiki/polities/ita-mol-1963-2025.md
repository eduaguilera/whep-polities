# Molise

## Summary

Molise is one of the 20 regions (regioni) of the Italian Republic, located in south-central Italy on the Adriatic coast between Abruzzo to the north, Lazio and Campania to the west and south, and Puglia to the southeast. It is Italy's second-smallest region by area (after Valle d'Aosta) and its second-least populous, comprising the provinces of Campobasso and Isernia, with Campobasso as the regional capital. This entry is typed `subnational` because Molise has never been a sovereign state or held international personality of its own: it is, and always has been since its creation, a constituent administrative region of Italy, exercising the devolved regional powers set out in Title V of the Italian Constitution (health, regional planning, some economic development functions) while defence, foreign affairs, and most fiscal policy remain with the national government in Rome. Molise did not exist as a distinct administrative unit until 1963; before that date the territory was combined with Abruzzo in the single compartimento (and later region) of Abruzzi e Molise, itself formed shortly after Italian unification in the 1860s from the former Kingdom of the Two Sicilies' Abruzzo Citeriore, Abruzzo Ulteriore I and II, and Contado di Molise provinces.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry was NOT created from any data row in this repository's ingested sources -- no reporting unit in the current panel is labelled "Molise" or reports statistics at Molise's specific territorial scope, and no `unit_id` is attached to the routing decision. It was created purely for container-completeness: Italy's regional-level members in this table summed to only 0.956 of the country's total territory without a Molise row, because Molise (created 1963) was simply missing from the set of 20 modern Italian regions otherwise represented. Adding this row lets ITA-1919-2025's members tile the whole country rather than leaving an unexplained 4.4% gap concentrated in one identifiable region.

What confirms Molise is a distinct administrative entity, independent of any data source routing to it: Italian national law itself. Legge 27 dicembre 1962, n. 1841 split the compartimento of Abruzzi e Molise into the two separate regions Abruzzo and Molise, effective 1963, and Molise has been listed as a separate first-level region in every Italian statistical and constitutional enumeration since (it is explicitly named as one of the 20 regions in Article 131 of the Italian Constitution as amended). This is a case where the entity's distinctness rests on administrative/legal fact rather than on any source in this repository currently tabulating it separately -- a caveat this page states explicitly rather than implying a source reports Molise on its own.

This matches published Italian national statistics (ISTAT lists Molise at 4,460 km2), which is the independent confirmation that the polygon's extent is correct for the territory it claims to represent.

**Locating it on a map:** Molise sits on Italy's Adriatic side, roughly 200 km southeast of Rome and directly south of Abruzzo. It is bounded by the Apennines inland and a short (about 35 km) Adriatic coastline around Termoli in the northeast. Its two provinces, Campobasso (regional capital) and Isernia, together cover the whole region; no part of the modern region's boundary has changed since the 1970 provincial reorganisation that created Isernia province out of part of Campobasso province, and the regional boundary itself has been fixed since 1963.

## Predecessors and successors

Molise has no declared predecessor in this table: the region did not exist as a separate administrative unit before 4 December 1963, when Italian law (Legge 27 dicembre 1962, n. 1841, effective 1963) split the historical compartimento of Abruzzi e Molise into two autonomous regions, Abruzzo and Molise. That undivided predecessor region has no corresponding row in the current polity table (see open question oq-missing-abruzzi-e-molise-predecessor-row), so predecessor is left empty rather than pointed at an unrelated neighbour. Molise has no successor: it remains one of the 20 first-level regions of the Italian Republic through the present, so successor is empty and end_year 2025 marks the edge of this table's modelled horizon, not a real dissolution.

## Open questions

### oq-missing-abruzzi-e-molise-predecessor-row

**No predecessor row exists for the pre-1963 Abruzzi e Molise region**

Molise was not created from bare unclaimed territory: from Italian unification through 1963 it was administered jointly with Abruzzo as the single region Abruzzi e Molise (a compartimento gazetted in the 1860s). No row in the current polity table represents that joint region for any span before 1963 -- ITA-ABR-1970-2025 only begins in 1970, after the split, and there is no ITA-CAM-1970-2025-style row for the undivided predecessor. Until such a row is created (something like ITA-ABM-<start>-1963), this page cannot honestly claim a predecessor, and Abruzzo's own page should be checked for whether it makes the same omission. Resolving this would let both Molise and Abruzzo declare a real predecessor edge instead of leaving 1861-1963 uncontainered by any subnational Abruzzi/Molise row.
