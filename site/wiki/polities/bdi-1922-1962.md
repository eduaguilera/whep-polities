# Burundi (within Ruanda-Urundi)

## Summary

This row covers Urundi/Burundi as the southern residency of the Belgian
mandate (later UN trust territory) of Ruanda-Urundi, from the 1922 League
of Nations mandate to independence on 1962-07-01. It exists because
FAOSTAT reports Burundi (area 29) separately already in 1961, i.e. the
data's territory is Burundi alone — per the sub-territory rule it must
NOT be matched up to a Ruanda-Urundi aggregate (CShapes cowcode 515),
whose extent is roughly twice as large.

## Territorial extent

The Urundi residency's boundary is continuous with the independent
Republic of Burundi (the mandate's internal Ruanda/Urundi split follows
the pre-colonial kingdoms, which became the two successor states
unchanged). CShapes 2.0 feature cowcode 516 at vintage 1962 measures
27,308 km2.

## Predecessors and successors

- Successor: BDI-1962-2025 (independent Burundi, same extent).
- The parent trust territory Ruanda-Urundi (CShapes 515) is not currently
  modelled as a WHEP polity; if aggregate-labelled data ("Ruanda-Urundi")
  surfaces in pre-1961 sources it needs its own polity, not this row.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **29 "Burundi"** reports from 1961; the 1961 year
routes here, 1962 onward to BDI-1962-2025
(`pipelines/faostat-era-matching`, iso3 BDI; shared boundary years route
to the successor).

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 cowcode 516 at vintage 1962 (independence).
  Zero-drift in practice: the residency boundary equals the successor
  state's boundary.
