## Summary

The former German East Africa territory (Tanganyika) under British military administration, 1920-1922, in the transitional period between the German defeat in WWI and the formal establishment of the League of Nations Mandate. "The country was formed in 1964 as a result of the union of mainland Tanganyika" and Zanzibar [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania). Tanganyika was a British mandate then trustee territory; independent in 1961 [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
