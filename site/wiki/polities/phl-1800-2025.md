# Philippines (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for the Philippines, 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. It spans
Spanish rule to the Treaty of Paris (1898-12-10), American colonial rule —
including the Philippine-American War (1899-1902), the Commonwealth
(1935-1946) and the Japanese occupation (1942-1945) — independence
(1946-07-04), and the republic since. The archipelago's extent is unchanged
across the span (~24.27 sq deg in the CShapes feature); every event above
changed sovereignty, not territory.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature 840 at its
  **1898** vintage, back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025. That
  feature's status is colony, ruler USA. Area ~24.27 sq deg.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** No predecessor row (WHEP temporal boundary at 1800).
- **Successor:** No successor row; this row runs to the present.

Splits at 1898 and 1946 were once planned and never applied; both are changes of
sovereign over the same archipelago (2026-08-17, issue #25).

## Sourced claims

- American rule of the islands lasted nearly 50 years; independence followed in 1946 after Japanese occupation during WWII. [biger-1995 §philippines](../sources/biger-1995.md#philippines)
- **1899-1902: Philippine-American War.** Armed conflict between Filipino
  republicans and the United States.
- **1935: Commonwealth established.** Philippine Commonwealth created as
  transition to full independence.
- **1942-1945: Japanese occupation.** Japan occupied the Philippines
  during WWII.
- **1946-07-04: Independence.** Republic of the Philippines established.
  Not a row boundary: the row continues to 2025.
