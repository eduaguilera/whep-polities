# China (1895-1913)

## Summary

This row covers China from the **Treaty of Shimonoseki (1895)** through the
end of **1912** (under [start, end) convention: years 1895-1912 inclusive),
spanning the late Qing Dynasty and the opening year of the Republic of China
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 710
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The row begins with the Treaty of Shimonoseki (17 April 1895), ceding Taiwan
and the Pescadores to Japan. It ends at 1913 (exclusive), covering the Xinhai
Revolution (1911), the proclamation of the Republic of China (1 January 1912),
and the abdication of the last Qing emperor Puyi (12 February 1912). The
year 1912 is deliberately included in this row: the Xinhai Revolution was a
regime change, not a territorial change, so the Qing-era CShapes polygon
(feature 710, vintage 1899) adequately represents China's territory throughout
1912. The next CShapes polygon change for COW 710 occurs at 1913, driving the
boundary with CHN-1913-1914.

**End year extended from 1912 to 1913** to close the structural one-year gap
identified in issue `china-mainland-chn-1912-polity-dates-gap`. Under WHEP's
[start, end) convention, `end_year=1912` excluded 1912 from coverage; setting
`end_year=1913` places 1912 inside [1895, 1913) and eliminates the gap.

Predecessor per CSV: [chn-1800-1895](chn-1800-1895.md). Successor per
CSV: [chn-1913-1914](chn-1913-1914.md).

  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

  the row's end (1913 exclusive). The Xinhai Revolution of 1911-1912 was
  an internal regime change with no significant territorial cession or
  acquisition, so the 1899 polygon remains a reasonable approximation for
  1912.
- **1898: New Territories leased to Britain** (99-year lease). A lease, not
  a cession; the New Territories are not subtracted from the CShapes polygon.
