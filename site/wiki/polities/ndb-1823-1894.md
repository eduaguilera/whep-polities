# Ndebele Kingdom

## Summary

The Ndebele Kingdom (also known as the Matabele Kingdom) is a WHEP polity row covering
Matabeleland — the southwestern region of modern Zimbabwe — from 1823 to 1894. The 1823
start date reflects the founding of the kingdom by Mzilikazi kaMashobane after he broke
from Shaka's Zulu Kingdom and led his followers northward. The 1894 end date marks the
defeat of the Ndebele military by the British South Africa Company (BSAC) at the
conclusion of the First Matabele War (1893–1894), after which the territory was
incorporated into BSAC-administered Rhodesia. The polygon is from Paine et al. (2024),
as this pre-colonial polity predates CShapes' 1886 coverage.

## Territorial extent

Matabeleland, the core territory of the Ndebele Kingdom, centred on the Bulawayo plateau
in southwestern Zimbabwe. The kingdom exercised tribute and raiding authority over a
wider zone including parts of modern Botswana and western Zimbabwe, but the sedentary
political core was Matabeleland proper. The WHEP polygon source is Paine et al. (2024);
it is a pre-colonial polygon with inherent spatial uncertainty for this period.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 records the territory from 1891 under cowcode 552 as a British colony
  (owner=200), meaning CShapes treats the Ndebele polity as already subsumed into the
  BSAC colonial structure by 1891. The WHEP NDB row captures the pre-colonial kingdom
  that CShapes does not model. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- "In 1889, Cecil Rhodes, who had secured mineral concessions in Matabeleland, was
  chartered and received many of the powers of the British government. In 1893, the
  British South Africa Company seized Matabeleland" (p.395). [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- ZAPU (Nkomo, Ndebele) and ZANU (Mugabe, Shona) launched guerrilla war against the
  white-minority government in the 1970s — Biger's reference to the Ndebele ethnic base
  of ZAPU situates Matabeleland's political identity as persisting into the independence
  era. [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)

## Open questions

### oq-mzilikazi-founding-date

Founding date precision. The 1823 start year reflects the period when Mzilikazi broke
from the Zulu Kingdom and began the northward migration. The kingdom was fully
consolidated in Matabeleland closer to 1838–1840 after the Boer-Ndebele wars expelled
the Ndebele from the Transvaal. A future ingest should pin the 1823 vs. 1838 question
with a sourced academic reference.

### oq-first-matabele-war-end-date

End date of First Matabele War. The CSV records 1894 as the end year. The war is
conventionally dated 1893–1894, with the fall of Bulawayo in November 1893 and formal
annexation in 1894. Whether 1893 or 1894 is the correct WHEP end year should be
confirmed against a sourced account of the annexation.
