# Loire-Atlantique (département)

## Summary

This entry captures Loire-Atlantique, a French département on the Atlantic coast at the mouth of the Loire, seat Nantes, INSEE code 44. It is `type: subnational` because France held continuous national sovereignty over this territory throughout the entry's span -- the row exists to carry a sub-national reporting/administrative unit, not a separate state, mirroring how ESP's province rows (ESP-AS, ESP-CA, etc.) sit beneath the Spanish national chain. Loire-Inférieure (its name until a 1957 rename to Loire-Atlantique) was one of the original départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform, and unlike Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, or the overseas départements, it was not one of the four documented departures from that default creation-and-continuity pattern, so its natural span runs from 1790 to the present. This entry's start_year is set to 1800 rather than 1790, a deliberate departure explained in the decisions below, because the FRA national container chain in the polity table does not extend earlier than 1800 and a container edge cannot be built for an uncovered decade. Across its span the département's identity and (per the routing decision) its boundary are treated as constant; no geometry is attached because no département-level boundary source for France is currently registered in this repository.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id FRA-FRG01) identifies Loire-Atlantique, a French département, as a distinct reporting unit with no row count or year-range given in the decision payload itself -- the payload documents an identity/routing decision (that this administrative unit needs its own polity row) rather than enumerating the underlying data rows, which is the harness's normal division of labour between the matcher stage and this authoring stage. What is stated explicitly is the territorial identity to be captured: the modern département of Loire-Atlantique (formerly Loire-Inférieure until 1957), INSEE code 44.

Previously matched to, and why wrong: per the routing_reasoning, any statistics keyed to this département would previously have fallen through to one of the FRA national rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, or FRA-1919-2025), each of which represents the whole of metropolitan France -- roughly 80-times the area of a single département -- and none of which distinguishes Loire-Atlantique from any other French région or département. Routing département-level statistics to a national total silently discards the sub-national resolution the source data actually carries.

What confirms this entity is distinct: Loire-Atlantique/Loire-Inférieure is one of France's original 1790 Revolutionary départements, a legally and administratively distinct unit with its own INSEE code, préfecture (Nantes), and continuous existence to the present day -- the same kind of formally constituted sub-national administrative unit that already justifies the 57 ESP province rows and other <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT> entries in this table (e.g. DZA-CVD-1902-1919). No Federico-Tena or other cross-reference was consulted for this specific entry in this session; that remains open below.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: `none`. No polygon is available in the GeoPackage for this period, and none is being borrowed as a proxy, because no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml carries French département-level geometry at any vintage -- cshapes-europe and cshapes-2.0 (the two FRA sources with local data present) digitise France only as a single national polygon, and gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a subset of 81 countries that excludes France. Attaching France's national outline as a stand-in would overstate this département's territory by roughly two orders of magnitude and was rejected for that reason (documented in the routing decision as `polygon_route: new_source_needed`).

Territory description: Loire-Atlantique is a French département on the Atlantic coast of Brittany/Pays de la Loire, at the estuary of the Loire river, with Nantes as its préfecture and Saint-Nazaire as its major port city. On a modern map it is bounded by the départements of Morbihan and Ille-et-Vilaine to the north, Maine-et-Loire to the east, Vendée to the south, and the Atlantic Ocean to the west. Its modern area is approximately 6,815 km2 (the officially published INSEE figure for the département), which this entry treats as approximately constant across its whole 1800-2025 span per the routing decision's span_basis, since Loire-Atlantique was not affected by any of the boundary-altering events (Belfort's 1871 split, Alsace-Lorraine's 1871-1919 detachment, Corsica's 1976 split, or the 1946 creation of overseas départements) that motivate departures from the country default elsewhere in FRA's subnational set. This 6,815 km2 figure is drawn from published modern administrative statistics, not measured from any polygon attached to this entry -- none is attached, per the polygon status above.

## Predecessors and successors

Loire-Atlantique has no predecessor or successor polity row: it was created directly by the 1790 Revolutionary reform of France into départements (as Loire-Inférieure, covering the mouth of the Loire around Nantes) and has persisted under French sovereignty ever since, renamed to Loire-Atlantique in 1957 with no territorial change. It is not carved from, nor merged into, any other entry in this table -- it sits as a subnational leaf beneath the FRA national chain (FRA-1800-1871 → FRA-1871-1919 → FRA-1919-2025) for its entire span, which is why both predecessor and successor lists are empty.

## Sourced claims

- Loire-Atlantique was created in 1790 as Loire-Inférieure, one of the original départements of Revolutionary France, and was not among the départements later detached, merged, or newly created (Belfort split off from Haut-Rhin in 1871, Alsace-Lorraine annexed 1871-1919, Corsica split into two départements in 1976, or the overseas départements created in 1946).
- The département was renamed from Loire-Inférieure to Loire-Atlantique in 1957 by decree, with its territorial boundary unchanged; it carries INSEE code 44 and its préfecture is Nantes.
- No French département-level polygon layer (GADM ADM2 for FRA, or IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS) is currently registered in scripts/sources.yaml or present in the local geodata store, per the polygon_reasoning recorded in the routing decision that produced this entry.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the département's actual 1790 creation date**

The routing decision's span_basis states Loire-Atlantique (as Loire-Inférieure) was created in 1790 by the Revolutionary départemental reform and is not among the four documented departures from the country default (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica split, overseas départements), so its natural span would run 1790-2025. This entry instead starts at 1800 because the FRA national container chain in the polity table begins at FRA-1800-1871 -- there is no FRA row covering 1790-1800 -- and the harness's own rule requires every container edge to fall inside both parties' spans with no gap in the tiling. Rather than leave 1790-1800 either uncontained or covered by a fabricated edge referencing a non-existent row, this entry's start_year is pulled forward to 1800 to match the earliest available container, and the resulting truncation of the département's true history is flagged as an open question rather than silently absorbed.

## Open questions

### oq-1790-1800-gap-uncontained

**The département's actual 1790-1800 existence is not represented by any row**

Loire-Atlantique (as Loire-Inférieure) was created in 1790 along with the other original French départements, per the routing decision's span_basis. This entry's start_year was set to 1800 instead of 1790 solely because the FRA national chain's earliest row, FRA-1800-1871, does not extend back to 1790, and no container edge can be built for a decade with no covering national row. If a future FRA-<earlier>-1800 national row is ever added to the polity table, this entry's start_year should be revised down to 1790 and a new container edge added, rather than leaving the first decade permanently unaccounted for.

### oq-no-polygon-source-needed

**No département-level boundary source is registered for France at any vintage**

polygon_source is 'none' because, per the routing decision's polygon_reasoning, every FRA source registered in scripts/sources.yaml and present locally (cshapes-europe, cshapes-2.0) digitises France only at the national level, and gadm-4.1-adm1's local file excludes France from its 81-country subset. A département-level layer -- global GADM ADM2, or IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS -- would need to be registered and licence-checked before any polygon, including a modern-boundary proxy, could be attached here. Until then this entry, and any other French département row, carries no geometry at all rather than a borrowed national outline that would grossly overstate the territory.

### oq-1957-rename-continuity

**Loire-Inférieure renamed Loire-Atlantique in 1957 -- confirm no boundary change accompanied the rename**

The routing decision states the 1957 renaming was of the same territory, with no boundary change, which is why a single row spans both names. This has not been independently verified against an administrative-history source (e.g. INSEE's own département history, or a French statistical yearbook of the period) in this session; if the rename in fact coincided with any commune transfer (as happened to some neighbouring Loire-region départements during 20th-century boundary tidying), the 'essentially unchanged' assumption underlying treating this as one continuous row would need re-examination.
