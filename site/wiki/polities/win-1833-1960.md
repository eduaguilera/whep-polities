## Why this polity exists

FAO 1952 reports the British Windward Islands as a reporting unit in its own right,
alongside — not inside — its island sub-entries. Three label variants carrying 13 rows
had no polity to land on (issue 18):

| label | rows | years | table |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Windward Islands` | 6 | 1950 | land use (all six use classes) |
| `British West Indies Windward Islands` | 5 | 1937, 1951 | population |
| `British West Indies  Windward Islands` | 2 | 1949-1950 | centrifugal sugar |

Its sibling BLI-1833-1960 (British Leeward Islands Colony) was created for exactly this
reason and by exactly this argument. The Windward half was left out, so the label went
unresolved while the Leeward one routed.

The only two alternatives were both wrong by a large factor. BWI-1833-1962, the whole
British West Indies aggregate, is ~34,766 km² — 16× this colony. Picking any single
island understates it 3–6×. The FAO rows are group totals: the same 1950 land-use table
carries `Windward Islands` at 215.0 thousand ha AND `Windward Islands  Grenada` at 34.0,
`Windward Islands  St Lucia` at 62.0 and `Windward Islands  St Vincent` at 34.0 — a
parent row and its parts, in the same column.

## Territorial scope

The composition is not assumed; the source's own group total measures it.

| Island | ISO3 | GADM 4.1 adm0 (km²) | WHEP polity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grenada (with the Grenadines it holds) | GRD | 359.5 | GRD-1800-2025 |
| Saint Lucia | LCA | 614.4 | LCA-1838-2025 |
| Saint Vincent and the Grenadines | VCT | 397.9 | VCT-1833-2025 |
| Dominica | DMA | 754.2 | DMA-1800-2025 |
| **union** | | **2,126.1** | |

FAO 1952's `Windward Islands` land-use *total* for 1950 is **215.0 thousand hectares =
2,150 km²**. The four-island union is 2,126.1 km², a **1.1% agreement** — which is what
fixes the composition rather than leaving it to be argued. The three island sub-entries
printed in the same table sum to only 1,300 km²; Dominica's 754 km² is the difference,
so Dominica is in and Barbados (434.6 km²) is out. Both facts match the administrative
history: Barbados left the Windward Islands government in 1885, and Dominica was
transferred *in* from the Leeward Islands in 1940.

Per-island cross-checks against the same table, in km² (FAO thousand-ha × 10 against
GADM):

| sub-entry | FAO 1950 | GADM | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Windward Islands  Grenada` | 340 | 359.5 | −5.4% |
| `Windward Islands  St Lucia` | 620 | 614.4 | +0.9% |
| `Windward Islands  St Vincent` | 340 | 397.9 | −14.6% |

Population is consistent with the same four-island reading: FAO gives the group 247
thousand in 1937 and 279 thousand in 1951. Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and
Dominica together were ~286 thousand in 1951; without Dominica ~234 thousand. FAO
evidently applied its 1951 composition across the whole series, which is why the single
polity row is the honest representation of what the source reports.

## Span

Start year 1833: the year the Windward Islands were placed under a single
Governor-in-Chief, and the same start BLI-1833-1960 and BWI-1833-1962 use, so the three
colonial-aggregate rows of the British Caribbean begin together. End year 1960: the
Windward Islands government was dissolved on 1 January 1960 when Grenada, Saint Lucia,
Saint Vincent and Dominica became separate colonies within the West Indies Federation.

**Composition drift inside the span, stated rather than smoothed.** The polygon is the
post-1940 composition, which is the composition the data measures (1937–1951, with the
group totals reflecting the four-island unit throughout). For 1833–1885 the colony also
included Barbados, and for 1833–1940 it excluded Dominica; a strict rendering would be
three polity rows. This row does not split, for the same reason BLI-1833-1960 does not:
the FAO period straddles the transfer, and no more granular source for this group is in
the repository. See the open question below.

## Polygon decision

`polygon_source: constructed`, `polygon_method: composed-union`, built by
`build_win_1833_1960()` in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py` as the union of GADM
4.1 adm0 GRD, LCA, VCT and DMA. Status is **proxy**, not assigned, because the recipe is
a modern-boundary stand-in for a historical administrative grouping, not a historical
feature.

`polygon_area_km2: 2150` is **FAO 1952's own stated figure** (215.0 thousand hectares,
total land use, 1950), not a measurement of this page's polygon. That is deliberate:
`validate_polygons.py` check A2 refuses a declared area within 0.1% of its own geometry,
on the ground that such a figure is not evidence about the territory. The built union is
2,126.1 km², 1.1% below the source, and the gap is the real disagreement between GADM's
coastlines and a 1952 yearbook — visible only because the two numbers come from
different places.

Why constructed rather than an existing source:

- **CShapes 2.0** carries sovereign states and a small set of dependencies; it has no
  Windward Islands Colony feature. Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and Dominica
  appear only from their 1974–1979 independence dates.
- **Cliopatria** has no Windward Islands entry.
- **GADM 4.1** has adm0 polygons for all four constituents, and all four are islands
  whose physical extent is invariant over 1833–1960, so the union carries no coastline
  vintage error — only the composition caveat above.

**Polygon confidence:** medium. The extent is right to 1.1% against the source's own
stated group area for the data years; the ~35% area error for the pre-1940 configuration
(−754 km² Dominica, +435 km² Barbados before 1885) is not captured.

## Relationship to the other British Caribbean aggregates

| polity | km² | this colony as a share |
|---|---|---|
| BWI-1833-1962 British West Indies | 34,766 declared / 32,912 built | 6.1% |
| WIN-1833-1960 Windward Islands | 2,150 declared / 2,126 built | — |
| BLI-1833-1960 Leeward Islands | 1,716 declared / 1,727 built | — |

BLI and WIN both claim Dominica, and that is not a modelling error: the island was a
Leeward Island until 1940 and a Windward Island after, and both rows span 1940. The
double claim is confined to the 754 km² of Dominica and is recorded here and on
`bli-1833-1960.md`.

A measurement worth recording while it was in hand: FAO 1952's `Leeward Islands` 1950
land-use total is 109.0 thousand ha = 1,090 km², against BLI's 1,727 km² built polygon —
because by 1950 the Leeward Islands no longer held Dominica. ATG + KNA + MSR + VGB is
972.9 km², and adding Anguilla (~91 km², administered with Saint Kitts-Nevis until 1967)
gives ~1,064 km², within 2.4% of the FAO figure. That confirms BLI's own documented
Dominica caveat from the source side. It is left as BLI's open question, not changed
here.

## Open questions

### oq-win-composition-split

A strict rendering is three rows: WIN-1833-1885 (GRD+LCA+VCT+BRB, ~1,806 km²),
WIN-1885-1940 (GRD+LCA+VCT, ~1,372 km²) and WIN-1940-1960 (GRD+LCA+VCT+DMA, ~2,126 km²).
The FAO 1952 rows that motivate this polity are 1937–1951 and their group totals use the
four-island composition throughout, so a split would route the 1937 population row to a
polity whose extent the source itself does not use. Revisit if pre-1937 Windward Islands
data enters the repository.

### oq-win-grenadines-double-count

The Grenadines chain is administratively divided between Grenada (Carriacou, Petite
Martinique) and Saint Vincent. GADM 4.1 assigns each island to exactly one of the two
adm0 polygons, so the union neither double-counts nor drops any of it. Recorded because
the split is a standing trap for anyone re-deriving this area from a per-island list.

### oq-win-dominica-shared-with-bli

Dominica's 754 km² is inside both BLI-1833-1960 and WIN-1833-1960 for the years both
rows span. Any analysis that sums the two aggregates double-counts it. The correct fix is
the composition split in both rows (this page's `oq-win-composition-split` and
`bli-1833-1960.md`'s `oq-dominica-split`); until then the overlap is documented in both
places.
