# Vietnam (combined reporting: DRV and RVN)

## Summary

This row is a FAOSTAT reporting-area composite for divided-era Vietnam,
from the 1954 Geneva partition to the 1975 fall of Saigon. FAOSTAT
reporting area 237 "Viet Nam" carries a single series back to 1961 whose
territory is the WHOLE of Vietnam — both the Democratic Republic (north,
DRV-1954-1975) and the Republic (south, RVN-1954-1975) — so its 1961-1974
years can be routed neither to DRV nor to RVN without misstating the
extent by roughly half. Aggregates are first-class per WHEP rules; this
composite is the honest routing target, exactly as F51/F228/F248 are for
FAOSTAT's Czechoslovakia/USSR/Yugoslav SFR areas.

The F-prefix follows that convention: F237 = FAOSTAT area code 237.

## Territorial extent

The union of North and South Vietnam equals the territory of the
post-1976 unified state (the partition line was internal). Polygon:
CShapes 2.0 cowcode 816 at vintage 1976 (unified Vietnam), 325,295 km2.
`polygon_status: estimate` because the feature vintage post-dates the
period — but the union extent is identical, so the drift is nominal.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: VNM-1887-1954 (French Indochina Vietnam).
- Successor: VNM-1975-2025 (unified Socialist Republic).
- Components (already modelled): DRV-1954-1975, RVN-1954-1975. Data
  labelled for one zone alone must route to the component, never here.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **237 "Viet Nam"**: 1961-1974 routes here;
1975 onward to VNM-1975-2025 (`pipelines/faostat-era-matching`, iso3
VNM; shared boundary years route to the successor).

## Decisions

- **Composite over components:** FAOSTAT's back-cast series covers both
  zones; splitting it 60/40 across DRV/RVN would be an invention.
- **Span 1954-1975:** the divided configuration's lifetime; the start
  matches the partition even though FAOSTAT data begins 1961, so any
  1954-1960 whole-Vietnam data found later routes here too.
