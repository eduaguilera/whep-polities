# Libya (1934-1943)

## Summary

Italian Libya as a unified colony from the 1934 merger of Italian Tripolitania and Italian Cyrenaica into a single "Italian Libya" to the 1943 Allied defeat of Axis forces in North Africa. CShapes records this period as `status=colony, ruler=ITA` per CSV notes.

### the Fezzan question cannot be answered from these series

Recorded 2026-08-18. See `lby-1925-1934` for the shared label analysis.

A queued note reported that "sampled IIA staple crops for 1934-1942 (olives,
olive oil, grapes, unmanufactured tobacco, wine)" are coastal-belt crops whose
composition does not change after the **1939 Fezzan incorporation**, partially
answering `oq-lby-1939-fezzan`.

**Three of those five series end in 1937** and a fourth in 1939: grapes, tobacco
and wine all stop at 1937, olives at 1939. Only **olive oil** reaches 1940-1945.
So four of the five cited series cannot speak to a 1939 change at all — they had
already ended — and the fifth ranges from 0 (1935) to 4,100 (1925).

The note's *reasoning* is sound in kind: Fezzan's oasis agriculture would show up
as new items or a composition shift, and none appears. But with the evidence
ending before or at the event, `oq-lby-1939-fezzan` **stays open** rather than
being partially answered. What can be said is narrower and worth keeping: for the
items IIA reports, coverage stops before Fezzan could have entered them.
