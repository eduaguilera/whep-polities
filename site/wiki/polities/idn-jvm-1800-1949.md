# Java and Madura (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Java with Madura, the Dutch East Indies' standard statistical unit (Java en Madoera), until the 1949 transfer of sovereignty (IDN-JVM-1949-1951 continues it inside Indonesia). The two islands' own rows, [idn-jav-1800-1949](idn-jav-1800-1949.md) and [idn-mad-1800-1949](idn-mad-1800-1949.md), are now inside this one.

## Why this entry exists

The IIA and FAO yearbooks report Java and Madura together for 1909-1948 (`dutch java; dutch madura`, 45 pairs and 2,138 value rows in the harmonized dataset, its largest single unrouted label); the islands had rows only one by one. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** The union of IDN-JAV-1800-1949's and IDN-MAD-1800-1949's recipes (GADM 4.1 adm1/adm2), **132,674 km2**, the same outline as the post-1949 IDN-JVM-1949-1951 (132,000 declared). Measured: **132,674 km2** (ESRI:54034). Share of the polygon inside each container: IDN-1800-1945 97%, IDN-1945-1949 97%.
