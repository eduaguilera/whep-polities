# Hungary (1918-1920)

Covers the two years 1918 and 1919, from the Republic proclamation of 1918-11-16 through
end-1919. Polygon: histogis-1860-habsburg, 325,421 km² (pre-Trianon Transleithania). Polygon
vintage drift (~58-60 years ahead of 1860 vintage) is benign: Transleithanian borders were
stable 1867-1920.

## Trianon boundary: why end_year=1920 and the row still stops at 1919

The Treaty of Trianon (4 June 1920) reduced Hungary from ~325,000 km² to ~93,000 km² — a 72% territorial loss. Annual agricultural data spanning 1918-1920 includes harvest-year 1920 observations (wheat n=2, maize n=2, sugar beet n=3). The sugar beet n=3 confirms a 1920-harvest observation is present.

Harvest-year 1920 data reflects the post-Trianon rump state (~93,000 km²). Matching it to this polity's 325,421 km² polygon would overstate territory ~3.5×. Year-1920 data must therefore route to HUN-1920-1938 (CShapes 2.0, ~93,000 km²) — and it does, because **`end_year` is EXCLUSIVE**: `end_year: 1920` covers 1918 and 1919 and stops short of 1920, which is exactly the intent recorded here.

**Corrected 2026-08-17 (issue 252).** On 2026-06-30 this row's `end_year` was moved 1920 -> 1919 to keep year-1920 data off the pre-Trianon polygon. Under the exclusive convention that shortening was one year too far: it made the row cover 1918 ALONE and left **1919 in no Hungarian row**, contradicting the routing table immediately below, which has always said 1919 belongs here. `end_year` is back at 1920 — which excludes 1920 by itself — and the code with it.
