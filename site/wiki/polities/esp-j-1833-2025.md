# Jaén (province of Spain)

## Summary

Jaén is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, and since 1981 part of the Andalusia autonomous community (one of the eight provinces making up that community, unlike the one-province-equals-one-community exceptions such as Asturias or Madrid). This entry is type: subnational because Jaén is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated Jaén separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES616, for over a century and a half. Jaén is Spain's largest producer of olive oil, a fact that anchors the kind of production/trade data series this database is built to carry at province granularity rather than folded into the national ESP total.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id ESP-ES616) captures valued statistical rows for unit ES616 ("Jaén"), country Spain, under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (A Coruña/ES111, Asturias/ES120, Madrid/ES-MD, and onward through ES241), following the system default start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform, applied because Jaén is not one of the Canary Islands departure units that take a different start) and an open end_year of 2025 for a still-current unit. Previously matched to: nothing specific -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly that "no existing polity represents Jaén specifically," with the only candidates being A Coruña, Madrid, and national Spain, none of which are Jaén. The fallback in the absence of a dedicated row would have been the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, which would collapse a province-level series into a country-level one, exactly the mismatch already rejected for the other province rows -- Jaén at roughly 13,496 km2 is about 1/37 the area of the ~505,000 km2 national aggregate. What confirms distinctness: Jaén's status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES616) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since the 1833 reform is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling province rows -- continuous, separately-tabulated provincial statistics (notably olive/olive-oil production, Jaén's defining agricultural output) distinguish it from the national aggregate.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_route is new_source_needed pointing at mapspain-ign (provinces.gpkg) with id_column=cpro (the Spanish INE province numeric code), under which Jaén is conventionally cpro 23, but the polygon_detail states plainly that mapspain-ign, while registered in sources.yaml with the correct schema and id_column and already used for the sibling ESP province polities, "is not present_locally": the file has not been downloaded or placed on disk, so the route cannot execute until that data is fetched -- exactly the unmet precondition recorded for ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CA-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025. gadm-4.1-adm1 was checked and returns zero matching ESP features at this granularity (its 18 ESP rows are autonomous communities, not provinces), so it was correctly ruled out rather than substituted. Territory description: Jaén occupies inland northeastern Andalusia, bordered by Ciudad Real and Albacete (Castilla-La Mancha) to the north and east, Granada to the south, and Córdoba to the west -- landlocked, dominated by the Guadalquivir river valley and the Sierra Morena and Sierra de Cazorla ranges. Its capital is Jaén city; its economy is famously dominated by olive cultivation, covering a large share of the province's land area and making it Spain's (and the world's) leading olive-oil-producing province. On a modern map its outline is the same as the current province of Jaén within the Andalusia autonomous community, unchanged in outline since the 1833 reform. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 13,496 km2 -- this is the province's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet. Provincial boundaries in Spain have been essentially unchanged since 1833, so once the IGN provinces layer is acquired, vintage risk across the full 1833-2025 span should be low, matching the assessment already made for the sibling ESP province rows.

## Predecessors and successors

Jaén has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory it now covers was organized as the historic Reino de Jaén, one of the four kingdoms carved from the medieval Reino de Granada partition, under the Antiguo Regimen's own jurisdictional structure distinct from the 1833 province in administrative form even though the name and rough territory persisted, and this database does not model sub-kingdom Andalusian divisions at that granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to the sibling ESP province rows. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain (and, since 1981, part of the Andalusia autonomous community) as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention established for the other Spanish NUTS3 units.

## Sourced claims

- INE (Instituto Nacional de Estadistica) and Eurostat both report Jaén as a NUTS3 statistical unit (ES616) with continuous province-level data collection since the 19th century, consistent with its creation as one of the 50 provinces in the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform.
- The routing decision for unit ESP-ES616 records that Jaén is one of the 1833 Javier de Burgos provinces, not affected by the 1927 Canary Islands split departure that applies a different start_year to some other Spanish units, and that no existing polity in this database represented Jaén specifically before this decision.
- INE's official administrative area figure for Jaén province is approximately 13,496 km2, making it the largest province in Andalusia -- Jaén is widely documented (INE/Junta de Andalucía agricultural statistics) as Spain's leading olive-oil-producing province, which is the kind of production series this NUTS3-level row exists to carry separately from the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-as-abbreviation

**Used the single-letter abbreviation J rather than the province numeric code, matching the ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern already established for Spanish provinces (ESP-AS, ESP-CA, ESP-CO, ESP-MD, and the batch of sibling verdicts ES112-ES241) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short letter abbreviation of the unit name rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES616) or the INE province numeric code (23). This page follows that convention exactly, minting ESP-J-1833-2025 rather than a numeric-coded alternative such as ESP-23-1833-2025, so the sibling set of Spanish province rows stays internally consistent and predictable. J was chosen because it is both the standard postal/vehicle-plate abbreviation for Jaén province and the everyday shorthand used in Spanish administrative documents; unlike some provinces that share an initial letter with a neighbour (forcing two-letter codes like CO for Cordoba or CA for Cadiz/Cantabria), Jaén has no other ESP province competing for the single letter J, so no lengthening was needed.

### oq-pre-1833-territory-unmodeled

**The pre-1833 Reino de Jaén has no distinct predecessor polity in this database**

Before the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform, the territory roughly corresponding to modern Jaén province was part of the historic Reino de Jaén, one of the four kingdoms of the old Reino de Granada/Andalusia partition dating to the Reconquista, with its own Antiguo Regimen jurisdictional structure that does not correspond cleanly to the 1833 province's administrative form. This database does not model that earlier structure as a distinct polity row here, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row, mirroring the treatment recorded for the other ESP province entries. Whether the pre-1833 Reino de Jaén (or the wider four-kingdoms structure) should eventually be modeled as its own polity is an open question for the ESP country convention generally, not specific to this entry, but it is live here because Jaén, like Asturias, carries a well-documented pre-1833 name and identity that a future author might mistake for grounds to add a predecessor row without checking the harness's bespoke-code precedent (ALK, HYD, RYU).
