# Rwanda

## Summary

Rwanda is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Rwanda from 1962 to the present. Ruanda-Urundi became two separate nations on 1 July 1962; Ruanda was renamed the Republic of Rwanda.

## Territorial extent

Rwanda (area 10,169 sq. miles; 26,327 sq. km; population 7,700,000 [1993]) is bounded by Uganda (105 miles; 169 km) in the north, Tanzania (135 miles; 217 km) in the east, Burundi (180 miles; 290 km) in the south, and Zaire (135 miles; 217 km) in the west. Capital: Kigali. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Colonized by the Germans in the late nineteenth century, the region, along with neighboring Burundi, was conquered by the Belgians in World War I" (p.453). Catholic missionaries eventually persuaded the colonial authorities to transfer their support to the majority Hutu. The Tutsi king was deposed and in 1962 Rwanda became an independent state. [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- "In 1963, Tutsi refugees who had fled to Uganda attempted to invade Rwanda and reestablish their hegemony. The Hutu responded by massacring 12,000 Tutsi. By 1964 as many as 150,000 Tutsi fled Rwanda" (p.453). [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- "Rwanda fell within the German sphere of influence from 1898 and was administered as part of German East Africa" (p.457). After WWI, Belgium was mandated to hold the area known as Ruanda-Urundi. [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
