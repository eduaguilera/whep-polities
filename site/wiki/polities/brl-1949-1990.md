# Berlin (whole city, West and East, 1949-1990)

## Summary

The whole of Greater Berlin, all four sectors, for the years it was divided into West Berlin (the
three Western sectors, [wbl-1949-1990](wbl-1949-1990.md)) and East Berlin (the Soviet sector, capital
of the GDR, which has no row of its own). It is a statistical unit, not a government: from 1949 no
single authority ran the whole city. It exists because FAO 1952 reports Berlin as one unit after the
division, exactly as it did before it ([brl-1945-1949](brl-1945-1949.md)).
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §berlin](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#berlin)

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952's `Germany Berlin` 1949-1951 rows (24, with the double-spaced `Germany  Berlin` 1951) routed
to [wbl-1949-1990](wbl-1949-1990.md), on the reading that "post-1949 FAO 'Germany Berlin' refers to
Western-bloc West Berlin". The population row refutes that: **3,343 thousand in 1951**. West Berlin
had "about two million inhabitants"; the 1937 figure for the undivided city under the same label is
4,284 thousand. 3.3 million is the whole city, West plus East, not the Western sectors. The label's
1948 row (milk 31 thousand t) sits on [brl-1945-1949](brl-1945-1949.md), the undivided city, and the
1949 value is of the same size (milk 38 thousand t); the 1950 and 1951 milk rows read 0.

`Berlin` (fao1952, goats 5 thousand head in 1949, 1950 and 1951) is the same unit in another table:
its 1949 row routed to BRL-1945-1949 by name, and 1950-1951 to nothing.

Nothing else can carry these rows: WBL-1949-1990 is half the city and has no polygon, and there is no
East Berlin row to register a whole against its parts.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `gadm-4.1-adm1` feature `DEU.3_1` (Land Berlin), the Greater Berlin
perimeter of 1920 that the city kept through the division and after 1990: **891.9 km²** against the
declared 892. The same feature, for the same reason, as [brl-1945-1949](brl-1945-1949.md) and
[brl-1938-1945](brl-1938-1945.md).

## Decisions

- **A whole-city row, not a West Berlin polygon.** The rows are the whole city, so drawing West
  Berlin would not help them; and no fetched source draws the sector boundary (GADM's Berlin has no
  borough level, and CShapes has no city units), so WBL-1949-1990 stays without geometry and now
  receives no row.
- **Inside divided Germany.** The city lies inside [deu-1949-1990](deu-1949-1990.md)'s outline (the
  FRG plus the GDR). FAO 1952 reports `Germany` for these years beside `Germany Berlin`; whether its
  post-1949 `Germany` totals include Berlin is not decided here (for 1948 they do: milk 11,380 =
  Eastern 2,025 + Western 9,324 + Berlin 31), and the pair is registered in the composition table
  with the cells the measurement finds.
