# Ascension Island

## Summary

Ascension Island, "an isolated volcanic island, near 8° south of the Equator in the South Atlantic Ocean". It "was garrisoned by the British Admiralty from 22 October 1815 to 1922", when it became a dependency of Saint Helena ([shn-1834-1967](shn-1834-1967.md), whose polygon is Saint Helena island alone).
