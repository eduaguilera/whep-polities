# Romania (1918-1919)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **2 years**, the transitional "Greater
Romania" formation period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Romania gained massively from WWI: Transylvania and Banat from
Austria-Hungary, Bukovina from Austria, Bessarabia from Russia.
"Greater Romania" roughly doubled in size. Treaty of Trianon (4 June
1920) formalized the Hungarian territorial transfers. The 1919 end
date may align with Treaty of Saint-Germain (Austrian side) or an
intermediate boundary settlement.

Predecessor: [rou-1913-1918](rou-1913-1918.md) -- end of WWI.
Successor: [rou-1919-1920](rou-1919-1920.md) -- border settlement.

- **1918: Territorial gains.** Romania gained Transylvania, Banat, and
  parts of the Partium from Hungary; Bukovina from Austria; Bessarabia
  from Russia. This is the start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

  territorial cessions including Bukovina to Romania.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1920-06-04: Treaty of Trianon.** Formalized the transfer of
  Transylvania and Banat from Hungary to Romania (ratified after the

## Polygon decision

Use the CShapes Trianon-era polygon (~296,087 km²) as a proxy for ROU-1918-1919. **Corrected 2026-08-05 (issues 99, 100): this said `polygon_feature_year=1920`, which did not deliver it.** gwcode 360 has three steps containing 1920 — `1919-1920` (148,934 km²), `1920-1920` (251,719) and `1920-1940` (296,087) — and **two of them start in 1920**, so the tie-break could not choose and shapefile order returned the 251,719 one. The row therefore measured 251,742 km² while this page, and its `polygon_area_km2: 295000`, both said ~295,000: a 15% shortfall against its own documented intent. `polygon_feature_year: 1921` falls inside exactly one candidate span and selects the 296,087 km² Trianon feature, which is what the reasoning below always asked for. The 1918-vintage CShapes polygon (~128,470 km²) is demonstrably wrong: maize production 3,590,551 t and step-changes in beer/rapeseed/flax are consistent only with Greater Romania (~295k km²). De facto Greater Romania existed from late 1918; formal treaties ratified 1919-1920 lagged de facto integration by 1-2 years. The 1920-vintage polygon may slightly overstate boundaries at contested margins (Banat partition etc.) but resolves the 2.3x understatement of the 1918 polygon. Flag: `polygon_vintage_proxy=true`.
