# Sweden (with Finland to 1809)

## Summary

This row tracks Sweden including Finland for **10 years**, from the
WHEP database floor to the Treaty of Fredrikshamn
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Sweden was a major
Baltic power; Finland had been part of Sweden since the 12th century.
The 1808-1809 Finnish War (Russia vs Sweden) ended with the Treaty of
Fredrikshamn (17 September 1809), which ceded Finland to Russia --
a -42% territory loss (~800,000 to ~443,000 km²). The Finland loss
is one of the largest territorial changes in 19th-century Europe.

Predecessor: NA (database floor). Sweden was a major Baltic power.
Successor: [swe-1809-1814](swe-1809-1814.md) -- Treaty of Fredrikshamn
(17 September 1809) ceded Finland to Russia. -42% territory loss
(~800K to ~443K km²).
