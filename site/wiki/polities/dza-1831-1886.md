# Algeria

## Summary

This row tracks French colonial Algeria from 1831 to the 1886 CShapes dependency baseline. French conquest began 1830; the three northern departments were established by 1848. The Sahara was not formally annexed until 24 December 1902 (see DZA-1902-1919).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 feature year 1886, GW code 615. Area = 655,481 km². Covers northern Algeria (departments of Algiers, Oran, Constantine) and the High Plateau zone; does NOT include the Saharan interior.
- **Polygon vintage approximation.** CShapes 2.0 has no Algeria entry before 1886; the earliest available polygon (feature year 1886) is used as a proxy for the full 1831-1886 period. For the first half of the period (1831-c.1860), effective French control was substantially smaller — limited to the coastal littoral and Tell Atlas; consolidation of the interior Tell and High Plateau proceeded through the 1840s-1860s (after defeat of Emir Abdelkader in 1847). The 655k km² polygon overstates controlled territory for approximately the first two decades of the period.
- **Data footprint.** Production data (wheat, oats, tobacco, livestock) measures output from the northern agricultural zones only; this matches the polygon's northern coverage and is not affected by the Saharan exclusion.

## Decisions

- polygon_feature_year corrected from 1962 to 1886: the 1962 CShapes polygon (post-independence, ~2.38M km²) was a severe vintage drift. Replaced with the earliest CShapes entry for Algeria (feature year 1886, 655,481 km²), which covers northern Algeria pre-Sahara annexation and is the same polygon used by DZA-1886-1902. See issue dza-1831-1886-polygon-vintage-drift-1962.
