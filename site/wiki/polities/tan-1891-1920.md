# Tanzania (1891-1920)

## Summary

> **`polygon_status` migrated missing -> unassigned (2026-07-29).** `missing` is a legacy value that predates the documented vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md#page-schema), which says not to use it in new pages. It was never migrated, so the manifest's `claims_polygon_status` set omitted it and consumers were told this row does not claim a polygon. This row has no geometry at all, which is exactly what `unassigned` means. See [lint-polygon-status-vocabulary](../log.md#lint-polygon-status-vocabulary).


This WHEP polity row covers the territory of German East Africa (Tanganyika) from 1891
to 1920, under German colonial administration until World War I, and then under British
occupation/administration until the formal League of Nations mandate assignment in 1922.
The 1891 start date corresponds to the CShapes entry for cowcode=510 as a German colony
(owner=255, start=1891-01-01, area≈81.05 sq.deg.). The 1920 end date reflects the
handover point in the TAN chain; the successor row [tan-1920-1922](tan-1920-1922.md)
covers 1920–1922 and [tan-1922-1964](tan-1922-1964.md) covers the formal mandate period.

The former GEA-1884-2025 row (German East Africa) has been deleted. CShapes has
no separate GEA entity — cowcode 510 (Tanzania/Tanganyika) starting 1891 IS
the German colony, including Ruanda-Urundi territory (area 81.05 sq deg).

## Territorial extent

> **Binding added 2026-08-13 (issue 155).** The frontmatter said `polygon_source: none` while this
> section, the summary and a sourced claim all named CShapes cowcode/gwcode 510 and quoted its area
> — a binding described in prose and never written into the fields, so the build attached nothing and
> this row's **46 layer-B rows** were matched but spatially unusable.
>
> Bound to **gwcode 510 at `polygon_feature_year: 1900`**, which resolves to the single step
> 1891-01-01 → 1916-10-08 (German colony). Measured **991,096 km²** in ESRI:54034, against CShapes'
> own stated `area` of 991,094.75 — and against 81.05 sq.deg., the figure this page already quoted
> for that step. The two later steps inside this row's period (1916-10-09 → 1919-06-27 and
> 1919-06-28 → 1920-06-27) carry the **identical geometry**, so the choice of vintage inside the
> period is immaterial here; 1900 is used because it is the only year that is not shared by two
> steps, which is what `validate_polygon_binding_determinism` requires.
>
> **Documented overstatement, 1919-1920.** CShapes keeps Ruanda-Urundi inside 510 until the
> 1920-06-28 step, so for the last ~18 months of this row the polygon includes the ~52,300 km² that
> Belgium had occupied since 1916 and that [rwb-1919-1922](rwb-1919-1922.md) nominally covers. That
> row carries no geometry today, so nothing is double-counted in the published GeoPackage — but if
> RWB-1919-1922 is ever bound, this pair overlaps and one of the two has to give. Recorded here
> rather than silently absorbed. `polygon_status` is `assigned` because the territory this row
> documents *is* German East Africa including Ruanda-Urundi, which is exactly what the feature is.

German East Africa (Tanganyika) comprised the territory of modern mainland Tanzania,
Rwanda, and Burundi — substantially larger than present-day Tanzania alone. CShapes
records cowcode=510 area≈81.05 sq.deg. for the 1891–1916 period (German colony, owner=255)
and the same area for 1916–1920 (British occupation, owner=200). The stable area across
the German and British occupation periods confirms no territorial change at the WWI
handover — consistent with one WHEP sub-row up to 1920 (or through to 1922, depending
on how the chain is structured).

Tanzania (including Zanzibar) area: 364,900 sq. miles [944,726 sq. km.], population:
28,200,000 [1994]. Bounded by Uganda (246 miles; 396 km.), Kenya (478 miles; 769 km.),
Mozambique (295 miles; 475 km.), Malawi (210 miles; 338 km.), Zambia, Zaire
(305 miles; 490 km.), Burundi (280 miles; 451 km.), Rwanda (135 miles; 217 km.).

## Sourced claims

- "Colonized by the Germans in the late nineteenth century, the region [Rwanda-Urundi],
  along with neighboring Burundi, was conquered by the Belgians in World War I." (p.453).
  This confirms German East Africa included the Ruanda-Urundi sub-region that became
  the Belgian mandate. [biger-1995 §rwanda](../sources/biger-1995.md#rwanda)
- "After World War I, German East Africa, consisting of Rwanda-Urundi and Tanganyika,
  was administered under League of Nations mandates with Belgium responsible for
  Ruanda-Urundi ... and Britain administering Tanganyika until its independence in 1961."
  (p.490). [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)
- "Tanganyika was a British mandate then trustee territory; independent in 1961." (p.489).
  [biger-1995 §tanzania](../sources/biger-1995.md#tanzania)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=510 with status=colony, owner=255 (Germany) from
  1891-01-01 to 1916-10-08 (area≈81.05 sq.deg.); then status=occupied, owner=200
  (Britain) from 1916-10-09 to 1920-06-27 (area≈81.05 sq.deg.). The identical area
  across the German and British occupation periods confirms no territorial change at
  the WWI handover event. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Contradictions

**CShapes area vs. TAN sub-row split at 1920.** CShapes records a different area
(76.79 sq.deg.) for the period 1920-06-28 to 1922-07-19 (cowcode=510, occupied/mandate).
This area reduction from 81.05 to 76.79 sq.deg. between 1920 and 1922 corresponds to
the separation of Ruanda-Urundi (cowcode=515) from the British-administered mainland.
This territorial change is the event that justifies splitting TAN-1891-1920 from
TAN-1920-1922 in the WHEP chain.
