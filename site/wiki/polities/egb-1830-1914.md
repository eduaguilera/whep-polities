# Egba United Government (Abeokuta)

## Summary

The Egba polity centred on Abeokuta from its founding c.1830 (following the collapse of the Oyo Empire in the first half of the 19th century) to 1914, when Egba autonomy was formally ended and the territory was incorporated into the newly-unified Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria. Uniquely among Yoruba states, Egba retained a formally-recognised "Egba United Government" through the late 19th and early 20th centuries, with the Alake as paramount ruler.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Centred on Abeokuta ("under the rock", referencing Olumo Rock) in modern Ogun State, Nigeria. The polity's effective territory extended over the Egba clan's heartland around Abeokuta.

## Sourced claims

- The Egba became independent following "the spectacular collapse of Oyo in the first half of the 19th century"; they subsequently founded Abeokuta, whose name translates as "under the rock", after wars with Dahomey.
