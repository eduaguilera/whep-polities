# Saudi Arabia (before the 2000 boundary treaty)

This reporting territory uses the coherent CShapes feature for its side of
the 2000 Saudi-Yemeni boundary change.

The inherited 1924 start and use of a 1935 polygon for earlier years remain a
pre-existing historical approximation; this correction establishes the 2000
end only. It does not newly validate pre-1935 boundaries.

## Annual boundary convention

The database uses the post-change representation from calendar year **2000**.
CShapes 2.0 changes both GW 670 and GW 678 on **12 June 2000**; the treaty
entered into force on **4 July 2000**. An annual period cannot represent both
parts of that year. The 2000 boundary follows the source polygon transition's
year, not a claim that the border changed on 1 January or that every annual
statistical observation was collected on the new footprint.
[saudi-yemen-boundary-treaty-2000 §dates-and-scope](../sources/saudi-yemen-boundary-treaty-2000.md#dates-and-scope)

## Representation and limits

The coherent CShapes pre-2000 pair and post-2000 pair each have zero raw
spherical overlap in the bounded source probe. Combining pre-2000 Saudi Arabia
with post-2000 Yemen instead creates approximately **2.994 million ha** of raw
overlap. These are measured polygon diagnostics, not independently observed
land transfers. The former registry combined precisely those incompatible
vintages. Source geometries still undergo the existing producer operations;
this change does not certify their shared topology or a global partition.

The US Department of State estimated Yemen's acquisition at **35,000 km²**,
while CShapes' supplied area attributes change by approximately **29,845 km²**.
The bulletin gives no uncertainty interval or demonstrated area calculation;
its estimate is not a calibration target. It describes a previously undelimited
segment, an unpublished datum, an indefinite boundary and pending demarcation.
It also reports a small Saudi acquisition near Jabal ath Tha'ar that is not
represented as a positive Saudi gain by these CShapes features. Retain that
source limitation rather than inventing a polygon to match the estimate.
[us-state-saudi-yemen-boundary-2001 §territorial-effect-and-precision](../sources/us-state-saudi-yemen-boundary-2001.md#territorial-effect-and-precision)

This scoped correction retains the original source and distinguishes legal
dates, annual representation and uncertain cartographic detail. It does not
choose between overlapping claims using source-code priority or area size.
