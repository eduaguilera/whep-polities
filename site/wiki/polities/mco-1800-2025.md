# Monaco

## Summary

This row tracks the Principality of Monaco for **226 years** from the
database start year to the truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Monaco is a
microstate on the Mediterranean coast of southeastern France, near the
Italian border. It shares a single semicircular land boundary with
France of 2.7 miles (4.4 km)
[biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco). During the
period covered by this row, Monaco passed through French Republican
control (pre-1814), Sardinian protection (1814--1861), and French
protection from 1861 onward.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV

- **Area.** Microstate; surrounded on three sides by the French

  [biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco) (p.227).
- **Boundary.** Single boundary with France: 2.7 miles (4.4 km),

## Sourced claims

- The Principality of Monaco was established in 1070 under the
  Grimaldis; it came under Genoa (1297), Spain (1524--1641), and
  French suzerainty from 1641.
  [biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco) (p.227)
- Monaco was taken by the French Republic in 1793; in 1814 it was
  restored to the Grimaldi family and placed under the Kingdom of
  Sardinia; in 1861 it was placed under France on condition of
  incorporation should the ruling prince have no children.
  [biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco) (p.227)
- Monaco contains three small towns: Monaco, Monte Carlo, and La
  Condamin.
  [biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco) (p.227)
- No disputes concerning the France-Monaco boundary alignment;
  recognized by both states.
  [biger-1995 §monaco](../sources/biger-1995.md#monaco) (p.227)
