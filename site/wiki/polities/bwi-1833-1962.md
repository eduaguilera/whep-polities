## Why this polity exists

FAO 1952 reports 24 rows (1937–1951) under the bare label "British West Indies" covering cotton lint, cottonseed, groundnuts, and a population aggregate. These are group-level figures with no island qualifier and cannot be routed to any single island polity. An aggregate polity is required to receive the data without overstating any one territory.

## Territorial scope

The British West Indies colonial aggregate covered the Crown Colony grouping administered from London, including (at minimum): Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Bahamas, Antigua, Grenada, St. Vincent, Dominica, St. Lucia, St. Kitts-Nevis, and Montserrat. British Guiana and British Honduras are sometimes included in administrative sources but are mainland territories; they are excluded here pending review of whether FAO figures include them.

Start year 1833: the year slavery was abolished across British Caribbean territories, marking the beginning of the modern colonial administrative period used in most economic datasets. End year 1962: dissolution of the West Indies Federation and independence of Jamaica and Trinidad & Tobago.

## Polygon decision

Polygon status is **unassigned**. No single historical GIS source (CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, GADM) covers the British West Indies as an administrative aggregate. The correct method is a composed union of GADM 4.1 adm0 polygons for JAM, TTO, BRB, BHS, ATG, GRD, VCT, DMA, LCA, KNA, MSR (~34,766 km² combined). VGB, CYM, and TCA are excluded pending creation of those polity entries.

All constituents are islands with stable physical extents; the composed union is geographically invariant across 1833–1962. The polygon must be built and added to `data/geodata/constructed/constructed.geojson` before `polygon_status` can be set to `assigned`.

## Open questions

- **VGB-1800-2025, CYM, TCA**: British Virgin Islands, Cayman Islands, and Turks & Caicos should be added to the composed-union polygon once their polity entries exist.
- **Leeward/Windward sub-aggregate labels**: Some FAO sources distinguish "Leeward Islands" and "Windward Islands" as sub-aggregates. If those labels appear in data, separate polities (or additional aliases routing to BWI-1833-1962) may be needed.
- **British Guiana / British Honduras inclusion**: Verify whether any FAO 1952 "British West Indies" totals include mainland territories; if so, polygon area estimate must increase.
