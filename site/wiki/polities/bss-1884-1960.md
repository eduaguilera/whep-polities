# British Somaliland Protectorate (1884-1960)

## Summary

The British Somaliland Protectorate was a British colonial possession in the northern Horn of Africa, covering approximately 137,600 km² of territory that became the northern (former British) part of Somalia at independence. Britain concluded protection treaties with the northern Somali clans (Gadabuursi, Issa, Habar Awal, and others) in 1884–1886, establishing the protectorate. The WHEP row starts in 1884, the year of the first treaty; it ends in 1960 when British Somaliland became independent on 26 June 1960 and united with Italian Somaliland on 1 July 1960 to form the Somali Republic. CShapes 2.0 records British Somaliland (cowcode 521, owner=200, status=colony) from 1886-01-01 to 1960-06-30 across two time-steps; the 1884 WHEP start pre-dates CShapes coverage (which begins at 1886) by two years.

British Somaliland was administered separately from Italian Somaliland (ITS-1908-1960, ~500,000 km²), which lay to the south and east. At independence the two territories merged into a single Somali Republic. Data labeled 'somalia' or with ISO code 'som' from sources such as IIA and Mitchell for the period 1920–1959 may reflect either territory or their aggregate; routing to BSS is appropriate where the data's territorial scope corresponds to the northern (British) portion.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), cowcode 521, time-step FID 424–425. Capital: Berbera (10.44°N, 45.02°E). CShapes records two time-steps at identical area (~14.13 sq deg):

- 1886-01-01 to 1897-04-13: status=colony, owner=UK (200), area ~14.13 sq deg
- 1897-04-14 to 1960-06-30: status=colony, owner=UK (200), area ~14.13 sq deg (boundary adjustment)

The 1897 boundary adjustment reflects the Anglo-Ethiopian treaty of 1897, which delimited the border between British Somaliland and Ethiopia. The polygon area is stable throughout the colonial period; no significant territorial changes are recorded. The WHEP start year of 1884 predates CShapes coverage (hard floor: 1886); the 1886 polygon is used as the earliest available approximation, with a two-year vintage drift documented here.

Historical area: approximately 137,600 km² (~68,000 sq. miles). This is roughly 27% of Italian Somaliland's area (~500,000 km²). The two Somalilands together constitute the territory of modern Somalia (~637,000 km²; Biger gives 246,201 sq. miles = 637,414 km²). The apparent discrepancy between the CShapes area in sq. degrees and the historical km² figure reflects the projection used; the polygon geometry itself is authoritative.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None recorded in WHEP. The 1884 start corresponds to the first British protection treaties with Somali clan leaders; the territory was previously under the sovereignty of independent Somali clan confederacies, which are not WHEP-covered polities.
- **Successor:** [som-1960-2025](som-1960-2025.md) — Somalia from 1 July 1960 (British Somaliland merged with Italian Somaliland)

## Data routing note

This polity was created to resolve a coverage gap: 81 rows of IIA/Mitchell data labeled 'somalia' (iso_in_data='som') for 1920–1959 could not be routed because only SOM-1960-2025 (post-independence) existed in the database. The territory of present-day Somalia in 1920–1959 was divided between Italian Somaliland (ITS-1908-1960, southern/larger portion ~500,000 km²) and this British Somaliland Protectorate (northern portion ~137,600 km²). Where source data aggregates both Somalilands under a single 'somalia' label, Italian Somaliland (ITS) is the larger territory (~78% of combined area) and should be treated as the primary routing target for aggregate figures. BSS-specific routing is appropriate when the source explicitly covers northern/British Somali territory or reports trade through Berbera.

## Contradictions

The WHEP start year of 1884 predates CShapes 2.0's hard floor of 1886-01-01. CShapes cannot provide a polygon for 1884–1885; the 1886 polygon is used as an approximation. Given the stable borders throughout the protectorate period (no CShapes changes are recorded for BSS), this two-year drift is minor and does not materially affect territorial extent at WHEP reporting granularity.

## Open questions

### oq-bss-1884-cshapes-floor

CShapes starts British Somaliland on 1886-01-01; the WHEP row starts 1884. The 1884–1885 period lacks a polygon; the 1886 CShapes polygon is used as the earliest approximation. Since borders were stable throughout (no CShapes area changes recorded), this is a minor vintage drift. Document only.

### oq-bss-somalia-label-routing

Data labeled 'somalia' or iso 'som' for 1920–1959 may aggregate both British and Italian Somaliland. Without source-level documentation of territorial scope, it is not possible to split the 81 IIA/Mitchell rows between ITS-1908-1960 and BSS-1884-1960 by magnitude alone. The alias routes all pre-1960 'somalia' labels to BSS; a complementary alias 'somalia' -> ITS-1908-1960 for the same period should be added once the ITS routing is confirmed. If the data represents aggregate figures, the routing to BSS overstates the BSS portion.
