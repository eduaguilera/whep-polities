# Argentina (to 1899)

## Summary

This row tracks Argentina from **1800** to **1899** — covering the
independence and consolidation period through the Puna de Atacama
arbitration. COW: ARG, ccode=160. Succeeded by ARG-1899-1902.

Split from the former ARG-1800-2025 collapsed row on 2026-07-01 to
restore the autonomous-34-south-america three-period design.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `gwcode=160`, vintage **1886**.
  This is the earliest available CShapes vintage for Argentina.
- **Polygon vintage drift.** Data start year is 1800 (or 1816 for
  post-independence data); polygon vintage is 1886 — a drift of up
  to 86 years. Acceptable because:
  - All WHEP production data for 1872–1881 is Pampas-based (grain,
    livestock), entirely within the Río de la Plata heartland.
  - The only meaningful pre-1886 territorial ambiguity is Patagonia
    (claimed 1820s, effectively settled with Chile by the
    Boundary Treaty of 1881). The 1886 polygon already includes
    Patagonia as settled.
  - Pre-1881 the 1886 polygon slightly overstates actual effective
    control in Patagonia; this is documented here as an acceptable
    approximation since Patagonia contributed negligible economic
    output in the data years concerned.
- **Open question.** If Cliopatria provides a pre-1881 Argentina
  polygon in future, a further split at 1881 should be evaluated to
  capture the Patagonian boundary settlement precisely.
- **COW.** ARG, ccode=160
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).
- **1881 Treaty of Limits (Chile).** Established the Andean watershed
  as the boundary, resolving competing claims over Patagonia and
  Tierra del Fuego (partially).
- **1898–1899 Puna de Atacama arbitration.** The Puna region
  (formerly Bolivian territory ceded to Chile in 1884 Truce of
  Ancón) was divided between Chile and Argentina by the 1899
  arbitral award, closing this row.
