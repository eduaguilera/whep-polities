# Kingdom of Buganda

## Summary

The Kingdom of Buganda was a pre-colonial kingdom in central East Africa, centered on the northern shore of Lake Victoria in the area of modern Uganda. Founded c. 1300, Buganda was the most powerful of the interlacustrine kingdoms and gave its name to Uganda. The database tracks it from 1800 until 1894, when Britain established a protectorate. Biger discusses Buganda in the context of the Tanzania-Uganda boundary and the East African colonial partition.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom was centered around the shores of Lake Victoria in what is now central Uganda. Its territory included the Nile valley and areas around Lake Victoria.

## Contradictions

None identified. Note that Biger's reference to "the Kingdom of Burundi" in the Uganda protectorate context (p.490) appears to be an error or conflation; Burundi was part of German East Africa, not the British Uganda Protectorate. The Kingdom of Buganda was the primary client kingdom of the Uganda Protectorate.
