# Mozambique (1891-1975)

## Summary

This row covers Portuguese Mozambique from 11 June 1891 to 24 June 1975 (eve of independence). The 1891 start corresponds to a territorial expansion in CShapes (area increased from 54.21 to 66.97 degree-squared units). Mozambique remained a Portuguese overseas province or colony until independence on 25 June 1975.
