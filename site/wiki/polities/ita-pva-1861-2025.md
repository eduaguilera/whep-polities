# Piemonte with Valle d'Aosta

## Summary

Piedmont together with the Aosta valley: the statistical compartment "Piemonte" as it stood from unification until the Aosta valley became an autonomous region after the Second World War (decrees of 1945, special statute of 1948), and since then the union of the regions Piemonte and Valle d'Aosta. `type: subnational`: Italy held this ground throughout.

**Why this entry exists.** The Mediterranean subnational panel ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)) reports a unit labelled `ITC1`. In NUTS that code is Piemonte alone, and the unit was routed to ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (25,274 km2), with its 1880-1969 years back-cast onto it. The unit's landuse block says it is larger: the per-year sum of its land-cover classes is a **constant 28,653.28 km2 in every one of its 141 landuse years**. Piemonte plus Valle d'Aosta (ITA-VDA-1948-2025, 3,256) is 28,529 in the published table (+0.4%). The panel has no `ITC2` (Valle d'Aosta) unit, so the valley's data was riding on Piemonte's row. Claude created this entry on 2026-09-24 while correcting the Southern-Europe alias defects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned, constructed. The union of GADM 4.1 adm1 Piemonte (ITA.13_1) and Valle d'Aosta (ITA.19_1) ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)), built by `build_ita_pva_1861_2025()`; those are the features ITA-PIE-1970-2025 and ITA-VDA-1948-2025 bind. Measured in ESRI:54034: 28,538 km2 against the panel's 28,653.28 (+0.4%); Piemonte alone is 25,290, 0.88 of the panel total.

**Historical extent, and why confidence is medium.** The polygon is the modern pair of regions. Before the 1947 Treaty of Paris the compartment also held Tende and La Brigue and several crest adjustments (Mont Cenis plateau, Chaberton and others) ceded to France; those few hundred km2 are not added back, because no fetched source carries the pre-1947 border at this resolution. The panel's own land total is constant across 1880-2023, so the compiler reports a single extent throughout, and that extent is the modern one to within 0.4%.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. The post-war separation of the Aosta valley happened inside this ground, and the panel keeps reporting the union afterwards, so this row continues as a reporting aggregate. ITA-PIE-1970-2025 and ITA-VDA-1948-2025 are its parts, coexisting with it.

## Decisions

### d-observed-from-1861

**All of the unit's years, 1880-2023, are observation of this territory**

The compartment of Piemonte included the Aosta valley from 1861, so the pre-1970 years are this territory reported as itself, not a reconstruction. `juan-subnational` `ITC1` routes 1880-2023 here, and its former `back_cast` onto Piemonte is removed. This also retires two ledger segments that had matched the unit's 1880-1969 years to the whole of Italy (11x its area), which `validate_matched_target_territory.py` carried as baselined container matches.

### d-panel-slugs-only

**The official NUTS slugs keep ITC1 = Piemonte**

The `eurostat-nuts2013`, `eurostat-nuts2016` and `jrc-nuts2016` rows for `ITC1` are left on ITA-PIE-1970-2025, because in those code systems ITC1 is Piemonte.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1947-western-border

**The pre-1947 extent is understated by the ceded crest communes**

Tende, La Brigue and the crest adjustments of 1947 belong to this territory before 1947. Adding them would need a historical boundary source for the 1947 line; with one, this row would split at 1947.
