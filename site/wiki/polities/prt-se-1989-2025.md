# Setúbal (NUTS III region of Portugal)

## Summary

Setúbal is a NUTS III statistical district of Portugal, located immediately south of Lisbon across the Tagus estuary and covering the Setúbal Peninsula and the western Alentejo littoral, including the city of Setúbal, the industrial belt around Barreiro and Seixal, and the Arrábida coast. This entry captures it as a subnational reporting unit (`type: subnational`) from 1989, when Portugal's district-level statistics began to be organized under the EU's NUTS classification, through the present. It is deliberately NOT the national Portugal row: PRT-1800-2025 is the sovereign state, and Setúbal is one of several district-level statistical units (siblings PRT-GU/Guarda, PRT-LE/Leiria, PRT-PG/Portalegre, PRT-SA/Santarém and others already staged in this same batch) that report data at a finer grain than the country total. Matching Setúbal-level figures to the national row was the original routing error this entry corrects -- every one of Portugal's eighteen districts could equally claim the national row under that logic, which is precisely the container-claim failure already rejected for sibling PRT-PTFA (Faro). This entry exists so that data reported specifically for Setúbal, at Setúbal's own territorial scale, has a polity to attach to instead of silently inflating or diluting national Portugal totals.

**Why this entry exists.** **Input data:** The routing decision (unit_id PRT-PTSE, admin_name PT_SE) covers Setúbal district-level reporting for Portugal across a span of at least 1880-2023 (144 data years referenced by the harness), of which the harness proposes 1989-2025 as this new polity's span, following the country's established NUTS-adoption convention already applied to sibling districts. **What it was previously matched to and why that was wrong:** years in this unit's span had been matched to PRT-1800-2025, the national Portugal row -- the container-claim error the harness flagged, since matching a district's data to the national row is indistinguishable from matching any other of Portugal's eighteen districts to that same row, which is exactly the pattern already rejected for sibling PRT-PTFA (Faro). **What confirms this is a distinct reporting unit:** Setúbal is one of Portugal's formally defined NUTS III statistical regions under the EU's Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics, adopted for Portugal in 1989 -- a real, currently-used administrative/statistical division distinct from the national total, and the same convention already established for six other Portuguese district siblings in this batch (PTAV, PTBJ, PTEV, PTGU, PTLE, PTPG: pre-1989 unroutable, 1989-onward proposed as its own polity) is applied here rather than invented fresh.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. polygon_source is `none` and polygon_status is `unassigned` because the polygon_route determined for this entry was `new_source_needed`: none of the sources currently registered in scripts/sources.yaml (gadm-4.1-adm1, cshapes-2.0, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, reporting-areas, constructed) carry any Portuguese subnational boundary at all -- gadm-4.1-adm1 is missing PRT entirely from its admin-1 subset, and the country-level-only sources (cshapes variants) have no district geometry to subdivide or union. There is consequently no candidate feature to assign and nothing to construct from a union/difference of existing registered geometries. The correct fix is registering a new source -- most plausibly Eurostat GISCO's NUTS boundary files or Portugal's DGT/CAOP administrative boundaries -- with its licence verified, which has not been done (see open question).

**Territory description:** Setúbal District/NUTS III region sits on the south bank of the Tagus estuary directly across from Lisbon, comprising the Setúbal Peninsula (concelhos including Setúbal, Almada, Barreiro, Seixal, Montijo, Moita, Palmela) and extending south along the Atlantic coast to include Sesimbra and the Arrábida range, with the southern boundary running toward the Sado estuary and Alcácer do Sal. On a modern map this is the industrial and port-city belt immediately south of greater Lisbon, roughly bounded by the Tagus to the north, the Atlantic to the west, and the Alentejo plain to the east and south. No polygon is attached to measure an area from, so no km² figure -- measured or stated -- is given here; a district of this shape and extent in Portugal's official NUTS III statistics is typically on the order of a few thousand km², but that estimate is not sourced and should not be treated as evidence pending an actual registered boundary.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: Setúbal as a NUTS III statistical district has no institutional ancestor of its own before the 1989 NUTS classification created it as a reporting unit -- the territory it covers was previously reported, if at all, only inside the national Portugal row (PRT-1800-2025) or under older concelho/distrito administrative divisions that this entry does not attempt to trace (see open question on the 76% pre-1989 coverage gap). No successor: the NUTS III district remains current as of 2025, so end_year uses the open-end convention (2025, exclusive) and no successor polity exists.

## Sourced claims

- Setúbal is one of Portugal's NUTS III statistical subdivisions, part of the broader Área Metropolitana de Lisboa / Alentejo Litoral grouping depending on the NUTS revision in force -- the district boundary is an administrative construct of Portugal's official statistical geography (INE, Instituto Nacional de Estatística), not a historically continuous polity.
- The routing decision that created this entry records that the data previously routed to this territory (matched=PRT-1800-2025 for early years) was rejected as an error, on the stated grounds that every sibling NUTS district (PTAV, PTBJ, PTEV, PTFA, PTGU, PTLE, PTPG, and this one) could equally claim the national row under the same matching logic that produced the error.

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-dominant-pattern

**Code follows the ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke name**

PRT-SE-1989-2025 follows the dominant 57-of-66 pattern (<ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>), matching siblings PRT-GU, PRT-LE, PRT-PG already staged in this batch. Setúbal is a modern NUTS III statistical district, not a historical territory with a name and identity of its own the way ALK or HYD are -- it did not exist as an administrative or political entity before the 1989 NUTS classification was applied to it, so there is no bespoke-code case to make here. The two-letter subunit code SE is chosen to mirror the NUTS district abbreviation actually used in Portugal's own statistical geography (Setúbal), consistent with GU=Guarda, LE=Leiria, PG=Portalegre already used for siblings in this batch.

### d-start-year-1989-not-1986

**Start year set to 1989, the routing decision's proposed value, not 1986**

The routing decision flagged this as an open question -- whether 1986 (Portugal's EU accession) or 1989 (formal NUTS classification adoption) is the more precise start year. The proposed span in the decision object uses 1989 and the routing_reasoning explicitly ties the span to 'the 1989 EU NUTS adoption'. Absent access here to data/final/nuts_code_names.csv to adjudicate the harness's own flagged uncertainty, this page follows the decision's own proposed value rather than silently substituting 1986, and carries the ambiguity forward as an open question rather than resolving it unverified.

## Open questions

### oq-1986-vs-1989-start

**Is 1986 (EU accession) or 1989 (formal NUTS adoption) the correct start year?**

The routing decision itself flagged this as unresolved: 'Whether 1986 or 1989 is the more precise start year for this district's NUTS classification should be confirmed against data/final/nuts_code_names.csv or harness NUTS introduction records.' This page uses 1989 following the decision's own proposed span, but if data/final/nuts_code_names.csv shows Setúbal-level reporting beginning in 1986 (immediately at EU accession, before the formal 1989 Council Regulation classification took effect), start_year and the polity code both need to shift to PRT-SE-1986-2025, and the container edge start_year would need the same correction. This should be checked against harness records before the entry is treated as final.

### oq-no-polygon-source

**No registered source carries a Setúbal NUTS III boundary; what would the real fix look like?**

polygon_route was new_source_needed: none of the registered sources (gadm-4.1-adm1, cshapes-2.0, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, reporting-areas, constructed) carry any Portuguese subnational geometry to assign or construct from. The likely correct source is Eurostat GISCO's NUTS boundary files or Portugal's own DGT/CAOP administrative boundaries, either of which would need registering in scripts/sources.yaml with its licence verified (licence_known was recorded as false in the routing decision) before this entry's polygon_status can move past unassigned. Until that registration happens, this polity carries data with no territory boundary at all, which is the state the page-requirements spec treats as the worse of the two 'no polygon' cases -- explicitly not a proxy, just absent.

### oq-sibling-prtfa-same-bug

**Does sibling PRT-PTFA still carry the same rejected matched=PRT-1800-2025 verdict?**

The routing_concerns note that sibling PRT-PTFA's recorded verdict still shows the identical rejected pattern (pre-NUTS years routed to the national row) and 'likely needs the identical correction applied to it.' This page does not touch that verdict file, so whether PRT-PTFA has actually been corrected to match the pattern used here (pre-1989 unroutable, 1989+ its own polity) is unverified and should be checked in pipelines/agent-harness/state/routing_verdicts.csv.

### oq-coverage-gap-pre-1989

**109 of 144 data years (76%) are pre-1989 and left unroutable -- is that the right call for all of them?**

The harness flagged that after 2 retries, coverage still does not tile 1880-2023: the bulk of this unit's measured years fall before the 1989 NUTS classification existed. This page follows the established sibling convention (PTAV, PTBJ, PTEV, PTGU, PTLE, PTPG all: pre-1989 unroutable, 1989+ proposed) on the reasoning that pre-NUTS Setúbal-level reporting has no distinct district-level polity to route to. But the harness's own objection stands unaddressed here: if the source reports values for those years, the territory presumably existed under some earlier Portuguese administrative division (a concelho or distrito predating NUTS), and a correct fix might be a second, earlier-spanning polity for that district-equivalent rather than leaving 76% of the data unrouted. This page does not attempt that earlier entry; it only creates the 1989+ NUTS-era row.
