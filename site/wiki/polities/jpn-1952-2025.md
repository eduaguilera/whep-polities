# Japan

## Summary

This row tracks modern Japan for **74 years**, from the restoration of
sovereignty to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes:
"Post-occupation. Okinawa reverted 1972. Kuril Islands dispute with
Russia unresolved" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-occupation Japan. ~378,000 km2. Economic miracle of the 1950s--1980s.
Okinawa reverted from US administration in 1972. The Kuril Islands
dispute with Russia remains unresolved.

active).

## Open questions

### oq-cow-code-na

All four JPN rows (JPN-1800-1895, JPN-1895-1945, JPN-1945-1952,
JPN-1952-2025) have `cow_code = NA` in the CSV
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW assigns Japan
code 740. The NA across all four rows is likely a systematic omission
rather than a per-row error.

### oq-okinawa-split-candidate

Okinawa reverted in 1972 (~2,271 km2 plus surrounding islands). Under a
strict reading of the WHEP polity-definition rule, this is a real
territorial change. The CSV does not split at 1972. The territorial
change is modest relative to the home islands, so the non-split is
reasonable but worth noting.
