# Congo Free State (to 1891)

## Summary

The Congo Free State from its 29 May 1885 proclamation by Leopold II (following the Berlin Conference) to the CShapes-defined 1891 boundary event (which aligns with the Stairs Expedition's 1891 subjugation of Katanga). The CSV records this as a brief early-colonial-era row in the COD chain. Note: this is the Congo Free State under Leopold's personal rule, *not* the Belgian Congo (which begins with the Belgian Parliament annexation on 15 November 1908).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes begins coverage for COD in 1886, so the 1885 start year is a small extrapolation. The polygon reflects the post-Berlin Conference boundaries of the Free State before the eastern boundary was finalised with the UK.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessors:** [lba-1800-1885](lba-1800-1885.md) — Luba Empire; [lnd-1800-1887](lnd-1800-1887.md) — Lunda Empire. Both African precolonial states whose territory was absorbed into the Free State.
- **Successor:** [cod-1891-1894](cod-1891-1894.md) — Congo Free State 1891–1894 (post-Katanga incorporation).

## Sourced claims

- "On 29 May 1885, after the closure of the Berlin Conference, the king announced that he planned to name his possessions 'the Congo Free State'", which officially replaced the International Association of the Congo on 1 August 1885. [wikipedia-african-colonial-2026-04-15 §congo-free-state](../sources/wikipedia-african-colonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- In 1891 Leopold "sent the Stairs Expedition to secure Katanga", marking the start of effective Free State control over the southeastern territories. [wikipedia-african-colonial-2026-04-15 §congo-free-state](../sources/wikipedia-african-colonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
