# Denmark

## Summary

This row tracks modern Denmark for **106 years**, from the **1920**
Northern Schleswig plebiscite to the **2025** database ceiling
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV has
`notes = NA` and `verification_status = VERIFIED`.

At 1920, Denmark regained Northern Schleswig via a plebiscite mandated
by the Treaty of Versailles. Biger confirms: "After World War I only
the Danish-populated zones of northern Schleswig reverted to Denmark
after a referendum"
[biger-1995 §denmark](../sources/biger-1995.md#denmark). Metropolitan
borders have been stable since 1920. WWII German occupation
(1940--1945) did not trigger a WHEP split because there was no
permanent territorial change.

Denmark still includes Greenland and the Faroe Islands as autonomous
territories; neither has gained full independence within this row's
window.

Predecessor: [dnk-1864-1920](dnk-1864-1920.md) -- Northern Schleswig
returned after WWI plebiscite.
Successor: NA (still active).

## Sourced claims

- **1920: Northern Schleswig plebiscite.** The start-of-row event.
  "After World War I only the Danish-populated zones of northern
  Schleswig reverted to Denmark after a referendum; Southern Schleswig
  joined Germany. Today the 50,000-60,000 (8-10%) Danish-identified
  citizens in South Schleswig are very well integrated"
  [biger-1995 §denmark](../sources/biger-1995.md#denmark).
- **1940--1945: German occupation.** Denmark was occupied by Nazi
  Germany from 9 April 1940 to 5 May 1945. No permanent territorial
  change resulted; metropolitan borders unchanged at liberation.
  [biger-1995 §denmark](../sources/biger-1995.md#denmark)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 390, continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 -- no break at 1920 or any other date
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
