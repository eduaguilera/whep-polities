# Libya under UN Transitional Administration (1949-1951)

Bridging polity for all-Libya data in the transitional years. Full-territory CShapes polygon
assigned (gwcode=620, 1943 feature, ~1.6M km²).

> **Start year corrected 1950 → 1949 on 2026-08-05, because this row did not do what its own
> summary said it did.**
>
> The sentence here read *"Closes the 1949–1951 gap in the LBY chain"* while the frontmatter said
> `start_year: 1950`. It therefore left **1949 uncovered** — the exact year it was created to
> cover. Third instance of this shape found today, after `NNI-1904-1913` (PR 132) and
> `TCD-1920-1960`, since renamed `TCD-1919-1960` (PR 135): prose stating one thing while the frontmatter that generates the
> database states another.
>
> **1949 is also the better date on its own merits.** UN General Assembly Resolution 289 of
> **21 November 1949** decided Libya should become independent and set up the transitional
> framework; independence followed on 24 December 1951. The UN transitional period is
> conventionally dated from that resolution, so 1949–1951 is the span the row's own name implies.
>
> **What it fixes in the data.** 28 rows labelled `Libya` for 1949 were resolving to
> `CYR-1949-1951` — the Emirate of **Cyrenaica alone**, roughly the eastern third of the country —
> because no all-Libya row was live in 1949. They now reach this row.
>
> **And it corrects the reasoning in `validate_period_gaps`,** which accepted the 1949 hole
> because *"covered by CYR-1949-1951 and TRP-1943-1951 … Libya was split into Cyrenaica and
> Tripolitania"*. Libya was split into **three**. Fezzan — about 550,000 km², a third of the
> country — was under **French** military administration while Britain held Tripolitania and
> Cyrenaica, and it has no row at all. Two thirds of a country is not coverage.

## Territorial extent

The CShapes gwcode 620 polygon is the whole of Libya (~1,617,537 km²), which is correct for an
all-Libya reporting row and is why this row exists alongside `TRP-1943-1951` and `CYR-1949-1951`
rather than instead of them. Those two are components; this is the whole.

**The 8.1% gap to FAO's 1,759,500 is not a polygon defect (issue 196).** CShapes 1,617,537, GADM
4.1 1,616,058 and Natural Earth 10m 1,623,761 agree to 0.5%. The outlier is 1,759,540, an Italian
1930s figure for a larger Libya: IIA 1933 gives 1,638,000, and the January 1935
Laval-Mussolini treaty then put Libya's southern boundary on the Aouzou line — 114,000 km², never
ratified, awarded to Chad by the ICJ in 1994. Declared area and `polygon_status` therefore stand
unchanged; working on [fez-1943-1951](fez-1943-1951.md).

**Both components carry no geometry** (`polygon_source: none` / empty, `polygon_area_km2: null`), so
the three-way partition of occupied Libya cannot currently be represented, and `LBY − TRP − CYR`
cannot be used to construct Fezzan. Tracked separately.
