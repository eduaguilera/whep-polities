# Northern Territory (territory of Australia)

## Summary

The Northern Territory is a Commonwealth territory of Australia covering the central-northern portion of the continent, bounded by Western Australia to the west, South Australia to the south, and Queensland to the east, with Darwin as its capital and administrative seat on the north coast. It was administered as part of the colony (later state) of South Australia from 1863, but South Australia struggled to develop or fund the remote territory, and on 1 January 1911 it was transferred to direct Commonwealth control under the Northern Territory Acceptance Act 1910, becoming a federal territory rather than a state. It has remained a Commonwealth territory since, gaining a Legislative Assembly with substantial self-government in 1978 (short of full statehood, which remains unresolved as of 2026). `type: subnational` reflects that it is a first-order administrative division within the Australian federation, not a sovereign entity or a full state — it sits below the national AUS row in the same way ACT does, and exists here to carry a distinct territorial and statistical identity separate from both the national totals and the former South Australia colonial administration.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures agricultural/production data reported for Australia under a `Northern Territory` label spanning 1880-2022 (per the routing decision), which predates the territory's own 1911 creation and was previously routed to national AUS totals (AUS-1901-2025 / AUS-1800-1901) because no candidate polity existed that specifically represented the Northern Territory as a reporting unit. That routing was wrong for the post-1911 portion of the series because the Northern Territory has been tabulated separately from both South Australia and the rest of Australia in statistical collections since Commonwealth takeover — it is administered, gazetted, and reported as its own unit, analogous to how the Australian Capital Territory (AUS-ACT-1911-2025) already has its own row. No candidate IS the Northern Territory itself: AUSA-1836-1900 is the South Australia colony that administered the area only until 1900/1901, and the AUS national rows are aggregates that erase the distinction. The Commonwealth's own Northern Territory Acceptance Act 1910 and the continuous existence of a separate Northern Territory administration since 1911 confirm this is a distinct, separately-governed entity, not a subdivision folded into another state's statistics.

**Territory description:** The Northern Territory occupies the central-north of the Australian continent, roughly the region between longitude 129°E and 138°E, from the Timor Sea/Arafura Sea coast in the north to the South Australian border in the south. Its capital is Darwin. Its area is approximately 1,420,970 km2 (about 18.5% of Australia's landmass) and its borders have been stable since the 1863/1911 transfers — no boundary revision has occurred in the modern era, so vintage risk for a GADM ADM1 fetch would be low once obtained.
