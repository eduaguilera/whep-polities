# Peru (1884-1909)

## Summary

This row tracks Peru from **1884** to **1909** — the period following
the Treaty of Ancón (20 October 1884) through the next territorial
boundary at 1909. The 1884 start marks the permanent cession of
**Tarapacá** to Chile; Tacna and Arica remained under Chilean
occupation during this entire period (resolved by the 1929 Treaty of
Lima).

This polity was created by splitting the former PER-1825-1909 row at
the Tarapacá cession boundary. See the Decisions section.

Predecessor: [per-1825-1884](per-1825-1884.md).
Successor: [per-1909-1922](per-1909-1922.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `cowcode=135`, feature vintage
  1886-01-01.
- **Polygon fit.** The 1886 polygon represents post-Ancón Peru
  (Tarapacá already excluded), making it a near-exact geographic match
  for this row. Polygon vintage drift is ~2 years (1886 polygon used
  for a period starting 1884), which is negligible.
- **Tacna/Arica note.** These territories were under Chilean
  administration 1884–1929 but were not formally ceded. Their status
  in the 1886 polygon is a secondary issue; the primary territorial
  definition (Tarapacá ceded, rest of Peru intact) is correctly
  captured.
- **Polygon status.** `assigned` (polygon vintage 1886, drift ~2y).
- **COW.** PER, ccode=135
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1884-10-20: Treaty of Ancón.** Tarapacá permanently ceded to Chile.
  Tacna and Arica placed under Chilean administration pending a
  plebiscite (never held; resolved 1929).
- **1886-01-01: CShapes polygon vintage.** The CShapes 2.0 feature for
  cowcode=135 begins at this date, reflecting post-cession Peru.
