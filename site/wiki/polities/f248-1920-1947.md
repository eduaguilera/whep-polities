# Yugoslavia (1920-1947)


> **`iso3` set to `YUG` on 2026-08-05 (issue 55).** This row previously carried none,
> so it belonged to no ISO family at all and could not be reached by
> `matchlib.pick_by_year`'s ISO route -- only by an explicit alias.
>
> `YUG` is the **ISO 3166-3** code for Yugoslavia, and using it follows the rule the
> database already applies: a dissolved entity inherits its successor's code when it has
> exactly ONE successor, and takes its own 3166-3 code when it split into several.
> `ANT-1961-2010` and `SCG-1992-2006` already do the latter. Czechoslovakia became two
> states and Yugoslavia several, so no successor's code is the right one.
>
> Verified before applying: this creates **no family tie**. Every row in the family is a
> consecutive period, so no pair shares the code over overlapping years -- which matters
> because a wrong `iso3` is exactly what put a Belgium-Luxembourg row in Belgium's family
> (issue 40) and made "Malaysia" resolve to British North Borneo (issue 44).
## Summary

Split from F248-1920-1991. Period-accurate 1920 CShapes polygon (post-Trianon borders). Row ends 1947-02-10 when the Paris Peace Treaty ceded Istria, Zadar, and related territory from Italy to Yugoslavia. See f248-1947-1991.md for the post-1947 SFRY period.

Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes (renamed Kingdom of Yugoslavia in 1929). WWII Axis occupation and partition (1941-1945). Tito's communist Yugoslavia established 1945.

COW: YUG, 345, gap 1941-04-20 to 1944-10-20 (WWII occupation).

- **Area.** ~255,000 km² (~28.72 sq deg). Territory stable through interwar period.

## Polygon decision

CShapes 2.0 (`cshapes2_full.gpkg`, `cowcode=345`) uses identical geometry from 1920-06-04 to 1991-11-19 without any 1947 boundary update. This is consistent with CShapes' documented exclusion of territorial transfers under 10,000 km² (Schvitz et al. 2022). The 1920 geometry is accurate for this sub-period; the same geometry carries an `estimate` flag for F248-1947-1991 due to the ~4,165 km² post-1947 acquisition.

## Sourced claims

- **1929: Renamed Yugoslavia.** Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes renamed to Kingdom of Yugoslavia.
- **1941-04-06: Axis invasion.** Germany, Italy, Hungary, and Bulgaria invaded Yugoslavia.
- **1941-04-20: COW gap begins.** Yugoslavia ceased to exist as a sovereign state per COW.
- **1944-10-20: COW gap ends.** Liberation of Belgrade. Tito's partisan government recognized.
- **1945-1946: SFRY established.** Federal People's Republic of Yugoslavia proclaimed.
- **1947-02-10: Paris Peace Treaty.** Italy ceded Istria, Zadar, and related territory (~4,165 km²) to Yugoslavia. End-of-row event; see F248-1947-1991.

## Decisions

- **Split from F248-1920-1991 (2026-07-01).** The original 1920-1991 row spanned the 1947 Paris Peace Treaty border change. Split at 1947-02-10 to give each sub-period its own polygon entry. The pre-1947 period uses the 1920 CShapes geometry with `polygon_status=assigned`.

## Known data-quality issues

### sfr-label-is-a-generic-country-name

Sources that label 1920s-1940s data **"Yugoslav SFR"** are using their generic
modern country name, not asserting the socialist federation's territory: the
SFR did not exist before 1945. Verified 2026-07-24 (`yugoslav sfr|juan|1920-1946`)
— the magnitudes (wheat, maize, rye, barley, oats) match this row's documented
~255,000 km² interwar Kingdom territory. Read such labels by their years, not
their name.


### fao1952-population-series-is-five-indicators

Verifying `yugoslavia|fao1952|1937-1939` surfaced a **source-level**
convention, not a problem with this row. The earlier reading of it was
wrong, and is corrected here (issue 13, re-measured 2026-08-14).

**What was recorded:** that the FAO 1952 population series reports a
median ~5.08M for Yugoslavia, about one third of the actual ~14-16M
(1931 and 1948 censuses), so the series was "almost certainly
agricultural/rural population rather than total population" — with the
Saar reading as a true total treated as an unexplained contradiction.

**What the data actually says.** FAO 1952 carries **five distinct
population indicators under one item code**
(`r_fao_population_1952_10_18`): total population, agricultural
population, population *economically active* in agriculture, and the
male/female splits of the last. Layer B keeps them apart in its
`indicator` column; the item code does not. For Yugoslavia 1937 the
three substantive ones are

| indicator | 1937 (000) |
|---|---|
| population total | 15,722 |
| population agricultural | 10,629 |
| population agricultural, economically active | 5,083 |

The total, 15.72M, **is** the true total (1931 census 13.9M, 1948 census
15.77M). The "~5.08M" is precisely the economically-active-in-agriculture
row — and precisely the median of the five indicator-mixed 1937 rows the
evidence bundle keyed on the item code alone. The same arithmetic
reproduces the Turkey figure exactly: the recorded "~6,086k" is
(5,712 + 6,459) / 2 = 6,085.5, the midpoint of ten mixed rows, while
Turkey's actual FAO totals are 16,823k (1937) and 20,935k (1951).

Two parts of the old note are also **false as stated**: the claimed
uniform ~⅓ ratio does not hold (economically-active-in-agriculture over
total, 1937: USA 6.5%, Germany 12.4%, Japan 20.2%), and FAO 1952 carries
**no USSR population row at all** — its aggregate line is literally
`GENERAL TOTAL EXCLUDING THE USSR`.

**How to read the series.** Read the `indicator`, then use the figure.
`population total` is usable as total population; a figure near a third
of the total is the agricultural-occupied indicator and is neither a
defect nor evidence of a territorial mismatch. This row's other
magnitudes (poultry, draft animals, crop areas) remain consistent with
the full pre-1947 Kingdom of Yugoslavia territory. Recorded for all
sources in
`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/source_conventions.csv`, which the
intake step attaches to future assertion evidence bundles; `00_intake.py
--indicator-col` now reports each measure separately so a bundle can no
longer show a median of incommensurable series.

## Open questions

### oq-wwii-occupation-gap

COW shows a gap from 1941-04-20 to 1944-10-20. During this period Yugoslavia was partitioned among Axis powers (Germany, Italy, Hungary, Bulgaria) and puppet states (Independent State of Croatia, Serbia under Nedic). The WHEP row spans this gap without further splitting.
