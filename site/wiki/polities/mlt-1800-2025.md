# Malta

## Summary

This row tracks Malta for **226 years** from the database start year to
the truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Malta is an island
state in the Mediterranean Sea between Italy and Libya, with no
international land boundaries
[biger-1995 §malta](../sources/biger-1995.md#malta). During this
period, Malta transitioned from rule by the Knights of Malta through
British colonial control (19th century) to independence (1964) and
republic status (1974).

## Sourced claims

- Malta: area 122 sq. miles (316 sq. km.), population 380,000
  (1994), capital Valletta, no land boundaries.
  [biger-1995 §malta](../sources/biger-1995.md#malta) (p.377)
- Malta was colonized successively by Phoenicians, Greeks,
  Carthaginians, and Romans; Arabs from Tunis occupied it in 870;
  the Sicilian Normans conquered it in 1090.
  [biger-1995 §malta](../sources/biger-1995.md#malta) (p.377)
- In 1530 Charles V gave Malta to the order henceforth known as the
  Knights of Malta.
  [biger-1995 §malta](../sources/biger-1995.md#malta) (p.377)
- In the nineteenth century, Britain turned Malta into a major naval
  base. After World War II the Maltese obtained autonomy; full
  independence came in 1964 and Malta became a parliamentary
  democratic republic in 1974.
  [biger-1995 §malta](../sources/biger-1995.md#malta) (p.377)
