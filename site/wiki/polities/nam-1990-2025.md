# Namibia (1990-2025)

## Summary

Namibia (1990-2025) covers independent Namibia from 21 March 1990 to the present. On that date Namibia gained independence from South Africa after a prolonged guerrilla war led by SWAPO. SWAPO won the first multi-party elections and formed the government [biger-1995 §namibia](../sources/biger-1995.md#namibia). ~~The territory is unchanged from the preceding mandate/colonial period.~~ **Corrected 2026-09-24:** not quite. **Walvis Bay** (about 1,124 km², with the Penguin Islands) was annexed by Britain in 1878, attached to the Cape in 1884 and administered by South Africa, and it stayed South African at Namibian independence. It was transferred to Namibia only on **1 March 1994**. So Namibia's territory grew in 1994, after this row starts.

## Territorial extent

Namibia is bounded by Angola and Zambia in the north, Botswana in the east, South Africa in the east and south, and the Atlantic Ocean. The Caprivi Strip extends northeast to the Zambia boundary. Area approximately 317,939 sq. miles (823,144 sq. km). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records status=independent, owner=NAM from 1990-03-21.

## Open questions

### oq-nam-walvis-bay

**Answered 2026-09-24.** CShapes 2.0 does not model the change. Every Namibia step from 1886-12-30 to 2019 measures the same 824,759 km² (ESRI:54034), and Walvis Bay is inside it throughout. So this row overstates 1990-1993 by Walvis Bay's ~1,124 km² (0.14%), and every earlier NAM row carries it as well, although it was never part of German South West Africa. The difference is too small to justify a split, so the page records it here instead.

Walvis Bay was retained by South Africa until 1994 when it was transferred to Namibia. CShapes may not capture this small territorial change. The next ingest should verify whether the polygon includes Walvis Bay throughout or only from 1994.
