# Gabon (1912-1919)

## Summary

French Gabon in its reduced 1912-1919 configuration within French Equatorial Africa (AEF), covering the period 30 March 1912 to 27 June 1919. CShapes records area 18.42 sq deg for this period (COW 481, status=colony, owner=France COW 220), down from 21.18 sq deg in the preceding GAB-1839-1912 period. The reduction corresponds to the reorganisation of AEF following the Fez Treaty of 1912, which transferred territory to German Kamerun (the so-called "Neukamerun" strip). After Germany's defeat in WWI, Gabon's area was restored to 21.18 sq deg at 1919-06-28. "On 17 August 1960 Gabon became independent" [biger-1995 §gabon](../sources/biger-1995.md#gabon).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 18.42 sq deg for the period 1912-03-30 to 1919-06-27, status=colony, owner=France (COW 220). The area reduction from 21.18 to 18.42 sq deg reflects the loss of the "Neukamerun" strip to Germany under the 4 November 1911 convention. Bounded by Equatorial Guinea (217.5 miles; 350 km), Cameroon (185 miles; 298 km), and Congo (1,183 miles; 1,903 km) [biger-1995 §gabon](../sources/biger-1995.md#gabon).
