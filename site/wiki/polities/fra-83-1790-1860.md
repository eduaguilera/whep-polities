# Var (département, to 1860)

## Summary

The French département of Var from its creation in 1790 to 23 June 1860, when the arrondissement of
Grasse (Grasse, Cannes, Antibes, Vence) was detached to form Alpes-Maritimes with the annexed County of
Nice [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §var](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#var). Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old `FRA-83-1790-2025` row at 1860 (issue 686);
modern Var is [fra-83-1860-2025](fra-83-1860-2025.md).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried modern Var back to 1790, without the arrondissement of
Grasse, which was part of Var for seventy years. No data reaches these years (the panel's Var starts in
1862); the split is for the territorial record and makes the 1800-1859 container edge honest.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. Constructed (`build_fra_83_1790_1860` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): GADM 4.1 adm2 Var (`FRA.13.5_1`) plus the adm3 arrondissement of
Grasse (`FRA.13.2.1_1`) [gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md).

**km² reasoning (Mollweide).** 7,287 km²: modern Var 6,045 km² plus Grasse 1,242 km². The modern
arrondissement is the 1860 one; it has kept its communes on the right bank of the Var since. Modern
Var's own boundary has not changed since 1860.
