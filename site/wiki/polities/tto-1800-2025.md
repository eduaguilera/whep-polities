## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Trinidad and Tobago, two islands in the Caribbean Sea. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. "The islands were neglected Spanish possessions until 1797, when they surrendered to a British naval expedition" (p.494). "Trinidad was acquired by the British in 1802 and administratively combined with Tobago in 1889" (p.494) [biger-1995 &sect;trinidad-and-tobago](../sources/biger-1995.md#trinidad-and-tobago).

## Territorial extent

Trinidad and Tobago has a total land area of 1,980 sq. miles (5,126 sq. km). No land boundaries. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected).
