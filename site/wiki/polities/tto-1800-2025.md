# Trinidad and Tobago (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Trinidad and Tobago, two islands in the Caribbean Sea, covering 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. "The islands were neglected Spanish possessions until 1797, when they surrendered to a British naval expedition" (p.494). "Trinidad was acquired by the British in 1802 and administratively combined with Tobago in 1889" (p.494) [biger-1995 &sect;trinidad-and-tobago](../sources/biger-1995.md#trinidad-and-tobago); independence followed on 31 August 1962. The 1889 administrative union and the 1962 independence changed who governed the two islands, not their extent, so one row carries the whole span.

## Territorial extent

Trinidad and Tobago has a total land area of 1,980 sq. miles (5,126 sq. km). No land boundaries. The polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature 52 at its 1886 vintage, back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025; the boundary being two coastlines, the back-projection carries no territorial error.
