# Chile (1899-1902)

## Summary

This row tracks Chile from **1899** to **1902** — a **4-year
transitional row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). ~~The split

**Corrected 2026-09-24:** the two splits are the two Chile–Argentina
boundary awards, not the War of the Pacific:

- **1899, the Puna de Atacama.** Chile had held the Puna since taking the
  Bolivian littoral. Bolivia ceded it to Argentina in 1889, and Chile and
  Argentina submitted the dispute to arbitration. The Buenos Aires conference
  and the award of the US minister William Buchanan (March 1899) gave most of
  the Puna, about 64,000 km² of some 75,000, to Argentina.
- **1902, Patagonia.** The award of King Edward VII (20 November 1902)
  fixed the Andean boundary south of about 40°S, dividing the disputed
  ~94,000 km² into roughly 54,000 km² for Chile and 40,000 for Argentina.

The committed polygons show both steps: CHL-1884-1899 is 745,190 km², this
row 697,939 km² and CHL-1902-2025 742,597 km² (ESRI:54034).
The award areas are the rounded figures in the usual accounts. They are
related to these deltas but not identical to them, because CShapes also
redraws the lines in other places.
