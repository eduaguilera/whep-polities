# Norfolk Island (Australian Territory)

## Summary

This row tracks **Norfolk Island** as an **Australian External Territory** from **1914** (when Australia assumed administration from Britain) through the database truncation year 2025. Norfolk Island is a tiny volcanic island (~34.6 km²) in the south Pacific Ocean, approximately 1,400 km east-northeast of Sydney.

**Why this entry exists.** The FAO 1952 yearbook (`fao1952` source) reports Norfolk Island **separately from Australia** with 9 rows of land use and tractor count data for 1949–1951. Routing these rows to `AUS-1901-2025` (~7.69 million km²) would overstate Australia's territory by a factor of approximately 220,000. WHEP rules prohibit folding sub-territory data up into the parent/aggregate polity.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** GADM 4.1, feature `NFK`, vintage 2023. Area: **~35 km²**.
- **Method.** Modern-constructed estimate (`proxy`). Norfolk Island's boundaries are the coastline of a fixed volcanic island; no territorial changes have occurred since Australian administration began in 1914. The 2023 GADM polygon accurately represents the territory throughout 1914–2025.
- **polygon_status: assigned.** The GADM 4.1 NFK feature provides a clear, stable, accurate boundary. The fixed island geography makes the modern polygon an appropriate representation.
- **Polygon vintage drift.** The GADM polygon vintage is 2023; data years are 1949–1951 (~74 years drift). For a fixed island territory with no territorial changes, this drift is inconsequential.
- **CShapes 2.0 note.** CShapes 2.0 does not include Norfolk Island as a distinct feature. Neither Cliopatria nor Paine-2024 contain a Norfolk Island feature.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** Australia assumed administration from Britain on 1 March 1914 under the Norfolk Island Act 1913. No WHEP predecessor row exists for the British colonial period (1788–1913).
- **Successor.** Still an Australian External Territory as of 2025.

## Sourced claims

- **FAO 1952 yearbook.** Reported Norfolk Island separately from Australia, 9 rows, years 1949–1951, covering land use categories and tractor counts.
- **Australian administration from 1914.** Under the Norfolk Island Act 1913 (Cth).
- **Area.** ~34.6 km² (Australian Bureau of Statistics; GADM 4.1 feature NFK).
- **Stable territory.** No territorial changes since 1914. The modern GADM polygon is an accurate proxy for the historical extent.

## Decisions

- **polity_code.** `NFK-1914-2025`. "NFK" is the ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code for Norfolk Island. Start year 1914 = commencement of Australian administration; end year 2025 = database truncation (still active).
- **type.** Assigned `territory` — Norfolk Island is an Australian external territory, not a sovereign state.
- **iso3.** `NFK` — the standard ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code for Norfolk Island.
- **cow.** `NA` — not a COW state-system member.
- **Polygon selection.** GADM 4.1 NFK feature selected as the only available digital polygon. Fixed island geography makes the modern boundary assignment valid with no meaningful vintage drift.
- **Single continuous row.** No territorial changes within 1914–2025 trigger a WHEP split. The 2015 governance reform (abolishing the Legislative Assembly) is an internal administrative change.
- **Predecessor not set.** The British colonial period (1788–1913) is not represented in WHEP.

## Open questions
