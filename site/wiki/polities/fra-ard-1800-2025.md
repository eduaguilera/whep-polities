# Ardèche

## Summary

Ardèche is a French département in the south of the Rhône valley, one of the roughly 83 original départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform out of the former province of Vivarais. It has not been split, merged into a neighbor, or had its borders redrawn by war or annexation since creation, unlike the four departure cases this database treats specially (Territoire de Belfort, the Alsace-Lorraine départements, the Corsican split, and the overseas départements). `type: subnational` because Ardèche was never a sovereign entity: France held continuous sovereignty over it across the whole span, and this row exists purely to carry a reporting territory that is smaller than any national FRA row so it does not compete with the national chain for statistics genuinely collected at département level. The row's own span, 1800-2025, is shorter than Ardèche's actual administrative life (from 1790): the earliest FRA container row in this database is FRA-1800-1871, so the entry starts where a container becomes available rather than at the department's true founding date, and that gap is flagged as an open question below.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures 20+ rows of French subnational statistics keyed to the label "Ardèche" or its historical predecessor references, spanning 1862-2023, currently mismatched to the national-total rows FRA-1800-1919 / FRA-1919-2025 (which are ~87x Ardèche's own area and mix in every other département's figures). No existing polity in the database is named Ardèche or matches its extent, so match_existing was inapplicable and a new subnational row was required. The routing decision records the unit as 97.6% observed/blank-method data, i.e. genuine collected département-level statistics rather than a back-cast reconstruction, which rules out needing a back_cast segment. Historical confirmation of Ardèche as a distinct administrative and statistical reporting unit comes from its continuous existence as one of the 1790 Revolutionary départements, administered separately (prefecture at Privas) and reported separately in French national statistical yearbooks (Statistique générale de la France and its successors) throughout the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The correct registered source is gadm-4.1-adm1 (GADM's admin-1 layer, keyed by GID_1 — French départements appear as codes like FRA.7_1), and GADM does publish this boundary globally, but the locally fetched gadm-4.1-adm1.gpkg is a curated 81-country subset that currently contains zero France features. This makes the route registered_source_unfetched rather than new_source_needed: the source is identified and known to exist, just not present in this repository's data yet. polygon_source is set to the slug gadm-4.1-adm1 itself (not the route name), polygon_feature_id is left null since no GID_1 value should be guessed, and polygon_area_km2 is left unset — no measured or stated area accompanies this page.

**Territory description:** Ardèche is a département in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of southeastern France, bounded by the Rhône river to the east and the Cévennes mountains to the west, with its prefecture at Privas. On a modern map it corresponds exactly to INSEE department code 07. Its modern area is approximately 5,529 km2, and because French département boundaries of this kind have been geographically stable since 1790, that modern extent is a faithful description of the territory across the full 1800-2025 span this entry covers — there is no boundary-change history to account for, unlike departments affected by the four listed exception cases.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor or successor: Ardèche's territory was established in 1790 and has remained a single continuous département without merger, split, or renaming through to the present, so there is no other polity row this one continues from or hands off to. The entry's own start_year is 1800 rather than 1790 only because no FRA container row exists before 1800 in this database (see decisions and open questions), not because of any real administrative discontinuity in Ardèche itself.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-moved-to-1800

**start_year moved from the proposed 1790 to 1800 to avoid an uncontained gap**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, reasoning that Ardèche was created that year in the original Revolutionary département reform and has been stable since. However, the earliest FRA row available as a container in this database is FRA-1800-1871 (1800-1871); there is no FRA row covering 1790-1799, and the containment gate rejects an edge that falls outside either party's span. Rather than fabricate a container edge that would point outside FRA-1800-1871's own recorded span, this page starts at 1800, matching the earliest year a container actually exists. This shortens the entry by 10 years relative to Ardèche's true administrative founding, which is recorded as its own open question (oq-1790-1800-gap) rather than silently absorbed, since the unit's actual data (1862-2023) is unaffected by the change and it is unclear whether any data source needs the 1790-1800 decade at all.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-not-fetched

**GADM adm1 polygon for Ardèche is registered but not present locally**

The natural source for a French département boundary is gadm-4.1-adm1 (keyed by GID_1, e.g. FRA.7_1 for Ardèche), but the local gadm-4.1-adm1.gpkg is a curated subset covering only 81 countries and currently has zero France features. polygon_status is therefore unassigned and polygon_feature_id is null rather than a guessed GID_1 value. Resolving this requires re-fetching or re-curating the GADM 4.1 admin-1 layer to include France, then setting polygon_feature_id to the correct FRA.*_1 code for Ardèche and polygon_status to assigned. Until then this page carries no geometry and no area figure, and any downstream computation that needs Ardèche's shape (e.g. dasymetric reallocation) has no source to draw on.

### oq-1790-1800-gap

**1790-1800 administrative existence is not covered by any container row**

Ardèche was created in 1790 along with the other original Revolutionary départements, but the FRA polity chain in this database only begins in 1800 (FRA-1800-1871). This page's start_year was moved to 1800 to avoid an uncontained gap (see decisions), meaning the first decade of Ardèche's existence as a distinct administrative unit is not represented by any row in this database, national or subnational. Whether that decade matters depends on whether any dataset actually reports Ardèche-level statistics before 1800 — the routing decision's own data span (1862-2023) suggests it does not, but this should be confirmed before treating the gap as harmless, and before any future FRA-1790-1800 container row is added that might want this page's start_year restored to 1790.

### oq-department-boundary-stability

**Has Ardèche's boundary genuinely never changed since 1790, including minor commune reassignments?**

The routing reasoning and this page both assert boundary continuity since 1790, which is broadly true for French départements at the level that matters for GADM adm1 polygons. But départements have occasionally had small commune-level boundary adjustments with neighbors via canton or arrondissement reorganizations, and Ardèche specifically has not been checked against a historical-boundary source (rather than just modern GADM) for this claim. A cross-check against a source like Cassini or INSEE's historical commune-boundary records would confirm whether any adjustment large enough to matter for a national-accounts-scale figure occurred in this span, before the eventual GADM polygon is assigned and treated as valid back to 1800.
