# Biobío and Ñuble (Biobío region before the 2018 split)

## Summary

The ground of Chile's Región del Biobío as it was from 1976 until Ñuble was split off in 2018:
modern Biobío ([chl-bi-1976-2025](chl-bi-1976-2025.md)) plus modern Ñuble
([chl-nb-2018-2025](chl-nb-2018-2025.md)). Chile's regions were "created in 1974", replacing the 25
provinces, and the country convention dates them from 1976, when they became operative; Ñuble "was
created from what was then the Ñuble Province of the Biobío Region ... It became operational in
September 2018."
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §chile-regions](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#chile-regions)

It is typed `subnational` and sits inside [chl-1902-2025](chl-1902-2025.md). The span 1976-2025 is
not the region's administrative life (that ended in 2018) but the span over which the LatAm
subnational panel reports a series on this ground: it keeps Biobío's crops on the pre-split region in
every year, including after 2018. It is the same kind of row as
[chl-otr-1976-2025](chl-otr-1976-2025.md), a fixed union of GADM regions that one panel unit reports
as one.

## Why this entry exists

The panel's `CHL-BI` unit (`whep_production_subnational.parquet`, measured 2026-09-25) files three
kinds of series under one id, and they are two territories:

| indicator | rows | territory | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| area, production, yield (21 crops) | 5,045 (2,798 from 1976, 2,247 back-cast 1935-1975) | **Biobío + Ñuble** | the panel's `CHL-NB` unit has no crop row in any year; Biobío's share of Chile's crop area does not step at the split (0.251, 0.248, 0.247, 0.261 in 2016-2019), and its wheat area goes 66,695 ha in 2017 to 67,132 in 2018 |
| landuse (7 classes) | 868 | modern Biobío | the classes sum to 23,310 km² in every year, 0.97x [chl-bi-1976-2025](chl-bi-1976-2025.md)'s polygon; Ñuble's own landuse sums to 13,012 |
| livestock_stock | 420 | modern Biobío | Ñuble carries its own cattle series from 1910 |

So the crop rows were on 0.65x their territory while they routed to CHL-BI-1976-2025. PR #703 added an
optional `indicator` scope to the alias registry for exactly this unit shape; this row is the
territory the crop scope routes to. The measurements are recorded on
[chl-bi-1976-2025 §oq-biobio-unit-is-two-territories](chl-bi-1976-2025.md#oq-biobio-unit-is-two-territories).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the union of GADM 4.1 adm1 `CHL.6_1` (Biobío) and
`CHL.13_1` (Ñuble), the same features the two parts' own rows carry, so whole and parts come from one
source and tile exactly. It measures **37,077 km²** at source (ESRI:54034) and 37,133 km² as shipped
in `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`, against 24,043 for modern Biobío and 13,086 for Ñuble as
shipped (sum 37,129, 0.9999x the whole; the difference is each outline being simplified on its own). [gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

**km² reasoning.** Chile publishes about 37,000 km² for Biobío before the split and about 24,000 after
it (see [chl-bi-1976-2025](chl-bi-1976-2025.md)); the union is 1.54x modern Biobío, the factor by
which the crop rows were mis-sized. No boundary of the two regions moved between 1976 and 2018 other
than the split itself, so the modern GADM outlines are the region's own ground, not a proxy for it.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1976 the ground was the Concepción, Arauco, Bío-Bío and Ñuble provinces of the
pre-1974 system, which have no rows; the panel's 1935-1975 crop rows reach this row as `back_cast`,
as Biobío's own pre-1976 landuse and livestock rows reach chl-bi-1976-2025. The 2018 split is not a
succession here: the series this row carries continues on the whole.

## Data routing

- **Panel, crops.** `CHL-BI` (`whep-lab-latam`) and `Biobío` (`juan-subnational`), indicators `area`,
  `production`, `yield`: 1976-2023 observed, 1900-1975 `back_cast` (data from 1935). **5,045 rows**,
  all moved from CHL-BI-1976-2025.
- **Panel, landuse and livestock.** The same labels scoped to `landuse` and `livestock_stock` stay on
  CHL-BI-1976-2025. Every rule of the split is scoped, so no blank rule shares a label and year with
  a scoped one (`validate_aliases.py` check 8).
- **Layer B**: none.

Whole and parts receive disjoint indicators: crops here, landuse and livestock on the parts, and the
panel's `CHL-NB` carries no crop row. No (item, indicator, year) cell is on both this row and one of
its parts, so nothing is averaged or double-counted (registered as `separate_series` in
`polity_composition.csv`).

**Consumer.** WHEP reads the `indicator` column only from eduaguilera/whep#1294; a resolver without it
would see five rules for one label and years. That PR must merge before WHEP re-syncs past this row.

## Decisions

- **Code `CHL-BINB`**, the two parts' ISO 3166-2-style abbreviations joined (BI + NB), because the
  row names their union and neither part's code may be reused.
- **Span 1976-2025, not 1976-2018.** The row carries a statistical series on this ground, which does
  not end at the split; ending it in 2018 would leave 2018-2023 crop rows with no territory of their
  own, the defect this row exists to remove.
- **Crops only.** Landuse and livestock are measured on modern Biobío and stay there.

## Open questions

None specific to this row. Whether the panel should report Ñuble's crops separately from 2018 is a
question for the panel's maintainers.
