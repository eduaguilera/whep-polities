# Swazi Kingdom

## Summary

The pre-colonial Swazi Kingdom from the 1800 database floor to the London Convention of 1894, which placed Swaziland under South African Republic (Transvaal) administration as a protected state. The Swazi state was consolidated in the early 19th century under Sobhuza I (reigned 1815–1839), who incorporated surrounding clans. Although the 1894 convention ended Swazi sovereignty, the Ngwenyama (Swazi kingship) institution has continued unbroken to the present — making modern Eswatini one of the few surviving African precolonial monarchies.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Covers the modern territory of Eswatini (formerly Swaziland), bounded east by Mozambique and elsewhere by South Africa.
