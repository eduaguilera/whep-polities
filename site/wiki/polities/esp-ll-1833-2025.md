# Lleida (province of Spain)

## Summary

This entry captures the province of Lleida, one of the four provinces of Catalonia in northeastern Spain, from its creation in the 1833 Javier de Burgos territorial reform to the present. Lleida sits inland, bordering Aragon to the west, Andorra and France to the north (via the Pyrenees), and the provinces of Barcelona and Tarragona to the east and south; its capital is the city of Lleida. `type: subnational` because the province was never sovereign — it has always been a first-order administrative subdivision, first of the unitary Spanish state and, since the 1979 Statute of Autonomy, of the Autonomous Community of Catalunya — and the entry exists purely to carry province-level statistical reporting that is finer-grained than the national ESP totals and distinct from Catalunya-wide regional aggregates. No existing ESP polity in the table is Lleida: the only prior ESP candidates were the national total and unrelated provinces (A Coruña, Madrid), so data keyed to Lleida specifically had nowhere correct to land before this row.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: routing decision for unit ES513 (INE province code Lleida, Spain, iso3 ESP) proposed a new polity spanning 1833-2025, following the same 1833 Javier de Burgos reform default applied to the other Spanish province entries; row count and specific source series were not enumerated in the routing payload, only the unit identity and country. Previously matched to: nothing correctly — the routing note states no existing ESP candidate matched Lleida by name; the only ESP rows available before this entry were the national total and unrelated provinces (A Coruña, Madrid), so any Lleida-labelled data would either have been misrouted to the national total (understating the specificity of a province-level statistic) or left unmatched. What confirms the entity was distinct: Lleida is one of the 49 original provinces created by the 1833 decree, administratively distinct from its neighbours (Barcelona, Tarragona, Huesca/Aragon) from that date forward, and INE has continuously reported statistics under its own province code (cpro 25) ever since — the same institutional basis already accepted for the 50 sibling ESP province entries in this table.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The intended source, `mapspain-ign` (INE/IGN provincial boundaries, keyed by cpro code — Lleida is cpro 25), is registered in `scripts/sources.yaml` and is already the polygon source of record for sibling entries such as ESP-A, ESP-AB, ESP-AL and ESP-BU, but the underlying file (`data/geodata/mapspain-ign/provinces.gpkg`) has not been fetched into this repository (`present_locally: false`), so no feature id can be assigned yet. GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer for Spain resolves only to the 18 autonomous communities and cannot substitute, since it has no province-level unit for Lleida. `polygon_status` is therefore `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `estimate`: the correct source is known, just not yet on disk.

**Territory description:** Modern Lleida province covers approximately 12,150 km2 of inland Catalonia, spanning from the high Pyrenees in the north (the Aran Valley and Pallars comarques) down through the pre-Pyrenean ranges to the flat, irrigated Segrià plain around the city of Lleida in the south. Provincial boundaries in Spain have been essentially stable since the 1833 reform — occasional comarcal/municipal reorganizations within Catalonia (e.g. the 1936-1939 Republican-era comarcal reform, later reversed) did not alter the province-level boundary used for statistical reporting — so once fetched, a modern IGN provincial boundary is expected to be a low-risk proxy for the entire 1833-2025 span, consistent with how the other 49 Spanish provinces are already handled in this table. No area figure is stated here as a measurement of this entry's own geometry, since no geometry is yet attached; the ~12,150 km2 figure above is the commonly cited modern administrative area of the province and is territory evidence, not a polygon measurement.

## Predecessors and successors

Lleida has no predecessor entry: it was created directly by the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform out of the former Principality of Catalonia's territorial divisions, not by fission or merger of another WHEP polity already in the table. It has no successor: the province still exists today as a first-order administrative division of Spain (within the Autonomous Community of Catalunya since 1979), so end_year 2025 is an open, ongoing span rather than a dissolution, matching the convention used for the other 49 Spanish provinces already in the table (e.g. ESP-B, ESP-BU).
