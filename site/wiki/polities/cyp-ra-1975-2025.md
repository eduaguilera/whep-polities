# Republic of Cyprus government-controlled area (1975-2025)

## Summary

The part of the island of Cyprus south of the 1974 ceasefire line, which the statistics of the
Government of the Republic of Cyprus cover from 1975. A **reporting-area row**: the polity
`CYP-1879-2025` stays the whole island, as CShapes 2.0 codes it, and this row takes no position on
the status of the northern part. The wording follows the phrase used in the Republic's own and in
EU statistics, "areas under the effective control of the Government of the Republic of Cyprus".

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`. FAOSTAT area **50
Cyprus** was routed to `CYP-1879-2025` for 1961-2024 with no split at 1974, but its series drop at
1975 to the southern part (production pin 20260325T111448Z-4fffe, harvested area in ha):

| | 1973 | 1974 | 1975 | 1976 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| wheat | 84,281 | 86,957 | 26,756 | 28,763 |
| barley | 74,917 | 73,579 | 29,432 | 32,776 |
| all items below code 1700 | 321,152 | 300,826 | 169,088 | 179,234 |

Total harvested area falls by 44% in one year and does not recover, which is a change of coverage,
not of harvest. 1974's cereal harvest (May-June) was gathered before the events of July-August, so 1974 stays on
the whole island. `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes area 50 1975-2024 here
through `manual_territory_routes`.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** proxy, constructed by `build_cyp_ra_1975_2025()`: CShapes 352 (the outline
`CYP-1879-2025` binds) **intersected** with Cliopatria's "Republic of Cyprus" 1976-2023 step, whose
northern edge is the ceasefire line. See [CShapes 2.0's border coding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)
and [Cliopatria](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md).

```
CShapes 352 (whole island)                 9,128 km²   ESRI:54034
Cliopatria Republic of Cyprus 1976-2023    7,198 km²   coarse coast
intersection = this polygon                5,952 km²
published in polities_database.gpkg        5,961 km²   after build_database simplification (+1.1% vs declared)
remainder (the northern part)              3,176 km²   centred 33.48 E, 35.25 N
declared                                   5,896 km²   island 9,251 less the ~3,355 km² northern part (+1.0%)
```

The outline comes from the container and only the line comes from Cliopatria, because Cliopatria's
coastline is coarse (its whole-island step measures 11,437 km² against CShapes' 9,128). Point tests:
Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, south Nicosia and Dhekelia inside; north Nicosia and Morphou outside.

`proxy` for two reasons. The UN buffer zone (about 346 km²) and the British Sovereign Base Areas
(about 254 km²) are not separated; the declared figure includes both, and whether FAOSTAT's series
includes their farmland is not established. And Cliopatria's line is coarse at its eastern end:
a test point in Famagusta town (33.94 E, 35.12 N), which lies north of the line, falls inside the
polygon.

## Predecessors and successors

None declared: this is a reporting area inside `CYP-1879-2025` from 1975, recorded as a containment
edge.

## Contradictions

It lies inside its container by construction. Use either `CYP-1879-2025` or this row for the same
year, never both.
