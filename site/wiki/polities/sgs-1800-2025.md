# South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands
## Summary

A British Overseas Territory in the South Atlantic: South Georgia, about 1,400 km southeast of the
Falklands, and the South Sandwich Islands further south. Claimed by Britain in 1775, a **Falkland
Islands Dependency** from 1908, and a separate territory since **1985**. Uninhabited apart from a
research station at King Edward Point; the whaling stations closed in 1965.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = SGS`.

```
GADM 4.1 measured      4,096.7 km²
official land area     3,903 km²
ratio                  1.050x
```

**`polygon_status: estimate`.** A 5.0% divergence on a territory that is largely glaciated is a
coastline-definition difference — where the ice front is drawn moves the figure — rather than a
disagreement about which islands are included. The declared figure is the **official land area**,
not what the polygon measures, so check A compares the geometry against something independent of it
(issue 195).

**It does not overlap the Falklands, and that was measured rather than assumed.** South Georgia was
administered *from* Stanley for 77 years, so `FLK-1833-2025`'s polygon might have been expected to
include it; the intersection is **0 km²**. The dependency relationship was administrative, and the
polygon correctly reflects territory rather than administration — the same distinction that keeps
`NCL` out of `WLF`'s chain fields.

## Span

`1800-2025`, the constant-territory convention — the islands' coastlines have not moved, and no
boundary exists to have shifted. The administrative history (British claim 1775, Falklands
Dependency 1908-1985, separate territory since) is **sovereignty and administration**, not
territory, and this schema has no sovereignty field. Same treatment as
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md) and [fro-1800-2025](fro-1800-2025.md).

Argentina claims the territory as part of Tierra del Fuego province. This database records
*de facto* administration, as it does for the Falklands, so the claim is noted here and not
modelled — `FLK-1833-2025` sets that precedent.

## Predecessors and successors

Both empty. There is no territorial predecessor: the islands were uninhabited when claimed. The
Falkland Islands Dependency was an administrative arrangement, not a predecessor entity, so
`FLK` does not belong in `predecessor:`.

## Contradictions

None identified.

## Open questions

### oq-sgs-zero-data

FAOSTAT area 271 carries **no production rows**, and the territory has no agriculture — it is ice,
tussock and a research station. This row exists so that a registry that lists it can resolve, not
because data is expected. If it never receives any, that is the correct outcome rather than a gap:
compare `oq-tkl-pre-1961-data`, where the row also predates any data reaching it.

### Already inside the statistical buckets, and that is not a new overlap

Measured before creating this row:

```
SGS ∩ RLAM-1850-2025   3,961.4 km²   96.7% of the territory
SGS ∩ ROW-1850-2025    3,959.8 km²   96.7%
SGS ∩ FLK-1833-2025            0 km²   the Falklands polygon does NOT contain it
```

That is the same relationship the other 22 territories in this class already have — `FRO`,
`NCL`, `MKD`, `GLP`, `MTQ` and the rest are all components of a regional "Other" bucket *and*
have their own polity. The buckets are in `validate_spatial_containment`'s
`LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` for exactly this reason. What changes is that data reported against this
territory can now reach it instead of only reaching a bucket.

### Why this entry exists

**WHEP's GLEAM 3.0 registry lists it as a country that exists today, and this database had no row
for it** (issue 187). `gleam_geographic_hierarchy` carries 204 rows; 201 resolved to a polity and
three did not — `ATF`, `SGS` and `WLF`.

The evidence is **weaker than for Aruba and the Holy See** (issue 185), and the difference is
worth stating: those two carried 244 and 268 published LUH2 rows, whereas FAOSTAT areas 71, 243
and 271 carry **zero** production rows today. So no whep value is currently mis-attributed. What
exists is a registry row in a source whep ships, whose crosswalk promotes this area out of the
Rest-of-World fold at read time while leaving it the shared `ROW-1850-2025` code — a bucket rather
than an identity. This repo already recorded the state as deliberate, in
`pipelines/faostat-era-matching/state/registry_unmapped.csv`: "registry area with no polity family
(non-country/aggregate)".
