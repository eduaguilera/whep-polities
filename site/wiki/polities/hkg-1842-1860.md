# Hong Kong (Hong Kong Island, 1842-1860)

## Summary

The British colony of Hong Kong as ceded by the Treaty of Nanking (29 August 1842): Hong Kong Island
and its islets [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §hong-kong](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#hong-kong). Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old `HKG-1842-2025` row
(issue 686); the later years are [hkg-1860-1898](hkg-1860-1898.md) and
[hkg-1898-2025](hkg-1898-2025.md).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried the modern SAR (about 1,135 km²) back to 1842, about 14
times the colony's real extent. No data reaches these years; the split is for the territorial record.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. Constructed (`build_hkg_1842_1860` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`): the union of the four GADM 4.1 districts on Hong Kong Island
(Central and Western, Wan Chai, Eastern, Southern) [gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md), north of
22.19 N. The cut drops the Po Toi islands, which belong to Southern District today but were leased only
in 1898.

**km² reasoning (Mollweide).** 84.0 km², against the about 80 km² usually given for the island; the
difference is Ap Lei Chau and the islets of the four districts and GADM's coast.
