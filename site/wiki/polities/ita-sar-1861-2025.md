# Sardegna (region of Italy)

## Summary

Sardegna is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni), comprising the island of Sardinia in the western Mediterranean together with minor offshore islands (notably the La Maddalena archipelago). This entry is `type: subnational` because Sardegna has never been sovereign in this period under Italy: it was the core territory of the pre-unification Kingdom of Sardinia (Regno di Sardegna), which itself supplied the crown and constitution of the new unified Kingdom of Italy proclaimed in 1861, so the island's incorporation into ITA at that date represents a change in the name and scope of the containing state rather than a conquest or annexation of the island itself. It has remained an administrative region of Italy continuously since, gaining special-statute autonomous status in 1948 (Regione Autonoma della Sardegna) without any change to its outer boundary. The entry exists to carry a *reporting territory* distinct from the national ITA rows: regional statistical series (agricultural production, population, and similar NUTS2-level data) report Sardegna specifically, at roughly 8% of Italy's total land area and with a distinct economic and agronomic profile (Mediterranean island agriculture — cork, sheep pastoralism, viticulture — quite unlike the national aggregate or the industrial northern regions), so routing this data to a national ITA row would misattribute a regional figure to the whole country. It is a sibling entry to ITA-LOM-1861-2025, ITA-PIE-1970-2025, ITA-LIG-1970-2025, and ITA-ABR-1970-2025, all created under the same country convention for Italy's NUTS2 regions, but Sardegna's own start_year is 1861 rather than 1970 because it is not one of the two documented departure regions (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia joining in 1919, Molise splitting from Abruzzi e Molise in 1963) and so falls to the default rule of the earliest containing national row's start_year.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a regional-level statistical series for Sardegna (Eurostat NUTS2 code ITG2, unit_id ITA-ITG2 in the routing decision), routed under the country convention for Italy's subnational regions defined in pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json. The routing decision treats it as a standard "region of Italy" case, the twelfth such NUTS2 unit processed after ITC1, ITC3, ITC4 (Lombardia), ITF1, ITF3-ITF6, ITG1, ITH1, ITH3, and the earlier-created ITA-LOM/ITA-PIE/ITA-LIG/ITA-ABR rows. Previously, absent a polity row for Sardegna, a first proposed verdict split the span into an unroutable 1861-1947 segment and a proposed 1948-2023 segment — rejected here because the territory existed as part of Italy continuously across that whole period (1948 marks only a change in Sardegna's internal governance status, from ordinary to special-statute autonomous region, not a territorial or sovereignty change), so nothing about this span is genuinely unroutable. Absent any Sardegna-specific row, this data's only other home would be the national ITA rows, which is wrong because those represent all of Italy (roughly 12-13x Sardegna's own area) and mixing an island-specific agricultural or population series into a national total would misattribute a distinctly regional figure to the whole country. What confirms Sardegna as a distinct reporting entity rather than a routing convenience: it is Eurostat's own standard NUTS2 statistical division of Italy (used because ISTAT itself reports Italian statistics at the regional level as a first-class unit), the same justification already applied to its eleven-plus already-created NUTS2 siblings, and additionally Sardegna was the named core territory of a distinct pre-unification sovereign state (the Kingdom of Sardinia), giving it a documented separate identity beyond its current administrative role.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The only registered source type that digitises country subdivisions at Sardegna's level (ADM1/NUTS2) is `gadm-4.1-adm1`, but the copy of that source present in this repository is an explicit curated subset of 81 countries that does not include Italy at all (zero ITA features), matching the situation already documented for ITA-LOM-1861-2025 and the other Italian regional siblings created in this batch. No other registered source (cshapes, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) covers modern Italian regional boundaries. `polygon_source` is therefore `none` and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` rather than a placeholder route name, per this pipeline's rule that `new_source_needed` and `registered_source_unfetched` are both rejected values for `polygon_source` itself.

**Territory description:** Sardegna covers approximately 24,100 km2, making it the second-largest island in the Mediterranean (after Sicily) and about 8% of Italy's total land area. A reader can locate it as the large island west of the Italian mainland and south of Corsica (separated from Corsica by the narrow Strait of Bonifacio), bounded by the Tyrrhenian Sea to the east and the Sardinian Sea (part of the western Mediterranean) to the west; its capital is Cagliari, at the island's southern tip. Unlike a mainland region, Sardegna's outer boundary is a coastline rather than a land border shared with neighbouring administrative units, so it has been geometrically fixed for the entire 1861-2025 span regardless of any Italian administrative reorganization; the only territorial ambiguity concerns whether small offshore islets (La Maddalena archipelago in the north, other minor islands) are included in a given source's outline, which is a minor vintage question rather than a boundary-change question. This stability makes Sardegna, if anything, a lower-risk future polygon proxy than the mainland regions in this same batch, once a source that actually covers Italy (e.g. GADM's full global release, not the local 81-country subset) is fetched.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first polity row created specifically for Sardegna, and it starts exactly at the region's entry into the unified Kingdom of Italy in 1861 (continuing the pre-unification Kingdom of Sardinia's own unbroken possession of the island), so there is no distinct prior polity to chain from. No successor: end_year 2025 is open, matching Sardegna's still-current status as a special-statute autonomous region (Regione Autonoma della Sardegna, since 1948) of the Italian Republic; a successor row would only be needed if a future reorganization abolished or redrew the region. The four container edges (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) tile the whole 1861-2025 span with no gap or overlap, reflecting that none of the national-level changes those edges track (Veneto 1866, Rome 1870, the WWI settlement of 1919) touched Sardegna's own boundary, which as an island has been geographically fixed throughout.

## Decisions

### d-polity-code-pattern

**Chose ITA-SAR-1861-2025 following the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern**

57 of 66 subnational rows in the table follow <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, including the near-identical sibling ITA-LOM-1861-2025 (Lombardia, same country, same 1861-2025 span, same routing situation). Sardegna is a modern first-level administrative region (NUTS2 ITG2) that has existed continuously as a subdivision of unified Italy since 1861 with no name change or discontinuity of its own, so it does not fit the bespoke-code criterion (a historical territory with a name and identity distinct from its role as a current administrative subdivision, like ALK or HYD). The routing decision's admin_name field gives 'ITG2' as the NUTS2 code and 'Sardegna' as the conventional Italian region name, so SAR is used as the subunit abbreviation rather than the NUTS2 code itself, matching the convention already used for ITA-LOM, ITA-PIE, ITA-LIG, ITA-ABR (region names, not NUTS codes).

### d-start-year-1861

**Set start_year to 1861 per the country convention's default rule**

The routing decision states Sardegna is not one of the two documented departures from the default start rule (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia joining in 1919, or Molise splitting from Abruzzi e Molise in 1963), so per country_conventions.json its start_year is the start_year of the earliest container national row whose territory includes it, ITA-1861-1866, giving 1861. This matches the precedent of ITA-LOM-1861-2025, which used the identical reasoning for Lombardia. Historically this is doubly apt for Sardegna specifically: the island was already the core of the pre-unification Kingdom of Sardinia (Regno di Sardegna), so 1861 marks a change of the containing state's name and scope, not a change to the island's own territory or administrative status, which was continuous through unification.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-source-unresolved

**No fetched polygon source covers Italian NUTS2 regions, Sardegna included**

As with all other ITA-<region> rows created in this pass (ITA-LOM, ITA-PIE, ITA-LIG, ITA-ABR), the only registered source type that digitises country subdivisions at this admin level is gadm-4.1-adm1, but the local copy of that source is an explicit 81-country curated subset that excludes Italy entirely (zero ITA features on disk), and no other registered source (cshapes, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) covers modern Italian regional boundaries. Sardegna's case is not identical to the mainland regions, though: as an island it has a much cleaner, more stable outer boundary (no province-level land-boundary reshuffling could ever touch its coastline), so once GADM's full global release or ISTAT's own regional cartography is fetched, a present-day Sardegna outline should be an unusually safe proxy back to 1861 — arguably safer than for any mainland region in this batch. This remains open until that source is actually fetched and licence-checked; polygon_source stays none and polygon_status stays unassigned rather than guessing a route name.

### oq-la-maddalena-sassari-boundary

**Minor offshore/administrative boundary questions not resolved by a region-level outline**

Sardegna's modern region includes small offshore archipelagos (notably La Maddalena in the Strait of Bonifacio, and other minor islets) whose administrative attachment to the region has been continuous, but whose precise inclusion in a generic 'Sardegna' polygon depends on which vintage of source is used. Separately, the region underwent internal provincial reorganizations in this period (creation and later abolition/reinstatement of provinces such as Olbia-Tempio, Ogliastra, Carbonia-Iglesias, and Medio Campidano between 2001 and 2016) that redrew provincial, not regional, boundaries — the same caveat noted for Lombardia's provinces — but this has not been independently verified against a source for Sardegna specifically. Whether any data series routed to this polity reports at the provincial rather than regional level, which would need a different (sub-regional) polygon than the one this entry will eventually carry, is unresolved.
