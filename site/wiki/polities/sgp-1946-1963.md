# Singapore (Crown Colony)

## Summary

This row tracks **Singapore** as a **British Crown Colony** from its
establishment on **1946-04-01** — when the Straits Settlements were
dissolved and Singapore was separated as a distinct Crown Colony — to
**1963-09-15**, the eve of Singapore's merger into the Federation of
Malaysia (16 September 1963).

The row exists because FAO/IIA yearbook **footnotes** for 1953 and
1960 report production or trade data for the **Federation of Malaya**
with Singapore's figures **composed into (folded under) the Malaya
host** rather than reported independently. Singapore therefore needs
its own WHEP entity to represent the geographic territory whose data
are subsumed under the Malayan/Malaysian aggregates in those years.

Before 1946, Singapore was part of the **Straits Settlements** (a
Crown Colony administered together with Penang and Malacca), which
is covered by [gbm-1895-1946](gbm-1895-1946.md). After dissolution
of the Straits Settlements in 1946, Singapore was carved out as a
separate Crown Colony while the remaining Malayan territories were
reorganised into the Malayan Union and then the Federation of Malaya.
Singapore maintained its own governor, legislative council, and
eventually self-government under the State of Singapore (1959),
though it remained formally a Crown Colony until merger into Malaysia
in September 1963. Singapore was then briefly part of Malaysia
(1963–1965) before being expelled and becoming fully independent on
1965-08-09.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` (Schvitz et al. 2022), feature
  `gwcode=830`, "Singapore", dated `1946-04-01` to `1962-12-31`
  (gwsdate/gwedate). Feature FID: 624.
- **Method.** `exact_historical` — the CShapes 2.0 feature matches
  the Crown Colony entity and geographic extent closely. The feature
  end date of 1962-12-31 is one year earlier than the formal end of
  Crown Colony status (1963-09-16), but the geographic territory is
  identical: the island of Singapore and adjacent smaller islets.
  CShapes records the next Singapore feature beginning 1965 (after
  independence), so the 1963–1965 period under Malaysia is not
  separately resolved for Singapore's territory in CShapes; the
  1946-1962 feature is the best available vintage.
- **Area.** 544.72 km² (as recorded in CShapes 2.0). This is
  consistent with the commonly cited land area of Singapore island
  (~725 km² today, but historically smaller due to land reclamation;
  the CShapes figure reflects the pre-reclamation extent).
- **Confidence.** High for geographic extent; moderate for the
  feature end date (CShapes ends the feature at 1962-12-31 rather
  than the historical 1963-09-15, but the territory is unchanged).
- **No proxy needed.** The CShapes 2.0 feature covers the entity's
  period and geography with minimal discrepancy.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [gbm-1895-1946](gbm-1895-1946.md) — British
  Malaya (1895–1946), within which Singapore was part of the Straits
  Settlements Crown Colony. When the Straits Settlements were
  dissolved on 1946-04-01, Singapore separated as its own Crown
  Colony.
- **Successors.**
  - [mys-1963-1965](mys-1963-1965.md) — Malaysia (1963–1965):
    Singapore merged into the Federation of Malaysia on
    1963-09-16, becoming one of its constituent states. During this
    period Singapore's data were folded under Malaysia.
  - [sgp-1965-2025](sgp-1965-2025.md) — Singapore (independent):
    Malaysia expelled Singapore on 1965-08-09, establishing the
    independent Republic of Singapore. The WHEP entry SGP-1965-2025
    covers this period.

## Contradictions

- CShapes 2.0 ends the Singapore 1946 feature at `1962-12-31`, not
  at the historically correct date of `1963-09-15` (eve of Malaysia
  merger). The one-year gap (Jan–Sep 1963) is not separately
  represented in CShapes. This is a known CShapes limitation for
  the transitional period; the geography is unchanged so the polygon
  is still faithful.
- The FAO/IIA footnote years (1953 and 1960) are within the Crown
  Colony period confirmed by CShapes; no contradiction in the
  annotation.

## Decisions

- **Period.** Start year set to 1946 (Crown Colony establishment);
  end year set to 1963 (year of Malaysia merger), consistent with the
  historical record rather than CShapes' 1962 feature end.
- **Polygon binding.** CShapes gwcode=830, feature year 1953 (inside
  the polity period 1946–1963, and inside the CShapes feature range
  1946–1962). `polygon_feature_id` set to `"830"` (gwcode, integer
  cast to string per WHEP convention).
- **Type.** Set to `colonial` — Singapore was a Crown Colony, not
  an independent sovereign state, during this period.
- **iso3.** Set to `SGP` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 for Singapore). No
  separate historical ISO code exists for Crown Colony Singapore.
- **Predecessor.** GBM-1895-1946 confirmed to exist in the database.
- **Successors.** MYS-1963-1965 and SGP-1965-2025 both confirmed
  to exist in the database.
- **No separate 1963–1965 Singapore row.** The brief period when
  Singapore was a state within Malaysia (Sept 1963–Aug 1965) is
  covered under [mys-1963-1965](mys-1963-1965.md). A separate
  SGP-1963-1965 entity is not warranted because Singapore did not
  report separately during this period.

## Open questions

### oq-cshapes-end-date

CShapes 2.0 ends the gwcode=830 Singapore feature at `1962-12-31`
rather than the historically correct `1963-09-15`. It is unclear
whether this reflects a data entry decision (treating the 1963
transitional year as folded into Malaysia) or a simplification. The
next Singapore feature starts at `1965-08-09` (independence), leaving
a ~2.75-year gap (Jan 1963–Aug 1965) unrepresented in CShapes for
Singapore specifically. The MYS-1963-1965 row covers the Malaysia
period; this gap does not affect WHEP coverage.

### oq-area-land-reclamation

The CShapes figure of 544.72 km² is considerably smaller than modern
Singapore's area (~733 km² in 2023). The difference is explained by
extensive land reclamation undertaken from the 1960s onward. It is
worth confirming whether the CShapes polygon represents the
pre-reclamation coastline accurately, or whether it uses a simplified
boundary.

### oq-straits-settlements-predecessor

The Straits Settlements (including Singapore, Penang, Malacca) are
covered by GBM-1895-1946. It may be appropriate to create a more
specific predecessor entry for the Straits Settlements distinct from
the broader British Malaya entry, if Straits Settlements data were
reported separately in historical sources.
