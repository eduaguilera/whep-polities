# Togo

## Summary

Togo is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Togo from 1960 to the present. French Togoland became independent Togo on 27 April 1960.

## Territorial extent

Togo is bounded by Ghana (545 miles; 877 km) in the west, Burkina Faso in the north, and Benin in the east. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "Germany established a protectorate over the coastal area west of Togoland (later Togo) in the second half of the nineteenth century, establishing the colony of Gold Coast (later Ghana) on 13 January 1886" (p.244). Britain captured the area on 5 July 1884. [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- The Ghana-Togo boundary "follows a dividing line between the British and French mandate areas in the former German protectorate of Togoland. It runs for 545 miles (877 km.) between the boundary tripoint with Burkina Faso in the north and the Gulf of Guinea in the south" (p.244). "French Togoland became independent Togo on 27 April 1960" (p.244). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- The division of the Awe people between northern Togoland and Ghana by the British-French line of 1919 "later became an international boundary between two independent states. The Awes are calling for reunification, which may result in a change in the line" (p.245). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- **German territory until WWI; divided between France and Britain as mandate** (p.492). British part joined Ghana 1957; French Togoland independent April 1960 (p.492). Olympio overthrown 1963 by Eyadema (p.493). [biger-1995 §togo](../sources/biger-1995.md#togo)

## Open questions

### oq-togoland-partition

Togoland partition. The former German colony of Togoland was divided between Britain and France after WWI. British Togoland was united with Gold Coast on 6 March 1957. The partition history and its impact on present boundaries needs further investigation.
