# Greenland

## Summary

This row tracks Greenland as a territorial-economic unit for **226 years**,
from the **1800** WHEP database floor to the **2025** database ceiling
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Greenland is a
"self-governing island region of Denmark in the North Atlantic. It has no
land boundaries." [biger-1995 §greenland](../sources/biger-1995.md#greenland).
It is the world's largest island, covering approximately 836,000 km² of
ice-free land (total area ~2.1 million km²).

Throughout this entire period, Greenland has been administered by Denmark —
first as a colony, then as a county of Denmark (from 1953), then as a
home-rule territory (from 1979), and as a self-governing territory (from
2009). **No territorial change occurred** across any of these status
transitions. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
these are regime/status changes, not territorial changes, and one row for
the full 1800–2025 period is correct.

- **COW = NA.** Greenland has never been listed in the COW state system
  as an independent state. It does not appear in CShapes 2.0 as a
  separate unit — CShapes' population threshold for dependencies is
  250,000, and Greenland's population has historically been below this
  threshold [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies).
- **Polygon source: GADM 4.1.** Because CShapes does not include
  Greenland as a separate unit, the CSV uses GADM 4.1 as the polygon
  source [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **Relationship to DNK chain.** The Denmark chain
  ([dnk-1800-1864](dnk-1800-1864.md), [dnk-1864-1920](dnk-1864-1920.md),
  [dnk-1920-2025](dnk-1920-2025.md)) tracks metropolitan Denmark only.
  Greenland is a distinct WHEP row because it is a non-adjacent territory
  with its own production and trade statistics.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `GADM 4.1` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes 2.0 does
  not cover Greenland as a separate unit — it is either subsumed under
  the Denmark polygon or excluded altogether because Greenland's
  population has historically been under the 250,000 dependency
  threshold [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies).
  GADM 4.1 provides the administrative boundary polygon.
- **Island geography.** Greenland has no land boundaries
  [biger-1995 §greenland](../sources/biger-1995.md#greenland). Its
  borders are defined by coastline and have not changed over the
  WHEP period.
- **Area stability.** No territorial changes occurred between 1800
  and 2025. The island's geographic extent is fixed.
- **Verification status.** CSV records `VERIFIED`
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

## Sourced claims

- **Self-governing island region of Denmark.** "Greenland is a
  self-governing island region of Denmark in the North Atlantic. It has
  no land boundaries."
  [biger-1995 §greenland](../sources/biger-1995.md#greenland)
- **Not in CShapes 2.0.** CShapes includes dependent territories with
  population above 250,000. Greenland's population in 1994 was
  approximately 55,000 — well below the threshold — meaning CShapes
  properly excludes it as a dependency unit
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies).
- **Not in COW state system.** Greenland (`GRL`, iso3) does not appear
  in the COW state-system membership list (v2024). It has never been
  listed as an independent state
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion).
- **GADM polygon.** The CSV records `GADM 4.1` as the polygon source
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). GADM provides
  administrative boundary polygons for territories that are not covered
  by CShapes.
- **Data sources.** The CSV records `FT; FAOSTAT; M49; CShapes` as data
  sources, indicating that FAOSTAT and other international statistical
  databases report production/trade data for Greenland as a distinct
  unit — justifying its status as a separate WHEP row rather than being
  absorbed into Denmark [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

## Open questions

### oq-cshapes-absence

**CShapes and the pre-1886 polygon.** CShapes 2.0 excludes Greenland
entirely (population below 250,000 threshold). GADM 4.1 provides the
current administrative boundary. Whether the GADM polygon is
appropriate for the full 1800–2025 window — including the colonial
period — is not yet verified. A historical source documenting the
formal Danish colonial demarcation of Greenland would strengthen the
polygon provenance for the pre-home-rule period.

### oq-home-rule-economic-impact

**1979 Home Rule / 2009 Self-Governance economic implications.**
Greenland gained home rule in 1979 and full self-governance (with
control over natural resources) in 2009. These are status changes, not
territorial changes, and do not warrant a WHEP row split under the
territorial-change rule. However, if FAOSTAT or FT trade statistics
began reporting Greenland separately at one of these dates (rather than
including Greenland under Denmark), the effective data-coverage
start may differ from the nominal WHEP start of 1800. A data-sources
audit would clarify whether Greenland has distinct trade records
throughout or only from a specific date.

### oq-independence-trajectory

**Independence referendum trajectory.** Greenland's government has
expressed intent to pursue independence from Denmark. If Greenland
becomes independent within the WHEP project's active period, this row
would need to be split and a successor row created. No action needed
now (still active at 2025 ceiling), but the open question is flagged
for monitoring.
