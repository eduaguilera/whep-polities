# Lithuania (interwar republic)

## Summary

The Republic of Lithuania as an independent state from its restoration of
independence on 16 February 1918 to Soviet annexation on 3 August 1940.
Independence was contested through the Lithuanian Wars of Independence
(1918-1920) against Soviet Russia, the Bermontians, and Poland. The
Soviet-Lithuanian Peace Treaty of 12 July 1920 recognised Lithuania including
the Vilnius region, but Poland seized Vilnius in October 1920 and held it until
1939 — so the effective interwar capital was Kaunas. The Klaipėda (Memel) region
was added in 1923 and lost to Nazi Germany in 1939.
