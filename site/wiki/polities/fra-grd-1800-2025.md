# Gard (département)

## Summary

Gard is a French département in the Occitanie region of southern France, on the Mediterranean coast west of the Rhône, containing the cities of Nîmes (prefecture), Alès, and Bagnols-sur-Cèze. It was created in March 1790 as one of the roughly 83 original départements of the Revolutionary territorial reform, replacing the pre-Revolutionary province structure, and its boundaries have remained essentially unchanged since — it is not among the départements affected by the major 19th-20th century departures the country's routing convention tracks (the 1871 loss of Alsace-Lorraine, the later Belfort split, the Corsican division, or the creation of overseas départements). This entry is `type: subnational`: Gard was never a sovereign state and France held continuous national sovereignty over it throughout; the row exists purely to carry a sub-national statistical reporting unit distinct from the whole-country totals in the FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025 national chain, so it must not compete with those rows for family/sovereignty ranking in the matcher.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id FRA-FRJ12, admin_name FRJ12) captures a France-labelled data extract for Gard beginning in 1862, described as ~97.6% observed/blank-method (direct measurement, not back-cast reconstruction onto a not-yet-existing boundary). Before this entry, no polity in the FRA vocabulary represented Gard specifically — only three national-level FRA polities existed (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025), and Gard-level data would have had nowhere to route except incorrectly up to one of those whole-country rows, which is roughly 100x Gard's territory and would blend a single département's series into national totals. What confirms Gard is a distinct, long-standing administrative entity rather than an ad-hoc statistical grouping is its formal creation in the March 1790 départements reform, which is the founding act of the modern French administrative map and predates any of the data sources this pipeline ingests; Gard has been tabulated as a discrete administrative unit in French statistical publications (e.g. INSEE and its predecessors) continuously since.

**Territory description:** Gard covers roughly 5,853 km² in the Occitanie region of southern France, bordered by the Rhône river to the east, the Cévennes mountains to the northwest, and the Mediterranean coast (the Camargue's western edge) to the south. Its prefecture is Nîmes; other notable towns include Alès, Bagnols-sur-Cèze, and Uzès. A reader can locate it on a modern map as the département directly north of the Bouches-du-Rhône/Camargue area and south of Ardèche and Lozère. No area figure is measured from an attached polygon here, since none is yet assigned; 5,853 km² is the modern INSEE-published département area, cited as context for locating the territory, not as a geometry measurement.

## Decisions

### d-code-choice-fra-grd

**Chose FRA-GRD-1800-2025 over the taken FRA-GARD-1800-2025**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790 following the country's default rule for original 1790 départements, but the container chain's earliest row, FRA-1800-1871, only begins in 1800, so 1790-1800 would be uncontained under the containment gate's requirement that an edge fall inside both parties' spans. I moved start_year to 1800 to tile the whole span with no gap, per the routing_concerns note flagging this exact mismatch. Separately, the code FRA-GARD-1800-2025 is already held by an unrelated page not present in the built table, so I used the abbreviation GRD instead of GARD for the subunit code, following the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows (e.g. ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CA-1833-2025).

## Open questions

### oq-fra-grd-start-year

**Is 1790-1800 legitimately uncontained, or should this row start in 1790 with a gap flagged elsewhere?**

Gard was created in March 1790 as one of the original ~83 Revolutionary départements and has existed continuously since. The routing decision's span_basis argued for start_year 1790 following the country's default rule for original départements, but the only available FRA container row for the early period, FRA-1800-1871, does not begin until 1800. I chose to start this entry at 1800 to satisfy the containment gate's tiling requirement, which leaves 1790-1800 administratively real but unrepresented in this database. Whether that decade should instead be covered by extending FRA-1800-1871 backward, by adding a new pre-1800 FRA container row, or left as a known gap in national coverage is unresolved.

### oq-fra-grd-polygon-fetch

**GADM 4.1 adm1 has no French features locally — when will Gard's boundary be fetched?**

gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered in sources.yaml and is the right source for a French département boundary (Gard's shape has been essentially stable since 1790, with only minor commune-level adjustments over two centuries), but the local file data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg is a subset covering only 81 countries and does not include France. polygon_status is left unassigned rather than proxy or assigned. This entry cannot get a polygon until someone fetches the full GADM 4.1 adm1 layer or a France-specific admin boundary extract and adds a Gard feature to the local geopackage.
