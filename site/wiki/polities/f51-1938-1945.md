# Czechoslovakia (1938-1945)


> **`iso3` set to `CSK` on 2026-08-05 (issue 55).** This row previously carried none,
> so it belonged to no ISO family at all and could not be reached by
> `matchlib.pick_by_year`'s ISO route -- only by an explicit alias.
>
> `CSK` is the **ISO 3166-3** code for Czechoslovakia, and using it follows the rule the
> database already applies: a dissolved entity inherits its successor's code when it has
> exactly ONE successor, and takes its own 3166-3 code when it split into several.
> `ANT-1961-2010` and `SCG-1992-2006` already do the latter. Czechoslovakia became two
> states and Yugoslavia several, so no successor's code is the right one.
>
> Verified before applying: this creates **no family tie**. Every row in the family is a
> consecutive period, so no pair shares the code over overlapping years -- which matters
> because a wrong `iso3` is exactly what put a Belgium-Luxembourg row in Belgium's family
> (issue 40) and made "Malaysia" resolve to British North Borneo (issue 44).
## Summary

This row tracks Czechoslovakia for **8 years**, covering the Munich
Agreement through the end of WWII
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Munich Agreement (30 September 1938) -- Sudetenland ceded to Germany.
First Vienna Award (2 November 1938) -- southern Slovakia ceded to
Hungary. German occupation of rump Bohemia-Moravia (15 March 1939) --
Slovakia became a puppet state. CShapes shows area shrinking: ~17.3 to
~14.4 to ~12.4 sq deg. COW gap 1939-03-15 to 1945-05-10.
Czechoslovakia ceased to exist as a sovereign state 1939-1945.

Predecessor: [f51-1918-1938](f51-1918-1938.md). Successor:
[f51-1945-1947](f51-1945-1947.md).

## Contradictions

### juan-series-ignores-wartime-shrinkage

**The `juan` / Mitchell-FAO agricultural series contradicts the ~28%
territorial shrinkage this page documents.** Found 2026-07-24 by assertion
verification of `czechoslovakia|juan|1938-1944`.

Crop areas reported under the label "Czechoslovakia" show **no discontinuity**
at either the 1938 Munich/Vienna-Award boundary or the 1945 restoration —
barley (ha): 671k (1937), 660k (1938), 656k (1939) … 549k (1944), 550k (1945),
546k (1946); potatoes behave the same. That is incompatible with the CShapes
shrinkage this page records (17.3 → 14.4 → 12.4 sq deg).

The series therefore appears compiled on a **constant, pre-Munich territorial
basis straight through the occupation years**, rather than tracking the actual
shrinking wartime state — a boundary-vintage mismatch, not a scope error. No
existing polity spans 1938-1944 on that constant basis, so this row remains the
best available match and the assertion is banked `best_available`. Downstream
users of `juan` agricultural data for 1938-1944 should know it likely
**overstates this polity's true (shrunk) territory**. Recorded source-wide in
`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/source_conventions.csv`.

### iia-czechoslovakia-1938-1945-block-is-internally-corrupt

**Distinct from the `juan` contradiction above, and not the same kind of
problem.** Found 2026-08-18 while attributing cross-source disagreements for
`czech republic|iia|1938-1944`.

The `juan` case is a *boundary-vintage* mismatch: coherent numbers on the wrong
territory. The IIA case is not a territorial question at all — the raw
`czechoslovakia` 1938-1945 block simply contains **numbers attached to the wrong
`(item, variable)` slot**, so the two sides of a single year contradict *each
other*.

Rye, from the raw IIA extract, with the implied yield:

| year | area (ha) | production (t) | t/ha | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1938 | 1,016,000 | 1,906,100 | 1.88 | consistent |
| 1939 | **98,000** | 1,729,400 | 17.6 | the **area** cell is wrong |
| 1940 | **78,000** | 867,000 | 11.1 | the **area** cell is wrong |
| 1941 | **78,000** | 981,300 | 12.6 | the **area** cell is wrong |
| 1942 | 861,000 | **29,500** | 0.03 | the **production** cell is wrong |
| 1943 | **26,000** | **36,000** | 1.38 | internally consistent, both far too small |
| 1944 | 915,000 | **32,500** | 0.04 | the **production** cell is wrong |
| 1945 | 802,000 | **28,000** | 0.03 | the **production** cell is wrong |

Czechoslovak rye ran ~800k-1,050k ha at ~1.7 t/ha, so the plausible figures are
the large ones and a yield check identifies the defective cell in each year.
`juan`'s series agrees with IIA **to the digit** on 1919-1941 (1930: both
1,046,335 ha), which is what makes the divergent cells attributable rather than
merely suspicious.

**Two readings considered and refuted.**

*Not digit-shifting.* That was the first diagnosis and it does not survive: a
dropped digit would leave area and production still consistent with each other,
and here they are not.

*Not a scope flip between whole-Czechoslovakia and the Protectorate.* This was
the more attractive reading, since `iia_label_provenance.csv` does classify a
`czechoslovakia` / `czechoslovakia: bohemia, moravia and silesia` pair as
`whole_with_sub_siblings` / `sub_of_sibling`. But the raw extract's
`german bohemia and moravia` label carries **only butter, cheese and milk** —
21 rows, 1939-1945, **no rye at all** — so there is no sub-component rye series
for the label to alternate with. The alternation is corruption, not scope.

**Same block, same shape, other items**, all already logged separately: `tobacco`
1939 reads 6,000 ha with 933,000 t and `hops` 1939 reads 10,500 ha with 763,400 t
(`iia-tobacco-implausible-magnitudes`, `iia-hops-x100`), and the 1942
`flax fibre and tow` area of 4,000 is the **linseed** figure — raw carries
`flax: fibre` 43,000 and `linseed` 4,000 for that year
(`iia-flax-fibre-item-is-mostly-linseed`).

Recorded in `state/data_errors.csv` under
`iia-attributable-single-cell-errors`. **Not repairable by rescaling**: each cell
needs the correct value from the other side of the yield identity or from `juan`.

### decision-polygon-feature-year-1939

**`polygon_feature_year` moved `1938` -> `1939` on 2026-08-13 (issue 100).** All three of
the CShapes steps this page's "Area shrinkage" paragraph describes contain the year 1938,
and two of them start in it:

| step | dates | area |
|---|---|---|
| pre-Munich | 1918-11-11 – 1938-09-29 | **140,398 km²** (17.3 sq deg) |
| post-Munich, pre-Vienna | 1938-09-30 – 1938-11-01 | **116,616 km²** (14.4 sq deg) |
| post-Vienna Award | 1938-11-02 – 1945-05-07 | **100,822 km²** (12.4 sq deg) |

With two candidates starting in the queried year, `find_feature` could not decide and the
polygon was whichever the shapefile listed first — the **33-day** middle step. `1939` falls
inside the post-Vienna-Award step and inside no other, so the binding is now decided by the
data, and it is also the extent the row actually held for **6.5 of its 7 years**
(1938-11-02 onward). Measured area moved 116,616 -> 100,713 km² (-13.6%).

This makes the shrinkage the `juan` contradiction below describes slightly larger: the
series is compiled on a ~140,000 km² pre-Munich basis against a polygon now measuring
~101,000 km².
