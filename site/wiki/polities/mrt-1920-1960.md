# French Mauritania

## Summary

Colonie de Mauritanie (French Mauritania) was a French colonial territory in West Africa, formally constituted as a full colony by decree of 10 January 1920 (upgrading from protectorate/civil territory status). It formed part of the Afrique Occidentale Française (AOF) federation and was administered from Saint-Louis (Senegal) and later from Nouakchott. The colony covered the present territory of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania, bounded by Spanish Sahara to the north, French Sudan (present-day Mali) to the east and south, Senegal to the south-west, the Atlantic Ocean to the west, and Algeria to the north-east. French Mauritania was sparsely populated — predominantly nomadic — and occupied a vast desert and Sahel region. The territory acceded to independence on 28 November 1960 as the Islamic Republic of Mauritania.

This entry covers WHEP data for Mauritania reported under colonial administration, primarily 1921–1959.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 (full dependencies layer), cowcode 435, colony status, boundary period 1920-01-12 to 1944-07-04. Feature index 302 in the CShapes full GPKG. Polygon area approximately **826,366 km²** in the 1920 boundary configuration.

**Polygon method:** `exact_historical` — CShapes 2.0 provides the actual colonial boundary for Mauritania as a dependency of France (owner code 220) throughout this period.

**Boundary change note:** CShapes records two distinct colonial boundary periods for Mauritania:
- 1920-01-12 to 1944-07-04 (index 302): ~826,366 km², eastern boundary at approximately −6.35° longitude.
- 1944-07-05 to 1960-11-27 (index 303): ~1,038,483 km², eastern boundary shifted eastward to approximately −4.81° longitude (+212,117 km²).

The 1944 boundary expansion reflects a reorganisation of the Mauritania/French Sudan (Mali) border in the mid-colonial period. The 1920 boundary polygon (feature year 1920) is recorded as the canonical feature for this entry; the difference from true territory is up to +212,117 km² in the 1944–1960 sub-period where the eastern boundary had expanded. This is documented as a known intra-period drift; the polygon captures the territorial core throughout and is classified **assigned** (not proxy).

**Why this entry exists:** WHEP data for Mauritania covers colonial years 1921–1959, which fall within the lifespan of French Mauritania as a distinct administrative colony. The AOF-level entry (AOF-1895-1960) covers data reported at the federation aggregate; this entry routes data reported for Mauritania specifically.
