# Senegal (1886-1960)


> **`end_year` moved 1959 -> 1960 on 2026-08-13 (issue 77): the year 1959 belonged to no Senegalese
> polity, and 14 observed rows were already landing here anyway.**
>
> `end_year` is **exclusive**, so this row covered 1886-1958 while
> [sen-1960-2025](sen-1960-2025.md) begins in 1960. **1959 fell between them.**
>
> **The hole was NOT latent, which is where issue 77 was wrong.** It asserted "no source currently
> has data in any of the four" gaps. The consolidated layer B (`mitchell`) holds **14 Senegal
> observations for 1959** — groundnuts, millet, maize, rice, cassava, and five livestock series —
> and `data/compiled/pre1961/matched.csv` shows all 14 already routed to `SEN-1886-1959`. The
> matcher was reading `end_year` as inclusive, so the rows were being attributed to a period that
> formally did not contain their year. Nothing had broken visibly because the two candidate
> polygons are identical; the defect was silent, not absent. Extending the span makes the routing
> that already happens legitimate rather than accidental.
>
> **1959 is assigned to THIS row, and the territory says the choice is free.** Measured in
> ESRI:54034 the CShapes 2.0 Senegal steps 1886-01-01 -> 1959-04-03 and 1960-06-21 -> present are
> both **195,995.8 km²** — Senegal's boundaries did not move when it entered the Mali Federation on
> 4 April 1959, nor when it left. So the year cannot be decided on geometry. It is decided on the
> event date, as in the `CHL-1810-1884` precedent: for the first three months of 1959 Senegal was
> still the colony this row describes, and starting `SEN-1960-2025` in 1959 would credit an
> independent Senegal with a year in which it was neither independent nor separate from the
> Federation.
>
> **What is deliberately left open:** the Mali Federation itself still has no WHEP polity, so a row
> labelled *Mali Federation* (rather than *Senegal*) for 1959-1960 still has nowhere to go, and
> French Sudan's side of the same 16 months is untouched here. This fix closes the coverage hole for
> Senegal-labelled data only. See the open question below.

## Summary

Senegal (1886-1960) covers the French colonial territory of Senegal from 1 January 1886 — the CShapes 2.0 temporal baseline, coinciding with the post-Berlin-Conference colonial partition — through the end of 1959. CShapes records the colony proper ending on 3 April 1959, when Senegal entered the Mali Federation; the remainder of 1959 is carried here because Senegal's boundaries were unchanged by the Federation and no other polity claims them (see the note above). This period encompasses Senegal's role within French West Africa (Afrique Occidentale Française, AOF), which France federated from 16 June 1895. CShapes records the entire period as status=colony, owner=FRA (COW 220), with a constant area (~16.43 CShapes units), indicating no territorial boundary changes throughout this 73-year colonial span.

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory bounded by Mauritania (north), Mali (east), Guinea and Guinea-Bissau (south), the Atlantic Ocean (west), and with The Gambia as a near-enclave running along the Gambia River. CShapes 2.0 area ~16.43 (constant throughout the colonial period). Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

Note: Several predecessor entities for portions of this territory exist in the WHEP database (CAY-1800-1886, BND-1800-1887, FTT-1800-1862, WLO-1800-1855, SNE-1800-1887, JOL-1800-1890, SLM-1800-1887), reflecting pre-colonial polities absorbed by French expansion in the 1880s.

## Decisions

- **sen-1959-assigned-to-colonial-row** (2026-08-13, issue 77) — `end_year` 1959 -> 1960, so this row covers 1886-1959 and meets `SEN-1960-2025`. Evidence: (a) 14 `mitchell` observations for 1959 were already being routed here, so the year was live, not latent; (b) the two candidate CShapes polygons are identical at 195,995.8 km², so no territorial precision is spent either way; (c) the Federation was joined on 4 April 1959, so the earlier row holds the first quarter of the year and the later, independent row holds none of it. Not decided: whether the Mali Federation deserves its own aggregate row.

## Open questions

### oq-sen-mali-federation-entity

The Mali Federation (Senegal + French Sudan, April 1959 – August 1960) still has no WHEP polity. The 1959 COVERAGE hole is closed — `SEN-1886-1960` now spans it — but a source that reports the Federation as its own unit, rather than reporting Senegal and Sudan separately, has nowhere to land, and French Sudan's side of the same 16 months has not been examined. Creating the Federation would also raise the question of whether 1959-1960 Senegal data should route to it in preference to this row, which is a modelling decision beyond issue 77.
