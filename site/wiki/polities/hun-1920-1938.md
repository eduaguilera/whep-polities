# Hungary (1920-1938)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **19 years**, the interwar Horthy period
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Trianon Hungary under Horthy regency. Reduced territory (~93,000
km², down from ~325,000 pre-Trianon). CShapes: ~11 sq deg. Revisionist
foreign policy seeking to regain lost territories. First Vienna Award
(2 November 1938) returned southern Slovakia from Czechoslovakia --
this is the end-of-row event.

Predecessor: [hun-1919-1920](hun-1919-1920.md) -- Treaty of Trianon.
Successor: [hun-1938-1947](hun-1938-1947.md) -- Vienna Awards period.
