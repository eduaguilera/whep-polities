# Sweden (1809-1814)

## Summary

This row tracks Sweden without Finland for **6 years**, a transitional
period between the Finland loss and the Norway acquisition
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Sweden lost Finland
to Russia (Treaty of Fredrikshamn, 17 September 1809) and had not yet
gained the personal union with Norway (Treaty of Kiel, 14 January 1814).
The Revolution of 1809 replaced King Gustav IV Adolf; a new constitution
was adopted.

Predecessor: [swe-1800-1809](swe-1800-1809.md) -- Finland lost.
Successor: [swe-1814-1905](swe-1814-1905.md) -- Treaty of Kiel
(14 January 1814) gave Sweden a personal union with Norway.
