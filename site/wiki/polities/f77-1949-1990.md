# East Germany

## Summary

This row tracks the German Democratic Republic (Deutsche Demokratische
Republik, GDR) for **42 years**, from its proclamation on 7 October
1949 to German reunification on 3 October 1990
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The GDR was established in the Soviet occupation zone of defeated Nazi
Germany. Biger notes that the occupation zones "matured into the
Federal Republic of Germany (FRG) and German Democratic Republic (GDR),
respectively"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany). After the war,
"10,000,000 ethnic Germans were expelled from Central and Eastern
Europe"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

The Berlin Wall was erected on 13 August 1961 and fell on
9 November 1989. The GDR was absorbed into the FRG on
3 October 1990.

Predecessor: NA per CSV -- same occupation-gap issue as West Germany.
The GDR descended from the Soviet occupation zone of defeated Nazi
Germany ([deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md)).

Successor: [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- absorbed into the FRG
at reunification.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA per CSV. The GDR was established from the Soviet
  occupation zone of defeated Nazi Germany
  ([deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md)). The 1945-1949 occupation
  period had no sovereign German state -- this is a chain gap.
- **Successor:** [deu-1990-2025](deu-1990-2025.md) -- German
  reunification 3 October 1990. The GDR acceded to the Federal
  Republic under Article 23 of the Basic Law.

## Open questions

### oq-cow-start-1954

COW starts the GDR at 1954-03-25 but WHEP's row starts at 1949. The
5-year gap is because COW treated the GDR as not fully sovereign until
1954, when the USSR formally granted sovereignty to the GDR (25 March
1954). The GDR was proclaimed on 7 October 1949 but remained under
heavy Soviet control. The WHEP row's 1949 start date is defensible
(the GDR existed as a state from 1949) but differs from COW's
sovereignty criterion.

### oq-predecessor-occupation-gap

The 1945-1949 Allied occupation period is not captured as a WHEP row.
[deu-1938-1945](deu-1938-1945.md) has `successor=NA` and this row has
`predecessor=NA`. The ~4 years of four-power occupation (with no
sovereign German state) are a genuine gap in the chain. See also the
same open question on [f78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md).
