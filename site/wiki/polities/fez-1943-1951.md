# French Military Administration of Fezzan

## Summary

The southwestern desert third of Libya, under **French** military administration from 1943 to
1951 — Tripolitania and Cyrenaica were British. Fezzan was governed from Sabha as an extension
of French Algeria's Saharan commands, joined the federal United Kingdom of Libya on 24 December
1951 and remained one of its three provinces until the 1963 reorganisation.

## Why this entry exists

**It was the missing third of a reporting unit this database could only represent as a whole.**
Between 1943 and 1951 Libya was three separately administered territories; `TRP-1943-1951` and
`CYR-1949-1951` existed and Fezzan had **no row at all**, so `LBY-1943-1949` and `LBY-1949-1951`
carried the all-Libya polygon while none of the parts were representable (issue 156).

FAO's 1952 yearbook reports **`Libya Fezzan`** as a distinct unit, with its own stated area:

```
Libya Cyrenaica     1948    855,400 km²
Libya Fezzan        1948    551,100 km²
Libya Tripolitania  1951    353,000 km²
Libya               1951  1,759,500 km²   = exactly the sum of the three
```

Those five `Libya Fezzan` rows previously resolved to nothing. Routing them to `LBY` instead
would have been worse than dropping them: Fezzan is 29% of Libya by FAO's own numbers, so a
Fezzan figure booked to the national row overstates the territory it describes by 3.5x, and
double-counts against the already-matched Tripolitania and Cyrenaica series.

The prior `TRP-1943-1951` page anticipated this row and guessed the code `FZN-*`
([oq-trp-fezzan](trp-1943-1951.md)). It is **`FEZ`** here, for consistency with `TRP` and `CYR`;
nothing referenced `FZN`, so no alias or data row is affected by the choice.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** constructed — a union of **5** GADM 4.1 adm1 *shabiyat*: Murzuq, Sabha, Wadi al
Hayat, Wadi ash Shati' and Ghat. **463,596 km²** against FAO's **551,100 km²** (**−15.9%**), the
largest of the three deviations.

> **The three territories are a partition, and that is the deliverable.**
>
> ```
> TRP-1943-1951   314,598 km²
> CYR-1949-1951   837,876
> FEZ-1943-1951   463,596
> union         1,616,070 km²
> GADM Libya    1,616,070 km²   ratio 1.000000, both differences 0.0000 km²
> pairwise intersections            0.0000 km² (all three)
> ```
>
> Per-territory data therefore sums to the national total, which is what makes these three usable
> alongside `LBY` rather than in competition with it. `audit_family_shadowing`'s
> `PINNED_DISJOINT` re-measures the three pairs on every run.

### No source in this repo draws these territories

Checked rather than assumed: **CShapes has only gwcode 620 (Libya entire)** for every step
1912-2019, and Cliopatria's nearest features are `Ottoman Tripolitania` (1840-1911) and an
**ancient** `Cyrenaica` (−331 to −92). Hence modern *shabiyat*.

### Why Fezzan's deviation is the largest, and why FAO's 551,100 is the wrong denominator

The earlier reading was that GADM's Libya is 8.2% short of the accepted 1,759,540 km² and that
Fezzan is where the shortfall concentrates. **Issue 196 measured that and it is wrong in both
halves.** GADM is not short: three independent boundary products agree on Libya to 0.5% —

```
GADM 4.1        union of 22 adm1 shabiyat, WGS84 geodesic   1,616,058 km²
CShapes 2.0     gwcode 620, every step 1934-2019            1,617,537
Natural Earth 10m  ADM0_A3 = LBY                            1,623,761
accepted / FAO 1952                                         1,759,540 / 1,759,500
```

**1,759,540 is the outlier, and it is an Italian 1930s figure for a larger Libya.** It is exactly
the `LIBYE` row of the **IIA 1938 edition** (data year 1937) in this repo's own
`data/final/source_stated_areas.csv`. The **1933 edition** states Tripolitania 900,000 + Cyrenaica
738,000 = **1,638,000** for the same country, within **+1.4%** of all three modern products
(+0.9% on Natural Earth, +1.3% on CShapes, +1.4% on GADM); the 1925 and 1929 editions give
900,000 + 600,000 = 1,500,000, and the 1932 edition carries only the Tripolitania row. Between
1933 and 1938 the **January 1935 Laval-Mussolini treaty** moved Libya's southern boundary onto the
Aouzou line — **114,000 km²**, 79% of the 143,482 km² gap and close to the **+121,540** the IIA
figure itself jumps — a transfer never ratified, conventionally shown as Libyan until 1955, and
awarded to Chad by the ICJ in 1994. All three modern products draw the 1899/1919 boundary instead,
verified here from Chad's own boundary vertices: **15.9851°E / 23.4447°N** (the Tropic of Cancer at
16°E) and **19.4961°N at 23.9813°E** (19°30'N at 24°E), the two named points of that line. CShapes
agrees by construction and not only by area: gwcode 620 steps in July **1934** (1,524,956 →
1,617,537, the Sarra Triangle transferred from Sudan) and has **no 1935 step at all**.

So the 143,482 km² is **territory lying outside the polygon, not area missing from inside it**, and
the residual after the strip (**1.8%**) is the same order as official-versus-GIS excesses measured
by the same method elsewhere — Chad **+1.4%** (accepted 1,284,000 vs 1,266,282 Natural Earth) and
Indonesia **+0.8%** (accepted 1,904,569 vs 1,890,243 GADM). The pattern is not Libya-specific
either: Niger's accepted 1,267,000 km² sits **7.2%** above GIS (1,181,301 Natural Earth, 1,182,032
CShapes) with no cause identified here. The IIA's own Niger figures do **not** corroborate that one
— 1,200,000 (1925), 1,320,000 (1929), 1,250,850 (1933), 1,293,810 (1938) — and an earlier draft of
this page cited an "IIA 1932 Niger 1,179,500" that does not exist in `source_stated_areas.csv`; it
has been removed.

The consequence for this page is that **FAO's Fezzan 551,100 is not a clean denominator**: FAO's
three territorial figures sum to exactly its 1,759,500, so the 1930s over-count is inside them
too, and it sits on the Chad frontage — which in this partition is Murzuq (Fezzan) and Al Kufrah
(Cyrenaica), the only two *shabiyat* touching that boundary. Measured along the shared Libya-Chad
line, it is **1,051.5 km**, split **470.2 km (44.7%) Murzuq** / **581.3 km (55.3%) Al Kufrah**.
Against a strip-corrected 1,645,500 (551,100 − 50,977 for Fezzan) the deviation here is
**−7.3%, not −15.9%**.

**The deviation is still stated rather than corrected:** scaling a polygon to hit a stated area
would make the check that compares the two circular, which is the failure mode
`IDN-OTH-1949-1951` and `IND-1800-1886` both demonstrated, where a declared figure agreed with its
own geometry while both were wrong. Fezzan remains the emptiest quarter of the country — five
*shabiyat* over 463,596 km², an average of 92,700 km² each with almost no settlement to pin a
boundary to — so coarse desert delineation still costs proportionally most here; that is now a
secondary effect rather than the explanation.

`polygon_status: proxy` for the same reason — modern *shabiyat* standing in for historical
territories whose boundaries are known to differ. Check A reports `proxy` rows as documented
rather than failing them, so −15.9% stays visible in every run.

### The shabiya assignment is a judgement, and only two units are in doubt

The disagreement between historical and modern boundaries sits in **Surt** (78,659 km², the Sirte
basin) and **Al Jufrah** (111,245 km², the Jufra oases). Fezzan's own five units are unambiguous
by geography. All four placements were measured against FAO's areas:

```
placement                        TRP        CYR        FEZ      worst
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->TRP      -10.9%      -2.0%     -15.9%     -15.9%   <- chosen
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->FEZ      -42.4%      -2.0%      +4.3%     -42.4%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->FEZ      -20.1%     -11.2%      +4.3%     -20.1%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->TRP      +11.4%     -11.2%     -15.9%     -15.9%
```

**Al Jufrah is not in Fezzan here**, and the stated reason was the consistency test rather than
the average error: if GADM's whole is 8.2% under FAO, every part should be under too, and the two
placements that put Jufra in Fezzan push Fezzan **above** FAO while the whole is below.

**That test has been withdrawn (issue 196).** The whole is not 8.2% under a correct figure; FAO's
1,759,500 is 8.1% over Libya's actual extent. Against a strip-corrected baseline — 353,000 /
792,377 / 500,123, summing to 1,645,500 — the four placements read:

```
placement                        TRP        CYR        FEZ      worst
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->TRP      -10.9%      +5.7%      -7.3%     -10.9%   <- still chosen
Surt->CYR, Jufrah->FEZ      -42.4%      +5.7%     +14.9%     -42.4%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->FEZ      -20.1%      -4.2%     +14.9%     -20.1%
Surt->TRP, Jufrah->TRP      +11.4%      -4.2%      -7.3%     +11.4%
```

**No placement now puts every part under FAO**, and the chosen one leads the fourth by 10.9% to
11.4% worst error instead of being uniquely consistent. The assignment is left **unchanged** here:
issue 196 answered the area question, and the strip's 55.3/44.7 split between Cyrenaica and Fezzan is
an estimate from Chad frontage rather than a measurement of the strip, so it cannot decide a
placement on its own.

**What remains a guess:** whether the 1943-1951 administrators drew the Jufra oases into Fezzan
or Tripolitania. Under Italian rule the Jufra oases sat in the Territory of the Libyan Sahara
alongside Fezzan, which argues for placing them here; the area evidence argues the other way, and
now less strongly than before.

## Predecessors and successors

`predecessor: [LBY-1934-1943]` — Italian Libya, from which the French took Fezzan in 1943.
`successor: [LBY-1951-2025]` — the United Kingdom of Libya, which Fezzan joined as a federal
province on 24 December 1951. The same chain as `TRP-1943-1951`, deliberately: the three
territories entered and left occupation together.

## Contradictions

None identified. Fezzan is geographically disjoint from Tripolitania and Cyrenaica (measured at
0.0000 km² against both), and the union of the three reproduces GADM's Libya exactly.

## Open questions

### oq-fez-1951-1963-provincial-period

Fezzan remained a **province** of the federal Kingdom of Libya from 1951 to 1963, so a source
reporting "Fezzan" for those years has no destination: this row ends exclusive at 1951 and
`LBY-1951-2025` carries the whole. Extending the row to 1963 is defensible history, but it is a
span change and therefore a code rename, and the same question applies identically to
`TRP-1943-1951` and Cyrenaica. All three should be decided together (issue 156 records this).

### oq-fez-shabiya-placement-consistency-withdrawn

The Surt/Al Jufrah placement was chosen because it was the only one of four in which **every part**
came out under FAO, matching a whole that appeared to be 8.2% under FAO. Issue 196 showed the whole
is not under a correct figure at all, and against a strip-corrected baseline no placement puts
every part under (table above); the chosen placement now leads the fourth only 10.9% to 11.4% on
worst error, and the fourth — Surt in Tripolitania — is the one the documentary evidence favours,
since the 1934 Italian provinces put Sirte in Misurata. **The assignment is unchanged and the
argument for it is now weak.** Deciding it needs either an administrative source for the 1943-1951
boundaries or a measured Aouzou-strip polygon to replace the 55.3/44.7 frontage estimate; the same
question governs `TRP-1943-1951` and `CYR-1949-1951` and should be settled for all three at once
(issues 192 and 156).
