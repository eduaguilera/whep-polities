# Ardennes (département)

## Summary

Ardennes is a French département in the Grand Est region, occupying the northeastern tip of France on the Belgian border, corresponding roughly to the historic northern portion of the province of Champagne plus a strip of the former Ardennes forest region. It is one of the roughly 83 original départements created by the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform that replaced the old provinces with a uniform grid of units, and it is not one of the four recognized departures from that default history that this pipeline tracks for French subnational units (it was not carved from Alsace-Lorraine, not split off like the Territoire de Belfort, not part of the 1975 Corse split, and not a post-1946 overseas département), so it has kept its present administrative form continuously since creation. This entry is `type: subnational` rather than `national` because Ardennes is an ordinary administrative subdivision of the sovereign state of France, not a state in its own right and not a historical territory with an identity distinct from the modern département -- the same reasoning already applied to the sibling département entries created in this same pipeline run (Aisne, Calvados, Cher, Côte-d'Or, Doubs, and others). It exists as a row in this table only because a statistical source (mitchell) reports production/agricultural data for Ardennes specifically, at a finer grain than any national FRA row carries; before this entry, that data had no département-level home and would have had to be routed to whichever national FRA row spans the relevant year -- a roughly ninety-fivefold territorial overstatement relative to Ardennes's actual extent, since metropolitan France has around 95-96 départements.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: mitchell reports a production/agricultural series labelled specifically for Ardennes, beginning in 1862 per the routing decision, entirely inside this entry's 1800-2025 span; the exact row count was not independently re-verified in this authoring pass, and is flagged as such in sourced_claims. Previously, without a département-level Ardennes row, that data would have had to be routed to whichever national France row spans the relevant year (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919 or FRA-1919-2025), which would silently blend a single département's figures into a national aggregate roughly 95 times larger by département count, overstating the geographic scope the underlying series actually describes -- the same pattern already documented for the sibling entries FRA-02 (Aisne), FRA-14 (Calvados) and others in this run. What confirms Ardennes is a distinct, real, historically continuous entity rather than a source-invented label: it is one of the roughly 83 original départements created by the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary reform, it has carried its own INSEE administrative code (08) and préfecture (Charleville-Mézières) ever since, and GADM 4.1's global admin-1 layer digitises it as a discrete first-order administrative feature of France, even though the copy of that dataset present in this repository (an 81-country subset) happens to exclude France.

**Territory description:** Ardennes is a département in the far northeast of France, in the Grand Est region, bordering Belgium (Wallonia) to the north and the départements of Aisne, Marne and Meuse to the south and east. Its administrative seat (préfecture) is Charleville-Mézières; other notable towns include Sedan (site of the decisive 1870 and 1940 battles) and Rethel. On a modern map it corresponds to INSEE département code 08, traversed by the Meuse river and covering the southern edge of the wooded Ardennes massif that gives it its name. Its modern area is approximately **5,229 km²** (a figure quoted from published French administrative statistics for the present-day département, not measured from any polygon attached to this entry, since none is attached -- consistent with the general pattern that 1790-era départements were drawn to a broadly similar size, crossable on horseback in a day from any point to the seat).

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: mitchell reports a production/agricultural series labelled specifically for Ardennes, per the routing decision, beginning in 1862 and falling entirely inside this entry's 1800-2025 span (row count not independently verified in this authoring pass -- flagged in sourced_claims). Previously, in the absence of any Ardennes-level polity, this data had no natural home except one of the national France rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, or FRA-1919-2025 depending on year), which would have meant attributing département-scale series to the whole of metropolitan France. What confirms Ardennes is a distinct, real administrative entity rather than an artifact of the source's labelling: it is one of the original 1790 départements, it carries its own INSEE code (08) and préfecture (Charleville-Mézières), and GADM 4.1's global admin-1 layer digitises it as a discrete feature -- this is not a source inventing a unit with no corresponding jurisdiction.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the département's 1790 creation date, per the container floor**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, citing the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary reform that created Ardennes (from parts of the former province of Champagne) along with the roughly 83 original départements. That administrative date is correct, but it is not usable here: the FRA container chain in the polity table begins at FRA-1800-1871 (cshapes-europe, first vintage 1816), and there is no France row covering 1790-1800 for this entry to container against. The containment gate rejects any edge that falls outside either party's span, so a 1790 start with no container for the first decade would fail immediately -- this is the container-floor rule the harness now enforces (commit 11e8ca1). Rather than leave a gap or invent a France row this pipeline wasn't asked to create, this entry starts at 1800, matching the earliest available container and the identical decision already made for the sibling département entries (FRA-02, FRA-14, FRA-18, FRA-21, FRA-25, FRA-27, FRA-28 and others created in this same run). The 1790-1800 gap is left open below.

### d-subunit-code-is-insee-number

**Subunit code '08' follows INSEE numbering, matching the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT pattern and the sibling département entries**

French départements are conventionally identified by their INSEE two-digit code rather than a postal or historical abbreviation. Ardennes's INSEE code is 08, giving the polity code FRA-08-1800-2025. This keeps the code in the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> family (57 of 66 subnational rows, and the established convention for every French département entry created in this pipeline run) rather than treating Ardennes as a bespoke historical territory. Ardennes has no name-of-its-own distinct from the modern département and is not one of the four listed departures from the default département history tracked by this pipeline (not the Territoire de Belfort, not an Alsace-Lorraine département, not the 1975 Corse split, not a post-1946 overseas département), so bespoke-code treatment (like ALK or HYD) would be unwarranted despite Ardennes's distinctive military history.

## Open questions

### oq-1790-1800-gap-uncontained

**1790-1800 creation decade has no France container row to attach to**

Ardennes existed as a département from March 1790, a full decade before this entry's start_year of 1800. The FRA container chain in the polity table begins at FRA-1800-1871, so there is currently no polity row representing France 1790-1800 for Ardennes (or any other French département entered so far in this pipeline run) to container against. If a pre-1800 France row is ever added to the table, this entry's start_year should be reconsidered and moved back to 1790, consistent with the département's actual creation date and the routing decision's original span_basis reasoning.

### oq-no-polygon-source-registered

**No registered, locally-present source can supply an Ardennes boundary today**

The routing decision's polygon_reasoning found that gadm-4.1-adm1, the only registered source with département-level granularity for France, is present locally only as an 81-country subset that excludes France entirely (0 rows for FRA). cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe, the sources used for the national FRA rows, only carry whole-country geometry, not sub-national units. No union/difference of currently registered, locally-present features can construct Ardennes's boundary. A real boundary certainly exists (GADM 4.1's full/global adm1 layer, IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS, or IGN GEOFLA for France specifically) but is not registered in scripts/sources.yaml today, so polygon_source is 'none' and polygon_status is 'unassigned' rather than a proxy. This should be revisited once gadm-4.1-adm1 is re-sourced as its full global layer, or an IGN product is registered.

### oq-wwi-invasion-route-data-quality-unaddressed

**Ardennes's repeated role as a WWI/WWII invasion corridor is not reflected in the territorial/data record**

Ardennes sits directly on France's northeastern invasion route and was occupied by German forces for nearly the entire First World War (from the Battle of the Ardennes in August 1914 until the Armistice in November 1918), and again during the Second World War including the 1940 and 1944-45 Battle of the Bulge fighting. This entry's container basis treats 1871-1919 and 1919-2025 as continuous, unaffected metropolitan French territory in the strict sovereignty/administrative sense, which is correct (France never formally ceded Ardennes), but any agricultural or production series (mitchell reports data from 1862, per the routing decision) covering 1914-1918 or 1940-1944 may reflect wartime occupation, evacuation, or non-reporting rather than ordinary peacetime figures. Whether the underlying series this entry receives actually span those years, and whether a data-quality flag is warranted, has not been checked in this authoring pass.
