# Czech lands (Bohemia, Moravia and Silesia within Czechoslovakia, 1945-1992)

## Summary

The Czech lands inside restored Czechoslovakia, from the liberation in May 1945 (the handover year belongs to the incoming row) to the 1993 dissolution, when the same territory became the Czech Republic (CZE-1993-2025). [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §bohemia-moravia](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#bohemia-moravia)

[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
