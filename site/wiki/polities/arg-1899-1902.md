# Argentina (1899-1902)

## Summary

Short transitional row tracking Argentina from **1899** to **1902** —
after the Puna de Atacama arbitral award (1899) and before the General
Arbitration Treaty / British arbitral award settling remaining
Argentina–Chile Andean disputes (1902). COW: ARG, ccode=160.

This row is the symmetric counterpart of CHL-1899-1902 (cowcode=155,
vintage-1899). Zero polygon vintage drift.

Split from the former ARG-1800-2025 collapsed row on 2026-07-01 to
restore the autonomous-34-south-america three-period design.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `gwcode=160`, vintage **1899**.
  Zero vintage drift — polygon year exactly matches row start year.
- **Opening event.** Puna de Atacama arbitration (1898–1899): the
  disputed high-altitude territory was divided between Chile and
  Argentina, adjusting the northern Andean boundary.
- **Closing event.** 1902 General Arbitration Treaty and the
  simultaneous British arbitral award resolving competing
  Argentina–Chile claims in southern Andes and Patagonian
  archipelago. This finalised the core Andean boundary that has
  remained stable since.
- **COW.** ARG, ccode=160
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).
