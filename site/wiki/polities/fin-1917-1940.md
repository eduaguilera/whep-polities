# Finland (1917-1940)

## Summary

This row tracks Finland for **24 years**, from independence to the
Winter War territorial losses
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Finland declared independence from Russia on 6 December 1917. Previously
part of the Russian Empire as the Grand Duchy of Finland since 1809
(cross-ref the F228 chain and [swe-1800-1809](swe-1800-1809.md) which
lost Finland to Russia). Finnish Civil War 1918. Treaty of Tartu (1920)
confirmed borders with Soviet Russia. The Winter War (November 1939 --
March 1940) ended with the Moscow Peace Treaty (12 March 1940), in which
Finland lost Karelia and other territories to the USSR (~10% of its
area). The 1940 end date aligns with this treaty.

Predecessor: NA per CSV. Successor: [fin-1940-2025](fin-1940-2025.md).

## Open questions

### oq-russian-predecessor

Finland was part of the F228 chain (Russian Empire) as the Grand Duchy
of Finland from 1809. The CSV lists `predecessor = NA`, which is
expected for a newly independent state but means the predecessor graph
cannot trace back through Russia to Sweden
([swe-1800-1809](swe-1800-1809.md)).

### oq-winter-war-losses

The Winter War resulted in significant territorial loss (~10% of
Finland's area, including Karelia, Salla, and parts of the Rybachi
Peninsula). The 1940 row break aligns with the Moscow Peace Treaty.
