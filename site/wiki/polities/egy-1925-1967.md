# Egypt (1925-1967)

## Summary

A **43-year row** covering Egypt from the Kingdom period through the early
Republic [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 1967
split is the Six-Day War -- Israel occupied the Sinai Peninsula.

Major events: Kingdom of Egypt under Fuad I and Farouk, 1952 Free Officers
revolution (Nasser), Republic declared 1953, Suez Crisis 1956, United
Arab Republic with Syria (1958-1961), Six-Day War (June 1967).

Predecessor: [egy-1899-1925](egy-1899-1925.md). Successor:
[egy-1967-1979](egy-1967-1979.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 per the CSV. Egypt proper, ~1M km2.
- The UAR (1958-1961) was a political union with Syria, not a territorial
  merger in the WHEP sense -- the two territories were not contiguous.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [egy-1899-1925](egy-1899-1925.md) -- Sudan separated
  (1925), area reduced from ~137 to ~90 sq deg.
- **Successor:** [egy-1967-1979](egy-1967-1979.md) -- Six-Day War
  (June 1967), Israel occupied the Sinai Peninsula.

## Sourced claims

- **1936: Anglo-Egyptian Treaty.** Granted Egypt greater sovereignty;
  British troops confined to the Suez Canal Zone.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1952-07-23: Free Officers revolution.** King Farouk overthrown by
  Nasser and the Free Officers.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1953: Republic declared.** Muhammad Naguib first president, then
  Nasser from 1954.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1956: Suez Crisis.** Nasser nationalized the Suez Canal; Anglo-
  French-Israeli invasion failed.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1958-1961: United Arab Republic.** Political union with Syria,
  dissolved 1961.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1967-06-05: Six-Day War begins.** Israel occupied the Sinai
  Peninsula, Gaza Strip, West Bank, and Golan Heights. End-of-row
  event [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
