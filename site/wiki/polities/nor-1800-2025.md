# Norway

## Summary

This row tracks Norway as a territorial-economic unit from **1800**
(the WHEP database floor) to **2025**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is a
**226-year unbroken row** despite Norway undergoing two major
sovereignty transitions within the period: Denmark-Norway dissolved in
1814 (Treaty of Kiel), after which Norway entered a personal union with
Sweden (1814-1905), and Norway gained full independence in 1905.

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
splits trigger on substantial territorial change to the polity itself.
Neither the 1814 nor the 1905 transition involved significant changes
to Norway's metropolitan borders -- the same territory was transferred
between sovereigns and then became independent. This justifies a single
continuous row.

- **1800 start** is the database floor. At 1800 Norway was part of
  Denmark-Norway.
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; Norway is still active.

Predecessor: NA (database floor). At 1800 Norway was part of
Denmark-Norway.
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Sourced claims

- **1814-01-14: Treaty of Kiel.** Denmark ceded Norway to Sweden.
  This ended the Denmark-Norway union that had existed since 1380.
  Norway's territory itself did not change -- it was transferred
  from Danish to Swedish sovereignty.
  [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)
- **1814-05-17: Norwegian constitution.** Norway adopted its
  constitution at Eidsvoll on 17 May 1814 (still celebrated as
  Norway's national day). Norway briefly declared independence before
  being compelled into a personal union with Sweden by the Convention
  of Moss (August 1814).
  [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)
- **1814-1905: Sweden-Norway personal union.** Norway was in personal
  union with Sweden, sharing a monarch but maintaining its own
  constitution, parliament (Storting), and domestic governance.
  [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)
- **1905-06-07: Dissolution of the union.** Norway declared the union
  with Sweden dissolved on 7 June 1905. Sweden accepted the
  dissolution after a Norwegian referendum (August 1905) showed
  overwhelming support for independence. No territorial changes
  accompanied the dissolution.
  [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists Norway (`NOR`,
  `ccode=385`). The CSV records `cow_code=385`. COW may start Norway
  at 1905 (independence from Sweden) rather than 1816, since Norway
  was not a fully sovereign state before 1905. See
  [oq-1905-independence](#oq-1905-independence).
  [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)
- **Unified monarchy ninth century; Viking expeditions** (p.408). Union of Kalmar 1397 with Sweden and Denmark (p.408). Christianity adopted 1030 (p.408). Kingdom proclaimed 1905 after separation from Sweden (p.409). Occupied by Nazi Germany 1940 (p.409). [biger-1995 §norway](../sources/biger-1995.md#norway)

## Open questions

### oq-1814-kiel-status

Norway went from Danish to Swedish rule in 1814 via the Treaty of
Kiel. Under the WHEP rule, was there a territorial change? The row
does not split, implying no. The justification is that Norway's
metropolitan borders remained the same -- only sovereignty changed.
This is consistent with the WHEP polity-definition rule, but worth
documenting as a deliberate design choice.

### oq-1905-independence

Norway gained full sovereignty in 1905 when the personal union with
Sweden was dissolved. This is not a WHEP split because metropolitan
borders did not change. COW may start Norway at 1905 rather than
1816 -- if so, the WHEP row extends further back than COW coverage.
The CSV has `cow_code=385`, so the COW entry exists; the exact COW
start date needs verification.
