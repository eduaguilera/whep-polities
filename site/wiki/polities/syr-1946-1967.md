# Syrian Arab Republic (1946-1967)

## Summary

Syria (1946-1967) covers the period from Syrian independence (17 April 1946, when French forces evacuated) until the June 1967 Six-Day War, when Israel captured the Golan Heights. CShapes records a polygon change on 1967-06-10, reducing Syria's area from approximately 185,701 km² to approximately 184,629 km² — reflecting the Israeli seizure and de facto annexation of the Golan Heights. The 1958–1961 period includes the United Arab Republic (union with Egypt), but CShapes shows no area change during this interval, and the WHEP CSV treats it as a single continuous row. COW codes Syria as ccode=652 and records a gap in state-system membership during the UAR period (1958-02-01 to 1961-09-28), which CShapes also reflects by omitting Syria from the 1958–1961 window.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records Syria (cowcode=652) with area approximately 185,701 km² in the 1946–1967 period. The post-1967 step shows approximately 184,629 km², a reduction of approximately 1,072 km² — the Golan Heights area seized by Israel during the Six-Day War. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

Note: CShapes records a gap in Syria's entries from 1958-02-01 to 1961-09-28 during the United Arab Republic period. The UAR may require a separate WHEP row or an explanation of how this gap is handled.

## Contradictions

The WHEP CSV covers the 1958–1961 United Arab Republic (UAR) period within the SYR-1946-1967 row, treating it as a single continuous entity. CShapes, however, shows a gap in Syria's entries from 1958-02-01 to 1961-09-28 — during which the UAR existed as a separate political entity. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, if the territory did not change, this is correctly one row. CShapes confirms the area was identical before and after the UAR period (~185,701 km²), supporting the one-row treatment. The COW gap (1958–1961) reflects COW's state-system definition, not a territorial change, and should not drive a WHEP split. This is recorded here as a definitional difference, not a contradiction requiring resolution.

## Open questions

### oq-uar-period

During the United Arab Republic (1958-02-01 to 1961-09-28), Syria was nominally merged with Egypt. CShapes shows no polygon for Syria during this period. Whether WHEP should add a UAR row (for the combined Egypt+Syria entity) or leave this handled by the continuous SYR row is an architectural question. The Egyptian WHEP rows for this period should be checked for overlap.

### oq-golan-heights-area

CShapes records a ~1,072 km² area reduction in 1967, but the commonly cited Golan Heights area is approximately 1,800 km² (total Syrian Golan) vs. approximately 1,200 km² under Israeli control after the ceasefire. The CShapes figure may reflect the de facto ceasefire line rather than the full pre-war Syrian Golan. A future ingest should check ISRAEL-SYRIA boundary section in Biger for the exact area.
