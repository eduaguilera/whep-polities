# Colonial Nigeria (1886-1914)

## Summary

Colonial Nigeria covers the period from the chartering of the Royal Niger Company in 1886 through the amalgamation of the Northern and Southern Nigeria Protectorates on 1 January 1914. The Royal Niger Company administered the Niger and Benue river regions until its charter was revoked on 1 January 1900, when the British government assumed direct control and created two separate protectorates.
