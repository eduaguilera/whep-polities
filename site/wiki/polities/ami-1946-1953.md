# Amami Islands (US-administered)

## Summary

This row tracks the **Amami Islands** (Amami Oshima group, part of the
northern Ryukyu chain) during the period of **US military and civil
administration**, from Japan's 1945 surrender through to the
**formal reversion to Japan on 1953-12-25**.

After Japan's August 1945 surrender the Amami Islands were placed under
US military government together with the rest of the Ryukyu Islands.
The San Francisco Peace Treaty (in force 1952-04-28, Article 3)
designated the Ryukyus — including the Amami group — as US-administered
territory pending a possible UN trusteeship. Unlike the rest of the
Ryukyu chain, the Amami Islands were returned to Japan separately by the
Amami Islands Reversion Agreement, which took effect on **1953-12-25**.
From that date onward, the Amami Islands became part of Kagoshima
Prefecture in Japan and are covered by [jpn-1952-2025](jpn-1952-2025.md).

**Why this entry exists.** The IIA/FAO 1960 yearbook footnote reports
Japan's agricultural data including the Amami Islands as a **boundary
clarification** — indicating that pre-1953 data for this territory had
been reported separately from Japan (as part of the US-administered
Ryukyu Islands) and that the 1960 figure reflects the post-reversion
inclusion. The FAO/IIA footnote for Japan in the 1960 series therefore
implicitly requires a distinct WHEP entity to handle data that was
attributed to the US-administered area during 1946–1953. This polity
covers that gap.

Note that the Amami Islands were part of the broader US-administered
Ryukyu area initially captured under [ryu-1945-1972](ryu-1945-1972.md).
That row, however, uses the GADM Okinawa Prefecture proxy polygon, which
**excludes** the Amami Islands (which are in Kagoshima Prefecture today).
A separate row for the Amami Islands' US-administration period is
therefore warranted to represent the distinct administrative and data
geography. AMI-1946-1953 ends at the 1953 reversion; the successor row
[jpn-1952-2025](jpn-1952-2025.md) then carries the territory from
1953-12-25 onward as part of sovereign Japan.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: unassigned.** No faithful-vintage polygon for the
  Amami Islands under US administration was found in any queried source:
  - **CShapes 2.0** (`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`):
    searched `cntry_name` for "amami", "ryukyu", "okinawa"; no Amami-
    specific feature found. CShapes 2.0 covers the Ryukyu Islands as a
    single undifferentiated entity; the Amami sub-group is not split out.
  - **Cliopatria** (`data/geodata/cliopatria/cliopatria_polities_only.geojson`):
    no entry for the Amami Islands under any name variant.
  - **Paine-2024** (`data/geodata/paine-2024/PCS.shp`): no entry for
    the Amami Islands.
  - **GADM 4.1 adm1** (`data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg`): the
    subset loaded covers small islands and territories only; Japan (JPN)
    is not present in this slice. Even if present, the modern Kagoshima
    Prefecture polygon would include the Amami Islands alongside the
    mainland Kyushu portion of Kagoshima, making it an over-large and
    inappropriate proxy.
- **Honest gap documented.** The Amami Islands form a small island group
  (Amami Oshima and surrounding islands) within the northern Ryukyu
  chain. Published land area is approximately 1,231 km² (Amami Oshima
  alone ~712 km²; the broader Amami group including Tokunoshima,
  Okinoerabujima, and Yoronjima ~1,231 km²). This figure is cited for
  orientation only and cannot be verified against a polygon query.
- **Confidence: low.** The absence of a polygon source means spatial
  attribution cannot be confirmed. The polygon should be constructed or
  extracted from a Ryukyu sub-group source if one becomes available.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [ryu-1937-1945](ryu-1937-1945.md) — the Amami Islands
  were part of Okinawa Prefecture (and earlier the Ryukyu domain) under
  Japanese administration. The US military government established
  control after the Battle of Okinawa (June 1945) and Japan's surrender
  (August 1945); the Amami group was administered together with the
  rest of the Ryukyus from 1945 onward under USMGRI/USCAR.
- **Successor:** [jpn-1952-2025](jpn-1952-2025.md) — the Amami Islands
  were returned to Japan on 1953-12-25 under the Amami Islands Reversion
  Agreement, becoming Kagoshima Prefecture territory. From that date
  all Amami data routes to the Japan polity.

## Decisions

- **Separate row warranted.** Although the Amami Islands were
  administered as part of the broader US-administered Ryukyu territory
  (1945–1953), the existing [ryu-1945-1972](ryu-1945-1972.md) polygon
  already explicitly excludes the Amami Islands. A distinct row is
  necessary to route any production or agricultural data attributed
  to the Amami area during 1946–1953 that would otherwise fall into a
  coverage gap between RYU-1945-1972 (which uses the Okinawa proxy)
  and JPN-1945-1952 / JPN-1952-2025 (which cover the home islands
  without the Ryukyus).
- **Start year 1946, not 1945.** US military government was established
  in mid-1945, but formal administrative structures and data collection
  began in 1946. The start year 1946 aligns with the first full year of
  US administration and with the IIA footnote context.
- **End year 1953.** The Amami Islands Reversion Agreement took effect
  1953-12-25. The polity ends at that date; the day-of-year precision
  is not stored in WHEP (integer year), so end_year = 1953 is used.
- **iso3: NA.** During US administration the Amami Islands had no ISO
  3166-1 assignment; neither JPN nor USA is appropriate for the
  administered period. iso3 is left NA.
- **cow: NA.** The Correlates of War project does not assign a separate
  state-system code to the US-administered Amami Islands.
- **Predecessor set to RYU-1937-1945.** The Amami Islands were part of
  Okinawa Prefecture (Japanese administration) before 1945; RYU-1937-1945
  covers this period. The predecessor is the broader Ryukyu entry rather
  than a Japan-home-islands entry because the Amami group was
  geographically and administratively part of the Ryukyu chain.
- **polygon_status: unassigned.** No faithful-vintage Amami polygon
  found after querying all four WHEP geodata sources. The honest gap
  is preferred over fabricating or over-extending a proxy.

## Open questions

### oq-amami-polygon-source

The Amami Islands (~1,231 km²) do not appear as a distinct feature in
CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, Paine-2024, or the GADM 4.1 slice available
in this repository. A faithful polygon could potentially be constructed
from GADM 4.1 full Japan data (Amami island sub-districts within
Kagoshima Prefecture), or from a Japanese historical administrative
atlas. If such a feature is located, the polygon_status should be
updated from `unassigned` to `proxy` (modern admin boundary used as
spatial proxy) with the appropriate feature id and area.

### oq-amami-data-existence

It is not confirmed whether FAO, IIA, or other WHEP data sources
actually contain production rows specifically attributed to the Amami
Islands for years 1946–1953 (as distinct from the broader "Ryukyu
Islands" label). If no data rows exist for this exact territory, the
row may be informational only (a boundary-gap note). Data users should
query the database for Amami-attributed rows before treating this polity
as data-bearing.

### oq-start-year-1945-vs-1946

The US military government (USMGRI) for the Ryukyu Islands was
established in mid-1945 after the Battle of Okinawa. If data for 1945
is attributed specifically to the Amami group (rather than the broader
Ryukyu Islands), the start year should be revised to 1945.
