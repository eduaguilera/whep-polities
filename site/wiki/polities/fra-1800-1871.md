# France (with Alsace-Lorraine, to 1871)

## Summary

This row tracks France as a territorial-economic unit from **1800**
(the WHEP database floor) to **1871** (Treaty of Frankfurt). The 71-year
window spans the Consulate/First Empire, Bourbon Restoration, July
Monarchy, Second Republic, and Second Empire. The defining territorial
characteristic of this polity is that it includes the Alsace-Lorraine
region, which was ceded to Germany by the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May
1871).

- **1800 start** is the database floor, not a territorial event.
  At 1800 France was the French Republic (Consulate period under
  Napoleon Bonaparte).
- **1871 end** is a real territorial event: the Treaty of Frankfurt
  (10 May 1871) ceded Alsace and parts of Lorraine to Germany
  [biger-1995 §france-germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-germany)
  [wikipedia-france-2026-04-12 §1871-alsace-lorraine](../sources/wikipedia-france-2026-04-12.md#1871-alsace-lorraine).
  The loss is estimated at ~14,500 km² (~2.7% of metropolitan France).

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [fra-1871-1919](fra-1871-1919.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes-Europe` feature 220, vintage 1816
  (polygon_status=assigned). This pre-1886 polygon includes
  Alsace-Lorraine, which is the critical territorial distinction for
  this polity relative to its successor FRA-1871-1919.
- **Approximation caveat — Nice/Savoy gap.** The 1816 polygon was
  drawn before the March 1860 cession of Nice and Savoy from Piedmont
  to France (8,261 km²; Treaty of Turin, 24 March 1860)
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
  For the sub-period 1860–1871, the 1816 polygon **understates**
  France's true extent by approximately 8,261 km² (~1.5% of
  metropolitan France). Direction: understate. Magnitude: minor.
  No polygon replacement performed per the polygon_replacement_policy
  (do not replace existing CShapes polygons). This approximation is
  documented here; the 1816 polygon remains in use.
- **Alsace-Lorraine.** The 1816 polygon includes Alsace-Lorraine,
  correctly representing France's extent for the period 1800–1871
  before the Treaty of Frankfurt cession.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1816-database-floor

Data routed to this polity for years 1800–1815 falls before the
CShapes-Europe polygon vintage of 1816. The Napoleonic period had
highly variable French extent (Empire vs. Consulate). The 1816 polygon
represents post-Congress of Vienna France, which is approximately
correct for the Consulate period at 1800 but does not capture the
First Empire's peak extent (1810). If data from 1804–1815 is matched
to this polity, the polygon may misrepresent that sub-period's
territorial scope.

### oq-1860-nice-savoy-split-candidate

The March 1860 cession of Nice and Savoy (8,261 km²) is a candidate
split point under the WHEP territorial-change rule. Not split here
because: (1) the area gain is minor relative to metropolitan France
(~1.5%); (2) the 1871 split already partitions this polity at its end;
(3) no polygon is available that precisely captures the 1860 boundary.
Low priority.
