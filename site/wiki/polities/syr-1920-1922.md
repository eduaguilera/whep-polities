# Syria (French Mandate, 1920-1922)

## Summary

Following the San Remo Conference of April 1920, France received a League of Nations mandate over Syria. French forces defeated King Faisal's Arab Kingdom of Syria at the Battle of Maysalun on 24 July 1920 and occupied Damascus. In 1922, the League of Nations formally approved the mandate, and France subdivided the territory into several autonomous states.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0, gwcode 652, `polygon_feature_year: 1921` — measured **188,105 km²**, roughly modern Syria (185,180 km²) plus Lebanon during the initial French mandate period. (The sentence previously here, "Polygon: Not yet assigned", was already stale: the frontmatter has carried a CShapes binding since the page was created.)

**Which step, and why 1921 (issue 100, 2026-08-13).** CShapes splits gwcode 652 inside 1920: a first step 1920-04-26 – 1920-12-23 (**182,452 km²**) and a second 1920-12-24 – 1922-12-01 (**188,004 km²**). Both contain the year 1920 and both *start* in it, which is the tie `build_database.find_feature` cannot break, so with `polygon_feature_year: 1920` the polygon was decided by shapefile row order rather than by the data. `1921` falls inside the second step and inside no other. It is also the right step on the merits: this row exists to host the pre-1961 input data labelled "Syria" for **1921** (see "Why this entry exists"), and the second step is the one in force for the whole of that year. Measured area moved 182,452 -> 188,105 km² (+3.1%).

**Why this entry exists:** Fills a 2-year gap in the WHEP database between [SYR-1918-1920](syr-1918-1920.md) (ends 1920) and [SYR-1922-1945](syr-1922-1945.md) (starts 1922). The pre-1961 input data has "Syria" for 1921 which fell into this gap. This is the transitional period when France was establishing the mandate administration (San Remo Conference April 1920, State of Greater Lebanon September 1920).
