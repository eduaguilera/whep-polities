# Hungary (1938-1940)

## Summary

This row tracks Hungary for **2 years**, from the First Vienna Award to the
Second Vienna Award.

The First Vienna Award (2 November 1938) returned the southern strip of
Slovakia (~11,830 km²) and parts of Subcarpathian Ruthenia from
Czechoslovakia to Hungary, bringing Hungarian territory from Trianon
~93,000 km² to ~109,000 km². This expanded border held until the Second
Vienna Award (30 August 1940), which is the end-of-row event.

Predecessor: [hun-1920-1938](hun-1920-1938.md) — First Vienna Award.
Successor: [hun-1940-1944](hun-1940-1944.md) — Second Vienna Award.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, cowcode=310, polygon vintage
  1938-11-02. This is the post-First-Vienna-Award boundary.
- **Area.** ~108,785 km²: Trianon base (~92,991 km²) plus First Vienna
  Award southern Slovakia (~11,830 km²), plus minor Subcarpathian
  Ruthenia strips.
- **Polygon accuracy.** The CShapes 1938 polygon is accurate for this
  sub-period (1938-11-02 to 1940-08-29). It does not reflect the Second
  Vienna Award, which belongs to the successor polity HUN-1940-1944.
- **Split rationale.** This polity is one of three created by splitting
  HUN-1938-1947 at confirmed border-change dates (First Vienna Award
  1938-11-02, Second Vienna Award 1940-08-30, Soviet-era reversion
  ~1944-10). The split is required by the WHEP rule that a polygon
  represents only its vintage year.

## Sourced claims

- **1938-11-02: First Vienna Award.** Germany and Italy arbitrated the
  return of southern Slovakia and parts of Subcarpathian Ruthenia to
  Hungary. Biger records this as the key post-Trianon Hungarian
  territorial gain.
  [biger-1995 §hungary](../sources/biger-1995.md#hungary) (pp.266-268)
- **1940-08-30: Second Vienna Award.** "Hungary regained almost 40% of
  the area in the Vienna Award of 30 August 1940," adding northern
  Transylvania (~43,492 km²). This is the end-of-row event.
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria) (p.51)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 310, continuous.
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
