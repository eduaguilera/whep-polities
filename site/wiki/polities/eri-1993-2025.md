# Eritrea

## Summary

Independent Eritrea from the 1993 referendum to the present.
The EPLF fought a 30-year liberation war (1961-1991) after
Ethiopia annexed the federated territory in 1962. Independence
referendum held 24 May 1993; admitted to the UN on 28 May 1993.
Same territory as colonial-era Eritrea — CShapes area 10.18 sq
deg (~93,662 km² per Biger).

Predecessor: NA in the direct chain sense (Eritrea was an
Ethiopian province 1962-1993, part of the ETH chain). The
territory was previously [eri-1889-1952](eri-1889-1952.md).
Successor: NA (ongoing).

## Open questions

### oq-eritrea-ethiopia-war

The 1998-2000 Eritrea-Ethiopia border war resulted in territorial
disputes (Badme). The Eritrea-Ethiopia Boundary Commission ruled
in 2002 but implementation was delayed until 2018. This may
warrant a further split if the border actually moved, but CShapes
shows the same area throughout.
