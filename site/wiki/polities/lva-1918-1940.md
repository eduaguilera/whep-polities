# Latvia (interwar republic)

## Summary

The Republic of Latvia as an independent state from its declaration of
independence on 18 November 1918 to Soviet annexation on 5 August 1940.
Independence was consolidated through the Latvian War of Independence (1918-1920)
against Soviet Russia and Baltic German forces, and formalised in the
[Latvian-Soviet Peace Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian%E2%80%93Soviet_Peace_Treaty)
of 11 August 1920. "Latvia and Lithuania became fully independent in 1920"
[biger-1995 §latvia](../sources/biger-1995.md#latvia).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 367, time-step 1918-1940, area 65,966 km²
(combines historical Livonia, Courland, and Latgale; Abrene district in the
east was added by the 1920 treaty and later transferred to the RSFSR in 1944).
Modern Latvia is ~64,600 km².

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 agricultural input data reports
"Latvia" for 1920-1939, consistent with the interwar republic's own statistical
office. The previous wiki chain
([LVA-1800-1918](lva-1800-1918.md) → [LVA-1940-1991](lva-1940-1991.md)) left a
gap for this period, causing 428+ input rows to fail matching.
