# Turkiye (1918-1920)

## Summary

This row tracks Turkiye for **3 years**, the post-WWI dissolution
period [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Armistice of Mudros (30 October 1918) ended Ottoman participation in
WWI. Treaty of Sevres (10 August 1920) would have partitioned the
Ottoman Empire drastically but was never ratified. Turkish War of
Independence (1919-1923) under Mustafa Kemal began during this period.
The 1920 split date likely aligns with Sevres or the start of the
independence war.

Predecessor: [tur-1914-1918](tur-1914-1918.md) -- end of WWI.
Successor: [tur-1920-2025](tur-1920-2025.md) -- modern Turkey.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CShapes
  `cowcode=640`.
- **Polygon vintage drift.** `polygon_feature_year=1918` predates the
  Armistice of Mudros (30 Oct 1918). The CShapes snapshot records the
  pre-armistice Ottoman footprint and cannot represent the post-Mudros
  territorial reality. This is a genuine extent question, not a
  bookkeeping artifact.
- **Overstatement — itemized occupied territories.** The CShapes 1918
  polygon includes the following territories that were under Allied
  military occupation from late 1918 onward and were not under Ottoman
  / Turkish administrative control during the 1918-1920 period:
  - **Palestine** (~27,000 km²): British military occupation from
    September 1918; British Mandate formalized 1920.
  - **Syria** (~185,000 km²): Franco-British occupation late 1918;
    French Mandate territory by 1920.
  - **Mesopotamia / Iraq** (~435,000 km²): British military occupation
    throughout 1918; British Mandate from 1920.
  - Total overstatement: **~400,000–500,000 km²** (rough order of
    magnitude; exact boundary delineations vary by source).
- **Actual administered territory.** Post-Mudros Turkish authority was
  effectively limited to Anatolia (~750,000 km²) plus a rump of
  eastern Thrace; the rest of the CShapes polygon reflects pre-war
  Ottoman claims, not post-armistice administrative reality.
- **No better polygon available.** No post-Mudros GIS polygon for this
  rump Ottoman / proto-Turkish state has been identified in CShapes,
  Cliopatria, Paine, or GHGIS. The CShapes 1918 snapshot is retained
  as the best available approximation; the overstatement is documented
  here and in frontmatter.
- **Spatial-containment evidence.** PAL-1920-1948 (British Mandate
  Palestine) lies fully inside the TUR-1918-1920 CShapes polygon,
  confirming that Palestine is counted twice if Palestine-labelled data
  are routed to TUR. Data for Palestine 1918-1920 should be routed to
  PAL-1920-1948 (or a predecessor polity if one exists), not to this
  polity.
- **Practical data-routing impact.** Layer-B matching yielded 3
  wine-production rows assigned to TUR-1918-1920. These rows are
  consistent with Anatolian production and do not appear to represent
  Palestine/Syria/Iraq output; no double-count has been identified.
  If future data sources include Palestine or Syrian/Iraqi production
  labelled as "Turkey" or "Ottoman" for 1918-1920, those rows must be
  routed to the appropriate sub-territory polity, not here.
- **COW code 640** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
  COW lists Turkey as `TUR, 640`, continuous from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Sourced claims

- **1918-10-30: Armistice of Mudros.** The Ottoman Empire signed the
  armistice, ending its participation in WWI. This is the start-of-row
  event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md).
- **1919: Turkish War of Independence begins.** Mustafa Kemal organized
  resistance against Allied occupation and the partition plans.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md)
- **1920-08-10: Treaty of Sevres.** Signed but never ratified. Would
  have partitioned the Ottoman Empire drastically, creating an
  independent Armenia and Kurdistan and ceding large territories to
  Greece, France, Italy, and Britain. This is the end-of-row event.
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 640 (TUR), continuous
  from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
