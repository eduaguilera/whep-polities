# Guam (US Territory)

Guam (~549 km²) is an unincorporated organized territory of the United States. The Guam Organic Act (1950-08-01) ended US Navy administration, conferred US citizenship on residents, and established a civilian government — marking the transition from GUM-1898-1950. Territory is unchanged: the same single island in the Mariana Islands, western Pacific.

## Polygon

GADM-4.1 GUM feature, vintage 2023. The island boundary has not changed since 1898 (no land accessions or cessions); the modern GADM polygon is an accurate representation of the territory throughout this period. polygon_status=assigned, polygon_area_km2=550.

## Polygon vintage drift note

The polygon vintage (2023) is used across 1950-2025. Island borders are fixed, so drift is negligible.
