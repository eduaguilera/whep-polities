# Algeria (1902-1919)

## Summary

This row covers French colonial Algeria from 24 December 1902 (formal annexation of the Sahara) to 12 September 1919. The 1902 Sahara annexation nearly tripled the CShapes area from 63.52 to 224.74 degree-squared units. The 1919 end corresponds to a slight area decrease to 213.51 in CShapes (possibly a boundary adjustment with post-WWI border redrawing).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
- **Area.** CShapes area = 224.74 degree-squared units. Includes the Saharan territories formally annexed in 1902.
- **The source's territorial basis is NOT this polygon, and the gap is the largest in the database.** The IIA yearbooks state Algeria's own area as **575,511 km²** for the data years they publish, against this polygon's **2,442,683 km²** measured on ESRI:54034 — a ratio of **4.24×**. Neither figure is an error. IIA counted the three **civil departments** of northern Algeria (Alger, Oran, Constantine), which is the administrative unit French agricultural statistics were collected on; the polygon is the colony including the **Southern Territories** annexed in 1902, which is the right answer to "what did France hold". A yield or intensity series that divides IIA's Algerian production by this polygon therefore understates by a factor of four, and nothing else in the pipeline can see it: the production number is right, the polygon is right, the join is right, and only the basis differs.

  **RESOLVED 2026-08-31 (issue #400).** The data moved rather than the polygon: `mitchell` and `iia` `algeria` 1902-1918 — 418 layer-B rows — now route to [dza-cvd-1902-1919](dza-cvd-1902-1919.md), a row for the civil departments carrying IIA's stated 575,511 km² as its declared area. **This row keeps its polygon and its meaning unchanged** (it is still the right answer to "what did France hold") and now carries **0 layer-B rows**, the same shape as [ser-1918-1945](ser-1918-1945.md) after #374. The 4.24× is therefore no longer a live denominator error; it is the *measurement that identified the split*, which is why the paragraph above is kept rather than deleted.

  One consequence, stated because it is a loss in the published contract: `validate_stated_areas.analyse()` compares a stated area only against a polity that HAS geometry, and the new row has none, so the `(DZA-1902-1919, iia)` row **leaves** `data/final/source_stated_area_basis.csv` along with its `basis_flag = review` (issue 166). The three IIA statements now resolve to a polity with no polygon to compare them to. The warning disappears at the moment the problem does, and this paragraph is where a reader who came looking for that row should land.

### mitchell shows the same civil-departments basis as iia

Recorded 2026-08-18. The civil-departments reporting gap already documented here
for IIA (issue 166) holds for `mitchell` as well, which makes it a property of
French colonial agricultural statistics generally rather than of one yearbook.

Across the **1902 annexation of the Southern Territories**:

| series | pre-1902 | 1902-1918 |
|---|---|---|
| wheat (ha) | 1,295,000 (n=26) | 1,387,000 (n=17) |
| barley (ha) | 1,423,000 (n=26) | 1,344,000 (n=17) |
| camels (heads) | 203,000 (n=24) | 199,000 (n=13) |

No step. Adding the Sahara to the colony added no measurable cropland to the
reporting basis — barley area even falls slightly — so the series covers the three
civil departments (Alger, Oran, Constantine) throughout.

**The cost is a denominator error, and it is large.** This row's polygon is
2,442,844 km² including the Sahara, while IIA states **575,511 km²** for the
territory these figures describe. Any per-km² intensity computed from this
polygon understates by roughly **4.2x** for the whole span. That divergence is
already recorded as a scope entry in `scripts/validate_stated_areas.py`.

This **now resolves** `oq-dza-civil-departments-as-a-reporting-unit`: as of
2026-08-31 there is somewhere to route the data to, and both series above are
routed there — [dza-cvd-1902-1919](dza-cvd-1902-1919.md). What unblocked it was a
decision, not a polygon: the civil-departments row still has none. `RWB-1922-1962`
(issue #355) and the pre-1871 German aggregate on
[deu-1800-1866](deu-1800-1866.md) remain blocked, and are now blocked only on
being done rather than on the question this one was waiting for.

### oq-dza-civil-departments-as-a-reporting-unit

**RESOLVED 2026-08-31 — YES.** See `dec-dza-1902-civil-departments-split` above and
[dza-cvd-1902-1919](dza-cvd-1902-1919.md). The maintainer's decision on
[issue #400](https://github.com/eduaguilera/whep-polities/issues/400) settles the general question
this asked, and the specific one: *"if the territory is clearly different, we need a polity for that
territory ... for the one you mentioned its clear we need a separate territory defined."*

Two things this question got wrong are worth keeping, because they were the reasons it stayed open:

- **"It would need a polygon."** It did not. `CAN-1866-1870`, `PRY-1811-1870` and `SAC-1935-1947`
  all carry a declared area with `polygon_source: none` and `polygon_status: unassigned`, and here
  the source states the area itself — 575,511 km². `MMR-LWR-1852-1885`'s page documents the same
  reasoning for a reporting unit that had no polygon when it was created. The new row has no
  polygon and is still strictly better than routing the data to a 4.24× territory, because the
  declared area is *visible to a consumer* and the basis mismatch was not.
- **"It would only pay for itself once data arrives labelled as the civil departments."** The data
  was already here; it just arrives labelled `algeria`. That is what the no-step measurement in the
  Contradictions section establishes — the label names the colony and the figures measure the
  departments — and waiting for a differently-spelled label would have meant waiting forever.

Still open, and now unblocked rather than undecided: **French West Africa and the Saharan rows have
the same civil/military split** and need the same treatment. So does IIA's own Algerian data for
1919-1928, which is still on the civil-departments basis while sitting on
[dza-1919-1962](dza-1919-1962.md) — tracked as `oq-dza-cvd-basis-after-1919` on the new page.
