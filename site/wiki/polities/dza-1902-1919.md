# Algeria (1902-1919)

## Summary

This row covers French colonial Algeria from 24 December 1902 (formal annexation of the Sahara) to 12 September 1919. The 1902 Sahara annexation nearly tripled the CShapes area from 63.52 to 224.74 degree-squared units. The 1919 end corresponds to a slight area decrease to 213.51 in CShapes (possibly a boundary adjustment with post-WWI border redrawing).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
- **Area.** CShapes area = 224.74 degree-squared units. Includes the Saharan territories formally annexed in 1902.
- **The source's territorial basis is NOT this polygon, and the gap is the largest in the database.** The IIA yearbooks state Algeria's own area as **575,511 km²** for the data years they publish, against this polygon's **2,442,683 km²** measured on ESRI:54034 — a ratio of **4.24×**. Neither figure is an error. IIA counted the three **civil departments** of northern Algeria (Alger, Oran, Constantine), which is the administrative unit French agricultural statistics were collected on; the polygon is the colony including the **Southern Territories** annexed in 1902, which is the right answer to "what did France hold". A yield or intensity series that divides IIA's Algerian production by this polygon therefore understates by a factor of four, and nothing else in the pipeline can see it: the production number is right, the polygon is right, the join is right, and only the basis differs. Both figures are now published per (polity, source) in `data/final/source_stated_area_basis.csv`, where this row carries `basis_flag = review` (issue 166).

### oq-dza-1919-area-change

CShapes shows area decreasing from 224.74 to 213.51 at 1919-09-12. This may relate to post-WWI boundary adjustments. Investigate what territory was lost.

### oq-dza-civil-departments-as-a-reporting-unit

**Should the statistical unit get its own row?** Issue 166 asks it, and it is deliberately left open here rather than answered. The precedent for modelling a reporting unit that is not a polity runs the OTHER way — `MASG` and `PAPNG` exist because sources AGGREGATE two territories into one column — while northern Algeria is the opposite shape: the source reports a **sub-unit** of a polity we model. Creating a `DZA-north` row would need a polygon for the three civil departments (no source in `scripts/sources.yaml` carries one today), and it would only pay for itself once data actually arrives labelled as the civil departments rather than as "Algeria". Until then the basis is **recorded** rather than resolved, in `data/final/source_stated_area_basis.csv`. French West Africa and the Saharan rows have the same civil/military split and would need the same decision.
