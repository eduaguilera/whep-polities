## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Jamaica, an island country in the Caribbean Sea. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. Jamaica was a Spanish colony from 1509 until captured by Britain in 1655, and remained a British colony throughout this period [biger-1995 &sect;jamaica](../sources/biger-1995.md#jamaica).
