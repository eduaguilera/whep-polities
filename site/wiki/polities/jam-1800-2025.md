# Jamaica (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Jamaica, an island country in the Caribbean Sea, covering 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. Jamaica was a Spanish colony from 1509 until captured by Britain in 1655, remained a British colony until independence on 6 August 1962, and has been independent since [biger-1995 &sect;jamaica](../sources/biger-1995.md#jamaica). None of those transitions moved a boundary: the territory is an island, so one row carries the whole span.

## Territorial extent

Jamaica has an area of approximately 10,991 sq. km. As an island state with no land boundaries, its territory has been constant. The polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature 51 at its 1886 vintage, back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025; because the boundary is the coastline, that back-projection carries no territorial error.

## Contradictions

- **FAO 1952 states 1,420 sq. km for this island, and it is a transcription error rather than a
  different territory (issue #111).** The FAO 1952 land-use table gives `British West Indies
  Jamaica` as 142 thousand ha = 1,420 sq. km, 7.7x below this row's 11,001 sq. km polygon. The
  IIA yearbooks state 10,880-11,526 sq. km for the same `JAMAIQUE` line across six editions
  (1909-1938), and the sibling FAO rows in the same block are right (Barbados 450 against 435,
  Cayman Islands 240 against 281), so the polygon and the alias routing that label here are both
  correct. The error is not a decimal shift: x10 gives 14,200 (1.29x too large) while restoring a
  dropped leading digit gives 11,420 (1.039x). A previous reading, since withdrawn, took IIA's
  231-271 sq. km as its Jamaica figure; that belongs to the `JAMAIQUE: CAIMANS` sub-label, i.e.
  the Cayman Islands ([CYM-1800-2025](cym-1800-2025.md)), a separate reporting unit indented under
  Jamaica. **The database follows its own polygon.** The printed figure is carried unchanged in
  `data/final/source_stated_areas.csv` and logged in
  `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/data_errors.csv`; anyone computing a Jamaican yield from FAO
  1952 must not use 1,420 sq. km as the denominator.

## Open questions

### oq-jamaica-biger-coverage

Jamaica has limited coverage in Biger 1995 because it has no land boundaries. A future ingest should find a more detailed Caribbean-focused source for Jamaica's territorial and political history.

### oq-split-not-applied

**Resolved 2026-08-17 in favour of the single row; kept as the record of the
decision.** Flagged 2026-07-24 by assertion verification of
`jamaica|mitchell|1850-1960` (`page_suspect`): the body was titled "Jamaica (to
1886)", presented itself as a pre-CShapes back-projection row ending where
CShapes coverage begins, and named a successor `JAM-1886-1962` that does not
exist. The frontmatter and CSV carry one row, **JAM-1800-2025**.

Unlike the Brazil case there is **no territorial reason** to split here: Jamaica
is an island whose boundary is its coastline, unchanged across the whole span,
so a single row with a back-projected polygon is defensible. The 1886 boundary
was about *polygon source coverage*, not territory.

So the body above now describes the single row, and says plainly that the
polygon is the 1886 CShapes vintage back-projected to 1800 — the territory being
constant, that back-projection carries no territorial error. The sourced claims
were preserved unchanged.
