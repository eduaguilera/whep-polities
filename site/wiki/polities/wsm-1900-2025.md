# Samoa

## Summary

This row tracks Samoa (formerly Western Samoa) for **126 years** from
the database start year to the truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Samoa is a group
of islands in the South Pacific Ocean with no international land
boundaries
[biger-1995 §western-samoa](../sources/biger-1995.md#western-samoa).
It was administered by Germany from 1899, then by New Zealand from
1919 as a League of Nations / UN Trust Territory, before achieving
independence in 1962.

## Sourced claims

- Western Samoa: area 1,093 sq. miles (2,831 sq. km.), population
  200,000 (1994), capital Apia, no land boundaries. Two main islands:
  Upolu (with the capital) and Savi'i, plus about 20 small islands.
  [biger-1995 §western-samoa](../sources/biger-1995.md#western-samoa)
  (pp.512--513)
- Western Samoa was administered by Germany from 1899 and by New
  Zealand from 1919. It became the independent State of Western
  Samoa in 1962.
  [biger-1995 §western-samoa](../sources/biger-1995.md#western-samoa)
  (p.513)
- It was a United Nations Trust Territory under the administration
  of New Zealand until achieving its independence in 1962.
  [biger-1995 §western-samoa](../sources/biger-1995.md#western-samoa)
  (p.513)
