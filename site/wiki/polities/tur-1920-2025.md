# Turkiye (1920-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks modern Turkiye for **106 years**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Treaty of Lausanne (24 July 1923) established current borders,
replacing the unratified Treaty of Sevres. Republic proclaimed 29
October 1923. Sultanate abolished 1 November 1922. Metropolitan
borders stable since Lausanne. Hatay Province annexed from French
Syria in 1939. This completes the Ottoman/Turkey chain: OTT-1800-1886
-> OTT-1886-1908 -> OTT-1908-1912 -> TUR-1913-1914 -> TUR-1914-1918
-> TUR-1918-1920 -> TUR-1920-2025.

Predecessor: [tur-1918-1920](tur-1918-1920.md) -- post-WWI dissolution.
Successor: NA (still active).

COW: continuous from 1816 at ccode=640.

## Sourced claims

- **1922-11-01: Sultanate abolished.** The Ottoman sultanate was
  formally abolished by the Grand National Assembly.
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey)
- **1923-07-24: Treaty of Lausanne.** Replaced the unratified Treaty
  of Sevres. Established Turkey's current borders, recognized the
  Republic's sovereignty. This is the key border-setting event for
  this row.
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey)
- **1923-10-29: Republic proclaimed.** The Republic of Turkey was
  formally proclaimed with Mustafa Kemal Ataturk as president.
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey)
- **1939: Hatay Province annexed.** Hatay Province (~5,500 km²)
  annexed from French Syria following a referendum.
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 640 (TUR), continuous
  from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
- **Biger 1995: area and borders.** Turkey: 300,870 sq mi [779,253 sq km],
  situated in two continents -- Asia and Europe
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey) (p.495).
- **Biger 1995: 1922-1923 republic.** "The Turkish war of independence
  in 1922-1923 resulted in the establishment of a republic"
  [biger-1995 §turkey](../sources/biger-1995.md#turkey) (p.496).

## Verification notes

- **No visible 1939 Hatay step in Mitchell's crop areas (2026-07-24).**
  Mitchell reports Turkish cereal acreage (wheat, barley, oats, maize,
  potatoes) 1920-1960 at magnitudes consistent with the Republic's
  post-Lausanne territory across the whole span, with no discontinuity at the
  1939 Hatay annexation — either Hatay's agricultural contribution was too
  small relative to national totals to register, or it was folded in without a
  visible step. The assertion is banked `best_available` rather than
  `verified_equal` because this row spans the annexation without splitting.
