# Algeria (1886-1902)

## Summary

This row covers French colonial Algeria from 1886 (CShapes start for dependency data) to 24 December 1902, when the Sahara region was formally annexed by France. CShapes shows two sub-periods (1886-1901 and 1901-1902) with identical area (63.52 degree-squared units), merged here because there is no territorial change.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
- **Area.** CShapes area = 63.52 degree-squared units. This covers only northern Algeria (the three French departments), before the 1902 Sahara annexation.
