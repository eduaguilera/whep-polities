# Korea south of the 38th parallel (US Army Military Government, 1945-1948)

## Summary

The American occupation zone of Korea, from the Japanese surrender in September 1945 to the
proclamation of the Republic of Korea on 15 August 1948. The United States Army Military Government
in Korea governed everything south of the 38th parallel; the Soviet Civil Administration governed
the north.
[wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16 §Articles referenced](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md#articles-referenced)

[kor-1945-1948](kor-1945-1948.md) is the whole occupied peninsula in the same years. This row is
the southern zone only, and continues the southern series of [krs-1910-1945](krs-1910-1945.md)
into [kor-1948-2025](kor-1948-2025.md). It is typed `colonial`, as its predecessor is: a territory
under a foreign administration, not a state.

## Why this entry exists

Mitchell's Korea columns become **South Korea only from 1945**: C1/C2 note 18 reads "South Korea
only from 1945", and the C16 fisheries column is headed "South Korea" (issue 688). The break is in
the numbers: rice area falls from 1,319,000 ha in 1944 (the peninsula) to 1,046,000 ha in 1945 and
runs on to 1,044,000 in 1949 on KOR-1948-2025, and wheat output falls from 261,000 t to 44,000,
because the wheat was grown in the north.

Layer B had these rows on the whole-peninsula polity KOR-1945-1948:

- `korea` (mitchell) 1945-1947: **34 rows**, moved here by an alias.
- `south korea` (mitchell) 1945-1947: **12 rows**, moved here by an alias.
- `korea` (mitchell) 1948: **12 rows**, moved to [kor-1948-2025](kor-1948-2025.md), by extending
  the existing `korea` 1949-1960 alias back one year. 1948 is the Republic's first year, and a row
  is inside the polity whose span starts that year.

The iia `south korea` 1945 rows (5) are not Mitchell's and are left on KOR-1945-1948.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: CShapes 2.0 gwcode 730 (Korea, its 1910-1945 step)
clipped to latitudes below 38° N. It measures **93,994 km²** (ESRI:54034). The zones were defined
by the parallel itself, so the clip is this territory's own definition, not an approximation.
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-borders](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders)

- **Why not CShapes' Republic of Korea (gwcode 732).** CShapes draws 732 with the 1953 armistice
  line from 1945 onward. Measured against the parallel, that outline holds 5,644 km² north of 38° N
  (the east-coast salient gained in the war) and leaves 2,443 km² south of it (the Ongjin
  peninsula and Kaesong, held by the north after 1953) outside. Both are on the wrong side for
  1945-1948, so the 732 outline (97,195 km²) is not used here, although
  [krs-1910-1945](krs-1910-1945.md) and [kor-1948-2025](kor-1948-2025.md) use it.
- **Why 730.** It is CShapes' whole-peninsula feature, the one [kor-1945-1948](kor-1945-1948.md)
  carries, so this row is exactly the southern part of its whole-peninsula neighbour.
- Ulleungdo is not in CShapes' Korea feature at all, so it is not in this polygon either (about
  73 km²).

## Decisions

- **Span 1945-1948**, end exclusive: 1948 is the Republic's year.
- **The polygon is the parallel, not the 1953 line**, for the reason under *Territorial extent*.
- **Aliases, not item corrections**, because every Mitchell `korea` and `south korea` series in
  1945-1947 is south-only by the note.
- **The 1944 footnote-marker cells** (`kor-1944-footnote-marker-read-as-18000-ha`) are 1944 and stay
  on the peninsula polity; they are unaffected.
