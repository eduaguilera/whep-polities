# Colombia (to 1903)

## Summary

This row tracks Colombia from **1830** to **1903** — a **74-year
row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV
notes: "Republic of New Granada / Colombia after Gran Colombia.
~2.8M km2. Lost Panama 1903"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

After the dissolution of Gran Colombia, the territory was known
successively as the Republic of New Granada (1831-1858), the
Granadine Confederation (1858-1863), the United States of Colombia
(1863-1886), and the Republic of Colombia (1886 onward). The end-of-row
event is the separation of Panama in November 1903.
