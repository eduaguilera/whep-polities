# Italy (1866–1870)

## Summary

This sub-period covers the Kingdom of Italy from the acquisition of
**Venetia** (Vienna Peace Treaty, 3 October 1866) to the capture of
**Rome** (20 September 1870). It was split from the former single row
`ITA-1861-1919` to correct a polygon-vintage overstatement: the CShapes
1886 polygon includes Rome/Lazio (~17,000 km²), which was not Italian
territory during 1866–1870. The split was right; the polygon it was given
was not. Since 2026-08-13 this row uses CShapes-Europe's **own 1867–1870
step**, which excludes Lazio.

Predecessor: [ita-1861-1866](ita-1861-1866.md).
Successor: [ita-1870-1919](ita-1870-1919.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-Europe, `Id=325`, polygon vintage **1867**,
  which selects that source's `From=1867, To=1870` step. Areas, all on the
  equal-area projection: **272,718 km² as published** in
  `polities_database.gpkg`, 272,655 km² for the source step before the build's
  validity repair, 272,048 km² in the source's own `Area` field — agreeing to
  0.25%. Its capital field reads **Florence** — Italy's capital
  1865–1871 — confirming the step is the pre-Rome one.
- **True 1866–1870 extent.** Post-Venetia Italy: ~269,000 km². Includes
  Venetia (~18,000 km², gained October 1866); excludes Rome/Lazio (Papal
  temporal sovereignty until 20 September 1870). The bound step is **+1.4%**
  against that figure.
- **Why the vintage is 1867 and not 1866.** CShapes-Europe drops transition
  years: there is no step covering 1866. 1867 is inside this row's span
  (1866–1869 inclusive), so no proxy or out-of-span vintage is involved.
- **What this row used to carry, and why it was wrong.** Until 2026-08-13 the
  frontmatter said `polygon_feature_year: 1886`, `polygon_status: estimate`,
  and this section stated that no suitable polygon "exists in CShapes 2.0 or
  CShapes-Europe". That was false: CShapes-Europe Id 325 has a 1867–1870
  step, and it is exactly the post-Venetia, pre-Rome Italy described above.
  The row published 284,847 km² — **12,129 km² more than the step it should
  have used (+4.4% of it)** — and published geometry byte-identical to
  `ITA-1861-1866` and `ITA-1870-1919`. Issue 121, signals A and B of
  `validate_polygon_period_fit`.

## Sourced claims

- **1866-10-03: Vienna Peace Treaty.** Italy gained Venetia from Austria
  following the Third Italian War of Independence
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This event begins this sub-period.
- **1870-09-20: Capture of Rome.** Italy took the Papal States, ending Papal
  temporal power. Capital relocated from Florence to Rome
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1870-rome](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1870-rome). This event ends this sub-period.

## Magnitude step-change at 1866 (Venetia annexation)

Data matched to this polity (1866–1870) shows a genuine step-up vs.
[ita-1861-1866](ita-1861-1866.md) for staple crops:

| Crop  | ITA-1861-1866 | ITA-1866-1870 | Change  |
|-------|--------------|--------------|---------| 
| Rice  | baseline     | +~88%        | Venetia was Italy's primary rice-growing region (Po Valley east of the Adige) |
| Wheat | baseline     | +~17%        | Veneto wheat lands added |
| Maize | baseline     | +~25%        | Veneto maize production added |

These jumps are **not a data routing error**. They reflect the genuine
addition of Venetia's agricultural output. Venetia (~18,000 km², ~2.6 million
people in 1866) was one of the most productive agricultural regions in
northern Italy, with the Po Valley lowlands as Italy's main rice belt. The
magnitudes are consistent with the territorial gain. This is documented
here to prevent future re-routing of 1866–1870 data to ITA-1861-1866 or
to a separate Venetia polity.

## Decisions

- **Split from ITA-1861-1919 (2026-06-29).** The Rome (1870) acquisition is a
  genuine border-change event within the former single row. That reasoning
  stands.
- **Rebound 1886 → 1867 vintage, `estimate` → `assigned` (2026-08-13).**
  Measured before: 284,847 km². Measured after: 272,718 km². Against the
  ~269,000 km² external figure the error fell from +5.9% to +1.4%. Lazio is
  no longer inside the polygon, so the documented approximation is gone
  rather than merely disclosed.
- **No declared `polygon_area_km2`.** Left null deliberately: the only figure
  to declare would be copied off the geometry, which `validate_polygons`
  classes as self-referential.
- **Constructed polygon no longer needed.** The former
  `oq-gis-constructed-polygon` proposed subtracting Lazio from the 1886
  feature. CShapes-Europe already ships that geometry.
- **No data routing error.** The staple-magnitude step-change between
  ITA-1861-1866 and ITA-1866-1870 reflects the legitimate addition of
  Venetia (rice +88%, wheat +17%, maize +25%). Data is correctly matched.

## Open questions

### oq-gis-constructed-polygon

**CLOSED 2026-08-13 — the premise was wrong, not the priority.** This asked
for a GIS-constructed polygon subtracting Lazio from the CShapes 1886
feature, on the belief that no contemporaneous polygon existed.
CShapes-Europe Id 325 has always carried a 1867–1870 step at 272,718 km²
as published, with Florence as its capital. The row is now bound to it; nothing needs
constructing.

### oq-1866-transition-year

CShapes-Europe has no step covering 1866 itself (it jumps 1862–1866 →
1867–1870). This row's first year is therefore represented by the
1867 geometry, which already includes Venetia. Since Venetia was ceded on
3 October 1866 this is right for most of that year, but the pre-October
1866 months are overstated by ~18,000 km². Sub-annual, and this database's
resolution is the year, so it is recorded rather than fixed.
