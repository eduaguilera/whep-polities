# North Carolina (state of the United States)

## Summary

North Carolina, one of the thirteen original states, ratified the U.S. Constitution on 1789-11-21 (the second of two holdout states, after rejecting it in 1788) and has existed continuously as a state within the union since. This entry is type subnational because it sits below the national USA chain rather than competing with it for sovereignty in the matcher's ranking; the United States, not North Carolina, is the sovereign. The entry exists to carry state-level statistical reporting that the national USA rows cannot represent at the correct spatial resolution. start_year is set to 1800, not the state's 1789 founding date, because no polity in this database's USA chain covers 1789-1800: the earliest national row is USA-1800-1803, and the container-tiling requirement cannot be satisfied for 1789-1800 without inventing a container that does not exist in the table. This is a deliberate departure from the proposed 1789 start, recorded in decisions below.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures state-level data reported under the label North Carolina in production and trade series that distinguish U.S. states individually rather than reporting national U.S. totals. The routing decision that created this page identified rows keyed to the sub-unit id USA-NORTHCAROLINA with no existing candidate polity to match against; only the national USA chain and Territory of Alaska existed as USA subnational or national rows before this entry. Before this entry existed, any state-labelled row would have had no correct home: routing it to a national USA-era row would misrepresent a state-scale figure as a country-scale one, understating the true national total if summed, or double-counting if both national and state figures were retained under the same code. North Carolina's status as a distinct, long-continuous administrative and statistical reporting unit is not in question; it has been one of the fifty constituent states since 1789, with continuously stable boundaries recognized in U.S. Census Bureau reporting and in GADM's own admin-1 division of the country, which is why GADM 4.1 already carries a matching feature, USA.34_1, with no construction step needed.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: assigned. polygon_source is gadm-4.1-adm1, feature USA.34_1, a direct registered-source match, since GADM 4.1's ADM1 layer for the United States already contains a North Carolina polygon and no construction or proxy substitution is needed. Territory description: the U.S. state of North Carolina, in the southeastern United States, bordered by Virginia to the north, South Carolina and Georgia to the south, Tennessee to the west, and the Atlantic Ocean to the east, including the Outer Banks barrier islands. The state's external boundary has been essentially fixed since a handful of 19th-century boundary settlements with Tennessee, South Carolina and Georgia; no boundary change affects any part of this entry's 1800-2025 span in a way that would require a distinct proxy period. Modern reference area is approximately 139,400 km2, the Census Bureau figure for total area including inland and coastal waters; this is a figure quoted from external reference for locating the territory on a modern map, not a measurement of the attached polygon. Vintage risk is low: GADM reflects present-day, circa 2018-2022, state boundaries, and North Carolina's boundaries have been stable across the entire 1800-2025 span, so using the modern GADM feature as the polygon for the full span does not misrepresent any sub-period.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first entry in this database to carry North Carolina at state scale; it does not supersede any prior polity code, since the previously-existing USA rows are all national-scale and remain valid at that scale for non-state-level data. No successor: the state continues to the present, end_year 2025 per the open/current convention used elsewhere in this table (e.g. USA-1959-2025), so there is no dissolution or renaming event to chain forward to.

## Sourced claims

- North Carolina ratified the U.S. Constitution on November 21, 1789, becoming the twelfth state to join the union, after initially rejecting ratification in the 1788 Hillsborough convention, a documented founding date distinct from the 37 later states' territorial-admission dates.
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer for the United States contains a feature identified as USA.34_1 corresponding to the state of North Carolina, usable directly as this entry's polygon without construction.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1789

**start_year set to 1800, departing from the proposed 1789 ratification date**

The routing decision this page implements proposed start_year 1789, North Carolina's Constitution-ratification date, with the caveat that no container polity exists covering 1789-1800 in the given chain. Checking the polity table confirms that caveat: the earliest national USA row is USA-1800-1803, and no polity of any kind in this table covers 1789-1800. The container-tiling requirement is that every year of an entry's span fall inside some container edge, and every edge must fall inside both the member's and the container's own recorded span; an edge naming USA-1800-1803 for the years 1789-1800 would fail that check outright, since 1789-1799 falls outside USA-1800-1803's own start_year. Rather than leave a nine-year gap uncontained or invent a container row that doesn't exist in the table, this entry's start_year is set to 1800, matching the earliest available national container, with the code changed to USA-NC-1800-2025 to keep the code's years equal to the frontmatter's years as the gate requires. The 1789-1800 gap in North Carolina's own statehood is real and is not modelled by this entry; see the open question on this below.

### d-code-includes-subunit-abbrev

**Code follows the dominant 57-of-66 pattern, ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end**

North Carolina is an ordinary constituent state without a separate historical name of its own, unlike the bespoke cases ALK, HYD, RYU, so the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern applies rather than a bespoke code. SUBUNIT is set to NC, the standard USPS/ISO 3166-2 abbreviation for North Carolina, consistent with the existing convention visible in sibling entries such as ESP-AS-1833-2025 and ESP-CA-1833-2025, which use their own region's short codes rather than full names.

## Open questions

### oq-1789-1800-gap-not-modelled

**North Carolina's actual 1789-1800 statehood is not covered by any entry**

North Carolina was a state within the union from November 1789, but this entry starts at 1800 solely because no container polity exists for the earlier span in this database, see decision d-start-year-1800-not-1789. If a future data source reports North-Carolina-scale figures dated before 1800, there is currently no polity row to route them to, and none can be constructed until either a pre-1800 national USA container is added to the table or the tiling rule is otherwise relaxed for founding-era states. This same gap likely affects other original-13-state entries, such as a prospective USA-VA, USA-SC, or USA-GA row, and may be worth resolving once, at the container level, rather than once per state.

### oq-secession-era-not-flagged

**North Carolina's 1861-1865 Confederate secession is absorbed into the container edge without comment**

North Carolina seceded from the Union in May 1861 and was not represented in the U.S. Congress until 1868, post-Reconstruction, yet the container edge for USA-1848-1867 treats 1848-1867 as continuous inside the union for containment purposes, following the same simplification the national USA chain itself makes, since there is no separate Confederate-era polity in this table. Whether state-level production or trade data for 1861-1865 should instead route to a Confederate States entity, if one is ever added, is unresolved; for now this entry silently carries those years as ordinary USA statehood, which may misrepresent the reporting or political context for that five-year window.

### oq-no-north-carolina-data-verified-yet

**No specific data rows have been confirmed to require this polity beyond the routing decision's stated existence claim**

The routing decision that produced this page asserts a sub-unit id USA-NORTHCAROLINA needing a home but does not itself quote the underlying source labels, row counts, or year range of the data driving the creation of this entry, unlike the exemplar MAN-1950-1955 page, which tabulates exact source, label, row-count and year evidence. This page's why-this-entry-exists section is therefore argued from the absence of a matching candidate rather than from inspected source rows; the actual dataset or datasets that will populate this polity should be confirmed and, ideally, cited by name once ingestion runs.
