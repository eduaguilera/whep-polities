# Gran Colombia

## Summary

This row tracks Gran Colombia from the **database floor (1800)**
to **1830** — a **31-year row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The CSV notes:
"Includes Gran Colombia period (1819-1830): Colombia + Venezuela +
Ecuador + Panama. ~4.3M km2 at peak. Dissolved 1830"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

At 1800 the territory was under Spanish colonial rule. Bolivar's
campaigns led to the creation of Gran Colombia in 1819. The
federation dissolved in 1830 when Venezuela and Ecuador seceded.

Predecessor: NA (database floor — Spanish colony).
Successors: [col-1830-1903](col-1830-1903.md), [ven-1830-2025](ven-1830-2025.md), [ecu-1830-1942](ecu-1830-1942.md).
