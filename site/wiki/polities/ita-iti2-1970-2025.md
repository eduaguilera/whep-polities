# Umbria (region of Italy)

## Summary

Umbria is one of Italy's fifteen ordinary-statute regions (regioni a statuto ordinario), a landlocked NUTS2/ADM1 administrative division in central Italy comprising the provinces of Perugia and Terni, with its capital at Perugia. This entry is `type: subnational` because Umbria has never been sovereign: throughout its existence it has been a constituent administrative region of the Italian Republic, and the entry exists purely to carry the *reporting territory* that Italian regional statistics are published against, separately from national Italy-wide totals. The span starts in 1970 rather than at national unification (1861) or at the founding of the Republic (1946) because Umbria, like Italy's other thirteen ordinary-statute regions, was only actually constituted as a functioning administrative and statistical entity in 1970, when Law 281/1970 was implemented and the first direct regional councils were elected — this is the same 1970 start date used across the sibling Italian region rows (ITH5, ITC1, ITC3, ITF1, etc.) already adjudicated in this pipeline, and it deliberately excludes the two named exceptions (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia, annexed and organized earlier in 1919-1948; Molise, split from Abruzzo in 1963) neither of which applies to Umbria. The end_year remains 2025 (exclusive), open-ended, because Umbria is a current, still-existing administrative region.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Umbria as a distinct NUTS2/ADM1 reporting unit of Italy, created because the routing pipeline found no existing polity candidate that IS Umbria — the national Italy rows (e.g. ITA-1919-2025) contain Umbria's territory but report only country-wide aggregates, and no boundary or name match exists for a regional-level Umbria row anywhere in the current polity table (0 existing ITA subnational rows before this batch). The input data motivating this row consists of Italian regional statistics tabulated at NUTS2/ADM1 level for Umbria specifically (following the same sibling-unit pattern as ITA-ITH5, ITA-ITC1, ITA-ITC3, ITA-ITF1, each independently adjudicated and each starting in 1970 for the identical structural reason). Before this entry, any such Umbria-labeled rows would have been forced onto the national Italy row, which is roughly 36 times Umbria's area (301,340 km² national vs. ~8,456 km² regional) and would silently misattribute regional figures as national ones. What confirms Umbria is a distinct, statistically tracked entity rather than a mere reporting convenience is that Italy's own national statistical institute (ISTAT) and Eurostat's NUTS classification both publish continuous, independently sourced regional-level series for Umbria from 1970 onward, treating it as a first-class reporting geography — the same standing this pipeline has already granted its sibling Italian regions.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is available in the GeoPackage for this period, and `polygon_source` is set to `none` rather than to any candidate name, because the only source route identified (`registered_source_unfetched`, candidate `gadm-4.1-adm1`) points to a registered source whose *locally stored file* excludes Italy — the global GADM 4.1 ADM1 dataset would need to be fetched separately to obtain feature `ITA.19_1`. Per this pipeline's rule, an unfetched-but-registered source is recorded as `polygon_source: none` with the desired source noted as an open question, not written into the frontmatter as `registered_source_unfetched` (which is a route category, not a slug, and is rejected by `validate_declared_sources`).

**Territory description:** Umbria occupies the landlocked heart of central Italy, bordered by Tuscany to the northwest, Marche to the east, and Lazio to the south — a reader can locate it on a modern map as the roughly diamond-shaped region between Florence, Perugia and Terni, containing no coastline. It comprises two provinces, Perugia (the larger, northern) and Terni (the smaller, southern), and covers approximately **8,456 km²** (Italy's national statistical institute ISTAT figure for the modern region), making it Italy's fourth-smallest region by area. This figure is an external, independently published statement of the region's extent, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet attached.

## Predecessors and successors

Umbria has no predecessor or successor polity row: it is not a continuation of an earlier subnational entity and nothing succeeds it, since it remains a current administrative region of Italy as of 2025 (end_year 2025 is exclusive, matching the country convention that keeps still-current polities open-ended in this table). Before 1970, statistics collected on the territory that is now Umbria are routed to whichever national-level Italian polity was sovereign at the time (following the routed convention used for sibling Italian regions such as ITH5, ITC1, ITC3, ITF1), not to a predecessor row of this polity, because Umbria did not exist as a distinct administrative/statistical entity before the 1970 creation of Italy's ordinary-statute regions.

## Sourced claims

- Italy's fifteen ordinary-statute regions, including Umbria, were constituted as actual administrative and statistical bodies only in 1970, under Law 281/1970 and the first direct regional council elections held that year, decades after the 1948 Constitution had nominally provided for them.
- GADM 4.1 publishes Italy's ADM1-level administrative boundaries, including Umbria under feature id GID_1='ITA.19_1', but this repository's locally stored gadm-4.1-adm1 file is a curated 81-country subset that does not include Italy, so this feature is not currently fetched or available in the local GeoPackage.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-fetch-and-vintage

**Polygon requires fetching global GADM 4.1 ADM1 (Italy absent from local subset) and carries post-2001 boundary vintage**

No registered source in this repository currently carries a usable Umbria boundary: gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered with the correct schema (GID_1 id column) but the locally stored file is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Italy entirely (0 matching features), and cshapes-europe/cshapes-2.0 operate only at the national level. The correct remedy is to fetch the global GADM 4.1 ADM1 dataset (gadm41_adm1.gpkg) and extract feature GID_1='ITA.19_1'. Two things remain open: (1) GADM's redistribution licence is restricted for commercial use and requires attribution — this has not been checked against this repository's licensing policy, so it cannot yet be declared as the polygon_source; (2) GADM ADM1 geometry reflects present-day (post-2001) administrative boundaries, while this entry's span begins in 1970 — regional boundaries have been largely stable since then, but the polygon, once fetched, should be documented as an approximation of the 1970 extent, not an exact historical match.
