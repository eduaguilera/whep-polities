# Corsica (whole island)

## Summary

The whole island of Corsica, whatever its internal division at the time: two départements (Golo and Liamone) to 1811, the single département of Corse from 1811 to 1975, the two départements Corse-du-Sud (2A) and Haute-Corse (2B) from 1 January 1976, and since 1 January 2018 the single territorial collectivity (Collectivité de Corse) that replaced the region and the two departmental councils. `type: subnational`: France held the island throughout.

**Why this entry exists.** The Mediterranean subnational panel ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)) reports a unit labelled `FRM01`. In NUTS 2016 that code is Corse-du-Sud, and the panel's unit was routed to FRA-2A-1975-2025 (4,006 km2), with its 1862-1974 years back-cast onto the southern département. The unit's landuse block contradicts the label: the per-year sum of its land-cover classes is a **constant 8,716.75 km2 in every one of its 141 landuse years**, which is the whole island, not its southern half. There is no `FRM02` (Haute-Corse) unit in the panel at all, so nothing else carries the northern half. No existing polity was the island -- FRA-2A-1975-2025 is half of it and the national rows are 63 times larger -- so the unit needed its own row. Claude created this entry on 2026-09-24 while correcting the Southern-Europe alias defects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. GADM 4.1 adm1 feature FRA.5_1, "Corse" ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)), which is the region and therefore the whole island with its offshore islets.

**Measured.** In ESRI:54034 the feature is 8,743 km2 against the panel's constant 8,716.75 (-0.3%), inside the -1.0% to +0.1% band that the panel's other French units show against their own GADM features. Corse-du-Sud alone (GADM FRA.5.1_1) is 4,031 km2, 0.46 of the panel total, so the southern-département reading was off by a factor of two. The island's outline has not changed since 1800, so the modern polygon is the extent for the whole span; only its internal division moved (1811, 1976, 2018), and none of those moves ground in or out.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. The internal re-divisions of 1811, 1976 and 2018 happened inside this ground; the 1976 départements (FRA-2A-1975-2025 is the only one with a row) are its parts, not its successors. The span starts at the France convention's 1800 floor, since the island was already French and already a département territory then.

## Sourced claims

- The panel's `FRM01` landuse total is 8,716.75 km2 in every landuse year, and the panel carries no `FRM02` unit; measured 2026-09-24 ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)).
- GADM 4.1 adm1 FRA.5_1 is Corse, 8,743 km2 in ESRI:54034; its two adm2 children are Corse-du-Sud (4,031) and Haute-Corse (4,712) ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)).

## Decisions

### d-whole-island-not-2a

**The panel's FRM01 is routed here, not to Corse-du-Sud**

The land total is the whole island to 0.3%, and half the island to a factor of two. The unit's label is a NUTS code the compiler used for Corsica as a whole, which is the ordinary pre-1976 unit (the single département of Corse) carried forward. So its 1862-2023 years are observation of this territory, not a back-cast onto a southern département that did not exist before 1976.

### d-panel-slugs-only

**Only the slugs that carry the panel route here; the official NUTS slugs keep FRM01 = Corse-du-Sud**

`whep-lab-france-livestock` (`FRA-FRM01`) now routes 1862-2023 here, and a `juan-subnational` row for `FRM01` is added, since the panel's own slug had none. The `eurostat-nuts2016` and `jrc-nuts2016` rows for `FRM01` stay on FRA-2A-1975-2025: in those code systems FRM01 is Corse-du-Sud, and data genuinely coded to it must keep landing there.

## Open questions

### oq-2b-row

**Haute-Corse has no row**

With this entry carrying the whole island, a Haute-Corse row is needed only if a source reports the northern département on its own. None in this repository does today.
