# Cambodia (to 1904)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Cambodia from 1800 to 1904, covering the late independent period and early French protectorate. Cambodia was caught between the expansionist powers of Siam (Thailand) and Vietnam through the early 19th century. France established a protectorate over Cambodia in 1863. "In 1954 Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam were created as independent states and the French colonial boundary was adopted as the northern boundary" [biger-1995 §cambodia](../sources/biger-1995.md#cambodia). The 1904 end date corresponds to a CShapes boundary change. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. Cambodia's territory in this era was considerably smaller than its medieval Khmer Empire extent. Under the French protectorate (from 1863), Cambodia's western provinces remained under Siamese control until they were returned in 1907. The 1800-1904 polygon thus reflects Cambodia without the Battambang and Siem Reap provinces.
