# Cape Verde (to 1886)

## Summary

The Portuguese archipelago of Cabo Verde before 1886, the year at which CShapes
2.0 coverage — and therefore WHEP's Cape Verde chain — begins. The islands had
been Portuguese since their settlement in the fifteenth century and were
administered as an overseas territory throughout the nineteenth: a
provisioning and, until abolition, slaving entrepôt on the Atlantic routes, with
Praia replacing Ribeira Grande as capital in 1770 and Mindelo (São Vicente)
developing from the 1850s as a British-served coaling station. Nothing about the
territory changed in 1886.

This row exists because WHEP's Cape Verde chain began at 1886 while a source
reports the archipelago's trade from 1800. Federico-Tena's *List of trading
polities* carries `CapoVerde` with series running **1800–1938**; of the 318 rows
that produces in the intake table, **158 fall in 1800–1885**, before
`CPV-1886-1975` opens (measured 2026-08-17 against
`data/final/polities_database.csv`). `cpv-1886-1975` said so itself — its
`predecessor` was empty with the note "pre-1886 Cape Verde colonial history is
not yet modelled" — and this row is that gap filled.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, gwcode **402**, feature year **1886** — the
  earliest of the two steps CShapes offers (1886–1975, 1975–2019) and the same
  binding `CPV-1886-1975` uses. 4,033 km²: ten inhabited islands and five
  islets, some 570 km west of the Senegal-Mauritania coast, with no land
  boundary of any kind.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

- **`polygon_area_km2` is left blank on purpose.** The 4,033 km² figure would be
  copied from the successor row, which took it from this same geometry, so
  declaring it here would be a number agreeing with itself — the pattern
  `validate_polygons`' self-referential-area check exists to stop growing. The
  area is stated in this prose, where it cannot be mistaken for independent
  evidence about the territory.

- **Why `proxy` and not `assigned`.** This is the weakest possible reservation
  and it is recorded honestly rather than waved away: the binding's vintage
  (1886) lies OUTSIDE this row's span (1800–1886, `end_year` exclusive), so the
  geometry is formally a back-projection. Substantively it is exact — an
  archipelago with no land border cannot have had a different extent in 1800, and
  the same argument already licenses the `proxy` binding on
  [dwi-1800-1917](dwi-1800-1917.md), a Caribbean island group carrying a 2023
  GADM footprint. `assigned` is reserved here for a vintage inside the span, of
  which CShapes has none before 1886.

- **No split.** There is no candidate date for one. Portuguese sovereignty,
  island footprint and administrative unity are all continuous across the span.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none. Portuguese Cape Verde predates this database's 1800
  floor by three centuries.
- **Successor:** [cpv-1886-1975](cpv-1886-1975.md) — the identical territory
  from the first CShapes step to independence in 1975; that row's `predecessor`
  now points back here instead of carrying the "not yet modelled" note.

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0's Cape Verde entry (gwcode 402) begins 1886-01-01 and is coded
  status=colony, ruler=PRT for the whole step; the 1886 start is a property of
  the source's coverage, not of the colony.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
- Federico-Tena reports `CapoVerde` from 1800, and its early series are
  reconstructed rather than observed, so the start year is evidence about the
  compilers and not about a change in the territory.
  [federico-tena-2019 §known-limitations](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#known-limitations)
- The source page recorded `CapoVerde` 1800–1938 among the labels that route on
  the label but observe years the candidate chain does not cover; this row is the
  half of that gap that a polity row can close.
  [federico-tena-2019 §what-the-66-yearuncovered-labels-are](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#what-the-66-yearuncovered-labels-are)

## Decisions

- **[2026-08-17] Created as a span extension of an existing family, not as a new
  identity.** The name pattern "(to 1886)" and the shared `iso3` mark that: this
  is the same territory as `CPV-1886-1975`, and consumers should treat the two as
  one continuous series.
- **[2026-08-17] `cow_code` left empty**, though `CPV-1886-1975` carries 402.
  402 is the Gleditsch-Ward code CShapes uses; Cabo Verde is not a COW
  state-system member before independence in 1975.

## Open questions

### oq-island-level-reporting

Nineteenth-century Cape Verdean trade was concentrated in São Vicente and Praia,
and some yearbook series report Mindelo's bunkering separately from the
archipelago's trade. Whether any WHEP source does so is not established here; if
one does, it is a reporting-unit question about this row and not a territorial
one.
