# Huila (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Huila is a department in south-central Colombia, in the upper Magdalena River valley between the Central and Oriental branches of the Andes, with its capital at Neiva. This entry captures Huila as a first-order administrative division of Colombia from 1905 — the year it was constituted as a separate department, split off from the former Estado Soberano del Tolima / Departamento de Neiva — through the present day. `type: subnational` because Colombia held sovereignty throughout this span; the entry exists to carry departmental-level reporting territory, not statehood, matching the treatment already given to sibling departments Antioquia, Arauca, Atlantico, Bolivar, Boyaca, Caldas and Cauca in this pipeline. No existing COL-* row was a department-level match for Huila, and the routing decision confirms this is a genuinely new entry rather than a re-match, so it sets its own precedent as the first Huila-specific row in this country's chain.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Colombian department-level statistical data for Huila, most plausibly spanning at least 1915-2023 by analogy with the sibling department rows (Antioquia, Arauca, Atlantico, Bolivar, Boyaca, Caldas, Cauca) already created in this pipeline pass, though the exact row count and source label were not restated in the routing decision itself and are flagged as an open question.
