# Cape Colony (to 1910) — SUPERSEDED

## Summary

**SUPERSEDED** (2026-08-14, issue 22): this row held **one** CShapes vintage —
the 1886-1895 step, 600,802 km² — across 110 years, while CShapes records
**two** time-steps for gwcode 561. It has been split into:

- [cap-1800-1895](cap-1800-1895.md) — the colony on the 1886 step (600,802 km²),
  still a back-projection for the pre-1886 decades and documented as such;
- [cap-1895-1910](cap-1895-1910.md) — the colony after the British Bechuanaland
  annexation, on the observed 1895-1910 step (716,102 km²).

The measured defect that forced the split: for every data year from 1895 the old
binding **understated** the Cape by 115,300 km² (-16.1% against the correct step),
and the fix needed no new source, only the second time-step of the CShapes
release already in use. 128 layer-B rows dated 1895-1909 were affected.

Original summary: the British Cape Colony as a separate statistical /
territorial unit from the permanent British takeover in 1806 to the Union of
South Africa on 31 May 1910, when Cape Colony became the Cape Province within
the new union. Reported in pre-1961 agricultural data variously as "Cape
Province" or "Cape of Good Hope".
