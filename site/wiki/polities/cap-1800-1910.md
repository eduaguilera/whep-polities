# Cape Colony (to 1910)

## Summary

The British Cape Colony as a separate statistical / territorial unit from
the permanent British takeover in 1806 (Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1814 confirming
occupation) to the Union of South Africa on 31 May 1910, when Cape Colony
became the Cape Province within the new union. Reported in pre-1961
agricultural data variously as "Cape Province" or "Cape of Good Hope";
agricultural statistics were compiled separately from the other British South
African territories until union.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 561, time-step 1886-1895, area 600,802 km².
CShapes records two time-steps for the Cape Colony:

| Range | Area | Territory |
|---|---:|---|
| 1886-1895 | 600,802 km² | Core Cape Colony (Cape Town + eastern expansion) |
| 1895-1910 | 716,102 km² | After annexation of British Bechuanaland (1895) |

The 1886-1895 polygon is used as the primary binding because it represents
the larger fraction of the 1852-1909 data window. Cliopatria also records
"British Cape Colony" with many time-steps 1796-1884, which could provide a
more accurate pre-1886 polygon if needed — current binding uses CShapes as
a stable proxy for the entire pre-union period.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Cape Province" /
"Cape of Good Hope" for 123 rows spanning 1852-1909. Without this row the
matcher's `name_override` rules — which already map both strings to code
`CAP` — had nothing to resolve against.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1886-territory

Cape Colony was smaller pre-1886 (before the 1885-1890 expansion into the
Transkei). Using the 1886-1895 CShapes polygon as a proxy over-represents
territory for 1852-1885 data by ~10-15%. Cliopatria could provide
period-specific polygons if this becomes load-bearing.
