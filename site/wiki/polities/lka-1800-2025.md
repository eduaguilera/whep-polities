# Sri Lanka (1800-2025)

## Summary

Single row for Sri Lanka (Ceylon), an island state in the Indian Ocean south of India, covering 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 2025. Sri Lanka was a British colony from 1815 [biger-1995 &sect;sri-lanka](../sources/biger-1995.md#sri-lanka) and independent from 4 February 1948. Neither transition moved a boundary, so one row carries the whole span.

## Territorial extent

Sri Lanka is an island; no land boundaries exist. The polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature 780 at its **1948** vintage — the frontmatter's `polygon_feature_year`, and the year CShapes opens Ceylon as a state — back-projected to 1800 and carried forward to 2025. The boundary being the coastline, that back-projection carries no territorial error.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** No predecessor row (WHEP temporal boundary at 1800).
- **Successor:** No successor row; this row runs to the present.

split: the attached feature vintage is 1948, not 1886, and either way a
polygon-source date is not a territorial change (2026-08-17, issue #25).
