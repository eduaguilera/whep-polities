# Montserrat

## Summary

Montserrat is a British Overseas Territory (BOT) in the Leeward Islands, Eastern Caribbean. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row MSR-1800-2025, covering the period from 1800 through the present under British administration. Montserrat was a British colony from the seventeenth century, administered under the Leeward Islands federation (1871-1956) and subsequently as a direct Crown Colony and BOT. It has never become independent and has no COW state-system entry. The island's administrative extent has been stable throughout: approximately 102 km² island territory. The 1997 Soufriere Hills volcanic eruptions caused population displacement and establishment of an exclusion zone in the south, but the ISO 3166-1 administrative boundary (used by GADM) was not altered and remains the authoritative territorial reference for statistical reporting.

### Why not exact_historical (CShapes / Cliopatria)?

CShapes 2.0 covers sovereign states and colonial dependencies from 1886 onward but **does not include Montserrat** — it appears in neither `cshapes2_full.gpkg` nor `cshapes2_sovereign.gpkg`. Cliopatria/Seshat (3400 BCE-2024) has no Montserrat entry. No other exact historical GIS source (CHGIS, Paine, GHGIS) covers Eastern Caribbean British territories. GADM ADM0 is therefore the best available source and the correct fallback per WHEP polygon hierarchy.
