# Free City of Danzig

## Summary

The Free City of Danzig, "a city-state under the protection and oversight of the League of Nations
between 1920 and 1939, consisting of the Baltic Sea port of Danzig (now Gdańsk, Poland) and nearly 200
other small localities in the surrounding areas", "established on 15 November 1920, per the terms of
Article 100 ... of the 1919 Treaty of Versailles". Germany annexed it in September 1939.
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §danzig](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#danzig)
