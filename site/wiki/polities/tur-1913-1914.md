# Turkiye (1913-1914)

> **The polygon was publishing a CShapes step that begins after this row ends. Fixed 2026-08-12
> (issue 123).**
>
> The row covers **1913** — `end_year` is exclusive — and carried `polygon_feature_year: 1914`.
> CShapes 640 has two steps containing 1914, and `find_feature` prefers the one whose `start_year`
> equals the queried year:
>
> ```
> 1913-1913   1,912,569 km²
> 1913-1914   1,784,488 km²   <- what this page declares (1,785,218, a 0.04% match)
> 1914-1918   1,705,783 km²   <- what it actually published
> ```
>
> So the geometry was **1,705,971 km² published against 1,785,218 declared** — a 4.5%
> disagreement between a page and its own polygon, and the wrong territory: the 1914-1918 extent
> for a row about 1913. It also made this row and [tur-1914-1918](tur-1914-1918.md) publish
> **identical** geometry, which is why the period-fit gate carried this row twice, once as an
> out-of-span binding and once as a duplicate-geometry pair.
>
> **No `polygon_feature_year` can fix it.** Both 1913 steps *start* in 1913, so `1913` is a tie
> that `find_feature` resolves by shapefile row order — the order-dependence
> `validate_polygon_binding_determinism` exists to forbid. The step has to be named by its bounds,
> which is what `_cshapes2_step(640, 1913, 1914)` in the constructed builder does.
>
> **Why the `1913-1914` step rather than `1913-1913`:** CShapes splits 1913 because the Ottoman
> border moved during it — the Treaty of London in May, Bucharest in August, and the
> Ottoman-Bulgarian treaty in September that returned Edirne. This page's declared area has always
> named the second, and for a row covering a full year the settled end-of-year extent is the more
> useful one.

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire / Turkiye for **2 years**, from the
Balkan Wars settlement to WWI entry
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

After the Balkan Wars (Treaty of London, May 1913; Treaty of Bucharest,
August 1913), the Ottoman Empire lost nearly all of its European
territory -- "83 percent of their European territory and almost 70
percent of their European population"
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1912-1913](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1912-1913).
CShapes shows `cowcode=640` area dropping dramatically at 1912-1913.
This row covers the brief period between the Balkan War settlements
and WWI entry (November 1914).

The CSV uses "Turkiye" naming and `TUR-` prefix, while the `OTT-*`
rows use "Ottoman Empire" naming. The wiki's existing
[log proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication](../log.md#proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication)
documents this overlap. The Republic of Turkey was not proclaimed until
29 October 1923 -- so this row uses `TUR` naming for what is
historically still the Ottoman Empire.

Predecessor: TUR-1800-1912 per CSV (the overlapping national row that
coexists with the `OTT-*` rows -- see
[log proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication](../log.md#proposal-tur-1800-1912-duplication)).
The [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md) page lists this row as a
successor.

Successor: [tur-1914-1918](tur-1914-1918.md).

## Contradictions

### Albania spatial containment (resolved)

CShapes `cowcode=640` has two rows that span this polity's years:

| CShapes row | Start | End | Area (km²) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Row A | 1913-01-01 | 1913-05-29 | ~1,941,758 |
| Row B | 1913-05-30 | 1913-12-31 | ~1,941,758 |
| Row C | 1914-01-03 | 1914-11-02 | ~1,785,218 |

The build script's "prefer exact start-year" logic was selecting Row A
(or B), which pre-dates the Treaty of London and spatially contains
Albania (~28,748 km²). CShapes first codes Albania (`cowcode=339`) as
independent from 1914-01-03. Using `polygon_feature_year=1914` selects
Row C, which correctly excludes Albanian territory. See decision
[dec-polygon-feature-year-1914](#dec-polygon-feature-year-1914).

### Cyprus residual overstatement (known approximation)

Row C (1914-01-03 to 1914-11-02) still contains Cyprus (~9,251 km²,
~0.5% of total area). CShapes does not code Cyprus as a separate British
territory until 1914-11-05, which falls after this polity's `end_year`
(1914). This residual overstatement is unavoidable within CShapes 2.0
and is documented here as an approximation.

### dec-polygon-feature-year-1914

**Decision:** use `polygon_feature_year=1914` (CShapes row 1914-01-03
to 1914-11-02, area ~1,785,218 km²) instead of the default start-year
1913.

**CShapes row timeline for cowcode=640 around this polity:**

| Row | Start | End | Area (km²) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| … | 1912-xx-xx | 1913-01-01 | ~3,400,000 | pre-Balkan Wars |
| A | 1913-01-01 | 1913-05-29 | ~1,941,758 | includes Albania + Cyprus |
| B | 1913-05-30 | 1913-12-31 | ~1,941,758 | includes Albania + Cyprus |
| **C** | **1914-01-03** | **1914-11-02** | **~1,785,218** | **Albania excluded; Cyprus still in** |
| D | 1914-11-03 | … | ~1,776,000 | Cyprus excluded (post end_year) |

**Reasoning:** The build script's "prefer exact start-year" heuristic
selects Row A, which pre-dates Albania's independence (Treaty of London,
30 May 1913, with CShapes registering the change on 1914-01-03 for
Albania `cowcode=339`). Row A spatially contains ~28,748 km² of Albanian
territory — a 1.6% overstatement that is a real territorial error, not
merely a vintage drift. Row C is the earliest CShapes row that correctly
excludes Albania. The area reduction from Row A to Row C (~156,540 km²)
is primarily attributable to Albania plus minor Aegean island adjustments
from the Balkan Wars peace settlement.

Cyprus (~9,251 km², ~0.5%) remains in Row C. Row D (excluding Cyprus)
starts 1914-11-03, after this polity's `end_year`. The Cyprus
overstatement is accepted as an unavoidable approximation and documented
in the Contradictions section.

### dec-turkey-1913-1914-alias

**Decision:** add year-ranged alias `turkey` / `turkiye` → `TUR-1913-1914` for years
1913–1914 in `applied_aliases.csv`.

**Basis:** The existing unbounded alias `Turkey → TUR-1920-2025` (confidence=high,
rows=323) routes all unqualified "turkey" labels to the post-1920 national polity.
Data years 1913 and 1914 labelled "turkey" or "turkiye" in any source measure the
Ottoman state in its post-Balkan-Wars configuration (Anatolia + Eastern Thrace + Arab
provinces, ~1,785,218 km²). Routing such rows to `TUR-1920-2025` would assign them to
a polygon covering the reduced post-WWI / post-Treaty-of-Lausanne Republic of Turkey
(~783,356 km²) — an understatement of ~2.3× for the correct territory.

The year-ranged alias takes priority over the unbounded alias for years 1913–1914.
No source ambiguity: any data point labelled "turkey" for 1913 or 1914 unambiguously
refers to this polity.

**Routing entry appended 2026-07-01:**
`turkey,,1913,1914,Türkiye (1913-1914),TUR-1913-1914,high,...`

## Open questions

### oq-oats-data-anomaly

Layer B data shows an approximately 4.4x jump in oats production for
Turkey between 1912 and 1913. This is anomalous: the Balkan Wars
settlement (Treaty of London, May 1913; Treaty of Bucharest, August
1913) substantially _reduced_ Ottoman/Turkish territory — losing most
European provinces including major grain-producing areas of Macedonia and
Thrace. A 4.4x increase in oats output in the very year of territorial
reduction is implausible on its face.

Possible explanations to investigate:
1. **Reporting artefact / unit change.** The data source may have
   switched reporting units, coverage area, or methodology between 1912
   and 1913.
2. **Polity routing error upstream.** Data labelled "Turkey 1913" in the
   source may include Anatolian + Arab provinces while "Turkey 1912"
   covered only the European footprint, producing an apparent surge that
   is really a coverage expansion.
3. **Different source for the two years.** Layer B often splices
   multiple source series; the splice point may fall at 1912/1913.

This anomaly should be investigated before oats data for TUR-1913-1914
is used in production. Flag: `oq-oats-data-anomaly`.

### oq-ott-tur-naming

This row uses `TUR` naming for what is historically still the Ottoman
Empire in 1913-1914. The Republic of Turkey was not proclaimed until
29 October 1923. The rename from `OTT` to `TUR` at 1913 is an
editorial choice by the CSV, not a COW or CShapes distinction (both
use code 640 throughout). This naming question is also discussed in
[oq-1912-1920-gap on ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md#oq-1912-1920-gap).
The choice should be recorded in a `decision`-kind log entry.
