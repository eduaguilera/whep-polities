# Labuan

## Summary

Labuan, part of the Straits Settlements from 1 January 1907 (a separate settlement from 1912) until it joined the North Borneo Crown Colony on 15 July 1946, until the Malayan Union of 1 April 1946 (the handover year belongs to the incoming row, MYS-1946-1957). [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §labuan](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#labuan)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report Labuan on its own; the label list and the harmonized dataset carry it as `british labuan`, `british straits settlements: labuan`. No row was the state: only its grouping and British Malaya (GBM-1895-1946) existed. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
