# Rhône

## Summary

Rhône is a French département in the southeast of the country, centred historically on the city of Lyon, created in 1790 as one of the original 83 Revolutionary départements out of the former province of Lyonnais together with parts of Beaujolais and Forez. It has been a continuous, ordinary administrative subdivision of France ever since -- never annexed, never a separate sovereign entity, never one of the departures (Alsace-Lorraine, Belfort, Corsica, overseas territories) that get bespoke treatment elsewhere in this table. `type: subnational` reflects exactly that: it is a piece of French administrative geography, always under French national sovereignty, entered here only because it is the level at which some statistical series report. This entry follows the dominant `<ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>` pattern (57 of 66 subnational rows use it) rather than a bespoke code, because Rhône is an ordinary NUTS/INSEE administrative unit with no distinct historical identity of its own -- unlike Alaska or Hyderabad, it was never a separately governed territory before becoming a département.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a French NUTS/administrative unit labelled FRK26 (admin_name), country France, proposed span 1790-2025 by the routing pass and revised here to 1800-2025 for containment reasons (see decisions). The routing record does not itself carry a row count in this excerpt, but confirms the unit is Rhône, an original 1790 Revolutionary département with no special-case history (not Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, or an overseas territory). Previously matched to: nothing -- the routing search returned zero name candidates in the existing polity vocabulary, so no prior mis-routing exists to correct; this is a first-time creation, not a fix. Confirmation of distinctness: Rhône is one of France's 96 metropolitan départements, each an administrative and statistical reporting unit distinct from the national total and from its neighbours; INSEE and Eurostat NUTS both tabulate at this level (NUTS-3 code FRK26), which is the concrete evidence that data is collected on this specific unit rather than only nationally.

**Why this entry exists:** it captures the Rhône unit (NUTS/INSEE admin-1 code FRK26, `admin_name: FRK26` in the source routing table) as a French départment-level reporting territory with no prior match in this database's vocabulary -- the routing pass found zero existing name candidates for it. **Territory a reader can locate:** the modern French département of Rhône, seat Lyon, in the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region of southeastern France, bordered by the Loire, Ain, Isère and Saône-et-Loire départements. Today's Rhône (excluding the separately governed Métropole de Lyon since 2015) covers roughly 2,700 km²; together with the Métropole de Lyon area it split from, the historical département that existed continuously from 1790 to 2015 covered roughly 3,250 km². No figure here is measured from an attached polygon -- none is attached yet -- these are published administrative-area figures for the modern and pre-2015 units respectively, offered so a reader can locate the territory pending the GADM fetch.

## Predecessors and successors

Rhône has no polity predecessor or successor in this table: it was created directly as a Revolutionary département in 1790 (administratively continuous ever since, aside from the later split-off of the Métropole de Lyon collectivity), and it remains a current département of France with no dissolution or absorption. ## Sourced claims

- Rhône was created as one of France's original départements by the National Constituent Assembly's law of 22 December 1789, effective 4 March 1790, grouping the former province of Lyonnais with parts of Beaujolais and Forez.
- The Métropole de Lyon became a full département-level territorial collectivity separate from Rhône on 1 January 2015 (following the loi de modernisation de l'action publique territoriale et d'affirmation des métropoles, 2014), narrowing the modern GADM-mapped Rhône relative to its 1790-2015 extent.
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 layer (GID_1 schema, already registered in scripts/sources.yaml) is the standard global source for French département boundaries, but the locally staged copy is a curated 81-country subset that omits France entirely, so no FRA feature can currently be cited.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Start year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790**

The routing decision proposed start_year 1790, reasoning that Rhône is an original Revolutionary département created that year. But the polity table's earliest FRA container row is FRA-1800-1871/FRA-1800-1919 (both start 1800) -- there is no French national polity entry covering 1790-1800 to contain a subnational edge in that decade. The containment gate rejects an edge falling outside either party's span, so a container edge for 1790-1800 cannot be constructed against the current table. Rather than leave a decade uncontained, this entry starts at 1800, aligned with the earliest available FRA container. The true administrative origin (1790, Revolutionary reorganization) is recorded in the summary and as an open question below, since a future FRA pre-1800 polity row would let this entry's start_year move back to 1790.

### d-container-choice-unsplit-row

**Contained by the unsplit FRA-1800-1919 row, not the Alsace-Lorraine split**

The table carries both an unsplit FRA-1800-1919 row and a split pair (FRA-1800-1871 with Alsace-Lorraine, FRA-1871-1919 without). Rhône, in south-eastern France near Lyon, is unaffected by the Alsace-Lorraine question that motivates the split, so the simpler unsplit row is used as the sole container for 1800-1919 rather than replicating the split with two near-identical edges that would carry no additional information for this territory.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1800-span

**Should this entry's start_year move to 1790 once an earlier FRA container exists?**

Rhône was created as one of France's original 83 Revolutionary départements by the law of 22 December 1789 (effective 4 March 1790), ten years before this entry's current start_year of 1800. The polity table has no French national row covering 1790-1800, so no container edge could be constructed for that decade under the current tiling rule. If a pre-1800 FRA polity row is ever added, this entry's start_year and code should be revisited to move back to 1790, matching the département's actual administrative origin rather than an artefact of table coverage.

### oq-polygon-fetch-and-lyon-split

**GADM adm1 France needs fetching, and the fetched feature will mismatch pre-1968 Rhône**

polygon_status is unassigned because the locally staged GADM 4.1 adm1 subset (scripts/sources.yaml) does not include France -- confirmed by a zero-feature check for FRA. Once fetched, the modern GID_1 feature for Rhône will reflect the post-1968/2015 boundary, after Lyon and its inner-suburb communes were split out to form the separate Métropole de Lyon collectivity. The historical département that existed continuously since 1790 was larger, including the city of Lyon itself. Whoever fetches the feature should flag this gap explicitly rather than treating the modern polygon as covering the full 1800-2025 span uniformly; a constructed union of modern Rhône + Métropole de Lyon GADM features may be the more faithful proxy for the pre-1968 period.

### oq-lyon-metropole-overlap

**Does a future Métropole de Lyon entry overlap this one after 1968/2015?**

The Métropole de Lyon became a département-level collectivity distinct from Rhône in stages (urban community from 1969, full département-level status from 2015). If a separate polity row for Métropole de Lyon is created later, this entry's post-1968 (or post-2015) territory and any attached polygon would need to exclude Lyon to avoid double-counting area and data between the two rows. No such row exists yet, so this is unresolved rather than actively conflicting.
