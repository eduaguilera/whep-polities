## Summary

Nauru is the world's smallest island nation, an 8,000-person republic on a single raised coral island in the central Pacific. WHEP tracks it from 1888, when Germany formally annexed the island, to 2025. Nauru subsequently came under Australian administration after WWI (1914), received a League of Nations mandate jointly administered by Australia, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom in 1920, and became independent on 31 January 1968. CShapes records one time-step beginning 1999-09-14 (COW entry, delayed for small Pacific states). COW ccode=970 (stateabb=NAU); state-system entry 1999-09-14.

## Open questions

### oq-1888-start-year

The WHEP start year 1888 reflects the German annexation date (November 1888). Before this date, Nauru was an independent Pacific island polity. No WHEP row covers the pre-1888 period. Whether a pre-colonial Nauru row is needed for coverage completeness should be assessed.

### oq-phosphate-economy

Nauru's economy was dominated by phosphate mining from the early twentieth century. WHEP's interest in territorial-economic units suggests that the phosphate-mining period (broadly 1906–1980) may deserve specific documentation for trade data purposes, but this is a data-source question rather than a territorial one.
