# United States (1848-1867)

## Summary

This row tracks the United States from the **Treaty of Guadalupe
Hidalgo (1848)** to the **Alaska Purchase (1867)** — a **20-year
row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This
period begins with the massive Mexican Cession (+1.4M km2:
California, Nevada, Utah, most of Arizona, New Mexico, parts of
Colorado and Wyoming), includes the Gadsden Purchase (1854), and
spans the entire Civil War (1861–1865). Territory reaches ~4.3M km2
pre-Alaska.

The end-of-row event is the **Alaska Purchase (1867)** from Russia,
adding ~1.7M km2 of territory. This is the same event documented
from the Russian side in
[f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) — "Russia sold Alaska to the
United States for $7.2 million"
[f228-1856-1905 §oq-alaska-sale-split](f228-1856-1905.md#oq-alaska-sale-split).

Predecessor: [usa-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md). Successor:
[usa-1867-1959](usa-1867-1959.md).

## Open questions

### oq-civil-war-cow-continuity

COW records no break for the United States during the Civil War
(1861–1865). The Confederacy is not a separate COW entity. Under
the WHEP rule, the Civil War is not a territorial-change split
event (Union territory was contested but not permanently ceded).
The CSV does not split here. Consistent, but worth documenting
for trade-data analysis: Civil War years may have anomalous trade
patterns.

### oq-gadsden-purchase-polygon

The Gadsden Purchase (1854, ~77,000 km2 from Mexico) is a minor
territorial acquisition within this row. The Cliopatria 1859–1860
polygon post-dates the purchase so it should include the Gadsden
territory. Not verified against Cliopatria geometry.
