# Marshall Islands


> **Polygon area declared 2026-08-06, and `polygon_status` lowered to `estimate` because it is one.**
>
> ```
> GADM 4.1 polygon as shipped   252.35 km²
> official land area            181 km²
> ratio                         1.39x
> ```
>
> `polygon_area_km2` now states the **official land area** rather than what the polygon measures.
> That is the field's purpose — it is the page's own figure, which `validate_polygons` check A
> compares the geometry against — and declaring the measured value instead would make the check
> self-referential and unable to detect anything.
>
> Because the divergence exceeds check A's 25% tolerance, `polygon_status` moves from `assigned` to
> **`estimate`**. That is not a workaround: `estimate` means the polygon approximates the territory,
> which is exactly the situation. Check A fails only for rows claiming `assigned`, and reports
> `estimate` rows as documented — so the disagreement stays visible in every run instead of being
> hidden behind a declared value chosen to agree with itself.
>
> **This is a class, not a defect in this row.** Comparing every micro-territory in the database
> against its official land area:
>
> ```
> TUV 1.39x   MHL 1.39x   TKL 1.33x   KIR 1.17x   NIU 1.01x   NRU 0.82x   MDV 0.58x
> ```
>
> The errors run **both directions**, from 42% under to 39% over, so this is not "GADM encloses
> atoll lagoons" — Maldives, the most lagoon-heavy of all, is 42% **short**. GADM's accuracy for
> territories of a few tens of km² is variable and unpredictable, and no single correction applies.
>
> **Any per-km² figure computed on this row is unusable.** At this magnitude the absolute
> uncertainty is comparable to the territory.
## Summary

The Marshall Islands is a Pacific island nation comprising 29 atolls and 5 islands in the central Pacific. WHEP tracks it from 1874 to 2025. The 1874 start year likely reflects early German commercial/administrative contact; Germany formally established a protectorate in 1885. The islands subsequently passed through German (1885–1914), Japanese mandate (1919–1944), and U.S. Trust Territory (1944–1986) administration before becoming a freely associated state in 1986 and entering the COW system in 1991 (UN membership). CShapes records one time-step beginning 1991-09-17 (UN membership/COW entry). COW ccode=983 (stateabb=MSI); state-system entry 1991-09-17.

## Open questions

### oq-1874-start-year

The WHEP start year 1874 is not explained in the CSV (notes=NA). Germany established a formal protectorate over the Marshall Islands in 1885, not 1874. The 1874 date may reflect early German trading-station presence or a data entry artifact. The rationale should be identified and documented.

### oq-pre-1991-polygon

CShapes records no Marshall Islands polygon before 1991. The polygon for 1874–1991 is back-projected and covers multiple administrative regimes (German, Japanese, American). Whether a single polygon is appropriate for this entire period, or whether separate polygons should be used for the different colonial phases, should be assessed.
