# Brunei Darussalam

## Summary

Brunei as tracked by WHEP from 1888, when it became a British protectorate. Brunei is situated on the island of Borneo, surrounded by the Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah. It gained full independence on 1 January 1984.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Brunei is bounded by Malaysia (Sarawak) on all land sides, with coastline on the South China Sea. The country is divided into two non-contiguous parts by the Limbang district of Sarawak.

## Sourced claims

- The Anglo-Dutch boundary agreements on Borneo separated the Dutch Indonesian regions of East and West Kalimantan from the Malaysian states of Sarawak and Sabah. [biger-1995 §brunei](../sources/biger-1995.md#brunei)
- Borneo resisted and repulsed occupation for over two centuries, with Brunei situated between the Sarawak and Sabah territories. [biger-1995 §brunei](../sources/biger-1995.md#brunei)
