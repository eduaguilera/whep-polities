# Ghana (1888-1898)

## Summary

The British Gold Coast colony in its intermediate configuration from 1 December 1888 to 13 June 1898, following the expansion of the coastal colony northward but before the larger consolidation with Ashanti and the Northern Territories from 14 June 1898. "The Ghanaians were the first people in sub-Saharan Africa to become independent from colonial rule when they gained independence from the British in 1957" [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). CShapes records area 10.46 sq deg for this period — more than double the preceding (1886–1888) phase at 5.58 sq deg, but less than the post-1898 phase at 17.43 sq deg. The 1888-12-01 start date marks a territorial expansion; the 1898-06-14 end date corresponds to the next expansion (coinciding with the Anglo-French boundary demarcation that fixed the northern boundaries of the Gold Coast). This is a WHEP-distinct row because both boundary events represent genuine territorial changes.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes records area 10.46 sq deg for 1888-12-01 to 1898-06-13, status=colony, owner=United Kingdom (COW 200). Ghana full area: 92,098 sq mi [238,533 sq km] [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). Bounded by Ivory Coast (415 mi; 668 km), Burkina Faso (341 mi; 549 km), and Togo (545 mi; 877 km) [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana). The 1888–1898 polygon (10.46 sq deg) covers the coastal colony plus the Protectorate of the Northern Territories declared in 1892, but not yet the Ashanti hinterland fully incorporated in the 1890s–1900s period.
