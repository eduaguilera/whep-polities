# Morelos

## Summary

Morelos is one of the 31 states of the Mexican federation, a small landlocked state in south-central Mexico immediately south of Mexico City, historically famous as the heartland of the Zapatista agrarian movement (1910-1920) and, in the 19th century, as a major sugar-producing region. It was carved out of the State of Mexico (Estado de México) by decree of Benito Juárez in April 1869, in the same wave of post-Restored-Republic territorial reorganization that also produced the state of Hidalgo. This entry is `type: subnational`: Mexico's sovereignty is carried by the national chain (MEX-1800-1848, MEX-1848-2025), and Morelos exists here purely as a sub-national reporting/administrative unit, so it must not compete with the national row in matcher ranking. The state has had no territorial changes of note since 1869 — unlike Baja California, Baja California Sur, and Quintana Roo, which were federal territories promoted to full statehood in the 20th century, Morelos was created directly as a state and its boundary has been stable for over 150 years.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry is created to route Mexican sub-national data keyed to the modern state of Morelos (INEGI code 17), which the routing decision found had zero existing polity or boundary candidates: no polity row previously represented Morelos specifically, and all Mexican sub-national statistics for this state had nowhere to go but the national MEX-1848-2025 row, which spans the entire country (roughly 1.96 million km2) versus Morelos's own ~4,900 km2 — a mismatch of about 400x. That national routing was wrong whenever a source publishes state-level breakdowns (e.g., agricultural production or population censuses tabulated by INEGI entidad federativa), because it silently attributes a state-sized figure to the whole country's territory. Morelos's distinctness as an administrative entity is confirmed by its own 1869 founding decree (separating it from the State of Mexico) and by its continuous, unbroken existence as one of Mexico's federal entities in every national census and INEGI administrative boundary release since. No production/trade row count is cited here because this page was generated by the polity-routing (name/boundary) stage rather than a specific data-ingest batch; the first rows to be routed to MEX-MOR-1869-2025 will be whichever INEGI-state-level series are matched to it downstream.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision identified `gadm-4.1-adm1` as the correct registered source family (GADM's global admin-1 layer, which does include Mexican states), but the specific file cached in this repository (`data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg`) is a curated 81-country subset that does not include Mexico, so no `polygon_feature_id` (e.g., the expected `MEX.17_1` for Morelos) can be assigned yet. `polygon_status` is therefore `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `assigned`: the source is registered and correctly identified, it simply has not been fetched for this country. No existing polygon in this repository (CShapes MEX-1848-2025, Cliopatria MEX-1800-1848) covers Morelos alone — both are national-level Mexico covers — so no proxy copy is possible or appropriate.

**Territory description:** Morelos is a small, landlocked Mexican state directly south of Mexico City and the State of Mexico, bordered also by Puebla and Guerrero. Its capital is Cuernavaca. On a modern map it sits entirely within the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt / Balsas depression, roughly 60-100 km south of central Mexico City. It is the second-smallest Mexican state by area, at approximately **4,879 km2** (INEGI's official figure) — a widely cited, independently sourced figure, not one measured from any polygon attached to this entry (since none is attached yet). Its boundaries have been essentially unchanged since the 1869 decree that created the state from municipalities previously belonging to the State of Mexico.
