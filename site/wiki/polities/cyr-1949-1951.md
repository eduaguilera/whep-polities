# Emirate of Cyrenaica

## Summary

This row tracks the **Emirate of Cyrenaica** from its proclamation on
**1 June 1949** — when the British administration granted Cyrenaica
self-government under Emir Muhammad Idris al-Senussi — to its
dissolution on **24 December 1951**, when Cyrenaica merged with
Tripolitania and Fezzan to form the **United Kingdom of Libya**.

The row exists because FAO/IIA yearbook footnotes (1953) report
Cyrenaica's agricultural data as **included in Libya** (the host
country). The footnote signals that Cyrenaica was a distinct
administrative entity whose data were separately enumerated before
being folded into the all-Libya total; WHEP therefore requires a
separate polity entry to correctly attribute this territory.

Cyrenaica is the eastern region of modern Libya, bounded roughly
by Egypt to the east, Sudan and Chad to the south, and Tripolitania
(Syrte basin) to the west. Under British military administration
from 1943, it was formally recognised as a state with internal
self-government in 1949, retaining the status of emirate under Idris.
At independence, Idris became King of Libya; Cyrenaica became one of
three federated provinces (Cyrenaica, Tripolitania, Fezzan) under
the 1951 constitution.

Predecessor: [lby-1943-1949](lby-1943-1949.md) (British Military
Administration of Libya). Successor:
[lby-1951-2025](lby-1951-2025.md) (Kingdom/Republic of Libya).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** No faithful-vintage polygon has been located
  for the Emirate of Cyrenaica as a distinct sub-Libyan territory.
  - **CShapes 2.0** (`gwcode=620`, "Libya", 1949-11-21 to
    1951-12-23, area ≈ 1,617,537 km²) covers all three Libyan
    provinces together (Tripolitania, Cyrenaica, Fezzan). The CShapes
    feature is not a faithful proxy because it represents a territory
    roughly 2–3 times larger than Cyrenaica alone.
  - **Cliopatria** has Cyrenaica entries only for the ancient/classical
    period (from c. 331 BCE to c. 110 BCE, area ≈ 30,000–116,000 km²).
    No modern (1949–1951) Cyrenaica entry exists in that dataset.
  - **GADM 4.1 adm1** does not contain Libya administrative units in
    the installed dataset (zero features returned for GID_0='LBY').
  - **Paine 2024** covers water bodies, not polities.
- **Method.** No polygon could be bound. `polygon_status: unassigned`
  is the honest and accepted WHEP outcome for this case.
- **Area estimate (contextual only, not bound).** Cyrenaica's
  historical land area is commonly cited at approximately 850,000–
  900,000 km² (roughly the eastern half of modern Libya). This figure
  is not used for `polygon_area_km2` because it lacks a source file
  feature to anchor it.
- **Confidence.** N/A (unassigned).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [lby-1943-1949](lby-1943-1949.md) — Libya under
  Allied military occupation (1943–1949). The British administered
  Cyrenaica and Tripolitania, while France administered Fezzan.
  The CYR row begins when Britain granted Cyrenaica self-government
  on 1 June 1949; the preceding period is covered by LBY-1943-1949.
- **Successor.** [lby-1951-2025](lby-1951-2025.md) — United Kingdom
  of Libya proclaimed 24 December 1951. Upon independence Cyrenaica
  became a federated province; its territory is subsumed under the
  unified Libyan polity from that date.

## Sourced claims

- **Emirate proclaimed 1 June 1949.** Following UNGA Resolution 289
  (IV) on 21 November 1949 (which decided Libya should become
  independent by 1 January 1952), Britain had already granted
  Cyrenaica a degree of self-government in June 1949. Emir Idris was
  recognised as head of state. [spec hint: "Emirate of Cyrenaica
  1949-1951, merged into Kingdom of Libya 1951"]
- **Merged into Kingdom of Libya 24 December 1951.** The United
  Kingdom of Libya was proclaimed on independence day, 24 December
  1951, uniting Cyrenaica, Tripolitania, and Fezzan under King Idris I.
  [cshapes-2.0 gwsdate for Libya 1951 row: 1951-12-24]
- **CShapes Libya 1949 feature.** CShapes records "Libya" (gwcode=620)
  from 1949-11-21 to 1951-12-23, area=1,617,537 km², capital=Tripoli.
  This reflects the broader Libyan territory under UN trusteeship
  preparation, not the Cyrenaica emirate alone.
  [cshapes-2.0 gwcode=620 gwsyear=1949]
- **FAO/IIA 1953 footnote.** The IIA 1953 yearbook footnote folds
  Cyrenaica into Libya (the host country), indicating its data were
  enumerated separately before 1951 and subsequently included in
  all-Libya aggregates. [spec: hosts="Libya", years="1953",
  action="composed_union"]

## Contradictions

- **CShapes covers all-Libya, not Cyrenaica.** The CShapes 1949-1951
  Libya polygon (≈1.6M km²) cannot be used as a proxy for the
  Emirate of Cyrenaica (≈850–900k km²) without overstating the
  territory by roughly double. This is the primary reason for the
  unassigned polygon status.
- **COW code.** CShapes assigns gwcode=620 to "Libya" throughout the
  1943–2019 window, with no separate COW code for Cyrenaica. The
  WHEP polity therefore inherits cow=620 as the closest available
  identifier, with the caveat that 620 represents all of Libya rather
  than Cyrenaica specifically.

## Decisions

- **Period 1949–1951.** The emirate's self-government began 1 June
  1949 and ended at Libyan independence on 24 December 1951.
  `start_year=1949`, `end_year=1951` captures this window.
- **polity_code CYR-1949-1951.** `CYR` is the natural three-letter
  abbreviation for Cyrenaica (no ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code exists for
  the historical emirate). No collision found in
  `/wiki/polities/` for any CYR-prefixed file.
- **iso3 LBY.** No separate ISO3 code exists for historical
  Cyrenaica; LBY is assigned as the modern sovereign successor state.
- **cow 620.** Inherited from CShapes Libya coding. Flagged as
  approximate (see Contradictions).
- **polygon_status unassigned.** No faithful polygon found after
  checking all four canonical sources (CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria,
  Paine 2024, GADM 4.1 adm1). This is the honest gap; it is an
  accepted WHEP pattern.
- **Predecessor LBY-1943-1949.** The British Military Administration
  row directly precedes the emirate and covers the same territory.
  The gap noted in LBY-1943-1949's Open questions (oq-lby-1949-1951-gap)
  is partially addressed by this CYR row; however that open question
  referred to a possible bridging row for unified Libya in 1949-1951,
  not Cyrenaica specifically.

## Open questions

### oq-cyr-polygon

A faithful polygon for the Emirate of Cyrenaica as a distinct
sub-national territory (eastern Libya, c. 850–900k km²) has not been
located in the four WHEP canonical sources. Potential future sources:
the HGIS of the Middle East and North Africa, the Barrington Atlas
of the Greek and Roman World (which records historical Cyrenaica),
or a custom construction from Italian colonial administrative maps.
If a source is found, `polygon_status` should be updated to
`assigned` and `polygon_source`/`polygon_feature_id`/`polygon_area_km2`
filled in.

### oq-cyr-self-government-date

The exact date on which Britain granted Cyrenaica self-government
(and Idris was formally recognised as Emir) is cited as "1 June 1949"
in secondary sources. The UNGA resolution (289 IV) is dated
21 November 1949. The relationship between these two dates — and
which should anchor `start_year` — should be verified against primary
administrative records. This row uses 1949 for `start_year` in both
cases.

### oq-cyr-lby-1943-1949-gap

The existing LBY-1943-1949 page (Open question oq-lby-1949-1951-gap)
notes a gap between LBY-1943-1949 (end 1949) and LBY-1951-2025
(start 1951). This CYR-1949-1951 row covers Cyrenaica specifically;
the Tripolitanian and Fezzanese counterparts for 1949-1951 (if
needed) remain uncreated. Unified Libya 1949-1951 may warrant
a separate LBY bridging row for Tripolitania and Fezzan data.
