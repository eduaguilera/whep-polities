# Guinea-Bissau

## Summary

Guinea-Bissau is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Portuguese Guinea / Guinea-Bissau from 1879 to the present. The start year 1879 reflects the establishment of Portuguese Guinea as a separate colonial administration. Guinea-Bissau declared independence on 24 September 1973 and was recognized by Portugal after the 1974 Carnation Revolution.

## Territorial extent

Guinea-Bissau is bounded by Senegal in the north and Guinea in the south and east. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
