# Indonesia (1949-1963)

## Summary

Independent Indonesia from the December 1949 transfer of sovereignty to the
**1963-05-01** hand-over of West New Guinea from UNTEA to Indonesian
administration. `end_year` is exclusive, so this row covers **1949-1962**: the
years in which Indonesia's territory was the Dutch East Indies *minus* western
New Guinea, which the Netherlands retained
([nng-1949-1963](nng-1949-1963.md)) [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
COW code 850 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Konfrontasi (the Indonesia-Malaysia confrontation, 1963-1966) falls in the
successor row and transferred no territory in either source.

## Territorial extent

The row's polygon is bound to `cshapes-2.0` feature `850` (Indonesia's
Gleditsch-Ward code), `polygon_feature_year: 1949`. In the underlying CShapes 2.0
shapefile (`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`, measured directly,
ESRI:54034 equal-area CRS — not a wiki-source citation), gwcode 850 has seven
time-steps; the one bound here runs `1949-12-29 → 1969-09-16` and measures
**1,466,882 km²**, matching the committed
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` geometry for this row (**1,466,396.5 km²**;
the ~0.03% difference is topology simplification, not a different feature).

The step immediately before (`1945-08-17 → 1949-12-28`) and the step immediately
after (`1969-09-17 → 1976-07-16`) both measure **1,877,243 km²** — 410,361 km²
larger, which is exactly western New Guinea, carried by CShapes as its own
feature (gwcode 851) for 1949-1969
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

**The row's span is shorter than the polygon step's.** That is deliberate and is
the whole point of this row's re-dating: the polygon is constant 1949-1969 in the
source because CShapes stops carrying feature 851 separately only at the Act of
Free Choice, while the territory itself passed to Indonesia on 1963-05-01 — a date
CShapes' own change log for feature 851 records (see
[nng-1949-1963](nng-1949-1963.md#the-1963-vs-1969-question-settled-2026-08-14) for
the measurement). Under
[log decision-row-boundary-dating](../log.md#decision-row-boundary-dating), the row
boundary follows the territorial fact and the polygon binding records the source's
own vintage; the two fields are allowed to disagree, and here they do.

## Sourced claims

- Indonesian independence "was finally granted in 1949"
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia).
- "Indonesia has expanded to include western Iran [sic, Irian], formerly western
  New Guinea (1963)" [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) —
  Biger's "western Iran" is a transcription artifact for "western Irian". The same
  entry misdates the East Timor annexation as "1955" for 1975, so date typos in it
  are a known pattern; 1963, however, is corroborated to the day by CShapes' own
  time-step for feature 851 (`1963-05-01`), so it is not one of them.
- CShapes 2.0 codes territorial change as one of three types
  (creation/dissolution, sovereignty transfer over dependent territory, or border
  adjustment ≥ 100×100 km) and dates each change to the postwar/post-agreement date
  where a change was made permanent by treaty
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). This is
  why feature 850's own enlargement is stamped `1969-09-17` (the Act of Free
  Choice) even though feature 851's change log carries `1963-05-01`.
- Indonesia (COW state abbreviation `INS`, ccode 850) entered the COW state system
  on **1949-12-27**, two days before this row's start year, with a single unbroken
  tenure through the end of the COW v2024 series (direct read of
  `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, row `INS,850`; no COW-coded gap
  during the 1965-66 regime change, consistent with WHEP's polity-definition rule
  that regime change without territorial change is not a new row
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)).
  This row is not covered in the `key-dates` prose of
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates),
  so the date is cited as a direct data reference.

## Open questions

### oq-faostat-indonesia-territorial-basis

Does FAOSTAT's `Indonesia` (area 101) series report a **constant modern
territory** across 1961-2024, or the territory actually administered in each year?
If constant, then even 1961-1962 — the years this row now covers — carry
West-New-Guinea-inclusive figures on a West-New-Guinea-exclusive polygon, and the
residual mismatch is a reporting-basis question for `territory_basis`, not a row
boundary. Re-dating the boundary to 1963 removes six of those eight years either
way, which is why it was not blocked on this question. No FAOSTAT land-area series
is committed to this repo, so it could not be measured here.

## Status corrected to `draft` (2026-07-24)

This page was flagged **`wiki_status: reviewed`** while containing **no source
citations at all** — its territorial content was a stub. The `reviewed` label is
supposed to mean a human checked the claims against sources, so carrying it here
was misleading: it invited both readers and the verification pipeline to treat
unsourced assertions as settled.

An audit found **10 of 72 `reviewed` pages** in this state — the whole IND chain,
the whole IDN chain, and CHN-1914-1921. All ten are downgraded to `draft`, which
is what they actually are. Nothing about the polity's data or polygon changed;
only the honesty of the label.

To earn `reviewed` this page needs its **Territorial extent** and **Sourced
claims** sections filled from real sources (Biger 1995, CShapes 2.0, the COW
state-system list) with inline citations, and the specific territorial changes
inside its span identified. Until then the assertion-verification pipeline treats
it as an unverified draft and corroborates its claims independently — see
[log decision-reviewed-status-audit](../log.md#decision-reviewed-status-audit).
