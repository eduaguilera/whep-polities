# Zimbabwe (1900-1953)

## Summary

This WHEP polity row covers the territory of Southern Rhodesia under British colonial
administration from 1900 to 1953. The 1900 start date corresponds to the CShapes
territorial change when Northern Rhodesia was separated from the BSAC administrative
unit, leaving a smaller polygon (area≈33.46 sq.deg.) representing Southern Rhodesia
alone. In 1923 Southern Rhodesia became a self-governing colony under British oversight.
The 1953 end date marks the creation of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland
([frn-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md)), into which Southern Rhodesia was absorbed.
CShapes records this row as cowcode=552, status=colony, owner=200 throughout.

## Territorial extent

Southern Rhodesia — modern Zimbabwe — is a landlocked country (area: 150,873 sq. miles
[390,610 sq. km.]) bounded by Zambia to the north (Zambezi River), Mozambique to the
east, South Africa to the south, and Botswana to the west. Capital: Salisbury (now
Harare). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=552, status=colony, owner=200,
start=1900-01-29, end=1953-07-31, area≈33.46 sq.deg.).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [zwe-1891-1900](zwe-1891-1900.md) — BSAC-administered Rhodesia
  (Southern + Northern) before the formal separation.
- **Successor:** [frn-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md) — Federation of Rhodesia and
  Nyasaland (1953–1964), which absorbed Southern Rhodesia.

## Sourced claims

- "In 1923 Southern Rhodesia became a white-dominated, self-governing colony." (p.520).
  This is the key constitutional change during this WHEP row, though CShapes does not
  record a territorial change at 1923 (area is the same throughout the 1900–1953 period),
  consistent with the WHEP rule that regime changes without territorial change do not
  create new rows. [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)
- "After 1896, Northern Rhodesia was constituted as a colonial protectorate which status
  it retained when the British South Africa Company lost its charter in 1923. In 1964,
  Northern Rhodesia became an independent republic as Zambia." (p.395). The separation
  of Northern Rhodesia by 1900 is confirmed by this lineage. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- Zimbabwe area: 150,873 sq. miles [390,610 sq. km.], bounded by Zambia (495 miles;
  796 km.), Mozambique (760 miles; 1,223 km.), South Africa (140 miles; 225 km.),
  Botswana (505 miles; 813 km.). Capital: Harare. [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=552, status=colony, owner=200 for 1900-01-29 to
  1953-07-31, area≈33.46 sq.deg. No intermediate boundary change is recorded during
  this 53-year period, confirming a single WHEP row is appropriate.
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)
