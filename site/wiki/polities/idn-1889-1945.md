# Indonesia (1889-1945)

## Summary

CSV row for Indonesia from **1889** to **1945** -- a **57-year row**
covering the Dutch East Indies from the Anglo-Dutch boundary treaty
(1889) through Japanese occupation to the declaration of Indonesian
independence (17 August 1945).
COW code 750 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
-- same cow=750 sharing issue as
[idn-1800-1889](idn-1800-1889.md#oq-cow-750-shared).

CShapes splits at 1945-08-17 (independence declaration) with no area
change (152.87 sq deg both before and after). The end year was
corrected from 1949 to 1945 to eliminate the overlap with successor
IDN-1945-1949 and reflect the sovereignty transition at independence.

## Territorial extent

CShapes 2.0 polygon, area ~152.87 sq deg throughout (no territorial
change during this period). The 1889 start aligns with the
Anglo-Dutch treaty of 20 June 1889 that finalized boundaries on
Borneo between Dutch and British possessions.

- **Successor:** [idn-1945-1949](idn-1945-1949.md) -- Indonesian
