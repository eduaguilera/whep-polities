# Uganda (1926-1962)

## Summary

Uganda (1926-1962) covers the British Protectorate of Uganda from 27 September 1926 — when CShapes records a boundary change reducing the protectorate — to 8 October 1962, the day before independence. CShapes records this as status=protectorate, owner=GBR (COW 200). The area (~19.68 CShapes units) is smaller than the preceding UGA-1910-1926 period (~22.97), reflecting the 1926 boundary adjustment that transferred the Rudolf Province area and other territories. The Biger source confirms Uganda became independent, though gives the date as "8 September 1967" (an apparent error — independence was 9 October 1962).

## Territorial extent

East African interior territory bounded by Sudan (north), Kenya (east), Tanzania (south), Rwanda (south-west), Congo/Zaire (west), and with Lake Victoria at the southern boundary. CShapes 2.0 area ~19.68 (from 1926-09-27 to 1962-10-08). This is the territory that became independent Uganda. Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). ## Sourced claims

- "In 1894 the British government was persuaded to make Uganda a protectorate. Uganda became independent on 8 September 1967." (p.490) Note: Biger gives an incorrect independence date; the actual date of independence was 9 October 1962. See Contradictions. [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda)
- "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894." (p.490) The protectorate structure documented here continued through this period until independence. [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=500, status=protectorate, owner=200 (UK), from 1926-09-27 to 1962-10-08, area ~19.68. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Contradictions

Biger (p.490) states "Uganda became independent on 8 September 1967." Both the date and year are wrong. Uganda achieved independence on 9 October 1962. The "1967" may be a typographical error for 1962, and "8 September" for 9 October. The WHEP database follows CShapes and historical record: independence 9 October 1962 (CShapes records independent status from 1962-10-09).

## Open questions

### oq-uga-1926-boundary-change

CShapes records an area reduction at 1926-09-27 (~22.97 to ~19.68). The specific boundary adjustment — which territory was transferred and to whom — has not been identified from the available sources. The Sudan-Uganda and Kenya-Uganda boundary histories should be consulted to identify the 1926 transfer.

### oq-uga-biger-independence-date

Biger (p.490) gives Uganda's independence date as "8 September 1967" — both date and year appear incorrect (actual: 9 October 1962). This error in Biger should be documented to prevent future incorrect citations from this source.
