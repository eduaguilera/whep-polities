# Indonesia (1945-1949)

## Summary

CSV row for Indonesia from **1945** to **1949** — a **5-year row**
covering the Indonesian independence struggle
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 850
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Independence declared **17 August 1945**. Dutch-Indonesian
conflict (1945-1949). Transfer of sovereignty **27 December 1949**.

## Territorial extent

The bound polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature `850` at the `1945` time-step
(`polygon_feature_year: 1945`). The CSV leaves `polygon_area_km2`
blank for this row; re-measuring the committed geometry in
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034)
gives **1,876,795 km²** — within rounding of the 1,877,243 km² recorded
on the neighbouring rows (`IDN-1800-1889` at the 1886 time-step,
`IDN-1889-1945` at 1900). CShapes assigns essentially the same polygon
across the 1886/1900/1945/1949 time-steps of feature 850, which by the
dataset's own logic means it registered no qualifying border change
across this whole span: "for countries with no changes in the period,
we simply backdated their borders to 1886"
[cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding), subject
to the 100×100 km size floor on what counts as a change at all
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

That static polygon is anachronistic for this row in one specific,
checkable way: it already includes West New Guinea. Testing the
committed geometry directly against known West New Guinea coordinates
(Jayapura 140.7°E/2.5°S, Manokwari 134.06°E/0.86°S, Biak
136.08°E/1.17°S) puts all three inside the bound polygon. But West
New Guinea was **not** part of Indonesia during 1945-1949 — the
Netherlands retained it as a separate colonial territory after the
1949 transfer of sovereignty and only ceded it in 1963:
"Indonesia has expanded to include western Iran [sic — almost
certainly a transcription artifact for "Irian", i.e. Irian
Jaya/West New Guinea], formerly western New Guinea (1963)"
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). This is
logged as a contradiction below rather than silently corrected, since
fixing the polygon (or adding a West-New-Guinea carve-out) is a
polygon-source decision for a human, not a wiki-page edit.

Inside the 1945-1949 window itself, actual control of the claimed
territory fluctuated sharply — the Republic held only parts of Java
and Sumatra for much of the period, against Dutch-administered areas
and federal states after the two Dutch "Police Actions" (1947, 1948)
and the Renville Agreement's Van Mook Line (1948) — but no ingested
source documents this internal military geography, so it is recorded
here as uncited general history, not as a sourced claim, per the
wiki's citation rule.

COW does not recognize Indonesia as a state-system member at any
point during this row's span. The raw COW membership file backing
[cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
records a single Indonesia (`INS`, `ccode=850`) tenure beginning
**1949-12-27** — the Round Table Conference transfer-of-sovereignty
date, not the 17 August 1945 declaration this row starts from. That
gap is exactly the pattern the source describes for COW's
recognition-based criteria
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states):
COW requires diplomatic recognition by a major power (pre-1920) or
League/UN membership (post-1920) plus a population floor, and does
not treat an unrecognized revolutionary government as a state
regardless of territorial control. Per the WHEP polity-definition
rule, this divergence between the WHEP start year (1945) and the COW
entry date (1949) is expected, not a contradiction to resolve —
recorded here as a sourced claim instead.

## Sourced claims

- Independence "was finally granted in 1949"
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) (p.295)
  — Biger's own periodization treats 1949, not 1945, as the moment
  Indonesian sovereignty became real, consistent with the COW entry
  date below and with why this row's *end* year (1949) is the more
  externally corroborated of its two boundary years.
- COW lists Indonesia (`ccode=850`) as entering the state system on
  **1949-12-27**, not 1945
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
  (read directly from the underlying `statelist2024.csv`, the same
  file that section's other examples are drawn from). No tenure row
  exists for Indonesia before that date.
- The bound polygon (CShapes feature 850, 1945 time-step) is
  unchanged in area from the 1886 and 1900 time-steps used by the
  predecessor rows
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes),
  i.e. CShapes records no qualifying territorial change across the
  whole pre-1949 span — but see the caveat on backdated placeholder
  polygons in *Territorial extent* above.
- The 100×100 km floor on what CShapes codes as a border adjustment
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)
  means this "no change" reading cannot rule out smaller adjustments
  during the Dutch-Indonesian conflict (e.g. the Van Mook Line was a
  military demarcation, not a redrawn international boundary, so it
  would not register regardless of size).

## Contradictions

- **CShapes vs Biger 1995 on West New Guinea.** The bound CShapes
  polygon for this row (feature 850, 1945 time-step) already contains
  West New Guinea (verified directly against the committed geometry —
  see *Territorial extent*). Biger 1995 places West New Guinea's
  transfer to Indonesia in 1963, sixteen years after this row starts
  and fourteen years after it ends
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). The
  database currently follows the CShapes polygon (i.e. treats West
  New Guinea as Indonesian territory from 1945), which is the
  Biger-contradicting position. Not resolved here — flagged for human
  review, since correcting it means either sourcing a West New Guinea
  carve-out polygon for `IDN-1945-1949`/`IDN-1949-1963` or accepting
  the CShapes placeholder as a known approximation.

## Open questions

### oq-west-new-guinea-polygon

The bound polygon for this row (and, on the same static feature 850,
its predecessor and its `IDN-1949-1963` successor) geometrically
includes West New Guinea, but per Biger 1995
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) West New
Guinea was not Indonesian territory until 1963. Logged as a
contradiction above; not split here because the discrepancy is a
polygon-accuracy issue on an already-static CShapes placeholder
(same area 1886→1949), not a border movement that occurred inside
this row's own 1945-1949 span. Worth a follow-up ingest that checks
whether `IDN-1949-1963` should carry a West-New-Guinea-excluded
polygon (`polygon_status: proxy`) until the actual 1963 transfer.
