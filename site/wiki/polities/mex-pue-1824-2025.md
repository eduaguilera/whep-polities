# Puebla

## Summary

Puebla is one of the 31 states of the United Mexican States, located in east-central Mexico between the national capital and the Gulf coast, bordering Mexico State, Tlaxcala, Hidalgo, Veracruz, Oaxaca, Guerrero, and Morelos. Its capital, also named Puebla (formally Heroica Puebla de Zaragoza), is Mexico's fourth-largest city. This entry is `type: subnational` because Puebla has never been a sovereign polity in its own right — it is, and has continuously been since 1824, a first-order administrative division of the Mexican federation — and the existing MEX rows in this database (MEX-1800-1848, MEX-1848-2025) model the national sovereign chain, not any individual state. The entry was created because production and trade data specific to Puebla (spanning 1900-2023) had no home other than the national MEX rows, which is wrong for a sub-national reporting unit: a state's agricultural or industrial output series is not evidence about all of Mexico, and folding it into the national total silently double-counts or misattributes it. Puebla was admitted to the federation in 1824 under the original Constitution as one of the 19 founding states — it is explicitly not one of the three states that were promoted from federal territories later (Baja California, Baja California Sur, Quintana Roo), so the default admission-year start rule applies cleanly rather than needing a bespoke justification. The state persists to the present day, so end_year is the open 2025 bound used elsewhere in this table for still-current polities.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: production/trade series for Mexico's Puebla state, spanning years 1900-2023 (per the routing decision this page implements), previously present in the pipeline's intake but with no dedicated polity row to attach to. Prior (mis)matching: this data had nowhere to go but the national MEX rows (MEX-1800-1848 / MEX-1848-2025), which is wrong because a state is not its country — routing sub-national Puebla-specific figures to the national total either inflates the national series if double-counted alongside other sources, or silently misattributes a regional economy's output as if it were nationwide. No existing polity in this table matches Puebla by name, and Mexico currently has zero subnational rows, so there was no near-miss to correct, only a genuine gap. Confirmation of distinctness: Puebla is one of the 19 original states enumerated in the 1824 Constitution of the United Mexican States and has functioned as a distinct first-order administrative and statistical reporting unit continuously since then — the same status that justifies subnational entries for other countries' constituent states elsewhere in this table (e.g. the ESP province codes, the ARG province codes, the COL department codes).

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. Neither of the two sources already used for MEX in this repository — cshapes-2.0 and cliopatria — digitizes anything below country level, so both are structurally incapable of supplying a Puebla boundary. gadm-4.1-adm1, which does carry first-order administrative boundaries in general, is present in this repository only as a curated subset of 81 countries that does not include Mexico (0 Mexican features), so it cannot be cited as the source for this feature even though the broader GADM product does cover Mexico. Fetching and registering either the global (non-curated) GADM adm1 layer or INEGI's own Marco Geoestadístico Nacional would be the correct next step, and that gap is recorded as an open question rather than papered over with a placeholder source string. Territory description: Puebla state covers approximately 34,290 km² in east-central Mexico (a widely cited modern INEGI figure for the state, not a measurement from any geometry attached to this entry, since none is attached). It is a landlocked, mountainous state anchored by the Valley of Puebla, straddling the eastern flank of the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt (including the Pico de Orizaba massif on its eastern border with Veracruz) and the northern end of the Sierra Madre del Sur. On a modern map it sits directly southeast of Mexico City and Mexico State, wrapping around Tlaxcala to its north. The state's outer boundary has shifted modestly since 1824 — most notably cessions to Tlaxcala and to Veracruz during the 19th century — so a present-day boundary, once a source is registered, would need to be flagged as an approximation for the earlier portion of this span rather than treated as exact back to 1824.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: Puebla was created directly by the 1824 Constitution as one of the original 19 founding states of the federation, with no prior distinct polity in this database standing in for the same territory under Spanish colonial administration (New Spain's intendancy of Puebla is not separately modeled here). No successor: Puebla remains a current constituent state of the United Mexican States, so end_year 2025 is the open/present bound rather than a transition to another entity, matching the treatment of other still-current subnational rows in this table.

## Sourced claims

- Puebla was one of the 19 original states named in the Acta Constitutiva de la Federación and the 1824 Constitution of the United Mexican States, alongside states such as Jalisco, Oaxaca and Veracruz.
- Puebla is not among the three states that entered the federation later as reclassified former territories (Baja California and Baja California Sur becoming states in 1952, Quintana Roo in 1974), which is why the default 1824 admission-year rule applies rather than a later start_year.
- Mitchell-style historical statistical compilations and modern INEGI state-level economic data both report Puebla state totals (e.g. agricultural production) as distinct from national Mexico totals across the 1900-2023 window that motivated this entry's creation.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Followed the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern**

Puebla is an ordinary current Mexican state with no bespoke historical name of its own (unlike ALK or HYD), so the dominant 57-of-66 pattern applies: MEX-PUE-1824-2025. No departure from precedent is needed here; the SUBUNIT token PUE is chosen as a short, unambiguous abbreviation of Puebla, distinct from any other Mexican state code already in use (none exist yet, since MEX has zero prior subnational rows).

## Open questions

### oq-adm1-source

**No registered polygon source carries Mexican state boundaries**

gadm-4.1-adm1 is present locally only as a curated 81-country subset that excludes Mexico entirely (0 features for MEX), and the two sources already used for MEX in this repository (cshapes-2.0, cliopatria) are both country-level, not state-level. A correct polygon for Puebla would come from either the global (non-curated) GADM adm1 layer or from INEGI's Marco Geoestadístico Nacional, the official Mexican state-boundary source. Until one of those is fetched and registered, this entry carries polygon_status: unassigned and no geometry. Licence terms for both candidates are still unchecked.

### oq-boundary-history

**Puebla's boundary is not perfectly stable across 1824-2025**

Puebla ceded territory to Tlaxcala and to Veracruz over the 19th century (most notably in border adjustments during the Reforma and Porfiriato periods), so a present-day INEGI or GADM boundary, once fetched, would be a stated approximation for the earlier decades of this span rather than an exact match. The magnitude of that discrepancy has not been quantified here and should be checked once a polygon is assigned, since it affects how far back the polygon can be treated as a valid proxy versus needing a documented caveat.
