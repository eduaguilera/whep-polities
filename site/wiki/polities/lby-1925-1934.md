# Libya (1925-1934)

## Summary

Italian Libya during the 1925–1934 CShapes window — the late-stage Italian "pacification" campaign in Cyrenaica (1923–1932, culminating in the defeat of Omar Mukhtar's resistance in 1931) and the period just before the 1934 merger of Tripolitania and Cyrenaica into unified "Italian Libya". CShapes status="colony", ruler="ITA".

### the iia "libya" label is flagged MIXED, and its item coverage stops early

Recorded 2026-08-18 while clearing the wiki notes queue. This analysis covers all
three Libya rows (`LBY-1925-1934`, `LBY-1934-1943`, `LBY-1943-1949`), which share
one label and one problem.

**What the provenance table actually says.** `state/iia_label_provenance.csv`
carries three Libya labels, and all three read `territory_signal = mixed`:

| raw label | kind | territory_signal |
|---|---|---|
| `libya` | `whole_with_sub_siblings` | **mixed** |
| `libya: cyrenaica` | `sub_of_sibling` | **mixed** |
| `libya: tripolitania` | `sub_of_sibling` | **mixed** |

`mixed` is one of the three signals `scripts/validate_iia_label_provenance.py`
treats as failing. **Queued notes cited `mixing_observed = no` as corroboration
that the label is a clean all-Libya aggregate.** That column reads `no`, but it is
the weaker of the two measures and the stronger one disagrees: layer B's single
`libya` label (122 rows, 1922-1945) draws on a whole and its two provinces without
a recoverable split. Citing the column that supports the conclusion while the
other says otherwise is the error worth recording here.

**What the item coverage limits.** Layer B's `libya` label carries:

| item | n | span |
|---|---|---|
| oil, olive, virgin | 21 | 1922-1945 |
| olives | 24 | 1924-1939 |
| grapes | 20 | **1930-1937** |
| tobacco, unmanufactured | 18 | **1930-1937** |
| wine | 19 | **1930-1937** |

So for **1940 onward, only olive oil exists.** Any claim about how the reporting
basis behaved after 1939 rests on that one series, and it is noisy: 4,025 (1922),
602 (1930), **0 (1935)**, 4,000 (1939), 1,200 (1942), 2,700 (1945). A series
ranging from zero to 4,100 cannot demonstrate continuity across a boundary — the
same limitation recorded for the New South Wales wheat series at the 1859
