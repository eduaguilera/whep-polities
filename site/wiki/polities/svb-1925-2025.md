# Svalbard

## Summary

The Svalbard archipelago (Spitsbergen), under Norwegian sovereignty by the Svalbard Treaty. The
treaty "was signed on 9 February 1920" and "came into force on 14 August 1925", and in 1925 "the
Norwegian authorities officially renamed the islands 'Svalbard'".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §svalbard](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#svalbard)

The row starts in 1925, when the treaty came into force. Before that the archipelago was terra
nullius and no source in layer B reports it. `iso3` is `NA`: the ISO code `SJM` covers Svalbard and Jan
Mayen together, and Jan Mayen has its own row ([jmy-1930-2025](jmy-1930-2025.md)), so neither row
claims it. FAOSTAT's area 260 (Svalbard and Jan Mayen Islands) stays listed as having no polity.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `Norway Svalbard` separately from Norway: a 1949 land-use table whose `use total`
is 6,211 thousand ha, i.e. **62,110 km²**. It also reports a 1937 population row, `Norway Svalbard
and Jan Mayen Land` (1 thousand people). **3 rows** resolved to no polity. Svalbard lies **outside**
the polygon of [nor-1800-2025](nor-1800-2025.md), which is mainland Norway (CShapes 385); before this
row Longyearbyen fell only in the reporting-area aggregates `REUR-1850-2025` and `ROW-1850-2025`.

The combined Svalbard-and-Jan-Mayen population row is routed here too. Jan Mayen is 0.7% of the
combined land (400 of 60,343 km² in GADM) and had only a weather station, so the combined unit is
this territory to within that. The alternative, an aggregate row for one population cell, was not
created.

## Territorial extent

(ESRI:54034), against FAO's 62,110 (0.97). The difference is glacier-front and coastline convention.

## Decisions

- **Svalbard and Jan Mayen are two rows**, because FAO reports each on its own (`Norway Jan Mayen`,
  1948, is 370 km²): routing both to one combined polygon would put the Jan Mayen land-use table on
  a territory 160 times its size.
