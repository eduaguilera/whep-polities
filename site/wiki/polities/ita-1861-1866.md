# Italy (1861–1866)

## Summary

This sub-period covers the Kingdom of Italy from its proclamation on
**17 March 1861** to the acquisition of Venetia at the **Vienna Peace
Treaty of 3 October 1866**. It was split from the former single row
`ITA-1861-1919` to correct a polygon-vintage overstatement: the CShapes
1886 polygon includes both Venetia (~18,000 km²) and Rome/Lazio
(~12,000 km²), neither of which was Italian territory during 1861–1866.

Predecessors: [sar-1800-1860](sar-1800-1860.md), [pap-1800-1870](pap-1800-1870.md),
TWO-1800-1860, TUS-1800-1860, DMO-1800-1860, DPA-1800-1860.
Successor: [ita-1866-1870](ita-1866-1870.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes-Europe, `cowcode=325`, polygon vintage 1886.
  This is an **ESTIMATE**: the 1886 polygon represents post-1870 Italy
  (Venetia + Rome included; Trentino/Trieste excluded). Applied to
  1861–1866, it **overstates** true Italian territory by approximately
  **30,000 km² (~12%)**: ~18,000 km² for Venetia (Austrian, 1861–1866)
  plus ~12,000 km² for Rome/Lazio (Papal, 1861–1866).
- **True 1861 extent.** At proclamation, Italy had 250,320 km²
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §area](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#area).
  Regions included: Piedmont, Lombardy, Liguria, Tuscany, most of the
  Papal States (except Rome/Lazio), Emilia-Romagna, Sicily, Sardinia, Umbria.
- **What is excluded.** Venetia (Austrian Lombardo-Venetia, ceded 1866);
  Rome and Lazio (Papal temporal sovereignty until 1870).
- **Polygon fix needed.** The proper polygon for this sub-period is a
  GIS-constructed feature subtracting Venetia + Rome/Lazio from the 1886
  CShapes feature. No such polygon exists in CShapes 2.0 or CShapes-Europe
  (Italy has a single backdated polygon). See
  [oq-gis-constructed-polygon](#oq-gis-constructed-polygon).

## Sourced claims

- **1861-03-17: Kingdom of Italy proclaimed.** Victor Emmanuel II of Sardinia
  declared King of Italy. Population: 21,777,334; area: 250,320 km²
  [wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12 §1861-unification](../sources/wikipedia-italy-2026-04-12.md#1861-unification).
- **1866: Third Italian War of Independence.** Italy allied with Prussia against
  Austria; gained Venetia by the Vienna Peace Treaty of 3 October 1866
  [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria). This event ends this sub-period.

## Decisions

- **Split from ITA-1861-1919 (2026-06-29).** The Venetia (1866) and Rome
  (1870) acquisitions are genuine border-change events within the former
  single row. The 1886 CShapes polygon is an ESTIMATE for 1861–1866,
  overstating territory by ~30,000 km² (~12%). Documented here rather than
  silently applied.
- **Polygon status: estimate.** No alternative pre-1866 polygon exists.
  The approximation is flagged and its direction/magnitude documented.

## Open questions

### oq-gis-constructed-polygon

A GIS-constructed polygon for 1861–1866 Italy could be derived by
subtracting the Venetia and Rome/Lazio features from the CShapes 1886
polygon. Source candidates: Austrian Lombardo-Venetia from CShapes or
CHGIS; Lazio from Cliopatria or a Papal States boundary dataset. This
would reduce overstatement from ~12% to near zero. Tier B — low priority
(short 5-year sub-period), but relevant for any production data matched
to this sub-period.
