# Meta (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Meta is a department of Colombia in the eastern Llanos plains, with its capital at Villavicencio roughly 90 km southeast of Bogota. Prior to 1959 it existed as the Intendencia Nacional del Meta, a national territory administered directly by the central government rather than a self-governing department. Law 118 of 1959 elevated it to full department status, giving it its own governor and departmental assembly; this entry's span therefore begins in 1959 rather than at the earliest year for which production data exists (1915), because the harness's default rule ties a subnational entry's start to the unit's formal constitution as the administrative type it claims to be (`type: subnational`, specifically a department), not to when statistical reporting happens to begin. Meta remains a current department, so `end_year` uses the table's standard open-end convention of 2025. Over its history as a department Meta lost territory to several later Llanos department creations (Casanare and Guaviare both split off in 1991), but it continued to exist as a distinct administrative unit throughout, so no successor entry is created and this row's span is not truncated by those later splits.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Colombian department-level production/statistical data for Meta, tabulated under labels such as 'Meta' across sources spanning 1915-1958 that previously had no department-specific polity row to route to (all existing COL-* candidates in the table are national-total rows for the whole Republic of Colombia, not any single department). Routing Meta-specific data to the national COL row was incorrect because it conflates a single Llanos department's output with the entire country's, a scale mismatch of roughly the same kind the Manchuria exemplar documents for China. What confirms Meta is a distinct administrative entity rather than an artefact of source labelling is its formal legal status: it has been a department of the Republic of Colombia, with its own governor, assembly, and administrative apparatus, continuously since Law 118 of 1959 elevated it from Intendencia status, and it remains one of Colombia's 32 current departments today. This entry's start_year (1959) is later than the earliest data (1915) by design, per this pipeline's back-cast rule: data predating a subnational unit's formal constitution is a back-cast case to be resolved separately (open question oq-1915-1958-data-disposition), not grounds for extending the department's own declared span backward past its legal creation date.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is available in the GeoPackage for this period. The registered source `gadm-4.1-adm1` is the correct source category (GADM's ADM1 layer digitises first-order administrative divisions worldwide, including Colombian departments), and it is properly declared in `scripts/sources.yaml`, but the local file at `data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg` is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Colombia entirely (0 COL features on disk). No other registered source in this repository (cshapes-2.0, cliopatria) carries subnational Colombian boundaries — both stop at country-level COL rows. `polygon_source` is therefore recorded as the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` per this pipeline's rule for the `registered_source_unfetched` route, `polygon_status` is `unassigned`, and `polygon_feature_id` is left null pending the fetch (see open question oq-gadm-col-adm1-not-fetched). No area figure is given here because no geometry is attached to this entry; any km2 quoted before the GADM layer is fetched would be an estimate, not a measurement.

**Territory description:** Meta covers the western Llanos Orientales and part of the eastern Andean foothills of Colombia, bordered by Cundinamarca and Huila to the west (across the Cordillera Oriental), Casanare to the north, Vichada to the east, and Caqueta and Guaviare to the south. Its capital, Villavicencio, sits at the base of the Andes on the edge of the plains. The modern department (post the 1991 losses to Casanare and Guaviare) covers approximately 85,600 km2, making it one of Colombia's larger departments by area despite its relatively low population density outside the Villavicencio corridor. This is a description of the territory located on a modern map, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry — no polygon is attached (see Polygon status above).

## Predecessors and successors

Meta has no predecessor polity row in the current table: before 1959 the territory was administered as the Intendencia Nacional del Meta, a national-territory-level unit governed directly from Bogota rather than as a department, and no separate polity entry currently represents that Intendencia-era administration (see open question oq-1915-1958-data-disposition). Meta has no successor: it remains a current, functioning department of Colombia through the 2025 open-end convention used throughout this table, and no dissolution or re-absorption has occurred. Later departments carved from Meta's original Llanos territory (Casanare in 1991, Guaviare in 1991, and others) are represented as their own separate polity rows rather than as successors, since Meta itself continued to exist as a smaller but still-active department after each split.

## Sourced claims

- Meta was elevated from Intendencia Nacional del Meta to a full department of Colombia under Law 118 of 1959, per the routing decision's span_basis and standard accounts of Colombian territorial-administrative history.
- Mitchell-style and FAO-adjacent production series exist for the Meta territory from 1915 through 1958, predominantly using interpolated_scaled/scaled methods, per the routing decision's data-method summary for this unit.
- Casanare and Guaviare, both bordering modern Meta, were elevated to full departments in 1991 under Colombia's new constitution, having previously been part of the Llanos national-territory administration that included Meta's original extent.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit-years

**Code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years pattern, not the bespoke form**

Of 66 subnational rows in the polity table, 57 use <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>. Meta is an ordinary first-order Colombian department with no name of its own distinct from its administrative identity (unlike Alaska or Hyderabad, which carry historical names independent of a modern country's admin hierarchy), so COL-META-1959-2025 follows the dominant convention rather than a bespoke code. COL has no existing subnational rows, so this entry sets the country's own precedent going forward -- future Colombian department pages (Antioquia, Boyaca, etc., several of which already exist as untracked wiki files in this working tree) should follow the same COL-<SUBUNIT> shape for consistency.

### d-start-year-1959-elevation

**start_year is the 1959 department elevation, not the earlier data tail**

The routing decision documents mitchell/fao-style production data reaching back to 1915 for the Meta territory while it was still an Intendencia (a national-territory-level unit directly administered from Bogota, not a department with its own elected government). Per the harness's default span rule, start_year is fixed to the year the administrative unit was formally constituted -- 1959, under Law 118 of 1959 -- rather than to the earliest year data exists for the underlying territory. The 1915-1958 data is a back-cast case to be handled separately (e.g. via constant-territory reallocation onto this polygon once assigned, or matched to the containing national COL row), not by stretching this department's own span backward past its legal creation.

### d-polygon-source-registered-unfetched

**polygon_source is the registered slug gadm-4.1-adm1, status unassigned**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: GADM 4.1 ADM1 is a correctly registered source in scripts/sources.yaml and does digitise Colombian departments globally, but the local file at data/geodata/gadm-4.1/gadm41_adm1.gpkg is a curated 81-country subset that does not include Colombia (0 features for COL). Per this pipeline's rule, when the route is registered_source_unfetched the slug itself is the polygon_source value (not 'registered_source_unfetched', which is a route category and was previously rejected by validate_declared_sources), and polygon_status is 'unassigned' with polygon_feature_id left null until the full GADM COL layer is fetched and a specific Meta feature is identified.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-col-adm1-not-fetched

**GADM 4.1 ADM1 has not been fetched for Colombia; no feature_id assigned**

The local GADM 4.1 admin-1 GeoPackage covers only 81 countries and Colombia is not among them. No other registered source in this repository carries subnational Colombian boundaries: cshapes-2.0 and cliopatria only have country-level COL rows. Until the full global GADM 4.1 ADM1 file (or the Colombia-specific extract) is fetched into data/geodata/gadm-4.1/, this entry cannot be assigned a polygon_feature_id and polygon_status must remain 'unassigned'. Fetching that layer and locating the Meta department feature (GID_1 for COL, department name 'Meta') is the concrete next step.

### oq-1915-1958-data-disposition

**What happens to the 1915-1958 Meta-territory data that predates this entry's start_year**

Mitchell/FAO-style production series exist for the Meta territory going back to 1915, per the routing decision, but this entry's start_year is fixed at 1959 (the year Meta became a department under Law 118 of 1959) rather than extended back to cover the earlier data. It is not yet decided whether that 1915-1958 data should be (a) routed to the containing national COL row as an unassigned-territory placeholder, (b) reconciled via a constant-territory back-cast once this entry's polygon is assigned, or (c) left to a separate predecessor entry representing the Intendencia-era unit if one is ever created. No predecessor code is declared here because no such Intendencia-era polity row currently exists in the table.

### oq-exact-creation-date-unverified

**The 1959 creation date has not been independently verified against a primary source**

The routing decision itself flags that the exact 1959 elevation date (Law 118 of 1959) was not independently checked against a primary or authoritative secondary source in that session, and notes some sources cite 1960 for the department's full effective powers. If a primary source (e.g. the Colombian Diario Oficial, or a standard administrative-history reference) instead supports 1960, start_year and the container basis text here would need to shift by one year, and the polity_code itself would need to change to keep the code and frontmatter years in sync.

### oq-neighbouring-departments-overlap

**Whether Meta's boundaries overlap in reporting scope with neighbouring untracked department pages**

The working tree already contains untracked wiki pages for several other Colombian departments (e.g. col-casanare, col-caqueta, col-guaviare, col-huila) created in this same session/pipeline run, some of which were carved out of Meta's original 1959 territory in later decades (Casanare, Guaviare and others were historically part of Meta's Llanos territory before their own department elevations). This entry does not attempt to determine whether Meta's declared 1959-2025 span correctly nets out the later loss of area to those newer departments, or whether that loss should instead be reflected in a mid-span container/territory change. That reconciliation is left open pending review of the full set of Colombian department pages together.
