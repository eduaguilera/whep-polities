# Indonesia (1969-1976)

## Summary

CSV row for Indonesia from **1969** to **1976** — an **8-year row**
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 850
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Act of Free Choice. East Timor invasion (December 1975). The
1976 end-of-row event: **formal annexation of East Timor** (17 July
1976) — a real territorial change.

## Territorial extent

This row's polygon is CShapes 2.0 feature `cowcode=850`, time-step
`1969` [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states) (CShapes is loaded in its
COW-coded distribution, so the 1969 step reflects COW's dating of
Indonesian territorial changes, not GW's). Measured directly from
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034),
this row's polygon is **1,876,795 km²**. That is **larger than the
predecessor row** `IDN-1949-1969` (1,466,397 km², same CRS) by
~410,400 km² — consistent with the incorporation of **West New
Guinea (West Irian)**, whose land area is ~421,981 km², into
Indonesia — and **smaller than the successor row** `IDN-1976-2002`
(1,891,777 km²) by ~14,982 km², consistent with the later addition of
**East Timor** (~14,874–14,919 km² by most estimates). Both
comparisons are within a plausible margin for coastline/projection
differences and are not independently cited beyond the geometry
itself; they are offered as a geometric sanity check on the CSV's
row boundaries, not as a sourced historical claim.

Substantively, per Biger 1995, Indonesia "has expanded to include
... formerly western New Guinea (1963) and the former Portuguese
colony of East Timor" [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). West New Guinea's transfer
to Indonesian administration (UNTEA hand-over, 1962–63) was
confirmed by the 1969 "Act of Free Choice" — an event this row's
CSV label already flags in its Summary as the row's starting point,
and which is plausibly why CShapes' COW-coded time-step for the
territorial change is dated 1969 rather than 1963 (see
[oq-west-new-guinea-dating](#oq-west-new-guinea-dating) below and the Contradictions
section). East Timor was invaded by Indonesia in December 1975 and
formally annexed 17 July 1976 [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) — the event
that ends this row and starts `IDN-1976-2002`.

**Within the 1969–1976 span itself, the polygon is constant**: this
row cites a single CShapes time-step (`polygon_feature_year: 1969`)
and there is no second CShapes observation for Indonesia before the
1976 East Timor change [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). Both
territorial changes bracketing this row (West New Guinea in, East
Timor not yet in) are already the reason the row's start and end
years exist as separate CSV rows; no further split is warranted
inside the span. The 1975 invasion and the 1976 formal annexation are
treated as one event for WHEP purposes (de facto control preceded
de jure annexation by about 19 months), consistent with CShapes'
own practice of coding sovereignty transfers by the date borders
were redrawn, not the date of military action
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

## Sourced claims

- Indonesia "has expanded to include ... formerly western New Guinea
  (1963) and the former Portuguese colony of East Timor" — Biger
  gives East Timor's addition as "1955" in the printed text, which
  is almost certainly a typo for **1975**, the actual year of the
  Indonesian invasion [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia).
- "The East Timorese long resisted Indonesian rule. The Central
  Moluccas also attempted to establish an independent state but the
  revolt was quelled" [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) — the Moluccas (RMS) revolt
  predates this row (1950), but the sentence is Biger's framing of
  ongoing internal resistance during the period this row covers.
- The Portuguese colony of East Timor was invaded by Indonesia in
  1975 [biger-1995 §timor-leste](../sources/biger-1995.md#timor-leste) (Biger's separate Timor-Leste entry, compiled
  from the same p.295 passage).
- CShapes 2.0 is distributed in two versions (COW-coded and
  GW-coded); the file on disk for this database is the **COW-coded**
  distribution [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states), so this row's `polygon_feature_year:
  1969` inherits COW's dating conventions, not GW's.
- CShapes codes territorial changes to dependent/annexed territory
  as one of three change types, including "transfers of sovereignty
  over dependent territories" [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes) — the category
  the East Timor annexation falls into, and the reason CShapes
  records a new time-step at the 1976 boundary rather than mid-row.
- Per the underlying COW state-system list (`statelist2024.csv`,
  columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema)), Indonesia (`ccode=850`)
  has a **single continuous tenure entry, 1949-12-27 to
  2024-12-31**, with no COW-coded exit or re-entry anywhere in this
  row's 1969–1976 span. This confirms there was no state-system-level
  discontinuity (COW's diplomatic/legal criteria) during this row —
  any territorial change in the period is a border change, not a
  change of statehood, which matches the WHEP polity-definition rule
  that regime continuity ≠ territorial continuity and vice versa.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): the
  polygon assigned to this row in `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`
  has an equal-area (ESRI:54034) area of **1,876,795 km²**, versus
  1,466,397 km² for the predecessor row and 1,891,777 km² for the
  successor row — see *Territorial extent* above for the
  West-New-Guinea / East-Timor interpretation of these two jumps.

## Contradictions

- **Dating of the West New Guinea incorporation.** Biger 1995 states
  West New Guinea was added "1963" [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). The predecessor/successor
  polygon jump that plausibly reflects this addition falls at this
  row's start, CShapes time-step **1969**, not 1963
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states). This is not necessarily an error in either
  source: administration of West New Guinea passed from the
  Netherlands to a UN transitional authority (UNTEA) in 1962 and then
  to Indonesia in May 1963 under the New York Agreement, but the
  transfer was not confirmed as permanent until the August 1969 "Act
  of Free Choice" referendum. Biger dates the *de facto* transfer;
  CShapes' COW-based coding plausibly dates the *de jure*/permanent
  one (CShapes explicitly excludes provisional wartime/transitional
  borders unless made permanent by a later agreement
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)). The 1963 vs. 1969 discrepancy is
  recorded here, not resolved — this row's WHEP start year (1969)
  follows the CShapes/CSV dating, consistent with the polygon
  actually on file.

## Open questions

### oq-west-new-guinea-dating

Why does this row's CShapes time-step date the West New Guinea
incorporation to 1969 rather than 1963? See *Contradictions* above —
plausibly de facto (1963, UNTEA/New York Agreement) vs. de jure
(1969, Act of Free Choice) transfer, but no source directly states
which convention CShapes' underlying Territorial Change Dataset used
for this specific case. Worth checking against the raw CShapes gpkg
change log (`data/geodata/cshapes2_full.gpkg`) directly if a future
ingest wants to close this with certainty rather than plausibility.
