# Bermuda (self-governing British Overseas Territory)

## Summary

This row continues the Bermuda chain from the 1968 constitution (internal
self-government) to the 2025 database ceiling. The existing
BMU-1684-1968 row (British Crown Colony of Bermuda) ends in 1968, which
left FAOSTAT-era data from 1969 onward without a covering period; this
row closes that gap. The territorial extent is identical to the
predecessor: the ~54 km2 (GADM polygon 68 km2 including reef islets)
North Atlantic archipelago has no boundary history.
