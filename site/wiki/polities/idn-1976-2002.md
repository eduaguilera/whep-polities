# Indonesia (1976-2002)

## Summary

CSV row for Indonesia from **1976** to **2002** — a **27-year row**
covering Indonesia including East Timor
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 850
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Suharto era through Reformasi (1998). East Timor independence
referendum (1999). The 2002 end-of-row event: **East Timor
formal independence** (20 May 2002) — a real territorial loss.

## Territorial extent

This row's polygon is bound to the CShapes 2.0 feature for ccode/gwcode
850 at `polygon_feature_year: 1976`
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-states](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-states),
i.e. the Indonesian archipelago **including East Timor**. Measured
directly from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg` (ESRI:54034
equal-area projection), that feature's area is **≈1,891,777 km²**.
Biger's figure for "Indonesia" alone (i.e. without East Timor) is
1,947,999 km² [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) —
note Biger's number is *larger* than our polygon despite excluding East
Timor, which is expected since it is a 1990s-vintage figure with its
own rounding/definition of territorial waters and is not directly
comparable to the CShapes polygon measurement.

Biger dates Indonesian independence to 1949
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia), consistent
with COW's own entry date for Indonesia (ccode 850): `1949-12-27 →
2024-12-31`, a single unbroken tenure row with no COW-recorded gap or
re-entry (direct inspection of
`wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, cited under
[cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates)
methodology). This row's 1976 start does not correspond to any
COW-recorded state-system change — it is a WHEP-internal split driven
by the East Timor annexation (below), consistent with the polity
being defined territorially rather than by state-system membership
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition).

**East Timor annexation (row start, 1976).** Portugal's decolonization
collapse in 1974–75 was followed by Indonesian military invasion of
Portuguese Timor in December 1975; Indonesia formally annexed the
territory as its 27th province ("Timor Timur") under Law No. 7/1976,
effective 17 July 1976
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia)
(Biger's entry gives the date as "1955," which the source page itself
flags as an evident typo for 1975, the actual invasion year) and
[biger-1995 §timor-leste](../sources/biger-1995.md#timor-leste) ("The
Portuguese colony of East Timor was invaded by Indonesia in 1975;
independence followed in 2002"). This is why the row starts in 1976
rather than continuing the prior `IDN-1969-1976` row unchanged: the
polygon gained East Timor's territory, a genuine area increase, not
merely a change of Indonesian government or regime.

**West New Guinea is not a change inside this span.** Biger notes
Indonesia "has expanded to include western [New Guinea] (1963)"
[biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia) — that
transfer (from the UN-administered former Dutch New Guinea, via the
1969 "Act of Free Choice") predates this row's 1976 start by over a
decade and is already folded into the polygon by the time this row
begins; it is not a territorial change internal to 1976–2002.

**East Timor separation (row end, 2002).** The 1999 UN-supervised
referendum led to Indonesian withdrawal and a UN transitional
administration (UNTAET) from October 1999, with formal, internationally
recognized independence on 20 May 2002
[biger-1995 §timor-leste](../sources/biger-1995.md#timor-leste). COW's
own statelist agrees the successor state (East Timor, ccode 860)
enters the state system precisely at `2002-09-27` (direct inspection
of `statelist2024.csv`), i.e. COW dates East Timor's independent
statehood to 2002, not 1999 — corroborating that the row's 2002 end
year tracks the same de jure transfer date used elsewhere in this
database, rather than the earlier de facto loss of administrative
control. See
[oq-timor-1999-vs-2002-split](#oq-timor-1999-vs-2002-split) in *Open
questions* for why this is flagged as a candidate for a finer split
rather than settled.

  including East Timor, matching the row's stated territorial scope in
  the Summary above.

  Malaysia (ICJ ruling in December 2002, awarding both islands to

## Open questions

### oq-timor-1999-vs-2002-split

Should this row split at 1999 instead of (or in addition to) 2002?
Indonesia lost effective administrative control of East Timor after
the August 1999 referendum, with a UN transitional administration
(UNTAET) running the territory from October 1999
[biger-1995 §timor-leste](../sources/biger-1995.md#timor-leste), three
years before formal, internationally recognized independence on 20 May
2002. The current `IDN-1976-2002 → IDN-2002-2025` split follows the
2002 de jure date, which both COW's ccode-860 state-system entry
(`2002-09-27`) and Biger's account treat as the operative transition —
so the existing split is defensible on those grounds. But the
territory itself (CShapes' actual test for a WHEP split
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition))
arguably left Indonesian control in 1999, not 2002. Resolving this
needs either (a) evidence that Indonesia's polygon/administration was
unchanged 1999–2002 (in which case 2002 stands and this note can be
closed), or (b) a CShapes/Biger-sourced polygon for the 1999–2002
window showing East Timor already excluded, which would argue for an
`IDN-1976-1999` / `IDN-1999-2002` split instead. Not resolved by this
ingest — recommend a human decision before acting on it.
