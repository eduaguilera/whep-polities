# Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales)

## Summary

Great Britain in its political sense: "the whole of England, Scotland and Wales in combination, but
not Northern Ireland", including the offshore islands (Wight, Anglesey, Scilly, the Hebrides,
Orkney, Shetland). "This territory, together with Northern Ireland, constitutes the United Kingdom."
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §great-britain](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#great-britain)
From 1801 to the 1921 treaty the United Kingdom was Great Britain and all of Ireland.
[wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12 §1801-union](../sources/wikipedia-uk-2026-04-12.md#1801-union)

One row for 1800-2025, typed `subnational`: Great Britain's own ground did not change, only what
the United Kingdom held beside it. It sits inside [gbr-1800-1921](gbr-1800-1921.md) and then
[gbr-1921-2025](gbr-1921-2025.md). The Channel Islands and the Isle of Man are not part of it (nor of
the United Kingdom); they have their own rows, [chi-1800-2025](chi-1800-2025.md) and
[imn-1800-2025](imn-1800-2025.md).

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `United Kingdom Great Britain` beside `United Kingdom`: tractors in 1949 (total
293,230, of which 278,370 wheeled and 14,860 crawler) and agricultural population in 1937 and 1951;
and a bare `Great Britain` population row for 1937. The tractor total is below the United Kingdom's
308,450 for the same year, the gap being Northern Ireland, so this is the UK minus Northern Ireland,
not the UK under another name. **10 rows** resolved to no polity. An alias to GBR-1921-2025 would
have put a part beside its own whole under the same (item, unit, year) key, where a consumer that
takes the mean averages them.

`NA` in GADM 4.1), Scotland (`GBR.3_1`) and Wales (`GBR.4_1`). The union measures **230,708 km²**
(ESRI:54034) before the build's simplification. No area is declared: FAO states none for the unit.
