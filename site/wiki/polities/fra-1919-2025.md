# France

## Summary

This row tracks France as a territorial-economic unit from **1919**
(Treaty of Versailles) to **2025** (database ceiling)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 107-year window
spans the interwar period, Vichy France / German occupation (1940-1944),
the Fourth Republic, and the Fifth Republic. Metropolitan borders have
been stable since 1919, with minor post-WWII adjustments (268 sq miles
transferred from Italy per Biger).

Under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition),
this row begins at a real territorial event — the Treaty of Versailles
(signed 28 June 1919) returned Alsace-Lorraine to France.

- **1919 start** is a real territorial event: Treaty of Versailles
  returned Alsace-Lorraine. CShapes records the polygon change at
  1919-06-28, area ~64.35 sq degrees
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **2025 end** is the database ceiling; France is still active.

Predecessor: [fra-1800-1919](fra-1800-1919.md) — Treaty of Versailles
returned Alsace-Lorraine.
Successor: NA (still active at 2025).

## Open questions

### oq-vichy-occupation

COW has a gap 1942-11-11 to 1944-08-25 but WHEP does not split.
Under the WHEP rule, occupation does not change territory — the
metropolitan borders of France were not altered by the German
occupation or the Vichy regime. The COW gap reflects a sovereignty
interruption, not a territorial one.

### oq-post-wwii-italy-transfer

268 sq miles transferred from Italy to France per Biger
[biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
This is a minor but real territorial change within this row. Under a
strict reading of the WHEP rule, any territorial change is a candidate
split — but 268 sq miles is very small relative to metropolitan France
(~212,000 sq miles). Not split in the CSV. Low priority.
