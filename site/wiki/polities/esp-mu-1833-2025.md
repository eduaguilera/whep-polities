# Murcia (province of Spain)

## Summary

Murcia is one of the 50 Spanish provinces created simultaneously by the 1833 Javier de Burgos territorial reform, which redrew Spain into provinces along largely their present boundaries. Since 1982 the province of Murcia has also constituted, uniquely among the historic provinces, its own single-province Autonomous Community (Región de Murcia), but administratively as a province its territory and boundary have been continuous since 1833. This entry is `type: subnational` because it represents one province within the sovereign Spanish state, not a sovereign polity in its own right; it exists as a distinct row because Spanish statistical series (agricultural, demographic, fiscal) are routinely reported at the province level, and the existing 50 ESP-XX-1833-2025 sibling entries establish that this repository tracks each Spanish province as its own reporting unit rather than folding all of them into the national ESP-1800-2025 total.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Murcia as a province-level reporting unit within Spain from 1833 (its creation under the Javier de Burgos reform) to 2025 (current, open end_year), in parallel with the 50 existing ESP-XX-1833-2025 sibling provincial entries (ESP-17, ESP-A, ESP-AB, ESP-AL, ESP-AS, ESP-AV, ESP-B, ESP-BA, ESP-BI, ESP-BU already in the table). It was proposed under unit_id ESP-ES620 (NUTS admin_name ES620), part of a batch of Spanish NUTS-coded provincial units being routed to create_new. Before this decision, no existing candidate represented Murcia specifically: ESP-CO and ESP-MD are different, unrelated provinces (Córdoba and Madrid), and the only entry whose territory contains Murcia is the national total ESP-1800-2025, which is roughly 45x Murcia's area and would silently absorb province-level statistics into an all-Spain figure if used instead. GADM confirms Región de Murcia as a genuine adm1-level territory distinct from its neighbors, and Federico-Tena-style province-level Spanish historical statistics (as well as modern INE province-level series) treat Murcia as its own reporting unit, confirming it is a distinct entity worth its own row rather than a subdivision folded into a larger one.

**Territory description:** The province of Murcia occupies the southeastern Iberian peninsula on the Mediterranean coast, bordered by Almería and Granada to the southwest, Albacete to the north, Alicante to the northeast, and the Mediterranean Sea to the east and south. Its modern extent is approximately 11,313 km², centered on the city of Murcia, and includes the Mar Menor lagoon and the Segura river valley. A reader can locate it on a modern map as the Región de Murcia autonomous community, whose boundary has been essentially unchanged since the province's 1833 creation — no known territorial reorganization has altered its borders in the intervening ~190 years, which is why mapspain-ign's present-day boundary would be an exact (not merely approximate) proxy once fetched, with negligible vintage risk.

Murcia has no predecessor or successor polity in this table: it was created directly as a province by the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform (no prior distinct administrative unit is tracked here for its territory before 1833), and it remains a current province of Spain with open end_year 2025, so it has not been superseded. It is contained throughout by ESP-1800-2025, the national Spain row, under a single container edge spanning 1833-2025.

## Sourced claims

- The 1833 Javier de Burgos royal decree (division of Spain into 49 provinces, later 50 with the 1927 Canary Islands split) established Murcia as one of the original provinces, a partition still used administratively today.
- The Región de Murcia became a single-province Autonomous Community under Spain's 1982 Statute of Autonomy (Ley Orgánica 4/1982), the constitutional structure that governs it as of 2025 without altering the underlying provincial boundary set in 1833.

## Decisions

### d-code-follows-esp-xx-pattern

**Code follows the established ESP-XX-1833-2025 provincial pattern**

This entry uses ESP-MU-1833-2025, matching the dominant pattern for 419 of 433 subnational rows (<ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>) and consistent with the 50 existing ESP-XX-1833-2025 sibling provinces (ESP-17, ESP-A, ESP-AB, ESP-AL, ESP-AS, ESP-AV, ESP-B, ESP-BA, ESP-BI, ESP-BU, etc.). MU is the natural two-letter mnemonic for Murcia and does not collide with any of the 50 existing ESP subunit codes. No bespoke code is warranted: Murcia is an ordinary administrative province created by the 1833 reform, not a historical territory with its own name predating or superseding the province (unlike ALK or CAL). The span 1833-2025 (open end_year, i.e. current) follows the same default-start-year rule already applied to the sibling NUTS units ES112-ES241, since Murcia is not among the 1927 Canary Islands departures that got a different start.

### d-polygon-registered-unfetched

**polygon_source is the registered slug mapspain-ign, not the route name**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched, which per the harness instructions means the slug itself IS the source and polygon_status must be 'unassigned' -- not 'new_source_needed' or 'registered_source_unfetched' literally, both of which were rejected by validate_declared_sources in this harness before. mapspain-ign is registered in scripts/sources.yaml as the convention for all 50 existing Spanish provinces, keyed by INE province code (cpro); Murcia's INE code is 30 (cpro=30), but the underlying file data/geodata/mapspain-ign/provinces.gpkg is not present locally (present_locally=False), so the feature cannot be extracted today and polygon_feature_id is left null with polygon_status: unassigned rather than assigned.
