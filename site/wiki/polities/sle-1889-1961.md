# Sierra Leone (1889-1961)

## Summary

Sierra Leone (1889-1961) covers the British colony of Sierra Leone from 10 August 1889 -- following the Anglo-French boundary agreement that reduced the colony's northern and eastern extent -- to 26 April 1961, the eve of independence. This row was formed by merging SLE-1889-1895 and SLE-1895-1961, because CShapes records identical areas (~5.95 sq deg) across the 1895 boundary adjustment. The Sierra Leone Protectorate was formally proclaimed on 31 August 1896, separate from the Colony of Sierra Leone (Freetown area). "Sierra Leone became independent from British colonial rule in 1961." [biger-1995 §sierra-leone](../sources/biger-1995.md#sierra-leone).

## Territorial extent

West African coastal territory bounded by French Guinea / Guinea (north and east), Liberia (south-east), and the Atlantic Ocean (west). CShapes 2.0 area ~5.95, constant from 1889-08-10 to 1961-04-26. Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records status=colony, owner=GBR (COW 200). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Open questions
