# Peru (to 1884)

## Summary

This row tracks Peru from **1825** to **1884** — covering independence
through the end of the War of the Pacific (Treaty of Ancón, 20 October
1884). Peruvian independence was secured at the Battle of Ayacucho
(9 December 1824). The 1884 endpoint marks the permanent cession of the
province of **Tarapacá** to Chile under the Treaty of Ancón, a
territorial discontinuity significant enough to warrant a separate
polity row.

This polity was created by splitting the former PER-1825-1909 row at
the Tarapacá cession boundary. See the Decisions section.

Predecessor: none (independence).
Successor: [per-1884-1909](per-1884-1909.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 `cowcode=135`, feature vintage
  1886-01-01 (post-cession Peru).
- **Polygon approximation warning.** CShapes 2.0 begins coverage at
  1886, after the Treaty of Ancón. No pre-1884 Peru polygon is
  available in CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria, or any other source currently
  in the repository. The 1886 polygon therefore represents post-cession
  Peru, which **excludes Tarapacá** (~59 000 km², a desert nitrate zone
  on the Pacific coast roughly between latitudes 18°S and 22°S). This
  is an underestimate of ~4–5% relative to the pre-1884 territory
  (~1.28M km² with Tarapacá vs ~1.22M km² without). For agricultural
  staples data (cotton, sugar), the underestimate is immaterial because
  Tarapacá was a mining/nitrate province with negligible crop
  production. For area-denominated indicators the direction is
  downward-biased.
- **Polygon status.** `assigned` (best available; approximation
  documented above).
- **COW.** PER, ccode=135
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none (independence at Battle of Ayacucho,
  9 December 1824).
- **Successor:** [per-1884-1909](per-1884-1909.md) — post-Ancón
  (Tarapacá ceded, Tacna/Arica under Chilean occupation).

## Sourced claims

- **1824-12-09: Battle of Ayacucho.** Secured Peruvian independence
  from Spain; COW entry for Peru begins 1825.
- **1879-1884: War of the Pacific.** Peru and Bolivia vs Chile;
  Peru lost the nitrate province of Tarapacá permanently.
- **1884-10-20: Treaty of Ancón.** Tarapacá (~59 000 km²) ceded
  permanently to Chile; Tacna and Arica placed under temporary Chilean
  administration (returned/divided 1929). This is the split point for
  this polity row.

## Decisions

- **Split from PER-1825-1909.** The former row spanned 1825–1909 across
  the Treaty of Ancón cession (1884), creating a silent polygon_vintage_drift:
  the CShapes 1886 polygon (post-cession, ~1.22M km²) was used for
  pre-cession data going back to 1825 (~1.28M km² territory). The split
  honest-documents this territorial discontinuity. Issue:
  per-1825-1909-polity-dates-war-of-pacific.
- **No pre-1884 polygon available.** CShapes 2.0 starts at 1886 for
  Peru. No Cliopatria or CHGIS feature covers pre-1884 Peru. The 1886
  polygon is used as a documented underestimate; see Territorial extent
  above.
