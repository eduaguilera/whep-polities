# Serbia and Montenegro (1992-2006)

## Summary

Rump Yugoslavia / Serbia and Montenegro from the dissolution of
Yugoslavia to the Montenegrin independence referendum
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

15-year row covering two political configurations: the Federal Republic
of Yugoslavia (FRY, 1992-2003) and the State Union of Serbia and
Montenegro (2003-2006). Dissolved after Montenegrin independence
referendum on 21 May 2006.

Predecessor: [f248-1991-1992](f248-1991-1992.md). Successor:
[ser-2006-2008](ser-2006-2008.md) and
[mne-2006-2025](mne-2006-2025.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **Area.** Combined Serbia (~77,000 km²) and Montenegro (~14,000 km²)
  plus Kosovo (~11,000 km²). Total ~102,000 km².

- **Predecessor:** [f248-1991-1992](f248-1991-1992.md) — rump

## Sourced claims

- **1992-04-27: FRY proclaimed.** Federal Republic of Yugoslavia formed
  by Serbia and Montenegro.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1998-1999: Kosovo War.** Armed conflict in Kosovo. NATO bombing
  campaign (March-June 1999). UN administration of Kosovo from 1999.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **2003-02-04: State Union formed.** FRY reconstituted as the State
  Union of Serbia and Montenegro.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **2006-05-21: Montenegrin independence referendum.** Montenegro voted
  for independence. End-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
