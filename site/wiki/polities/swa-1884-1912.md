# Spanish West Africa (to 1912)

## Summary

Spanish colonial territory on the northwest African coast from the Bonelli treaty of 28 November 1884 to 1912. The database start was corrected from 1800 to 1884, reflecting that the territory before 1884 was under Sahrawi tribal control without formal European sovereignty. "In 1976 Spain handed its Spanish Sahara territory over to Morocco and Mauritania" [biger-1995 §mauritania](../sources/biger-1995.md#mauritania). The 1912 end date corresponds to the Treaty of Fez / Franco-Spanish Treaty of 27 November 1912, which reorganised Spain's African possessions and created the separate Spanish Sahara and Spanish Morocco entities. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). No COW code is assigned. The territory encompassed Spain's coastal possessions in what is now Western Sahara, including the trading posts at Dakhla (Villa Cisneros) and the Saquia el-Hamra region. The interior was only nominally controlled in this early period. The database notes: "Start corrected from 1800 to 1884 (Bonelli treaty 28 Nov 1884). Sahrawi tribal territories before 1884."

## Open questions

### oq-swa-1884-1912-extent

The precise extent of Spanish control in this early period is unclear. Spain's claims were initially coastal trading posts, with the interior only nominally claimed. The CShapes polygon may overstate effective control in 1884-1900.
