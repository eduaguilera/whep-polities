# Austria

## Summary

The current CSV tracks this row as Austria from **1918**
(dissolution of Austria-Hungary) to **2025** (present)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). This is a successor state of
[auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md), one of the seven entities that
emerged from the dissolution of Austria-Hungary in 1918
[wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §successor-states](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#successor-states).

The row covers the Republic of German-Austria (proclaimed
November 1918), the First Austrian Republic (post-Saint-Germain,
1919), the Anschluss period (1938-1955, during which Austria
ceased to exist as a sovereign state under COW), and the
restored Second Republic (1955-present). The CSV also contains
an orphan row AUT-1918-1919 (same cow=305, same predecessor)
flagged as a candidate duplicate per
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 2. See
[oq-aut-1918-1919-duplicate](#oq-aut-1918-1919-duplicate).

Predecessor per CSV: [auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md). Successor: NA
(still active).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Full CShapes coverage from 1918
  onward [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope).
- **Massive territorial reduction within the row window.** From
  1918-11-03 CShapes records the Republic of German-Austria at
  ~25.5 sq deg (a larger initial territorial claim including
  German-speaking areas of former A-H). From 1919-09-10
  (Treaty of Saint-Germain) the polygon shrinks to ~10.04 sq
  deg — modern Austria. This is a reduction of more than 60%
  within the first year of the row.
- **Anschluss gap.** From 13 March 1938 to 27 July 1955 Austria
  does not appear in COW as a sovereign state — absorbed into
  Nazi Germany (1938-1945), then under Allied occupation
  (1945-1955). CShapes presumably codes Austria as part of
  Germany during this period. See
  [oq-anschluss-1938](#oq-anschluss-1938).
- **Modern extent.** 83,879 km², population ~8 million. COW code
  305, ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 AUT [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

## Sourced claims

- **1918-11-03: Republic of German-Austria proclaimed.** CShapes
  records Austria from this date at ~25.5 sq deg, representing
  the initially claimed territory of the German-speaking rump
  state [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md).
- **1919-09-10: Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye.** "Officially
  settled Austria's status as successor state"
  [wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11 §1919-09-10](../sources/wikipedia-austria-hungary-2026-04-11.md#1919-09-10).
  CShapes records the post-treaty polygon at ~10.04 sq deg.
  Biger corroborates: "In the Treaty of Versailles (1919), the
  boundaries of 1866 were restored and Austria was declared an
  independent republic" [biger-1995 §austria](../sources/biger-1995.md#austria).
- **1938-03-13: Anschluss.** Austria annexed by Nazi Germany.
  COW records Austria as ceasing to exist on this date (COW
  entry ends 1938-03-13) [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).
- **1955-07-27: Independence restored.** COW records Austria as
  re-entering the state system (COW entry begins 1955-07-27)
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md). The Austrian State Treaty was
  signed 15 May 1955; the last occupation forces left by
  25 October 1955.

## Open questions

### oq-aut-1918-1919-duplicate

The CSV contains an orphan row AUT-1918-1919 with the same
cow=305 and the same predecessor (AUH-1908-1918) as this row.
This is flagged as a candidate duplicate per
[log proposal-auh-chain-audit](../log.md#proposal-auh-chain-audit) Finding 2. The orphan
row may represent the brief Republic of German-Austria
(November 1918 to September 1919, when Saint-Germain reduced
the territory) or it may simply be a data-entry error. Needs
human decision on whether to merge into this row or keep as a
separate 1-year transitional row.

### oq-anschluss-1938

COW records Austria as ceasing to exist from 1938-03-13 to
1955-07-27 — a 17-year gap during which Austria was absorbed
into Nazi Germany (Anschluss) and then under Allied occupation.
Under WHEP rules, this is a territorial absorption and
dissolution. The current CSV treats the entire 1918-2025 period
as a single row, which papers over a 17-year period of
non-existence. Should this row be split at 1938 and 1955?

## Verification note (added 2026-07-24)

Crop-area magnitudes in the `juan` source (wheat ~247,000 ha, barley ~168,000 ha,
potatoes ~380,000 ha) stay **stable across 1919-1960 including the 1938-1945
Anschluss years**, showing no step at either 1938 or 1945. So this source reports
Austria on its First-Republic territorial basis throughout and does not switch to
Greater-German reporting during the annexation — the data is correctly matched to
this row for the whole span.
