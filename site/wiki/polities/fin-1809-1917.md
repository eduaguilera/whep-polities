# Grand Duchy of Finland

## Summary

Autonomous grand duchy within the Russian Empire from **1809** (Treaty of Fredrikshamn, ceding Finland from Sweden) to **1917** (Finnish declaration of independence). Finland retained its own Diet and legal system, and introduced its own currency, the markka, in 1860 [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [FIN-1917-1940](fin-1917-1940.md) (independent Finland). Finland's borders were essentially unchanged at independence in 1917 — the Grand Duchy had the same territory as the new republic. CShapes 2.0 tracks Finland from 1917; this proxy is a close match for the entire 1809-1917 period.. The Grand Duchy's boundaries were essentially those of modern Finland (minus Petsamo, plus parts of Karelia lost in 1940). CShapes 2.0 tracks Finland only from 1917 independence. A pre-1917 polygon could be sourced from Cliopatria or historical boundary projects.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Finland" separately from "Russia" throughout 1878-1916. Finland was a distinct statistical and administrative unit within the Russian Empire (own Diet, own customs, own currency from 1860). Previously, the pipeline routed FIN pre-1917 → F228 (Russian Empire), which merged Finnish agricultural data into the entire Russian Empire polygon — losing the geographic specificity the data compiler intended. Federico-Tena (2019) also treats Finland as a separate trading polity from 1812.
