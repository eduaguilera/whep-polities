# Brunei (greater)

## Summary

Brunei (greater) covers the pre-colonial Brunei Sultanate from 1816 until 1888, when Brunei became a British protectorate. During this period the Sultanate underwent dramatic territorial contraction as James Brooke and his successors progressively acquired Sarawak from the 1840s onward, and the British North Borneo Company took control of Sabah. By 1888, Brunei had been reduced to a fraction of its former territory on Borneo.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. In the early 19th century Brunei once dominated the entire island of Borneo and neighboring islands, including parts of the Philippines. By 1888 it had been reduced to two enclaves divided by Sarawak's Limbang district.
