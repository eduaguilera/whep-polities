# Namibia (1915-1920)

## Summary

Namibia (1915-1920) covers South West Africa under South African military occupation from 16 August 1915 — the day after German forces surrendered — to 16 December 1920, when the League of Nations formally awarded the mandate to the Union of South Africa. CShapes records this period as status=occupied, owner=ZAF (COW 560). The territory's borders were unchanged from the German colonial period: the occupation did not alter the spatial extent of South West Africa.

## Territorial extent

Identical to the preceding German colonial period. South-western Africa bounded by Portuguese Angola (north), Bechuanaland/British sphere (east), South Africa (south), and the Atlantic Ocean (west), with the Caprivi Strip. CShapes 2.0 area ~72.31 (unchanged from the German period). Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)
