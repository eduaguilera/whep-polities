# Egypt (1885-1899)

## Summary

This polity covers **Egypt proper** after the loss of Sudan to the Mahdist
revolt through the establishment of the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium. It
corresponds to Egypt without Sudan, under British occupation while nominally
remaining under Ottoman-Khedival authority.

Predecessor: [egy-1820-1885](egy-1820-1885.md). Successor:
[egy-1899-1925](egy-1899-1925.md).

65 data rows (Mitchell, 1885-1899).
