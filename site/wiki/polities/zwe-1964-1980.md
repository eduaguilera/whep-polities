# Zimbabwe (1964-1980)

## Summary

Zimbabwe (1964-1980) covers Southern Rhodesia from the dissolution of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland on 22 January 1964 through the period of the Unilateral Declaration of Independence (UDI) on 11 November 1965 until the establishment of black majority rule in April 1980. During 1964-1965, Southern Rhodesia reverted to British colony status. Ian Smith's white minority government declared UDI in 1965, which was not internationally recognized [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe). ZAPU (Nkomo) and ZANU (Mugabe) waged a guerrilla war that ended with free elections in 1980 [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe).

## Territorial extent

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa bounded by Zambia (495 miles), Mozambique (760 miles), South Africa (140 miles), and Botswana (505 miles). Area 150,873 sq. miles (390,610 sq. km). Capital: Harare (formerly Salisbury). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. CShapes records two sub-periods: colony (1964-01-22 to 1965-11-10) and independent (1965-11-11 onward), both with the same territorial extent (area ~33.46).

## Open questions

### oq-udi-recognition

Rhodesia's UDI (1965) was not recognized by any state except South Africa. CShapes records status=independent from 1965-11-11, but this reflects de facto control rather than de jure sovereignty. The next ingest should consider whether this merits a note on the polygon's legal status.
