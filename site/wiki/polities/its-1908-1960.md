# Italian Somaliland (1908-1960)

## Summary

Italian Somaliland was an Italian colonial possession in the Horn of Africa, covering the territory of what would become the southern part of Somalia. The WHEP row ITS-1908-1960 starts in 1908, when Italy purchased the remaining Benadir ports and concessions from the Sultan of Zanzibar/Oman, consolidating full administrative control; it ends in 1960 when Italian Somaliland merged with British Somaliland on 1 July 1960 to form the independent Somali Republic. "The independent Somali Democratic Republic was formed in 1958 [de facto 1960 when British and Italian Somaliland merged]" [biger-1995 §somalia](../sources/biger-1995.md#somalia). CShapes records Italian Somaliland (cowcode 5200) across multiple time-steps from 1889 to 1960, with a territorial expansion in 1924 (Jubaland/Oltregiuba transfer from British Kenya) and a status change to occupied territory during British military administration (1941–1950), followed by UN trusteeship (1950–1960). The 1908 WHEP start date reflects the completion of Italian direct control rather than CShapes administrative categories.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source: CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Somalia area: 246,201 sq. miles (637,414 sq. km.); capital: Mogadishu [biger-1995 §somalia](../sources/biger-1995.md#somalia).

CShapes records Italian Somaliland (cowcode 5200) with the following time-steps:

- 1889-11-19 to 1924-06-30: status=colony, owner=Italy (325), area ~30.26 sq deg
- 1924-07-01 to 1941-03-19: status=colony, owner=Italy (325), area ~37.94 sq deg (Jubaland added)
- 1941-03-20 to 1950-03-31: status=occupied, owner=United Kingdom (200), area ~37.94 sq deg
- 1950-04-01 to 1960-06-30: status=mandate, owner=Italy (325), area ~37.94 sq deg

[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

The area increase in 1924 reflects the transfer of Jubaland (Oltre Giuba / Trans-Juba) from British Kenya to Italy under the Treaty of London (1924). The occupied and mandate phases do not alter territory. The WHEP row uses the expanded post-1924 polygon, which persists to independence.

The Kenya-Somalia boundary was established in 1891 by an Anglo-Italian treaty delimiting respective spheres of influence (p.337–338) [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya).

## Contradictions

The CShapes start for Italian Somaliland is 1889-11-19, but the WHEP row starts in 1908. The CSV has separate rows ISM-1889-1924 and ISM-1924-1960 (with COW=325) alongside ITS-1908-1960 (with COW=NA). These rows appear to overlap in time (ISM-1889-1924 and ITS-1908-1960 both cover 1908–1924; ISM-1924-1960 and ITS-1908-1960 both cover 1924–1960). This is a potential duplication that requires audit — see [oq-its-predecessor-chain](#oq-its-predecessor-chain).

Biger gives the formation date of the Somali Democratic Republic as 1958 (p.471), with an editorial note in the biger-1995 source file that the de facto date is 1960 (merger of the two Somalilands). The CSV end year 1960 follows the de facto territorial merger, which is consistent with the WHEP polity-definition rule (territorial change drives row boundaries).

## Open questions

### oq-its-predecessor-chain

The CSV contains both ITS-1908-1960 (COW=NA) and ISM-1889-1924 / ISM-1924-1960 (COW=325, both with predecessor=NA and no successor field linking to SOM-1960-2025). These three rows appear to cover overlapping territory and time ranges. Either ITS-1908-1960 is the correct single row (replacing both ISM rows) or ISM rows are the correct representation and ITS is a duplicate. The ITS predecessor field is NA, leaving the 1889–1908 period unaccounted. A chain audit is needed: if ITS-1908-1960 is the canonical row, ISM-1889-1924 and ISM-1924-1960 may need to be reviewed or their relationship clarified.

### oq-its-1924-split

CShapes records a real area change at 1924-07-01 (Jubaland transfer, ~30.26 → ~37.94 sq deg). Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, a genuine territorial change of this magnitude (roughly 25% area increase) should trigger a row split. The current row ITS-1908-1960 spans this event without a split. A split into ITS-1908-1924 and ITS-1924-1960 may be warranted; the ISM rows in the CSV are already split at 1924, which is consistent with the CShapes territorial change. This is a candidate CSV correction.

### oq-its-1941-british-occupation

CShapes records Italian Somaliland as "occupied" (owner=UK) from 1941-03-20 to 1950-03-31 (British East Africa Campaign conquest), then as "mandate" (owner=Italy) from 1950-04-01 (UN Trust Territory of Somalia). The WHEP row ITS-1908-1960 spans these status changes without a split. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, no territorial change occurred, so no split is required — but the occupied and mandate phases are historically distinct and may warrant notation.
