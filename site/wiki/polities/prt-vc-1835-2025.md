# Viana do Castelo (district / NUTS III region of Portugal)

## Summary

Viana do Castelo is one of mainland Portugal's historic districts (distritos), located in the country's far northwest on the Spanish (Galician) border, with its seat at the city of Viana do Castelo on the Lima river estuary. It was established as part of the 1835 territorial reorganization that replaced the old comarca system with the modern district structure, and it continues today, largely unchanged in extent, as the NUTS III statistical region of the same name inside the Norte NUTS II region. This entry is `type: subnational` because Portugal is a unitary sovereign state throughout the whole span (captured separately as PRT-1800-2025); Viana do Castelo never held independent sovereignty, but statistical series exist that report it as a distinct sub-national reporting unit from well before the EU's NUTS classification was invented, which is what this row exists to carry rather than to assert statehood. The span 1835-2025 follows the historic-district-continuity reading established for the sibling entry Aveiro (PRT-PTAV): rather than treat the unit as a post-1986 NUTS creation (which would leave 106 of 144 reported data-years unroutable), it treats the NUTS III region as the continuation of the 19th-century district that reported data long before NUTS existed.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit PRT-PTVC / admin_name PT_VC) found that this reporting unit carries 96.2% observed values starting in 1880 -- 144 data-years in total, of which 106 predate the 1986 NUTS floor. Those pre-1986 rows had been implicitly treated as unroutable to any sub-national entity because no polity row existed for a pre-1986 'Viana do Castelo', which would have forced them either to go unmatched or to be folded into the national total (PRT-1800-2025), discarding the sub-national detail the source actually reports. That was flagged as wrong because the district-level administrative unit measuring these figures existed continuously from 1835, long before the NUTS label was ever applied to the same ground. What confirms this is a distinct, continuously identifiable entity rather than an artefact of one dataset's labelling is the same administrative-continuity argument already accepted for the sibling unit Aveiro (PRT-PTAV): Portugal's mainland districts are 19th-century creations that were never dissolved, only later given an additional EU statistical label (NUTS III) that maps onto essentially the same footprint. No existing polity row already represents this district/region under any name or boundary, so this is a create_new rather than a match_existing case, per the routing decision's own conclusion.

**Territory description:** Viana do Castelo district (NUTS III region) occupies the northwesternmost corner of mainland Portugal, north of the Cávado/Ave region and Braga district, south of the Minho river which forms the border with Spanish Galicia, and bounded on the west by the Atlantic coast. Its seat, the city of Viana do Castelo, sits at the mouth of the Lima river. The district comprises the municipalities of Arcos de Valdevez, Caminha, Melgaço, Monção, Paredes de Coura, Ponte da Barca, Ponte de Lima, Valença, Viana do Castelo and Vila Nova de Cerveira. Its area is commonly cited at approximately **2,255 km2** in Portuguese national statistics (INE/IGP district-area tables) -- this figure is a general reference estimate from public sources, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry, since none has yet been fetched or assigned here.

## Open questions

### oq-district-founding-year

**1835 start year is assumed by analogy with Aveiro, not independently confirmed for Viana do Castelo**

The routing decision states plainly that it could not independently verify the exact founding year of the Viana do Castelo district and assumed 1835 only by analogy with the already-recorded Aveiro (PRT-PTAV) departure. Portugal's 1835 reforma administrativa did establish the modern district system nationwide, but Viana do Castelo's own district was reorganized more than once in the 19th century (it was briefly merged with, and later separated again from, neighbouring districts during subsequent boundary revisions before the district map stabilized later in the century). Confirming the precise year Viana do Castelo became a standing district under its current name and extent, against a primary or secondary source on Portuguese administrative history, would firm up start_year and might shift it later than 1835.

### oq-nuts-vs-district-boundary-drift

**Whether the current NUTS III boundary exactly matches the historic district boundary this entry claims to inherit**

This entry treats the NUTS III region 'Viana do Castelo' and the historic distrito of the same name as the same territory for continuity purposes, but NUTS III boundaries were drawn for statistical/administrative EU purposes in the 20th century and are not guaranteed to be pixel-identical to the 19th-century district boundary at every point (municipality-level adjustments between district and freguesia over 190 years are plausible). No polygon has been fetched or compared here (polygon_status is unassigned), so this cannot yet be checked against a geometry; it should be revisited once the gadm-4.1-adm1 data for Portugal is actually fetched.
