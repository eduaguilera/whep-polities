# Egypt (to 1820)

## Summary

This polity covers **Egypt proper** from the database floor to the Sudan
conquest (1820-1824). It corresponds to the early Muhammad Ali dynasty
period when Egypt's territory was confined to the Nile Valley and Delta
without southward expansion.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor). Successor:
[egy-1820-1885](egy-1820-1885.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** Cliopatria "Muhammad Ali dynasty" 1811 vintage
  (~604,513 km2). The 1805-1810 Cliopatria entry for the same dynasty is
  anomalously small (~1,771 km2, likely capturing only the Alexandria
  foothold at the moment of Muhammad Ali's consolidation of power) and is
  excluded. The 1811 vintage represents stabilised Ottoman Egypt proper.
- **Approximation note.** The 1811 polygon vintage is used for the full
  1800-1820 span. The database floor is 1800; the period is treated as
  Egypt-proper extent throughout.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA (pre-database floor). Egypt was an Ottoman province.
- **Successor:** [egy-1820-1885](egy-1820-1885.md) -- Sudan conquest begins
  1820-1824, expanding Egyptian territory to ~1.83M km2.

## Sourced claims

- **1805: Muhammad Ali consolidates power.** Appointed Wali of Egypt;
  founded a dynasty that ruled until the 1952 revolution.
- **1811: Cliopatria polygon vintage.** Captures Egypt proper after internal
  consolidation; ~604,513 km2.
- **1820: Sudan conquest begins.** Muhammad Ali dispatches armies southward;
  marks end of this polity period.

## Decisions

- **Split from EGY-1800-1899.** This polity was created when EGY-1800-1899
  was split at the Sudan border changes (1820 and 1885). No data rows exist
  in this sub-period (earliest data is 1866); polity retained for territorial
  correctness.
- **1805 Cliopatria entry excluded.** ~1,771 km2 is implausibly small for
  Egypt proper; treated as a foothold/city-level entry, not a national-scale
  polygon.
