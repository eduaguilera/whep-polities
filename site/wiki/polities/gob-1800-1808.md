# Kingdom of Gobir

## Summary

The Hausa Kingdom of Gobir from the 1800 database floor to the fall of its capital Alkalawa on 3 October 1808 at the hands of Usman dan Fodio's Fulani jihad. Gobir was one of the original Hausa Bakwai (Seven Hausa states) and the primary target of the 1804 jihad because dan Fodio had been a religious teacher within Gobir before being expelled by Sarki Yunfa. The 1808 capital fall effectively ended Gobir's independence; a government-in-exile continued limited resistance from Tsibiri.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Gobir's heartland was in the far northwest of modern Nigeria and parts of southern Niger — the western Hausaland frontier facing the Sahel.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. The 1800 start is a database floor; Gobir as an established Hausa kingdom predates the 15th century.
- **Successor:** [sok-1804-1903](sok-1804-1903.md) — Sokoto Caliphate, the successor that absorbed Gobir after 1808 (though sporadic resistance continued from Tsibiri for decades).
