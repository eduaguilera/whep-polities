# Romania (1913-1918)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **6 years**, from the Second Balkan War
to the end of WWI
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Balkan Wars Romania, gained Southern Dobruja from Bulgaria.
Joined the Allies in WWI (August 1916). Overrun by Central Powers,
signed the Treaty of Bucharest (May 1918) but re-entered the war in
November 1918.

Predecessor: [rou-1859-1913](rou-1859-1913.md) -- Balkan Wars
territorial gain (Southern Dobruja).
Successor: [rou-1918-1919](rou-1918-1919.md) -- post-WWI Romania.

## Sourced claims

- **1913: Treaty of Bucharest.** (10 August 1913) concluded the Second
  Balkan War. Romania gained Southern Dobruja (~7,500 km²) from
  Bulgaria. This is the start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1916: Romania joins Allies.** Romania entered WWI on the side of
  the Allies (August 1916), seeking to gain Transylvania from
  Austria-Hungary.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1918: Treaty of Bucharest (WWI).** Romania, overrun by Central
  Powers, signed the Treaty of Bucharest (7 May 1918) ceding
  territory. Romania re-entered the war in November 1918 as the
  Central Powers collapsed. This is the end-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
