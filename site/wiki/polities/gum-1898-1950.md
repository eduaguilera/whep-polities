# Territory of Guam

Guam (~549 km²) ceded by Spain to the United States under the Treaty of Paris (1898-12-10). Administered by the US Navy as an unincorporated territory until the Guam Organic Act (1950-08-01) conferred US citizenship and civilian government. FAO 1952 source: 47 rows, 1937-1951 (cattle, buffaloes, goats, tractors).
