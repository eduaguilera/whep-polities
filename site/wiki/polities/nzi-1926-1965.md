# New Zealand island territories (Cook Islands, Niue, Tokelau)

## Summary

New Zealand's three Polynesian island territories taken together. The Cook Islands and Niue were
brought within New Zealand's boundaries by the same Order in Council, effective 11 June 1901; New
Zealand "took over administration of Tokelau from the United Kingdom on 11 February 1926", and
Tokelau "formally became part of New Zealand on 1 January 1949".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §cook-niue-tokelau](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#cook-niue-tokelau)

The span is 1926 (all three under New Zealand) to 1965, when the Cook Islands became
self-governing in free association and the three stopped being administered as one group. Each has
its own row for the whole period: [cok-1800-2025](cok-1800-2025.md), [niu-1800-2025](niu-1800-2025.md),
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md). This row is typed `aggregate`.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports the three as one unit under two labels: `Cook Niue and Tokelau Islands`
(population 18 thousand in 1937, 22 thousand in 1951) and `New Zealand Niue Cook & Tokelau` (the
1947 land-use table, `use total` 53 thousand ha, i.e. **530 km²**). **4 rows** resolved to no
polity. No single island territory is that unit: Cook Islands alone is about half of it.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: GADM 4.1 adm0 COK (283 km²) ∪ NIU (267) ∪ TKL (16) =
**566 km²** measured (ESRI:54034), against FAO's 530 (0.94). Nothing is estimated; the three
territories' outlines did not change.

## Predecessors and successors

None as rows: before 1926 Tokelau was in the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony
([gei-1916-1976](gei-1916-1976.md)), and after 1965 each territory continues on its own row.

## Data routing

`Cook Niue and Tokelau Islands` (2) and `New Zealand Niue Cook & Tokelau` (2), fao1952 1937-1951, by
alias: **4 rows**, previously unrouted. fao1952 also prints `Cook Islands`, `Niue` and `Tokelau` on
their own (copra, 1949-1951); those rows stay on the three territories' rows, so the whole and its
parts are on different polities and share no (item, unit, year) cell.

## Decisions

- **Aggregate beside its parts, not registered in `polity_composition.csv`.** The whole and its
  parts carry disjoint items (population and land use against copra), so there is no double count
  to record, and the parts' own GADM outlines are simplified differently in the build (Tokelau's
  atolls ship at about half their area inside this union), which the registry's containment check
  would read as a mis-binding.
