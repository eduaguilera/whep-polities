# Germany

## Summary

This row tracks reunified Germany for **36 years**, from reunification
on 3 October 1990 to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Stable borders
since 1990. Area approximately 357,000 km².

Biger notes: "in 1990 the German Democratic Republic (East Germany)
was integrated into the Federal Republic of Germany (West Germany),
becoming a united country"
[biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).

Predecessor per CSV: [f78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md) (West Germany)
and [f77-1949-1990](f77-1949-1990.md) (East Germany). German
reunification 3 October 1990. This completes the German chain to the
present, with one gap: 1945-1949 (occupation period).

Successor: NA (still active).

## Territorial extent

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [f78-1949-1990](f78-1949-1990.md) (West Germany)
  and [f77-1949-1990](f77-1949-1990.md) (East Germany) per CSV. The
  reunification on 3 October 1990 was the formal accession of the GDR
  to the Federal Republic under Article 23 of the Basic Law. This is
  a real territorial event (~108,000 km² added from the GDR).
- **Successor:** NA (still active as of 2025).

## Sourced claims

- **1990-10-03: German reunification.** The GDR was integrated into
  the Federal Republic. Biger: "in 1990 the German Democratic
  Republic (East Germany) was integrated into the Federal Republic
  of Germany (West Germany), becoming a united country"
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).
- **1990-09-12: Two Plus Four Agreement.** The treaty between the
  two Germanies and the four Allied powers (US, UK, France, USSR)
  settled borders and sovereignty. The Oder-Neisse line confirmed as
  the permanent German-Polish border.
  [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 260, continuous from
  1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
