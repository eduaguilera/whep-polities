# Seine-et-Oise with suburban Seine

## Summary

This entry is the territory around Paris that was the département of **Seine-et-Oise plus the part of the Seine département outside the city of Paris**, which since 1 January 1968 has been six départements: Yvelines (78), Essonne (91), Hauts-de-Seine (92), Seine-Saint-Denis (93), Val-de-Marne (94) and Val-d'Oise (95). It is Île-de-France less Paris and less Seine-et-Marne. It is `type: subnational`: France held this ground throughout, and the row carries a reporting territory, not a sovereignty.

**Why this entry exists.** The Mediterranean subnational panel ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)) reports a unit labelled `FR104`. In every NUTS vintage `FR104` is Essonne alone, and that is where it was routed (FRA-91-1968-2025, 1,819 km2, with its 1862-1967 years back-cast onto it). The panel's own landuse block says the unit is far larger: the per-year sum of its land-cover classes (cropland, pasture, forest, urban, other semi-natural) is a **constant 6,036.57 km2 in every one of its 141 landuse years**, 3.3 times Essonne. No existing polity is that territory -- the national FRA rows are 90 times larger and each département row is a part of it -- so the unit needed its own row. Routing it to Essonne put six départements' crops and livestock on one of them. Claude created this entry on 2026-09-24 while correcting the Southern-Europe alias defects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned, constructed. The polygon is the union of the six GADM 4.1 adm2 features named above ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)), built by `build_fra_sso_1860_2025()` in `scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`.

**Which départements, measured.** Areas in ESRI:54034 against the same GADM features:

| candidate extent | km2 | vs panel 6,036.57 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Essonne alone (the NUTS meaning of FR104) | 1,819 | 0.30x |
| Seine + Seine-et-Oise = 75, 78, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95 | 6,140 | -1.7% |
| **the same without Paris = 78, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95** | **6,035** | **+0.03%** |

The other eight French units in the same panel sit between -1.0% and +0.1% of their own GADM feature (Seine-et-Marne, `FR102`, is 5,928.17 against 5,928.1; Hautes-Alpes -0.6%; Alpes-de-Haute-Provence -1.0%; Corsica -0.3%). The with-Paris reading falls outside that band and the without-Paris reading sits in the middle of it, so Paris is excluded. It matters for area, not for values: the city of Paris (105 km2) holds essentially no farmland.

**Historical extent.** Seine-et-Oise and Seine were created in 1790 and dissolved on 1 January 1968 under the law of 10 July 1964. The 1968 reform redistributed communes between the new départements but inside this ground; the outer limit against Seine-et-Marne and the départements beyond Île-de-France is the old Seine-et-Oise limit. The one boundary inside the period that does move is the city of Paris: its 1860 annexation of the communes inside the Thiers wall fixes the limit this entry assumes, which is why the row starts in 1860 rather than at the France convention's 1800 floor; the incorporation of the Bois de Boulogne and Bois de Vincennes into the city (about 18 km2, 1929) is inside the polygon's error.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. This is a reporting aggregate that spans the 1968 reorganisation rather than a unit that the reorganisation created or dissolved: the six post-1968 départements are its parts, not its successors, and Seine-et-Oise is its main part, not its predecessor. Only Essonne and Seine-et-Marne have rows in this table today; Essonne (FRA-91-1968-2025) lies wholly inside this entry.

## Decisions

### d-paris-excluded

**Paris is left out of the polygon because the land total excludes it**

The first reading of this unit, in the audit that raised it, was the whole of Seine plus Seine-et-Oise (seven modern départements). Re-measured, that union is 1.7% above the panel's land total and the six-département union is 0.03% above it, against a band of -1.0% to +0.1% for every other French unit in the same panel. Paris is the only piece whose inclusion separates the two, and it has no agriculture to speak of, so leaving it out costs no data and makes the area right.

### d-start-1860

**The row starts in 1860, the year Paris took its modern limits**

The unit's data starts in 1862, so any start up to 1862 carries it all. The France convention's floor is 1800, but "Seine-et-Oise plus suburban Seine" had a different extent before 1 January 1860, when Paris annexed the communes inside the Thiers wall and the suburban Seine shrank accordingly. Starting at 1860 claims only the years the polygon actually describes.

### d-panel-slugs-only

**Only the slugs that carry the panel route here; the official NUTS slugs keep FR104 = Essonne**

`juan-subnational` (`FR104`) and `whep-lab-france-livestock` (`FRA-FR104`) now route 1862-2023 here as observation. The `eurostat-nuts2013`, `eurostat-nuts2016` and `jrc-nuts2016` rows for `FR104` are left on FRA-91-1968-2025, because in those code systems FR104 IS Essonne and data genuinely coded to the Eurostat code must keep landing there. The same reasoning scoped the Balearic correction to one slug in d4f0c20.

## Open questions

### oq-other-paris-units

**Does the panel carry Paris anywhere else?**

The panel has only two Île-de-France units, `FR102` (Seine-et-Marne) and `FR104` (this entry). If the compiler dropped Paris because it has no farmland, nothing is lost; if its small amount of market gardening was folded into one of the two, this entry's values are slightly high relative to its polygon. The panel does not say which.
