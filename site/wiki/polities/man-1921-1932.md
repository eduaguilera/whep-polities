# Manchuria (region, 1921-1932)

## Summary

The Manchuria region is the Three Eastern Provinces of the Republic of China:
Fengtian (renamed Liaoning in 1929), Jilin (Kirin) and Heilongjiang. This row
covers it from 1921, when the national row CHN-1921-1932 begins, to the
founding of Manchukuo on 1 March 1932. In those years the region was governed
by the Fengtian clique under Zhang Zuolin and, from 1928, Zhang Xueliang. From
the end of 1928 it was nominally under the Nationalist government.

This is a **subnational region entry, not a state**, and it follows
[man-1945-1950](man-1945-1950.md) and [man-1950-1955](man-1950-1955.md).
`type: subnational` is deliberate: the row must not compete with the national
[chn-1921-1932](chn-1921-1932.md) in the matcher's family ranking. It exists
only to carry the reporting territory of a regional series.

**Why this entry exists.** Mitchell's `china, manchuria province of` is a
regional series that runs from 1928 to 1952. For 1932-1945 it routes to
[man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md) (Manchukuo), and for 1945-1955 to the two
Manchuria region rows. Before 1932 no Manchuria row existed, so its 1928-1931
values (27 rows) went to CHN-1921-1932 and were published as the whole of
China. By area, the national polygon (7,496,474 km2 in ESRI:54034) is 7.0x
FAO's stated 1,069,300 km2 for Manchuria. By magnitude:

| mitchell 1931, tonnes | `china, manchuria province of` | `china, mainland` |
|---|---:|---:|
| wheat | 1,580,000 | 21,618,000 |
| maize | 1,701,000 | 6,387,000 |
| rice, paddy | 321,000 | 43,079,000 |

Mitchell reports `china, mainland` separately. On CHN-1921-1932 the two labels
shared 3 (polity, source, year, item, unit) keys in 1931, the three items
above, and a `mean()` consumer averaged a region with the country. The series
also runs without a break across the 1932 founding of Manchukuo: wheat
1,580,000 in 1931 and 1,135,000 in 1932, maize 1,701,000 and 1,542,000. So
1928-1931 and 1932 onward are one regional series, split only by the regime
change.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon (proxy).** The constructed `MAN-1932-1945` feature, reused, as
MAN-1945-1950 and MAN-1950-1955 already do. It is the union of the modern
provinces Heilongjiang, Jilin and Liaoning from GADM 4.1 admin-1
(`CHN.11_1`, `CHN.17_1`, `CHN.18_1`), 791,708 km2 in ESRI:54034
([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)). `polygon_area_km2` is left null because
that figure is the reused feature's own measurement (issue 195).

**Jehol is correctly outside.** Jehol (Rehe) was a separate special
administrative region, a province from 1928, and not one of the Three Eastern
Provinces. It joined Manchukuo only in 1933. The reused polygon already
excludes Jehol ([man-1932-1945#oq-rehe-annexation-1933](man-1932-1945.md#oq-rehe-annexation-1933)),
so for 1921-1932, as for 1945-1950, that exclusion is the intended territory
and not an approximation. No Jehol difference needs constructing.

**Why `proxy` and not `assigned`: the modern provinces are smaller than the
1920s ones.** FAO's stated 1,069,300 km2 for Manchuria is 1.35x the polygon,
so the polygon is 0.74x the stated area. This is the same gap already recorded
for MAN-1945-1950 and MAN-1950-1955
([man-1950-1955#oq-three-province-proxy-under-covers-by-26pc](man-1950-1955.md#oq-three-province-proxy-under-covers-by-26pc)).
Historical Heilongjiang and Fengtian included the western banners, Hulunbuir
and the Xing'an/Jirim leagues. These are now in Inner Mongolia, so a modern
three-province union omits them. Drawing the 1920s boundaries would need a
new source feature, and a rebuild of `data/geodata`, that this change does
not make. The direction of the error is known: the polygon under-covers the
reporting territory by about a quarter of its area, and it never over-covers
it. That is much closer than the 7.0x over-coverage of the national row these
rows sat on before.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Container:** [chn-1921-1932](chn-1921-1932.md), for the whole span.
- **Successor:** [man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md), Manchukuo, founded
  1 March 1932 over the same three provinces (Jehol added in 1933).
  MAN-1932-1945's own predecessor stays CHN-1921-1932, because Manchukuo was
  carved out of the Republic, not out of a regional entity. The link here
  records only that the reporting series continues.
- **Predecessor:** [chn-1914-1921](chn-1914-1921.md). This follows
  MAN-1932-1945, whose predecessor is the national row it was carved from.
  Before 1921 the region is inside the CHN rows, and no source in the panel
  reports it separately before 1928. The edge also keeps
  `data/final/iso3_successor_map.csv` answering pre-1921 `MAN` years with the
  CHN chain, as it did before this row existed.

## Decisions

### d-created-2026-09-24

Created 2026-09-24 by Claude. The source-agnostic alias
`china, manchuria province of | 1921-1931` was retargeted from CHN-1921-1932
to this row. It moves 27 mitchell rows (1928: 3, 1929: 4, 1930: 10, 1931: 10)
and removes 3 averaged keys on CHN-1921-1932. `pipelines/pre1961-matching/match.R`,
which routes the same label in `before_1961.csv`, now sends 1921-1954 to the

Typed `subnational` for the same reason as MAN-1945-1950. The polygon is
reused as a `proxy` and not constructed afresh, for the reason under
Territorial extent.

## Open questions

### oq-1920s-provincial-boundaries

A polygon for the Three Eastern Provinces on their 1920s boundaries, including
Hulunbuir and the eastern Inner Mongolian leagues, would close the 0.74x gap
for this row and for MAN-1945-1950. It needs a dated historical admin-1
source, and none is in `data/geodata`.

### oq-groundnut-equals-soybean

Mitchell's `groundnuts, with shell` for this label (unit `tons`, 1930-1933) is
identical to its `soybeans` series in every year (5,298,000 / 5,227,000 /
4,288,000 / 4,601,000). It looks like an item-label error in the
transcription, not a separate crop. Recorded here but not changed, because it
is an item defect, not a routing one.
