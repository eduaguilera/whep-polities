# French Sudan (1890–1960)

## Summary

Soudan français (French Sudan) was a French colonial territory in West Africa corresponding to the present-day territory of Mali. It was formally established as a military territory in 1890 out of the Upper Senegal administrative zone and reorganised several times under French administration before becoming the Sudanese Republic in 1958 and achieving independence as the Republic of Mali on 22 September 1960 (following the brief Mali Federation with Senegal, which dissolved 20 August 1960). Throughout its existence French Sudan was a constituent territory of the Afrique Occidentale Française (AOF) federation (1895–1960), but the AOF federation entry ([AOF-1895-1960](aof-1895-1960.md)) and this entry are at distinct administrative levels: AOF was the federation aggregate, while MLI-1890-1960 covers the individual territorial unit.

French Sudan was reorganised multiple times: it was briefly dissolved (merged into the "Haut-Sénégal-Niger" territory, 1902–1920), reconstituted in 1920, partially reduced when Upper Volta was created in 1919, regained territory when Upper Volta was dissolved in 1932, then lost territory again when Upper Volta was reconstituted in 1947. CShapes 2.0 records these boundary changes across seven time-steps (1905–1960) under cowcode 432.

This WHEP entry receives source-data rows attributed to "Mali" (iso=MLI) in the WHEP coverage-gap dataset for the years 1918–1959.

**Chosen polygon:** CShapes feature cowcode=432 at vintage year 1905 (row 282), area ~1,736,746 km². This is the earliest and largest CShapes snapshot and corresponds to the pre-WWI boundaries. WHEP data coverage is 1918–1959; the 1905 vintage slightly over-represents the territory for most data years because Upper Volta's creation in 1919 reduced Sudan's area. The 1947 snapshot (~1,252,292 km²) is the best fit for post-1947 data years. A single snapshot must be chosen for the CSV row; the 1905 vintage is recorded with the multi-step area variation documented here.

**Pre-1905 gap (1890–1904):** CShapes 2.0 does not carry a separate French Sudan entry before 1905-01-01. For 1895–1904 CShapes records French West Africa as a whole (cowcode 430, area ~3.8–5.6 M km²). Before 1895 the territory was organised as the Upper Senegal military zone but no distinct GIS polygon exists for it. The 1905 CShapes snapshot is used as a **period proxy** for the 1890–1904 sub-period. This is a minor caveat: the 1890–1904 period predates the WHEP coverage-gap data window (1918–1959), so no data rows fall in this gap.

**Polygon confidence:** High for 1905–1960 (CShapes exact historical); medium for 1890–1904 (period proxy, no data rows affected).

**Measured consequence of the 1905 vintage (2026-08-17, issue 10).** The over-representation noted
above is not only a caveat about this row — it is a double claim against a sibling.
`scripts/validate_composition_sums.py` sums the eight AOF constituents against the federation
polygon and finds them 272,441 km² too large (4,895,509 against 4,623,068, 1.0589x), while their
*union* matches the federation to 70 km². Intersecting the members pairwise locates all of it here:
**272,319 km² of [BFA-1947-1960](bfa-1947-1960.md)'s 272,322 km² (99.999%) lies inside this row's
polygon**, because the 1905 vintage predates Upper Volta's creation in 1919. Any area-weighted
allocation over 1947–1960 that uses both rows counts Upper Volta twice. The fix is a vintage or span
decision for this row — bind the 1947 snapshot (1,252,292 km², CShapes row 287) for the post-1947
period, or split the row at 1919/1947 — and is deliberately not taken here; the sum check baselines
`AOF-1895-1960` with the measurement until it is.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None recorded in WHEP. French Sudan was established by French military administration from pre-existing Upper Senegal arrangements; no WHEP polity entry covers the pre-colonial or early-conquest Mali territory that would be a direct predecessor.
- **Successor:** [MLI-1960-2025](mli-1960-2025.md) — independent Republic of Mali from 22 September 1960 (following dissolution of the Mali Federation with Senegal on 20 August 1960).

## AOF federation relationship

French Sudan was a constituent territory of the AOF federation ([AOF-1895-1960](aof-1895-1960.md)) throughout the federation's existence. AOF-1895-1960 is the federation-level aggregate polity carrying data reported at the federation level; MLI-1890-1960 carries data attributed specifically to the French Sudan territory. These are not successor/predecessor of each other but a part/whole relationship within the same time range.

## Key dates

- 1890: French military territory of Upper Sudan established
- 1895: Incorporated into the new AOF federation
- 1899–1902: Territory divided; reorganised as Haut-Sénégal-Niger
- 1902: Haut-Sénégal-Niger replaces Sudan administratively (CShapes tracks cowcode 432 from 1905)
- 1920: French Sudan re-established as a distinct colony
- 1932-09-05: Upper Volta dissolved; parts added to Sudan
- 1947-01-04: Upper Volta reconstituted; Sudan territory reduced
- 1958-11-24: Sudanese Republic proclaimed within French Community
- 1959-01-17: Mali Federation formed with Senegal
- 1960-06-20: Mali Federation becomes independent
- 1960-08-20: Mali Federation dissolved; Senegal withdraws
- 1960-09-22: Republic of Mali proclaimed (independent)

## Open questions

### oq-mli-1890-polygon-multistep

The CShapes 2.0 data has seven time-steps for cowcode 432, ranging from 1,252,292 km² to 1,736,746 km². A single polygon vintage (1905) is recorded in the CSV. For spatial analysis requiring period-accurate extents, the pipeline should use the time-step whose end date overlaps the data year. The post-1947 snapshot (~1,252,292 km²) is the appropriate fit for most data-years in the coverage-gap window (1918–1959 data being overwhelmingly in the 1947-period boundary).

### oq-mli-1890-pre1905-proxy

For the 1890–1904 period, no CShapes polygon exists for French Sudan separately. The 1905 CShapes snapshot is used as period proxy. If a more precise polygon for the early Upper Sudan territory is required, the Paine (2024) precolonial Africa dataset or a constructed estimate could be used. Since WHEP data coverage begins 1918 this has no practical impact on data routing.

### oq-mli-aof-overlap

MLI-1890-1960 and AOF-1895-1960 overlap in time (1895–1960). The AOF entry carries federation-level aggregate data; this entry carries territory-specific data. Clear routing rules for source rows labelled "French West Africa" vs "Mali" / "French Sudan" are: AOF aggregate rows go to AOF-1895-1960; Mali-specific rows go to MLI-1890-1960.
