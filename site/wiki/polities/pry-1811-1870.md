# Paraguay (pre-Triple Alliance)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay from independence (1811) to the end of the War of the Triple Alliance (1870). Paraguay lost approximately 60% of its pre-war territory (~392,000 km² → ~157,000 km²) under the post-war treaties. No authoritative GIS polygon exists for pre-1870 Paraguay; polygon_status is unassigned/ESTIMATE. Successor: pry-1870-1932.

## Territorial extent

- Polygon status: UNASSIGNED — ESTIMATE only. CShapes starts at 1886; Cliopatria does not include a pre-war Paraguay feature.
- Area estimate ~392,000 km²: derived by back-calculation from the post-war CShapes 1886 polygon (~157,000 km²) and the canonical 60% loss figure. Range: 340,000–400,000 km².
- Direction of error: data matched to the post-war 1886 polygon understates true pre-1870 extent by approximately 2.5×.
- Future option: compose a polygon by unioning the 1886 CShapes Paraguay with the territories ceded to Brazil (northern Mato Grosso do Sul region) and Argentina (Misiones/Formosa) under the 1870–1872 treaties.
