# Paraguay (pre-Triple Alliance)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay from independence (1811) to the end of the War of the Triple Alliance (1870). ~~Paraguay lost approximately 60% of its pre-war territory (~392,000 km² → ~157,000 km²) under the post-war treaties.~~ **Corrected 2026-09-24 (see Territorial extent):** Argentina and Brazil annexed about 140,000 km² of territory Paraguay had claimed. Most accounts put this at somewhat under half of the pre-war extent. The "60%" and the 157,000 km² figure do not match this database's own post-war polygon. No authoritative GIS polygon exists for pre-1870 Paraguay, so `polygon_status` is `unassigned` and `polygon_source` is `none`. (Until 2026-08-25 this row wrote `polygon_source: ESTIMATE`, putting a status word in the field documented as taking a slug registered in `scripts/sources.yaml` — it named no source and duplicated what `polygon_status` already said.) Successor: pry-1870-1932.

## Territorial extent

- Polygon status: UNASSIGNED — ESTIMATE only. CShapes starts at 1886; Cliopatria does not include a pre-war Paraguay feature.
- ~~Area estimate ~392,000 km²: derived by back-calculation from the post-war CShapes 1886 polygon (~157,000 km²) and the canonical 60% loss figure. Range: 340,000–400,000 km².~~
- ~~Direction of error: data matched to the post-war 1886 polygon understates true pre-1870 extent by approximately 2.5×.~~
- **The derivation above is wrong, and both of its inputs fail (corrected 2026-09-24).** The post-war polygon is not ~157,000 km². The successor [PRY-1870-1932](pry-1870-1932.md) is bound to CShapes 150 at its 1886 vintage and measures **293,547 km²** in `data/final/polities_database.gpkg` (ESRI:54034), because CShapes gives post-war Paraguay much of the Chaco Boreal. Nor is 60% a territorial figure. It resembles the estimates of population loss in the war, and the territory lost is usually given as about **140,000 km²** (Britannica: Argentina took most of Misiones and the Chaco between the Bermejo and the Pilcomayo, and Brazil enlarged Mato Grosso). On this database's own basis, the pre-war extent is therefore roughly 293,547 + 140,000 ≈ **434,000 km²**. That is outside the old 340,000–400,000 range, and data matched to the 1886 polygon would understate the pre-war extent by about a third, not 2.5×.
- `polygon_area_km2: 392000` is left unchanged for now: this row has no polygon, the figure is recorded as an estimate, and replacing one derived number with another needs the polygon that `oq-pre-1870-polygon` asks for. It should be read as having the derivation corrected above.
- Future option: compose a polygon by unioning the 1886 CShapes Paraguay with the territories ceded to Brazil (northern Mato Grosso do Sul region) and Argentina (Misiones/Formosa) under the 1870–1872 treaties.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: NA — independence 1811-05-14/15.
- Successor: pry-1870-1932. Split at 1870 corresponds to the end of the Triple Alliance War (Cerro Corá, 1 March 1870, and the preliminary peace accord of 20 June 1870). **Corrected 2026-09-24:** this line named that accord "Treaty of Loizaga-Cotegipe, 1870-06-20". The Loizaga–Cotegipe treaty with Brazil was signed on 9 January 1872. The Argentine boundary followed in the Machaín–Irigoyen treaty (3 February 1876), and the Hayes arbitration (12 November 1878) decided the Chaco between the Verde and the Pilcomayo in Paraguay's favour.
