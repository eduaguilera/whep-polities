# Aden Protectorate (1839-1963)

## Summary

The Aden Protectorate was a British colonial entity covering approximately 290,000 km² of the southwestern Arabian Peninsula hinterland, administered from the Colony of Aden (a small port enclave of ~192 km² on the Gulf of Aden). Britain captured Aden in January 1839, and the Protectorate was formally established through a series of protection treaties with local tribal rulers throughout the 19th and early 20th centuries. The territory comprised the Western Aden Protectorate and Eastern Aden Protectorate, covering the coastal areas and hinterland between British-controlled Aden and the Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen (north) and Oman (east).

The Aden Protectorate existed as a distinct British colonial entity until 18 January 1963, when it was merged into the Federation of South Arabia. The Federation of South Arabia in turn became the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen (PDRY / South Yemen) on independence on 30 November 1967. South Yemen unified with North Yemen in 1990 to form the Republic of Yemen.

This WHEP row exists to route 18 rows of FAO 1952 data labelled 'Aden Protectorate' for years 1937-1951. The data measures production within British-administered southern Arabia, a territory entirely distinct from the independent Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen to the north (MKY-1918-1962, CShapes cowcode 679, ~195,000-200,000 km² of highlands and Tihama coast). Routing the 'Aden Protectorate' label to MKY-1918-1962 would be territorially wrong: the two entities occupied non-overlapping territories on the Arabian Peninsula.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0; cowcode 680 (People's Democratic Republic of Yemen / South Yemen), polygon_feature_year 1967. The PDRY polygon covers the successor-state territory of the Aden Protectorate and Colony of Aden (minus minor border adjustments), approximately 333,000 km² for the full PDRY including the Colony of Aden. This is used as a **period proxy** for the Aden Protectorate footprint.

**Polygon_status: proxy** (corrected 2026-07-29 from `unassigned`; see the decision
note at the end of this page). No dedicated historical GIS polygon for the Aden Protectorate (pre-1963) is available in CShapes 2.0, Cliopatria v0.1.3, or GADM-4.1. CShapes first records cowcode 680 (South Yemen / PDRY) from 1967-11-30. The PDRY polygon is the closest available spatial proxy because:

1. The PDRY's territory is essentially the Aden Protectorate plus the Colony of Aden, with no major boundary changes at the 1963 or 1967 transitions (the eastern border with Oman was not formally delimited until 1992 under unified Yemen).
2. The approximation direction: PDRY (~333,000 km²) is slightly larger than the Protectorate hinterland alone (~290,000 km²) because the Colony of Aden city port (~192 km²) is included in the PDRY. The difference is negligible for production-data routing purposes.
3. No polygon for the intermediate Federation of South Arabia (1963-1967) exists separately in CShapes.

**Polygon vintage drift:** The CShapes 680 polygon is dated 1967 but used here as a proxy for 1839-1963. The territory changed very little between the protectorate era and PDRY: the eastern border with the Hadhrami sultanates was already incorporated in the Eastern Aden Protectorate. The main uncertainty is the exact extent of tribal treaty boundaries, which were not formally surveyed in detail.

**Colony of Aden merged:** The Colony of Aden (city port, ~192 km²) is treated as part of this WHEP row, not as a separate polity. The FAO data labelled 'Aden Protectorate' almost certainly encompasses production across both the Colony and the Protectorate hinterland (they were administered together as 'Aden' in British colonial statistics). This merge is appropriate given the data's lack of distinction.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP (British occupied Aden 1839 from the Sultanate of Lahej; no WHEP row for pre-1839 Lahej exists).
- **Successor:** No direct WHEP row for the Federation of South Arabia (1963-1967) or PDRY (1967-1990) exists. The eventual successor in the WHEP chain is YEM-1990-2025 (unified Republic of Yemen), but the intermediate South Yemen rows are a coverage gap. See open question [oq-south-yemen-successors](#oq-south-yemen-successors).

## Sourced claims

- "In 1967, a republican government took control in the north and in south Yemen the Yemen Democratic People's Republic was formed." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen) (pp.414-415) — confirms the PDRY was established in 1967 as the successor to the British-administered southern territory.
- "In 1990 they merged to form the Yemen Republic." [biger-1995 §yemen](../sources/biger-1995.md#yemen) (p.415) — confirms PDRY (successor of Aden Protectorate) merged into unified Yemen in 1990.
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode 680 (South Yemen / PDRY) from 1967-11-30 to 1990-05-21, covering ~333,000 km². This is used as the period proxy polygon. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Contradictions

- **CShapes cowcode for Aden.** CShapes 2.0 does not record the Aden Protectorate as a separate entity before 1967 (British colonial dependencies are not all tracked). The first CShapes entry for southern Yemen (cowcode 680) starts at 1967 independence. The pre-1963 protectorate period has no dedicated polygon in any standard GIS dataset known to the project.

## Decisions

- **dec-ade-status-correction (2026-07-29):** `polygon_status` changed from
  `unassigned` to `proxy`. The status contradicted this page: `unassigned` means no
  polygon is claimed, yet a polygon IS attached (CShapes 680) and the section above
  calls it a "period proxy" and documents its vintage drift. The consequence was
  that a consumer trusting `polygon_status` — which is what the manifest's
  `claims_polygon_status` set is for — would conclude Aden has no polygon while it
  has one. `proxy` is the status for a polygon borrowed from a DIFFERENT entity,
  which CShapes 680 is: it is the PDRY, Aden's successor state.
  `polygon_vintage_drift` was considered and rejected, since that status is used
  where the polygon is the same entity at another date (`BRA-1800-1903` with an 1890
  feature, `IND-1800-1886` with an 1880 one). Found by checking which rows declare no
  polygon yet carry geometry: six do, and this was the only LIVE one — the other
  five are superseded or retired rows whose geometry lingers in the GeoPackage.
- **dec-ade-proxy-polygon (2026-06-26):** Created ADE-1839-1963 using CShapes cowcode 680 (PDRY, 1967) as a period proxy for the Aden Protectorate footprint. Polygon_status set to 'unassigned' with documented reason above. The proxy overstates slightly by ~192 km² (Colony of Aden) but understates if the Protectorate included tribal areas not captured in PDRY boundaries. Net effect: approximation within ~15% of the ~290,000 km² Protectorate hinterland, acceptable for production-data routing.
- **dec-ade-colony-merge (2026-06-26):** Colony of Aden city port (~192 km²) merged into this row rather than creating a separate polity, because FAO 1952 data labelled 'Aden Protectorate' does not distinguish between colony and protectorate production.
- **dec-ade-routing (2026-06-26):** FAO 1952 rows labelled 'Aden Protectorate' (18 rows, 1937-1951) routed to ADE-1839-1963 via alias in applied_aliases.csv. These rows MUST NOT be routed to MKY-1918-1962 (Mutawakkilite Kingdom of Yemen): that polity covers the north Yemeni highlands and Tihama coast (~195,000-200,000 km²), entirely disjoint from the British-administered southern coast and hinterland.

## Open questions

### oq-south-yemen-successors

The successor chain from ADE-1839-1963 to YEM-1990-2025 requires two intermediate polities not yet in WHEP:
1. Federation of South Arabia (FSA, 1963-1967): successor to the Aden Protectorate upon merger.
2. People's Democratic Republic of Yemen (PDRY / South Yemen, cowcode 680, 1967-1990): independent state that merged into unified Yemen in 1990.

Both are coverage gaps. When created, FSA should be linked as successor to ADE-1839-1963, and PDRY as successor to FSA.

### oq-eastern-protectorate-boundary

The Eastern Aden Protectorate (covering the Hadhramaut region and sultanates east of Aden) had an imprecisely defined eastern boundary with Oman. The 1992 Oman-Yemen treaty formally delimited this border under unified Yemen. The CShapes 680 polygon (used as proxy) may not accurately represent the Hadhramaut extent as it existed under British administration. If a more accurate historical polygon for the Eastern Aden Protectorate is found (e.g., in British India Office or METO survey maps), the polygon should be updated.

### oq-pre1963-cshapes-search

CShapes 2.0 includes British protectorates/colonies for some regions (e.g., ARE-1892 for Trucial States). It is possible but unconfirmed that CShapes records the Colony of Aden (separate from the Protectorate hinterland) as a British dependency. If found, the polygon_source should be updated to use the Colony or Protectorate entry directly rather than the PDRY proxy.
