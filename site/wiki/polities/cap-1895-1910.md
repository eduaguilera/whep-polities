# Cape Colony (1895-1910)

## Summary

The British Cape Colony from the annexation of **British Bechuanaland**
(CShapes 2.0 date 1895-11-16) to the Union of South Africa on 31 May 1910,
when the colony became the Cape Province. This is the Cape at its maximum
pre-Union extent.

**Why this row exists.** It was split out of `CAP-1800-1910` on 2026-08-14
(issue 22). That row held **one** CShapes vintage — the 1886-1895 step,
600,802 km² — across 1800-1910, while CShapes records **two** time-steps for
gwcode 561 and the second is 19.2% larger:

| CShapes step | Area | Territory |
|---|---:|---|
| 1886-01-01 → 1895-11-15 | 600,802 km² | Core colony + Griqualand West (1880) + Transkeian consolidation |
| 1895-11-16 → 1910-05-29 | 716,102 km² | After annexation of British Bechuanaland |

So the pre-split binding **understated** the colony by 115,300 km² for every
data year from 1895 on, and the correction needed no new source: the observed
polygon was already in the repository's own CShapes release. Measured in layer B,
**128 rows** dated 1895-1909 carry Cape labels (`cape of good hope` 27,
`cape province` 29, `south africa` 72) and now resolve to this 716,102 km²
territory instead of the 600,802 km² one — a +19.2% change in the denominator
of any area-normalised figure computed from them.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0, gwcode 561, time-step 1895-11-16 → 1910-05-29,
  **716,102 km²**. Vintage sits inside this row's span and the step's start
  *is* this row's start year, so `polygon_status: assigned` — no drift, no
  approximation, and no caveat to carry.
- The 1895 boundary follows CShapes' own transition date for gwcode 561, the
  same convention [nat-1895-1910](nat-1895-1910.md) uses for the Zululand
  incorporation (CShapes 1895-04-23) rather than a legislative date.
- Nothing further changed before Union: the CShapes step runs unbroken to
  1910-05-29, the date of Union.

## Data routing

Three aliases were split at this row's start year rather than repointed
wholesale (`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/applied_aliases.csv`, 2026-08-14):

| label | years → CAP-1800-1895 | years → CAP-1895-1910 |
|---|---|---|
| `Cape of Good Hope` | 1800-1894 | 1895-1909 |
| `cape province` | 1800-1894 | 1895-1909 |
| `south africa` | 1852-1894 | 1895-1909 |

`Cape of Good Hope` was previously **unranged** — it matched every year,
including modern ones, and was baselined as harmless in
`scripts/validate_unranged_aliases.py` because no present-day source writes
the label. It is now bounded on both sides, and its baseline entry was
removed: verified in layer B that no row carrying the label has a missing
year (all 205 are dated 1854-1904), so bounding it drops nothing.

The `south africa` → Cape routing decision itself is unchanged and is argued
on [cap-1800-1895](cap-1800-1895.md#dec-south-africa-label-1852-1909); the
split only makes the late part of that span land on the right territory.

## Decisions

### dec-split-at-1895

2026-08-14, issue 22. `CAP-1800-1910` was one row with one vintage across 110
years, which the issue lists as overstating the early decades. Measuring it
found the same row *understating* the last 15 years by 115,300 km², and unlike
the early-period problem this half was fully sourced — CShapes already ships
the 1895-1910 step. Split performed; `CAP-1800-1910` marked `superseded`.

The pre-1886 half was **not** split, because no defensible source exists for
it — see [cap-1800-1895](cap-1800-1895.md#oq-cape-expansion-splits) for the
Cliopatria measurement that ruled the obvious candidate out.
