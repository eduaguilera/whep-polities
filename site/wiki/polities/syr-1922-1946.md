# Syria (1922-1946)

## Summary

French Mandate of Syria from the formal establishment of the League of Nations mandate in December 1922 to the end of 1945. "The Conference of San Remo (24 April 1920) established the mandates: Syria and Lebanon under France, Palestine and Mesopotamia under Britain" [biger-1995 §syria](../sources/biger-1995.md#syria). CShapes records area 18.57 sq deg for the period 1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31 (COW 652, status=mandate, owner=France COW 220). Syria formally gained independence on 17 April 1946 when French troops withdrew.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 18.57 sq deg for the period 1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31, status=mandate, owner=France (COW 220). The territory corresponds to the inland area east of Lebanon and north of Palestine/Transjordan. "The Golan Heights ... have been Israeli-occupied Syrian territory since 1967" [biger-1995 §syria](../sources/biger-1995.md#syria). The mandate borders were drawn by the Anglo-French agreements following WWI.

## Decisions

### dec-syr-1945-hole-closed

**`end_year` 1945 -> 1946 on 2026-08-17 (issue 252), code renamed `SYR-1922-1945` -> `SYR-1922-1946`.**
`end_year` is EXCLUSIVE, so the old columns covered 1922-1944 and left **1945 in no
Syrian row of this family**: [syr-1946-1967](syr-1946-1967.md) begins in 1946. The
Summary and Territorial extent sections above have always said this row runs "to the end
of 1945" and cited the CShapes step **1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31**, whose successor step
opens **1946-01-01** — so the columns disagreed with the page's own prose, and the prose
matches the source. Both CShapes steps measure 187,993.23 km², so absorbing 1945 changes
no published area; independence (17 April 1946) still falls on the successor row.

`validate_period_gaps.py` had annotated this gap "covered by SYL-1944-1953", the
fao1952 "Syria and Lebanon" joint reporting unit. That is a real coverer for
Syria-and-Lebanon-labelled data, but it is a LARGER territory than Syria — so it could not
serve Syria-labelled data for 1945 without overstating the area, and it was never a reason
for the mandate row to stop in 1944.

## Open questions

### oq-syr-mandate-internal-divisions

France divided the mandate territory into several sub-states (State of Damascus, State of Aleppo, Alawite State, Jabal Druze). The CShapes polygon treats Syria as a single entity; the internal administrative divisions may be relevant for understanding boundary events.

### oq-syr-1920-1922-gap

The database starts at 1922, but the San Remo Conference was in April 1920. CShapes shows a record from 1920-12-24 to 1922-12-01 (status=mandate). Why does the WHEP row start at 1922 rather than 1920?

## Territorial note (added 2026-07-24)

**The Sanjak of Alexandretta was ceded to Turkey inside this row's span** —
flagged by assertion verification of `syrian arab republic|iia|1922-1945`, and
not previously mentioned here. The district (~5,524 km², about 3% of the
mandate's area) became the autonomous Hatay State in 1938 and was annexed by
Turkey in 1939, so this row's polygon overstates Syrian territory for 1939-1945
by roughly that share. Small enough not to distort the agricultural series
materially, but it should be stated rather than implied.
