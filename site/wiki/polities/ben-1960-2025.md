# Benin

## Summary

Benin is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Benin (formerly Dahomey) from 1960 to the present. Dahomey gained independence from France on 1 August 1960 and was renamed Benin in 1975.

## Territorial extent

Benin is bounded by Nigeria (480 miles; 773 km), Burkina Faso, Niger, and Togo. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0. The boundary with Nigeria follows colonial-era French-British demarcations.
