# West Pakistan (1949-1971)

## Summary

The **western wing of Pakistan** before 1971: the territory of today's Pakistan, as one part of
the two-wing state `PAK-1949-1971`. It is a **reporting-area row**, not a separate state. It exists
because FAOSTAT's area **165 Pakistan** reports only this wing for 1961-1970, while the eastern wing
reports separately as area **16 Bangladesh** (`BGD-1947-1971`, East Pakistan).

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-09-24 from an audit of `data/final/faostat_area_polity_map.csv`. The matcher routed
area 165 1961-1970 to `PAK-1949-1971`, whose polygon holds **both** wings, so the same East
Pakistan ground was claimed by two FAOSTAT areas at once: area 165 through `PAK-1949-1971`, and
area 16 through `BGD-1947-1971`. Measured in ESRI:54034, `BGD-1947-1971` (137,848 km²) lies
**entirely** inside `PAK-1949-1971` (1,014,357 km²; intersection 137,848 km²).

FAOSTAT's own series say which ground area 165 covers (production pin 20260325T111448Z-4fffe):

| 1961 | area 165 Pakistan | area 16 Bangladesh |
|---|---:|---:|
| rice, paddy | 1,690,000 t | 14,426,184 t |
| jute | 0 t | 1,314,540 t |

Jute, East Pakistan's defining crop, is **zero** in area 165 for every year 1961-1970, and area
165 shows no step at 1971 (rice 3,298,400 t in 1970, 3,392,600 t in 1971). So area 165 1961-1970
is West Pakistan, and `pipelines/faostat-era-matching/match.R` now routes it here through
`manual_territory_routes`. Area 16 was already right.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned, constructed by `build_pak_wp_1949_1971()` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`: CShapes 770's 1967-1971 step (the feature family
`PAK-1949-1971` binds) **minus** CShapes 771 (Bangladesh, the feature `BGD-1947-1971` binds). See
[CShapes 2.0's border coding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-borders).

```
PAK-1949-1971 ground (CShapes 770, 1967-1971)   1,014,381 km²
minus CShapes 771 (Bangladesh)                     137,848 km²
= this polygon                                     876,534 km²   ESRI:54034
published in polities_database.gpkg               876,509 km²   after build_database simplification
CShapes 770 post-1971 step (PAK-1971-2025)         876,534 km²   symmetric difference 0.0
declared                                           881,913 km²   Pakistan including Azad Jammu
                                                                  and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan
```

The declared figure is the official area of Pakistan **with** the Pakistan-administered parts of
Kashmir, because that is the scope CShapes draws (-0.6%); a province-only figure for West
Pakistan excludes those areas and is therefore not comparable. The
polygon is the same ground as `PAK-1971-2025`, which is the point: 1971 changed the state, not the
western wing's boundary.

## Predecessors and successors

None declared. This is a part of `PAK-1949-1971`, recorded as a containment edge, not a link in a
succession chain; its ground continues as `PAK-1971-2025`, which succeeds `PAK-1949-1971` itself.

## Contradictions

It lies inside its container by construction. Any spatial or area-weighted use must pick one
level: `PAK-1949-1971`, or this row plus `BGD-1947-1971`. Summing all three double-counts.
