# England and Wales

## Summary

England and Wales, "one of the three legal jurisdictions of the United Kingdom", which "covers the
two constituent countries of England and Wales, and was formed by the Laws in Wales Acts 1535 and
1542". Statistics for Britain were routinely compiled for this jurisdiction separately from Scotland,
and it is Great Britain ([gbr-gb-1800-2025](gbr-gb-1800-2025.md)) without Scotland
([gbr-sct-1800-2025](gbr-sct-1800-2025.md)).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §england-and-wales](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#england-and-wales)

## Why this entry exists

The label `united kingdom: england, wales` reports England and Wales together for 1909 to 1951
(**45 (year, label) pairs**), beside separate `united kingdom: scotland` and `united kingdom: north
ireland` series. No polity was England and Wales. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: GADM 4.1 adm1 England (`GBR.1_1`, whose `NAME_1` is `NA` in GADM 4.1) and Wales
(`GBR.4_1`). The union measures **151,751 km²** (ESRI:54034); with Scotland's 78,957 km² it gives Great
Britain's 230,708 km².
