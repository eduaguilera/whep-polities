# Caroline Islands (Japanese Mandate, 1920-1945)

## Summary

This row covers the **Caroline Islands** (the archipelago now constituting
the Federated States of Micronesia) during the period of **Japanese
administration under the League of Nations Class C Mandate**, commonly
known as the **South Seas Mandate** (Nan'yo-cho / 南洋庁). Japan received
the mandate over the former German colony of German New Guinea's Pacific
territories — including the Caroline, Marshall, and Mariana Islands — at
the Paris Peace Conference (1919), formally confirmed by the League on
**17 December 1920**.

WHEP tracks only the **Caroline Islands sub-territory** (FSM-geographic
extent: Yap, Chuuk/Truk, Pohnpei/Ponape, Kosrae) under this code. The
Marshall Islands (now MHL-1874-2025) and the Northern Mariana Islands (now
NMI) are distinct territories administered under the same Nan'yo-cho
umbrella but tracked separately.

The mandate ended with Japan's defeat in World War II. US forces occupied
the Carolines from 1944-1945 onward; formal US trusteeship (TTPI — Trust
Territory of the Pacific Islands) was established in 1947. No WHEP row
currently covers the 1945-1991 transition period for the Caroline Islands
(US trusteeship 1947-1986, Compact of Free Association 1986-1991); the
successor FSM-1991-2025 picks up at UN membership.

**Why this entry exists.** IIA agricultural data labeled "micronesia
(federated states of)" for years 1932-1942 (eggs, sugar, tobacco) records
production in the Caroline Islands under Japanese administration. That
label anachronistically uses the modern FSM name for its geographic extent.
Routing this data to FSM-1991-2025 (which starts in 1991) would
back-extend the sovereign FSM by ~60 years into a qualitatively different
colonial entity; this dedicated row prevents that conflation.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon status: proxy.** No faithful pre-1945 polygon for the Caroline
  Islands under Japanese administration was found in CShapes 2.0,
  Cliopatria, or Paine-2024. CShapes 2.0 lists Japan (owner=740) and its
  colonies (Taiwan, Korea, Sakhalin) but has no separate entry for the
  South Seas Mandate territories. Cliopatria has no entry for the
  Carolines or any Nan'yo sub-territory.
- **Proxy polygon used.** GADM 4.1 adm0 `FSM` (Micronesia) is used as a
  modern-border proxy. FSM's land area is approximately **702 km²**
  dispersed across ~2.6 million km² of ocean. The archipelago extent has
  not changed materially between 1920 and the present; the modern polygon
  is a reasonable approximation for the Caroline Islands' land territory.
- **ESTIMATE flag.** Because the polygon is a modern administrative
  boundary used in place of a 1920-1945 historical GIS source, it is
  flagged as a period proxy / ESTIMATE. Production data attributed to
  this polity should note the polygon vintage mismatch (~75-95 year drift).
- **Not the full Nan'yo-cho mandate.** The Japanese South Seas Mandate
  covered the Caroline Islands (~702 km²), Marshall Islands (~181 km²),
  and Northern Mariana Islands (~464 km²) — a combined land area of
  ~1,347 km². This row uses only the Caroline Islands polygon; data for
  the Marshall Islands or Marianas would route to separate polities.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [jpn-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md) — The Caroline
  Islands were part of German New Guinea / German Micronesia until World
  War I. Japan seized them militarily in 1914 and administered them under
  an informal mandate before the 1920 formal League confirmation. JPN-
  1895-1945 is the closest predecessor covering the 1914-1920 interim
  military occupation; no separate WHEP row exists for the German-colonial
  period of the Carolines.
- **Successor:** [fsm-1991-2025](fsm-1991-2025.md) — The Federated States
  of Micronesia joined the United Nations on 17 September 1991. No WHEP
  row currently covers the 1945-1991 gap (US military government
  1944-1947, UN Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands 1947-1986, Compact
  of Free Association 1986-1991). See open question below.

## Decisions

- **CAR-1920-1945 scoped to Caroline Islands only** (FSM-geographic
  extent), not the full Nan'yo-cho mandate. The IIA data label "micronesia
  (federated states of)" specifically implies FSM's territory (the
  Caroline Islands archipelago). Data for the Marshall Islands or Marianas
  would not carry this label and should route separately.
- **Polygon_status: proxy.** Modern GADM FSM polygon used in absence of
  any pre-1945 historical GIS source; flagged as ESTIMATE.
- **Start year 1920.** The League of Nations mandate was formally
  confirmed on 17 December 1920. Japan occupied the islands from 1914 but
  legal administrative authority begins at the mandate date.
- **End year 1945.** Japan's defeat (August 1945) ended the mandate.
  US military occupation began de facto before that; the conventional end
  date follows Japan's surrender.
- **iso3: NA.** The Caroline Islands under Japanese mandate had no
  independent ISO 3166 code.
- **cow: NA.** The Correlates of War project does not assign a state-
  system code to the Japanese South Seas Mandate or its sub-territories.
- **polity_type: colonial.** Administrative entity without independent
  sovereignty; a League of Nations Class C mandate held by Japan.
- **Palau phosphate data 1920-1932 routed to CAR-1920-1945.** IIA data
  labeled 'palau' (iso3c=plw, item_code=3006, phosphate rock) for years
  1920-1932 records Angaur phosphate production under the Japanese South
  Seas Mandate. Palau was administered under the same Nan'yo-cho umbrella
  (HQ in Koror, Palau) as the FSM-geographic Caroline Islands. No separate
  Palau mandate polity exists in WHEP; CAR-1920-1945 is the closest
  available match. polygon_vintage_drift applies: GADM FSM polygon
  (~702 km2) does not cover Palau land area (~459 km2). A future
  NAN-1920-1945 full-mandate polity could consolidate both if needed.

## Open questions

### oq-1945-1991-gap

No WHEP polity covers the Caroline Islands / FSM-geographic territory
for 1945-1991: US military government (1945-1947), UN Trust Territory of
the Pacific Islands / TTPI (1947-1986), and Compact of Free Association
period (1986-1991). If data exists for this period labeled as "Micronesia"
or "Trust Territory", a separate TTPI polity would be needed.

### oq-german-colonial-predecessor

**RESOLVED.** A German colonial predecessor polity [GCAR-1899-1914](gcar-1899-1914.md)
(German Caroline Islands / Karolinen, 1899-1914) has been created. IIA
phosphate data labeled 'palau' (iso3c=plw) for years 1909-1914 records
Angaur phosphate production under German administration and routes to
GCAR-1899-1914. The predecessor field on CAR-1920-1945 has been updated
to note that JPN-1895-1945 covers the 1914-1920 interim military occupation,
and GCAR-1899-1914 covers the German colonial period (1899-1914).

### oq-full-nanyo-mandate

If data for the full South Seas Mandate (Carolines + Marshalls + Marianas
combined) is encountered, a broader NAN-1920-1945 polity for the full
mandate would be appropriate. The current CAR-1920-1945 covers only the
Caroline Islands (FSM-extent).

### oq-nanyo-polygon-upgrade

A faithful polygon for the Caroline Islands 1920-1945 may be constructable
from GADM 4.1 sub-national features or a Pacific historical GIS atlas. If
found, polygon_status should be upgraded from proxy to assigned with the
appropriate source.
