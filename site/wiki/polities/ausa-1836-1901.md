# South Australia (to 1900)

## Summary

The Colony of South Australia from its proclamation at Glenelg on 28 December 1836 to the eve of Australian federation. Unlike the other Australian colonies, South Australia was established as a planned free-settlement colony (no convicts), founded under the South Australia Act 1834. The WHEP row ends on 31 December 1900 before the 1 January 1901 federation. The CSV uses the non-standard `AUSA` prefix to distinguish this colony from the federated `AUS`.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependency-level). South Australia administered the Northern Territory from 1863 to 1911, but the polygon here reflects the colony's core — roughly the modern state of South Australia. The 1863–1911 NT administration is not part of WHEP's NSW/VIC/QLD/TAS/SA/WA chain and is flagged below.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in the WHEP chain. Nominally NSW had legal jurisdiction over the area before 1836, but WHEP does not record a territorial-change row in NSW-1800-1901 for the 1836 SA proclamation.
- **Successor:** [aus-1901-2025](aus-1901-2025.md) — Commonwealth of Australia from 1 January 1901.

## Contradictions

CSV code prefix `AUSA` is non-standard; the corresponding Wikipedia / ABS convention uses `SA`. AUSA is a WHEP-internal disambiguator against the `AUS` federation code. Flagged as a coding-convention issue, not a factual contradiction.

## Decisions

- [log dangling-refs-audit-2026-04-15](../log.md#dangling-refs-audit-2026-04-15) — page created to resolve the AUSA-1836-1901 dangling reference. CSV `notes` field already recorded the start-year correction (1800→1836).

### d-end-year-1901-not-1900

**The colony existed through all of 1900, so its exclusive `end_year` is 1901**

The Commonwealth of Australia was proclaimed on 1 January 1901, so this colony existed for every day of 1900 and ceased on the last of it. With `end_year` exclusive that is 1901, and the row previously said 1900 -- covering only to the end of 1899 and leaving 1900 with no colony at all.

The error was visible inside this table before it was visible in the data: the aggregate row [AUS-1800-1901](aus-1800-1901.md) already ends in 1901 and the successor states all begin in 1901, so the six colonies were the only rows dating federation to 1900. What surfaced it was the panel -- six Australian units report 1900 and those rows resolved to nothing, because the colony alias stopped at 1899 and the state alias began at 1901. Corrected 2026-09-17.

## Open questions

### oq-ausa-northern-territory

South Australia administered the Northern Territory from 1863 to 1911. The 1863 territory transfer from NSW to SA, and 1911 transfer from SA to the Commonwealth, are WHEP territorial changes not captured by the current AUSA-1836-1901 single-row model. Whether to split the row or add a subnational NT row is an open question.

## Type corrected to `colonial` (2026-07-24)

Was typed `national`. South Australia was a **self-governing British colony** before the
1901 federation, not a national polity, so `colonial` is the accurate
description.

It also removes a matching hazard flagged by
`scripts/audit_family_shadowing.py`: sharing `iso3_code: AUS` with
[aus-1800-1901](aus-1800-1901.md) (Australian Colonies, the aggregate for the
same era) and overlapping it, both rows were `national`, so family ordering
decided which received Australia-labelled data — and the aggregate is ~3x
larger. `colonial` ranks below `national`, so a label naming Australia as a whole
now reaches the aggregate while this row keeps the south australia-specific
series it was created for.
