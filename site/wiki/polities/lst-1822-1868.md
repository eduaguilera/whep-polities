# Basotho Kingdom

## Summary

The Basotho Kingdom (also Lesotho Kingdom or Kingdom of Moshoeshoe) was a southern African state created by the Sotho leader Moshoeshoe I in the early 1820s in response to the Mfecane/Difaqane upheavals caused by Zulu and Ndebele expansion. Moshoeshoe united scattered Sotho-speaking refugees on the mountain fortress of Thaba Bosiu. The database records the start year as 1822, the approximate date of the kingdom's founding. The end year is 1868, when Moshoeshoe himself requested British protection to forestall complete annexation by the Boer Orange Free State. Biger notes that "Basuto people lived in upper Orange River basin; clashes with British forces led to recognition of independence of Basutoland on 23 February 1854" and that British Commission was abolished "1 August 1964" with Lesotho becoming independent "on 4 October 1966." The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). The Basotho Kingdom centred on the Drakensberg highlands and the Caledon River valley, occupying what is now the territory of Lesotho plus surrounding lowlands. The kingdom's effective territory contracted through conflicts with the Orange Free State in the 1850s–1860s.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none; the Basotho Kingdom was a new political formation created by Moshoeshoe I from dispersed Sotho clans.

## Contradictions

Biger records "recognition of independence of Basutoland on 23 February 1854" — this refers to the Sand River and Bloemfontein Conventions by which Britain recognised the Boer republics, not specifically Basotho independence. The 1868 date in WHEP is the British protectorate declaration. These are different events and are not contradictory; Basotho sovereignty was effectively exercised 1822–1868.

## Open questions

### oq-chain-gap

The chain jumps from LST-1822-1868 to LSO-1966-2025, skipping the Basutoland protectorate (1868–1966). Biger provides the 1966 independence date but the nearly century-long British protectorate is not a separate WHEP row. This is a significant coverage gap for the period 1868–1966.

### oq-start-year

The start year 1822 is the conventional founding date for the Basotho state, but Moshoeshoe's consolidation was gradual. An alternative is 1818 (earlier settlement at Butha-Buthe) or c. 1820 (retreat to Thaba Bosiu). The 1822 date in the CSV needs sourcing.
