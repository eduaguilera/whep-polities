# Cocos (Keeling) Islands

## Summary

The Cocos (Keeling) Islands, "two atolls made up of 27 coral islands". "The British annexed the islands in 1857, and for the next century they were administered from either Ceylon or Singapore"; "The islands were made part of the Straits Settlement under an Order in Council of 20 May 1903", and they became Australian in 1955.
