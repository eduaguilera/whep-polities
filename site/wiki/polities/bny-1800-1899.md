# Kingdom of Bunyoro

## Summary

The Kingdom of Bunyoro (Bunyoro-Kitara) was a pre-colonial kingdom in western Uganda, founded c. 1300. Once the dominant power in the Great Lakes region, it was eventually eclipsed by the rival Kingdom of Buganda. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1899, when the British conquered the kingdom. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894" [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom was centred in western Uganda between Lake Albert and Lake Kyoga. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). The territory fluctuated across the 19th century as Bunyoro lost land to Buganda and later to British-backed expansion.
