# Saône-et-Loire

## Summary

This entry represents Saône-et-Loire, a French département in the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region, formed in 1790 substantially from territory of the former Duchy of Burgundy's Mâconnais and Charolais districts, with its prefecture at Mâcon and major towns including Chalon-sur-Saône, Autun, and Le Creusot. It is coded as `subnational` because a département is an administrative subdivision of France, not a sovereign state -- France's sovereignty over this area is continuous and undisputed throughout the entry's span, and the national FRA rows in this table already carry that sovereignty claim for the whole country. This row exists solely to carry a *sub-national reporting territory*: statistics keyed to "Saône-et-Loire" specifically, as opposed to all-France totals, need somewhere to route that isn't the national polygon. The entry follows the country convention's default start rule (1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform) as adjusted here to 1800 to match the earliest available French national container row (see decisions), and runs to the open end_year 2025 since the département remains an active administrative unit of France today with no successor.

**Why this entry exists.** This page implements the routing decision for unit FRA-FRC13 (admin_name "FRC13", country France), which resolved via create_new because no existing FRA polity IS this territory -- the national FRA rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) describe the whole country, and a département cannot be represented by its country's row; the only FRA-* candidates the routing decision considered are national totals across different eras, none of which IS this département (they contain it, but do not equal it). Before this decision, any data keyed to Saône-et-Loire specifically was, by construction of the routing pipeline, unmatched rather than misrouted -- match_existing was excluded outright since no existing row's territory equals this département's. What confirms Saône-et-Loire as a distinct, statistically-reported entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision is that it is one of the original Revolutionary départements of 1790 and has been tabulated as a discrete unit under numeric code 71 in French statistical series ever since. The routing decision records that the underlying juan-subnational data for this unit runs 1862-2023, but per this database's convention the entry's span covers the full administrative existence (1790, adjusted to 1800) rather than the narrower data span, so a single create_new polity suffices.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision already established this is a `none_available` case: no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at this resolution. gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated subset of 81 countries that omits France entirely (even though GADM's global release does cover French départements at admin-1); cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe are country-level only and cannot supply a sub-national boundary. The standard remedy, per the routing decision's polygon_detail, would be to register GADM's global adm1 layer or French IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS product, neither of which is on disk in this repository today.

Territory description: Saône-et-Loire covers roughly 8,575 km² in the southern part of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, bordering Côte-d'Or to the north, Jura and Ain (across the Saône) to the east, Rhône and Loire to the south, and Allier and Nièvre to the west. Its prefecture is Mâcon; it includes the industrial town of Le Creusot, the wine-producing Mâconnais and Chalonnais hillsides, and the medieval abbey town of Cluny. This description is drawn from present-day knowledge of the département's location and standard reference figures for its area, not measured from any polygon attached to this entry, since none is yet assigned.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the routing decision's proposed 1790, to match the earliest available FRA container row**

The routing decision's span_basis argues for 1790 (the year of the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary départements reform) under the country convention's default start rule, since Saône-et-Loire is not one of the four enumerated departures (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, overseas départements). But no FRA national polity row in this database covers 1790-1800 -- the earliest is FRA-1800-1871 -- and the containment gate rejects an edge falling outside either party's own span. I therefore set start_year to 1800 to match the earliest FRA container, exactly as FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher), FRA-21-1800-2025 (Côte-d'Or), and the other sibling FRA département entries already do, rather than leaving a decade uncontained. The polity_code year (1800) equals the frontmatter start_year, satisfying the code/frontmatter equality gate. The 1790-1800 gap is recorded as an open question below, consistent with every other FRA département sibling.

### d-subunit-code-insee-71-not-frc13

**Subunit code is the INSEE/ISO 3166-2 numeric code 71, not the routing decision's admin_name FRC13**

The routing decision labels this unit 'FRC13' (a NUTS3-style code used internally by the source pipeline) and its unit_id 'FRA-FRC13'. Following the precedent set by FRA-18-1800-2025 (Cher, INSEE 18) and FRA-21-1800-2025 (Côte-d'Or, INSEE 21), I used the INSEE/ISO 3166-2:FR two-digit numeric code 71 for Saône-et-Loire rather than the internal NUTS-style admin_name, since 71 is the identifier a reader recognises from French postcodes (71xxx), car plates, and INSEE statistical tables, whereas FRC13 is not in ordinary public use. This gives the code FRA-71-1800-2025, matching the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern with a subunit token a reader can actually place.

### d-polygon-unassigned-not-proxy

**Polygon left unassigned rather than proxied from the national polygon or a neighbouring département**

The routing decision's polygon_route is none_available: no registered source in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at this resolution, and the candidate_new_source in polygon_detail (GADM 4.1 full adm1 download, or IGN ADMIN-EXPRESS) is not present locally -- the local gadm-4.1-adm1 file is a curated 81-country subset excluding France entirely, and cshapes-2.0/cshapes-europe are country-level only. I did not substitute the national FRA polygon or a hand-drawn union as a stopgap proxy, because doing so would defeat this row's purpose, which is to isolate Saône-et-Loire's own reporting territory from the national one. polygon_source is set to the 'none' sentinel already used by FRA-18-1800-2025, FRA-21-1800-2025, and other unassigned French département rows in this table.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap-uncovered

**The 1790-1800 decade of Saône-et-Loire's existence as a département has no container row and is not represented here**

Saône-et-Loire was created in the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary départements reform, ten years before this entry's start_year of 1800. No FRA national row in this database extends back that far -- FRA-1800-1871 is the earliest -- so this entry, like every other FRA département sibling (FRA-18, FRA-21, FRA-28, FRA-36, FRA-37, FRA-41, FRA-58, FRA-77), simply begins at 1800 and leaves 1790-1799 uncontained rather than misrepresented. If a pre-1800 FRA national row is ever added to this database, this entry's start_year and container list should be revisited together, not just extended in isolation, since the containment gate ties the edge to the container's own span.

### oq-data-gap-1790-1862

**Whether the 1862 start of the underlying data reflects a real administrative or reporting change, or simply source coverage**

The routing decision's routing_concerns note the underlying juan-subnational data for FRA-FRC13 begins only in 1862, though the département itself dates to 1790 (adjusted here to 1800). It is unclear whether this reflects a genuine change in French statistical reporting practice around départements in the 1860s (e.g., the start of a specific INSEE-predecessor series) or is simply an artefact of what this particular data source happened to digitise. This does not affect the proposed 1800-2025 span, since that span is justified by the département's own administrative continuity rather than by the data's extent, but a reader relying on this entry for time-series coverage should not assume data exists before 1862.

### oq-departmental-boundary-changes

**Whether Saône-et-Loire's own boundary has been perfectly stable since 1790/1800, or has had minor cantonal-level adjustments**

This entry treats Saône-et-Loire's territory as unchanged across the full 1800-2025 span, consistent with the treatment of sibling départements in this database. French départements have occasionally undergone small boundary adjustments between neighbouring communes/cantons (distinct from the larger, well-documented changes affecting Alsace-Lorraine, Belfort, Corsica, or the Paris petite couronne in 1968). No such adjustment specific to Saône-et-Loire is known to the author of this entry, but none has been actively verified either, and the absence of an assigned polygon means no boundary-vintage check has actually been run against a candidate source.
