# Czechoslovakia (1947-1992)

## Summary

Czechoslovakia (1947-1992) tracks the post-World War II Czechoslovak state from 1947 (when the CShapes polygon changes after the Trieste free territory settlement and post-war adjustments) through the Velvet Divorce on 1 January 1993. During this period Czechoslovakia was a Soviet-aligned communist state (from the 1948 coup), experienced the Prague Spring (1968) and subsequent Warsaw Pact invasion, and transitioned to democracy via the Velvet Revolution (1989) before peacefully splitting into the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Czechoslovakia was a landlocked state bounded by Poland (382 mi; 615 km), Slovakia (122 mi; 197 km on the internal federal boundary), Austria (285 mi; 456 km), and Germany (507 mi; 815 km). The boundaries largely followed lines traced to those existing on 3 August 1914.
