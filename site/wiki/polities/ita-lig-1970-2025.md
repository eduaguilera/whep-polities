# Liguria (region of Italy)

## Summary

Liguria is one of Italy's 15 ordinary-statute regions (regioni a statuto ordinario), a narrow coastal strip in Italy's northwest along the Ligurian Sea, bordering France to the west, Piemonte to the north, Emilia-Romagna and Toscana to the east, with Genoa (Genova) as its capital. This entry is `type: subnational` because Liguria never held sovereignty of its own in this period — Italy held sovereignty throughout 1970-2025 — the row exists purely to carry a reporting/administrative territory below the national level, the same rationale already documented for the sibling entry ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte) and for other regional subnational rows in this table (e.g. the Manchuria region entries). Liguria became an operative administrative and statistical unit in 1970, when Italy's ordinary-statute regions held their first regional council elections mandated by the 1948 Constitution; before that date regional government existed on paper only and statistics were not compiled at this level, so this row's span begins in 1970 rather than at unification (1861) as the routing decision's own span_basis argued — see decisions below for why 1861 was rejected in favor of the 1970 convention already established by the Piemonte sibling. The end_year of 2025 is open — the region remains current.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: this row is created to receive statistical series reported under Italian administrative unit `ITA-ITC3` (admin_name ITC3, i.e. Liguria) in NUTS/GADM-style subnational classifications; the routing decision names the unit with no row count given in the decision object itself, flagged for creation because the routing pipeline identified Liguria-labelled data with no existing polity representing it (0 name candidates). Previously matched to: all such data was being matched to the national ITA polities (ITA-1919-2025 and its predecessors), which is wrong for any year from 1970 onward because Liguria-level reporting is a real, distinct statistical/administrative practice that folding into the national total obscures. What confirms distinctness: Liguria is a constitutionally recognized ordinary-statute region of Italy with its own directly elected regional council since 1970, its own regional government (Giunta regionale), and its own budget and statistical reporting apparatus (e.g. Istat, Eurostat under NUTS2 code ITC3), reported separately from national totals — the same standing already established for Piemonte's ITC1 in ITA-PIE-1970-2025.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: statistical series reported under Italian administrative/NUTS unit ITC3 (Liguria), a first-order Italian region, per the routing decision for unit `ITA-ITC3`; no row count is given in the routing decision object itself, only that the unit was flagged with 0 existing name candidates matching it. Previously matched to: the national ITA rows (ITA-1919-2025 and its predecessors, e.g. ITA-1870-1919), which folds region-level Liguria statistics into all-Italy totals — a regional figure folded into a national total cannot be recovered or distinguished from other regions' contributions, which is wrong for any year from 1970 onward once Liguria became a real statistical reporting unit. What confirms distinctness: Liguria is a constitutionally recognized ordinary-statute region with its own directly elected regional council since 1970, its own regional government, and its own Istat/Eurostat (NUTS2 ITC3) statistical reporting separate from the national total — the same standing already documented for its sibling entry ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte), which sets the working precedent for how Italy's ordinary-statute regions are entered into this table.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-subunit

**Use ITA-LIG-1970-2025, the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years pattern**

57 of 66 existing subnational rows follow <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (e.g. DZA-CVD-1902-1919, ESP-AS-1833-2025). Liguria is an ordinary Italian administrative region with no name-of-its-own historical identity distinct from being a region of Italy (unlike Manchuria or Hyderabad, which earned bespoke codes as historical territories), so the bespoke-code exception does not apply. ITA already has one subnational row created under this pipeline, ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte), which sets the country's precedent; LIG is the natural three-letter abbreviation for Liguria and is consistent with that precedent.

### d-start-year-1970-not-1861

**Start year 1970, departing from the routing decision's own stated span_basis of 1861**

The routing decision object for this unit argues start_year=1861 under a 'default start rule' for Italian regions not among two enumerated 1919/1963 departure regions, reasoning that Liguria was part of the Kingdom of Italy from unification. That reasoning is superseded here: a sibling entry already created in this same pipeline run, ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte), establishes the actual working convention for Italy's fifteen ordinary-statute regions — start_year=1970, the year of the first regional council elections mandated by the 1948 Constitution, because before that date regional government existed on paper only and no region-level statistics were compiled. Liguria is one of the fifteen ordinary-statute regions (not one of the five special-statute regions: Sicilia, Sardegna, Trentino-Alto Adige, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Valle d'Aosta), so the same 1970 convention applies to it, not the 1861 unification-based default the routing decision proposed. Using 1861 here would create an inconsistency where two sibling ordinary-statute regions, created in the same run under the same routing logic, disagree on when the administrative tier they both represent came into existence. Following the router's literal 1861 span_basis was rejected in favor of matching the precedent this repository has already committed to disk.

When fetched, polygon_status should move to assigned and this question can close.

### oq-pre1970-liguria-rows

**Where does pre-1970 Liguria-labelled data land, and is it double-counted against ITA national rows**

Under the 1970 convention adopted here, any historical statistical series labelled 'Liguria' before 1970 should fall to whichever national ITA row was current at the time (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, or ITA-1919-2025), since no distinct regional administrative/statistical tier existed before the 1970 council elections. This has not been checked against routing_verdicts.csv to confirm no pre-1970 Liguria-labelled row was already mis-routed to this new entry, nor whether a Kingdom-of-Sardinia-era Liguria administrative unit (Liguria was a Napoleonic-era Ligurian Republic/department before unification, and later a compartimento of the Kingdom of Italy) constitutes an earlier distinct reporting unit that this start_year should account for instead.

### oq-adjacent-region-boundary-overlap

**Modern GADM boundary vs. Liguria's 1970 administrative boundary, and overlap with neighbouring regional rows**

Once fetched, the GADM ADM1 feature will reflect present-day regional boundaries. Italian regional boundaries have been largely stable since 1970 with only minor municipal reassignments between neighbouring regions (Liguria borders Piemonte, Lombardia, Emilia-Romagna, Toscana, and France), so vintage risk is low but unverified against a historical atlas. Separately, this entry has not been cross-checked against any other subnational Italian row that might claim overlapping territory or years — only ITA-PIE-1970-2025 is known to exist as a sibling, and the two do not share territory, but this has not been confirmed against the full wiki/polities directory for every Italian region.
