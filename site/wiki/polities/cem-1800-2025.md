# Ceuta and Melilla

## Summary

The two Spanish *plazas de soberanía* on the North African coast: Ceuta, on the
Strait of Gibraltar, Spanish since 1668 (Portuguese from 1415, retained by Spain
at the Treaty of Lisbon), and Melilla, on the Rif coast, Spanish since 1497.
Both were fortified presidios with their own garrisons, their own customs
treatment and their own trade returns throughout the nineteenth century, and
both are still Spanish today.

This row exists because a source reports the pair as one trading unit and WHEP
had no row for it of any kind. Federico-Tena's *List of trading polities* carries
a polity **`Ceuta Y Melilla`** with an import series running 1800–1938, which is
342 rows of the intake table built by
[prepare_federico_tena.py](../../pipelines/polity-autoimprove/prepare_federico_tena.py).
Measured against `data/final/polities_database.csv` on 2026-08-17, no polity in
the database covered this territory in any year: the label matched nothing by
name, by alias or by ISO code, and no candidate existed to route to. It was the
single largest such gap among the labels Federico-Tena never routes — every
other high-exposure label turned out to have a WHEP polity for at least part of
its window (see [Decisions](#decisions)).

The relationship to Spain is the same one [icn-1800-2025](icn-1800-2025.md) (the
Canary Islands) already has: integral Spanish territory carried as its own WHEP
row because distinct trade statistics are attributed to it. The plazas are not a
colony and never were an overseas province; they are, to this day, outside the
EU customs union and VAT area, so "separately reported customs territory" is not
merely a nineteenth-century fact about them.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source:** GADM 4.1 ADM1, feature `ESP.7_1`, whose `NAME_1` is
  literally *Ceuta y Melilla* — GADM carries the two cities as a single
  first-level unit, so the feature's extent is exactly the reporting unit's
  extent and no composition or union was needed. Measured in the repository's
  equal-area projection the feature is **29.2 km²**; the published municipal
  figures are 18.5 km² for Ceuta and 12.3 km² for Melilla, so 30 km² is
  declared. CShapes 2.0 offers nothing here: it codes sovereign states and
  qualifying dependencies, and the plazas are subsumed in Spain's metropolitan
  polygon (gwcode 230, one step 1886–2019).
  [gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

- **Why `proxy` and not `assigned`.** The modern city limits are POST-1860
  limits. Ceuta's landward boundary was fixed by the Treaty of Wad Ras (1860)
  and Melilla's perimeter was extended to roughly its present 12 km² in 1862,
  after the 1859–60 Hispano-Moroccan war; before those settlements the Spanish
  perimeter at Melilla was a fortified enclave of well under a square kilometre,
  the "cannon-shot" limit. So for the first six decades of this row's span the
  polygon overstates the territory, by a factor that is large in ratio and
  trivial in absolute terms (tens of km²). The polygon is kept as a proxy for
  the whole span rather than split at 1860, because a 12 km² step change is
  below the resolution at which any WHEP consumer uses these areas, and because
  no fetched source offers the pre-1860 perimeters. That limitation is recorded
  here rather than hidden in a split.

- **Nesting.** The feature lies inside the polygon of
  [esp-1800-2025](esp-1800-2025.md) for the whole span, so the two rows overlap
  in space and time by construction. This is the Canary Islands pattern and not
  a defect; a consumer summing WHEP areas over coexisting rows must not treat
  this row as additive to Spain.

## Identifiers

- **`iso3_code` is deliberately EMPTY.** There is no ISO 3166-1 code for the
  plazas; ISO 3166-2 has `ES-CE` and `ES-ML`, which are subdivision codes and do
  not belong in this column. Inventing a local `CEM` value would enlarge the
  published `iso3_code` vocabulary that
  `scripts/validate_local_iso_codes.py` baselines as an interface, for a row no
  ISO-keyed consumer can match anyway. 68 rows already carry an empty
  `iso3_code` for the same reason.
- **`cow_code` is empty.** COW identifies states. The plazas are not a COW
  state-system member and never were; Spain is COW 230. Giving this row 230
  would create exactly the collision that
  `scripts/validate_cow_codes.py` exists to prevent — the mistake already found
  and corrected on the Canary Islands row, which had carried COW 20 (Canada).

## Predecessors and successors

None. Sovereignty over both plazas is continuous across the whole 1800–2025
window, so there is no predecessor to name and no successor to point at. The
row is not a chain segment.

## Sourced claims

- Federico-Tena's *List of trading polities* carries `Ceuta Y Melilla` as a
  distinct trading polity with an import series from 1800; the database is a
  list of reporting units, which is why a unit of 30 km² sits in it beside
  empires. [federico-tena-2019 §polity-granularity](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#polity-granularity)
- The same source page records that a WHEP row must not be created merely
  because a Federico-Tena row exists. The justification offered here is not the
  existence of the F-T row but the territory itself: two continuously Spanish
  enclaves with their own customs treatment, for which WHEP had no
  representation at all. [federico-tena-2019 §known-limitations](../sources/federico-tena-2019.md#known-limitations)
- GADM 4.1 supplies the first-level unit *Ceuta y Melilla* (`ESP.7_1`); GADM is
  the source WHEP already uses for Spanish sub-national territory that CShapes
  does not code separately. [gadm-4.1 §why-it-was-ingested](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#why-it-was-ingested)

## Decisions

- **[2026-08-17] Created, as one row for the pair rather than two.** GADM's own
  first-level unit pools them, Federico-Tena's series pools them, and no source
  fetched into this repository separates their trade. Splitting Ceuta from
  Melilla would produce two rows neither source can populate independently.
- **[2026-08-17] Span 1800–2025 rather than 1800–1939.** The span of a WHEP row
  is the life of the territorial identity, not the coverage of the source that
  prompted it. Both plazas are still Spanish and still a separate customs
  territory, and ending the row at Federico-Tena's horizon would invent a
  discontinuity in 1939 that did not happen.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1860-perimeters

The pre-1860 Ceuta boundary and the pre-1862 Melilla perimeter are not available
from any source fetched into this repository. If one is added, this row should
gain an earlier segment rather than keep a modern proxy for 1800–1860.

### oq-trade-attribution

Whether nineteenth-century Spanish trade returns for the plazas are net of
re-export to Morocco is not established here. Federico-Tena's compiler note for
the unit was not transcribed into this page, and the repository stages the
source's documentation only, not its values, so no magnitude claim can be
checked against it.
