# West Bank (Jordanian-annexed)

## Summary

This row tracks the **West Bank** during the period of **Jordanian
annexation (1950–1967)**. The West Bank — the portion of Mandatory
Palestine west of the Jordan River that Transjordanian/Jordanian forces
held after the 1948 Arab–Israeli War — was formally annexed by the
Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan on **1950-04-24** via a resolution of the
Jordanian parliament. Jordan renamed itself from "Transjordan" to
"Jordan" to reflect the enlarged territory. The annexation was
recognized by only the United Kingdom and Pakistan among major states;
the Arab League suspended Jordan temporarily over it. The West Bank
remained under Jordanian administration until the **Six-Day War
(1967-06-05 to 1967-06-10)**, when Israeli forces captured the
territory. This row ends on **1967-06-09** (the date CShapes 2.0
records as the close of the West Bank feature).

This row exists because **IIA (International Institute of Agriculture)
yearbook footnotes for 1953** report West Bank data as **folded into
Jordan** ("composed_union"): the host country Jordan's figures include
the West Bank's agricultural statistics rather than recording them
separately. The WHEP database requires a distinct entity to track this
territory's inclusion so that the composed-union relationship can be
represented explicitly.

Predecessor: [pal-1920-1948](pal-1920-1948.md) (British Mandatory
Palestine, which dissolved in 1948). Successor: [isr-1967-1979](isr-1967-1979.md)
(Israel, which administered the West Bank from 1967; the row closest
to the territorial handover in the WHEP chain).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` (Schvitz et al. 2022), feature
  `gwcode=6631`, "West Bank", dated **1948-05-14 to 1967-06-09**.
  Area: **5,871 km²** (as recorded in CShapes).
- **Method.** `exact_historical` — CShapes 2.0 includes the West Bank
  as a distinct feature for precisely this period, coinciding with
  Jordanian control (the feature begins at the end of the British
  Mandate, 1948-05-14, and ends with Israeli capture, 1967-06-09).
  The CShapes feature covers the period 1948–1967, which contains the
  full WHEP row period 1950–1967; the feature_year 1953 (the IIA
  footnote year) falls within the feature's coverage window.
- **Area.** ~5,871 km² (CShapes 2.0 recorded value). This is
  consistent with standard published land area estimates for the West
  Bank (commonly cited as ~5,655–5,878 km² depending on measurement
  methodology and whether the Dead Sea shoreline is included).
- **Confidence.** High. CShapes 2.0 explicitly models the West Bank
  as a distinct territorial unit with gwcode 6631, covering the
  Jordanian-administered period. The polygon faithfully represents
  the territory's extent during 1950–1967.
- **Note on Jordan polygon.** As noted in
  [jor-1946-2025](jor-1946-2025.md), CShapes 2.0 assigns Jordan
  (gwcode 663) a constant area of ~89,262 km² throughout its existence,
  which appears not to include the West Bank. The West Bank is modelled
  separately by CShapes under gwcode 6631. This confirms that the two
  entities require separate WHEP rows.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [pal-1920-1948](pal-1920-1948.md) — British
  Mandatory Palestine, which ended on **1948-05-14** with the
  termination of the British Mandate. The West Bank territory was part
  of Mandatory Palestine until that date. Jordanian forces entered the
  West Bank during the 1948 war; formal annexation followed in 1950.
- **Successor.** [isr-1967-1979](isr-1967-1979.md) — Israel captured
  the West Bank in June 1967 and administered it as occupied territory.
  The ISR-1967-1979 row is the closest WHEP successor; no separate
  "occupied West Bank" row currently exists for the post-1967 period.

## Contradictions

- The CShapes feature for the West Bank begins **1948-05-14** (end of
  Mandate) rather than **1950-04-24** (formal annexation). The two-year
  gap (1948–1950) represents de facto Jordanian military control without
  formal annexation. The WHEP row start_year of 1950 reflects formal
  annexation; the polygon feature predates it by approximately two years.
  This is a minor inconsistency: the polygon is still faithful to the
  territorial extent, even if its start date is slightly earlier than the
  WHEP row's formal start.
- Jordan's CShapes polygon (gwcode 663, ~89,262 km²) does not visibly
  increase when the West Bank is annexed in 1950, confirming CShapes
  models the two territories separately throughout. This is consistent
  with the IIA footnote treatment.

## Decisions

- **polity_code.** `WBK-1950-1967`. "WBK" is a compact three-letter
  abbreviation for West Bank (no ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code exists for the
  West Bank under Jordanian annexation; "PSE" refers to the post-Oslo
  Palestinian Authority, not applicable here). Start year 1950 =
  formal annexation; end year 1967 = Israeli capture.
- **type.** Assigned `colonial` rather than `national`: the West Bank
  was annexed territory administered by Jordan but not a sovereign
  state; it had no separate international legal personality.
- **iso3.** Assigned `JOR` — the administering power during this
  period was Jordan.
- **cow.** CShapes 2.0 assigns gwcode 6631 to the West Bank. This
  does not correspond to a COW state-system member (Jordan's COW code
  is 663). The value 6631 is used here as the CShapes polygon
  identifier.
- **Polygon selection.** CShapes 2.0 gwcode=6631 is an exact
  historical match. Cliopatria, paine-2024, and gadm-4.1-adm1 were
  not needed.
- **Successor choice.** ISR-1967-1979 is selected as successor
  because Israel administered the West Bank from 1967; no separate
  "occupied West Bank 1967–" row exists in WHEP at this time.
- **Composed_union relationship.** The spec records this as
  `composed_union` with host=Jordan: West Bank data is included in
  Jordan's IIA 1953 figures. This row creates the separate WHEP entity
  so the inclusion relationship can be tracked.
- **WHEP period vs. CShapes period.** The CShapes feature runs
  1948–1967; the WHEP row runs 1950–1967. The polygon_feature_year
  1953 (the IIA footnote year) sits well inside both windows. Using
  the CShapes feature is correct despite the two-year offset at the
  start.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1950-west-bank

The period **1948-05-14 to 1950-04-24** — between the end of the
British Mandate and formal Jordanian annexation — is not covered by
the current WHEP row (start_year=1950). CShapes includes the West
Bank under gwcode 6631 from 1948-05-14. Consider whether a separate
short row for the interim period (Jordanian military administration
without formal annexation) is warranted, or whether the PAL-1920-1948
successor end date should extend to 1950.

### oq-successor-west-bank-post-1967

No dedicated WHEP row for the **occupied West Bank (1967–1993)** or
the **Palestinian Authority West Bank (1993–present)** currently
exists. ISR-1967-1979 is used as successor here as the closest
available row, but it covers all of Israel/occupied territories, not
the West Bank specifically. If a separate Palestinian territory chain
is created (e.g., PAL-1993-2025), this successor field should be
updated.

### oq-arab-league-non-recognition

The Jordanian annexation was recognized by only the UK and Pakistan.
The Arab League suspended Jordan temporarily. Whether WHEP should
model this as a "disputed annexation" or simply as de facto
administration is a policy question for the WHEP editorial board.
