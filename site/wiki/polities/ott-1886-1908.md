# Ottoman Empire (1886–1908)

## Summary

This row tracks the Ottoman Empire as a territorial-economic unit
from **1886** to **1908**. Both boundaries are artefacts of data
availability more than historical events:

- **1886 start** is the CShapes 2.0 temporal floor
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope), not a territorial change. The actual
  territorial state of the empire in 1886 is the post-1878
  Congress of Berlin configuration — the Berlin settlement took
  effect eight years earlier, in 1878
  [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878). WHEP carries a separate
  row [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md) for the Cliopatria-sourced pre-1886
  window. See [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory](#oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory) on
  [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md) for the audit.
- **1908 end** is a real territorial event: on 5 October 1908
  Austria-Hungary formally annexed Bosnia and Herzegovina (which
  it had been occupying under a Congress of Berlin mandate since
  1878), Bulgaria declared independence, and Crete declared
  union with Greece [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1908). These
  three simultaneous events ended what remained of the 1878 de
  facto settlement. The WHEP split is here for a real reason.

Predecessor: [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md). Successor: [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV (`polygon_source`).
  WHEP uses the COW variant of CShapes 2.0 — see
  `log.md 2026-04-11 decision-cshapes-is-cow-based`. CShapes
  records the Ottoman Empire under `cowcode=640` for this period;
  the polygon reflects the **post-1878** territorial
  configuration (Romania, Serbia, Montenegro, Bulgaria autonomous
  and carved out; Cyprus under British administration;
  Bosnia-Herzegovina nominally Ottoman but under Austro-Hungarian
  occupation) [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878).
- **The "Bosnia ambiguity" 1886–1908 — resolved.** Under the
  Berlin settlement Bosnia was legally Ottoman but
  administratively Austro-Hungarian. CShapes codes Bosnia
  (`cowcode=3461`) and Herzegovina (`cowcode=3462`) as
  **separate polygons** with `status: occupied` and
  `owner: 300` (Austria-Hungary) for 1886-01-01 to 1908-10-06
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The Ottoman polygon (`cowcode=640`)
  **excludes** them. Evidence: the Ottoman area drops by ~10.28
  sq degrees at 1908-10-05, matching Bulgaria's newly
  independent polygon exactly (`cowcode=355`, 10.28 sq degrees);
  Austria-Hungary's polygon grows by ~5.80 sq degrees at
  1908-10-07, matching Bosnia (4.68) + Herzegovina (1.12)
  combined. **No double-counting** between `OTT-1886-1908` and
  `BOS-1878-1908` / `HZG-1878-1908` in WHEP — the polygons are
  non-overlapping. See [log autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count](../log.md#autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count).
- **1886 "as-of" date interpretation.** Even though 1886 is not
  a territorial event, the row's polygon *as of 1886* is a
  correct representation of Ottoman borders *at 1886*. The
  problem is not the polygon itself but that it is also used
  as a proxy for the preceding row (`OTT-1800-1886`) via
  Cliopatria back-projection; see [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1878 (inherited from predecessor row).** The Congress of
  Berlin reshaped Ottoman borders: Austria-Hungary gained the
  right to "occupy Bosnia and Herzegovina"; Britain took Cyprus;
  Romania, Serbia, and Montenegro were formally recognized as
  independent; Bulgaria became an autonomous principality under
  Ottoman suzerainty [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878).
  This is the *state* of the Ottoman Empire at the start of
  this row, not an event within it.
- **1881-05-12: French protectorate over Tunisia (Treaty of
  Bardo).** "Tunisia was likewise a province of the Ottoman
  Empire in the nineteenth century, until French protection
  was imposed by force by a treaty signed on 12 May 1881"
  [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia). Biger's ALGERIA–TUNISIA subsection
  (p.28) provides the exact treaty date. Tunisia was an
  Ottoman province from 1564 and semi-autonomous from the 17th
  century [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia). Its exit five years before
  this row's 1886 start means the Ottoman polygon for 1886
  onward correctly excludes Tunisia, but the Cliopatria 1800
  polygon used for the predecessor row [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md)
  probably still includes it — the Tunisia exit is a
  predecessor-row problem, not a this-row problem.
- **1882: British occupation of Egypt.** Same status — a
  territorial fact about Ottoman sovereignty that precedes this
  row's 1886 start, relevant for the predecessor row. Not
  captured in the current Wikipedia snapshot; see
  [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md) and open questions there.
  [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia)
- **1885: Bulgarian unification with Eastern Rumelia.** Also
  pre-1886 and thus not covered by this row, but materially
  affects what the CShapes 1886 polygon shows. Not captured in
  the current Wikipedia snapshot.
  [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia)
- **1908 (end of row): three simultaneous events.** "Bulgarian
  Declaration of Independence on 5 October"; Austria-Hungary
  formally annexed Bosnia-Herzegovina; Crete declared union
  with Greece [wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1908](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1908). These are
  the events that trigger the split to [ott-1908-1912](ott-1908-1912.md). In
  territorial terms, the annexation of Bosnia formalized what
  had been de facto since 1878; the Bulgarian independence
  upgraded an autonomous principality to a sovereign state.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists a single continuous
  tenure for `TUR, 640` from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). COW makes no split at
  the 1886, 1908, 1912, or even 1923 transitions. The 1886
  boundary of this WHEP row has no counterpart in COW at all;
  the 1908 boundary is also not marked in COW. See the
  parallel discussion on [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md).
- **CShapes polygon convention for occupied territories.** CShapes
  codes Bosnia (`cowcode=3461`) and Herzegovina (`cowcode=3462`)
  as separate polygons with `status: occupied` by
  Austria-Hungary (`owner: 300`), not as part of the Ottoman
  sovereign polygon [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope). The Ottoman polygon
  (`cowcode=640`) at 1886-01-01 has area ~283 sq degrees; it
  drops by ~10.28 sq degrees at 1908-10-05 (matching Bulgaria's
  independence, not Bosnia's annexation). Austria-Hungary's
  polygon (`cowcode=300`) grows by ~5.80 sq degrees at
  1908-10-07, exactly matching Bosnia + Herzegovina combined.
  See [log autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count](../log.md#autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count).
- **WHEP continuity model.** Under the WHEP polity definition
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition), the 1908
  end-of-row is a legitimate split (substantial territorial
  change — Bosnia annexation + Bulgaria independence + Cretan
  union). The 1886 start is the problematic boundary; see
  [oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory](#oq-1886-split-is-polygon-not-territory) on [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md).
  [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia)

## Open questions


### oq-bosnia-double-count

**Resolved 2026-04-12** by [log autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count](../log.md#autonomous-1-bosnia-double-count).
CShapes **excludes** Bosnia and Herzegovina from the Ottoman
polygon during 1886–1908. `ogrinfo` on `cshapes2_full.gpkg`
confirms: Bosnia (`cowcode=3461`, 4.68 sq deg) and Herzegovina
(`cowcode=3462`, 1.12 sq deg) are coded as separate polygons
with `status: occupied`, `owner: 300` (Austria-Hungary). The
Ottoman polygon's 10.28 sq-degree drop at 1908-10-05 matches
Bulgaria's independence polygon exactly — not Bosnia. No
double-counting between `OTT-1886-1908` and `BOS-1878-1908`.

### oq-tunisia-boundary

**Resolved 2026-04-11** by [log biger-ottoman-batch-ingest](../log.md#biger-ottoman-batch-ingest).
Biger 1995 gives the exact date: "French protection was
imposed by force by a treaty signed on 12 May 1881"
[biger-1995 §algeria](../sources/biger-1995.md#algeria) (p.28, ALGERIA–TUNISIA subsection),
corroborated by [biger-1995 §tunisia](../sources/biger-1995.md#tunisia) (main TUNISIA entry,
p.494) and [biger-1995 §libya](../sources/biger-1995.md#libya) (LIBYA–TUNISIA subsection,
p.361). Tunisia was an Ottoman province from 1564 and
semi-autonomous from the 17th century, and became a French
protectorate via the **Treaty of Bardo signed 12 May 1881**.
Five years before this row's 1886 start. The CShapes Ottoman
polygon for 1886 onward therefore correctly excludes Tunisia;
the issue is in the predecessor row [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md), whose
Cliopatria 1800 polygon almost certainly includes Tunisia.
