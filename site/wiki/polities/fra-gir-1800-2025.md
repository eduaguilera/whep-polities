# Gironde (département)

## Summary

Gironde is a French département in the southwest, on the Atlantic coast around the Gironde estuary, containing Bordeaux as its capital and historic core of the Bordeaux wine trade. It is one of the original ~83 départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary administrative reform that replaced the old provinces (Gironde corresponds roughly to part of the former province of Guyenne). This entry is `type: subnational` because a département is a sub-unit of the French state, never itself sovereign — it sits inside the national FRA chain in the containment hierarchy rather than beside it, and Gironde was not one of the small set of French départements (Alsace-Lorraine's, Territoire de Belfort, the Corsican split, the overseas départements) whose own borders or national attachment changed between 1790 and today. It is created here because no existing FRA polity row represents anything below the national level, and per the routing decision, national FRA totals are not a substitute for a série reported at département scale.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures the routing decision for unit_id FRA-FRI12 (admin_name FRI12, country France) — a proposal to create a polity for the département of Gironde. No row/year-count of source data rows is given in the routing decision itself (it describes a candidate unit from an admin boundary/routing pipeline, not a specific ingested data table), but the decision states the unit needs its own polity because prior to this entry there was no French sub-national polity at all: the only FRA candidates in the table are the four national-scale rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025, and the superseded FRA-1800-1919). Any data keyed to Gironde specifically would previously have had no correctly-scaled polity to match against — only the national total, which is roughly two orders of magnitude larger in territory. What confirms Gironde is a distinct, real administrative entity rather than an arbitrary GIS polygon is that it is one of the original ~83 départements created by the 1790 Revolutionary reform and has been continuously administered as such since, per the routing decision's span_basis reasoning, which is not among the four documented departures from that continuity (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine's départements, the Corsican split, or the overseas départements).

**Why this entry exists:** it captures a statistical series reported at the level of the French département named Gironde (unit id `FRA-FRI12` in the routing decision, admin_name `FRI12`), which prior to this entry had no polity to route to other than the national French rows (`FRA-1800-1871`/`FRA-1871-1919`/`FRA-1919-2025`) — a mismatch of roughly two orders of magnitude in territory (a département is one of ~96 metropolitan French départements). The routing decision's own reasoning is that Gironde is a real, continuously-existing administrative unit since 1790, distinct from every national-scale row, and that no other candidate polity in the table represents it.

**Territory a reader can locate:** Gironde is the largest département in metropolitan France by area, in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region, bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the west, the Landes and Lot-et-Garonne départements to the south and east, and Dordogne and Charente-Maritime to the north. Its modern area is approximately **10,000 km²** (commonly cited as 9,976-10,000 km² for the present-day département), centered on the city of Bordeaux at the confluence of the Garonne and Dordogne rivers forming the Gironde estuary. No measurement is attached here since no polygon is assigned; the ~10,000 km² figure is drawn from present-day administrative reference data (INSEE/IGN), not from any geometry in this repository, and should be treated as an approximation pending the GADM fetch described above and pending the boundary-stability check raised in the open questions.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Start year set to 1800, not the proposed 1790**

The routing decision's default-start rule gives Gironde 1790, the year of the Revolutionary départements reform. But the polity table's FRA container chain — FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025 (the superseded FRA-1800-1919 duplicate aside) — begins at 1800, ten years later. The containment gate rejects any container edge that falls outside either party's own span, so a Gironde row spanning 1790-2025 would leave 1790-1800 with no valid container edge to attach. Rather than fabricate a container edge the polity table does not support, this entry's start_year is set to 1800 to match the earliest available FRA container era. The code is FRA-GIR-1800-2025 accordingly, per the rule that the years in the code must equal the years in the frontmatter. This is a data-availability constraint, not a claim that Gironde as a département did not exist 1790-1800 — it did, under the same 1790 Revolutionary reform cited in the routing decision — and is flagged as an open question below in case a pre-1800 FRA container is added later.

### d-subunit-code-gir

**Subunit code chosen as GIR, not a GADM-derived code**

The dominant subnational pattern is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (57 of 66 rows), e.g. ESP-CA-1833-2025. No GADM feature is locally available for France (see territorial extent), so there is no GID_1-style code to borrow the way Spanish provinces borrow their INE-derived two-letter codes. GIR is a plain abbreviation of Gironde chosen for legibility; it carries no external-source meaning and should be treated as this repository's own label, not a code copied from GADM or INSEE.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1800-container

**No FRA container edge exists for 1790-1800**

Gironde has existed as a département, under that name and (per the routing decision's span_basis) essentially in its 1790 Revolutionary-reform form, since 1790. The polity table's earliest active FRA national row is FRA-1800-1871; nothing in the table covers France 1790-1800 as a container. This entry therefore starts at 1800 rather than 1790 (see decisions), and the ten years 1790-1800 are simply not represented for Gironde at all. If a pre-1800 FRA national polity is ever added, this entry's start_year and container list should be revisited to extend back to 1790.

### oq-no-polygon-fetched

**No polygon is attached and GADM's France coverage is unverified as stable**

The routing decision flags that gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered in scripts/sources.yaml with the right schema (id_column=GID_1) but the locally present file is a curated 81-country subset that excludes France entirely (0 features for FRA). Someone needs to either re-fetch/extend that source to include France, or find an alternative admin-1 source for French départements, before this entry can move from polygon_status: unassigned to assigned. Separately, even once fetched, GADM 4.1 reflects present-day département boundaries; whether Gironde's boundary has actually been stable since 1790 is asserted by the routing decision as 'plausible' but was not independently verified there, and should be checked (e.g. against a historical INSEE or Cassini-map boundary source) before treating a modern GADM polygon as valid for the full 1800-2025 span.

### oq-departement-reform-history

**Whether any 19th/20th-century boundary adjustment touched Gironde is unchecked**

The routing decision lists four documented departures from département continuity since 1790 (Belfort split from Haut-Rhin in 1871, the Alsace-Lorraine départements' 1871-1919 loss, Corsica's 1975 north/south split, and the overseas départements), and asserts Gironde is not among them. That list was not exhaustively cross-checked against every minor arrondissement/canton reorganization Gironde may have undergone (e.g. any 19th-century canton boundary tidying with INSEE). No specific contradicting event was found, but the check was a name-matching exercise against a short list, not a dedicated boundary-history search for Gironde specifically.
