# Germany (1938-1945)

## Summary

This row tracks Nazi Germany for **7 years**, from the Anschluss with Austria (13 March 1938) to total military defeat and unconditional surrender (8 May 1945). It covers radical, multi-step territorial changes: expansion through annexations and occupation 1938-1942 followed by total contraction and occupation 1943-1945.

The row has 266 data rows in Layer B. Staple magnitudes — wheat ~4,122,500 t and sugar ~6,877,900 t — are consistent with the 1939-1942 Greater Germany which incorporated major Czech and Polish sugar-beet regions, and are significantly higher than the Weimar-era (DEU-1920-1938) levels as expected from the annexations.

The 1945 end date marks complete military defeat and Allied occupation — Germany ceased to exist as a sovereign state until the Federal Republic and German Democratic Republic were established in 1949. COW code 260 is continuous throughout [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` feature 255, COW code 260, polygon_feature_year 1938 [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md). This is the post-Anschluss polygon of Germany-plus-Austria. **Area ~541,000 km²** (Germany ~470,000 km² + Austria ~84,000 km²; Sudetenland ~28,000 km² added October 1938 is also in this polygon per CShapes feature year 1938).
- **CShapes wartime exclusion.** CShapes 2.0 explicitly excludes wartime territorial changes, recording only changes "made permanent in treaties signed after the war" [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes). This means there is **no CShapes polygon for Greater Germany 1939-1942** (Protectorate of Bohemia-Moravia, Danzig/West Prussia, Alsace-Lorraine, etc.) nor for the contraction phase 1943-1945. The entire 1938-1945 period uses a single 1938-vintage polygon.
- **Polygon approximation — direction and magnitude (required disclosure):**
  - **1938-1939 (Anschluss + Sudetenland phase):** The 1938 CShapes polygon covers this phase accurately. Approximation error: minimal.
  - **1939-1942 (maximum expansion phase):** Germany annexed the Protectorate of Bohemia-Moravia (~49,000 km²) in March 1939, Memel (~2,400 km²) in March 1939, and incorporated Danzig (~1,900 km²) and much of western Poland (Reichsgau Danzig-West Prussia ~26,000 km², Reichsgau Wartheland ~44,000 km²) after September 1939. Alsace-Lorraine (~14,000 km²) and Luxembourg (~2,600 km²) were incorporated administratively 1940-1941. **The 1938 polygon (~541,000 km²) understates the incorporated territory by approximately 100,000-200,000 km²** for data years 1939-1942. This is consistent with the elevated sugar/wheat staple magnitudes (Czech Bohemia-Moravia and incorporated Polish regions were major agricultural producers). Data rows for years 1939-1942 carry systematic polygon_vintage_drift in the under-coverage direction.
  - **1943-1945 (contraction phase):** German-administered territory shrank rapidly from late 1942 onward. By 1944-1945 the effective territory was approaching or below the 1938 polygon boundaries. The 1938 polygon **overstates** actual territory for these years. Data rows for 1943-1945 carry polygon_vintage_drift in the over-coverage direction.
- **Why a split is deferred.** A split into DEU-1938-1939, DEU-1939-1942, and DEU-1942-1945 is the correct long-term design. It is blocked by the absence of a reliable polygon source for Greater Germany at peak expansion: CShapes excludes wartime changes; Cliopatria covers pre-1886 periods; no other ingested dataset (GADM, Paine et al.) covers this configuration. A composed-union polygon (Germany 1938 + Protectorate + Wartheland + Danzig-West Prussia + Alsace-Lorraine) could in principle be constructed from subnational boundary data but no such construction has been attempted. Splitting without a polygon would only shift the problem without resolving it. Reopen when a composed-union polygon for the 1939-1942 phase is constructed.

## Sourced claims

- **1938-03-13: Anschluss.** Austria absorbed into Nazi Germany. Start of row. ~84,000 km² added. [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1938-09-30: Munich Agreement.** Germany gained the Sudetenland (~28,000 km²). Incorporated into 1938 CShapes polygon. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1939-03-15: Protectorate of Bohemia-Moravia.** Germany occupied and incorporated Bohemia and Moravia (~49,000 km²). [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1939-03-22: Annexation of Memel.** ~2,400 km² from Lithuania. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1939-09-01: Invasion of Poland / start of WWII.** Resulted in annexation of Danzig (~1,900 km²), Reichsgau Danzig-West Prussia (~26,000 km²), Reichsgau Wartheland (~44,000 km²) as incorporated territory; remainder of occupied Poland treated as occupied territory, not incorporated sovereign territory. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1940-1941: Administrative incorporation of western territories.** Alsace-Lorraine (~14,000 km²) and Luxembourg (~2,600 km²) incorporated administratively. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1942-1945: Progressive contraction.** Eastern Front reverses from late 1942; Western Front from June 1944; Soviet advance into Germany from early 1945. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **1945-05-08: Unconditional surrender.** Germany ceased to exist as a sovereign state. Allied occupation began. [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany)
- **Post-1945: division into zones.** Biger: "After World War II Germany was laid waste and occupied by the Allied powers. They divided it into Western and Eastern zones" [biger-1995 §germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#germany).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 260, continuous from 1816-01-01 to 2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

### oq-territorial-expansion-1938-1942

**Status: researched, documented, split deferred.**

Germany's 1939-1942 incorporated-territory additions (Protectorate ~49,000 km²; Danzig + incorporated Polish Reichsgaue ~72,000 km²; Alsace-Lorraine + Luxembourg ~17,000 km²) total approximately 138,000 km² above the 1938 CShapes polygon. CShapes explicitly excludes wartime territorial changes [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes), so no better polygon is available within current ingested sources. The polygon approximation — under-coverage ~100,000-200,000 km² for 1939-1942 data; over-coverage for 1943-1945 data — is documented in Territorial extent above. A split into three sub-period polities is the correct long-term design but is deferred pending a composed-union polygon for the 1939-1942 phase.
