# Kenya (1926-1963)

## Summary

Kenya Colony, the British colonial polity governing present-day Kenya, from a 1926 boundary change (transfer of the Rudolf Province from Uganda) until independence on 12 December 1963. "Kenya was declared a British protectorate in 1895" [biger-1995 kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya). "KANU won the elections and Kenyatta led Kenya to independence in December 1963" [biger-1995 kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya). The 1926 start date reflects the protectorate order that transferred the Rudolf Province from Uganda to Kenya. CShapes codes it as a British colony (ruler=GBR).
