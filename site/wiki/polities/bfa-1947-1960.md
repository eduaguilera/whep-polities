# Burkina Faso (1947-1960)

## Summary

The reconstituted colony of Upper Volta (present-day Burkina Faso) within French West Africa, from its re-establishment on 4 September 1947 to independence on 5 August 1960. Upper Volta had originally been created in 1919 but was dissolved and divided among neighboring colonies on 5 September 1932. "On 4 September 1947 it was reconstituted and became a French territory of the French Union" [biger-1995 burkina-faso](../sources/biger-1995.md#burkina-faso). CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
