# Syria and Lebanon (French Mandate, combined reporting unit, 1920-1944)

## Summary

The territory of the French Mandate for Syria and the Lebanon, taken as one reporting unit: the
Syrian mandate states and Greater Lebanon, which France administered together from 1920 (the
Battle of Maysalun and the proclamation of Greater Lebanon) under one High Commissioner in Beirut,
with a common currency and customs administration.
[wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16 §Articles referenced](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md#articles-referenced)

It is the mandate-era predecessor of [syl-1944-1953](syl-1944-1953.md), the joint "Syria and
Lebanon" unit of the FAO 1952 yearbook, and like it is typed `aggregate`, because both parts have
rows of their own ([syr-1920-1922](syr-1920-1922.md), [syr-1922-1946](syr-1922-1946.md),
[lbn-1920-1944](lbn-1920-1944.md)).

## Why this entry exists

Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics: Africa, Asia and Oceania* footnotes several of its
Syria columns as including Lebanon (issue 688). Five of them are moved here:

| series | footnote | rows moved |
|---|---|---|
| olive oil, 1925-1939 | C4 note 5: to 1939 (1st line) | 15 |
| sheep, 1922-1938 | C11 note 7: to 1938 (1st line) for sheep | 17 |
| goats, 1922-1939 | C11 note 7: to 1939 (1st line) for goats | 18 |
| wheat output, 1920-1940 (tonnes) | C2 note 17: "Including Lebanon to 1940" | 21 |
| barley output, 1920-1940 (tonnes) | C2 note 17 | 21 |

The first 50 rows had been routed to [syr-1922-1946](syr-1922-1946.md), whose polygon is Syria
without Lebanon, about 5% smaller than the territory counted. Of the 42 output rows, 38 (1922-1940)
were on SYR-1922-1946 and 4 (1920-1921) on [syr-1920-1922](syr-1920-1922.md). All are moved by
corrections in `data/final/source_label_item_corrections.csv`, since the label's other series are
Syria alone. The wheat and barley rules are scoped `unit=tonnes` (added 2026-09-25): see below.

**FAO 1952's pre-war joint figures.** FAO's `Syria and Lebanon` label carries a 1934-1938 tobacco
average (area and production, 2 rows) that the 1944-1953 alias of SYL-1944-1953 could not reach, so
it had resolved to no polity. It now routes here by alias.

**Wheat and barley: output moved, area not** (moved 2026-09-25). C2 note 17 ("Including Lebanon to
1940") is on the OUTPUT table only. Mitchell's wheat and barley AREA rows for 1922-1938 (C1,
`page_31_table_1`, ha) carry no such note, yet share source, label, item and year with the output
rows, so a plain item correction would have moved them too. The correction table's optional `unit`
column scopes the two rules to `tonnes`, and the area rows stay on SYR-1922-1946. Control: Mitchell's
own `lebanon` label carries no wheat or barley output, and its wheat area begins in 1941, the year
after the note stops applying, so nothing lands here twice. **Scope caveat for 1939-1940:** those
two output years also carry C2 note 18, "Excluding Lattakia and Jebal Druze" (the two states France
detached from Syria in 1939), so they count Syria and Lebanon less those two states. That is
narrower than this polygon, but closer to it than to SYR-1922-1946, which leaves out Lebanon and
includes both states.

**The seam years.** Each footnote says the Lebanon-inclusive series is the *first line*, ending in
1938 or 1939. Layer B holds one value per year, so for the boundary year it is not recorded which
line was captured. The spans follow the footnotes (issue 688's own counts), so the boundary year is
taken to be the first line.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the union of CShapes 2.0 gwcode 652 (Syria,
1922-1945 step, 188,004 km²) and gwcode 660 (Lebanon, 1920-1944 step, 10,209 km²), both sampled at
1930. The union measures **198,213 km²** (ESRI:54034), the same ground as SYL-1944-1953, because
CShapes draws neither border as moving between 1922 and 1953.
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

- **Against the IIA's own stated area.** The IIA yearbooks state 203,000 km² for `Syrie et Liban` at data
  years 1925, 1929, 1932, 1933 and 1938 (`data/final/source_stated_area_basis.csv`): the union is 2.3%
  below that, and the verdict there is `agrees`.
- **Hatay.** The Sanjak of Alexandretta was part of the Syrian mandate until its cession to Turkey
  in 1939 (roughly 5,000 km², about 2.5% of this union). CShapes' 652 step does not include it at any date
  in 1922-1945, and SYR-1922-1946 has the same convention, so this row follows it rather than
  inventing a split the rest of the Syria family does not have.
- **1920-1922.** CShapes' first 1920 Syria step (182,452 km²) is slightly smaller. Most rows moved here
  begin in 1922. The wheat and barley output rows begin in 1920, so four of them (1920-1921) fall in
  the years of that smaller step. The union is sampled at 1930 and is not re-cut for them, because
  the difference is under 3% of the union.

## Polygon status

`assigned` — constructed union of two CShapes 2.0 features, 198,213 km².

**Declared area.** `polygon_area_km2` is the IIA yearbooks' own figure for `Syrie et Liban`
(203,000 km², data years 1925-1938), not the polygon's, so the build's area check compares the
polygon against a source: 198,213 km² measured, 2.3% below it.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none. The Ottoman vilayets before 1920 are carried by the Ottoman rows.
- **Successor:** [syl-1944-1953](syl-1944-1953.md), the same joint unit after Lebanese independence.

## Decisions

- **Span 1920-1944**: from the mandate to Lebanon's independence, where SYL-1944-1953 begins.
- **A new segment rather than re-spanning SYL-1944-1953.** A polity code carries its start year, so
  extending that row back would re-code it and every alias and page that names it.
