# France (with Savoy, Nice and Alsace-Lorraine, 1860-1871)

## Summary

This row tracks France from **1860** (Treaty of Turin) to **1871**
(Treaty of Frankfurt): the last eleven years of the Second Empire and the
first months of the Third Republic. It is the only period in which France
held both the territory annexed from Sardinia in 1860 (Savoy and the
County of Nice) and Alsace-Lorraine, lost to Germany in 1871.

- **1860 start** is a real territorial event: the Treaty of Turin
  (24 March 1860) ceded Savoy and Nice from the Kingdom of Sardinia to
  France, confirmed by plebiscites in April and transferred on 14 June
  1860 [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy)
  [wikipedia-france-2026-04-12 §1860-nice-savoy](../sources/wikipedia-france-2026-04-12.md#1860-nice-savoy).
  A handover year belongs to the incoming polity here, so 1860 is this
  row's first year.
- **1871 end** is a real territorial event: the Treaty of Frankfurt
  (10 May 1871) ceded Alsace and part of Lorraine to Germany
  [biger-1995 §france-germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-germany).

**Why this entry exists.** It was split out of the former `FRA-1800-1871`
on 2026-09-25. That row held the 1816 polygon for all 71 years, so every
French national value for 1860-1870 was measured against a territory
13,631 km² (2.5%) smaller than the one it reports. This row receives
those **166 layer-B rows** (juan and mitchell `france`, 1860-1870; 147
earlier rows stay with [fra-1800-1860](fra-1800-1860.md)), and it is the
container for the three départements created in 1860.

Predecessor: [fra-1800-1860](fra-1800-1860.md).
Successor: [fra-1871-1919](fra-1871-1919.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes-Europe` feature 220, the step dated
  1861-1870 (polygon_status=assigned). CShapes-Europe is year-grained and
  opens the enlarged France in 1861, the year of the 7 March 1861
  boundary convention; this row starts in 1860, the year of the cession.
  No other boundary change is recorded between the two, so the 1861 step
  is the 1860 territory.
- **Area.** `polygon_area_km2` 546,414 is CShapes-Europe's own stated
  `Area` for the step. Measured planar in `ESRI:54034` the source step is
  547,636 km² and the published (simplified) polygon 547,642 km² (+0.22%,
  the same offset as the 1816 step).
- **What changed at 1860, measured.** The 1861 step is a strict superset
  of the 1816 step (0 km² of the 1816 polygon lies outside it), and the
  difference is **13,631 km²**. Against GADM 4.1 it falls
  4,733 km² in Haute-Savoie, 5,745 km² in Savoie and 2,623 km² in
  Alpes-Maritimes (2,331 km² in the arrondissement of Nice, 288 km² in
  Grasse). The remaining 539 km² lies along the Rhône, Lake Geneva and
  the Alpine border, where the generalised CShapes line and GADM's
  modern one disagree. All 13,631 km² lie inside CShapes-Europe's
  1816-1860 Piedmont polygon, so the gain is exactly the ceded Sardinian
  territory.
- **Containment of the 1860 départements** (published polygons).
  Savoie is 97.8% inside this row (6,181 of 6,319 km²), Haute-Savoie
  96.9% (4,738 of 4,889) and Alpes-Maritimes 96.4% (4,180 of 4,335).
  Under the former binding to the 1816 polygon the same figures were
  7.1%, 0% and 35.6%, i.e. the three départements' container did not
  contain them.
- **Known overstatement: Tende and La Brigue.** The generalised Alpine
  line in this step puts Tende and La Brigue (upper Roya valley, a few
  hundred km²) inside France. They stayed Italian until the 1947 Treaty
  of Paris. Left as the source draws it; direction: overstate, magnitude
  under 0.1% of the row.
- **Monaco.** Menton and Roquebrune, bought from Monaco on 2 February
  1861, are inside this step (a point at 7.48 E, 43.80 N tests inside);
  Monaco itself is a hole in it and is its own row
  ([mco-1800-2025](mco-1800-2025.md)).
- **Neighbour after 1860.** Italy from 1861
  ([ita-1861-1866](ita-1861-1866.md)) overlaps this polygon by 13 km²,
  boundary noise.

## Sourced claims

- **1860-03-24: Treaty of Turin.** Biger: "the historic Italian region of
  Savoy and the town of Nice were given to France, which helped the
  Italians in their war of independence against the Austrian Empire"
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
  Start-of-row event.
- **1861-03-07: boundary convention.** A "subsequent convention of 7 March
  1861 created a boundary that departed from the details of the
  originally projected alignment"
  [biger-1995 §france-italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-italy).
- **1871-05-10: Treaty of Frankfurt.** Biger: "the 1870-1871 war... placed
  the region of Alsace-Lorraine in the hands of the victorious Germany in
  1871" [biger-1995 §france-germany](../sources/biger-1995.md#france-germany).
  End-of-row event.
- **COW state-system membership.** COW lists France (`FRN`, `ccode=220`)
  continuously from 1816
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).

## Contradictions

  13,631 km² measurement.
