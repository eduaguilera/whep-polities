# Austria-Hungary (1866-1908)

## Summary

This row covers **43 years** from the loss of Venetia (1866)
through the formal annexation of Bosnia (1908). The 1867
Ausgleich created the dual monarchy of Austria-Hungary — a
constitutional change, not a territorial one. The Congress of
Berlin (1878) authorized the occupation of Bosnia-Herzegovina,
which was formally annexed on 6 October 1908.

CShapes area for this period: 74.7 sq deg (~680K km²).

Predecessor: [auh-1859-1866](auh-1859-1866.md) — loss of Venetia.
Successor: [auh-1908-1918](auh-1908-1918.md) — Bosnia formally
annexed (6 October 1908, +5.8 sq deg).

## Open questions

### oq-1878-bosnia-split-candidate

**Measured 2026-09-25, split not applied.** This row's polygon is
Cliopatria's 1880-1899 Austria-Hungary step, which already contains
occupied Bosnia-Herzegovina, so for 1866-1877 it claims territory that was
still Ottoman: it overlaps [ott-1800-1886](ott-1800-1886.md) by 54,182 km²
(planar, `ESRI:54034`). Cliopatria ships the pre-occupation extent as its
1873-1879 step (622,912 km²); the 1880 step is 54,701 km² larger, of which
48,861 km² lies in modern Bosnia-Herzegovina and about 3,976 km² is the
Sanjak of Novi Pazar. Bound to the 1873 step, a 1866-1878 row would overlap
the Ottoman row by 47 km². No layer-B row routes to this polity, so a split
at 1878 would move no data.

It was tried and backed out because of one side effect:
`data/final/iso3_successor_map.csv` walks predecessor chains to a
`MAX_DEPTH` of 6, and one more AUH period pushes HRV, SVN, SCG and BIH for
1866-1877 past that depth (48 pairs lose their mapping; MKD and YUG
1859-1865 move from AUH-1859-1866 to SER-1816-1878, DDR 1866-1877 from this
row to AUT-1800-1918). BIH losing an Austro-Hungarian answer for years it
was Ottoman is a correction; Croatia and Slovenia losing theirs is not. The
split needs either a shallower chain for those successors or a deliberate
change to the depth limit, which is a registry-wide decision.
