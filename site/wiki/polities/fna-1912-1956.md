# French North Africa (Algeria, French Morocco, Tunisia, 1912-1956)

## Summary

"A term that is often applied to the three territories that were controlled by France in the
Maghreb region of North Africa during the colonial era, comprising Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia"
— "never more than a term of convenience to refer to the three separately-governed territories".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §french-north-africa](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#french-north-africa)

The span runs from the French protectorate over Morocco (1912) to the independence of Morocco and
Tunisia (1956). Each of the three has its own row for these years —
[dza-1919-1962](dza-1919-1962.md) (and [dza-1902-1919](dza-1902-1919.md) before it),
[mar-1911-1958](mar-1911-1958.md) and [tun-1881-2025](tun-1881-2025.md) — so this row is typed
`aggregate`. Morocco here is the **French zone**: the Spanish zone and Tangier are not in it.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 prints `French North Africa` totals in its fertilizer tables (nitrogen, phosphoric acid and
potash; 1934-1938 averages and 1949-1951), beside the three territories' own rows (`Algeria`, `Morocco`,
`Tunisia`, and in 1950-1951 `French North Africa Algeria` / `Morocco` / `Tunisia`, which PR 699
routed to the part rows). The whole is their sum: 1950 nitrogen 12.2
thousand t = Algeria 9.0 + Morocco 1.4 + Tunisia 1.8. The 1934-1938 and 1949 cells carry the whole
alone, so it holds information no part row does. **15 rows** resolved to no polity.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: the union of CShapes 2.0 gwcode 615 (Algeria,
2,317,513 km², its 1919-1962 step), 600 (Morocco's 1912-1956 step, 350,331 km², the French zone
without the Spanish protectorate and Tangier) and 616 (Tunisia, 155,359 km²), all at 1930. The union
measures **2,823,203 km²** (ESRI:54034). No area is declared: FAO states none for the unit.
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)

- **1912-1919.** CShapes' Algeria before September 1919 is larger (2,441,791 km²). The 1930 union therefore understates
  the 1912-1918 whole by about 124,000 km² (4%). No row this polity receives is before 1934.
