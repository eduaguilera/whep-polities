# Austria (Cisleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Austrian lands from **1800** through the end of **1918**. Initially the core of the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Cisleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich (Compromise). The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from **HistoGIS 1860 (ACDH-CH, Austrian Academy of Sciences)**. Built by dissolving 14 Cisleithanian crownlands from the [Austrian Empire Crownlands 1860 shapefile](https://histogis.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/shp-samples): Lower Austria, Upper Austria, Styria, Carinthia, Carniola, Küstenland (Trieste/Gorizia/Istria), Tyrol & Vorarlberg, Bohemia, Moravia, Silesia, Galicia, Bukovina, Dalmatia, Salzburg. Venetia excluded (lost to Italy 1866). Measured area ≈ **300,560 km²** (ETRS89 LAEA), matching Wikipedia's 1910 figure of 300,005 km² to within 0.2%. The 1867 Ausgleich was constitutional not territorial, so 1860 crownland boundaries hold for the whole 1800–1918 period. Source shapefile under open academic license. Assembly script: `scripts/sources/histogis-1860-habsburg/build.py`. Output GeoPackage: `data/geodata/histogis-1860-habsburg/habsburg_cisleithania_transleithania.gpkg` (gitignored).

**Why the post-1918 polygon was not used:** post-1918 Austria ([AUT-1918-1919](aut-1918-1919.md), ~84,000 km²) lost ~72% of Cisleithanian territory at the Treaty of Saint-Germain (1919) — Bohemia, Moravia, Galicia, Bukovina, Slovenia, Dalmatia, South Tyrol all went to successor states. Cisleithania covered ~300,000 km² (modern Austria, Czechia, southern Poland, western Ukraine, Slovenia, parts of Italy and Romania).

**Data territory confirmed from BOTH sources:** Mitchell's *International Historical Statistics* explicitly defines "Austria up to 1918" as **the Cisleithanian part of the Habsburg monarchy**. Juan's team (WHEP project) data, despite using FAOSTAT area codes, also covers Cisleithanian territory — verified by wheat area values: ~970k-1,037k ha in the 1870s, consistent with Cisleithania (1,213,000 ha in 1913 per *1914-1918-Online Encyclopedia*), NOT modern Austria (~270,000 ha). This confirms the polygon rejection was correct: the post-1918 Austria polygon represents only ~28% of the data's territory.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Austria" and "Hungary" as separate countries throughout 1851-1917, reflecting the real administrative split within the Habsburg Monarchy (especially post-Ausgleich 1867). Mitchell confirms this follows Cisleithania/Transleithania boundaries. Previously, the pipeline routed AUT pre-1918 → AUH (Austria-Hungary), merging Austrian data into the combined dual-monarchy polygon. This lost the distinction the data compilers preserved.
