# Austria (Cisleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Austrian lands from **1800** through the end of **1918**. Initially the core of the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Cisleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich (Compromise). The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. The Austrian Crown lands (Cisleithania post-1867) covered modern Austria, Czechia, southern Poland, western Ukraine, Slovenia, and parts of Italy and Romania. CShapes 2.0 tracks Austria only from 1918 independence; the pre-1918 polygon is the combined Austria-Hungary (AUH entries). A Cisleithania-specific polygon could be sourced from historical administrative boundary datasets.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Austria" and "Hungary" as separate countries throughout 1851-1917, reflecting the real administrative split within the Habsburg Monarchy (especially post-Ausgleich 1867). Previously, the pipeline routed AUT pre-1918 → AUH (Austria-Hungary), merging Austrian data into the combined dual-monarchy polygon. This lost the distinction the data compilers preserved.
