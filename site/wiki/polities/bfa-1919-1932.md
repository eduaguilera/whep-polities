# Upper Volta (1919-1932)

## Summary

The colony of Upper Volta (Haute-Volta), a French colonial territory within Afrique Occidentale Française (AOF), from its creation in 1919 until its dissolution on 5 September 1932. It was established by decree detaching regions from the former colony of Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger. Upper Volta was dissolved by France and its territory partitioned among three neighboring colonies: the bulk of the territory went to Côte d'Ivoire, with portions to French Sudan (Mali) and Niger. The territory was later reconstituted as a French colonial unit on 4 September 1947 (see [BFA-1947-1960](bfa-1947-1960.md)).

This polity was created to receive IIA data rows labelled "burkina faso" (iso3: bfa) for 1922–1932, covering cotton lint, cotton seed, groundnuts, sesame seed, eggs, and tobacco (68 rows). These are sub-territory level data — the data magnitudes (cotton lint 90,000–215,000 in 1923–1925, groundnuts 20,000–140,000) are consistent with a single colony within AOF and wholly inconsistent with routing to [AOF-1895-1960](aof-1895-1960.md) (~4.77 million km², ~17× territory overstatement).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Period proxy using CShapes 2.0 gwcode 439 at vintage year 1947. The original 1919 extent is not separately coded by CShapes 2.0 (which begins tracking gwcode 439 only from the 1947 reconstitution). The proxy is appropriate because Biger (1995) states the 1947 reconstitution followed "the former colony boundaries in accordance with the limits set in the 1938 statute" [biger-1995 burkina-faso](../sources/biger-1995.md#burkina-faso), meaning the 1947 polygon closely approximates the 1919–1932 territorial extent.

**Vintage drift:** The polygon vintage year is 1947, while this polity covers 1919–1932 — a drift of approximately 15–28 years. However, because the 1947 reconstitution was explicitly based on the 1919/1938 statute boundaries, the geometric approximation is strong. No significant boundary adjustments between Upper Volta's 1919 creation and 1932 dissolution are documented in available sources.

**Approximation confidence:** Medium-high. The 1919 statute boundary and 1947 reconstitution boundary were explicitly identical (statute reference preserved in Biger 1995). Sub-threshold boundary micro-adjustments (under CShapes' 100×100 km criterion) are possible but not documented.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** No direct predecessor in WHEP. Upper Volta was carved out of the colony of Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger in 1919; [MOS-1800-1897](mos-1800-1897.md) covers the pre-colonial Mossi Kingdoms. No WHEP entry exists for Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger as a separate polity.
- **Successor:** None in WHEP. On 5 September 1932 Upper Volta was dissolved and its territory partitioned among Côte d'Ivoire, French Sudan, and Niger. [BFA-1947-1960](bfa-1947-1960.md) is the reconstituted successor unit (not a direct continuation — a 15-year gap 1932–1947 separates them).

- start_year set to 1919 (colony established by decree) rather than 1922 (first year of IIA data), as the polity boundary predates the data series.
- polygon_status set to assigned using BFA-1947-1960's CShapes polygon (gwcode 439, vintage 1947) as a period proxy, justified by the statute-identical reconstitution boundary.
- cow code set to 439 (same CShapes gwcode as BFA-1947-1960) as the COW/GW identifier for this territory; Upper Volta was not an independent state and does not appear in COW system membership for this period.

## Open questions

### oq-bfa-1919-1932-predecessor-polity

No WHEP entry exists for Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger (the predecessor colonial unit from which Upper Volta was detached in 1919). CShapes does not track Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger as a separate gwcode. If data labelled "Haut-Sénégal-et-Niger" or "French Sudan" for pre-1919 periods is found, a separate issue should address its routing.

### oq-bfa-1932-1947-partition-gap

Between dissolution (1932-09-05) and reconstitution (1947-09-04), Upper Volta's territory was divided among Côte d'Ivoire, French Sudan, and Niger. Any data for this period attributed specifically to "Upper Volta" territory during 1932–1947 would need to be disaggregated across those three polity entries; no single WHEP entry covers this partition period.
