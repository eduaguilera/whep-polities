# Guatemala

## Summary

Guatemala is a Central American country bounded by Mexico to the north and west, Belize to the northeast, Honduras to the east, and El Salvador to the southeast. Biger 1995 shows Guatemala on the regional map of Central America (p.264) and notes that the Central American colonies separated from Europe in 1821 and in 1823 combined to form the United Provinces of Central America [biger-1995 &sect;guatemala](../sources/biger-1995.md#guatemala). WHEP tracks Guatemala from 1821 (independence from Spain) as a continuous row because the territory remained essentially constant after separation from the United Provinces in the late 1830s.

## Territorial extent

Guatemala is bounded by Mexico to the north and west, Belize to the northeast, Honduras (159 miles; 256 km.) to the east, and El Salvador to the southeast [biger-1995 &sect;honduras](../sources/biger-1995.md#honduras).
