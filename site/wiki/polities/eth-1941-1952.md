# Ethiopia (1941-1952)

## Summary

An **11-year row** covering restored Ethiopian sovereignty after British liberation from Italian occupation. Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941. Row ends with the federation of Eritrea with Ethiopia (1952).

Predecessor: [eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md). Successor: [eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md). Occupation period: [aoi-1936-1941](aoi-1936-1941.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0, COW code 530, feature dated 1941-05-05 to 1952-09-14 — an exact match to this polity's period (polygon_status=assigned). The polygon was originally miscoded as polygon_feature_year=1907 / proxy; CShapes 2.0 in fact carries a dedicated 1941 feature (FID 433) whose date range aligns with post-liberation Ethiopia.
- **Area.** ~92.7 sq deg (~1,000,000 km²), excluding Eritrea.
- **Eritrea non-overlap confirmed.** Spatial intersection of CShapes feature 433 (ETH, 1941) with CShapes feature 439 (ERI, 1941) returns zero area. The ETH polygon does NOT include Eritrean territory (~90,000 km², COW 531); there is no double-counting risk with eri-1889-1952.
- **Border note.** The CShapes 1941 and 1907 ETH polygons are geometrically identical: Ethiopian borders were stable before the Italian invasion and after liberation. The Ogaden boundary with British and Italian Somaliland was disputed but did not change the CShapes polygon.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [eth-1907-1936](eth-1907-1936.md) -- sovereignty interrupted by Italian occupation 1936-1941.
- **Successor:** [eth-1952-1993](eth-1952-1993.md) -- Eritrea federated with Ethiopia (1952), expanding territory by ~125,000 km2.

## Sourced claims

- **1941-04-06: British forces enter Addis Ababa.** East Africa Campaign defeated Italian forces. AOI dissolved de facto.
- **1941-05-05: Haile Selassie returns.** Sovereignty restored. Start-of-row event.
- **1941-11-27: Last Italian resistance (Gondar) surrenders.** AOI completely dissolved.
- **1952: Eritrea federated with Ethiopia.** UN General Assembly Resolution 390 (V). End-of-row event; see ETH-1952-1993.

## Data caveat: beer of barley

The Mitchell series for "beer of barley" in Ethiopia begins only at 1949 (page_960_table_1); no pre-occupation observations exist. A step-change flag (160→2280) at the ETH-1907-1936/ETH-1941-1952 boundary is an artefact of the missing data gap (1936-1948), not a unit or source break. Mitchell's earliest post-liberation observation is 160 tonnes (1949), growing steadily thereafter. No discontinuity in Mitchell's source page reference (page_960_table_1 throughout).

## Decisions

- **Split from ETH-1907-1952.** The old 46-year row was split at 1936 and 1941. This row covers restored sovereignty only.
- **Polygon corrected from proxy to assigned.** CShapes 2.0 carries feature 433 (dates 1941-05-05 to 1952-09-14, COW 530) — an exact match. polygon_feature_year=1907/proxy was incorrect; corrected to polygon_feature_year=1941/assigned.
- **No Eritrea overlap.** Spatial analysis confirms ETH and ERI polygons for 1941-1952 are disjoint. No double-counting exists.
