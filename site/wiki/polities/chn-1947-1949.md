## Known data-quality issues

### mitchell-manchuria-series-folded-up

Mitchell's **"China, Manchuria province of"** series (recurring 1928-1952 in
our data) reports the **Manchuria region only**, not all of China: wheat runs
~300k-1.4M tonnes per year against whole-China totals of ~18-20M tonnes in the
same era. Verified 2026-07-24, independently by two agents.

The series is routed to whichever polity held sovereignty over Manchuria in
each span — CHN-1921-1932, then [man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md) (Manchukuo,
which is the dedicated regional polity), then this row for 1946-1949, then
CHN-1950-2025. For **1945-1949 no dedicated Manchuria-region polity exists**,
so the regional figures land on this national row as *best available*: China
held sovereignty, but the reporting territory is a fraction of it. Treat
Manchuria-labelled rows in this period as regional, not national, and see
[oq-manchuria-region-1945-1950](#oq-manchuria-region-1945-1950).

## Open questions

### oq-manchuria-region-1945-1950

**No polity covers the Manchuria region between Manchukuo's dissolution
(1945) and the PRC (1950),** yet the Mitchell regional series continues
across that gap (see
[mitchell-manchuria-series-folded-up](#mitchell-manchuria-series-folded-up)).
Its data is currently folded up into this national row, overstating the
reporting territory.

Options: create a Manchuria-region polity for 1945-1950 (consistent with
[man-1932-1945](man-1932-1945.md) existing for the Manchukuo years), or accept
the fold-up and document the approximation. Needs a human decision; the
affected assertions are banked `best_available`, so creating such a polity
will automatically reopen them for re-verification.
