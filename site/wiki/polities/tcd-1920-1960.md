# Chad (1920-1960)

## Summary

Chad (1920-1960) covers the French colonial territory of Chad from 17 March 1920 — when CShapes records the colony starting — to 10 August 1960, the eve of independence. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=FRA (COW 220). The WHEP split date of 1920 aligns with the administrative reorganization of French Equatorial Africa: "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated." (p.146, cited in biger-1995). The CShapes area is ~107.30 throughout, matching the pre-independence territory.

## Territorial extent

Landlocked central African territory bounded by Libya (north), Sudan (east), Central African Republic (south), Cameroon (south-west), Nigeria (west), and Niger (north-west). CShapes 2.0 area ~107.30 (from 1920-03-17 to 1960-08-10). The preceding TCD-1912-1919 period had area ~103.11, indicating boundary additions between 1919 and 1920 (specifically after the Treaty of Versailles/Cameroon partition). ## Sourced claims

- "In 1920 Chad and the Central African Republic were separated." (p.146) This administrative separation is the event that defines the start of this WHEP row. [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- "Chad is one of the poorest and least developed countries in Africa." (p.148) [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad)
- "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were together." (pp.146–147) This was the administrative unit preceding the 1920 separation. [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic)
- CShapes 2.0 records cowcode=483, status=colony, owner=220 (France), from 1920-03-17 to 1960-08-10, area ~107.30. [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)

## Contradictions

The Biger source states separation occurred "In 1920" (p.146) without a specific date. CShapes gives 1920-03-17 as the precise start of the colony record. This is not a contradiction but a precision difference; the CShapes date is adopted for the WHEP row.

## Open questions

### oq-tcd-1919-1920-area-increase

CShapes records an area increase from ~103.11 (TCD-1912-1919) to ~107.30 (TCD-1920-1960). The additional territory likely came from the partition of German Kamerun following WWI, where parts of the former German colony were assigned to France and incorporated into French Equatorial Africa. The specific treaty provision and transferred districts should be identified.
