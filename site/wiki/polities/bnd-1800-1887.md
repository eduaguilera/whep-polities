# Kingdom of Bundu

## Summary

The Kingdom of Bundu was a Fula Islamic state on the Senegal-Mali border in the Faleme River valley, founded c. 1690s. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1887, when France conquered the kingdom. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). After its fall, the territory was incorporated into French West Africa and is eventually succeeded by modern Senegal.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom occupied the Faleme River valley along the present-day Senegal-Mali border. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). The territory would have been approximate and defined by the reach of Bundu's political authority rather than fixed surveyed boundaries.
