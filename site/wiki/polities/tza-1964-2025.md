# Tanzania

## Summary

Tanzania is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of the United Republic of Tanzania from 1964 to the present. Tanganyika became independent on 9 December 1961, merged with Zanzibar in 1964, and became the United Republic of Tanzania in October 1964.

## Territorial extent

Tanzania is a large East African country bounded by Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Zaire, Zambia, Malawi, and Mozambique, with a coast on the Indian Ocean. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
