# Lesotho (1886-1966)

## Summary

Basutoland, the British colonial protectorate in southern Africa, from the beginning of CShapes coverage (1886) until independence on 4 October 1966 as the Kingdom of Lesotho. "Basuto people lived in upper Orange River basin; clashes with British forces led to recognition of independence of Basutoland on 23 February 1854" [biger-1995 §lesotho](../sources/biger-1995.md#lesotho). "British Commission abolished 1 August 1964; Basutoland became independent as Lesotho on 4 October 1966" [biger-1995 §lesotho](../sources/biger-1995.md#lesotho). CShapes records a single time-step from 1886-01-01 to 1966-10-03 at constant area 2.84 sq deg (followed by an independence step on 1966-10-04 at the same area), confirming this is a single continuous WHEP row with no territorial change across 80 years. The pre-1886 history of Basutoland — which became a British protectorate effectively from 1868 — is outside CShapes coverage and requires a predecessor row.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes records area 2.84 sq deg continuously from 1886-01-01 to 1966-10-03, status=colony, owner=United Kingdom (COW 200). Lesotho is a landlocked country entirely surrounded by South Africa; area 11,725 sq mi (30,355 sq km); population 1,900,000 (1994) [biger-1995 §lesotho](../sources/biger-1995.md#lesotho). An enclave within southern South Africa; common boundary 565 miles (909 km) with South Africa [biger-1995 §lesotho](../sources/biger-1995.md#lesotho). Capital: Maseru. No boundary changes from 1886 to independence; the area is constant across the entire colonial period.

## Contradictions

Biger mentions the 23 February 1854 recognition of Basutoland's independence, yet the CSV has no pre-1886 predecessor row. Basutoland became a British protectorate in 1868 (after the Basuto Wars) and a Crown Colony in 1884. The 1886 CShapes start date is a data-coverage artifact, not a historical boundary event. A predecessor row covering at least 1868–1886 should be created.

## Open questions

### oq-lso-pre-1886-predecessor

Basutoland has a documented history as a British protectorate (from 1868) and Crown Colony (from 1884) before CShapes coverage begins in 1886. A pre-1886 WHEP row (approximately LSO-1868-1886 or similar) is needed to close the chain and achieve the coverage goal for southern Africa from 1800. Biger provides the 1854 independence recognition date and the general trajectory; additional sources would be needed for polygon data.

### oq-lso-1886-start-artifact

The 1886 start of LSO-1886-1966 is a CShapes coverage artifact (CShapes begins 1886), not a historical boundary event. The same issue affects many African colonial entries in WHEP. This should be noted in a broader audit of African pre-1886 coverage gaps.
