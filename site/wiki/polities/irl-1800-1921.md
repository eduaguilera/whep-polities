# Ireland (within United Kingdom)

## Summary

Tracks Ireland as a distinct statistical unit within the United Kingdom from **1800** (Act of Union) to **1921** (Anglo-Irish Treaty and creation of the Irish Free State). Ireland maintained separate agricultural statistics and census records throughout this period, justifying a distinct WHEP row despite not being a sovereign state [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Copied from [IRL-1921-2025](irl-1921-2025.md) (Irish Free State / Republic). The pre-1921 data covers the whole island of Ireland (32 counties). The post-1921 polygon is the Republic of Ireland (26 counties, excluding Northern Ireland). This is an approximation — the pre-1921 territory was ~15% larger. A full-island polygon would be more accurate.. Ireland's boundaries were the full island of Ireland (32 counties) throughout this period. CShapes 2.0 tracks Ireland only from 1921 (Irish Free State). The pre-1921 boundary is the same as post-1921 minus Northern Ireland, but for this period the data covers the whole island. A polygon could be sourced from GADM (Ireland) or historical boundary projects.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Ireland" separately from the "United Kingdom" throughout 1850-1920. Ireland had separate agricultural surveys, separate census, and distinct administrative structures within the UK. Previously, the pipeline routed IRL pre-1921 → GBR (United Kingdom), merging Irish agricultural data into the UK polygon — which visually attributed Irish crop data to Great Britain.
