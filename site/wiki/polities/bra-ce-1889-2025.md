# Ceará

## Summary

Ceará is a state in the Nordeste (Northeast) region of Brazil, on the Atlantic coast, with its capital at Fortaleza. This entry captures Ceará as a subnational reporting unit from 1889, when the Proclamation of the Republic converted Brazil's imperial provinces into states, through the present. `type: subnational` is used, matching the treatment of other Brazilian and comparable states in this database, because sovereignty over Ceará rests with Brazil throughout — this row exists to carry Ceará-specific statistical reporting, not to claim any independent sovereignty. Ceará has not split, merged, or been elevated/demoted in status since 1889, unlike several other Brazilian states (Acre, Mato Grosso do Sul, Rondônia, Amapá, Roraima, Tocantins) that were carved out of federal territories or other states later; this is the ordinary case the country's default convention was written for, so the span runs uninterrupted from 1889 to the open end_year of 2025.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry is created to give Ceará-labelled statistical data (e.g. Mitchell- or IBGE-style series tabulating "Ceará" or "Brazil, Ceará" for agricultural, trade, or population data from 1900 onward) a subnational polity to route to, rather than being folded into the national BRA-1909-2025 row, which is roughly 57 times Ceará's own land area (8.5 million km2 national vs ~149,000 km2 for the state) and would badly distort any per-area or reporting-scope analysis. Previously, any such Ceará-specific rows had no dedicated polity and were matched to the national Brazil chain (BRA-1800-1903 / BRA-1903-1909 / BRA-1909-2025), which is correct for sovereignty but wrong for reporting granularity. Ceará's distinctness as a reporting and administrative unit is confirmed by its continuous existence as a first-order Brazilian administrative division (province, then state) since the early 19th century, its own IBGE statistical apparatus, and its listing as a standard ADM1 unit in modern boundary sources such as geobr-ibge; per the routing decision, Ceará is one of the ordinary (non-exception) Brazilian states, so its start year follows the country's default 1889 conversion rule with no back-cast segment needed, since the earliest known data (1900) falls entirely inside the proposed 1889-2025 span.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: not yet assigned. polygon_route was registered_source_unfetched — geobr-ibge is a real, registered source purpose-built for Brazilian ADM1 state boundaries (keyed by abbrev_state, which would be "CE" for Ceará), but its file (data/geodata/geobr-ibge/states.gpkg) is not present locally, so no feature id or measured area can be assigned in this pass. polygon_source is set to the slug "geobr-ibge" itself (not a route name), with polygon_status "unassigned" and polygon_area_km2 left blank, per the routing rule that an unfetched-but-registered source still names its slug rather than "new_source_needed".

Territory description: Ceará occupies the north-easternmost bulge of Brazil's Atlantic coastline, bordering the states of Piauí to the west, Pernambuco and Paraíba to the south, and Rio Grande do Norte to the east, with its capital Fortaleza on the coast. Its modern area is approximately 148,920 km2 (IBGE), comparable to Bangladesh or to New York State. Ceará's state boundaries have been broadly stable since 1889 relative to its neighbors, aside from documented early-20th-century adjustments in disputed sections of the Piauí and Pernambuco/Paraíba frontiers; the geobr-ibge modern boundary is expected to be a reasonable proxy for the full 1889-2025 span once fetched, but this has not been verified against a period-specific source (see open questions).

## Sourced claims

- Ceará was elevated from a captaincy to a province of the Kingdom/Empire of Brazil in the early 19th century and became a state (estado) of the Republic upon the Proclamation of the Republic on 15 November 1889, per the standard Brazilian provinces-to-states conversion applied to this country's default start-year rule.
- IBGE (the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics) reports Ceará's current land area as approximately 148,920 km2, making it roughly the size of Bangladesh or of the US state of New York; its capital is Fortaleza on the Atlantic coast.
- geobr-ibge, the registered polygon source proposed for this entry, is an R/Python package distributing IBGE's official administrative boundary shapefiles keyed by abbrev_state, which is exactly the ADM1-level identifier needed for a Brazilian state such as Ceará; it is registered in this repository's source list but its states.gpkg file is not yet present locally under data/geodata/geobr-ibge.

## Decisions

### d-code-uses-postal-abbreviation

**Polity code uses the two-letter postal abbreviation CE, not a full-name slug**

The routing decision's unit_id was BRA-CEARA, but 57 of 66 existing subnational rows follow <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> where SUBUNIT is a short code (e.g. ESP-AS, ESP-CA), not the full admin name. Brazil has no existing subnational precedent to match against (0 rows), so this entry sets it: BRA-CE-1889-2025, using CE, the ISO 3166-2:BR and IBGE postal abbreviation for Ceará. This keeps the code short and consistent with the dominant pattern rather than inventing a bespoke code, since Ceará is an ordinary state with no separate identity as a historical territory (unlike ALK or HYD).

### d-three-container-edges-not-one

**Container is split into three edges to tile 1889-2025 without gaps**

The routing decision proposed container_code BRA-1909-2025 alone, but that row only starts in 1909, which would leave 1889-1909 uncontained and fail the containment gate. The Brazil national chain in the polity table is BRA-1800-1903, BRA-1903-1909, BRA-1909-2025, so this page uses one container edge per era of that chain, each edge trimmed to fall inside both this polity's span (1889-2025) and the container row's own span, per the tiling requirement.

## Open questions

### oq-boundary-vintage-geobr

**geobr-ibge gives a modern boundary; how much did Ceará's border actually move since 1889?**

polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: geobr-ibge is the right source family (ADM1 Brazilian state boundaries with abbrev_state as id column) but is not yet fetched into data/geodata, so no feature id or area can be assigned here. Ceará's borders are usually described as stable since statehood, but the state did cede or clarify territory in boundary disputes with Piauí and Pernambuco/Paraíba in the early 20th century, and I have not located a specific dated boundary dataset to confirm the 1889 extent matches the present ~148,920 km2 figure to within a meaningful tolerance. Once geobr-ibge is fetched, check whether a pre-1920s boundary revision measurably changes the polygon before treating the modern state feature as a like-for-like proxy across the full 1889-2025 span.

### oq-pre-1889-province-coverage

**No WHEP polity represents the Ceará province of the Empire before 1889 — is that gap intentional?**

This entry starts in 1889 per the country's default convention (province-to-state conversion at the Proclamation of the Republic), matching the routing_reasoning's stated rule. But the Province of Ceará existed with essentially the same territory from 1799, and any Federico-Tena or Mitchell-style series with pre-1889 Ceará-labelled rows would currently have no dedicated subnational polity to route to and would fall back to the national BRA rows. I have not checked whether such pre-1889 provincial-level data exists for Ceará; if it does, a predecessor entry analogous to this one (spanning province-era years) may be needed, and this page's predecessor field would need to be updated to point to it.
