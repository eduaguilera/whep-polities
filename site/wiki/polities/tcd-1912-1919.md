## Summary

French Chad in its 1912-1919 configuration, following the reorganisation of French Equatorial Africa after the Franco-German Convention of 1911. "The Central African Republic and Chad constituted a French colony known as Oubangui-Chari-Tchad when they were united in 1906" [biger-1995 §central-african-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#central-african-republic). The 1919 end date corresponds to the post-WWI redistribution of former German Kamerun territory (Treaty of Versailles), which reorganised the AEF boundaries once more.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. No COW code is assigned for this period. The territory covered the Chad basin and surrounding areas. Chad (area: 495,753 sq mi [1,284,000 sq km]) [biger-1995 §chad](../sources/biger-1995.md#chad). Bounded by Libya to the north, Sudan to the east, Central African Republic and Cameroon to the south, and Niger/Nigeria to the west.
