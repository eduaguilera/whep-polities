# Bas-Rhin (département)

## Summary

Bas-Rhin is a French département in the Grand Est region, in historic Alsace, on the Rhine frontier with Germany. This entry covers it only from 1919 to 2025 -- the period since its restoration to France -- rather than from its 1790 Revolutionary-era creation like most other French départements in this table, because between 1871 and 1918 it was annexed by the German Empire as part of the Reichsland Elsass-Lothringen (governed as the Bezirk Unterelsass) and was not French territory at all. The country convention singles out Alsace-Lorraine départements as an explicit departure from the default départment-history rule for exactly this reason. `type: subnational` because, in its current 1919-2025 form, Bas-Rhin is an ordinary administrative subdivision of the sovereign French state, not a state or historical territory with an identity distinct from the modern département -- the annexation episode explains the span's start year, not the type. It exists as a row in this table because mitchell reports a production/agricultural series specifically labelled for Bas-Rhin (source unit FRF11), including a back-cast tail into 1862-1918 that the routing decision assigns to this same polity as a territorial reconstruction rather than to a separate German-administration entity; before this entry, that data had no département-level home and would have had to be routed to whichever national FRA row spans the relevant year.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: mitchell reports a production/agricultural series labelled for FRF11 (Bas-Rhin), per the routing decision, with a back-cast tail beginning in 1862 -- straddling both the pre-1871 genuinely-French years and the 1871-1918 German-annexation years -- and continuing through the present within this entry's 1919-2025 span; exact row count not independently re-verified in this authoring pass. Previously, in the absence of any Bas-Rhin-level polity, this data had no natural home except one of the national France rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, or FRA-1919-2025 depending on year), which would have meant attributing département-scale series to the whole of metropolitan (or, pre-1871, Alsace-Lorraine-inclusive) France -- a large territorial overstatement. What confirms Bas-Rhin is a distinct, real administrative entity, not an artifact of the source's labelling: it is a long-standing French département with its own INSEE code (67) and préfecture (Strasbourg), it has a well-documented distinct sovereignty history (French to 1871, German 1871-1918, French again from 1919) that no other FRA row in this table individually represents, and GADM 4.1's global admin-1 layer digitises it as a discrete feature (even though the locally-present GADM subset omits France).

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: `none` -- no geometry is attached, and none is a proxy. gadm-4.1-adm1 is the only registered source with the right granularity for French départements, but the copy present in this repository is an 81-country subset that excludes France (0 FRA rows), so the route cannot execute yet; cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe (used for the national FRA rows) carry only whole-country geometry. polygon_status is `unassigned`, not `proxy`, because there is no wrong-vintage or wrong-territory Bas-Rhin polygon sitting around to reject -- there is simply no polygon at all. See open question oq-no-polygon-source-registered.

Territory description: Bas-Rhin ("Lower Rhine") occupies the northern half of historic Alsace, bordering Germany (Baden-Württemberg) across the Rhine to the east, Haut-Rhin to the south, Moselle and Vosges to the west, and the German Palatinate/Saarland region to the north. Its préfecture is Strasbourg, seat of several European Union institutions (European Parliament, Council of Europe); other notable towns include Sélestat and Haguenau. It corresponds to INSEE département code 67. Since 2021 its département council has merged with Haut-Rhin's into the Collectivité européenne d'Alsace, though the département itself persists as a statistical and administrative unit. Its modern area is approximately **4,755 km²**, quoted from published French administrative statistics for the present-day département, not measured from any attached polygon since none exists here.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1919-not-1862

**start_year set to 1919 (French restoration), not 1862 (earliest data year); pre-1919 data back-cast onto this polity**

The routing decision reports mitchell data for FRF11 (Bas-Rhin) beginning in 1862, well before this entry's start_year. Bas-Rhin was a French département from before 1862, but was annexed by the German Empire as part of Alsace-Lothringen/Elsass from 1871 to 1918 (Treaty of Frankfurt), and only became a French département again -- in its modern administrative sense -- in 1919 under the Treaty of Versailles. Per the country convention's explicit departure rule for Alsace-Lorraine départements, this entry starts at 1919 rather than following the default départment-creation-date pattern used for other French départements in this run (e.g. Aisne, which starts at the container floor of 1800). The 1862-1918 data is not discarded: per the routing decision's back_cast rule, it is treated as a reconstruction of Bas-Rhin's territory onto the same modern boundary, and routes to this same FRA-67-1919-2025 polity rather than to a separate German-administration entry, even though those years fall outside this entry's own start_year. This mirrors the constant-territory back-casting approach used elsewhere in this database and is flagged as an open question below because the alternative -- a distinct 1871-1918 German-administration polity -- was not ruled out with certainty.

### d-subunit-code-is-insee-number

**Subunit code '67' follows INSEE numbering, matching the dominant pattern and sibling département entries**

French départements in this table are identified by their INSEE two-digit code (Aisne=02, etc.) rather than a postal or historical abbreviation, keeping the code in the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> family (57 of 66 subnational rows). Bas-Rhin's INSEE code is 67. Although Bas-Rhin has a genuine name-of-its-own history (the Alsace-Lorraine annexation episode gives it more historical distinctiveness than an ordinary Revolutionary-era département), it is still, in its present form, an ordinary administrative subdivision of France rather than a historical territory whose 1919-2025 identity differs from the modern département -- so the ISO3-SUBUNIT pattern is followed rather than a bespoke code like ALK or HYD. The annexation history is instead captured entirely in the start_year choice and the container basis.

## Open questions

### oq-1871-1918-german-administration-not-modeled

**1862-1918 back-cast years may reflect German, not French, administrative statistics**

The routing decision itself flags this: the pre-1919 data for FRF11 (1862-1870 while genuinely French, and 1871-1918 while annexed by Germany as Elsass) is currently routed as a back-cast reconstruction onto this same Bas-Rhin polity, on the assumption the source reports values from a French-perspective reconstruction of the modern département's territory. If the source's 1871-1918 rows for FRF11 instead reflect German administrative statistics for the Bezirk Unterelsass (a very similar but not necessarily identical territorial unit, administered as part of the Reichsland Elsass-Lothringen), a separate German-administration polity spanning 1871-1918 might be the more correct home for those years rather than this entry. This has not been checked against the actual source rows in this authoring pass.

### oq-no-polygon-source-registered

**No registered, locally-present source can supply a Bas-Rhin boundary today**

As with the other French département entries created in this pipeline run (e.g. Aisne), gadm-4.1-adm1 is the only registered source with département-level granularity for France, but the copy present in this repository is an 81-country subset that excludes France entirely (0 rows for FRA). cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe, used for the national FRA rows, carry only whole-country geometry. No union/difference of currently registered, locally-present features can construct Bas-Rhin's boundary, and NUTS/GISCO (mentioned in the routing decision's polygon_detail as a candidate) is likewise not currently registered in scripts/sources.yaml. polygon_source is 'none' and polygon_status is 'unassigned' until gadm-4.1-adm1 is re-sourced as its full global layer, or a NUTS/GISCO source is registered.
