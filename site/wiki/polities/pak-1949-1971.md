# Pakistan (1949-1971)

## Summary

Pakistan (1949-1971) covers the period of united Pakistan -- comprising both West Pakistan and East Pakistan (later Bangladesh) -- from 1949, when a CShapes polygon change occurs (likely reflecting the 1949 Karachi Agreement on the Kashmir ceasefire line), until the secession of East Pakistan in 1971. The 1971 civil war and Indian intervention led to the creation of Bangladesh from the eastern wing.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Pakistan during this period consisted of two geographically separate wings: West Pakistan (the core territory) and East Pakistan (the Bengal region), separated by approximately 1,000 miles of Indian territory.
