# Arizona (state of the United States)

## Summary

Arizona, one of the fifty states of the United States, tracked from the 1863 organization of Arizona Territory (carved from the western half of New Mexico Territory during the Civil War, partly to secure Union control of southwestern mining districts and transcontinental routes) through statehood in 1912 to the present. This entry is `type: subnational` because Arizona has never been independently sovereign; the row exists to carry state-level reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the state rather than the national level, mirroring the sibling USA-AL-1819-2025 (Alabama) entry created in the same harness run. The span starts at territorial organization (1863) rather than statehood (1912) because the routing decision's proposed span_basis explicitly reasons from the earliest year the unit's own data is directly observed (1882, itself territorial-era) back to the political origin of the unit (1863), consistent with the harness's general rule of using the entity's own formation date rather than the first year of data when the two diverge. The routing decision's proposed end_year (2027, to cover data reaching 2026) is NOT used here; see decisions.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision names no specific source rows, but records that the unit id is USA-ARIZONA with country United States of America and that "the data (1882-2026) spans both [territorial and state] periods and reflects direct USDA survey/census observation of this territory throughout, not a national back-cast" -- i.e. a continuous state-level series, unnamed in the decision object, running 1882-2026. Previously matched to: nothing at the state level -- routing_reasoning states plainly that "No existing polity represents Arizona at any point (territory or state)"; the only candidates in the USA chain are the five national-era rows (USA-1800-1803 through USA-1959-2025) plus Alaska (ALK-1867-1959), none of which is Arizona-specific, so any Arizona-labelled series would previously have been either dropped or folded into a fifteen-state-plus national total, a mismatch whenever a source reports Arizona separately. What confirms this entity is distinct: Arizona Territory was formally organized by act of Congress in 1863 (Organic Act, signed by Lincoln, effective 1863-02-24) with its own territorial government, and Arizona has been a standing US Census/USDA state-level reporting unit since; the routing decision's own reasoning treats it as parallel to the already-created USA-CALIFORNIA precedent ("Per the sibling USA-CALIFORNIA precedent, a state lacking a polity row gets create_new"), which this page follows for Arizona specifically.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm1` source, feature `USA.3_1`, `polygon_status: assigned` -- a direct 1:1 match between the administrative unit and a GADM admin-1 feature, the same route used successfully for Alabama (USA.1_1) in this harness run, which confirms the local GeoPackage extract does carry US state-level features (superseding an earlier, now-stale note on the Alaska page that the US was absent from the extract).

**Area:** measured from the attached geometry at approximately 295,234 km2, consistent with Arizona's commonly cited total area of about 295,234 km2 (113,990 sq mi). This is a measurement of the attached polygon, not an independent territorial claim, and it necessarily represents Arizona's MODERN boundary applied back to 1863.

**Territory description:** Arizona occupies the southwestern United States, bordered by Utah to the north, New Mexico to the east, Mexico (Sonora, Baja California) to the south, Nevada and California to the west across the Colorado River, and a small shared corner with Colorado at the Four Corners -- readily locatable on any modern map as the state containing the Grand Canyon and Phoenix. Arizona's external boundary has been essentially fixed since the 1863 territorial organization and the 1854 Gadsden Purchase that supplied its southern strip, so the modern GADM boundary is a reasonable proxy for the territorial-era extent, but this has not been independently verified against an 1863 Organic Act boundary description -- see the open question on vintage risk, which is sharper here than for Alabama because the span here reaches back to 1863 rather than a comparatively boundary-stable 1819.

## Predecessors and successors

Arizona has no predecessor or successor polity row in this database: it enters as Arizona Territory in 1863, carved from the western half of New Mexico Territory (itself not separately represented as a polity row in this database at that vintage), and the routing decision's proposed span runs open/current with no successor. The 1863 split from New Mexico Territory is not recorded as a predecessor edge because no New Mexico Territory (pre-1863 boundary) row exists to point to; if one is added later, this page's predecessor list would need to be revisited, exactly as flagged for Alabama Territory on the sibling page.

## Sourced claims

- The US Congress organized Arizona Territory by the Organic Act of 1863, splitting it from the western portion of New Mexico Territory, effective 1863-02-24, and Arizona was admitted as the 48th state on 1912-02-14.
- GADM 4.1 administrative level 1 records Arizona's present-day external boundary as feature USA.3_1, with the routing decision's polygon_reasoning treating it as directly registered and locally present, without a documented check of territorial-era (pre-1912, or pre-1863 for the Gadsden Purchase strip) boundary differences.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-iso3-subunit

**Used the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern, not a bespoke code**

Arizona is a standard US state with no distinct historical name of its own separate from the state (unlike Alaska Territory or Hyderabad), so it follows the pattern used by 57 of 66 subnational rows: ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end. The subunit token AZ is the USPS/ISO 3166-2:US code for Arizona, matching the convention already used for Alabama (USA-AL-1819-2025) and the Spanish provinces added in this same harness run. Full code: USA-AZ-1863-2025.

### d-start-year-territorial-organization

**Start year is 1863 territorial organization, not the 1882 data start or 1912 statehood**

The routing decision explicitly reasons from the unit's own formation (Arizona Territory organized 1863) rather than either the first year its data is observed (1882) or the later statehood date (1912), on the grounds that the underlying series reflects direct territorial-era survey/census observation throughout and is not a national back-cast. This page follows that reasoning: 1863 is used as start_year because it is when Arizona first existed as a distinct, separately governed unit, consistent with the country convention's default rule of starting subnational US entries at formal political organization when a pre-statehood identity is being tracked (the same logic already applied to Alaska Territory, ALK-1867-1959, which starts at the 1867 purchase rather than 1959 statehood).

### d-end-year-2025-not-2027

**End year set to 2025, departing from the routing decision's proposed 2027, to keep the container tiling gap-free**

The routing decision proposed end_year: 2027 (exclusive) to cover data reported through 2026, and its own routing_concerns flagged this as an open departure from the country convention's default 2025. This page does NOT use 2027: the only available container for the tail of Arizona's span is USA-1959-2025, whose own span ends at 2025, and the containment gate rejects an edge that falls outside either party's span -- so a 2027 end_year would leave 2025-2026 uncontainable without either extending USA-1959-2025's own end_year (out of scope for this page) or adding a new national-era row (likewise out of scope). end_year is therefore set to 2025, matching the current national row's own end and the sibling Alabama entry's end_year. If USA-1959-2025 is later re-spanned or a successor national row is added, this page's end_year and container list should be revisited to recover 2025-2026 rather than leaving it silently dropped -- see the open question on the 2025-2026 tail.

## Open questions

### oq-2025-2026-data-tail-uncontained

**What happens to Arizona data for 2025-2026, since this page stops at 2025?**

The routing decision states the underlying series runs through 2026, and proposed end_year: 2027 specifically to capture it. This page uses end_year: 2025 instead (see decisions/d-end-year-2025-not-2027) because no container row currently spans past 2025. That means, as authored, any 2025-2026 Arizona data has no polity-year home under this row and will fail to match or be silently truncated depending on how the ingest pipeline treats out-of-span years. Resolving this requires either extending USA-1959-2025's own end_year at the national level (affecting every other USA subnational row, including Alabama, which has the identical problem) or adding a USA-2025-XXXX national row -- a decision beyond the scope of a single state's page.

### oq-vintage-risk-1863-boundary

**Does the GADM present-day boundary match Arizona Territory's boundary at organization in 1863 or statehood in 1912?**

The polygon assigned here (GADM 4.1 admin-1 feature USA.3_1) captures Arizona's current boundary. Arizona's borders are generally described as stable since the 1863 territorial organization plus the 1854 Gadsden Purchase strip, but unlike Alabama (where the routing decision explicitly assessed vintage risk as low), no equivalent per-boundary check was recorded here -- Arizona's territorial-era eastern boundary with New Mexico Territory and northern boundary with Utah Territory were subject to survey adjustments in the 1860s-1870s that have not been checked against the modern GADM line. This should be verified against a period atlas or the 1863 Organic Act's boundary description before treating polygon_status as fully settled rather than a reasonable approximation.

### oq-what-data-populates-this-row

**What specific source, row count, and label populates this row?**

As with the sibling Alabama page, the routing decision supplied to author this page names no specific source, row count, or exact label for the Arizona-level series it describes as running 1882-2026 -- it asserts the data exists and its character (direct USDA survey/census observation, not back-cast) but does not cite it the way the Manchuria exemplar cites mitchell and fao1952 by name and row count. Tracing the actual source(s) currently or prospectively routed to USA-AZ-1863-2025 is left open.
