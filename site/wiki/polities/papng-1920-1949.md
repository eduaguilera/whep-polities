# Papua and New Guinea (combined reporting unit)

## Summary

Australia administered two separate territories in eastern New Guinea between the wars: the
**Territory of Papua** (Australian from 1906, the former British New Guinea) and the **Territory of
New Guinea** (the former German New Guinea, held as a League of Nations Class C mandate from 1920).
They had separate administrations and separate legal status until the *Papua and New Guinea Act
1949* joined them.

**Statistical sources did not respect that separation.** Mitchell's *International Historical
Statistics* publishes 23 rows for 1922-1940 under the single label `papua new guinea`, covering
both territories as one reporting unit. This row exists so those figures route to the territory
they actually describe.

## Why this entry exists

Before it, all 23 rows resolved to `TNGU-1920-1949` — the Territory of **New Guinea alone**:

```
routed to    TNGU-1920-1949   237,536 km²   New Guinea only
should be    both territories 461,684 km²
omitted      TPAP-1906-1949   224,148 km²   Papua — 48.6% of the reporting unit
```

The mechanism was **family order**, not a bad alias. For 1920-1948 no `PNG` member is typed
`national` — both territories are `colonial` — so `matchlib.pick_by_year`'s type filter leaves zero
candidates and the fallback takes the first row of the family list. `TNGU` sorts before `TPAP`.
This is the class of defect inventoried by `scripts/validate_order_decided_families.py`, and it is
why that gate lists the `PNG` family: the choice was being made by list position.

Issue 137 recorded it as one of five labels that could not be fixed by an alias because the
territory they name **has no row**. This creates the row.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** union of two CShapes 2.0 features, built by `build_papng_1920_1949()`.

| component | CShapes | step | area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Territory of Papua | gwcode 911 | 1920-1949 | 224,148 km² |
| Territory of New Guinea | gwcode 912 | 1920-1949 | 237,536 km² |
| **union** | | | **461,684 km²** |

**Independently validated.** CShapes codes the post-1949 combined Territory of Papua and New Guinea
as its *own* feature, gwcode 910, at **461,689 km²** — a **5 km², 0.001%** difference from the union
computed here. The union is therefore not an approximation of the combined territory; it reproduces
the geometry CShapes assigns to it. This is the same validation route
[gct-1919-1956](gct-1919-1956.md) uses, where the Gold Coast + British Togoland union matched the
post-plebiscite Ghana polygon exactly.

**The constituents intersect in zero area**, so the union double-counts nothing.

Modern Papua New Guinea is ~462,840 km², i.e. 0.25% larger — the difference is post-independence
maritime and boundary detail, not territory.

## Polygon status

`polygon_status: **assigned**` — constructed union, high confidence, area declared.

Declaring `polygon_area_km2` is deliberate. `validate_polygons`' area comparison is **opt-in via
that field**, so a row that leaves it null is never area-checked (issue 59). Two defects fixed on
2026-08-05 — Tuscany drawn as the Duchy of Massa, and Romania missing Bessarabia — were both
invisible for exactly that reason.

## Type

`aggregate`, following [masg-1946-1963](masg-1946-1963.md) (Malaya + Singapore),
[codru-1922-1960](codru-1922-1960.md) (Belgian Congo + Ruanda-Urundi),
[egysud-1934-1956](egysud-1934-1956.md) and [syl-1944-1953](syl-1944-1953.md). All are combined
reporting units over polities that also exist separately.

Typing matters here beyond bookkeeping. `audit_family_shadowing` compares same-iso3 rows that
overlap in time, and [gct-1919-1956](gct-1919-1956.md)'s polygon is still **unattached** precisely
because it and `BTL-1920-1957` are both typed `colonial` and overlap at a 9× area ratio (issue 47).
`aggregate` is the type that says "this row is a reporting unit over others", which is what makes
the coexistence describable rather than a collision.

## Key dates

- **1906**: British New Guinea transferred to Australia as the Territory of Papua
- **1920-12-17**: League of Nations Class C mandate over former German New Guinea confirmed
- **1922-1940**: Mitchell publishes combined `papua new guinea` figures — the data this row serves
- **1942-1945**: Japanese occupation of much of both territories
- **1949-07-01**: *Papua and New Guinea Act 1949* creates a single administrative union

## Contradictions

None identified. The union reproduces CShapes' own combined-territory polygon to 0.001%.

## Open questions

### oq-papng-separate-series

Mitchell also publishes a small number of rows under `New Guinea`, `New Guinea australian` and
`Papua` separately (1937, 1939). Those route to `TNGU-1920-1949` and `TPAP-1906-1949` and should
**not** be moved here — they are component series, and this row is the aggregate. Any consumer
summing across `PNG`-iso3 rows for 1922-1940 must therefore choose a level, or it will count Papua
twice: once inside this aggregate and once in `TPAP`. The same whole-versus-part caveat is recorded
for [fid-1887-1954](fid-1887-1954.md), where 22 years carry both.
