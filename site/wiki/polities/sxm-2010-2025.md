# Sint Maarten

## Summary

The southern half of the island of Saint Martin, Dutch since 1648 and administered within the
Netherlands Antilles until that federation was dissolved on 10 October 2010. Since then a constituent
**country** of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, alongside Curaçao and Aruba. The northern half of the
island is French (Saint-Martin, `MAF`) and is a separate territory.

## Why this entry exists

Created 2026-08-24 to close issue 554, which measured the consequence of its absence downstream: in
`eduaguilera/whep`, FAOSTAT/FABIO area code 151 (Netherlands Antilles) carries commodity-balance food in
every year from 1961 to 2010 — 1,421 rows, 7.05 Mt — and has **no population denominator**, because both
population sources WHEP reads are keyed on a present-day ISO3 and neither publishes `ANT`. UN WPP 2024
publishes the successors separately instead (`CUW`, `SXM`, `BES`).

The `successor` relation is the only route a dissolved territory has to a present-day source, and WHEP
already walks it. Of 297 reporting periods checked against UN WPP's vocabulary, exactly one outside the
Rest-of-World bucket reached nothing: `ANT-1961-2010`. Sint Maarten's absence was half the reason —
before this page it resolved into `NLD-1830-2025` in the crosswalk, which would have sent a successor
walk to the Netherlands' 17 million people.

**This repo already knew the composition.** `scripts/sources/reporting-areas/build.py` builds
`ANT-1961-2010`'s polygon as the union of exactly `BES`, `CUW` and `SXM` GADM adm0 outlines. So the
successor list recorded on that page is not an inference drawn here; it is the membership the polygon was
already composed from, now also expressed as chain links.

## Territorial extent

`polygon_status: estimate`, bound to the GADM 4.1 adm0 outline for `SXM`.

The outline measures **38.1 km² geodesic** (38.0 km² projected in `ESRI:54034`, the convention this
repo's declared areas follow — see issue 569 for why those two differ). Sint Maarten's land area is
about **34 km²**, so the polygon runs **1.12×** large.

That is not treated as a defect of this binding, because it is the documented behaviour of GADM's
small-island outlines: issue 570 measures six such territories running 5–26% large, with Bermuda at
1.257× and `MSR-1800-2025` correct to 0.6% as the control. `polygon_area_km2` is declared as **38** —
the polygon's own measurement, matching what the source gives — rather than the 34 km² reference, because
declaring a figure the geometry does not produce would make the two disagree silently. The status is
`estimate` rather than `assigned` for the same reason, and it matches how `CUW-2010-2025` is bound.

Boundary with French Saint-Martin (`MAF`) is a land border on the island, unchanged since 1648 and not
in dispute.
