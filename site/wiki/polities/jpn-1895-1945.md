# Japanese Empire

## Summary

This row tracks the Japanese Empire for **51 years**, the period of
colonial expansion [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
CSV notes: "Empire period. +Taiwan (Treaty of Shimonoseki 1895),
+S. Sakhalin (Treaty of Portsmouth 1905), +Korea (annexed 1910).
~658,000 km2 at peak. Manchukuo (1932-45) tracked separately as
MAN-1932-1945" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Japan acquired Taiwan (1895), South Sakhalin (1905, Treaty of Portsmouth
-- cross-ref [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) for the Russian side),
and Korea (annexed 1910). Manchukuo is tracked as a separate WHEP row.
The row ends with Japan's defeat in WWII (1945).

Predecessor: [jpn-1800-1895](jpn-1800-1895.md). Successor:
[jpn-1945-1952](jpn-1945-1952.md).

## Data caveats

The pre-1961 agricultural data matched to this polity represents
metropolitan Japan only (home islands, ~370,000 km2), not the full
empire polygon (~658,000 km2, including Korea, Taiwan, and southern
Sakhalin). Korea and Taiwan data are tracked separately under KOR and
TWN entries. The empire polygon therefore overstates the geographic
coverage of the matched data.

  Empire [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

  separately as MAN-1932-1945 in the CSV

  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1945-08-15: Japan's surrender.** End-of-row event. All colonial
  territories stripped.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)

### oq-portsmouth-cross-ref

The Treaty of Portsmouth (1905) is the end-of-row event for
[f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md) and a mid-row event for this row.
Russia ceded South Sakhalin (~36,000 km2) and the Kwantung leased
territory to Japan. Both sides now have polity pages documenting the
same event.
