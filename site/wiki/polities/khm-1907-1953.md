# Cambodia (1907-1953)

## Summary

Cambodia (1907-1953) covers the French protectorate/colonial period from 1907, when a CShapes polygon change occurs (likely reflecting the Franco-Siamese Treaty of 1907 which returned the provinces of Battambang and Siem Reap to Cambodia from Siam), until independence in 1953. During this period Cambodia was administered as part of French Indochina. CShapes classifies the status as colony under French rule.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). CShapes classifies this as a colony under French rule. The 1907 treaty with Siam significantly expanded Cambodia's territory by restoring the northwestern provinces.
