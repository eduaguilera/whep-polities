# French Southern and Antarctic Lands (sub-Antarctic districts)
## Summary

France's scattered sub-Antarctic possessions: the **Kerguelen** archipelago, the **Crozet**
islands, **Saint-Paul and Amsterdam**, and the **Îles Éparses** in the Mozambique Channel. Grouped
as a single overseas territory in **1955**; the Éparses were transferred into it from Réunion's
administration only in **2007**.

## Territorial extent, and what the polygon deliberately excludes

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = ATF`, whose four adm1 districts are:

```
Kerguelen                     7,387.6 km²
Îles Crozet                     372.1
Îles Saint Paul et Amsterdam      64.3
Îles Éparses                      43.6
union                         7,867.5 km²
official land area            7,747 km²   ratio 1.016x
```

> **Adélie Land is not in this polygon, and the polity's legal extent is about 55x larger than
> what is drawn.**
>
> The French territory also comprises the **District of Terre Adélie**, an Antarctic claim of
> roughly 432,000 km². GADM's `ATF` contains no Antarctic feature at all — its southernmost
> latitude is −50.0°, and Adélie Land lies below −66° — so the polygon is the sub-Antarctic
> districts only.
>
> **That is the right scope for this database, and the reason is not GADM's.** No polity here
> models Antarctica: there is no `ATA` row, no `continent: Antarctica`, and the Antarctic Treaty
> suspends sovereignty claims, so a polygon asserting French territory below 60°S would assert
> something the treaty system does not recognise. The polity is therefore named for what it
> contains, and `polygon_status` is **`proxy`** rather than `assigned` — the geometry stands in for
> a territory whose legal definition is larger, which is a scope difference rather than an
> approximation of the same ground.
>
> **Any per-km² figure computed on this row uses the 7,747 km² denominator**, which is correct for
> the inhabited and reported districts and wrong by a factor of 56 for the territory as France
> defines it. A consumer aggregating French overseas area must decide which it wants.

The declared area is the **official land area of the sub-Antarctic districts**, not what the
polygon measures, so check A compares the geometry against something independent of it (issue 195).

## Kerguelen has appeared in this database before, in the wrong place

Until PR 178, `FID-1887-1954` (French Indochina) was bound to a Cliopatria feature that contained
**Kerguelen, Réunion and New Caledonia** — 15 parts spanning 55.1°E to 168.0°E — so a containment
test put Port-aux-Français inside French Indochina. That feature is a record of French possessions
east of Africa rather than of Indochina, and `build_fid_1887_1954()` now clips it to an envelope.
This row is where that territory actually belongs.

## Span

`1800-2025`, following the constant-territory convention used for
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md) and [fro-1800-2025](fro-1800-2025.md) — the islands' coastlines
have not moved. **One real territorial change is hidden by it:** the Îles Éparses joined this
territory in 2007, having been administered from Réunion before, so for 1800-2007 this polygon
overstates the territory by their 43.6 km² — **0.55%**. Recorded rather than modelled with a
split, because 0.55% does not justify a period boundary that no data source observes; the
enclave-absorption class this belongs to is issue 197.

Annexation dates for the rest are earlier than the territory: Saint-Paul and Amsterdam claimed
1843 and formally annexed 1892, Crozet and Kerguelen 1893. So 1800-1893 is a back-projection in
the sense issue 22 describes — for those decades the islands were unclaimed rather than French,
which is a sovereignty question and not a territorial one.

## Predecessors and successors

Both empty. There is no predecessor: these were uninhabited islands claimed from no one.

## Contradictions

None identified in the geometry. The Adélie Land scope difference is stated above and is a
definitional disagreement rather than a measurement one.

## Open questions

### oq-atf-continent-oceania

`continent: Oceania` follows this dataset's own regional grouping — WHEP's `ROCE-1850-2025`
("Oceania Other") lists `ATF` as a component, alongside `HMD` (Heard and McDonald), which is
likewise a sub-Antarctic Indian Ocean group. Geographically the islands are in the southern Indian
Ocean, and the Éparses are in the Mozambique Channel next to Madagascar, so `Africa` would be the
physical answer and is what [reu-1816-1946](reu-1816-1946.md) uses for Réunion. The grouping is
consistent within the dataset rather than geographically accurate, and this row follows the
dataset so a consumer joining on it gets the same answer from both sides.

### Already inside the statistical buckets, and that is not a new overlap

Measured before creating this row:

```
ATF ∩ ROCE-1850-2025   7,491.3 km²   95.2% of the territory
ATF ∩ ROW-1850-2025    7,499.3 km²   95.3%
```

That is the same relationship the other 22 territories in this class already have — `FRO`,
`NCL`, `MKD`, `GLP`, `MTQ` and the rest are all components of a regional "Other" bucket *and*
have their own polity. The buckets are in `validate_spatial_containment`'s
`LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` for exactly this reason. What changes is that data reported against this
territory can now reach it instead of only reaching a bucket.

### Why this entry exists

**WHEP's GLEAM 3.0 registry lists it as a country that exists today, and this database had no row
for it** (issue 187). `gleam_geographic_hierarchy` carries 204 rows; 201 resolved to a polity and
three did not — `ATF`, `SGS` and `WLF`.

The evidence is **weaker than for Aruba and the Holy See** (issue 185), and the difference is
worth stating: those two carried 244 and 268 published LUH2 rows, whereas FAOSTAT areas 71, 243
and 271 carry **zero** production rows today. So no whep value is currently mis-attributed. What
exists is a registry row in a source whep ships, whose crosswalk promotes this area out of the
Rest-of-World fold at read time while leaving it the shared `ROW-1850-2025` code — a bucket rather
than an identity. This repo already recorded the state as deliberate, in
`pipelines/faostat-era-matching/state/registry_unmapped.csv`: "registry area with no polity family
(non-country/aggregate)".
