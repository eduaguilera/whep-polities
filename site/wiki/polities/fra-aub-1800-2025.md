# Aube (département of France)

## Summary

Aube is one of the original départements of Revolutionary France, created by the decree of 4 March/26 February 1790 that replaced the Ancien Régime provinces with a grid of roughly 83 new administrative units, largely carved from the former province of Champagne. This entry is type: subnational because Aube is a sub-unit of the sovereign French state, never itself independent -- France held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1800-2025, and the row exists purely to carry a statistical reporting geography (INSEE department code 10, Eurostat NUTS3 code FRF22) smaller than and distinguishable from the national aggregate rows FRA-1800-1871/FRA-1871-1919/FRA-1919-2025. Unlike Alsace-Lorraine, Belfort, Corsica, or France's overseas départements -- the four listed departure cases in the country convention for which special start rules apply -- Aube is an interior north-central département with no annexation, partition, or overseas-status history, so the convention's default start rule applies unmodified in principle. In practice this page starts the span at 1800 rather than 1790 because no FRA container row currently reaches back to 1790 (see decisions and open questions).

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision for unit_id FRA-FRF22 (admin_name FRF22, official name "Aube" per nuts_code_names.csv) reports the data as 97.6% observed/blank, meaning the source measured this specific département directly rather than filling a residual national bucket -- consistent with a genuine administrative reporting unit, not noise. No row count or specific year range is given beyond that the routing_reasoning frames Aube as one of many NUTS3-level French units being routed individually. Previously matched to: nothing at the département level -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly "No existing polity represents Aube specifically," with only the national-level FRA-1800-1871/FRA-1871-1919/FRA-1919-2025 rows as candidates, which contain Aube's territory but are roughly 550,000 km2 each against Aube's true area of about 6,004 km2, a difference of nearly two orders of magnitude that would collapse a département-level series into a national one. What confirms distinctness: Aube's continuous existence and identity as an INSEE/Eurostat NUTS3 statistical unit (FRF22) since its 1790 creation is the evidentiary basis, matching the treatment already accepted for Spanish NUTS3 provinces (ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025) in this database -- a reporting unit that statistical agencies have tabulated separately for over two centuries qualifies for its own polity row under the policy in issue 400.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period, and polygon_source is set to 'none' because, per the routing decision's polygon_reasoning, no registered source in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at all -- gadm-4.1-adm1 is present locally only as an 81-country subset excluding FRA, and even a complete gadm-4.1-adm1 would model régions rather than départements, the wrong admin level for this unit. The polygon_route is correctly recorded as new_source_needed rather than a fabricated registered-source claim, naming GADM ADM2, IGN ADMIN-EXPRESS, or Eurostat NUTS3 as plausible future candidates, none of which are currently registered or present on disk. Territory description: Aube occupies a landlocked interior area of north-central France in the historic Champagne region, roughly 150 km southeast of Paris, bordered by the départements of Marne (north), Haute-Marne (east), Yonne (west), and Seine-et-Marne (northwest). Its préfecture is Troyes, a historic textile and hosiery manufacturing centre; the département is otherwise largely agricultural (cereals and the southern Champagne vineyards around Bar-sur-Aube and the Côte des Bar). On a modern map its outline is the same as the current INSEE département 10 / NUTS3 FRF22 boundary. Its official INSEE area is approximately 6,004 km2 -- this is the département's own administrative area figure from INSEE, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet.

## Predecessors and successors

Aube has no predecessor polity in this database: before its 1790 creation the territory was organized under the Ancien Régime as part of the province of Champagne (with small portions from Burgundy and Île-de-France), a form of division this database does not model at sub-national granularity for pre-Revolutionary France, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row. It has no successor either: Aube remains a current, active INSEE département and Eurostat NUTS3 statistical unit as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the convention already used for FRA-1919-2025 and the Spanish NUTS3 sibling rows.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**Set start_year to 1800, not the proposed 1790, to satisfy container tiling**

The routing decision proposes start_year 1790, citing Aube's creation as one of the original ~83 départements under the 4 March 1790 Revolutionary reform, under the country convention's default start rule. However, no FRA polity row in the database currently has a start_year earlier than 1800 (the earliest is FRA-1800-1871, itself starting at 1800 rather than the Revolutionary founding date) -- there is no container edge available to cover 1790-1800, and an edge that falls outside the container's own span is rejected by the containment gate. Rather than leave a ten-year gap uncovered or fabricate a container edge that doesn't exist in the table, this page sets start_year to 1800 to match the earliest FRA container actually present, and documents the discrepancy here rather than silently departing from the proposed span. If a pre-1800 FRA national row is added later (e.g. covering the Revolutionary and Directory period 1790-1800), this entry's start_year and code should be revised to 1790 to match the historically correct founding date.

### d-code-format-subunit-abbreviation

**Used <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> with a short letter code, following the dominant 57/66 pattern and the ESP house convention**

France has zero existing subnational rows, so there is no FRA-specific precedent to follow, but the dominant pattern across the table (57 of 66 subnational rows) is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and the closest sibling case -- Spanish NUTS3 provinces such as ESP-AS-1833-2025 (Asturias) and ESP-CO-1833-2025 (A Coruña) -- uses a short 2-3 letter abbreviation of the unit name rather than a numeric INSEE/NUTS code. This page follows that same convention for Aube, choosing AUB (three letters, since the two-letter INSEE department code '10' is purely numeric and not usable as a code component, and 'AU' would collide with no current FRA row but reads ambiguously) over the INSEE numeric code or the NUTS3 code FRF22, so that a future author minting sibling French département rows (e.g. for the other 100 départements) can predict the pattern without a lookup table.

## Open questions

### oq-no-polygon-source-registered

**No registered geodata source digitises French départements at all**

The routing decision's polygon_reasoning concludes new_source_needed: gadm-4.1-adm1 is present locally only as an 81-country subset that excludes FRA entirely, and even if it included France it would be the wrong admin level (régions, not départements). No registered source in scripts/sources.yaml (chgis-v6, cliopatria, constructed, cshapes-2.0, cshapes-europe, gadm-3.6, gadm-4.1-adm0, gadm-4.1-adm1, geobr-ibge, histogis-1860-habsburg, mapspain-ign, paine-2024, reporting-areas, usaboundaries-newberry) carries French département-level boundaries. polygon_source is therefore set to 'none' rather than any of these slugs. A real boundary almost certainly exists in IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS, Eurostat NUTS3 (FRF22), or GADM ADM2, but none of it is registered or present on disk in this repository. Until a source is registered and fetched, this row and any sibling French département rows will remain unassigned.

### oq-start-year-discrepancy

**start_year 1800 vs. Aube's actual 1790 founding date needs reconciliation once an earlier FRA container exists**

As recorded in decisions/d-start-year-1800-not-1790, Aube's département was actually created in 1790, ten years before this entry's start_year. This is a stopgap forced by the absence of any FRA national row earlier than FRA-1800-1871. It is unclear whether the correct fix is to add a new pre-1800 FRA national container row (e.g. FRA-1790-1800 for the Kingdom/First Republic transition), to extend FRA-1800-1871's own start_year back to 1790, or to accept the ten-year discrepancy as a database-wide simplification (given that mitchell/fao-era statistical data for Aube is very unlikely to exist before 1800 in any case). This should be resolved consistently across all future French département entries, not decided ad hoc per département.

### oq-department-boundary-vintage-and-stability

**Whether Aube's département boundary has been genuinely stable across the full 1800-2025 span is unverified**

French départements are often cited as administratively stable since their 1790/1800 creation, unlike, e.g., cantonal or arrondissement boundaries, but this has not been specifically verified for Aube against IGN historical records -- minor boundary adjustments between neighbouring départements (Aube borders Marne, Haute-Marne, Yonne, and Seine-et-Marne) are possible and have not been checked. Until a boundary source is acquired (see oq-no-polygon-source-registered), vintage risk across the 225-year span cannot be assessed with actual geometry, only asserted by convention.
