# Iran

## Summary

This row tracks Iran for **198 years**, from the Treaty of Turkmenchay
to the present [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV
notes: "Post-Turkmenchay borders. CShapes polygon (1622521 km2)
approximately matches modern Iran"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Caucasus-loss Iran. The Qajar dynasty continued until 1925, when
Reza Shah Pahlavi established the Pahlavi dynasty. The Islamic Revolution
(1979) established the current Islamic Republic. Borders have been
remarkably stable since 1828, with only minor adjustments. Iran is one of
the oldest continuous states in the world -- 198 years without a
row-breaking territorial change is notable.

Predecessor: [irn-1800-1828](irn-1800-1828.md). Successor: NA (still
active).

## Open questions

### oq-198-year-single-row

A 198-year single row is unusual. The row spans the late Qajar dynasty,
the Pahlavi dynasty, and the Islamic Republic -- three fundamentally
different regimes. Under the WHEP polity-definition rule, regime changes
alone do not trigger splits (only territorial changes do), and Iran's
borders have been stable since 1828. The single-row treatment is
consistent with the rule, but the row's length is notable.
