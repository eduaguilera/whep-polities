# Congress Poland

## Summary

Congress Kingdom of Poland, created at the Congress of Vienna (3 May 1815) as a semi-autonomous kingdom in personal union with the Russian Empire. Initially had its own constitution, Sejm, and army. Autonomy curtailed after the November Uprising (1830-31), abolished after the January Uprising (1863-64). Renamed "Vistula Land" (Привислинский край) 1867. Before 1815: Polish territories were directly partitioned between Russia, Prussia, and Austria (1795-1807), then briefly the Napoleonic Duchy of Warsaw (1807-1815). [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. **Proxy deliberately not copied** because post-1918 Poland ([POL-1918-1919](pol-1918-1919.md), ~389,000 km²) was ~3x larger than Congress Poland (~128,000 km²). Post-1918 Poland incorporated Galicia (from Austria), Poznań and Silesia (from Germany), and the Kresy (eastern borderlands) — none of which were part of Congress Poland. Using the post-1918 polygon would be severely misleading. Congress Poland covered only central Poland (roughly the modern voivodeships of Masovia, Łódź, Lublin, Podlasie, and Kielce). A purpose-built polygon should be sourced from historical maps of the Congress Kingdom / Vistula Land.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Poland" for 1913. Congress Poland was a distinct administrative unit with its own statistical identity. Previously routed to F228 (Russian Empire).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** Duchy of Warsaw (1807-1815, Napoleonic); before that, partitioned territories (Third Partition 1795).
- **Successor:** [pol-1918-1919](pol-1918-1919.md) -- independent Republic of Poland.

## Key dates

- **1815:** Congress of Vienna creates Congress Kingdom of Poland.
- **1830-1831:** November Uprising; autonomy curtailed.
- **1863-1864:** January Uprising; remaining autonomy abolished.
- **1918:** Polish independence restored (11 November 1918).
