# Gambia (to 1889)

## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Gambia, a narrow West African territory along the Gambia River. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1889, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. The British established a station at the mouth of the Gambia River in 1588 and founded Bathurst in 1826 [biger-1995 &sect;gambia](../sources/biger-1995.md#gambia).

## Territorial extent

Gambia is a narrow country along the Gambia River, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its short Atlantic coastline. Area 4,127 sq. miles (10,689 sq. km). The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected).
