# Viseu (district/NUTS region of Portugal)

## Summary

Viseu is one of mainland Portugal's 18 historic distritos, located in the north-central/Beira region around the city of Viseu. It was created in the 1835 liberal administrative reform that replaced the old ancien-régime comarcas with a uniform grid of districts, each subdivided into concelhos (municipalities). Viseu district has been a stable administrative unit since then, surviving the transition from monarchy to republic (1910), the Estado Novo (1933-1974), and the post-1974 democratic constitution, and today also carries the label NUTS III region "Viseu Dão-Lafões" (with some boundary adjustments under the current NUTS classification). `type: subnational` is used because sovereignty over this territory has always belonged to Portugal — this row exists purely to carry a sub-national reporting territory, not to compete with the national PRT chain. The 1835-2025 span is chosen (rather than 1986, when NUTS III first applied) because the distrito, not the NUTS label, is the entity that was administratively and statistically real across almost two centuries, mirroring how the Aveiro district departure is handled in this country's convention.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures unit `PRT-PTVI` (admin_name `PT_VI`), a Portuguese sub-national reporting series spanning roughly 1880-2023, previously routed to the national `PRT-1800-2025` row where it was one of many un-disaggregated sub-national series folded into all-Portugal totals — losing the district-level detail the source actually reports. The routing verdict for this unit explicitly follows the precedent already set for Aveiro (`PRT-AV`, per this country's convention document), which is the only other Portuguese distrito departure so far confirmed; the same HASC district-code provenance ties Viseu to the 1835 reform. Viseu district's administrative distinctness is confirmed by its continuous existence as a first-order Portuguese subdivision since 1835 (it is one of the 18 mainland distritos listed in every Portuguese administrative gazetteer since then, e.g., the Direção-Geral do Território's CAOP), and by its current status as a NUTS III statistical region, meaning both historical administrative practice and modern statistical practice treat it as a distinct reporting territory.

**Territory description:** Viseu district covers roughly 5,007 km² in north-central Portugal (Beira Alta), centred on the city of Viseu, bordered by Aveiro and Coimbra districts to the south/west, Guarda to the east, and Vila Real/Porto to the north. It comprises 24 municipalities (concelhos) including Viseu, Tondela, Mangualde, Nelas, Sátão, and Vila Nova de Paiva. This figure (~5,007 km²) is the commonly cited modern distrito area (e.g., INE); it is stated here as territory evidence from the administrative record, not as a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached.

## Sourced claims

## Decisions

### d-follow-aveiro-precedent

**Follow the Aveiro departure precedent rather than the NUTS floor**

The country convention for Portugal already departs from the default NUTS-era start (1986) for Aveiro district, justified by the 1835 administrative reform predating NUTS by 150 years. This entry applies the same reasoning to Viseu by analogy, since both are HASC-coded distritos created in the same 1835 reform and both have continuous data series predating 1986 (Viseu's from ~1880). Using 1835 rather than 1986 avoids leaving a ~150-year gap in the district-level series that would otherwise have to fall back to the national PRT row, consistent with how Aveiro was handled. The routing verdict flagged that, unlike Aveiro, Viseu is not explicitly named in the convention's departures list; this decision documents that the same reasoning is being extended by analogy rather than by an explicit convention entry, and that an Aveiro-style explicit citation for Viseu's own 1835 creation date would strengthen this in future.

### oq-viseu-1835-date-unconfirmed

**1835 creation date for Viseu specifically is inferred by analogy, not independently confirmed**

The country convention explicitly verifies 1835 as Aveiro district's creation date but does not name Viseu. This page assumes the same 1835 reform created Viseu district (all 17 mainland distritos other than the later-split Aveiro/Porto/Lisboa cases are conventionally dated to the same 1835 decree), but no district-specific historical source has been checked here to confirm Viseu was not created or renamed at a different date (e.g., some Portuguese distritos were reorganized again in 1836 or later in the 19th century). This should be verified against a Portuguese administrative-history source before treating 1835 as settled fact for Viseu.

### oq-viseu-nuts-boundary-drift

**Modern NUTS III boundary may not exactly match the historic 1835-defined distrito boundary**

Like many Portuguese distritos, Viseu's municipal composition has seen minor adjustments since 1835 (municipality mergers/splits under 19th- and 20th-century reforms), and the current NUTS III "Viseu Dão-Lafões" region groups municipalities somewhat differently from the classic distrito for statistical purposes. Whatever polygon is eventually assigned (see the source open question above) will need to state which vintage of the boundary it represents and flag any resulting area mismatch against the ~5,007 km² figure cited here, rather than presenting a single modern boundary as valid unchanged back to 1835.
