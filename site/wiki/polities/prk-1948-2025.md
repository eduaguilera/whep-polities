# North Korea

## Summary

North Korea as tracked by WHEP from 1948, when the Korean peninsula was formally divided into the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (North) and the Republic of Korea (South). The China--Korea boundary follows the Yalu River, defined by an 1895 boundary treaty.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. North Korea is bounded by China along the Yalu and Tumen rivers, by Russia for a short stretch along the Tumen, and by South Korea along the DMZ.

## Sourced claims

- Korea is divided into North Korea and South Korea; the peninsula was divided after World War II. [biger-1995 §north-korea](../sources/biger-1995.md#north-korea)
- A boundary treaty signed in April 1895 defined the Yalu River as the boundary between China and Korea; when the peninsula was divided, North Korea inherited this boundary. [biger-1995 §north-korea](../sources/biger-1995.md#north-korea)
