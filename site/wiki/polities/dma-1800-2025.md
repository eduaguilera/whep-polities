# Dominica

## Summary

Dominica is an island state in the Lesser Antilles in the Caribbean Sea, situated between Martinique and Guadeloupe. Biger 1995 records its area as 290 sq. miles (751 sq. km.) with an estimated population of 88,000 in 1994; it has no land boundaries [biger-1995 &sect;dominica](../sources/biger-1995.md#dominica). The island was first colonized by the French in 1727 and ceded to the British in 1763 (p.193). WHEP tracks Dominica as a continuous row from 1800 because its island territory has remained constant.
