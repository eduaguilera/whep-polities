# Duchy Modena

## Summary

The Duchy of Modena and Reggio was a pre-unification Italian state in the Po Valley, ruled by the House of Habsburg-Este. The database tracks it from 1800 (start of the WHEP time window) until 1860, when it was absorbed into the emerging Kingdom of Italy through plebiscite during the Risorgimento. The duchy was one of several small Italian states that Biger references as part of Italy's fragmented political landscape prior to unification in the 1860s.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Cliopatria (Duchy of Modena and Reggio 1815-1847). The duchy occupied territory in the northern Italian peninsula between the Duchy of Parma to the west, the Papal States to the south, and the Grand Duchy of Tuscany to the east.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Successor:** [ITA-1861-1919](ita-1861-1919.md) -- absorbed into the Kingdom of Italy following plebiscite in 1860

## Sourced claims

- Italian unity was "only finally achieved in the 1860s, under the kingdom of Sardinia, by a combination of astute diplomacy and popular action, particularly Giuseppe Garibaldi's Expedition of the Thousand, which defeated the French Bourbon and papal forces. Plebiscites ratified the unification and established a monarchy." [biger-1995 §italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy)
- Prior to unification, "the particularistic city-states also laid the base for the political fragmentation of the Italian peninsula that was not to endure until the nineteenth century." The duchies of Modena, Parma, and Tuscany were among these fragmented polities. [biger-1995 §italy](../sources/biger-1995.md#italy)

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1815-status

The duchy was dissolved during the Napoleonic period and reconstituted at the Congress of Vienna in 1815. The database start year of 1800 spans both the Napoleonic interregnum and the restored duchy; the Cliopatria polygon covers 1815-1847 only. The next ingest should clarify whether the 1800-1815 gap needs a separate treatment.
