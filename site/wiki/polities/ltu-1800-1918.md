# Lithuania (Russian governorate)

## Summary

Tracks the Lithuanian lands as a Russian governorate from **1800** to **1918**. Following
the partitions of Poland-Lithuania (completed 1795), Lithuanian territory was administered
as part of the Russian Empire's Northwestern Krai (Severo-Zapadny Kray). The core
administrative unit was the **Kaunas (Kovno) Governorate** (~40,500 km²); the Vilna
Governorate and parts of the Suwałki Governorate (then in the Kingdom of Poland) also had
substantial Lithuanian-speaking populations.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 368, time-step **1918** (first CShapes entry for
Lithuania), area **83,882 km²**. This represents the maximum extent of Lithuania's
independence claim at the moment of its 16 February 1918 declaration, including the
claimed Vilnius region before Poland seized it in October 1920.

**Approximation — direction and magnitude:**
The 1918 independence-claim polygon is used as a proxy for the Russian-era Lithuanian
governorate territory across the entire 1800–1918 span (~118 years). Key discrepancies:

| Territory | Status in Russian period | Status in 1918 polygon |
|---|---|---|
| Kaunas (Kovno) Governorate (~40,500 km²) | Core Lithuanian admin unit | Included |
| Vilna Governorate (~40,000 km²) | Partly Lithuanian-speaking; mixed | Included (claimed 1918, lost 1920) |
| Suwałki Governorate (~12,000 km²) | Congress Poland; Lithuanian strip | Not included in 1918 polygon |
| Memel/Klaipėda strip (~2,400 km²) | Prussian until 1923 | Not in 1918 polygon |

The polygon likely **overstates** the administratively bounded Russian-era Lithuanian
territory by including the full Vilna region, which was not consistently governed as a
single Lithuanian administrative unit. The Suwałki area (now northeast Poland) was part
of Congress Poland (not Northwestern Krai) and is correctly absent from the 1918 polygon.

**Direction of approximation:** slight overstatement (~15–25%) relative to the narrowly
defined Kaunas Governorate core, but reasonable representation of the broader territory
whose agricultural production is attributed to "Lithuania" in pre-1918 Russian statistical
sources, which often reported on the Northwestern Krai as a whole.

**No split recommended:** The pre-1918 data is sparse (n≈10 rows), and the core territory
(Kaunas Governorate) was stable throughout 1800–1918. CShapes 2.0 does not map
sub-national boundaries, so no earlier granular polygon is available from that source.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Lithuania" for years including
1913. Previously routed to F228 (Russian Empire). A granular polity for Russian-era
Lithuania avoids the ~7x territory overstatement that routing to the full empire would
produce.

- **1795:** Completion of the Third Partition of Poland; Lithuanian territory enters the
  Russian Empire as the Northwestern Krai.
- **1918:** Lithuanian declaration of independence (16 February 1918); end of this polity.
