# Côte d'Ivoire

## Summary

Côte d'Ivoire is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Ivory Coast from 1960 to the present. Ivory Coast gained independence on 7 August 1960, adopting the colony's borders as its international boundary.

## Territorial extent

Côte d'Ivoire is bounded by Ghana, Burkina Faso, Mali, Guinea, and Liberia. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [civ-1947-1960](civ-1947-1960.md) — colonial Ivory Coast

## Sourced claims

- The boundary between Ghana and Ivory Coast runs for 415 miles (668 km) from the boundary tripoint with Burkina Faso on the Black Volta River to the Gulf of Guinea in the Atlantic Ocean. The present boundary follows the previous colonial boundary between the British and French possessions (p.243). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- "The British established the Gold Coast colony on 13 January 1886" and France established itself in the Gulf of Guinea "organizing French Sudan (1898), dissolving it on 17 October 1899 and forming the colony of Ivory Coast" (p.244). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- Gold Coast became an independent state on 6 March 1957, adopting the name Ghana. Ivory Coast gained independence on 7 August 1960 (p.244). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
