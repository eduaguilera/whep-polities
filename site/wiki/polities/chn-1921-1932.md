# China (1921-1932)

## Summary

An **11-year row** covering the Republic of China from March 1921 through the
establishment of the Manchukuo puppet state in March 1932. The period spans the
late Warlord Era and the Northern Expedition (1926-1928), by which Chiang
Kai-shek's Nationalist government nominally reunified China and established the
Nanjing government. The end-of-row event is the formal proclamation of
Manchukuo on 1 March 1932, after the Japanese Kwantung Army's seizure of
Manchuria following the Mukden Incident of 18 September 1931.

This polity is the first half of a split of the former CHN-1921-1945 row. The
split was introduced because the 1921 CShapes polygon (7,496,578 km2) includes
the three Manchurian provinces (Heilongjiang, Jilin, Liaoning), which became
Manchukuo territory from 1932. Retaining the unsplit row from 1921 to 1945
created a ~786,000 km2 spatial double-count with MAN-1932-1945 for the period
1932-1945. See [chn-1932-1945](chn-1932-1945.md) for the post-1932 sub-period
with the composed-difference polygon.

Key periods within this row:

- **1921-1926**: Continued warlord fragmentation. Republic of China (Beijing
  government) exercises nominal sovereignty.
- **1926-1928**: Northern Expedition; Nationalist government re-establishes
  central authority over most of China proper.
- **1928-1931**: Nanjing Decade begins; Manchuria under Zhang Xueliang
  nominally aligned with the Nationalist government.
- **1931**: Mukden Incident (18 September). Japanese Kwantung Army seizes
  Manchuria; end-of-row event triggering the split.

Predecessor: [chn-1914-1921](chn-1914-1921.md). Successor:
[chn-1932-1945](chn-1932-1945.md) (China proper, Manchuria excluded).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, feature 710 (cowcode), vintage 1921-03-13.
Area: **7,496,578 km2**.

**Scope:** Full Republic of China territory as recognized in CShapes 2.0 at the
1921 vintage, including the three Manchurian provinces (Heilongjiang, Jilin,
Liaoning) and Outer Mongolia (still nominally Chinese-claimed). This is correct
for the 1921-1932 period when Manchuria, though under warlord control, was
formally part of the Republic of China and internationally recognized as such.

**Polygon vintage drift:** None — the 1921 CShapes polygon is appropriate for
the full 1921-1932 span. Manchuria was nominally Chinese throughout this
sub-period (Zhang Zuolin/Zhang Xueliang era).

**Polygon status:** assigned (CShapes 2.0 exact match for cowcode 710, vintage
1921).

## Sourced claims

- Start year 1921: CShapes 2.0 feature boundary; COW code 710 state system entry.
- End year 1932: Manchukuo proclaimed 1 March 1932 following Japanese occupation
  of Manchuria from September 1931. [biger-1995 §manchuria]
- Polygon area 7,496,578 km2: computed from CShapes 2.0 feature 710 (1921
  vintage) via EPSG:6933 equal-area projection.
- CShapes does NOT split China at 1932; it uses a single 1921-1945-08-14
  polygon entry for China (cowcode 710). The split is introduced by WHEP to
  resolve the spatial double-count with MAN-1932-1945.

## Decisions

- **Split from CHN-1921-1945** (2026-06-29): CHN-1921-1945's single CShapes 1921
  polygon (7.5M km2) was found to overlap 99.3% with MAN-1932-1945's polygon
  (791,708 km2), creating a ~786,000 km2 spatial double-count for 1932-1945.
  The fix is to split CHN-1921-1945 at 1932:
  - CHN-1921-1932 retains the 1921 CShapes polygon (includes Manchuria; correct
    for this pre-Manchukuo sub-period).
  - CHN-1932-1945 uses a composed-difference polygon (CShapes-1921 minus
    MAN-1932-1945) = 6,710,264 km2 (China proper only).
