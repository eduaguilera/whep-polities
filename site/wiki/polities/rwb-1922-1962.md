# Ruanda-Urundi (1922-1962)

## Summary

Ruanda-Urundi is a WHEP polity row covering the Belgian League of Nations mandate
(later UN trusteeship) over the combined territory of modern Rwanda and Burundi, from
1922 to 1962. The 1922 start date reflects the formal League of Nations mandate
assignment to Belgium (20 July 1922), which replaced the earlier post-WWI occupation
period covered by [rwb-1919-1922](rwb-1919-1922.md). The 1962 end date marks the
independence of both Rwanda and Burundi on 1 July 1962, when the combined mandate
dissolved into two successor states. CShapes records a stable area (≈4.256 sq.deg.)
for the entire 1922–1962 period, confirming one WHEP row for the full mandate period.

## Territorial extent

Ruanda-Urundi encompassed the territory of modern Rwanda and Burundi combined, in the
Great Lakes region of east-central Africa. Area approximately 54,172 km² (Rwanda 26,327
km² + Burundi 27,845 km²). Capital: Usumbura (now Bujumbura). Bounded by Uganda to the
north, Tanzania to the east, the DRC (Belgian Congo) to the west, and the two states
shared borders along ethnic/administrative lines internally. The polygon source is
CShapes 2.0 with dependencies=TRUE (cowcode=515, status=mandate, owner=211 [Belgium],
start=1922-07-20, end=1962-06-30, area≈4.256 sq.deg.).

## Contradictions

CShapes records the first entry for cowcode=515 starting 1920-06-28, while the WHEP row
starts at 1922. This corresponds to the distinction between the Belgian occupation
(1919–1922) tracked in [rwb-1919-1922](rwb-1919-1922.md) and the formal League of
Nations mandate (from 1922-07-20). The two CShapes entries have identical areas, so the
split at 1922 in the WHEP chain is driven by the legal/mandate status change, not a
territorial change. Under strict WHEP polity-definition rules (area change = new row),
this could be argued as one row; the split is preserved for consistency with the
existing CSV chain structure.

## Decisions

### dec-rwanda-mitchell-routing

Mitchell agricultural data labeled `rwanda` for years 1953–1960 is routed to this polity (RWB-1922-1962). Rwanda was the northern constituent territory of the Belgian UN Trust Territory of Ruanda-Urundi; RWB-1922-1962 fully covers the period 1953–1960. No separate Rwanda-only WHEP polity exists for this period (Rwanda did not become independent until 1 July 1962). The Mitchell `rwanda` label therefore refers to the Rwanda portion of the combined mandate, not an independent state.

**Sub-territory approximation:** Rwanda-only data (~26,327 km²) is routed to a polygon covering the full Ruanda-Urundi mandate (~54,172 km²), introducing an approximately 2× overstatement of territorial extent. For agricultural aggregates this approximation is acceptable — no finer-grained Rwanda-only polygon is available for the mandate period — but users should note that production figures labeled `rwanda` represent Rwanda's share within the combined territory. A routing alias `rwanda` (1953–1960) → RWB-1922-1962 has been added to applied_aliases.csv. No new polity is required for this rematch.

### dec-burundi-iia-routing

> **SUPERSEDED 2026-08-25 (issue 355).** The 82 `iia` `burundi` rows now route to
> [bdi-1922-1962](bdi-1922-1962.md), and the 24 `mitchell` `rwanda` rows to
> [rwa-1922-1962](rwa-1922-1962.md). This row keeps only the 262 rows whose labels name the whole
> mandate (`ruanda-urundi`, `ruanda urundi`, `rwanda and burundi`).
>
> The decision below rests on the premise that *"no finer-grained Burundi-only polity exists for this
> period"*. That premise is false: `BDI-1922-1962` and `RWA-1922-1962` both exist, spanning 1922–1962,
> with 27,176 km² and 25,186 km² polygons and their own pages. The decision's own closing sentence —
> that these rows *"represent Burundi's share of production within Ruanda-Urundi, not the whole
> mandate"* — is precisely why they belong there. Retained below unedited as the record of what was
> decided and why it changed.

IIA agricultural data labeled `burundi` for years 1922–1961 is routed to this polity (RWB-1922-1962). Burundi was the southern constituent territory of the Belgian Ruanda-Urundi mandate and had no separate administrative statistics outside the combined mandate framework before independence in 1962. The commodities reported (cotton lint, cotton seed, groundnuts, tobacco) are consistent in scale with a sub-territory of Ruanda-Urundi; however, since no finer-grained Burundi-only polity exists for this period, RWB-1922-1962 is the correct match. The polygon covers the full mandate area (Rwanda + Burundi combined, ~54,172 km²). Users of this data should note that `burundi`-labeled rows represent Burundi's share of production within Ruanda-Urundi, not the whole mandate. A routing alias `burundi` (1922–1961) -> RWB-1922-1962 has been added to applied_aliases.csv. Separately, a `burundi` (1919–1921) -> RWB-1919-1922 alias routes any pre-mandate occupation-period data.
