# Andorra

## Summary

This row tracks the Principality of Andorra for **226 years** from the
database start year to the truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Andorra has been a
co-principality since 1278, ruled jointly by the president of France
(formerly the king) and the bishop of Urgel (Spain). It is a
landlocked microstate in the eastern Pyrenees with boundaries that
have remained unchanged since the thirteenth century
[biger-1995 §andorra](../sources/biger-1995.md#andorra).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **Area.** 175 sq. miles (453 sq. km.)
  [biger-1995 §andorra](../sources/biger-1995.md#andorra) (p.30).
- **Boundaries.** France to the north (37.5 miles; 60 km) and Spain
  to the south (40 miles; 64 km)
  [biger-1995 §andorra](../sources/biger-1995.md#andorra) (pp.30--31).
