# Cook Islands

## Summary

This row tracks the Cook Islands as a territorial-economic unit from the
1800 WHEP database floor to the 2025 ceiling. The 15-island South Pacific
archipelago passed from independent chiefdoms through a British
protectorate (1888), annexation to New Zealand (1901), and free
association with New Zealand (1965). Political status changed; the
territorial extent of the island group did not, so a single period covers
the whole span, following the precedent of FRO-1800-2025.

## Territorial extent

All 15 islands (Rarotonga, Aitutaki, Mangaia, ...), land area ~236 km2
official; the GADM 4.1 adm0 polygon measures 284 km2 including reef
fringe. No boundary changes over the span.

## Predecessors and successors

None. Status transitions (protectorate 1888, NZ annexation 1901, free
association 1965) did not alter the extent, so no period split is
warranted for data routing.

## Data routing

FAOSTAT reporting area **47 "Cook Islands"** reports 1961-2024 (42,137
data rows observed across the WHEP FAOSTAT pins). Routed here by
`pipelines/faostat-era-matching` via iso3 COK. Pre-1961 printed
statistics, if any surface, also belong here.

## Decisions

- **Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `COK` (modern extent, valid for the
  whole span given no boundary changes).
- **Single period:** no evidence of extent change; created to close the
  FAOSTAT-era coverage gap (area 47 previously had no polity family).
