# Guinea-Bissau (1974-2025)

## Summary

This row covers independent Guinea-Bissau from 1974 to the present. Guinea-Bissau unilaterally declared independence on 24 September 1973 during the independence war; Portugal recognized it on 10 September 1974 after the Carnation Revolution. CShapes uses 1974-09-10 as the independence date. Area unchanged from colonial period (2.76 degree-squared units).

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0.
