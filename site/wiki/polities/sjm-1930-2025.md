# Svalbard and Jan Mayen (combined reporting unit)

## Summary

Norway's two Arctic dependencies reported together: [svb-1925-2025](svb-1925-2025.md) and [jmy-1930-2025](jmy-1930-2025.md). A statistical unit, not a territory of its own.

## Why this entry exists

The labels `norway: svalbard and jan mayen` (1937-1960, 6 pairs) and `norway: jan mayen, svalbard` (1937, 1 pair) report the two together: **7 (year, label) pairs**. Routing them to either island would put both on one. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
