# Pakistan

## Summary

Pakistan as tracked by WHEP from 1971, following the secession of East Pakistan (Bangladesh). Pakistan gained independence in August 1947, split into East and West wings by the large expanse of India. The 1971 civil war and Indian intervention led to the creation of Bangladesh, reducing Pakistan to its western wing.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Pakistan is bounded by Afghanistan (1,510 mi; 2,430 km), China (325 mi; 523 km), India (1,810 mi; 2,912 km), and Iran (565 mi; 909 km).

- In 1971, civil war broke out in East Pakistan, and with the help of India the independent state of Bangladesh was created there; modern Pakistan is now comprised of what was West Pakistan. [biger-1995 §pakistan](../sources/biger-1995.md#pakistan)
