# Cyprus

## Summary

Cyprus as tracked by WHEP from 1879, following the 1878 British acquisition from the Ottoman Empire. Cyprus became independent in 1960 but was partially occupied by Turkey in 1974. The island has sovereign British base areas (Dhekelia and Akrotiri).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (feature year 1960), representing the full island of Cyprus (~9,251 km²). Cyprus is an island with stable physical boundaries; its coastline did not change between 1879 and 2025. The 1960 polygon is therefore a valid approximation for the entire pre-1974 period (1879–1974). The two British Sovereign Base Areas (Dhekelia and Akrotiri, ~254 km² combined) remain UK territory but are geographically contained within the CShapes polygon.

**Post-1974 approximation (polygon_vintage_drift).** In July 1974 Turkey occupied approximately 3,355 km² (~36%) of the island's northern area, creating the de facto Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC). From 1974 onward the Republic of Cyprus (RoC) government controls only ~5,896 km² (~64% of the island). The 1960 CShapes polygon (~9,251 km²) overstates the RoC-controlled area by roughly 57% for post-1974 data rows. Any production or economic data labelled 'cyprus' after 1974 should be understood as reflecting only the RoC-controlled south. A split at 1974 (CYP-1879-1974 with the full-island polygon, CYP-1974-2025 with a government-controlled-area polygon) would correctly represent post-1974 extent; this is deferred pending a suitable polygon source for the RoC-controlled area.

## Alias routing

IIA rows labelled 'cyprus' (iso_in_data='cyp') spanning 1879–2025 are routed to CYP-1879-2025 via a year-ranged alias (year_min=1879, year_max=2025). Cyprus is an island; there is no territorial ambiguity for the pre-1974 period. Post-1974 rows carry a polygon_vintage_drift flag reflecting the TRNC partition described above.

## Sourced claims

- Cyprus was colonized by Greeks by 1000 BCE or earlier; it passed through Phoenician, Ptolemaic, Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman hands. [biger-1995 §cyprus](../sources/biger-1995.md#cyprus)
- In 1878 Cyprus came under British administration under continued Ottoman suzerainty as a naval base against Russia; Turkey relinquished its claim in 1923. [biger-1995 §cyprus](../sources/biger-1995.md#cyprus)
- Cyprus became an independent state in 1960; in July 1974 Turkey invaded the island, occupying over one-third of its area (~3,355 km², ~36% of total). [biger-1995 §cyprus](../sources/biger-1995.md#cyprus)

## Decisions

### dec-alias-1879-2025

All IIA (and other source) rows labelled 'cyprus' / iso='cyp' within years 1879–2025 are matched to CYP-1879-2025. Rationale: Cyprus is an island with no territorial border changes in this period (pre-1974). The 1960 CShapes polygon represents the full island extent validly for 1879–1974. Post-1974 rows are accepted with a documented polygon_vintage_drift approximation (see Territorial extent) rather than a coverage_gap, because the polity span and island identity are unambiguous.

### dec-post-1974-approximation

Post-1974 production data routed to CYP-1879-2025 carries an implicit polygon overstatement of ~57% (full island vs. RoC-controlled area). This is documented here as an approximation; the flag polygon_vintage_drift applies. A future split at 1974 (CYP-1879-1974 / CYP-1974-2025) is recommended if a RoC-controlled-area polygon becomes available.

## Open questions

### oq-trnc-split

Consider splitting CYP-1879-2025 at 1974 into:
- CYP-1879-1974: full-island polygon from CShapes 2.0 (feature year 1960, ~9,251 km²)
- CYP-1974-2025: RoC government-controlled area polygon (~5,896 km²); no CShapes polygon exists for this; would require a constructed estimate or modern GADM boundary clipped to the UN buffer zone

Until the split is implemented, post-1974 rows remain under CYP-1879-2025 with the documented approximation.
