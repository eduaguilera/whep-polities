Captures FAO "Germany Western" data for 1938–1948. Polygon: CShapes gwcode 260, vintage 1949, used as period proxy (~248,000 km²). Symmetric with SBZ-1938-1949 for the eastern counterpart. The FRG (F78-1949-1990) did not legally exist until 23 May 1949; this polity fills the date gap while preserving the same geographic footprint.

## Polygon Decision

CShapes 2.0 gwcode=260, polygon_feature_year=1949, used as period proxy for WZO-1938-1949. The 1949 polygon (~248,000 km²) is the most authoritative vector for the Western occupation zone boundary. Approximation documented: (1) <2% Saar overstatement for 1945–1948; (2) retrospective geographic frame for 1938–1944 pre-war data. polygon_status=assigned.

## Why This Entry Was Created

FAO 1952 reports "Germany Western" as a distinct label spanning 1937–1951. The FRG (F78-1949-1990) was formally established on 23 May 1949. Routing 1938–1948 FAO data to F78-1949-1990 would assign it to a polity that did not yet legally exist, creating a date-coverage error. WZO-1938-1949 fills this gap symmetrically with SBZ-1938-1949 (Soviet occupation zone), which was already a first-class polity in the database.

## Territorial Scope

The Western occupation zones (American, British, French) covered approximately 248,000 km² — essentially the future FRG territory minus the Saar protectorate (which was under French administration 1945–1957, ~2,570 km²). For 1938–1944, FAO retrospectively applied this western-zone frame to pre-war German statistics; the polity is coded start_year=1938 to match the FAO data range and for symmetry with SBZ-1938-1949 and DEU-1938-1945.
