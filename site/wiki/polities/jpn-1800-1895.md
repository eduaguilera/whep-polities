# Japan (to 1895)

## Summary

This row tracks Japan for **96 years**, from the database floor to the
Treaty of Shimonoseki [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
CSV notes: "Pre-imperial Japan. Home islands only (~370,776 km2). Treaty
of Shimonoseki (1895) marks beginning of colonial expansion"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The row spans the late Tokugawa Shogunate (1800--1868) and the first
decades of Meiji Japan (1868--1895). Perry's expedition (1853) forced
Japan open; the Meiji Restoration (1868) ended feudal rule and launched
rapid modernization. The home islands remained the sole territory
throughout the row -- colonial expansion begins with the successor row.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor). Successor:
[jpn-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md).

## Sourced claims

- **Island state, no land boundaries.** "Japan is located in the
  northern Pacific Ocean. It consists of four large islands (Hokkaido,
  Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu) and many smaller islands." Total area
  145,870 sq mi (377,657 sq km).
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan) (p.329)
- **1853: Perry expedition.** US Commodore Perry arrived with "black
  ships," forcing Japan to end its isolation policy.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1854: Convention of Kanagawa.** Japan opened ports to US trade.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1868: Meiji Restoration.** End of the Tokugawa Shogunate; Emperor
  Meiji assumed direct rule. Rapid modernization began.
  [biger-1995 §japan](../sources/biger-1995.md#japan)
- **1894--1895: First Sino-Japanese War.** Japan defeated Qing China.
  "After the war of 1894--1895 between Japan and China (won by Japan),
  China agreed to grant Korea its independence."
  [biger-1995 §korea](../sources/biger-1995.md#korea) (p.159)
- **1895-04-17: Treaty of Shimonoseki.** Japan acquired Taiwan, the
  Pescadores, and the Liaodong Peninsula (later retroceded under Triple
  Intervention). End-of-row event
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
