# Northern Rhodesia within Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (1953-1964)

## Summary

Northern Rhodesia was a constituent territory of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland from 1 August 1953 to 24 October 1964, when it gained independence as Zambia. Data reported for "zambia" or "northern rhodesia" during this period refers exclusively to the ~751,924 km² Northern Rhodesia territory, NOT to the full Federation (~1,260,000 km²). Routing such data to FRN-1953-1964 would overstate territorial scope by ~1.7×. Analogous polities exist for SRH-1953-1964 and MWI-1953-1964.

## Territorial extent

Area: ~751,924 km² (CShapes 2.0, cowcode=551, 1953 vintage). Geographically identical to NRH-1911-1953 and ZMB-1964-2025; borders unchanged from 1911 through 1964.

**Polygon source:** CShapes 2.0, cowcode=551, boundary-stable proxy at 1953 vintage (last pre-federation record; CShapes does not record cowcode=551 separately during 1953-1964). Confidence: HIGH.

**Do NOT fold 'zambia'-labeled data into FRN-1953-1964**: FRN covers ~1,260,000 km². See [frn-1953-1964](frn-1953-1964.md).
