# South Korea

## Summary

This row tracks South Korea for **78 years**, from independence to the
database truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Republic of Korea proclaimed 15 August 1948. Korean War (1950-1953).
Post-war economic miracle ("Miracle on the Han River"). Area ~100,210
km². Borders stable since 1953 armistice.
