# Vanuatu

## Summary

This row tracks Vanuatu (formerly New Hebrides) for **226 years** from
the database start year to the truncation year
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Vanuatu is a chain
of islands in the South Pacific Ocean, east of Australia, with no
international land boundaries
[biger-1995 §vanuatu](../sources/biger-1995.md#vanuatu). It was
jointly governed by an Anglo-French condominium from 1906 until
independence in 1980.

## Sourced claims

- Vanuatu: area 4,706 sq. miles (12,184 sq. km.), population
  200,000 (1994), capital Port-Vila, no land boundaries. Formerly
  known as New Hebrides.
  [biger-1995 §vanuatu](../sources/biger-1995.md#vanuatu) (p.510)
- Vanuatu was jointly governed by an Anglo-French condominium
  established in 1906; the islanders achieved independence in 1980.
  [biger-1995 §vanuatu](../sources/biger-1995.md#vanuatu) (p.510)
- In March 1988, Vanuatu signed an agreement with Papua New Guinea
  and the Solomon Islands to form the "Spearhead Group" to preserve
  Melanesian cultural traditions.

until 1980 and the Anglo-French condominium was only established in
1906. The 1800 start year likely reflects CShapes polygon availability
rather than a political event. Future ingest should clarify whether
this row should be split into colonial/condominium and independence
periods.
