# Czech lands after Munich (Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia from 1939)

## Summary

The Czech lands left after the Munich Agreement: the rump Czech lands of the Second Republic (October 1938 - March 1939), then the Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia established on 16 March 1939, until Germany's surrender in May 1945. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §bohemia-moravia](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#bohemia-moravia)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbook for 1941-1945 reports `czechoslovakia: bohemia, moravia, silesia` for 1939-1944 (6 pairs): the Protectorate's figures under the pre-war name. No row was the Protectorate. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** The modern Czech Republic (CShapes 316) intersected with CShapes' post-Munich Czecho-Slovakia step (315, 1938-1945), i.e. the Czech lands without the Sudetenland and the Teschen strip: **54,971 km2** (ESRI:54034). The Protectorate's stated area is about 49,000 km2, so the CShapes cession line gives back roughly 6,000 km2 of the Sudetenland; no fetched source draws the 1938 line more closely. Measured: **54,971 km2** (ESRI:54034). Share of the polygon inside each container: F51-1938-1945 100%.
