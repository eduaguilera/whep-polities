# South Africa (Union and Republic)

## Summary

The Union of South Africa, formed on 31 May 1910 by the unification of the Cape Colony, Colony of Natal, Transvaal Colony, and Orange River Colony. Declared a republic on 31 May 1961 and left the Commonwealth. Apartheid regime 1948–1994. Democratic transition under Mandela 1994. Still active as of 2025. Area ~1.22M km².

**Previous code ZAF-1828-2025 retired 2026-07-01.** That entry incorrectly set start_year=1828, predating the Union by 82 years and creating a direct territorial conflict with the concurrent Cape Colony rows (CAP-1800-1910, ~601k km², superseded 2026-08-14 by cap-1800-1895 / cap-1895-1910). The 1828 start had no documentary basis; the correct start is 31 May 1910.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 cowcode=560, vintage 1910, area ~1,221,037 km². Covers the full Union of South Africa (Cape Province + Natal + Transvaal + Orange Free State) from 1910. South West Africa (Namibia) administered separately under League of Nations mandate and tracked under NAM-1920-1990.

## Data routing

### dec-natal-label-1910-1957

Data labelled **'natal'** in Mitchell sources for years **1910–1957** is routed to ZAF-1910-2025.

**Basis:** The Colony of Natal was absorbed into the Union of South Africa on 31 May 1910 as Natal Province. The polities NAT-1843-1895 and NAT-1895-1910 end in 1910 and have no coverage of post-Union years. Mitchell's data for 'natal' 1910–1957 records provincial figures within South Africa.

**Approximation:** Natal Province (~94,361 km²) represents approximately 6% of ZAF's total area (~1,221,037 km²). Routing provincial data to the national polity overstates territorial scope roughly 13×. This is a known sub-territory approximation — no Natal-within-ZAF granular polity currently exists in WHEP.

**Alias added:** `natal` 1910–1957 → ZAF-1910-2025 in `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/applied_aliases.csv` (2026-07-02).

**Superseded 2026-09-24: the alias was removed and these years are unrouted.** Layer B has only 7 post-Union `natal` rows (mitchell): horses 1945-1957 and pigs 1957. On this polity the 1957 pigs (16,000) shared a key with `south africa`'s own 1957 pigs (564,000), so a `mean()` consumer published 290,000. A Natal-province polity was considered and rejected, because the rows are not provincial. The `natal` horses run 687,000 (1945) to 487,000 (1957), against Natal's own 40,000-67,000 in 1900-1908. They fill exactly the years where `south africa` has no horse value, and they sit continuously between its 1937 figure (778,000) and its 1959 figure (472,000). So they read as South Africa's national series filed under the wrong label. Routing them to a Natal row would put a national series on a province. The label-level alias cannot split the horses from the pig, so the label is left unrouted and the re-labelling is filed as an upstream issue. `pipelines/pre1961-matching/match.R` was changed the same way.

### dec-natal-horses-1945-1957

**The six `natal` horse rows (1945-1957) now reach this polity, by an item-scoped re-label (issue 675).** `data/final/source_label_item_corrections.csv` relabels mitchell `natal` / `horses` / 1945-1957 as `south africa` before matching, and that label routes here. The evidence adds an independent source to the continuity argument above. fao1952's own `Union of South Africa` horse column reads 778 thousand at 1937, equal to Mitchell's national `south africa` figure (778,000), which identifies the column. At 1950 it reads 679 thousand, equal to the `natal` label's 1949 value (679,000) and within 0.5% of its 1950 value (676,000). No cell collides: Mitchell's `south africa` has no horse value in 1938-1958. The 1957 `natal` pig row is **not** relabelled. It sits beside a real `south africa` pig value, so it is not the same shape, and it stays unrouted. See [log decision-item-scoped-label-corrections](../log.md#decision-item-scoped-label-corrections).
