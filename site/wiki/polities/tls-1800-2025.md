# Timor-Leste

## Summary

Timor-Leste (East Timor) as tracked by WHEP from 1800. A former Portuguese colony on the eastern half of the island of Timor, it was invaded by Indonesia in 1975 and occupied until 1999. Following a UN-supervised referendum, Timor-Leste became independent on 20 May 2002.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Timor-Leste shares a land boundary with Indonesia on the island of Timor, plus the Oecusse exclave on the north coast of West Timor.

### the iia record for Portuguese Timor is two disjoint windows

Recorded 2026-08-18. The page is silent on the Portuguese colonial economy and on
IIA-sourced data; this records what exists, **and the gap a queued note glossed
over**.

| item | n | span | median |
|---|---|---|---|
| coffee, green (t) | 12 | **1930-1940** | 1,050 |
| cacao beans (t) | 8 | **1909-1916** | 10 |

The note described these as reported "across 1909-1940". They are not: the two
windows are **disjoint**, with a **14-year gap** between 1916 and 1930 and no year
in which both items appear. "1909-1940" is the union of two separate records, not
a continuous span.

That matters for what the data can support. The Portuguese/Dutch Timor boundary
was fixed in 1904 and arbitrated by the Permanent Court of Arbitration in 1914, so
it was stable across both windows and this label maps to the same territory as
today's Timor-Leste. But the observations cannot show continuity *through* the
1910s-1920s, because for most of that period there are none.
