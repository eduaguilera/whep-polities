# Allier

## Summary

Allier is one of the 83 original departments created by the French Revolutionary Assembly in 1790, formed largely from the former province of Bourbonnais plus small parts of Nivernais, Marche and Auvergne. It sits in central France, in the modern Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region, with its prefecture at Moulins. `type: subnational` because this is a first-order administrative division of a continuously sovereign French state, not a separate polity in the WHEP sense — the national chain (FRA-1800-1919, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) already carries sovereignty and France's international status; this entry exists purely to carry department-level reporting territory so that data tabulated at the department level is not silently folded into national totals. The department has had essentially static borders since its creation: no French department has been split, merged, or transferred across an international boundary in the entire 1800-2025 window (unlike Alsace-Lorraine's departments, which is exactly why the FRA national chain itself is split at 1871/1919). `start_year` is set to 1800, not the department's actual 1790 creation, because no containing FRA row in this database extends earlier than FRA-1800-1871 — see `decisions` for why the ten-year gap could not be closed.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures department-level input data for Allier (unit_id FRA-FRK11 in the routing ledger, admin_name "FRK11", country France) that the pipeline had been defaulting to route to the national row FRA-1919-2025, a mismatch of roughly two orders of magnitude in area (France ~551,700 km2 vs. Allier ~7,340 km2) and one that erases which of France's ~90 present-day departments a given row actually describes. The routing reasoning recorded for this decision states plainly that "Allier is an original 1790 department, unaffected by any listed departure. No FRA candidate is Allier itself (all national totals)" — i.e. every existing FRA-* row in the table is a whole-country aggregate, and none of them was ever a candidate match for a single department's figures. Allier's distinctness as a reporting/administrative unit is not in dispute: departments are France's standard first-order statistical and administrative division, used continuously since 1790 by INSEE and its predecessors for census, agricultural, and production statistics, which is precisely the kind of sub-national reporting series this row is meant to receive. No Federico-Tena or Cliopatria trading-polity entry is needed to establish this, since French departments are a domestic administrative fact, not a matter of external recognition.

**Territory description:** Allier is a landlocked department in central France, part of the modern Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes administrative region, bordering the departments of Cher and Nièvre to the north, Saône-et-Loire to the east, Puy-de-Dôme to the south, and Creuse to the west. Its prefecture is Moulins, and it includes the towns of Montluçon and Vichy (the latter the seat of the wartime French State, 1940-1944). Its area is approximately **7,340 km2**, a figure taken from present-day INSEE/GADM department boundaries rather than measured from any polygon attached to this entry, since none is attached. The department's boundaries have been effectively unchanged since their 1790 creation, so this figure should hold for the whole 1800-2025 span once a polygon is fetched.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1790

**start_year set to 1800, not the department's actual 1790 creation date**

The routing decision's span_basis calls Allier an 'Original 1790 Revolutionary department' and proposes start_year: 1790. I did not follow that literally. The container gate rejects any edge that falls outside either party's own span, and the earliest FRA container row in the polities table is FRA-1800-1871 (1800-1871) — there is no FRA-* row of any kind covering 1790-1800, and no pre-1800 French national or provincial entity exists in this database to container against instead. Setting start_year: 1790 would leave a decade uncontained and fail the tiling requirement described in this prompt ('the container edges must tile the whole span'). Rather than fabricate a container row or leave a gap, I moved this entry's start_year to 1800 to match the earliest available container edge, and documented the discrepancy here rather than silently departing from the proposed decision. If a pre-1800 FRA container is added later (e.g. to model the actual 1790 department creation under the ancien-regime-to-Revolution transition), this entry's start_year should be revisited to 1790 at that point.
