# Sultanate of Oman

## Summary

The Sultanate of Oman as tracked by WHEP from 1856, when Oman and Zanzibar became separate sultanates. Oman has been an independent sultanate since the ninth century but was under Persian control from the 13th to 16th centuries, then Portuguese rule until 1650. From 1891 it was a British protectorate until 1977. The country changed its name from Muscat and Oman to Oman in 1970.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Cliopatria. Oman is bounded by the UAE (336 mi; 540 km), Saudi Arabia (420 mi; 676 km), and Yemen (179 mi; 288 km). The Musandam peninsula is an exclave separated from the main territory by the UAE.
