# Asia Other


> **End year moved 2021 -> 2025 on 2026-08-05 (issue 50), replacing `RASI-1850-2021`.**
>
> This is a statistical reporting bucket, not a historical state. Its previous end
> year was an artefact of when the row was written, not a fact about any territory:
> FAOSTAT still reports this area through **2024**, so the bucket stopped before the
> data did and its most recent years resolved to `NA`.
>
> `end_year` is EXCLUSIVE, so `2025` covers through 2024. 220 live rows already
> end at 2025, which is the convention for a row covering the present.
>
> The polygon is unchanged: `scripts/sources/reporting-areas/build.py` keys its
> features by `polity_code`, so the source was regenerated with the new code rather
> than the binding being left to dangle.
## Summary

> **`polygon_status` migrated derived -> assigned (2026-07-29).** `derived` is a legacy value that predates the documented vocabulary in [wiki/README.md](../README.md#page-schema), which says not to use it in new pages. It was never migrated, so the manifest's `claims_polygon_status` set omitted it and consumers were told this row does not claim a polygon. The polygon is composed from its members by `scripts/sources/reporting-areas/build.py`, so it IS this row's territory by construction. See [lint-polygon-status-vocabulary](../log.md#lint-polygon-status-vocabulary).


WHEP aggregate reporting-area row for residual Asian and Pacific trust-territory entries in WHEP harmonization tables. The 1850-2025 range follows (it was 1850-2021 until issue 50; see the note above) the current WHEP land-footprint production rows for area code 902.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** derived WHEP `reporting-areas` source.
- **Components:** British Indian Ocean Territory plus the Pacific Islands Trust Territory proxied by Micronesia and Marshall Islands current GADM 4.1 adm0 polygons.
- **Proxy note:** This is a residual reporting-area polygon, not a country polygon.

> **Northern Mariana Islands and Palau removed from the components on 2026-08-17 (issue 48).**
> They were two of four proxies for the Pacific Islands Trust Territory, while
> [roce-1850-2025](roce-1850-2025.md) claims both as members in their own right — so two
> aggregates that each receive data claimed the same ground, and allocating both spatially
> put two different aggregates' values on the same islands. The source table settles which
> claim is wrong: the `polity_code` column of `inst/extdata/harmonization/regions_full.csv`
> in WHEP gives `RASI` exactly two members, `IOT` and `PCI` (Pacific Islands Trust
> Territory), and files `MNP` and `PLW` under `ROCE`. The Asia Other claim was an artefact
> of how `PCI` was proxied, not a membership the source asserts.
>
> Measured 2026-08-17: this polygon falls **2,128 km² -> 1,140 km²** (MNP 499, PLW 473 of
> the removed area). The proxy now covers roughly half of the Trust Territory's former land
> area, which is the price of not double-claiming; a faithful `PCI` footprint would need
> per-year aggregate geometry, which this builder does not emit. `ROW-1850-2025` is
> unchanged, because `MNP` and `PLW` still enter its union through `ROCE`.
>
> `FSM` and `MHL` remain, even though both also have polity rows of their own
> ([fsm-1991-2025](fsm-1991-2025.md), [mhl-1874-2025](mhl-1874-2025.md)). That is the
> intended pattern documented on [row-1850-2025](row-1850-2025.md): an aggregate overlaps
> the own-data rows for its members. What is not allowed is two AGGREGATES overlapping.
