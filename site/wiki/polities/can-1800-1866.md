# Canada (pre-Confederation)

## Summary

Canada (pre-Confederation) covers British North America from the start of the
WHEP time window (1800) until 1866. The polygon is a composed union of GADM
province boundaries for Ontario, Quebec, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, and Prince
Edward Island (~1.21 M km²), used as a static colonial-era proxy for the core
administered British North American colonies. This replaces the former CShapes
1886 proxy (9.55 M km²), which overstated pre-Confederation territory ~8×.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: proxy — composed from GADM provincial boundaries; modern
boundary shapes applied to the colonial period.**

**Polygon source:** GADM composed union of provinces ON + QC + NB + NS + PE
(area: ~1,209,852 km²). Component areas: Province of Canada (Ontario + Quebec)
~1,076,000 km²; New Brunswick ~72,908 km²; Nova Scotia ~55,284 km²;
Prince Edward Island ~5,660 km². Computed as a static polygon fixed to a
reference year of 1800 (colonial-era proxy).

**Why this polygon.** CShapes 2.0 has no Canada feature before 1886. The
1886 CShapes feature (9.55 M km²) incorporates post-Confederation acquisitions
(Rupert's Land/Manitoba 1870, BC 1871, PEI 1873, prairies 1905), overstating
pre-Confederation territory roughly 8×. Cliopatria v0.1.3 has no British North
America entry before 1932. No other WHEP priority source (CHGIS, Paine 2024)
covers pre-1867 British North America.

**Approximation caveats.**

- Modern GADM provincial boundaries are used; the exact colonial borders
  (especially Rupert's Land frontier of the Province of Canada) differed
  somewhat. The approximation direction is conservative: actual
  pre-Confederation administered territory was probably slightly smaller (some
  unsettled northern Ontario/Quebec was not effectively governed). Error
  magnitude: likely <5% of the ~1.21 M km² figure.
- Newfoundland (separate British colony, did not join Canada until 1949) is
  excluded, which is correct — it needs its own polity row.
- The Hudson's Bay Company territory (Rupert's Land) and other western British
  territories are excluded because they were under HBC administration, not
  direct colonial governance, during this period.

**Improvement over prior proxy.** Replacing the CShapes 1886 feature
(9,553,644 km²) with this composed-union polygon reduces polygon_area_km2 from
~9.55 M to ~1.21 M km² and eliminates the 8× overstatement. The
polygon_vintage_drift issue is resolved for spatial-density calculations.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Successor:** [CAN-1866-1870](can-1866-1870.md) — Confederation 1867 united

## Sourced claims

- CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=20) records Canada from 1886-01-01, area ~9.55 M km²;
  no earlier feature exists. Direct inspection of cshapes2_full.gpkg,
  2026-06-29.
- Cliopatria v0.1.3 has no British North America feature; its earliest Canada
  entry (SeshatID ca_canada_dominion) begins 1932. Direct inspection of
  cliopatria_polities_only.geojson, 2026-06-29.
- Province of Canada area (~1,076,000 km²): Ontario (GIS area ~1,076,395 km²)
  plus Quebec are combined in the colonial Province of Canada. New Brunswick
  ~72,908 km², Nova Scotia ~55,284 km², PEI ~5,660 km² from GADM level-1
  province features.

## Decisions

**2026-06-29 — polygon replaced with GADM composed union:** Former CShapes 1886
proxy (9,553,644 km²) replaced with a composed union of GADM province boundaries
for ON + QC + NB + NS + PE (~1,209,852 km²). This resolves the 8× overstatement
documented in oq-british-north-america-polygon. polygon_source updated to
gadm-composed-union; polygon_feature_year set to 1800 (colonial-era reference);
polygon_status remains proxy (modern borders used as colonial-era approximation).

**2026-06-29 — polygon_vintage_drift documented (superseded):** Prior decision
to retain CShapes 1886 proxy. Superseded by the polygon replacement above.

## Open questions

### oq-multiple-colonies

Whether to split into separate colony-level rows (Upper/Lower Canada,
Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI, Newfoundland) depends on whether source data
provides colony-level series. Most 19th-century sources (Mitchell pre-1961)
report aggregate "Canada" values. Newfoundland (outside Confederation until
1949) needs its own WHEP polity covering 1800–1949.
