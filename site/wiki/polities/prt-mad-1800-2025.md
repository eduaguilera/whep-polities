# Madeira

## Summary

Madeira, "an autonomous region of Portugal, in the Atlantic Ocean about 805 km (500 miles) southwest
of mainland Portugal". "The archipelago includes the islands of Madeira, Porto Santo, and the Desertas,
administered together with the separate archipelago of the Savage Islands." It is one of the two
archipelagos of [prt-azm-1800-2025](prt-azm-1800-2025.md); the other is
[prt-azo-1800-2025](prt-azo-1800-2025.md).
