# Indonesia (1949-1969)

## Summary

> **RETIRED 2026-08-14 — replaced by [IDN-1949-1963](idn-1949-1963.md) (issue 23).**
> This row ended at 1969 because CShapes stamps Indonesia's enlargement
> `1969-09-17`, the Act of Free Choice. Measuring the shapefile instead of
> paraphrasing it shows CShapes' own change log for feature 851 opens a step on
> **1963-05-01**, the UNTEA-to-Indonesia hand-over, which is Biger's date too: the

CSV row for Indonesia from **1949** to **1969** — a **21-year row**
covering early independent Indonesia
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). COW code 850
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

West Papua transferred from the Netherlands (1963). Confrontation
with Malaysia (Konfrontasi, 1963-1966). The 1969 end-of-row event:
**Act of Free Choice** (1969) — West Papua formally annexed.

## Territorial extent

The row's polygon is bound to `cshapes-2.0` feature `850`
(Indonesia's Gleditsch-Ward code), `polygon_feature_year: 1949`. In
the underlying CShapes 2.0 shapefile (`data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`,
measured directly, ESRI:54034 equal-area CRS — not a wiki-source
citation), gwcode 850 has seven time-steps. The one bound to this row
runs `1949-12-29 → 1969-09-16` and measures **~1,466,882 km²**. This
matches the area independently computed from the committed
`data/final/polities_database.gpkg` for `IDN-1949-1969`
(**1,466,396.5 km²**; the ~0.03% difference is topology
simplification, not a different feature). Both the step immediately
before (`1945-08-17 → 1949-12-28`) and the step immediately after
(`1969-09-17 → 1976-07-16`) measure **~1,877,243 km²** — about
410,000 km² larger. That gap is consistent with the landmass of
Western New Guinea (West Irian / West Papua), which CShapes excludes
from Indonesia's polygon for exactly this row's span and includes on
both sides of it
[cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).

Two things follow from this:

1. **Within this row's own span, CShapes codes no area change** — the
   1949–1969 polygon is constant. Per the WHEP polity-definition rule
   (`ST_Area(geom)` constant between time-steps ⇒ same WHEP polity),
   CShapes alone gives **no evidence for a split**. The West New
   Guinea transfer, in CShapes' coding, happens exactly at this row's
   own boundaries (1949 start, 1969 end), not mid-span.
2. **Biger 1995 dates the same transfer to 1963, not 1969**: "Indonesia
   has expanded to include western Iran, formerly western New Guinea
   (1963)" [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia)
   (Biger's "western Iran" is a transcription artifact for "western
   Irian", i.e. West Irian/West Papua — the same Biger entry
   separately misdates the East Timor annexation as "1955" instead of
   1975, so date typos in this entry are a known pattern
   [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia)). 1963
   is **inside** this row's 1949–1969 span, not at its boundary. This
   is a genuine disagreement between two territorial-extent sources
   about when the border actually moved — see *Contradictions* below.

Historically, both dates are real events, not a simple error: the
Netherlands transferred administration of Netherlands New Guinea to
a UN Temporary Executive Authority (UNTEA) under the 1962 New York
Agreement, and Indonesia took over administrative control from UNTEA
on 1 May 1963. The **Act of Free Choice** — the internationally
supervised (and widely disputed) vote confirming permanent integration
— did not happen until August 1969, which is the date already named
in this page's Summary as the row's own end-of-span event. CShapes
codes the change at the 1969 legal-completion date; Biger's prose
names the 1963 de-facto administrative-transfer date. Both are
defensible; they are not the same date (uncited general history for
the UNTEA/1962 New York Agreement detail — no ingested source covers
it, see *Open questions*).

Konfrontasi (Indonesia–Malaysia confrontation, 1963–1966) did not
transfer any territory and is not a boundary event in either
CShapes or Biger's Indonesia entry; it is retained in the Summary as
political-context only.

  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia).
- "Indonesia has expanded to include western Iran [sic, Irian],
  formerly western New Guinea (1963)"
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia).
- Modern Indonesia (post-1963/1975 expansions) has land boundaries
  with Malaysia on Borneo (1,108 mi / 1,782 km) and Papua New Guinea
  (510 mi / 820 km); total national area given by Biger as 752,410
  sq mi (1,947,999 km²) as of 1994, i.e. after both the West New
  Guinea and East Timor annexations, so it is not directly comparable
  to this row's own polygon
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia).
- CShapes 2.0 codes territorial change as one of three types
  (creation/dissolution, sovereignty transfer over dependent
  territory, or border adjustment ≥ 100×100 km) and dates each change
  to the postwar/post-agreement date where a change was made
  permanent by treaty
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes).
  The West New Guinea transfer is coded as a sovereignty-transfer
  event at the `1969-09-17` time-step boundary of gwcode 850, which
  is this row's own end year (direct measurement of
  `data/geodata/cshapes-2.0/CShapes-2.0.shp`, not a wiki-source
  citation).
- Indonesia (COW state abbreviation `INS`, ccode 850) entered the COW
  state system on **1949-12-27**, two days before this row's own
  start year, with a single unbroken tenure through the end of the
  COW v2024 series (direct read of
  `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, row `INS,850`; no
  COW-coded gap during Konfrontasi or the 1965-66 regime change,
  consistent with WHEP's polity-definition rule that regime change
  without territorial change is not a new row
  [log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)).
  This specific row is not covered in the `key-dates` prose of
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates),
  so it is cited here as a direct data reference per option (a),
  not as a resolvable source-file anchor for the Indonesia-specific
  date itself.

## Contradictions

- **When did West New Guinea/West Irian become Indonesian
  territory?** Biger 1995 dates the transfer to **1963**
  [biger-1995 §indonesia](../sources/biger-1995.md#indonesia). CShapes
  2.0's own polygon time-steps for gwcode 850 date the corresponding
  area change to **1969-09-17** (direct measurement, see *Territorial
  extent*), which coincides with this row's own end year and the Act
  of Free Choice. The database currently follows **CShapes' 1969
  date** for this row's polygon and its end year, because
  `polygon_source: cshapes-2.0` is this row's declared polygon
  authority and the 1949→1969 boundary already matches a real
  CShapes time-step break — not because 1963 has been evaluated and
  rejected. If 1963 (administrative transfer under UNTEA, per
  general uncited history) is judged the better territorial-change
  date, this row would need to split into `IDN-1949-1963` (without
  West New Guinea, matching the CShapes 1949-1969 polygon) and
  `IDN-1963-1969` (with West New Guinea, requiring a polygon this
  source does not provide a matching CShapes time-step for — CShapes
  jumps straight from the 1949 polygon to the 1969 polygon with no
  intermediate 1963 step). Flagged for human review rather than
  resolved here.

## Open questions

### oq-west-new-guinea-split-date

Should this row split at 1963 instead of ending at 1969? Biger 1995
dates Indonesia's absorption of West New Guinea to 1963 (UNTEA-to-
Indonesia administrative transfer under the 1962 New York Agreement),
while CShapes' polygon time-step for gwcode 850 carries the change to
1969-09-17 (the Act of Free Choice). No ingested source in this wiki
documents the 1962 New York Agreement or UNTEA directly — that detail
above is uncited general history. If a source is ingested confirming
1963 as the operative territorial-control date, this row likely needs
to split into `IDN-1949-1963` and `IDN-1963-1969`, and a new polygon
for the 1963 West-New-Guinea-less/full-Indonesia boundary would need
to come from a source other than CShapes, which has no time-step at
1963 for gwcode 850. See *Contradictions* above.
