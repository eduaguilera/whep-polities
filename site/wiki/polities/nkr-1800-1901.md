# Kingdom of Nkore (Ankole)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Nkore (also known as Ankole) was a pre-colonial kingdom in southwestern Uganda, founded c. 15th century. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1901, when it was incorporated into the Uganda Protectorate. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). "The Protectorate of Uganda, which largely consisted of the Kingdom of Burundi and other client kingdoms and attached territories, dates from 1894" [biger-1995 §uganda](../sources/biger-1995.md#uganda).

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom was centred in southwestern Uganda between Lake Edward and the Kagera River. No CShapes data is available for this pre-colonial polity (no COW code assigned). The territory of Nkore was relatively stable, occupying the hilly grasslands of southwestern Uganda.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** none recorded in WHEP; the kingdom predates the 1800 database floor.
- **Successor:** [uga-1894-1902](uga-1894-1902.md) — Uganda Protectorate (from British incorporation ~1901); also [uga-1962-2025](uga-1962-2025.md) — modern Uganda
