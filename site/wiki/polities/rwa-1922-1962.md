# Rwanda (within Ruanda-Urundi)

## Summary

This row covers Ruanda/Rwanda as the northern residency of the Belgian
mandate (later UN trust territory) of Ruanda-Urundi, from the 1922 League
of Nations mandate to independence on 1962-07-01. It exists because
FAOSTAT reports Rwanda (area 184) separately already in 1961, i.e. the
data's territory is Rwanda alone — per the sub-territory rule it must NOT
be matched up to a Ruanda-Urundi aggregate (CShapes cowcode 515). Twin of
BDI-1922-1962.

## Territorial extent

The Ruanda residency's boundary is continuous with the independent
Republic of Rwanda (the mandate's internal split follows the pre-colonial
kingdoms). CShapes 2.0 feature cowcode 517 at vintage 1962 measures
25,250 km2.

- **Polygon source:** [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md), feature cowcode **517**, vintage 1962,
  `polygon_status: assigned`.
- **Area: 25,250 km²**, the CShapes feature's own figure and independent of our geometry. Our shipped
  polygon measures **25,186 km²**, a ratio of **0.997** — inside every tolerance and well clear of the
  0.1% band that would make the declaration a readout of itself (issue 195). Modern Rwanda is 26,338
  km²; the difference is Lake Kivu's share, which CShapes cuts differently from GADM.
- **Why `assigned` rather than `proxy`:** the residency boundary did not move between 1922 and 1962,
  and it is the same line the independent republic inherited, so the 1962 vintage describes the whole
  span rather than approximating it. That is the opposite of the mandate row
  [rwb-1922-1962](rwb-1922-1962.md), whose polygon is the two residencies unioned.
- **Sub-territory rule.** This row exists because sources report Ruanda separately from Urundi while the
  mandate was a single administrative unit. Its polygon must therefore never be used as a denominator
  alongside `RWB-1922-1962`'s in the same year — the mandate polygon (52,351 km²) already contains this
  one, and the pair is exactly the enclosure shape issue 197 catalogues.
