# Rwanda (within Ruanda-Urundi)

## Summary

This row covers Ruanda/Rwanda as the northern residency of the Belgian
mandate (later UN trust territory) of Ruanda-Urundi, from the 1922 League
of Nations mandate to independence on 1962-07-01. It exists because
FAOSTAT reports Rwanda (area 184) separately already in 1961, i.e. the
data's territory is Rwanda alone — per the sub-territory rule it must NOT
be matched up to a Ruanda-Urundi aggregate (CShapes cowcode 515). Twin of
BDI-1922-1962.

## Territorial extent

The Ruanda residency's boundary is continuous with the independent
Republic of Rwanda (the mandate's internal split follows the pre-colonial
kingdoms). CShapes 2.0 feature cowcode 517 at vintage 1962 measures
25,250 km2.

routes here, 1962 onward to RWA-1962-2025
