# Kenya (1924-1926)

## Summary

Kenya Colony in its reduced 1924-1926 configuration following the transfer of Jubaland (Trans-Juba) to Italian Somaliland. CShapes records area 44.02 sq deg for the period 1924-07-01 to 1926-09-26 (COW 501, status=colony, owner=Britain COW 200). The area dropped from 51.69 sq deg following the Jubaland transfer and then increased slightly to 47.31 sq deg in the next step. "Kenya was declared a British protectorate in 1895" [biger-1995 §kenya](../sources/biger-1995.md#kenya).
