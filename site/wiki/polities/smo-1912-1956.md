# Spanish Morocco (1912-1956)

## Summary

The Spanish Protectorate in Morocco from its establishment by the Franco-Spanish treaty of 27 November 1912 to the reunification of Morocco on 7 April 1956, when the northern Spanish zone was returned to the independent Kingdom of Morocco shortly after France relinquished its own protectorate. The Cape Juby / Tarfaya southern zone was ceded separately via the Treaty of Angra de Cintra on 1 April 1958, after the brief Ifni War.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature ID 602, vintage year 1912. The Spanish Protectorate was a non-contiguous territory: a northern zone along the Mediterranean coast (facing the Strait of Gibraltar, including Larache and Tetuán) and a southern zone around Cape Juby (Tarfaya). The Spanish plazas de soberanía (Ceuta, Melilla, Peñón de Vélez, Peñón de Alhucemas, Chafarinas) and Ifni were distinct territories with distinct sovereignty claims and are not captured by this row.

**Polygon vintage drift and Tangier approximation.** The CShapes polygon is dated 1912 (the year the Protectorate was established), but the WHEP data runs 1939-1951 — a 27–39 year drift. No major interior boundary changes occurred within the 1912-1956 span, so the drift is benign for the land area of the administered zone itself. The key approximation concerns the **Tangier International Zone**: the 1912 Spanish zone claim included Tangier, but by the Tangier Statute of 18 December 1923 Tangier (~373 km²) was placed under a separate international administration (France, Spain, UK, later others) and remained so until October 1956 when it was reintegrated into independent Morocco. If the CShapes 1912 polygon includes Tangier, the polygon **slightly overstates** the territory actually administered by Spain during the 1939-1951 data years by approximately 2% (~373 km² out of ~20,000 km²). This is a minor directional bias (area overcount) that does not affect production-data routing in any material way. The data itself is very thin (55 rows, rice only, n=2 observations at 1,000 t total), consistent in magnitude with a ~20,000 km² protectorate and insufficient to distinguish the Tangier-in or Tangier-out scenario. No polity split is warranted. Approximation direction: **slight area overcount (~2%)** if Tangier is included in the polygon; benign drift otherwise.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor (CSV):** SWA-1884-1912 (Spanish West Africa). This predecessor is geographically inconsistent — Spanish West Africa refers to the Saharan protectorates (Río de Oro / Saguia el-Hamra), not to the Spanish Zone of Morocco. SWA's successor arrow to SMO is factually wrong; it should be treated as a CSV error. See [swa-1884-1912](swa-1884-1912.md) if it exists.
- **Successor:** None (CSV field NA). The correct successor is the reunified Kingdom of Morocco from 7 April 1956 (partial — northern zone) and Tarfaya-adjusted Morocco from 1 April 1958 (southern zone).

## Contradictions

**Predecessor is wrong.** SWA-1884-1912 (Spanish West Africa / the Saharan protectorate) did *not* have Spanish Morocco as its successor. Spanish West Africa's successor territory is the Spanish Sahara → Western Sahara / Morocco-Mauritania partition, not Spanish Morocco. This is a candidate CSV fix: SMO-1912-1956's predecessor should be the pre-protectorate Alawi-dynasty Morocco (MAR-* or MOR-* chain) rather than SWA.
