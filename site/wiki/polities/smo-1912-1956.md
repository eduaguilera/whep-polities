# Spanish Morocco (1912-1956)

## Summary

The Spanish Protectorate in Morocco from its establishment by the Franco-Spanish treaty of 27 November 1912 to the reunification of Morocco on 7 April 1956, when the northern Spanish zone was returned to the independent Kingdom of Morocco shortly after France relinquished its own protectorate. The Cape Juby / Tarfaya southern zone was ceded separately via the Treaty of Angra de Cintra on 1 April 1958, after the brief Ifni War.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** Since 2026-09-25 the polygon is `constructed`
(`build_smo_1912_1956()`): CShapes 2.0 feature 602 (its single 1912-1956 step, 52,905 km²) **minus
the Tangier Zone** ([tng-1912-1956](tng-1912-1956.md)) and the sea in the zone's bounding box, which
is **52,576 km²** (ESRI:54034) at source. The Spanish Protectorate was a non-contiguous territory: a
northern zone along the Mediterranean coast (facing the Strait of Gibraltar, including Larache and
Tetuán) and a southern zone around Cape Juby (Tarfaya); the polygon has both parts. The Spanish plazas
de soberanía (Ceuta, Melilla, Peñón de Vélez, Peñón de Alhucemas, Chafarinas) and Ifni were distinct
territories and are not in it.

**Why the zone is removed (2026-09-25).** CShapes 602 draws Tangier inside the Spanish zone: GADM's
Tanger, Charf and Boukhalef communes are 39.5 of 46.7, 34.7 of 35.2 and 192.1 of 193.8 km² inside it.
But the convention of 1912 that created the protectorate already set Tangier apart ("Article 7 ...
stipulated that Tangier would be granted a special status and defined its geographical
boundaries"), and the international administration ran from 1925 to 1956.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §tangier](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#tangier)
FAO 1952 reports `Tangier` separately from `Spanish Morocco`, so leaving the zone in this polygon put
one reporting unit's ground inside another's. The removal is 329 km², 0.6% of the row.

The only period Tangier was governed from Tetuán is the Spanish occupation of June 1940 to October
1945. No row of this polity (FAO: 1939 and 1949-1951; the 1934-1938 averages) and no Tangier row falls
in those years, so the polity is not split for it; see [tng-1912-1956](tng-1912-1956.md).

*Superseded 2026-09-25, kept as the record:* the text below described the CShapes feature as used
directly and judged the Tangier inclusion a benign ~2% overcount with no split warranted. The
magnitude was right; the conclusion was not, because the zone is a separately reported unit.

**Polygon vintage drift and Tangier approximation.** The CShapes polygon is dated 1912 (the year the Protectorate was established), but the WHEP data runs 1939-1951 — a 27–39 year drift. No major interior boundary changes occurred within the 1912-1956 span, so the drift is benign for the land area of the administered zone itself. The key approximation concerns the **Tangier International Zone**: the 1912 Spanish zone claim included Tangier, but by the Tangier Statute of 18 December 1923 Tangier (~373 km²) was placed under a separate international administration (France, Spain, UK, later others) and remained so until October 1956 when it was reintegrated into independent Morocco. If the CShapes 1912 polygon includes Tangier, the polygon **slightly overstates** the territory actually administered by Spain during the 1939-1951 data years by approximately 2% (~373 km² out of ~20,000 km²). This is a minor directional bias (area overcount) that does not affect production-data routing in any material way.

## Predecessors and successors

This predecessor is geographically inconsistent — Spanish West Africa refers to the Saharan protectorates (Río de Oro / Saguia el-Hamra), not to the Spanish Zone of Morocco. SWA's successor arrow to SMO is factually wrong; it should be treated as a CSV error. See [swa-1884-1912](swa-1884-1912.md) if it exists.
- **Successor:** None (CSV field NA). The correct successor is the reunified Kingdom of Morocco from 7 April 1956 (partial — northern zone) and Tarfaya-adjusted Morocco from 1 April 1958 (southern zone).

## Contradictions

**Predecessor is wrong.** SWA-1884-1912 (Spanish West Africa / the Saharan protectorate) did *not* have Spanish Morocco as its successor. Spanish West Africa's successor territory is the Spanish Sahara → Western Sahara / Morocco-Mauritania partition, not Spanish Morocco. This is a candidate CSV fix: SMO-1912-1956's predecessor should be the pre-protectorate Alawi-dynasty Morocco (MAR-* or MOR-* chain) rather than SWA.
