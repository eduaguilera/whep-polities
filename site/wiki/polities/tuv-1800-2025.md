# Tuvalu

## Summary

Tuvalu is a Polynesian island nation comprising nine small atolls in the central Pacific, northeast of Fiji. WHEP tracks it as a single continuous row from 1800 to 2025. The islands (formerly the Ellice Islands) were administered jointly with the Gilbert Islands as the Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony. Following a 1974 referendum, the Ellice Islands separated and became the independent state of Tuvalu on 1 October 1978. Tuvalu joined the United Nations in 2000-09-05, which is the COW state-system entry date for small Pacific states. COW ccode=947 (stateabb=TUV); state-system entry 2000-09-05.

Tuvalu is one of the world's smallest nations by land area, though its Exclusive Economic Zone covers approximately 900,000 km² of ocean. [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope)

## Open questions

### oq-pre-independence-polygon

CShapes records no Tuvalu polygon before 2000. The WHEP row starts in 1800. The polygon for 1800–2000 is back-projected. During the joint Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony administration (1916–1975), a separate Tuvalu polygon would not have existed. Whether the back-projection correctly reflects the Ellice Islands' geography throughout the colonial period should be confirmed.

### oq-climate-vulnerability

Tuvalu faces existential threat from sea-level rise; some atolls may become uninhabitable within decades. This does not yet affect the WHEP territorial extent but may require future row restructuring if territory is formally lost or if Tuvalu's government relocates (as discussed in 2023–2024 agreements with Australia).
