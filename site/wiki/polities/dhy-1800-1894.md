# Kingdom of Dahomey

## Summary

The Kingdom of Dahomey was a pre-colonial West African kingdom in the area of modern Benin, founded c. 1600. The database tracks it from 1800 (start of the WHEP time window) until 1894, when it was conquered by France in the Second Franco-Dahomean War. Biger identifies Dahomey as "the great kingdom that controlled the area in precolonial times" and notes that the French colony created in 1883 was renamed Dahomey one year later in recognition of this historical kingdom.

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). The kingdom was centered in the southern part of modern Benin, with its capital at Abomey. Its territory extended from the coast (the Slave Coast) inland into the savanna.
