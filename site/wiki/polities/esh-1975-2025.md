# Western Sahara

## Summary

Western Sahara since Spanish withdrawal (1975-11-14). Sovereignty
disputed between Morocco and the Polisario Front (SADR). Area 102,703
sq. miles (265,898 sq km). Bounded by Morocco, Algeria, and Mauritania.
Capital El Aaiun.

Predecessor: [esh-1958-1975](esh-1958-1975.md). Successor: NA
(ongoing; sovereignty disputed).

## Territorial extent

Western Sahara is located in northwest Africa on the Atlantic coast.
Bounded by Morocco to the north, Algeria to the northeast, and
Mauritania to the east and south. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [esh-1958-1975](esh-1958-1975.md) -- Spanish Sahara.
  Spain withdrew 1975-11-14.
- **Successor:** NA (ongoing; sovereignty disputed).

## Sourced claims

- "Western Sahara's sovereignty is undetermined at present." [biger-1995 §western-sahara](../sources/biger-1995.md#western-sahara) (p.512)
- **1975-11-14: Spain withdraws.** Madrid Accords; territory divided
  between Morocco and Mauritania. Start-of-row event.
- **1976: Polisario Front declares SADR.** Sahrawi Arab Democratic
  Republic proclaimed.
- **1979: Mauritania withdraws claim.** Morocco then occupied the
  Mauritanian zone as well.
- **1991: UN ceasefire.** MINURSO deployed; referendum never held.
