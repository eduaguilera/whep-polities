# China (1932-1945)

## Summary

A **13-year row** covering the Republic of China from the establishment of the
Manchukuo puppet state (1 March 1932) through the Japanese surrender (15 August
1945). This polity represents geographic China proper — i.e., mainland China
excluding the three Manchurian provinces that were under Japanese occupation as
Manchukuo — as a **statistical reporting unit** in international compilations
(FAO, IIA, Mitchell). The territorial-economic scope of the data, rather than
the political-control boundary, drives this polity's definition.

The period spans the Nanjing Decade (1932-1937), the Second Sino-Japanese War
(1937-1945), and the wartime Nationalist government's relocation to Chongqing.
The end-of-row event is the Japanese surrender (15 August 1945), after which
China recovered Manchuria and other occupied territories.

This polity is the second half of a split of the former CHN-1921-1945 row. The
split was introduced to resolve a ~786,000 km2 spatial double-count with
MAN-1932-1945 that arose because the CShapes 1921 vintage polygon for China
(cowcode 710) includes Manchuria. See [chn-1921-1932](chn-1921-1932.md) for
the pre-1932 sub-period.

Key periods within this row:

- **1932-1937**: Nanjing Decade. Nationalist government administers China proper.
  Manchukuo occupies the three Manchurian provinces under Japanese control.
- **1937-1945**: Second Sino-Japanese War. Japan's full-scale invasion from
  July 1937 (Marco Polo Bridge Incident). Nationalist government relocates to
  Chongqing. Japanese forces occupy large parts of eastern China; however,
  international statistical agencies (FAO, IIA) continued to track "China" as
  the Nationalist-controlled territory (often labeled "China 22 provinces" for
  China proper excluding the three Manchurian provinces).
- **1945-08-15**: Japanese surrender. Manchuria returned to China.

Predecessor: [chn-1921-1932](chn-1921-1932.md). Successor:
[chn-1945-1947](chn-1945-1947.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon source:** Composed-difference: CShapes 2.0 feature 710 (1921 vintage,
7,496,578 km2) minus MAN-1932-1945 polygon (constructed from GADM 4.1
Heilongjiang + Jilin + Liaoning union, 791,708 km2). Result area:
**6,710,264 km2**.

**Method:** `cshapes2_full.gpkg` feature 710 (1921-03-13 to 1945-08-14) was
used as the base. The MAN-1932-1945 polygon (from `polities_database.gpkg`)
was subtracted using Shapely `difference()` after `.buffer(0)` topology repair.
The resulting MultiPolygon is valid and spatially non-overlapping with
MAN-1932-1945.

**Scope:** China proper (Republic of China controlled/claimed territory) from
1932 to 1945, excluding the three Manchurian provinces (Heilongjiang, Jilin,
Liaoning) occupied by Japan as Manchukuo. Note that the Nationalist government
claimed all of China including Manchuria diplomatically, but did not administer
it; the polygon reflects the de-facto administered/non-Manchukuo territory, which
matches the geographic scope of data reported as "China" or "China 22 provinces"
in international statistics.

**Polygon vintage drift:** The composed-difference polygon represents the
Manchukuo-period boundary (1932-1945), derived from a 1921 base minus a modern
provincial approximation of Manchukuo. The approximation excludes Rehe (Jehol),
which Manchukuo annexed in 1933 (~180,000 km2 of eastern Inner Mongolia/western
Liaoning). This means the polygon slightly overstates China's administered
territory for 1933-1945 by ~180,000 km2 in the Rehe/Jehol zone. This
approximation is accepted; refining the Rehe boundary is a Phase-3 task if
Rehe-specific data becomes load-bearing.

**Polygon status:** assigned (composed-difference; source polygon is
CShapes-quality for the China body; Manchukuo polygon is GADM-provincial
approximation).

**Polygon confidence:** medium (composed-difference from CShapes + GADM
provincial approximation of Manchukuo; Rehe exclusion unresolved; otherwise
sound).

**Polygon represents:** de-facto Nationalist China proper, 1932-1945 vintage.

## Decisions

- **No split at 1937** (2026-06-29, issue chn-1932-1945-polygon-vintage-drift-1937): Proposal to split at 1937 (outbreak of Second Sino-Japanese War) was evaluated and rejected. Rationale: (1) FAO/IIA data labeled "China, mainland" for 1937-1945 represents geographic all-of-China statistics, not Nationalist-administered territory only — confirmed by staple magnitudes consistent with national totals (rice ~46.7M t, wheat ~10.97M t); (2) no adequate GIS polygon exists for Japanese-occupied North China / Nationalist-controlled inland territory; any CHN-1937-1945 polygon would be a speculative ESTIMATE; (3) since the data scope is geographic China proper (excl. Manchukuo), the existing polygon representing that same scope is the correct match. The polygon vintage drift flag is real but does not indicate a data scope mismatch — it indicates political-control fragmentation within a consistent geographic reporting unit.

- **Split from CHN-1921-1945** (2026-06-29): Spatial double-count found between
  CHN-1921-1945 (CShapes 1921 polygon, 7,496,578 km2) and MAN-1932-1945
  (791,708 km2, contain_ratio=0.993). The 1921 CShapes entry for China includes
  Manchuria and covers the period 1921-08-14 as a single polygon — CShapes 2.0
  does NOT update the China polygon at 1932 to exclude Manchukuo. Therefore:
  - CHN-1921-1945 is split into CHN-1921-1932 (CShapes 1921 polygon) and
    CHN-1932-1945 (composed-difference polygon).
  - The previous wiki page for CHN-1921-1945 incorrectly stated that
    "CShapes 2.0 polygons for the CHN chain already exclude Manchukuo territory
    from 1932 onward." This was wrong; CShapes uses a single 1921-1945 entry.
  - Rehe (Jehol) exclusion (~180,000 km2) is a known approximation accepted at
    this phase.

## Open questions

### ~~oq-flax-step-change-1932~~ CLOSED 2026-06-29

**Audit findings:** The issue description contained two errors that are now corrected.

1. **The 180,894 figure is hectares (area), not tonnes (production).** It belongs to CHN-1913-1914 (year=1914 IIA data), not CHN-1932-1945. The original open question incorrectly attributed this value to CHN-1932-1945.

2. **The 11.3x step-change is a large DECREASE in area, not an increase in production.** The comparison is CHN-1913-1914 (180,894 ha flax area, 1914) vs CHN-1932-1945 (16,000 ha flax area, 1934-1938 period average) — a family-wide decrease of ~11x from 1914 to the 1934-1938 period, not the 1921-1932 → 1932-1945 tonne-production increase that the issue assumed.

3. **Actual CHN-1932-1945 IIA flax values are small:** ~16,000 ha and ~3,500 t for the 1934-1938 period average; ~9,700 t for 1939.

4. **A genuine but minor source-level double-count does exist:** Both IIA "china, mainland" (routed to CHN-1932-1945) and FAO 1952 "China Manchuria" (routed to MAN-1932-1945) report identical values of ~3,500 t / ~16,000 ha for 1934-1938. This likely reflects IIA "china, mainland" 1934-1938 flax capturing Manchurian-origin output that FAO 1952 also recorded separately under "China Manchuria". The double-count is minor (~3,500 t) and does not affect routing: the existing polity split and label routing (IIA "china, mainland" → CHN-1932-1945; FAO "China Manchuria" → MAN-1932-1945) is correct. No routing or alias change warranted.

**Conclusion:** No CSV row change, no routing change, no polygon change. The composed-difference polygon for CHN-1932-1945 is correct and unaffected by this audit.

### oq-rehe-jehol-1933

Manchukuo formally annexed the Rehe (Jehol) province in 1933. The MAN-1932-1945
polygon (constructed from 3 GADM provinces) does not include Rehe. This means
the CHN-1932-1945 composed-difference polygon includes ~180,000 km2 of Rehe
territory that was effectively under Manchukuo control from 1933. This is a
known approximation. Phase-3 refinement if Rehe-specific data becomes
load-bearing.
