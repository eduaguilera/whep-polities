# Kenya (1888-1891)

## Summary

Kenya under the Imperial British East Africa Company from the 1888 royal charter to the 1891 CShapes boundary cut. The IBEAC received its charter on 3 September 1888 from the British government, giving it administrative rights over territory in East Africa leased from the Sultan of Zanzibar. The company's administration was financially unsustainable and effectively ceded its rights to the Crown on 1 July 1895 — modelled in [ken-1894-1902](ken-1894-1902.md) onward.
