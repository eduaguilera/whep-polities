# Kingdom of Porto-Novo

## Summary

The Kingdom of Porto-Novo (Hogbonou or Ajashe) was a Gbe (Aja-Fon) coastal kingdom on the Bight of Benin, founded c. 16th century as an offshoot of the Allada kingdom. Porto-Novo became an important slave trade port and maintained close commercial ties with Brazilian and Portuguese traders. The database tracks it from 1800 (the WHEP window floor) until 1882, when France formally established a protectorate. An earlier French protectorate had been sought by King Toffa in 1863, but it was effectively formalised and made permanent in 1882. Biger notes that "the colony of Benin was created from the Etablissements dues Golfe de Guinee (the name given to the French protectorates along the Bight of Benin in 1883), and was renamed Dahomey one year later." Modern Benin's capital is Porto-Novo. The polygon source is Paine et al. (2024).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is Paine et al. (2024). Porto-Novo's territory was limited to the coastal lagoon zone between modern Cotonou and the Nigerian border, an area of a few thousand square kilometres. It was much smaller than the neighbouring Kingdom of Dahomey to its west.

## Contradictions

The Biger entry refers to "the French protectorates along the Bight of Benin in 1883" and the colony being renamed Dahomey; the WHEP end date of 1882 (one year earlier) requires independent sourcing. The difference between 1882 and 1883 may reflect the distinction between the protectorate treaty (1882) and the formal colonial establishment (1883).

## Open questions

### oq-1863-vs-1882-end-date

Porto-Novo first came under French protection in 1863 (King Toffa's request). French protection lapsed and was reinstated multiple times before being formalised in 1882. The WHEP end year of 1882 needs verification: does this mark the final, permanent protectorate treaty?

### oq-overlap-with-dhy

Porto-Novo and the Kingdom of Dahomey ([DHY-1800-1894](dhy-1800-1894.md)) were contemporaneous neighbours. Their polygons should not overlap. The boundary between the two kingdoms along the coast needs verification.

### oq-deeper-sourcing

Biger covers Benin at the Dahomey colony and independence level without discussing Porto-Novo as a distinct pre-colonial entity. A specialist source (e.g., Akinjogbin 1967 *Dahomey and its Neighbours*; Manning 1982 *Slavery, Colonialism and Economic Growth in Dahomey*) is needed.
