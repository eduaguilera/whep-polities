# Suriname (SUR-1975-2025)

## Summary

Suriname gained full independence from the Netherlands on 25 November 1975. This polity covers the independent period from 1975 to present. It is the renamed and updated successor to the former monolithic SUR-1886-2025 entry; Biger 1995 boundary claims are retained here as they describe independent Suriname's borders.

## Territorial extent

Suriname's boundary with Brazil runs for 371 miles (597 km.) [biger-1995 §suriname](../sources/biger-1995.md#suriname). The territory is ~144,985 km², identical to predecessor polities. CShapes 2.0 feature FID 57 covers 1975-11-25 to 2019-12-31 for COW 115.

## Sourced claims

- Suriname is listed among Brazil's northern neighbors with a boundary of 371 miles (597 km.) (p.91). [biger-1995 §suriname](../sources/biger-1995.md#suriname)
- Berbice (now eastern Guyana) and Suriname were both Dutch territory from the mid-seventeenth century (p.260). [biger-1995 §suriname](../sources/biger-1995.md#suriname)
- In the late eighteenth century Suriname and Berbice disputed ownership of land west of the Courantyne River; in 1794 the Dutch government ruled that Berbice extended to the river's west bank (p.260). [biger-1995 §suriname](../sources/biger-1995.md#suriname)
