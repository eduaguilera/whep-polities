# Lunda Empire

## Summary

The Lunda Empire from the 1800 database floor to its colonial partition in 1884–1887. Founded c.1665 (Mwane-a-Yamvu title established after Ilunga Tshibinda's marriage to Lueji A'Nkonde), the Lunda heartland spanned present-day southern DR Congo, eastern Angola, and northwestern Zambia. Wikipedia dates the colonial partition to 1884, at the start of the colonial era; the WHEP end year of 1887 reflects completion of the partition into the three colonial spheres.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from Paine et al. (2024). Covers the Lunda heartland (Mwantiyanvwa centre in modern Katanga) and its tributary reach eastward into modern Zambia and westward into modern Angola.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** None in WHEP. The 1800 start is a database floor; the empire was founded c.1665.
- **Successors:** The CSV lists [cod-1960-2025](cod-1960-2025.md) and [cod-1885-1891](cod-1885-1891.md). In reality Lunda territory was partitioned across DR Congo, Angola, and Zambia. The current WHEP chain captures only the COD share; the Angolan and Zambian shares are not linked back to LND.

- Lunda's ruling title Mwane-a-Yamvu was established c.1665, with "Ilunga Tshibinda from the Luba Kingdom with Lueji A'Nkonde" producing the first paramount. [wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15 §lunda](../sources/wikipedia-african-precolonial-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- "At the start of the colonial era (1884), the Lunda heartland was divided between Portuguese Angola, King Leopold II of Belgium's Congo Free State and the British in North-Western Rhodesia", setting boundaries that underpin modern Angola, the DR Congo, and Zambia.
