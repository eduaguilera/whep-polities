# Kenya (1902-1907)

## Summary

The East Africa Protectorate (later Kenya) in the 1902–1906 CShapes window. In 1902 administration transferred to the Colonial Office and the protectorate's borders were extended to include the Eastern Province of Uganda (the territory west of modern Kenya's border with Uganda, including Kisumu and the Nandi Hills). In 1905 the capital moved from Mombasa to Nairobi. The 1906 CShapes boundary cut anticipates further administrative consolidation.

## Open questions

### oq-ken-1906-event

The 1906/1907 CShapes cut does not correspond to a single named political event (the EAP did not become the Colony of Kenya until 23 July 1920), and the step it opens is geometrically identical to the one it closes — 772,553.06 km² on both sides. It may reflect a minor internal reorganisation or a CShapes cartographic revision. Consequence, noted 2026-08-17: because no territory moves at that date, nothing about this row's polygon depends on which side of it 1906 falls.
