# Zimbabwe

## Summary

Zimbabwe is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Zimbabwe (formerly Southern Rhodesia / Rhodesia) from 1890 to the present. The 1890 start date reflects the establishment of the British South Africa Company's administration. Zimbabwe became fully independent with a black majority government in April 1980.

## Territorial extent

Zimbabwe is a landlocked country in southern Africa bounded by Mozambique, South Africa, Botswana, and Zambia. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- "In 1889, Cecil Rhodes, who had secured mineral concessions in Matabeleland, was chartered and received many of the powers of the British government. In 1893, the British South Africa Company seized Matabeleland" and subsequently expanded operations (p.395). From 1891 operations extended north of the Zambezi River. [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- "After 1896, Northern Rhodesia was constituted as a colonial protectorate which status it retained when the British South Africa Company lost its charter in 1923. In 1964, Northern Rhodesia became an independent republic as Zambia" (p.395). Southern Rhodesia made a unilateral declaration of independence on 11 November 1965 which was not recognized by Britain. "Only after the establishment of a black majority regime in April 1980 did Zimbabwe become fully independent" (p.396). [biger-1995 §mozambique](../sources/biger-1995.md#mozambique)
- **Southern Rhodesia became white-dominated self-governing colony 1923** (p.520). Ian Smith UDI 1965 (p.520). ZAPU (Nkomo) and ZANU (Mugabe) guerrilla war (p.520). 1980 free elections ended Smith regime (p.520). ZAPU-ZANU merger 1987-1988 (p.520). [biger-1995 §zimbabwe](../sources/biger-1995.md#zimbabwe)
