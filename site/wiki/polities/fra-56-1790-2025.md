# Morbihan (department)

## Summary

Morbihan is a French département in southern Brittany, on the Atlantic coast, with Vannes as its prefecture. It was created in 1790 as one of the roughly 83 original départements of the Revolutionary administrative reform that replaced the old provinces of the Ancien Régime, and it has remained one of France's standard administrative subdivisions ever since, with no boundary change of the kind that affected Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, the Territoire de Belfort, or the overseas départements. `type: subnational` is used because this entry exists to carry a reporting territory below the national level, not a sovereign state: France held sovereignty over Morbihan throughout 1790-2025, and the entry must not compete with the national FRA chain (FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025) in matcher ranking. The span 1790-2025 follows the country's default start rule (1790, the Revolutionary reform date) rather than the underlying data's first attested year (1862), because Morbihan is not among the four documented departure cases that would justify a later or different start.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision that produced this entry names unit_id FRA-FRH04 ("Morbihan"), country France, but does not itself state which source table, row count, or year range backs it -- unlike source data explicitly cited in sibling exemplar pages. The decision's own text says the data's first attested year is 1862, implying an ingest source (not named in the decision payload) carries Morbihan-labelled or FRH04-coded rows starting there, which is why the default-start rule (1790) had to be explicitly invoked to override the data's own first year rather than simply matching it. This gap is recorded as an open question (oq-mitchell-fao-data-not-checked) rather than papered over. Previously, any Morbihan-scoped rows would have had nowhere department-level to go: the routing decision states explicitly that none of the three existing FRA-* candidates (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) IS Morbihan -- they are national totals, and Morbihan contains none of them -- so absent this entry such rows would be forced up to the national row, silently conflating a single département's data with all of France, at roughly 1/13,000th the reporting scale (Morbihan ~6,823 km2 vs. metropolitan France ~551,000 km2). What confirms Morbihan is a distinct, statistically-tracked administrative entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision: it is one of the original départements created by the 1790 law dividing France into administrative units for direct central governance and statistical reporting, a status it has held continuously (as a first-order administrative and statistical unit) for over two centuries, making it a natural unit for a national statistical agency (e.g. INSEE, or historical predecessors) to report on separately from the national aggregate.

Its prefecture is Vannes; other notable towns include Lorient and Pontivy. It covers approximately **6,823 km2** (INSEE figure for the modern département) -- this is a stated territory figure from a modern administrative source, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry, and must not be confused with `polygon_area_km2`, which is left blank because no geometry exists here.
