# Finland (1940-2025)

## Summary

This row tracks Finland for **86 years**, from the post-Winter-War
territorial configuration to the present
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Winter-War Finland. Continuation War (1941-1944) -- Finland briefly
regained Karelia then lost it again. Paris Peace Treaty (1947) confirmed
1940 losses plus Petsamo (Arctic access lost). Modern Finland ~338,000
km². EU member 1995. NATO member 2023.

Predecessor: [fin-1917-1940](fin-1917-1940.md). Successor: NA (still
active).

## Sourced claims

- **1940-03-12: Moscow Peace Treaty.** Start-of-row event. Finland ceded
  Karelia and other territories to the USSR.
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland)
- **1941-1944: Continuation War.** Finland allied with Germany, briefly
  regained Karelia, then lost it again after armistice (September 1944).
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland)
- **1947-02-10: Paris Peace Treaty.** Confirmed 1940 losses plus Petsamo
  (Finland lost Arctic coast access).
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland)
- **1995-01-01: EU accession.** Finland joined the European Union.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **2023-04-04: NATO accession.** Finland joined NATO.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **Area: 130,552 sq mi (338,000 sq km).** Bounded by Norway (453 mi),
  Russia (616 mi), Sweden (364.5 mi).
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland) (p.218)
- **Territorial losses since 1917.** "Since 1917 they have lost 10%
  of their original territory, suffered massive destruction and loss
  of life, and were forced to absorb 500,000 Karelian refugees."
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland) (p.219)
- **Finland-Russia boundary.** "Today's Finnish-Russian boundary is
  the former Finnish-Soviet Union line, which stretches 816 miles
  (1,313 km.)"
  [biger-1995 §finland](../sources/biger-1995.md#finland) (p.220)
