# Italy (1919-1947)

## Summary

The Kingdom of Italy (from 1946 the Republic) between the Treaty of Saint-Germain (10 September 1919),
which gave it Trentino, South Tyrol, Trieste and Gorizia, and the Treaty of Paris (10 February 1947),
which took Venezia Giulia, Fiume, Zara and the Adriatic islands to Yugoslavia and Briga and Tenda to
France [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §italy](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#italy). Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old `ITA-1919-2025` row at 1947 (issue
686); the later years are [ita-1947-2025](ita-1947-2025.md). The Dodecanese are a separate row,
[itaeg-1912-1947](itaeg-1912-1947.md).

**Why this entry exists.** About 5,000 layer-B rows report Italy for 1919-1946 (juan, IIA, Mitchell).
They were measured against CShapes' post-1947 polygon, which leaves out Istria, the Julian March and
Tenda, Italian for all of those years. This row gives those years the interwar territory.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** proxy. Constructed (`build_ita_1919_1947` in
`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`):

- CShapes 2.0 gwcode 325, the 1919-2019 step, which has the post-1947 border
  [cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes);
- plus the Austrian Littoral (Kuestenland: Gorizia-Gradisca, Trieste and Istria with Cres and Losinj)
  from the HistoGIS 1860 crownlands, without Krk, which the Treaty of Rapallo (12 November 1920) gave to
  the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes. Rapallo gave Italy "territories generally corresponding to
  the peninsula of Istria and the former Princely County of Gorizia and Gradisca, with the addition of
  the Snezhnik Plateau" [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §italy](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#italy);
- plus the GADM 4.1 communes of Tende and La Brigue [gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md), ceded to
  France in 1947.

**km² reasoning (Mollweide).** The result measures 307,356 km²: 300,215 km² of CShapes, 6,887 km² of
the Littoral beyond it, and 262 km² of Tende and La Brigue beyond it. Italy's stated area in the 1930s
was about 310,200 km², so the polygon is about 0.9% short. What is missing is named, not estimated: the
Carniolan districts Italy also held (Idrija, Postojna, Ilirska Bistrica and the Snezhnik plateau), Fiume
(from 1924), Zara and Lagosta, and the smaller 1947 adjustments on the French border (Mont Cenis, Mont
Thabor, Mont Chaberton). No fetched source draws an outline for any of them. Hence `proxy`.

**Sub-eras not split.** Rapallo (November 1920) fixed the eastern border a year after this row starts,
and Fiume was annexed in 1924 (about 21 km²). Both are inside this row, on the Rapallo extent.

**Known overlaps.** Yugoslavia's rows use CShapes 345's 1920-1991 step, which already draws Istria as
Yugoslav from 1920, so this row and [f248-1920-1947](f248-1920-1947.md) both cover about 6,900 km² of
Istria and the Julian March in 1920-1946. The same holds for Tende and La Brigue against the French
national row. Both are the CShapes error this split corrects on the Italian side only.
