# Mauritania

## Summary

Mauritania is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of Mauritania from 1979 to the present. The 1979 start date reflects a territorial change: Mauritania renounced its claim to the southern portion of Western Sahara in 1979, after having occupied it since 1975. Mauritania originally became independent from France on 28 November 1960.

## Territorial extent

Mauritania (area 395,956 sq. miles; 1,025,130 sq. km; population 2,280,000 [1994]) is bounded by Western Sahara (970 miles; 1,561 km) in the north, Algeria (288 miles; 463 km) and Mali (1,390 miles; 2,237 km) in the east, Senegal (505 miles; 813 km) in the southwest, and the Atlantic Ocean in the west. Capital: Nouakchott. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.
