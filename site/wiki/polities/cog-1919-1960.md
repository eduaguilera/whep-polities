# Congo (1919-1960)

## Summary

French Congo (Middle Congo / Moyen Congo), the colonial polity governing the territory of the present-day Republic of the Congo, from a 1919 boundary change until independence on 15 August 1960. "The Congolese gained their independence from the French in 1960" [biger-1995 congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). Both Congo and Gabon became divisions of French Congo in 1889 and 1903; "Moyen (Middle) Congo and Gabon became divisions of French Congo (which also included today's Chad and Central African Republic). On 11 February 1906 a decree was published in which the four colonies were defined separately in general terms" [biger-1995 congo-zaire](../sources/biger-1995.md#congo-zaire). CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
