# Rio Grande do Norte

## Summary

Rio Grande do Norte is a Brazilian state occupying the easternmost tip of South America, the point where the coastline turns from running north-south to east-west, bordered by Ceará to the west, Paraíba to the south, and the Atlantic on its remaining two sides. This entry covers Rio Grande do Norte from 1889, when the Proclamation of the Republic converted the former Imperial province of Rio Grande do Norte into a state of the federation, to the present. `type: subnational` is used because Rio Grande do Norte has never been a sovereign polity: it was a captaincy and then Imperial province under Portuguese and Brazilian rule, and has been a first-order administrative division of the Brazilian federation continuously since 1889. It is not among the six BRA units whose start year departs from the default rule (Acre, Mato Grosso do Sul, Rondônia, Amapá, Roraima, Tocantins) — those departed because of later territorial elevation, splits, or contested sovereignty; Rio Grande do Norte, like Maranhão, Bahia, Ceará and Alagoas, was a direct province-to-state conversion with no such interruption, so the default 1889 start applies per the routing decision's span_basis. The 2025 end reflects that Rio Grande do Norte remains a current Brazilian state.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id BRA-RIOGRANDEDONORTE) found no existing BRA polity representing Rio Grande do Norte specifically: the only BRA rows in the database are the three national totals (BRA-1800-1903, BRA-1903-1909, BRA-1909-2025), none at state scale. Source data attributed to Rio Grande do Norte was drawn from a Brazilian state-level panel spanning roughly 1900-2023, entirely contained within the proposed 1889-2025 span with no unroutable years, per the routing decision's proposed disposition and consistent with sibling verdicts BRA-ALAGOAS, BRA-MARANHAO, BRA-BAHIA, BRA-CE, BRA-ES, BRA-GOIAS, BRA-PARANA, BRA-PERNAMBUCO, BRA-PIAUI and BRA-RIODEJANEIRO created in the same batch. Previously, any state-level row for Rio Grande do Norte had nowhere correct to route except the national BRA-1909-2025 container, a unit roughly 152 times Rio Grande do Norte's own area, which would misattribute state-scale production and demographic figures to the whole country. What confirms Rio Grande do Norte as a distinct reporting unit rather than a component folded into national totals is the same evidence cited for the sibling entries: IBGE and its predecessor statistical bodies have reported Rio Grande do Norte as a named first-order administrative unit continuously since the Republic's founding, the standard practice for Brazilian state-level agricultural and demographic statistics.

**Territory description:** modern Rio Grande do Norte covers approximately 52,800 km2, one of Brazil's smaller states by area, occupying the easternmost salient of South America where the coast bends from a north-south to an east-west orientation — the "horn" of the Brazilian Nordeste. It is bordered by Ceará to the west, Paraíba to the south, and the Atlantic Ocean along both its northern and eastern coasts, with its capital, Natal, sited near the mouth of the Potengi river close to Cabo de São Roque, the continent's easternmost point. Unlike Maranhão, whose borders with Pará and Piauí shifted repeatedly in the early 20th century, Rio Grande do Norte's frontiers with Ceará and Paraíba have been comparatively stable administrative lines since the Imperial era, though this has not been independently verified against historical province maps in this session. No `polygon_area_km2` is recorded here since no geometry is yet attached; the 52,800 km2 figure above is the modern state's commonly cited area, not a measurement of any geometry attached to this entry.

## Decisions

### d-code-iso3-subunit-start-end-pattern

**Code follows the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-start-end pattern**

BRA-RN-1889-2025 follows the dominant subnational pattern (<ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>), matching the sibling BRA state entries created in this same batch (BRA-ALAGOAS, BRA-BAHIA, BRA-MARANHAO, BRA-CE, etc.). The subunit token RN is the standard two-letter abbreviation used by IBGE for Rio Grande do Norte (abbrev_state='RN' in geobr-ibge), matching the two-letter form already used for BRA-CE (Ceará) and BRA-ES (Espírito Santo) rather than the full-name-concatenation form used for BRA-MARANHAO or BRA-BAHIA; both forms coexist among the existing 15 BRA subnational codes, so RN follows the shorter precedent since a two-letter abbreviation is unambiguous and already the source dataset's own id_column value. Rio Grande do Norte is not a historical territory with a name distinct from its modern administrative identity in the way that would call for a bespoke code like ALK or HYD, so the bespoke-code pattern does not apply here.

### d-three-container-edges-tile-span

**Three container edges tile the full 1889-2025 span with no gap**

The container list has three edges — BRA-1800-1903 (1889-1903), BRA-1903-1909 (1903-1909), and BRA-1909-2025 (1909-2025) — because the containing national chain itself changes rows at 1903 and 1909, and the containment gate rejects an edge whose interval falls outside either party's own span. A single edge citing only BRA-1909-2025 across 1889-2025 would leave 1889-1909 uncontained by that row, since Rio Grande do Norte's state-level existence begins at the 1889 Proclamation of the Republic, twenty years before the national row's own 1909 start. This mirrors the sibling BRA-MARANHAO/BRA-ALAGOAS/BRA-BAHIA entries created in the same batch, and corrects the routing decision's own proposed container_code of BRA-1909-2025 alone, which would have left 1889-1909 uncontained.
