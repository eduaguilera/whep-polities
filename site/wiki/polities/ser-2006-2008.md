# Serbia (2006-2008)

## Summary

> **RETIRED 2026-08-05 — duplicate of [srb-2006-2008](srb-2006-2008.md).** Both rows
> were live and covered Serbia for 2006-2007. Issue 43. Route everything here to
> `SRB-2006-2008`, which describes the same territory and is correct on every axis
> this row is wrong on:
>
> | | `SER-2006-2008` (this row) | `SRB-2006-2008` (kept) |
> |---|---|---|
> | iso3 | `SER`, a local code, though Serbia has a real ISO3 | `SRB` |
> | cow | `NA` | `345` |
> | polygon binding | gwcode **345** at year **1992** | gwcode **340** at year 2007 |
> | what that polygon IS | Serbia **and Montenegro**, 101,959 km² | Serbia with Kosovo, 87,993 km² |
> | declared area | `null`, so nothing compared it | 87,745 km², within 0.3% |
> | rows routed to it | **0 observed rows** — one year-ranged alias entry, no FAOSTAT-map entry | 68,875 observed rows, area 272 |
>
> **Its own prose convicts it.** The Territorial extent section below claims
> "~88,000 km² (Serbia proper ~77,000 km² plus Kosovo ~11,000 km²)" — which is
> `SRB-2006-2008` exactly, and contradicts the 101,959 km² polygon it is actually bound
> to by ~14,000 km². The page intends Serbia-with-Kosovo and is bound to
> Serbia-and-Montenegro.
>
> **The consequence while it was live:** its polygon wholly contained
> [mne-2006-2025](mne-2006-2025.md) — 14,003 km², 100.0% of Montenegro — for 2006-2007,
> double-counting Montenegro's territory in any spatial use, and it swallowed
> `SRB-2006-2008` itself at 99.9%. Both invisible to `validate_period_overlaps`, which
> compares periods within one prefix, and these are `SER-*` against `SRB-*` (issue 82).
>
> Nothing was lost by retiring it: the successor list it carried
> (`SRB-2008-2025`, `KOS-2008-2025`) was better than the kept row's and has been ported
> across, and no data reached it. The one thing that pointed here was the year-ranged
> alias `serbia` 2006-2008 in `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/applied_aliases.csv`,
> retargeted to `SRB-2006-2008`. It carried no source and no observed rows, which is why
> the first count of routed data read zero — `validate_aliases` caught it as soon as the
> status changed, which is the gate doing its job.
>
> **The diagnosis was already in the repo.** `state/territory_basis.csv` line 600 records
> this row as `back_projected` with the reason "polygon vintage 1992 is BEFORE the period
> starts (2006)". The pipeline detected the wrong vintage and nothing acted on it.


Transitional Serbia between Montenegrin independence and Kosovo's
declaration of independence
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

3-year row. Montenegro's independence (June 2006) left Serbia as sole
successor of Serbia and Montenegro. Kosovo declared independence on
17 February 2008, splitting this row into SRB and KOS.

Predecessor: [scg-1992-2006](scg-1992-2006.md). Successors:
[srb-2008-2025](srb-2008-2025.md) and
[kos-2008-2025](kos-2008-2025.md).
