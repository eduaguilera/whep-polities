# Romania (to 1913)

## Summary

This row tracks Romania for **55 years**, from the union of Wallachia
and Moldavia to the Second Balkan War
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). CSV notes: NA.
`verification_status = VERIFIED`.

Romania formed from the personal union of Wallachia and Moldavia
under Alexandru Ioan Cuza in 1859 (both former Ottoman vassal
states). Romania gained independence from Ottoman suzerainty at the
Congress of Berlin (1878), gained Northern Dobruja from the Ottomans
(losing Southern Bessarabia to Russia). The 1913 end date aligns
with the Second Balkan War (Treaty of Bucharest, 10 August 1913),
when Romania gained Southern Dobruja from Bulgaria.

Predecessor: NA per CSV. Romania formed from the union of Wallachia
and Moldavia in 1859, both Ottoman vassal states.
Successor: [rou-1913-1918](rou-1913-1918.md).

## Sourced claims

- **1859: Union of Wallachia and Moldavia.** Alexandru Ioan Cuza
  elected prince of both principalities, creating the United
  Principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia (later Romania). This is
  the start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1878: Congress of Berlin.** Romania gained full independence from
  Ottoman suzerainty. Territorial exchange: gained Northern Dobruja
  from the Ottoman Empire, lost Southern Bessarabia to Russia. The
  Congress of Berlin is a major mid-row event with both a sovereignty
  change and territorial exchange.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1881: Kingdom of Romania.** Romania proclaimed a kingdom under
  Carol I. A political change (principality to kingdom) but not a
  territorial change.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1913: Second Balkan War.** Romania gained Southern Dobruja from
  Bulgaria (Treaty of Bucharest, 10 August 1913). This is the
  end-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)

## Open questions

### oq-ottoman-predecessor

The CSV has `predecessor = NA` but Romania formed from Ottoman vassal
states (Wallachia and Moldavia). The predecessor chain should
reference Ottoman territory. Neither Wallachia nor Moldavia has a
WHEP row.

### oq-1878-congress-of-berlin

The Congress of Berlin (1878) involved a territorial exchange: Romania
gained Northern Dobruja (~15,000 km²) from the Ottomans but lost
Southern Bessarabia (~10,000 km²) to Russia. Under the WHEP
territorial-change rule, this is a candidate mid-row split point
(specific date, significant area change, sovereignty change from
vassal to independent). The CSV does not split at 1878.
