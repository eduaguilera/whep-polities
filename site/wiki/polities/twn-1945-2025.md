# Taiwan

## Summary

This row tracks Taiwan under **Republic of China (ROC) administration** from ROC retrocession (25 October 1945) to the present (2025). The ROC government retreated to Taiwan in 1949 following the Communist victory in the Chinese Civil War, and has administered the island since. Taiwan's international legal status is complex and not universally recognized as sovereign.

Area ~36,000 km² — Taiwan island plus the Pescadores (Penghu) and other smaller islands. The territorial extent has been stable since 1945 retrocession.

This row was created as part of the split of the original TWN-1896-2025 (which incorrectly spanned the Japanese colonial period) at the 1945 retrocession. The correct successor to TWN-1895-1945 (Formosa/Japanese Taiwan).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** CShapes 2.0 feature cowcode=713, vintage year 1949. The island's territorial extent was unchanged at 1945 retrocession — no border adjustment occurred. polygon_vintage_drift of ~4 years (1945 vs. 1949 vintage) is benign.
- **Area.** ~36,000 km² (Taiwan main island + Pescadores/Penghu). Biger 1995 records 13,900 sq mi [35,987 km²]; "no land boundaries" [biger-1995 §taiwan](../sources/biger-1995.md#taiwan) (p.487).
- **COW code 713.** Taiwan's COW membership status reflects its unique political situation. The polygon feature uses cowcode 713.
