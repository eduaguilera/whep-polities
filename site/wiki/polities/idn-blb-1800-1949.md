# Bali and Lombok (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Bali with Lombok, the residency of Bali and Lombok in the Outer Provinces of the Dutch East Indies, until the 1949 transfer of sovereignty (IDN-BLB-1949-1951 continues it). The islands' own rows, [idn-bal-1800-1949](idn-bal-1800-1949.md) and [idn-lom-1800-1949](idn-lom-1800-1949.md), are now inside this one.

## Why this entry exists

The IIA and FAO yearbooks report Bali and Lombok together for 1934-1948 (`dutch bali; dutch lombok`, 6 pairs); the islands had rows only one by one. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
