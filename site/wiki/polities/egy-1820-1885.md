# Egypt with Sudan (1820-1885)

## Summary

This polity covers **Egypt including Sudan** from the completion of the
Sudan conquest to the Mahdist loss. It encompasses the Khedivate's maximum
territorial expansion including Equatoria (~3M km2 by 1877-1884) and the
subsequent contraction.

Predecessor: [egy-1800-1820](egy-1800-1820.md). Successor:
[egy-1885-1899](egy-1885-1899.md).

19 data rows (Mitchell, 1866-1884).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** Cliopatria "Muhammad Ali dynasty" 1848 vintage
  (~1,834,433 km2). This carries over the original EGY-1800-1899 polygon.
  The 1848 vintage is a reasonable central estimate for the Sudan-included
  period.
- **Polygon vintage drift.** Data rows span 1866-1884; the polygon vintage
  is 1848 — drift of up to 28 years. Cliopatria shows area varying
  considerably: ~1,643,207 km2 (1822-1833), ~1,834,433 km2 (1848),
  ~3,002,206 km2 (1877-1884 with Equatoria). The 1848 polygon under-
  represents the Equatoria peak but is the best available single vintage
  for the middle of the span. This approximation is documented here; a
  more precise match would require time-sliced polygons for the Equatoria
  expansion sub-period.
- **Recommended future split.** If Equatoria-period data (post-1874)
  becomes distinct from pre-Equatoria data, consider splitting at 1874
  (Equatoria annexation) using Cliopatria's ~3M km2 vintage.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [egy-1800-1820](egy-1800-1820.md) -- Sudan conquest
  1820-1824 expands Egypt to ~1.83M km2.
- **Successor:** [egy-1885-1899](egy-1885-1899.md) -- Fall of Khartoum
  1885; Sudan effectively lost to Mahdist revolt.

## Sourced claims

- **1820-1824: Sudan conquest.** Egypt expands southward; start-of-row
  event.
- **1848: Cliopatria polygon vintage.** ~1,834,433 km2; central estimate
  for the Sudan-included period.
- **1869: Suez Canal opened.** Major geopolitical event.
- **1874: Equatoria annexed.** Area expands to ~3M km2; not yet captured
  in a separate polygon.
- **1882: British occupation.** Egypt remains nominally Ottoman/Khedival.
- **1885: Fall of Khartoum.** Death of General Gordon; Sudan effectively
  lost to the Mahdist State. End-of-row event.
