# Palau

## Territory and evidence

This row represents Palau after the Woleai islands were separated from Palau District for transfer to Yap. The contemporaneous government announcement specifies 1 April 1953 and names Gaferut, Faraulep, West Fayu, Pikelot, Olimarao, Woleai, Lomotrek, Ifalik, Elato, Satawal and Eauripik. It says the new district alignment restores that of the Japanese period. [Wilson 1953](../sources/micronesian-monthly-1953-02.md).

The announcement is prospective; the actual signed transfer order has not been located. The 1980 Code independently corroborates the resulting Palau district bounded by 2–11°N and 130–136°E, excluding those eastern islands. The annual 1953 boundary adopts the announced transfer year; do not infer that this record describes Palau before 1953. [Code 1980, Title 3 §1](../sources/ttc-1980.md).

GADM 4.1 ADM 0 PLW falls within those island-selection bounds and includes the main archipelago and southwest islands, including Hatohobei and Sonsorol ADM 1 units. Modern coastline area 484.170249 km² (ESRI:54034) is a proxy, not an exact historical shoreline or marine-jurisdiction polygon. The attached geometry is unchanged from the former record. The territorial inference rests on district bounds and named islands, not equality of areas.

The unsupported 1991 start and the proposed 1994 independence alternative both confuse status with territory. FishStat reports Palau from 1981. FAOSTAT area 180’s observed map continues to start 2000; no older FAOSTAT observations are manufactured. Earlier consumer resolution remains conditional on coherent snapshot testing.
