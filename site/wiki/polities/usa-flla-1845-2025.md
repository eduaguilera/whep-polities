# Louisiana and Florida (combined reporting unit)

## Summary

Louisiana and Florida reported together, as sugar statistics did for the two cane-growing states: [usa-la-1812-2025](usa-la-1812-2025.md) plus [usa-fl-1845-2025](usa-fl-1845-2025.md). A statistical unit, not a territory of its own. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Why this entry exists

The label `united states: florida, louisiana` reports the pair for 1928-1932 to 1945 (**15 (year, label) pairs**), beside `united states: louisiana` alone (1909-1929). No polity was the pair. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
