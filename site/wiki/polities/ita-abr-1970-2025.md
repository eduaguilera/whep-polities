# Abruzzo (region of Italy)

## Summary

Abruzzo is one of Italy's 15 ordinary-statute regions (regioni a statuto ordinario), located in central Italy, bordering the Adriatic Sea to the east, Marche to the north, Lazio to the west, and Molise to the south, with L'Aquila as its capital. This entry is `type: subnational` because Abruzzo never held sovereignty of its own in this period — Italy held sovereignty throughout 1970-2025 — the row exists purely to carry a reporting/administrative territory below the national level, the rationale already established for sibling entries ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte) and ITA-LIG-1970-2025 (Liguria). Abruzzo became an operative administrative and statistical unit in 1970, when Italy's ordinary-statute regions held their first regional council elections mandated by the 1948 Constitution; before that date regional government existed on paper only and statistics were not compiled at this level, so this row's span begins in 1970, following the same convention already committed to disk by the two sibling entries, rather than the 1861 unification-based default the routing decision itself proposed. The end_year of 2025 is open — the region remains current.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: this row is created to receive statistical series reported under Italian administrative unit ITA-ITF1 (admin_name ITF1, i.e. Abruzzo) in NUTS/GADM-style subnational classifications, per the routing decision for unit ITA-ITF1; no row count is given in the routing decision object itself, only that no existing polity IS Abruzzo. Previously matched to: all such data was being matched to the national ITA polities (ITA-1919-2025 and its predecessors), which is wrong for any year from 1970 onward because Abruzzo-level reporting is a real, distinct statistical/administrative practice — folding it into the national total makes a regional figure impossible to recover or distinguish from other regions' contributions. What confirms distinctness: Abruzzo is a constitutionally recognized ordinary-statute region of Italy with its own directly elected regional council since 1970, its own regional government (Giunta regionale), and its own budget and statistical reporting apparatus (Istat, Eurostat NUTS2 code ITF1), reported separately from national totals — the same standing already documented for the sibling entries ITA-PIE-1970-2025 and ITA-LIG-1970-2025, which set the working precedent for how Italy's ordinary-statute regions enter this table.

**Territory description:** Abruzzo sits in central Italy on the Adriatic coast, dominated by the Apennines (including Gran Sasso, the highest peak of the range) in its interior and a narrow coastal strip to the east, bordering Marche to the north, Lazio to the west, and Molise to the south, with L'Aquila as its capital. Its present-day administrative area is approximately **10,832 km²**, comprising four provinces: L'Aquila, Teramo, Pescara, and Chieti. This figure is a widely cited present-day administrative-area statistic, not a measurement of any geometry attached to this entry (none is attached yet) — once the GADM ADM1 feature is fetched, `polygon_area_km2` should be populated from that geometry directly and can be checked against this figure.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern-subunit

**Use ITA-ABR-1970-2025, the dominant ISO3-SUBUNIT-years pattern**

57 of 66 existing subnational rows follow <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> (e.g. DZA-CVD-1902-1919, ESP-AS-1833-2025). Abruzzo is an ordinary Italian administrative region with no name-of-its-own historical identity distinct from being a region of Italy (unlike Manchuria or Hyderabad, which earned bespoke codes as historical territories), so the bespoke-code exception does not apply. ITA already has two subnational rows created under this pipeline, ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte) and ITA-LIG-1970-2025 (Liguria), which set the country's precedent; ABR is the natural three-letter abbreviation for Abruzzo and is consistent with that precedent.

### d-start-year-1970-not-1861

**Start year 1970, departing from the routing decision's own stated span_basis of 1861**

The routing decision object for this unit argues start_year=1970 in its 'proposed' block but its own routing_reasoning text also floats a 'default start rule' implying 1861 for regions without a specific departure, reasoning that Abruzzo was part of the Kingdom of Italy from unification. That 1861 reasoning is superseded here: two sibling entries already created in this pipeline, ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (Piemonte) and ITA-LIG-1970-2025 (Liguria), establish the actual working convention for Italy's fifteen ordinary-statute regions — start_year=1970, the year of the first regional council elections mandated by the 1948 Constitution, because before that date regional government existed on paper only and no region-level statistics were compiled. Abruzzo is one of the fifteen ordinary-statute regions (not one of the five special-statute regions: Sicilia, Sardegna, Trentino-Alto Adige, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Valle d'Aosta), so the same 1970 convention applies to it. Using 1861 here would create an inconsistency where sibling ordinary-statute regions created in the same pipeline disagree on when the administrative tier they all represent came into existence, so 1970 is used, matching the precedent this repository has already committed to disk. This confirms and formalizes the routing decision's own routing_concerns note that the 1970 convention for ordinary regions is inferred from sibling verdicts rather than stated explicitly in the country convention file, and that the convention file should be updated to state it explicitly.

## Open questions

### oq-gadm-fetch-pending

**gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered but the local file excludes Italy**

sources.yaml registers gadm-4.1-adm1 as the mechanism for constructing sub-national region polygons, and it is the correct source type for an ordinary Italian region (a standard GADM ADM1 unit, GID_1 ITA.13_1 for Abruzzo per GADM's own coding). The file present in this repo's local geodata is an 81-country curated subset that does not include Italy, so 0 ITA.x features are available from it today. Until the full/global GADM 4.1 ADM1 layer is fetched and Abruzzo's feature extracted, this row has polygon_status: unassigned and polygon_feature_id: null. When fetched, polygon_status should move to assigned and this question can close.

### oq-pre1970-abruzzo-rows

**Where does pre-1970 Abruzzo-labelled data land, and is it double-counted against ITA national rows**

Under the 1970 convention adopted here, any historical statistical series labelled 'Abruzzo' before 1970 should fall to whichever national ITA row was current at the time (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, or ITA-1919-2025), since no distinct regional administrative/statistical tier existed before the 1970 council elections. This has not been checked against routing_verdicts.csv to confirm no pre-1970 Abruzzo-labelled row was already mis-routed to this new entry, nor whether the pre-unification Kingdom of the Two Sicilies-era administrative divisions covering Abruzzo (it was split into Abruzzo Ulteriore I/II and Citeriore under Bourbon rule before 1861) constitute an earlier distinct reporting unit that should be tracked separately rather than folded silently into the national row.

### oq-adjacent-region-boundary-overlap

**Modern GADM boundary vs. Abruzzo's 1970 administrative boundary, and overlap with neighbouring regional rows**

Once fetched, the GADM ADM1 feature will reflect present-day regional boundaries. Italian regional boundaries have been largely stable since 1970 with only minor municipal reassignments between neighbouring regions (Abruzzo borders Marche, Lazio, and Molise, plus a short Adriatic coastline), so vintage risk is low but unverified against a historical atlas. Separately, this entry has not been cross-checked against every other Italian subnational row for possible overlap: only ITA-PIE-1970-2025 and ITA-LIG-1970-2025 are known siblings, and neither shares territory or years with Abruzzo, but this has not been confirmed against the full wiki/polities directory, nor against whether Molise (which historically administered jointly with Abruzzo as 'Abruzzi e Molise' until 1963, per the country convention's own departure note) creates any pre-1970 boundary ambiguity relevant to how this row's predecessor data should be understood.
