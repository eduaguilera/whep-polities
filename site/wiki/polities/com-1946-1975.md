# Comoros (1946-1975)

## Summary

The Comoros as a French overseas territory, from 1946 (when it became a separate territory from Madagascar) until independence declared unilaterally in 1975. "The Comorians voted for independence from France in a referendum conducted in 1974" and "Independence was declared unilaterally in 1975 for all the islands. Mayotte, however, is still governed by France" [biger-1995 comoros](../sources/biger-1995.md#comoros). The Comoros consist of three main islands in the Indian Ocean between the southeastern African mainland and Madagascar, with a total area of 863 sq mi (2,235 sq km) and no land boundaries [biger-1995 comoros](../sources/biger-1995.md#comoros). CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
