# Burkina Faso

## Summary

Burkina Faso is a WHEP polity row covering the territory of independent Burkina Faso (formerly Upper Volta) from 1960 to the present. Upper Volta gained independence from France on 5 August 1960 and was renamed Burkina Faso in 1984.

## Territorial extent

Burkina Faso is a landlocked West African country. The boundary with Ghana follows the colonial boundary between British and French possessions. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0.

## Sourced claims

- The boundary between Ghana and Burkina Faso runs for 415 miles (668 km) from the Black Volta River tripoint with Ivory Coast to the boundary tripoint with Togo. [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
- The Ghana-Togo boundary "runs for 545 miles (877 km.) between the boundary tripoint with Burkina Faso in the north and the Gulf of Guinea in the south" (p.244). [biger-1995 §ghana](../sources/biger-1995.md#ghana)
