# Queensland (state of Australia)

## Summary

This entry covers Queensland as a constituent state of the Commonwealth of Australia, from Federation on 1 January 1901 to the present (end_year 2025 under this project's open-end convention). Queensland existed earlier as a separate self-governing British colony (1859-1900, tracked in the sibling entry QUE-1859-1900), but Federation changed its sovereignty status: it ceased to be a colony reporting to the British Crown directly and became a state within the new federal Commonwealth, with defence, foreign affairs, and other powers ceded upward. `type: subnational` is used, not `national`, because Queensland has never itself been a sovereign state independent of a larger federation or empire during this span -- the same reasoning already applied to the five sibling state/territory entries (AUS-NSW-1901-2025, AUS-SA-1901-2025, AUS-TAS-1901-2025, AUS-WA-1901-2025, AUS-NT-1911-2025, AUS-ACT-1911-2025). Administratively, Queensland's internal governance (a state parliament, premier, and unicameral Legislative Assembly since abolishing its Legislative Council in 1922) and its external boundary have been stable across the full span; the significant discontinuity is the 1901 sovereignty transfer itself, which is why this row starts there rather than in 1859.

**Why this entry exists.** This entry captures Queensland-labelled statistical data for the Federation-era and modern period (1901-2025), continuing the reporting territory that the colonial entry QUE-1859-1900 tracks for 1859-1900. Prior to this entry, any Queensland-labelled rows dated 1901 or later that were routed by an automated matcher risked falling to the national aggregate AUS-1901-2025, which is roughly 4.4x Queensland's own area (1,852,642 km2 vs Australia's ~7.7 million km2) and would badly overstate the reporting unit's extent for any per-area or density measure. Federation itself, and the Commonwealth of Australia Constitution Act 1900, is the historical event that confirms Queensland became administratively distinct from its pre-1901 self as a colony -- it retained its own parliament and territory but ceded sovereignty upward -- which is exactly the kind of discontinuity this project's country convention treats as a new row for mainland states, matching the precedent already set for NSW, SA, TAS, and WA (all AUS-<STATE>-1901-2025) and for ACT and NT (both starting 1911, the later date of their own creation as federal territories).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `registered_source_feature` route, using `gadm-4.1-adm1` feature `AUS.7_1`, which is registered in scripts/sources.yaml and present locally. `polygon_status: assigned` because this is a direct, present-day administrative boundary match for a polity whose own external borders have not changed since Federation -- unlike Western Australia or the Northern Territory, Queensland has had no comparable post-1901 boundary transfer.

**Territory description:** Queensland occupies the northeastern quarter of the Australian mainland, bordered by the Coral Sea and the Pacific Ocean to the east, the Gulf of Carpentaria and Torres Strait to the north, the Northern Territory to the west, and South Australia and New South Wales to the south. It contains the Great Barrier Reef along its coast, the Cape York Peninsula, and major cities Brisbane (the state capital), Gold Coast, Townsville, and Cairns. Its area is approximately 1,852,642 km2 (measured from the attached GADM 4.1 geometry AUS.7_1, an equal-area computation, not an independently sourced historical figure) -- roughly the size of Mexico, and Australia's second-largest state by area after Western Australia.

No proxy substitution issue arises here as it does for polities with boundary discontinuities: Queensland's border has not moved in a way that would make a present-day feature a poor stand-in for 1901, so no under- or over-coverage correction is recorded against this figure.

## Sourced claims

- Queensland was proclaimed a separate British colony, split from New South Wales, on 6 June 1859, and became a founding state of the Commonwealth of Australia at Federation on 1 January 1901 (Commonwealth of Australia Constitution Act 1900).
- GADM 4.1's admin-1 feature AUS.7_1 delineates the present-day state of Queensland, whose external boundaries with New South Wales, South Australia, and the Northern Territory have not changed since Federation, unlike the Northern Territory's own 1911 transfer from South Australian to Commonwealth administration.

## Decisions

### d-code-pattern

**Code follows the dominant <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> pattern, matching sibling AUS states**

425 of 436 subnational rows use <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, and all six existing AUS subnational rows (AUS-ACT-1911-2025, AUS-NSW-1901-2025, AUS-NT-1911-2025, AUS-SA-1901-2025, AUS-TAS-1901-2025, AUS-WA-1901-2025) follow it. Queensland is not a bespoke historical territory like Alaska or Hawaii with a name independent of the modern federation -- it is a mainland state whose administrative identity and name are continuous with the colony and the present-day state. AUS-QLD-1901-2025 was chosen over AUS-QUEENSLAND-1901-2025 to keep the subunit token short, consistent with ACT, NSW, NT, SA, TAS, WA all being three-letter-or-shorter abbreviations rather than full names.
