# Málaga (province of Spain)

## Summary

Málaga is one of Spain's 50 provinces, created by the Javier de Burgos administrative reform of 30 November 1833 alongside every other Spanish province, and since 1981 also part of the Andalusia autonomous community on Spain's southern Mediterranean coast (the Costa del Sol). Unlike Asturias, Cantabria, Madrid or the other single-province autonomous communities, Málaga is one of eight provinces making up Andalusia, so its 1833 province boundary and any autonomous-community boundary are not identical -- Andalusia is subdivided the way Galicia's four provinces or Castile and León's nine are, not coextensive with a single province. This entry is type: subnational because Málaga is a sub-unit of the sovereign Spanish state, not a state in its own right: Spain held sovereignty over this territory throughout 1833-2025, and the row exists only to carry a statistical reporting geography smaller than the national aggregate ESP-1800-2025 and distinguishable from it because Spanish and EU statistical agencies (INE, Eurostat/GISCO) have tabulated Málaga separately from the national total, at NUTS3 code ES617, for over a century and a half. This mirrors the treatment already given to sibling NUTS3 Spanish province units such as Asturias (ES120), A Coruña (ES111) and Madrid (ES-MD), all of which were create_new decisions under the same country convention.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: the routing decision (unit_id ESP-ES617) captures valued statistical rows for unit ES617 ("Málaga"), country Spain, under the same country convention already applied to sibling NUTS3 Spanish units (A Coruña/ES111, Madrid/ES-MD, Asturias/ES120, and onward through the ES6xx Andalusian series), following the system default start_year of 1833 (the Javier de Burgos provincial reform, applied because Málaga is not one of the enumerated departure units such as the Canary Islands split) and an open end_year of 2025 for still-current units. Previously matched to: nothing -- the routing_reasoning states explicitly that no existing polity matches Málaga province by name or identity, that the closest candidates (A Coruña, Madrid) are different provinces, and that Spain-national (ESP-1800-2025) is a container rather than this specific territory; the fallback in the absence of a dedicated row would have been the national aggregate, which would collapse a province-level series into a country-level one and was rejected for the same reason it was rejected for the sibling provinces. What confirms distinctness: Málaga's status as a first-order Eurostat NUTS3 unit (ES617) and an INE statistical division reporting at province granularity since the 1833 reform is the same evidentiary basis already accepted for the sibling ESP province rows -- continuous, separately-tabulated provincial statistics distinguish it from the national ESP-1800-2025 aggregate, which at roughly 505,000 km2 is about 69x the area of Málaga.

## Territorial extent

Polygon status: Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_feature pointing at mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro (the Spanish INE province numeric code), under which Málaga is conventionally cpro 29, giving the anticipated feature_id ESP-MA-1833-2025 -- but scripts/sources.yaml currently marks mapspain-ign present_locally=False, so the provinces.gpkg file has not been downloaded or placed on disk and the route cannot be executed until that data is fetched. This is the same unmet precondition already recorded for ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025, ESP-MD-1833-2025 and the other sibling provinces, flagged in the routing decision as a concern rather than asserted resolved (contradicting its active use for 7 sibling provinces). gadm-4.1-adm1 has no matching feature for ESP provinces at this granularity (it models Spain at the 17-autonomous-community level, and Andalusia's adm1 feature does not carry province-level attribution), so it was correctly ruled out.

Territory description: Málaga province occupies a strip of Spain's southern Mediterranean coast within Andalusia, bordered by Cádiz and Seville to the west, Córdoba to the north, and Granada to the east, fronting the Alboran Sea/Mediterranean along the Costa del Sol. Its capital and largest city is Málaga; other significant towns include Marbella, Fuengirola, Ronda and Antequera. On a modern map it corresponds to the Málaga province polygon within the Andalusia autonomous community, one of eight provinces making up that community rather than a single-province community like Asturias or Madrid. Its administrative area, per INE, is approximately 7,308 km2 -- this is the region's own official area figure, not a measurement of any attached polygon, since none is attached yet. Provincial boundaries in Spain have been essentially unchanged since the 1833 reform, so once the IGN provinces layer is acquired, vintage risk across the full 1833-2025 span should be low, matching the assessment already made for the sibling ESP province entries.

## Predecessors and successors

Málaga has no predecessor polity in this database: before 1833 the territory it now covers was administered as part of the Kingdom of Granada's Antiguo Régimen structure, which this database does not model at that granularity, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row -- the same treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025. It has no successor either: the province remains a current, active administrative and statistical unit of Spain as of 2025, hence the open end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention already established for the other Spanish NUTS3 units.

## Decisions

### d-code-format-as-abbreviation

**Used the two-letter abbreviation MA rather than the province numeric code, matching the ESP house convention**

The dominant subnational pattern for Spanish provinces already established by ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025 is <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end> using a short letter abbreviation of the unit name rather than the NUTS3 digits (ES617) or the INE province numeric code (29). This page follows that convention exactly, minting ESP-MA-1833-2025 rather than a numeric-coded alternative, so the sibling set of Spanish province rows stays internally consistent and any future province entry can be predicted from the same rule without a lookup table. MA is both the modern ISO 3166-2 subdivision code (ES-MA) and the everyday abbreviation used in Spanish statistical series, reducing ambiguity for a future author matching a new data source against this row. The routing decision's own polygon_feature_id anticipation, ESP-MA-1833-2025, already assumes this exact code, so departing from it would have created a mismatch between the routing record and the page.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-source-not-acquired

**The mapspain-ign polygon source is registered but not fetched, and Málaga's cpro=29 feature is unconfirmed**

scripts/sources.yaml lists mapspain-ign with id_column=cpro, the Spanish INE province numeric code, under which Málaga is conventionally cpro 29 -- but the polygon_reasoning for this routing decision flags that sources.yaml marks mapspain-ign present_locally=False even though it is the active, already-relied-upon source for seven sibling provinces (Asturias, Cantabria, A Coruña, Lugo, Madrid, Ourense, Pontevedra), all of which likewise carry polygon_status: unassigned pending the same fetch. Until the provinces.gpkg file is downloaded and the cpro=29 feature is confirmed as matching the modern Málaga province outline, this entry must stay unassigned with no geometry. Whoever resolves the sources.yaml present_locally flag should verify there is exactly one province-level feature for Málaga before assigning it here, and should reconcile why the flag says False while eight ESP province pages already depend on the source being available.

### oq-pre-1833-territory-unmodeled

**The pre-1833 territorial antecedents of Málaga province have no distinct predecessor polity in this database**

Before the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform, the area now forming Málaga province was part of the larger historic Kingdom of Granada's administrative and fiscal divisions under the Antiguo Régimen, which does not correspond cleanly to the 1833 province boundary. This database does not model that earlier structure as a distinct polity row, so predecessor is left empty rather than pointing at a non-existent row, mirroring the treatment given to ESP-AS-1833-2025, ESP-CO-1833-2025 and ESP-MD-1833-2025. Also open: whether the routing decision's container edge (ESP-1800-2025, 1833-2025) should instead be split at any point where Spain's own national row's containment basis changes (e.g. the Second Republic 1931-1939, the Franco dictatorship, or the 1978 Constitution establishing the autonomous communities), since Málaga's status as a province is continuous but the sovereign national structure containing it was not -- the sibling ESP pages treat this as a single edge, and this page follows that precedent without independently verifying the national row's own era boundaries in polities_database.csv.
