# Brazil

## Summary

Brazil from the **1909 boundary settlements** to the present. CShapes records
identical geometry for 1909-1960 and 1960-2019, i.e. Brazil's external borders
have not changed since 1909 — this is the stable modern territory, and the row
carrying the overwhelming majority of Brazilian data.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 gwcode 140, feature year **1930** (the 1909-1960
  time-step), **8,472,100 km²**, identical to the 1960-2019 feature.
- **`polygon_status: assigned`** — borders constant across the whole span, so a
  single vintage is exact rather than approximate. This is the strongest
  territorial-constancy claim of the three Brazil rows.

## Sourced claims

- **1808: Portuguese court relocates.** The Portuguese royal family fled to
  Brazil following Napoleon's invasion of Portugal; Rio de Janeiro became the de
  facto capital of the Portuguese Empire.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1822-09-07: Brazilian independence.** Dom Pedro I proclaimed independence
  from Portugal (the "Cry of Ipiranga"); the Empire of Brazil was established.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1889-11-15: Republic proclaimed.** The Empire of Brazil (1822-1889) ended;
  the First Brazilian Republic followed.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1903-11-17: Treaty of Petrópolis.** Bolivia ceded the Acre territory to
  Brazil during the rubber boom — the largest single territorial acquisition of
  Brazil's independent history. CShapes records the resulting boundary change as
  a jump from 8,311,721 km² to 8,437,289 km² (**+125,568 km²**).
  [cshapes-2.0](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md)
- **COW state-system membership.** BRA, ccode 140
  [cow-state-system-v2024](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md).
