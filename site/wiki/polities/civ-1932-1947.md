# Côte d'Ivoire (1932-1947)

## Summary

The enlarged French colony of Côte d'Ivoire during the period 5 September 1932 to 3 January 1947, when the colony incorporated territory from the dissolved Upper Volta colony. "Cote d'Ivoire and Mali were both units within French West Africa, which was established in 1895" [biger-1995 §ivory-coast](../sources/biger-1995.md#ivory-coast). CShapes records the area as 38.45 sq deg for this period — substantially larger than the standard 26.34 sq deg of the earlier (1902–1932) and later (1947–1960) Ivory Coast — reflecting the absorption of Upper Volta territory by French decree on 5 September 1932. The territory reverted to approximately the standard Ivory Coast boundary on 4 January 1947 when Upper Volta was reconstituted. This is a WHEP-distinct row because the area change at both endpoints is genuine.
