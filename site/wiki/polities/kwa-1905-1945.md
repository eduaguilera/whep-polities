# Kwantung Leased Territory

## Summary

The Japanese leased territory at the tip of the Liaodong peninsula (Dairen and Port Arthur), held from the 1905 Treaty of Portsmouth to the Soviet occupation of August 1945. [wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01 §kwantung](../sources/wikipedia-harmonize-audit-2026-10-01.md#kwantung)

## Why this entry exists

The IIA yearbooks report Kwantung for 1909-1939 (`japanese kwantung`, 31 (year, label) pairs, 498 value rows in the harmonized dataset); no row was it. `us kwantung` (1931-1932) stays unrouted as a normalisation slip. Created in the harmonize audit (2026-10-01, issue #706); the labels are described in
[whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `proxy`.** GADM 4.1 adm3 Dalian (the urban districts), Lushunkou (Port Arthur), Jinzhou and the Changhai islands, plus the parts of Wafangdian and Xinjin (Pulandian) south of 39.45 N. The leased area reached the northern shore of Yadang (Adams) Bay on the west and Pikou on the east; no fetched source draws that line, so a straight parallel between the two bay heads stands in for it. **3,327 km2** (ESRI:54034) against the **3,500 km2** Wikipedia states (0.95); moving the parallel to 39.40 N or 39.50 N gives 2,900 or 3,854 km2, so 39.45 is the closest of the three. Measured: **3,327 km2** (ESRI:54034). Share of the polygon inside each container: CHN-1895-1913 90%, CHN-1913-1914 90%, CHN-1914-1921 90%, MAN-1921-1932 96%, MAN-1932-1945 96%.
