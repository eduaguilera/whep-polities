# Paraguay (post-Triple Alliance)

## Summary

This row tracks Paraguay from the end of the War of the Triple Alliance (1870) to the outbreak of the Chaco War (1932). Borders were stable throughout this period after the post-war treaties with Brazil and Argentina were concluded. CShapes 1886 polygon (cowcode=150, ~157,000 km²) is an exact fit. Predecessor: pry-1811-1870. Successor: pry-1932-1938.

## Territorial extent

- Polygon source: CShapes 2.0, cowcode=150, vintage 1886.
- Area: ~157,000 km² (post-war reduced territory).
- Borders stable 1870–1932; no significant annexations or cessions in this period.
- polygon_vintage_drift: 1886 polygon sits within the 1870–1932 span — low drift, no concern.

## Predecessors and successors

- Predecessor: pry-1811-1870. Split at 1870 = end of Triple Alliance War.
- Successor: pry-1932-1938. Split at 1932 = outbreak of Chaco War.

## Sourced claims

- 1870-06-20: Treaty of Loizaga-Cotegipe (Brazil); subsequent treaties with Argentina finalized 1872. [cow-state-system-v2024]
- Borders stable 1870–1932; Gran Chaco ownership disputed with Bolivia but no formal territorial change until the 1935 peace treaty.

## Polygon caveat (added 2026-07-24)

`polygon_status` downgraded from `assigned` to **`estimate`** by
`scripts/validate_polygons.py`: the attached geometry measures **293,549 km²**
against this page's stated **157,000 km²** (87% divergence).

The stated figure reflects the territory Paraguay *effectively held* between the
War of the Triple Alliance (1870) and the Chaco War (1932). The CShapes feature
includes much of the **Chaco Boreal**, disputed with Bolivia throughout this
period and only awarded to Paraguay by the 1938 arbitration. The polygon
**overstates effective territory by roughly 87%**, all of it in the sparsely
populated Chaco — which matters less for agricultural data (concentrated in the
eastern region) than the raw figure suggests, but should be stated rather than
implied.

**Re-measured 2026-08-14 (issue 22), figure confirmed and no split available.**
CShapes gwcode 150 stated area for the 1886-01-01 → 1938-07-20 step is
293,506.62 km²; against this page's 157,000 km² that is +86.9%, so the issue's
"~87%" is right. It is also the **only** CShapes step covering 1870-1932 — the
next begins 1938-07-21 at 398,805.62 km², after the arbitration — so no observed
polygon of eastern Paraguay alone exists to split to, and no estimated
Chaco-excluding polygon has been invented. This row is therefore case 2 of
[wiki/README.md §long-span rows](../README.md#long-span-rows-when-territory-changed-mid-span):
one row, `polygon_status: estimate`, direction and magnitude stated above.
