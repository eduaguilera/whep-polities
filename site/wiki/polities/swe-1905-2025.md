# Sweden

## Summary

Modern Sweden from the dissolution of the Sweden-Norway personal union
on 7 June 1905 to the present. Norway declared the union dissolved on
7 June 1905; Sweden accepted after a Norwegian referendum in August 1905,
with formal dissolution on 26 October 1905. CShapes records a real
territorial change at this date: area drops from ~138.5 to ~78.6
square degrees (~450,000 km2), reflecting the loss of Norway. Sweden's
metropolitan borders have been stable since 1905.
