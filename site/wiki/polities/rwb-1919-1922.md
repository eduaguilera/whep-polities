# Rwanda and Burundi (1919-1922)

## Summary

Rwanda and Burundi (1919-1922) tracks the brief interregnum between German East Africa's defeat in World War I and the formal League of Nations mandate assignment to Belgium. Biger notes that "Colonized by the Germans in the late nineteenth century, the region, along with neighboring Burundi, was conquered by the Belgians in World War I." CShapes records the entity as "Rwanda-Urundi" (cowcode 515) beginning 1920-06-28 — the date of the Treaty of Versailles, which formally ended German sovereignty — with a second time-step beginning 1922-07-20 when the League of Nations officially assigned the mandate to Belgium. The WHEP row begins in 1919, somewhat earlier than the CShapes start of 1920-06-28, likely reflecting the de facto Belgian military administration that began in 1916 or the 1919 Paris Peace Conference. The area is identical in both CShapes entries (~4.26 sq deg), confirming no territorial change at the 1922 mandate formalization.

## Contradictions

The WHEP row starts at 1919, but CShapes begins Rwanda-Urundi (cowcode 515) only at 1920-06-28 (Treaty of Versailles). The 1919 start likely reflects de facto Belgian military administration, which began when Belgian forces captured the territory in 1916, or it may represent the 1919 Paris Peace Conference decision. The CShapes predecessor for the 1919 period would be German East Africa (TAN-1891-1920), which ends 1920. This creates a one-year (1919-1920) period where the WHEP row exists but CShapes has no dedicated entry.

## Open questions

### oq-1919-start-date

The WHEP row begins in 1919, before the Treaty of Versailles (1920-06-28). CShapes does not record a Rwanda-Urundi entity before 1920-06-28. The 1919 start could correspond to: (a) the 1919 Paris Peace Conference decisions; (b) Belgian de facto administration beginning ~1916; or (c) a WHEP convention for post-WWI transitions. The exact justification for 1919 vs 1920 should be documented.

### oq-tan-predecessor-link

The CSV predecessor is listed as TAN-1891-1920, but the TAN chain splits at 1920 into TAN-1920-1922 and the RWB chain. The TAN-1920-1922 row covers Tanganyika proper (not Rwanda-Burundi) under British mandate. The territorial handover from German East Africa to Belgian administration for Rwanda-Burundi specifically needs to be traced through the TAN chain more carefully.
