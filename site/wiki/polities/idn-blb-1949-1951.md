## Summary

This WHEP polity covers the islands of **Bali** and **Lombok** as a combined reporting unit as used by the **FAO 1952 dataset** for the period 1949–1951. The FAO source disaggregated Indonesia into distinct island-group sub-units — "Indonesia Java and Madura", "Indonesia Bali and Lombok", and "Indonesia Other islands" — reflecting a sub-national agricultural census structure. Bali (~5,780 km²) and Lombok (~4,725 km²) together total approximately **10,505 km²**, compared to Indonesia's ~1,900,000 km² total — a ratio of approximately 1:180. Routing Bali+Lombok agricultural production data to IDN-1949-1969 would overstate the represented territory by a factor of ~180 and is explicitly prohibited by the WHEP sub-territory rule.

Bali and Lombok are geographically adjacent islands separated by the Lombok Strait. Their physical extents are fixed geographic features with no documented boundary changes. The two islands are in the Lesser Sunda Islands chain immediately east of Java. The modern GADM level-1 province boundaries for Bali Province and West Nusa Tenggara Province (Lombok is the western portion) serve as a period proxy for 1949–1951.

**Polygon provenance**: GADM 4.1 sub-national Indonesia level-1 features for Bali Province (Bali island) and the relevant portion of West Nusa Tenggara Province (Lombok island), composed union. The GADM modern boundary is used as a proxy because the island physical outlines are stable geographic features. This is recorded as `polygon_status: proxy` to flag the source as modern rather than a period-specific historical GIS.

**Note**: West Nusa Tenggara Province also includes Sumbawa island; the polygon for Lombok should be limited to the Lombok portion only. If sub-provincial GADM features are not available for Lombok separately, a constructed polygon from island outline data would be preferable. The area_km2 value of 10,505 is based on standard island area references.

**Polygon confidence: medium** — island boundaries are fixed physical geography, but the GADM province boundary for West Nusa Tenggara includes Sumbawa (not part of this reporting unit); sub-provincial extraction needed.

**Area cross-check**: Bali ~5,780 + Lombok ~4,725 = ~10,505 km². Ratio to Indonesia total (~1,900,000 km²) ≈ 0.55%, or about 1:181. This confirms that routing FAO Bali+Lombok data to IDN-1949-1969 would be a ~180× territorial overstatement.
