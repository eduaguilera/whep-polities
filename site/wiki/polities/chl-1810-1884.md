# Chile (to 1883)


> **`end_year` moved 1883 -> 1884 on 2026-08-06: the year 1883 belonged to no Chilean polity.**
>
> `end_year` is **exclusive**, so this row covered 1810-1882 while
> [chl-1884-1899](chl-1884-1899.md) began in 1884. **1883 fell between them** — an off-by-one in a
> pair of adjacent spans, not a historical question.
>
> **`validate_period_gaps` already knew.** The pair `("CHL-1810-1883", "CHL-1884-1899")` sat in its
> baseline as an accepted one-year gap, alongside genuine ones like `CZE-1804-1918 -> CZE-1993-2025`
> where the state really did not exist. So the gate was not wrong and this was not a discovery — the
> gap was recorded and nothing distinguished an arithmetic slip from a deliberate hole.
>
> What changed is that **whep_crops v1.0 puts 7 observed crop rows into 1883**, so an accepted hole
> became a routing failure with data falling through it. That is the whole value of the external
> panel here: not finding the gap, but making it matter.
>
> **1883 is assigned to the earlier row, not the later one, on the event date.** The War of the
> Pacific ended with the Treaty of Ancón on **20 October 1883**, so for roughly ten months of that
> year Chile did not yet hold Tarapacá. Extending this row is therefore the correct direction;
> starting `CHL-1884-1899` in 1883 would have credited Chile with the annexation for the whole of
> the year it was signed in. This is the convention question raised in issue 23 — do row boundaries
> follow the source's date or the historical event — answered here on the event.
>
> The row still has **no polygon**, deliberately: see [#158] and issue 155. Cliopatria's pre-1883
> Chile (~313,000 km²) would create a 2.4x step against this family's CShapes-sourced siblings
> (~745,000) that is mostly a difference between mapping conventions rather than territory. Both
> IIA and FAO independently state Chile at ~741,770 km², which is what the siblings carry.
## Summary

Pre-War-of-the-Pacific Chile. This polity covers Chile from independence
in **1810** to the end of the War of the Pacific in **1883** (Treaty of
Ancón, October 1883). During this period Chile's northern boundary ran
near 24°S (the Chilean–Bolivian border established in 1866), and the
Atacama Desert provinces of Antofagasta (Bolivia) and Tarapacá (Peru)
were not yet Chilean territory.

## Sourced claims

- **~1818: Chilean independence.** Battle of Maipú (5 April 1818)
  secured independence from Spain.
- **1866: Atacama boundary treaty.** Chile and Bolivia fixed their
  border at 24°S (Parallel Treaty), with shared exploitation rights
  between 23°S and 25°S.
- **1879–1883: War of the Pacific.** Chile declared war on Bolivia
  (February 1879) and Peru (April 1879). The war ended with the
  Treaty of Ancón (October 1883) ceding Tarapacá to Chile, and a
  separate agreement (Truce of Valparaíso, April 1884) ceding
  Antofagasta to Chile. Territorial transfer completed 1883–1884.
