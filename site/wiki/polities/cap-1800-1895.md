# Cape Colony (to 1895)

## Summary

The British Cape Colony as a separate statistical / territorial unit from the
permanent British takeover in 1806 (Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1814 confirming
occupation) to the annexation of British Bechuanaland on 1895-11-16, after
which the colony is held by [cap-1895-1910](cap-1895-1910.md) until the Union
of South Africa. Reported in pre-1961 agricultural data variously as "Cape
Province" or "Cape of Good Hope"; agricultural statistics were compiled
separately from the other British South African territories until union.

**This row was `CAP-1800-1910` until 2026-08-14** (issue 22), when the 1895
Bechuanaland annexation was split out because CShapes ships an observed
polygon for it. What remains here is the part of the same problem that **no
source can fix** — see the open questions.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `cshapes-2.0` gwcode 561, time-step 1886-01-01 → 1895-11-15, area
600,802 km². CShapes records two time-steps for the Cape Colony:

| Range | Area | Territory |
|---|---:|---|
| 1886-1895 | 600,802 km² | Core Cape Colony + Griqualand West (annexed 1880) |
| 1895-1910 | 716,102 km² | After annexation of British Bechuanaland (1895) |

The second step is no longer this row's problem: it is
[cap-1895-1910](cap-1895-1910.md).

**`polygon_status: polygon_vintage_drift`** (was `assigned`). One 1886 vintage
is held across 1800-1895 and it is *not* representative of the early decades,
which is exactly what this value marks per
[wiki/README.md](../README.md#polygon-workflow). Direction and magnitude of the
approximation, which is what the value obliges the page to state:

| Annexation | Year | Territory added | Approximate area |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Griqualand West | 1880 | Diamond-fields region around Kimberley | ~85,000 km² |
| Transkeian territories | 1885-1894 | Eastern frontier consolidation | included in the 1886 step |

Verified rather than assumed: the CShapes 1886 polygon **contains Kimberley**,
so Griqualand West is inside it. Data years before 1880 (roughly 1854-1879 in
the source data) are therefore matched to a territory larger than the colony
then held, by on the order of **85,000 km² — about 14%** of the 600,802 km²
figure. The direction is one-sided: the Cape only ever grew in this period, so
the single vintage never *understates* the pre-1886 colony.

**Why the row is not split further.** See
[oq-cape-expansion-splits](#oq-cape-expansion-splits): CShapes coverage begins
in 1886 and the one candidate alternative source measured — Cliopatria's
"British Cape Colony", which has 39 time-steps from 1796 — turns out to
measure something else entirely.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Cape Province" /
"Cape of Good Hope" for 205 rows spanning 1854-1904. Without this row the
matcher's `name_override` rules — which already map both strings to code
`CAP` — had nothing to resolve against.

### dec-south-africa-label-1852-1909

Data labelled **'south africa'** in Mitchell and IIA sources for years **1852–1909**
(218 rows) is routed to the Cape: 1852-1894 to this row, 1895-1909 to
[cap-1895-1910](cap-1895-1910.md) since the 2026-08-14 split.

**Basis:** Cape Colony was the dominant British territorial entity in southern Africa throughout the nineteenth century and was routinely described as "South Africa" in European statistical publications before the Union. The Orange Free State (OFS) and the South African Republic (Transvaal) do not have WHEP polities covering this period, so no finer routing is available.

**Approximation risk:** If any source aggregate included OFS or Transvaal figures alongside Cape Colony figures under the label 'south africa', routing to the Cape would understate the true territorial scope. This risk is accepted given the absence of the relevant granular polities.

**Approximation risk resolved in favour of this routing (2026-07-24, assertion
verification).** The magnitudes rule out an OFS/Transvaal aggregate: this
source's median flock is 13,432,000 sheep and 4,939,000 goats, while the 1904
Cape Colony census independently recorded ~11.8M sheep (8,465,000 woolled +
3,353,000 other) — Cape-alone scale. A combined-provinces total would be far
larger; the Union's four provinces together held on the order of 25-30M sheep by
1911. So the label reports Cape Colony's own agricultural returns, not an
aggregate.

**The assertion remains banked `best_available`, not `verified_equal`,** and the
1895 split narrows but does not close the reason: the Cape's territory did not
stay constant across 1852-1894 either — it annexed British Kaffraria,
Griqualand West (1880) and the Transkeian territories (1885-1894) inside this
row's span. No single polygon vintage can equal the reporting territory across
the whole of it. What changed on 2026-08-14 is that the *last* of the four
annexations, British Bechuanaland, is no longer approximated at all.

**Aliases:** `south africa` 1852–1894 → CAP-1800-1895 and 1895–1909 →
CAP-1895-1910 in `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/applied_aliases.csv`
(2026-07-02, split 2026-08-14).

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1886-territory

Cape Colony was smaller pre-1880 (before Griqualand West). Using the 1886
CShapes polygon as a proxy over-represents territory for 1854-1879 data by
roughly 85,000 km² (~14%). This is recorded, quantified and one-directional;
no source in the repository can remove it (see below).

### oq-cape-expansion-splits

**The Cape Colony's territory grew substantially within this row's span.**
Raised 2026-07-24 by assertion verification of `south africa|mitchell|1852-1909`;
narrowed 2026-08-14 by the 1895 split.

The old page proposed splitting at **1865, 1880 and 1895**. Only 1895 was
sourceable, and it has been done. The other two are **withdrawn as
unsourceable, on measurement**, not left as aspirations:

- **CShapes 2.0** has no Cape feature before 1886-01-01. There is nothing to
  bind a pre-1880 row to.
- **Cliopatria v0.1.3** does carry "British Cape Colony" with 39 time-steps
  from 1796 to 1884, and was the obvious candidate. Measured against the
  colony's actual history it is **not the same object**:
  - its 1877-1879 and 1880-1884 steps are **990,436 and 989,965 km²** — 38%
    *larger* than the Cape's maximum pre-Union extent (716,102 km², reached only
    in 1895) — and both polygons **contain Pretoria**, i.e. the Transvaal under
    the 1877-1881 British annexation, which was never part of the Cape Colony;
  - its 1848-1852 step (762,535 km²) **contains Bloemfontein**, i.e. the Orange
    River Sovereignty, which Britain abandoned in 1854;
  - and the 1880-1884 steps **do not contain Kimberley**, so they miss
    Griqualand West in the very years after its 1880 annexation, while the
    CShapes 1886 polygon does contain it.

  A series that includes an annexed Transvaal but excludes the annexed
  diamond fields is measuring a British sphere of control, not the colony whose
  agricultural returns this data reports. Adopting it would also **invert the
  documented error**: the early decades would be overstated by ~65% rather than
  ~14%.

So the honest state is the one recorded above: one vintage, a known direction,
a quantified magnitude, and no source that improves on it. Per the convention
written down at [wiki/README.md §long-span rows](../README.md#long-span-rows-when-territory-changed-mid-span),
this row stays whole and states its error.
