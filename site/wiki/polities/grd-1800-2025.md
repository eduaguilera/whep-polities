# Grenada

## Summary

Single WHEP row for Grenada spanning the whole 1800-2025 window, added to cover FAOSTAT/FABIO reporting code GRD. **167 layer-B rows** route here (`pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`), mostly colonial-era cocoa, nutmeg and mace. The row is undivided because Grenada's territory did not change across the span: at 359 km² it is one of the smallest polities in the database, an island group with no land boundary, and every sovereignty event in the period transferred the whole of it.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 adm0 feature `GID_0 = GRD`, carried as a **proxy** (`polygon_status: proxy`). Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **359 km²** — Grenada proper plus Carriacou and Petite Martinique. The conventional figure is 344-348 km²; the 3-4% excess is coastline generalisation on a small, indented island, and at this size a few square kilometres of resolution difference is proportionally large without being substantively meaningful.

**Why a modern polygon is not a temporal approximation here.** Biger's Grenada entry is two sentences: "Grenada is a Caribbean island state. It has no land boundaries" ([biger-1995 §grenada](../sources/biger-1995.md#grenada)). For a boundary encyclopaedia the *brevity is the finding*: there is no boundary history to record because there is no land frontier and no boundary event. An island group's extent can change only by islands being added or removed wholesale, and none was in this span.

**Territorial changes inside 1800-2025: none. The row should not be split.** For completeness, the events that a reader might expect to be row boundaries and why each is not:

- **1877** — Grenada becomes a separate British Crown Colony (having been administered within the Windward Islands). *Administrative*: the islands governed were the same before and after. Uncited general history; no source on disk gives this date.
- **1958-1962** — membership of the West Indies Federation. This is the one event with a plausible claim on the row, because it changes the *reporting* unit rather than the territory: statistics for 1958-1962 may be compiled Federation-wide. It did not change Grenada's extent. Uncited; flagged as [oq-grd-west-indies-federation](#oq-grd-west-indies-federation) because it is a data-scope risk this page cannot settle.
- **1967-1974** — Associated State of Grenada, self-governing in internal affairs with Britain retaining defence and external relations. Constitutional, not territorial.
- **1974-02-07** — independence. A sovereignty transfer over an unchanged territory, which under the WHEP polity definition does not open a new row ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)). Dated to the day by two independent files: COW's `statelist2024.csv` and `decolonization_events.csv`.
- **1983-10-25** — US-led invasion. A six-week occupation with no change of borders, and precisely the kind of transient event CShapes excludes: "we also excluded wartime territorial changes, unless they were made permanent in treaties signed after the war" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)).

**Why CShapes is not used, and why it could not be.** Two of CShapes' documented thresholds each exclude Grenada on their own. Dependencies are limited "to those units with a population greater than 250,000 during the sample period" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)) — Grenada's population has never reached 250,000 — so colonial Grenada is outside CShapes' dependency list by construction, and CShapes' own note that "smaller dependencies are silently excluded" applies. And border adjustments are coded only above 100 × 100 km ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)), which is 28 times Grenada's entire area. So **no CShapes evidence, positive or negative, can ever bear on this row** — a useful thing for the verification pipeline to know, because "CShapes shows no change" would be vacuous here rather than confirmatory.

## Sourced claims

- "Grenada is a Caribbean island state. It has no land boundaries" ([biger-1995 §grenada](../sources/biger-1995.md#grenada)). Biger's entry is a brief one (p.250) and records no boundary event whatsoever.
- **COW `ccode=55` has a single tenure, `1974-02-07 → 2024-12-31`** — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). COW therefore covers only 51 of this row's 226 years; the colonial period is absent by design ([cow-state-system-v2024 §inclusion](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#inclusion), and limitation 1 of [cow-state-system-v2024 §known-limitations](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#known-limitations)). The frontmatter `cow: 55` is a code assignment for the modern state, not a membership claim across the row.
- `decolonization_events.csv` records Grenada independence from Britain on 1974-02-07 — the same day COW gives. The two files were compiled independently, so this is a real two-source confirmation.
- CShapes excludes dependencies below 250,000 population ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-dependencies](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-dependencies)) and territorial changes below 100 × 100 km ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)); Grenada fails both tests, so CShapes is structurally silent about this polity and its silence carries no information.
- CShapes excludes wartime territorial changes "unless they were made permanent in treaties signed after the war" ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)) — the rule under which the 1983 invasion is correctly a non-event for boundary purposes.

## Open questions

### oq-grd-west-indies-federation

Were Grenada's 1958-1962 agricultural returns reported separately, or folded into a West Indies Federation aggregate? This is a *label* question, not a territorial one, and it is the only real risk to this row's 167 data rows: if a source reported the Federation as one line, then either those years are missing for Grenada or a Federation figure has been misrouted to it. Checkable by looking for a Federation-scoped label in the layer-B label lexicon (`data/final/source_label_lexicon.csv`) and for a discontinuity in Grenada's series across 1958 and 1962.
