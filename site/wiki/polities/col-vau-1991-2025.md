# Vaupés (department of Colombia)

## Summary

Vaupés is a department of Colombia in the Amazonian southeast of the country, bordering Brazil, with its capital at Mitú on the Vaupés River. This entry captures the department-level administrative unit from 1991, when the 1991 Constitution converted the former national territories (intendencias and comisarías) into full departments, through 2025 (open-ended, as the unit is still current). `type: subnational` reflects that Colombia holds sovereignty throughout; this row exists to carry the reporting territory for statistics tabulated at the department level, distinct from the national COL total and from whatever lower-tier unit governed the same area before 1991. No predecessor or successor polity exists in the current vocabulary: the pre-1991 comisaría/intendencia tier is not represented, and the department is still the live administrative status.

**Why this entry exists.** No existing polity in the database represents Vaupés — no name match and no iso3 candidate is subnational for Colombia (COL previously had zero subnational rows). The routing decision found 4,794 valued rows for the unit `COL-VAUPES` across 1915-2025; only the 1991-2025 portion is captured by this entry, per the country convention's explicit departure rule placing Vaupés's department-status start in 1991 rather than the 1886-system default. Before this fix, any data tagged to this unit that reached national scope would have been folded into `COL-1922-2025`, a national total roughly 24x the department's area — clearly the wrong container for department-level statistics. Federico-Tena and comparable historical trade-polity vocabularies do not tabulate Colombian departments separately, but the routing decision's own source evidence (mitchell/mixed department-level statistical series feeding this unit_id) is what confirms the reporting unit is distinct from the national total: the data itself is collected and published at department scale, which is the standing policy (per the Manchuria exemplar's issue 400 precedent) for creating a row regardless of sovereign status.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is currently available in the local GeoPackage subset for Colombia at all — the registered source `gadm-4.1-adm1` covers first-level administrative divisions worldwide including Colombian departments, but the locally fetched copy is a subset of 81 countries that does not include Colombia (0 features). This is a fetch gap, not a source gap: `polygon_source` is set to the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` per the `registered_source_unfetched` route, and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` until COL adm1 features are added to the local data.

**Territory description:** Vaupés department occupies Colombia's southeastern Amazon region, bordering Brazil along its eastern and southern edge, roughly between the Vaupés and Apaporis rivers. Its capital, Mitú, lies on the Vaupés River near the Brazilian border. It is one of Colombia's least populated departments, covered mostly by dense rainforest. Its officially stated area (DANE) is approximately 54,135 km2 — this figure comes from the Colombian national statistics agency's own published department area, not from any geometry attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned here.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor polity exists in this vocabulary for Vaupés before 1991: it was a comisaría (national territory) and then an intendencia under direct national administration, tiers not currently represented as distinct polities, so this entry has no predecessor edge despite 4,794 valued rows existing for 1915-1990 (see open question). No successor exists either, since the department is still the current administrative status as of end_year 2025 (exclusive), matching the country convention's treatment of still-current units with an open end year.

## Decisions

### d-code-uses-iso-code-not-full-name

**Subunit token uses the ISO 3166-2 code (VAU), not a truncated full name**

57 of 66 subnational rows follow <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, but the table shows both abbreviation styles (e.g. ESP-AS, ESP-CA use ISO-2-letter codes) and full-name styles among the untracked COL pages already staged this run (col-cundinamarca, col-narino). Since COL has zero existing subnational codes and sets its own precedent, I chose the ISO 3166-2:CO-VAU code (VAU) over a full-name token to keep the code short and match the ESP precedent of using the official subdivision code rather than a truncated place name. This departs from some sibling COL pages staged in this same batch (e.g. col-antioquia, col-cundinamarca use full names) - if the harness expects internal consistency across the COL batch, a rename to col-vaupes-1991-2025 would align it with those, but VAU is the more defensible long-term key since it is the actual DANE/ISO subdivision code and will not collide with abbreviation drift.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1991-comisaria-unrouted

**1915-1990 comisaría/intendencia data (4,794 rows) has no polity to route to**

The routing decision explicitly left 1915-1990 unrouted: Vaupés existed as a comisaría (to 1974) and then intendencia (1974-1991) before becoming a department in 1991, and no polity in the current vocabulary represents that lower administrative tier. This is the bulk of the valued rows for this unit. Open question for the project: should a separate lower-tier predecessor polity (e.g. COL-VAU-1915-1991, type subnational, admin tier 'comisaría/intendencia') be created to capture this data, or is it intentionally left as unrouted/discarded under the honest-unassigned-territory policy? If a predecessor is created later, this page's predecessor field should be updated and the container chain re-checked for the earlier span.

### oq-polygon-fetch-and-vintage

**GADM COL adm1 features are not yet in the local subset, and vintage risk for the department boundary is unverified**

polygon_source is set to gadm-4.1-adm1 with polygon_status unassigned because the routing decision's own polygon_detail states COL is entirely absent from the locally fetched 81-country GADM subset (0 features), not merely missing the Vaupés unit. Two things remain open: (1) whether COL will be added to the local fetch or whether a different registered source should be preferred instead, and (2) once fetched, whether the GADM adm1 boundary for Vaupés matches the 1991 department boundary or reflects a later administrative adjustment - Colombian department boundaries have seen minor municipio-level reassignments since 1991 that could shift the department polygon at the margins.

### oq-start-year-source-confirmation

**1991 start year relies on the country convention's stated departure list, not a directly cited constitutional source in this page**

The 1991 start year comes from the routing decision citing the country convention's list of intendencias/comisarías converted to departments by the 1991 Constitution. This page has not independently verified against a Colombian administrative-history source that Vaupés's conversion date was exactly 1991 (Article 309 of the 1991 Constitution names Vaupés among the former intendencias/comisarías elevated to departments), rather than a later implementing law taking effect in a subsequent year, which is a common pattern in comparable constitutional conversions.
