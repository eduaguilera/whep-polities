# Montenegro (1913-1915)

## Summary

This row tracks Montenegro for **3 years**, from the Balkan Wars to
Austrian occupation
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Post-Balkan Wars Montenegro. Gained territory in the First Balkan War.
Entered WWI in August 1914. Overrun by Austria-Hungary in January
1916. The CSV successor=NA reflects Montenegro's absorption into
Yugoslavia -- the territory went to F248 (Yugoslavia) but the CSV
chain is broken.

Predecessor: [mne-1878-1913](mne-1878-1913.md) -- Balkan Wars
territorial gains.
Successor: NA per CSV (Montenegro was occupied by Austria-Hungary
1916, then absorbed into Yugoslavia 1918).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [mne-1878-1913](mne-1878-1913.md) -- the Balkan
  Wars territorial gains created the split.
- **Successor:** NA per CSV. Montenegro was occupied by Austria-Hungary
  (January 1916), then absorbed into the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and
  Slovenes (Yugoslavia) in 1918. The territory eventually maps to
  [f248-1918-1919](f248-1918-1919.md) (Yugoslavia) but the CSV chain
  is broken. See [oq-successor-yugoslavia](#oq-successor-yugoslavia).

## Sourced claims

- **1913: Balkan Wars conclusion.** Treaty of Bucharest (10 August
  1913). Montenegro gained territory in the First Balkan War,
  including parts of the Sandjak of Novi Pazar. This is the
  start-of-row event.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1914-08: WWI entry.** Montenegro entered WWI alongside Serbia
  against Austria-Hungary.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **1916-01: Austrian occupation.** Austria-Hungary overran Montenegro
  in January 1916. King Nicholas I fled into exile. This effectively
  ended Montenegrin sovereignty.
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv)
- **COW state-system membership.** COW code 341
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates).
