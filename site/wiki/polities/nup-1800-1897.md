# Nupe Kingdom (Bida Emirate)

## Summary

The Nupe Kingdom (from the early 19th century, the Bida Emirate under Sokoto/Gwandu suzerainty) from the 1800 database floor to the British Niger Company capture of Bida in January 1897. Nupe was a middle-Niger trading polity famous for brass and glass-bead manufacture and cloth production; nominally subordinate to Sokoto Caliphate's Gwandu wing, it remained operationally autonomous under the Etsu Nupe.

## Territorial extent

The middle Niger basin between the Niger and Kaduna rivers in modern central Nigeria, with Bida as capital (6.01°E, 9.08°N per CSV notes). No polygon source assigned in the CSV; the `polygon_source` field is `NA`. Paine et al. (2024) covers this region for the precolonial period but Nupe is not explicitly in the CSV polygon source field.
