# Hong Kong

## Summary

Hong Kong as a distinct statistical and territorial unit from the 1842 Treaty
of Nanking (cession of Hong Kong Island to Britain) through the 1860 Treaty
of Beijing (Kowloon), the 1898 Second Convention of Peking (99-year New
Territories lease), British Crown Colony administration (1842-1941,
1945-1997), Japanese occupation (1941-1945), and the 1997 transfer of
sovereignty to the People's Republic of China as a Special Administrative
Region. Agricultural / economic data for Hong Kong has been compiled
separately from China throughout.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** `gadm-4.1-adm1` feature `CHN.HKG` (modern Hong Kong SAR — Hong
Kong Island + Kowloon + New Territories, ~1,104 km²). Used as a stable proxy
for the full 1842-2025 period: the territory grew incrementally to its
current extent by 1898 and has been stable since. For pre-1898 data the
polygon over-represents by including the New Territories area (which wasn't
added until the 1898 lease), but the relevant agricultural datasets in the
WHEP dataset are from 1948-1960 — well after territorial consolidation.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data has 12 rows for Hong Kong
(iso3c=HKG, country="China, Hong Kong SAR") for 1948-1960. Previously these
came back `match_status: none` with no WHEP polity covering HKG. A single
1842-2025 row matches the WHEP rule that governance changes (1941 Japanese
occupation, 1997 handover) that don't move territory don't split a polity.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** NA — pre-1842 Hong Kong was part of Qing China ([chn-1800-1895](chn-1800-1895.md))
  with no separate statistical identity.
- **Successor:** NA — Hong Kong remains a distinct statistical unit post-1997
  as a PRC Special Administrative Region.
