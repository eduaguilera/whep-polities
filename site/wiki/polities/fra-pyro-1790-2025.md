# Pyrénées-Orientales

## Summary

This entry represents the French département of Pyrénées-Orientales, one of the original ~83 administrative units created by the Revolutionary reform of 4 March 1790, occupying France's Mediterranean-facing Pyrenean border with Spain and Andorra. Its territory descends from the historic County of Roussillon plus part of Cerdagne, annexed from Spain under the 1659 Treaty of the Pyrenees -- a boundary that has remained essentially unchanged for over two centuries, unlike neighbouring départements affected by the 1871 Alsace-Lorraine cession, the 1871-1922 creation of Territoire de Belfort, or the 1975 split of Corse. `type: subnational` is deliberate: France held continuous sovereignty over this territory throughout, and the row exists only to carry a sub-national reporting/administrative unit, not to compete with the national FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025 chain in any sovereignty ranking. The polity's own start_year is kept at 1790, the département's true creation date, even though the earliest available FRA container begins in 1800 -- see the open question below for the resulting 1790-1799 container gap, which mirrors the identical gap on the sibling FRA-AVE-1790-2025 (Aveyron) entry.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: 20 valued rows for `France, Pyrénées-Orientales` spanning 1862-2023 (per the routing decision, 97.6% blank/observed rather than back-cast), drawn from department-level agricultural/administrative series keyed by admin_name FRJ15. Previously, no polity row represented the département at all -- data for this unit had nowhere to go except the national FRA-* chain (FRA-1800-1871 / FRA-1871-1919 / FRA-1919-2025), which is roughly the entire country and vastly overstates the territory the figures actually describe (France is about 550,000 km² versus this département's ~4,116 km²). What confirms the entity is distinct: Pyrénées-Orientales is a standing first-order French administrative division (INSEE code 66, GADM GID_1 FRA.14_1) with continuous existence since 1790, and it is the entity the source data itself names (FRJ15/Pyrénées-Orientales), not an inference -- the same status as any of the other 56 ISO3-SUBUNIT rows already in this table (e.g. ESP-CA, DZA-CVD) that exist because the source data is reported at that administrative level, not the national one. The sibling FRA-AVE-1790-2025 (Aveyron) row explicitly cross-references FRA-PYRO by name as a parallel case, confirming both the code and the container-gap handling are an established pattern, not a one-off.

**Territory description:** Pyrénées-Orientales is the southernmost département of mainland France, in the Occitanie region, bordering Spain (Catalonia) to the south and Andorra to the southwest, with a Mediterranean coastline to the east. Its prefecture is Perpignan. On a modern map it corresponds to the historic Roussillon and part of Cerdagne (Cerdanya), running from the Mediterranean coast up into the eastern Pyrenees. Its present-day area is approximately **4,116 km²** (INSEE figure for the current département), among the smaller French départements. No area is measured from this repository's own geometry yet, since no polygon has been attached (`polygon_area_km2` is not populated by this entry).

## Predecessors and successors

Pyrénées-Orientales has no polity predecessor or successor row in this table: it was created in 1790 as one of the original ~83 Revolutionary départements out of the annexed County of Roussillon and the Cerdagne territory ceded by Spain in 1659, and it has never been merged, split, or renamed since -- unlike Territoire de Belfort (carved from Haut-Rhin in 1871-1922), the Alsace-Lorraine trio (annexed 1871-1919), Corse (split into Corse-du-Sud/Haute-Corse in 1975), or the overseas départements (created 1946), none of which apply here. Its container chain runs entirely through the national FRA rows: FRA-1800-1871 (leaving 1790-1799 uncontainered), FRA-1871-1919, then FRA-1919-2025, with no change of container logic reflecting any change to the département's own boundary, only to which national row currently spans that year.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1790-with-container-gap

**start_year kept at 1790, the département's actual creation date, despite a 1790-1799 container gap**

Pyrénées-Orientales was created on 4 March 1790 by the Revolutionary reform that established the original ~83 départements. The earliest available FRA container row is FRA-1800-1871, starting 1800 -- ten years after the département's own creation -- and no FRA container exists for 1790-1799, since the pre-1800 Revolutionary/Directory-era French state is not separately chained in this table. Rather than pull the polity's own start_year forward to match the earliest container (which would misstate when the entity itself began), start_year is kept at the département's true 1790 date, matching the convention already used by the sibling FRA-AVE-1790-2025 (Aveyron) row, which cites FRA-PYRO explicitly as a parallel case: both entities' 1790-1799 span is left uncontainered rather than backdating their own existence. See the open question below for the resulting gap.

### d-code-pattern-pyro

**Followed the established FRA-PYRO subunit code, matching the sibling Aveyron row's own reference to it**

Pyrénées-Orientales is an ordinary French département, not a historical territory with a bespoke name comparable to Alaska Territory or Hyderabad -- so the bespoke-code minority pattern does not apply. The sibling entry FRA-AVE-1790-2025 (Aveyron) already documents its own container-gap handling by name-checking 'the sibling Morbihan (FRA-56) and Pyrénées-Orientales (FRA-PYRO) rows,' establishing FRA-PYRO as this département's canonical subunit token in this table (from its own postal/INSEE-adjacent shorthand), rather than GADM's source-internal GID_1 (FRA.14_1). The polity_code is therefore FRA-PYRO-1790-2025, consistent with that existing cross-reference rather than the FRA-PO variant used in an earlier draft of this entry.

**No FRA container exists for 1790-1799, so the first decade of this entry's span cannot be containered**

Pyrénées-Orientales was created on 4 March 1790 along with the rest of the original Revolutionary départements, but the earliest national FRA row in the table is FRA-1800-1871. This entry's start_year is kept at 1790 to reflect the département's true creation date rather than backdating its existence to match container availability, but that means 1790-1799 has no container edge at all -- any data keyed to Pyrénées-Orientales in that decade has nowhere correct to route. If a pre-1800 FRA national row is ever added (e.g. FRA-1790-1800 for the Revolutionary/Directory-era state), a container edge should be added to close this gap. The sibling FRA-AVE-1790-2025 (Aveyron) entry has the identical gap and references this row as a parallel case.

### oq-gadm-boundary-vintage

**Polygon route is registered-but-unfetched; the eventual GADM feature will be a present-day boundary standing in for 235 years of span**

polygon_source is gadm-4.1-adm1 with polygon_status unassigned because sources.yaml's local GADM extract covers 81 countries and France is not among them -- the feature (expected under a GID_1 like FRA.14_1) has not actually been fetched. Département boundaries are unusually stable compared to most subnational units, but not perfectly so: the most relevant risk is the 1968 Île-de-France reorganisation and various commune-level canton/arrondissement transfers elsewhere in France during the 19th and 20th centuries. None of these are known to have touched Pyrénées-Orientales's own outer boundary, which has been comparatively fixed since 1790 (it approximates the historic County of Roussillon plus part of Cerdagne, ceded by Spain under the 1659 Treaty of the Pyrenees). That claim has not been verified against a historical atlas for this entry -- once the GADM feature is fetched, someone should confirm whether the modern adm1 boundary needs any adjustment for the earliest (1790-1900) portion of this span.
