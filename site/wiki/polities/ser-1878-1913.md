# Kingdom of Serbia (1878-1913)

Independent kingdom recognised at the Congress of Berlin (1878), ~48,000 km². CShapes 2.0 feature 340 (cowcode=345, vintage 1886) accurately represents the post-Berlin-Congress borders; borders were stable until the First Balkan War (October 1912). Elevated from Principality to Kingdom in 1882. The 1886 polygon vintage is a good fit for the full 1878-1913 span.
