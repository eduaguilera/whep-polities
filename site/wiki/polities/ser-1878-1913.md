# Kingdom of Serbia (1878-1913)

## Summary

Independent Serbia between the Congress of Berlin (1878) and the Balkan Wars settlement (1913). This is the row for Serbia at its smallest post-independence extent: sovereign, but before the 1912-13 conquests that nearly doubled it. Elevated from principality to kingdom in 1882 — a title change with no territorial content, which is why it does not split the row. Both row boundaries are territorial events, and both are dated by sources on disk.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** CShapes 2.0 feature `340` (cowcode 345), time-step **1886**, `polygon_status: assigned`. Measured in an equal-area CRS (ESRI:54034) from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`: **52,539 km²**.

**The 1886 vintage covers this row well, and the successor comparison proves it.** [SER-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md) binds the same feature number at time-step **1915** and measures **90,538 km²** — **72% larger**, a jump of 37,999 km². That is the Balkan Wars: Serbia's gains in Kosovo, the Sanjak and Vardar Macedonia under the 1913 Treaties of London and Bucharest. The size of the jump matters methodologically, because CShapes only codes border adjustments larger than 100 × 100 km ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)); at ~38,000 km² this change is comfortably above the threshold and so is genuinely observed by CShapes rather than inferred. Conversely, the *absence* of any CShapes time-step between 1886 and 1915 for this feature means no above-threshold Serbian border change occurred in between — CShapes' own backdating rule states that where a unit has no coded change its borders are simply carried back ([cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding)).

**Territorial changes inside 1878-1913: none above the CShapes threshold, and none named by Biger.** Biger's Serbia entry moves directly from "Following the Balkan crisis of 1875-1878 Serbia gained independence and enlarged its territory" to "In 1882 Serbia was proclaimed a kingdom" and then to the twentieth century, naming no boundary event between 1878 and the Balkan Wars ([biger-1995 §serbia-and-montenegro](../sources/biger-1995.md#serbia-and-montenegro)). COW likewise records no discontinuity: `ccode=345` runs `1878-07-13 → 1941-04-20` as one unbroken tenure. **The row should not be split.** The 1882 elevation to kingdom is a change of regime style, which the WHEP polity definition explicitly does not treat as a territorial event ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)).

**On the 1878 start date, WHEP and COW agree exactly.** COW's tenure for `ccode=345` begins **1878-07-13** — the date the Treaty of Berlin was signed. The WHEP row starts 1878, so the row boundary is not a convenience rounding: it is the treaty. Biger corroborates the substance ("Serbia gained independence and enlarged its territory"), and Serbia's independence in 1878 is the same Berlin settlement that also enlarged it, so 1878 is simultaneously a sovereignty event and a territorial one.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [SER-1816-1878](ser-1816-1878.md) — the autonomous Principality of Serbia under Ottoman suzerainty. Its polygon binding is `cliopatria` feature `Serbia` (vintage 1830) and its status is `unassigned`, so no measured area is available from the GeoPackage; its frontmatter declares 38,000 km². If that declared figure is right, the Berlin settlement added roughly 14,500 km² to reach this row's measured 52,539 km² — broadly the four *nahiyah* districts (Niš, Pirot, Vranje, Toplica) Serbia gained in 1878. That arithmetic is offered as a sanity check on the row boundary, not as a sourced claim; the predecessor's declared area is not independently corroborated here.
- **Successor:** [SER-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md) — Serbia after the Balkan Wars, measured **90,538 km²**. Note this successor binds the `cshapes-europe` extension rather than CShapes 2.0 proper.

## Key dates

- 1878-07-13: Treaty of Berlin. Serbia recognised as independent and enlarged; COW's `ccode=345` tenure begins this day. Start-of-row event.
- 1882-03-06: Serbia proclaimed a kingdom ([biger-1995 §serbia-and-montenegro](../sources/biger-1995.md#serbia-and-montenegro) gives the year 1882; the day is uncited general history). No territorial content.
- 1912-10-08 to 1913-05-30: First Balkan War, ending in the Treaty of London.
- 1913-08-10: Treaty of Bucharest ends the Second Balkan War, fixing Serbia's enlarged borders. End-of-row event.

## Sourced claims

- "Following the Balkan crisis of 1875-1878 Serbia gained independence and enlarged its territory" ([biger-1995 §serbia-and-montenegro](../sources/biger-1995.md#serbia-and-montenegro)) — the start-of-row event, and note that Biger couples independence *and* enlargement in the same clause, which is why 1878 is a territorial boundary and not merely a diplomatic one.
- "In 1882 Serbia was proclaimed a kingdom" ([biger-1995 §serbia-and-montenegro](../sources/biger-1995.md#serbia-and-montenegro)) — an in-span event with no territorial content, recorded here so that a future pass does not mistake it for a missing row boundary.
- Biger's Serbia entry names no boundary change between 1878 and the twentieth century ([biger-1995 §serbia-and-montenegro](../sources/biger-1995.md#serbia-and-montenegro)). In a boundary encyclopaedia, silence across a 35-year window is weak but real evidence of stability.
- **COW records `ccode=345` as `1878-07-13 → 1941-04-20`, then `1944-10-20 → 2006-06-03`, then as `SRB`/Serbia from `2006-06-03`** — direct inspection of `wiki/sources/data/cow-v2024/statelist2024.csv`, columns per [cow-state-system-v2024 §schema](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#schema). Two things follow. First, the 1878-07-13 start corroborates this row's start year to the day. Second, **COW names `ccode=345` "Yugoslavia" for the whole 1878-2006 stretch**, i.e. it threads Serbian and Yugoslav statehood through a single code. WHEP does not: it keeps [SER-1878-1913](ser-1878-1913.md), [SER-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md) and the Yugoslav rows separate, because their *territories* differ even where COW's state-system continuity does not. This is the polity-definition rule working as intended ([log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition)), and it is a trap for any matcher that keys on COW code alone.
- CShapes codes border adjustments only above a 100 × 100 km threshold and dates wartime changes to "the date of postwar agreements" rather than the fighting ([cshapes-2.0 §coding-changes](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#coding-changes)) — which is why the Balkan Wars appear in CShapes as a single 1913-dated step rather than as separate 1912 and 1913 changes, and why this row's end year is 1913.
- Where a unit has no coded change, CShapes backdates its borders rather than observing them ([cshapes-2.0 §geocoding](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#geocoding)) — so this row's 1886 polygon is best read as "Serbia's borders as they stood when the next change was coded, carried back", which for 1878-1886 is an inference from stability rather than a measurement.
- Geometric measurement (this ingest, not an external source): this row **52,539 km²**; [SER-1913-1918](ser-1913-1918.md) **90,538 km²**. Both ESRI:54034 from `data/final/polities_database.gpkg`.
- Data footprint (this ingest): **355 layer-B rows** route to this code per `pipelines/polity-autoimprove/state/territory_basis.csv`.

## Contradictions

- **Declared area 48,000 km² vs measured 52,539 km².** This row's frontmatter `polygon_area_km2` says 48,000 and the page previously said "~48,000 km²", but the polygon actually attached in `data/final/polities_database.gpkg` measures **52,539 km²** in ESRI:54034 — 9.5% larger. The 48,000 figure is close to the ~48,300 km² usually given for the Kingdom of Serbia between 1878 and 1912, so the likely explanation is that a historian's figure was written into a field that is supposed to describe the *polygon*. The declared value is left unchanged here because `polygon_area_km2` is consumed by other gates whose baselines would move, but the discrepancy is recorded rather than papered over, and the measured figure is the one this page's reasoning uses. See [oq-ser-declared-area](#oq-ser-declared-area).
