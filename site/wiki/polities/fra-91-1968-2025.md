# Essonne

## Summary

Essonne is a French département in the southern Paris region (Île-de-France), created in 1968 when the former Seine-et-Oise département was split into six new units (Essonne, Yvelines, Val-d'Oise, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis, Val-de-Marne) as part of a reorganisation of the greater Paris area. This entry is coded `type: subnational` because a département is an administrative subdivision of France, not a sovereign state; France's national sovereignty over this territory is continuous and already carried by the FRA national rows in this table (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025). This row exists solely to carry the sub-national reporting territory: statistics keyed to "Essonne" or its INSEE code 91, as distinct from all-France totals or from the pre-1968 Seine-et-Oise totals that included it. The span starts at 1968, the département's own creation date, rather than at any country-convention default, because Essonne's administrative existence genuinely begins there -- unlike the ~83 original 1790 Revolutionary départements (e.g. Seine-et-Marne, see fra-77-1800-2025), Essonne is a 20th-century administrative creation and applying an earlier default would assert it existed before it did. It runs to the open end_year 2025 since Essonne remains an active département of France today with no further split or merger.

**Why this entry exists.** This page implements the routing decision for unit FRA-FR104 (admin_name "FR104", country France), which resolved via create_new because no existing FRA polity IS Essonne's territory -- the national FRA rows (FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1800-1919, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025) describe the whole country, and a département cannot be represented by its country's row. The unit's data spans 1862-2023, predating Essonne's 1968 creation, so the pre-1968 portion is a back-cast reconstruction problem onto a boundary that did not yet exist and is deliberately left unresolved here (see open question oq-pre-1968-data-routing) rather than folded into this entry's span, which covers only Essonne's own 1968-2025 existence as a département. Before this decision, the data for FR104 was unmatched by construction: match_existing was excluded because no existing row's territory equals Essonne's, and no back_cast candidate (e.g. a Seine-et-Oise row) currently exists to absorb the pre-1968 portion either. What confirms Essonne as a distinct, statistically-reported entity rather than an arbitrary subdivision is that it has been one of France's ~96 metropolitan départements, tabulated under INSEE code 91 in French statistical yearbooks, continuously since its 1968 creation -- the same institutional status the exemplar and sibling pages (e.g. fra-77-1800-2025) rely on for Seine-et-Marne.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The routing decision already establishes this is a `new_source_needed` case: no source registered in scripts/sources.yaml digitises French départements at sub-national resolution. gadm-4.1-adm1's local file is a curated 81-country subset that omits France entirely, even though GADM's global release does cover French départements; cshapes-2.0 and cshapes-europe are country-level only; mapspain-ign is specific to Spain; none of the other registered sources (gadm-3.6, geobr-ibge, cliopatria, paine-2024, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) offer French sub-national coverage either. The standard remedy, as for the sibling département pages created alongside this one, would be to register GADM's global admin-2 layer or French IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS product, neither of which is on disk here today.

**Territory description:** Essonne occupies the southern part of the Île-de-France region, south of Paris. On a modern map it is bounded by Hauts-de-Seine and Val-de-Marne to the north, Seine-et-Marne to the east, Yonne and Loiret to the south, and Yvelines and Eure-et-Loir to the west; its préfecture is Évry-Courcouronnes. It covers approximately 1,804 km2 -- a published modern administrative-area figure for the département, not a measurement from any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet assigned (see polygon status above). The département spans dense northern suburbs close to Paris (around Évry, Massy and Corbeil-Essonnes) as well as substantial agricultural land in the Beauce and Hurepoix to the south, and includes the Paris-Saclay research and technology cluster.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1968-not-1790

**Start year is 1968 (Essonne's own creation), not the country's 1790 default**

The France country convention's default start rule (1790, the Revolutionary départements reform) applies to the ~83 original départements, of which Essonne is not one -- Essonne did not exist until 1968. This is not one of the 4 listed departures in the convention (Belfort, Alsace-Lorraine, Corsica, overseas départements) either, but the routing decision explicitly notes that Essonne's administrative history departs from the 1790 default on its own facts: it was carved out of Seine-et-Oise in 1968 as part of the Paris-region reorganisation that also created Yvelines, Val-d'Oise, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis and Val-de-Marne. Applying the 1790 default here would assert Essonne existed 178 years before it did. The precedent set by [fra-77-1800-2025](fra-77-1800-2025.md) (Seine-et-Marne, a genuine 1790 département) does not transfer: that page's 1800 start reflects the earliest FRA national container row, not the département's own creation date, whereas here the département's own creation date (1968) is later than the container's start and so is the binding floor.

### d-single-container-edge

**Single container edge spans 1968-2025 under FRA-1919-2025, not split at any interior year**

The routing decision's proposed container_code (FRA-1919-2025) already covers Essonne's entire 1968-2025 span without a metropolitan boundary change in between: no Alsace-Lorraine-style territorial event touches the Paris region between 1968 and the present, and no further split or merger of Essonne itself is known. A single edge therefore tiles the full span with no gap, satisfying the containment-tiling requirement without introducing spurious interior breakpoints the data does not support.

### d-pre-1968-data-not-modeled

**Data before 1968 (the routing decision notes 1862-2023 coverage) is not addressed by this entry**

The routing decision's admin_name FR104 extract runs 1862-2023, predating Essonne's 1968 creation by over a century, and flags this as requiring a back_cast treatment for the pre-1968 years. This page only creates the polity for Essonne's own observed span (1968-2025); reconciling the pre-1968 rows against Seine-et-Oise (or another predecessor territory) is left open below rather than resolved here, since establishing that predecessor relationship is a separate routing decision this page's mandate does not cover.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1968-data-routing

**Where does the 1862-1967 portion of the FR104 data belong?**

The routing decision that created this page describes an extract spanning 1862-2023 for admin_name FR104 (Essonne), but Essonne itself only existed from 1968. The pre-1968 years (1862-1967) were presumably reported under Seine-et-Oise, the département Essonne was split from in 1968 along with Yvelines, Val-d'Oise, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis and Val-de-Marne. No Seine-et-Oise polity row exists in this table yet (checked against the France rows: only FRA-1800-1871, FRA-1800-1919, FRA-1871-1919, FRA-1919-2025, plus the new FRA-77 Seine-et-Marne and other new FR- département pages in this same batch). Whether the pre-1968 FR104 rows should predecessor-link to a future Seine-et-Oise entry, or whether they cannot be disaggregated from Seine-et-Oise data at all (since a value reported for the whole pre-split département cannot be attributed to the Essonne portion alone without an areal or population weighting), is unresolved and should be decided when Seine-et-Oise itself is routed.

### oq-polygon-availability

**Is a modern IGN or global-GADM Essonne boundary actually obtainable, and under what licence?**

The routing decision's polygon_reasoning concludes new_source_needed because the locally registered gadm-4.1-adm1 file is a curated 81-country subset that excludes France, even though GADM's global (non-subset) release and French IGN's ADMIN-EXPRESS product are both stated to cover this boundary. Neither has been verified as licence-compatible or actually downloaded into this repository. Until one is registered, this page carries polygon_status: unassigned and no polygon_area_km2, so any area figure used in the territorial-extent description below is a stated administrative figure, not a geometry measurement -- and even that stated figure has not been cross-checked against a second independent source.

### oq-other-1968-split-siblings

**Do Yvelines, Val-d'Oise, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis and Val-de-Marne need matching entries, and do they share this same pre-1968 gap?**

Essonne is one of six départements created by the single 1968 Seine-et-Oise split. If the source dataset that produced admin_name FR104 also carries FR-codes for the other five, they will face the identical pre-1968 routing question raised above, and their container/start_year reasoning should be consistent with this page's. This page does not check whether those five already exist as separate polities elsewhere in the same ingest batch, or whether their creation is still pending.
