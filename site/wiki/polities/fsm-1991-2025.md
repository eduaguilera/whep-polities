# Micronesia (Federated States of)

## Summary

The Federated States of Micronesia (FSM) is a Pacific island nation comprising four states (Yap, Chuuk, Pohnpei, Kosrae) in the western Pacific. WHEP tracks it from 1991, when FSM joined the United Nations and entered the COW state system. FSM entered into a Compact of Free Association with the United States in 1986 (effective 3 November 1986), but COW state-system membership came later, at UN admission on 17 September 1991. The WHEP start year 1991 reflects the COW/CShapes entry date rather than the Compact of Free Association date or any earlier administrative change. COW ccode=987 (stateabb=FSM); state-system entry 1991-09-17.

Note: The CSV also assigns cow_code=987 to FRS-1977-2025 (Djibouti), which is a data error on the Djibouti row — FSM's cow_code=987 is correct here.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1991-coverage

FSM's territory was part of the U.S. Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands from 1947 to 1986, and the Compact of Free Association period ran 1986–1991. No WHEP row covers FSM's geographic territory before 1991. This is a coverage gap; the territory existed and may have trade data. A pre-1991 row (possibly covering the Trust Territory period) may be needed for completeness.
