# Canada (to the Newfoundland accession)

## Summary

> **Boundary corrected 2026-08-05 — this row now covers 1886 through 1948 (issue 15).**
> It replaces `CAN-1886-1948`, whose `end_year` of 1948 is exclusive and so covered only
> 1886-**1947**, one year short of what its own Summary claimed. The missing year is 1948,
> which belongs here: Newfoundland acceded on **31 March 1949**, so for all of calendar
> 1948 Canada was still the Newfoundland-excluded territory this row's polygon describes
> (9,553,644 km², against 9,952,067 km² after accession).
>
> CShapes dates its Canadian boundary change to **1948-07-22**, the date of the second
> Newfoundland referendum, and the database had inherited that. A referendum is a
> political event; the territorial transfer was the accession. See
> [can-1949-2025](can-1949-2025.md) for the other half of the same correction, and
> issue 23 for the general convention question this is an instance of.

This row tracks Canada from 1886 through 1948, ending just before
Newfoundland's accession (31 March 1949, covered in the successor row
CAN-1949-2025). The 1886 start year aligns exactly with the earliest
CShapes 2.0 polygon vintage for Canada (gwcode 20, gwsyear 1886),
yielding zero polygon vintage drift.

This polity was split from the former CAN-1866-1948 row (which spanned 82
years with a single 1886 polygon) to isolate the stable post-1886 period
from the earlier era of major territorial acquisition.

Key developments within this row: Alberta and Saskatchewan created (1905);
Statute of Westminster (1931) confirmed legislative independence; Dominion
status throughout. Territory essentially stable relative to the 1886 polygon.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon source.** `CShapes 2.0` gwcode 20, gwsyear 1886 (~9,553,560 km²),
`polygon_status: assigned`. The polygon vintage matches the row start year
exactly (drift = 0). Minor boundary changes within the row (e.g. Alberta
and Saskatchewan split from NWT in 1905) do not affect the total area
meaningfully; the 1886 CShapes polygon already reflects the post-Rupert's
Land acquisition extent of Canada.

**Area note.** The polygon area of ~9.55M km² reflects Canada's near-full
territorial extent by 1886. Newfoundland (~405,720 km²) is excluded, as it
remained a separate Dominion until 1949 (covered by NFL-1907-1949).
