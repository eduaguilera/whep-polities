# Namibia (1920-1990)

## Summary

Namibia (1920-1990) covers South West Africa / Namibia from 17 December 1920 -- when the League of Nations mandate under South Africa began -- to 20 March 1990, the eve of Namibian independence. This row was formed by merging NAM-1920-1966 and NAM-1966-1990, because CShapes records identical areas (~72.31 sq deg) across the mandate and post-mandate periods. The 1966 UN revocation of South Africa's mandate (UN General Assembly Resolution 2145, 27 October 1966) was a legal status change, not a territorial change -- South Africa continued to administer the territory throughout. A prolonged guerrilla war led by SWAPO eventually forced the transition to independence in 1990 [biger-1995 §namibia](../sources/biger-1995.md#namibia).

## Territorial extent

Identical territory throughout. South-western Africa bounded by Portuguese Angola / Angola (north), Bechuanaland / Botswana (east), South Africa (south), and the Atlantic Ocean (west), with the Caprivi Strip. ## Open questions

### oq-nam-un-revocation

The UN revoked the mandate on 27 October 1966, but South Africa rejected this. The legal status of South African administration 1966-1990 (illegal occupation vs. continued mandate) varies by source. CShapes reflects the change as status=mandate to status=colony.
