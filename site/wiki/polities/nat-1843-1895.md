# Natal (to 1895)

## Summary

The British Colony of Natal from its proclamation on 4 May 1843 (after Britain annexed the short-lived Boer Republic of Natalia) to the 1895 CShapes boundary cut. The 1895 WHEP end year does not match a specific Natal-Wikipedia event; the canonical Zululand-annexation-to-Natal date is 1897 per Wikipedia.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE). Covers the core pre-Zululand Natal colony (roughly the modern KwaZulu-Natal south of the Tugela).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [zul-1816-1879](zul-1816-1879.md) — Zulu Kingdom, defeated in the 1879 Anglo-Zulu War. Natal's territorial link to ZUL is geographically adjacent (Natal lies south of the Tugela; Zululand lies north) rather than successor-of-Zulu proper; the British-Zulu war's immediate territorial outcome was a Zululand protectorate, not a direct Natal expansion.
- **Successor:** NAT-1895-1910 (per CSV). That row should represent Natal after the Zululand annexation (formally 1897) and before the 1910 Union of South Africa.

## Sourced claims

- "The Colony of Natal was a British colony in south-eastern Africa. It was proclaimed a British colony on 4 May 1843 after the British government had annexed the Boer Republic of Natalia." [wikipedia-second-order-gaps-2026-04-15 §natal](../sources/wikipedia-second-order-gaps-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- Zululand was annexed to Natal in 1897 (not 1895) — "eighteen years later the kingdoms were annexed to the Natal colony, doubling its size" — counting from the 1879 Battle of Ulundi. [wikipedia-second-order-gaps-2026-04-15 §natal](../sources/wikipedia-second-order-gaps-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)

## Contradictions

**End-year conflict.** CSV has NAT-1843-1895 → NAT-1895-1910, cutting at 1895. Wikipedia dates the Zululand annexation to Natal at 1897. Possible reconciliations: (a) the 1895 cut corresponds to a different administrative event (responsible-government status, etc.); (b) the date is a CShapes cartographic-revision artefact and the effective territorial change is 1897.
