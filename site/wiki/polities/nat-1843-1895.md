# Natal (to 1895)

## Summary

The British Colony of Natal from its proclamation on 4 May 1843 (after Britain annexed the short-lived Boer Republic of Natalia) to the 1895-04-23 CShapes boundary change when Zululand was administratively incorporated under Natal, roughly doubling its area.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE), feature id 562, vintage 1886, valid 1886-01-01 to 1895-04-22. Area 43,663 km². Covers the core pre-Zululand Natal colony (roughly modern KwaZulu-Natal south of the Tugela). CShapes does not record a distinct polygon for pre-1886 Natal; the 1886 vintage is used as the best available proxy for the 1843-1895 period.

**polygon_vintage_drift note:** The 1886 polygon is used for data years 1843-1895 (up to 43 years before vintage). If production data for early decades of this period appears anomalously large or small relative to what a ~43,000 km² colony would produce, check whether the data covers a different territorial scope (e.g. including Zululand before the formal annexation).

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor:** [zul-1816-1879](zul-1816-1879.md) — Zulu Kingdom. Natal's territorial link to ZUL is geographic adjacency rather than strict succession; Natal lay south of the Tugela, Zululand to the north. The 1879 Anglo-Zulu War resulted in a Zululand protectorate, not immediate Natal expansion.
- **Successor:** [nat-1895-1910](nat-1895-1910.md) — Natal (1895-1910), post-Zululand expansion, CShapes vintage 1895, 72,516 km².

## Resolved questions

### oq-nat-1895-vs-1897 (RESOLVED 2026-06-30)

CSV end_year=1895 vs Wikipedia's 1897 Zululand annexation. **Resolution:** the 1895 split is correct. CShapes 2.0 records the territorial change at 1895-04-23 (administrative incorporation of Zululand under Natal). Wikipedia's 1897 is the formal Natal Act legislative date. WHEP uses CShapes dates; the proposed rename to NAT-1843-1897 / NAT-1897-1910 is rejected. See [nat-1895-1910](nat-1895-1910.md) for full documentation.
