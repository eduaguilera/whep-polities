# Dominican Republic

## Summary

The Dominican Republic occupies the eastern region of the Caribbean island of Hispaniola, sharing a common boundary with Haiti in the west (222 miles; 358 km.). Biger 1995 records its area as 18,704 sq. miles (48,443 sq. km.) with a population of 7,600,000 in 1994 [biger-1995 &sect;dominican-republic](../sources/biger-1995.md#dominican-republic). The country gained independence from Spain in 1821 but was occupied by Haiti almost immediately (1822-1844), and Spain annexed the island again in 1861 before a War of Restoration restored independence in 1865. WHEP tracks it as a continuous row from 1800 because the territory on the eastern portion of Hispaniola remained constant.
