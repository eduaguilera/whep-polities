# Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (1953-1964)

## Summary

The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland (also called the Central African Federation) existed from 1 August 1953 to 21 January 1964. It merged Southern Rhodesia (now Zimbabwe), Northern Rhodesia (now Zambia), and Nyasaland (now Malawi) into a single federal entity under British authority. CShapes records this as COW 550, status=colony, owner=GBR (COW 200), area ~106.16. The Federation dissolved when Northern Rhodesia and Nyasaland gained independence as Zambia and Malawi respectively.

## Territorial extent

The Federation encompassed the territories of present-day Zimbabwe, Zambia, and Malawi. Total area ~106.16 (CShapes units). Polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (dependencies=TRUE).
