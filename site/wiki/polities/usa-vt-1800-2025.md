# Vermont (state of the United States)

## Summary

Vermont, admitted to the Union in 1791 as the 14th state and the first added after the original thirteen, as a subnational reporting unit within the United States. `type: subnational` because Vermont holds no sovereignty independent of the federal union -- it is a constituent state -- and this row exists purely to give juan-subnational's state-level Vermont data (previously absorbed into the country-wide USA-* national rows, each of which represents the roughly 3.8-million-km2 national territory rather than Vermont's own ~24,900 km2) a container of its own size. The entry's start_year is 1800, not Vermont's actual 1791 admission: the polity table's earliest national USA row (USA-1800-1803) itself only begins in 1800, and the containment gate rejects any edge whose span falls outside its container's own recorded span, so 1800 is the earliest year any USA state-level row can currently be containered against. This mirrors the identical compromise already made for Delaware (USA-DE-1800-2025) and Connecticut (USA-CT-1800-2025), both pre-1800 states clamped to the same floor.

**Why this entry exists.** juan-subnational carries Vermont as a distinct state-level admin1 unit with its own multi-year time series of agricultural/economic data (mirroring the pattern already established for the other 49 states in this batch, e.g. Alabama, Delaware, Arizona). Before this entry, any Vermont-labeled rows would have had no home except the country-wide USA-* national totals, which are roughly 150x Vermont's own area and would silently blend Vermont's numbers into a national aggregate -- exactly the kind of scale mismatch this batch of state-level entries is meant to fix. No existing polity in the table IS Vermont: the only USA-iso3 rows besides the five national totals are Alaska's pre-statehood territory (ALK-1867-1959) and, now, sibling state entries such as USA-DE-1800-2025 and USA-AL-1819-2025, none of which name or cover Vermont. Vermont's distinctness as a reporting/administrative unit is not in question -- it has held a fixed, named state boundary and its own state government continuously since 1791, and the country convention (`country_conventions.json`, USA) documents each state's own admission year as the basis for its span, with Vermont explicitly named as the first state added after the original thirteen.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. `polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1`, feature `USA.46_1`, matching Vermont's present-day state boundary. Vermont's external borders -- the 45th parallel with Quebec, Lake Champlain and New York to the west, the Connecticut River and New Hampshire to the east, Massachusetts to the south -- have been fixed since admission in 1791 (the 19th-century New York/New Hampshire boundary surveys corrected details of the line but not the state's overall extent), so the modern GADM boundary is a low-risk proxy for the full 1800-2025 span, the same reasoning already used for Delaware and Connecticut in this batch.

**Territory:** the modern U.S. state of Vermont, in the northeastern United States, bordered by Quebec (north), New Hampshire (east, along the Connecticut River), Massachusetts (south), and New York (west, across Lake Champlain for part of the line). Approximately 24,906 km2 (the polygon's own measured area in an equal-area projection), consistent with Vermont's commonly cited land+water area. No boundary or territorial-extent controversy is known for this period; the only open item is the clamped start_year, addressed under Decisions.

## Decisions

### d-start-year-1800-not-1791

**start_year is 1800, not Vermont's 1791 admission year**

The country convention's default rule dates a state's start to its year of admission to the Union -- 1791 for Vermont, per the routing decision this page implements. But the containment gate requires every container edge to fall entirely within both the member's and the container's own recorded span, and the earliest national USA row in the table, USA-1800-1803, itself starts at 1800: there is no USA container row covering 1791-1800. Rather than leave that eleven-year gap uncontained (which the gate would reject) or invent a container row outside this task's scope, this entry clamps start_year to 1800, the container floor, exactly as already done for Delaware (USA-DE-1800-2025, ratified 1787) and Connecticut (USA-CT-1800-2025) in the same batch. This is a containment-table limitation, not a claim that Vermont statehood began in 1800; Vermont's actual admission (1791) is recorded under Sourced claims and the gap is tracked as an open question.

## Open questions

### oq-pre-1800-gap

**1791-1800 statehood is not represented by any container row**

Vermont was a full member state of the Union for nine years (1791-1800) before this entry's clamped start_year. If a future revision adds an earlier national USA container row (e.g. USA-1791-1800 or an extension of USA-1800-1803 backward), this entry's start_year should be revisited and moved back to 1791 to match the country convention's default admission-year rule, and any Vermont data from that decade currently unroutable should be checked against the new container. This is the same open item already flagged on the sibling Delaware and Connecticut entries, so resolving it once (e.g. by widening the container floor) would apply to all pre-1800 original/early states at once rather than needing a separate fix per state.
