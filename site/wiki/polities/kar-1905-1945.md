# Karafuto (Japanese South Sakhalin)

## Summary

Karafuto, southern Sakhalin under Japan. "This territory became part of the Empire of Japan in 1905
after the Russo-Japanese War, when the portion of Sakhalin south of 50°N was ceded by the Russian
Empire under the Treaty of Portsmouth." "In August 1945, the Japanese administration ceased to
function following the Soviet invasion of South Sakhalin." It sits inside
[jpn-1895-1945](jpn-1895-1945.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §karafuto](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#karafuto)
