# Sudan (1934-1956)

## Summary

Anglo-Egyptian Condominium of Sudan in its late colonial phase. This period covers WWII (Sudan Defence Force served with Allied forces in Eritrea, Libya, and Ethiopia), post-war political development, and the path to independence. Key milestones: Advisory Council for Northern Sudan (1944), Legislative Assembly (1948), self-government agreement (1953), independence 1 January 1956.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Not yet assigned. Sudan boundaries during this period were those of the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium (modern Sudan + South Sudan combined, ~2.5M km²). CShapes 2.0 has earlier Sudan entries ([SUD-1910-1934](sud-1910-1934.md)) whose polygons could serve.

**Why this entry exists:** Fills the 1934-1956 gap between [SUD-1910-1934](sud-1910-1934.md) and [SUD-1956-2011](sud-1956-2011.md). Without this, 153 rows of Sudan data for 1935-1955 were unmatched.

## Key dates

- 1936: Anglo-Egyptian Treaty — revised terms of condominium
- 1940-1941: Sudan Defence Force fights in East Africa campaign
- 1944: Advisory Council for Northern Sudan established
- 1948: Legislative Assembly replaces Advisory Council
- 1953: Anglo-Egyptian Agreement on self-government and self-determination
- 1956-01-01: Independence as Republic of Sudan
