# Territory of Papua and New Guinea (1949-1975)

## Summary

The Territory of Papua and New Guinea was an Australian-administered territory formed in 1949 by the administrative union of the Territory of Papua (an Australian external territory) and the Territory of New Guinea (a UN Trust Territory formerly under League of Nations mandate). Australia governed the combined territory until independence on 16 September 1975.
