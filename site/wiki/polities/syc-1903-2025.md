# Seychelles

## Summary

The Seychelles is an archipelago of 115 islands in the western Indian Ocean, northeast of Madagascar. WHEP tracks it from 1903, when the islands became a separate Crown Colony independent of Mauritius, to 2025. Prior to 1903, the Seychelles were administered as a dependency of Mauritius; the 1903 separation is the WHEP start date for this row. The Seychelles became independent on 29 June 1976. CShapes 2.0 records a single time-step for the Seychelles beginning 1976-06-29 (the COW independence date), covering the post-independence period. The polygon source in the CSV is CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=591), consistent with COW stateabb=SEY, ccode=591.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=591). CShapes records one time-step: 1976-06-29 to 2019-12-31, area=0.031 sq deg (approximately 460 km² total land area). The CShapes polygon covers the independence-era territory. For the 1903–1976 colonial period, no separate CShapes polygon exists; the WHEP polygon is back-projected from the independence-era polygon, which is reasonable given that no territorial change occurred between the 1903 separation from Mauritius and independence.

- **Predecessor:** Seychelles as dependency of Mauritius (pre-1903). No separate WHEP row exists for this period; the territory would have been subsumed under a Mauritius-linked row. See [mus-1800-2025](mus-1800-2025.md) for the Mauritius row.
- **Successor:** None; SYC-1903-2025 is the terminal row.
