# Panama (before Canal Zone return)

## Summary

Panama from the 1800 database floor to the 1979 return of the Panama Canal Zone territory under the 1977 Torrijos-Carter Treaties. This WHEP row compresses several historically distinct regimes: (i) part of the Viceroyalty of New Granada 1800–1819; (ii) part of Gran Colombia 1819–1830; (iii) part of the Republic of Colombia / New Granada / United States of Colombia 1830–1903; (iv) independent Republic of Panama 1903–1979 *with the US-administered Canal Zone as an exclave*. The 1979 WHEP boundary marks the return of Canal Zone territory to Panamanian administration.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0. CShapes's Panama polygon for 1886–1903 shows the territory as part of Colombia, and from 1903–1979 as the Republic of Panama with the Canal Zone depicted separately (under US control 1903–1979). The 1979 return of the Canal Zone is the single biggest territorial change in this row.

## Sourced claims

- Panama declared independence from Colombia on 3 November 1903: "Panama, tacitly supported by the United States, proclaimed its independence." [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §panama](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- The 1903 Hay-Bunau-Varilla Treaty granted the US rights "as if it were sovereign" over a ~16 × 80 km canal zone where the US would "build a canal, then administer, fortify, and defend it 'in perpetuity'." [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §panama](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)
- Torrijos-Carter Treaties signed 7 September 1977; "The surrounding territory was returned first, in 1979", restoring the Canal Zone to Panama. Full canal handover followed on 31 December 1999. [wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15 §panama](../sources/wikipedia-self-ref-chain-2026-04-15.md#what-it-adds)

## Contradictions

**Regime compression.** PAN-1800-1979 compresses pre-independence (part of Colombia) and post-independence (1903–1979) Panama into one row. A more historically clean chain would have separate rows for pre-1903 Colombian Panama and 1903–1979 independent Panama. This is a candidate CSV split.

## Open questions

### oq-pan-1903-split

Whether to split PAN-1800-1979 at 3 November 1903 (independence from Colombia). The 1903 separation is a sovereignty change on territory that does *not* substantially change size — the WHEP territorial-economic-unit rule suggests no split, but the entity change is so large (departmental→sovereign) that a split may be warranted.

### oq-pan-canal-zone-subnational

The US-administered Canal Zone 1903–1979 is a major exclave that is arguably a distinct WHEP polity. Whether to add a CZN-1903-1979 subnational row is open.
