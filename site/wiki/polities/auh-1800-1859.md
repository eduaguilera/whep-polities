# Austrian Empire (to 1859)

## Summary

This row covers **60 years** of the Habsburg state from the
database floor (1800) through the loss of Lombardy (1859). It
spans the Austrian Empire proclamation (1804), the dissolution
of the Holy Roman Empire (1806), the Congress of Vienna (1815),
and the 1848 revolutions. The row ends when Lombardy (~23,000
km²) was lost to Sardinia/France in the Second Italian War of
Independence.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor).
Successor: [auh-1859-1866](auh-1859-1866.md) — loss of Lombardy
(Treaty of Zurich, 10 November 1859).
