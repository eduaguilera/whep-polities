# Austrian Empire (to 1859)

## Summary

This row covers **60 years** of the Habsburg state from the
database floor (1800) through the loss of Lombardy (1859). It
spans the Austrian Empire proclamation (1804), the dissolution
of the Holy Roman Empire (1806), the Congress of Vienna (1815),
and the 1848 revolutions. The row ends when Lombardy (~23,000
km²) was lost to Sardinia/France in the Second Italian War of
Independence.

Predecessor: NA (pre-database floor).
Successor: [auh-1859-1866](auh-1859-1866.md) — loss of Lombardy
(Treaty of Zurich, 10 November 1859).

## Tuscany removed from the polygon (2026-09-25)

**Polygon status: `assigned`, rebuilt.** `constructed` (`build_auh_1800_1859()`): Cliopatria's
"Austrian Empire" at its 1820-1827 step (691,741 km²) **minus six modern Italian regions** it wrongly
draws as Austrian, **666,434 km²** (ESRI:54034) at source. Measured inside Cliopatria's feature: GADM
4.1 Toscana 19,677 of 23,018 km² (Florence is inside), Emilia-Romagna 2,485, Piemonte 1,319, Umbria 763,
Lazio 388, Marche 288. None of that ground was Austrian: the Grand Duchy of Tuscany was a separate
monarchy (a Habsburg-Lorraine secundogeniture) "from 1569 to 1860", and Austria's Italian crown land
was the Kingdom of Lombardy-Venetia, "a constituent land (crown land) of the Austrian Empire from 1815
to 1866", north of the Po and east of the Ticino. Cliopatria has no separate Tuscany polity in 1820,
which is why the Grand Duchy is inside its Austrian Empire.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §tuscany-and-lombardy-venetia](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#tuscany-and-lombardy-venetia)

The six regions are widened by 20 km before subtraction, with GADM's Lombardia, Veneto,
Friuli-Venezia Giulia and Trentino-Alto Adige cut back out of the widening, because Cliopatria's
Tuscan coast and islands do not follow GADM's (its Elba, 233 km², would otherwise survive as an
Austrian island). The Austrian regions keep all their ground. The change is 3.7% of the row. No layer-B
row routes here, so no data moves.
