## Summary

Namibia (1886-1915) covers German South-West Africa from 1 January 1886 — the CShapes 2.0 temporal baseline, coinciding with the post-Berlin-Conference colonial partition — to 15 August 1915, when South African forces completed the conquest of the territory during World War I. CShapes records this as status=colony, owner=DEU (COW 255). The territory was formally a German Protectorate, proclaimed in 1884, and became the main German colony in southern Africa. The WHEP split from NAM-1884-1886 reflects the CShapes coverage start at 1886 and a small polygon change (area increases from ~61.61 to ~72.31 CShapes units at 1886-12-30), indicating a boundary adjustment in late 1886.

## Territorial extent

South-western Africa bounded by Portuguese Angola (north), Bechuanaland/British sphere (east), South Africa (south), and the Atlantic Ocean (west). The Caprivi Strip extends northeast to the Zambia/Zambezi boundary. CShapes 2.0 records two polygon entries for this WHEP period: area ~61.61 (1886-01-01 to 1886-12-29) and area ~72.31 (1886-12-30 to 1915-08-15). The area increase at end-1886 reflects the formal demarcation of the southern and eastern boundaries following Anglo-German colonial agreements. The Angola-Namibia boundary runs 855 miles (1,376 km).
