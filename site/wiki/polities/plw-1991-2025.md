# Palau

## Summary

Palau is a Pacific island nation comprising approximately 340 islands in the western Pacific (Micronesia region). WHEP tracks it from 1991 to 2025. Palau was the last component of the U.S. Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands to achieve a Compact of Free Association with the United States, which entered into force on 1 October 1994. COW state-system entry is 1994-12-15 (UN membership). The WHEP CSV start year 1991 differs from both the Compact entry date (1994-10-01) and the COW entry date (1994-12-15) — this appears to be a data inconsistency worth flagging. CShapes records two time-steps both at identical area, both independent from 1994 onward.

## Territorial extent

Polygon from CShapes 2.0 (cowcode=986). CShapes records two time-steps: start=1994-12-15 to 2006-10-07 (area=0.031 sq deg), and 2006-10-08 to 2019-12-31 (area=0.031 sq deg). ## Contradictions

**WHEP start year 1991 vs. actual independence 1994.** The CSV start_year=1991 for PLW-1991-2025 does not correspond to any known Palauan political event. The Compact of Free Association was approved in a 1993 referendum and entered into force in 1994. The CShapes polygon begins in 1994-12-15. The 1991 WHEP start year may be a copy from another Micronesian row (FSM-1991-2025 and MHL-1874-2025 both have significant 1991 events) or a data entry error.

## Open questions

### oq-start-year-1991

The WHEP start year 1991 for PLW-1991-2025 is inconsistent with all known Palauan events. The relevant dates are: 1986 (Compact approved by Palau's congress but rejected by referendum), 1993-11-09 (successful referendum), 1994-10-01 (Compact enters into force), 1994-12-15 (UN membership/COW entry). The start year should likely be corrected to 1994. A proposal should be filed.

### oq-2006-cshapes-split

CShapes records two time-steps for Palau: 1994–2006 and 2006–2019, at identical area. The 2006 split point has no known corresponding Palauan territorial event. Under the WHEP polity definition (same area = same row), the CShapes split should not generate a WHEP split. The single PLW-1991-2025 row is correctly maintained, but the 2006 CShapes anomaly should be noted.
