# Ciudad Real (province of Spain)

## Summary

This entry is the province of Ciudad Real, one of the 50 present-day provinces of Spain and one of the original provinces created in the 1833 Javier de Burgos territorial reform that replaced Spain's historic kingdom-based divisions with a uniform grid of administrative provinces. It sits in the south of the modern autonomous community of Castilla-La Mancha, covering the southern part of the historic comarca of La Mancha, with its capital city of the same name. The entry is `type: subnational` because Ciudad Real has never been a sovereign state in its own right -- it has always been an administrative subdivision of Spain, first of the unitary 19th- and 20th-century state and later, from 1978, of the decentralized Spain of autonomous communities -- and the row exists purely to carry statistical data reported at the province level rather than to represent an independent polity. Its start year, 1833, is the date of the de Burgos reform that created the province with essentially its current boundaries; its end year, 2025, is the open, still-current convention applied uniformly to Spanish province rows in this table, reflecting that Ciudad Real remains an active administrative unit with data collected through at least 2021, not any real dissolution.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: this row is created to receive Spanish province-level statistical series reported under the administrative unit code ES422 (NUTS/admin code for Ciudad Real) within the country="Spain" dataset driving this pipeline's routing decisions; the routing record identifies it as unit_id ESP-ES422, admin_name "ES422", one of the standard set of Spanish province-level reporting units the pipeline is working through (siblings ES111 through ES241+ in the same batch). Previous matching: before this decision, ES422/Ciudad Real data had no polity to route to -- the routing reasoning explicitly checked for a name match against existing ESP polities and found none, noting the two existing ESP subnational siblings are A Coruna and Madrid, unrelated provinces, so there was no prior mismatch to correct, only a genuine gap where no polity existed for this admin unit. (Separately, git history in this session shows a previously-created esp-cr-1833-2025.md page for this same code was deleted as part of a repair pass on the batch of 50 ESP province pages, meaning this create decision restores a polity that existed before, rather than introducing a new one for the first time.) Confirmation of distinctness: Ciudad Real is confirmed as a genuinely distinct administrative and statistical unit by its continuous existence as one of Spain's 50 provinces since the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform -- the same reform, and the same style of evidence, already used to justify the other 49 ESP province rows in this table (e.g. ESP-BU for Burgos, ESP-LE for Leon) -- and by the fact that INE and other Spanish statistical agencies report population, agricultural, and economic data at exactly this provincial granularity, distinguishing Ciudad Real from its neighboring provinces of Toledo, Cuenca, Albacete, Cordoba, and Badajoz.

Ciudad Real specifically has no existing polity row to be confused with -- the two other ESP subnational siblings visible in this batch, A Coruna and Madrid, are different provinces -- and Federico-Tena-style provincial series and modern INE statistics both treat Ciudad Real as a distinct reporting unit going back to the 1833 reform, which is the historical source confirming this is a genuinely separate entity rather than an artifact of the routing pipeline. Territory description: Ciudad Real province occupies the southern portion of Castilla-La Mancha in central Spain, bordered by Toledo to the north, Cuenca and Albacete to the east, Jaen and Cordoba (Andalusia) to the south, and Badajoz to the west. It is a real, locatable administrative unit on any modern map of Spain's 50 provinces, corresponding to the southern half of the historic region of La Mancha. Its present-day area is approximately 19,813 km2, one of the larger Spanish provinces by area though sparsely populated; this figure is a known reference value for the modern province, not a measurement of any polygon attached to this entry, since no polygon is yet attached here.

## Predecessors and successors

Ciudad Real has no predecessor: it was created directly by the 1833 Javier de Burgos reform, which redrew the whole of Spain into 49 (later 50, after the Canary Islands split) provinces in a single administrative act rather than by splitting or merging any prior unit. It has no successor either: unlike some Spanish provinces that underwent later boundary adjustments, Ciudad Real has persisted as a stable administrative unit from 1833 to the present, so end_year 2025 is the open, still-current convention applied to all ESP provinces with data through at least 2021, not the marker of an actual dissolution or transformation event.

## Sourced claims

- The province of Ciudad Real was created, along with the other provinces of mainland Spain, by the territorial reorganization decree of 30 November 1833 promoted by Secretary of State Javier de Burgos, which replaced the old kingdom-based territorial divisions with 49 provinces.
- Ciudad Real is one of the five provinces of the modern autonomous community of Castilla-La Mancha, occupying the southern part of the historic region of La Mancha, and its capital, also named Ciudad Real, lies roughly 200 km south of Madrid.

The routing decision this page implements found that data/geodata/mapspain-ign/provinces.gpkg does not exist locally, even though mapspain-ign is registered in scripts/sources.yaml and is already the convention source for the other 49 ESP province rows. Until that file is fetched and the Ciudad Real feature (cpro code 13) is located inside it, polygon_status must stay unassigned and polygon_feature_id must stay null -- setting either prematurely would fail validate_declared_sources, which already rejected polygon_source values of new_source_needed and registered_source_unfetched on earlier attempts at this same page. Fetching the source and re-running the harness against this row is the next concrete step, not a modeling question.

### oq-cr-boundary-stability-unverified

**Whether Ciudad Real's 1833 provincial boundary matches the present-day IGN boundary has not been checked against a historical source**

This page assumes, per the convention already applied to the other 49 ESP provinces, that Spanish provincial boundaries have been stable enough since 1833 that a present-day IGN polygon is an acceptable proxy for the full 1833-2025 span. That assumption has not been specifically verified for Ciudad Real -- it is a carried-over convention, not a checked fact for this unit. Ciudad Real has not had documented boundary changes of the kind that affected some Spanish provinces (e.g. minor municipal transfers between neighboring provinces during the 19th and 20th centuries), but no source has been consulted here to confirm that no such transfer altered Ciudad Real's extent. If a future check of INE historical boundary records finds a discrepancy, polygon_status should move from unassigned to proxy with a stated km2 gap, following the pattern used elsewhere in this repo for provinces whose modern boundary is known to diverge from the historical one.
