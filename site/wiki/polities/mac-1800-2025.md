# China, Macao SAR

## Summary

Draft WHEP row for Macao, added to cover FAOSTAT/FABIO reporting code MAC. The row uses a current GADM 4.1 adm0 polygon as a proxy for the full WHEP period.

## Territorial Extent

- **Polygon source:** GADM 4.1 adm1 (`GID_1 = CHN.MAC`).
- **Proxy note:** The current polygon is used for the whole 1800-2025 row pending historical boundary review.
- **Area:** the GADM polygon measures about 33.6 km2, the modern extent after a century of land
  reclamation. FAO's 1952 yearbook states Macau as 20 km2 (1947 land-use table), which is the
  peninsula and the islands of Taipa and Coloane before most of that reclamation. So the polygon
  overstates the territory of the 1930s-1950s by about 1.7x. The data are routed here anyway
  because no other polity holds the territory, and the label names it unambiguously
  ([GADM 4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)).

## Data Received

- **fao1952 `Macau`, 1937-1951 (4 rows):** total population 317 thousand (1937) and 188 thousand
  (1951), plus the 1947 land-use `use total` and `use builton wasteland` cells. Routed on
  2026-09-25 by an audit of unrouted layer-B rows (Claude). Before that the label matched no
  polity and the rows were dropped. Checked: no other fao1952 row lands on this polity in those
  cells, so nothing is counted twice. The 1937 figure is swollen by refugees from the
  Sino-Japanese war, and the 1951 figure reflects their return, so the fall is population, not a
  change of scope.

## Sourced Claims

- Biger 1995 describes Macao as a Portuguese overseas territory scheduled to become part of China in 1999 ([Biger 1995](../sources/biger-1995.md)).
