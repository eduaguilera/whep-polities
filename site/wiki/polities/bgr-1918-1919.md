# Bulgaria (1918-1919)

## Summary

This row tracks Bulgaria for **2 years**, a transitional post-WWI
period [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Armistice September 1918. Treaty of Neuilly (27 November 1919) --
Bulgaria lost Western Thrace to Greece (losing access to the Aegean
Sea), and small territories to Yugoslavia. The 1919 end date aligns
with Neuilly.
