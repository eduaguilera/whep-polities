# Angola (to 1890)

## Summary

Portuguese Angola from the 1800 database floor to 20 August 1890, the CShapes-defined start of the 1890–1891 transitional row (see [ang-1890-1891](ang-1890-1891.md)). Over this 90-year window the Portuguese presence was largely coastal (Luanda, Benguela, and the Cabinda exclave with intermittent control) and only slowly extended inland. The 1885–1886 agreements with the Congo Free State, Germany and France formed the first modern boundary of "the province", and the 20 August 1890 CShapes cut marks the onset of the transition to the 1891 Anglo-Portuguese Treaty extent.
