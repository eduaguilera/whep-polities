## Sourced claims

- The Kenya-Somalia boundary context notes that "Except for a short Italian occupation between 1935 and 1940, Ethiopia was the only African state that was not conquered by a European power" (p.191). By contrast, Sierra Leone was a British colony with boundaries established through Anglo-French agreements in the late 19th century. [biger-1995 §djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti)
- The Senegal entry confirms that France established the federation of French West Africa on 16 June 1895, which established boundaries between French and British possessions in West Africa, including the Sierra Leone-Guinea boundary (p.377). [biger-1995 §senegal](../sources/biger-1995.md#senegal)
- **Independence from British colonial rule 1961** (p.467). Political life unstable; ethnic struggle between Temne and Mende (p.467). Involved in Liberian civil war from 1990 (p.468). [biger-1995 §sierra-leone](../sources/biger-1995.md#sierra-leone)
