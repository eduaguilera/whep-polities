# Cambodia

## Summary

Cambodia as tracked by WHEP from 1953, when it gained independence from France. Cambodia's boundaries with Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam follow colonial-era French delimitations from the late 19th century. The country endured the Khmer Rouge regime (1975--1979) and Vietnamese occupation (1979--1989).

## Territorial extent

Polygon source is CShapes 2.0. Cambodia is bounded by Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam, with coastline on the Gulf of Thailand.
