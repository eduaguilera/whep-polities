# Wallis and Futuna
## Summary

Three volcanic islands in the South Pacific, about 400 km northeast of Fiji: Uvea (Wallis), Futuna
and Alofi. A French protectorate from **1887** (Uvea) and **1888** (Futuna), administered as a
dependency of New Caledonia until **1961**, a separate overseas territory from 1961, and an
overseas collectivity since 2003.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** GADM 4.1 country outline, `GID_0 = WLF` — the three GADM adm1 units are Uvéa (82.1
km²), Alo (50.6) and Singave (23.0).

```
GADM 4.1 measured      155.7 km²
official land area     142 km²
ratio                  1.096x
```

**`polygon_status: estimate`**, because at 142 km² a 9.6% divergence is the low-magnitude regime
rather than a defect. The same comparison across this database's micro-territories runs from 42%
under to 39% over (`MDV` 0.58x, `TUV` 1.39x, `MHL` 1.39x, `TKL` 1.33x), documented on
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md). The declared figure is the **official land area**, not what the
polygon measures, so `validate_polygons` check A compares the geometry against something
independent of it — the point issue 195 is about.

## Span

`1800-2025`, a 226-year single-vintage row — normally the shape issue 22 warns about. It is safe
here for a reason that does not generalise: these are three islands whose coastlines have not
moved. The administrative history — protectorate 1887/1888, New Caledonia dependency to 1961,
separate territory, collectivity from 2003 — is a sequence of **sovereignty and administration**
changes, not territorial ones, and this schema has no sovereignty field. Same treatment as
[tkl-1800-2025](tkl-1800-2025.md), [fro-1800-2025](fro-1800-2025.md) and
[abw-1800-2025](abw-1800-2025.md).

## Predecessors and successors

Both empty, deliberately. Wallis and Futuna's administrative predecessor is New Caledonia, which
**governed** these islands rather than preceding them — putting `NCL` in `predecessor:` would
assert a chronology that did not happen, the error PR 135 removed from Greenland and Iceland.
`verdicts_thinpages10.json` already noted this relationship and that there was "no separate polity
in the DB to route it to"; there is now, and the relationship stays in prose.

## Contradictions

None identified. The GADM-versus-official difference is 9.6% and is discussed above.

## Open questions

### oq-wlf-type-inconsistent-with-pyf-ncl

`type: territory` is used here because Wallis and Futuna is not sovereign. But
[pyf-1800-2025](pyf-1800-2025.md) and [ncl-1800-2025](ncl-1800-2025.md) — French overseas
collectivities in the same ocean with the same status — are both typed `national`, while `TKL`,
`FRO` and `ABW` are `territory`. The vocabulary is applied inconsistently across the database, and
this row follows the more accurate reading rather than the nearest neighbour. Issue 55 covers the
broader question.

### Already inside the statistical buckets, and that is not a new overlap

Measured before creating this row:

```
WLF ∩ ROCE-1850-2025   135.6 km²   87.1% of the territory
WLF ∩ ROW-1850-2025    137.4 km²   88.3%
```

That is the same relationship the other 22 territories in this class already have — `FRO`,
`NCL`, `MKD`, `GLP`, `MTQ` and the rest are all components of a regional "Other" bucket *and*
have their own polity. The buckets are in `validate_spatial_containment`'s
`LEGITIMATE_CONTAINERS` for exactly this reason. What changes is that data reported against this
territory can now reach it instead of only reaching a bucket.

### Why this entry exists

**WHEP's GLEAM 3.0 registry lists it as a country that exists today, and this database had no row
for it** (issue 187). `gleam_geographic_hierarchy` carries 204 rows; 201 resolved to a polity and
three did not — `ATF`, `SGS` and `WLF`.

The evidence is **weaker than for Aruba and the Holy See** (issue 185), and the difference is
worth stating: those two carried 244 and 268 published LUH2 rows, whereas FAOSTAT areas 71, 243
and 271 carry **zero** production rows today. So no whep value is currently mis-attributed. What
exists is a registry row in a source whep ships, whose crosswalk promotes this area out of the
Rest-of-World fold at read time while leaving it the shared `ROW-1850-2025` code — a bucket rather
than an identity. This repo already recorded the state as deliberate, in
`pipelines/faostat-era-matching/state/registry_unmapped.csv`: "registry area with no polity family
(non-country/aggregate)".
