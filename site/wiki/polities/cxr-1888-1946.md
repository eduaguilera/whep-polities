# Christmas Island (Straits Settlements dependency)

## Summary

Christmas Island in the Indian Ocean, "an area of 135 square kilometres", from its annexation to its
transfer to Singapore. Phosphate "led Britain to annex the island in 1888 and begin commercial mining
in 1899"; it was attached to the Straits Settlements, and "After the end of Japanese occupation, the
island's administration was restored to Singapore, but it was transferred to Australia in 1958". The
Singapore period is [cxr-1946-1958](cxr-1946-1958.md).
[wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §christmas-island](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#christmas-island) [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §straits-settlements](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#straits-settlements)

## Why this entry exists

The label `british christmas island` runs from 1909 to 1956. From 1946 it is CXR-1946-1958; its
**34 (year, label) pairs before 1946** had no polity. Its value is phosphate, which is why the island
is reported at all. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).

The island was not Australian until 1958, so the earlier flow flag (rows kept on AUS-1901-2025, origin CXR) is retired.
