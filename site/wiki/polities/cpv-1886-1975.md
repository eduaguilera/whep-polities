# Cape Verde (Portuguese colony)

## Summary

Portuguese Cape Verde from the earliest CShapes 2.0 coverage (1886) through independence on 5 July 1975. The Cape Verde archipelago was a Portuguese overseas territory administered as a province of Portugal. Cape Verde gained independence following the 1974 Carnation Revolution in Portugal, with the PAIGC/PAICV leading the independence movement. The territory is geographically unchanged at independence — the same 10 inhabited islands and 5 uninhabited islands of the archipelago.

## Territorial extent

Cape Verde Islands (area ~4,033 sq. km). The archipelago lies in the North Atlantic Ocean, approximately 570 km west of the African coast (Senegal/Mauritania). No land boundaries. Territory identical to the post-independence republic (CPV-1975-2025) — the political transition did not alter the archipelago's extent. Polygon from CShapes 2.0, feature id 402, vintage 1886.

The CShapes 2.0 polygon records Cape Verde as status=colony, ruler=PRT over this window. The same geographic islands form the post-independence state, so the polygon is equally valid for both periods; polygon_vintage_drift is not a concern here because the territory is an isolated archipelago with no land boundary changes possible.
