# Russian Empire (to 1856)

## Summary

This row tracks the Russian Empire from **1800** (the WHEP database
floor) to **1856** (Treaty of Paris ending the Crimean War)
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The 57-year window covers Russia's
massive expansion from ~17.4 million km² to its approximate
pre-Crimean maximum, including the acquisitions of Georgia, Finland,
Bessarabia, Congress Poland, and the ongoing Caucasus conquest.

- **1800 start** is the database floor. At 1800 the Russian Empire
  under Paul I was already the largest contiguous state in the world.
- **1856 end** is a real territorial event: the Treaty of Paris
  (30 March 1856) ended the Crimean War, forcing Russia to cede
  southern Bessarabia and demilitarize the Black Sea
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856).

The CSV notes field is rich and worth quoting: "Russian Empire
pre-Crimean War. ~17.4M km². Acquisitions: Georgia (1801), Finland
(1809), Bessarabia (1812), Congress Poland (1815). Caucasus conquest
ongoing. Crimean War (1853-56) ends this period"
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

Predecessor: NA (database floor).
Successor: [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (Russian Empire 1815-1823)` per the
  CSV — a single Cliopatria time-step from 1815–1823 applied across
  57 years [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Same single-time-step caveat as the
  successor row [cliopatria-v0.1.3 §single-time-step](../sources/cliopatria-v0.1.3.md#single-time-step). The polygon
  represents the post-Congress-of-Vienna configuration (~17.4M km²)
  and understates the pre-1801 extent (no Georgia) while overstating
  the 1800 extent (includes Finland gained 1809, Poland gained 1815).
- **No CShapes coverage.** CShapes 2.0 starts in 1886
  [cshapes-2.0 §scope](../sources/cshapes-2.0.md#scope), so this row has no CShapes validation.
- **COW code NA** in the CSV, but **should be 365**. COW records
  Russia (`RUS`, `ccode=365`) continuously from 1816-01-01 to
  2024-12-31 [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). The NA is an
  omission — see [log autonomous-3-cow-russia-dates](../log.md#autonomous-3-cow-russia-dates) on
  the successor page.

## Sourced claims

- **1801: Annexation of Georgia.** Russia secured Georgia after
  the Russo-Persian War. "After Russian armies liberated the Eastern
  Georgian Kingdom (allied since the 1783 Treaty of Georgievsk) from
  the Qajar dynasty's occupation of 1802"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856). The CSV notes confirm
  "Georgia (1801)" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **1809: Acquisition of Finland** from Sweden. Russia "gained
  Finland from the war against a weakened Sweden"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856). The Treaty of
  Fredrikshamn (17 September 1809) is the standard date. The CSV
  notes confirm "Finland (1809)" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). Finland's loss is
  the split event for the Swedish row `SWE-1800-1809`.
- **1812: Acquisition of Bessarabia** from the Ottoman Empire via
  the Treaty of Bucharest. The CSV notes confirm "Bessarabia (1812)"
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **1812: Napoleonic invasion.** "It was catastrophic for France,
  whose army was decimated during the Russian winter"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856). Not a territorial
  change for Russia but a defining event of the period.
- **1815: Congress of Vienna / Congress Poland.** Alexander I
  presided over the Congress; "ultimately made Alexander the monarch
  of Congress Poland"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856). The CSV notes
  confirm "Congress Poland (1815)" [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
- **1826–1828: Russo-Persian War.** "Russia managed to favorably
  bring an end to the war with the Treaty of Turkmenchay, including
  the formal acquisition of what are now Armenia, Azerbaijan, and
  Iğdır Province"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856).
- **1853–1856: Crimean War.** Russia's "defeat in the Crimean War
  (1853–1856)" led to the Treaty of Paris (30 March 1856). The
  treaty "mandated 'demilitarization of the Black Sea' and forced
  Russia to cede southern Bessarabia to Moldova"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856). End-of-row event.
- **Area.** ~17.4M km² per CSV notes [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). By 1895 the
  empire reached 22.8M km², "the third-largest empire in history"
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1856](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1856).
- **COW state-system membership.** COW records Russia (`RUS`,
  `ccode=365`) from 1816-01-01 onward
  [cow-state-system-v2024 §key-dates](../sources/cow-state-system-v2024.md#key-dates). The CSV has
  `cow_code = NA` for this row — an omission, not a deliberate
  choice. See [log autonomous-3-cow-russia-dates](../log.md#autonomous-3-cow-russia-dates).

### oq-pre-1801-georgia-date

The exact date of Georgia's annexation needs pinning. Wikipedia
says "1801" but the Russo-Persian War was 1804–1813 and the
Treaty of Georgievsk was 1783. The CSV says "Georgia (1801)".
The annexation decree was issued by Alexander I on 12 September
1801. Needs a source with the exact date.

### oq-finnish-war-1809

The Treaty of Fredrikshamn (17 September 1809) ceded Finland to
Russia. This is a substantial territorial gain (~300,000 km²)
within the row. Under a strict WHEP rule reading, it could be a
candidate mid-row split. The Swedish CSV chain already splits
at 1809 (`SWE-1800-1809` → `SWE-1809-1814`), recognizing this
as significant from the Swedish side — but the Russian side
does not split. Same asymmetry as Italy 1870 (Papal States
perspective splits, Italian perspective doesn't).

### oq-caucasus-conquest-timeline

The CSV notes say "Caucasus conquest ongoing" but the specific
dates and territorial gains are not pinned. The 1826–1828
Russo-Persian War and 1828–1829 Russo-Turkish War are partially
sourced from Wikipedia but lack km² figures. A Biger or atlas
source would quantify this.
