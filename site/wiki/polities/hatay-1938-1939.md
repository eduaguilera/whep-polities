# Sanjak of Alexandretta / Hatay State

## Summary

This row tracks the **Sanjak of Alexandretta** — subsequently the
**Republic of Hatay (Hatay Devleti)** — from its establishment as an
autonomous entity detached from the French Mandate of Syria
(September 1938) to its annexation by Turkey (23 July 1939).

The row exists because IIA yearbook **footnotes** for 1953 report that
Syrian data **excludes** the Sanjak of Alexandretta / Hatay, noting
it as a territory separated from French Mandate Syria before Syrian
independence. The footnote context is: Syria 1953 data excludes this
territory, which had already been ceded to Turkey in 1939. The
exclusion note signals that data reporters treated it as a distinct
administrative unit from Syria proper, warranting a separate WHEP
entity.

The Sanjak of Alexandretta (Turkish: *İskenderun Sancağı*; Arabic:
*Liwā' al-Iskandariyya*) was a district of French Mandate Syria along
the northeastern Mediterranean coast. Under Franco-Turkish pressure,
France detached the sanjak and granted it autonomous status in
September 1938. Elections held in August 1938 produced a
Turkish-majority assembly. On 2 September 1938 the autonomous state
adopted a constitution. On 29 June 1939 the assembly proclaimed the
independent **Republic of Hatay**. On 23 July 1939 Turkey and France
signed an agreement transferring the territory to Turkey; the Grand
National Assembly formally annexed it on 7 July 1939 (ratified
23 July 1939). The territory became Hatay Province of Turkey.

Predecessor: [syr-1922-1945](syr-1922-1946.md) — the territory was
detached from the French Mandate of Syria.
Successor: [tur-1920-2025](tur-1920-2025.md) — annexed and incorporated
as Hatay Province.

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon source.** No faithful-vintage polygon was found in any
  available WHEP geodata source. Queries of cshapes-2.0
  (`cntry_name` containing "alexandretta", "hatay", "sanjak"),
  cliopatria (`Name`), paine-2024 (`PCS`), and gadm-4.1-adm1
  (Turkey not covered in the subset present) all returned no match.
  CShapes 2.0 records Turkey (gwcode=640) as a single polygon from
  1923-10-14 onward with a constant area of ~779,964 km², without
  encoding the 1939 Hatay annexation as a distinct boundary event.
  CShapes 2.0 records Syria (gwcode=652) with area ~187,993 km² from
  1922 through 1945, also without a distinct Alexandretta feature.
- **Method.** No polygon assigned. The entity's territory is
  approximately 5,400–5,500 km² based on the commonly cited area of
  modern Hatay Province in Turkey (~5,524 km²), but this estimate
  cannot be verified against the available polygon sources and is not
  encoded here.
- **Confidence.** Low: no polygon binding is possible from the
  available sources. This is an honest gap.
- **polygon_status.** `unassigned`.

## Predecessors and successors

- **Predecessor.** [syr-1922-1945](syr-1922-1946.md) — the Sanjak
  of Alexandretta was administered as part of the French Mandate
  of Syria (COW 652, CShapes feature gwcode=652, 1922–1945). France
  detached it in September 1938 under the 1938 Franco-Turkish
  Agreement on the Sanjak.
- **Successor.** [tur-1920-2025](tur-1920-2025.md) — Turkey formally
  annexed the territory on 23 July 1939. It became Hatay Province
  (İl). The TUR-1920-2025 page notes the Hatay annexation (~5,500 km²)
  as an open question (oq-hatay-1939).

## Sourced claims

- **September 1938: Autonomous sanjak established.** France and
  Turkey agreed in July 1938 to detach the sanjak from Syrian
  administration; the autonomous Sanjak of Alexandretta came into
  effect 2 September 1938 with its own constitution.
  [tur-1920-2025 §oq-hatay-1939](tur-1920-2025.md#oq-hatay-1939)
- **29 June 1939: Republic of Hatay proclaimed.** The elected assembly
  proclaimed the Republic of Hatay (Hatay Devleti), briefly
  independent.
- **7 / 23 July 1939: Annexation by Turkey.** The Grand National
  Assembly voted to annex Hatay on 7 July 1939; the Franco-Turkish
  agreement ratifying the transfer was signed 23 July 1939.
  [tur-1920-2025 §sourced-claims](tur-1920-2025.md#sourced-claims)
- **IIA 1953 footnote.** The IIA yearbook for 1953 reports Syrian
  data with a footnote noting the exclusion of the Sanjak of
  Alexandretta / Hatay, recognizing it as a distinct unit not
  included in Syrian statistics. This is the primary driver for
  creating a WHEP row for this entity.
- **CShapes 2.0 Syria feature.** gwcode=652, area=187,993 km²,
  1922-12-02 to 1945-12-31. No distinct Alexandretta sub-feature.
  [cshapes-2.0]
- **CShapes 2.0 Turkey feature.** gwcode=640, area=779,964 km²,
  1923-10-14 to 2019-12-31. No distinct Hatay sub-feature.
  [cshapes-2.0]

## Contradictions

- The CShapes 2.0 Syria polygon for 1922–1945 (area ~187,993 km²)
  apparently does not exclude the Sanjak of Alexandretta even though
  the territory was detached from Syrian administration in 1938–1939.
  This may mean the CShapes polygon represents pre-separation borders,
  or that the dataset did not encode the Alexandretta cession as a
  boundary event. Without a separate pre/post-1938 Syria feature in
  CShapes, the boundary change cannot be verified from this source
  alone.

## Decisions

- **polity_code.** Assigned `HATAY-1938-1939`. The code uses "HATAY"
  as the entity was known as the Hatay State at the point of its
  brief independence, and the period 1938–1939 covers from autonomous
  detachment to annexation. An alternative would be `ALX-1938-1939`
  (Alexandretta) but "HATAY" is the name used in Turkey and in the
  English-language historiography for this entity.
- **start_year = 1938.** The autonomous constitution came into effect
  2 September 1938, marking the formal separation from French Mandate
  Syria administration.
- **end_year = 1939.** Turkey formally annexed the territory in
  July 1939.
- **type = colonial.** The entity existed as an internationally
  administered transitional zone (French mandate territory under
  Franco-Turkish agreement) before annexation; "colonial" captures
  this better than "national" (it was not a recognized sovereign
  state in the COW sense) though neither fits perfectly.
- **iso3 = TUR.** The territory is part of modern Turkey; no
  separate ISO 3166 code exists for historical Hatay/Alexandretta.
- **cow = null.** Neither COW nor GW assigns a separate state code
  to the Sanjak of Alexandretta or Republic of Hatay. It is not
  in the COW state system.
- **polygon_status = unassigned.** No faithful polygon found in
  cshapes-2.0, cliopatria, paine-2024, or gadm-4.1-adm1. This is
  an accepted WHEP pattern; the honest gap is preferable to a
  fabricated binding.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-construction

A construction estimate for Hatay Province could be derived by
differencing the CShapes Syria 1922–1938 polygon from Syria's
post-1939 extent, or by using the modern Hatay Province boundary from
a full Turkey GADM layer. The full GADM dataset (not just the
subset in this repo) includes a Hatay Province polygon. If the build
pipeline is extended to use a full GADM Turkey layer, this polygon
could be bound here with `polygon_status: assigned` and
`polygon_feature_id: TUR.21_1` (or similar). This is an open
engineering task.

### oq-type-classification

The `type` field "colonial" is the closest available fit but imprecise.
The Hatay State was a briefly independent republic (29 June – 7 July
1939), making it arguably "national" for those 9 days. A dedicated
type value such as "transitional" or "mandate" would be more accurate.
Review with WHEP type taxonomy.

### oq-cshapes-syria-area-discrepancy

CShapes 2.0 records the same Syria area (187,993 km²) from 1922 to
1945 without any reduction corresponding to the 1938–1939 Alexandretta
separation. This is either an error in CShapes or a deliberate choice
(recording de jure borders). The SYR-1922-1946 page should note that
its polygon may over-represent Syria's territory for 1938–1939.
