# United States (original)

## Summary

This row tracks the United States from the **1800** database floor
to **1803** — a **4-year row** [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).
It covers the original USA territory east of the Mississippi River:
the 13 original states plus early admitted states and territories
(Ohio, Kentucky, Tennessee, Vermont). The CSV notes estimate
~1.2M km2 [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

The end-of-row event is the **Louisiana Purchase (1803)** from
France, which approximately doubled US territory by adding ~2.1M km2
west of the Mississippi. This is a massive territorial change that
fully justifies the split under the WHEP polity-definition rule
[log decision-whep-polity-definition](../log.md#decision-whep-polity-definition).

Predecessor: NA (database floor). Successor:
[usa-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Polygon (since 2026-09-24).** `constructed/USA-1800-1803`: CShapes 2.0
  gwcode 2's 1886-1959 step (the contiguous United States, US share of the
  Great Lakes included) **minus** everything west of the Mississippi,
  Florida, and Alabama and Mississippi south of 31°N — the 1783 Treaty of
  Paris territory as fixed at 31°N by Pinckney's Treaty (1795). It measures
  **2,322,628 km²** (EPSG:6933), 2,165,716 km² without the 156,912 km² of
  Great Lakes water. `polygon_status: assigned`, not `proxy`: nothing changed
  between 1795 and 1803, so this is the whole row's extent. See
  [decision-polygon-constructed-1783-territory](#decision-polygon-constructed-1783-territory).
  The Cliopatria description below is the row's previous binding, kept as
  written.
- **Polygon source.** `Cliopatria (United States of America
  1800-1802)` per the CSV
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). A single
  Cliopatria time-step covering 1800–1802, applied across the
  full 4-year row. Given the row is only 4 years and the polygon
  time-step aligns closely, this is a good temporal fit.
- **Coverage.** Original 13 states plus Vermont (1791), Kentucky
  (1792), Tennessee (1796), and Ohio (admitted 1803). Territory
  east of the Mississippi, south of the Great Lakes, north of
  Spanish Florida. Approximate area ~1.2M km2
  [database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv).

### decision-polygon-constructed-1783-territory

**`polygon_source` moved `cliopatria` (`United States of America`, 1800) ->
`constructed` (`USA-1800-1803`) on 2026-09-24.** Written up by Claude (Claude
Code); open for review in the pull request that made it.

**Why.** Issue 655's follow-up (PR 671) found the next-but-one row,
[USA-1848-1867](usa-1848-1867.md), omitting about 4.37M km² of the
trans-Mississippi West, because Cliopatria draws the United States by
settler-frontier extent rather than by sovereign territory. The same audit
applied here: Cliopatria's 1800-1802 step measures **1,184,595 km²**, about half
of the territory the United States held under the 1783 treaty — it leaves out
most of the Northwest Territory and the Mississippi Territory, which were
federal territory, not foreign ground. The page's own "~1.2M km²" was read off
that polygon.

**How it is built** (`scripts/sources/constructed/build.py`,
`build_usa_1800_1803`). The contiguous US minus later acquisitions, so that the
row keeps the Great Lakes water every CShapes US row carries and nests exactly
inside [USA-1803-1848](usa-1803-1848.md) and [USA-1848-1867](usa-1848-1867.md):

| removed from the contiguous US | line used | source |
|---|---|---|
| every state wholly west of the Mississippi (Louisiana Purchase and later) | state lines along the river | [GADM 4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md) adm1 |
| Minnesota west of the Mississippi | the river, closed due north from its source to the border (the 1783 "due west" line from the Lake of the Woods is impossible as written) | Natural Earth 10m centreline, shipped inside [Paine et al. 2024](../sources/paine-et-al-2024.md) |
| Florida, and Alabama/Mississippi south of 31°N (Spanish West Florida until 1810-1813) | the 31st parallel, which is also the Alabama-Florida line | GADM 4.1 adm1 + parallel |

Minnesota east of the river is 83,330 of the state's 225,350 km².

**Measured against the census.** *Historical Statistics of the United States*
series J 1-2 ([Census Bureau 1975, ch. J](https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/1975/compendia/hist_stats_colonial-1970/hist_stats_colonial-1970p1-chJ.pdf))
gives 888,811 sq mi (2,302,010 km²) for 1790 and 1800, land and inland water,
Great Lakes excluded (its 1960 water column, 52,747 sq mi for all 48 states, is
smaller than the 156,912 km² = 60,584 sq mi of US Great Lakes water measured here). That figure includes the Red River of the North basin
south of 49°N (a footnote; series J 3-7 gives the basin as 29,602 thousand acres
= 119,795 km²), which this row leaves out because it was Hudson's Bay Company
land until the 1818 Convention.

| | km² |
|---|---|
| polygon | 2,322,628 |
| polygon without Great Lakes water | 2,165,716 |
| census 1790-1800, minus the Red River basin | 2,182,215 |
| residual | **-0.76%** |
| of which: CShapes' own offset for the whole contiguous US (7,783,059 without lakes vs census 1860 7,827,946) | -0.57% |

About -0.2% is left for the Minnesota line and the 31°N strip.

**Approximations, stated.** Maine carries its post-1842 (Webster-Ashburton)
line; before 1842 the US claimed more. The Yazoo strip between 31°N and 32°28'
is counted as American throughout, which is what Pinckney's Treaty confirmed in
1795. Georgia's western lands are included (federal after the 1802 cession, US
territory either way).

## Open questions

### oq-cow-pre-1816

COW coverage begins 1816-01-01. This row (1800–1803) and its
successor (1803–1848) both predate or partially predate COW entry.
The CSV assigns `cow_code = NA` for the first four USA rows
(1800–1959) — only USA-1959-2025 has `cow_code = 2`
[database](../../data/final/polities_database.csv). The correct
COW code for all five rows is 2 (continuous from 1816). The NA on
pre-1959 rows is likely an omission from the pipeline.
