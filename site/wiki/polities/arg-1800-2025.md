## Why this row was retired

The ARG-1800-2025 row was a collapsed single entry covering 225 years
with a single CShapes 1902 polygon. This created polygon_vintage_drift
for all data years before 1902, and suppressed the three distinct
territorial periods separated by:

1. **1881 Treaty of Limits** — Andean watershed boundary with Chile
   (Patagonia).
2. **1899 Puna de Atacama arbitration** — Northern Andean boundary
   adjustment (formerly Bolivian/Chilean Puna).
3. **1902 British arbitral award + General Arbitration Treaty** —
   Final resolution of Andean and Patagonian archipelago disputes;
   the stable boundary to present.

The IIA alias (`argentina → ARG-1800-2025`) has been retargeted to
ARG-1902-2025. Year-ranged Mitchell aliases route pre-1902 data to
ARG-1800-1899 and ARG-1899-1902 respectively.
