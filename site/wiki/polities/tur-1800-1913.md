# Turkey (Anatolia, to 1913)

## Summary

This entry tracks "Turkey" as reported in Mitchell's Historical
Statistics and FAOSTAT for present-day borders retroactively. Pamuk
confirms that these sources report Turkey for modern Anatolian borders.
Previously routed to OTT (Ottoman Empire), which attributed Anatolian
agricultural data to an empire spanning Arabia, the Levant, and North
Africa. 30 rows, 1880-1912.
