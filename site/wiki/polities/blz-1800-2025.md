# Belize (to 1886)

## Summary

Pre-CShapes coverage row for Belize (formerly British Honduras), a Central American territory on the Caribbean coast. This row covers the period from 1800 (WHEP temporal boundary) to 1886, when CShapes polygon coverage begins. Belize was a British settlement that became a formal colony (British Honduras) in 1862. Biger 1995 shows Belize on the regional map of Central America, noting that in 1821 the Central American colonies separated from Spain and in 1823 Honduras, Guatemala, Nicaragua and Belize combined to form the United Provinces of Central America [biger-1995 &sect;belize](../sources/biger-1995.md#belize).

## Territorial extent

Belize's territory has remained constant at approximately 22,966 sq. km. The polygon source is CShapes 2.0 (back-projected). Guatemala has maintained a longstanding territorial claim to Belize, but the boundaries reflect the internationally recognized borders.
