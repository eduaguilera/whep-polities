# Channel Islands (Bailiwicks of Jersey and Guernsey)

## Summary

The Channel Islands off the Normandy coast: the Bailiwick of Jersey and the Bailiwick of Guernsey
(Guernsey with Alderney, Sark, Herm and the smaller islands). Both are Crown Dependencies, and they
"are not part of the United Kingdom".
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25 §channel-islands](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-2-2026-09-25.md#channel-islands)

One row for 1800-2025, typed `territory`: the islands' ground and their constitutional position did
not change in the period (the German occupation of 1940-1945 moved no border). `iso3` is `NA`
because the unit spans two ISO codes, JEY and GGY.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `United Kingdom Channel Islands` on its own: population 94 thousand (1937) and 103
thousand (1951), and a 1939 land-use table whose `use total` is 19 thousand ha, i.e. **190 km²**.
**6 rows** resolved to no polity, and they could not honestly go to the United Kingdom: the islands
lie **outside** the polygon of [gbr-1921-2025](gbr-1921-2025.md) (CShapes 200 does not draw them;
St Helier and St Peter Port fall in no polygon at all), and they are not part of the United Kingdom,
so an alias to GBR would have filed two Crown dependencies' figures as the UK's.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status: `assigned`.** `constructed`: GADM 4.1 adm0 JEY (125 km²) ∪ GGY (89 km²) =
**214 km²** measured (ESRI:54034). FAO's own `use total` is 190 km² (published through `source_stated_area_basis.csv`); the 12% gap is
GADM's coastline, which includes foreshore and islets that land statistics leave out.
[gadm-4.1 §what-it-adds](../sources/gadm-4.1.md#what-it-adds)

GADM's JEY and GGY were not in the repository's extract; they were added to
`scripts/sources/gadm-4.1/fetch.sh` for this row, appended last so no existing feature moved.
