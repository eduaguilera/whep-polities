# Russian Empire (1905-1914)

## Summary

This row covers **10 years** of the post-Russo-Japanese War
Russian Empire. The Treaty of Portsmouth (5 September 1905) cost
Russia South Sakhalin. Domestically, the 1905 Revolution forced
Tsar Nicholas II to issue the October Manifesto, establishing
the State Duma. The Stolypin reforms followed. This is the
pre-WWI period of the Russian Empire.

Predecessor: [f228-1856-1905](f228-1856-1905.md).
Successor: [f228-1914-1917](f228-1914-1917.md).

## Territorial extent

- **Treaty of Portsmouth (5 September 1905)** — Russia ceded
  South Sakhalin (~36,000 km²) and the Kwantung leased territory
  (~3,500 km²) to Japan
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1905](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1905).
  This is the start-of-row territorial event.
- **No major territorial changes 1905-1914.** The Russian Empire
  remained largely stable in territory during this decade. The
  major events were domestic (1905 Revolution, Stolypin reforms,
  Duma politics).

## Sourced claims

- **1905-09-05: Treaty of Portsmouth.** End of the Russo-Japanese
  War. Russia lost South Sakhalin and the Kwantung lease
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1905](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1905).
- **1905: October Manifesto and 1905 Revolution.** The Tsar
  conceded a constitution and a legislative Duma. Regime change
  from autocracy to constitutional monarchy, but not a
  territorial event
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11 §1905-revolution](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md#1905-revolution).
- **1906-1911: Stolypin reforms.** Agricultural reform program
  aimed at creating a class of independent farmers. Stolypin
  assassinated September 1911
  [wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11](../sources/wikipedia-russian-empire-2026-04-11.md).
