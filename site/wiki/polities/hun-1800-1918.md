# Kingdom of Hungary (Transleithania)

## Summary

Tracks the Kingdom of Hungary from **1800** to **1918**. Initially within the Austrian Empire, then from 1867 the Transleithanian half of Austria-Hungary under the Ausgleich. The polity dissolved with the collapse of Austria-Hungary at the end of World War I [wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16](../sources/wikipedia-late-colonial-2026-04-16.md).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon:** Assigned from **HistoGIS 1860 (ACDH-CH)** — dissolved from 5 Transleithanian crownlands. File: `data/geodata/histogis/habsburg_cisleithania_transleithania.gpkg` (layer `habsburg_halves`, feature `HUN-1800-1918`).

**Crownlands included:** Kingdom of Hungary (Königreich Ungarn), Grand Principality of Transylvania (Großfürstentum Siebenbürgen, merged into Hungary 1867), Kingdom of Croatia-Slavonia (Königreich Kroatien und Slawonien), Voivodeship of Serbia and Banat of Temeschwar (Woiwodschaft Serbien und Temeser Banat, abolished 1860 → merged into Hungary), Military Frontier (Militärgrenze, dissolved 1881 → merged into Croatia-Slavonia).

**Source caveats:** Boundaries digitized from 1860 Austrian Empire maps. The Voivodeship of Serbia appears as a separate unit in the 1860 dataset (was being dissolved that year) but is properly assigned to Transleithania for historical completeness. License: open access (ACDH-CH, Austrian Academy of Sciences). See [HistoGIS SHP samples](https://histogis.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/shp-samples).

**Why the post-1918 polygon was not used:** Post-Trianon Hungary ([HUN-1918-1919](hun-1918-1919.md), ~93,000 km²) lost ~71% of Transleithanian territory at the Treaty of Trianon (1920) — Slovakia, Transylvania, Croatia-Slavonia, Vojvodina, Carpathian Ruthenia all went to successor states.

**Why this entry exists:** The pre-1961 input data reports "Hungary" separately from "Austria" throughout 1851-1917. Hungary had its own parliament (Diet/Országgyűlés), its own agricultural administration, and its own statistical office. Previously, the pipeline merged Hungarian data into the Austria-Hungary polygon.
