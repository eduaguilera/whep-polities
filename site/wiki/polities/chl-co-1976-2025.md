# Coquimbo (region of Chile)

## Summary

Coquimbo is the fourth region of Chile (Región de Coquimbo, Región IV), a semi-arid territory in the country's Norte Chico spanning roughly from 29°S to 32°S, centered on the Elqui, Limarí and Choapa river valleys. It was created in its present form in 1976 when Decreto Ley 575 replaced Chile's older provincial structure with numbered regions; before that date the area existed as the province of Coquimbo within a different administrative grouping but was not a standalone first-order statistical unit in the sense the modern regional series use. This entry is `type: subnational` because Chile's sovereignty over the area is continuous and undisputed throughout 1976-2025 — the entry carries a sub-national reporting territory, not a competitor to the national CHL row in the matcher's sovereignty ranking. Coquimbo's economy centers on mining (notably copper and iron), Pisco grape/wine production in the Elqui valley, and increasingly astronomical observatories (La Silla, Cerro Tololo) that exploit the region's clear desert skies. end_year is set to 2025 (exclusive) because the region is still current and the database's open convention closes all live polities at that year.

**Why this entry exists.** The routing decision (unit_id CHL-CO, admin_name "Coquimbo") found source data reported at the Chilean-region level for Coquimbo with no existing polity able to receive it: the only CHL rows in the table are whole-country national totals (CHL-1810-1884, CHL-1884-1899, CHL-1899-1902, CHL-1902-2025), which are containers for this territory, not substitutes for it — routing regional figures to the national row would recreate the exact container-claiming conflict already rejected for CHL-LI (every sibling region could equally claim the national row). Under this country's convention, Chilean regions default to a 1976 start year (the date DL 575 became operational) unless a unit-specific departure is documented; Coquimbo is not one of the three later-split departures (Arica y Parinacota, Los Ríos, Ñuble), so no departure applies and the 1976 default is used, matching the pattern already established for sibling regions CHL-AI, CHL-AN, CHL-AT and CHL-BI. This is a newly created polity rather than a re-span of an existing one, since CHL-1902-2025 must remain intact as the national container for all sibling regions. The harness's retry note recorded that 76 of this unit's 124 data years (61%) were being marked `unroutable` for years before 1976, which is the wrong disposition for years where a source is actively reporting values — those pre-1976 years belong to whatever earlier administrative unit covered this territory (the historical Coquimbo province), not to no polity at all, and are flagged below as an open question rather than silently absorbed by this entry.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon is attached to this entry today, but a registered source can supply one and has not yet been fetched. `polygon_source` is set to `gadm-4.1-adm1` — the source is registered (GADM adm1 is the standing source for first-level administrative regions worldwide, licence-known, free for non-commercial use) but the copy of that dataset currently present in this repository is an 81-country curated subset that excludes Chile entirely (0 matching features for `GID_0 == "CHL"`). This is a `registered_source_unfetched` route, not `new_source_needed`: the fix is to replace the local file with the full global GADM 4.1 release, not to register a new source. `polygon_status` is accordingly `unassigned` rather than `proxy` or `estimate`, since no geometry — proxy or otherwise — has been attached.

**Territory description:** Coquimbo occupies Chile's Norte Chico, bordered by the Atacama region to the north, Valparaíso and Argentina to the south and east respectively, and the Pacific to the west — roughly the area a reader would locate between 29°S and 32°S on a modern map of Chile, encompassing the provinces of Elqui, Limarí and Choapa. The region's officially published area is approximately 40,580 km². No km² figure is stated here as a measurement of the (currently absent) attached geometry, since none is attached; the 40,580 km² figure is the region's own officially published area, not derived from any polygon in this repository.

## Open questions

### oq-pre1976-coquimbo-coverage

**What polity should carry pre-1976 Coquimbo data years?**

The harness reported that 76 of 124 data years for this unit (61%) fall before 1976 and were being marked unroutable, even though the source reports values for those years, implying the territory existed and was being measured under an earlier administrative name (likely the historical Coquimbo province, part of a larger administrative agrupación before DL 575). This entry deliberately does not extend backward to absorb those years, since doing so would misstate its span_basis (tied specifically to the 1976 DL 575 reform) and would need its own confirmation of what unit and boundary applied pre-1976. A follow-on predecessor polity, or an extension of this one with a corrected span_basis, should be created once the pre-1976 administrative history and territory are confirmed.

### oq-gadm-vintage-check

**Does the modern GADM Coquimbo boundary match the region's boundary across 1976-2025?**

Once gadm-4.1-adm1 is fetched globally and a feature is attached, the geometry will reflect present-day administrative boundaries. Coquimbo's boundary has been broadly stable since 1976 with no major reported splits, but this has not been verified against Chilean regional boundary legislation for the full 1976-2025 span (e.g., any commune-level reassignments between Coquimbo and neighboring Atacama or Valparaíso regions), so the eventual polygon_status of 'assigned' should not be applied without that check.

### oq-sibling-consistency-check

**Is the routing pattern used here consistent with all other Chilean regional siblings once they are all created?**

This entry follows the same code pattern, 1976 start-year default, container basis and polygon route documented for CHL-AI, CHL-AN, CHL-AT and CHL-BI. As more Chilean region siblings are processed (the routing_concerns note references CHL-LI's rejected national-row coverage as precedent), it is worth a later cross-check that all of them ended up with the same conventions and none silently diverged (e.g., a different subunit-abbreviation scheme or a different container start year).
