# Calabria (region of Italy)

## Summary

Calabria is one of Italy's twenty first-level administrative regions (regioni), occupying the southwestern "toe" of the Italian peninsula, bordered by Basilicata to the north and surrounded on the other three sides by the Tyrrhenian and Ionian Seas, with the Strait of Messina separating it from Sicily. Its capital is Catanzaro. This entry is `type: subnational` because Calabria has never been a sovereign state in this period: it was part of the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies until annexed to the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont in 1860-61 and has been an administrative subdivision of the unified Kingdom of Italy, then the Republic, ever since. The entry exists to carry a *reporting territory* distinct from the national ITA rows: NUTS2-level regional statistical series (agricultural production, population, and similar data) report Calabria specifically, a rural, historically poorer southern region very different in economic profile from national Italy-wide totals or from the industrialized north (e.g. Lombardia), so routing this data to the national ITA-1919-2025 row would misattribute a regional figure to the whole country. No prior polity row represented Calabria on its own; the routed data would otherwise have fallen to the national chain, which contains but is not this region. Calabria is a standard region case (unlike Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia, annexed in 1919, or Molise, split from Abruzzo in 1963), so per the country convention's default rule its start_year is set to the earliest national container's start, 1861, exactly as already done for the sibling entry ITA-LOM-1861-2025.

**Why this entry exists.** Input data: a regional-level statistical series for Calabria (NUTS2 code ITF6, per the routing decision's admin_name field), routed under the country convention for Italy's subnational regions (pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json), following the same pattern already applied to eleven sibling NUTS2 units (ITC1, ITC3, ITF1, ITF3, ITF4, ITF5, ITG1, ITG2, ITH1, ITH3, and the just-created ITA-LOM). Previously, absent a polity row for Calabria, this data had no distinct home other than the national ITA rows (ITA-1861-1866 through ITA-1919-2025) — which is wrong because those rows represent the whole of Italy (roughly 20x Calabria's area at any given date) and mixing a regional series into a national row would either overstate national totals if double-counted, or misattribute a distinctly regional figure (e.g. Calabria's agricultural output or population, historically among Italy's poorest and most agrarian) to the nation as a whole. What confirms Calabria is a distinct reporting entity rather than an artefact of routing convenience: it is Eurostat's own standard NUTS2 statistical division of Italy, used because Italy's own official statistics (ISTAT) report at the regional level as a first-class unit, separate from national totals — the same reasoning already applied to its NUTS2 siblings including Lombardia.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** Not yet assigned. No polygon available in the GeoPackage for this period. The only registered source type that digitises country subdivisions at Calabria's level (ADM1/NUTS2) is `gadm-4.1-adm1`, but the copy of that source present in this repository is an explicit curated subset of 81 countries that does not include Italy at all (zero ITA features). No other registered source (cshapes, cshapes-europe, paine-2024, cliopatria, histogis-1860-habsburg, constructed, reporting-areas) covers modern Italian regional boundaries. A correct polygon almost certainly exists in GADM's full global release (a superset of the local subset), but it has not been fetched here, so `polygon_source` is set to the registered slug `gadm-4.1-adm1` per the registered_source_unfetched route, and `polygon_status` is `unassigned` rather than `assigned`, exactly as done for the sibling ITA-LOM-1861-2025 and ITA-ABR/ITA-LIG/ITA-PIE entries. See open question oq-polygon-fetch-needed-cal.

**Territory description:** Calabria covers approximately 15,080 km2, making it about 5% of Italy's total land area — one of the smaller and more elongated regions, forming the peninsula's toe between the Tyrrhenian Sea (west) and Ionian Sea (east). A modern-map reader can locate it as the narrow, mountainous strip of southern Italy south of Basilicata, terminating at the Strait of Messina opposite Sicily; its main cities are Catanzaro (capital), Reggio Calabria, and Cosenza. The region includes the Sila and Aspromonte mountain massifs and a long coastline on both seas. The region's own outer boundary has been stable since unification in 1861; administrative reorganizations in this period (such as the 1992 creation of the province of Vibo Valentia out of Catanzaro province) redrew *provincial* boundaries within Calabria, not the region's own outer boundary, so a single present-day regional outline should be a reasonably safe proxy back to 1861 once a source is fetched — a vintage-drift caveat that belongs on that future polygon assignment, not a reason to fabricate one now. No area figure here is measured from an attached geometry, since no polygon is yet assigned; the 15,080 km2 figure is Calabria's well-documented modern administrative area, cited as territory context, not a polygon measurement.

## Predecessors and successors

No predecessor: this is the first polity row created for Calabria specifically, and it starts exactly at the region's incorporation into the unified Kingdom of Italy in 1861 (Calabria having been part of the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies annexed by Piedmont-Sardinia in 1860), so there is no distinct prior polity to chain from. No successor: the end_year of 2025 is open, matching the still-current status of Calabria as a first-level administrative region of the Italian Republic; if a future administrative reorganization abolished or redrew Calabria, a successor row would be added then. The four container edges (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919, ITA-1919-2025) tile the whole span without gap or overlap, reflecting that Calabria's own territory was unaffected by any of the national-level changes (Veneto 1866, Rome 1870, WWI settlement 1919) that motivate those national row boundaries.

## Sourced claims

- Calabria is one of Italy's twenty regioni and corresponds to the Eurostat NUTS2 statistical unit ITF6, per the routing decision's admin_name field.
- Calabria (the 'toe' of the Italian peninsula) was part of the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies until annexed by the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont in 1860-61 and incorporated into the newly proclaimed Kingdom of Italy in 1861, which is why the region's polity span begins in 1861 rather than at a later national milestone.
- Calabria's region-level (NUTS2) outline has not changed since unification, though its internal provincial boundaries have (e.g. the province of Vibo Valentia was created out of Catanzaro province in 1992), a distinction consistent with the reasoning already applied to Lombardia (ITA-LOM-1861-2025).

## Decisions

### d-code-subunit-cal

**Code uses ITA-CAL, not the source's NUTS id ITF6**

The routing decision names this unit by its Eurostat NUTS2 code ITF6, but NUTS codes are a statistical classification, not a name a reader of this wiki would recognize, and they have been reassigned across NUTS revisions. Following the dominant 57-of-66 pattern <ISO3>-<SUBUNIT>-<start>-<end>, the subunit token is set to CAL, a readable abbreviation of the region's own name (Calabria), matching the precedent already set for the sibling entry ITA-LOM-1861-2025 (LOM for Lombardia) rather than reusing a codelist id that changes underneath the reader. iso3 stays ITA per the dominant pattern; polity_code is therefore ITA-CAL-1861-2025.

### d-start-year-1861-cal

**start_year set to 1861 per the country's default rule, matching the routing decision and the Lombardia precedent**

The routing decision explicitly states that Calabria is not one of the two enumerated departure cases (Trentino-Alto Adige/Friuli-Venezia Giulia annexed 1919, Molise split from Abruzzo in 1963), so the country convention's default rule sets start_year to 1861, the start of the earliest container national row (ITA-1861-1866) whose territory includes Calabria. This matches how ITA-LOM-1861-2025 was already created under the same convention. The routing decision's own segmentation note (back_cast pre-1919, proposed 1919-2023) is not carried into two separate rows here, for the same reason given for Lombardia: this is a single continuous polity from 1861, and splitting it would only re-litigate a disposition the convention already resolves as one span.

### d-container-tiling-cal

**Container edges use the four fine-grained ITA eras, not the coarse ITA-1861-1919 aggregate**

As with ITA-LOM-1861-2025, the polity table carries both a fine partition of the Kingdom of Italy (ITA-1861-1866, ITA-1866-1870, ITA-1870-1919) and a coarse aggregate row (ITA-1861-1919) covering the same span. Using the aggregate would double-count the interval against the fine rows if both are ever queried together. The four container edges here (1861-1866, 1866-1870, 1870-1919, 1919-2025) tile 1861-2025 with no gap and no overlap using the finer, non-aggregate rows, consistent with the sibling entry's precedent.

### d-polygon-route-unfetched-cal

**polygon_source is gadm-4.1-adm1 (the registered slug), not a route name, with polygon_status unassigned**

The routing decision's polygon_route is registered_source_unfetched: gadm-4.1-adm1 is registered in sources.yaml with id_column GID_1 and is the correct mechanism for a modern Italian ADM1 region, but the locally present gadm-4.1-adm1.gpkg is a curated 81-country subset that excludes Italy (0 ITA features). Per the harness rule that registered_source_unfetched means the slug IS the source and polygon_status is unassigned, this entry sets polygon_source: gadm-4.1-adm1 (not 'new_source_needed' or 'registered_source_unfetched' as a literal string) and polygon_status: unassigned, with polygon_feature_id left null until the full/global GADM 4.1 ADM1 release is fetched and Calabria's feature (likely ITA.6_1 or similar) identified.

## Open questions

### oq-polygon-fetch-needed-cal

**No fetched polygon source actually covers Calabria yet**

The locally present gadm-4.1-adm1 file is an explicit 81-country subset that excludes Italy entirely (0 features for ITA), and no other registered source in sources.yaml digitises Italian ADM1 boundaries; cshapes/cshapes-europe are country-level only. The fix is to fetch the full/global GADM 4.1 ADM1 release (a superset of the local subset) and identify Calabria's feature id within it, likely ITA.6_1 given GADM's typical alphabetical/positional numbering of Italian regions, but this has not been confirmed. Until fetched, polygon_feature_id stays null and polygon_status stays unassigned. This mirrors the identical open question already recorded on ITA-LOM-1861-2025 and on the other Italian region siblings (ITA-ABR, ITA-LIG, ITA-PIE), so a single future fetch of the global GADM layer would likely resolve all of them at once rather than one at a time.

### oq-sibling-segmentation-conflict-cal

**This entry's single 1861-2025 span may conflict with how other Calabria-adjacent or earlier-processed Italian regions were routed**

The routing decision itself flags that the proposed coverage segmentation (back_cast for pre-1919 years, proposed for 1919-2023) could equally have been split into two rows mirroring an ITC1 sibling pattern, but was instead kept as a single continuous 1861-2025 polity following the ITA-LOM-1861-2025 precedent. If earlier-processed siblings such as ITA-ABR-1970-2025 or ITA-PIE-1970-2025 (both starting 1970, not 1861) reflect an intentional narrower reading of the country convention rather than a routing-verdict inconsistency, then this entry's 1861 start is the outlier and should be checked against pipelines/agent-harness/state/country_conventions.json before more Italian regions are processed. This is the same unresolved tension already logged on ITA-LOM-1861-2025 as oq-sibling-precedent-conflict, now recurring on a second region.

### oq-molise-departure-boundary-cal

**Calabria's own boundary vintage relative to the 1948 region-formalization and any 20th-century provincial reshuffles is unchecked**

Calabria's provinces have changed over the 20th century (e.g. the province of Vibo Valentia was carved out of Catanzaro province only in 1992), but these are sub-region (provincial) boundary changes, not changes to the region's own outer boundary, which is assumed stable since unification by analogy with Lombardia. This assumption has not been independently verified for Calabria specifically (e.g. against ISTAT's historical administrative maps), and should be checked once a polygon source is fetched, in case Calabria's outer boundary saw any 19th- or early-20th-century adjustment that would make the modern GADM outline an imperfect proxy for the 1861-1948 span.
