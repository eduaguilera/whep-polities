# Madura (Dutch East Indies)

## Summary

Madura, "an Indonesian island off the northeastern coast of Java", whose administered area includes "various smaller islands to the east, southeast and north that are part of Madura's easternmost Sumenep Regency". [wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30 §madura](../sources/wikipedia-whep-normalize-2026-09-30.md#madura)

## Why this entry exists

The label `dutch madura` reports it alone for 1909-1948 (**46 (year, label) pairs**), beside `dutch east indies` and its other islands. No polity was this island before 1949; the 1949-1951 rows (IDN-JVM, IDN-BLB, IDN-OTH) group islands differently and begin in 1949. The label list is described in [whep-normalize](../sources/whep-normalize.md).
