# Tangier Zone (International Zone from 1925)

## Summary

The city of Tangier and its surroundings on the Strait of Gibraltar, set apart from both Moroccan
protectorates. The Franco-Spanish convention of 1912 that divided Morocco "stipulated that Tangier
would be granted a special status and defined its geographical boundaries" (Article 7); the
international administration itself "eventually started on 1 June 1925", and the zone lasted "until
its reintegration into independent Morocco in 1956, with interruption during the Spanish occupation
of Tangier (1940–1945)". It is usually given as **382 km²**.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §tangier](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#tangier)

Typed `city-territory`: a small territory around one city, under neither protectorate.

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports `Tangier` on its own: population 95 thousand (1937) and 172 thousand (1951), and a
1947 land-use table (`use total` and `use builton wasteland`, both **35 thousand ha = 350 km²**).
**4 rows** routed to no polity. Beside them, FAO reports `Spanish Morocco` and `French Morocco`
separately, so Tangier is in neither; and [smo-1912-1956](smo-1912-1956.md)'s CShapes polygon held the
zone, which is why that row's polygon is corrected in the same change.

the east), but on a coast so coarse that 38 of the 47 km² of the city commune fall outside it. The
city is the zone's core, so its communes are added. The result is **0.85x** the quoted 382 km² and
**0.92x** FAO's own 350 km² land total. `proxy` because the inland line is Cliopatria's
reconstruction; the commune set alone (Tanger + Charf + Bni Makada + Boukhalef = 293 km²) would be a
different guess of similar size, and nothing fetched draws the 1923 line itself.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1912 Tangier was part of the Sultanate (`MOR-1904-1911`'s ground); in 1956 it
rejoined independent Morocco, whose rows are the French-zone chain [mar-1911-1958](mar-1911-1958.md)
onward.

## Data routing

## Decisions

- **Span from 1912, not 1925.** The 1912 convention both defined the zone's limits and kept it out of
  the Spanish zone, so from 1912 there is no row whose territory includes it except this one. The
  1923 Paris Convention and its 1925 entry into force changed who governed it, not its ground.
- **The 1940-1945 Spanish occupation is not split out.** Tangier was "effectively annexed to the
  Spanish Protectorate of Morocco on 23 November 1940" until October 1945. Neither this row nor
  [smo-1912-1956](smo-1912-1956.md) has a data row in 1940-1945 (FAO's are 1937, 1947 and 1951; Spanish
  Morocco's 1939 and 1949-1951), so two more splits would move nothing. It is recorded here instead.
