# Aden Colony (State of Aden from 1963)

## Summary

The port of Aden and the islands administered with it, a British Crown colony "from 1937 to 1963"
that "consisted of the port city of Aden and also included the outlying islands of Kamaran, Perim and
the Khuria Muria archipelago". "Prior to 1937, Aden had been governed as part of British India ... On
1 April 1937, Aden was separated from British India to become a Crown colony". In 1963 it "was
reconstituted as the State of Aden within the newly created Federation of South Arabia", on the same
ground, and British rule ended on 30 November 1967.
[wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §aden-colony](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#aden-colony)

The hinterland, the Aden Protectorate, is [ade-1839-1967](ade-1839-1967.md).

## Why this entry exists

FAO 1952 reports the colony and the protectorate as **separate units in the same tables**:

| label | year | item | value |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Aden Colony` | 1937 | population | 50 thousand |
| `Aden Protectorate` | 1937 | population | 600 thousand |
| `Aden Colony` | 1951 | population | 100 thousand |
| `Aden Protectorate` | 1951 | population | 650 thousand |
| `Aden Colony Inc Perim and Kuria` | 1947 | land total | 21 thousand ha |
| `Aden Protectorate` | 1947 | land total | 29,008 thousand ha |

The five colony rows routed to no polity, while [ade-1839-1967](ade-1839-1967.md)'s polygon, the
PDRY outline, held the colony's town districts, and that page recorded a decision
(dec-ade-colony-merge) that FAO "does not distinguish between colony and protectorate production".
The table above refutes that: FAO does distinguish them. So the colony gets its own row and polygon,
and the protectorate's polygon no longer contains it.

The colony's three local bodies were "the Aden municipality, which covered the town, Tawali, Ma'alla
and Crater, the Township authority of Sheikh Othman and finally Little Aden", which is the mainland
set above. Dar Saad (65.7 km², north of Sheikh Othman) was Lahej territory and stays in the
protectorate. Kamaran is not included: FAO's land-use label names only Perim and Kuria, and Kamaran is
not in FAO's colony total.

**Against FAO's 21 thousand ha (210 km²) for `Aden Colony Inc Perim and Kuria`, the polygon is 1.41x.**
The overstatement is in the mainland: GADM's modern districts include the Khormaksar salt pans and
airport ground and Little Aden's clip is a latitude line, not the colony boundary. Wikipedia's "192
km2" cannot be used as a check, since it says it includes Kamaran, Perim and Kuria Muria (see the
source page's caveat). `proxy`, then: the parts are the colony's, the outlines are modern districts.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1937 the Aden Settlement was part of British India; no row here carries it,
and neither this row nor the protectorate's polygon claims the town before 1937 (the protectorate's
row now excludes it for its whole span). In 1967 the ground passed to South Yemen (no row yet,
`oq-south-yemen-successors` on [ade-1839-1967](ade-1839-1967.md)); the Kuria Muria Islands went to
Muscat and Oman.

## Decisions

- **End 1967, not 1963.** The State of Aden (1963-1967) is the colony's ground under a new name inside
  the Federation, still British; the same convention as [ade-1839-1967](ade-1839-1967.md), whose end
  was moved to 1967 on 2026-09-24.
- **Kuria Muria included** because FAO's own label includes it; the islands were administered "by
  the Governor of Aden until 1953". No polygon in the database contained them before.
  [wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25 §kuria-muria](../sources/wikipedia-geodata-batch-3-2026-09-25.md#kuria-muria)
- **Not registered in `polity_composition.csv`**: the colony and the protectorate are disjoint
  (0 km² shared), and no source routes a whole Aden (colony plus protectorate) row except the one
  dates row discussed above.
