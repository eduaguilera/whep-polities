# Principality of Bulgaria (1878-1885)

## Summary

The autonomous Principality of Bulgaria from the Congress of Berlin (1878) to its union with Eastern
Rumelia in September 1885 [wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07 §bulgaria](../sources/wikipedia-border-eras-2026-10-07.md#bulgaria). Created on 2026-10-07 by splitting the old
`BGR-1878-1913` row at 1885 (issue 686); the later years are [bgr-1885-1913](bgr-1885-1913.md).

**Why this entry exists.** The old row carried the post-unification polygon from 1878, so for
1878-1884 it included Eastern Rumelia, about 35,700 km² that was an autonomous Ottoman province.
The Congress of Berlin created the principality as a vassal state
[wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11 §1878](../sources/wikipedia-ottoman-2026-04-11.md#1878).

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. CShapes-Europe Id 355, the 1879-1885 step, which is the principality
north of the Balkan range without Eastern Rumelia. It measures 57,594 km² (Mollweide). The next step,
1886-1912, measures 93,278 km², so the union added about 35,700 km², in line with the ~35,000 km²
issue 686 cites. CShapes-Europe starts the step in 1879, a year after the Congress of Berlin; the
1878 year of this row uses the same step.

Plovdiv, the capital of Eastern Rumelia, falls inside the 1886 step and outside this one, which is
the check that the right step is bound.
