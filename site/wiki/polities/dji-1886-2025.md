# Djibouti (1900-1977)

## Summary

French Somaliland (later the French Territory of the Afars and Issas), the colonial polity governing the territory of present-day Djibouti, from a boundary change in 1900 until independence on 26 June 1977. "Colonization efforts by the French only began in 1862, continuing until 1900" [biger-1995 djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti). "In 1977 it became the last country in continental Africa to achieve independence" [biger-1995 djibouti](../sources/biger-1995.md#djibouti). Djibouti's strategic value as a port at the entrance to the Red Sea made it an important French and European outpost. CShapes codes it as a French colony (ruler=FRA).
