# Trentino-Alto Adige (region of Italy)

## Summary

The two autonomous provinces of Trento and Bolzano/Bozen taken together: the ground Italy acquired from Austria's crownland of Tyrol south of the Brenner in 1919. It was administered first as Venezia Tridentina, then (from 1927) as the provinces of Trento and Bolzano, and since the 1948 special statute as the region Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol, whose powers passed largely to the two provinces under the 1972 statute. `type: subnational`: the row carries a reporting territory inside Italy.

**Why this entry exists.** The Mediterranean subnational panel ([juan-subnational](../sources/juan-subnational.md)) reports a unit labelled `ITH1`. In NUTS that code is the province of Bolzano alone, and the unit was routed to ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 (7,379 km2). The unit's landuse block says otherwise: the per-year sum of its land-cover classes is a **constant 13,599.41 km2 in every one of its 141 landuse years**. That is Bolzano (7,379) plus Trento (ITA-ITH2-1919-2025, 6,218) = 13,598 in the published table, to 0.01%. The panel has no `ITH2` unit, so Trento's data was riding on Bolzano's row. No existing polity was the two provinces together, so the unit needed this row. Claude created it on 2026-09-24 while correcting the Southern-Europe alias defects.

## Territorial extent

**Polygon status:** assigned. GADM 4.1 adm1 feature ITA.17_1, "Trentino-Alto Adige" ([gadm-4.1](../sources/gadm-4.1.md)): 13,595 km2 in ESRI:54034 against the panel's 13,599.41 (+0.03%). Its two adm2 children are the features the Bolzano and Trento rows already bind (ITA.17.1_1, ITA.17.2_1), so this row and its parts come from one source.

**Historical extent.** The polygon is the modern region. The 1919-1923 extent was somewhat larger: in 1923 Cortina d'Ampezzo, Livinallongo and Colle Santa Lucia were moved to the province of Belluno (Veneto), a few hundred km2 that the modern region no longer holds. The panel's own land total is constant across 1880-2023, so the compiler reports on the modern extent throughout, and the polygon matches what the data describes rather than the 1919-1923 administration.

## Predecessors and successors

None recorded. Before 1919 the territory was part of Austria's crownland of Tyrol, which is out of scope for this Italy-rooted chain; the 1880-1918 years of the panel unit are routed here as `back_cast` rather than as observation of an Italian polity that did not exist. The two provincial rows ITA-ITH1-1919-2025 and ITA-ITH2-1919-2025 are its parts, coexisting with it, not its successors.

## Decisions

### d-region-not-bolzano

**The panel's ITH1 is the whole region, and routes here**

The land total separates the readings cleanly: Bolzano alone is 0.54 of it, Bolzano plus Trento is 1.0001 of it. `juan-subnational` `ITH1` now routes 1919-2023 here as observation and 1880-1918 here as `back_cast`.

### d-panel-slugs-only

**The official NUTS slugs keep ITH1 = Bolzano**

The `eurostat-nuts2013`, `eurostat-nuts2016` and `jrc-nuts2016` rows for `ITH1` are left on ITA-ITH1-1919-2025. In those code systems ITH1 is the Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano/Bozen, and data genuinely coded to it must keep landing on Bolzano.

## Open questions

### oq-1919-1923-extent

**Should the 1919-1923 years carry the pre-1923 extent?**

If a source ever reports Venezia Tridentina at its 1919-1923 limits, the three communes moved to Belluno in 1923 would have to be added back for those years. The panel does not need it -- its land total is the modern region in every year -- so the polygon is not split here.
