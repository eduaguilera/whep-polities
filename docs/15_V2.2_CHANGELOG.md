## 1. Polygon Fixes

### FIX: OTT-1800-1912 polygon override
- **Problem**: OTT-1800-1912 (Ottoman Empire) had a small CShapes-Europe polygon
  (~64 deg2, only Balkans+Anatolia subset), while TUR-1800-1912 had the correct
  large CShapes 2.0 polygon (~283 deg2, full Ottoman territory including Levant,
  Mesopotamia, Arabia, North Africa).
- **Root cause**: The unified polygon builder tries CShapes-Europe first, and
  CShapes-Europe has a match for "Ottoman Empire" — but it's only the European
  portion.
- **Fix**: R/17 copies TUR-1800-1912's geometry to OTT-1800-1912.
- **polygon_source**: Changed to `CShapes 2.0 (via TUR-1800-1912)`

### FIX: DRO-1800-1982 Dronning Maud Land polygon created
- **Problem**: Norwegian Antarctic territorial claim had no polygon.
- **Fix**: Constructed sector polygon: 20 deg W to 44 deg 38' E, 60 deg S to 90 deg S.
- **polygon_source**: `Constructed (Antarctic sector claim)`
- **Severity**: LOW (minor territory, no trade relevance)

### FIX: NEU-1800-1982 Neutral Zone polygon created
- **Problem**: Saudi-Kuwait Neutral Zone (Uqair Protocol 1922) had no polygon.
- **Fix**: Constructed rectangular polygon from approximate Uqair Protocol boundaries.
- **polygon_source**: `Constructed (Uqair Protocol 1922 boundaries)`
- **Severity**: LOW

## 2. New Subnational Entries

### Japan 47 Prefectures (ADD)
- **Count**: 47 entries
- **Code pattern**: `JP{XX}-1888-2025` (e.g., `JP01-1888-2025` for Hokkaido)
- **Start year**: 1888 (Meiji prefectural reorganization)
- **Parent**: JPN-1800-2025
- **Source**: GADM 3.6 admin-1
- **Boundary stability**: 5/5 (unchanged since 1888)
- **Rationale**: Japan is the 4th-largest economy, major silk/tea exporter in
  19th century. Previously had only 1 entry for 226 years with 0 subnational.
  Identified as Tier 2 structural weakness in gap analysis.
- **Requires**: GADM 3.6 GeoPackage at configured path

### UK 4 Nations (ADD)
- **Count**: 4 entries
- **Codes**: GBENG-1800-2025, GBSCT-1800-2025, GBWLS-1800-2025, GBNIR-1921-2025
- **Parent**: GBR-1921-2025
- **Source**: GADM 3.6 (dissolved from admin-1 counties/regions)
- **Boundary stability**: 5/5 (England/Scotland boundary since 1237 Treaty of York;
  Wales since Laws in Wales Acts 1535-1542; NI since 1921 partition)
- **Rationale**: UK was the center of global trade 1800-1950 but had 0 subnational
  entries. Four nations are the minimum meaningful subdivision.
- **Requires**: GADM 3.6 GeoPackage at configured path

## 3. New Historical/Interwar Entries

### Saar Territory (ADD: SAA-1920-1935)
- **Type**: mandate
- **Period**: 1920-1935 (16 years)
- **Polygon**: GADM 3.6 Saarland (proxy — boundaries very similar)
- **Historical context**: League of Nations mandate under the Treaty of Versailles.
  Governed by a League commission. Returned to Germany by plebiscite on Jan 13,
  1935 (90.7% voted for reunification with Germany).
- **Previously**: Not in database. Identified in polygon accuracy audit Section 9.1.

### Memel Territory (ADD: MEM-1920-1923)
- **Type**: mandate
- **Period**: 1920-1923 (4 years)
- **Polygon**: GADM 3.6 Klaipeda County, Lithuania (proxy)
- **Historical context**: Separated from Germany by Treaty of Versailles. French-
  administered League mandate. Seized by Lithuania in Klaipeda Revolt (Jan 1923).
  Formalized by Klaipeda Convention (1924).
- **Previously**: Not in database. Identified in polygon accuracy audit Section 9.1.

### Free City of Fiume (ADD: FIU-1920-1924)
- **Type**: historical
- **Period**: 1920-1924 (5 years)
- **Polygon**: Constructed point buffer (~28 km2 around Rijeka)
- **Historical context**: Created by Treaty of Rapallo (1920). Annexed by Italy
  via Treaty of Rome (1924). Now Rijeka, Croatia.
- **Previously**: Not in database. Identified in polygon accuracy audit Section 9.1.

### Bogd Khanate of Mongolia (ADD: MNG-1911-1921)
- **Type**: historical
- **Period**: 1911-1921 (11 years)
- **Polygon**: CShapes 2.0 (earliest Mongolia polygon)
- **Historical context**: Bogd Khan declared independence from Qing China in
  December 1911 during the Xinhai Revolution. De facto independent until 1921
  Soviet-backed revolution established the Mongolian People's Republic.
- **Previously**: 10-year gap where Mongolia existed as independent state but had
  no entry. CShapes starts Mongolia at 1921. Identified in polygon accuracy audit
  Section 9.2.
- **Predecessor/successor**: Linked to next Mongolia entry in database.

### India Aggregate (ADD: IND-1800-2025)
- **Type**: aggregate
- **Polygon**: Modern India sovereign polygon
- **Rationale**: India is a top-10 trade country but had no aggregate entry for
  continuous trade data linkage. Gap analysis Section 2 recommended this.

## 6. Remaining Work (Not Addressed in v2.2)

### Requires manual download (HGIS Germany)
- **~15 German pre-unification states** use inaccurate GADM modern Lander
  boundaries. HGIS Germany project has accurate 1820-1914 historical GIS data.
- **Download**: NYU Spatial Data Repository (search "ghgis" at geo.nyu.edu) or
  email Harvard (`hgl_ref@hulmail.harvard.edu`). Automated download is broken
  (Harvard GeoServer proxy decommissioned).
- **Integration**: Would require a new R/18 script similar to R/13 (CHGIS).

### Known polygon accuracy limitations (documented, no fix available)
- ETH-1800-1889: 2-3x too large (post-Menelik polygon back-projected)
- EGY-1800-1899: Missing 60% territory for 1820-1882 (Egypt controlled Sudan)
- ZAN-1800-1890: Missing 90% territory (mainland coastal empire)
- CHN-1800-1912: Missing ~10-15% (Outer Mongolia)

These are documented in `docs/08_POLYGON_ACCURACY_AUDIT.md`. Fixing them requires
either new polygon sources or manual digitization from historical maps.

Note: Paine et al. (2024) data is already integrated for ETH, EGY, and MOR, but
those polygons represent the pre-colonial state boundaries, not the full
historical territory at different time periods. A more complete fix would require
period-specific polygons.

### Verification backlog
- 235 entries remain UNVERIFIED (primarily 137 African historical/colonial)
- Africa has worst verification rate at 52.7%
